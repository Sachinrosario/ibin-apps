## Sasya Sampada – Version 1 – Original (1.0.0)

**Sasya Sampada** (Digitized Inventory of Plant Resources of India – Medicinal & Economically Important Plants) was planned as a program of its own and later merged into Jeeva Sampada. It now comes both ways: inside Jeeva Sampada as before, and as this separate program. Both are built from the same rebuild, so a fix to one reaches the other.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside (the original screens)
- **Species Details** menu: Vernacular Names/Common Names, Scientific Names, Species List By Major Category, Criteria Search through DataBase, Criteria Search through Webpages, Exit; **Credits**.
- The ten buttons for a plant: Taxonomic Hierarchy, Common Names, Synonym Data (the floras that list it), Distribution Data (thematic grid map), HTML Files (2,600 original plant pages), Images (969 pictures), Accession Details, Uses and Actions, Pest Links and Patents (26,900 patents). 1,956 plants in 11 major categories.

Every menu, dialog and button was tested on a computer screen and on a phone screen before this release, and all 1,956 plants were opened on every window.
