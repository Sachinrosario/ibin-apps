## Sasya Bharathi – Version 2 – Updated names (2.0.2)

Version 1 of **Sasya Bharathi (India)** (2004-05) with the names brought up to date. The screens, flow and the 2005 data are unchanged; today's details are added in brackets and in a small card.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **Current names in brackets** in every list and title, from the Catalogue of Life (2026): for example *Abelia triflora (Zabelia triflora)*. 13,375 of the 30,959 names of 2005 have a different current name or are now treated at species level.
- **Current families in brackets** for 49 families of 2005 that are no longer used, for example PAPILIONACEAE (Fabaceae), LABIATAE (Lamiaceae), ASCLEPIADACEAE (Apocynaceae).
- **Taxonomic Hierarchy** tab: a card with the name in 2005, the Catalogue of Life name, author and status, the family now, the **IUCN Red List** category now (1,329 names are threatened or extinct: 148 CR, 338 EN, 459 VU, plus NT, DD, EX, EW), and **DNA records**: GenBank sequences and COI barcodes with links to GenBank and BOLD Systems.
- `name-changes.csv`: every 2005 name with its current name, status, family, IUCN category and GenBank counts.
- 4,760 names of 2005 are known to the Catalogue of Life only at genus level (old or misspelt names); they are shown without a bracket.

### What is fixed in this version
- The lists of families, genera, species and sub-species are now drawn by the program itself, so they work the same on phones, tablets and computers (on phones they had become drop-down boxes).
- Clicking between two districts of a state now selects the state.
- "To List Correct & Synonym Names" has an Open button (for touch screens, where there is no double-click).
- Every screen and button was tested before this release: 69 checks on a computer screen and on a phone screen.
- On the IBIN web site, the plant records and pictures are read straight from their shared copy (faster).
