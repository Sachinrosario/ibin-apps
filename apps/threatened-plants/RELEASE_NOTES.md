## Threatened Plants of Western Ghats – Version 1 – Original (1.0.1)

The title-bar buttons now work: minimise hides the window behind a button at the bottom, the square button switches full screen on and off, and the close button on the opening screen closes the program.

A faithful rebuild of the **RET Kit, A Field Guide to Threatened Plants of Western Ghats** (Prof. K.N. Ganeshaiah, School of Ecology and Conservation, University of Agricultural Sciences, Bangalore; ©2015–2016). The screens, wording, pictures and matching rules are taken from the original program, and were checked against it running on Windows.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- **Choose Characters**: click the tree (Habit, Leaf, Floral Characters, Floral Parts, Fruit, Habitat, Conservation Status) and pick thumbnails from 26 characters.
  - Values within one character combine with OR; values across characters combine with AND.
  - The program fills in every character the remaining species share.
  - Characters that can no longer narrow the list are greyed.
- **Character pictures**: the small blue button on a thumbnail shows its photographs.
- **Species Details**: a detail sheet for each of the 101 species.
- **Species Search**: by family, genus and species.

### Differences from the 2015 program
- **Clear** works on every screen. In the original it closes the program with "Run-time error 9" when used on a character screen.
- **The first thumbnail** of a screen can be combined with others. The original dropped or ignored it in a multiple choice.
- **Floral Parts**: when no flower part can narrow the list, clicking Floral Parts does nothing. The original left the window half-drawn.
- **All 101 species sheets open.** The original could not open eight of them, because their folder names contain a different kind of space.
- **Pictures**: they open in the program's own viewer instead of the Windows photo viewer.
- **Opening screen**: carries the credit lines and the version number.
