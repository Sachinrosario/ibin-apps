Identified future needs

 

The future plan is suggested keeping in view the demand of fashion accessories, furnishings & draping, improvements of economically backward sections of the society along with the presentations of nature’s wealth.

 

1.          Establishment of luxuriant germplasm with diverse floral and faunal gene pool stock required for genetic improvement.

2.          Improvement in the productivity through physiological, biochemical, genetical and biotechnological approach should be attempted.

3.          Biotechnological Research on synchronization of moth emergence and higher fecundity should be kept on top priority.

4.          Studies on tasar silkworm genomics should be taken up in order to know the genetic repertoire and genes responsible for economic traits and resistant to diseases.

5.          Thrust on domestication of tasar silkworm should be continued to get better cocoon yield. Artificial diet studies are required to be initiated.

6.          In order to arrest declining population of tasar silkworm ecoraces conservation programme should be taken up on top priority. In this connection education on conservation of nature’s wealth along with utilization of tasar may be provided to tribal of tasar zone through need based training programme.

7.          To improve the economic conditions of the weaker section of the society, strengthening of seed organization, extension mechanism and marketing sector are required to be taken up on top priority.

8.          Research and on utilization of by products and non-textile use of tasar pupae and moths for the production of pharmaceutical products etc. should be taken up.

9.          Post cocoon technology sector of tasar is required to be given priority with   special reference to product diversification.
