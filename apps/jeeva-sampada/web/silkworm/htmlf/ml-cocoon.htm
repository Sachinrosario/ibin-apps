Cocoon production

 

          Presently, in India, 1,28,491 tones of reeling cocoons are produced, of which more than 98% cocoons are contributed by the five traditional silk producing states viz., Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu and Jammu and Kashmir. Of the total cocoons produced, 95% is either from multivoltine x multivoltine or multivoltine x bivoltine hybrids whereas only 5% cocoons are produced from bivoltine hybrids.

 

 

          The cost involved in silkworm rearing (cocoon production) directly governs the overall income of the sericulturists. The economics of production of  bivoltine (CSR) hybrid and multi x bivoltine hybrid worked out at CSRTI, Mysore (Hiriyanna et al., 2002) indicated that mulberry leaf was found to be the major input in silkworm rearing accounting for  Rs. 2157.03/100 dfls for CSR (bivoltine) hybrids and  Rs.1560.26 for multi x bi hybrid. The total cost incurred on silkworm rearing was worked out  to br Rs.6044.25 for CSR hybrid  and Rs. 4475.32 for multi x bi hybrid. The cost benefit ratio was higher (1:1.92) for CSR hybrid compared to multi x bi hybrid (1:1.35).
