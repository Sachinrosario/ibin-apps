Seed Production

 

          Production and supply of quality seed (disease free layings) in required quantity  is one of  the  most  important  prerequisites  of  tasar  silk  industry. 

Variability in Tasar Silkworm Seed

At present tasar silkworm sector (CSB units and state government agencies) is unable to fulfill the demand of seed due to various reasons.  By combined efforts CSB and state agencies produce only one fourth of the total requirement leaving a wide gap in Dfls demand & supply.

 

          Contribution of CSB units and production of total quantity of quality silkworm seed is depicted in the following table which indicates that highest number of Dfls were supplied by CSB units during 1993-94 (17.42 lakh Dfls).  Thereafter, there is a decline except in 1997-98 and 2001-02 when the dfls supply reached to the tune of more than 15 lakhs.
