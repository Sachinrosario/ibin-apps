GLOSSARY:

 

Balkal   

:

Yarn produced from peduncle waste on Ambar Charkha

Bivoltine

:

Generation twice in a year

Boil-off loss

:

Loss of sericin during the cocoon cooking process

Denier

:

   Weight of filament (g)       x  9000

Length of silk filament (m)

Diamond woven silk

:

Silk woven as regular twill like which is reversed horizontally and a zig zag effect is formed and when the course of this zig zag is reversed vertically, a diamond is developed.

Drafting

:

Process of inserting lengthwise parallel yarns of warp through healed eyes as well as reed

Elongation

:

Ratio of the Extension of length at breaking point  to the Original length of the yarn

Ganda

:

Local unit of tasar cocoons in number

Ghicha

:

 Yarn drawn and spun by hand on inverted pitcher out of waste tasar cocoons without any twist

Grainage

:

Sum total of activities leading to production of quality seed

Hyaline

:

Transparent portion of eye spot of tasar moth

Instar

:

Stage in life cycle of silkworm

Jacquard

:

Accessory of loom for making variety of designs and motifs

Jhuri

:

Yarn spun out of uncleaned tasar waste without subjecting it to opening process

Kahan

:

Unit of tasar cocoons in number

Katia

:

 Yarn spun out of tasar waste after opening and cleaning on pedal operated spinning machine

Moultinism

:

Number of moults undergone by the silkworm during the life cycle

Neatness

:

Defects which are less than the minor defects of the cleanliness (it is a quality parameter)

Organzine silk

:

Silk fabric woven out of highly twisted warp and weft yarns giving specific look and feel to the woven silk

Plain woven silk

:

Silk woven by interlacement of any two continuous threads of either warp or weft in the exactly contrary manner to each other with every thread in each categories present alternately throughout the fabric

Peduncle waste

:

A sizeable quantity of silk waste obtained from cocoon peduncle which is cut and separated before cooking of cocoons for reeling purpose. These peduncles after degumming get converted into peduncle silk

Pon

:

Local unit of tasar cocoons in number

Raw Silk

:

Ratio of silk weight to the total weight of the cocoons

Reelability

:

Percentage in ratio of length of non-breakable filament to the entire length of cocoon filament

Renditta

:

Quantity of cocoons required to produce one kilogram of silk

Spun silk

:

Yarn produced out of staple fibres after drawing and twisting either manually or mechanically

Spinning

:

Spinning is the process for formation of yarn by a combination of drawing twisting and winding operation applied to prepare fibre mass

Silk Grade

:

Cumulative quality of silk (4A to E)

Silk recovery

:

Silk recovered after reeling   X  100

    Cocoon shell weight

Tenacity

:

Weight in grams required to break one denier of silk filament and expressed as tenacity g/d

Trivoltine

:

Generation thrice in a year

Univoltine

:

Generation once in a year

Warping

:

Process of parallelization of  lengthwise yarn for fabric weaving after passing the parallel lengthwise yarn through heald and reed

Winding

:

Process of transferring silk yarn from hank onto double flanged bobbins for further preparatory processes
