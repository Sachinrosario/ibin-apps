Conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity presupposes a reliable data base and documentation of bio-diversity available in the country along with its eco-system and natural habitat.  It is laudable that the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India has launched a nation wide programme to document the available bio-diversity of the country covering the entire fauna and flora for effective conservation and sustainable and efficient use of this national heritage.  As a part of this national programme, Central Sericultural Research and Training Institute, Mysore has been rightly assigned the task of preparing an inventory of the natural resources of silkworms of the country. This project  proposes to document relevant information on different types of silk being produced in India, besides developing a database for indigenous races, improved varieties and commercial hybrids being exploited in the country.  The Director, CSR&TI and the team at Mysore have taken pains to collect and compile the information   on all the five types of silk i.e., Mulberry, Tasar, Oak Tasar, Eri and Muga has been compiled with the support of other Sericulture Research Institutes.  Besides, attempt is also made to provide useful information on all aspects of sericulture industry, detailed information of races, improved strains and also the hybrid combinations of all the five types of silk. This inventory will serve as an authenticated document on the silkworm resources of India.  The database  developed  will in particular be useful to all the academicians and researchers working in the field.  The digitized inventory is the first of its kind and  would give authentic and precise information on silkworm wealth of India.

 

          I take this opportunity to compliment CSR & TI, the Department of Biotechnology and the Zoological Survey of India for this  nation wide effort. I am also happy to note that Central Silk Board have been able to develop an excellent database which would help in protecting our rich sericulture resources.  Wishing all success.
