Import & Export of silk

 

          Looking to the international silk trade status, it is found that today China is the largest supplier of raw silk to the international market. India by contrast  has become the world’s largest importer of silk (7000 tonnes, Silk Review of ITC, 1997). India is also able to increase the export in the recent years. During 1998-99 the total export of silk fabric was around 1250 crores as against 990 crores in the preceding year. To increase the export of these fabrics in the international market, India needs to import some quantity of  quality silk.

 

          India’s share in the world silk export market has been fluctuating in the range of 1% to 4.6% .  India exports high fashion items.  Traditional items like sarees and carpets have given way to garments and made-ups. Today’s export range includes Jackets, Lingerie, Thermal wear, Bridal wear, Sand washed silk, boxer shorts, ties, scarves, etc. Traditional weaving strengths  were replaced by production of need based, market oriented products. Handloom products are giving way to machine woven. Selling fabrics has shifted to selling high fashion sequined ladies gowns. USA is the largest importer of Indian silk products followed by Germany. The greatest asset which Indian silk exporter has the high value and low volume. India has a 47% share in the U.K. USA imports a large quantity of silk garments specially ladies evening wear, notably from Delhi. Germany is a fabric consuming centre  both as dress materials and as materials for furnishings. Japan, traditionally has been importing silk scarves from India. Singapore and Hong Kong continued to be the  major trading centres for Indian silk. The former USSR is a potential market. South Africa  is becoming a large market for Indian silk.
