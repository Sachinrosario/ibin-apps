Source of Silkworm Germplasm

 

          India is the only country in the world where the germplasm of silkworm Antheraea mylitta Drury is being maintained and evaluated. Central Tasar Research and Training Institute, Nagri, Ranchi-835303, Jharkhand, India (CTR&TI) and its different Regional Tasar Research Stations (RTRSs) have the following ecoraces in the form of germplasm material for tropical tasar crop improvement:
