Gap areas identified

 

        Though the newly developed silkworm breeds show 20-25% more productivity, only a few breeds are in the field for commercial exploitation. Presently, the farmers have realized only 50% of the yield potential. There is still scope for further improvement in almost all the rearing activities. The main reasons for low yield in the field are mentioned below.

 

Ø      Lack of knowledge by the farmers/ extension workers about characteristics of new breeds/hybrids.

Ø      Poor awareness and non-adoption of new technology developed for the improvement of silkworm crop.

Ø      Higher pathogen load in the rearing house due to inadequate disinfection and overlapping rearing.

Ø      Lack of proper extension support required for introduction of new breeds/hybrids in the field.

Ø      Lack of  scientific quality based cocoon price fixation system in the cocoon market.

Ø      Lack of reeling facilities particularly for bivoltine cocoons.

Ø      Farmers’ reluctance to test newly developed breeds / hybrids without any incentives, insurance etc.

Ø      Lack of genuine hybrid preparation by grainages specially for cross breeds, which results in cocoon size and cocoon colour variation.
