Pricing of tasar cocoons

 

It is very much essential to fix the cocoon price at national level so that cocoon growers can get remunerative cocoon price and cocoon consumers can purchase the cocoons according to the quality  of cocoons. For this purpose a price fixation formula according to quality of cocoons has been established both for reeling and spinning cocoons. The price fixation of the cocoons is done by Tropical Tasar Advisory Committee (TTAC) under the Chairmanship of the Director, CTR&TI, Ranchi. All state governments are taking initiative to implement the minimum support price for the cocoons in the cocoon markets based on the existing yarn price.

 

For transaction of tasar cocoons in different states various units are being used. Uniform counting system at national level should be implemented and metric Kahan i.e. 1000 numbers/unit will be very suitable for this purpose.
