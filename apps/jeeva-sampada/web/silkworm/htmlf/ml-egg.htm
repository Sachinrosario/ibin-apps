Egg Production

 

Quality silkworm seed production has been one of the priority sectors of Indian Silk Industry for the last three decades. The country’s present  requirement is about 30 crores (dfls) of commercial seed and Karnataka, the major silk producer  alone needs about 13 crores of dfls per year. The major production of the seed (about 76.4%) comes from licensed seed producers (LSPs) while NSSP contributes around 6.3% of the total seed production and DOS of respective states contribute the remaining 17.3 %.
