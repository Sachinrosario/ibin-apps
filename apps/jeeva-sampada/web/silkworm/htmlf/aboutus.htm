Central Silk Board  (CSB) established in 1948 under the Act of Parliament, looks after the development of Sericulture Industry in the Country and  advises the Central Government on various issues related to the Industry and accordingly framing and modifying the policies. CSB with its network of Research Institutes, Regional stations and Extension units through out the country is catering to the needs of all the five types of silk sectors.  The R & D efforts of CSB has elevated India to the second position among the silk producing countries in the world with an annual production of 15,000 MT of raw silk.

 

          The main functions assigned to the Board under the Act are

 

1.          Promoting the development of silk industry by such measures as it thinks fit.

2.          Undertaking, assisting and encouraging scientific, technological and economic research.

3.          Devising means for improved methods of mulberry cultivation, silkworm rearing, developing and distributing healthy silkworm seeds, improving  methods of silk reeling, spinning of cocoon & silk waste, improving the quality and production of raw silk, by making its compulsory to test and grade all the raw silk in silk testing and conditioning houses before marketing.

4.          Improving the marketing of raw silk.

5.          Collection of statistics.

6.          Advising the Central Government on all matters relating to the development of silk industry including import and export of raw silk.

7.          Preparing and furnishing reports relating to the silk industry as may be required by the Central Government.

 

For providing the required R & D and technological support to the industry the silk producing states the following Institutes have been established.

 

Mulberry Silk

 

µ     Central Sericultural Research & Training Institute,  Mysore – To cater to the needs of the southern states (Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala), Gujarat and Maharashtra.

µ     Central Sericultural Research & Training Institute, Berhampore – To cater to the needs of the eastern region covering the states of North east, West Bengal, Bihar, Orissa and Madhya Pradesh.

µ     Central Sericultural Research & Training Institute, Pampore – It caters to the needs of the temperate and sub-tropical sericulture in the states of Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Uttaranchal and Uttar Pradesh.

µ     Central Sericultural Germplasm Resources Center, Hosur– Established for maintenance of mulberry and silkworm germplasms for their utilization in development of improved genotypes/  breeds.

µ     Seribiotech Research Laboratory, Bangalore – To provide information on molecular aspects of mulberry and silkworm facilitating in improving silk productivity.

 

Non-mulberry Silks

 

µ     Central Tasar Research & Training Institute, Ranchi - To provide technological support to tasar industry in the states of Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Orissa, Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra and NE states.

µ     Central Muga and Eri Research & Training Institute, Ladoigarh, Jorhat – It caters to the R & D needs of the muga and eri silk industries in Assam and NE regions.

 

Silkworm Seed support

 

µ     National Silkworm, Seed Project, Bangalore - Production of quality disease free layings under hygienic condition for better harvest of cocoon crop at farmers level through out the country.

µ     Silkworm Seed Technology Laboratory, Bangalore – To provide R & D support in production of quality silkworm seeds.

 

Silk Technology support

 

µ     Central Silk Technological Research Institute, Bangalore -      With its sub-units all over the country, it provides R & D support to the reeling, spinning and weaving sector of the silk Industry.

 

 

CSR&TI,  MYSORE

 

 

The Central Sericultural Research and Training Institute (CSR&TI), Mysore, being the premier Sericultural Institute in the country, par excellence, is engaged in carrying out S & T activities in the field of mulberry sericulture since four decades.  Over the years, the Institute with its well developed R & D infrastructure for advanced research, has provided commendable support for enhancement of silk productivity and quality and also the generation of higher income for the farmers, of the Southern peninsula.  The Institute could make breakthroughs in tropical sericulture technology and has acclaimed recognition at international level as a lead institution for promotion of tropical sericulture technology.  The focus of the Institute has been towards development of low cost and pro-environment technologies to suit different levels of the rural society.  Of late, the Institute has adopted the strategy of popularizing the technologies of mulberry cultivation and silkworm rearing as package to achieve the benefits of the same under field conditions.  The active involvement of the DOS of southern states in this venture is praise worthy.  The role of the Institute towards generation of trained human resource has always been on high pedestals, both at domestic and international levels. So far, it has trained over 14,000 persons in different aspects of sericulture science and technology including about 500 students from abroad.

 
Vision

 

To be a model organization second to none for providing R & D services in sericulture to rural development & upliftment and generation of human resources both at Domestic and International level

 

Mission

 

     I.       Generate Pro-environment, Cost-effective and Pro-women technologies for effective resource utilization and improvement of silk production in the country.

 

 II.       Work for the improvement of field productivity through low cost innovative technologies for state-of-art services as well as for overall improvement of socio-economic conditions of sericulturists.
