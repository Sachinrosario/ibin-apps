Taxonomic Details

Common Name: *Bengal Bush-Lark.

 

 

Scientific Name: Mirafra assamica.


Synonyms: Rufous-winged Bushlark, Bush Lark, Rufous-winged Lark.

Back to Top


Family: Alaudidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Resident. Mainly plains and plateaux of N, E and S subcontinent.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Stout bill, short tail, and rufous on wing; rufous-buff on outer tail feathers, pronounced dark spotting on breast, dark spotting on ear-coverts and malar region, and pale rufous-buff wash to underparts.

Back to Top

 

Voice: The call of M. a. assamica consists of a series of variable, thin, high-pitched short notes, whereas that of M. a. assamica is typically a short, high-pitched trill.

 

Habitat: Stony scrub and fallow cultivation.

Habits: Generally similar to those of Indian Bushlark. M. a. assamica is typically terrestrial.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Grass- and weed seeds, weevils, tiny beetles and other insects.



Status and distribution: Resident. From Punjab, India, east to NE India and Bangladesh and south through much of the E and S peninsula and Sri Lanka.
