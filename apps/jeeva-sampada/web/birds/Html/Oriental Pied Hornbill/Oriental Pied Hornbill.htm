Taxonomic Details

Common Name: *Oriental Pied Hornbill

 

Scientific Name: Anthracoceros albirostris.


Synonyms: Indian Pied Hornbill, Pied Hornbill.

Back to Top


Family: Bucerotidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Resident. Himalayan foothills, NE India and Bangladesh.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Cylindrical casque with  black patch at tip, mainly black tail with white tips to outer feathers, and pale blue throat patch. Female has smaller casque, with black at posterior end, and black at tip of bill. 

Back to Top

 

Voice: A variety of loud, shrill squeals and raucous cackles, including a loud cackling kleng-keng, kek-kek-kek-kek-kek-kek which rises in pitch and volume.

Habitat: Open forest, groves and large fruit trees near villages.

Habits: Often feeds in parties. Quite shy. Mainly arboreal, but regularly feeds on the ground on fallen fruit, termites and other insects; sometimes hawks flying termites.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Wild figs, drupes and berries. Also lizards, snakes, and any other small animal that can be come by.



Status and distribution: Resident, subject to local movements, depending on fruiting season. From Haryana east through the Himalayan foothills to Arunachal Pradesh; NE and E India, and Bangladesh.
