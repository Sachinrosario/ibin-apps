Taxonomic Details

Common Name : Eurasian Tree Pipit.

 

 

Scientific Name: Anthus trivialis.


Synonyms: Tree Pipit.

Back to Top


Family: Motacillidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Resident and winter visitor. Breeds in NW Himalayas; widespread in winter; unrecorded in Sri Lanka.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Buffish-brown to greyish ground color to upperparts, and buffish fringes to greater coverts, tertials and secondaries.  

Back to Top

 

Voice: Call is typically a harsher teez than Olive-backed's; song is louder and far-carrying, ending in finch-like trill, chik-chik....chia-chia-wich-wich-tsee-a-tsee-a tsee-a.

Habitat: Breeds on grassy slopes at treeline; winters in fallow cultivation and open country. 

Habits: Habitually perches in trees, and readily walks along branches; usually flies into trees if disturbed. In song flight, the male flutters upwards a short distance from the top of a tree, then glides on stiff.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Insects and grass- and weed seeds; flower nectar.



Status and distribution: Resident, short-ranged migrant and winter visitor. Breeds in NW Himalayas from Pakistan east to N Himachal Pradesh; winters widely in the subcontinent, except parts of the Himalayas, northwest, northeast and south.
