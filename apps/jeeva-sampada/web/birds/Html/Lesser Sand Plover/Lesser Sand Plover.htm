Taxonomic Details

Common Name: Lesser Sand Plover.

 

Scientific Name: Charadrius mongolus.


Synonyms: Mongolian Plover.

Back to Top


Family: Charadriidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Breeds in N Himalayas, India; winters on coasts of subcontinent.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Larger and longer-legged than Kentish, lacking white hindcollar. In flight, feet do not usually extend beyond tail and white wing-bar is narrower across primaries.

Back to Top

 

Voice: A hard chitik, chi-chi-chi and kruit-kruit, which are rather short and sharp.

 

Habitat: Breeds in high-altitude semi-desert on drying-out edges of lakes and marshes. Winters on coasts, rocky and sandy shores; rarely on lakes and rivers inland on passage.

 

Habits: Gait and feeding behaviour typical of other plovers. 

 

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Chiefly small crabs, sandhoppers and marine worms. 



Status and distribution: Breeds in Ladakh, Lahul and Sikkim, India, above 3400 m and possibly also in Baltistan, Pakistan; winters on coasts of the subcontinent. Rare on passage inland.
