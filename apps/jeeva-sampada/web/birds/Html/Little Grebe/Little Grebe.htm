Taxonomic Details

Common Name : Little Grebe

Scientific Name:Tachybaptus ruficollis


Synonyms: Podiceps ruficollis

Back to Top


Family: Podicipedidae

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Resident, subject to local or long-distance movements depending on water conditions.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Small size. In breeding plumage, has rufous cheeks and neck sides and yellow patch at base of bill. In non-breeding plumage has brownish-buff cheeks and flanks.

 

Back to Top

Voice: A drawn-out whinning trill in breeding season, and a sharp wit wit in alarm.

 

Habitat: Mainly fresh water wetlands.

Habits: Often keeps singly or in pairs among aquatic vegetation when breeding; in the non- breeding season easily seen on water; sometimes in small loose flocks. Can often be detected by its bubbling trill. Swims buoyantly.

Back to Top

Food preference:  Fish, frogs, tadpoles, crustacea, molluscs, aquatic insects, etc.



Status and distribution:  Throughout much of the subcontinent, excepts parts of the northwest, northeast and Himalayas.
