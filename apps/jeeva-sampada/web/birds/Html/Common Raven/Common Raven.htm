Taxonomic Details

Common Name: Common Raven.

 

Scientific Name: Corvus corax.


Synonyms: Raven.

Back to Top


Family: Corvidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Resident. Pakistan and NW India, and high Himalayas. 

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Very large; long and angular wings, prominent throat hackles, wedge-shaped tail, and deep resonant call. 

Back to Top

 

Voice: Typical call is a loud, deep, resonant, croaking wock..wock, quite different from that of other corvids; also has a high, knocking toc-toc-toc.

 

Habitat: Race subcorax in lowland desert and semi-desert; tibetanus in dry rocky areas above treeline.

 

Habits: Found singly, in pairs or in family parties in the breeding season, although has communal roosts and may gather in flocks at carcases or other rich food sources. Wanders in small flocks in winter. Walks with a wadding gait, and hops occasionally.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: All embracing: animal as well as vegetable matter - carrion. eggs and young or sickly birds, small mammals, lizards, fish, frogs, insects; ripening fruits and cereal grains; kitchen scraps and refuse.

 

Status and distribution: C. c. subcorax Pakistan and NW India. Resident, subject to local movements, from plains up to 600 m, occasionally higher.
