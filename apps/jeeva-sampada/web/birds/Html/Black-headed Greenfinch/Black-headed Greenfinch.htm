Taxonomic Details

Common Name : *Black-headed Greenfinch.


Scientific Name: Carduelis ambigua.


Synonyms: 

Back to Top


Family: Fringillidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Resident? N Arunachal Pradesh.  

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Yellow wing patches and tail sides, greenish mantle, greyish underparts. Male has dull black head.

Back to Top

Voice: Typical call is a jumbled, jingling sound, titutitu, titu-titu..titu-tittritititit etc, mixed with harder chututut ot jutututut, quiet, rising, buzzy jieuu and soft chu-chu etc.

 

Habitat: Open forest and shrubberies.

Habits: Very like those of Yellow-breasted Greenfinch.

             

Back to Top

Food preference: Seeds of many kinds, including wild hemp, sunflower, millet, rice, etc. Also berries and insects.



Status and distribution: Possibly resident in NE Arunachal Pradesh; very locally fairly common; 1300 m, 2800-3110 m, subject to altitudinal movements.
