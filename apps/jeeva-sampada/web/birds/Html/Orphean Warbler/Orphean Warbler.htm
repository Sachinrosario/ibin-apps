Taxonomic Details

Common Name: Orphean Warbler.

 

Scientific Name: Sylvia hortensis.


Synonyms: 

Back to Top


Family: Sylviinae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Summer visitor to Pakistan; winters mainly in India.   

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Larger and bigger-billed than Lesser Whitethroat. Adult has blackish crown, pale grey mantle, blackish tale, and pale iris. First-year has crown concolorous with mantle, with darker grey ear-coverts.

Back to Top

 

Voice: Song is a strong, varied, thrush-like warble; call is a tack or tchak, which is deeper and harder than calls of Lesser Whitethroat, and has a loud trrr rattle.

Habitat: Breeds on bush-covered hillsides; winters in scrub and groves.

Habits: Usually solitary. Has slower and more deliberate movements than other warblers. Carriage is slightly above the horizontal, and sometimes cocks its tail.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Mainly insects; flower-nectar and berries are taken in autumn and winter; also determined are seeds and a snail.

 

Status and distribution: Summer visitor to Baluchistan and NW Pakistan; winters mainly from Gujarat east to E Maharashtra and south to S Karnataka. Passage migrant.
