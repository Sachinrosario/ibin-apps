Taxonomic Details

Common Name: Comb Duck.

Scientific Name: Sarkidiornis melanotos.


Synonyms: 

Back to Top


Family: Anatidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Resident. Widespread in India, also Nepal lowlands and Bangladesh.   

Back to Top

Diagnostic features:  Whitish head, speckled with black, and whitish underparts. Upperwing and underwing blackish. Male has fleshy Comb.

 

Back to Top

 

Voice: Unlike the two whistling-ducks, generally silent, but sometimes utters low croak when flushed. 

 

Habitat: Lowland pools, jheels and irrigation tanks with plentiful aquatic vegetation in well-wooded country. 

Habits: Usually keeps in family parties or small flocks of 25-30. Feeds chiefly by grazing; also by wading and dabbling in the shallows. Perches readily in trees.

Back to Top

 

Food preference: Largely vegetarian: corms, shoots, and seeds of aquatic and marsh plants, and grains of wild and cultivated rice. Also water insects and their larvae; occasionally frogs and fish. 



Status and distribution: Resident from Gujarat east to Bangladesh, north to Haryana and south to N Kerala and N Tamil Nadu; locally migratory, depending on water conditions.
