Taxonomic Details

Common Name : Great Crested Grebe.

Scientific Name: Podiceps cristatus.


Synonyms:

Back to Top


Family: Podicipedidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Breeds in NW subcontinent; winter visitor to N Subcontinent. 

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Large and Slender-necked, with pinkish bill. Black crown does not extend to eye, and has white cheeks and foreneck in non-breeding plumage. 

Back to Top

Voice: At breeding sites, a harsh rolling aooorrr and chattering kek-kek.

 

Habitat: Lakes, reservoirs and coastal waters.

Habits: A typical grebe. Swims with body low in the water and neck held erect.

Back to Top

Food preference:  Fish, tadpoles, frogs, water insects, etc, with some vegetable matter.



Status and distribution: Winter visitor to the N subcontinent from Pakistan east to NE India, and south to Maharashtra and NE Andhra Pradesh.
