Taxonomic Details

Common Name : Sooty Shearwater. 

Scientific Name: Puffinus griseus.


Synonyms: 

Back to Top


Family: Procellaridae

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Vagrant and  Sri Lanka.

                                                                                                                                                Back to Top

Diagnostic features:  Sooty-brown, with whitish flash on underwing-coverts. Slim, pointed wings, and strong, deliberate flight action with stiff flapping and glides.

Back to Top

Voice: Nil information.

 

Habitat: Pelagic and offshore waters.

Habits: Often in large numbers with other seabirds; sometimes follow boats.

Back to Top

Food preference:  Not known



Status and distribution: Vagrant to coastal waters .
