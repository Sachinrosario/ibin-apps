Taxonomic Details

Common Name: Ruddy Turnstone.

 

Scientific Name: Arenaria interpres.


Synonyms: Turnstone.

Back to Top


Family: Scolopacidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Widespread winter visitor to coasts.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Short bill and orange legs. In flight, shows white stripe on wings and back and black tail. Juvenile has buff fringes to upperparts, and blackish breast.

Back to Top

 

Voice: A rolled trik-tuk-tuk-tuk or a shorter tuk-er-tuk, and when flushed may give sharp chick-ik or kuu.

Habitat: Rocky coasts; also tidal mudflats and sandy shores in Pakistan and Gulf of Kutch; rarely inland waters.

 

Habits: Often feeds in small parties. Runs actively on the shore, turns over pebbles and shells and flicks seaweed to catch prey sheltering beneath. Flight is strong and direct, usually low over the shore.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Molluscs, crustaceans, sandhoppers, and marine worms. Sand crabs with carapace up to c. 5 cm. across recorded elsewhere.



Status and distribution: Winter visitor, mainly to coasts of the subcontinent; first arrives in mid August, and most leave by mid May.
