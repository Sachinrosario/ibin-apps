Taxonomic Details

Common Name: White-breasted Waterhen.

 

Scientific Name: Amaurornis phoenicurus.


Synonyms:

Back to Top


Family: Rallidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Widespread resident.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: Adult has grey upperparts, and white face, foreneck and breast; undertail-coverts rufous-cinnamon. Juvenile has greyish face, foreneck and breast, and olive-brown upperparts. 

Back to Top

 

Voice: Usually silent except during the monsoon breeding season, when calls include a metallic krr-kwaak-kwaak, krr-kwaak-kwaak etc and a kook..kook..kook, usually preceded by loud roars, croaks and chuckles.

Habitat: Occurs wherever water is surrounded by some thick cover: reedy marshes, edges of flooded paddy-fields, ponds, ditches. Often seen in villages and in public parks in towns and cities.  

 

Habits: Similar to those of other rails, but is less shy than most of the family. Very noisy in the breeding season, sometimes calling throughout the night.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Insects and their larvae, molluscs, worms, seeds and shoots of marsh plants. 



Status and distribution: Resident throughout much of the subcontinent, except most of the northwest and Himalayas.
