Taxonomic Details

Common Name: Eurasian Curlew.

Scientific Name: Numenius arquata.


Synonyms: Curlew.

Back to Top


Family: Scolopacidae.

Back to Top

Seasonal distribution: Widespread winter visitor, mainly to coasts.

Back to Top

Diagnostic features: large size and long, curved bill. Rather plain head. underwing-coverts are white and rump/back mainly white; white ground colour to belly and vent.

Back to Top

 

Voice: Typically a mournful, rising cur-lew, and an anxious were-up when alarmed.

Habitat: Intertidal mudflats, tidal estuaries and creeks, mudbanks of mangroves; also uncommonly on large rivers and inland lakes.

 

Habits: Usually keeps singly or in small groups. Feeds by walking on mud and probing deeply, also by picking from the surface. Flocks often fly in V-formation. Frequently calls in flight.

 Back to Top

 

Food preference: Molluscs, crustaceans and sand crabs, mudskippers, insects, and occasionally berries.



Status and distribution: Widespread winter visitor, mainly on coasts; first arrives in early August, departing by May.
