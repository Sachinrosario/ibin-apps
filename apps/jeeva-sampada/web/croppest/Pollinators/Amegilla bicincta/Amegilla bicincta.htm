 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is not wide as thorax; ocelli in a triangle on the vertex; clypeus strongly produced and convex; labrum subquadrate, anterior margin rounded; tongue elongate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint short, second segment longest, 3-6 decreasing gradually in length; thorax ovate; forewing with marginal cell broad, rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, first and third subequal, second narrowed at apex, sometimes pentagonal; legs short, posterior tibiae and basal joints of tarsi in female densely pubescent; intermediate tarsi in some species with tufts of hairs on apical joint above claws; gaster with distinct transverse pubescent fasciae above basal gastral segments without fasciae, gastral segments four to five only with a fasciae (1).

Coloration: (♀.) Hairs greenish white; thorax with mixed whitish hair with bluish tinge, anterior marginal band a median stripe connecting that of anterior margin; entire dorsum of labrum except anterior margin and two basal lateral round spots creamy white, mandibles creamy white at base; wings transparent with dark brown venation.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster finely and densely punctate; clypeus flat; first labial palpi 4 times as long as second segment; mean number of hamuli on right and left wing 25, range 21-27 (1).
