English Name: Gram cutworm

Family: Noctuidae

Order: Lepidoptera

 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

A flammetra are much bigger in size, average wing expense in 56 mm. Its forewing have characteristic markings and smoky patches, two-thirds of the proximal areas being pale. On each wing, there is a semi circular spot below the pale area and a greyish brown kidney shaped spots towards the apical area. The caterpillars are dark grey or dull green measuring 40-50 mm. The pest is active from October-April in the plains and migrates to mountains in summer. In October, the moths lay eggs on the underside of leaves, on shoots, stems or in the soil, within 5-9 days of their life of 7-13 days. A female lays up to 980 eggs in her life time. The eggs hatch in 4-7 days during summer and 10-14 days during winter. Larvae are full fed in 4-7 weeks. The pupal stage lasts 12-15 days. the life cycle is completed in 7-11 weeks and there are generally two generations in a year (1).
