 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is triangular, Ocelli in an arch on vertex; antennae filiform; tongue acute; the labial palpi four segmented, basal joints as long as following two, apical three gradually decreasing in length; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint shortest, following joints gradually decreasing in length to apical one; mandibles bidentate; thorax ovate, legs moderately long, wings with radial cell narrowing towards apex, sharply acute, three sub marginal cells, first about as long as second and third combined, second small subquadrate; gaster ovate, apical segment with a longitudinal rim. Head either as long as broad or longer. It is small species measuring 7.31mm length; propodium rugose, some rugae reticulate.

Colouration: ♀. Black bee; body and legs covered with white hairs; clypeus covered with few long white hairs; head and scape covered with white plumose hairs; scape, pedicel and first two flagellar segments dark brown colour, remaining flagellar segments light brown colour; anterior and middle of mesothorax with few white, simple hairs; lateral sides of thorax and abdomen covered with white plumose hairs; wings transparent with dark brown venation; anterior margin of gastral segments end with white, short, plumose scale-like hairs.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster finely and closely punctate; labrum shiny, rectangular; clypeus coarsely punctate; supraclypeal area tubercle; parapsidal lines prominent; frontal line carinate; propodeum longitudinally rugose, rugae extending on entire dorsal surface, sometimes appear reticulate; gaster beneath covered with hairs; number of hamuli on right wing 5 and on left wing 7 (1).
