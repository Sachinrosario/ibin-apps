 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The Ceratina binghami's ocelli in a triangle on the vertex; antennae short, subclavate; mandibles stout, tridentate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi four segmented, basal three segments subequal, apical three grually decreasing in length; thorax ovate; forewing with the marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, second forming truncated triangle; gaster slightly narrowed at base; colour vary from black to brilliant metallic green; some species with maculations on face, thorax and gaster. Body coarsely pitted; mesonotun and abdomen without integumental white or yellow markings. Metabasitarsus without integumental white markings. Labrum entirely dark brown or black; yellow marking on fore femora occupying half length (1).

Colouration: ♀. Metallic dark green; labrum dark brown; clypeus yellow, marginated by black band; antennal scape, pedicel and first two flagellar segments black, remaining segments brown; pronotal lobe yellow; lower outer margin spot at apex, longitudinal stripe on outer margin of fore tibia, basal 1/2 on the outer margin of hind tibia yellow; wings transparent with dark brown venation; gasteral segments 2-4 with four black spots; intersegmental area of tergum black.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster coarsely and closely punctate; labrum rectangular, coarsely punctate; clypeus flat, coarsely, scattered punctate, inverted T shaped, anterior end with sharp edges; median carina present between antennae; mesothorax with five carinae; all gastral segments covered with few short hairs (1).
