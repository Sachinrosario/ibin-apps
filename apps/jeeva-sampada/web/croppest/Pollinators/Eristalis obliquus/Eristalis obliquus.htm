 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for more than half distance from vertex to frons, bare except for upper surface which is covered with short, erect, brown hairs; pale yellowish-green with individual facets brown or red, scattered, little more aggregated just behind frons; facets on upper half larger. Vertical triangle nearly equilateral, black with ocellar prominence conspicuously elevated bearing three large crimson ocelli, covered with erect black hairs. Frons, face and cheeks greyish yellow, covered with tomentum except for lower cheeks, top of facial tubercle, base of antennal prominence, centre of frontal triangle and two semicircular spots on eye margin just behind antennal prominence. Frons with longish, erect, black hairs, apical tips directed forwards; face with suberect, dense, shining, yellowish hairs directed downwards; cheeks with very short similar hairs, especially on top. Antennae pendulous, orange-yellow, first segment almost half length of second, both with few short spiny hairs at tip, dorsally; third segment more than twice length of first two together, brownish dorsally; arista bare, orange yellow, blackish at tip, longer than antenna. Occiput black with white tomentum and whitish hairs on lower side.

Thorax with mesonotum greyish-yellow with fine punctulation and four shining black longitudinal stripes subequal width placed almost equidistant from each other, the two median ones nearer together; posterior tips of outer stripes narrowed, almost reaching margin, those of median stripes broader, subtruncate, fading away well before margin, tips brownish. whole of dorsum with dense pile of reect, golden-yellow, longish hairs which are longest on humeri. Pleura greyish black, upper parts of mesopleuron and sternopleuron dusted with greyish-yellow, the whole covered with short, yellow pubescence. Scutellum honey-yellow, transperent, densely punctulate with shortish erect hairs on dorsum, longest on posterior margin, those in the centre black.

Wings hyaline, trichose; stigma fairly opaque, brownish on inner and outer ends, more extensive on the former; wing baase yellowish; squamae greyish with dense margin of greyish hairs closely appressed together. Halteres pale yellow.

Legs with all femora brown except at tips; fore and mid femora with dark brown median ring; hind femora entirely brownish black except tip which is paler; fore and mid tibiae yellowish on basal half, brown on apical half, hind tibiae bow shaped, yellow at basal tip, dark brown at apical, lighter on rest with a very pale median ring. Tarsi yellow, brownish at tips; hairs yellowish, shorter and golden yellow on tarsi and lower half of tibiae, black on fore femora, dorsal apical half of fore tibiae, ventral portion of hind tibiae, posterior tip of mid tibiae laterally black. Tarsi with black spiny hairs on each segment laterally and ventrally, longest on posterior corners. Claws yellow at base, balck at tip.

Abdomen triangular in shape, widest at base of tergite 2, narrowing towards apex. Tergite 1 pale yellow, tergite 2 darker yellow, brownish on margin; anterior margin broadly brownish especially at lateral corners, inconspicuous in middle extending medially but not reaching posterior margin; tergite 3 similar with median stripe broader; tergite 4 trapezoidal, anterior margin half as posterior, the tergite brownish, paler on posterior margin, anterior margin black, a pair of oval whitish spots placed obliquely, the distance between the slightly less than their length; hairs on lateral abdominal margin short, pale yellow throughout except on second segment where it is balck. Posterior corner of tergite 2 with a dense longish tuft of white hairs; hairs concolorous with background, longer in centre than at sides.

Tergite 9 and cerci normal in shape; surstylus with a median emargination on posterior margin and a hook; sternite 9 and superior lobe of usual shape with two hooks; sternite 9 and superior lobe of usual shape with two hooks and a median blunt process between them.

female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space half as wide at vertex as at level of median ocellus black, thence producted into a pair of black stripes, for a short distance; rest of frons and face as in male, frons darker; hairs on vertex, upper and lower borders of frons black, shorter and yellow in between.

Legs with hind femora black except at tip; hairs on legs more yellowish than anywhere else. Abdomen more elongate than in male, triangular, dark brown, anterior margin balck. Tergite 1 pale yellow, a wide median black patch on posterior margin not reaching anterior margin; tergite 2 with a basal broad yellow band somewhat constricted in centre, posterior corners brownish; tergites 3 and 4 with large, almost oval pair of whitish spots placed diagonally, their inner ends contiguous with the posterior margin, outer ends not reaching lateral margins; spots on tergites 3 more separated than those on tergite 4 in middle; tergite 5 triangular with a pair of whitish spots contiguous with basal margin at each corner; no black hairs on lateral margins (1).
