 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

An ocelli of Ceratina hieroglyphica  in a triangle on the vertex; antennae short, subclavate; mandibles stout, tridentate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi four segmented, basal three segments subequal, apical three grually decreasing in length; thorax ovate; forewing with the marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, second forming truncated triangle; gaster slightly narrowed at base; colour vary from black to brilliant metallic green; some species with maculations on face, thorax and gaster. Body finely punctuate; mesonotum and abdomen with integumental white or yellow markings (1).

Colouration: ♀: Black coloured with yellow marking; labrum dark brown with central rectangle yellow patch; clypeus yellow with transverse band above clypeus, stripe on each side broad basally, narrowed towards tip; a pair of yellow patch between antennae and ocelli; mesonotum with three stripes, median and lateral pairs in some bees absent; a broad stripe on the pronotum; center of scutellum with yellow patch; posterior end of femora and tibia with yellow patch; wings transparent with dark brown venation; 1st to 5th of gastral segments with yellow band, some times interrupted in the middle, 6th segment black.

Structure: Head with coarse, and scattered punctate; labrum rectangular; clypeus inverted T-shaped, flat, finely punctate; thorax and gaster finely and closely punctate; mesonotum with three carinae; mesonotum at centre shiny, smooth.

♂: Markings or stripes broader than ♀; labrum dark brown (1).
