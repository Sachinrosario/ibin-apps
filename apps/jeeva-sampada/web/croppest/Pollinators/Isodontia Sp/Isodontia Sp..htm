 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Identification: Medium-sized dark sphecids, often seen carrying grass to nests. C. auripes is distinctive with its brown legs.
Habitat: Open areas, fields, grass
Food: Adults take nectar
Life Cycle: Females make nest in a tree or other cavity, divide into sections and close with grass. They provision with orthoptera (Tettigoniidae or Gryllidae). This Grass-carrying Wasp is two generations per year (2).
