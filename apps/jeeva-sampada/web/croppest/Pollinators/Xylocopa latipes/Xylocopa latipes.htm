 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is subrounded; Ocelli in a triangle below vertex; antennae geniculate, scape sometimes dialated or incrassate; mandibles short, stout, grooved exteriorly, apex with two, sometimes three teeth; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal three segments thick, long, apical three slender and short; labial palpi four segmented, thorax short; forewing with marginal cell elongate, acute at apex; three submarginal cells, third longest, second subtriangular; gaster either elongate or broad and flat, fringed with long hairs on sides of the segments. Pygidial plate or spine armed on each side with subapical spine. Mandibles bidentate at apex; rear basal angle of mandibles before posterior margin of eye; scutellum in profile forming rounded projection, overhanging entire metanotum. Wings fuscous, deep metallic blue at base, then metallic green, followed by deep rosy coppery rest apex .

Colouration: Head, thorax and gaster black; hairs on face black, lateral sides of the thorax and gaster; wings shiny greenish-blue at base and coppery brown at tip.

Structure: Hairs on face simple; head, thorax and gaster finely, closely punctates; median ocellus larger than lateral ocelli; clypeus flat, with few hairs, with a median keel extending from median ocellus to middle of antennal sockets; length of palpal segments increase distally; labial palpi four segmented, first segment three times longer than second, last two minute; mesonotum with central area shiny, devoid of punctures; tergite with sparse punctations in the mid dorsal area and becoming numerous towards lateral area; mean number of hamuli on right wing 40.4, range 33-47, on left wing 40.8, range 32-47; fore tibial anterior porus long, curved with a rounded tip(1).
