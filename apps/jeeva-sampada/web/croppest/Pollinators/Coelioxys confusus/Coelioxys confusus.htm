 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is as broad as thorax; ocelli in a  triangle on vertex; antenne is filiform type;  labial palpi four segmented; maxillary palpi four segmented; basal two segments elongate, second somewhat larger than first, apical two minute; mandibles tridentate; maxillary palpi three segmented, basal segment shortest; labrum elongate, sides nearly parallel; thorax short; scutellum with a lateral curved tooth at apex; two submarginal cells unequal in length; gaster somewhat elongate, conical, base truncate, acute at apex in females, obtuse and dentate in males.

Colouration: ♀. Head, thorax and gaster black; labrum black; clypeus black; wings hyaline.

Structure: Head and thorax coarsely punctate; head covered with white hairs; labrum elongate, closely and finely punctate; clypeus slightly convex, closely, finely and deeply punctate; slightly tuberculate at base of antennae; scutellum with lateral curved tooth at base; first three median gasteral segments deeply, sparsely punctate; number of hamuli on right wing 14 and left wing 13; gaster elongate, narrowed posteriorly; dorsal plate of the apical segment acute, medially carinated towards apex with a depression on each side; scopa absent.

♂. Clypeus and base of antennae densely covered with white hairs; number of hamuli on both right and left wings are 13 and 12, respectively; dorsal gasteral segment divided by a deep groove at apex and armed with six teeth (1).
