 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous (but not actually fused) for about half distance from vertex to frons, bare, black; facets gradually decreasing in size from top to bottom. vertical triangle fairly elongate, sides about 11/2 times length of base, black, with anterior ocellus smaller, rounder and darker orange than the other two; ocellar prominence not very much raised, hairs short, erect, black, Frons, face and cheeks black, closely white tomentose except on lower cheeeks, with long, silky white hairs on frons and face, those on former suberect, on latter subdepressed and pointing downwards; face concave below antennae, facial tubercle only very slightly evident, angulate, bare and shining on top. Antennal prominence shining black; antennae dark brownish-balck, third segment broadly oval; arista bare, orange-brown, dark at tip and as long as antenna. Occiput white tomentose, few yellow hairs behind vertex, longer white hairs on lower border.

Thorax with mesonotum dirty yellowish grey, quadrate, uniformly punctulate; a broad black band across middle of thorax, a little towards base than apex, upper corners near wing bases, lower corner truncate; dorsum densely pubescent with shortish yellow hairs on yellowish portions, black on black parts; hairs on humeri slightly longer and rufous red. Pleura yellowish grey with upper part of mesopleuron covered with longish rufous-red hairs. Scutellum black, punctulate, with short, suberect black hairs on most of dorsum, except for basal and apical margins with yellow hairs, longest on outer margin.

Wings hyaline, trichose; areas adjacent to veins yellowish; stigma very short, dark brown; squamae dark greyish-brown with marginal hairs yellowish grey. Halteres yellow.

Legs shining black; basal half of fore and mid and base of hind tibiae yellowish; hairs yellowish, black non black parts of fore tibiae, upper inner side of fore femora, mid tibiae with black mixed hairs on apical half of dorsum and underside, apical part black-haired; apical half of hind femora and dorsal tip of smae black haired, lower part of hind tibiae also balck haired. claws yellow at base, black at tip.

Abdomen yellowish orange, darker progressively to tip; middle of tergite 1 blackish; tergite 2 with narrow dark brown margin, produced into a triangle in centre, tip reaching only 1/3 length of segment; tergite 3 with similar but indistinct, more extensive brownish triangle on apical margin, sometimes reaching basal margin; tergite 4 almost orange brown with a black patch in middle of anterior margin; hairs on margin yellow, shorter on basal half of tergite 2, longest on apical corners of tergite 4; hairs on dorsum very short, erect, yellow, longest on middle of tergite 2; short, black on brown triangular area of tergite 2.

Tergite 9 obtriangular; cercus not as elongate; surstylus without emargination at anterodorsal corner and without posteroventral lobe, posterodorsal lobe with stout hairs only at apical margin; sternite 9 and superior lobe narrower with the apical projection blunt at tip.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space almost 3/4 as wide at vertex as at base of antennae. Vertex black, separated from lower frons by some yellowish tomentum and hairs; hairs on vertex black, on yellow portion yellow, short, erect; on rest of face and frons, same as in male. Abdomen as in male, but the dark brown portions a little more extensive (1).
