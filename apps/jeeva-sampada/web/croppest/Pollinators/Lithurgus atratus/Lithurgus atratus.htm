 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is as broad as thorax; mandibles narrow, thick, not flattened as in Megachile, tri dnetate at apex; labial palpi four segmented; maxillary palpi four segmented; ocelli is placed in a triangle on front; thorax short; scutellum often projecting posteriorly and overhanging median segment; legs stout; wings broad, ample; fore wing with marginal cell moderately broad, more or less rounded at apex; two submarginal cells; gaster broad, flattened above, more convex beneath.

Colouration: ♀. Head, thorax and gaster black; clypeus covered with mixture of white and dark hairs; face covered with white plumose hairs; base of wing covered with white hairs; wings hyaline with brown or reddish tinge; each sternal segment margined posteriorly with a band of white plumose hairs; scopa with long brown simple hairs.

Structure: head coarsely and closely punctate; clypeus flat, anterior projection at base of clypeus; face raised between and below antennae; mandibles 3 toothed; thorax running into transverse striate; mean number of hamuli on right wing 11.25, range 11-12, on left wing 11.5, range 11-12; abdomen closely and finely punctuates (1).
