 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is generally as wide as the thorax; ocelli in a triangle placed forward on vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles stout, broad and flattened towards apex, 4 or 5 toothed, exterior tooth acute; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, subequal, apical two minute; maxillary palpi two segmented, short; labrum oblong, with sides parallel; thorax short, scutellum often projecting posteriorly and over hanging median segment; legs stout; wings broad, ample; forewing with marginal cell moderately broad, more or less rounded at apex; two submarginal cells; gaster variable, subcordate, oblong or elongate; ventral segments with a more or less dense pollen brush. Gaster with entirely black pubescens, face with black pubescence (1).

Colouration: ♂. Head, thorax and gaster jet black; labrum black; clypeus covered with few plumose hairs; face covered with dense, plumose black hairs; wings coppery with purple tinge; lateral sides and tip of thorax covered with dense black plumose hairs at base; thorax covered with few plumose hairs; gaster meso with long, simple, black hairs laterally; dorsal surface with few simple hairs; pollen brush black.

Structure: Head finely, closely and deeply punctate; labrum oblong, anterior margin rounded; clypeus slightly convex; labial palpi 4- segmented, first segment 1.2 times longer than second segment; thorax closely, finely, deeply punctate; median thorax sparsely punctate; number of hamuli on right and left wing with 21 and 22, respectively; gaster shiny, polished with differential punctations, more on lateral aspect and posterior aspect of each tergum (1).
