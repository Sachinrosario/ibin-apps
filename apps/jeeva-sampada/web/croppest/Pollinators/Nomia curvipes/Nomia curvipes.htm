 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Ocelli is arranged in a arch on the vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles with two teeth; labial palpi 4 -jointed, tongue short tifid, plumed; maxillary palpi 6-jointed, length of palpal segments increases distally; wings short and broad, marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, third longest, strongly constricted towards marginal cell, second generally square; gaster as broad as or broader, than thorax, oval, elliptical. Gasteral segment black often with segmental light coloured rim along the posterior margin. Mesonotum with a mat of golden yellow hair; T3 and T4 with segmental iridescent band occupying more than half tergal length; tibia reddish brown.

Colouration: ♀. Black, hairs golden yellow, reddish brown; antennae dark reddish brown; thorax with a felt of reddish brown hair, tegulae with reddish brown hair; all coxae and basal 2/3rd of femora black; apical margin of wing broadly fuscous with a coppery reflection at certain angles; venation light brown; pro and meso thorax covered with reddish brown hairs, sides and posterior part of thorax with golden yellow hairs; apical margins of gasteral segments 1-4 bright iridescent reddish yellow, with green tinge, bands much broader; band completely covers 4th gasteral tergum except anteriolateral areas.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster closely, finely punctuate; clypeus convex, finely, closely punctate; base of antennae slightly tubercle; ocelli same size; mean number of hamuli on right wing 16.3, range 14-19, on left wing 16.6, range 16-17 (1).
