 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is broader than long. Abdomen is  reddish brown, without white hair bands; propodeum with a short anterior margin having longitudinal rugae; clypeus excavated. Ocelli in an arch on  vertex; antennae filiform; tongue acute; labial palpi four segmented,  basal joint as long as following two, apical three gradually decreasing in length; maxillary palpi six segmented,  basal joint shortest, following joints gradually decreasing in length to apical one; the mandibles bidentate; thorax ovate, legs moderately long, wings with radial cell narrowing towards sharply acute apex, three submarginal cells,  first about as long as second and third combined, second small subquadrate ; gaster ovate,  apical segment with a longitudinal rim.

Colouration: ♀. Head and thorax black, covered with white hairs; legs dark brown colour; wings transparent with dark reddish brown hairs; pronotum end with a white scale-like hairs; gastra reddish brown with apical margin dark brown , 4th and 5th gasteral terga brown.

Structure: Punctae on head run into longitudinal rugae; clypeus concavely depressed, dorsal margin raised ridge like; labrum rectangular; supraclypeal area  slightly raised; parapsidal lines prominent; frontal line carinate; mesonotum sparsely and finely punctate; anterior propodeum with short longitudinal rugae; gaster shiny(1).
