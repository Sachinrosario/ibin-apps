 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is triangular, Ocelli in an arch on vertex; antennae filiform; tongue acute; the labial palpi four segmented, basal joints as long as following two, apical three gradually decreasing in length; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint as long as following two, apical three gradually decreasing in length; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint shortest, following joints gradually decresing in length to apical one; mandibles bidentate; thorax ovate, legs moderately long, wings radial cell narrowing towards apex, sharply acute, three sub marginal cells, first about as long as second and third united, second small subquadrate; gaster ovate, apical segment with a longitudinal rim. Mesonotum with punctuation as dense as on head. Gasteral terga without dense band of white hair.

Colouration: ♀.Shiny, coppery green bees; head covered with few white hairs; legs dark brown covered with white and light brown hairs; tarsi reddish brown; gaster beneath covered with white pubescence; wings transparent with dark brown venation.

Structure: Head and thorax finely and closely punctate; clypeus rectangular, coarsely, sparsely punctate, covered with few plumose hairs; supraclypeal region less tuberculate; frontal line carinate; parapsidal lines prominent; gaster very minutely and closely punctate, covered with few short hairs; lateral sides of thorax and abdomen covered with long hairs; number of hamuli on right wing 6 and on left wing 7 (1).
