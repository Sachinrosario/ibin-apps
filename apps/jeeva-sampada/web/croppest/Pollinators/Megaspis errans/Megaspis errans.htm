 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for 2/3 distance from vertex to frons, brownish black, bare; facets larger on top. vertical triangle brownish-black, equilateral, not conspicuously raised; ocelli crimson, large; hairs short, blackish, extending along eye margins. Face, frons and cheeks orange-yellow with whitish grey hairs, semierect on frons; white, subdepressed and pointing downwards on face; cheeks bare and more orange; facial tubercle not prominent, tip angulate pointing downwards. Antennal prominence yellowish; antennae pale ornage yellow, first segment short, second longer, third ovate, twice as long as first together; arista concolorous, darker at tip, microscopically pubescent, longer than antenna.

Thorax with mesonotum quadrate, black except at sides and base; poasterior corners of black area produced at both ends as triangles, not reaching margin, punctulate, covered densely with white hairs. pleura almost wholly yellowish, covered with long, yellow hairs. scutellum as mesonotum, brownish black, with short black hairs in centre of dorsum.

Tergite 9 wider than long, with some minute hairs; cerci more elongate, with long hairs on margin; surstylus with anterodorsal corner entire, not emarginate and with some longish hairs; a small posteroventral lobe present, posterodorsal lobe with uniform stout hairs all round margin except on ventral part; sternite 9 and surstylus not distinct from each other, tip ending in a pointed process.
