 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head with eyes contiguous for about half distance from vertex to frons, brownish-red; facets almost uniform, decreasing gradually in size from top to bottom, bare. Vertical triangles elongate, sides more than three times as long as base, black. Ocelli red, placed a little anterior to middle of vertical triangle which is covered with very short, sparse, black hairs. Frontal triangle, face and cheeks yellow with whitish tomentum, and short, sparse white hairs; a semicircular shining brownish spot on the upper side of antennal prominence which is bright yellow; face in profile with tubercle fairly prominent, orange-yellow. Oral cavity almost four times as long as wide, with orange yellow borders; shortish, yellow, erect pubescence on frons; antennae orange, third segment as long as first two together, the latter about equal, with short, stiff black hairs, third segment dark brown dorsally; arista bare, dark brown. Occiput black, finely dusted with pollen and with fairly long, stiff white hairs on margin.

Thorax with mesonotum shining black with yellow lateral margins, finely punctulate, covered with shortish, erect, yellow pubescence; a dense collor of long, stiff, erect golden hairs on the entire anterior margin; sides with similar hairs, longest on humerus. Pleura black with fine white tomentum; mesopleuron with long yellow hairs; sternopleuron with such hairs on upper postrior border. Scutellum semicircular, bright yellow with sparse yellowish hairs on dorsum. Posterior border with conspicuous black hairs, finely punctulate.

Wings clear, trichose, very pale yellowish-grey, not hyaline; stigma and base darker; squamae similar to wings in colour. Halteres pale orange.

Legs orange-yellow; tarsi dark brown; claws black distally, yellow proximally. Pubescence yellowish throught except on tarsi where it is black.

Abdomen broadly oval; tergite 1 orange-yellow, anterior border narrowly black; tergites 2 to 5 orange yellow, with broad black anterior margin 1/5 as wide as length of segment. Pubescence short, fairly dense, subdepressed, dark brown and black.

Terminalia fairly large; tergite 9 some what subquadrate, elongate anteriorly, covered with minute hairs; cercus fairly small, with some large and many small hairs; surstylus broad basally, with irregularly narrrowed, subtruncate apex, with a few shortish stout hairs; sternite 9 irregular in shape in lateral view with some small spinules near the middle of the posterior margin; superior lobe subquadrate with a posteroventral process and a stout, subcylindrical, strongly curved, posterolaterally directed hook with a slightly enlarged base and acute apex; aedegus very short, curved caudad, concave posterorly, scleritized only on basal half.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. head with eyes separated, interocular space three times as wide at level of antennal bases as at vertex. vertex shining black with violet reflections, extending as thin line to anterior edge of semicircular apot near antennal base. Frons with short, erect black hairs from ocellar prominence to midway between this and antennae. Face in profile with tubercle less distinct than in male.

Thorax with scutellum wholly covered with black suberect hairs except on posterior one-third.

Abdomen with tergite 1 having a broad median dark brown stripe, anterior border very faintly dark; tergite 6 visible, orange yellow with slightly dark anterior margin and with longish black hairs; hairs on tergite 1 wholly yellow, on the rest wholly black (1).
