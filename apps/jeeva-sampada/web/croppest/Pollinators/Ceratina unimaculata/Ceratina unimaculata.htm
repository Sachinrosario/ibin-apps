 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

An ocelli in a triangle on the vertex; antennae short, subclavate; mandibles stout, tridentate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi four segmented, basal three segments subequal, apical three grually decreasing in length; thorax ovate; forewing with the marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, second forming truncated triangle; gaster slightly narrowed at base; colour vary from black to brilliant metallic green; some species with maculations on face, thorax and gaster. Body coarsely pitted; mesonotun and abdomen without integumental white or yellow markings. Metabasitarsus without integumental white markings. Labrum with a yellow spot; yellow marking on fore femora occupying 3/4th of length (1).

 Colouration: ♀. Shiny bluish green; clypeus and labrum creamy color; labrum creamy at centre, followed by brown color; wings coppery brown; ventral surface of anterior femora and tibia off with.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster slightly, coarsely and closely punctates; clypeus flat and coarsely punctates, labrum pentagonal in shape; median of mesothorax finely and closely punctates; mean number of hamuli on left wing 5.2 and range 5-6, on right wing 5, range 5; abdomen bare, center finely and closely punctates; 5th and 6th segments with small, few simple hairs (1).
