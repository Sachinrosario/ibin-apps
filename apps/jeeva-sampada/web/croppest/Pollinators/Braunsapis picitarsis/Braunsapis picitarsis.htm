 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The bees are black coloured; labrum dark brown colour; clypeus yellow colour; anterior, lateral and posterior sides of thorax covered with white simple hairs; pronotal lobe yellow; yellow colour spot on anterior end of fore tibia; wings transparent with dark brown venation.

Colouration: ♀. Black coloured bees; labrum dark brown colour; clypeus yellow colour; anterior, lateral and posterior sides of thorax covered with white simple hairs; pronotal lobe yellow; yellow colour spot on anterior end of fore tibia; wings transparent with dark brown venation.

Structure: Head, thorax and abdomen finely and closely punctates; clypeus flat constricted before apex; labrum rectangular; a median carina with ridge between antennae; labial palpi 4 segmented, first segment 1.6 times longer than the second segment; mesothorax covered with short few hairs, with three carina; mean number of hamuli on both right and left wings and range are 5 and 5 respectively; first gastral segment depressed at centre; gaster covered with short few hairs; 2nd and 3rd gastral segments larger than remaining segments .

♂: Similar to female; ventral sides of antennae scape with yellow mark; Colour on clypeus broader than female; two yellow stripes on either side of clypeus (2).
