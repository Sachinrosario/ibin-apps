 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is generally as wide as the thorax; ocelli in a triangle placed forward on vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles stout, broad and flattened towards apex, 4 or 5 toothed, exterior tooth acute; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, subequal, apical two minute; maxillary palpi two segmented, short; labrum oblong, with sides parallel; thorax short, scutellum often projecting posteriorly and over hanging median segment; legs stout; wings broad, ample; forewing with marginal cell moderately broad, more or less rounded at apex; two submarginal cells; gaster variable, subcordate, oblong or elongate; ventral segments with a more or less dense pollen brush. Gaster with entirely black with 1-2 white pubescens (1).

Colouration: ♀. Head, thorax and gaster black; base of antennae with plumose hairs; wings hyaline with basal area almost transparent apical 3/ 4th with brownish tinge at angle reflects like metallic coppery; median segment and basal segment of gaster with long white to yellow pubescence; pollen brush jet black.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster closely and finely punctate; labrum elongate; clypeus flat, posterior part of clypeus slightly tuberculate, with few simple hairs; labial palpi 4 segmented, first segment 1.1 times longer than second segment; median thorax bare; mean number of hamuli on right wing 17.5, range 17-18, on left wing 18.8, range 16-21.
♂. Base, sides and apex of the clypeus with white pubescence; mean number of hamuli on right wing 14.4, range 13-16, on left wing 14.4, range 12-16 (1)
