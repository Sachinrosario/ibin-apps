Scientific Name: Xylocopa varipuncta Patton

English Name: The Valley carpenter bee,

Family: Apidae

Order: Hymenoptera

Back to top

 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Size: 18-26 mm.
Identification: Female is black with brassy reflections, perhaps. Male is a striking tawny brown. Apparently, no other Xylocopa are so sexually dimorphic.
Habitat: Valleys and foothills with deciduous trees such as oaks.
Food: Adults presumably take some pollen, like others of the genus.
