 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head either as long as broad or longer, Larger species 7.31mm length; propodeum rugose. Ocelli in an arch on  vertex; antennae filiform; tongue acute; labial palpi four segmented,  basal joint as long as following two, apical three gradually decreasing in length; maxillary palpi six segmented,  basal joint shortest, following joints gradually decreasing in length to apical one; the mandibles bidentate; thorax ovate, legs moderately long, wings with radial cell narrowing towards sharply acute apex, three submarginal cells,  first about as long as second and third combined, second small subquadrate ; gaster ovate,  apical segment with a longitudinal rim.

Colouration: ♀. Shiny, black bees; head, lateral sides of thorax and gaster, post scutellum covered with white hairs; gaster beneath covered with light brown hairs; legs black, covered with brown hairs, all tarsi reddish brown; wings transparent with dark brown venation; anterior margin of gasteral segments end with white, short, plumose scale-like hairs.

Structure: Head closely punctate; clypeus sparsely and coarsely punctate with anterior margin truncate; supraclypeal area with a tubercle; parapsidal lines prominent; frontal line carinate; mesonotum sparsely punctate; propodeum with longitudinal rugae; gaster very minutely punctate; all gasteral segments covered with few hairs (1).
