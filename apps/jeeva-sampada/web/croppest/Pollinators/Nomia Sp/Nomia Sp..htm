 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Ocelli is arranged in a arch on the vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles with two teeth; labial palpi 4 -jointed, tongue short tifid, plumed; maxillary palpi 6-jointed, length of palpal segments increases distally; wings short and broad, marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, third longest, strongly constricted towards marginal cell, second generally square; gaster as broad as or broader, than thorax, oval, elliptical. First gasteral segment with an iridescent marginal band. Iridescent bands on gastral segments 3 and 4 at most 1/3rd as long as medium length of the segments; fore and middle tibia black.

Colouration: ♀. Black, hairs mixture of black and white; antennal socket with white plumose hairs; gaster with green bands; basal gastral segment reddish brown; hind femora and tibia brown, tarsi black; middle and fore legs black; wings transparent with dark brown venation; gasteral segments 1-4 with dark green band at apical margin, smooth and shiny.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster closely, finely punctuate; clypeus flat, with a median carina not reaching apical margin, subquadrate, striated with few simple white hairs; antennal base slightly tuberculate, with a median keel; labial palpi four segmented, first segment 1.5 times longer than second segment; meso notum sparsely, finely punctuate; mean number of hamuli on right wing 13.6, range 12-15, on left wing 14.4, range 13-17; gasteral segments depressed at posterior part, smooth, black and shiny (1).
