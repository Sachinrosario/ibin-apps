 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The eggs are laid in clusters of 50-80 each, during day time, on both sides of leaves. The incubation period is 3 days in May and 18 days in December-January. The newly hatched caterpillars lead a gregarious life and not move away from the egg cluster. There are 5 larval instars and the duration of the larval period is about 16 days in April-May and 40 days in December-January. Up to the third instar, the larvae consume very little food; consequently they do not leave the plant on which they have hatched. In subsequent instars they feed veraciously and migrate. Fully grown larvae disperse from food plants to suitable places like tree trunks for population. Some times pupation  may also occur in the soil. The pupal period is 7 days in May and 28 days in December - January. The butterflies copulate in about 6 hours after emergence and start laying eggs a day after copulation. The adult stage lasts 4-5 days (1).

 Appearance

Egg: Elongated in the shape of sugar-cakes 1.5 mm high and 0.6 mm at their base, ribbed, lemon yellow and placed one beside the other (2).                                                                                                                                                                                       

 

 

 

 

                                                                                                                                                     http://www.inra.fr/internet

Larva (Male/Female): The size is 45 to 50 mm, head black in front, grey at the back, black thoracic plate. Greyish green body with 3 longitudinal yellow lines, numerous bristles and small black spots over all the body (2).

 

 

 

 

                                                                                                                                           http://www.kolumbus.fi/silvonen/lnel

Pupa (Male/Female): Held with a belt-like grindle of silk, not in a cocoon. Pupation lasts 10 to 15 days.                   

 

 

 

 

                                                                                                                                                   http://www.inra.fr/internet

Adult (Male/Female): The size is 40 to 60 mm wingspan. White wings. The fore wings are smoky at the apex, the hind wings have a small black spot on the anterior border; the female also has 2 black rounded spots on the fore wing (2).
