 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The head of Amegilla violacea is not wide as thorax; ocelli in a triangle on the vertex; clypeus strongly produced and convex; labrum subquadrate, anterior margin rounded; tongue elongate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint short, second segment longest, 3-6 decreasing gradually in length; thorax ovate; forewing with marginal cell broad, rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, first and third subequal, second narrowed at apex, sometimes pentagonal; legs short, posterior tibiae and basal joints of tarsi in female densely pubescent; intermediate tarsi in some species with tufts of hairs on apical joint above claws; gaster without distinct transverse pubescent fasciae above; thorax with black or dark brown pubescence (1).

Coloration: ♂. Black most of dorsum of labrum, anterior transverse band on clypeus and median triangular spot; three triangular spots on face near clypeus and a small spot at base of each mandible creamy white; hairs black most part, lateral aspect of thorax, terga and sterna whitish.
Structure: Clypeus and front below the anterior ocellus somewhat coarsely and closely, vertex of the head and the thorax above finely and densely punctate; clypeus convex; Mean number of hamuli on right wing 32.5, range 23-33, on left wing 32, range 22-33; gaster smooth, the surface finely aciculate.

♀. Facial coloration absent; except for a very small spot on anterior part of clypeus, rest of the coloration similar to male; first labial palpal segment 3 times as long as second segment; mean number of hamuli on right wing 31.0, range 29-35, on left wing 31.5, range 28-35 (1).
