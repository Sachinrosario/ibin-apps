 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female is black coloured bee; wings coppery brown in colour; posteriorly labrum dark brown, contains two light brown color spots on either side; clypeus covered with blue coloured hairs; anterior, lateral margin patch of hair white with blue tinge, median stripe in anterior half of mesonotum white with blue tinge; two pairs of spots one on disc, one on posterior margin, on either side of median line white with blue tinge; first gastral segment marginal bluish white patch of hair interrupted more widely on cardal line narrowly on anterior mid line; 2nd gastral segment with lateral L-shaped with bluish white hair; 3rd , 4th and 5th with lateral bands of white patch; gastral sterna 1-3 with small patch of bluish white hair.

Structure- Head, thorax and abdomen closely and finely punctuates; labrum wider than long; labrum subquadrate, anterior margin rounded; clypeus flat, finely and closely punctuate; face covered with blue hairs; labial palpi 4 segmented, first segment 1.6 times longer than second segment; scutellum w-shaped with more rounded caudolateral angles, a patch of long white hair arising at hind margin medially below the scutellar plate; mean number of hamuli on right wing 19.2 and range 17-21, on left wing 21.6 and range 15-22; first abdominal segment 1.4 times longer than second abdominal segment.

Colouration: ♀.
