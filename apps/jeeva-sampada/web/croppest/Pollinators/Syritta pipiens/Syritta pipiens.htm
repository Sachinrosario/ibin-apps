 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for less than half distance from vertex to frons, dark brownish-black, bare; facets almost uniform; larger in front. vertical triangle elongate, balck, with some white tomentum on the apical 1/3, sides thrice as long as base; ocelli reddish, median one smaller; ocellar prominence only slightly raised. Frons, face and cheeks brownish-orange with sparse white tomentum; frons very small; face with shortish, subdepressed, white hairs pointing downward, facial tubercle absent, face a little concave below antennae; cheeks almost without any tomentum. Oral cavity brownish, rounded at base, narrow at tip, three times as long as wide, oral tubercle angulate. Antennal prominences small; antennae pale yellowish orange, first segment small, second twice as long, both with few black hairs on anterodorsal margin, third segment broadly oval; arista brown, darker at tip, as long as second and third together.

Thorax with mesonotum longer than wide, black with greyish-white humeri and a longitudinal stripe from it to suture, continuing inwards along suture to a sharp point; posterior angles also greyish white in a transverse hyphenshaped band on the inner end of which is a similar coloured spot; two greyish median stripes from anterior margin, but terminating at level of posterior margin of humerus. Dorsum densely punctulate with very short subdepressed pale yellow hairs directed backwards. Pleura dull greyish-white with pubescence similar to dorsum but sparser. Scutellum black, densely punctulate, covered with similar hairs as on thorax.

Wings hyaline, trichose; stigma and base uniformly pale yellow; squmae dull whitish, margins yellow, with whitish bipectinate marginal hairs. Halteres yellow.

Legs brownish orange; proximal half of fore and mid tibiae paler, distal half dark brown; hind femora greatly enlarged with double row of stout black spines on underside; colour variable, usually brownish-orange on proximal 3/5 and black on distal 2/5; in some examples, the black continues dorsally towards base and in others the femur is fully black; hind tibiae dark brown with median brownish-orange band; fore and mid tarsi orange, hind tarsi brown. Pubescence of short yellow hairs, black on underside of hind tibiae.

Abdomen narrower than thorax, widest at base, narrowest at base of tergite 3; tergite 1 balck; tergite 2 with apical margin broadly black and a black spot in middle of basal margin; tergite 3 similar to preceding but without basal black spot; tergite 4 a little convex with a tubercle in middle of apical margin, balck with some brownish orange on apical and basal margins, hind corners with whitish spots variablae in size; lateral margins with very short, white pubescence except on base of tergite 1 where it is longer in the form of tuft. Pubescence on dorsum concolorous, mostly yellow, sparse, subdepressed, with some black hairs in middle of tergite 2 and 3.

Terminalia very complex, fairly large. Tergite 9 obtriangular with some pubescence; surstylus in the form of a fairly long lobe with dense spine and a broadly rounded tip; superior lobe is small, with serrated edges.

Female: similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, frons about half as wide at vertex than at base of antennae. Vertex black including ocellar prominence which is slightly raised and a little to front of middle of ocellus; rest of frons, face and cheeks greyish white with a black band placed midway on frons between vertex and antennal base; black spot on top of  antennal prominence. Face carinate in middle, rest as in male. cheeks greyish without white tomentum. Ocelli pale orange. Vertex and frons with white hairs, tips directed forwards, face with similar but shorter and sparser hairs. Antennae orange, as in male.

Abdomen as in male; tergite 2 with a basal spot, joined to anterior marginal balck band by a mid-dorsal stripe; tergite 3 with anterior black band very extensive, reaching almost to middle of segment, thence produced as a mid-dorsal faint stripe not reaching basal margin; sometimes black also extends on lateral margins; tergite 4 shining balck, anterior margin pale yellow, basal white spot on corner extending towards anterior corners as a marginal stripe (1).
