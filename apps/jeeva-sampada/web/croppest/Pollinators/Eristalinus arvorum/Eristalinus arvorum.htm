 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male; Face in profile with prominent tubercle as in that species; hairs on frons denser and black. Pleura almost entirely with white tomentum, except lower half of sternopleuron. Halteres yellow.

Legs deep yellow; all femora with a black roundish spot made up of stout short black spinules at the base on the lower side of hind femora, more on inner lateral portion than lower; tips of tarsi brownish, black hairs on fore and mid femora except at base and apex on dorsal surface; hind tibiae with all black hairs.

Abdomen triangular in shape, widest at base of tergite 2, narrowing towards apex. Tergite 1 pale yellow, tergite 2 darker yellow, brownish on margin; anterior margin broadly brownish especially at lateral corners, inconspicuous in middle extending medially but not reaching posterior margin; tergite 3 broadely black; tergite 4 trapezoidal, anterior margin half as posterior, the tergite brownish black, paler on posterior margin, anterior margin black, a pair of oval whitish spots placed obliquely, the distance between the slightly less than their length; hairs on lateral abdominal margin short, pale yellow throughout except on second segment where it is balck. Posterior corner of tergite 2 with a dense longish tuft of white hairs; hairs concolorous with background, longer in centre than at sides.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. head with eyes separated, interocular space twice as wide at level of antennae as at vertex; frons slightly darker than twice as wide at level of antennae as at vertex; frons slightly darker than face with two blackish oval spots near eye margins on frons away from median ocellus as far as occiput in length. whole of frons down to antennae with short, black, erect hairs of equal length.

Legs as in male except lower surfaces of hind femora which are black except at base.

Abdomen narrower at apex; triangle darker brown than in male; tergite 1 pale yellow; tergites 2, 3 and 4 with white hands reducing in width progressively from tergites 2 to 4; sides not confluent with lateral margins, arcuate in centre, more away from posterior margin in centre on tergite 2 than on tergite 3; that on tergite 4 confluent with posterior margin; tergite 5 with two whitish subtriangular spots on base at each corner (3).
