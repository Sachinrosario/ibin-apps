 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is generally as wide as the thorax; ocelli in a triangle placed forward on vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles stout, broad and flattened towards apex, 4 or 5 toothed, exterior tooth acute; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, subequal, apical two minute; maxillary palpi two segmented, short; labrum oblong, with sides parallel; thorax short, scutellum often projecting posteriorly and over hanging median segment; legs stout; wings broad, ample; forewing with marginal cell moderately broad, more or less rounded at apex; two submarginal cells; gaster variable, subcordate, oblong or elongate; ventral segments with a more or less dense pollen brush. Gaster with pubescens other than black, Gastral segments 1-2 with reddish brown pubescence. Dorsum of thorax covered with mixture of white plumose and reddish brown pubescence; gastral segments three to six with reddish brown hair bands.

Colouration: ♀. Head, thorax and gaster black; sides of clypeus, face covered with white dense pubescence; wings fusco hyaline; scutellum, entire gaster covered with fulvous red pubescence; lateral sides of gaster covered with white pubescence; pollen brush white.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster finely punctate; labrum elongate; clypeus slightly convex; labial palpi 4 -segmented, first segment 1.1 times longer than second segment; right and left wings with an average 17.4 (range 15-19) hamuli (1).
