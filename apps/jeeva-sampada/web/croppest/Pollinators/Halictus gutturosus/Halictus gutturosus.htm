 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is triangular, Ocelli in an arch on vertex; antennae filiform; tongue acute; the labial palpi four segmented, basal joints as long as following two, apical three gradually decreasing in length; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint as long as following two, apical three gradually decreasing in length; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint shortest, following joints gradually decreasing in length to apical one; mandibles bidentate; thorax ovate, legs moderately long, wings radial cell narrowing towards apex, sharply acute, three sub marginal cells, first about as long as second and third united, second small subquadrate; gaster ovate, apical segment with a longitudinal rim. Mesonotum is sparsely punctuated, compared to punctuations on the head .

Colouration: ♀. Black coloured bees; body and legs covered with white hairs; base of antennae covered with plumose white hairs; lateral sides of thorax, gaster above and beneath covered with long white hairs; post scutellum covered with dense white pubescens; gasteral segments end with a dense white scale like hair band; tarsi dark brown with light brown hairs; wings transparent with dark brown venation.

Structure: Head, gaster finely and closely punctate; labrum rectangular, covered with hairs; clypeus slightly convex, with anterior angles produced, coarsely and sparsely punctate; supraclypeal area tubercle; frontal line carinate; parapsidal lines prominent extending beyond anterior margin of tegulae; mesothorax shiny, sparsely punctate (1).
