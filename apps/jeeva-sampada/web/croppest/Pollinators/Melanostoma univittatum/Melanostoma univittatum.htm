 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for about half distance from apex of frontal triangle to vertex, red, bare; facets almost uniform, gradually decreasing in size from top to bottom. Vertical triangle equilateral, elevated, black with fairly long, light brown, black-tipped hairs; ocelli red. Frontal triangle, face and cheeks black, with sparse short pale yellow hairs, little longer on frontal triangle; face in profile nearly perpendicular but only forming a slight angle; tubercle absent. Oral cavity 2/3 as wideas long. Occiput black, lined with very short, yellowish hairs. Antennae orange, first two segments equal in length, with short black hairs dorsally at anterior margin; third segment ovate, as long as first two together, dark brown dorsally; arista bare, black; light brown at proximal one-third, almost as long as antennae.

Thorax with mesonotum quadrate, shining black with greenish reflections, minutely punctulate; dorsum with fairly dense, short yellow hairs which are longer at sides; humerus with a small tubercle. Pleura black, faintly pollinose, covered sparsely with shortish yellow hairs, longest on posterior mesopleuron. Scutellum black, with pubescence and punctation as on mesonotum, hairs slightly longer.

Wings clear, trichose, yellowish grey, not hyaline; stigma and base more yellowish; squamae with short yellow hairs on margin. Halteres orange

Legs orange-yellow with median dark brown ring on hind tibiae; last two tarsal segments dark brown ring on hind tibiae; last two tarsal segments dark brown; hairs yellow-brown except on tarsi where they are black. Claws yellowish on proximal and dark on distal halves.

Abdomen elongate, sides subparallel; tergite 1 black with fairly long, yellowish hairs; tergite 2 orange-yellow, basal and anterior margin narrowly black; fairly broad, median, longitudinal, black stripe gradually narrowing anteriorly and diverging at tip to meet anterior border. Pubescence short, yellowish, black on anterior one third; tergite 3 similar to preceding but much narrower and less distinct median stripe; tergite 4 similar to preceding, half as long as tergite 2, median stripe almost evanescent and with more extensive pubescence; tergite 4 asymetrical, orange yellow with irregular black anterior margin.

Terminalia normal sized; tergite 9 obtriangular with fairly sparse, short pubescence; cercus densely haired; surstylus very thin and elongate, with a kink near the base and three areas of haired patches interspersed with bare areas; sternite 9 weakly sclerotized and irregular; superior lobe somewhat quadrate with two minute process ventrally.

Female: similar to male but with the following differences. head with eyes separated, interocular space 2/3 as wide at vertex as at level of antennae.

Abdomen elongate-oval; tergite 1 black; tergite 2 also black with two yellow oblique spots attaining lateral margin at posterior corners; tergite 3 black with two subtriangular yellow spots touching posterior margin and with parallel inner edges; tergite 4 similar to foregoing but slightly shorter in length; tergite 5 black with a pair of yellow spots on hind margin separated in the middle; tergite 6 black, yellow at sides. Pubescence of short, suberect hairs; some shorter black, subdepressed hairs on anterior half of tergites (1).
