 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Ocelli in a triangle on the vertex; antennae short, subclavate; mandibles stout, tridentate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi four segmented, basal three segments subequal, apical three grually decreasing in length; thorax ovate; forewing with the marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, second forming truncated triangle; gaster slightly narrowed at base; colour vary from black to brilliant metallic green; some species with maculations on face, thorax and gaster. Body coarsely pitted; mesonotum and abdomen without integumental white or yellow markings. Metabasitarsus without integumental white markings. Labrum with a yellow spot; gaster segments four-six with rectangular black patches (1).

Colouration: ♀. Metallic dark green; labrum largely yellow with anterior and lateral margins broadly and a median spot at base dark brown or black; clypeus yellow; hind basitarsus with a longitudinal integumental white color on outer edge; pronotal lobe yellow; wings transparent with bark brown venation; 2nd and 3rd gastral segments with four black colored marks; 4th, 5th and 6th with black square shaped marks.

Structure: Head, thorax and abdomen coarsely and closely punctate; clypeus inverted T-shaped, with anterior sharp edges, coarsely and scatterely punctate; mesothorax with five carinae; lateral side of gaster with few hairs (1).
