 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Colouration: ♀. Head and thorax black with golden yellow setae; legs except coxae and trochanter, antennae except last two segments reddish brown; the last 2 segments of the antennae dark brown; clypeus covered with white hairs; antennal scape with long yellowish plumose hairs; tegulae reddish brown, pterostigma and apical band on forewing fuscous, venation dark brown; anterior part of thorax, lateral sides covered with creamy yellowish hairs; most of ventral segments reddish brown except 5th sternum largely black; wings transparent with dark brown venation.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster closely and finely punctate; clypeus flat, subquadrate in shape; between antennae at base slightly raised; median ocellus larger than lateral ocelli; antennal socket covered with creamy yellowish plumose hairs; anteriolateral areas of pronotum ampulated in form of a rim; number of hamuli on right wing 14 and on left wing 16; gasteral segments closely and coarsely punctate, depressions posteriorly (1).
