 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The head of Amegilla confusa  is not wide as thorax; ocelli in a triangle on the vertex; clypeus strongly produced and convex; labrum subquadrate, anterior margin rounded; tongue elongate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint short, second segment longest, 3-6 decreasing gradually in length; thorax ovate; forewing with marginal cell broad, rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, first and third subequal, second narrowed at apex, sometimes pentagonal; legs short, posterior tibiae and basal joints of tarsi in female densely pubescent; intermediate tarsi in some species with tufts of hairs on apical joint above claws; gaster with distinct transverse pubescent fasciae above basal gastral segments with fasciae, Gaster with fasciae of white pubescence on thorax fulvous or grayish black mixed with black hairs (2).

Coloration: ♀. Clypeus and face between clypeus and eyes yellow; labrum yellow; setae on clypeus, labrum more numerous, light golden; hairs on thorax and face mixed with black and white; gasteral segments with white bands.

Structure: Head, thorax and gaster closely and densely punctate; head above and thorax densely pubescent; first labial palpal segment 6.5 times as long as second segment. Mean number of hamuli on right wing 22.50, range 21-25, on left wing 20.80, range 17-24. Male is Similar to ♀, smaller; the clypeus with broader yellow markings; the fifth gastral segment with a transverse pubescent fascia on its apical margin; mean number of hamuli on right wing 23.00, range 21-25, on left wing 21.00, range 17-24 (2).
