 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is subrounded; Ocelli in a triangle below vertex; antennae geniculate, scape sometimes dialated or incrassate; mandibles short, stout, grooved exteriorly, apex with two, sometimes three teeth; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal three segments thick, long, apical three slender and short; labial palpi four segmented, thorax short; forewing with marginal cell elongate, acute at apex; three submarginal cells, third longest, second subtriangular; gaster either elongate or broad and flat, fringed with long hairs on sides of the segments. Pygidial plate or spine armed on each side with subapical spine, mandibles tridentate at apex; apex of basi tibial plate well beyond middle of tibia; maximum interocular distance greater than length of eye; scutellum convex in profile, not divided in to subhorizontal dorsal surface and subvertical posterior surface; body with golden brown, sometimes rufescens pubescence (1).

Colouration: ♀. Head, thorax and gaster black colour, covered with golden yellow hairs; legs with brownish hairs; wings fulvo- hyaline.

Structure: Head, thorax and abdomen finely, closely and deeply punctate; ocelli large compared to other three species, equal in size, close together; clypeus with a median smooth shining ridge not reaching margin; labrum tuberculate; mandibles bidentate; maxillary palpi six segmented, palpal segments increases distally; labial palpi four segmented, first segment 3.5 times longer than second segment; a tubercle present between bases of antennae, face with numerous plumose hairs; median area of meso scutellum divide if punctures; mean number of hamuli right wing 34.25, range 33-37, on left wing 34.5, range 33-37; I, IV, V, and VI gasteral segments covered with dense hairs (1).
