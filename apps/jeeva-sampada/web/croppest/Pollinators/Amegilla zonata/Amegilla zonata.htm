 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The head of Amegilla zonata is not wide as thorax; ocelli in a triangle on the vertex; clypeus strongly produced and convex; labrum subquadrate, anterior margin rounded; tongue elongate; labial palpi four segmented, basal two elongate, apical two minute; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal joint short, second segment longest, 3-6 decreasing gradually in length; thorax ovate; forewing with marginal cell broad, rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, first and third subequal, second narrowed at apex, sometimes pentagonal; legs short, posterior tibiae and basal joints of tarsi in female densely pubescent; intermediate tarsi in some species with tufts of hairs on apical joint above claws; gaster with distinct transverse pubescent fasciae above basal gastral segments with fasciae, Gaster with fasciae of metallic blue pubescence; pubescence on thorax dull rufofulvous (1).

Coloration: ♀. Clypeus has two slightly curved rather rectangular divergent marks one on either side of median line laterally; setae on clypeus more numerous, light golden yellow; first labial palpal segment 5 times as long as the second segment; flagellar segment darker; outer surface of fore and middle femora with white setae, dorsal margin with orange setae; four gasteral segments with greenish blue band.

Structure: Head and thorax densely, clypeus and the bases of abdominal segments sparsely pubescent; head, thorax and gaster finely and closely punctate; mean number of hamuli on right wing 25.00, range 21-27, on left wing 25.4, range 21-27;

♂. Clypeus and labrum yellow; a spot near base on lateral margin and sutural area of clypeus before the spot anterior margin and a spot on either side of labrum proximally black; basal half of mandible yellowish white; five gasteral segments with greenish blue band; flagellae much darker; first labial palpal segment 5 times as long as the second segment; mean number of hamuli on right wing 23.25, range 23-24, on left wing 23.25, range 22-24 (1).
