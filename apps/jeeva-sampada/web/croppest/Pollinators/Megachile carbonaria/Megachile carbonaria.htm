 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is generally as wide as the thorax; ocelli in a triangle placed forward on vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles stout, broad and flattened towards apex, 4 or 5 toothed, exterior tooth acute; labial palpi four segmented, basal two segments elongate, subequal, apical two minute; maxillary palpi two segmented, short; labrum oblong, with sides parallel; thorax short, scutellum often projecting posteriorly and over hanging median segment; legs stout; wings broad, ample; forewing with marginal cell moderately broad, more or less rounded at apex; two submarginal cells; gaster variable, subcordate, oblong or elongate; ventral segments with a more or less dense pollen brush. Gaster with entirely black pubescens, face with golden yellow pubescence .

Colouration: ♂. Head, thorax and gaster black; clypeus and face with dense plumose, reddish brown hairs; head, ventral part of prothorax dull golden yellow, rest of thorax fuscous; lateral margins of mesonotum whitish tinged with yellow hairs; wings coppery brown; inner side of hind tarsi with fuscous, outer side with white brush of hairs; lateral side of gaster with long simple, black hairs.

Structure: Head, finely, closely and deeply punctate; clypeus flat, finely and closely punctate; labial palpi 4-segmented, first segment 1.2 times longer than second segment; thorax and gaster closely and finely punctate; hamuli on both right and left wing 18 (range 17-19)(1).
