 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Head is subrounded; Ocelli in a triangle below vertex; antennae geniculate, scape sometimes dialated or incrassate; mandibles short, stout, grooved exteriorly, apex with two, sometimes three teeth; maxillary palpi six segmented, basal three segments thick, long, apical three slender and short; labial palpi four segmented, thorax short; forewing with marginal cell elongate, acute at apex; three submarginal cells, third longest, second subtriangular; gaster either elongate or broad and flat, fringed with long hairs on sides of the segments. Pygidial plate or spine unarmed: posterior margin of scutellum projecting beyond posterior margin of mesonotum; thorax with yellow pubescence .

Colouration: ♀. Head, thorax and abdomen black colour; hairs white, black and yellow; wings shiny, purplish; Thorax with yellow plumose hairs.

Structure: Head, thorax and abdomen finely, closely and deeply punctate; clypeus flat; clypeus and face covered with plumose hairs; tubercule between bases of antennae on face short, contuning as a ridge connected to the median ocelli or pit; mandibles bidentate; maxillary palpi six segmented, palpal segments increase in length distally; labial palpi four segmented, first segment 3.4 times longer than second; anterior pores of fore tibia very short, pointed at apex; mean number of hamuli right wing 38.4, range 35-43, left wing 39.0, range 36-43; dorsal surface of abdomen covered with simple few hairs (1).
