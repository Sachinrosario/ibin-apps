 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Ocelli is arranged in a arch on the vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles with two teeth; labial palpi 4 -jointed, tongue short tifid, plumed; maxillary palpi 6-jointed, length of palpal segments increases distally; wings short and broad, marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, third longest, strongly constricted towards marginal cell, second generally square; gaster as broad as or broader, than thorax, oval, elliptical. First gasteral segment without an iridescent band on posterior margin.

Colouration: ♀. Head and thorax black; gaster black with iridescent green colour; hairs white and black; wings transparent with dark brown venation; gasteral segments 2-5 with transverse bright iridescent bands on posterior margins.

Structure: Head coarsely punctuates; clypeus convex, longitudinally striate, a median vertical carina extends over frons; surface between antennae slightly tuberculate; ocelli of same size; labial palpi four segmented, first segment 1.4 times longer than second segment; thorax coarsely, closely punctate; mesothorax with a median straight line; hind femora with scopa; mean number of hamuli on right wing 13.8, range 13-17, on left wing 13.4, range 12-14; abdomen finely, closely and deeply punctate  (1).
