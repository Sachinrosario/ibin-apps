 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Ocelli is arranged in a arch on the vertex; antennae is geniculate type; mandibles with two teeth; labial palpi 4 -jointed, tongue short tifid, plumed; maxillary palpi 6-jointed, length of palpal segments increases distally; wings short and broad, marginal cell rounded at apex; three submarginal cells, third longest, strongly constricted towards marginal cell, second generally square; gaster as broad as or broader, than thorax, oval, elliptical. First gasteral segment reddish brown with discal black patch.

Colouration: ♀. Black, face with yellow plumose hairs; lower half of flagellar segments brown, dorsal aspect chocolate brown, all tarsi lighter in colour; wings transparent with dark brown venation; median thorax with plumose, short, white, long reddish brown hairs; first gasteral segment reddish, with a central black spot, remaining segments black with apical white band smooth, shiny; gaster with simple, few black hairs.

Structure: head, thorax and gaster closely, finely punctate; clypeus flat, covered with long, yellow plumose hairs; between antennae at base slightly tuberculate; scutellum coarsely punctate, shiny; hind femora greatly enlarged, posterior margin with serate or dentate; hind tibia with a spur like process at base bearing a pair of spurs; number of hamuli on both right and left wings 12; posterior part of gasteral segments is depressed (1).
