Ragi root Aphid - Tetraneura hirsuta  

 

Symptom of damage:

  1. 1.Gradual yellowing/stunted growth and drying of plants in patches.  

2. The black ant Camponotus compressus can be seen attending on the aphids for their honey-dew excretion and the presence of the ants at the base of tillers is a sure indication of root aphid infested plants.

 

Management:

  1. 1.Pull out plants, which are yellow and stunted severely, with roots intact and destroy them. 

  2. 2.Mix dimethoate 30 EC 3 ml in 1 lit of water and drench the rhizosphere of the infested and surrounding plants.
