Oriental Army Worm - Mythimna separata (Noctuidae: Lepidoptera)

 

Nature of injury: The larvae feed during night hours on leaves voraciously and defoliate completely leaving hard midrib.

 

Symptom of damage:  The larvae feed on the leaves, leaving only the midrib. Presence of faecal pellets in Jowar leaf whorl is a major symptom of attack. Armyworm attacks are sporadic nature.

 

Management:

  1. 1.Clean cultivation and lower plant population help in reducing insect damage. 

  2. 2.Spray endosulfan 0.07%  

  3. 3.Spread 10 kg of monocrotophos bait prepared as per the directions given in previous section on IPM concepts and tools.
