The sorghum ear head midge - Contarinia sorghicola (Cecidomyiidae:Diptera)

 

Symptom of damage: The maggot feeds on developing ovary cause empty glumes. If the glume is squeezed a pinkish ooze comes out is a major symptom of damage.

 

Management:

Early and uniform sowing of sorghum over large areas is the most widely accepted method of reducing midge damage.

 

Spray malathion 50 EC at 0.05% when 10% of the earhead emerges out and repeat the same when 50% of the earheads emerges out or dust the earheads with malathion 5D at 20-25 kgs/ha.
