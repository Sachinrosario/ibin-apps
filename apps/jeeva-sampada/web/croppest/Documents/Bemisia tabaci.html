1.

English name: The cotton white fly

Scientific name: Bemisia tabaci Gennadius

(Hemiptera; Aleyrodidae)

2.

Distribution: Western regions of the Indian subcontinent.

3.

Appearance:

  1. a. 

  1. a.Eggs: Eggs are stalked, sub-elliptical and light yellow at first and turn brown later on. 

  2. b.Nymph: The louse-like nymphs, are sluggish creatures, clustered together on the under surface of the leaves. 

  3. c.Adults (Male / Female): Pale - yellow bodies are slightly dusted with a white waxy powder. They have two pairs of pure white wings and have prominent long hind wings. 

4.

Nature of injury: Damage by this pest is caused in two ways.

1) The vitality of the plant is lowered through the loss of cell sap.

2) Normal photosynthesis is interfered with owing to the growth of a sooty mould on the honeydew excreted by the insect.

5.

Symptom of damage: The attacked crop gives a sickly black appearance. In severe infestation results in premature defoliation.

6.

Alternate hosts: Cabbage, Cauliflower, Sarson, Toria, Melon, Potato, Brinjal, Okra, and some weeds.

7.

Biology / Ecology / Behaviour: The insect breeds throughout the year and all the developmental stages are noticed, although in the cold season the adults are predominant. The females lay eggs singly on the underside of the leaves, averaging 119 eggs per female. The eggs are stalked, sub-epithelial and light yellow at first, turning brown later on. The life cycle is completed in 14-122 days and 11 generations are completed in a year.

9.

Management: Avoid nitrogen application and irrigation.

Avoid ratooning, have synchronised planting.

Avoid usage of synthetic pyrithroids regularly.

Crop rotation of cotton with non-preferred host viz., Sorghum, ragi, maize etc.

Destruction of malvaceous weeds in near by areas of field. Ex: Abutilon indicum.

Avoid using any chemical: which encounters growth.

Always avoid extending the crop duration beyond a limit.

Avoid scheduled regular application of insecticides.

Neem oil application:  5 ml / litre + 1-2 teepol or Sordovit

Also can use FORS 2% [1kg of fish oil resin soap in 40 litre of water]

Use of phosalone 35 EC at 0.07 %

Quinolphos 25 EC at 0.05 %

Systematic chemical – methyl oxydemeton 25 EC- 0.05%.
