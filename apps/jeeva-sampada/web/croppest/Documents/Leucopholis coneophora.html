1. English name: The White grub

Scientific name: Leucopholis coneophora Burm

(Scarabaeidae: Coleoptera)

2. Distribution: Western cost of peninsular India, middle and northern parts of Kerala.

3. Appearance:

  1. a.Eggs:  

  2. b.Grub: Full white in colour. 

  3. c.Adults (Male / Female): Large reddish brown in colour. 

4. Nature of injury: The grubs feed on roots.

5. Symptom of damage: Delayed flowering and stunted growth of the tree.

6. Alternate hosts: Sweet potato, Tapioca, Colocasia and Banana.

7. Biology / Ecology / Behaviour: The adult beetles emerge after monsoon showers during the early half of June and soon after the mating start laying the eggs in the soil at a depth of 7 –15 cm. The incubation period is about 20 days. The grubs feed on the root and become full-grown in about 10-11 months.  After a prepupal period of 9-12 days it pupates in the soil. The pupal period lasts for about 25 days that coincides with the receipt of monsoon showers necessary for its emergence. The life cycle is annual.

9. Management:

At the time of abundance the soil may be ploughed deep so as to expose the grubs which will be picked up by the  predatory birds, wild boars and other natural enemies.
