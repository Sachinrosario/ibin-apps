Mango-hoppers - Idioscopus niveosparsus (Lethierry) Amritodus atkinsoni (Lethierry)

                               (Hemiptera; Cicadellidae)

 

Nature of injury: Both nymphs and adults cause injury by sucking the cell sap from the inflorescence and tender shoots.  It shooty mold developed on leaves and other parts of trees because of accumulation of honeydew.

 

Symptom: Because of continues sucking inflorescence and young fruits start drying.  Young fruits and inflorescence break off at the axil and fall down as the wind blows.  The growth of the young trees is much retarded and the older trees do not bear much fruit.  Damage to mango crop may be as high as 60 %. Withering and shedding of flower buds and flowers is a common symptom when pest attack is severe.

 

Management:

  • Proper trimming of the canopy to allow sunlight to enter inside.  

  • Not to irrigate too much  

  • Apply recommended dose of fertilizers. Do not give higher dose of N2 fertilizer which would give more number of flushes and attracts more number of pests.  

  • NSKE 4% is also recommended for the control of pest effectively 

  • Two spray application of carbaryl 50WP 40g or 15ml monocrotophos 36EC in 10 lit of water are required to overcome this problem. 

  • Spray with Imidacloprid 17.80 at 25 ml/10 lit of water when the tree is at flowerbud and furits are at peanut size.
