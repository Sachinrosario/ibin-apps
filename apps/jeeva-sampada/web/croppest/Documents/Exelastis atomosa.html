Redgram Plume Moth - Exelastis atomosa (Lepidoptera: Proterophoridae).

 

Nature of injury: Larvae feed on flower buds and seeds. First scrape the pod surface and gradually make a hole and thrust the head into it and feed on the seeds from outside. The caterpillars cause damage by boring into and cutting through the pods while eating the grains.

 

Symptom of damage: Holes on the pods and seeds.

 

Management:

Spraying of 4-5 % NSKE at fort nightly interval

Spray application of endosulfan 0.07 % afternoticing the pest.
