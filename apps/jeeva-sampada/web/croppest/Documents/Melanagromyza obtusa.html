Red Gram Pod Fly - Melanagromyza obtusa (Agromyzidae; Diptera)

 

Nature of injury: The maggots mines first below the epidermal layer and later reach the seeds and eat away only a part of the seed.  The partially damaged seed becomes subject to bacterial and fungus infections. The damaged grains are thus unfit for consumption.

 

Symptom of damage: Feeding on the seeds and damage is seen only on some part of the seed.  Presence of empty locules in the pods. It destroys the seeds up to 60 % when infestation is severe.

 

Management: Spraying malathion 50 EC at 0.05% when the pods are in tender stage.
