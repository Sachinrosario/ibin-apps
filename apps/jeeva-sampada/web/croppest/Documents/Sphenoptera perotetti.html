Groundnut stem-borer - Sphenoptera perotetti (Coleoptera; Buprestidae)

 

Nature of injury: The grub bores into the stems and starts feeding on the inner content of stem.  The feeding injury to the roots causes death of the plant. The damage appears in patches.

 

Symptom: Due to its feeding on the inner content of the stem, there will be formation of tunnelling, which affects the translocation of food materials leading to the wilting of plants in patches.  Such plants come out  easily when pulled out.

 

 

Management:

  • Remove and destroy the affected branches so as to suppress the pest, thus preventing the grubs from entering the taproot. 

 

  • Apply chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 0.04 %  only if physical method of control fails to check the spread of the pest.
