Mango mealy-bug - Drosicha mangiferae Green or Monophlebus stebbingi Green

                                  (Hemiptera; Coccidae)

 

Nature of injury: Only nymphs are cause damage to the plant by sucking the plant juice.

Symptom: The tender shoots and flowers dry up immediately. The young fruits also become juiceless and drop-off, shoots dry up when pest attack is severe.

 

Management:

  • Cut the branches which are touching the ground. Wherever possible plough the soil so that adult females in soil are disturbed.  

  • Spray monocrotophos 36 SC 1 ml/ lit of water after pruning. 

  • Wrap 20-25 cm wide alkathene (400 guage) around the trunk and seal the edges with mud.  

  • Spray 40 ml of neem oil pongamia oil along with 50 ml of liquid soap in 10 litres of water.  Repeat the same spray after 10 days.   

  • Release lady bird beetle at 20 grubs per tree at the site of infestation.
