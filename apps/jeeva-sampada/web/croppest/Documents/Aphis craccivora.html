Groundnut aphid - Aphis craccivora (Hemiptera; Aphididae)

 

Nature of Damage: Both nymphs and adults suck the sap from the surface of the younger leave.  They also suck the sap from tender shoots of 2-3 months old.  

 

Symptom: Due to the continuous feeding of nymphs and adults, the younger leaves and tender shoots give withered and blighted appearance.  This severely affects the growth and development of the crop.  It also affects the flower formation and pod development.   Further, it is also known to be a vector of ‘rosette’ disease of groundnut.

 

Management:  

  • Natural enemies: There are quite a few natural enemies recorded on groundnut aphids.  Several species of parasites and predators cause mortality of aphids. Coccinelids: Menochiles sexmaculata (Fab); Coccinella repanda var transversalis Fab.  Syrphid: Ischiodon scutellaris Fab.; I. Javan (Weidmann) Chrysopidae: Chrysoperla cornea 

 

  • Mainly Coccinelids predators reduce the population of aphids considerably. 

 

  • Destruction of volunteer groundnuts and weeds. 

  • Spray application of dimethoate 0.05% or  Methyl demeton 0.025 % are effective in controlling the pest. 

  • DO NOT UNDERTAKE SPRAYING IF THE ACITIVITY OF PREDATORS IS NOTICED
