Mango Stem-borer - Batocera rufomaculata Dejean and Batocera robus Linnaeus

(Coleoptera; Cerambycidae)

 

Nature of injury: The damage is caused by the grubs, which bore into the main stem/branches and start feeding on the internal tissues.  As a result of this translocation of water gets affected.  

 

Symptom: It is very difficult to make out the presence of grubs unless we see the sap or frass coming out of its bored hole.  External symptoms of attack are not always visible; the site can be located from the sap or frass that comes out of the hole. Wilting of the branches/tree is a common symptom.  

 

Management:

Incidence of stem borer is seen to increase when the trees face continous water stress due to successive drought. Protective irrigation, if feasible, should be resorted to reduce the moisture stress to the trees and significant reduction in pest incidence can be achieved through this.  However, this is not practical in many situations and monitoring of trees for damage and physical removal of larvae is important.

 

  • Swab the basal part of the plant with carbaryl (0.4%) regularly to avoid the adult oviposition. 

  • Clean the affected area and pour solution of dichlorovas 76 EC @ 20 ml/ 10 lit of water in to the holes and seal. 

  • Collect adult beetles and kill them. 

  • As soon as galleries are observed, destroy larvae or pupae; cut off dried branches. 

  • Remove the frass and inject dichlorovos.
