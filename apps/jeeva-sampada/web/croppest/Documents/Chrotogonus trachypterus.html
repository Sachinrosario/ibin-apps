Scientific name: Chrotogonus trachypterus Blanchard

(Acrididae: Orthoptera)

2.

Distribution: India, Africa and throughout orient.

4.

Nature of injury: Both the nymphs and the adults feed on leaves by cutting germinating plant. The infestation is more serious in low-rainfall areas.

5.

Symptom of damage: The heavily infested areas seen like not sown area.

6.

Alternate hosts: Groundnut, wheat, Berseem, Sugarcane, barley, etc.

7.

Biology / Ecology / Behaviour: One female lays 36-434 eggs, with an average of 186 eggs, in 2-15 clusters in 6-122 days, in the soil at the depth of 3-6 cm. The post oviposition varies from 1-10 days. The insect is generally active throughout the year and the population is highest in August.

8.

Other species with similar biology: C. oxypterus

9.

Management:

Early damaged fields have to be re-sown.
