Red hairy caterpillars -  Amsacta albistriga  (Arctidae; Lepidoptera)

 

Nature of injury: The early instar larvae feed gregariously by scraping the under surface of tender leaflets causing skeletonisation .  As they grow they feed voraciously on leaves leaving behind only the hard petiole and midribs.  

 

Symptoms: Heavy feeding of larvae leaving behind only the petiole and midribs gives entire field look as if cattle have grazed it. Young crop if infested heavily necessiate resowing.

 

Management:

Cultural:

  1. a.Deep ploughing immediately after the harvest of the crop.  

  2. b.Use sesamum, sunflower and cowpea as trap crop  

 

Mechanical:

  1. a.Running light traps/ setting bonfires for 1-2 days after receiving soaking rains.  Collect the adults and destroy them.  This has to be continued at least for a week for better control of the pest. 

  2. b.Collection and destruction of egg masses (Eggs are laid in-group and are easy to locate). 

 

Poison baiting technique -  apply the bait in the field in evening hours at the rate of 10 kg per acre. The method of bait prepartation is given in the IPM concepts and tools sections.
