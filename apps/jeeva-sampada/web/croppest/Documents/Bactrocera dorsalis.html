Mango Fruit – fly - Bacterocera  dorsalis Handel (Diptera: Tephritidae)

Nature of injury: Maggot feed on pulp, turning the fruit unfit for human consumption.

 

Symptom of damage: Dropping of fruits, premature ripening and rotting.

 

Management:

  • Raking up the soil will destroy the pupae  

  • Sanitation of the orchard - destruction of attacked fruits helps to suppress the pest.  

  • Use methyl eugenol traps both for monitoring and controlling the pest 

  • Poison baiting can reduce the fly population in the entire area. 

  • Spray solution ---- 1ml of methyl eugenol + 1 lit of water + 1 ml of monocrotophos 50 EC, divided in to ½ lit and spread in the field. 

  • Timely harvesting is very important; do not keep the matured fruit in field or do not allow to ripe on tree itself. 

  • Chemical control; Spraying of Deltamethrin 2.5 EC @ 0.025 % or carbaryl 50 WP @ 0.1 % will effectively control the flies.
