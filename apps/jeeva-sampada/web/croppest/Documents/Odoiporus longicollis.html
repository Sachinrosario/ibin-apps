1.

English name: Banana stem weevil

Scientific name: Odoiporus longicollis Olivier start (Coleoptera: curculionidae

2.

Distribution:

3.

Appearance:

  1. a. 

Eggs: Yellowish white and cylindrical in shape

Grubs: Apodous, soft, fleshy, wrinkled and sparsely covered with brown hairs of varying lengths.

Adults (Male or Female): Weevils are reddish brown and black with a pointed head.  

4.

Nature of injury:  Grubs feeding on tissue around the air chamber of leaf sheath and then bore inside the pseudostem.

5.

Symptoms of damage: The pseudostem thus riddled becomes weak and starts rotting, ultimately the tree breaks down with the strong blast of wind.  The number of spikes becomes smaller and inferior.

Pest is more active during- summer and monsoon months

6.

Alternate hosts:

7.

Biology / Ecology / Behaviour:

8.

Other species with similar biology:

9.

Management:

    1. 1.Uproot and burn the infested plant  

    2. 2.If the plants are in the initial stage of infestation, 3 aluminium phosphate tablets (0.5 g each) should be inserted into pseudostem.  

    3. 3.Rhizome treatment with carbofuran granules @1 Kg/ ha  Which would protect from nematode also. 

    4. 4.Soil application of granules like carbofuran / Phorate 1 Kg/ ha  

    5. 5.Spray carbaryl @ 20 g/ 10 litre of water or 20 ml of Endosulfan per 10 litre of water.
