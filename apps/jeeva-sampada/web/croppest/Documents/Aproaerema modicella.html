Groundnut Leaf-miner - Aproaerema modicela (Lepidoptera; Glechiidae)

 

Nature of injury: As soon as larvae hatch, the early instar larvae move along the midrib.  They start scraping the green matter from the leaves.  Then mine into the tender leaflets and web together the adjacent leaves and feed on leaf tissues.

 

Symptoms: The leaflets get disorted and due to feeding gets dry up in course of time.  Leaves start showing burnt up appearance under water stress conditions. In the badly infested fields, the crop gives brunt up appearance.  

 

Management:

  • As bunch type varieties generally show more infestation than spreading type, in endemic areas use bunch type varieties. 

  • Rotation of groundnut with non-leguminous crop should be followed to reduce leaf minor population. Rotation of groundnut with soybean should be avoided. 

  • As adults are phototropic setting light traps in infested field between 4 am to 6 am and 8pm to 10pm helps in effective control. Setting up of petromax light traps at ground level, early in the season found to reduce the pest incidence.  

  • If the incidence occurs between 30 and 60 days of the crop run thorny brushwood over the crop lightly to expose the larvae to natural enemies. 

  • Important ones are Trichogramma sp. Aspergillus flavus (fungus); Mermethid nematode.   

  • Spray any one of the insecticides, viz., Chlorpyriphos EC 0.05 %, Quinalphos 0.05 Monocrotophos 0.05 % after 2-3 days after peak collection of moths by light traps.
