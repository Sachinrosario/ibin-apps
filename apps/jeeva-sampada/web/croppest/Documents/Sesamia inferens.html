Ragi Pink Stem Borer - Sesamia inferens  (Noctuidae: Lepidoptera)

Symptom of damage:  

  1. 1.At seedling stage “deadheart” are formed 

  2. 2.At earhead stage “chaffy earhead” are the visible symptoms of damage 

 

Commonly seen during the dry pre-monsoon period.  In general the pest occurrence is negligible.

 

Management:

  1. 1.Generally, no intervention is required due to low incidence of the pest.  If required, spraying of Endosulfan at 0.05% at earhead emergence stage.
