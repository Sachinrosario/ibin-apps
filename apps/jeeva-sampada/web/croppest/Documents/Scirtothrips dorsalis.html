Chilli thrips - Scirtothrips dorsalis (Thripidae: Thysanoptera)

 

Nature of Damage: Both nymph and adults suck the sap from young tender leaves there by causing damage to the crop.

 

Symptom of damage: Initially the leaf curls upwards, later twisting and deformation of the leaf occurs. There will be heavy dropping of flowers and flower buds. Finally plants become sickly in appearance. It transmits leaf curl disease of chilli (Murda disease).

 

Management:

  • Grow resistant varieties like pusa jwala, EA 7-6-6 

  • Periodical spray application of systemic insecticides like dimethoate 0.05 % or methyl demeton 0.05 % or acephate 0.075% or methyl demetron 0.025% four times at monthly interval commencing a month after transplanting may afford appreciable protection.
