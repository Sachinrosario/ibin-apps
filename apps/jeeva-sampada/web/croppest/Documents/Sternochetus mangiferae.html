Mango-stone Weevil - Sternochetus mangiferae (Coleoptera: Curculionidae)

 

Nature of injury: The grub feeds on he endocarp and the seed coat and spoils the entire fruit when the weevils make an exit holes through ripened  mangoes.

 

Symptom: Affected fruits start dropping and we can see holes on fruits when adults emerge out.  The fruit does not show/exhibit any outward sign of infestation. Adults make exit hole by boring the pulp, during which excreta is mixed with the pulp and fruits will become unfit for consumption.  

 

Management:

  • Collection and destruction of affected fruits.  The pest can be suppressed by destroying all fallen fruits, infested mangoes and by disposing of refuge, stones, debris, etc. Once the infestation occurs, the weevil being an internal feeder throughout its development, cannot be controlled with any of the insecticides. 

  • As a preventive measure, spray deltamethrin 2.8 EC @ 0.0025%, second spray fenthion 50 EC 10 ml/ lit of water six weeks after fruitset when fruit is approximately marble sized.
