Sorghum earhead-bug - Calocoris angustatus.(Miridae: Hemiptera)

 

Nature of injury: Both the nymphs and adults feed in the green ear heads. As a result the grains remain chaffy or shrivelled.

 

Symptom of damage:  Both nymphs and adults infest the panicles and suck sap from the developing grain.  Consequently, grain attacked in an early stage of development is shrivelled, reducing crop yield.  Older grain shows distinct feeding punctures that reduce grain quality.  

 

Management:

1. Encourage the natural enemies like reduviid bug and a lygaeid bugs which are predacious on the insect.  Do not undertake spraying if predators are present.

2. Spray application of recommended pesticides like Endosulfan 0.07% or Malathion 0.05 % is very effective against the earhead bug or dust Malathion 5D or Carbaryl 10D @ 10 kg per acre.
