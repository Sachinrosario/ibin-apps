1. English name: The Rhinoceros Beetle

Scientific name: Oryctes rhinoceros Linnaeus

(Coleoptera; Scarabaeidae)

2. Distribution: Throughout India in coconut growing belt of India.

3. Appearance:

  1. a.Eggs: The oval creamy white seed like 100- 150 eggs are laid 5-15 cm below the soil, in decaying wet organic matter.  

  2. b.Larvae: It is stout, sluggish and white in colour with a pale brown head and is usually found at a depth of 5-30 cm. 

  3. c.Adults (Male / Female):  The shining stout black or reddish black beetle has a pointed long horn on its head in case of male, in female the horn is short. It stoutly built, is elongate and cylindrical and measures about 4-5 cm. It has well developed wings and can fly long distances.  

4. Nature of injury: It is harmful only in the adult stage and it feeds on the crown of the coconut tree.

5. Symptoms of damage: The damaged leaves show characteristic clippings or holes in the leaflets. Frequent infestation results in stunting of trees and death of growing point in young plantations. The chewed fibrous material present near holes can easily make out the infestation.

6. Alternate hosts: Pineapple, Sugarcane, Aloe, African oil palm, Palmyrah, Date palm and Tail pot palm.

7. Biology and Ecology:  The female lays the oval, creamy white eggs in manure pits, decaying vegetable matter, undisturbed heaps, etc. to the depth of 5 to 15 cm. A female may lay upto 140 eggs and the incubation period is 8-18 days. The grub feeds on the decaying matter and the larval stage lasts for 99 to 182 days. The grubs pupate in earthen cells at a depth of 0.3 to 1 meter and emerge as adults in 10-25 days. The total life cycle from egg to adult ranges from 3½ to 8 months and adult longevity extents upto 290 days.

Beetles are active in the night. Adults live for more than 200 days.

9. Management:

Avoid breeding grounds of the beetle and destroying the early stages of the insect such as eggs, grubs, and pupae.  

Manure heaps and pits in the vicinity of gardens where the insect often breeds should be periodically examined and the grubs destroyed.

The crown less trunks and dead trees should be cut and dried; else the beetles will breed in them.

Beetles should be extracted from the crown with the help of iron hooks.

Metarrhizium anisopliae and Beauveria bassiana as a biocontrol agents for grubs.

Pieces of tender coconut stem split longitudinally and treated with fresh toddy will also be an effective trap.
