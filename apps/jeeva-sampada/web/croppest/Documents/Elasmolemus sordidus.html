Pod sucking bug - Elasmolemus sordidus  (Lygaeidae: Hemiptera)

 

Nature of injury: It feeds on pods when soil has cracked. It feeds on kernel from freshly harvested pods heaped on field.

 

Symptom of damage: Due to its direct feeding on kernel, it affects the quality of kernel, and it also reduces oil content and consumer acceptability.  An affected seed fails to germinate.

 

Management:

Mechanical: keeping rubbish heaps at various places can trap bugs, such heaps may later be burnt.

Storage material (gunny bag) has to be treated with 0.05 per cent Malathion dust.
