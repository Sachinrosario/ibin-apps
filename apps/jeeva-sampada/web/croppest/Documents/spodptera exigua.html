Cut worm - Spodoptera exigua  (Noctuidae: Lepidoptera)

 

Symptom of damage: Appearance of cut dried seedling / defoliated plants.

 

Management:

1. The larvae hiding under the clods in the field and along the bunds can be collected and destroyed.

  1. 1.Spread 10 kg of monocrotophos bait per acre, prepared as per the directions given in previous section on IPM concepts and tools. 

3. If incidence recurs, spray with chlorpyrifos 0.05% during evening at the base of the plant.
