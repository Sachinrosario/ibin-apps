Diagnostic characteristics:   
     Female: Cephlon and pedigeous segment I fused, pedigerous segment V produced posteriorly into asymmetrical spines, right spine slightly longer than left; Urosome I to III beset with triangular spikes on their disto-dorsal margins; urosome I barrel shaped, and provided with clusters of spinules on anterior half of lateral and dorsal margins; urosome IV bears on its dorso-lateral posterior angle finely serrated shields on either side; antenna I: 21 segmented, and with a specialized seta with 8-9 small spinules on its mid margin, on the 19th segment; Leg 5: exopod 1 with an outer distal spine, exopod 2 reduced at the inner distal corner into a foliaceous, stout spine; and articulated spine, finely serrated along the inner margin and with a secondary spine at its mid-length present on segment; another small spine present on outer edge proximal to point of insertion of articulated spine.
    Male: Posterior corners of pedigerous segment V pointed; urosome I short, urosome II longest and urosome IV with a serrated shield as in female; posterior margins of urosome II to IV fringed with small spinules; Antenna I: 21 segmented, 4 large spines, one each on segments 10-13; spine on segment 10 recurved at its tip and with a small secondary spine; segment 18 carries a finely denticulated plate at its dorsal margin; Leg 5: right leg with basipod 2 produced internally into a double spinous process (exopod), one with a bifid tip and the other with a small hairy projection at its tip; exopod I with a cluster of small blunt teeth along inner distal margin and a long ‘Y’ shaped process, with an inner short arm, and an outer long arm, latter with a bifid tip; a small spine present at its base; exopod 2 with a long outer spine distally, and a few teeth in front of its base; exopod 3 sickle shaped, with a blunt tooth and a bristle on proximal inner margin and a small seta on mid-outer margin; left leg: with basipod 2 produced at its inner distal corner into a digitiform inwardly curved process- the endopod; exopod 1 with a distal outer spine; exopod 2-3 plate loke; twice as long as broad, with a spine on its inner distal margin; its distal margin is serrated and produced into a beak-like structure on its finer extremity.

Additional remarks:
     This species has been redescribed by Ummerkutty (1961) and Desai and Bal (1961). P. ardjuna  shares with P. hickmani, P. salinus, P. marinus, P. stuhlmani, P. cornutus and P. colefaxi the characteristic bifurcated exopod on the right leg.  The specimens described by P. ardjuna by Wellershaus (1969) actually is P. jonesi Pillai. The larval development of P. ardjuna is studied by Alvarez and Kevalramani (1970).
