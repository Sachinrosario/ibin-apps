SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:   

Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female: Cephalothorax: 2.75 mm; urosome: 1.07 mm. The cephalothorax slender. The genital opening is situated proximally; the segment has a slight swelling on the ventral margin between the genital opening and the distal margin of the segment. The furcal rami 5 times as ling as wide, and they are divergent. The anal segment and furca are coarsely covered with short fine hairs on the ventral surface.
     The 1st antenna exceeds the end of the furca by distal 4 segments. The mouth parts and swimming legs as described and figured by Giesbrecht. In the 5th pair of legs the inner marginal spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod reaches the base of the 2nd inner marginal seta of the 3rd segment of the exopod. 

     Male: Left antennule prehensile. 5th pair of legs asymmetrical, left exopodite with long subulate bristle.

Additional  remarks:
     The specimen though much alike to A. glacialis  Sars, differs from it in its small size, slender cephalothorax and in the proportional lengths of the abdominal segments and furca.  Claus specimen measured 3.5-4.0 mm; Giesbrecht’s specimen measured 3.7-3.85 mm; Farran’s specimen from the South Atlantic measured 2.04-2.16mm.
