Descriptive characters:  
The following details of this species are based on the specimen from Krusadai Island in the Gulf of Mannar:  Corallum submassive, with a greater spread of 20 and a total height 7 cm.  The top of the colony is dead.  Calices rounded or oval in outline, 2.5 to 4 mm in diameter.  Wall flat 1 to 1.5 mm thick, not much elevated.  Septo-coastae low, confluent, 30 to 36 on an average, sometimes upto 40.  Alternating in size, larger ones narrow down within the calices and join the columella.  Septal sides granular, edges entire.  Columella styliform or a little flattened.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
