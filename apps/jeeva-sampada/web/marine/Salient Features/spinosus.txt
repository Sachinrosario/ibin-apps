SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 

Postlarva: A long, strong dorsomedian spine present on the 3rd abdominal segment followed by smaller spines on 4th and 5th abdominal segments. Rostrum is very long, sword-like with dorsal and ventral teeth (14 /8) respectively; antennal and pterygostomian spines present on carapace. First 2 pairs of  thoracic legs chelate; exopodites present on all legs; that of the 5th slightly reduced. Tergum of 2nd abdominal segment broad and overlaps the 1st and 3rd abdominal somites. Telson is very long, narrow and bearing a fused, acicular median spine with  8 pairs of spines.
Diagnostic characteristics:   The sword-like rostrum with many dorsal and ventral teeth; the absence of  a hepatic spine on the carapace; long and strong dorsomedian spine on  the 3rd abdominal segment are  very prominent characters. The outer margin of the antennal scale is armed with 6  spines and much elongated, and inner side with numerous setae. The exopod of the 5th leg is reduced; telson long with a fused median spine;   3 pairs of marginal spines and with 5 pairs of inner setae (8+1+8). The exopod of uropod broad and bears a spine at the externodistal margin; inner margin highly setose.

Sex attributes: Separate (heterosexual).Descriptive characters :Rostrum very long,with dorsal and ventral teeth. A long, strong dorsomedian spine on the 3rd abdominal segment. Outer side of exopod of  antennal scale with (6) strong spines. Second leg with a large chela.Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.

Main food: plankton,algae,polychaetes and detritus with sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous.

Size and age: 31.0mm

Longivity (y)  (wild) : 1-3 years.         

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Postlarva: length.31mm.Rostrum long, sword-like, bearing dorsal and ventral teeth; antennal and pterygostomian spines present; a long, strong dorsomedian spine present on the 3rd abdominal somite,followed by smaller spines on 4 and 5. Legs with exopods, that on the 5th reduced; telson with a median acicular, fused spine with 8 pairs of spines   

Characteristics:The long  rostrum with strong dorsal and ventral  teeth; a long median spine on the 3rd abdominal segment.
