Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum forms tufted thick branches 4 to 5 cm high and 2 to 5 cm thick or may remain club-shaped.  Corallites polygonal, 1.5 to 3 mm in length, deep.  Wall thick highly fenestrate.  Septa in one or two cycles, very thin, mostly only projecting slightly from the wall.  Primaries thicker and longer than the secondaries.  Some times the septa extend to the centre of the fossa, and fuse to form a columella like structure (Klunzinger, 1879).  Intercorallite wall with spines.  An epitheca is visible at the lower parts of the corallum.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
