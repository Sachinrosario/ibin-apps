SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony flabellate, branches often in a helicoid pattern, main stem 
about 3 mm in diamter at its base; median groove visible at places.

			Calyces alternate, lateral and about 1.8 mm apart; conical in shape 
with a height of 1.2 mm and a basal diameter of 1 mm. 

Cortex thin and axis calcareous; branches and branchlets break off easily. 

			Spicules are : (1) Dumbbells. Size 0.050 x 0.033 mm (Fig.a) (2)
 Bean -shaped spicules. Quite characteristic, size 0.126 x 0.029 mm (Fig.b) (3) Crosses. 
Diameter upto 0.037 mm (Fig.c).


Colour		:	Colony red, axis greenish and spicules colourless.

Size attained	:	Up to 10 cm
