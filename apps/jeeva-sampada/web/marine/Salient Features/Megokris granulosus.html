SALIENT FEATURES:             Ref No:31, 103, 197

 

Morphological:  Body densely pubescent. Rostrum reaching to distal half of 2nd antennular segment or little beyond, up curved in females, straight in males, armed with 9 to 11 dorsal teeth; hepatic and cervical grooves discernible, longitudinal suture short, pterygostomian angle blunt; mid-dorsal crest on last 4 abdominal segments; telson armed with a pair of subapical, small movable spines and 2 or 3 pairs of spinules; epipod  absent on 1st and 2nd pereopods, present on 3rd pereopod; 5th pereopod extending beyond antennular peduncle, ususlly by dactyl and part of propodus. Petasma with very broad distolateral projections, their tips pointed, curving forward in a broad sweep and then inward; distomedian projections small, curved ventrally. Anterior plate of thelycum flat or slightly concave, rounded distally and with a posterior tongue-like projection which can be very prominent and is often fused to posterior plate; posterior plate excavate on either side of median convexity.

Diagnostic characteristics: Epipods absent on 1st and 2nd pereopods; petasma with very broad distolateral projections, their tips pointed, curving forward in a broad sweep and then  inward; anterior plate of thelycum flat or slightly concave, rounded distally and with a posterior tongue-like projection which is fused with posterior plate.

 

        Colour: Body pinkish to red, sometimes yellowish or paler on sides; uropods red with

         generally distinct yellow margins.

 

Size and age:

   Maximum length(cm)(male/female/unsexed): Male 7.2;  Female 9.5
