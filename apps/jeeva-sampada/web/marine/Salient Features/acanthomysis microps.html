Morphological:

Diagnostic characteristics:
Body short and stout. Anterior margin of carapace broadly rounded. Eyes globular, cornea much narrower than stalk. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Carpopropodus three segmented, dactylus with long slender nail. Telson linguiform, widened basal part with three to four spines, distal two third with twenty five to twenty seven long and short spines, long spines alternating with one or two short spines, distal border rounded and armed with six to eight large flattened similar spines. Endopod of uropod with three spines below statocyst.
