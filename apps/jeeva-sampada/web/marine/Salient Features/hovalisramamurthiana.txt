SALIENT FEATURES OF Halophila ovalis subsp. ramamurthiana   Ravikumar & Ganesan  

Leaves: Paired at each node, petiolate, petiole 0.3 to 6 cm long, lamina oblong, glabrous, assymmetrical at base with entire margin, cross-veins 7-16 pairs, sub-opposite or alternate, mid-rib never extend beyond the intramarginal nerve at the apex 

Inflorescence: Solitary, flowers axillary, dioecious covered by two spathes; female flowers sessile 

Perianth: Tepals three, ovate, mucronate at apex

Ovary: Ellipsoid and ovoid, papillose adaxially 

Fruits: Ovoid, normally with 6-12 seeds, sometimes with 18 seeds in viscous fluid 

Seeds: Globose, beaked at both ends 

Flowering and fruiting: Throughout the year
