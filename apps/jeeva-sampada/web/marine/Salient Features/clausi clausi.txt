SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:                                 Diagnostic characteristics:        The width of the pereon (dorsal view) is much greater than that of the pleon.  The 2nd segment of pereopods I is not very strongly broadened and its maximum width much less than its length. Pereopods VI  are only  slightly longer than pereopods V. In pereopods VII the 4th and 5th segment are equal. The telson reaches ½-2/3 the length of the basipodite of uropods III.

Sex attributes:Dimorphic
Male: 1st antenna well developed , female: 1st antenna reduced.
