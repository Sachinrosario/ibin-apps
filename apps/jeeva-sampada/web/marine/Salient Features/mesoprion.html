Descriptive characters:
  Body slender, its depth 3.2 to 3.5 times in standard length, usually deeper than head; head without spines, its anterior part scaleless. Suborbital depth about half of vertical eye diameter.
  Teeth villiform in jaws, the outer row slightly enlarged and conical, 4 to 10 slender canine teeth in front of upper jaw, none on lower jaw.
Dorsal fin single, with 10 spines and 9 soft rays; pelvic fins with an axillary scale; anal fin with 3 spines and 7 soft rays. Caudal fin forked; its upper lobe prolonged into a short, fine filament, no filaments in other fins.
Scales fairly large; lateral line with 45 to 47 scales. Ref.No: 25,38,39
  

Colour:
   Upper half of head and body rosy, below silvery. Head with yellow streaks from eye to below nostrils and from eye to middle of upper jaw. Two broad pale yellow lines along flanks, but other pale yellow lines may also be visible. A red spot below origin of lateral line, but no dark saddles on back.. Dorsal fin with a yellow margin and a broad yellow median longitudinal band which subdivides towards tail into 3 yellow bands separated by blue lines. Pelvic fins pink, with elongated first rays deep red and axillary scales yellow; anal fin chalky pinkish, with two yellow longitudinal lines; caudal fin reddish, median rays yellow, outer rays and filament red.     Ref.No.25,38,39

  The body cavity is pinkish colour, while in other species not so.

Feeding habit:
  It is a demersal carnivore, the major food items consisting of crustaceans, fishes, molluscs and annelids.

Main food:
Acetes - was present in the diet in almost all months with the highest consumption in September and the lowest in April.

Fishes - constituted the second most important item in the diet, composed mainly of juveniles of Nemipterus japonicus, Nemipterus mesoprion, sciaenids, Myctophum spp and Harpadon nehereus. Feeding on fishes was observed to be maximum in March and minimum in February.

Prawns - were represented by Solenocera spp, Parapenaeopsis spp, and Metapenaeus spp, with the maximum consumption in January and the lowest in October.

Other Crustaceans - constituted by crabs and Squilla was consumed maximum in April and minimum in November

Molluscs - are constituted by Sepia and Loligo spp. and Gastropods

Annelids - Nereis spp and more during November & February.

Locality: Veraval              Ref No.20

The food mainly consists of teleosts and crustaceans. Among the crustaceans small ones of both penaeid and non penaeid prawns, viz. Penaeus sp., Metapenaeus sp., Acetes sp and Solenocera sp. mainly contributed to the food besides small crabs and Squilla sp.
