Salientfeatures:
 Morphological:

	Male:- The body is perfectly ovate and dorso-ventrally flattened, with the margine fringed with fine hairs interspersed with short setae. The cephalon is one third to one half as long as broad and the area between the eyes is somewhat rectangular. The dorsal surface of the cephalon has a pair of submedian posterior submarginal tubercles. The eyes are small and placed on distinct eye lobes placed well inside the lateral border and sunk deep into the first peraeon segment. The antero-median part of the cephalon is faintly trilobed and the rostral process is hardly indicated. The first peraeon segment is the longest, the second is subequal in length to the sixth, segments three to five are nearly equal. Each peraeon segment has four nearly equidistant tubercles near the hind border. The coxal plates are large but completely fused with the paraeon segments and the line of fusion is indicated by a shallow longitudinal depression. The pleon is composed of a single segment without lateral sutures, and like the peraeon segments, has four tubercles. The telson is broadly triangular, its antero-lateral parts are drawn out and overlap the base of the uropods, its distal border is very short and slightly concave. The proximal median part of the telson is tumid and, the like segments of the body, carries four tubercles. The inner part of tubercles is followed by two parallel longitudinal rows of smaller tubercles forming two prominent ridges. The tubercles on the body are continued forward as faint ridges so that the four longitudinal rows thus formed give the dorsal surface of the animal a quadricarinate appearance which has suggested the specific name.
	The first two segments of the peduncle of the first antenna are large and visible in dorsal view, the third segment is slender, the flagellum is six-segmented. The fourth and fifth peduncular segments of the second antenna are subequal, the flafellum is eight-segmented, the first three flagellar segments are long and stout. The epistme is squarish or slightly rectangular with the upper lip sunk into a fairly deep depression on its hind border, the anterior border of the epistome is straight or slightly convex and hairy; in dorsal view the epistome is visible as a triangular lobe projecting between the bases of the first antennae. The incisor process of the mandible is five-dentate, the accessory lobe is tridentate, the spine row is is composed of three to five spines, the molar process is large and toothed. The first segment of the palp of the mandible is long, the second has four setae and the third a row of five pectinate setae. The outer lobe of the first maxilla has nine teeth and the inner lobe four spine setae. The lobes of the second maxilla are subsimilar, the inner border of the inner lobe is hairy and its distal border has five setae, each of the other two lobes has four spines. The apex of the maxilliped is hairy and setoes, the segments of the palp are slightly produced inwards. 
	The fourth segment of the first peraeopod is dorsally expanded and carries two setae, the fifth segment is small, the sixth is stout and carries a barbed spine on the inner side, the dactylus is biunguiculate. The seventh peraeopd is long and slender, the inner margine of segments three to six is covered with bushy hairs. The first pleoped is small, its peduncle carries three coupling hooks, the endopod is narrow and more than three times as long as broad, the expod is longer than the enpod. The second pleopod has a very long stylet which is more than twice as long as the endopod and characteristically folded near its base, the margine of the stylet is spiny and its apex is pointed. The exopod of the third pleopod is two-segmented, that of the fifth has three thickened areas. The endopod of the uropod is fused with the peduncle to form a large lamina closely fitting on either side of the telson thus preserving the oval outline of the body, the exopod is small and roughly ovate, less than one fourth the length of the endopod and lodged in an excavation on the endopod.

	The body is dark brown in colour.
	The female is similar to the male but slightly larger and darker in colour. The mouth parts are not metamorphosed. 


Additional Remarks:

The Holotype male is kept in the Indian Museusm, Kolkatta.The rectangular eptisome is a distinct character of the Genus Dies. The quadridentate dorsal surface of the body readly distinguishes this species from others under the genus Dies.
