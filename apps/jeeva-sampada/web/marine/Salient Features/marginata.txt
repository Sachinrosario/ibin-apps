SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      
Female: Prosome and 1st pedigerous segment fused. Fourth and 5th pedigerous segments separate. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment rounded. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment slightly swollen on the ventral side. First antenna 18-segmented and reaches up to the end of the cephalothorax. Fifth leg highly reduced, segments slightly cylindrical. Free joint with a very small outer marginal spine, slightly longer apical spine and still longer and stouter inner marginal spine. The inner marginal spine is with spinnules on the outer margin.
