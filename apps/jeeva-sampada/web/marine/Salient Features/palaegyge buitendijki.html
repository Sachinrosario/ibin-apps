Salient features:
Morphological:

Female: The pigment on the dorsal surfaceis almost entirely restricted to the posterior halves of the 2nd, 3rd and 4th segments, the small, inconspicuous colour-spots that do exist on the posterior thoracic segments extend also on the first two abdominal somites. There is no pigment around the head in my specimens.

  The head, is clearly formed of two halves, and its posterior margin, which is much shorter than the anterior, is almost straight.

  The pleura of the first four thoracic somites are in the form of very narrow plates lying lateral to the ovarian bosses and separated from them by distinct longitudinal grooves.

  The abdomen is large and is about 2/5 the total length of the body. The lines of demareation between some of the posterior segments are not distinctly visible in the mid-dorsal region. The pleural lamellae are totally absent. The sixth segment is largeand its posterior margin is slightly notched about the middle.

  The three parallel flods below the articulation of the palp of the maxilliped are not seen. The median lobe of the posterior lamina has a large finger-shaped process projecting well beyond all the other structures.

  The posterior lobe of the first oostegite is a large broad structure with its distal end broadly rounded and the inner margin beset with hairs. The peraeopods have, a double boss on the basal joint, the lower boss being much the largesr of the two. All the other joints are distinct.

  The pleopods do not overlap one another in the median line, nor do they extend much beyond the sides of the abdomen. The ventrial surface of some of the anterior abdominal segments is raised into strong ridges. The uropods are small pear-shaped structures at the base of the sixth segment.

  The male is about three times as long as broad, and is almost white in colour without any colour-makings on its dorsal or ventral surface.
  The antennules are three-segmented with the third segment very small, and the antennae are composed of two segments only, the distal being long and cylindrical. Both have small hairs along the marhins.

  The abdomen is about 1/3 the length of the body and is a little longer than it is broad. The sixth segment is small and rounded, indistinctly sepatedfrom the fifth and not even one-sixth the total length of the abdomen.

Diagnostic characteristics: ABDOMEN OF FEMALE DISTICTLY LESS THAN HALF TOTAL LENGTH OF BODY AND BROADER THAN LONG; ALL ABDOMINAL SEGMENTS WELL DEFINED DORSALLY; PLEURAL LAMELLAE OF ABDOMINAL SEGMENTS ABSENT; MEDIAN LOBE OF POSTERIOR LAMINA OF HEAD PRODUCED IN ITS MIDDLE IN A SMALL FINGER LIKE PROCESS; MARGINS OF ABDOMINAL SEGMENTS ALMOST CONTONUOUS; SIXTH ABDOMINAL SEGMENT IN FEMALE BROAD, WITH ITS POSTERIOR MARGIN SLIGHTLY EMARGINATED ABOUT THE MIDDLE; SIXTH ABDOMINAL SEGMENT IN MALE A SMALL INCONSPICUOUS STRUCTURE, NOT DISTINCT FROM FIFTH.
