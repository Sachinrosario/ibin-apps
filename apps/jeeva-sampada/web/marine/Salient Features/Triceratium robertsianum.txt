SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Box like cells with three cornered valvar plane and short Pervalvar axis, sides more convex.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve corners with hollow cylindrical process. Cell membrane strongly sculptured, areolate. Areolae 10-25 in 100µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Sides of valve 142µ.
