SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small species with short neck and two well developed spines. No Antapical horn. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus somewhat dilated, slightly excavated. Dorsoventral flattening little pronounced.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 30-68µ; transdiameter: 36-70µ.
