Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  The following are the details of the holotype preserved in the Hessian State Museum, West Germany.  Corallum explanate, attached by one edge, mostly free, underside within epitheca.  Greater diameter 8 cm, thickness at the growing edge about 1 cm, at the broken attachment area 2.5 cm.  Calices neatly rounded, close together, 1.5 to 1.75 mm in diameter, 0.5 mm deep.  Intercorallite wall very thin, 0.1 to 0.2 mm thick only.  Smaller calices (young ones) average 1 mm diameter.  Sepa 12, subequal, hair-like, placed horizontal to the plane of the axial fossa.  The primaries reach to the centre of the calyx to form a columella about one-third the diameter of the calyx.  The second cycle of the septa either reach the columella or unite to the first near the columella.  Interseptal loculi oval.  Corallum very light.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  A. superficialis is very near to A. ocellata Wells.  However, in A. ocellata the calices are with projecting wall and the primaries are larger than the secondaries.  More specimens are required to ascertain their status.


IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
