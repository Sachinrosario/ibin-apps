SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Female: Body fairly broad, but appearing slender as the sides is usually folded downwards. Cephalon roughly triangular and constricted in front of the eyes, forming a large rostrum, anteriorly rounded in the dorsal view, but bent downwards and forming a movable lamina. Anterior margin of first peraeon segment feebly trisinuate and the lateral corners rounded; fifth and sixth segments sub equal, first three pairs of coxal plates short but broad, last three narrow and long. First pleon segment partially hidden by the seventh peraeon segment, fifth segment very well produced over the telson. Telson distinctly longer than broad, distal border rounded and slightly raised upwards.

First antenna geniculate. Second antenna slender, ten jointed and twice as long as first. Dactylus of anterior peraeopods with only a dorsal and ventral bulge, without well developed nodules. Rami of uropods sub equal in length, endopod slightly overreaching the tip of the telson; inner distal border of rami hairy.

Body redish brown, one specimen with the deeper colour on the right side and the other on the left side; the deeper colouration always on the side folded downwards. 

Male: Body border than in the female. Cephalon twice as broad as long and not distally bent down. First peraeon segment longest, others becoming successively shorter. Coxal plates well developed, as long as the respective peraeon segment. Pleon comparatively long, segments successively increasing in length distal wards. First antenna stout, nine jointed and the five distal segments with a thick bunch of short sensory setae; not geniculate. Second antenna slender, nine jointed, third and fourth segments with a row of long plumose setae on the inferior border. Uropods with plumose setae on the borders of the rami and the inner border of the peduncle; rami broader than in the female.

	Body white, with a brownish tint due to the presence of uniformly distributed dark chromatophores.

Size and age: Length of female: 25.0 mm; male: 14.5 mm.
