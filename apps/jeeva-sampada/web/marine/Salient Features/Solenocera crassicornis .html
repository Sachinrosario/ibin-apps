SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No:103

 

Morphological:  Rostrum straight, slightly ascending, reaching to distal margin of eye or little

beyond, armed with 8 to 10 dorsal teeth; its ventral margin slightly convex or straight; postrostral crest low and rounded, reaching posterior margin of carapace; cervical groove deep, reaching to , or nearly to, dorsal midline; postorbital spine present; suprahepatic and branchiostegal spines absent; pterygostomian angle unarmed; hepatic crest curved ventrally on anterior part, delimiting a broadly rounded loop slightly behind frontal margin of carapace; branchiocardiac crest slightly sinuous and sloping anteroventrally; telson unarmed, not trifurcate; 5th pereopod without a coxal spine.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum reaching distal margin of eye or little beyond, postrostral

crest low and rounded, ptergostomian angle unarmed, suprahepatic spine absent, telson unarmed and not trifurcate, 5th pereopod without a coxal spine.

 

Colour:Body and appendages reddish-orange to red; posterior margin of abdominal segments darker.

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:   In male the median lobe of petasma slightly surmounts tip of lateral

lobe; setae set on distolateral border of lateral lobe somewhat vestigial; spatulate process on lateral lobe without setae on distal edge.

             In female, thelycal sternite between 4th pereopods without prominence; quadrate area of  last thoracic sternite with a low conical elevation.

   and debris.

         Feeding type:  Omnivorous bottom feeder.

Eggs and larvae:                Ref. No: 55, 90, 97.

 

  Characteristics: Mature ovarian egg is spherical and measures 0.25 mm in diameter. Its surface

appears corrugated due to presence of rod-shaped  peripheral bodies. Protozoea and mysis stages have been noticed in the planktons of east and west coasts of India. Protozoea III is 3.3 to 3.5 mm in total length, and characterised by the surface of carapace covered with very minute spinules and its margin fringed with 5 pairs of barbed lappets. Each abdominal somite has one median dorsal and a pair of lateral spines. Telson is bifurcate with a spine  formula of 8+8. Of the two stages of mysis described (Menon, 1940) stage I is 5.5 mm in TL. Its carapace is produced forward into a long and pointed rostrum, and provided with characteristic spines and papillae; each abdominal somite has a large median dorsal spine, a pair of lateral spines, another pair of ventro-laterals and a median ventral ; all cephalothoracic appendages well developed; no pleopods on abdominal somites; telson forked, inner margin of forks with 4 pairs of setae. Stage II (Last stage)  is 7.25 mm in total length; all abdominal somites with biramous pleopods.
