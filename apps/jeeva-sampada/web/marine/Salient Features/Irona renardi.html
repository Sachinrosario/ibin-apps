Salient features:
Morphological:
Adult Female
Body elongate oblong and asymmetrical, about two and a half times as long as broad. Cephalon slightly broader than long, with the anterior border nearly rounded; considerably immersed in the sublunate first peraeon segment. Eyes dark, situated in the middle of the lateral border. First peraeon segment arched forwards, antero-lateral corners produced into blunt processes reaching the level of the eyes; postero-lateral corners faintly demarcated by a slight sinuosity on the posterior border. All the successive peraeon segments subequal in length and breadth. Coxal plates well developed, posterior border rounded, all projecting beyond the lateral border of the corresponding peraeon segment; fourth peraeon segment slightly longer than the others and its coxal plate, especially that on the longer side, largest, none of the coxal plates reaching beyond the posterior border of the segment. Seventh segment highly asymmetrical, the right side being expanded and considerably overlapping the pleon. Pleon long, narrower than the peraeon but fairly well immersed, first segment hidden by the seventh peraeon segment, on the shorter side all the succeeding segments clearly visible but on the longer side all except the fifth are hidden by the peraeon. Telson almost circular, very slightly broader than long and slightly broader than the fifth pleon segment, a fairly prominent median longitudinal carina present.
First antenna stouter and longer than second, both eight jointed, apical segment of both with a tuft of small setae. Second segment of maxilliped with four prominent hooks, first maxilla with four hooks, outer hook stouter; inner apical lobe of second maxilla with three teeth, its surface crossed by serrated ridges, outer lobe with two teeth. Incisor part of mandible produced, molar indicated by a lobe, palp stout, three jointed, apical segment very small.
First peraeopod with second segment stout, third long and slender, sixth segment stout, with the outer basal dinner distal parts expanded, dactylus strongly curved, with a small unguis. Seventh leg very long and massive, sixth segment very much 'expanded, with a characteristic inner distal bulge and outer basal excavation; dactylus with an unguis, very much curved. Uropods flattened, exopod a little less than twice as long as the endopod and reaching far beyond the telson.
Body yellowish in alcohol, with numerous dark small chromatophores scattered allover the body, especially on the dorsal side of the telson, lateral and posterior parts of the peraeon and pleon segments and on the dorsal side of the cephalon.
Male:
Body twice as long as broad. Cephalon slightly immersed in the first peraeon segment, roughly triangular due to the presence of an antero-median angle; eyes large. Peraeon oval, posteriorly narrower, first segment longest, third broadest. Coxal plates well developed, first two longer than the rest. Pleon narrower than peraeon, only slightly immersed, segments subequal in length and breadth. Telson narrower than pleon, longer than broad. Appendages more or less like those of the female.
Body grey due to the presence of dark chromato- phores concentrated on the dorsal side of the telson and the lateral and median parts of the peraeon and pleon segments.
Additional remarks:  
  A noteworthy and very characteristic feature of Irona renardi is the massive nature of the sixth segment of seventh peraeopod with a characteristic outer based excavation and an inner distal bulge.
  Pillai (1958) suspects that the illustration of the seventh peraeopod given by Nair (1950) is not palp. Because of the various similarities, Pillai (1958) feels that Irona robust Nair is a synonym of Irona renardi (Bleeker)
