SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming straight and loose. Aperture large, oblong.

Diagnostic characteristics: Setae not strong. Chromatophore one in each cell.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 6.5µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Obligate Phototroph, which requires vitamin B12 (Lactobacillus type). Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
