SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cuneate frustules with narrow slightly rounded angles and truncate bases. Clavate valves rounded at the apex and very much elongated below transversed by two parallel longitudinal lines.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve striated, striae fine, 21-24 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: 784µ long and 9µ broad.
