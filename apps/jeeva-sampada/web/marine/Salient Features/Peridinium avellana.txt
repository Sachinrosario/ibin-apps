SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells somewhat nut shaped, irregular. No apical horn. Girdle conspicuously sinistral, excavated, striated with narrow lists not supported by spines.

Diagnostic characteristics: Theca thickly covered with pores and striae. First apical not reaching to the apex the fourth apical coming over ventrally like a hood.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter of cell: 30-42µ.
