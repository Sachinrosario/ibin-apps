Descriptive characters:  The main branches are slender, prostrate and subdividing.  At the underside the branches form a lose reticulum.  On the upper side of the corallum there are plenty of immersed corallites with a few nariform ones.  The axial corallites are 1.5 mm in diameter, not much dilated at the basal portion.  They are 4 to 6 mm long and are a little curved.  The primary cycle of septa is well developed; secondaries not generally recognizable.  The present specimen seems to be referable to var. minor.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
