SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting or massive.  Coenosteum compact.  Corallites circular or oval, projecting upto 4 mm, close together or upto 3 mm apart.  Intercorallite grooves well defined.  Calices 3 to 8 mm diameter in different colonies.  They are shallow or may be upto 5 mm deep.  Septa 16 to 30, average 25; about 1 mm exsert, arched.  10 to 18 meeting the columella.  Septa thickened at the wall, alternating in size, smaller ones fusing to the sides of the major ones.  Exsert parts of septa, dentate.  Major septa with paliform lobe.  Costae correspond to septa, either stopping at the middle of the intercorallite area or fusing each other.  Edges of costae dentate.  Columella spongy or trabecular.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
