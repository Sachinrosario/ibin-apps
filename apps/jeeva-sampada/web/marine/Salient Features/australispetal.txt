SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics: 
Genus : Psudopetalophthalmus  Bravno and Murano     Body elongated. Carapace short leaving last three or four thoracic somites dorsally exposed, rostrum short and rounded. Eyes with visual elements. Antennular peduncle long and slender. Antennal scale broad, outer margine straight and naked ending in one spine, apex and inner margin setose. Mandibular palp strong and prehensile. First thoracic limb without exopod, epipod present, ischium with small lobe tipped with prominent spine. Second thoracic limb without exopod and epipod, ischium with large lobe. Thoracic endopods 3-8 slender, with terminal claw. Pleopods of male biramous and natatory, of female biramous, not natatory. Telson large, quadrangular, apex truncate with spines and plumose setae arranged in definite series, lateral margin with spines. Exopod of uropod two segmented, endopod without spines.
Pseudopetalophthalmus australis     Body long and slender. Carapace short, rostrum small and rounded with small denticle on either side. Eyes well developed, elongated, cornea broader than stalk, stalk with minute tubercle. Antennal scale shorter than antennular peduncle; antennal peduncle shorter than scale. Second segment of mandibular palp long with few spines on inner margin, third segment short, ¼ length of second with seven strong spines. 1-8 thoracic endopods as for the genus. Male pleopods biramous, first pleopod with one segmented endpod and thirteen segmented exopod, remaining pleopods with eleven segmented endopod and thirteen segmented exopod without modified setae. Female pleopods biramous, first pleopod with single segmented endopod and five segmented exopod, remaining pleopods with two segmented endopod and five segmented exopod. Telson quadrangular, lateral margin parallel with four short well spaced spines, apex truncate carrying twentyfive spines, outer two pairs long and barbed, remaining spines arranged in definite series with eight long plumose setae in between. Uropod longer than telson, exopod longer and broader, two segmented, basal segment distally armed with three spines and single seta.
