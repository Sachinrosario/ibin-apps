SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony  1 meter in length, unbranched. The base consists of two long expanded, lobular limestone processes, extending laterally from the bottom of the stem. The diameter of the axis, near the base is 3.3 mm. The internode is 3.5 mm long and the length is 3.3 cm. The horny nodes are short, and the longest (basal) one measures 3 mm in length averaging 1 mm. The polyps are biserially arranged. They all curve upward toward the distal end of the colony, with their adaxial sides almost contiguous with the coenenchyma of the stem. The individual calyx is columnar or cylindrical, but swollen with ova in their proximal portion. A typical one is 6 mm in height to the end of the crown of points and 2.5 mm broad at the slightly expanded margin. There are needle-like spicules placed vertically in the abaxial side of the calyx. There are also eight vertical spicules in the distal part of the calyx walls, whose points form a conspicuous crown. The coenenchyma is thick and fleshy.
		Spicules: The spicules of the calyx walls are long slender, needle-like form, sparsely disposed, minute and thorny points (Fig. a, b &c).

Colour	:	The colony is bright reddish brown and axis is pure white.
