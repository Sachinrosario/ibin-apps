SALIENT FEATURES:						         Ref No: 52,83,144,212

Morphological:   Rostrum straight reaching tip of antennular peduncle, armed with 6 to 8 dorsal 
teeth; carapace with stridulating organ consisting of 13 to 25 (mostly 18-23) ridges on a narrow strongly curved and inconspicuous band on posterior branchiostegite; mid – dorsal carina on 3rd abdominal somite flat or slightly convex and non-sulcate; 4th somite with short subcarinae; length of 6th somite almost twice its width; telson reaching to tip of uropods. Petasma asymmetrical; right distoventral projection shorter than left and tipped with 3 spinules on a protuberance; left distoventral projection with narrowing tip, bearing 8-9 short processes on median margin and 3-4 longer processes on outer margin; inner intermediate strip twice as long as outer, thin and leaf-like; outer intermediate strip oval in shape with a small triangular lobe on outer margin. Anterior thelycal plate broader than long with rounded and elevated anterolateral corners.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Carapace with stridulating organ consisting of 13 to 25 (mostly 
18-23) ridges on a narrow strongly curved band, mid-dorsal carina on 3rd abdominal somite non-sulcate; right distoventral projection of petasma shorter than left, left distoventral projection  with narrowing tip and bearing 8-9 short processes on median margin and 3-4 longer processes on outer margin apically; anterior thelycal plate broader than long with rounded and elevated anterolateral corners.
