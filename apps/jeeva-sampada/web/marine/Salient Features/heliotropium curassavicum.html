Salient Features :
Glabrous, prostrate, villous halophytic herbs.
Leaves fleshy, entire.
Flowers white, yellow in center or bear a violet purple eyes.
Corolla infundibuliform, lobe 5, imbricate and bending inwards in the tip.
Anthers acuminate, ovary 4-celled, stigma capitate, glabrous.
Fruits separating into 4 nuts.
