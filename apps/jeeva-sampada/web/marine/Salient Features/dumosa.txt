Descriptive characters:  Corallum bushy, subarborescent with several branches arising from the base.  Branches subdividing with many ramiculii similar to A. echinata.  Main branches 8 to 10 mm thick, branchlets bearing ramiculii 1.5 to 2 cm long.  Axial corallites conical, 1.5 mm to 2 mm in diameter at the top, about 2.5 mm at the base.  A few buds are seen at the basal parts of the axials.  Larger radial corallites resemble the axials in shape and size.   They are 5 to 8 mm long and may bear buds and grade towards the ramiculii.  Mixed among the tubular radials there are nariform and subimmersed corallites.  The nariform corallites are 2 to 3 mm long and may ultimately form tubular ones.  Opening small.  Both the axial and radial corallites have only six septa.  Wall of the radials striated with strong rounded echinulations.  Surface coenenchyme echinulate, dense in section.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
