SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves narrowly naviculoid in the center. Raphe on the middle line, central nodule round and small.

Diagnostic characteristics: Transversal striae more evident than the longitudinal, over 30/27-28 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 125-130µ, width 7.8-9.4m.
