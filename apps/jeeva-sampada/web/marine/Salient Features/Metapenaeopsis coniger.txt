 SALIENT FEATURES:							     Ref No:52, 184

Morphological:  Rostrum reaching second segment of antennular peduncle in male and markedly 
surpassing tip of antennular peduncle in female, and armed with 6-9 teeth dorsally; carapace with well developed antennal, hepatic and pterygostomian spines, no stridulating organ;  3rd to 6th abdominal somites strongly carinated, subcarinae indistinct on 4th and 5th abdominal terga; telson clearly shorter than inner uropod, terminating in sharp spine and armed with 3 pairs of movable and a distal pair of fixed spines; lower antennular flagella 1.0 to 1.3 times length of peduncle, stylocerite extending slightly beyond eye; 3rd maxilliped with a well developed basial spine, 1st pereopod with basial and ischial spines, 2nd pereopod with a small basial spine. Petasma asymmetrical, about 1/3 as long as carapace. Thelycum with the sternal plate between 4th pair of pereopods raised as a ‘T’ shaped ridge, its vertical limb occupying about middle ¼ and extending posteriorly to about 2/5 of posterior plate; posterior plate broad and depressed with elevated antero-lateral borders.


Diagnostic characteristics:     Rostrum reaching second segment of antennular peduncle in male 
and markedly  surpassing tip of antennular peduncle in female, and armed dorsally with 6-9 teeth; lower antennular flagella 1.0 to 1.3 times length of peduncle; petasma about 1/3 as long as carapace; thelycal plate between 4th pair of pereopods ‘T’ shaped, its horizontal limb narrow and vertical limb narrow and short.

        Colour:  Entire body golden brown.
