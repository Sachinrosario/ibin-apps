SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small flask shaped species. Epitheca narrowly conical with tapering apical horn and concave sides, convex near girdle. Girdle slightly depressed, with lists, slightly dextral. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Hypotheca evenly rounded, sulcus flailing widely, right and left lists equally developed, concurrent with two long, slender, divergent spines.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
