SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body flat and elongate,  snout rounded, rostral hook rather  short and usually  not  reaching below middle  or posterior part of lower eye; angle of mouth  below posterior part  of lower eye ,   nearer gill-opening  than to  tip  of snout . Two lateral lines  on ocular  side,  no lateral line on blind  side. Scales  ctenoid on  both side of body ; 13 to 15 scales between  upper and middle lateral  lines.  Dorsal  fin with  77 to 94 rays; anal  fin with 76 to 82 rays.

Colour: Ocular   side  uniformly    brownish , blind  side   whitish tinged with yellow.
