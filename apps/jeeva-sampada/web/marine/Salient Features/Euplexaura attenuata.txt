SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony  is weakly branched. The polyps stand far apart and are shiny. The operculum is raised high. The individual calyces are verruciform. 

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are thick warts arranged in regular belts.
 
Colour	:	The colony is light brown and the axis is dark brown in colour.
