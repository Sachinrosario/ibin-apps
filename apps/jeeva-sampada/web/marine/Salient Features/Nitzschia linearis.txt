SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Long linear valves, almost in the form of a boat, with apices rounded at the external, and attenuated at the internal portion.

Diagnostic characteristics: Keel 8 to 10 dots in 10µ. Striae finely punctate, 29 or 30 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Length 70-160µ, width 5µ.
