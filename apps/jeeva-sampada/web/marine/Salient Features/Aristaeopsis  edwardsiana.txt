SALIENT FEATURES: 						Ref No: 186

Morphological: Rostrum moderately elongate in females and juvenile males, reaching beyond apex of scaphocerite; somewhat shorter in adult males, not reaching third segment of antennular peduncle; armed dorsally with 3 teeth. Carapace with antennal and branchiostegal spines on anterior margin; orbital, postantennal, and hepatic spines absent; cervical sulcus  ventrally deep, rather weak dorsally, almost reaching dorsal midline; gastro–orbital, hepatic, and branchiocardiac carinae strong. First abdominal somite dorsally rounded; second somite with barely discernible rounded mid–dorsal carina in posterior half; third to sixth somites with sharp carina ending posteriorly in short spine. Telson pointed, armed with 4 pairs of lateral movable spinules on posterior half. Upper antennular flagellum short, flattened; lower flagellum elongate, not modified in adult male. Lamella of scaphocerite in adult male produced distally into elongate slender structure, subequal in length to proximal broad part. Pereopods  lacking exopods; first and second pereopods lacking movable distal meral and ischial spines; 4th and 5th  pereopods more slender than preceding ones. Exopodes of all pleopods very long. 


Diagnostic characteristics:  Rostrum reaching beyond apex of scaphocerite in females and juvenile males, much shorter and not reaching third segment of antennular peduncle in adult males, armed with 3 dorsal teeth; postantennal and hepatic spines absent on carapace; 3rd to 6th abdominal somites with sharp mid-dorsal carinae, . each ending posteriorly in a short spine; lamella of scaphocerite in adult male produced distally into elongate slender structure; exopods absent from 1st to 5th pereopods; meral and ischial spines lacking on first and second pereopods.

Colour: Pink or reddish pink throughout the body.

Sex attributes:
       Descriptive characters: Adult males have comparatively shorter rostrum than in females.
