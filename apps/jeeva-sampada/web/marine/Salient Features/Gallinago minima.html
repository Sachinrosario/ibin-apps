SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Small wading bird with short looking legs.



Diagnostic characteristics: Small with short bill. Horizontally divided supercilium but lack pale crown stripe. Mantle and scapular stripes very prominent.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Solitary bird and slow in movements even whole picking marine micro-invertebrates.

Main Food: Insects and other micro-invertebrates

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Breeds in the northern hemisphere.
