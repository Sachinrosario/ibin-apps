Descriptive characters:  
Description of a specimen from Manauli Island:  Corallum encrusting, layer or layer, the living layer 5 to 8 mm thick.  From the surface there rises a few thick digitiform branches about 2 mm thick and 6 to 8 cm long.  Calices 0.7 to 0. 85 mm in diameter, shallow but well bounded by a thecal wall, close together.  Primary septa subequal, usually meet at the centre of the axial fossa over a rudimentary columella.  Second cycle poorly developed, 3 to 4 per calyx.  Papillae regularly nipple-shaped, occupying the entire intercalicular area, 1 to 1.5 mm thick at the base, about 1.5 mm in height.  On the branches often two to three papillae run together to form short ridges.  Surface coenenchyme reticulate with minute echinulations.  Section shows a closes streaming layer with upwardly directed trabeculae resting on an epitheca.  Living coral yellowish-brown.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
