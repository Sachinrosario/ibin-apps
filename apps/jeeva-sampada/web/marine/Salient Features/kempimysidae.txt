SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:   

Diagnostic characteristics:       Rostrum short, triangular and obtusely rounded; dorsomedian tubercles present. Antennal sympod with two long equal spines and one or two very small spines. Thoracic endopods three to seven with three carpopropodial segments. Endopod of eighth thoracic limb long and unsegmented overreaching basal segment of exopod, cylindrical and straight in male; longer and more slender in female with small apical tooth. Telson with fourteen spines on lateral borders, inner pair of distal spines slightly longer than outer.  

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult male and female: 10.2 mm
