
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:         

Female:  Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Line of fusion between 4th and 5th pedigerous segment broadly rounded. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment not swollen below. First antenna reaches up to the caudal furca. Fifth leg single jointed, flat, with a long apical spine. At the base of the apical there is a short spine.      

Male:  Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. In 5th leg 2nd segment of the exopod of the right leg with 2 protuberances, one on the middle and on the other near the distal end. Third segment ends up in a bud- like structure. In left leg endopod is slightly longer than the 1st joint of the exopod.

Maximum length (cm) (male/ female/ unsexed)                                                
Female:  1.65mm            Male:     1.64 mm.                                               
Ref. No.:        T.C.Gopalakrishnan, 1982
