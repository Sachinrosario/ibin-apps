SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony unbranched, virgate, fou-edged and tender towards the tip. The polyps are absent at the basal part and are arranged in 4 separate longitudinal rows; their calyces are low, wart-like. The bark is very thin. 
		Spicules:  The spicules of the coenenchyma are long double-clubs, which are equally and closely warted with rounded ends measuring 0.09-0.12 mm long spindles and double spindles are also found. 

Colour	:	The colony is glowing orange yellow in colour.
