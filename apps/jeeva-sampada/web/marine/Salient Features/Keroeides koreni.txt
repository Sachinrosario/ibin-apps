SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:
	Colony incomplete, very fragile, strictly  flabellate, with flattened branches, 7.5 cm high and with a spread of 14.5 cm. The main stem is round proximately, 3 mm in diameter and 8 mm high to first branch. At that point it sends off two opposite long tortuous branches, which are round proximally and flattened distally. Their proximal branches are broken off, with one exception; but they fork distally into compound branchelets are slightly flattened. The main stem is broken off 3.4 cm above its base and is distinctly flattened above the first pair of branches, having a section of 3.3 X 2 mm. It gives off two short stubs and one compound branch one on one side and one compound branch on the other. The compound branches are flattened, tortuous, and gives off rudely alternate branchlets, two of which are compound and anastomose with branches blow. The terminal twigs are really round, but appear to be flattened on account of the lateral disposition of the calyces, which are very uneven in distribution.
		The individual calyces are quite variable in size and shape. The calyx consisting of a fence of upright spicules with their points projecting in a series around the margin. The calyx measures 1.5 mm in height and above the dame in the diameter. In some calyx walls the large vertical spindles tend to an en chevron arrangement, in some they are vertical and in still other they are mainly horizontal. The polyps are retractile. The collaret is feeble and the tentacle bases bear rather strong spindles arranged en chevron, and above these each tentacle is armed with strong curved longitudinal spindles.

Spicules: The coenenchyma  and calyces are filled with heavy spindles which are verrucae and not in definite whorls.  Some of them measure 2.5 mm in length and 1.5 mm in diameter. The spicules of the axis are smaller than those of the coenenchyma. 

Colour		:
	Colony is brilliant scarlet colour; axis is light yellow , polyps are whitish and the spicules are crimson and yellow.
