SALIENT FEATURES	
Description		:	Colony branching in plane. The polyps stand on all sides, vertical and n various broad intervals at the stem and partly leaving a surface free for the proximal parts of the branches. The operculum is conical and consists of rings of transversal spindles arranged in 3 rows, on which the operculum sclerites of 0.2 to 0.8 mm length arranged triangularly. The calyces are 2.3 mm high and 1.5 mm broad and are covered with sclerites, scaly in appearance. These are provided with blunt projections. The calyx sclerites are with irregular plates measuring 0.6 mm long.  

		Spicules: The spicules of the bark are flat stick-like, irregular discs and with 0.7 mm long spindles with leaf-like projection on side. 

Colour		:	Colony is white, stem and branches grayish  in colour and the 
			spicules colourless.
