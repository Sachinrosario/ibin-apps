SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elongated frustules, more or less rectangular, strongly constricted in the middle. Valve linear lanceolate with acute ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Wing unilateral, projecting above the central nodule. Transverse striations finely punctate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 85-120µ, breadth: 15-25m.
