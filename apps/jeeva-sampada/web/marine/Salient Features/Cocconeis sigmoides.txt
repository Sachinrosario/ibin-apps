SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elliptic cells. Raphe less valve with slightly radial transapical striae, striae about 18 in 10m. Transapical striae crossed by fine longitudinal striae. Pseudoraphe very narrow.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve with sigmoid raphe. Striae radial punctate, about 20 in 10m. Axial area narrow.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 18m long and 10m broad.
