SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum submassive, surface rising into gibbosities in old colonies.  Corallites polygonal, calices polygonal or rounded and oval.  11 to 12 mm long and 8-10 mm broad to 15 to 20 mm long and upto 15 mm broad.  Depth ranges from 6 to 12 mm in different cases.  Intercorallite wall 1.4 to 2.5 mm thick.  One side of the corallite elevated in some cases.  Septa 35 to 45, depending on the size of the corallites of which half the number reach the columella.  Septal sides with 6 to 8 prominent teeth, the last teeth simulating palus.  Septal sides granular so that the corallum has a rough appearance.  Septa exsert and arched, continuous over the wall or stopping at the middle of the intercorallite wall.  Two to three subsidiary septa turn and fuse to the side of the major septa.  Columella trabecular, 3-5 mm in diameter.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
