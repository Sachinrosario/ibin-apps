SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells with dark colour and large size. Areolation large with a central rosette. Areolae increase in size slightly towards the margin 3 in 10m around the rosette, 2.5 in 10m still farther out and at the margin smaller, being 4-5 in 10m. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Inner chamber openings clear. Margin small, radially striated, striae 6 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 160-170m.
