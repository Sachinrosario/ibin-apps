Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum initially encrusting, sending up digitiform or tufted thickened branches that may undergo fusion at the lower parts.  Calices rounded, 0.5 to 0.6 mm in diameter, superficial, arranged in shallow valleys formed by the coenenchyme.  Septa subequal, closely placed.  Pali five or six, all prominent, stand above the level of the wall.  One septal denticle between the palus and the wall.  Surface  coenenchyme forms conical papillae, that remain single or may unite to form ridges.  Under the lens they look spiny (After Pillai and Scheer, 1976).

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Common in Northern Lakshadweep.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
