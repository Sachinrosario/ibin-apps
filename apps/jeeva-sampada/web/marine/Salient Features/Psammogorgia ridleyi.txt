SALIENT FEATURES
		
Description	:	Colony 26.5 cm  high and 12 cm in breadth , branching is in one plane and they alternate with slightly club-shaped  terminals. The main branch is 70 mm in diameter. The coenenchyma is thin near the base but thicker in the smaller twigs. Their walls bear very minute spindles. The polyps are scattered over the whole surface as dark pits and it is retractile. A distance of 0.5 mm separates them. The verruca opening is elongated in the direction of the axis, measuring 1.25 mm in length and 0.5 mm in breadth. They are separated by distances of about 0.5 mm and give the surface a markedly pited appearance. The anthocodiae are wholly retractile. When the tentacles are infolded they form an eight-rayed, star-like, almost horizontal tentacular operculum which becomes hidden by the closing of the verrucae. The axis is horny and fibrous. It measures 2.5 mm at the base and diminishes gradually to a hair-like fineness at the tip.

Spicules: The   spicules are with tuberculate warts in four or five 

whorls. Thick densely warted spindles with blunt ends and quadri  -radiate with distinct crosses are found.
 
Colour	:	The colony is light brown and the axis is golden brown in colour.
