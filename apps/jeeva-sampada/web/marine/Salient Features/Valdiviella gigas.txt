SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:     

Diagnostic characteristics:  
     Female: Copepodid Stage V: The proportional lengths of the cephalothorax and abdomen are 67 to 34, so that in this case the abdomen is slightly longer than half of the anterior region.
     The cephalothorax is oval in shape. The cephalon and the 1st pedigerous segment are fused but  the line of demarcation between them can be seen; the 4th and 5th pedigerous segments are separate. The posterior pedigerous margins are produced in a small sharp triangular spine.
     The abdomen consists of 4 segments. Both the 2nd and 3rd segments are armed across the dorsal aspect of the posterior margin with a row of small spines. 
     The 1st antenna reaches to the posterior margin of the 2nd pedigerous segment. 
     The 2nd antenna agrees exactly with that of Valdiviella insignis Farran and Valdiviella oligarthra Steuer.
     The mouth parts at the stage of development show  but little trace of regeneration. The mandible is devoid of biting edge. The basal segment of the mandibular palp bears a curved seta and a small straight spine like seta about the middle of its length. Exopod is composed of 5 segments of which the proximal 4 segments bear one seta each and the distal segment carries a pair. The endopod is 2 jointed, the proximal segment bearing 2 setae and the distal 8 (? 9).
    The 1st maxilla appears to have the same structure as in the adult female of Valdiviella oligarthra Steuer.
     The second maxilla and maxilliped closely resemble the corresponding appendages in the adult females of Valdiviella insignis and V. oligarthra.
     The swimming legs also are the same as in the adult females; in the 1st pair of legs the exopod consists of only two segments and the endopod  of a single segment where as in the adult males the exopod invariably possesses three joints. In the second pair of legs the exopod also consists of only two free segments and the endopod of a single segment.   

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
 Female: 4.0 mm
