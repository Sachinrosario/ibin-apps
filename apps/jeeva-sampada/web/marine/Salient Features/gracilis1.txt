SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics: -                Body very slender. Rostrum triangular with blunt apex ; in female apex more acute. Eyes small, cornea not wider than stalk. Antennal scale much shorter than antennular peduncle with distinct apical suture. Propodus of thoracic limbs with distinct partition, claw very long. Telson linguiform, apex rounded with small tridentate plate,  pair of long spines and pair of plumose setae; lateral border with spines regularly increasing in length. Pleopods of male not modified ; pseudobranchial rami spirally coiled. Endopod of uropod overreaching exopod with closely set row of spines  on inner margin arranged in series of longer and shorter ones ; exopod with 2-3 spines on outer margin.

Additional  remarks :    S. gracilis is a widely distributed species  generally co-existing with S. thompsonii  but it has not been recorded from the Atlantic Ocean.

Size and age :

Adult  male :   5-7.4mm, adult female : 4.5-6 mm
