Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

	Body oblong and somewhat compressed. Snout projecting, mouth large, with small teeth; upper lip absent, lower lip well develop; eyes very small (eye diameter 7 times in head length), with adipose tissue. Pectoral fins in two parts, upper part with most rays unbranched, lower with 5 free filamentous rays, of which the upper is the longest, reaching nearly to anal fin origin; caudal fin deeply lunate, with lobes ending in filaments. Scales small ctenoid (rough to touch).

Colour : body purple/black, with faint dusky stripes; all fins yellow.
