Morphological:  Rostrum long and slender in females, reaching well beyond antennular peduncle; short in adult males, not reaching end of antennular peduncle; armed dorsally with 3 teeth above orbit. Carapace without orbital , postantennal and hepatic spines; postrostral carina extending beyond gastric region; branchiocardiac sulcus well marked; buttress of pterygostomian  spine moderately long; branchiohepatic sulcus placed well  above level of pterygostomian spine and its anterior part slightly curved upwards.  Third abdominal tergum rounded, no median spine on its posterior edge.  Telson shorter than endopod of uropod, apex acute, slender.  Dorsal antennular flagellum short and flattened.  Pereopods lacking exopods; first pereopod reaching a little beyond  middle of scaphocerite, its chela about 0.14 to 0.33 times longer than carpus and in male the inner edge of merus straight; second pereopod extending almost to distal end of scaphocerite, its chela as long as carpus; third pereopod surpassing scaphocerite by half or more of dactylus, its carpus 0.25 to 0.35 times longer than chela; fourth and fifth pereopods slender.  Pleurobranchia on segments X- XIII in the form of minute papillae (0.3 to 1.0 mm) without pinnules.


Diagnostic characteristics:    Rostrum long reaching well beyond antennular peduncle in females, much shorter and not reaching end of antennular peduncle in males, armed with 3 dorsal teeth; carapace without postantennal and hepatic spines, buttress of pterygostomian spine moderately long,  branchiohepatic sulcus placed well above level of pterygostomian spine and its anterior part slightly curved upwards; chela longer than carpus in first pereopod, as  long as carpus in second pereopod and much shorter than carpus in third pereopod; pleurobranchia on segments X  to XIII in the form of minute papillae without pinnules.

Colour: Pink with reddish bands on posterior border of all abdominal segments.
