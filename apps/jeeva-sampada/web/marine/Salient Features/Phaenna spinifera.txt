SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:               

Immature female:  The cephalothorax rounded in dorsal aspect. The abdomen 4-segmented. The 1st antenna exceeds the distal end of the furca by 1 or 2 joints.            
The 1st leg has 3-jointed exopodite and 1-jointed endopodite. The 2nd leg has 3-jointed exopodite and 2-jointed endopodite. In the 4th leg the 2nd joint of the exopodite is furnished with transverse rows of acicular spines on the posterior surface. The 5th pair of legs absent.
