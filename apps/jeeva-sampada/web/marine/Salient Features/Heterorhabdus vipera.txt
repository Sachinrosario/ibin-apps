SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                

Diagnostic characteristics:      
Female:  Length of cephalothorax: 1.68 mm; abdomen: 0.93 mm.     The frontal margin of the prosome truncate. The genital segment about 1.5 times as long as wide; the genital flap depressed.    The antenna 1 extends to the end of the anal segment when fully reflexed.  The mandible has a long ventral tooth on the cutting edge of the left side. In the 2nd maxilla the spines on the terminal segments and the distal lobes are more slender than those of papilliger section. The maxilleped has a slender normal seta on the 1st basal segment about the middle of the anterior margin.     In the 5th pair of legs the inner marginal spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod is rather straight and is longer than the 3rd segment of the exopod
