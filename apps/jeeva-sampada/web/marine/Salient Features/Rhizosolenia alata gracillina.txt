SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical rod-shaped cells, with shortly conical valves ending in a tube like curved process. A small depression at the base of the tube, into which the apex of the adjoining cells, if any, fits.

Diagnostic characteristics: Intercalary bands scale-like, in two rows.

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 7-29m; Length: up to 644m.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
