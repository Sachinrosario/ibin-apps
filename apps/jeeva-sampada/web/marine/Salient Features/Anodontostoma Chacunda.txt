SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body very deep and compressed.  belly  with a keel of scutes. Head small, mouth interior, snout rounded and projecting; lower jaw flared at corners; gillrakes slender and numerous (50 to 100 on lower limb of first gill arch) . Dorsal fin origin just before midpoint of  body, last dorsal fin ray not filamentous; anal fin short (about 20 rays). Pre-dorsal scales forming a single (median) series. 
Colour: back blue/green, flanks bright silver;  a large jet black spot behind gill opening.
