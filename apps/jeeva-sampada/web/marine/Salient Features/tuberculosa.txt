Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corullum encrusting with free edges, tending to become massive.  Calices 0.4 to 0.5 mm in diameter, a diameter to 1 mm apart, look like deep punctures, a well cut thecal wall absent.  Both the cycles of septa fully developed almost filling the axial fossa.  One or both the directives decisively exsert and broad.

The surface coenenchyme is fovelolate and papillate.  In some specimens as seen in the type of M. sinensis the faveolations form ramparts around the calices.  Papillae, small, 0.75 mm thick, 1 to 1.5 mm high, remain single or may fuse together to form small crusts a marked feature of this species.  Generally 2 to 3 papillae fuse together.  At the edges of the corallum the papillae resemble the tubercles of tuberculate species.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
