SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small to medium sized, broadly ovate cell in valve view; compressed in side view. Valves covered with pores and shallow depressions. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Anteriorly two opposed short spines bordering the periflagellar area. Ornamentation more developed centrally.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 37m.
