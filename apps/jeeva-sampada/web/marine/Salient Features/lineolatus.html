Morphological:

A medium sized (reaching maximum about 90cm) pelagic migratory fish. Body elongate and strongly compressed. Upper jaw reaching to below hind margin of pupil; teeth in jaws pointed and strongly compressed. Gill rakers 2-4 on the upper limb and 8-12 on the lower limb of the first gill arch. Two dorsal fins; first dorsal with 15-17 spines, second dorsal fin with 19 or 20 soft rays followed by 8 to 10 finlets; distance from origin of second dorsal fin to base of caudal fin greater than the distance from second dorsal fin origin to tip of snout. Anal fin with 18 to 20 soft rays, followed by 8 to 10 finlets. Lateral line with a wavy pattern to below second dorsal fin and then gradually bent downward towards caudal keel. Intestine with two folds and three distinct limbs.

Colour: Bluish grey on back of body, the sides silvery, with a pattern of spots and dashes on the sides.

Feeding habit: Carnivore
Main food:                Ref.No. 4,12

   Study area: Gulf of Mannar, Palk Bay and Waltair coast. The food of streaked seer exclusively consisted of fishes. The stomach contents of 368 fishes amounted to 1397 ml comprising 85.2% Sardinella spp, 11.9% Anchoviella spp, 1.1 % Selar and 0.4% leiognathids. There was no significant difference between Palk Bay and Gulf of Mannar in the composition of diet. Juveniles 41-120 mm was found to prefer only Anchoviella spp. Streaked seer is moderate carnivore. This species is intermediate between the King seer and Spotted seer in ts predatory habits
