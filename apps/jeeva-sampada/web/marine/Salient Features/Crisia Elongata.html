SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Delicate zoarium. Projecting peristomes give the branches a serrated appearance. Zooecia long and adnate, the peristome placed obliquely directing forwards. No ovicell noticed. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:   the close similarity shown by the form collected by Menon with the figures and descriptions of Crisia elongata given by Waters (1914), Harmer (1915) and Osburn (1953) made it possible to assign the form to C. elongata. Since no complete internode was represented in the fragment nothing could be stated about the number of zooecia occupying the internode. But it was evident from the fragment that the width of the internode was considerable because of the extension of the proximal ends of the zooecia along the median sides of the preceding zooecia.
