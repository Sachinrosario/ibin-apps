SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Pear shaped cells with straight or curved contours and dorso ventrally flattened. Apical has clearly demarcated. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcal margin terminates, left in two, right in one long broadly winged process.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 62-96µ; breadth: 40-72µ.
