SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony  7.3 cm long formation of verticels are common, 1.4 cm apart and spring from the horny internodes. The branchlets are curved, each consisting of a single calcareous internode measuring 1.3 to 1.8 cm in length. The calyces are arranged in loose irregular spirals and separated by 2 to 4 mm in length. The individual calyces are columnar, 4 mm in height with an average of 1.6 mm diameter. The calyces project at right angles from the branch, but bend upward or distally. 
		Spicules: The spicules of the calyx walls are filled with curved spindles arranged horizontally in the basal parts and obliquely in the distal parts of the walls, those on the abaxial side being larger than those on the adaxial side of the polyp, ad extend from the abaxial mid-line upward and obliquely around the calyx walls, a reversed chevron arrangement. The adaxial side is filled with smaller spicules, disposed horizontally (Fig.a, b & c).

Colour	:	The colony is ivory white with whitish polyp.
