SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological:
Rostrum short,acute and a dorsomedian hump on carapace at the anterior region behind the eyes; eyes large and with a peculiar shape; pterygostomian angle pointed; pereopods 2-4 appear late; 1 and 5 funtional, 5th with a characteristically produced dactylus and uniramous; a distinct gap exists between pereopods 4 and 5; abdomen
6- segmented and has a characteristic bend at abdominal segment 3; telson quadrangular with 7+7 setae, terminally.

Diagnostic characteristics:
Large peculiar eyes; characteristic bend at abdominal segment 3; 5th pereopod large,uniramous, leaving a wide gap between pereopod 4 and 5.

Eggs:
Caridean prawns generally carry their eggs (berried females) with their body and they hatch out into an advanced larval stage,the Zoea.Eggs are greenish in color with a diameter of 0.2-0.3mm; becomes transperant before hatching.
LARVAL STAGES:
Zoea I: length 1.4mm.
Rostrum indistinct, carapace smooth with a dorsomedian hump behind the eyes; eyes large and sessile; abdomen nearly 4 times the carapace, smooth, 5- segmented and particularly bent at segment 3. Antennular peduncle unsegmented,inner ramus represented by a long ,plumose seta. Antennal scale with a distal spine on inner margin,3- segmented distally; mandible poorly developed, its processes not clearly distinguished; pereopod buds present, 1st large and biramous and 5th very large but uniramous,others not yet developed; the gap bet.4 and 5 exists; telson triangular with a slight median notch, with 7+7 terminal , plumose setae.

Zoea II: length 1.7mm
Rostrum small but distinct; eyes stalked; dorsomedian hump on carapace slightly shifted posteriorly. Antennular peduncle segmented; telsosn with an additional pair of plumose setae,8+8. Uropod buds are seen through cuticle.

Zoea III: length 1.8mm
Rostrum acute, longer and tip slightly upturned; pterygostomian margin angular; pereopods1 and 5 functional, 5th with characteristically produced dactylus and without exopod; abdomen 6- segmented,telson quadrangular,spine formula reduced to 7+7; Uropods biramous, with functional exopods. The chromatophore pattern is used to identify the species.
