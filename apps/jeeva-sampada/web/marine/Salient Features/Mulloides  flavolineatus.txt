SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body very elongate, the depth 3.6 to 4.7 times in standard length. Chin with a pair of slender barbells which usually do not reach rear  margin of preopercle, their  length 1.4 to 1.8 times in head; snout 1.7 to 2.6 times in head; mouth small, the maxilla not reaching front edge of eye; villiform teeth in a band in jaws; no teeth on roof of mouth; gillrakers on first arch 25 to 30. First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first spine very small; pectoral fin rays 16 or 17. Lateral line scales 33 to 38; 5 rows of scales between dorsal fins and 11 or 12 rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour: 
silvery white with a yellow stripe on body at level of eye; a blackish spot often present in the yellow stripe beneath first dorsal fin; barbells white; fins whitish to faintly yellowish
