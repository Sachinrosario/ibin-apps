SALIENT FEATURES:             Ref No: 54

 

Morphological:  Rostrum short, reaching anterior 1/3 of eye, upper margin more or less straight

and armed with 8-10 teeth including epigastric, ventral profile slightly convex; postrostral crest present on posterior half of carapace; cervical and hepatic grooves sharply defined, cervical groove extending upto mid-dorsal line and indents the dorsal profile at anterior 4/6 of carapace; branchiostegal and pterygostomian spines absent; branchiocardiac sulcus well defined; antennular flagella nearly twice as long as carapace, and tapering distally; outer lobes of uropod without spine on posterolateral angle; telson trifurcate.

 

Diagnostic characteristics: Rostral teeth 8-10, postrostral crest present on posterior half of

carapace, cervical groove indents carapace mid-dorsally, branchiocardiac sulcus well defined, antennular flagella nearly twice as long as carapace, outer lobes of uropod without spine.

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters: In male, median and lateral lobes of petasma bearing 20-23 and 18-

20 spines respectively on distolateral margin; process ‘b’ with 8-9

teeth, second being longest. Basal piece of appendix masculina

possessing short, blunt and finger-like projection  without long wavy  

hair near the base.

        In  female, thelycum has a quadrangular area with a median bald prominence    

in front of two spine-covered prominences between 5th pereopods.
