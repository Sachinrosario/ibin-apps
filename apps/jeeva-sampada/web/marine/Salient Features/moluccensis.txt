Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  The corallum is elongated and oval, 12 cm long and 6.5 cm broad.  The central part of the upper surface is very arched along the axial fossa, which is 5.5 cm in length.  Here the height of the corallum is about 4 cm.  Septa are unequal, at the marginal part of the corallum, irregularly fusing together.  Edges of projecting septa dentate to lacerate, the lower ones of the higher orders granualte.  Costae heavily granulated with knob-like papillae, only at the periphery enequal.  Lateral sides of upper surface with numerous secondary calices, which give contorted appearance to the corallum.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
