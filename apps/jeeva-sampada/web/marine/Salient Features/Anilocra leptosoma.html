SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Body narrow and elongate, symmetrical. Cephalon slightly broader than long; rostrum apically sub truncate when viewed dorsally, apically rounded and feebly projecting in ventral view; eyes sub dorsal and placed wide apart. Sixth peraeon segment longest, seventh slightly shorter than sixth, anterior border of first segment evenly convex, antero-lateral corners produced. First three coxal plates small, last three pairs elongate, narrow in front and broad behind; none of them reaching the posterior border of the segment. Fifth pleon segment not projecting over the telson. Telson much longer than broad, pentagonal, postero-lateral sides straight, meeting to form an angular apex, with a few short hairs; antero-lateral borders slightly raised upwards, with a sub marginal groove continued along the base and ultimately ending in the median longitudinal carina.

First antenna geniculate, eight jointed, second antenna ten jointed. Dactylus of anterior peraeopods with a circlet of three to four large nodules. Uropods lamellar, rami equal in size, not reaching the apex of telson, inner distal part of both rami setose.

Body white, with a greyish tint due to the presence of dark branched chromatophores on the cephalon and the proximal part of the telson.

Additional remarks: In some earlier records, the uropods were shown as overreaching the telsonic apex and the endopod longer than the exopod. But according to Pillai (1958) the uropods do not reach the tip of the telson and the rami are sub equal.

Size and age: Length: 30.5 mm.
