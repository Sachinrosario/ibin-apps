SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Has long horns and spines. Sulcus is only slightly open at the rear, slightly excavated.

Diagnostic characteristics: The sculpture is represented by tiny alveoles-like markings and by very robust punctae and striae.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length without spines: 56-68µ. Diameter: 49-56µ.
