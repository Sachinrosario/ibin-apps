SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum thin, attached by a narrow base, underside costate, dense or vesiculate.  Collines 1 to 3 mm high, lengthy, concentrically arranged, almost continuous.  Summit of colline thick and rounded, distance between top to top of adjacent collines 2 to 3.5 mm.  Septa subequal, continuous over the collines, rarely a few are thinner, 30 to 36 septa per cm length of colline.  Columella continuous, filling the bottom of the valley over which septa from either side fuse, or lamellar similar to that of P. rugosa.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
