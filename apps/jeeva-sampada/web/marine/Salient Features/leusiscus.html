SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:

Diagnostic characters:  Mouth pointing down when protracted. Second dorsal fin spine distinctly and anal fin spine slightly elongated. Scales present on breast.  

Sex attributes:  Sexes are differentiated by the visibility of the light organs. External sexual dimorphism is manifested by the presence of clear patches of skin on the sides of the body, which are associated with the light organ system. In males the clear patch is a roughly triangular area located  posterior to the pectoral fin. The transparent nature of the skin is strikingly visible when viewed from the lateral and latero-ventral positions, than when viewed from the dorsal aspect.                                                                          Ref no: 6   

Descriptive characters:  Body compressed and rather elongate . Dorsal and ventral profiles almost equally convex. Upper profile of head rises back with a little concavity. Snout pointed, both its profiles straight. Mouth small, pointing downwards when protracted. Cleft of mouth opposite lower third of eye. Mandibular profile slightly concave. A narrow band of villiform teeth in each jaw. Inter-orbital groove broadest anteriorly , becomes narrow posteriorly. Angle of pre-operculum oblique, lower margin of lower limb very indistinctly serrated. Gill rakers well developed, each witha row of sharp spine son either side. Scales deciduous and covering the entire body. Dorsal fin begins immediately behind the vertical from the base of the ventrals. 2nd dorsal spine elongated and filiform, upper half of which is flexible and extending  beyond middle of soft dorsal and also exceeding the height of body below below its origin in larger specimens (ie above 79 mm standard length ) and in smaller specimens (ie below 79mm in standard length) 2nd dorsal spine exceed only half height of the body and not reaching the middle of soft dorsal. 3rd dorsal spine also long , but only half the length or more of the preceding. 2nd anal spine filiform,        

its disatal one third flexible and lesser than one half length of the 2nd dorsal, extending up to origin of 7th anal ray in larger specimens. The anal fin commences vertically below the 8th dorsal spine. Pectoral fin short. Ventrals  reaching two-thirds to the anal origin. Caudal fin forked.    

Colour: Body silvery, back and sides marked with a number of irregular semicircular and undulated dark grey-brown spots and vermiculations. Yellow spots below lateral line in large specimens, fading almost completely on preservation in formalin. Pectoral axil with black minute dots. Membrane between dorsal fin spines yellow at mid-height, edge of soft dorsal fin also yellow. Posterior margin of caudal fin also yellowish.                                                                                                         Ref no: 3       

Meristic Characters: D.VIII, 16; A.III,14. Gill rakers  (on left outermost arch) are (9-11) + 1 + (4-5)           Ref  no: 2 They are well developed with a row of sharp spines on either side. Feeding Habit: Main food: It feeds mainly on shrimps, other crustaceans and polychaetes.  Feeding type: Carnivorous and bottom feeding.
