SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small to medium sized, elongate cell that is more than twice as long as broad. Pyriform rather than heart shaped with pointed posterior end in valve view. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves with shallow poroids and post median radial pore fields. Long, winged anterior spine adjacent to periflagellar area. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 51m.
