SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Body about three and a half times as long as broad. Head globular, first peraeon segment longest, and it has 2 oblique lateral grooves forming a broad V. First 4 pleon segments almost equal in median length to the fifth segment. Fifth segment dorsally has a yoke-shaped carina and a pair of bold setose lateral carinae and a pair of sub median, parallel ridges; border of telson with setae, tubercles and spine-like bristles absent.

Flagellum of second antenna with four articles. Second segment of maxilliped long and narrow, broadening, distal wards, stout palp, inner plate with 8 barbed spines; epipod clavate, first reaching the base of the palp; apex rounded, inner border nearly straight and outer convex, margin hairy. Mandibular palp normal with 3 articles. Lacinioid seta of right mandible short, with five apical denticles.

  Propodus of first peraepod with 2 spines, secondary unguis prominently bifid. Merus of seventh leg produced into a long spine-fringed process. Peduncle of uropod twice as long as endopod.

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi.

Distribution:
Minicoy Islands, Indian Ocean (Stebbing 1905) Miami Atlantic (Green field, 1950).

Remarks:
  L. (L) pfefferi remotely resembles L. (P) segnoides and L. (P) bituberculata in the shape of the epipod of the maxilliped, but these species are assigned to the subgenus Phycolimnoria.

Size and age: 3-5mm in length.
