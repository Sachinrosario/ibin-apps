SALIENT FEATURES   

 

Description: Colony (incomplete) flabellate, 10.6 cm. in height and a diameter of 3.3 cm. The main branches are 1.9 cm. from the base of the stem, projecting at right angles branch with coenenchyma arising 2 cm. above the bend. This branch gives origin to several lateral branchlets. The main stem also gives several simple branchlets, the distance between them being 5.6 cm. The calyces are distribution more or less on all sides of the colony are lateral in position.  The individual calyces are low verrucae, 0.5 mm. in height and 1.5 mm. in width at the base.  The calyx walls are filled with irregular plates from the sides are imbricate. The polyps are retractile, and the operculum usually well below the margin. The operculum consists of delicate spindles.  

Spicules: The spicules are unilateral with a sharp them point arising from an expanded and foliaceous base. There are also irregular heavy with rounded angles, oval discs.

 

Colour : Colony is light brown  in colour, the axis white and the

spicules colourless.
