SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells broad. Epitheca with concave or concavo-convex sides. Girdle right handed, slightly excavated with lists supported by fine spines. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Wings very broad. Hypothec ending in two broad hollow horns, each bearing a spine where the sulcus ends. Theca finely reticulated/ areolated.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length and breadth: 40-99µ.
