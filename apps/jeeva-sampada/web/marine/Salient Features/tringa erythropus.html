SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Sleek pointed body with long coral coloured legs. Tail pointed.



Diagnostic characteristics: Red at base of bill. Dark body feathers with white spots.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike. Breeding plumage is dark.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Feeds in tidal mudflats.

Main Food: Small crustaceans, marine worms and insects.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Non-breeding birds spend summer in India.
