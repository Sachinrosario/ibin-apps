SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum with a narrow attachment area.  Calicinal side flattened.  Growing edges with an epitheca.   Width of valleys 12 to 15 mm, depth 10-12 mm.  Length of the longest uninterrupted valley 7 cm.  Collines 1-1.5 mm thick, fenestrated, 8-10 septa per cm length of colline.  Larger septa with a prominent lower tooth simulating a paliform lobe.  Septal edges serrated or entire.  Columella centres well differentiated, circular in outline, average 4 mm in diameter, adjacent ones linked by lamellae.  Lamellae 1-2 mm thick and often swollen.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
