SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elliptical valves, large square central nodule. Longitudinal canals moderately broad, rather arcuate at the margin. Costae 7-12 in 10µ.

Diagnostic characteristics: Outside of the longitudinal canals to the valve margin present elongated and interlocked large, elongated alveoli, farming to 5 longitudinal undulating rows, more close towards the margin.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 40µ, width 25µ.
