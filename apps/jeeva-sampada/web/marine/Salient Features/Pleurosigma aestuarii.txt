SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Lanceolate valves, gently sigmoid with slightly produced ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Raphe more sigmoid than the valve; excentric. Transverse and oblique striae equidistant. 18-21 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 60-83m long and 16-20m broad.
