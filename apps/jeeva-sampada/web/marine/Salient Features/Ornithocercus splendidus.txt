SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: The most ornate species with very extensive, posterior reticulate cingular list and a posteriorly bilobed left sulcal list that reaches midpoint of the posterior cell margin in lateral view and is directed ventrally.

Diagnostic characteristics: Each prominent lobe with mass of sculptured thecal material and a well-developed rib. Other less developed ribs present. Surface with pores.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
