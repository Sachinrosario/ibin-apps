Commercial useIt has C4 photosynthesis and may be used as an oil seed and for grazing (Aronson 1989:58). Chaudhri et al (1964) considered its role in reclaiming saline and sodic soils.
Edible Uses
Leaves; Seed.
Young leaves - raw or cooked[2, 105, 177]. A pleasant salty flavour, they make a nice addition in small quantities to a salad[K]. They are often mixed with other vegetables in order to reduce their saltyness[183]. The young shoots are pickled in vinegar and eaten on their own or used as a relish[183].

Seed - raw or cooked.
