SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:          Body slender. Carapace produced in front into low triangular rostrum with rounded apex. Eyes short, cornea narrower than peduncle. Labrum produced in front  into very long spine. Endopod of third and fourth pleopods of male with modified setae. Telson with broadly rounded apex, armed with three pairs of long spines successively increasing in length, three small median spines and two long plumose setae; lateral margin with 11-13 well spaced spines ; in female apex of telson more truncate. Endopod of uropod shorter than exopod and inner margin armed with spines arranged in series, outer border of basal segment of exopod setose and armed with four equidistant spines.               

This species is unique in the presence of setae on the entire border of the basal segment of the exopod of the uropod and also in having four equidistant spines spread along the entire border.

Additional  remarks :       According to the descriptions published by Hansen and Ii, the apex of the telson is armed with four pairs of long spines successively increasing in length.  Pillai (1973) found the inner pair of spines slightly shorter than the middle pair in female and just the reverse in male.

Size and age :

Adult male and female -  6.5-8.5mm

Geographical Location:                        
Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea, Port Blair (Andaman Islands) East Indies, Great Barrier Reef, Philippines, Japan, South China Sea,  Colombia, Galapagos, Malacca Strait

Depth range : Shallow water
