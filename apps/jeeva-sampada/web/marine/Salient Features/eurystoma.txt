Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum corymbose with a narrow base of attachment.  Branchlets 2 to 3 cm long about 1 cm thick.  Axial corallites average 3 mm thick, wall very thick, opening about 1 mm.  Septa 12.  Radials corallites ascending, inner wall less developed, a few are proliferous and tubular.  Radials 3 to 4 mm long and 2 mm thick; wall thin, openings prominent and large, rounded or oval.  Immersed corallites large, 1 .5 mm in diameter.  Radials have six septa with fused directives.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The specimens can be detected by the presence of wide-mouthed radial corallites and comparatively large radials.  This species is herein reported for the first time from Indian fauna based on a specimen form northern Lakshadweep collected by the author.
