SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:

Diagnostic characters: 2nd dorsal spine less than half height of body, snout more or less equal to eye diameter, scales small; all over body except breast, mandibles strongly concave. Back greyish with close set faint bars on back and sides.   
      
Sex attributes: Undistinguishable externally.

Descriptive characters:  It is the biggest species of the family. Body oblong , deep and compressed. Dorsal profile more convex than the  ventral; gently elevated from the occipital region to form a strongly humped back. Rostro-occipital  profile curved with a concavity before nostrils and another in the occipital region. Snout blunt. Snout length nearly equal to orbit diameter. 
Mouth with thick lips. Mouth pointing downwards   when protracted. Commencement of gape of mouth below lower border of eye. End of maxilla reaching the front border of the eye. Lower margin of lower jaw strongly concave. Teeth, small, villiform, numerous,  as a narrow band in each jaw. Inter-orbital space flat , bounded by two ridges , which continue behind as the outer edges og the nuchal spine. The median portion of the spine is elevated. Two small spines on top of the head, opposite the front border of eye, the outer more prominent than the inner, which is opposite the ridge forming the outer boundary of the inter-orbital space. Pre-opercle with an obtuse angle, its lower margin slightly concave and serrated. 
Scales small , diaphanous, all over body except on head and breast. Lateral line conspicuous, concave at first, later on becomes convex, but less convex than the dorsal profile, and extends almost up to the base of the caudal fin. Dorsal and anal spines, weak, laterally compressed. 2nd dorsal spine is arched and compressed. 3rd and 4th dorsal spines and the 3rd anal spine serrated anteriorly for about half-length from the base. Ventrals do not reach the origin of the anal fin and has a prominent axillary scale, and a strong spine. Caudal fin not deeply forked, with rounded lobes.

Colour:  Body silvery; back greyish. Close set fine grey vertical bands descend from back to about mid-height, clearly seen in fresh specimens, but fade on preservation in formalin. Membrane between anal spines yellowish. Snout dotted black. Pectoral fin axil faintly dusky. Posterior margin of caudal lobes pale yellow and dusky.                                              Ref no: 7
Meristic Characters:D.VIII,16;A.III,14; Ll. 60.   Ref no: 10.

Feeding Habit: 
Main food: Mainly on polychaetes, small crustaceans  and small fishes. Juveniles of  about 4 cm are found to feed chiefly on copepods and also on cirripede larvae, lamellibranch larvae, gastropod larvae and phytoplanktonic organisms.                                                                        Ref no: 4
Feeding type: Mainly bottom feeder.
Maximum Length : 242mm    
Length Range in commercial : 180 mm (Ref no: 7), 125-200mm (Ref no: 16)
