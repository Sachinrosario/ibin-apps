SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  flabellate and reticulate. Many of the branches are not more than 0.5 mm in diameter, while the larger branches are scarcely 1 mm thick. 
		Spicules: These differ materially from the other forms in this genus. There is superficial incomplete layer of quite small double heads or dumb-shaped forms under which is a much thicker layer of comparatively large spindles, often curved, with thick-set verrucae arranged regular whorls. 

Colour		:	Colony is light grayish brown and calyces are yellow in colour.
