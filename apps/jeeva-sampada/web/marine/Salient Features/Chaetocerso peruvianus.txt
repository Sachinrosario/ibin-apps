SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Single cells, rarely chains of two or three. Dissimilar valves, upper rounded and lower flat with valve mantle. Setae of lower valve starting near the margin.

Diagnostic characteristics: Setae strong, four cornered, spined and striated, striae 18-25 in 10µ. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 9-31µ.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
