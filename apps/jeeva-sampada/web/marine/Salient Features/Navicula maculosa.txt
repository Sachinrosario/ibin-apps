SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Lanceolate valves with well produced sub capitate apices. Central pores small.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves surface striate, striae broken up into elongated puncta of different lengths. Striae radiate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 60-75m, width 14-16m.
