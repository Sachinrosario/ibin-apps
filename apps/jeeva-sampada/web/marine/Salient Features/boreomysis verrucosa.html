Morphological:

Diagnostic characteristics: Genus Boreomysis G. O. Sars
Antennal scale with outer margin entire, terminating in strong spine; apex truncate. Labrum without frontal process. Thoracic endopods three to eight with carpus distinct, propodus divided into two to three subsegments. Pleopods of male well developed, biramous, exopod of second and third pairs elongated, one or both with exopod distally modified. Telson distally cleft, cleft and lateral margin armed with teeth. Exopod of uropod with prominal outer border naked ending in one or two spines, a transverse indistinct articulation present.

Boreomysis verrucosa
Surface of thorax and abdomen covered with small tubercles, frontal plate considerably produced, extending forward to level of eyes; apex acuminate. Eyes small, ocular papilla prominent, major portion of cornea facing downwards. Antennal scale extending beyond antennualr peduncle, broader in middle, outer spine extending beyond apex of scale. Telson 3 ¼ times as long as broad, considerably narrowed at distal half, terminal cleft 1/6 total length, lateral margin armed at distal two thirds with serially arranged spines, about 10 long spines alternating with groups of three to seven small spines. Exopod of uropod with two spines.
