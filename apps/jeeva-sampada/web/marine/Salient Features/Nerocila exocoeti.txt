SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Female: Body roughly two and a half as long as broad. Cephalon broader than long, its anterior border perfectly rounded, posterior border trilobed, the median lobe being larger. First peraeon segment equal to fourth, second and third slightly shorter, fifth segment abruptly longer than the fourth, equal to sixth and seventh. Postero-lateral corners of segments five and six slightly and those of seventh fairly well produced. Coxal plates reaching beyond the posterior border of the respective segments, those of segments three to five slightly exceeding the postero-lateral corners of the segments and those of the last two segments considerably overreaching the postero-lateral corners of the respective segments; all the coxal plates comparatively narrow and apically slightly curved outwards. Pleon segments unequal in length, lateral processes of the first two segments flat, second almost reaching the postero-lateral corners of the fifth pleon segment. Telson almost equal in length and breadth, dorsal surface closely pitted.

First antenna comparatively stout, eight jointed, second antenna slender but longer, eleven jointed, third segment with one inferior seta; both antennae with apical setae. First segment of palp of mandible stout, third small, incisor process tooth like, molar indicated by a small lobe. Maxilliped in the ovigerous female flat and expanded, with several marginal setae, apical segment with three spines. First maxilla with two large and two small apical teeth; apical lobes of second maxilla each with two stout spines. Second segment of first peraeopod large, fourth and fifth short, dactylus stout and curved, reaching the base of the fourth segment when closed. Fifth segment of seventh peraeopod with one and sixth with four spines, dactylus moderately curved. Uropods long, endopod half as long as exopod, inner corner of peduncle produced.

Transition stage: Cephalon almost as long as broad, its anterior border somewhat sub truncate. First four peaeon segments short and narrow, first slightly longer and the fourth shortest. Segments five to seven abruptly broader than the other two. Anterior coxal plates poorly developed, posterior ones prominent. Pleon segments sub equal in length, first partially hidden by the seventh peraeon segment. Telson as long as broad.

	First antenna stouter than second, eight jointed, the last five segments with a bunch of setae, second antenna slender, eleven jointed, with several large and small setae. Second segment of first peraeopod large, fifth segment small, fourth segment of seventh peraeopod with one, fifth with three and sixth with five spines; dactylus of all the peraeopods moderately curved. Exopod of uropiod about twice as long as endopod, peduncle with a few plumose setae on the inner border.
	Length 14.0 mm.

Male: Body long elliptical. Cephalon almost as long as broad, its anterior border almost truncates. First peraeon segment longest and the seventh shortest, segments two to six sub equal. Coxal plates poorly developed. Pleon as long as broad, segments sub equal, first partially hidden by the seventh peraeon segment. Telson longer than broad, distal border rounded.

	First antenna uniformly broad, eight jointed, last five segments with long olfactory setae; second antenna slender, eleven jointed, segments six to eleven with dorsal setae. First peraeopod stout, fifth segment short, with two blunt teeth, sixth segment with four teeth; dactylus long and curved. Seventh peraeopod with one spine on the fourth segment, two on the fifth and five on the sixth, dactylus moderately curved. Rami of uropods flat and lamellar, exopod with two apical spines, inner and distal border with plumose setae; apex of endodpod with one spine, distal border and distal part of outer border setose.
	Length 10.0 mm.
 
First larva: Body long and slender. Cephalon large, constricted in front of the eyes, anterior border truncated; eyes large. Peraeon segments sub equal, coxal plates not developed. Pleon slightly shorter than the peraeon and almost as broad as the seventh peraeon segment. Telson longer than broad, distal part of the border rounded.

	First antenna stouter than second, eight-jointed second antenna eleven jointed. Sixth segments of anterior peraeopods expanded and with four blunt teeth, fifth segment with one tooth. Rami of uropods membranous and flattened. Spines and setae visible inside the still persistent larval skin.

	Female, transition stage and male steel blue, admirably matching the colour of the host. Under the microscope the dorsal side appears closely pitted, the pits showing a lighter shade.

Additional remarks:
	Infection by Nerocila exocoeti on the fish-Exocoetus mento (=Paraxocoetus brachypterus) is quite prevalent and Pillai (1958) observed as much as 75% of the fishes examined infested by this parasite. The adults are found attached invariably to the dorsal side of the head of the host and the immature ones on the surface of the body.

Size and age: Length of female: 25.0 mm; male: 10.0mm; Transition larva: 14.0mm.
