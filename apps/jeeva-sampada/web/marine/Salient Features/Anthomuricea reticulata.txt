SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony flabellate and reticulate, attaining a height of 75 cm., and a diameter of 80 cm. The main stem arises from a broad expanded base and consists of several agglomerated massive stems, which immediately break up into a number of branches of different sizes. Most of them fork. The main branches are compressed, the largest having a cross section of 13 mm by 6.5 mm. The calyces are implanted on all sides of the stem and branches .The individual calyces are in the form of short cylinders, a typical one being 1mm. high and having a diameter of 1.5 mm. The calyx walls are field with uniform small, stout spindles, which are, arranged en chevron on the upper parts, and have a circular disposition on the lower parts of the walls. The collaret and operculum are very well developed. Dome-shaped, and having nearly all of the spindles arranged en chevron, the ends of the tentacles only being provided with longitudinal spindles. The polyps are not fully retractile. 
		Spicule: The spicules of the stem and branches are spindles like those of the calyx walls small and stout spindles (Fig. a & c) and longitudinal (Fig. b) which encircle the basis of the calyces.		
		 

Colour		:	The colony is a dull woody brown, the axis is black and spicules are light yellowish brown.
