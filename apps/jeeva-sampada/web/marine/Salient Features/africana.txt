SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Body stout. Rostrum long narrow and pointed. Antennal scale broad, over reaching antennular peduncle, distal two third of outer margin armed with 13-15 short spines, terminal spine stout and projecting beyond rounded apex. Carpus and propodus distinct. Telson wedge shaped nearly three times as long as broad, steadily narrowing towards rounded apex, reaching beyond articulation of outer uropod; lateral borders almost throughout spiny, basal part with four large spines, others arranged in series of two to four, apex with pair of long spines, three small median spines and pair of plumose setae. Endopod of uropod with closely set row of spines; exopod longer than endopod  with fifteen spines on outer proximal border.              

Male unknown. 


Size and age
