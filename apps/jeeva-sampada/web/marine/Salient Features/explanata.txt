Descriptive characters:  The following are the details of a specimen collected from Mandapam (Palk Bay).  Corallum explanate, about 25 cm in greater spread, closely encrusting, maximum thickness 8 mm.  Upper surface of the coral is uneven due to the presence of large number of small gibbosities, 1 to 2 cm thick and 5 to 10 mm in height.  Calices 0.7 to 0.9 mm in diameter, irregular in outline, rather deep, crowded in appearance, a solid thecal septa well developed, one of the directives much procounced; second cycle usually not visible, if seen spiny.

Surface coenenchyme shows a fine reticulum the meshes of which bear small spines and occasional low, round-topped tubercles about 0.75 mm in height.  Section shows a solid lower layer and an upper thickening layer with vertical and horizontal trabeculae.

Remarks:  The characteristic feature of this species is the presence of small occasional tubercles resembling those of tuberculate species of Montipora.  The species forms a connecting link between glabrous and tuberculate forms; following Bernard it is treated along with the former  group here in.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
