SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	The specimens consist of a number of fragments, which are exceedingly fragile and brittle. One of the larger fragments consists of a branch 8 cm long. The denuded axis of the main stem of the branch is 1.9 cm long and is round, having a diameter of 1.2 mm. It bifurcates at the first node that is triangular in form, 2.8 mm long and also equilateral. Each of the branches bifurcates twice, but one resultant brachlet is missing, and the branches are separated by a comparatively wide angle. The furcation is angular and not U-shaped. The internodes are 1.4 mm long and 1.5 mm in diameter between the calyces. The calyces are all lateral and usually alternate, those on one side often showing a tendency to bend alternately to the front and back, like the teeth of a saw.
		The individual calyces are very large conspicuous and their golden yellow colour is remarkably vivid. They are in the form of symmetrical truncated cones, a typical one measuring 1.7 mm high and 2.1 mm broad at the base. The distant between the calyces is 1.5 mm. The margin bears 8 regular scallop or lobes, the polyps are retractile. They are heavily spiculated with a well-marked collarets composed of strong bent spindles in two or three encircling rows. Above the collaret the spindles are arranged en cheveron over the tentacle bases, forming 8 strong points, each point being composed of a bundles of spindles. Beyond these points the dorsal surfaces of the tentacles are armed with longitudinal spindles.
		Spicules. These are thorny and clubs shaped spindles. They are usually slender, numerous. There are small spindles with two whorls of verrucae in addition to the terminal knobs. The characteristic clubs show a proximal comparatively smooth acicular part and a clavate distal portion armed with numerous spiny points. 

Colour		:	The entire colony is a chrome yellow and the axis is bright 
			crimson.
