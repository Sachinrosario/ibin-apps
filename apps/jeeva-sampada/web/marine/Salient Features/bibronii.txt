SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  Lower eyelid with a transparent disc; ear - opening oval.  Dorsal and loateral scales with from 5 to 7 to sharp keels (pointed ridges); juveniles with 3 keels.  Digits long, with smooth or feebly keeled lamellae, from 14 to 20 beneath the fourth toe.

Diagnostic characteritics:  Light olive - brown above, with three stripes arranged as follows :  a light vertebral stripe broadly edged with black, a black dorso-lateral stripe, passing from the eye to the base of the tail, edge below and sometimes above, with white, and another white stripe extending from the armpit to the groin.  Underside whitish.

Additional remarks:  Though the seashore skink has been found inland, it seems to prefer the sea-coast.  In fact, it was rarely seen far from the seashore.  On the east coast, the skink is seen frequently on the sands of Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu in association with the agamid, Fan - throated lizard (Sitana ponticeriana).  Murthy has found a specimen on the shore of Barkuda Island, Chika Lake, Orissa.  The skink is said to burrow under low vegetation on sand dunes near sea. Like most skinks, the seashore skink lays eggs.
