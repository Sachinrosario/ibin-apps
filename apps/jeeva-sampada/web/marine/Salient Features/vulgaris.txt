SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Body slender. Rostrum narrowly triangular and apically sub- acute. Antennal scale narrower in male than in female, distal lobe unusually long. Carpus and propodus distinct. Pseudobranchial lobes spirally twisted, pleopods not modified. Telson reaching beyond articulation of exopod, base with two pairs of spines, more than half of lateral border with small spines gradually increasing in length, apex with pair of long spines, three small median spines and  pair of plumose setae. Exopod of uropod as long as or slightly longer than endopod with three to nine spines occupying less than one third of margin; endopod with serially arranged spines.


Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Male and female  - 5-7 mm                  

Geographical Location:   Arabian Sea, Andaman Islands, Indonesia, Hong Kong, Singapore, Peru, Queensland, Great  Barrier Reef, Philippines, Formosa, Japan.
