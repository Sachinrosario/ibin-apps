SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body oblong and somewhat compressed. Snout projecting, mouth large, with small teeth; upper lip absent, lower lip well developed; eyes large (eye diameter 3.8 to 4.0 times in head length), with adipose tissue. Pectoral fins in two parts, upper part with all rays unbranched, lower part with 5 free filamentous rays, of which the upper 2 are the longest, reaching to end of pelvic fin; caudal fin with lobes equal. Scales small ctenoid (rough to touch).
	Colour : body golden olive, with  narrow dusky stripes; pectoral fins black, inner side of pelvic fins white, outer side grey, dorsal and caudal fins grey-edged.
