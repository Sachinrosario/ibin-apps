SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells short and broad, flattened obliquely dorsoventrally. Hypotheca with two long hollow antapical horns, each provided on the inside with a tooth.

Diagnostic characteristics: Theca reticulated-intercalary striae large or small.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter 116-144m; length 116-200m.
