Salient features:
Morphological:
Female:
Body elongate oblong, very slightly asymmetrical, more than three times as long as broad. Cephalon not immersed, one and a half times as broad as long, with its anterior border perfectly rounded; eyes well developed. Peraeon segments one to five subequal. Fifth segment slightly broader than others, seventh short, prominently arched. Postero-lateral corners of all segments rounded and those of seventh overlapping the pleon segments. First four coxal plates large, broader behind; posterior two pairs uniformly narrow and slightly longer than the respective peraeon segments. Pleon immersed in peraeon, broader than the seventh peraeon segment, segments equal in length but becoming successively narrower towards the hind end. Telson roughly triangular, twice as broad as long, apex slightly produced, with a faint longitudinal carina.

First antenna slightly separated at their base, eight jointed, first three segments large and the apical segment reaching a little beyond the antero-lateral corners of first peraeon segment. Second antenna slender, ten jointed and longer than first. First segment of palp of mandible very large, second and third slender, latter with four stiff setae. Segments four and five of anterior, peraeopods very short, dactylus equal to fifth and sixth joints combined. Peduncle of uropod stout, rami slightly overreaching the apex of telson, exopod longer than endopod. .

Body white, turning yellowish brown in spirit, with minute branched uniformly distributed chromatophores showing slight concentration on cephalon, posterior border of peraeon segments and proximal part of telson.

Male:

Body similar to that of female, but perfectly parallel sided. First peraeon segment not produced antero-laterally, all segments subequal in length. Coxal plates comparatively broad but none of them exceeding the posterior border of the segment. Pleon not immersed. First antenna stouter .than second, eight jointed, last five segments with bunches of short sensory setae. Second antenna nine Jointed, with scattered plumose sensory hairs. Peraeopods without spines, uropods just reaching the apex of telson, exopod with a few short hairs at its apex.
