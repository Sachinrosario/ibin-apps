SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 


Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female: Cephalothorax 1.70 mm; urosome 0.54mm. The cephalothorax elongate ovate. The rostrum composed of 2 slender filaments. The genital segment produced below. The anal segment longer than wide. The furcal  rami 2 times as long as wide.
    The 1st antenna extends beyond the end of the furca. The second antenna has the endopod 1.2 times as long as the exopod. The mandible has the exopod which is about as long as the endopod. The maxillae, maxilliped and the 1st to 5th leg as described and figured as by Giesbrecht.
  
    Male:  Cephalothorax  1.07 mm; urosome 0.88 mm.  General appearance as in the female. The furcal rami 2 times as long as wide. The grasping antenna on the left. The mouth parts and swimming legs as in the female. The 5th pair of legs has 3-jointed exopod and exopod; in the right leg the 2nd and 3rd segments fused; the 2nd segment has a process on the inner proximal margin. In the left leg the distal segment of the exopod has a spine on the distal end and two spines on the posterior surface of the same segment. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
     Female: 2.24 mm          
     Male   : 1.95 mm

Ref. No.: Tanaka, 1964
