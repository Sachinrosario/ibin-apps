Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum half-funnel-shaped, the peripheral parts made of palmate plates the growing tips with several branches some of which undergoing coalascens.  The plates are 7 to 8 cm broad and upto 15 cm high.  Underside of the plates uneven with several appressed tubular corallites.  Upper sides with small branches but shorter than those of the growing tips.  Axial corallites 1.5 to 2 mm thick, 2 to 3 mm exsert.  Radial corallites tubular or tubo-labellate, scending, smaller ones intercalated, larger ones 4 to 5 mm long, average 1.5 mm thick.  Primary cycle of septa well developed in both the axials and radials, the second cycle is rudimentary.  Wall and surface striated and echinulate.  Corallum porous.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
