SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Body slender. Carapace anteriorly produced into triangular rostral plate with rounded apex. Mouth parts and thoracic limbs as for the genus. First pleopod of male with single segmented endopod and five segmented exopod. Second pleopod with six to eight segmented endopod and nine segmented exopod. Third pleopod with rudimentary endopod, exopod long and five segmented, fifth segment terminating in three spines, two unequal spines on apex and one slightly away from tip. Fourth and fifth pleopods with single segmented exopod and endopod, endopod short, exopod five times longer than endopod. Lateral margin of telson with eight widely spaced spines, cleft with 17-20 closely packed spines. Uropods shorter than telson, endopod with four sharp spines on inner margin. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult male    : 5.4 mm     
Adult female : 5 mm
