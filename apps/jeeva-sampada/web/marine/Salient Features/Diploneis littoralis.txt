SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves elliptical to elongate. Central nodule small, rectangular. Longitudinal ribs narrow, corresponding.

Diagnostic characteristics: Costae radiate at the valve ends, 12 in 10 µ, with double rows of faintly opposited alveoli between the costae.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: length 30-65µ, width 15-28µ.
