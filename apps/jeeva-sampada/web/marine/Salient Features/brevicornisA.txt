Descriptive characters:   Colony caespitose, ventopped.  Branches arise from an expanding base, branches 2 to 2.5 cm in height, flattened at the top, 2 to 3 cm broad, crowded, about 1 cm thick.  Verrucae, small uniform in size, average 2 mm in thickness and height, distance between adjacent ones average 4 mm.  Calices at the tips of branches and verrucae polygonal, shallow, 0.8 in diameter, circular at the basal parts of branches.  Septa and columella rudimentary.  Surface coenenchyme with small spines.

Remarks:  The specific status of P. brevicornis is still doubtful.  It could be a stunted growth form of some other species.  P. stechelli Hoffmester (type in USNM wash.) shows no noteworthy variation from P. brevicornis.  Veron and Pichon (1976) merged P. brevicornis along with P. favosa with P. damicornis.  However, it is unlikely that P. brevicornis is a skeletal variant of P. damicornis, though its affinity with some broad branched Pocillopora sp. like P. meandrina cannot be ruled out.

Feeding habit: Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.
