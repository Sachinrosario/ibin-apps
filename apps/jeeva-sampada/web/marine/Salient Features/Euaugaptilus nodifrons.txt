SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 


Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female: Cephalothorax, 4.40 mm; urosome, 1.07 mm. The urosome is 4.1 times longer than the cephalothorax. The cephalothorax is oblong ovate. The cephalic segment is longer than the combined lengths of the pedigerous segments. The lateral corner of the last pedigerous segment rounded. The rostrum is represented by a knob. The distal filaments absent.
     The urosome 3-jointed; the furcal rami as long as wide. 
     The 1st antenna extends to the distal end of the furca. The mouth parts as those of the male. 
     In the 5th pair of legs the inner marginal spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod about ¼ the length of the 3rd segment of the exopod.
     Male: Cephalothorax, 5.0 mm; abdomen 1.32 mm. General appearance as in the female. The abdomen 5-jointed; the furcal rami as wide as long. 
    The right 1st antenna 25-jointed, extends about to the end of the furca. The left antenna is modified into a grasping organ; the segments 19 to 21 are fused and so are the segments 22 with the 23. The 2nd antenna has the endopod 2.6 times as long as the exopod; the exopod 7-jointed; the 1st segment of the endopod narrow at the proximal. The mandible has the exopod 1.2 times as long as the endopod; the biting part well developed. The 1st maxilla has 9 setae on the 1st inner lobe; the 2nd and 3rd inner lobes have each a single seta; the endopod has 2 short setae; the exopod has 2 long and 1 short seta; the outer lobe has 6 long setae. In the 2nd maxilla the setae on the lobes are furnished densely with short spinules. The maxilliped very long.
