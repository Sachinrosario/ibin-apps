SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming twisted chains. Cell wall thick. Aperture large.

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle bands deeply constricted.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis of the cell 8-18µ.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
