SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Broadly elliptic-lanceolate valves with very weakly rostrate apices. Central area rounded, axial area narrow.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves surface striate, striae punctate, 11-12 in 10m, radial, in complete rows.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 50-90m, width 30-33m.
