SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves narrow, semi-navicular, acute and round at the ends. Raphe approximated to the ventral margin, straight.

Diagnostic characteristics: Axial and central areas distinct, broader on the dorsal side, narrower on the ventral side, striae fine, 17-19 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 40-61µ, width: 7-11m.
