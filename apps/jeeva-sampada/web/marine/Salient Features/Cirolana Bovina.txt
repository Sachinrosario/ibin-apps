SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :
Ref No: Pillai, 1958

Body roughly ablong in ovigerous females, considerably elongated and with parallel sides in males; anterior margin of head rounded, without a rostral point. Peraeon segments without spines, first twice as long as second, others subequal; hind margin of segments six and seven feebly crenulate. Coxal plates large, with setose border. First pleon segment hidden by the seventh peraeon segment, hind margin of segments two to five denticulate, denticles larger on posterior segments; segments two to four with twenty teeth, fifth with eleven ; two teeth on the fifth and three on the fourth segment considerably larger than the rest and submarginal  in position but projecting a little beyond the margins; median part of fourth pleon segment excavated and that of fifth convex. LAteral corners of fourth pleon segment drawn out into long narrow processes slightly overreaching the tip of fifth pleon segment. Telson triangular, lateral sides prominently sinous at the base, apex narrowly rounded and with eight spines in addition to short densely packed setae; dorsal side with two large proximal submedian triangular teeth.
First pair of antennae contigous at their base, peduncle  three(four) jointed, flagellum twelve jointed and reaching beyond the peduncle of the second antenna. Second antenna just reaching the anterior border of third peraeon segment and flagellum twenty four jointed. Frontal lamina pentagonal and slightly longer than broad; in the adult male its anterior border is broadly rounded, with a small median point. Fourth segment of first peraeopod with five tubercles and three to five spines; fifth segment triangular and immersed, with a stout spine; sixth segment cylindrical, with three spines and two apical setae, unguis short. Seventh peraeopod strongly spiny. Second pleopod of male with four hooks on the peduncle, exopod elongate and endopod oblong; male stylet slender, apically narrow and bent outwards, considerably longer than the endopod.

Body white with a greyish tint, due to the profuse pigmentation, forming patterns on the lateral parts of the body. A few specimens were perfectly white.

Additional Remarks:
Some specimens of Cirolana Bovina were seen with a Spirorbis anchored between the dorsal opines on the telson.

Size and Age : Maximum length :15.0 mm
