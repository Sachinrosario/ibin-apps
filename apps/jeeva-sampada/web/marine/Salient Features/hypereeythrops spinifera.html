Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics:
Body slender. Rostrum small and triangular. Eyes large, depressed and redish brown, peduncle with small dorsal papilla. Antennal scale five times as long as broad, longer than antennular peduncle, outer border entire terminating in strong spine, distal suture present. Thoracic endopods three to five slender with three segmented carpopropodus. Pleopods in male with broad branchial plate, pleopods two to five biramous, setae not modified. Telson longer than broad, lateral margin concave with 10-13 spines, terminal spine much longer, apex transverse with three pair of spines, inner pair very small, middle pair longer than outer, uropods slender without spines.
