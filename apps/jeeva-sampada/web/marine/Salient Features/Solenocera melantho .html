SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref No:52,64,83,111,113.

 

Morphological:  Rostrum short, slightly ascending, reaching beyond middle of eye or basal

segment of antennular peduncle, upper margin straight and  armed with 7-10 teeth; postrostral crest with a shallow sulcus on its posterior 1/3, not markedly elevated, not laminose, reaching posterior margin of carapace; cervical groove deep, reaching almost to  postrostral crest, but does not indent the latter; suprahepatic, branchiostegal and pterygostomian spines absent; hepatic crest curved down anteroventrally; branchiocardiac sulcus indistinct, does not form L-shaped groove on carapace; telson trifurcate;  antennular flagella about 1.1 to 1.5 times length of carapace, tapering distally.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Postrostral crest not markedly elevated, not laminose; cervical

groove does not indent postrostral carina; suprahepatic spine absent; no L-shaped groove on carapace; telson trifurcate, antennular flagella about 1.1-1.5 times length of carapace.

 

Colour: Ground colour of body pale pink.

 

 

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:  In male, median lobe of petasma considerably surpassing distal

margin of lateral lobe, and provided with about 18 sharply pointed setae on distal edge, anterior rim of lateral lobe with 14-16 setae and distal margin of ventral costa with 15 minute setae. Outer process of basial piece of appendix masculina rounded at apex, distal piece consisting of outer and inner scales.

 

In female, thelycal sternite between 4th pereopods with trapezoidal median prominence; anterior rim of quadrate area of last thoracic sternite attaining posterior base of 4th pereopods.
