SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:    

Post-larva:   Length.5.05mm   Rostrum shorter than eye; bearing 2 dorsal teeth + a minute  epigastric spine; a minute supraorbital ,a small antennal and a prominent pterygostomian spinespresent on carapace; no hepatic spine present..Antennal scale short and broad, broader proximally than anteriorly; the mandible with 2-segmented palp with seate; maxillipeds and pereopods bearing exopods but vestigial on legs 1-3;  thoracic legs 1-3 chelate, bearing 3 long setae terminally; endopods of legs 4 –5 are 5-segmented; small dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 5-6; 6th segment also bears a pair of ventrolateral spines posteriorly; pleopods well developed and 5th one with a small endopod bud; uropods well developed,the  spine on the externodistal margin of the exopod of the uropod is short and not very prominent; telson tapers towards the end and bears a median spine, the formula being 8+1+8.                            

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum short; hepatic absent; exopods of legs vestigial; antennal scale short and  broad with a stout spine at the tip, externally; no dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments(in late post larvae onwards); no externodistal spine on exopod of uropod; telson with a median spine and 8 pairs of spines(3 marginal,5 terminal).

Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: pelagic zone.Main food: smaller crustaceans like copepods,mysids,amphipods,algae etc.Feeding type: omnivorous.Additonal remarks: Size and age:  a size of 0.28mm in length in  nauplius I- 3.86mm in postlarva I.

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed)   11cm.

Longevity (y) (wild) :   2 years.                      

Eggs and larvae:                                                                      

Eggs with a diameter of 0.35-0.38mm; opaque with wide perivitalline space; yolk mass a dia. of 0.22-0.27mm; spherical in shape with a bluish tint.

Nauplii I-VI: length 0.27-0.37mm; pear shaped,ocellus present; 3 pairs of naupliar appendages present; antennule-uniramous ; antenna-biramous and mandible-biramous; 3 short lateral setae on inner margin and 2 long terminal setae present on the antennule; caudal furca 1+1 seta; only slight changes occur; 4th seta on the exopod of the antenna is bifid at tip from N2-N6; mandible bears 3 setae each on exopod and endopod. The setae on the caudal furca increased to 7+7 in N6.

Protozoea I-III: size 0.64 -1.55mm; antennule is longer than antenna; frontal organs rounded; telson is deeply forked with 7+7 setae; more appendages develop in 2nd stage; mandible,maxillae and maxillipeds appear; rostrum short; eyes stalked; no spines on carapace; telson unchanged. In 3rd stage dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 1-5; a pair of dorsolateral spines present on 5th seg.; and segment 6 has a pair of posteroventral spines; thoracic legs appear as biramous buds; the telsonic setae increased to 8+8 and uropods developed. 

Mysis I: rostrum slender,3/4 length of the eye and slightly curved ventrally; antennal,pterygostomian spines present; mandibular palp rudimentary; thoracic legs 1-3 chelate,bear long setae and endopod 2-segmented and exopod unsegmented; legs 4 -5 smaller; median spines short on ab.seg.5-6; telson 8+8 setae.

Mysis II: rostrum with a small dorsal tooth; mandibular palp elongated,unsegmented; legs 1-3 chelate,3rd pair longest; pleopods uniramous and small; median spines remain; telson unchanged.

Mysis III: length 4.17mm; rostrum with 2 dorsal and a minute epigastric tooth; mandibular palp 2-segmented; pleopods segmented,without setae; telson remain same.Postlarva I: size 5.05mm;  rostrum reaching middle of eye stalk; 3rd leg longest,extending to middle of antennal scale; median spines vanish from ab.seg.5-6; telson developing a median spine,8+1+8.

Abundance:  abundant along the southwest coast of Kerala
