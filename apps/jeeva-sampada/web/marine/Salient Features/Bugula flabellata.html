SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Tufted zoarium, height of the colonies two centimeters, greyish white. Zooecia arranged in rows of three to eight. Zooecia oblong, its width remaining more or less the same throughout the length. Front occupied by an opesia of the same expanse. Outer and inner distal angles with two spines each. Usually spines of moderate length, but inner spine of outer set may become long and may even branch. Moderately sized avicularia with abruptly hooked rostrum present both in inner and marginal zooecia of branches, placed at the proximal region of the upper half of zooecia. Ovicells sub-globular with wide apertures.Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:   Close resemblance is shown by these specimens with the description of Bugula flabellata (Thompson) given by Ryland (1961). The only noticeable difference is in the spine formula, which according to Ryland is 3:2 whereas here it is only 2:2. But this feature may not be of great specific importance, since Ryland has stated only the size and not the number of spines in his remarks on the outstanding features of B. flabellata. The present record of this species from Madras Harbour may be considered as having been brought about through the agency of ships from Britain.
