SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small species with a bilobed posterior left sulcal list moiety extending to the mid point of the posterior cell body.

Diagnostic characteristics: Two strong peripheral ribs inter connected with horizontal ribs; several less developed ribs in between. R1 bent posteriorly, sometimes with spur. Surface depresses with scattered pores.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
