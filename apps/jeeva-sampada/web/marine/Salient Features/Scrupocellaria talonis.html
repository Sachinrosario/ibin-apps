SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zooecia long and slender with elliptical opesia. Well developed spines, three external and one internal. Scutum usually absent, may be represented by a small spine. Lateral avicularia present minute with triangular mandibles. Large vibracular chamber, visible frontally. Vibracular groove transverse. Long seta nearly twice the length of the zooecium. Radicles simple without hooks. Ovicells perforate. Diagnostic characters: In the short and wide zooecia, vestigial spines and pointed oval nature of the opesia S. ferox can be distinguished from the present material.
S. pugnax is different from the present material in the absence of lateral avicularia and scuta; the longitudinal nature of the vibracular groove, and the proximo-lateral position of the radicle chamber.
S. varians is characterised by imperforate ovicells and elongate lateral avicularia.Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:   The present form agrees well with the description of S. talonis by Osburn, however, certain differences are also noticed. Osburn, observed frontal avicularium on the axial zooecium, his specimens had giant lateral avicularia, both these structures are, however, absent in this material.
It is not clear whether, the absence of large lateral avicularia; and the feebly developed spines alone can give specific distinction for the present material.Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit:
