Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting at first, later becoming saucer-shaped with labulated margins.  Underside costate.  A mother calyx is often visible at the centre of the corallum, around which the secondary calices are arranged in irregular concentric rows.  Secondary calices 4 to 6 mm in diameter, the proximal wall elevated.  When the corallites are close together in a row the adjacent wall run together to form a low ridge.  Septo costate alternating in size, confluent.  On the elevated parts of the wall often they are irregularly swollen (Pillai and Scheer, 1976) and present a very rough appearance.  Axial fossa 1 to 2 mm in diameter with a closely twisted trabecular columella.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
