SALIENT FEATURES   

 

Description: Colony (fragmentary) consisting of two long disconnected branches 19.5 cm and 16.7 cm. in length. They are 1.2 mm in diameter, and both are incomplete. The calyces are irregularly distributed on all sides of the branches and the distance between them is 1 to 2 mm. The individual calyces are rounded verrucae or domes, averaging 1.5 mm. in height and the same in diameter. The calyx walls are with thorny points.  The polyp is wholly retractile. The operculum is rather feebly developed, consisting of slender spindles disposed longitudinally along the dorsal surfaces of the infolded tentacles. There is a very delicate collaret, apparently composed of a single circlet of slender spindles. The coenenchyma is thin and filled with horizontally disposed spindles that average longer than those in the calyx walls, but are more slender.

Spicules: The spicules are unilateral with discs that are unsymmetrical, having one side produced into spinous processes of various sorts (Fig. a, b & c). The spindles are in the shape of crosses, stars, double stars and triradiate forms.

 

Colour : Colony is light brown  in colour, the axis white and the

spicules colourless.
