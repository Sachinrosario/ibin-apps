SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony simple  or complete (3.5 cm in height). The coenenchyma is thick and has a rough arenaceous appearance. The polyps occur over the whole surface of the stem and branches at intervals of about 2 mm. They measure 1 mm in height and 1.3 mm in diameter at the base. The verrucae are cylindrical, but when closed are dome-like. The anthocodiae are completely retractile. The operculum is feebly developed, consisting of eight triangular lobes at the base of the tentacles, surmounting a collaret of three or four rows of curved spindles.	
		

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are scale-like forms with x-shaped marking in the middle and are with thick warty spindles. The spicules of the polyps are spiny. 

Colour	:	The colony is white, axis brown and the spicules are colourless.
