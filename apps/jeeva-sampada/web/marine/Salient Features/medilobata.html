SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Calcareous portion of the blade somewhat club-shaped, basal part conical distally, stalk slender, distal half of the blade covered by a brownish black periostracal cup, which overlaps the calcareous portion. Periostracal cap more or less straight-sided, its outer distal margin nearly straight while the inner margin deeply U-shaped with a median lobate process. Shell similar to that of Teredo, posterior slope well developed placed postero-dorsally on a large disc.

Feeding habit: Feeds on the wood into which it bores.

Main food: Cellulose in wood, supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks: Tropical, sub-tropical regions of the Indo- Pacific (Distribution).
