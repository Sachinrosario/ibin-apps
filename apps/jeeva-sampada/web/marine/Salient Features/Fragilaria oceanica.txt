SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules linear rectangular in girdle view, forming a compact ribbon like chain. Valves broadly lanceolate with rounded ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Transapical striae delicate, towards the middle, slightly fainter, 14 in 10µ, punctate, punctae 15-18 in 10µ. Pseudoraphe narrow and linear.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: 11.5-31.5µ long, 6.5µ broad.
