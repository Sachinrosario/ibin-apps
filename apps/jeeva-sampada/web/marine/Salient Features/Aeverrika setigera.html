SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Delicate, yellowish or pale amber coloured colonies consisting of delicate stolons, peduncles and autozooids.  Primary stolons give rise to secondary or even tertiary stolons which are slightly curved. Zooecia placed in pairs, attached to the chitinised stolons by means of peduncles.  The peduncle short, separated from the stolon as well as the zooids by septa with single pores.  Autozooids usually paired, bilateral to the primary stolon, slightly converge from the place of attachment, and not parallel.  The elongate oval zooecia with two to four slightly elongated distal spines, placed on chitinous flaps bordering the orifice.  Setigerous collar long and slender, with thin regular chitinous setae, reinforcing the semi-transparent membranous folds of the collar.  Tentacles eight in number.  A gizzard present.

 

Diagnostic characters:  

Sex attributes:  Hermaphrodite

Descriptive characters:   A detailed study of this 'semi-tropical' species has been carried out by Rogick (1945).  It is evident from her record that the range of variations shown by this species is considerable.  The absence of "accuminate clasping processes", which were called "tubular adherent processes", by Hincks (1887, p. 128) and "spines by Harmer (1915, p. 87)" (from Rogick, 1945) - a factor of some importance is, however, absent in this material.  This was the only difference that had been noticed in the specimens collected by Menon when compared with the figures and descriptions of this species given by Harmer (1915), Marcus (1941), Rogick (1945) and Osburn (1953).  In the absence of these proximal structures, the form collected by Menon shows some resemblance to A. armata.  One of the colonies collected from the test panels, had many autozooids which lacked one of the partners of the pair.  In the place of the partner zooid a tertiary stolon was noticed.  It is not clear whether this may be considered as just a morphological variation found in this species.
