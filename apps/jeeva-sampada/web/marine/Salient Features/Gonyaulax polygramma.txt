SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elongated cell with tapered epitheca; epitheca angular with short to moderate apical horn. Hypotheca symmetrically rounded or truncate.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Mature cells with thecal reticulae and striae. Cells typically have two antapical spines. Cingulum descending about 1.5 X without an overhung.

 Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
