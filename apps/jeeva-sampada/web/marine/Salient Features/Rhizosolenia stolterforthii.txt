SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with uniformly bent pervalvar axis, forming compact, spirally coined chains.

Diagnostic characteristics: Intercalary bands ring shaped, numerous, without any visible structure. Chromatophores numerous small disc-shaped.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 18-35m and length up to 155m.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
