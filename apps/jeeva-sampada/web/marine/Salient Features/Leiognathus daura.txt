SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Diagnostic characters: 
Body  rhomboid, dorsal and ventral profiles of the body almost equally convex. Breast scaleless. Distal half of membrane between 2nd and 6th dorsal spines jet black, lined above by creamy white, a broad yellow band over lateral line, which disappears gradually on preservation. 

Sex attributes: Sexes undistinguishable externally.

Descriptive characters: 
Body rhomboid , elongated and compresed. Dorsal profile equally or slightly more convex than ventral profile. Occipital region with a concavity and the dorsal profile gently rises from this region to the spinous dorsal, making the profile more convex. Snout somewhat blunt. Eye diameter equal to the blunt snout. Mouth small with thick broad lips and pointing downward when protracted. Commencement of gape of mouth opposite lower third of eye. Posterior tip of maxilla extends far in front of front border of eye. Lower margin of mandible slightly concave. Pre-opercle with an obtuse angle; its lower margin finely serrate. Teeth small, numerous in a single row on both jaws. 
Inter-orbital space bounded by the supra-orbital ridges which continue as the outer edges of the nuchal spine. Median portion of the nuchal spine is elevated as a ridge. Two small spines on top of the head, opposite front border of the eye, the outer one more prominent 
than the inner which is opposite the ridge bounding the inter-orbital space. Orbital edge not serrated.
Scales, small distinct, all over the body except on head and breast. First part of lateral line with a concavity, later running less convex to than the dorsal profile, extending posteriorly to the base of the caudal fin. Dorsal and anal spines weak and compressed.Spines following the 2nd dorsal spine diminishing quickly in length. 2nd anal spine shorter than the 2nd dorsal spine. 3rd and 4th dorsal spine and the 3rd anal spine serrated anteriorly for about half length from the base. Pectorals broad. Ventrals with a strong spine and a large axillary scale, not reaching the anal fin. Caudal forked. 

Colour: 
Abdomen silvery and back grey. Black dots all over the ventral half of the body. Tip of snout black. Inner side of pectoral base grey. A deep blotch on the upper half of the spinous dorsal, extending from the posterior margin of the 2nd dorsal spine to the anterior margin of the 6th dorsal spine. A broad yellow band over the lateral line from the posterior margin of the eye to the caudal base, distinct in fresh specimens, but disappearing gradually on keeping in formalin.
Anal fin golden yellow from the 2nd spine onwards along the distal half of the fin. A black line running along the base of the soft dorsal.
