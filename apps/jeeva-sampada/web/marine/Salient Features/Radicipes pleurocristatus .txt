SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony simple, unbranched. Axis calcareous, brittle, the basal portions with numerous, broad, rhizome-like processes measuring 3 mm in diameter at the base to less than 1 mm at their apices. Coenenchyma rather thick, with numerous spicules; polyps crowded together, but in a uinserial manner on the axis; each polyp somewhat overlapping the one in front of it. When the tentacles are retracted the polyps measure from 5 to 6 mm. in height. 
		Spicules: The spicules of the polyp are large, spindle-shaped; those at the base of each tentacle are small, and are arranged in eight rows, so that when the tentacles are retracted they form an opercular covering over the oral cavity; some of the larger spicules are bent, rough at the ends prolonged into irregular projections.

Colour	:	The stem is  yellowish white in colour.
