SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:

Diagnostic characters: Head scaleless; conspicuous scales on breast. Mouth pointing downwards when protracted. Back with dark irregular vertical lines extending to about lateral line. A brown blotch on  nape which becomes diffuse on preservation in formalin.

 Sex attributes: Sexes are undistinguishable externally.  

 Descriptive characters: Body oval, compressed , rather elongate. Dorsal and ventral profiles almost equally convex, the former almost evenly curved from tip of snout to origin of dorsal fin. A slight concavity over the occiput. Snout pointed and not truncated. Mouth small, lips narrow and thin. The protracted mouth forms a tube directed downwards. Gape of mouth  opposite lower  third of eye. Tips of maxilla surpassing front border of eye and reaches to below first 3rd or middle of eye. Lower jaw strongly concave. Teeth small, numerous as a single narrow villiform band in each jaw. Inter-orbital space bounded by two ridges which continue as the outer edges of the nuchal spine. Median portion of the nuchal spine is elevated, especially at its anterior end, forming a notch on top of the head. Two small spines on top of the head, opposite the front border of the eye, the outer more prominent than the inner. Pre-opercle with an obtuse angle and finely serrated lower margin. Scales small, all over body including breast. First part of lateral line shows a concavity, later running less convex to the dorsal profile, extending posteriorly upto the base of the caudal fin. Dorsal and anal spines of moderate strength, compressed , 2nd dorsal spine longest, 3rd and 4th dorsal spines and the 3rd anal spine serrated anteriorly at their lower halves. Ventrals with axillary scales, tips not reaching the origin of the anal. Caudal fin deeply forked.  Colour: Abdomen more silvery than back, with black irregular bands extending down to about half level. A light brown blotch on nape, which covers an area from about the posterior half of the nuchal spine to origin of the dorsal fin. Membrane from about half level to the tip of the spines between 2nd to 7th dorsal spines black. Tip of snout dotted black. Fine black dots on ventral half of the body. Inner side of pectoral,  posteriorly dark coloured. Gill opening area covered by the lower half of  operculum also dotted black.                                                                                                                                Ref no: 3                                                                                                                                                         

Meristic Characters:D.VIII,16; A. III,14.  

Feeding Habit:  

Main food: Small Crustaceans, Foraminifera and Nematodes. It was also found to feed on small penaeids and Evadne. Polychaetes were occasionally found. Diatoms like Coscinodiscus and  Fragialria were also noticed in some.  

Feeding type: Plankton and bottom feeder                                            Ref no:3,7  Additional Remarks:
