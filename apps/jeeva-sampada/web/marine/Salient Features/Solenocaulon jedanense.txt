SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  pinnate 16.5 cm in height and with a spread of 4.8 cm. The main stem is oval in section, measuring 11 X 8 mm. 4.6 cm above its proximal end and it becomes tubular and gives off short tubular branches ending in small flattened, sometimes round, twigs. The tubular part of the main stem is compressed; but 9.4 cm from its base it looses its tubular character and becomes alternatively deeply channeled and belted, there being three belts about 1 cm broad and three deeply channeled portions. The branches tend to be lateral and alternate in position. They have a short broad tubular base and each of these tubular parts bears a long projection from its distal end. The calyces are borne on these processes and are always in lateral position.
		The individual calyces are 2 mm in diameter. The walls are armed with curved transverse spindles below and spindles arranged en chevron towards the upper part and on the tentacle bases. The tentacles are armed with longitudinal spindles, and their distal parts are bent abruptly inward and bear two rows of small spindles arranged en chevron and reaching to the base of the pinnules.
		A cross section of the stem shows the coenenchyma filled with discs and rounded, heavily tuberculate spindles. 
Spicules. They are slender at the axis (Fig.a). The inner walls of the calyces are coarse tuberculate clubs (Fig.b) and spindle shaped (Fig.c) Oval and round shaped spicules (Fig.d) on the outer layer of the calyx.
 
Colour		:	Colony is a very light brown.
