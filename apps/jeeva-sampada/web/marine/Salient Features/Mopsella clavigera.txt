SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	The  specimen shows strongly compressed  axis and main stem and with peculiar spicules. The calyces and polyps could not be studied.
		Spicules : The spicules are clubs with comparatively smooth surfaces, and others flattened and longitudinally grooved, forming figures like two or three extended fingers flattened and closely pressed together exceedingly various in form, but those thin, finely granulated cortex contain numerous tissues, 0.6 to 0.08 mm long, belted spicules (with 8 clubs), ‘operaglass-like’, 0.06 mm long forms, as well as individual thorned 0.6 to 0.9 mm long spindles. The axis is round or somewhat oval in cross section, is individually stripped on the surface and fine. 

Colour		:	The entire colony cortex cream white, polyps calyces salmon 
			pink, tentacles yellowish and  axis white in colour.
