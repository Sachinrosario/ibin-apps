SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony with flexible axis, strongly ramified branches, whose twigs form sharper angles with a main branch.  The stalk of the main branch arises from a calcareous basal expansion to a height of 100 mm. The slender stem bears twigs from a height of 20 mm upwards, which arise from 3 directions at an angles of about 400, at different heights, so that the bases describe spirals each comprises of 3 branches. The terminal twigs are hair-like. Polyps are pitcher-shaped with very broad bases, constricted beneath the circlet of tentacles. 

		Spicules	: The outer layer spicules are spindle shaped and rounded at one end and pointed at the other, often constricted in the middle. Little warts cover their surface and at blunt end with minute teeth. The coenenchyma consists of oval and small plates. The polyps consists of blunt end spindles measuring in length to breadth 0.41-0.07; 0.25-0.03 mm, and that of the coenenchyma is 0.4-0.04mm. There are also spindles with serrated edges.


Colour			:	Colony brown, axis yellowish-brown and spicules 
				colourless.
