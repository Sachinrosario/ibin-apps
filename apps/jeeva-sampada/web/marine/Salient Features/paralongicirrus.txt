
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics: -       

FEMALE: Forehead produced into a knobe in dorsal view. Antenna very long and plumose and extends beyond the end of the caudal furca by about nine segments.  Cutting edge of mandible with two teeth ,anterioir tooth bifurcated and with a small spine on the inner margin . Endopod and basis of maxillule with four and five setae respectively . Maxilla without spine on the 5th or 6th lobe .Coxa of the 5th leg with a small inner seta and basis with one long external seta . Genital segment very broad and spermathecae widely located .       

Male not recorded.

Additional  remarks :      
Female first recorded from the Indian Ocean (Saraladevi, 1977). And male not recorded. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                          Female : 2.31 – 2.7 mm.                                        K.Saraladevi, 1977
