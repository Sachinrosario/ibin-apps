SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body fusiform, fairly deep and compressed: belly with  a distinct keel of scutes. Top of head with numerous fronto-parietal striae; upper jaw with a distinct median notch; 2 supramaxillae present; gilrakers very fine and numerous (100 to 150 on lower limb of first gill arch), those on inner arches curried. Dorsal fin origin a little before mid-point of body; anal fin short and well behind dorsal fin base. Posterior part of scales with perforations.

Colour: 

back blue/ green, flanks silvery with a black spot behind gill opening and 3 to 7 similar spots along flanks (or high up near dorsal profile).
