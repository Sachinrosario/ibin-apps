SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony incomplete, unbranched, rod-like, attaining a height of 9 cm. The calyces are 1.5 mm in height and 2 mm in diameter. The polyps are retractile and the tentacles devoid of spicules
		
		
Spicules: The spicules are triradiate and quadriadiate, with one point much longer and smoother than the others. Many irregularly forked plates and spindles with irregular processes and tubercles(Fig.a,b&c). 

Colour		:	The colony is pinkish brown, in alcohol, the polyps being lighter and the axis brown
