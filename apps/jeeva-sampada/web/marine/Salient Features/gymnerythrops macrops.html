Diagnostic characteristics:
Body transparent, cephalothorax longer than rest of body. Carapace anteriorly produced into three prominent conical process, one medial rostral process and two lateral processes. Eyes prominent. Antenna represented by basipod and endopod, scale absent. Thoracic endopods extremely slender with few setae. Telson longer than broad, steadily narrowing to short concave distal border with two median setae; posterolateral corners spiniform. Exopod of uropod longer, endopod with swollen base housing large statocyst, both rami without spines.
