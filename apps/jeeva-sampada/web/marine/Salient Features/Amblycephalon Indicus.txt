Salient Features :
Morphological :
Ref No: Pillai, 1966

General Characters :
Body generally like that of Anilcora Leach, peraeon relaxed, with the potero-lateral corners of segments not produced; coxal plates except the last not reaching beyond the posterior border of the respective segments.Cephalon very characteristic, highly flattened and nearly squarish, its posterior border trilobed as in Nericila Leach. Eyes small, dorsal. Pleon narrower than peraeon, not immersed. Telson like that of Nerocila. Uropods very characteristic, exopod long and slender, apically curved inwards and more than twice as long as endopod, endopod short but broad, asymmetrically forked.

Species Characters:
Female: Body long and slender, sides subparallel. Cephalon large and expanded, squarish and dorso-ventrally flattened, antero-median part produced and antero-lateral birders rounded, dorsal side with a median    longitudinal ridge, more prominent anteriorly, posterior border of Cephalon trilobed, median lobe much broader than lateral lobes. Eyes small, dorsal and not pigmented. Peraeon segments very much like those of Antilocra, first segment three fourths as long as cephalon and equal to sixth, antero-laterally produced into into narrow processes reaching the level of the eyes, postero-lateral parts angular but not produced; segments two to four short, subequal, fifth slightly produced postero-laterally; first three pairs of coxal plates small, roughly oblong, last three pairs longer, last pair along projecting beyond the postero-lateral angles of the segment. Pleon narrower than peraeon, segments subequal in length and breadth, first two segments laterally drawn out into thin downwardly bent lamellar lobes. Telson very slightly longer than broad, broadest at base, postero-median part produced, with a bold longitudinal ridge, becoming faint towards the base of the telson.

Antennae hidden under the cephalon, first pair widely separated at base, eight segmented, segments one and two stout, third moderately stout, succeeding ones slender, each of the last five segments with a bunch of long setae. Second antenna shorter and more slender than first, even segmented, apical segement apparently formed by the fusion of more than one segments.

Mandible with incisor and molar lobes clearly indicated, palp well developed, first segment stout, subequal to second in length, third segment slender, with four stiff   setae. Distal segment of maxilliped with six curved teeth, three near the apex, one slightly lower and two still lower down, surface of the segment with groups of minute spinules. First maxilla with the usual four hooks. Distal lobes of second maxilla with two hooks each, distal part of inner lobe with sharp marginal spines.

Anterior peraeopods stout, second segment large , third to fifth short and subequal, fourth with one strong spine, sixth segment stout, only slightly shorter than second, dactylus very long, strongly falcate, equal to the combined length of segments three to six, with a small unguis. Posterior peraeopods comparitively slender, strongly spiny, fourth segment with three inner and one outer spine, fifth with three and sixth with seven spines, dactylus only slightly falcate. Peduncle of uropods inwardly produced into a large apically acute process, exopod long and reaching far beyond the tip of the telson and more than twice as long as endopod, basally broad and slightly narrowing distalwards, apex bent inwards, endopod broad, distal part forked, inner limb of the fork small and acute, outer large and apically blunt, border of outer limb cut into indistinct rounded lobes.

Body opaque white, with a median longitudinal dark band on the dorsal side extending from the tip of telson to the base of cephalon. Lateral parts of body and peduncle of uropods with scattered black chromatophores.

Additional Remarks:
The description of Amblycephalon indicus is based on two female specimen collected from the caudal fin of Sphyraena obtusata landed at Vizhinam (Kerala). Holotype is in the Indian Museum, Kolkata and Paratype in the Department of Aquatic Biology and Fisheries, Thiruvananthapuram.
