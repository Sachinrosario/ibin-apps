SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

	Body depth 3.0 to 3.3 times in standard length. Chin with 2 slender barbells which usually reach posterior to rear margin of prepercle, their length 1.3 to 1.5 times in head. Snout 1.8 to 2.0 times in head; mouth small, the maxilla reaching about 2/3 distance to front edge of eye; a single row of well-spaced, stout, conical teeth in jaws; no teeth on roof of mouth; gillrakers 29 to 31. First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first small, the third longer, contained 1.4 to 1.7 times in head; last dorsal and anal rays not prolonged; pectoral fin rays 15 to 17 . Lateral line scales 27 or 28; 3 vertical scale rows in space between  dorsal fins; 8 or 9 vertical  scale rows along upper part of  caudal peduncle.

Colour :
reddish to greenish brown dorsally shading to light red ventrally, with a large black spot dorsally on posterior part of caudal peduncle (above lateral line), preceded by a whitish spot of about equal size (whitish area often faint); a pale-edged dark brown band from front of snout through eye and continuing a short distance anteriorly on body along lateral line (dark band sometimes not persisting in preservative); fins without obvious markings.
