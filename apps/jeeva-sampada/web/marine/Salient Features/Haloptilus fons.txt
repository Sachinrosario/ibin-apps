
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:          

FEMALE (Stage V): - Prosome rounded. Antennule reaches up to caudal furca. Left antennule 24 segmented. Antenna biramous and with 2 segmented endopod and 8 segmented exopod. Mandibular blade not bifurcated as in other species. Cutting edge with 7 well developed spines. First inner lobe of maxillule with 9 setae 2nd inner lobe with 4 and 3rd with 3 setae; second basal segmented with 5 setae; endopod 2 segmented and carries 4 and 8 setae respectively, exopod elongated and furnished with 6 long setae and 4 small seate, outer lobe with 9 setae. Maxilla with 7 lobes, 1-6 lobes with 3 setae each and 7th lobe with 7 setae. Maxilliped elongated and carries several richly plumose setae.           Legs 1-4 with 3 segmented endopod and 3 segmented exopod. Coxa and basis of leg 1 with 1 small seta each, endopod segment 1 and 2 with a long outer spine and 1 inner seta each, segment 3 with 1 long marginal spine 2 terminal spines, one 3 times longer than the other and 4 inner setae. Coxa of leg 2 with 1 seta; basis without seta; endopod 1,2 and 3 carries 1,2 and 6 seta respectively; exopod 1 and 2 with 1 small outer marginal spine and 1 inner seta each; segment 3 with 2 small outer marginal spines and 2 terminal spines one 5 times longer than the other and 5 inner setae. Setations of leg 3 and 4 are similar, coxa with 1 small seta, basis without seta, endopod 1,2 and 3 with 1 marginal spine 1 inner seta, segment 3 with 2 outer marginal spines and 2 terminal spines one 5 times longer than the other and 5 inner setae. Leg 5 with 2 segmented exopod and endopod. Coxa with 1 small seta; basis with a long seta endopod segment 1 with 1 and segment 2 with 7 setae; exopod 1 with 1 small outer spine, exopod 2 with small outer spines and 2 terminal spines one 6 times longer than the other and 3 inner setae. Urosome 4 segmented. Spermathecae not developed.       
Male not recorded.

Additional  remarks:                    The characters of the present specimens agree well with the figures and description of adult specimen recorded by Rose (1933) and Sars (1925). Males unknown.
