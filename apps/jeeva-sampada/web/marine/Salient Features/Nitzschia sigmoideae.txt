SALIENT FEATURES

Morphological : Bacillifarm frustules. Ends attenuated to one direction, cuneate, Ends subsigmoid, one end more distinct than the other.

Diagnostic characteristics: Keel punctuate excentric, coarse and large square, 5-6 in 10µ. Striae fine, clear, 24-27 in 10µ.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 
Length : 166 – 705 µ
Width : 9 – 11 µ

Biochemical aspects : Extracellular carbohydrates are polysaccharider.
