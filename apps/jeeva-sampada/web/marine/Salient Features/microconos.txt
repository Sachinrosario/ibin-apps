Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum submassive, the largest specimen has a greater spread of 25 cm with wall developed hillocks resembling those of H. exesa.  Valleys continuous, sinuous, average 3 mm wide.  Monticules 1-3 mm thick at the base, pointed at the tips, about 3 mm high.  Septa narrow at first, broader below, alternating is size, edges dentate, side granular.  There are 6-18 septa on a continuous depending on the size of the latter.  Columella trabecular, encircle the monticle.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
