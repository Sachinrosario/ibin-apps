SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallets feather like with long stalk, cones quite distinct; calcareous portion of the blade deeply U-V-shaped on both inner and outer face; awns long, serrations on the outer face short, on inner face long.

  Shell typical of the genus Bankia.

Feeding habit: On the grated sawdust bored by the shell.

Main food: Cellulose, supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks:  
  Distribution: Tropical to subtropical, world wide (Turner, 1966). Balboa and Cristobal, Canal Zone, Panama (Bartsch).
