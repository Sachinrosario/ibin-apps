SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Female: Body elongate oblong, slightly more than three times as long as broad. Cephalon slightly broader than long; with the anterior border perfectly rounded. Postero-lateral corners of first peraeon segments acute and a little produced, those of segments two to five not produced, those of sixth slightly and of seventh very well produced. Peraeon segments five to seven long and equal, two to four short. Coxal plates narrow, with acute apex,those of second segment just reaching the postero-lateral corners of the segments, those of segments six and seven overreaching the respective segments. Pleon about a third of the length of the peraeon, segments equal in length; first broadest, the ventral processes of the first two segments broad and foliaceous, the posterior reaching the lateral apices of the fifth pleon segment. Telson roughly triangular, with the apex broadly rounded.

Antennae slender, first nine segmented, distal segments with bunches of setae; second antenna eleven jointed. Mouth parts normal; mandibular palp appears only two jointed, first segment very stout, probably a composite segment. Anterior peraeopods stout, fifth segment very small, dactylus with a small basal outer protuberance. Basis of seventh leg stout, merus with one, carpus with two and the propodus with four sharp spines. Uropods long and slender, exopod slightly less than twice as long as the endopod.

Length 18.0 mm.

Transition stage: Head more or less triangular, as long as broad. Peraeon segments one to four short, regularly broadening backwards, first longest, fourth shortest. Segments five to seven abruptly broader, with well-developed coxal plates. Pleon almost as long as broad, segments sub equal in length and breadth, first partially hidden by the seventh peraeon segment; ventral process of the first two pleon segments flat and broad. Telson roughly triangular, with the apex rounded. Exopod of uropod slightly longer than endopod, more or less flat, endopod flat, remotely resembling that of male.

  Length 16.0 mm.
Male: Head semicircular, eyes large. Peraeon segments sub equal in length and breadth, fourth shorter than the rest. Pleon slightly narrower than peraeon. Telson longer than broad, apex rounded, distal border setose. Exopod of uropod longer than endopod, with two apical spines, inner border setose; endopod very broad, with the distal corners produced, distal border setose. Sixth segment of anterior peraeopods expanded, with four stout spines, fifth segment with one spine. Seventh peraeopod with one spine on the fourth segment, two on the fifth and five on the sixth.

  Length: 15.0 mm.
Body uniformly white, turning cream in the preservative. Lateral parts of the pleon black due to the presence of chromatophore, this colour extending on to the exopod of the uropod. The cephalon, posterior part of the peraeon segments and the basal part of the telson with branched chromatophores.

Size and age: Length of female: 18.0 mm, Transition stage: 16.0 mm, Male: 15.0 mm.
