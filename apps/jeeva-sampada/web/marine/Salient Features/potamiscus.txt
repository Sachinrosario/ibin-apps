SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 

Rostrum slender,pointed,slightly decurved at tip; an epigastric tooth present in zoeal stages and more than 7 dorsal teeth and a few ventral teeth in postlarval stages; carapace with antennal and pterygostomian spines(Pl.I); the pterygostomian spine disappears in juvenile and adult specimens; pereopods 1 and 2 chelate with reduced exopods, the 4th has a delayed development; 5th uniramous and the longest;  abdominal somite 5 with a pair of dorsolateral spines which vanishes in postlarval stages; telson with 4 spines and 3 setae each; Diagnostic characteristics:-Rostrum very long, well developed with dorsal and ventral teeth; only antennal and branchiostegal spines present; pereopod 5 appears before 4th; pereopods with degenerated exopods, 5th without exopod.

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.

Main food:  Plankton, detritus with  sand and mud.

Feeding type:  Omnivorous.

Additonal remarks: Exhibit  cannibalism  occasionally.

Size and age: 

Zoeae I-VI:  length 2.4mm- 6.7mm.
Postlarva I- IX: length 6.3mm-25.0mm

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs: Caridean prawns generally carry their eggs (berried females) with their body and they hatch out into an advanced larval stage,the Zoea.

LARVAL STAGES:
Zoea I: length 2.2mm – 2.4mmRostrum slender, pointed and slightly decurved at tip; carapace smooth, anterolateral angles drawn out into a pair of pterygostomian spines; eyes large and sessile; antennae  biramous; biramous  buds of  pereopods 1 and 2   present; abdomen6-segmented and telson not separated from abdomen; broad and posterior margin concave and bears 7 spines on each side.Zoea II: length 2.3mm-2.4mmCarapace with supraorbital and pterygostomian spines; eyes stalked; 5th abdominal somite with a pair of dorsolateral spines; mandibles asymmetrical; telson broad,still not separated from abdomen; posterior  margin concave with 8+8 spines.Zoea III: length 2.7mm-3.2mmMajor changes are the presence of pereopod 3 as biramous bud and pereopod 5 as uniramous bud; 4th leg has a delayed development; telson separated from abdomen.Zoea IV: length 3.3mm-3.6mmRostrum with 1 dorsal tooth; pereopod 4 develops as biramous buds; 5th leg uniramous, endopod 5-segmented, terminal segment long with a claw and 1 small seta; rudimentary pleopods developed on abdominal somites 1-5; endopod of uropod setose; telson rectangular,with 5 terminal spines of which innermost and outermost are nonplumose, also 1 spine on lateral margin present.Zoea V: length 5.1mm-5.6mmPereopod 4 well developed; biramous buds of pleopods present on abdominal somites1-5; telson tapering with 4 terminal and 3 marginal spines; the 4th spine being the longest; uropods biramous with numerous setae.Zoea VI: length 6.2mm-6.7mm5-6 small plumose setae develop in front of epigastric spine; pereopods 1-2 chelate; exopod of uropods bears a distal, marginal spine.Postlarva I: length 6.3mmRostrum well developed with 7 or 8 dorsal teeth and carapace with pterygostomian and antennal (branchiostegal?) spines; pereopod 1 stoutest,and 5 longest; 1-3 with degenerated exopods and 4 &5 without exopods; telson with 4 terminal spines and 2 plumose setae; also with 2 lateral spines , shifted to the base of the telson plate.
