SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   

Diagnostic characteristics : 		
										Ref. No. 2
Body elongate with head strongly depressed.  Snout long, greatest diameter of orbit going into distance from anterior margin of orbit to tip of lower jaw about 2.5 to 3 times; a prominent papilla present on upper surface of eye;  bony ridges of head bearing spines; bony ridge crossing cheek below eye with a single spine under rear margin of eye;  preopercular spines 2, nearly equal in length, the lower much broader than the upper;  gillrakers on first arch 1 plus 4 or 5 (usually 5); interopercular membrane with a large, blunt lobe.  Second dorsal and anal fins with 11 soft rays; pectoral fins with 19 to 22 rays, usually 21.  Pored lateral line scales 51to 56, usually 53 or 54, anterior 1 or 2 scales with a small spine.  From 54 to 64 scale rows slanting downward and backward above lateral line.

Colour: 

body brownish to greenish above, pale below.  Caudal fin with 3 or 4 vertical dark bands; other fins with prominent dark blotches.
