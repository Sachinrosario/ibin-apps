SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony 330 mm long from which three branches arise in an alternate manner. The coenenchyma is thin, and is composed of slightly ovoid spicules arranged irregularly. The polyps are scattered over the whole surface on the lower portion, but near the ends of the branches they occur in a single row on each side, separated by two bare space where the black axis appears through the coenenchyma. The verrucae are small and compressed, slightly cylindrical when expanded, wart-like when closed. They are 1.75 mm long, 1.25 mm broad and 1 mm in height.  The anthocodiae are almost completely retractile.		

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are (1)  Warty (2) Spicules with tapering ends. (3) Club-shaped (4) Quadri-radiates with X-shaped marking (5) Spiny spindles are also found.


Colour	:	The axis is black in colour with a brownish tint.
