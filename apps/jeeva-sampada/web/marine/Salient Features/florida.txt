Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum subprostrate, robust with very thick branches 2 to 4 cm thick.  Main branches subdivide.  Undersides of branches devoid of branchlets and with nariform and subimmersed of subcylindrical branchlets, 1 to 2 cm high and 4 to 7 mm thick.  Some are proliferous.  Axials slightly conical in outline, about 3 mm at the base, wall about 0.75 mm thick, opening rounded.  In many calices the primary septa fuse at the axial fossa.  Radials nariform, 2 mm long, 1.5 mm thick.  Scattered among the radials seen numerous proliferous axials destined to form branchlets.  Smaller radials are also found.  Immersed corallites 0.75 mm in diameter.  Septa in the radials generally six with a few of the second cycle.  Corallite wall started and porous.

The species is very common in Andaman and Nicobar Islands often forming large colonies.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Scheer and Pillai (1974) described this species in detail from material based from Nicobar under the name A. gravida and listed A. vermiculata Nemenzo as synonymy.  However, Wallace (1978) felt, A. gravida is the same as A. florida and is followed here.  The other synonym includes A. miriabilis, A. compressa, A. affinis.  All these have been described by Brook (1893).

The Andaman and Nicobar specimens agree to Brook’s description of Madrepora gravida Dana.
