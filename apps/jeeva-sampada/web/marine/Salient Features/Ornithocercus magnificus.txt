SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small cell body with well-developed left sulcal list, the posterior moiety of which is trilobed with deeply arched interstitial areas. The main lobe typically has an accessory rib on either side of the V-shaped lobe, occasionally two to a side.

Diagnostic characteristics: R 1 bent posteriorly, sometimes with spur. Surface areolate with scattered pores.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
