The head seen dorsally is not raised above the general level of the

With the exception of the first, all the thoracic segments are distinct.
The ovarian bosses are not developed to as great an extent and are seen on the longer side of the body as narrow elliptical structures, occupying about half of the anterior part of the thoracic margin. The epimera have the usual shape and position.

  ANTERIOR PORTION OF FIRST OOSTEGITE IS POPORTIONNATELY SHORT AND IS ABOUT 1 AND HALF TIMES BROADER THAN LONG POSTERIOR PORTION WITH A LARGE ROUNDED DISTALLY AND SPARSELY BESENT WITH HAIRS ALONG ITS INNER MARGIN THE INTERNAL CREST IS DIGITATE OVER A GREATER PORTION AND THE NUMBER OF PROCESSES IS LARGER. The boss on the basal joint of the thoracic legs is covered with scale . the outer margins of the merus and carpus are in part hairy, and the latter has its usual small projection covered with a number of long hair. The projection of the propodus, against which the dactylus acts, is very conspicuous.
The pleopods are small and leave a large space uncovered in the
middle. They extend very slightly beyond the margins of the abdomen
and consequently are almost invisible from the dorsal surface. In the
fourth and fifth pairs, especially the fifth, the exopodites are considerably
longer than the endopodites. The uropods are small and oval and
appear to have no stalks.
