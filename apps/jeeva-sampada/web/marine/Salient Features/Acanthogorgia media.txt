SALIENT FEATURES		

Description		:	Colony branching and sometimes reticulate. The branches shoot up and stand alternately. The polyps are wide apart, measuring 1.3 mm long and 1.1 mm broad. Their reinforcement consists of 0.5 mm long, thin spindles, often with a longer weakly warted spindles. A number of long spindles measuring 0.45 mm long, projecting at the polyp. The thin bark and the polyp walls are found  with radiating stars shaped spicules. 
			 
			
Spicules: The spicules are small warty spindles with crosses and triradiate. 
 
Colour	:	The colony is golden brown in colour.
