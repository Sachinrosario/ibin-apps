SALIENT FEATURES:								Ref No:184

Morphological:   Rostrum reaching distal end of antennular peduncle and armed dorsally with 
7-8 teeth; carapace with well defined antennal, hepatic and pterygostomian spines, no stridulating organ; 3rd to 6th abdominal somites strongly carinated, 4th to 6th somites with feeble subcarinae; telson as long as or slightly shorter than inner uropod, terminating in sharp spine and armed  with   3 pairs of movable and a distal pair of fixed spines; lower antennular flagella 0.8 to 0.9 times length of peduncle, stylocerite extending slightly beyond eye; 3rd maxilliped with a sharp basial spine, 1st pereopod with basial and ischial spines, 2nd pereopod with a small basial spine. Petasma asymmetrical, more than half as long as carapace without rostrum. Thelycum with the median plate extending posteriorly and occupying about half of that plate, the same provided with a deep longitudinal groove giving the appearance of that plate being formed of two distinct lobes.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum reaching distal end of antennular peduncle and armed 
dorsally with 7-8 teeth; lower antennular flagella 0.8 to 0.9 times length of peduncle; petasma more than half as long as carapace; posterior extension of median plate of thelycum provided with a deep longitudinal groove giving bilobed appearance.
