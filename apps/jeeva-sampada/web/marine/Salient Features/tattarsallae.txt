SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:       Rostrum prominent, triangular and apically rounded, dorsomedian tubercles present. Antennal sympod with four spines, first two short and subequal, third long and barbed, fourth slightly shorter than third. Thoracic endopod with four carpopropodal segments. Eighth thoracic endopod small, two segmented in male, first segment with four long setae, second segment with two apical and one subapical seta, in female slender and two segmented, basal segment with one seta, distal segment with an apical cusp or spinule. Lateral border of telson with thirteen spines, distal border with inner pair of spines longer than outer.
