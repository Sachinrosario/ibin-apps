Genus: Anisomysis Hansen
Small mysids with slender body and large eyes with single or double cornea. Antennal scale narrow, apically rounded and setose all round. Second segment of mandibular palp with normal setae or small setules mounted on spine-like processes. Sixth joint of endopod three to eight usually divided into sebsegments. Pleopods in both sexes except fourth pair in male rudimentary unsegmented plates; endopod of fourth pair of male small lobe, exopod very long, three segmented, third segment carrying two stout often armed spines. Telson short, variously shaped, with or without distal sinus, median distal setae absent. Uropods with rami setose all round.

Anisomysis tattersallae
Body strongly built and hispid. Carapace anteriorly produced into large rostral lobe. Eyes large and double faceted. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Thoracic endopods slender, carpopropodus three segmented, dactylus small and ending in two small setae. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male long reaching tip of uropods, three segmented, first segment longer than combined length of second and third segments, third segment terminating in two spines, inner stouter armed with branch of stiff bristles in middle, outer with few barbs. Telson dumbel shaped with prominent constriction at distal one third, basal part with 4-5 small spines, distal border convex and armed with 6-8 peculiarly shaped blunt spines. Uropods long and narrow and setose all round.
