Salient Features:
Morphological:

Male - The body is elongate oblong and parallel sided with a pronounced dorso-ventral flattening. The cephalon is longer than all the peraeon segments and is almost triangular in shape. The postero-lateral parts of the cephalon are produced and immersed in the first peraeon segment. The first peraeon segment is as long as the sixth, the seventh segment is slightly loner than the sixth and its posterior border is convex. The coxal plates are posteriorly acute in the dorsal view and except the seventh, none exceeds the hind border of the respective peraeon segment. The posterior border of peraeon segments five to seven is granular and the granulation extends slightly on to the dorsal surface of the segments (not shown int the fig.). The dorsal surface of the peraeon is sparsely hairy. THe pleon is as long as the first peraeon segment and has two pairs of lateral surfaces, its hind border is tuberculated. The telson is as long as broad, its apex is deeply trifid, with the median lobe reaching slightly beyond the lateral lobes, its margin is tuberculated and hairy. The dorsal side of the telson has four large circular bulgings covered with pustules and fine hairs. The median lobe of the apex of the telson has a broad patch of pustules and each of the lateral lobes has a longitudinal row.

The first peduncular segment of the first anntenna is very stout and is distally produced into a short dorsal process and along curved ventral process, the latter almost reaches the tip of the nearly ovate second peduncular segment; the secondpeduncular segment has its proximal lower part produced into a curved hook-like process; The third segment of the  peduncle is very small and originates at the middle of the ventral side of the second segment so that the appendage is bent at right angles in the middle, the flagellum is short and eighteen-segmentd with long sensory filaments. The fifth peduncular segment of the second antenna is longest, the flagellum is twentyfive segmented. The epistome is triangular and apically pointed, the posterior arms are distally subtruncate. The second and third segments of the palp of the mandible have a comb of setae. The inner lobe of the first maxilla has four setae and a spine, and the outer lobe has ten spines. The inner lobe of the second maxilla has 7 or 8  stiff pulmose setae, the other two lobes are armed with stout spines. The distal part of the maxilliped os armed with short spines and stiff setae, the palp is well lobed.

The fifth segment of the first paraeopod is dorsally expanded and carries a spine, the ventral border has three spines and a sea, the fifth segment has four ventral spines and the sixth three ventral spines; The inferior border of segments 4 to 6 is hairy. The 7th peraeopod is long, the inferior border of segments 4 to 6 is covered with bushy hairs, each segments carries a seta on the upper border. The male appendix on the seventh paraeon segment consists of a pair of elongate cylindrical, apically rounded process with a spiny inner margin.

The outer border of the exopod of the first pleopod is serrate, its peduncle carries three coupling hooks. The outer border of both rami of the second pleopod is serrate, the male stylet is inserted far below the base of the endopod and progressively narrows towards its tip, the inner basal part of its border is spiny and the distal part is slightly curved inwards. The exopod of the third pleopod istwo-segmented, the outer border of the second segment is serrate. The outer border of both rami of the fourth pleopod is hairy, the inner proximal part of the endopod is produced. The exopod of the fifth pleopod is two-segmented and carries three or four digitiform process covered woith denticles. The endopod of the uropod has its outer distal part produced, the exopod is shorter than the endopood, the margin of both rami is setose and the endopod has two parellel longitudanal rows of tubercles on its dorsal side.
Length: 13.5mm.
The body is dark brown or flesh-coloured, perfectly matching the color of sargassam on which the specimens are often found.

Female - The female is smaller than the male and elongate oblong in shape. The cvephalon is comparitively large and about one and a half times as long as the first peraeon segment. The posterior border of the 7th paraeon segment is not tuberculated. The coxal plates are comparitively more produced backwards. The pleon is longer than in the male, nearly twice as long as th first peraeon segment, its hind border carries four small tubercles. The proximal part of the telson has three bulgings without tubercles. The apex of the telson is trifid but the incisions are very shallow and totally different from those of the male.

The peduncle of the first antenna is more slender than in the male and the second segment does not have the hook-like ventral process. The flagellum is fifteen segmented. The mouth parts are completely metamorphosised. The peraeopods are comparitively more spiny than in the male but are devoid of bushy hairs.

The uropods overreach the apex of the telson, the distal border of the endopod is subtruncate and the outer corner is provided with a few setae.

Additiona Remarks:
The holotype male and allotype female are deposited in the indian museum,Kolkatta.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size 9.5mm.
