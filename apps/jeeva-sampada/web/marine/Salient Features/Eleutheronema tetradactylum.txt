SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

	The largest of the threadfins, Body more or less elongate and compressed. Snout projecting, mouth very large, with small teeth; lips absent, except for lower lip near corner of mouth; eyes large (eye diameter 4.5 to 5 times in head length). Pectoral fins in 2 parts, upper part with all rays unbranched, lower with 4 free filamentous rays of which the upper filament is the longest, reaching to pelvic fin base; caudal fin forked with lobes equal. Scales small, ctenoid (rough to touch).

Colour : body silvery green above, cream below; dorsal and caudal fins grey, dusky at edges, pelvic and anal fins orange, pectoral filamentous rays white.
