SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:       .
Female: Cephalothorax, 4.45 mm; urosome, 2.12 mm. The genital segment asymmetrical; the genital area produced slightly below. The furca asymmetrical; the left ramus 4 times as long as it is wide at the distal. The urosomal segments and furca are covered with fine short hairs.     The 1st antenna exceeds the end of the furca by distal 3 segments. In the 2nd antenna the 1st basal segment bears a long seta; the exopod a little shorter than the endopod. The other mouth parts and swimming legs as described by Giesbrecht. In the 5th pair of legs the segments of the exopod are broad; the inner marginal spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod slender, about as long as the 3rd segment of the exopod; the outer edge spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod reaches halfway between the proximal outer marginal spine and the junction of the 3rd segment with the 2nd segment of the exopod.           

Immature Male: The urosome 4-jointed. The grasping antenna undeveloped, exceeds the end of the furca by distal 3 segment. The 5th pair of legs has 2-jointed exopod and endopod.

Additional remarks: Sewell’s specimen from the Arabian Sea has the genital segment provided with a pronounced swelling on the dorso-lateral region of the right margin (Sewell, 1947). This swelling is not remarkable in the specimen from the Indian seas (Sewell, 1932).     Several examples of palumboi have been recorded from the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans. Sewell’s specimens from the Arabian Sea measured 7.33-7.45 mm, and that from the Indian seas 7.0 mm in the female. Esterly’s D. grandis from the Pacific measured 8.3 mm in the female and 7.5 mm in the male. The smallest specimen is that of Giesbrecht taken from the Pacific; it measured 5.7 mm in female. Sewell is opinion that there is no ground for separating the various female into the Pacific and Atlantic forms or species.
