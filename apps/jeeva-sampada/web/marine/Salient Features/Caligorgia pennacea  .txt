SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branching in one plane and pinnate. The colony is small and high measuring 5-8 cm long and the branches shoot off at angles of 300-600  and the branching are strongly stretched. The polyps stand in verticel, mostly in five, often in fours and sixes. 5-6 verticels are arranged in 1 cm length, with an interval of 0.5 mm. The abaxial cover scales are 0.45 mm long and the inner lateral ones measure 0.25 mm. The adaxial ones measure 0.12 mm.   The polyps are 1.25 mm long, compressed and with low operculum. The abaxial longitudinal rows contain 6 or 7 scales; the outer lateral rows contain only 3 each distally arranged. The inner lateral and adaxial rows contain each with one small, fine and tender scale. The adaxial sides of the polyps are devoid of scales. The scales on the trunk are thick with irregular, serrated free edge. On the outer surface from the serration the carina goes downwards which end in warted spicules.  .
		Spicules: The spicules of the bark are thick measuring 0.36 to 0.54 mm in diameter.
