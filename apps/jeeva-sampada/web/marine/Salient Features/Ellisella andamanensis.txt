SALIENT FEATURES:

Description	:	Colonies subflabellate and often branch dichotomously;
 branches 4-5 mm in diameter.  Cortex thin; calyces arranged uniformly or spirally 
at places; dome-shaped and 1.5 mm in diameter, height 0.5 mm on an average.

		Spicules are represented by spindles (Fig a) and dumbbells (Fig b); 
and may measure 0.063 X 0.025 mm and 0.04 X 0.021 mm respectively.
		
Colour	:	Colony reddish brown to yellow when alive; axis pale white. 
 Larger spicules are amber coloured and smaller ones often colourless.

Size attained	:	Only bits could be collected.
