SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells strongly constricted and sides resemble 'bow' like appearance. Keel with hyaline margin.

Diagnostic characteristics: Keel puncta forming obliquely decussating rows, 15 rows in 10µ & striae curved.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 80-90µ, width: 38m.
