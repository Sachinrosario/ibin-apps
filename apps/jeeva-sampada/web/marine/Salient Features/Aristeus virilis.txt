SALIENT FEATURES:						

Ref No:2, 83

Morphological: Integument pubescent. Rostrum long, but shorter than length of carapace in larger females; armed dorsally with 3 teeth irrespective of sex and age. Carapace without hepatic spines; postrostral carina extending beyond middle of carapace; hepatic and  branchiocardiac sulcus well marked; antennal and branchiostegal spines well developed. Posterior three abdominal terga carinated along mid-dorsal line from about middle of fourth somite, carina in each somite ending in a sharp minute spine pointed backwards. Telson gradually tapering into accuminate tip and provided with 3 pairs of movable followed by a pair of immovable spinules. Fourth and fifth pereopods long and slender. Pleurobranchiae in front  of fourteenth body segment with a number of pinnules, the size of branchiae as well as number of pinnules progressively decreasing anteriorly.


Diagnostic characteristics:    Integument pubescent; rostrum shorter than length of carapace in larger females, armed dorsally with 3 teeth; carapace without hepatic spine; pleurobranchiae  in front of fourteenth body segment with a number of pinnules.


Sex attributes:
       Descriptive characters:    Sexual differences are observed in antennal scale and third maxilliped  besides external genetalia. In male  the  antennal scale is considerably thickened in some terminal portion, and the penultimate segment of third maxilliped is furnished with a tuft of hairs at distal upper end.
