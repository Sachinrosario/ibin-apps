
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with slightly convex valves. A depression in the middle of the valve and form a straight chain. The raised portion of the valve touches the adjacent cell.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valves with numerous short spines of varying length. Intercalary bands many collar-shaped. Surface of cell delicately punctate, punctae 12 in 10m. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 57-85m.
