SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves naviculoid, slightly sigmoid at the ends. Ends sub-acute to obtuse. Raphe in the middle, slightly excentric near the ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Oblique striae crossed at an angle at about 700 Oblique/ transverse striae in equal number, 14-22 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 150-310m and width 23-35m.
