SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony is flabellate, scanty; the branching are winding and hardly flat; sideways from them shoot off numerous shoot twigs. The polyps stand irregularly on a surface. The polyps calyces are cylindrically provided with eight longitudinal ribs canals and up to 2.7 mm high. The tentacles are 0.5 mm long and closely filled with small spicules. The thin, finely granulated cortex contain numerous tissues, 0.6 to 0.08 mm long, belted spicules (with 8 clubs), ‘operaglass-like’, 0.06 mm long forms, as well as individual thorned 0.6 to 0.9 mm long spindles. The axis is round or somewhat oval in cross section, is individually stripped on the surface and fine. 

Colour		:	The entire colony cortex cream white, polyps calyces salmon 
			pink, tentacles yellowish and  axis white in colour
