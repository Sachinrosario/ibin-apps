SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  is reticulate and  branched in a plane.  The stem is very broad and many times broken; thin cylindrical end branches spring up from the belt.The polyps are found  arranged in two lateral rows. The coenenchyme is thicker than in other forms. Even the tentacles contain spicules reaching the pinnules.
		
Spicules: (1) Slender, rod-like, comparatively smooth, but with scattered thorny points. (2) Oval or irregular spindles with coarse verrucae. 

Colour		:	Colony is dark brown.
