SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branched in a plane and reticulate. The branches are bent some what like waves and are flat. The calyces are about 1 mm high and 0.65 mm broad. The operculum is weakly developed and consists of spindles arranged each three in a triangle. The bark is thick and rough . Polyps are retractile.  		
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are broad-warted, thick and heavily thorned , clubs, double stars, crosses and leaf-clubs of different size spindles. 

Colour		:	The colony is white or light yellow to brown, calyces yellow and  axis black in colour.
