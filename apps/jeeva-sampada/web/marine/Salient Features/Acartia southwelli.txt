SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                      

Diagnostic characteristics:        Female: Cephalosome broadly round-triangular, rostral filaments thin and slightly curved. Prosome-urosome ratio 3.7:1. Cephalosome and pediger 5 rounded, naked. All urosome somites usually naked. Fine spinules shown on urosome somites of female and male present only in specimens from Portonovo; the number of spinules varies within this population; specimens examined from other localities without spinules on urosome somites.  However, spinules may be present on the urosome somites of animals from other localities; Tranter and Abraham (1971) have shown the female from Cochin possess some spinules.  Caudal rami symmetrical, 1.4 times longer than broad bearing 5 plumose setae and a setule. Urosome segments and caudal rami with proportions 46:19:12:23. Fifth legs symmetrical, 3 – segmented, both coxal segments fused, basis oblong with a small seta on outer margin at about distal two – thirds, base of terminal segment slightly swollen, tapering distally with a distinct suture at midlength, portion distal to suture serrate.          

Male: Prosome-urosome ratio 3.1:1. Posterior corners of pediger 5 naked, urosome segments also usually unarmed. Caudal rami nearly as broad as long, proportions of urosome segments and caudal rami 11:29:23:8:12:17.          

Right and left coxal segments of fifth leg fused. Right leg basis  with seta at distolateral  corner; first exopod segment with an oblong inner lobe bearing a spine; third segment curved with a thick spine at midlength on medial margin and another at tip. Left leg basis more than twice as long as broad with outer seta at midlength; first exopod segment short and unarmed; terminal segment medial margin with proximal tuft of hair, a spine embedded in a membraneous sheath, the sheath produced and of irregular shape and flanked on distal side by a recurved spine; tip of segment bifurcate, outer process much longer than inner process.  

Size and age: Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)Total length 
Male: 0.70 to 0.75 mm (av. 0.73 mm)                    Female: 0.79 to 0.84 mm (av. 0.81 mm)
