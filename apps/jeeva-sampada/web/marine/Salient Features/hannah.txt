SALIENT FEATURES:

Largest Venomous snake known and third longest snake of India. A pair of
large occipital shields on the head is a key character for identification.
No cuneate scale on the upper lip. Head flat,wider than neck. Body robust. 
Scales glossy, in 15 rows. Ventrals 240-254, caudals 84-104, partly single, 
partly in pairs.

The king cobra is olive green or blackish above, with numerous yellow 
crossbandswhich fade with age.The young are jet black, with 
conspicuous chevron-like transverse bars.
