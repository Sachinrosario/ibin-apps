
SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum initially encrusting, surface rising into small gibbosities.  Calices polygonal or circular, 1 to 1.5 mm in greater length, shallow, or looks like deep punctures.  Intercorallite wall thin, interrupted or even suppressed, thus several calices are placed in short series within small valleys.  Septa irregular in number and arrangement, often the lateral of the ventral triplet wanting.  In case they are present they may or may not form a trident.  Usually a single septa denticle is present between the wall and the palus.  Pali show wide range of variation in development.  The palar formula may be often complete.  Those of the lateral pairs of septa large, frosted reach to the level of the wall, or may stand low.  The pali of the dorsal and ventral septa and those of the laterals of the ventral triplet are always smaller than tose of the lateral pairs of septa.  Columella may or may not present, when present  a single compressed style; in the latter cause the bottom of the axial fossa is open surrounded by the inner synapticular ring.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
