Descriptive characters:  Corallum with a narrow base of attachment with a convex calicinal side.  Generally colonies remain small (10 to 15 cm broad).  Valleys radiating from the centre to the periphery of the corallum.  Collines 3-5 mm thick, ridged, septa alternating in size.  Width of valleys upto 2 cm, depth 12 to 15 mm.  Total septa 8-10 per cm length of collines, larger septa about 1 mm thick, all septa exsert and continuous over the colline.  Upper two or three teeth about 3 mm long and 1 mm thick.  Columella trabecular, rarely there are more than one centre in a valley, particularly at the peripheral part.  Columella centres linked by two to three lamellae.  Living coral yellow.  Polyps partly expanded, brown, the brilliant green colour of the oral disc of S. nobilis is absent in S. radians.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
