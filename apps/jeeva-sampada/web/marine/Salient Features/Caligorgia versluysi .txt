SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branching is dichotomous. The polyps stand vertically in 1-3 cm high. The space between them measures to 0.5 – 0.57 mm, sometimes 1 mm also. The longitudinal rows of polyps contains 10-11 scales, the outer lateral rows contain 5 scales and the inner rows are much reduced. The outer surface of the polyps are provided with projections radiating from the core, which end partly as toothed scales.
		Spicules: The spicules of the polyps are with warts. The bark contains strongly warted with irregularly edged spicules..
