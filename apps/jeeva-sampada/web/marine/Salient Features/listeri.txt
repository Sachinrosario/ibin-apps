Descriptive characters:  The Indian Ocean records of this species are from the Maldives and Nicobar Islands.  The Nicobar specimens reported by Scheer and Pillai (1974) from Tillanchong, Nicobar are both encrusting and pulvinate.  The corallites are 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter, level or projecting upto 2 mm.  They are deep, 3 to 4 mm.  Septa in two cycles, primaries meeting at the bottom of the axial fossa.  Surface coenenchyme with secondarily frosted echinulations.

A. listeri is closely related to A. profunda Verrill and A. gracilis Bernard and in all probability they may be one and the same.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
