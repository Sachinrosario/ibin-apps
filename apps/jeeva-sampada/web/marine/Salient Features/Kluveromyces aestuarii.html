Morphological:
Standard description of Kluyveromyces aestuarii
Growth in Malt extract: After 3 days at 28C the cells are spheroidal to ellipsoidal, (2.0 5.5) X (2.5 - 6.5) m, reproducing by budding and occur singly or in pairs. A slight ring and sediment , occasionally islets or a thin pellicle are formed. After one month at room temperature a ring and sediment are present.
Growth on Malt agar: After one month at room temperature the streak culture is butyrous, greyish-white, occasionally tinged with brown, shiny to dull, flat, spreading, smooth or verruculose towards the center, the margin is smooth, undulating to lobiform.
Dalmau plate culture on Corn Meal agar: Tree like structures or a rudimentary pseudomycelium may be formed. It is often better developed under anaerobic conditions. Formation of Ascospores: Conjugation usually immediately precedes ascus formation. The asci are evanescent. One to four spheroidal to ellipsoidal ascopores is formed per ascus. Released ascospores tend to agglutinate.
Sporulation is observed on 2% Malt extract agar, YM agar and the commonly employed sporulation media.
