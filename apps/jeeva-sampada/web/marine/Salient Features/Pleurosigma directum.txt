SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules membranous, hyaline and easily breaking down. Lanceolate valves, slightly sigmoid.

Diagnostic characteristics: Raphe very faint, axial area very narrow and central area almost invisible. Chromatophores two long dissected bands.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 238-518m long and 39-56m broad.
