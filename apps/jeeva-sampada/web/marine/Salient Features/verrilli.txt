Descriptive characters:  
The following details of this species is based on several specimens collected from Gulf of Mannar:  Encrusting, upto 20 cm in greater spread 1 to 2 cm thick at the central part with very thin edges closely adhering to the substratum.  An apitheca is generally present.  Upperside with small protuberances.  Calices 0.75 to 0.85 mm in diameter, smaller ones average 0.5 mm.  They are a diameter to 2 mm apart.  Septa in two cycles, the directives often fuse each other.  Secondaries of varying numbers.

There are 4 to 8 tubercles around a calyx, generally (5 or 6).  These remain either independent of each other or they may unite at their basal parts leaving the distal portions free, but giving an elevated appearance to the calices.  Larger tubercles about 1 mm in diameter and height, round topped.  The interstitial coenenchyme is covered over by numerous, very small, highly branching spines which give a rough appearance to the coral.  Surface reticulum moderately porous.  Living coral yellowish brown often with pinkish patches.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
