SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells oval, valves elliptical, central nodule large, rounded, furrows narrow, closely following the central nodule and its horns.

Diagnostic characteristics: Coastae strong and far apart alternating with double rows of alveoli, costae 12 in 10m.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 62m, width 36m.
