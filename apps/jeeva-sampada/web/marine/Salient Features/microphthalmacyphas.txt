Descriptive characters:  Corallum forms thick encrustations, 15-20 cm in greater spread, often the upper side rising to hillocks, or digitiform branches with a central worm tube.  Coenosteum dense with closely set spines.  Corallites rounded, 1 to 1.5 mm in diameter, level or only slightly projecting, adjacent ones almost touching to 2 mm apart.  Total number of septa 20 of which 10 reach the columella.  Sometimes giant corallites with double the number of usual septa are seen.  Septa exsert, arched, edges dentate, sides granular.  Costae not ridged, but their course is marked by rows of spines.  Columella trabecular.  

Living coral yellow dull brown.  This species extudes excessive quantity of mucus, when taken out of water.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
