SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells rectangular in broad girdle view. Apertures hexagonal and smaller than the cells. Setae arising slightly within valve margin.

Diagnostic characteristics: Inner setae almost straight, terminal setae shorter than others, usually forming a 'V'.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
