SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  flabellate and finely branched as to give a false appearance of reticulation. The main stem 4.5 mm in diameter and branches are round in section with indications of the median grooves . The terminal twigs are curve, round in section and average in about 1.5 mm in diameter. The calyces are sparsely distruibuted on the main stem and branches rather regularly distributed on all sides of the brachlets and twigs. The calyces are conical or dome-shaped, a typical one measuring 1 mm in height. The wall are filled with oval, densely tuberculate spicules and the margin bears eight marked lobes. The polyps are completely retractile and the tenctacle are heavily armed with spindles, which are arranged en chevaron on the basal parts and in broad longitudinal bands on the distal parts. 
		
		
Spicules:  Spicules of the coenenchyma are oval, very densely tuberculate forms. Under these are typical terrete spindles with crowded verrucae. True girdle spindles are absent. The spicules of the axis are the typical form of this genus. 

Colour		:	Colony is deep red and polyp is  yellow.
