SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells four sided in central view. Sides more or less straight to concave or at times wavy. Hypotheca with two thick horns. The ends of the horns blunt.

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle concave; its ends more or less displaced.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 80-118µ; breadth: 67-109µ.
