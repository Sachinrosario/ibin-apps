Salient Features:

Morphoplogical:

Body perfectly oblong, stouter than that of S. Annandalei. Anterior border of cephlonb prominently trilobed, antero-lateral borders raised and ridge like. Each peraeon segment with a transverse ridge the posterior ones broken up into 4 pairs of transversely elongated tubercl;es; those on seventh segment very high and widge-shaped, considerably larger than those of S. annandalei.

Pleon with 4 tubercles as in S. annandalei. Proximal middle part of telson with 5 tubercles as in  S. Annandalei, but the lateral longitudinal row is composed of only 2 tubercles. Also the first submedian pair is slightly smaller than the second. The whole surface of the telson is pustuose. Distal border perfectly rounded and ridge like, the margin with blunt spines. 

Antennule and antenna as in S. annandalei, forth and fifth pedencular segments of second antenna with sensory setae and the peduncle of both more hairy.

Epistome with the posterior arms a trifle stouter. Mouth parts as in S. annandalei. Outer lobe of first maxilla less hairy and with 12 barbed spines; inner lobe with 3 blunt and 2 small setae, all the setae with stiff hairs.

The distal spine on propodus of first peraeopod diiferently shaped. Ischium of seventh peraepod comparitively small, merus with 9 stout outer setae, carpus with 16 outer and inner spines, propodus of uniform width.

Male stylet on second pleopod more spiny and with a stout seta a little away from the tip.

Body grey, with isolated brick red patches on the border of segments.

Diagnostic Charecteristics:

Typical isopods; epistome longer than broad; seventh peraeor segment with large ridge-like tubercles; five prominent tubercles in the middle part of telson arranged on the form of 'v' as in S. annandalei; However, submedian pairs of tubercles on telson dissimilar ; the first pair being slightly smaller than the second distal border of telson rounded and raised; lateral smaller tubercles on telson arranged longitudinaly , few in number usually 2 and widely spaced than in s. annandalei.

Sex Attributes:
Embryo and larval developments completed within an internal brood pouch.
Feeding Habit:  Browsing on wood surface.

Main food:  Primary film and algal growth on wood surface.

Additional remarks:  S. annandalei travencoresis has been found in associated with s.annandalei and hence may be occuring in several estuarine areas alondg the Indian coast. This species is also capable of attacking live mangrove vegetation.
