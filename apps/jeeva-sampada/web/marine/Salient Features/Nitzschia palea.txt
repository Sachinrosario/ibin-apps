SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves being more linear lanceolate, with apices shortly rostrate; 10 to 12 carinal dots, the median not distinct and 33 to 36 striae in 20µ.

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle face linear- lanceolate, with apices rounded or truncate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Length 45-55µ.

Biochemical aspects: Copper inhibited photosynthesis more than it did growth. Extracellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides. Ref: Neilsen & Wium- Anderson, 1971.
