SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Strongly constricted valves, with sub-elliptical ends. Central nodule with approximate horns.

Diagnostic characteristics: Transverse costae 9 in 10m crossed by equidistant longitudinal costae curved outwards in the middle of the valve.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: valves 28-54m long, 10-24m broad and at the constriction 7-14m broad.
