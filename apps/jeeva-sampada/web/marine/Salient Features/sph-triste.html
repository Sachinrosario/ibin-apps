SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Body elongate ovate, broader behind. Dorsal side with an anterior sub marginal groove and 3 small tubercles, the lateral ones continued as faint ridges. Eyes large and found sunk in the first peraeon segment.

  First peraeon segment longest antero-laterally rounded to form lobes, overlapping the eyes, segments 2-5 equal, and seventh segment comparatively short. Coxal plates large with a prominent ridge first produced forwards. Each peraeon segment bears a transverse ridge.

  Pleon has a pair of large, conical, sub median tubercles. The dorsal side minutely shagreened. Telson one and a half times as broad as long, with the median part very convex and with 2 tubercles. Dorsal side of the telson with small pustules in longitudinal rows; each pustule with minute hairs. Distal half of telson triangular, border hairy.

First antenna with 9 and second antenna with 15-segmented flagellum. Triangular epistome, with long arms, apex pointed. First maxilla with a small outer and 3 large plumose setae on the inner plate. The large plumose setae on the inner plate. The inner plate of second maxilla rectangular. Maxilliped very much setose.

  Uropods have sub equal rami, outer border of exopod cut into 9 sharp teeth including the tooth-like apex, inner border with 2 indentations.

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi.

Distribution:
Nicobar Island, Malaya Peninsula.

Additional remarks:
  Resembles closely S. terebrans in the nature of the epistome, tubercular dorsal side of the telson and in the shape of the telsonic apex. In S. triste telson has only 2 tubercles while in S. terebrans pleon and telson have a transverse row of 4 tubercles each. Tubercles not setose.

S. triste can be distinguished by the large number of teeth on the outer border of the exopod of the uropod.

Ecological notes:
  S. triste is atruely marine species. Occurs along the coast of Tamil Nadu, Andaman-Nicobar Islands and Lakshdweep Archipelago.

Size and age: 8-10m in length.
