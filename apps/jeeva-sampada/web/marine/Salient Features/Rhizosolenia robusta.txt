SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells in the middle part with conical curved valves.

Diagnostic characteristics: Intercalary bands robust, many and collar shaped. Punctae very fine in three line system crossing one another.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 53-266m.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
