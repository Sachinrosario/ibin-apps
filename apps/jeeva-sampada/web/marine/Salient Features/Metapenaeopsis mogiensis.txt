 SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref No:31, 52, 113

Morphological:   Rostrum straight with tip slightly upturned, reaching middle of second segment 
of antennular peduncle, armed with 8-9 (usually 8) dorsal teeth; carapace with velvet texture and well defined antennal, hepatic and pterygostomian spines, hepatic sulcus deep and curving downwards anteriorly but not reaching branchiostegal border, no stridulating organ; mid-dorsal carina of 3rd abdominal somite distinctly sulcate, depth of 6th somite 5/8 its length; telson as long as inner uropods, non-sulcate, terminating in sharp spine and armed with 3 pairs of movable and a distal pair of fixed spines, proximal pair of movable spines almost reaching base of succeeding pair; tooth on lower antennular flagellum in males near to its base; ischial spine present on 1st pereopod. Distomedian lobule of petasma small and falls short of tip of distoventral flap. A pair of small teeth or platelets in posterior region of thelycal plate, anterior edge of anterior sternal plate between 5th pair of pereopods in female with 4 rounded teeth, 2 median ones being incurved; seminal vesicles tubular. Coxal plate of 4th pereopods with a smoothly rounded antero-median margin.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Carapace without stridulating organ, hepatic sulcus deep and 
curving downwards but not reaching brachiostegal border, mid-dorsal carina of 3rd abdominal somite distinctly sulcate, tooth on lower antennular flagellum in males near to its base, distomedian lobule of petasma small and falls short of tip of distoventral flap, anterior edge of anterior sternal plate between 5th pair of pereopods in female with 4 rounded teeth.

       Colour:  Body with close light-brown mottlings, pleopods washed with white.
