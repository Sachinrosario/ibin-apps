SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 

Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body compressed, fairly deep,  its depth  31 to 35%  of  standard length;  belly with strongly keeled  scutes, 23 to 26 pre – pelvic and 11 to 13 post  - pelvic .Pseudobranch  present,  exposed. Gillrakers 19 to 23 on lower arm of first arch.  Teeth uniserial in both  jaws.   Dorsal fin  with 14 to 17  branched  rays, equidistant between snout – tip  and caudal fin base or  slightly  nearer former. Anal  fin with 43 to 49  branched rays.   Pelvic fins very small , slightly  before dorsal fin origin Swimbladder with a single post – coelomic extensio, present  on right   side of body.

Colour: 

 Dorsal profile of body  dark brown, flanks  silvery white. Upper  third of dorsal fin dark brown, pectoral and pelvic fins  hyaline;  margin of anal and caudal fins dark.
