SALIENT FEATURES   

 

Description: Colony flabellate, 7.2 cm high and 5.5 cm wide. The main stem is 3 mm in diameter and gives of a branch 1.4 cm from its base. This branch curves outward and upward, giving off branchlets from its outer side only. Two of these branchlets bear branches of the their order in an irregularly alternate manner. The average distant between branchlets is about 5 mm. The calyces are regularly distributed on sides of the colony.  The individual calyces are low, evenly rounded verrucae with small apertures. The polyps are retractile, the calyces closing over them so as to leave but a small aperture. The operculum is very delicate.

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are with jagged edges

                        or irregular spicules. The spicules of the calyx are with regular

crosses, blunt stars and other radiate forms with warts and double clubbed spindles.

 

Colour     :  The colony is grayish black, the axis brown and the spicules

colorless.
