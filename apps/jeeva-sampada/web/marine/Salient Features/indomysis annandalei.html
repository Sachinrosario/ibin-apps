Diagnostic characteristics:

Diagnosis: Body slender. Eyes well developed. Rostral plate absent, anterolateral parts of carapace acute. Antennular peduncle in male without hirsute lobe. Antennal scale long, ovate, setose all round with distal suture. Carpopropodus of third thoracic endopod three segmented, dactylus with long slender nail. Carpopropodus two segmented in fourth and fifth endopods, sixth and seventh endopods long and modified, merus characteristically bent, carpopropodus two segmented; eight endopod normal, distal margin expanded with row of six short spines. Pleopods one, two, three and five unjointed plates. Fourth pleopod biramous exopod long and unsegmented terminating in single long barbed seta. Telson short, entire, apex truncate, lateral margin armed with four to eight short spines, truncate distal border armed with row of 30-36 short irregularly arranged spines and with two pairs of long spines at each angle. Uropod broad, longer than telson, setose all round, endopod with single spine in region of statocyst.
