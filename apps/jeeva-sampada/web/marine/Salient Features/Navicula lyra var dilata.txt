SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves olivary, striae and puncta sparser than that of the typical form.

Diagnostic characteristics: Puncta 12-14 in 10m. Striae 11 in 10m. Lateral areas equal in width, not tapered at the ends.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 70-100m, width 37-55m.
