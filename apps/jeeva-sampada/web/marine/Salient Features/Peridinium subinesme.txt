SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells bilaterally symmetrical, in girdle aspect square. The hypotheca is always at least same size as the epitheca. The lower part of the sulcus lies in a depression, which can be more or less deep and broad.

Diagnostic characteristics: The emarginated lower part of the cell body can have processes.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 60-103µ.
