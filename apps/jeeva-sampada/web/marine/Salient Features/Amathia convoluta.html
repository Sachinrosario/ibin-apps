SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Large zoarium erect, branched profusely, length of the zoarium nearly 100mm. Place of anchorage of the colony to the substratum very thick owing to the presence of numerous rootlets from various points. Usually more rootlets emerge from the stem just above the place of attachment to the substratum. Zooecia arranged biserially, in a spiral around the internode. The connate zooecia exposed to its greater length, the width of the zoeocia more or less the same, throughout their length. A distinct ridge developed at the distal end in between the paired zooecia of the spiral because of their connate arrangement. Usually the spiral takes 2 to 2½ turns of the internode. A distinct diaphragm separates the preceding set of zooecia from the succeeding one. Branches which alternate originate from the stem, immediately after the points where the diaphragm is placed. Basal regions of the zooecia (proximal) slightly rounded. A gizzard present. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:  In the present material, which contains colonies with numerous zooecia, all stages of the zooecial structure observed by Harmer had been noticed. The mammillate nature of empty zooecia with well developed striae at the distal end were also clearly depicted. The collar which was found in a pushed out condition in few zooids, looked like small funnel shaped structures. In those zooids with everted lophophore it was noticed that the distal region was free, and the everted portions of successive zooecia gave a zig-zag appearance for the distal region of the zooid rows.
  It seemed that the rows of zooids were interrupted at intervals, at the nodes and after each interruption the spiral seemed to follow a course, as if the whole row was continuous. But only one continuous spiral was represented in each internode.
  Harmer (1915) has stated that the diaphragm may not develop in certain cases and hence the internodes may be comparatively longer. Such a condition had, however, not been noticed in the present specimen. The diaphragm was provided with a hole in the centre through which the funiculus passes. A vague external constriction followed the obliquely placed internal diaphragm. Branching took place only to one side of the stem, the branch being separated from the main stem by a diaphragm. At the tip of the branches zooecia were arranged discontinuously.Meristic characteristics:
