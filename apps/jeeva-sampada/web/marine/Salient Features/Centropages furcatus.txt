SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics: -                  Female: The frontal margin of the prosome truncate. The prosome has a large ventral eye. The last pedigerous segment symmetrical, with a long process and a shorter on the lateral corner. The urosomal segments and furca are in the proportional lengths as 25:12:30:33=100. The antenna I 24-jointed, extends to the end of the anal segment; The 1st ,2nd , and  5th segments have each a strong spine on the anterior margin. Th inner marginal spine on the 2nd segment of the exopod of the 5th leg straight and stout.                

Male: The last pedigerous segment slightly asymmetrical. The anal segment well developed, about as long as the 3rd abdominal segment. The furcal rami 3 times as long as the anal. The outer edge spine on the 2nd of the exopod of the 4th leg long. The thumb on the 2nd segment of the exopod of the right 5th leg broad and long with 2 setae near the apex.
