SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Anterior border of carapace concave, produced into small acute rostrum flanked by pair of small processes, dorsal tubercle present just behind rostral process. Eyes with functional visual elements. Antennal scale shorter than first segment of antennular peduncle, antennal peduncle longer  than antennal scale. Telson with subparellel lateral margins, distal two thirds of lateral margins armed with 17-18 sharp spines, irregularly long and short, distal border with distinct median emargination lodging prominent barbed spine; each half of distal margin armed with six spines and two plumose setae, outer three spines barbed. Uropods long and slender, overreaching telson, endopod shorter than exopod; exopod  with 1-3 spines on outer margin.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Length: 10-18 mm
