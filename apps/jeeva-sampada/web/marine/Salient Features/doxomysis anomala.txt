SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological: Diagnostic characteristics:     Body smooth. Rostrum obtuse. Cornea of eye wider than stalk. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Endopod of thoracic limbs, with three carpopropodal sub equal segments with oblique partition. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male with modified setae on antipenultimate and penultimate joints, terminal joint with short single seta. Cleft of telson one fourth total length and armed with six pairs of spines and pair of plumose setae; lateral margin with seven to eight spines in two groups of three and five. Endopod of uropod with 20-25 closely set spines.
