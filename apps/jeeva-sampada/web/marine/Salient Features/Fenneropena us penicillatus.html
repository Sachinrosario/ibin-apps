SALIENT FEATURES:               Ref No: 103

 

Morphological: Rostrum long, almost reaching tip of antennular peduncle; armed with 7 to 9

teeth on dorsal and 3 to 5 teeth on ventral margins; its blade convex, becoming relatively high in large specimens. Carapace with postrostral carina ending distinctly before posterior margin; adrostral carina and sulcus extending just beyond epigastric tooth, sulcus shallow; gastrofrontal and hepatic carinae absent; gastro-orbital carina extending over middle third of distance between hepatic spine and orbital margin: Abdomen  carinated dorsally from posterior two-third of third somite. Telson unarmed along dorso-lateral margins. Dactyl of third maxilliped in adult male much longer (1.5 to 2.7 times) than propodus. Petasma (in males) with very short distomedian projections, generally not reaching distal margin of costae. Thelycum (in females) with lateral plates their median margin forming tumid lips; anterior process slightly concave, rounded distally ; posterior process ill-calcified, almost completely inserted between lateral plates.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Rostrum long, bearing 7-9 teeth on dorsal and 3-5 teeth on ventral

margins; adrostral carina and sulcus extending just beyond epigastric tooth; gastro-orbital carina extending over middle third of distance between hepatic spine and orbital margin ; dactyl of third maxilleped much longer than propodus ; petasma with very short distomedian projections.

 

Colour: Body white to yellow pinkish , or sometimes pink.
