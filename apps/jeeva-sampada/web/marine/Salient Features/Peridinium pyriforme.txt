SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells slender, broadly pear shaped to elliptical; hypotheca semicircular to broadly flattened, epitheca conical to half egg shaped with straight or convex sides.

Diagnostic characteristics: Ventral side of epitheca steeper than dorsal. Girdle broad, lying sub median, slightly dextral.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 42-70µ, breadth: around 10µ.
