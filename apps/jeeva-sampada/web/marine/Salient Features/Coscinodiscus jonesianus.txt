SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Large cells with areolae in the centre forming a rosette 4 in 10m; further outside about 9 in 10m. Chamber openings and radial rows and spiral rows of areolae are clear. Hyaline radial ribs running to the centre from small spinulae inside the margin.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Two large cone shaped processes present near the margin about 1200 apart.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 140-210m.
