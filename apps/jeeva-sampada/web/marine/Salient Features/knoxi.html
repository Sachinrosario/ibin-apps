SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Shell large and thick with prominent anterior slope and medium or small posterior slope.

  Pallet oval to broadly oval with a short stalk, blade convex on the outer and concave on the inner face, blade calcareous laid down in closely packed segments, blade scaling and flanky. A small indefinitely marked depression present on the outer distal end of the blade.

  Siphons partly separate, gills broad and flat, branchial groove well developed, labial palps free at the distal end, stomach globular, caecum small, intestine looping over the style sac, anal canal open, heart median, unpigmented auricles.

Feeding habit: Feeds on the wood into which it bores.

Main food: Cellulose, supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks: Distribution- warm temperate to tropical.
