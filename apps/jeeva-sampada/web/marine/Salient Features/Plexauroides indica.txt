SALIENT FEATURES
		
Description	:	Colony branching is in one plane, 150 mm high and 170 mm broad. The main stem is 12.5 cm high from which seven branches arise at intervals of 2 mm to 6 mm. The diameter near the base is 3 mm. The coenenchyma is moderately thick and very rough in appearance. The polyps are small, white and wholly retractile. The polyps are scattered over the whole surface, the verrucae being inconspicuous and the distance between them is 0.5 mm. The axis is horny and cylindrical. 

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are warty spindles with smooth globular expansion; tuberculate and irregular warty crosses  are also found. 
 


Colour	:	The colony is orange-yellow in colour.
