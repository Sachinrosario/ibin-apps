
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics: -                     

FEMALE : Prosome and 1st , 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused . Prosome with a median crest, which is low. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment produced into rounded lappets as in S. dauglishi, but smaller. Rostral plate deeply notched, rostral spines shorter than the depth of the notch. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment about equal to the 2 following segments, produced ventrally into a small triangle at the genital orifice but not overlapping the following the following segment. First 3 abdominal segments are fringed with the fine spines on the distal margin. First antenna 24-jointed, 8th and 9th segments fused, reaches up to the distal margin of the genital segment. In the 5th pair of legs the terminal joint with an apical spine and small spine at the base of the apical spine. The sub apical spine is 4 times as long as the terminal joint and with 2 rows of fine teeth.

Additional  remarks :       This species is considered as a synonym of S. helenae by Vervoot (1965). The IIOE specimens agree well with the descriptions given by Farran (1936). Hence it is given the status of a distinct species and in doing so this becomes the first record of the species from the Indian Ocean.
