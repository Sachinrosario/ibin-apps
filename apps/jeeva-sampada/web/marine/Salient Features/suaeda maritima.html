Salient Features :
Erect, green, glabrous, branched herbs.
Leaves numerous, narrowly linear, fleshy, sub acute, floral leves very small.
Flowers minute, linear or filiform, semiteret, fleshy, usually bi sexual; bracts and two bracteoles arranged on very slender spikes; perianth 5; stamens 5.
