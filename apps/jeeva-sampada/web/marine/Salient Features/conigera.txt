Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  It is a horizontal plate like corallum the underside being free.  The underside possesses subimmersed corallites.  The upperside is with stout subacute cones 2 to 3 cm high and 1.2 to 3 cm thick.  Some of the cones are without any marked axial corallites while others have axials 2 to 3 mm in diameter and 2 to 3 mm exsert.  Between the cones the corallum is supplied with labellate corallites.  Radial corallites erect labellate or tubular, 2 to 4 mm long, 1.5 to 2 mm broad with immersed and nariform ones intercalated.  Older radials proliferous.  The first cycle of septa well developed, the directives are more prominent than the laterals.

In a specimen obtained from Nicobar (Scheer and Pillai, 1974) the radials are spreading to 80 degrees and the axials have two cycles of septa.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
