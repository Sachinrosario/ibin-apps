SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Anterior   margin of carapace broadly and   evenly rounded without any  frontal plate. Carpopropodus of thoracic endopods without septum. Pseudobranchial lobes of pleopods two to four spirally twisted;  pleopods not modified. Exopods of uropod longer than endopod, basal segment with three spines; endopod with 10-17  widely spaced  spines. Telson short, 1 ½  times as long as broad, apex broadly rounded and armed with three  small spines, widened  basal part with three  marginal spines, distal half with 12 spines regularly increasing in length.       The shape and armature of the telson and uropods easily distinguish this species.

Size and age

Maximum length:  4-7mm 

Geographical Location:  Pamban, Gulf of Mannar (India)  Arabian gulf, Singapore and North East Australia
