SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony is flabellate and not reticulate exceedingly delicate and  fragile . Height 5.8 cm and spreads to 2.1 cm. The main stem forks 7.1 mm in diameter. The branching is in general dichotomous. The distal branches are less than 0.5 mm in diameter between the calyces. The basal node of the stem is 2 mm long, and the furcations are Y-shaped rather than U-shaped. The calyces are lateral in position and vary greatly in size as well as in spacing. They average about 1.5 mm apart.
		The individual calyces are tubular in form and more prominent. A typical one measures 0.9 mm in height and 1 mm in diameter at the base. The calyx walls are filled with spindles, which are irregularly horizontal on the basal parts and are arranged en chevron on the distal parts and polyps are retractile, but are often seen with their collarets resting just above the calyx margin. The polyps are armed with yellow spicules. There is collaret of relatively heavy spindles in two or three rows, those of the upper row forming low points, each point consisting of the approximated distal ends of two spicules on the tentacle base. Above these points a few other spindles form a rude en chevron arrangement, which is succeeded by vertically, placed spindles on distal parts of tentacles. The coenenchyma is thin and contains spindles and spiny clubs usually disposed longitudinally.
		Spicules: They are large in number of slender curved spindles, most of which bear regular whorls of verrucae. Others bear irregularly distributed thorny points. Clubs with thorny points are also found, but are much less numerous than the spindles.

Colour		:	The entire colony is bright scarlet and the polyps are white with chrome yellow spindles. The spicules are red and yellow.
