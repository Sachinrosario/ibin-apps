Description	:	Colony branching in one plane, small, vertical and erect. The individual calyces are low rounded verrucae. Their operculum consists of 4-5 transversal rows of up to 0.9 mm long spindles, on which 8 triangles are set. The polyps are retractile.
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are long, irregular, discs-like spindles with big occasionally leaf-like warts.
