SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:

Diagnostic characters: Mouth pointing downwards when protracted. Head scale-less but conspicuous scales on breast. Sides of body with grey wavy vertical lines descending from back to a little beyond the lateral line; an elongate yellow spot on belly below pectoral fin.

Sex attributes: Sexes undistinguishable externally.

Descriptive characters: Body oblong, moderately compressed. Dorsal and ventral profiles equally convex. Dorsal profile elevated and curved behind occipital profile and separated from it by a gentle concavity. Snout pointed , slightly longer than eye diameter. Mouth small and when protracted is directed downwards. Commencement of gape of mouth below lower margin of eye. Tip of maxilla extending slightly beyond the front margin of the eye. Mandibles inferiorly slightly concave. Teeth small and numerous. Intraorbital space bounded by two smooth supraorbital  ridges, commencing at the inner

supra-orbital spine and continue posteriorly as the outer edges of the nuchal spine. The median portion of the nuchal spine is elevated and curved according to the dorsal profile. Two small spines on top of the head, opposite the front border of the eye, the outer one more prominent than the inner, which is opposite the ridge bounding the inter-orbital space. Pre-opercle with an obtuse angle, its lower margins finely serrated. Scales small, inconspicuous and all over the body including breast. Lateral line begins with a concavity and runs less convex than the dorsal profile, extending beyond the end of the soft dorsal and anal fins , but stops short of the base of the caudal. Dorsal and anal spines strong , laterally compressed; second dorsal spine almost straight , the 3rd nearly as long. 3rd and 4th dorsal spine serrated anteriorly for about half length from base. Second anal spine stronger , but shorter than the 2nd dorsal; the 3rd anal spine rather shorter than the 2nd  and serrated anteriorly for about half length from base. Ventrals do not quite reach the annals. Ventrals with a strong spine and a large axillary scale. Caudal forked with rounded lobes.

 

COLOUR: Abdomen silvery , back brownish. Sides of body with dark, narrow , wavy, vertical  lines descending from the back to a little beyond lateral line , often fading on keeping after death. An elongate yellow spot on belly below pectoral fin Base of pectoral fin dark.                Ref no: 5,6,17.

 

Meristic Characters:D.VIII,16; A.III,14.

 

Feeding Habit:

 

Main food: Copepods, polychaetes, amphipods, bivalves, gastropods, and foraminiferans.

Feeding type: Bottom and column feeding.

Additional Remarks: No significant variation in the food of fish from different places in different seasons. Feeding habits of the fish do not change with age. The fish was found to feed more actively in the months of January, February, April and November at  Kilakarai        Ref no: 7.
