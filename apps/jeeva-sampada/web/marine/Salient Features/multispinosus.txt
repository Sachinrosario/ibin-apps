SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Closely resembles H. flagelliforma except in the armature of the telson and inner uropod. Lateral margin of telson with 11 – 13 pairs of spines, apical sinus deeper with 26 spines on each side. In female lateral margin of telson with 14-15 pairs of spines. Endopod of uropod with 12-13 long sharp spines.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult male    : 8 mm     
Adult female : 7 mm
