Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics:
Body long and slender. Carapace with low apically rounded rostrum. Eyes long, inner distal angle of peduncle produced into distinct papilla; cornea small. Antennal scale narrow, over reaching antennular peduncle, outer distal spine long; antennal peduncle four segmented. Thoracic endopods three to eight slender and sparsely setose, carpus and propodus distinct. Exopod of third pleopod of male with strong spines on last five segments, last segment with short spine and long stout spine with frilled flang on one side. Telson six times as long as broad, gradually narrowing towards tip,apical sinus shallow, lateral border with seven pairs of small sharp evenly spaced spines, apices with long spines sinus armed with eight pairs of well spaced spines. Exopod of uropod with single spine.
