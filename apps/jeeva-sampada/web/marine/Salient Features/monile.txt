Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum cushion-shaped or submassive.  Underside costate.  Calices 6-8 mm in diameter, about 2 mm deep, often 2 to 4 calicinal centers run together to form short valleys.  Intercorallite walls little developed.  Septa 40 to 55 per calyx, confluent, higher cycles unite to the next lower frosted teeth, sides granular.  Axial fossa circular, 0.75 to 1.25 mm in diameter with a papilliform columella.



Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
