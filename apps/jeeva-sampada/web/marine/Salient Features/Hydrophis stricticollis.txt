SALIENT FEATURES :

Morphological : Head small; one pre - and 1-2 postoculars; anterior temporal single  
(rarely divided) ; 7-8 supralabials, the 2nd in contact with the prefrontal, the 3rd
 and 4th touching the eye ; 4 infralabials in contact with the genials, both pairs of
 which are well defined. Body long and slender anteriorly, compressed posteriorly;
 scales in 45-55 rows at midbody posteriorly somewhat hexagonal in shape, subimbricate
and keeled; ventals 374-452, entire, not twice as broad as the adjacent dorsal scales,
each one with 2 keels.

DIAGNOSTIC CHARACTERITICS:

This sea snake is coloured greyish or olivaceous above, yellowish below, with from 45 to
65 dark bands which fade with age. The head is black to olive in colour , with yellow 
markings mainly on the snout and the sides of the head.

FEEDING HABIT: Piscivorous

MAIN FOOD: Fish

SIZE AND AGE :

Maximum Length : (male) 105cm
Average Length : (female) 105cm
