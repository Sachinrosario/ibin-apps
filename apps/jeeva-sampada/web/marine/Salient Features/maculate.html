SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 1,11
Morphological: A medium sized species.

Diagnostic characteristics : With a prominent snout, projecting beyond upper jaw. Mouth inferior; rostral pores 3, marginal pores 5, notching the edge to produce 3 lobes, mental pores 5, Teeth differentiated into large and small, the large ones forming the outer series in upper jaw and the inner row in lower jaw; no canine teeth. Gillrakers on lower limb of first arch 6 to 8,with toothed plates below. Dorsal fin with 10 spines, followed by notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 22 to 26 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 soft rays, the second spine strong, Caudal fin with a slightly convex hind margin, often angular above and rounded below. Scales cycloid on snout and below and behind eye, elsewhere ctenoid . swim bladder carrot shaped , with 19 to 21 pairs of appendages. the first appendage longer, extending into head and branching three, last simple parallel to the tubular end of the bladder, the others arborescent .

Colour : Five dark blotches extending from the back to lower part of flanks, and a sixth dark blotch on top of caudal peduncle, Spinous part of dorsal fin black expect v along base, soft part of dorsal fin with a black margin
