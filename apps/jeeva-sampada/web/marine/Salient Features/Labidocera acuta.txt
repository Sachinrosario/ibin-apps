SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      

Female: Cephalon rounded with a conspicuous  anterior rostral hook; lateral cephalic hooks absent; dorsal eye lenses moderately large, wider apart and separated by about twice eye lens diameter; rostrum deeply bifurcate;  prongs divergent distally;  pedigerous segments IV and V fused, pedigerous segment V proximally produced into acuminate lobes, tip reaching distal margin of genital segment; urosome three-segmented, about one-third length of prosome; genital segment with a stout postero-lateral conical process present on its right side; urosome II as long as broad, anal segment well developed; caudal rami asymmetrical, right ramus 1.7 times longer than broad; third and fourth caudal setae from outer margin proximally thickened; enlarged portion of setae distinctly longer than caudal rami all setae directed posteriad; antenna I with 25 segments; 5th leg markedly asymmetrical, left leg being stouter and longer; Re ending in three distal spines of which two subterminal ones are well developed; Re with three outer marginal spines and one inner marginal spinule.     

Male: Cephalon resembles that of female except that dorsal cuticular eye lenses are larger and placed close together; posterior corners of pedigerous segment V modified into an acutely pointed lobe on left side and a right lobe with a curved process turned lateroposteriad, tip of process not extending beyond urosome III; urosome five-segmented; genital segment on its right posterior margin bears a short spine inner to which is present a conical process which is less than half length of genital segment; caudal rami asymmetrical, right ramus being larger and twice as long as broad; caudal setae not bulged at base as in female; Right antenna-I : geniculate; segment 17 lacking in denticulated ridge; segment 18 with prominent denticulated ridge, extend proximad and overlapping almost whole length preceding segment; fusion segment 19-21 with well developed toothed plate extending to one-third length of the segment; segment 22 with distal spiniform process which is as long as segment itself. Right 5th leg chelate; basipod 2 large; hand of chela orbicular, with inner margin of hand carrying a triangular outgrowth; finger short, broad at middle and with 2 inner marginal and 2 terminal setae; left leg: terminal segment distally with three finger-like processes, a small cresecentic basal process and a distal outer marginal spine; inner margin of the segment setose; subterminal segment with a disto-lateral spine.      

Additional  remarks:      
L. acuta  is chiefly a neritic species, widely recorded from Indian  Seas. An oceanic cognate of L. acuta  has recently  been described as  L. pseudacuta  by Silas and Pillai (1969).

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     
Adult female: 3.01-3.60 (av. 3.32) mm     
Adult male: 2.73-3.21 (av. 3.02) mm
