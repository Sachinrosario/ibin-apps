SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells box shaped without a sharp constriction between valve and girdle zone in girdle view. Horns from each pole of apical axis well developed. Two strong spines on each valve, a short distance from the horns.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface studded with numerous tiny spines. On lower margin of valve mantle, s hyaline collar supported by ribs present.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.
