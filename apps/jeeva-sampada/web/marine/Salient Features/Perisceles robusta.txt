SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branching in one plane. The mode of branching is very indefinite; the ends of the branches are swollen-like spikes. The polyps are mostly lateral in position. The calyces are 1.5 mm in height and 1..3 to 1.7 mm in diameter. The folded tentacles form the operculum. The tips of the branches and twigs are occupied by two diverging almost opposite polyps. The verrucae are cylindrical and have the same texture as the coenenchyma.
		Spicules: The spicules of the crown consist of 2-3 transversal rows of club-shaped spindles. The spicules of the coenenchyma are irregular with four radiant stars.

 Colour		:	Unknown
