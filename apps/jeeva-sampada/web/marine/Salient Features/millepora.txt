Descriptive characters:  Corallum vasifrom, pedicellate, in Gulf of Mannar colonies are more than one metre in diameter and form big platforms 2 to 3 m in spread.  Main branches horizontal, coalascent, fusion sometimes complete enough to form thick plates or with wide interspaces in between.  At the underside the branches are pressed to the general plane.  In few specimens that were upturned during the 1964 cyclone later it was found that the original branchlets of the underside began to grow upwards.  Branchlets of the upperside single or groups of 3 to 5, angular, 1.5 to 3.5 cm long, 7 to 8 mm thick (sometimes smaller as in forma spicifera).  Tips with or without proliferations.  Branches at the growing edges horizontal and much more elongated than the branchlets on the uppersides.  Axial corallites 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter, 1 to 1.5 mm exsert; wall thin and porous; opening rounded or oval with two cycles of septa, primaries larger than the secondaries.  Prominent radial corallites labellate guttershaped, spreading but rarely at right angles.  Length of the radials 1.5 to 2 mm, width 1.25 to 1.5 mm.  Towards the basal parts of the branches and the main branches the radials become shorter and finally immersed.  Septa six.  Wall of the radials fragile, striated and serially echinulate.  Surface coenenchyme striated and echinulate, porous in section.  Living coral brown with white tips.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
