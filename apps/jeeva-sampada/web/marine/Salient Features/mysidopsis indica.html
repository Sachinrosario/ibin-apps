Genus: Mysidopsis G.O. Sars
Rostrum pointed. Antennal scale lanceolate, setose all round; distal segment small. Eyes large, usually with dorsal papilla. Endopod of first thoracic limb with segments three and four fused; endopod of limb three to eight with three carpopropodal segments. Pleopods of female tather broad, of male biramous, exopod of fourth pair longer than endopod ending in long barbed spine. Exopod of uropod much broader and longer than endopod, spines on latter confined to region near statocyst or extending whole border. Lateral margin of telson with varying number of spines, apex entire or with small median unarmed notch. The six segmented endopod of first thoracic limb is very characteristic of this genus.

Mysidopsis indica
Median dorsal part of carapace with three nodules; frontal plate broadly triangular. Eye stalk with large bidigitate process on dorsal surface. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Pleopods two to five of male with lamella-like expansion near base of endopod; exopod of fourth pleopod terminating in single stout plumose seta. Telson slightly longer than half length of uropods, roughly equal in length and width, distal part suddenly narrowed, apex with shallow notch, distal border with two pairs of spines, lateral border with two spines near base. Endopod of uropod with single spine near statocyst.
