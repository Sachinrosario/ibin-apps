SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells broadly elliptic and epiphytic on Polysiphonia. Raphe less valve with three well-defined hyaline areas demarcated by striated bands, the striae being unequal in length and with dot-like thickening at the center. 
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valve with sigmoid raphe. Striae radial punctate, about 21 in 10m. Axial area narrow dilating into a very small central area.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 20-40m long and 15-30m broad.
