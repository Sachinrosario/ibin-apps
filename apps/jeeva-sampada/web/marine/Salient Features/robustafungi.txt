IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum polycentric, flat to dome-shaped.  Larger ones 50 cm in greater diameter 20-30 mm thick.  Central corallite is not always distinct.  Secondary corallite formed by circum oral buddy.  Septa run from one centre to the next.  Principle septa thick and alternate with thinner ones.  Septal edges with teeth upto 2 mm high.  Columella centers irregularly developed, papillose.  Underside of corallum spiny, and perferate.

This species is similar to Halomitra but the latter is much lighter with septo costal characters similar to Fungia fungites.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
