Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  This species is characterized by dendroid corallum with radiating corallites 6 to 11 mm in diameter.  The depth of calices range from 4 to 5 mm.  Septa in four complete cycles with a few of the fifth.  Three cycles of septa unite to a conspicuous, projecting columella.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
