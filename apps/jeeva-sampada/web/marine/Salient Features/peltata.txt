Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum cup-shaped or flat and disc-shaped.  In the younger coralla the corallites are level, 6-7 mm in diameter, about 3 mm deep.  In the larger coralla the corallites display vide range of variations both in the size and elevation.  They range from 4 mm to 6 mm in diameter, level or upto 6 mm exsert.  Septa in three cycles with a few of the fourth.  The first two cycles subequal, larger than other.  Septa narrow at first, broader below, not exsert; edges entire or microscopically serrated, side granular.  Columella almost fill the bottom of the calyx, honey-comb shaped, projecting.  Coenenchyme with ridges and furrows.  The polyps are found readly expand during day and are upto 5 mm above the skeleton.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
