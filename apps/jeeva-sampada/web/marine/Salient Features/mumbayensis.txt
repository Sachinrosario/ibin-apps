SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        General form robust. Carapace with dorsomedian nodules., rostrum wide and obtuse. Antennal sympod with two short and two long spines. Thoracic endopods three to seven with four to seven carpopropodial segments. Endopod of eighth limbof male three segmented, second segment short with five long plumose setae, third segment long and bent and when straightened shorter than basal segment of exopod; in female single segmented, straight and shorter than basal segment of exopod. Lateral margin of telson with 11-12 long stout spines; outer pair of distal spines longer than inner.     

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)    

Adult male: 8-11 mm   

Female      : 9-12 mm.
