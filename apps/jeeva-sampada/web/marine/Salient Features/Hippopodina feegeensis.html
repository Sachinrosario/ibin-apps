SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Unilaminar or multilaminar. Zooecia arranged in regular longitudinal rows separated by thin calcareous lines. Zooecia of the superposed layers slightly inflated. Surface reticulopunctate. Orifice with a semicircular distal portion and slightly arched posterior region and indented with cardelles otherwise straight lateral sides. Avicularia typically present, occupy the disto-lateral region, directing inwards. Their tips do not touch each other in the median line. Mandibles pointed. The shape of the opreculum very similar to that of the orifice. Main sclerite marginal. Ovicell hyperstomial and porous, with minute pearly tubercles. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:   A study of the type description and the subsequent descriptions given by earlier authors shows that the most important characters of specific importance are the nature of the front, the shape of the orifice, the position of the avicularia and the nature of the ovicell. There is general agreement in these characters. Harmer (1957) in his discussion on this species has stated that the avicularian rostrum may join in the median line, but this feature is not observed in the present instance. The dissimilarity in the shape of the avicularia of the same pair is, however, noticed in the present instance also. Certain zooecia of the same colony do not possess avicularia and this is in close conformity with what Harmer noticed in the Ceylon specimen. Harmer found only one short unilateral avicularia in the Singapore specimens. The specimens of Levinsen (1909) and Crossland (as quoted by Harmer 1957) possessed avicularia with a disto-lateral placing and a proximal direction. So the difference noticed in the disposition of the avicularia may be of an intra-specific nature.
  The nature of the operculum is very much similar to that noticed by Busk in his type specimens, and by Waters and Harmer.
  The shape of the zooecia seems to be a very characteristic feature. The fertile zooecia are nearly rectangular. A slight difference was noticed between the orifice of the fertile and non-fertile zooecia. In the fertile zooecium the orifice is broader than long. In those zooecia where avicularia are absent the distal portion of the orifice is occupied by few tubercles.
