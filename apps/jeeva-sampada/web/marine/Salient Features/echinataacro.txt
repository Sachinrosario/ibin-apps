Descriptive characters:  Corallum semiarborescent about 25 cm in height.  Main branches upto 2 cm thick, subdividing.  Secondary branches average 1 cm thick or less supplies with numerous ramiculi that give the appearance of a bottle-brush to the branches.  They range from single elongate proliferations to small branchlets 1.5 to 2 cm long with radiating tubular corallites.  Axial corallites average 2 mm in diameter 2 to 3 mm exsert, wall thin and porous, opening large, rounded, only the primary cycle of septa present.  Radial corallites slender, tubular, sometimes curved, may be a little dilated at the base than at the top, 4 to 12 mm long, 1.5 to 2 mm thick.  The outer wall is slightly more projecting than the inner so that the tip has a braked appearance.  On the main branches and on the sides of the larger radials a few are nariform.  Wall of the radials porous, very thin.  Septa 6.  Outer wall of the corallites with plate-like echinulations.  Surface coenenchyme pitted and echinulate, the pores being rounded, oval or elongated.  On the main divisions there are many large immersed corallites placed wide apart.  Corallum porous in section.  

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
