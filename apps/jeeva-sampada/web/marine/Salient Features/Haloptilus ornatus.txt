SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  Head large; eye moderate; one pre - and 2-3 [pstpci;ars; 7-8 supralabials,the 2nd normally in contact with the prefrontal, the 3rd and 4th touching the eye; 2 superposed anterior temporals; 4 lowerlabials in contact with the genials, which are well developed, in contact with one another and the posterior pair rather ill - developed and separted by scales; no small scales at the oral margin between the infralabials.

Body robust, rather stocky, not elongate; scales imbricate, in 31-45 rows (males) 39-55 rows (females) at midbody; the sales posteriorly are more or less hexagonal, subimbricate or juxtaposed, each one with a central tubercle or short keel.  Ventrals 209-260 (males), 236-312 (females), broadest anteriorly and narrower posteriorly; preanals feebly enlarged.

Diagnostic characteritics:  The overall colour of the spotted sea snake is pale greyish or olivaceous, often almost white above and yellowish or whitish below.  The dorsal pattern consists of several dark bars or rhomboidal spots, usually numbering 41-45, which are wide and closely spaced on top and tapering on sides.  The head is olivaceous.

Feeding habit :  Piscivorous.

Main food : fish

Additional remarks:  The global range of this widely distributed are snake is from the Persian Gulf to the coasts of China and New guinea.  This species has caused human fatalies.

Geographical Location:  In India, this species is recorded from the waters of the Arabian Sea, Indian Ocean, and the Bay of Bengal.  Rare on the west coast.
