Morphological:  Ref No:  chopra, 1923
FEMALE:  THE BODY IS YELLOWISH AND ON THE DORSAL SURFACE ALONG THE DEFORMED SIDE, THERE ARE THREE BROAD COLOUR-PATCHES ON THE MARGINS OF THE SECOND, THIRD AND FOUTH SEGMENTS. THE HEAD IN ITS POSTERRIOR PORTION IS ALSO SEGMENTS COLOURED VENTRALLY ALL THE OOSTEGITES OF THE DEFORMED AND THE FIRST TWO OF THE NON-DEFORMED SIDE ARE COLOURED.
  The head is longer than broad and seems to be formed of two halves. The posterior border is much shorter than the anterior and, like the latter, is rounded.
  The abdomen is proportionately shorter than in P. ALCOCKI AND P. BUYTENDIJKI and is some what broader than long. The lines of demarcation of all the segments are well defined on almost entire dorsal surface. The pleural lamellae are absent, but the margins of the segments are well separated from one another and are not almost continuous, The sixth segments is long and comparatively narrow and has its posterior margin deeply cleft. In this respect the species differs from both P. ALCOCKI AND P. BUITENDIJKI
  The antennules are two-segmented while the antennae appear to be formed of three segments. The median lobe of the posterior lamina is broadly rounded, and is not pointed in the middle like that of the other two species. The secondary lobes are comparatively smaller, and of the two the outer is somewhat broader. The median lobe has streaks of pigment on its surface, and the secondary lobes also have a few similar streaks. The maxilliped has a well developed dome-shaped palp deeply set in the anterior side, and armed with five or six setae.
  The first oostegite is like that of the allied forms and its internal crest is well developed and digitate. The peracopods have the usual structure.
  The plcopods cover the entire surface of the abdomen, and consequently the male is completely hidden under them . The exopodites of some of the posterion pairs on the shorter side of the body project beyond the abdomen. The uropods are present.
  The male does not appear to differ much from that of the typical P. alococki. The abdomenis, however, markedly longer than broad, and the sixth segment, which is almost circular in outline is proportionately shorter. Four pairs of pleopods are present.   
  

Diagnostic characteristics:  ABDOMEN OF FEMALE DISTINCTLY LESS THAN HALF TOTAL LENGTH OF BODY AND BROADER THAN LONG, MEDIAN LOBE OF POSTERRIOR LAMINA OF HEAD REGULARLY ROUNDED, MARGINS OF ABDOMINAL SEGMENTS WELL SEPARATED, SIXTH ABDOMINAL SEGMENT IN FEMALE COMPARATIVELY NARROW, WITH ITS POSTERIOR MARGIN DEEPLY CLEFT; SIXTH ABDOMINAL SEGMENT IN MALE ROUNDED.
