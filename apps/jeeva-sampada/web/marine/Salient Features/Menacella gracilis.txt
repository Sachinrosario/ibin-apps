SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony is 115 mm in height and 80 mm in maximum breadth, branched in one plane. The main stem extends to the very top of the colony and tapers markedly from 125 mm at the base to an almost thread-like fineness.  There is no anastomosis. The axis is horny and soft, very flexible in its upper half. The coenenchyma is very thin and allows the dark axis to shine through in its lower parts. The spicules are arranged longitudinally and this disposition continues on the sides of the  verrucae. The polyps occur mainly on two sides of the stem and are branched. The verrucae are small and dome-like and are separated by intervals of from 1 to 2 mm. The polyps are completely retractile. There is a distinct tentacular operculum consisting of a collaret of two to three rows of curved spindles and eight points each composed of four to five spicules.

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are tubercular warts, slightly curved and S-shaped and some with X-shaped markings. 

 Colour	:	Colony creamy-white and the spicules are  colourless.
