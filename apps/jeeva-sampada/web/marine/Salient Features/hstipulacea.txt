SALIENT FEATURES OF Halophila stipulacea (Forsk.) Asch. 1867

Leaves: 2 at each shoot node, petioles upto 12 mm long, flat and with sheathing leaf base, lamina linear-oblong in shape with serrulate margin and hairy on both the surfaces, cross-veins, 4-14 pairs

Inflorescence: Flowers solitary, axillary covered by spathes. Male flowers not found so far 

Perianth: Absent Ovary: Ellipsoid with 3 styles 

Fruits: Ovoid to globose, seeds upto 27 

Seeds: Globose, beaked at both the ends 

Flowering and fruiting: Female flowers and fruits are noticed from February to April 

Special features: Male flowers are not found so far which is due probably to the appearance of male flowers for a very short period, and are ephemeral
