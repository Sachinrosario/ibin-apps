Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum arborescent with stout thick branches 2 to 4 cm in diameter.  Some branches may be stunted thick.  Branchlets 1 to 2 cm thick prostrate or spreading.  Axial corallites 2.5 to 3 mm thick, so much exsert.  Radial corallites much crowded, tubular, half tubular, dimidiate, labellate or subimmerse; all types mixed.  Some of the radials may be upto 4 mm long and proliferate.  Primary septa well developed, secondaries poorly developed.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  This is the first published record of the species from the Indian region and is based on authors collection from Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park; Wandoor.
