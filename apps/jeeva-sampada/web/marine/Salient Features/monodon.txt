SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:         Rostrum long with dorsal and ventral teeth; ventral teeth appears in late postlarval stages and few in number; supraorbital,pterygostomian and hepatic spines present; they become reduced in advanced post larvae,except hepatic spine; antennal scale is narrow and long with a terminal spine; the  scale and the antennular peduncle same in length; endopod is much shorter; abdominal somites 3-6 carry dorsomedian spines in early stages; paired posterolateral spines present on somites 5-6; somite 6 also carries a curved,ventromedian spine; mandible with 2-segmented palp; distal segment bearing 2 setae and proximal with 4 setae. The thoracic legs 1-3 chelate and functional in mysis stages; no long terminal setae on endopod; legs with vestigial exopods except on 5th pereopod; uropods well developed and uropods and telson of equal length; the uropodial endopod bear 25 plumose setae and exopod with 22 plumose setae and a non-plumose seta, shorter than distolateral spine inpost larva I.Telson with 8+8 spines.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum  long ,toothed dorsally and ventrally in late postlarval stages; hepatic spine long and strong;  antennular peduncle reaching the tip of antennal scale; antennal scale long and narrow; 6th abdominal somite very long; presence of paired posterolateral spines on somites 5-6; 3rd leg reaches beyond eyes; no exopod on 5th pereopod; pleopods uniramous; uropods and telson of equal length; telson with 8+8 spines.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.Main food: Small crustaceans,algal matters,polychaetes,molluscs,fish partsand insects (Hall 1962); debris, sand and silt, debris composed of mud andorganic matter(Kuttyamma,1973). 

Feeding type: omnivorous.  

Size and age:  

Protozoea I-1.1mm to post larva-I-4.7mm.

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed)  30.0cm.

Maximum weight : (g)  (male/ female/ unsexed)  142gms.

Longevity (y) (wild) :    1-2 years.                     

Eggs and larvae: 

Eggs:Spherical,yellowish green in color,somewhat translucent; 0.27 to 0.31mm in dia., the yolkmass 0.22 to 0.24mm; perivitelline space narrow.Nauplii I-VI: size 0.32-0.49mm. The naupliar eye(ocellus) is retained through out all  stages; 3 pairs of cephalic appendages present; antennule uniramous with 2+4+1 setae; antennal endopod with 2+2+1 setae and exopod with 5 setae. Mandible with 3 setae each on endo and exopods; from 2nd nauplius onwards the 4th seta of antennal scale is bifid; 5+1 short setae; caudal furca  bear1+1 seta in nauplii 1&2, it increases to 7+7 in nauplius VI.

Protozoea I: 1.1mm in size,frontal organs round; caudal furca broad with  7+7 spines.

Protozoea II: 1.68mm; carapace with long ,ventrally bent rostrum and bifid supraorbital spines; maxillipeds1 and 2 appear as small biramous buds; telson unchanged.

Protozoea III: 2.38mm; rostrum long; supraorbital not bifid; abdominal segments 1-5 with dorsomedian spines; somites 5 and 6 with a pair of posterolateral spines; buds of mxp3 and pereopods appear; uropods developed and telson with 8+8 spines.

Mysis I: 3.79mm; rostrum longer than eye,no dorsal tooth; supraorbital,pterygostomian and hepatic spines present on carapace; spines on abdomen remain same; pleopod buds present,telson unchanged.

Mysis II: 4.37mm; rostrum with a minute tooth; pleopods short,unsegmented; antenna with a long narrow scale with sharp spine at tip; mandible with developing palp; legs 1-3 minutely chelate; telson same.

Mysis III: 4.40mm; spines on rostrum,carapace and abdomen remain same; pleopods long, 2-egmented withouit setae; Md.with longer palp; pereopods 1-3 chelate,setate present,endopod 5-segmented; setae increases and telson no change.

Postlarva I: 4.70mm; supraorbital spne reduced in size; mandibular palp 2-segmented with setae; exopods on mxp.2&3 and pereopods 1-5 are shrunken,vestigial, without setae; telson cleft remains with 8+8 spines.The ventral spines appear on rostrum from postlarva VI and onwards.

Characteristics: rostrum with ventral spines; 5th pereopod without an exopod; 3rd leg chela reaching beyond antennal scale; antennal scale and peduncle equal in length; 6th abdominal segment longer than all other segments.

Abundance: they are caught in large numbers but not in abundance.
