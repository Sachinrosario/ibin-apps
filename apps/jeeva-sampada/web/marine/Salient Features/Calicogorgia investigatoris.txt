SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony is 235 mm in height and 205 mm in maximum breadth. The verrucae are truncated cones slightly compressed, having a basal diameter of over 2 mm and an average height of 3 mm the dome-like anthocodiae standing on the top to a height of 1:5 mm. They are disposed mostly on the two lateral surfaces, but in some parts occur all round.  is branched in one plane and anastomoses freely; it measures 6 cm in height and 9 cm at the broadest part. It consists of short portions of two main branches 3:5 cm apart. The polyps are very prominent, and are 0.75 mm in height and 1.25 mm in diameter. The operculum is formed from the top of the verrucae and consists of eight triangles that meet and close over the retracted anthocodiae.  
		
		
Spicules: The spicules of the verrucae are warty and spiny spindles, but a few crosses occur. The spindles vary in length from 1.8 mm 0.1 mm.  

Colour	:	Colour of the coenenchyma is pinkish-yellow; the verrucae are pale yellow.
