 SALIENT FEATURES:								Ref No:103

Morphological:   Body almost entirely pubescent; rostrum armed with 7 to 11 (9-10 being the 
most common) teeth along entire dorsal margin, nearly straight, slightly uptilted and reaching distal half of third antennular segment or just beyond; postrostral carina ending near posterior margin of carapace; adrostral carina ending behind second rostral tooth, sulcus behind epigastric tooth; branchiocardiac carina feeble, ending near posterior third of carapace; telson armed only with spinules; ischial spine on 1st pereopod minute or absent; in adult males merus of 5th pereopod with a proximal notch followed by a twisted keeled tubercle. Distomedian projections of petasma laminose and diverging; distolateral projections directed anterolaterally. In females anterior plate of thelycum flask-shaped, its anterior margin slightly convex and bearing 3 tubercles of subequal size; lateral plates kidney–shaped, often with angular contours.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum long, straight, armed with teeth along entire dorsal margin; 
branchicardiac carina feeble, ending near posterior third of carapace; ischial spine on 1st pereopod minute or absent; distomedian projections of petasma laminose and diverging; anterior plate of thelycum flask–shaped, its anterior margin slightly convex and bearing 3 tubercles.

        Colour:   Body semi–translucent pale green with brownish–green specks.
