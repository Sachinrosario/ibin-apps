SALIENT FEATURES:                                                

Morphological:Dense pubescence covering the body,including the rostrum. Rostrum extends beyond the first segment of antennular peduncle; smooth in early post larva and bearing 12 dorsal teeth in juvenile stage; long setae present ventrally. Antennal,pterygostomian and hepatic spines present on carapace; longitudinal and horizontal ridges present on carapace; last 3 abdominal segments bear dorsal and lateral  carinae; 5 rows of ventrolateral spines present on abdominal somite 6 in early post larva (8mm); 9-10 rows of spines in older postlarva (12mm); also ventromedian spines present on 1st 3 abdominal somites in 8mm larva and 1-4 in 12mm larva, whereas the spine present on somite 1 only in 17mm postlarva; 5th somite also has a pair of dorsolateral spines in 12mm postlarva. Telson tapering with 3 pairs of lateral spines and 12-16 spines at the indented posterior margin. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Antennule with prosartema; peduncle setose; mandibles symmetrical; incisor process sythe-like; palp large,setose and 2-segmented; exopod of maxilliped I lamellar and unsegmented; pereopods 1-3 chelate,with epipodites; legs 1-5 with small exopodites; basis and ischium of legs 1-2 with distal spine; pleopods uniramous in 8mm larva and biramous in others(.late stages); exopod of uropod with an externo-distal spine.

Sex attributes: Separate ,(Heterosexual)

Descriptive characters:Generally spines on body very prominent; body pubescent; dorsomedian and lateral spines absent on abdominal segments; dactylus of the 4th leg about ¼ th the propodus; leg 2 with a row of stiff setae along  merus and carpus; presence of rows of ventrolateral spines on 6th abdominal somite.

Meristic characteristics:
Feeding habit: Pelagic zone

Main food:  planktonic organisms,copepods,algae,polychaetes,earthworms; animal detritus; organic matter with sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous

Additonal remarks:       Majority of the larvae and postlarvae of Funchalia woodwardi are collected from upper 50-150m,the upper pelagic zone at all stations. However, they may live at depths of 1500-3000m (Murphy & Hijort,1914).In the Indian Ocean out of 1518 plankton samples collected,only 8 specimens  of Funchalia were obtained and that too from 7 stations.This shows its rarity and primitiveness. Another important factor is the pubescence on the body in older specimens; the disappearance and reappearence of the hepatic spine; it is well developed in early stages; reduced in juveniles or absent and reappear in sub-adults.

Size and age:  8mm-50mm,recorded from Indian Ocean.

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs and larvae unknown.

Postlarvae:Body smooth,slightly pubescent in 17mm specimen; rostrum with 5 dorsal teeth in 12mm  and 7 distinct dorsal teeth in 17mm specimens; no spines on carapace; procurved ventromedian spines present on 1-3 abdominal somites in 8mm specimen;1-4 in 12mm spn. and only on 1st somite in 17mm specimen. Sixth abdominal somite with a posteromedian spine and a pair of posteroventrals in all specimens and 5 rows of ventral spines in 8mm postlarva,increasing to 9/10 in the oldest specimen of post larva. Telson widely cleft,12+12 short spines on the posterior margin in the cleft with 3 pairs of lateral spines,with a long pair fused to the posterior margin of the telson. A strong median spine develops in 17mm specimen; pereopods without exopods; legs 1-3 chlate; pleopods uniramous in 8mm postlarva and biramous in others.
