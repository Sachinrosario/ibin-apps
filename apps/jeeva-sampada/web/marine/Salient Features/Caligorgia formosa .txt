SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branching in one plane, pinnate with short branches. The short branches stand exactly against each other and are 11.5 cm long and unbranched. The polyps stand in verticels of four or five, distally in three. 11 to 12 verticels are arranged in 3 cm long. The polyps are about 1.5 mm long, adaxially strongly bent inwards and swollen into club-shape at the distal end. In each longitudinal row there are about 10 layers of scales up to 4 mm broad, abaxially 10 in numbers, occasionally 12 in round to trapezium shape.
		Spicules: The spicules of the bark are narrow and spindle shaped 0.42 mm long.

Colour			:	Bark grayish blue, branches and polyp whitish yellow . 
				Axis yellowish-brown.
