SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony  large, thin, plate-like calcareous lobes and complete measuring 2.6 cm long. The fragment has an entire length of 26 cm. The first internode above the base is the shortest, being 3.5 mm long. The next is 1.7, 3.1, 4.3 and 4.5 cm and the remaining three gradually diminish in size. The diameter at the base is 3 mm; at distal end is 1.5 mm. The longest horny node is the third, which is 3 mm long and their length diminished at the distal node, which is 1.4 mm. Shallow longitudinal furrows mark the surfaces of the internodes. The individual calyces are long and slender. A typical calyx is 8 mm long and 2.2 mm in diameter at the margin and 1.1 mm at the narrowest oar if the pedicel, near the middle region. The coenenchyma is leathery and almost devoid of large spindles.
		Spicules: The spicules of the calyx walls are slender, needle-like and small bar-like forms.The coenenchyma has minute, short bars with expanded and rounded ends. (Fig. a, b & c).
 
Colour	:	The colony is ivory white and polyps are light brown.
