Descriptive characters:  
The following details of this species is based on a specimen from Mandapam Palk Bay: Corallum explanate, maximum thickness 15 mm surface with a few gibbosities.  Calices conspicusous, 0.7 to 0.8 mm in diameter, well cut out from the coenenchyme by a solid wall; a diameter to 1 mm apart.  Septa in two cycles, primaries large, the two directives invariably exsert, but in many calices all the primaries are exsert, and meet at the centre of the fossa over a rudimentary columella.  Second cycle of septa more or less complete and extend half way to width of the primaries.  The edges of larger septa minutely serrated.

Surface reticulum with minute plate-like spinules, secondarily serrated.  Between the meshes of the reticulum, circular open pores are present.  Section shows an upper thickening and a lower streaming layer.  Living coral yellowish-brown.

Remarks:  This species may cut huge colonies.  There is one specimen in the exhibits of the Madras Museum of this species nearly 75 cm in diameter spread with the surface rising into large conical or cylindrical elevations.
Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
