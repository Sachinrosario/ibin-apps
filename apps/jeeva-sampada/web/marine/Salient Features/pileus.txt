Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum thin upto 1 cm thick, unattached, explanate or arched at the underside forming highly inverted cup or bowls.  A central calyx, 10 to 25 mm in diameter is present around which secondary calices 8 to 12 mm in diameter are concentrically arranged.  Septa 8 to 14 around a calyx, alternating in size, major septa 5 to 7 mm high, continuous from calyx to calyx.  Septal edges dentate, 6 to 8 teeth per cm length of septum.  Under side of the disc highly porous.  Costae represented by rows of spines, more or less 1 mm thick and high at the base.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
