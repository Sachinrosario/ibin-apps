SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        General form slender. Carapace with broadly rounded rostral plate. Antennal sympod with two long and two short spines. Thoracic endopods three to seven with three and five carpopropodal segments. Eighth thoracic endopod in male three segmented, as long as basal segment of exopod, second segment with 3-4 plumose setae; in female endopod longer than basal segment of exopod, straight, apex produced into minute cusp with short seta. Latral border of telson with eleven spines. Distal spines long and sharply pointed, outer pair longer than inner.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)    
Adult male: 9.7 – 11 mm    
Female      : 9.6 – 11.7 mm.
