Salient Features :
A low-branched plant 6-10 in. height, with a woody stem and leafy branches.
Leaves flat, fleshy, glabrous or scarcely puberulous, spathulate-oblong, rounded at the apex, much tapering awt the base into an obscure petiole, which is dilated into an emplexicaul obliquely truncate sheath at the base. Scape somewhat angular, bearing a small subsecund panicle; bracts long elliptic, herbaceous.
Calyx long narrowly infundibuliform, pointed at the base; tube straight.
Petals longer than the calyx, spathulate-oblong, truncate or slightly emarginated at the apex.
Stamens long-exserted.
