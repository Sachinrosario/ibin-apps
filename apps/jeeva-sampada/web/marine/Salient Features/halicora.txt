SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Entrusting with level upper surface.  Corallites polygonal, calices circular or polygonal.  Intercorallite wall fused 1.5 to 2 mm at the top, thicker below.  Greater diameter of the calices 10 to 11 mm, lesser 8-10 mm; depth 3 to 4 mm.  Total number of septa 30 to 33 per calyx of which 18-20 septa reach the columella.  Septal edges dentate, the last tooth on major septa standing vertical and resembling a plus.  Columella 3-4 mm in diameter, formed of twisted trabeculae with upwardly directed compressed, frosted thin plates.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  F. halicora is likely to be the same as F. complanata (see Pillai and Patel, 1988).

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
