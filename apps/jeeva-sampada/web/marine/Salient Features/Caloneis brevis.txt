SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves short, broadly elliptical, with broad, rostrated apices. Valve surface striate, striae radiate and often appearing as though they were indistinctly punctate.

Diagnostic characteristics: Axial area narrow, dilated to form a large orbicular central area.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 46-60m, width 17-21m.
