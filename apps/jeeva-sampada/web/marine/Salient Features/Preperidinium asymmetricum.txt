SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells rather depressed, lens shaped, large left longitudinal list projecting but only reaching about half way across the radius of the cell, edge smooth, not bent over the flagellar pore. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle not displaced, indented. Transverse lists not supported by spines.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 50-89µ.
