Diagnostic characteristics:
Body slender. Carapace produced in front into short triangular rostrum with pointed apex. Eyes large, closely set together, cornea reniform, peduncle with small dorsal papilla. Antrennual scale over reaching antennular peduncle, outer margin straight and smooth terminating in strong spine, distal suture absent. Labrum with short anteromedian spine. Pleopods in male biramous and natatory, pseudobranchial process long and plate-like. Endopod of third and fourth pleopods with long modified terminal seta. Telson entire, 1,5 times as long as broad, lateral margins concave, armed with 11-12 spines, distal margin truncate and armed with four pairs of spines, middle pair short, third pair longest. Uropods longer than telson and setose all round.
