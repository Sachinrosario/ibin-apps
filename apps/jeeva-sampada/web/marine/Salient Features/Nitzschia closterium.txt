SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Free living cells and motile.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves spindles shaped in the middle, ends, extended into long breaks usually slightly bent or curved in opposite directions. Striae not visible.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: 35-154µ long and 3.5-7µ broad.

Biochemical aspects: Obligate Phototroph exhibiting growth on both glucose and acetate. Requires vitamin B12 and Thiamine. Extracellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides.
