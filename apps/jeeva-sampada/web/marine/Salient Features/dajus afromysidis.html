SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Female :
Body almost symmetrical, similar to that of D.siriellae Sars, with a remote resemblance to parasitic copepod. Cephalon considerably bent downwards, placed more or less in the middle of the ventral side of the peraeon. Hypopharynx directed backwards and broadly rounded apically. Maxillipeds projecting as in bopyrids. Peraeon much enlarged, with the lateral aspects swollen to accomadate the eggs. Dorsal side convex and the ventral concave, median part of dorsal side raised to form a broad longitudinal ridge with shallow grooves on either side. Dorsal side with five to six transverse grooves representing segments. Legs five pairs, placed close together in two transverse rows one on either side of the buccal mass. Brood lamellae two pairs. Abdomen comparatively large, with three very faint grooves on the ventral side, almost as long as the thorax, extreme tip abruptly narrow and blunt. Pleopods and uropods absent.

Body white with two dark spots below the cephalaon.

Additional remarks:
The less distinct segmentation of the body and the transverse arrangement of the legs distinguish Dajus afromysides from allied species.

The host Afromysis macropsis occurs in the plankton.
