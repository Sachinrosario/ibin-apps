Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  The holotype kepth in the Zoology Department of the Hessian State Museum, Germany has the appearance of the bovine ear lobe.  The under side of the folium has no calices.  Calices on the upperside average 1 mm in diameter arranged in transverse rows.  Distance between adjacent rows of calices 1 to 2 mm.  calices look like deep punctures, rounded or elongated.  Septo-coastae 16 to 18 around a calyx, some times upto 24, alternately thick and thin, half of the total septa reach the columella.  Septal edges and sides minutely granular.  Columella a compressed style.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
