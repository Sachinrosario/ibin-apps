SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium robust. Attachment to the substratum by tuft of strong, long rootlets. Narrow zigzag lines present on the basal side of zooecia. Zoaria of type 5 branching with biserial zooids. Projecting lateral sides of the zooecia give a serrated margin for the zooidal rows. The distal region of the zooecia raised and hence that region slightly overarches the proximal region of the succeeding one. Basal surface of the zooecium with a pointed or blunt calcareous spine, arising from the proximal region extending up to the distal end. The operculum not distinct. Opesia smaller than the aperture because of the slight development of a smooth cryptocyst proximally. Gymnocyst present proximally the extent of which differs in different zooecia of the same zoarium. Lateral avicularia placed over rounded prominences proximally. Rostrum strongly hooked, upper profile concave and with two cusps. Mandibles with slightly bent tip. No ovicell noticed.Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: Harmer has recorded two kinds of Bugula robusta in his Siboga report based on the colour of the zoarium, the presence or absence of cusps in the avicularian rostrum and the basal calcareous spine. Harmer (1926) states that "the Siboga specimens are of two kinds: (1) a form of a rich red colour (sometimes pale), the avicularia with strong triangular cusps; (2) a pale form, in which the avicularium is usually long and slender, with vestigial cusps or none at all, and the calcareous processes of the basal side of the zooecia are extremely long and narrow". Though in many respects, the present specimens resemble those of Harmers second kind, the development of the cusps in the present form is noteworthy. Regarding the point of origin of the calcareous spine also some difference can be observed. Harmer's figures show the origin of spines from the middle region of the basal side of the zooecium, but in the present specimens, the spines arise from the proximal region. Narrow zooecia with less projected lateral sides are noticed in the region of origin of rootlets in the present specimens. This is in agreement with Harmer's observation in the Siboga material.Meristic characteristics:
