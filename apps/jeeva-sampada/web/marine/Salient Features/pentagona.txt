SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corrallum encrusting, greater spread 12 cm.  Corallites polygonal, penta or hexagonal, larger ones 8-9 mm in greater diameter, calices circular or oval, 5-6 mm in diameter 3-4 mm deep.  Intercorallite wall 3 to 4 mm thick with a middle raised up ridge.  Total number of septa 24-28, of equal width throughout the length, exsert, exsert ends continuous over the wall and stopping at the ridge.  Edges with 8-12 teethc, the last tooth forming a paliform lobe.  Columella poorlty developed, encircled by the paliform ring.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of  CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
