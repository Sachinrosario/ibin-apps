Diagnostic characteristics:
Genus Acanthomysis Czerniavsky
Diagnosis: Antennal scale setose all round, long and narrow with rounded apex. Carpopropodus of thoracic endopods three to eight three segmented; pleopods in female rudimentary, unsegmented plates; in male pleopods one to three and five as in female; fourth pleopod with unsegmented endopod, exopod long, two to three segmented, terminating in two barbed setae. Telson subtriangular, elongated, apex entire, lateral margins with small number of proximal spines and large number of distal ones, arranged regularly or in distinct series, apex with one to two pairs of long spine. Endopod of uropod with spines in region of statocyst.
Acanthomysis indica
Diagnosis: Carapace produced in front into short triangular rostral plate with pointed apex. Antennal scale as long as antennular peduncle. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male reaching middle of sixth abdominal segment, two jointed, terminal joint short, one seventh of length of proximal joint and bearing two long barbed setae. Telson more than twice as long as broad, with proximal group of three spines successively increasing in length and distal group of about thirty spines showing serial arrangement into groups of short spines alternating with long spines, apex of telson with four to six long subequal spines. Endopod of uropod with fout to five spines below statocyst.
