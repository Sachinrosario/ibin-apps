SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:          

In general, the pattern of larval development in P.acclivirostris is similar to that of P.stylifera. The authors could culture only eggs,5 naupliar stages and 3 protozoeal stages. However, the characters are more or less similar to any species of Genus Parapenaeopsis. Rostrum short with 1-3 short dorsal spines; small in size; a minute supraorbital, small antennal and a pterygostomian spines are present; a hepatic spine is absent; dorsomedian spines present on abdominal somites 5 and 6; pereopods small with reduced exopods; antennal scale short but broad,broader proximally than anteriorly; telson with a median spine and 8 pairs of spines.Diagnostic characteristics:The adults of this species is smaller than P.stylifera,but the naupliar and protozoeal stages are larger; there are 3 lateral setae on the antennule in addition to 3 terminal setae in P.acclivirostris and only 3 terminal setae in N1 of P.stylifera. Similarly in N4 the antennal exopod is unsegmented in P.acclivirostris whereas it is segmented in P.stylifera.

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone 
Main food: Plankton, algae,debris with organic matter with sand and mud.
Feeding type: Omnivorous                                                                                                                                  
Additonal remarks:The myses and postlarval stages of this species are not known. 

Size and age:  
Nauplii I-V: length 0.25mm to 0.37mm.                       
Protozoeae I-III: length 0.74mm to 1.61mm.

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed) 6.5cm(George,1969).  

Eggs:  Size 0.32-0.34mm in diameter; spherical in shape; the embryonic massmeasures 0.19mm.
LARVAL STAGES:
Nauplii I-V: 0.25mm to 0.37mm in size; body pear-shaped,ocellus present; 3 pairs of appendages and a caudal furca with a pair of spines present; antennule uniramous and bears(endopod) 3 long setae at its distal end and 3 short setae at the inner margin. Antenna biramous,exopod bearing 2 long setae terminally and 2 short setae on the inner margin; mandible biramous, bearing 3 setae distally,each on exopod and endopod.The setae become plumose in the next stage,the number of setae increases; caudal furca with 2 pairs of spines;  further, increases to 7 in N5, the 4th spine being the longest.Protozoea I: length varies from 0.71mm-0.74mm; the anterior portion gets covered by a carapace; the narrow posterior portion becomes a 6-segmented thorax and an un- segmented abdomen; the naupliar eye persists; eyes sessile; mandible flattened and asymmetrical; maxillae and maxillipeds appear; telson bilobed,not separated from abdomen and bears 7 pairs of spines.Protozoea II: length.1.06mm; carapace develops a rostrum and eyes stalked; abdomen segmented; uropods not developed; telson with 7 spines on each lobe.Protozoea III: size 1.61mm in length; carapace with a rostrum; pereopods with exopods appear; dorsomedian spines present on 1-5 abdominal somites; a pair of dorsolateral spines on somite 5; 6th somite with a pair of posterolateral spines; Uropods present and biramous; exopod longer than endopod; telson separated from abdomen and with a wide gap,each ramus with 8 spines.
