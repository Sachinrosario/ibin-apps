SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells with triangular valvar plane and rounded corners.

Diagnostic characteristics: Cell-wall areolate, areolae rounded, 7-12 in 10µ scattered and of different sizes, frequently groups of areolae separated by a hyaline ring.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Valvar plane sides: 28-125µ.
