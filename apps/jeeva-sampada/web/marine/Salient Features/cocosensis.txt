Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of tufted branches arising from an encrusting base.  Branches 5 to 7 cm long and upto 2 cm broad.  The calices are surrounding by prominent foveolations which form ramparts around the former, sometimes two to three calices are found in single valley.  Distance between top to top of adjacent foveolations 1.5 to 2 mm.  Calices average 1 mm, conspicuous, well cut out from the surrounding coenenchyme by a solid wall.  Septa in two cycles, primaries reach the centre of the fossa or may even fuse over a false columella.  Secondaries turn and often fuse to the primaries as in a poritid pattern.  The coenenchyme under the lens reveals a fine reticulum with minute spines.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
