SALIENT FEATURES:         Ref No:49, 52, 103, 111

 

Morphological:    Carapace smooth. Rostrum armed with 9 to 12 teeth on dorsal and single tooth on ventral margins; adrostral carina and sulcus long, extending almost to posterior margin of carapace, the groove wide; postrostral carina well developed as far back as adrostral sulcus, with a deep medium groove throughout its length;  gastrofrontal carina present; gastrofrontal sulcus bifurcate posteriorily ; hepatic carina almost horizontal to base of antennal crest and from there sloping anteroventrally ; telson armed with 3 pairs of lateral movable spines; no ischial spine on first pereoped. Petasma with distomedian projections reaching to or slightly overhanging distal margins of costae; ventral costae largely broadened apically, unarmed on their free border but incised on their attached border ; outer surface of lateral lobes not tuberculate. Thelycum with lateral plates, their anteromedian angles divergent; anterior process with anterolateral edges raised, forming 2 subtriangular or cylindrical projections; posterior process triangular, its anterior edges raised in lateral ridges delimiting a median depressed area.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum with 9 to 12 dorsal teeth and a single ventral tooth; adrostral carina and sulcus long, extending almost to posterior margin of carapace, postrostral carina with a deep median groove throughout its length reaching as far back as adrostral sulcus; telson armed  with 3 pairs of lateral movable spines; ventral costae of petasma unarmed on their free border but incised on their attached border; thelycum with lateral plates on sternite XIV.

 

Colour: Body pale yellow to brown, not banded dorsally.
