SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        General form slender. Carapace with broadly rounded rostrum., dorso median nodules present. Antennal sympod with two long and two short spines. Thoracic endopods three to seven with four to seven carpopropodal segments. Eighth endopod in male and female shorter than basal segment of exopod. Lateral margin of telson with seven slender spines, distal spines sub equal. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)    

Adult male: 6.6 mm    
Adult female: 8.6 mm
