Diagnostic characteristics: Genus: Illigiella
Body slender. Eyes functionally normal, globular, not depressed dorso-ventrally. Thorax without sternal processes on ventral surface between pairs of thoracic legs. Pseudobranchial process from endopod of pleopod slender. Telson truncate armed on distal margin with pair of plumose setae and two pairs of spines, inner pair very short, lateral margin armed on distal one third with few short spines gradually growing longer posteriorly.

Illigiella brevisquamosa
Body slender. Front margin of carapce produced into broad triangular rostral plate with obtusely rounded apex. Eyes small, sub pyriform, slender papilla on inner distal part of stalk. Antennal scale about two and a half times as long as broad, outer distal spine small, apex of scale produced beyond spine. Carpopropodus of thoracic endopods three jointed. Pleopods of male well-developed, biramous, setae not modified. Telson one and a half times a slong as broad, lateral margins straight tapering to broadly truncate apex, armed on distal one third with five to eight pairs of spines becoming longer posteriorly, apex with median plumose setae and two pairs of spines, inner pair very small. Inner uropod with 2-5 spines along proximal inner margin.
