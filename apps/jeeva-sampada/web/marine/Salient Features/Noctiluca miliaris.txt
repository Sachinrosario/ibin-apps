SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Unarmored, one of the largest Dinoflagellates known. Body usually spherical to slightly reniform. Cingulum indistinguishable. Sulcus deep with longitudinal flagellum short.

Diagnostic characteristics: Posterior tentacle present. Protoplasm radially stranded.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter 200-2000mm.

Biochemical aspect: Accumulates large amounts of ammonia in the vacuole, which may be toxic to fish.
