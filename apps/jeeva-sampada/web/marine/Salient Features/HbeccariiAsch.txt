SALIENT FEATURES OF Halophila beccarii Asch.    

Leaves: 6-10, petiolate, arranged in pseudo-whorl with 2 scales at the base of the whorl; leaves lanceolate,  broadly acute at the tip with entire margin and a prominent midrib without any cross-veins 

Inflorescence: Solitary, axillary flowers

Perianth: Transparent white tepals with darker midrib

Ovary: Female flowers upto 1.7 cm long, ovary oblong with 2-4 ovules

Fruit: Ellipsoid to ovoid with 1-4 seeds

Seeds: Globose, reticulate, hard, reddish brown

Flowering and fruiting: January-March and August-October


Reports on bioactivity: 
	Antibacterial activity (Balasubramanian et al., 2000)
