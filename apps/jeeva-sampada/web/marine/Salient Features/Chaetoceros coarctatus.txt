SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with elliptical valve plane, united in straight chains. Mantle with deep clear ring like furrow. Usually found with a species of Vorticella attached.

Diagnostic characteristics: Outer setae of the end cell different, posterior terminal setae much thicker than the rest, ridged with minute spines.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 35-49m in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
