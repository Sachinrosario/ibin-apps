Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum caespitose or corymbose, perpheral branches prostrate with a pedicellate base.  Some main branches undergo fusion.  underside of the prostrate branches often flat with appressed tubular corallites mixed  with a few verruciform ones.  Branches on the upper side 8 to 10 mm thick 5 to 7 cm long with tapering tips.  Secondary branchlets are often seen.  Axial corallites varies from 2 to 3 mm in diameter average, 2.5 mm.  Septa in two cycles, primaries very prominent.  Radial corallites on the distal parts of the branches prominent, nariform, appressed to spreading, larger one 3 to 4 mm long 1.5 to 2 mm thick.  Mixed with are large verruciform and subimmersed corallites.  Radials have two cycles of septa.  Wall of the radials echinulate, surface coenenchyme echinulate.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The species is characterized by peripheral prostrate branches; upper branches often tapering.  Radials on the distal parts of the branches mostly of uniform size.  It is very common in Andaman and Nicobar Islands.  A. secale is very near to this species but has larger corallites.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
