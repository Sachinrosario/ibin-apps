SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:   

Diagnostic characteristics:
      The rostrum  bears a pair  of short spines  directed ventrally . A small cervical  groove crosses the dorsal  aspect  of the cephalon. The posterior  thoracic margin is produced slightly  backwards and is rounded. The genital aperture is not  markedly  prominent and is situated about the middle  of the segment; a row  of fine   hairs runs  transversely just in front of the aperture. The 2nd  furcal  seta is longer  and slightly thicker than the  others; its length is about  twice  that  of the others.
     The  Ist antenna  over –reaches the furca by about the last  three segments.
     The Ist  segment  is partly fused  with  the 2nd. The segments bear long delicate setae.
     In the 2nd antenna the two rami are nearly  equal  in length. The endopod  bears  6 setae on the outer lobe and 3 large and 4 small setae on the inner lobe; these latter small setae decrease in size from without inwards, the inner most seta being  minute. The exopod  is composed  of eight segments; segment 1 bears  a small delicate seta, segment 2  a seta of moderate  length, segments 3 to 6 each  bears a long seta, segment 7  a minute seta, and segment 8 , 3 large  and 1 small setae.
In the mandible  the biting  ramus is strong ,  but the edge is considerably  more  oblique than figured  by  Sars;  the arrangement of  the teeth  is , however , the same. The palp is rather  small and the basal  segment is slender; the endopod reaches to about  the end  of the 3rd  segment  of the exopod  and is composed  of two segments, the distal bearing 3 delicate setae. The exopod is composed  of 5 segments, segments 1and 2 are with out  setae, segments 3 and 4  each bear 1, and  segment 5 carries 2. 
The 1st maxilla bears 11 setae on first inner lobe, one large setae on the second inner lobe, one small setae on the third inner lobe and two setae on the second basal.  Exopod bears 4 setae on the outer margin and two large and one small distally.  Outer lobe bears six large and three small setae.   
The 2nd maxilla is well developed and bears  setae on  the various lobes.
The maxilliped  is also  well developed and bears lobe 1, 1 seta; lobe 2,3; lobe 3,3; lobe 4,2; lobe 5,2 of which   one is large; the  endopod  bears on segment 1,2 small and 2 large; segment 2,3 small and 1 large; segments 3 and 4 , 2 small and  1 large; and segment 5, 1 small and 1 large; all the large setae on lobe  5  and the endopod are provided  with “buttons”.
     In the Ist leg, the marginal spine on exopod 1 is large and reaches well beyond the end  of the ramus; the marginal spines on exopod 2 and 3 are  small  and delicate.
     The 5th  leg also agrees exactly  with Sars  figure.
The  only  real difference  between  this specimen and Sars ‘ examples  is the loss of one seta on the  2nd basal segment of the 1st maxilla.
