SALIENT FEATURES:             Ref No:115

 

Morphological:   Rostrum armed with 5 to 8 (generally 7) dorsal teeth, sigmoid-shaped, distally

edentate in females and young males, much shorter and devoid of edentate portion in adult males; postrostral crest almost reaching to posterior margin of carapace, feebly grooved; longitudinal suture extending to 3/4 of carapace, antennular flagella 0.5 times length of carapace in females and 0.66 times carapace in males; epipods present on 1st and 2nd pereopods; basial spine well developed on 1st pereopod in both sexes, much smaller on 2nd pereopod in females and absent on 2nd pereopod in males; telson unarmed. Petasma with small distomedian projections, parallel and directed anteriorly; distolateral projections short, fang-like  and directed laterally. Thelycum with anterior plate wider than long, concave, evenly rounded anteriorly beset with long setae; posterior plate with its rounded anterolateral conical lobes overlapping posterolateral region of median plate, and bearing 3 groups of long setae.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Rostrum armed with 5 to 8 dorsal teeth; basial spine on 2nd pereopod

much smaller in female and absent in male; distomedian projections of petasma small, parallel and directed anteriorly, distolateral projections fang-like and directed laterally; anterior plate of thelycum semicircular, its posterolateral region overlapped by anterolateral conical lobes of posterior plate.

        Colour: Body of large females reddish or purplish brown with darker transverse bands on

                  hind margin of carapace and 1-5 abdominal segments. Body of males and young

                  females olive green and brownish.
