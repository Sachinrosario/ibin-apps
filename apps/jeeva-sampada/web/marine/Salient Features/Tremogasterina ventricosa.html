SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium encrusting. Young zooecia distinct, separated by interjunctural pores, which get obscured as a result of calcification. Convex front tuberculated with small reniform pores. Aperture elongate oval. Thin peristome with three spines. Avicularia large with rounded tips. Mandibles with a sub-marginal sclerite. Ovicells not noticed.
Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   HemaphroditeDescriptive characters: This form resembles the description and figures of Tremogasterina ventricosa Canu and Bassler. However, certain differences are discernible as follows:-
  Only a few zooecia in the present instance have mucros, which are rather inconspicuous. The number of peristomial spines here is three where as Canu and Bassler found them to be five in their specimens. Frontal pores vary in number from 4 to 6. The avicularia are all with rounded tips and are arranged on either side of the orifice. In the whole colony a single large avicularium is noticed.
  Discussing the range of variation in this species Canu and Bassler state that the mucro is inconstant and may often be wanting. The number of frontal pores varies from one zooecium to another; the beak of the avicularium may be pointed or rounded. Their general comment about this species is that "this is a very variable species". On the basis of these observations the difference noticed in the present material are considered as falling within the range of variations of this species.
