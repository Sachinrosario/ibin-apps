SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Diagnostic characters: Second dorsal spine elongated, terminally flexible, nearly equal to height of body or longer; second anal spine similar but shorter. Upper half of body with indistinct grey-brown vertical bands (fresh and preserved condition) descending up to a little beyond lateral line.  In between lateral line and median line of body variable (usually 4) number of few big oval yellow blotches present in addition to a few smaller blotches of the same colour.

Sex attributes: No external sexual dimorphism present.

Descriptive characters: Body compressed, ovate and deep. Back more strongly arched than anterior part of belly. Dorsal profile with two convexities, first in front of front margin of eye, 2nd opposite to hind margin of eye. From the posterior concavity the dorsal profile is elevated behind occiput  to the origin of the spinous dorsal fin. Mouth horizontal and when protracted forms a tube with downward direction. Gape of  mouth when closed opposite and below lower margin of eye. Tip of maxilla just reaching the front margin of the eye. Mandibles slightly concave inferiorly. Teeth small and numerous. Inter-orbital space almost flat, bounded by two prominent ridges which continue as the outer borders of the nuchal spine. Central portion of the nuchal spine is elevated. A pair of spines above the anterior superior angle of the orbit, one nearer the margin of eye being more prominent than the one away from the margin of the eye. Pre-opercular with an obtuse angle, its lower margins finely serrated. 
Scales all over body except chest. Lateral line very slightly concave at commencement and convex thereafter, but less so when compared to the dorsal profile, and extends up to a little distance short of the base of caudal fin. Dorsal and anal fin spines moderately strong, flexible, laterally compressed. 2nd dorsal spine , filiform its tip extending up to the origin of 6-16th dorsal ray whwn flexed backward. 2nd anal spine elongate. 3rd and 4th dorsal spines and the 3rd anal spine  serrated anteriorly for about half length from base. Ventrals with prominent axillary scale (its tip reaching the  tips of the innermost rays). Ventrals does not reach the origin of anals. Caudal fin deeply forked.Caudal peduncle as high as long.     
  
Colour: Abdomen and  back silvery. Upper half with indistinct grey-brown vertical bands (fresh and preserved condition) descending up to a little beyond lateral line. In between lateral line and median line of body variable ( usually 4) no of few big oval yellow blotches present in addition to a few smaller blotches of the same colour. Inner side of pectoral base dotted black. Spinous anal fin with faint yellow colouring, continued marginally along rays. Caudal fin dusky.           (Ref no: 4,5,11)

Meristic Characters: D.VIII, 16; A.III,14; V.I,5; Ll. 62-65; Gill Rakers. 3-4+1+14

 Feeding Habit: 
Main food: It feeds mainly on polychaetes, small crustaceans and small fishes  Ref no: 5
Juveniles of 3-4 cm feed on copepods and other small crustaceans.      Ref no: 12
Feeding type: Carnivorous and mainly bottom feeding habit.
