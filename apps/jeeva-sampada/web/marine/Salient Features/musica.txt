Descriptive characters:  The genus Tubipora is monotypic with the single species musica.  Corallum composed of cylindrical vertical tubes connected by horizontal platforms.  Skeletal is internal and compose of fused spicules.  The tubes carry the polyps, each with eight feather-like tentacles.

The skeleton is permanent dark-red colour.
