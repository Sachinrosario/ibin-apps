SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca broadly conical with long cylindrical apical horn in dorsal view. Hypotheca broadly conical. Girdle in side view, at right angles to longitudinal axis dextral, excavated, winged with radial lists. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Two posterior spines with broad wings, that of ventral one broader and extending laterally covering base.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 51µ; breadth: 47µ.
