
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:         

FEMALE: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Prosome with a crest, which is characteristic in shape in a lateral view. Postero- lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment end in a small pointed spine. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment swollen on the ventral side. Rostrum with 2 stout short spines, which are shorter than the notch of the rostrum. In 5th leg the terminal joint is with a short stout apical spine and a long sub apical spine.  The sub apical spine with 2 rows of spinules at the distal two-third length and the arrangement of these spines on the outer margin of the sub apical spine as its tip gives the shape of a V as figured by Scott.
