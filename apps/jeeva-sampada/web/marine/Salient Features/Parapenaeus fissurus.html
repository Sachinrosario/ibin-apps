SALIENT FEATURES:         Ref No:18,49,52,64,83,144

 

Morphological:  Rostrum sigmoid, extending nearly to end of antennular peduncle in female, but

shorter in male, armed with 6-8 dorsal teeth; carapace with moderately distinct post-rostral crest, small branchiostegal spine forming the antero-ventral angle of carapace, longitudinal and transverse sutures present, the former extending almost to posterior border; 6th abdominal somite less than twice length of 5th and subequal to telson; 5th leg extending to middle of antennal scale. Petasma with process – a on distoventral margin of lateral lobe prominent, bifurcate and directed laterally. Thelycum pentagonal in outline, made up of closely set anterior, intermediate, and posterior plates, anterior plate deltoid in outline with rounded apex.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Carapace with small branchiostegal spine at anteroventral angle, 6th

abdominal somite less than twice length of 5th, 5th pereopod extending to middle of antennal scale.

 

 

        Colour:   In live condition body is salmon-red, cornea of eyes dark maroon.

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:   In male rostrum is shorter, antennular flagella longer and eye larger

           than in female.
