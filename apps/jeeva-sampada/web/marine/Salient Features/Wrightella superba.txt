SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  fan shape and not truly reticulate. The nodes are 2 mm in length and the internodes are 18 mm long. The branching is irregularly dichotomous and the branches are round in cross section. The calyces are closely arranged laterally as well as on the surface, leaving the posterior face of the colony conspicuously bear.  A typical calyx is 0.8 mm in height and 1.3 mm in diameter. The polyps are heavily speculated with a collaret composed of three or four rows of strong tuberculate spindles above which a pair on each tentacle base form a point by the meting of the distal ends. The tentacle bears a triangular area of spicules, some of which are thorny (stachelplatten). 
		Spicules: Calyces and coenenchyma are armed on the surfaces with ball-like leafy clubs. Besides, there are a number of ordinary spindles, clubs, bent spindles and variously branched forms.
 
Colour		:The entire colony is brilliant orange, polyps and calyces spicules are bright crimson. Polyps are white in colour.
