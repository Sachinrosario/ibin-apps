SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells in straight chains with narrow aperture.

Diagnostic characteristics: Terminal setae strongly divergent, thicker than the rest with spines arranged spirally. Chromatophore only one and plate like.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 8-18m in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
