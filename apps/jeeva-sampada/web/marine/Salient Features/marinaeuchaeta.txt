Diagnostic characteristics: -                   
Female:  Maxilla 1 inner lobe 1 with one anterior surface seta, three posterior surface setae, and seven spines along edge, exopod with 11 setae, outer lobe 1 with five setae. Maxilliped endopod segment 1 with five setae. Genital segment on dorsal view with anterior left side projection, right side with large dorsal most lateral projection not obscuring more ventral projection that extends from right genital pad. In ventral view genital field with genital valve region almost completely uncovered by genital pads, gap between right and left pads about as wide as right pad itself.                  
Male:  Leg 5 exopod segment with inner distal corner of lamella, which is bordered by very small teeth, separated by smooth excavation from coarser outer edge teeth. Thick spines arising at base of serrate lamella more than half-length of lamella. Thin – skinned lobe on distal part of left exopod segment 3 arises at base of terminal spiniform process.
