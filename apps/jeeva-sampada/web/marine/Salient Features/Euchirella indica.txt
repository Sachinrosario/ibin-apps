
SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteristics:       
Female: General body shape robust and resembles E.messinensis. Prosome without crest. Genital segment in dorsal view asymmetrical, produced on left dorsal side to form distinct elevation, which extends slightly over urosomal segment 2. Antenna 2 like that of E. messinensis. (Vervoort 1949).      
Male: Prosome with distinct low crest. Legs 5 like that of E.messinensis except left leg reaches beyond right leg endopod first thickening.

Additional remarks:       Tanaka and Omori (1969a) consider that E. indica is a subspecies of E. messinensis. As Vervoort (1963) indicated that females of E. messinensis he examined showed no variability in the dorsal sac on the posterior left side of the genital segment and the male leg 5 is different from that limb in E.messinensis The male described by Vervoot 1949, as E.messinensis also seems to belong to E. indica. The fifth leg Vervoort (1949) figures as E. indica seems to have proportions similar to that he identifies as E. bella.
