SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:
Body laterally compressed, ovate, short, with small deciduous scales, eyes lateral, gill opening moderately wide, lateral line complete, mouth small, jaws with single series of teeth, esophageal teeth present, dorsal fin large and single, spinous portion embedded in the skin. Caudal forked.


Diagnostic characteristics:
5-10 blade like spines in front of dorsal and anal fins; lower jaw slightly protruding, gill opening a vertical slit, dorsal and anal fin falcate; caudal deeply forked, lower lob longer; scales cycloid and deciduous; coloration upper surface gray, sides and head silvery tending to white on abdomen, small black dots all over. The young are dark in colour with vertical fins almost black and the lower lob of the caudal longer than in adult.
