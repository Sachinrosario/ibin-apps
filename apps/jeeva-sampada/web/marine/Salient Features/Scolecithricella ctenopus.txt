
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:       

Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segment fused. Line of fusion in both cases clearly visible.Postero- lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment produced into small pointed spines. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment swollen on the ventral side. First antenna 23- segmented, extends up to the posterior end of the abdomen. Fifth leg with 2 free joints. The terminal segment ends in a strong spine, with an inner marginal spine, which has got spinnules on the outer margin. Both the joints are provided with spinnules on the surface and are slightly cylindrical as against the usual plate- like nature in the genus.     

Male: Prosome and 1st pedigerous segment fused. Fourth and 5th pedigerous segments separate. Postero-lateral corners of the last thoracic segment not pointed. First antenna 20- segmented. Fifth leg with characteristic shape. Right leg very small. Left leg is very long and slender, without endopod, distal segment with 2 apical spines, of which outer one is long and curved. Distal segment bent at the middle and the proximal portion with a row of spines, which is comb- like.

Maximum length (cm) (male/ female/ unsexed)                                               
Female:  1.45 mm.     Male:      1.53 mm
