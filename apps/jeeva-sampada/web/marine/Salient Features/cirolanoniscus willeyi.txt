Salient features

Morphological:
Diagnosis:  Female-Body clongate-oblong and saccular, ventral side flat and dorsal convex, segmentation absent.  Ventral side with a deep median longitudinal groove, anteriorly passing around a globular head lobe with which the animal remains attached to the host.  Brood lamellae one pair, with three to four grooves starting from the ventral groove and going outwards.
Male-Body narrow and long-oval, eyes present.  Basal expansion of antennule broad, cut into large blunt teeth.  Coxal plates conspicuously pectinate.  Antennae long, peduncular segments transversely furrowed, flagellum five segmented.  Outer ramus of uropod shorter than linner.

Species characters:

Female.  Body sac-like, clongate cylindrical and completely filled with embroys, transparent but made opaque by the embryos.  Ventral side with a deep longitudinal groove extending from the hind end to one third the distance from the anterior end, further on running lateralwards thus demarcating a roughly circular cephalic lobe.  Ventral groove with four pairs of faint lateral grooves extending sideways.  Dorsal side convex.  Appendages absent.  A single pair of large brood lamellae present.
Male.  Shape of body long-ovate, cryptoniscan, cephalon, peracon and pleorn segments distinct.  Cephalon broader than long and anteriorly rounded, peracon segments increasing in breadth up to the sixth, first segment equal to sixth and seenth, succeeding segments narrower.  Telson slightly more than twice as broad as long, which a prominent posteromedian projection.  Eye dark, somewhat pear-shaped.
Basal segment of first antenna distally cut into four blunt teeth, rami two-segmented, inner ramus longer than outer, both tipped with long setae, three on outer and five on inner; lower border of basal plate expanded and segment stout, transversely grooved, its distal corners acute, segments two and three narrower, with one and two setae respectively, flagellum six-segmented, reaching beyond the middle of the fourth peraeon segment, first segment stout second and third short, fourth to sixth slender and long, all the segments with setae.  Ventral border of coxal plates irregularly toothed, the teeth becoming successively smaller backwards.  First peraeopod shortest, sventh longest, basis of first long, ischium dorsally bulged, merus and carpus small, propodus elongate-triangular, with three stout teeth on its lower distal part, dactylus subchelate, about half as long as the propodus.   Seventh peraeopod slender and long, basis subequal to propodus, dactylus long and slender, nearly straight.  Basal segment of uropod stout, rami unjointed, inner ramus one and a half times as long as outer, outer with three and inner with four long setae.

body white, with dark-brown patches.

Larva. Body ovate, anteriorly broader.  Gephalon as long as telson, peraeon and pleon segments subequal in length.  Eyes absent.  Second antenna with long apical setae.  Peduncle of uropod very stout, with a long outer seta, rami short, subequal, outer with three and inner with four.

Additional remarks:  Description of ciralanoniscus willeyi is based on a single female, with the male found within the brood pouch of cirolana willeyi, collected at Neendakara (Quilon, Kerla).  Holotyp, female is deposited in the indian museum, Kolkatta.
