SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming straight chains. Apertures high, elliptical and square to rectangular. Setae thin, basal part parallel to chain axis and then perpendicular to chain axis, far outer part usually bent toward one chain end.

Diagnostic characteristics: Terminal setae different from the others almost parallel in broad girdle view and more diverging in narrow girdle view.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 11-24µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
