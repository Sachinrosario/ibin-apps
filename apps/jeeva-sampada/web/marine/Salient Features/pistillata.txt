Descriptive characters:  A details of the species is based on specimens from Lakshadweep.  Corallum semi-arborescent.  Branches dividing repeatedly.  Tips obtuse or flattened (forma mordx) to about 50 cm.  Thickness 6 to 12 mm.  Calices rounded, crowded, touching each other to a diameter apart.  Primary cycle of septa very prominent, project from the top of the wall and then descent vertically down to meet the columella.  Second cycle sometimes seen but always rudimentary.  Columella styliform, very prominent.  Bottom of the calyx looks smooth that is without any spine.  The upper half of the calicular rim is provided with a hood.  The walls of the hoods striated and spiny.  Coenenchymal surface spiny.  Coenosteum in section dense.  Living coral yellowish brown.  Polyp not found expanded during day time.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
