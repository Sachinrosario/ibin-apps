
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:             

Female: Prosome and 1st pedigerous segment fused, line of fusion visible laterally. Forehead with a lens like organ at the base of the rostrum. 4th and 5th pedigerous segments separate. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment asymmetrical, each side drawn out into a stout curved spine with a distinct tooth at the base. Spine on the left side, longer than the spine on the right side. Urosome 4-segmented. Genital segment with a ventral backwardly directed protuberance. Posterior margin of 2nd and 3rd urosomal segments with fine spines. First antenna 23-segmented. Leg 5 absent.          

Male: Prosome and 1st pedigerous segment fused, with the line of fusion visible laterally. 4th and 5th pedigerous segment separate. Forehead with lens like organ. Postero-lateral corners of last pedigerous segment symmetrical with stout curved spines. Urosome 5-segmented. Posterior margin of 2nd, 3rd and 4th urosomal somite with fine spines. Fifth leg as illustrated in the figure. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                        Female :  3.15mm     
Male     :  2.95mm                                         
Ref. No.:             T.C.Gopalakrishnan,1973
