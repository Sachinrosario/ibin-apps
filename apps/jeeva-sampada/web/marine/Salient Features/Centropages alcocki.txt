SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        
Male:  Urosome four segmented, tenth, eleventh, and 12th  segments of right antennule, each with a curved spine. Fifth legs typical of the genus.     

Female:  Last metasome segment with its postero-lateral corners rounded, but a minute backwardly directed spine visible on each side in lateral view.  Urosome three segmented, genital segment with a tuft of spine like bristles on either side.  Inner spine of second exopod segment of fifth leg short and slightly curved, with two rows of very minute spines.

Additional  remarks:        C. aclocki shows a close resemblence to  C.trispinosus.

Size and age: 
Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     Female:  1.1 – 1.4 mm     
Male   :   1.0 – 1.2 mm

Ref. No.:Kasturirangan, 1963.
