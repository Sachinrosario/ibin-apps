Diagnostic characteristics:
This species has a compact appearance. The length of head exceeds that of the pereon by approximately 1/3 the length of the body. The rostrum is wide and at the base of the head there is a weakly pronounced dorsal depression, barely exceeding the length of the first segment of the perion
This species is closely affiliated with S. challengeri and especially to S.porcella. It is distinguished from the former by the absence of a distal acute-angled projection of article 2 of pereopod VI; from the latter by the structure of articles 5 and 6 of pereopods II, the palmar region of which has coarse, sharp teeth. None of the known species of Streetsia have a similar structure for pereopods II. A characteristic feature of S. palmaspinosa is a well-pronounced stepped notching along the lower edge of epimere III.
