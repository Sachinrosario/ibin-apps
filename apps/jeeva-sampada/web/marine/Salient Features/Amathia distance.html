Morphological: Zoarium repent with slender rootlet-like tubes which attach the zoarium at places to the substratum. Zoarium light brown in colour. Long internodes, with length varying from 2mm to 2.2mm. The diameter of the internodes range between 100 and 160. Biserial zooecia arranged in spiral groups at the distal part of the internodes. More than three fourth of the internode without zooecia. Branching more or less dichotomous. At the distal end of the internode just above the region where the zooecia end, a diaphragm is present. Less conspicuous joint present near the diaphragm from where the branching takes place. Zooecia not connate completely, the distal portion being free from the stem. Orifice terminal and more or less quadrangular. Tentacles eight in number. Diagnostic characters: . Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:  The diameter of internode showed a range of 100 to 160, whereas Harmer found it to be "about 100 only, even though Busk (1886) noted a diameter up to 160. The length of the zooecium was greater in present specimens often between 500 and 700. In the majority of cases the zooecia did not form a complete whorl around the internode. At the base of the zooecium a transparent rounded portion was noticed, through which the funicular strand passed into the zooid. The zooids at the tips of the branches were completely connate in those specimens. This character seemed to be different from what Busk observed in his specimens (pl. 7, fig. 1a). Even though no definite joints of branches have been noticed, thin chitinous lines were observed in the region of the diaphragm. Harmer (1915) states that "there are no definite joints in the region of the diaphragms, although the stem may be somewhat constricted, a short distance on the distal side of a diaphragm, both of the main stem and at the base of the branch". The circular striations noticed in the case of old zooecia by Harmer was present in the present material also. Numerous empty zooecia were noticed with the apertures closed.Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: Main food: Plankton / detritus
