SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells disc shaped, valves flat. Central area large. Areolae very near the margin about 5 in 10m; then become large 2 in 10µ, forming a dark broad band. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Chamber opening distinct only near the periphery of the valve. Outer membrane in the marginal region punctate. Two small asymmetrical processes present on the valve.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 476-532m.
