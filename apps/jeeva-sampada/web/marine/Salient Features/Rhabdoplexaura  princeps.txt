SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:Colony branching is thick and reticulate. The ends of the branches are short. The polyps stand on all sides and closely packed. Their calyces are very low. 

Spicules: The spicules of the bark are shor and oval (Fig. b) and long, stick-shaped (Fig. a) measuring 1.3 mm long which are smooth and finely thorned. 
 
Colour	:	The colony is grayish black in colour.
