SALIENT FEATURES OF Cymodocea serrulata (R.Br.) Asch. & Magnus 1870

Leaves: 2 - 5, in each branch; leaf sheaths broadly triangular 

Inflorescence: Flowers solitary, terminal and become lateral in due course due to the production of successive lateral shoots 

Perianth: No organized tepals 

Ovary: Globose 

Fruits: 2, ellipsoid, 4-angular, rarely an immature fruit is seen on each developed fruit 

Flowering and fruiting: During March - April and September - October
