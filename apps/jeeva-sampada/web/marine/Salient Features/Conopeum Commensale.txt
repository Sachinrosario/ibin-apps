SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:  Encrusting.  Colonies brownish in hue.  Zooecia arranged quincuncially, separately by distinct calcareous lines.  Gymnocyst present, occasionally with one or two proximal tubercles.  Cryptocyst narrow, furnished with minute calcareous spinules extending into the opesia.  Marginal spines distinctly present, mural in character.  Frontal membrane studded with very minute, transparent spinules arranged uniformly.  Operculum with a thick marginal sclerite.  No avicularia or ovicells.  
Diagnostic characters:  The presence of spinules on the frontal membrane is an important character of specific distinction between the species C. reticulum and C. commensale.
Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters:  The presence of spinules on the frontal membrane is an important character, which was observed in the specimens collected by Menon also.  The marginal spines being very delicate are easily lost during handling of the material and many zooecia of even the same colony may not possess them.  The cryptocystal characters resemble very much those of Electra crustulenta.  Proximal tubercles, which are shown in Osburn's (1950) figures are also present in many zooecia in the specimens collected by Menon.
