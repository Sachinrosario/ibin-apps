SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  Attached to the substratum by the long, delicate and transparent portion of the zooecium.  The tubular annulated peristome gradually expands to a non-annulated region with the frontal membrane and distally placed operculum.  The terminal part of the peristome slightly bent.  The rim of the operculum slightly thickened.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  

 

Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite

 

Descriptive characters: Waters (1913) had stated that he noticed ovicells "in a large number, perhaps hundreds".  In the material recorded by Menon, however, no ovicell was noticed.  The part of the zooecia effecting attachment with the substratum was clear, almost transparent and very delicate.  No lateral buds were noticed.
