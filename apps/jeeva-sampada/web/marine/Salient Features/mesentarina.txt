SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting, edges free, thickened towards the centre forming a hump.  Corallites 2.5 to 3 mm in diameter, level or thickened and projecting to 1.5 mm.  Touching each other to 3 mm apart.  Depth about 1 mm.  Septa 20, subequal, narrow projecting only to less than half of the radius circle.  A few of the septa look like bifurcated at the periphery of the wall and merge with the surrounding coenenchyme.  Septal edges entire, sides smooth.  Interseptal loculli wider than septum.  Columella prominent, projecting, filling the bottom of the fossa, spongy.  Surface coenenchyme with a fine system of ridges and furrows.  Ridges with minute spines.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
