SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves lanceolate with bluntly rounded ends, more or less constricted. Raphe straight, axial area very narrow.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Central area widened and connected to two small half- lanceolate areas together farming an H-shaped figure.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 19-21m long and 10m broad.
