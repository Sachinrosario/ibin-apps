SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: A wader with orange-red short legs and short bill.

Diagnostic characteristics: Conspicuous white chin and throat. Winter plumage is dark brown-and-white above. Summer plumage (breeding) shows black band from head to eye.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Turns over algae and pokes under stones and keeps moving along waves for intetidal micro-invertebrates.

Main Food: Marine micro-invertebrates.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Nests in Artic coasts.
