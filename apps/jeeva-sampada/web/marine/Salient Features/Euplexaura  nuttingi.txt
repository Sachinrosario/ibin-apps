SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony reticulate and irregularly branched. The polyps are 1 mm apart and fully retractile. The coenenchyma is thicker and the operculum is raised high. 
	
Spicules: The coenenchyma consists of small and thick spindles  and also with double spindles with regularly arranged belts of high warts.
 
Colour	:	The colony is light brown and the axis is dark brown in colour.
