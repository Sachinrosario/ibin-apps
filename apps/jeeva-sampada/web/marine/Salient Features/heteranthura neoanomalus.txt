Salient features:

Morphological:

Generic characters:  Eyes present, antennular flagellum 6-articulate; antennal flagellum 4-articulate.  Mouth parts somewat produced anteriorly.  Mandible columnar, lacking palp, lacinia and molar.  Maxilliped slender-elongate.  7-segmented, lacking endite.  Pereonites 1-6 each with mid-dorsal pit.  Pereopods 1-3 subchelate; pereopods 4-7 with carpus more or less rectangular, not underriding propodus.  Pleonites 1-6 free, pleopod 1 ramifused, operculiform.

Species characters:  Body clongate and narrow; integument indurate; pereonites 1, 2, 3, subequal; perconites 1 and 4 equal; pereonite 5 slightly longer than and pereonite 6 slightly shorter than pereonite 4, pereonite 7 shortest of all.  Cephalon with brodly rounded anterior margin, rostrum low and rounded.

Antennule clongate-slender, peduncle 4-articulate, articles 1 and 2 subequal, article 3 about thrice the length of the basal article, article 4 slightly longer than basal article, flagellum 4- articulate.

Antenna little longer than antennule, peduncle 3-articulate, the basal article longest.  Flagellum 5-articulate.

Mouth parts, when viewed laterally, clongate and drawn out antero-dorssally beyond anterior margin of cephalon beneath shield-like upper lip.

Mandible lacking palp, strongly indurate, armed distally with 3-4 strongly and few small selerolized cusps.  Maxilla elongate, induate, distally strongly curved, armed with one very strong and 4 smaller spines.  Maxilliped very clongate-slender, about 10 times as long as wide, 6-articulate, terminal article tiny.

Perteopod 1 uniquis, 3/4 length of dactylus, propodus clongated, palm straight, unarmed except for sensory spines.  Peropods 2-7 gradually increase in length, percopods 2-7 subsimilar.

Plconites free, subequal, each with prominent plumose seta latterally.  The exopod and endopod of pleopod 1 not fused, both provided with two plumose scetae each, not operculiform.

Telson shows fine serration on parallel sides, the posterior margin rounded and exhibitsa dentation, 8 in number, from between two consecutive ones arises a prominent plumose seta.  Setae six in number, a few simple setae also present, at the mix-apical region plumose seta present.

Uropodal exopod folds dorsally over telson, bipartite consisting of clongate, spike-like, basally irregular dorsal part, and a rounded ventral part at the apex of which a very long plumose seta is given off on either side, these exceed the posterior margin of telson: endopod oval, distally rounded, margin irregular or finely serrated, extending almost to or up to posterior margin of telson.

Additional remarks:  Holotype one female and paratypes two females are kept in the department of zoology, Andhra University Visakhapatanam, to be deposited in teh collections of the Zoological Survey of India, Kolkatta.

Heteranthura Neoanomalus Resembles H.Anomala in several characters, but differes from the latter in the segmentation of Antennules and antenna; in the number of spines in maxilla and number of sclerotised cusps of mandible; the length of dactylus of peraepod 1 not being fused; and exopod of uropod having a very long plumose seta and its endopod in not being acute; and in the nature of the posterior margin of telson which is dented and not serrated.

Size and age:  Length of female: 9:0 mm; breadth : 2.0 mm
