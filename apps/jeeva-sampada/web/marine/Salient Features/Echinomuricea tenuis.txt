SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony is delicate and branched in one plane, the diagonal leaf-nerves are heavy ad form a net-like appearance. The polyps are disposed over the whole surface with distance between them being 1-2 mm. Their spindles are 0.25 mm long and slightly thorned. Their calyces are 0.5 mm high, 0.5 mm broad. The operculum is weakly developed and flat.  The coenenchyma is moderately thick and the verrucae are almost hemispherical.
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are 0.3 to 0.425 mm length, with short, smooth or slightly warted up to 0.22 mm long. There are also scale-like spindles measuring 0.35 mm long with smooth surface and irregular edges. Irregularly crosses measuring 0.1 mm long spindles are also common in this species. 



Colour		:	Colony is  brown  and polyps are white in colour.
