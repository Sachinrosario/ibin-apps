Morphological:


Diagnostic characteristics:
Female: Prosome bearing short terminally rounded apical process. Rostral filaments long, hairy. Sides of terminal pedigerous segment ending in long spikes. Genital segment swollen laterally, in proximal part with two ventral elevations. Caudal rami of equal length and width. Anal segment bearing on each side a blunt conical process projecting beyond base of outer lateral bristle of caudal rami. Antennules 23-jointed, protruding beyond end of caudal armi. Fifth pair of legs 4-jointed; distal joint with three apical bristles of unequal length; inner bristle longest, three times as long as the outer, which lies apically on the joint. Al three free joints of the leg are nearly equal in length.

Male: Similar to female in structure, but with much shorter and blunter spikes of the last pedigerous segment. Right antennule geniculate. Right leg of 5th pair longer than the left; second and third joints of right leg bearing spines on inner margin; second joint of the left leg with massive process along the outer margin. Both legs of 5th pair 3-jointed.
