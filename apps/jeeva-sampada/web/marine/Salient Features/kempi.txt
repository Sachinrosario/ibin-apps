SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Body slender. Hind border of carapace deeply emarginate  with a pair of small triangular lobes, directed inwards and backwards, margin of carapace behind lobes with deep notch. Endopod of third pleopod of male seven segmented, exopod eight segmented, last segment with two stout barbed spines, distal spine two and a half times as long as other. Lateral border of telson with eight long spines, distal ones alternating with groups of small spines. Endopod of uropod with twelve spines.                           

Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Adult male and female : 7-9 mm
