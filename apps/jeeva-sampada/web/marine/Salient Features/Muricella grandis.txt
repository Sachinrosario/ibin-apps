SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 Colony roughly flabellate, 24.5 cm in height and with a spread of 18 cm. The branch is forked about 3 cm above its origin. The main branches are more or less flattened. The individual calyces are conical in shape attaining a height of 2.1 mm and a diameter of 3 mm. The calyx walls are filled with vertically disposed spindles. The polyps are completely bare and retractile. The collaret is formed of red spindles, arranged en chevron over the tentacles.
		
Spicules: The spicules are all tuberculated

Colour		:	Colony is deep crimson.
