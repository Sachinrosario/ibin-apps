Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive, meandroid.  Uninterrupted valleys upto 8 cm long.  They are 2.5 to 3 mm wide about 2 mm deep.  Collines thin, 0.5 mm to 1 mm thick at the summit, upto 2 mm below.  Septa subequal, or larger and smaller ones alternating.  Sixteen to 18 septa present per cm length of the coralline of which almost all reach the columella.  Septa exsert and continuous over the collines, edges with 2-5 teeth.  Columella lamellar, continuous, rarely broken.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
