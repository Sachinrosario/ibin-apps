Salient features:
Morphological:

	Compared with the females, the males are small. First antenna similar in both sexes and the second antenna in the female reaching the posterior border of the seventh peraeon segment. All the coxal plate long and produced backwards. Telson laterally convex, its truncated apex being one third the maximum width, crenulate and provided with two pairs of widely separated spines, each crenulation with a seta. Dorsal side of telson with a deep crescentic proximal median groove and a shallow but distinct groove on either side of it.
	Penultimate segment of the antennal peduncle as broad as long, with ten to thirteen long setae. Exopod of uropod with three minute spines on its outer apex, the outer smallest; endopod also with three spines, of which the middle one is the largest.
	Body white, with a grayish tint due to the presence of vermicular dark lines on the pleon and four equidistant rows of chromatophores on the peraeon.
