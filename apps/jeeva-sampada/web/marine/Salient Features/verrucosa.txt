Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum bushy.  Branches flattened or sometimes rounded at the base, expanding towards the top.  Thickness of branches 1 to 1.5 cm.  Growing edges with or without verruce.  Verrucae very conspicuous, ascending 3 to 5 mm thick and long.  Distance between adjacent ones 3 to 5 mm.  Calices on verrucae and at the distal parts of the branches polygonal, average 1 mm long crowded and rather deep.  At the older parts of the branches they become rounded.  Septa and columella as rule not seen, though in some old calices and primaries are represented.

Remarks:  P. meandrina var. nobillis is very near to P. verrucosa in growth form and minute details of calicular characters. 

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
