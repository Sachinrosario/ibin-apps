Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Encrusting, rarely submassive, with polygonal corallites separated by well defined intercorallite grooves.  Calices 3 to 4 mm long, 2 to 3 mm broad, 2-3 mm deep, wall average 1 mm thick.  Total number of septa 28 to 40 depending on the size of the corallites, slightly exsert and swollen at the wall with dentate edges and granulose sides.  Sometimes septal sides smooth.  Twelve to 16 septa reach the columella.  The directives of the primary cycle very broad often.  Columella trabecualar.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
