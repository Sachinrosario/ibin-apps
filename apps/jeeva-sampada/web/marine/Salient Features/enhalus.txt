SALIENT FEATURES OF Enhalus acoroides (L.f.) Royle   

Leaves: 4 - 6 at each erecting shoot, longer than broad (length: 10 - 23 cm and width: 2 - 3 cm) 

Inflorescence: Spathed inflorescence, dioecious; male inflorescence always submerged; female inflorescence floating on water surface before fertilization. After fertilization, the peduncle becomes coiled and contracted below the water surface 

Perianth: Tepals 3, elliptic, white in case of male flowers whereas in female flowers, reddish streaks are seen on white tepals

Ovary: Doom shaped, incompletely separated into six chambers

Fruit: Ovoid, covered with numerous black appendages, irregular opening during seed dispersal, 10 - 12 seeds

Flowering and fruiting: Throughout the year


Commercial Use:
The plants are used as manureFibre from leaves is used for making fish nets

Health: The seeds are edible and eaten by local people at Pamban and it is said to taste like groundnut

Nutrition: Food source for sea cows 

R&D:  In vitro culture (Axenic culture) (Kannan, et al., 1999; Kannan, 1999; Thangaradjou, 2000)

Large Scale Tank Culture (Kannan and Thangaradjou, 2001)
