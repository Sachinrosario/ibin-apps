Salient Features:

Morphological:

Body nearly 4 times as long as broad. Head transversely oblong. First peraeon segment with an arcuate groove. Posterior border of peraeon segments 2-7 with a row of setae. Each pleon segment with an oblique setose lateral ridge, that on fifth segment prominent and tubercular. Dorsal surface of telson and pleon without tubercles or ridges. Telson nearly circular, lateral crests with large tubercles. Distal border with a row of tubercles, but spine like bristles absent, border sparsely setose, submarginal dorsal side with a few stiff setules.

Third segment of flagellum of first antenna with a stout aesthete, flagellum of second antenna 5 segmented. Mandibular palp 3 segmented, incisor process with file like ridges and teeth; lacinioid seta small, apically curved and feebly spiny, setal row formed of a bundle of 8 feebly barbed setae. Maxilliped slender and long, epipodite broad lanceolate, not reaching the base of the palp, its border hairy.

Propodus of first peraeopod with the 2 usual barbed spines, secondary unguis clearly bifid, lower border of ischium, merus and carpus tubercular. Merus of 7th leg with the outer distal angle produced and carrying a row of 5 stout barbed spines, carpus with a stout lower inner tooth and a distal row of 6 strongly barbed spines.

Peduncle of uropod slightly longer than endopod. endopod about twice the length of exopod, exopod with a distinct claw.

Diagnostic Characteristics:
Antenna 2 with five flagellar articles; pleotelson without dorsomedial sculture; Dorsal surface of pleotelson without tubercles or pits; Tubercles on the lateral crests of the telson large.

Feeding Habit: Bores into wood and eats the wood particles

Main Food: wood cellulose

Additional Remarks:
This species cause heavy destruction to timber structures and test parks in mumbai waters. So far it is known from the type locality mumbai. L. Bombayensis is capable of attacking timber panels tricated with cca and crustacea(Santhakumaran,1969).

Size and Age: Maximum Length 2.2mm.
