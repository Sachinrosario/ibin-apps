SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Female: Body elongate oblong. Cephalon broader than long; anterior border perfectly rounded. Peraeon segments greatly relaxed; anterior border of first segment prominently trisinuous, postero-lateral corners produced into narrow, long processes reaching the middle of the lateral border of the second segment, postero-lateral corners of all the succeeding segments produced into triangular processes, progressively increasing in size in the successive segments; that of seventh segment very long and directed backwards and almost reaching the median distal border of the second pleon segment. Coxal plates long, narrow and apically pointed, all reaching considerably beyond the postero-lateral corners of the respective segments; the seventh plate reaching the center of the fourth pleon segment. First pleon segment longer and broader than the rest segments two to five sub equal in length. Lateral processes of the first two-pleon segments foliaceous, that of second reaching the lateral ends of the fifth pleon segment. Telson roughly triangular. 

First antenna nine jointed, segments four to nine with a bunch of setae; second antenna eleven jointed, fifth segment very large and with a stout seta. Anterior peraeopods with fourth and fifth segments very short, sub equal in size, dactylus very long, strongly falcate, with a slight basal bulge. Posterior peraeopods comparatively slender, fourth segment with one, fifth with two and sixth with four prominent spines. Palp of mandible three jointed, first segment short bur stout, second and third slender. Apical part of maxilliped with three hooks and several bunches of microscopic stiff spines. Exopod of uropod long and slender, twice as long as endopod.

Body brownish white, with the lateral part of the pleon deep brown or chocolate, this colouration extending on to the tip of the exopod of the uropod.
 
Size and age: Length : 17.5 mm.
