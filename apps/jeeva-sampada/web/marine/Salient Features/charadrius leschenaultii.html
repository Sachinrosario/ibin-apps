SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Short stout bill with sharp pointed wings. Legs longer with distinct yellowish or greenish tinge.







Diagnostic characteristics: Forehead supercilium prominent; crown, con coverts, upper parts and patches on sides of breast grey brown.






Sex attributes:


Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.




Meristic characteristics:





Feeding habit: Gregarious feeder along the shore.







Main Food: Minor crustaceous and worms.



Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder






Additional remarks: Winter migrant to India; breeding unknown.
