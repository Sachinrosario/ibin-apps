SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      General form slender. Rostrum rounded at apex. Mouth parts and thoracic limbs as on for the genus. Endopod of third to eighth thoracic limbs with 5 to 10 carpopodal segments. First pleopod of male with single jointed endopod and eight jointed exopod; second pleopod with five jointed endopod and eight jointed exopod; third pleopod with rudimentary endopod and five jointed exopod; ramus swollen at point of articulation between third and fourth joints. Fifth segment terminating in three processes; fourth and fifth pleopods with single segmented endopod and two segmented exopod. Telson with twelve spines on lateral margin, distal two spines long, penultimate spine longer; in female telson with 13-15 spines on lateral margin. Endopod of uropod with 5-6 well spaced spines. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult male    : 6 mm     
Adult female : 6.4 mm
