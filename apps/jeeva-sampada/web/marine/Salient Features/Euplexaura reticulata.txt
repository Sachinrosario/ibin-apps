SALIENT FEATURES		
Description	:Colony is reticulate. The polyps stands in distances of 2 mm, their calyces are low verrucae but have sharp edges. 

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are small, warted and belted spindles.  

Colour	:	The colony is light grayish brown in colour.

General Distribution	-	Malayan Archipelago
