SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony flabellate, 11.4 cm in height and with a diameter of 5.9 cm . The main stem is tortuous, but the bends are all in the same plane. The calyces are implanted on all sides of the branches, but are almost absent on the proximal parts of the colony. The distance between them is 1.8 mm. The individual calyces are very low subconical verrucae, measuring 0.8 mm in height and 1.5 mm in diameter. Stout vertical spindles surround the calyx walls distally. The operculum is composed of slender spindles, two of which lie along the surface of each tentacle with a shorter base. The collaret is well developed.	

Spicules: The spicules are irregular with rounded plates. 

Colour	: The colony is white, axis brown and the spicules are colourless.
