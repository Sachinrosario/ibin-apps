SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules linear, narrow with parallel sides, broadened at the base forming spiral colonies.

Diagnostic characteristics: The striae are very difficult to discern.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 46-103µ long and 7-10m broad.

Biochemical aspects: Release a fatty acid or extra cellular product, which inhibits bacterial growth (Aubert et. al. 1970). This antibacterial substance is inhibited by the release of a protein by Prorocentrum micans in mixed culture (Aubert, 1971 b).
