SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Ellipsoid in outline, flattened wider than high provided with a small truncate neck without antapical horns but with tiny spines. Its hypotheca is broadly rounded, slightly flattened at posterior pole.

Diagnostic characteristics: The girdle fins are broad reinforced by numerous lists.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 53-62µ, diameter: 53-64µ
