SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells nearly symmetrical, flattened dorsiventrally. Epitheca triangular in ventral view, with straight or slightly convex sides.

Diagnostic characteristics: Hypotheca with two horns filled with plasma, without spines. Girdle almost circular slightly sinistral, excavated, supported by narrow lists.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 70-80µ.
