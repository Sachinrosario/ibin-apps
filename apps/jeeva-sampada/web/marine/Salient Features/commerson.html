SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological            Ref.No. 32

  Diagnostic characteristics:

Elongate mackerel-like fishes without perceptible scales or corselet. Jaws with large flattened teeth. Caudal peduncle with a median sized lateral keel and alesser ridge above and below. Lateral line with a sharp bend behind soft dorsal.


  Sex attributes: No sexual dimorphism

  Descriptive characters:
  Body elongate and rather strongly compressed. Jaw teeth strong and compressed, serrated along their edges; fine teeth on vomer and palatines. Gill-rakers 0-1 on upper arm, 3-6 on lower arm (total 3 to 6) on first arch. Two dorsal fins, the first one with 14 to 17 spines and second with 14-19 soft rays, followed by 8 to 10 finlets. Anal fin with 14 to 18 soft rays, followed by 8 to 10 finlets. Pectoral fin some what falcate. Lateral line with a prominent dip under the second dorsal fin. Intestine with two folds and three distinct limbs.
  
Colour: Back of the body iridescent blue-grey, sides below lateral line silvery with bluish reflections, with large black blotches in juveniles which merge to form 20 to 50 wavy narrow grey bars in adults.


  
Meristic characters
  D.XIV-XVII; 14-17, 8-10 ; A 14-18, 8-10


  Feeding habit : Pelagic subsurface feeder

  Main food: The food items in the order of rankings were Sardinella spp. Anchoviella, digested matter, Hilsa, fishes, other carangids, Rastrelliger, Chirocentrus, Caranx, Ilisha Leiognathus, Amblygaster, Loligo, Slar, Dussumieria, Fish scales, Upeneus, Mugil, Isopods, Megalaspis, Tachysurus, Therapon, Gerrus, Thrissocles, Chorenemus, Rhynchorhamphus, Tetradon, etc. Juveniles 51-150 mm prefer Anchoviella.

                Ref.No. 13,21,25,30,34

Feeding type: Carnivore, Aggressive predator.

Additional remarks: Feeding is intense between 8.30 and 11.30 a.m. and between 7 and 10 p.m.

  Characteristics:

  Large numbers of late larval and juvenile specimens of S.commerson caught in the shore seines during February to May along the South Kerala coast and the specimens described here (Jones 1962) are mostly from Vizhinjam near Trivandrum. Juvenile specimens from Chennai and Visakhapatnam were also examined in the course of the study. The species has 43-44 vertebrae + urostyle.

  14.8 mm Stage: Head 2.3 to 4.2 in standard length. Snout long and pointed, teeth larger and prominent. Chromatophores are much fewer than in the corresponding stage of its congener and the dorsal and lateral bands are absent. Pigmentation in the wall of the abdominal cavity can be seen through.
