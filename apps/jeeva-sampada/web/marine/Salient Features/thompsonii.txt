SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:   

Genus Siriella  Dana

Diagnosis : Eyes well developed. Antennal scale with outer margin naked and terminating in a tooth. Labrum longer than broad, with long frontal process except for S. jonesi. Endopods of third to eighth thoracic limbs with carpus distinct, propodus sometimes with a faint division ; dactylus together with the unguis forming large claw encircled by circlet of peculiary serrated setae.   Pleopods of male biramous with curved or coiled  biramous pseudobranchial lobes ; third and fourth pleopods frequently modified in males ; pleopods rudimentary in females.  Exopod of uropod two segmented , proximal segment externally spiny ; inner border of endopod throughout spiny. Telson entire, lateral border and apex armed, apex always armed with three small spines and pair of long plumose setae between one or two pairs of stout spines.

Diagnostic characteristics:  Siriella thompsonii                

Body robust. Front marigin of carapace produced into narrow triangular rostral plate with pointed apex.  Eyes very large with expanded cornea. Antennal scale narrow, not reaching tip of antennular peduncle. Pleopods of male not modified; pseudobranchial lobes spirally  coiled. Telson narrow, apex truncate and armed with three pairs of stout  long spines, three small median spines and pair of plumose setae; lateral borders with spines regularly increasing in length. Exopod of uropod much shorter than endopod and armed with three spines; inner margin of endopod with row of spines arranged in series of long and short ones.

Additional remarks:        This species exhibits many intraspecific variations in the relative length and acuteness of the rostral projection, the number of spines on the outer uropod and on the lateral margin of the telson and the proportion of the length of the apical spines of the telson.      The conspicuously shorter exopod of the uropod, the sub truncate apex of the telson and the comparatively large dark red cornea of the eyes easily distinguish this species.    

Adult male 	-	6-11 mm 
Adult  female  	-    	4.5-9 mm
