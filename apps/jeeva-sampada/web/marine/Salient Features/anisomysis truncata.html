Diagnostic characteristics:
Body smooth. Carapace anteriorly produced into broad triangular rostrum. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Second segment of mandibular palp without denticles. Thoracic endopods with unjointed carpopropodus. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male long, three segmented, reaching beyond telson, third segment ending in two spines, outer naked, inner stout with strong barbs. Telson one and a half times as long as broad at base, lateral margins nearly straight, distal half armed with 4-5 short spines, distal border broadly truncate with two pairs of stout spines, outer pair twice as long as inner pair. Uropods long and slender without spines.
