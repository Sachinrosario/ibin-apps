SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves naviculoid, ends abruptly protracted into long and narrow rostrated ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Raphe on the central line straight at the middle portion of valves. Longitudinal/ transversal striae 24/24 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 120-128µ, width 9-10m.
