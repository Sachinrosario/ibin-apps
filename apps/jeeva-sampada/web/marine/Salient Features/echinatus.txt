
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:           

Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th, and 5th pedigerous segment fused. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment rounded. Prosome ovate, rostral filaments long and slender. Urosome 4-segmented.  First antenna with flattened proximal part having hyaline fringe. Fifth leg with distinct shape as illustrated in the figure. The terminal joint has 3 spines. The inner marginal spine provided with strong minute teeth on its outer margin.
