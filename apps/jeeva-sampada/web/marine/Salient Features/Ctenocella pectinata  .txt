Description	:	The colony is harp-shape, branching in a plane and dichotomous. The branching is parallel, median and the short branches lying one behind the other are lateral forming s single main branch. The ends of the main branch are tender and pointed. The polyps stand sideways in several longitudinal rows; their calyces are low, warted and their reinforcement at the base consists of a few small thorny double stars, while the tentacles contain 0.04 mm long blunt belt-like spindles in small numbers.
		Spicules: The calyces are reinforced with pointed 0.067 mm long, irregularly warted double spindles. The thin and smooth bark contains 0.052 mm long double clubs with large and warted edges.
