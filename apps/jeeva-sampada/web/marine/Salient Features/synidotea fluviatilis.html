SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Body linear, nearly parallel sided with a very slight bulging at the fourth thoracic segment. Cephalon squarish, with the antero-lateral corners angular; dorsal sunmargimal line with rounded corners but not semicircular. Antero-lateral corners of first mesomatic segment angular, fourth segment longest and broadest; median part of lateral borders of segments one to four bulged , sides of segments five to seven nearly straight, dorso-median ridge more prominent. The arcuate depressions of the anterior segments very conspicuous but narrower than in S.variegata. Metasome broader, one and a half times as long as broad, apical concavity shallow but broad, postero-lateral corners somewhat produced.

First antenna comparatively stout, with very large basal peduncular segment, flagellum equal to the combined length of segments two and three, with eight bundles of setae in addition to a large apical tuft of setae. Upper lip twice as broad as long, posterior border bilobed and sparsely setose, epistome narrower.

Outer lobe of first maxilla with nine comparitvely short and pointed teeth, inner lobe with two sparsely plumose setae devoid of barbs and a spinule. Second maxilla differs considerably from that of S.variegata, the lobes being subequal with proportionately smaller setae; outer lobe is not produced and the setae not comb like the inner four setae on the inner lobe alone are plumose. Incisor part of mandible slightly different and the middle spines number five as against three in S.variegata. Seventh segment of first peraeopod much longer than sixth; lower border of segments with a row of uniformly large plumose setae; sixth segment of second peraeopod stouter and longer than seventh, seventh peraeopod much elongated, with the sixth segment about twice as long as seventh. Endopod of uropod apically truncate and with sparsely setose borders.

Penial process single, nearest to that of Edotea oculata. It starts very near the anterior margin of the abdominal segment which is excavated to receive it. The two processes have undergone fusion to such an extent that the apical part is perfectly linguiform and the basal part has a prominent construction.

Length 12.0 mm
Body uniformly grey, with a slightly dark dorso-median line.

Additional Remarks:
Synidotea fluviatilis is the new name given by Pillai (1958) to specimen described by Chilton. (1924) as Synidotrea variegata collinge while studying the materials collected from Ashtmausi lake in Quilon (Kerala), Pillai (1958) found that they very closely resembled Chiltons specimens but different from orginal S.variegata in having a parallel sided body with laterally produced segments , the differently shaped second maxilla and the apically truncate uropod. Monod (1924) also had expressed that S.variegata Collinge is different from S.variegata Chiton.

Size and age : 12.0 mm
