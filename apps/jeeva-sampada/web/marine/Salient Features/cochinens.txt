SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological: Rostrum triangular with a dorsal tooth; carapace covering thorax,dorsally and laterally; supraorbital and pterygostomian spines present; a hepatic spine becomes prominent in post larval stages; abdominal somites 1-5 with short lateral spines,6th elongated with a posterior median  dorsal spine; telson median notch deep with 2 spines in each lobe (mysis); only 4 pereopods present; 1-3 biramous,endopod 5-segmented the terminal segment being an indistinct chela; exopodite unsegmented and with 4 plumose setae; pereopod 4 uniramous with 4 terminal setae. Pleopods present on 1st 3 abdominal segments and uniramous; uropodial exopod with a small sharp spine on the outer margin,at 1/3 length from the tip; the pleopods on abdominal somites 4 and  5 are rudimentary and non-functional.Diagnostic characteristics:The 5th pereopod absent; 1-3 biramous and 4th uniramous; rostrum small, triangular with a dorsal tooth; pleopods present on 1-3 abdominal segments, uniramous and unjointed protopod and exopod.    

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.
Main food:  Plankton, detritus with  sand and mud.
Feeding type: Omnivorous (filter feeders at early stages)

Additonal remarks: 

Size and age:  
Nauplii I-III: size 0.22mm- 0.32mm.
Protozoeae I-III: size 0.58mm- 1.80mm
Mysis: 2.00mm
Postlarvae: I-IV: size 2.5mm- 4.22mm. 

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            Eggs: Spherical and have a large perivitelline space,measuring 0.48mm-5.0mm in diameter; the embryonic mass is enclosed by a thin membrane,measuring 0.18-0.20mm.
Nauplii I-III: length 0.22mm to 0.32mm.Antennule uniramous in all, with 3 unequal terminal setae and a small lateral seta;in 2nd stage, 3 inner and an outer shorter,lateral seta and in 3rd stage the basal portion  is 5-segmented, outer lateral seta disappear; antenna and mandible biramous; caudal furca with a pair of seta in N1 and 4 pairs of setae in others. Protozoeae I: length 0.56mm-0.58mm.Carapace broader than long; with paired anterior and lateral spines with a single posterior median spine; anterior spines bifurcate; thorax short and segmented; abdomen short and unsegmented; mandible with incisor process having 7 large teeth and molar with 4 subequal teeth; maxilliped 3 bud-like and rudimentary with 2 short terminal setae; telson bilobed, each lobe with 5 spines.Protozoea II: length0.97mm. The major development in this stage is the formation of a prominent rostrum, eyes with stalk and the segmentation of the abdomen; anterior, lateral and posterior spines without lateral processes; thorax short and segmented; abdomen 6-segmented; telson lobe with 5 spines each.Protozoea III: length 1.80mm.The major changes are the development of uropods, and lateral spines in the abdominal segments; anterior spine lost its branching; thorax segmented with rudimentary appendages; abdomen 6-segmented with a pair of short posterolateral spines; telson no change; pereopods 1-3 biramous and small; pereopod 4 rudimentary.Mysis: length 2.00mm.Rostrum small with a minute dorsal spine; supraorbital and pterygostomian spines present; pereopods 1-3 biramous with 5-jointed endopod, terminal part with indistinct chela; pereopod 4 uniramous, uropodial exopod with a spine; pleopods present only on 1st 3 abdominal segments; telson median notch deep, 2 spines to each lobe.Postlarvae: length 2.5mm-5.6mmRostrum small, triangular with a dorsal tooth; abdominal somites 1-5 with lateral spines and  6th somite with a posterior,median, dorsal spine; pereopods 1-3 chelate and 4th disappears; pleopods present on 1st 3 abdominal somites; uropods with more setae; telson with 2 spines each on caudal lobes; the notch decreases and the number of spines reducing with a shallow depression.
