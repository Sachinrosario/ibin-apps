Salient Features:
Morphological:

Praniza larva:
Body about three times as long as broad; head small broader than long, ewith a short truncated rostrum; eyes very large and dark brown. First peraeon segment very small, intimately united with the head, second slightly broader than head, antero-lateral corners produced into broad truncated processes, third segment slightly longer and broader than secon. Segments four five and six broader and longer than third, each with a prominent lateral incision. Posterior bordeer of fourth segment with a medioan large bulge, seventh segment small narrower than pleon and wedged in between the postero-lateral prolongations of the sixth peraeon segment. Pleon abruptly narrower than peraeon, slightly more than twice as long as broad, segments subequal in sixe, last slightly longer; postero-lateral corners of all segments anularly produced. Telson triangular sides straight, as long as base, apex acute, with two small setae.

Mouth parts projecting beyond the head as a triangular mass. Basal segment of first antenna slightly shorter than second, third segment longer than the first two combined; flagellum four or five jointed, basal segment very small; second antenna longer flagellum seven jointed Peraeopod comparatively long with very few setae, inner border of segments four five and six of first peraeopod serrated. Uropods slightly exceeding the tip of telson, distal border of the rami with long setae, outer border of exopod hairy.

Body white, with a greyish tint due to the presence of branching chromatophores.

Additional Remarks:
Pillai (1958) reported this species from thankassery (Quilon) based on a single pramiza larva, the telson, uropods and the antenna of which showed close resemblence to that of Elaphognathia insolita as described by stebbing (1905).

Size and Age: Length 7.0mm.
