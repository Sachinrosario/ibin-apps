Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body fusiform, compressed; belly with a sharp keel of scutes from isthmus ( between  gill openings on underside of head ) to anus . Tip of snout above level of eye centre; Maxilla long, reaching at least halfway along pectoral fin ( or to pelvic base in large adults ) Gill rackers usually 17  -19 on lower limb of first arch , their serrae in distinct clumps. Anal fin long, usually with 30-35 unbranched rays
 
Colour : back blue / green, flanks silvery ; a black venuiose area  behind gill opening gill. Faint yellow on dorsal and caudal fins , later with a thin dusky hint margin ; other fins clear
