SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear rectangular cells in girdle view. Valves united to form a mat like colony, the individual cells of which exhibit gliding movements in the living condition.

Diagnostic characteristics: Linear spindle shaped valve in outline keil punctae 7-8 in 10µ. Transapical striae fine, 21 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: 112-196µ long; 6.5-9µ broad.
