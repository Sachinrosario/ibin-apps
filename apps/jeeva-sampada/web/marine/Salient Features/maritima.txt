Salient Features:

Description: 
Densely caespitose, errect, perrenials, 60-150 cm tall . 
Leaves few, solid. Flowers in decompound cymes, unequally pedunculate.
Capsule 0.5 – 0.6 cm long, ovoid or conical, trigonous, 
Seeds oblong or obovoid, caudate at either end.

Commercial use	
It was named after Louis Gérard a Provencal botanist
