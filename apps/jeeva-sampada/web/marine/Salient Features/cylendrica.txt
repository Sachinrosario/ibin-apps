Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum tufted, arborescent, upto 20 cm high and spread, lower parts of the main divisions dead.  Branches repeatedly dividing and anastomosing; top most branchlets crooked, flattened or digitiform.  In several cases the colonies are free, unattached lying over the sandy bottom.  Calices superficial, average 1 mm in diameter, polygonal, or rounded when the wall is thickened.  Wall rough due to well developed denticles, they are highly frosted.  The ventral triplet form a trident.  Septal sides granular.  There are two highly frosed septal denticles between the wall and the palus; the outer forming a complete ring near the wall.  Pali show variations in the degree of development.  Six of them can be seen i.e. on the lateral pairs of septa and on the directives.  In some specimens pali is only suggested.  Columella when developed is in the form of a single compressed style, joined to the septal ends by radii.  
 
Living coral grey or dull green.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Fairly common in the lagoons of Lakshadweep.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
