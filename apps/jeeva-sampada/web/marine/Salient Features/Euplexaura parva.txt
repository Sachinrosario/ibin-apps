General Distribution	-	Malayan Archipelago

SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branching is in one plane and they alternate at right angles with flattened terminals. The polyps are 0.8 mm long and are with clear calyces. 

Spicules: The spicules of the calyx are long, broad, flat and heavily speculated (Fig.a & b). The coenenchyma are long,  double spindles with hemispherical smooth warts.
 
Colour	:  The colony is whitish and the axis is dark brownish to yellow in colour.
