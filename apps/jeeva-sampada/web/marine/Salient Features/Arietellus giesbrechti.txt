SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:      

Female: Cephalothorax, 4.06 mm; abdomen, 1.06 mm. The prosome separates from the 1st pedigerous segment; the last two pedigerous segments are fused. The prosome obtusely triangular in front and terminates anteriorly in a very small pointed process directed forwards and slightly downwards. The last pedigerous segment produced on each side into an acute expansion but they are asymmetrical; the left side more produced than the right. The rostral filaments slender. The body is supplied with numerous cutaneous glands.     

The abdomen 4-jointed, the segments and furca are in the proportional lengths as 30:13:8:17:32=100. The genital segment abruptly narrowed about half way back; the genital opening produced below near the proximal. The furcal rami asymmetrical; the right ramus slightly wider than the left and are densely haired on inner and outer margin with dark auburn bluish tint.     

The 1st antenna 20-jointed, extends to the distal margin of the cephalothorax.     The 2nd antenna slender: the exopod much shorter than the endopod.      

The 1st to 4th legs have each 3-jointed exopod and endopod. The terminal spine of the exopod of the 3rd leg has coarse teeth.     

The 5th pair of legs asymmetrical: the left leg larger than the right; the 2nd basal segment 1-jointed, has no strong spine on the distal margin.        

Male: The general shape of the body resembles that of the female.The left 1st antenna is modified to form a grasping organ. The 5th pair of legs as figured.

Additional  remarks:       The present specimen agrees well with the description and figures of A. giesbrechti given by Sars except that the 5th pair of legs is asymmetrical and in the spinulation on the 2nd and 3rd segments.

Size and age: Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     
Female: 5.12 – 5.53 mm     Male: 4.67 mm
