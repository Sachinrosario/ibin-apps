SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Diagnostic characters: Body slender and  elongate, not deeper than head length. Maximum depth contained more than 3 times in standard length. Cheek and breast covered with small scales. Back with irregular green and dark marbling. 

Sex attributes: Sexual dimorphism present (Ref no: 5). The bioluminiscent light organs are visible to the exterior through clear patches of skin, present as a large roughly triangular area located posterior to the pectoral fin, in males, whereas in females, this clear patch of skin is absent. More details are given under the head ‘Sexual dimorphism in the structure of light organs’. 

Descriptive characters:  Body, elongate, slender and moderately compressed. Dorsal and ventral profiles, about evenly curved and tapering gently to the very short and gentle caudal peduncle. Upper surface of head weakly convex. Snout, sharp, pointed; eye diameter equal to snout. 
Protracted mouthparts point downwards. Narrow band of small peg like teeth in each jaw. Mandibular slightly concave. Inter-orbitals with a median ridge from snout to occiput, supra-ocular ridges enclosing a triangular space. Lower pre-opercular margins with very fine serrations.
Scales small and deciduous. It is the only species of the genus with scales on cheeks. Lateral line conspicuous. 2nd and 3rd dorsal spines longest. Amongst anal spines the 2nd one is longest. Caudal fin deeply forked. Scales everywhere covered with fine black punctuations, which are larger and more scattered over the lower sides of the head and body. Ventrals reaching halfway to the base of the anals. 
 
Colour: Body silvery. Back and sides marked with a number of irregular dark purplish spots and vermiculations. Horizontal yellow band at mid-height of spinous part of dorsal fin, most of margin of soft part orange. Anal fin between 2nd and 3rd spines yellow, as also margin of anterior part of fin. Scales everywhere covered with fine black punctuations that are larger and more scattered on lower side of head and body. A black spot at the base of each dorsal and anal ray, axil of pectoral black, posterior edge of gill cavity black showing through opercular edge. A short black band on tip of snout above mouth, fins without definite colour markings. Males have bluish longitudinal stripes on belly.         (Ref no: 3,7)

Feeding Habit:
Main food: It feeds mainly on small crustaceans, polychaetes and algae.
Feeding type: Bottom and column feeding.
