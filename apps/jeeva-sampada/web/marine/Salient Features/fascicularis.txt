SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum plocoid, sometimes surface rising to gibbosities.  Corallites 7 to 12 mm in diameter at the top, less below, 6-10 mm high.  Calices oval, circular or distorted, 2 to 3 mm deep.  Adjacent corallites 2-3 mm apart.  Total septa range from 40 to 50 per calyx, exsert, major septa upto 3 mm.  Exsert ends vertical, narrower at the top, edges entire, or minutely serrated, sides granular.  10-14 septa reach the columella.  Columella 1-2 mm in diameter.  Costae at the distal parts of the corallites visible, getting obsolete towards the base.  Coenosteum non-costate, with hollow vesicles.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
