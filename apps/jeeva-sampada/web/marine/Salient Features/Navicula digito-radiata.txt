SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Lanceolate to elliptical valves, with a very faint median inflation, apices sub-cuneate or weakly obtuse.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface costate, costae radiate, those in the median area of unequal length and often curved.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 44-84m, width 16-20m.
