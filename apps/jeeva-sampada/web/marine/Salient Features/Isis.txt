SALIENT FEATURES


Description	:	Colony branched more or less pinnately as in a feather.
 The main branch (median branch) is slightly thicker than the branchlets.
 No soft part is preserved intact.


Axis composed of nodal and internodal divisions, calcareuss internodes are fluted longitudinally. Nodes shorter and horny, they are narrower than the internodes; length up to 3 mm and are shaped like an `hour?glass' 
(Fig.b & c). Branches originate from internodes.

Spicules are (1) Tuberculated spindles (3) Dumbbells and (4) Crosses (Fig.a).

Colour		:	Colony brown, axis white and nodes dark brown.
