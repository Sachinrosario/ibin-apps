SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 Diagnostic characteristics:        Female: Prosome broadly triangular anteriorly. Rostal filaments present. Relative length of prosome and urosome 81:19%. Posterior margin of the prosome rounded without spines. A row of 4 to 5 small spinules present on the dorso lateral side of the fifth pedigerous segment. Relative lengths of urosome segments and caudal rami 2:0.9:0.5:0.7. Genital segment as long as wide. Caudal rami longer than wide with hairs on lateral margin.          Legs 1 to 4 typical for the genus with 2 segmented endopods and 3 segmented exopods. The first segment of the fifth leg fused medially. Second segment with long plumose setae twice as long as and extending beyond the third segment. Third segment broad basally and narrowing towards the distal ends which are curved inwards.     Male: Relative lengths of prosome and urosome 80:20%. Posterior corners of the prosome rounded and bearing 4 to 5 small spinules on the dorso-lateral sides of the last prosome segment as in female. Relative lengths of the urosome segments and caudal rami 1:3.5:1.5:1:1:1.5. Urosome 1 bilobed on the lateral sides with small hairs. Posterior margins of the urosome 2, urosome 3 and urosome 4 bear small spinules.          Right 5th leg bears a spine at about the middle in the second segment. Inner lobe of the third segment with a small spine. Fourth segment narrow and long with a strong spine at the distal end and 5 to 6 small spines on the lateral side. The second segment of the left pleopod 5 has a small spine on the lateral side at the distal end. A strong spine present at the tip to the third segment. 

Additional  remarks:           The species is named after Lakshadweep archipelago.
