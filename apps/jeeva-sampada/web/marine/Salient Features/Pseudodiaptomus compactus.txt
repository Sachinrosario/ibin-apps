Exopod 1 with shoulder-like knob at distal corner at base of outer spine. Exopod 2 narrow with 4 setae along medial edge, proximal ear-shaped protrusion, stout proximolateral spine and large ovoid hyaline process with patch of fine spinules distolaterally.

Additional remarks:
     Wellershaus’s specimen differs from Kasturirangan’s in the presence of a hyaline plate on right exopod 1 and spinules on hyaline process of left exopod 2. Otherwise, these two descriptions appear the same, with this species differing from P. mertoni in: i) left basipod 1 lacks elongate distal spine. ii) right basipod 2 with short outer spine, recurved at apex. iii) right exopod 1 elongate, divided near distal end with large auxiliary hyaline plate. iv) endopod more compact with distal two points only slightly separated. v) left basipod 2 with row of coarse spinules at inner margin. vi) proximolateral margin of left exopod 2 with short spine and somewhat circular hyaline process with small spinules along outer margin. These differences warrant a separate species status from P. mertoni. Specimens of P. compactus from the Cochin backwater were reported as P. mertoni, by Pillai (1980).
