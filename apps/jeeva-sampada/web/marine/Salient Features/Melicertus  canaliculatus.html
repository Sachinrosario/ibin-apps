SALIENT FEATURES:             Ref No:103

 

Morphological:    Carapace smooth. Rostrum armed with 9 to 11 teeth on dorsal, and a single tooth on ventral margins; adrostral carina and sulcus long, extending almost to posterior margin of carapace, the groove wide; postrostral carina well developed as far back as adrostral sulcus, with a deep median groove throughout its length; gastrofrontal carina present; gastrofrontal sulcus bifurcate posteriorly; hepatic carina almost horizontal to base of antennal crest and from there sloping anteroventrally ; telson lacking lateral spines; no ischial spine on first pereopod. Petasma with short distomedian projections, reaching or slightly overhanging distal margin of costae; ventral costae broadened apically and bearing minute spinules at tip ; outer surface of lateral lobes not tuberculate. Thelycum with lateral plates, their anteromedian margins diverging, then turning in a broad arc continuous with anterolateral margins; anterior process subacuminate or subovate; posterior process triangular, anterior edges raised in lateral ridges delimiting a median depressed area.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:    Rostrum with 9 to 11 dorsal teeth and a single ventral tooth; adrostral carina and sulcus long, extending almost to posterior margin of carapace, postrostral carina with a deep median groove throughout its length reaching as far back as adrostral sulcus; telson lacking lateral spines ; ventral costae of petasma bearing minute spinules at tip; thelycum with lateral plates on sternite XIV.

 

Colour: Body pale yellow with reddish–brown to brown transverse bands, last abdominal band reaching ventral margin.

Additional remarks: It is nocturnal in habit.

Size and age:

 

Maximum length(cm)(male/female/unsexed): Male: 14.5;  Female: 22.5        Ref. No:103, 192

Eggs and larvae:                  Ref. No.: 189.

 

Characteristics:     No information is available on eggs and larvae of the species. The earliest postlarval stage at the time of entering backwaters measure 9.4 to 10.6 mm in total length and has the following distinguishing characters:  Carapace with well  developed antennal and hepatic spines; rostrum shorter than eye, bearing six teeth on the upper margin, no ventral tooth; sixth abdominal segment bearing a prominent posteromedian dorsal spine and a row of 15-20 minute movable spines on either side of dorsal carina; telson with 8+8 spines, the four pairs of lateral spines being longer and stouter than those on the slightly convex posterior margin.

 

     In live condition the postlarvae are brilliantly pigmented with a streak of reddish brown chromatophores on the entire ventral side of the body.

 

Abundance:      In Cochin backwaters postlarvae occur from January to May, with peak abundance during April. This coincides with high salinity and temperature conditions prevailing in the environment during summer months.
