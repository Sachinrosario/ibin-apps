SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elliptical valves with swollen margins strongly sculptured with a few ribs inside. Cells forming long or short chains by attachment with mucilage pads at blunt end of their processes.

Diagnostic characteristics: Two blunt, rounded processes at the corners. Areolations on both valve and girdle.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 92µ in length.
