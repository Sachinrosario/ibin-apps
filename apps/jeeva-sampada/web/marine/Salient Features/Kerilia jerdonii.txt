SALIENT FEATURES

Morphological: Head short and very narrow, slopng downward at the snout ; nostril superior,
nasal shields in contact with one another ; prefrontals small, usually not in contact with
the supralabials ; one pre - and 1 postocular ; 6 supralabials , the last often merging with
the single anterior temporal, the 3rd and 4th in contact with the eye; 7-8 lower labials, the 
first three in contact with the genials which are clearly defined and in contact with one 
another. Eye moderate.

Body slender, of almost equal diameter throughout , covered with broader than they are long, 
imbricate and strongly keeled scales, in 21 or 23 (rarely 19) rows at midbody; 
ventrals 225-253 (in specimens from the coastal waters of India), narrow, slightly broader 
than the adjacent scales.

DIAGNOSTIC CHARACTERISTICS:

This sea snake is olive to olive brown above, yellowish or white below, with a series of
black spots or rhombs, broadest at the base and narrow at the sides; these spots may
completely encircle the body to form complete bands in the young, which also may have 
additional bars and spots in between  the larger markings.

FEEDING HABIT : Piscivorous
MAIN FOOD : Fish

Additional Remarks : the global range of Jerdon's sea snake is from the east coast of 
India and along the Srilankan coast to the Straits of Malacca, east as far as 
South Vietnam, and South to Borneo, Indonesia.

Murthy(1977) collected several samples of this species from the coastal waters of Chennai.
