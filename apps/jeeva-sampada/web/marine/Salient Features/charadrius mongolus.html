SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Similar to other plovers with short stout bill and sharp pointed wings. Tail short.







Diagnostic characteristics: Bare greenish grey legs and black bill. Ear coverts brownish and conspicuous on a dark patch behind eyes. White collar.






Sex attributes:


Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.




Meristic characteristics:





Feeding habit: Gregarious







Main Food: Insects, crustaceans and worms.



Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder






Additional remarks: Nests in Ladakh in June or July. Eggs pale green, stippled with reddish spots.












Size and age: 500
