SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells lenticular, large with small neck, without antapical or spines, with flattened posterior pole. Girdle ascendant, displaced twice width of girdle and ends projecting beyond.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus sinous with well-marked angle in the middle. Posterior sulcus flat.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 59-65µ, width: 74-85µ.
