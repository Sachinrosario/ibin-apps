SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony scanty and irregularly branched. The polyps are fully retractile. They are shielded with eight long rows of thin spindles and the tentacles are imbricated with fine spindles on their dorsal side, which are arranged in two lengthwise rows. 

Spicules: The spicules are bent or crooked and without warted belts.
 
Colour	:	The colony is light grayish to white and the axis is golden brown in colour.
