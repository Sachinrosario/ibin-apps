SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body elongate, the depth 3.6 to 4.2 times in standard length. Chin with 2 slender which nearly or just reach rear margin  of preopercle, their length 1.35 to 1.6 times in head; maxilla reaching to below anterior third of eye; villiform teeth in bands in jaws on vomer and palatines; gillrakers on first arch 21 to 24. First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first minute; pectoral fin rays 15 or 14 (usually 14); pelvic fins nearly as long as pectorals. Lateral line scales 36 to 38; 51/2 vertical rows of scales in space between dorsal fins; 12 or 13 vertical rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour : silvery pink to orangish on back, shading to silvery white on side and ventrally, with 2 narrow yellow strips (one at level of upper part of eye and the other at midpectoral base); barbells whitish to yellow; upper lobe of caudal fin with 6 transversa blackish bands and lower lobe with 4 or 5, the 2 lowermost broader and darker.
