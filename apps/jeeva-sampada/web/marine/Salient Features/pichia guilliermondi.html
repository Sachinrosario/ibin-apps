Morphological:
Standard Description of Pichia guilliermondii
Growth on 5% Malt extract agar: After 3 days at 25C, the cells are ovoidal to elongate (1.5 4.8) X (2.0 15.0) m, and occur singly, in pairs, or in short chains. Growth is smooth to wrinkled and tannish white in colour.
Growth on the surface of Assimilation media: Generally pellicles are not formed, but rings may be present.
Dalamau plate culture on Morphology agar: After 7 days at 25C, growth under the cover glass shows abundant, well branched pseudohyphae bearing whorls of blastospores. True hyphae are not present. Aerobic growth in tannish-white, sometimes butyrous, smooth and glistening or dull rugose. Margins are usually lobate.
Formation of Ascospores: This species is hetrothallic and only haploid forms have been isolated.Following the paring of complementary mating types, the resulting asci produce one to four hat-shaped spores. The spores are liberated soon after formation.
Spores were observed on 5% malt extract agar.
