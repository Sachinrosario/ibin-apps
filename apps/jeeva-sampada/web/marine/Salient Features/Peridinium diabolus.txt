SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Body rounded, slightly compressed dorso ventrally. Apical horn of medium length, conical. Both antapical processes winged, slightly spreading, their length approximately equal to half-cell width.

Diagnostic characteristics: Apical horn long and thin; the processes strongly spreading and longer than half cell width.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 85-180µ, breadth: 35-75µ.
