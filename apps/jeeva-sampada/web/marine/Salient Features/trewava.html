SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 1
Morphological: A medium sized species, with a elongate and moderately compressed body.
Diagnostic characteristics : mouth terminal, the upper jaw (maxilla) slightly less than half head; teeth in villiform , differentiated in size in both jaws, slightly enlarged teeth forming outer series in upper jaw and inner row in lower jaw. Gillrakers 11 on lower arm of first arch. Dorsal fin with 10 spines, followed by deep notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 24 to 26 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 soft rays, the second spine weak. Pectoral fins long nearly equal to body depth. Scales cycloid on head. weakly ctenoid on body. swim bladder carrot shaped , with about 25 pairs of arborescent appendages.

Colour :Back dark grey, on back, paler on belly; linings of mouth, gill chamber and body cavity blank.
