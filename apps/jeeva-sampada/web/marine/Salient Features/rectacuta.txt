SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:
Juvenile(male): Length 21mm.Rostrum bears 9 or 10 dorsal teeth+ epigastric tooth;ventral setae present. Strong antennal and hepatic spines present; the pterygostomian edge is angulated with a croud of setae. The thoracic legs with exopods; the ischium of the 2nd leg has no spine and telson having 3 marginal,movable spines on each side in addition to the posterior fused spine.The spine formula being 7+1+7.

Diagnostic characteristics: Carapace with minute supraorbital and pterygostomian spines,first 5 abdominal segments with dorsomedian spines and 5th with a pair of dorsolateral spines and 6th with a pair of ventrolateral spines(in Protozoea III).Telson with 7+1+ 7 spines in late post larvae  and mysis II and III.In the early myses  the carapace bears the supraorbital and pterygostomian spines with antero-inferior margin serrated. Telson bears 7+7 spines whereas in late mysis stage(mysis II) a median spine is present in telson (7+1+ 7).The 5th abdominal somite bearing a pair of lateral spines in all stages.

Sex attributes:  Separate, (Heterosexual).

Feeding habit: pelagic zone; feeds on plankton,algae and living  and dead  organic matters.

Main food:  plankton, detritus with  sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous.

Size and age: Protozoeae ranges from 0.84mm-2.2mm
Myses frtom 2.8mm-5.0mm
Post-larvae from 6.5mm-7.5mm(PL II)
Juvenile: 21mm.

3 protozoeal, 3myses and 2 postlarval stages are detailed.

Protozoea I: a length of 0.84mm; Eyes sessile; antenuule uniramous with 3 apical setae,one of which is very long. Antenna biramous,endopod with 2+2+4 setae; scale 9-segmented bearing 10 setae; a labral spine present. Mandible with incisor and molar parts developed. First maxilla with a papilla-like exopod with 5 plumose setae, endopod 3-segmented with each segment 2 setae. Second maxilla-protopodite with 5 endites,each armed with setae; exopod bearing 5 plumose setae, endopod3-segmented, distal segment with 3 terminal setae and others one seta each. Telson with rami very divergent,bearing 7+7 setae,one pair placed ventrolateraly.

Protozoea II: length 1.4mm – 1.8mm. Rostrum to the tip of eyes,which are stalked; no supraprbital spine; labral spine prominent.Antennular peduncle 3-segmented ,basal one subdivided and segmented into 4; 3 setae on distal segment. Antennal scale multisegmented, endopod 3-segmented,distal bearing 4 setae. Maxiillipeds biramous. Telson notch reduced in width, spines unchanged.

Protozoea III: length 2.2mm; Rostrum long. Carapace with minute supraorbital and pterygostomian spines. Dorsomedian spines present on 1st 5 abdominal segments; uropods developed; a pair of dorsolateral spines present on the 5th ab.seg.and a pair of ventrolateral on 6th seg. Antennule shows the origin of an inner flagellum,peduncle 3-segmented. Mandible has 6 denticular setae in between the incisor and molar processes. Maxillipeds biramous; legs 1-5 present in the form of biramous buds. Telson unchanged.

Mysis I: length 2.8mm-3.5mm. Rostrum extends beyond the eyes and bears a rostral tooth. A minute supraorbital and prominent pterygostomian spines present.Labral spine persists; antennal part is angular; antero-inferior margin of carapace is serrate. Antennule with 2 flagella,inner short and bearing a seta. Antennal scale with an outer marginal spine and an unsegmented endopod with a terminal seta. Mandible with no palp; abdomen with dorsomedian spines on 4 -6 segmnets and dorsolaterals on 5-6; also posteroventrals on 6th segment. Legs with long exopods; legs 1-3 tips bifid; no pleopods.Telsonparellel-sided with a deep,narrow posterior cleft. Exopod of uropods with a spine at tip.Telson with 7+7 spines.

Mysis II: length 4.0mm-4.2mm; Rostrum pointed,extends to the tip of the antennular peduncle,bears 2 dorsal teeth + epigastric. A very  small hepatic spine  appears on carapace.The serration of the anteroinferior margin of carapace reduced.Mandible with an unsegmented palp. Legs 1-3 chelate with exopods; pleopods in the form of buds. Telson bears a median spine (7+1+7).

Mysis III: length 4.4mm-5.0mm;Rostrum bears 3 dorsal + epigastric tooth;  small supraorbital,antennal and pterygostomian spines  persist.Mandibular palp segmented into 2,without setae.The denticular setae between incisor and molar processes increased to 8(6). Legs with exopods; dorsomedian spine on 4th abdominal segment reduced; pleopods uniramous and bearing no setae.Telson truncate with a median spine;  spine formula remains the same.

Postlarva I: length 5.1mm-6.5mm.Rostrum with 4 dorsal teeth,epigastric placed behind the eyes on carapace; hepatic well developed. Dorsomedian spine on 4th ab.seg.much reduced; those on 5&6 well developed; dorsolaterals present on ab.somite 5. Pleopods well developed ,uniramous and with setae. Legs with exopods and telson remains same. 

Characteristics:  the development of hepatic spine on carapace; serrate nature of anteroinferior margin of carapace(early stages); presence of dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 4 - 6; dorsolateral spines on 5&6 segments; pleopods uniramous and telson with a median spine and 7 pairs of marginal spines. 

Abundance:  Less
