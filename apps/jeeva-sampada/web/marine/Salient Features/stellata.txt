Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum repeatedly dividing.  The lower part of the corallum is dead and the living zone extends to 3 mm from top to bottom.  It is profusely branching with branchlets undergoing a little coalescent.  Branchlets short and subacute at the distal portion.  Calices small, irregularly distributed at the thicker parts of the branchlets, 0.5 to 0.6 mm in diameter.  The rim of the wall is elevated, giving a rounded appearance to most of the corallites, though rarely the upper wall is comparatively higher.  The primary cycle of septa is well developed and appears to be a little exsert.  A styliform or a little elongated columella is detectable.  In some order calices a second cycle of septa is visible.  The coenenchymal surface is covered with uniformly distributed spines.

Remarks:  Quelch (1886, p. 60) separates the present species from other members of the genus as follows: “The thick and strong branches, the shallow calices and subexsert columella, the great development of the prominent septa together with the raised continuous marginal rim to the calices will readily mark the species, which has much of the general habit of Stylophora subseriata, while the essential characters it seems to be fossil species, Stylophora costulata”.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
