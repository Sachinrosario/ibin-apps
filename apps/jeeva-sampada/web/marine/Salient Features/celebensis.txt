SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Rostrum long with a dorsal and an epigastric tooth; a ventral tooth present in postlarva I and rostral teeth serrated; carapace with supraorbital and pterygostomian spines; antero-lateral angle of carapace produced to form the pterygostomian and with a pair of supraorbital spines in Zoea IV; pereopods 1-2 appear as biramous buds in(Zoea I); pereopod 5 the longest and without exopods(Uniramous); a pair of prominent dorsolateral spines present on abdominal somite 5 (Zoea II-V); pereopods 3 and 4 developed in Zoea VI; first 1-2 legs chelate,the chelae form a claw surrounded by bristle-like setae; pereopods 4 and 5 are uniramous; exopods of legs 1-3 rudimentary (Pl.I); uropods well developed; telson broad,convex with 8+8 plumose setae in Zoea II,to a telson with posterior margin convex with a single,stout plumose seta and 2 pairs of spines ; outermost  the smallest and 2 lateral spines on either side of telson plate,slightly shifted to the basal portion.Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum with dorsal and ventral teeth; carapace with a pair of pterygostomian and branchiostegal spines, no hepatic spine; pereopods 1-2 only chelate, chela forming a claw at tip with bristle-like setae; the presence of a pair of dorsolateral spines on abdominal somite 5; the delayed development of legs 3 and 4; leg 5 develops before them and the longest; 4 and 5 uniramous; in certain situations,cannibalism is exhibited.

Sex attributes: Separate (Heterosexual).

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone
Main food:  Plankton, detritus with  sand and mud,small crustaceans.
Feeding type: Omnivorous

Size and age: 
Zoeae I-VII: 1.8mm-4.2mm                      
Postlarvae I-II: 4.3mm-4.6mm 

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            Eggs: Size 0.45mm-0.66mm; hatch out directly to Zoea I.

Zoea I: length 1.8mm.Rostrum slender, pointed, reaching 2/3 of antennular peduncle; carapace smooth; anterolateral edge slightly procurved; eyes large and sessile; pereopods 1 and 2 in the form of biramous buds; telson not separated from the abdomen, broad and concave posteriorly,carrying 7 pairs of plumose setae; uropods not developed.

Zoea II: length 2.00mm.Major changes- eyes stalked; pterygostomian spine present; pereopods 1 and 2 more developed; a uniramous 5th pereopod appears before the 3rd and 4th pereopods; a pair of dorsolateral spines present on the 5th abdominal somite; telson with 8+8 spines.

Zoea III: length 2.2mm.Rostrum with an epigastric tooth; a pair of minute dorsolaterals on 6th abdominal somite; telson separated from abdomen; uropods with endopods,without setae.

Zoea IV: length 2.7mm.Rostrum with 2 dorsal teeth; tip of rostrum and the innerside of the dorsal teeth serrated; carapace with supraorbital and pterygostomian spines; 5th pereopod developed and is the longest; pereopods 3 and 4 appear; 3rd biramous and 4th uniramous; endopod of uropod with plumose setae; telson with 6 pairs of spines, outermost non-setose.

Zoea V: length 2.9mm-3.0mm.Rudimentary chela present on 1st 2 pereopods; uniramous buds of pleopods on all abdominal somites present; telson rectangular.

Zoea VI: length 3.7mm.Chela of pereopods 1 and 2 well developed; legs 3 and 4 are also developed; telson narrowes posteriorly with 2 lateral and 5 terminal spines.

Zoea VII: length 4.2mm.Second leg with larger chela, forming a claw at tip; endopods 5-segmented; legs 1-3 with reduced exopods and 4 and 5 without or with vestigial exopods.

Postlarva I - II: length 4.2mm-4.6mm.Rostrum with 9 dorsal and 1 or 2 ventral teeth; carapace with pterygostomian and branchiostegal spines; pleopods biramous and setose; dorsolateral spines on abdominal somites 5 and 6 disppear; exopod of uropod with 2 spines of which one is movable; telson posterirly convex with a single,stout seta (plumose) and 2 spines on either side; outermost spine is smaller, 2 lateral spines on either side,slightly shifted towards the base.
Characteristics:Eggs are carried with the females; they hatch out at a late stage as Zoea I; hepatic spine absent on carapace; rostrum with dorsal and ventral teeth; first 2 pereopods chelate; delayed development of the 3rd and 4th legs; reduced or vestigial exopods on legs 1-3 and absence of exopods on legs 4 and 5.
