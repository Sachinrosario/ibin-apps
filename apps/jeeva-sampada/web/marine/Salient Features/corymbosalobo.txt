Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of cup-shaped corallites, larger colonies 50-75 cm in greater spread.  Branches close-set, mostly, monostomodaeal, sometimes valleys upto 7 cm long and 2 cm wide with 2 to 3 columella centres.  Monostomodaeal corallites 2-4 cm wide and 1 cm deep.  Wall thick, often with an apitheca.  Septa alternating in size, 8-10 per cm length of thecal wall, major septa about 1 mm thick, exsert upto 5 mm, edges with 5 to 7 teeth the top most being the largest.  Almost all the septa reach the columella.  Columella centres well differentiated, about 1 cm in diameter, in polystomodaeal valleys the adjacent ones are liked by toothed lamellae.  Costae correspond to septa, getting less prominent at the proximal parts of the wall, with 1-3 spines at the upper half.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  The species was abundant in Minicoy till early eighties, but mostly died out due to dredging of the lagoon.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
