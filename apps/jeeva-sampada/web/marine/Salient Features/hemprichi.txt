SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Calicinal surface usually convex, general shape more or less hemispherical.  Valley continuous and sinuous, or sometimes discontinuous but invariably meandering, width about 20 mm, (upto 35 mm), depth 10-12 mm (upto 20 mm).  Corallites with mono-to-tri-stomodaeal polyps absent.  Walls upto 6 or 7 mm in thickness.

Principal septa thick (1.5 or 2 mm), the more distant ones curving to meet columellar centres, margins with 4-6 teeth, exsert to 4-6 mm (including teeth, exsert ends with two conspicuous teetch 2-3 mm high.  Subsidiary septa thin, alternating with the thick principals.  Columella centres usually well developed, composed of closely twisted trabeculae, 4 or 5 mm wide and 10 13 mm apart, connected by 3 or 4 lamellae.  Costae conspicuous at rim, below margin of valley represented by small teeth placed far apart  (Matthai, 1928, p. 221).

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
