SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Body short, robust and hispid. Carapace produced infront into broad triangular rostral plate with pointed apex. Second thoracic endopod in male with large second segment, fifth segment expanded with two rounded teeth on distal half of inner border. Third thoracic endopod with three segmented carpopropodus furnished with four modified setae on distal end; seventh segment small. Exopod of third pleopod of male eleven segmented and, segments seven to eleven with modified setae; terminal segment ending in two spinose setae; ninth and tenth segments armed with shorter spinose seta at outer corner, and larger spinose seta at inner corner; seventh, eighth and ninth segments with bent seta at outer corner and long plumose seta at inner corner. Lateral border of telson and cleft armed with spines. Uropod shorter than telson, endopod longer than exopod, inner margin armed with spines arranged in series, exopod with 15 – 20 spines along outer margin. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Adult male : 5.5-7.4 mm
