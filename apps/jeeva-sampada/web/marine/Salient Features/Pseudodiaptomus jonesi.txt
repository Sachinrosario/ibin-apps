SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        Female:  Prosome and pedigerous segment 1 imperfectly fused. Corners of pedigerous segment 5 are obtuse with fine hairs at margins of urosome insertion.  Urosome 1 assymetrical, with rows of spinules placed dorsolaterally on the left side and a few spinules on the rught side, ventrally produced, genital boss with a pair of spines and proximally with a few spinules. Genital area with a row of spines and another row proximally. Urosome 1-3 with posterodorsal spine rows. Urosome 4 distal dorsolateral angles with shields. Caudal rami three times longer than wide, with six terminal setae on each. Urosome segments and caudal rami with proportions 32: 16: 20: 11: 21.  Leg 5 typical. Basipod 1 with spinule row, basipod 2 with surface seta, external rami 1 – 2 with distolateral setae. External rami 3 with spiniform process slightly longer than external rami 2 spiniform process.    

Male:  Prosome – urosome ratio 1.9:1. Right A1 geniculate with 21 segments. Urosome 2-4 with posterodorsal spine rows, urosome 2 with a ventral spinule row. Caudal rami 2.5 times longer than wide. Urosome segments and caudal rami with proportions 13: 22: 17: 17: 11: 20.     Right and left basipod 2 of leg 5 with spinule rows. Right leg:  Small spinules present on the oblique distolateral edge of external rami 1, spinules on external rami 2 between Se and external rami 3.     

Size and age: Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Female: 1.10 – 1.14 mm	Male:     0.90 – 0.93 mm
