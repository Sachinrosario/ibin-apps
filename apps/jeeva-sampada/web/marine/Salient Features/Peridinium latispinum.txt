SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells Globose to spherical. Epitheca surmounted by a straight cylindrical apical tube. Girdle slightly dextral, displaced about half a girdle width.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus narrow, edges winged, terminating in two Antapical winged spines, right one of average shape, left one slightly curved than the other. Theca surface smooth, very finely punctate.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 70-90µ, breadth: 50-60µ.
