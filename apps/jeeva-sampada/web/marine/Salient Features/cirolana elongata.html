SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:  Ref No:
Typical cirolanid with headhaving the characteristic bilobed front margin strongly developed in larger specimens. The males have the stylet on pleopod separate from and about as lons as the inner ramus. The vasadeferentia open on the seventh sternite flush with the surface, or in a very slightly raised tubercle, the opening about 0.65 mm apart. The penial processes may developed in larger males.
  No rostral point, based of first antennae contiguous telson and uropod transparent and delicate.

Size and age: Length of female 17 mm Length of male 12 mm
