
SALIENT  FEATURES :

Diagnostic characteristics:             

Female: Prosome fused with 1st pedigerous segment. Last pedigerous segment fused, but the line of fusion partially visible. Prosome with 1st antenna in dorsal view give this species a characteristic shape. Posterior - lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment angular and blunt. Urosome 4-segmented. In 1st antenna proximal part characteristically flattened and with hyaline fringe. Fifth pair of legs absent.           
MALE: Urosome 5-segmented. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                        Female:   1.15mm          Male   :    1.4mm
