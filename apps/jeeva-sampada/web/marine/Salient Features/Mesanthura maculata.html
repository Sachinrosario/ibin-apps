Salient features:
Morpholical:

  Head almost squarish, with a prominent blunt rostrum; eyes large. Peraeon segments one to six subequal iin length, seventh small, dorsal surface conspicuously pitted. Pleon equal in length to the sixth peraeon segment and its first three sutures laterally visible.
  First antenna with first segment of peduncle large, flagellum three jointed, with sensory setae; second antenna stout, flagellum five jointed and brush like. Maxilliped five jointed, third segment narrower than the adjacent ones. First maxilla apically four dentate, inner lobe minute, with a single seta. Second maxilla with two long setae. Mandibular palp stout, third segment smallest, cutting edge feebly toothed.
  First peraeopod stout, fifth segment apically blunt and slightly projecting, sixth with a median internal bulging, without teeth. Anterior peraeopods with fifth segment clearly underriding sixth but those of posterior legs normal, posterior legs with sixth segment serrated on the inferior border and with an apical spine.
  Body yellowish white, with suqrish black areas on the peraeon segments and the pleon. Each segment of the endopod of uropod with a similar coloured patch.
