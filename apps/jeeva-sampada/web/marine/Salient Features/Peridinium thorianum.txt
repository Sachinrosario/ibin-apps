SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells in ventral view with typical rhomboidal outline and convex to almost straight sides. Cross-section round. Girdle striated, excavated, its ends displaced about a furrow width. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus straight, slightly broad with lightly developed lists. Papillae present on plate surface.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 56-70µ.
