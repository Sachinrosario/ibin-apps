Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics:
Body slender. Carapace with prominent lateral indentation at anterior third, frontal plate forming low convex rostrum exposing base of eyes, antennules and antennae. Eyes large and globular. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle, outer border naked, curved and ending in large conical tooth. Thoracic endopods three to eight long and slender, carpopropodus three segmented and armed with different type of setae. Pleopods two to five biramous setae not modified; endopod of first single segmented. Telson elongate, triangular, distal half of lateral border with 10-15 spines slowly increasing in length, apex with pair of long spines, no setae. Uropods longer than telson, endopod with swollen base enclosing large statocyst.
