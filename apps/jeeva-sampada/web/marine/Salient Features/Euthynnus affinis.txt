Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Information on eggs is not available. Ripe ovum is reported to be of having an average diameter of 0.99 mm with single oil globule of 0.25 mm.     

Larval stages are characteristically long and slender and head conspicuously large. The abdominal sac is triangular, small and anteriorly situated. The vent is far forward of the mid point of total length. Eye diameter is more than the length of snout in early stages. Seven preopercular spines could be distinguished. There are 39 myomeres, of which 20 are precaudal and 19 caudal. Teeth are present on both jaws.     

Pigments are present on the membrane covering the brain and the sides of the head. An important distinguishing character is the presence of an irregular row of dark chromatophores along the posterior portion of the ventral midline, and a series of 4 or 5 chromatophores in anterior half of the mandible.
