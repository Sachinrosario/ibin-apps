SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:            FEMALE:  Prosome is triangular. The lateral angles of the last pedigerous segment are pointed. There are 2 segments between the anal and the genital segments. The 1st and 2nd segments of exopodite of the 2nd antenna are not fused; the 1st segment of endopodite is slightly longer than the 2nd, and over 3 times as long as its broad.     
The inner margin of the 2nd segment of basipodite of the mandible with 3 setae, is divided into 2 nearly equal portions by the insertion of the endopodite. The endopodite has 2 setae on the 1st segment, 5 setae on the 2nd.The maxilla with the 2nd lobe. The 3rd lobe of maxilla has 4 setae. The 2nd segment of basipodite has 5 marginal setae.          

MALE:  The lateral angles of the last pedigerous segment are pointed as like as the female. Both feet of the 5th pair are present ; the terminal segments with 1 seta respectively.
