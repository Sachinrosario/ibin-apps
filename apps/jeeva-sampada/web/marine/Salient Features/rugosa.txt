Descriptive characters:  The following is a revised description of the species base on many specimens examined.

Corallum attached spreading, unifacial, sometimes edges folded upward or inwards.  Calicinal surface with conspicuous collines, irregularly swollen, discontinuous, sometimes swelling up with secondary collines.  Height of collines, 2.5 to 3 mm, narrower at the summit than at the base, triangular in profile view, distance between top to top of adjacent collines average 3 mm.  s
Septacontinuous over the collines, 18 to 20 per cm length of collines, subequal of uniform width from top to bottom.  Septal edges minutely dentate, sides granular.  Columella lamellar, continuous or interrupted.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
