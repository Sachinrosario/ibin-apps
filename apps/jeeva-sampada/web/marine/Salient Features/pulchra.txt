SALIENT FEATURES:

Genus Hemisiriella Hansen

Diagnosis :  Carapace short.  Antennal scale shorter than antennal peduncle.  Endopod of third thoracic limb extremely elongated, sixth joint divided into two sub-joints, dactylus rudimentary.  Endopod of fourth to eight thoracic limbs as in genus  Siriella. Pseudobranchial lobes on second to fourth pleopods spirally twisted, none of the setae modified.  Telson and uropods nearly as in Siriella.

Diagnostic characteristics:      Body slender. Frontal plate low, triangle, with bluntly pointed apex. Antennal scale much shorter than antennular peduncle. Labrum with long spiniform frontal process. Third thoracic endopod extremely elongated, distal part of ischium carrying three strong spines with barbed edges, dactylus reduced; thoracic endopods four to eight with long slender dactylus. Pleopods not modified; pseudobranchial lobes spirally coiled. Telson not reaching articulation of exopod, 2 2/5 times as long as broad at base, steadily narrowing to rounded apex with well marked constriction at basal ¼, proximal widened part with two pairs of spines, distal lateral margin with 7-9 spines, apex with three small spines and two pairs of long spines, outer pair longer than inner pair. In female distal half of telson broader, apex more broadly rounded, lateral and distal spines longer and apical spines somewhat flattened. Endopod of uropod as long as or slightly shorter than endopod with dense row of spines on inner margin; exopod with 5-6 spines on outer margin.
