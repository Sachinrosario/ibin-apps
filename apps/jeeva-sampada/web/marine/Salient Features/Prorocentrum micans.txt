SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Armored, small, spheroid to pyriform in valve view. Convex to concave in lateral view. Cell with two anterior dissimilar flagella.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Cells with chloroplasts. Surface markings vary from pores to areolae to spines. Two opposing valves, one designated left and the other right.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Width: 22m.
