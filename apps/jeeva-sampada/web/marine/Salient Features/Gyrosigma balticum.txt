SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves linear with obliquely truncate and obtuse ends, Raphe slightly excentric and somewhat flexuous.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Central area small, oblique. Transverse and longitudinal striae equidistant, 12 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 294-332m long and 29-38m broad.
