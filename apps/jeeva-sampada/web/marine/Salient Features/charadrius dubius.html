SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Wading small birds. Beak pigeon like. Legs not very long, yellow or pinkish.







Diagnostic characteristics: Sandy brown upper points and white forehead. Black fore crow separated from sandy brown crown by a white line. White neck ring and a complete black band round the neck separates the collar from back.






Sex attributes:


Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.




Meristic characteristics:





Feeding habit: Feeds in mud flats and river beds and grassy shore land.







Main Food: Insects, sand hoppers, tiny crabs etc.



Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder






Additional remarks: Nests from March to May. Eggs are laid in open but harmonized with the surroundings by arranging pebbles and coloured stones.
