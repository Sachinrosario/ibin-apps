SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Female : Body ovoid with the anterior half tapering and slightly flattened, front margin bluntly rounded and not deflexed. On the mid-dorsal surface faint indications of segmentations are visible. The lateral parts of the body except at the anterior and the posterior ends are expanded and deflexed ventrally to overlap in the mid-ventral line and enclose a space, the brood cavity, leaving a fairly large opening at the anterior end and a slightly smaller opening at the posterior end. Inside the anterior opening is situated the mouth around which the cephalic and four pairs of thoracic appendages are crowded together (fig.4). The posterior opening houses a pair of uropods and the anal opening (fig.1). A pair of comparatively very small and apparently three segmented antenna is present, the members of the pair being placed far apart and situated at the anterior region of the ventral deflextion of the lateral parts of the body (fig 4A). The thoracic legs are apparently five segmented and sub-chelate ; the terminal segment is very small and claw-like (fig.3). The posterior pairs of legs are slightly more slender and longer than the anterior ones. From the base of each leg extends a brood-plate. Each uropod has about five blunt prominences (fig.5).

The juvenile female (figs. 6-10). In the juvenile specimen the ventral deflection of the lateral parts of the body has not taken place with the result that cephalic and the thoracic appendages are more spread out unlike in the adult. The body is broader towards the anterior end and gradually tapers posteriorly. The dorsal profile is arched and the ventral nearly flat. The segmentation of the body is shown by faint markings.

The first antenna are represented by a pair of unsegmented tubercles arising anteriorly (fig.10, A1). The second antenna arise laterally behind the first and are five-segmented. (fig, 10.A2). There is fairly large mouth-cone directed downwards and forwards and enclosing a pair of styliform mandibles (fig, 10, Md). Behind the mandible is a pair of tiny maxillipeds (fig10, Mxp). Four pairs of legs are present, the anterior pairs being slightly shorter and stouter than the posterior ones. The legs are chelate with the propodus swollen and large and the dactylus small and curved. (fig .9). The uropods are represented by two short pegs at the hind end of the body.

Size and Age: about 0.94 mm
