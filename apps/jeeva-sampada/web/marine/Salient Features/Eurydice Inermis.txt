Salient feature:
Morpholigcal:

	Body elongate oblong and moderately convex. Head twice as broad as long, almost semicircular; antero-median part forming a small point; eyes large and black. Sixth thoracic segment longest and broadest, seventh subequal to fifth; all the coxal plates well developed, those of the seventh segment considerably produced and acute. All the six abdominal segments distinct, their lateral corners acute and produced in dorsal view. Telson almost as long as broad, lateral borders serrate, the serrations becoming conspicuous towards the apex, apex truncate, with nine strong teeth, extreme lateral teeth long and pointed; ,margin between the extreme lateral teeth slightly concave and with seven teeth, the odd one in the middle being larger. 
	First antenna when bent backwards reaching a little beyond the penultimate penduncular segment of the second; first segment of penduncle vertically expanded, second broader than long; flagellum four jointed, first flagellar segment much longer than the other three combined and with several bunches of long setae. Second antenna reaching beyond the fifth thoracic segment; third penduncular segment robust, with stout setae, fourth longer; flagellum twenty jointed. Second segment of palp of mandible longer than the other two combined.
	Fifth segment of first peraeopod considerably immersed in fourth; seventh peraeopod shorter than sixth but more setose and spiny.
	Rami of uropods each with two apical spines and endopod stopping far short of the apex of telson.
	Body white, with a grayish mottling due to the presence of profusely branched chromatophores.

Additional remarks:

	Eurjdice inermis can be easily distinguished by the very short serrate posterior margin of the abdomen devoid of movable spines. The posterior border of the telson is only one-fourth the breadth of telson.

Size and age: Maximum length : 5.0 mm.
