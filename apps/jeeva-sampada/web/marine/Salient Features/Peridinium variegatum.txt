SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells round to egg shaped, medium sized, very variable. Girdle strongly dextral, ends displaced a girdle width.

Diagnostic characteristics: The sulcus widened towards the antapical pole and is enclosed by two wings of which the left is usually strongly developed.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 50-85µ; breadth: 50-90µ.
