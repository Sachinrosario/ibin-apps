Salient Features:
Morphological:

Head very short, slightly broader than long, rostrum prominent; eyes poorly developed and composed of a linear row of three to ofur lenses. Peraeon segments one and two subequal, third short, four to six again long and equal, seventh short. Pleon as long as the last two peraeon segments combined, segments becoming seccessively shorter backwards.

First antenna shorter than second, peduncle three jointed, with simple but stout plumose setae, flagellum three jointed and not clearly demarcated from the peduncle. Peduncle of second antenna five jointed, ultimate segment long with a two jointed secondary flagellum and a number of plumose setae; flagellum seven to eight jointed, basal segment large.

Maxilliped four jointed, second segment large and completely fused with the opposite number, ultimate segment with a subapical inner construction and three apical setae. Maxilla with five long spines. Mandible not observed.

First peraeopod with basis and ischium subequal merus externally drawn out, twice as broad as long, carpus short, its inner border cut into three lobes, propodus very large and oval, palmar border lobed serrated as in brevitelson, dactylus very long, overreaching the apex of carpus, with a prominent unguis and a short slender subsidiary spine. Second pereopod subequal to first, carpus long and projecting, its inner border broadly crenate; Propodus comparitively short, palmar border very short and serrated ; dactylus short and the unguis reaching beyond th apex of fifth segment. Third peraeopod subequal to second merus and carpus internally produced, former with a row of stout spines and both with long apical hairs; propodus comaritively short, dactylus shorter , with a very small unguis. Pereopods four to seven very short, merus and carpus small, each with a blunt spine, fifth segment slightly underriding sixth, sixth segment roughly oblong, with three serrated spines close to the distal border; dactylus stout, with five spine setae on the outer side; secondary unguis present. 

Pleopods similar, peduncle with hairy margin, produced inwards and carrying two apical hooks; endopod membranoius and non setose; inner border of exopod hairy, outer and distal borders with stout plumose setae.

Uropods and telson as in X. brecitelson Barnard.

Body White, with dark chromatophores.

Additional Remarks:
X. Linearis closelyresembles X. brevitelson Barnard, except in the following characters: The fifth segment of first and second peraeopods not apically acute, inferior border not spiny of serrated, but cut into orunded lobes. Seventh segment of first peraeopod long and reaching beyond the fifth segment. Pereopods four to seven with long spines on the sixth segment. Maxilliped four jointed and all the segments clearly visible. Endopod of uropod proximally expanded and with a group of four dagger shaped setae in the middle of the inner margin and the exopods overlapping the telson.

Among the similarities may be mentioned the presence of a two jointed secondary flagellum on the second antenna, the serrate palm of the firsr peraeopod, the spiny inferior border of the fourth segment of the third peraeopod and the externally serrate dactyli of the posterior peraeopods.

An important point noticed is the number of segments in the maxilliped. According to Barnard, the maxilliped in Xenanthura is three jointed. In some specimens the maxilliped was clearly four jointed and rarely the distal segment has a minute apical joint, thus making it five jointed. Even in specimen with a three jointed maxilliped, marginal constrictions are present. These constriction indicate that in this genus the number of segments of the maxilliped is perhaps getting progresseively reduced.

Size and age:  Length: 3.0 mm.
