
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells roundish oval. Apical horn short. Girdle broadly winged, dextral. Left sulcal border with broad wing, which runs curved towards antapex.

Diagnostic characteristics: On the right side winged border narrower, ending in a process. Rhomboid plate more or less asymmetrical.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length and breadth: 44-52µ.
