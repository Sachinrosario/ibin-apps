SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Female : Body roughly oval, slightly asymmetrical; ventral side moderately convex and the lateral borders upturned, abdomen bent upwards. Cephalon prominently raised from the rest of the body, transversely expanded and composed of two roughly oval lobes placed at an angle with each other and separated by a shallow groove. Anterior border very short. Frontal lamina narrow and more or less encircling the anterior and lateral borders of cephalon. Posterior lamina with two lobes , outer long and narrow and the inner short but broad. All the peraeon segments distinct, dorsomedian part depressed. Ovarian bosses prominent, anterior four pairs conspicuous. Sixth and seventh segments with a forwardly directed dorso-median process, that of sixth broad and rounded anteriorly and of seventh somewhat triangular with a rounded apex. Pleon segm,ents though indistinct, visible.


Lateral plates very long , surface smooth and borders tuberculated, folded over the dorsal surface of the body as in Tylocepon bonnieri; the anterior most reaches the posterior border of the third peraeon segment. Pleopods biramous, inner ramus short, outer long, lamellar and perfectly smooth. Uropods uniramous, similar to the pleopods but slightly longer.

First antenna three jointed and robust, second four segmented and stout. Posterior lobe of first oostegite slightly produced, inner crest fairly prominent. Maxilliped apparently without a palp, anterior part irregularly produced and the posterior lobe triangular. Peraeopods compact and stout, basis with a moderately developed boss.

Body straw yellow with the grooves between segments brownish yellow.

The general shape of the body and the elightly wavy margin of the lateral plates of the pleon are distinguishing characters of Paracepon merstraszi. However the species shows several differences with the general type and it is with some reservation Pillai (1958) included the species under Paracepon. According to him the specimen cannot be accomadated in any of the other known genera and hence pending discovery of additional specimens , the species is provisionally included in paracepon.
