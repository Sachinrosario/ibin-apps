Descriptive characters:  Corallum, composed of flattened reticulate branches with wide interspaces.  Tips of branches blunt.  Branches 1 to 1.5 cm wide.  Top branches 5 to 20 mm long,

Colour yellowish brown, in living condition.

Reproduction by medusa.

Main food:  Plankton.
