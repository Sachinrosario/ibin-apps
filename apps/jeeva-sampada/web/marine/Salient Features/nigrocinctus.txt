SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  Head moderate; a small loreal usually present; one, rearely 2, pre - and 1 or 2 postoculars; temporals 2+3, small and not distinguishable from other scales; 7-9 supralabials, the 2nd normally not in contact the prefrontal, the 3rd, 4th and 5th in contact with the eye, the last four or five very small; 4 lower labials touching the genials, the posterior pair of which are separated by scales.  Body elongate, robust (in the adult),  scales imbricate throughout and strongly keeled, in from 39-45 rows at midbody; ventrals 296-330, not twice as broad as the adjacent scales; preanals considerably enlarged.

Diagnostic characteritics:  The normal colour is white in the young and light grey in the adult, with 45-57 broad black bands encircling the body; these bands, somewhat wider dorsally and narrower on sides, are usually connected along the line of ventrals.  Head, chin and throat uniform black; a yellow streak on the temporal region.  Tail black or with lateral bars.
