SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Body exteremly flat, head with a short but broad rostrum. Eyes composed of two large Ocelli. Paraeon segments increasing in length to the fourth segments 4-7 subequal. Segments comparatively deeply icised with the free borders spiny. First three segments bent forwards, fourth nearly straight and 5-7 bent backwards. Coxal plates distinct free
Abdomen nearly equal in length and breadth, with the postero-median part angular.

First antenna short, six segmented; first segment very stout, second longest last two segments with one sensory seta each. Second antenna with 14-17 segmented flagellum; mandible with accessory lobe and median setae second segment of palp with 2 and third with 9 comb like aetae. Outer lobe of maxillule with spines iner with four setae, its outer distal angle produced into a hairy lobe. Palp of maxilipped comparatively narrow endite with two coupling hooks. Inferior claw of DACTYLUS of Peraeopods simple, first leg with two and the rest with three claws. Uropoda about half the length of Pleotelson.

Additional Remarks :

Iais singaporensis also is found in asosiation with Sphaeroma Terebrans.
Specimens of Iais singaporensis show considerable variation in characters and Menzies and Barnard (1951) suggest that this species has to be split into more than one species.
