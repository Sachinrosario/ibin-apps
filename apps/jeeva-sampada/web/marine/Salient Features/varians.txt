SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Encrusting, unifacial, upperside hydnophoroid with collines 2-4 mm high and 1 to 2 cm long enclosing one to tow rows of calicinal centers.  Distance between adjacent centers 1 to 3 mm.  Septa very narrow close together, about 30 per 5 mm length of colline, continuous over the colline or those of the opposite side fusing at the middle of the colline.  Edges of septa entire, sides granular.  Columella a single compressed style.  Living corallum yellow.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
