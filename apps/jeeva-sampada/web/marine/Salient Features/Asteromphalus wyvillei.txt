SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Round cells. Central area smaller compared to former species. Hyaline radii 15, more or less straight, 2.5 m broad; one considerably narrower. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Sector lines branched, segments wedge shaped, areolated in three lines system. Areolae 12 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 70m.
