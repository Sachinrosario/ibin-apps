SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum reptoid, often with isolated encrusting corallites usually found on the underside of dead corals.  Corallites rounded, 2-3 mm in diameter, rarely 4 mm; 2-3 mm in height.  A well developed epitheca present.  Total number of septa 20-24, of which the first two cycles are very broad and prominent.  Septa exsert, primaries the most, exsert parts vertical, not arched, with one or two deep notches.  Septal edges weakly dentate, sides granulose.  Twelve septa reach the columella.  Columella fill about one-third the bottom of the fossa with a few vertical papillae.

The species is found attached to other reef corals and is not a dominant element among reef corals.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
