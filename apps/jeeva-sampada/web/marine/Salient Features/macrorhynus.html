SALIENT FEATURES:         Ref. No. 3,4
Morphological: A medium sized species.
Diagnostic characteristics : snout rounded , projecting ,mouth horizontal and inferior, rostral pores 5, marginal pore 5; the marginal dividing the snout into deep lobes; mental pores 5 ; teeth differentiated into large and small in upper jaw, the large ones not widely spaced, forming the outer series; lower jaw with outer villiform teeth and slightly enlarged inner teeth posteriorly ; no canine teeth; gillrakers on lower limb of first arch 5 to 8, short and stumpy with minute spines, Dorsal fin with 10 spines, followed by deep notch, second part of the fin with one short spine and 26 to 30 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 soft rays, the second spine weak and about ¼ of head length, Caudal fin rhomboid. Pectoral fin, about 3/5 of head length. Scales on the body cycloid; lateral line scales reaching to tip of caudal fin. Gas bladder hammer shaped , with about 13 to 14 pairs of appendages , the first arborescent tubule branching into the head and sending an outer palmate branch to the front of pectoral arch.

Colour : Lower part of body with a golden tinge when fresh; pectoral , pelvic, and anal fins yellow; a faint steel blue blotch on opercle.
