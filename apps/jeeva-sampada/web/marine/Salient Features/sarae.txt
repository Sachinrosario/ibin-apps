SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  General form slender. Hind border of carapace without filaments or lobes. Endopod of third pleopod of male 7 segmented; exopod 4 segmented, fourth segment terminating in two stout spines, outer spine longer and barbed. First pleopod in female uniramous. Lateral border of telson with 23 to 25 short and long spines not arranged in definite series, cleft with 21 to 22 pairs of spines. Endopod of uopod with 13 to 16 spines.                             

Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Male   :  7.3 mm,  Female : 9.7 mm
