SALIENT  FEATURES :                         
Diagnostic characteristics:                  
Female: Rostrum in lateral view usually thick, short, and slightly curved. Ventral profile of the genital segment in lateral view somewhat undulant. Genital segment length equal to or less than urosome segment 3 and slightly greater than the length of urosome segment 2. Ventral lobe of seminal receptacle in lateral view relatively small; dorsal lobe large and bulbous; dorsal lobe conspicuous in lateral, dorsal, and ventral views. Antenna 1 with a single aesthete on segments 2-7, 10-19, 22, and fused segments 24-25; 2 aesthetes on fused segments 8-9. Leg 3 with form and spacing of basipod 2 spiniform processes in 2 and 3. Segment 3 of leg 5 equal to or more than 2.0 times as long as segment 1; terminal points of segment 3 never spinulate.            
Male: Rostrum in lateral view not well developed and not protruding ventrally. Leg 3 with basipod 2 spiniform processes 2 and 3 usually not tapering uniformly from their bases. Longer ramus of leg 5 and genital pore usually on the right side, occasionally on the left; longer ramus of leg 5 longer than the urosome; shorter ramus usually 2-segmented, distal ramus usually 2- segmented, distal segment small.

Additional  remarks:  This species belong to Frost and Fleminger’s(1968) Group III.
