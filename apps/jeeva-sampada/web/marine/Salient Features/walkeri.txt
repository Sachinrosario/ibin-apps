Salient Features:
Morphological:

Body elongate ovate, surface highly tubercular, large tubercles mixed with small ones of various sizes producing a warty appearance. Border of segments  beaded in large specimens.

Cephlon shot, with a transverse row of 5-6 low tubersles. Each pareaon segment with a transverse ridge, and all except the first, with 1-2 transverse rows of large tubercles anterior low laterally dividing into two rows in some specimens. Posterior border of segments beaded. Coxal plates with a longitudianal row of small tubercles. posterior border of pleon with a row of large tubercles and 2 oblique lateral rows of smaller ones. Telson almost equal in length and breadth, posterior border rounded. ridge like conspiciously raise and continued on to the dorsal side at the middle of the lateral borders. Tubercl;es longitudinally elongated and falling into 3 pairs of longitudinal rows the median row stopping a little short of the apex of the telson. The entire dorsal side is pustulose.

Anntennule comparitively slender, first segment of peduncule stout, with a conspicous external furrow, surface granular; third segmentslender; flagellum 14 segmented . Second antenna stout, peduncular segments succesively increasing in length, first 3 segments hairy flagellum 14 segmented, proximal 9-10 segments with a thick bunch of hairs

Epistome broad throughout, with a subapical construction apex, angular and posterior arms very short, Exposed surface with large pustules.

Mandible comparitively weak, incisor process conical and tridentate that of left with a bidentate ancessory lobe. Right mandible with 5 and left with 8 middle spines.

Maxillule with a Prominently hirsute outer lobe; spine on the outer side barbed on one side, distal border with 12 spines, all except 2 barbed. Inner lobe with a patch of hair internally and a row of setae externaly; distal border with 3 stout pectinate and barbe setae and 2 small pulmose setae. Maxilla similar to that of S. Terbrans.

Maxilleped with the sympod short but stout, inner plate broad and triangular; pal stout, segments 2 and 3 slightly produced, fifth segment longer than fourth.

Lower lip broader than in S. Terebrans and narrower than in S. Annandalei; distal par prominently hairy.

Five peraeopod with basis and ischium subequal carpus triangular. Lower distal spine on propodus barbed on the upper side and with a trigger like spinule. Ventral spine smaller than in S. Terebrans. Segments 3-6 with busy hairs on,lower border. Seventh peraepod very long thickly hairy; basis longer than ischium, segments 2-5 expanded, merus distally  produced with 8-10 stout setae, carpus with an outer row of 16 and  an inner row of 6 barbed spines; propodus very long, perfectly smooth in large specimens.

Pleopods as in S. Terebrans. Male Stylet on second pleopod less than one and half times as long as endopod. Apical lobe of endopod of fourth pleopod directed backwards, hairy and with 2 setae. Exopod of fifth pleopod with 5 spiny thickened areas, proximally segment with an inner distal seta. Penial process slightly elongated, apically rounded and placed a little apart.

Rami of uropods subequal endopod with 4 large and a few small tubercles; exopod with 5 teeth in addition to  the apical tooth.

Body white with faint dark chromatophores scattered all over.

Diagnostic Characteristics:
Typical isopod epistome broad throughout; entire dorsal surface of body tuberculate; telson with transverse rows of tubercles.

Feeding Habit: Browsing and substratum

Main Food: may be the same food like other species of spheoroma

Additional Remarks: 
The number and the arrangement of the tubercles on the telson show slight variation even among specimens from the same locality. In large 
specimen like the tubercles merge to produce prominent warts. In small specimens, however, the tubercles are distinct; the anterior segments have an anterior row, a short lateral low and an indistinct posterior row.

According to barnard (1936) the male stylet is one and a half times the length of the endopod. It was slightly shorter. In all the specimens the exopod of the uropod has only 5 teeth, exclusive of the tooth like apex. The flagellum of the first antenna has upto 14 segments and that opf the segment is conspicously setose in the male.

Compared to that of the other species, the incisor process of the mandible is rather weak and the spines on the outer lobe of the first maxilla pointed and barbed. In truly wood boring species some of the spines are strongly chitinised and blunt showing that they assist the mandibles. The difference in S. Walkeri is presumabely due to its free living habit.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size Recorded 13.5mm
