Salient Features :
It is an erect dwarf herb, 10-20 cm tall in height.
Stem slender, much branched, very hairy.
Leaves 0.4 0.8 cm, ovavate or elliptic.
Flowers white to pink, usually in small clusters in the axils of the upper leaves.
Seeds in fully ripe, capsules usually solitary.
