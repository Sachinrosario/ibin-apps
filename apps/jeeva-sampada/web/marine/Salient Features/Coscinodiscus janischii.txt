SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells with almost flat valves, which are slightly concave in the centre. Central area small. Areolae on a small marginal zone well marked and in the remaining slender, almost of same size 4 in 10µ; but at the margin 2.5 in 10m. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Chamber openings clear. Valve margin small, striated. Two pore canals placed asymmetrically.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 210m.
