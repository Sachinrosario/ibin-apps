SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Diagnostic characters: Second dorsal and anal fin spines greatly elongated (sometimes reaching  to caudal fin). A few faint unevenly spaced and horizontally elongate blotches on the back.

Sex attributes: Sexes undistinguishable externally

Descriptive characters: Body oval and compressed. Anterior part of the dorsal profile more strongly arched than the anterior part of the ventral profile The upper profile of head with a gentle concavity. Snout blunt. Mouth small and pointing downward when protracted. Cleft of mouth below lower edge of eye. Lower margin of lower jaw slightly concave. Teeth, small close set, in numerous rows. Supraorbital edge smooth. Two small spines above the anterior superior angle of the eye, inner one being the weaker and placed further forward. Pre-operculum with its lower arm distinctly and finely serrated. Minute scales covering entire body except head and chest. 
Lateral line strongly convex, extends beyond end of soft dorsal and anal, but stops a short  distance in front of the caudal fin. Dorsal spines weak. 2nd spines of dorsal and anal fin greatly elongated, the 2nd dorsal spine more than half the length of the body and much higher than the height of the body. 2nd anal spine elongate, as long as the head. 4th dorsal spine serrated at the base. Caudal  forked. Pelvic fins not reaching the origin of the anal fins. 

Colour: Abdomen more silvery than back, which shows a few unevenly spaced horizontally elongate grey brown streaks or blotches. Variable number of yellow blotches  along flank below lateral line.Soft anal and margin of soft dorsal fin yellow. Underside of pectoral fin base with minute black dots. Tip of snout grey. Margin of caudal lobes light grey.

Main food: It feeds mainly on small crustaceans and nematodes.          Ref no: 3
