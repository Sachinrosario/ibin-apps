SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Semicircular valves with ventral margin straight and obtuse end. Central area somewhat large and hyaline.

Diagnostic characteristics: Areolation fine, radiating from the centre, about 12 in 10µ. Spinule around the margin, with hyaline ribs arising from them and running to the centre.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
