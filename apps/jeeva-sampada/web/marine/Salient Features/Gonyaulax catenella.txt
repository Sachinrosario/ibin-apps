SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Medium sized, from chains of cells wider than long. Epi and hypotheca are equal in length. The APC is broad and sub triangular.

Diagnostic characteristics: The posterior sulcal plate is wide, almost five-sided, the attachment pore near the right margin.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 20-40mm, transdiameter: 18-37mm.

Biochemical aspects: Only healthy cultures produce endotoxins.
