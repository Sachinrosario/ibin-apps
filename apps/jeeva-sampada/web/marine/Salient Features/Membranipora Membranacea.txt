SALIENT FEATURES:  Morphological: Zoarium encrusting.  Very simple zooecia elongated.  Opesia occupy the whole front.  Gymnocyst and cryptocyst vestigial.  Operculum membraniporine.  Twinned ancestrula present.  Spines, avicularia and ovicells absent.  Diagnostic characteristics:   Presence of knobs at the distal corners, and "tower cells" in the median proximal part.   The ancestrula twinned with the simplicity in structure.  The typical shape of zooecia is elongated and quadrangular.
Sex attributes:  Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters:  Two differences were noticed in the material collected by Menon from what Osburn (1950) observed in his specimens.  The knobs at the distal corners and "tower cells" in the median proximal part noticed by Osburn were absent in the material collected by Menon.  	Differences were noticed in the shape of the zooecia in the young and old colonies.  The typical elongated quadrangular shape was seen only in old zooecia.  The size of the zooecium in the material collected by Menon was distinctly smaller than that observed by Borg in his specimens (Borg, 1931).
