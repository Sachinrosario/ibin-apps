Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum disc-shaped, rounded, flat or arched at the underside.  Sometimes, secondary mouths are developed.  Axial fossa very short.  Septal edges with uniform teeth, tooth average 1 mm in height, average 6 to 7 teeth per length of septum.  Sometimes some parts of septa devoid of any tooth.  Sides of septa granular.  Disc perforated.  Costae indistinct, represented by rows of spines which are larger at the middle and central parts of the disc than at the periphery.  Larger spines 3 to 5 mm in height, pointed sharp, sometimes adjacent ones fused to form clusters.  Diameter of the present specimens range from 9 to 20 cm.  Axial fossae 1.5 to 7 cm in length depending on the diameter of the corallum.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
