SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:

	Body comparatively stout, head slightly broader than long, eyes absent; rostrum very prominent, broadly triangular, apex rounded and slightly overreaching the lateral angles; peraeon segments almost sub equal, seventh shortest; pleon as long as sixth peraeon segment, segmentation medio-dorsally indistinct; telson broadly oval, apex perfectly rounded, statocysts paired and prominent.

	First antenna longer than head, first segment stout, second and third sub equal, flagellum short and two jointed, flagellum brush like, with indistinct segmentation. Mandibular palp stout, second segment very large and setose, third short and slender, with a row of six to seven setae. Maxilliped four jointed, last two segment hairy and with stout setae. Maxilla apically broad and cut into a large apical and five subapical teeth or hooks.

	First peraeopod stout, fifth segment slightly projecting, tooth on palmar border of sixth segment blunt, dactylus long, unguis half as long as the segment. Second and third peraeopods with fifth segment underriding sixth. Posterior peraeopods with sixth segment long and culindrical, its lower border serrate and carrying a large, barbed distal spine followed by an oblique row of four similar spines; dactylus with a small secondary unguis.

	Endopod of uropod slightly longer than telson, second segment broader than long, exopod reaching beyond the penduncle or uropod and folding over the telson.
	Length 7.0 mm.
	Body dirty brown, without chromatophores.
