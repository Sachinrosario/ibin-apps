Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of thick vertical, flabellate columns 10-15 cm in height and 10-15 cm broad, 3 to 5 cm thick.  Lower part often dead.  Calices superficial to 1 mm deep, 2 to 2.5 mm in diameter when measured from the outer ends of major septa.  Calices sometimes surrounded by low colonies, at places larger ones club-shaped; 8 to 12 reach the columella.  Axial fossa 0.5 mm in diameter with a single columellar style.  Septal edges dentate, sometimes bifurcated at the periphery.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
