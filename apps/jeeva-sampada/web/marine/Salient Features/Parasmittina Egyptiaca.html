SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Zoaria may consist of zooecia either separated by deep grooves arranged in longitudinal rows or arranged superposed. Pores small and areolae large. Front slightly convex and tuberculated. Secondary orifice with a narrow or broad sinus, demarcated by two cusps when narrow. Peristome moderate. Primary orifice broad distally with two small rounded spaces proximo-laterally created by two small proximally directed, pointed cardelles and a median dentate lyrule. Lyrule may be large or moderately sized. Large spatulate parallel-sided avicularia placed lateral to the orifice pointing proximally. Rostral tip somewhat broad. Avicularia rare. Avicularia of the pointed type absent. Ovicells globose, raised, ectooecium with regularly arranged small pores. Spines wanting. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:   The form under consideration resembles Smittina egyptiaca of Waters (1909). Minor differences were, however, noticed. Harmer (1957) observed small condyles in his specimens and they were present in the material collected by us as well, even though Waters (1909) has not noticed them in his type specimens, and therefore, not shown in his figures. Harmer (1957) states: "the condyles vary in size and may be very small". Here the condyles were rather small. The present form showed closer agreement with the Siboga material as they did not possess acute avicularia. Waters (1909) has stated that a small triangular avicularium is present directed proximally. The present form lacks this character completely. Spines are not represented in this form.Meristic characteristics:
