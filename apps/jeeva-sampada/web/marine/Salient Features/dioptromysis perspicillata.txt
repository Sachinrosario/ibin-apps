SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological: Diagnostic characteristics:      Body rather strongly built. Carapace produced into short apically blunt rostrum. Eyes large, cornea with large lens on distal lower part. Antennal scale setose all round, over reaching antennular peduncle. Labrum without frontal process. Thoracic endopods three to eight with three carpopropodal segments. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male seven segmented, seventh segment with long barbed spine and short seta. Telson more than twice as long as broad, apical cleft deep, one fourth total length, lateral border armed with eleven to thirteen sharp spines, more or less regularly distributed, last spine large, cleft armed with single pair of spines and pair of plumose setae. Endopod of uropod armed with row of seventeen sharp spines.
