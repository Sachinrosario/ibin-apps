Antennal scale short, broad, outer margin without setae, without apical spine, distal suture absent.  Tarsus of thoracic endopods three, four, six, seven and eight undivided, endopod of fifth limb longer than others with two jointed tarsus and prominent nail.  Eighth endopod much reduced, shorter than exopod.  Pleopods one, two, three and five small unjointed plates; fourth with basal joint carrying  one jointed endopod and large exopod terminating in very long seta, annulose at tip.  Telson stout, broad  triangular unarmed plate.  Uropods short and robust, subequal without spines, statocyst large.
