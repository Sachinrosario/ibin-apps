SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No:31,103

 

Morphological:  Rostrum nearly straight , uptilted and  armed with 6 to 9 teeth along entire

dorsal margin, reaching or exceeding 3rd antennular article in females, not extending beyond distal margin of 2nd article in males; epigastric tooth absent; postrostral crest broad, ending near middle of carapace; longitudinal suture reaching 2/3 of carapace length; epipods absent on 1st and 2nd pereopods; basial spine present on 1st and 2nd pereopods; basis of 3rd pereopod and telson unarmed. Distolateral projections of petasma with distal part slender in dorsal position, tapering and directed proximolaterally, distance between tips of projections about 2/5 of length of petasma; lateral lobes of petasma without wing-like projections. Anterior plate of thelycum semicircular and concave, posterior margin more or less straight; posterior plate broad and trapezoidal.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum without epigastric tooth, postrostral crest ending near

middle of carapace; epipods absent on first 2 pereopods; telson unarmed; distolateral projections of petasma with distal part slender, tapering and directed proximolaterally, distance between tips of same about 2/5 of length of petasma; anterior plate of thelycum semicircular and concave, its posterior margin more or less straight.

        Colour:   Pale with dull red specks on carapace.

Eggs and larvae:                  Ref. No:202

 

  Characteristics:   Eggs are spherical measuring 0.32 to 0.34 mm in diameter. The embryo hatches

out as nauplius in 8 hours after spawning. Five naupliar  and three protozoeal stages have been described based on laboratory rearing (Thomas et al.,1974). Protozoea III is 1.4 to 1.6 mm in total length. Its carapace bears a rostrum, and the posterior part extends upto 2 to 3 thoracic segments. Other distinguishing features are: compound eyes with stout stalks; longer abdominal segments 1 to 5, each segment with a backwardly directed dorsomedian spine; posterior border of 5th segment with an additional pair of posterolateral spine; 6th segment longest bearing a spine on each posterolateral corner; uropods well developed and biramous; telson cut off from 6th abdominal segment, bilobed, each lobe bearing 8 spines.
