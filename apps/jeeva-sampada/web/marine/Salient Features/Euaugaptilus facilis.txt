Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female:   Cephalothorax, 4.50 mm; urosome, 1.28 mm. The frontal margin of the prosome elongate ovate.  The frontal margin of the prosome obtuse in dorsal aspect. The last pedigerous segment narrowly rounded on the distal end. The rostral filaments short, arising from a well-marked prominence.
     The genital segment produced below with a patch of fine hairs on the genital area.  The furcal rami 2 times as long as wide.  The appendicular seta long, about as long as the combined length of the abdominal segments and furca. 
     The 1st antenna exceeds the end of the furca by distal 7 segments. The 2nd antenna has the exopod about as long as the endopod; the exopod has 8 free segments.  The mandibular palp feeble, has a small exopod and endopod; the endopod carries 3 terminal setae on the 2nd segment; the exopod 5-jointed, carries 4 setae; the biting part very strong.  In the 1st maxilla the endopod and 3rd inner lobe absent; the 1st inner lobe has 8 spines; the 2nd inner lobe has a single long and stout seta; the 2nd basal segment has a slender seta; the endopod is represented by a small process; the exopod has 3 setae; the outer lobe has 3 long and 2 short setae. The 2nd maxilla has the following numbers of setae on the lobes 1 to 6, and endopod: 3 setae and a short spine on the 1st lobe; 2 setae on the 2nd; 2 setae on the 3rd; 3 setae on the 4th; 2 setae on the 5th; 3 setae on the 6th; 7 setae on the endopod.  
      In the 1st leg the outer edge spine of the 1st segment of the exopod slender and long, exceeding the distal margin of the 3rd segment of the exopod; the 2nd segment of the exopod has no outer edge spines; the 3rd segment has a small outer edge spine.  In the 3rd to 4th legs the 2nd segment of the exopod each a blunt process overlapping the outer edge spine of the same segment; the 3rd segment of the exopod has a similar process at the base of the distal marginal spine.
    In the 5th pair of legs the 3rd segment of the exopod  has only 2 marginal spines; the inner marginal spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod short, reaching the base of the 1st inner marginal seta on the 3rd segment of the exopod.
