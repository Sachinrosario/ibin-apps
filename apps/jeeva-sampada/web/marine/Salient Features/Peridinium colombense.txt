SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca convex conical, narrowed towards the apical horn. Hypotheca rounded with two short processes. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle dextral, excavated. Sulcus much broadened towards posterior. Dorsal precingulus very large.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 28µ; breadth: 23µ.
