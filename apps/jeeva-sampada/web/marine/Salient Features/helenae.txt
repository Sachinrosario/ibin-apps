
SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:                                 Diagnostic characteristics:        

FEMALE:   Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused, line of fusion between  4th and 5th pedigerous segments visible. Prosome with a median crest. Rostrum with very short stout spines which are shorter than the notch.  Postero-lateral corners of the last thoracic segment produced into lappets which are pointed at the tip. Vervoot (1963) has mentioned them as strictly rounded. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment slightly swollen ventrally at proximal part. The abdominal segments are provided with small teeth on the dorsal posterior margin. First antenna 24- segmented. Fifth leg with a common basal portion and a free joint.  The free terminal joint with a short apical spine and a long sub- apical spine, which is more than 3 times the length of the joint. The sub apical spine with 2 rows of teeth.
