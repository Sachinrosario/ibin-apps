SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming curved chains. Number of chains occurs together in colonies. Setae very thin. The setae on one side of the chain almost all prolonged very much and sometimes attached to some foreign body.

Diagnostic characteristics: Aperture large, somewhat rectangular. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 5-14µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
