SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallets solid and elongate with a short cylindrical stalk and a long blade composed of a series of closely set compactly arranged transverse obtuse, V-shaped imbrications covered by an yellowish brown periostracum. The free periostracal margin bears double rows of finely serrated fringes. Lateral margins of cones more or less fused and end in short awns.
  Shell similar to other Bankia species.

Feeding habit: Boring into wood and feeds on the grated wood.

Main food: Cellulose, supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks:  
Distribution: Indo-Pacific, tropical to subtropical.
