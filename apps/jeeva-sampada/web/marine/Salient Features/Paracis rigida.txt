SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony is 55 mm in height and 40 mm in maximum breadth. The reaching is in one plane. Colony 22 cm high and 25 cm in maximum breadth. It is densely branched in one plane forming an almost felt-time fan-shape of 1.8 cm in diameter and 1.5 cm in height.  The coenenchyma is thin on the main stem and is composed of a single layer of scale-like plates very irregular in outline. The polyps occur over three sides of the stem and branches, leaving one side of the colony entirely free. Distances of about 1.5 mm on the larger branches separate them, but on the twigs the bases merge into one another. They are slightly cylindrical when open, but dome-like when closed, and measure about 1 mm in height and the same in diameter. The axis is horny and composed of longitudinal strands. It is very thick at the base, measuring 5.5 mm in diameter. The spicules show great diversity		

Spicules: The spicules are a) Irregular elongated plates b) circular plates with rugged edges c) smooth and bent with a few warts and d) spindles like caterpillar from side view. 

Colour	:	The colony is light grayish brown and the spicules are colourless.
