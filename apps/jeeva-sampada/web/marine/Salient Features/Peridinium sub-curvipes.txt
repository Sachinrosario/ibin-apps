SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells small, pear shaped, compressed to somewhat lens or cake shaped. There is a well-shaped small distinct apical horn. Girdle dextral displaced half width, not excavated, and with well developed fins held by ribs. 

Diagnostic characteristics: On the right side there is a real spine, of median size, somewhat curved, without fins.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 47µ; diameter: 44-45µ.
