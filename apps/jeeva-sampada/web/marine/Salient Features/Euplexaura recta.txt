SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony fan-shaped and not reticulate. The end of the branches is long, thin and running parallel upwards.  The polyps retractile into the bark. The conenchyma is very short. The axis is cornuculated. 
			
Spicules: The coenenchyma consists of ball-shaped, thickly warted spindles. 

Colour	:	The colony  dark brown and the axis  is black in colour.
