Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Arborescent, similar to A. formosa.  Main branches 1 to 1. 5 cm thick.  Top most branchlets 4-7 mm thick.  Axial corallites 1.8 to 2.3 mm thick (Wallace), exsert.  Radials small crowded, almost uniform in size, tubiform extend to 450 of the branch surface, about 1 mm in height and thickness.  Primary cycle of septa present both in axial and radials.  Coenoestem spiny at places.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  A. microphthalma is the smallest and finest of the arborescent Acropora.  Closest to A. formosa, A. horrida and A. vaughani (Veron, 1986, p. 158).

The inclusion of this species among the Indian fauna is based on Brook (1893) who reported a specimen from Rameswaram.  However, the specimen could not be located in BMNH, London in 1971 though, present author searched for it.
