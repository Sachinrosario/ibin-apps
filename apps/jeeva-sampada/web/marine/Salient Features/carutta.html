SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 9,10,
Morphological: Fairly small species.

Diagnostic characteristics : Rounded snout, mouth small, low set, nearly horizontal and inferior; no barbell on chin; rostral pore 5, marginal pore 5; mental pores in 3 pairs of which the first open close behind symphysis in a common pit.
Teeth differentiated into large and small in upper jaw only, the large ones close set, not canine like, forming the outer series, lower jaw teeth uniformly small. Gillrakers short, 8 or 9 on lower limb of first arch. Dorsal fin with 9 or 10 spines, followed by a deep notch, second part of fin with 1 spine and 25 to 30 soft rays; pectoral fins moderately long, about ¾ of head length, anal fin with 2 spines, and 7 soft rays; the second spine weak, about ¼ of head length; caudal fin rhomboid . Scales of head and upper part of body cycloid, elsewhere lower part of body and some scales weakly ctenoid; scales present also on soft parts of dorsal and anal fin. Lateral line scales reaching to tip of caudal fin. Swimbladder hammer shaped, with about 16 pairs of arborescent appendages, the first entering the head and sending a palmate branch to the front of pectoral arch.

Colour : Upper two thirds of body dark grey with a yellow or white streak along lateral line; upper two thirds of first dorsal black.
