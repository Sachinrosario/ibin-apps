SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:   The rostrum short,extends just beyond the base of the eyes; only one dorsal tooth present,small and triangular; antennal spine present; hepatic quite conspicuous. The anteroinferior margin of carapace not having pterygostomian spine. Dorsomedian spine present only on last abdominal segment(mysis III); a large ventromedian spine, bent forwardly, is present.Mandibles asymmetrical with jointed palp .Thoracic legs 1-3 chelate,last 4 -5 without exopods; pleopods uniramous,setose and functional. Telson bearing 7+7 seate(3 pairs of marginal and 4 pairs of terminal). Uropods well developed with exopods and endopods.Exopod bearing an externo-distal spine.     

Diagnostic characteristics:-Rostrum short; presence of a prominent hepatic spine; last  2 or 3 legs lacking exopods; dorsomedian  spine present only on 6th abdominal segment; telson always with 7 pairs of spines.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: feeds in the pelagic zone.

Main food:  plankton,algae,diatoms,minsed clams/fish muscles,organic matter mixed with  sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous.

Additonal remarks: This is the most abundant species of prawns  in the southwest coast of India - (Kerala).

Size and age:  Postlarvae I-VII.3.5mm-18.30mm

Longevity (y) (wild) :  2-3 years.  

Eggs and larvae:
Eggs are demersal; spherical in shape,translucent and have a large perivitelline space; measure 0.35mm-0.41mm in diameter. Embryonic development takes 16-17 hours.6 naupliar,3 protozoeal, 4 myses and a post larval stage are given.

Nauplii I-VI: length ranges from 0.26mm to 0.35mm; all stages having  a median naupliar eye(ocellus), 3 cephalic appendages, the  uniramous antennule;  the biramous antenna and  mandible; antennule bears 3 short lateral setae on the inner side; 2 long setae+ 1 spike- like seta terminally and a long seta distally on the outer margin. Antenna with exopods and endopods (biramous); exopod with 5 long setae on the inner distal margin and endopod with 2 short lateral setae and 2 long setae distally  and a rudimentary seta terminally. Mandible with 3 setae each on exopods and endopods,distally. The caudal furca bears 1+1 seta.In 2nd stage nauplius, the tip of the 4th seta of exopod of antenna, from the proximal end, is bifid and it is retained in all substages. The no.of furcal setae are increasing in number from stage to stage ,to 7+7 in Nauplius VI. Also the number of setae increaseson the exopod of antenna and  an enlarged swelling appears at the base of mandible.Duration of this substage is 16-24 hrs. A total of 56 hrs.(more than 2 days) are taken to complete the naupliar stages(I-VI).

Protozoea I-III: length  0.76mm-1.75mm;Frontal organs overhung by frontal horns. Carapace does not cover thorax completely. Antennule 3-segmented; proximal segment subdivided into5 segments; basal segment bears 1 distal seta ,middle segment, 2 long setae distally, and other short and terminal segment bears 3 setae,1 is very long .endopod  bears 1+2+1 setae. Antennal scale 10- segmented,10 long setae at the inner margin and 2 short setae on outer margin; endopod 2 segmented bearing 4 long,plumose setae and 1 short seta, terminally. Abdomen bifurcates posteriorly with furca bearing 7 setae each.

In protozoea II, the median eye is visible; the eyes are movable and stalked; a short pointed rostrum present; supraorbital spines are present. 5 pairs of thoracic legs are present in the form of small,knoblike rudiments. Telson with 7+7 spines.

In protozoea III  the 6th abdominal segment is demarcated to form the uropods;legs appear as small biramous buds. Dorsomedian spines are present on 1-5 abdominalsegment and 6th with a pair of small dorsolateral and a slightly larger ventromedian spines. Telson spines unchanged.

Mysis I-V: size ranges from  2.14mm-2.97mm.Rostrum  falls short of eyes and without teeth; antennal and pterygostomian spines present; a minute hepatic and a labral spine also present; abdominal segments 5-6 bear  dorsomedian spines at posterior end; also a minute  pair of posterolateral spines present on 6th; pleopods absent; telson with a deep cleft,each ramus with 7 setae.

In mysis III: rostrum has a small tooth; no supraorbital spine; 1st 3 pairs of legs minutely chelate; cleft in telson reduced.In mysis V,rostrum has 2 dorsl teeth; hepatic spine prominent; 2 unsegmented pleopod buds present; telson has no change.

Postlarval stages: size  3.0mm-3.9mm. Rostrum bears 2 dorsal teeth and a minute epigastric tooth; antennal and hepatic spines present; pterygostomian angle round; dorsomedian spine present only on 6th abdominal segment; pleopods uniramous and setose. Thoracic legs without exopods. The rostral teeth increases in number as the stage advances,to 8 in adults. 

Characteristics: Rostrum short,no pterygostomian spine; hepatic,anal and labral spines absent. The thoracic legs lacking exopods, pleopods uniramous without endopods. Dorsomedian spines absent on abdominal segments,except a minute one on 5-6 ab.segments. According to Burkenroad the exopods of legs do not completely disappear; they may remain as degenerated stumps in some individuals.

Abundance:  mostly abundant species in the prawn fishery of India, particularly on the southwest coast of India-(Kerala).
