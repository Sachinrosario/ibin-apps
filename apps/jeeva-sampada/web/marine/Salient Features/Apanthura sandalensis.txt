SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:

	Body long and slender and of uniform width. Head short, about half as long as first peraeon segment, its anterior border with a short acute rostrum, anterolateral corners slightly produced; eyes present. Peraeon segments one to three sub equal to one another, four to seven longer and sub equal. Pleon distinctly segmented.

	Superior antenna with basal peduncular segment prominently setose, flagellum three to four jointed; flagellum of inferior antenna four jointed. Maxilliped five jointed. Mandible with third segment of palp very short, with two apical setae, first segment with one long seta and the second with two. First peraeopod with fifth segment triangular and apically slightly projecting, sixth segment with a small tooth at the middle of the palm instead of the well marked palmar tooth at the base, distal half of the border excavate; dactylus when closed slightly overreaching the apex of fifth joint, unguis distinct. Second peraeopod with fifth segment considerably immersed in fourth, sixth segment ovate, dactylus with a small secondary unguis. Seventh peraeopod with fifth segment comparatively large, very slightly underriding sixth, with two inner spines, dactylus long, with a small unguis.
	Telson long and linguiform, its distal border rounded and setose. Endopod of uropod slightly overreaching the tip of telson, apex notched
	Body brownish white.
