Descriptive characters:  
The following description is based on several specimens collected from Mandapam and adjacent islands:  Corallum corymbose, larger colonies may attain a greater spread of 75 cm and vasiform.  Main divisions about 1 cm repeatedly dividing, branches coalascent, underside with numerous immersed or nariform corallites.  On the upper side the branchelets are either single or in groups of 3 to 5, 3 to 5 cm long and 6 to 8 mm thick.  Apices average 2 cm apart.  Axial corallites average 1.5 mm in diameter, rarely 2 mm, so much exsert; wall porous, reticulate and costulate.  First cycle of septa well developed with larger directives; secondaries smaller not more than 4.  Radial corallites, tubo-labellate or nariform, ascending, inner wall scarcely developed, outer, spreading, lips gutter-shaped; 2.5 to 3 mm long and 1 to 1.5 mm wide, larger ones may bear one or two buds.  Only the first cycle of septa is developed.  Wall of he radials deeply striated with acassional spinulation.  At the lower parts of the branches all the radials are immersed.  Living coral with white or purple tips.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  A. corymbosa is very near to A. hyacinthus.  But has labellate corallites with crescent shaped corallites.
