SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony of a grayish colour, 8 cm in height, 6 cm and 5 cm along the two diameters. The main stem arises from a very  an expanded base and attains a height of 3.5 cm where it measures 3.5 mm in diameter, the basal measurement being 6 mm. The coenenchyma is thin and is composed of a single layer of opaque, white, warty spindles or scales forming a sort of tessellated pavement-like structure, each spicule being visible to the naked eye. The polyps are distributed all over the surface of the coenenchyma. They are small and wart-like but have a rugose appearance, due to the projecting spines of perpendicularly arranged spicules. The polyps are not fully retractile. There is a well-developed tentacular operculum based by a cellaret of six or seven rows of curved warty spindles.		
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are 1) tri-or tetra-radiate forms with foliaceous expansion 2) spindles with pricky warts and 3) spindles with three or four spines sometimes bifurcated slender and delicate. 

Colour		:	The colony is light grayish brown, in alcohol, with the scarlet spindles of the mm showing contrast. The spicules are colourless.
