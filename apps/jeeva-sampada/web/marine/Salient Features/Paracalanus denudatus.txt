SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                     

The proportional lengths of the cephalosome and urosime are as 53 to 16 ; the abdomen is thus contained 3.31 times in the length of the anterior region of the body. The head and 1st thoracic segment are fused and the dorsal profile exhibits a slight hump about the middle of the fused mass, as is also seen in Paracalanus aculeatus Giesbrecht ; the anterior region of the body is, however slightly more slender and the fused mass of the cephalon and 1st thoracic segment is contained only 1-4 times in the length of the whole anterior region.  The forehead is uniformly rounded and the rostral filaments are directed downwards and backwards.  Thoracic segment 4 and 5 are fused and the posterior thoracic margin is rounded.  The urosome consists of four segment.        
The furcal rami are nearly three times as long as broad, the proportions of length to breadth being 20 to 7.  The inner furcal seta is delicate and is only about one-fourth the length of the next seta, thus closely resembling the condition found in Paracalanus aculeatus.      The 1st antenna agrees in its general plan with that of Paracalanus aculeatus, form a major, segments 1 and 2 and 8 and 9 respectively being fused together.  
The appendage reaches beyond the tip of the furcal ramus by approximately the extent of the last segment.
