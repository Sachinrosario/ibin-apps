SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branching irregularly in one plane; it is 60 mm. in height and 65 mm in maximum breadth. The axis is rigid with a diameter of 2.5 mm . The polyps are disposed irregularly on two sides and the twigs terminate with a pair at slightly different levels. The verrucae are small and wart-like.  The spicules are arranged longitudinally, into eight sheaves that give a serrate margin to the aperture. The anthocodiae are proportionately large and are completely retractile. They are abundantly supplied with spicules.
		
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenencyma spiny or warty spindles, curved or S-shaped . 

Colour		:	Colony creamy white and the axis brown in colour.
