SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Cephalon short, first peraeon segment longer but narrower than all the others. Segments 2-7 granular and pubescent along the posterior border. A lateral oblique ridge on all pleon segments, more conspicuous on the hinder segments. Fifth somite of pleon with a distal horse shoe-shaped carinae medially and an opposing proximal horse shoe-shaped carinae between which is an unornamented sulcus. Telson nearly circular, dorsally concave, margin strongly raised and conspicuously setose. Dorso-median part with a pair of sub median and a pair of lateral tubercles each followed by a strong ridge with 2 pair smaller ridges in between; the tubercles jointed by arched carinae. Lateral crests without tubercles. Dorsal surface of pleon and telson faveolate and with groups of microscopic setules.

Second antenna with 5-8 segmented flagellum. Second segment of maxilliped widening distal wards, epipod semi-triangulate, narrowing distally to a point and widest at base, length about three times the width, mandibular palp with 3 articles.

First peraeopod with 6-7 tubercles on third segment and one on the fourth segment. Propodus with 2 spines, secondary unguis bifid. Peraeopods 1-5 margin of segments serrate. On the sixth peraeopod outer apex of merus has 3-4 spines, carpus with 3 strong spines.

  Peduncle of uropod with a blunt outer knob; endopod half as long as peduncle, exopod half the length of endopod.

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi.

Distribution:
Andaman Islands (Barnard 1936).

Remarks:
  According to Barnard L. japonica and L. septima show resemblances. L. septima horse shoe-shaped carina on the fifth pleon segment which face each other, while L. japonica has V-shaped carinae opposing each other (Pillai 1961).

Size and age: 3-5mm in length.
