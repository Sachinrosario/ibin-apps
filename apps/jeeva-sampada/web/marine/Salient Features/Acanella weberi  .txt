Description	:	Colony  symmetrical and arborescent, 18.5 cm in height. Main stem is 2.2 cm long breaking up into a whorl of branches at its distal end, there being 5 branches in the whorl. The branchlets are in opposite direction, 8-10 mm from the base. The branches arise from horny nodes, which are calcareous. The calyces are biserial in arrangement. The individual calyces are club-shaped, and inclined towards the distal end of the colony. The tentacles are armed with numerous bar-like spicules irregularly place. The coenchyma is thin.
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are large spindles irregularly disposed and with many small, bar-like forms also. 

Colour	:	The colony is white with golden brown polyp.
