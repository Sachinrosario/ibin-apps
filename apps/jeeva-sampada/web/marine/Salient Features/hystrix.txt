Descriptive characters:  Corallum profusely branching, branches undergoing fusion, branches with numerous branchlets placed at varying angles (30 degrees to right angels).  Top branchlets 1.5 to 4 mm in thickness, main divisions upto 8 mm.  Branches and branchlets rounded or they are flattened acute or blunt.  Corallites arranged in regular longitudinal rows, close together, average 0.7 mm in diameter, moderately deep.  The primary cycle of septa fully represented in some cases.  Columella well developed, compressed in the plane of the directive septa.  The upper part of the calicular wall elevated to form hoods, which are more prominent at the distal parts of the branches.  Surface coenenchyme spiny.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
