SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum columar.  Calices 2.5 mm in diameter, shallow, close together.  Intercalicular area solid but not elevated.  Septa 14 to 16 per calyx, strikingly alternating in height, confluent over the wall.  Sides and edges of septa granular.  Septa with in the calices descent vertically to meet the columella.  Columella styliform or club-shaped.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Horst (1922) regarded the three species, P. clavus, P. duerdeni and P. maldivensis as one and the same.  However, Scheer and Pillai (1974) and Pillai and Scheer (1976) felt them to be separate.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
