SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with arched end faces. Areolae about 4-6 in 10µ, all of about the same size.

Diagnostic characteristics: A number of processes arranged in a circle at the end of the valves, those of neighboring cells touching these and forming a chain.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 51µ.
