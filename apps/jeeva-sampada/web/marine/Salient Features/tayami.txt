Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting, larger specimens from Gulf of Mannar collected are 2 to 7 cm in greater spread.  Corallites polygonal, penta on hexagonal, rarely two to three form short valleys.  Larger single calices 4 to 5 mm long rarely 6 mm, 3 to 4 mm wide.  Intercorallite wall very thin.  Total number of septa 40 to  50, continuous over the wall, 12 septa unite to the columella, others unite to the sides of the next lower cycles.  Edges of septa with 8-12 serrations.  Columella a solid mass, sometimes projecting.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
