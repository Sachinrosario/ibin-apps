Genus: Euchaetomera G.O. Sars
Carapace rather short, rostrum may or may not be present; in some margin of carapace and abdominal somites armed with fine spine-like process. Antennal scale slender, outer margin entire, terminating in spine. Eyes large and bilobate with two distinct areas of functional ocelli, peduncle with an inner flexible process, long in male. Third to eighth thoracic endopods long and slender, carpopropodus three segmented. Pleopods in male well developed and biramous without modified setae. Telson short, lateral margins convex, apex straight armed with one to two pairs of small spines and pair of long plumose setae; lateral margin may be armed or unarmed. Uropods long and slender with prominent statocyst.

Euchaetomera tenuis
Anterior border of carapace nearly transverse with slight median projection. Eyes roughly cylindrical, anterior group of ocelli confined to anterior one fourth, posterior group triangular, the two separated by long interval; inner ocular process projecting beyond eye. Antennal scale long and slender, five times as long as broad and overreaching antennular peduncle, apical spine long. Telson extending slightly beyond middle of statocyst, longer than broad, lateral margins unarmed, hind border very short and armed with two pairs of close set spines curved inwards and pair of long setae.
