SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:  POST LARVAL STAGES.

Post larvae  (I,II,IV & V):
They range a body length of  2.5 mm to 6.4 mm.
The Salient features are, the presence of  a dorsal tooth on rostrum and 3 dorsal teeth (including the epigastric tooth) in the late post larval stages; eyes elongated;  a reduction in the size of the exopods of pereopods; abdominal segments 1-5 bearing dorsolateral spines; 6th  bearing a posterior median dorsal spine. Telson distally bilobed  and each lobe with 4 spines.

Mandible devoid of teeth; pereopods 1-3 chelate; 4 -5 reducing or absent. Pleopods 1-4 uniramous with unsegmented protopod and exopod which is bordered with long plumose setae; 5th pair rudimentary; uropods with more setae. Pleopods appear in late post larva (V).  First 2 pairs uniramous with 17-18 setae; 3-4 biramous, endopod devoid of seate  , exopod with 14 -16 setae and 5th again uniramous and devoid of setae.

Diagnostic characteristics:        Rostrum with 2 short and a  strong tooth . The absence  or reduction of thoraciclegs  4 &5. Pleopods- first 2 pairs  uniramous with 17-18 setae; 3&4biramous,endopod devoid of setae and exopod with 14 -16 seate. Pleopod 5is uniramous and devoid of setae.Telson gap between spines wider andlateral spines reduced.

Sex attributes: separate (heterosexual)

Feeding habit: pelagic zone

Main food: plankton,copepods,diatoms etc.

Feeding type: omnivorous

Additional remarks:The protozoea of A.indicus is identified from those of  A.erythraeus,A.japonicus and A.cochinensis by the number of telsonic (processes) spines  and in the setation of various appendages. The characters like  the absence of teeth in the mandible, presence of dorsomedian spines  on 5-6 abdominal segments and 4 pairs of spines in the telson identify the mysis stages of A.indicus

Size and age: 0.5mm (protozoea I) to 6.4mm (postlarva V). 

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            
The eggs,3 protozoeae and a mysis stage (Mysis I)  are describedEggs: diameter 0.36 to  0 .40mm; collected mainly during  October-NovemberEggs spherical,golden brown and transluscent; perivitalline space wide with embryonicmass. In some,there appear a blastula, outline of the naupliar body with 3 bud like rudiments of appendages. The eggs hatch out into Nauplii I  

Nauplii I: a total length of  0.16 mm; body elliptical,opaque and golden brown;naupliar eye present , posterior end bears a pair of furcal spines; 3 pairs pfappendages (antennule,antenna and mandible) present. Antennule uniramous,unsegmented with  2 unequal terminal setae and an inner subterminal seta. Antennabiramous,unegmented,endopod with 2 terminal setae; exopod longer than endopodbearing 2 terminal and 3 lateral setae. Mandible biramous,endopod with 2 terminal and a subterminal seta.

Nauplii II: Size length ranging from 0.25 to 0.28 mm, body slightly elongate; furcallobes pronounced, each lobe with 4 unequal furcal spines. Except for  the development of more setae  there is no change.Nauplii III: Larva longer than broad and translucent; body length measuring from 0.33 to 0.39 mm; caudal  region distinctly lobed with 6 seatae (4 plumoseand 2 spines); rudiments of maxillae and 1st maxilliped appear as buds.Protozoea I: measures 0.53 to 0.58mm. Carapace forms a strange shape ,broader than long; bearing paired posterolateral spines with one spine in the anterolateral region and posterolateral spines with one median spine in the posterior region. The anterior pair bifurcates.  Thorax short and segmented,abdomen unsegmented.Telson bilobed,each lobe carrying 5 spines each. Internal appendages well developed. Mxp.3 is rudimentary.

Protozoea II: 1.00 to 1.08 mmRostrum develops in the anterior region medianly on the carapace; abdomen becomes segmented, eyes with stout stalks. Telson bilobed with spines.

Protozoea III: 1.26 to 1.45mm
The important change is the formation of the uropods and lateral spines on abdomen; eyes stalked, prominent and free of carapce; abdominal segments 1-5 with short posterolateral spines and  6th with 2 pairs of spines. Mandible with prominent teeth; pereopods biramous,rudimentary and bud like; uropods biramous.

Mysis I: 2.2 mm.The larva assumes a prawn like shape,carapace covers  thorax dorsolaterally.The anteroinferior and posteroinferior spines disappear. Rostrum with a minutedorsalspine; Eyes stalked; abdominal segments 1-5 with small ventrolateral spinesand 6th with a posterior,dorsal spine. Pleopods present on 1st 3 abdomnal segments.Telson bilobed; each  lobe with 4 spines, 2nd one from inner side ,the longest. First3 pairs of pereopods present with exopodites,endopod 5-segmented . Similarly,pleopods present only on 1-3 abdominal segments and uniramous. Exopod ofuropods bears a small  sharpspine at the externodistal margin.  

Characteristics:Abundance: 20 % of the estimated annual crustacean landings(Pillai,1973)
