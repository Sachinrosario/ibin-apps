SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Oblong elliptical valves with parallel valve margins, tapering to the ends. Raphe complex, terminal raphe common.

Diagnostic characteristics: Striae 6.5- 7.5 in 10m, slightly radiate at the middle, convergent at the ends, with a nearly parallel longitudinal line at about half of the striae.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 140-170m, width 20-40m.
