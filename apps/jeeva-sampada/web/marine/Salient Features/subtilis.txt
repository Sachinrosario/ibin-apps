Descriptive characters:  
Description of specimens from Manauli Island, Gulf of Mannar:  Corallum encrusting, layer over layer, upper most layer about 6 mm thick, growing edges only 2 to 3 mm thick.  An epitheca closely follows the growing edge.  Calices like minute punctures, irregular in outline, a well developed thecal wall not present, 0.35 to 0.52 mm in diameter, 1 to 2 mm apart.  Primary septa well developed, reach half way to the centre of the calyx, second cycle smaller, cycle rarely complete.

Coenenchyme under the lens reveals a fine reticulum with small pointed or compressed upright spindes.  Section of the coenenchyme shows the lower solid layer resting on an epitheca, and an upper reticular layer.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
