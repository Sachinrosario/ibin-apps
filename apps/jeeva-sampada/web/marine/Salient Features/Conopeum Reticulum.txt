SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:  Encrusting, colonies appear as whitish patches with uneven growing margin.	Zooecia quincuncial, chitinous outline distinct.  Shape of zooecia variable, but generally longer than wide, very much elongated in certain cases.  Gymnocyst present.  Tuberculated cryptocyst developed all around with tubercles projecting into the opesia.  The tubercles more or less of the same length.  In addition, small tubercles present in the proximal region of the cryptocyst.   Opesia elongate oval.  In certain cases, the proximal region of the opesia broader than the distal region.  Membraniporine operculum occupies three-fourth of the distal region.  A pair of rounded prominences present on the proximal corners of certain zooecia.  Proximal triangular depressions not evident.  	
Diagnostic characters:
Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: The triangular depressions sometimes roof over and convert into a pair of rounded eminences (Hincks, 1880; Harmer, 1926) and were clearly recognizable at the proximal corners of certain zooecia of the material collected by Menon.  Kirkpatrick (1923) considers this as vestigial zooecia.  Certain zooecia show distortions owing to the uneven nature of the substratum.  Such distorted zooecia are comparatively longer than normal ones with deeper cryptocysts.
