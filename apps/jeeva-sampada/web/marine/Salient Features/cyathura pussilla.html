SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
  
  Head squarish or slightly broader than long; rostrum prominent, reaching a little beyond the anterolateral angles; eyes prominent. First peraeon segment slightly longer than the others, almost twice as long as broad, segments two and three subequal to first and the others successively decreasing in length. Pleon approximately as long as third peraeon segment.

  First antenna with penduncle three segmented, first segment stout and subequal to third in length, flagellum short and three segmented. Second antenna longer and stouter than first, penduncle four jointed, flagellum three jointed and setose.

  Mandible with moderately stout palp, third segment small and subequal to first in length, with five setae, second segment with two setae and first with one seta; incisor process bidentate, cutting edge expanded and serrated. Maxilliped four jointed, third segment long, with faint transverse partition as in Haliophasma. Maxilla of the usual type.

  First peraeopod moderately stout, fifth segment apically acute and scarcely projecting, sixth broadly oval, palmar tooth small, dactylus very long, unguis as long as rest of the segment. Second peraeopod with fifth segment underriding sixth, sixth joint with an apical inner spine; dactylus with a small secondary unguis. Seventh peraeopod with fifth segment scarcely underriding sixth and the latter with a stout inner apical spine; dactylus long and slender, unguis and secondary unguis subequal in length. First pleopod with five hooks on its penduncle. Telson linguiform, its sides straight and the apex rounded. Endopod of uropod slightly longer than telson, second segment slightly longer than broad; sexopod broad, apex subacute and reaching the base of second segment of endopod.

  Body white, with dark chromatophores on the antero-lateral parts of the segments, on the endopod of the uropod and the telson.

Size and Age: Length: 5.6mm.
