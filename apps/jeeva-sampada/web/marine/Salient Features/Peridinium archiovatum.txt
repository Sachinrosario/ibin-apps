SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small spines, sub lenticular. Only two intercalary plates in the epitheca. Apical horn small, conical. No antapical spines.

Diagnostic characteristics: Meta girdle circular. Sculpture scattered punctae.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 23.5-29µ; width: 41.5-67µ.
