Salient features:

Morphological:

 

 Head with an apically rounded and curved roustrum and two prominent tubercles just iinner to the eyes; the space between the eyes concave; length of head approximately equal to that of first peraeon segment. First peraeon segment slightly longer than the second, third equal to first and fifth longest. Anterior coxal plates rounded and posterior ones produced into triangular setose processes. First pleon segment visible only laterally, fourth overhanging the fifth and posterior border of fourth and fifth with small denticles. Lateral sides of telson almost straight, apex perfectly rounded and armed with six spines, dorsal side sparsely setose.

 Penduncle of first antenna two jointed, basal composite segment large, flagellum seven jointed, basal segment very small; penduncle of second antenna with segments one to three together equal to fourth, flagellum fourteen jointed and densely setose. Frontal lamina pentagonal, broader than long. Mandible slender, second segment of palp one and half times as long as first. First maxilla with a large and well curved unguis. Second segment of maxilliped longer than the fifth, seventh segment clearly visible.

 First peraeopod with fourth segment carrying four stout blunt spines, fifth segment immersed in fourth; segments three to five of seventh peraeopod with their upper distal angle rounded and with a bunch of long setae. Endopod of uropod reaching slightly beyond the apex of telson, exopod shorter.

 Body white, with a grayish mottling, the chromatophopres forming uniform dark areas at the dorso-median part and symmetrical patterns laterally. Seventh peraeon segment with a characteristic dorso-median oval dark spot.

 

Additional remarks:

 

 Lanocira Gardineri exhibits pronounced sexual dimorphism. In the female the rostrum is not present and the second antenna is not feathery.

 

Size and age: maximum length : 7.5 mm.
