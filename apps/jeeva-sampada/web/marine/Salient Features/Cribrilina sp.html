Morphological: Encrusting. Zooecia small and oval, separated by distinct grooves. The sides of the zooecia arise from the grooves vertically, from which the costae arise and extend horizontally as a frontal shield. The distal end of the apertural bar formed by the fusion of the first pair of costae. The sub-oral lip with two cardelles. Lyrule absent. Mucro not present. The space between the two cardelles bend proximally. Two stumpy oral tubercles present, on either side of the orifice. The number of costae 5 to 6 on each side beaded and form distinct ridges. The inter-costal lines occupied by a few lacunae, sometimes longer than wide or even absent. Ovicells and vicarious avicularia not noticed.Diagnostic characters: The linear mode of growth and a pair of hollow spines take the species under consideration very near to Aspidelectra Levinsen (Ryland, 1966). This genus contains a single species Aspidelectra melanontha (Busk) that possesses a proximal spine and slits in between costae.
