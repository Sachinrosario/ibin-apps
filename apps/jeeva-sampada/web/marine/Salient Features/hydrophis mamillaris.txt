SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Head small, scarcely distinct from neck, which is rather and much elongated in adult; one pre - and 2 postoculars ; 2 (anterior) +3 (posterior) temporals, which are variable; 7 (or 8) supralabials, 2nd in contact with the prefrontal, 3rd and 4th touching the eye; 4 lower labials in contact with the genials. Eye moderate. Body slender anteriorly, much compressed posteriorly ; scales feebly imbricate anteriorly, becoming more or less juxtaposed posteriorly, each scale with a central tubercle or short keel; arranged in 35-43 rows at midbody; ventrals small, 302-390, distinct throughout, not twice as broad as the adjacent scales, and each one with two keels; four to six preanals.

Diagnostic characteritics: The normal colour is white in the young and light grey in the adult, with 42-57 broad black bands encircling the body; the bands, somewhat wider dorsally and narrower on sides, are usually connected along the line of ventra Is. Head, chin and throat uniform black; a yellow streak on the temporal region. Tail black or with lateral bars.

Feeding habit: Piscivorous. 
Main food: Fish. 
Feeding type:
Additional remarks: The Bombay sea snake is a rare species, most ofthe known species having been collected on and around the coastal Mumbai. A few specimens, including the type, are reported from Visakhapatnam on the east coast. Outside Indian coastal belt, this species has been recorded from the coasts and mangroves of Pakistan.

The markedly contrasting slender neck and moderately robust body can be best noticed in the juvenile of this species.
