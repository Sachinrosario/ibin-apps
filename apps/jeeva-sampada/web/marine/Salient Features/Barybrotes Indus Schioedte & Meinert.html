Salient features:
Morphological:
Body elongated, four to five times as long as broad. Head hemispherical, with an antero-median point; eyes large and reniform. Second peraeon segment shortest, subequal to seventh in length, segments four to six large, with a prominent dorso-lateral cleft; coxal plates large but not much produced. Pleon narrower than peraeon, roughly rectangular, all the segments distinct. Telson elongate triangular, with the dorso-median part arched; apex narrow and rounded, sub-truncate in large specimens, with six strong spines.
Basal segment of first antennal penduncle vertically expanded and dorsally visible; flagellum seven jointed, but the segments fused to some extent, penduncular segments with short plumose setae; composite flagellar segment with three tufts of setae, indicating the line of union of segments. Second antenna reaching a little beyond the middle of fifth peraeon segment; fifth peduncular segment with three stout, setae, two of them at the lower distal corner plumose, flagellum fourtyone jointed.
Mouth parts and peraeopods as illustrated by Monod, but the setae on the second segment of posterior peraeopods considerably longer. Frontal lamina club shaped, its anterior border rounded. Inner lobe of first maxilla with a smallspine at the base of the large spine. Exopod of uropod narrow, with nine spines, endopod apically truncate, with two spines.
Body white, with slight greenish tint. The food visible as an oval dark stone like mass, as observed by Hale (1925) in Aega.
