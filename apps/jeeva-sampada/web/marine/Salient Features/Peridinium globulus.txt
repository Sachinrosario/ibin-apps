SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells round to lens shaped. Apical horn knob like. Girdle more or less markedly dextral. Sulcus arched, rounded below, not reaching to the posterior end.

Diagnostic characteristics: Dorsal plate design variable, quadrate, hexa, symmetrical or asymmetrical.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 42-75 µ Long; 42-110µ broad.
