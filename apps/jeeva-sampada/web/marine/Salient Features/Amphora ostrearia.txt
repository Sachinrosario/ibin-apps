SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Weakly silicified frustules, elliptical to quadrate in outline. Zone with numerous divisions, striated.

Diagnostic characteristics: Striae 12 in 10µ, punctate, valves of various shape depending on position.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 68-79µ long and 18-21.5µ broad.
