SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:
Post-larva:   Length.16mm.
Body transperant and no pubescence; rostrum without teeth; only hepatic spine present on carapace; telson almost parallel-sided; posterior margin concave with short median spine; pereopods without exopods; pleopods biramous; endopod of1st pleopod rod-like;Juvenile: Length; 21mm.Body not pubescent; rostrum hardly reaches the end of 1st segment of the antennular peduncle; rostrum with 9 dorsal and 3 ventral teeth; antennal and pterygostomian spines present and hepatic spine well developed; longitudinal and oblique ridges on carapace; dorsal and lateral carinae on 5 and 6 abdominal somites; 6th somite with 5 rows of ventrolateral spines, and also a short dorsal and a paired ventrolateral spines on posterior margin; pereopods without exopods; pleopods biramous; outer margin of exopod of uropod with short spinules and a terminal spine; telson tapering abruptly at posterior end, with 3 pairs of lateral spines and many setae.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Body not pubescent,transperant; hepatic spine strong, ridges on carapace and carinae on 5and 6 abdominal somites; pereopods without exopods; rows of vetrolateral spines on 6th ab.somite; pleopods biramous.

Sex attributes:  Separate, (Heterosexual).Descriptive characters: rostrum with ventral teeth; the incisor process of mandible pointed but not prolonged as in F.wwoodwardi; 3rd leg reaches almost the tip of eyesand without exopods; pleopods biramous.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Pelagic zone.

Main food:  plankton, detritus with  sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous

Additonal remarks:       The 2 specimens were collected from off Madras coast and the Andaman sea,i.e.the western and central parts of the Bay of Bengal. The samples were collected from 200-0m,the absence of adult specimens from night halls suggest the restriction of such shrimps to deeper waters. Their absence could,however,be equally due to their ability to avoid plankton nets or to the scarcity of the genus.

Size and age: Postlarva: 16mm and Juvenile: 21mm.

                                                                                       
Eggs and larvae unknown.

Postlarva: Length 16mm.

Body transperant,no pubescence; rostrum without teeth,but few indentations present; hepatic spine present; legs without exopods; telson parallel-sided,posterior margin concave with median spine. Pleopods biramous.Juvenile: 21mm length.Rostrum with 9 dorsal and 3 ventral teeth; antennal,pterygostomian and hepatic spines present; dorsal carinae on 5th and 6th abdominal somites; legs without exopods and telson ending abruptly with  3 pairs of lateral spines  and many seate.

Characteristics: the presence of ventral teeth on rostrum is characteristic of F.balboae.The absence of exopodites from thoracic legs is a much more primitive character; rows of ventrolateral spines on abdominal somite 6.
