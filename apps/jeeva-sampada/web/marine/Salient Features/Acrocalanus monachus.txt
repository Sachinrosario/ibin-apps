SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                  

Female :  Dorsal surface of the body not inflated, with a shallow arch ; anterior head prolonged, in lateral view almost square in outline.  Antenna 1 extends beyond the caudal rami by its last 3 segments.  Out distal border of exopod segment 3 of leg 4 about 12 large teeth.

Male : Unknown

Diagnostic characteristics: -     The square appearance in lateral view readily distinguishes this species from others in the genus.
