Descriptive characters:  Corallum plocoid, with an encrusting base.  Fully developed corallites 6 to 8 mm in diamter, upto 10 mm high; 5-6 mm deep.  Septa in three cycles rarely with a few of the fourth.  The primaries are the broadest and a little exsert.  Septa of uniform width throughout their length.  Septal edges entire, sides granular.  Twelve septa reach the columella.  Columella trabecular, rudimentary.  Costae sinuous, extending to the base of the corallites, adjacent ones being united by transverse connections.  Inter costal spaces perforated.  Epitheca present.  Living coral eosine red.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
