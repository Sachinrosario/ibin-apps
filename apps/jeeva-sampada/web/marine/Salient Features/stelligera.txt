SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum thick entrusting, columnar, corallites circular or oval, level or slightly projecting.  Diameter of the calices 2 to 2.5 mm, touching each other to 1 mm apart, about 1 mm deep.  Septa 24, the larger and smaller ones alternating; exsert ends arched and slightly thickened at the wall.  Septal edges dentate, sides granular.  Costae correspond to septa.  Major septa reach the columella, each with a prominent paliform lobe.  Columella trabecular, filling about half the bottom of the calyx.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
