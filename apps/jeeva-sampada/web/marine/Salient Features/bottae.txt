SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive with small gibbosities.  Corallites rounded, slightly projecting (1 mm), almost touching each other or about 1 mm apart with well defined inter corallite grooves in between.  Diameter 3 to 5 mm, mode 4 mm, young corallites intercalated.  Depth 1-2 mm.  In full grown calyx there are 35-40 septa, those of the fourth cycle being rudimentary.  Primaries and secondaries subequal.  Septa exsert, not arched, swollen at the wall, upper ends vertical.  Septal edges serrated near the thecal rim, down with 4 to 6 septal teetch.  14 to 20 septa reach the columella, a few turn towards and fuse to the major septa.  Larger septa show well developed paliform lobes.  Columella looks solid upright papillate, occupies about one-third the bottom of the fossa.  Costae correspond to septa, usually stop at the middle of the intercorallite groove.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
