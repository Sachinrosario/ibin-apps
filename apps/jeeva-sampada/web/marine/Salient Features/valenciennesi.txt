SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting to submassive.  Corallites and calices polygonal, penta or hexagonal with a well defined intercorallite groove.  Greater diameter of calices 7-11 mm, lesser 6-9 mm, depth 3-5 mm.  Total number of septa 30-45, of which 12 to 16 reach the columella.  Septa exsert and arched.  Columella 2-3 mm in diameter formed of twisted trabeculae.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Some authors in the past have relegated this species to Genus Montastrea.  There is little reason to alter from Matthai, who is the dean of coral taxonomy of this centuary.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
