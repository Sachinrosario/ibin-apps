SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Large species, spherical to pear shaped. Slightly compressed dorsiventrally. Epitheca conical, apical horn long, sub conical, truncated, almost cylindrical most of its length. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Two long antapical spines. Only slightly divergent with conspicuous wings.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 60-95µ, diameter: 45-62µ.
