SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body slightly elongate, the depth 3.2 to 3.5 times in standard length. Chin with 2 slender barbells which usually reach posterior to rear margin of preopercle, their length 1.4 to 1.5 times in head; snout long and pointed 1.6 to  2.1 times in head (relatively longer in large individuals ), the dorsal profile  straight; mouth small, the maxilla reaching only about half distance to a vertical at front edge of eye; a single row of well-spaced, stout conical teeth in jaws; no teeth on roof of mouth; gillrakers on first arch 25 to 30. First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first minute, the third longest contained 1.2 to 1.3 times in head of adults; last dorsal and anal rays not prolonged; pectoral rays 16 to 18 (usually 17). Lateral line scale 28 to 30; vertical rows of scales in space between dorsal fins; 8 or 9 vertical rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour : 

brown to reddish brown dorsally, shading to whitish on sides and ventrally, the scale  edges narrowly reddish brown; a dark reddish brown stripe from front of snout through eye across upper end of gill opening and along upper side, ending beneath rear base of second dorsal fin; a round dark reddish brown spot larger than eye just anterior to mid base of caudal fin; diagonal pale  blue lines alternating with brownish yellow extending dorsoposteriorly and ventroenteriorly from eye, with 1 or 2 parallel on check; fins unmarked or second dorsal and anal fins may have faint narrow pale blue to pink bands. Fish taken from deeper water are more red and those from the shallows more brown.
