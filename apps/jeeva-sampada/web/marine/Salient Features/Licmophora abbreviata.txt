SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules cuneate in girdle view with strongly rounded angles. Lower end attached to mucous stalk. Cells form colonies. Septa project into the cell.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves oblonceolate with margins sub parallel towards the apex narrowed and elongated towards the base. Pseudoraphe distinct.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: 28-75µ long and 8-11µ broad.
