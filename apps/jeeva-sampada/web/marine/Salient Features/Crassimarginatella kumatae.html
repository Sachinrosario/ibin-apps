SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Zoarium appears as a whitish patch, slightly raised from the substratum. Zooecia oval, rarely showing any variation in shape. Gymnocyst slight, conspicuous on the proximal region. Opesia oval. Cryptocyst well developed with granulation and small tubercles projecting into the opesia. Vicarious avicularia present, very rare, with a triangular rostrum, and well developed cryptocyst. Glistening white and granulated ovicells present.

Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters:   The shape and size of the vicarious avicularia is a character which is considered as important for the species. They were distinctly oval in Silen's (1941) figure whereas in the material collected by Menon, they were with a triangular distal half. Further, the size of the vicarious avicularia in Menons specimen was nearly the same as the size of the autozooids, whereas Silen noted them as just half the size of the autozooids. The position of the vicarious avicularia, however agreed with what Silen has stated in the text.
