SALIENT FEATURES OF Cymodocea rotundata Ehrenb. & Hempr. ex Asch.

Leaves: Linear, narrowed at base, amarginate, rarely faintly serrulate at apex, 2.7 –18.5 cm long, 9-14 nerved

Inflorescence: Stalked male flowers and sessile female flowers are axillary in position

Perianth: No organized tepals

Ovary: Very small, stigma spirally coiled, up to 30mm long

Fruit: 1 or 2, sessile, semicircular in shape

Flowering and fruiting: The plants are seen in vegetative conditions only. (The description of flowers and fruits are adapted from Hartog (1970))
