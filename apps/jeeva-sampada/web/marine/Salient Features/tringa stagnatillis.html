SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: One of the large sand pipers. Bill and legs are longer to body proportion.
Diagnostic characteristics: Upper part is grey, fore neck and underparts white and legs greenish or yellowish.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Feeds more near island areas.

Main Food: Micro invertebrates.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Breeding extralimital.
