Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum explate or submassive.  Coenosteum vesicular or dense with spines.  Corallites rounded, oval, level, or projecting, sometimes one side more projecting, close together or touching, or even 2 mm apart.  Diameter 2 to 2.5 mm, upto  2 mm exsert.  Wall a little thickened.  Septa in three cycles.  Primaries thicker than the secondaries, all septa exsert and arched.  Edges dentate.  Primaries and secondaries reach the columella.  Costae correspond to septa, those of the first cycle largest.

The polyps were not found expanded during day time in specimens collected from the southeast coast of India.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
