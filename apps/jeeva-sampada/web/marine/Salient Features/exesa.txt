SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting at first, subsequently becoming submassive with hillocks.  Valleys continuous, 6-8 mm wide.  Monticules conical or elongated, running together for considerable length, especially at the peripheral parts of the corallum, often swollen with heavy deposition of endothecal vesicles.  Height of monticules 5-6 mm, length 7-10 mm.  Septa truncate and vertically descending, subequal or larger and smaller alternating.  Edges of septa dentate, side granular.  Larger septa reach the columella, latter well developed, continuous.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
