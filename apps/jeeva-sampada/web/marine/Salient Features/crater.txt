Descriptive characters:  Larger coralla measured upto 50 cm in greater spread, but retain the cup-shape characteristic of this species.  Corallites are confined to the inside of the cup.  They are arranged in wavy, irregular concentric rows, more regular in younger coralla.  In order coralla the corallites look more crowded.  Corallites 2 to 2.5 mm in diameter, slightly broader at the base than at the top, close together to 2 mm apart within a row.  Distance between adjacent rows 2-5 mm.  Corallites level or projecting upto 2 mm, usually 1 mm or so.  Septa 16 to 18, rarely in larger corallites upto 24.  Septa narrow, project to one-third the radius of the corallites, steeply descending, edges dentate, side profusely granular all join the columella.  Columella a compressed lamella, elongated.  The wall of the corallites with broken lamellate ridges with spines.  At sites the echinulations are very conspicuous in some coralla to give a wooly appearance to the surface.  Underside of the cup with ridges and furrows.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
