SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Bill short pigeon like legs moderately long, wings long and pointed and tail is short.







Diagnostic characteristics: Legs black coloured, white fore head supercilium, crown sandy grey, narrow brownish eye stripe and a small black patch on either side of breast.





Sex attributes:


Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike female dull coloured.




Meristic characteristics:





Feeding habit: Feeds on sandy and muddy shores and also on grass lands.







Main Food: Insects and crustaceans



Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder






Additional remarks: Nests from March to August. A dipression on sandy bed camouflaged with stoves and pebbles.
