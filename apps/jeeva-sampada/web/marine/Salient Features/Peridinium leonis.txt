SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells in ventral view rhombic. Epitheca three edged, tapering to a point. Hypotheca set out into two blunt wedge shaped horns, between which lies a deep notch and which ends in two short, more or less robust spines. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle more or less slightly sinistral.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
