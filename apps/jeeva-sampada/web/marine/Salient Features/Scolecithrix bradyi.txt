
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:            

Female:  This species can be very easily identified by its characteristic general appearance. Prosome and 1st pedigerous segments fused. Last 2 pedigerous segments separate. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment narrowly rounded, the one on the right side is more produced posteriorly thereby making the last pedigerous segment asymmetrical. Rostrum prolonged and with fine filaments. Abdomen 4- segmented. The genital segment along its lateral margin on the left side is swollen. First antenna 19- segmented and reaches the tip of the last pedigerous segment. Fifth leg highly reduced and asymmetrical.        Male. Prosome and 1st pedigerous segment fused. Last two pedigerous segments separate. In 5th leg right endopod is reduced, terminal segment of the exopod is forked. In left leg endopod is broad. Exopod 3- segmented.
