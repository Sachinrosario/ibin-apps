SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:-          

MALE:  Frontal region of the prosome with 2 distinct papilla. Geniculated left antennule 23 segmented and a knee joint is seen between 18th &19th segment. Exopod of antenna 8 segmented and endopod 2 segmented. Mandibular blade bifurcated, inner tooth carries 2 small indistinct spines and 1 small inner spine.First inner lobe of maxillule with 6 setae 2 of which are spiniform, second inner lobe with 1 and 3rd with 10 setae, outer lobe carries 6 long and 3 small setae, endopod 1 segmented and carries 4 setae. 5th and 6th lobes of maxilla bear one strong spine each. Maxilliped elongated and bears richly plumose setae. Coxa of left leg 5 with a strong and curved inner spine; basis of both sides with 1 seta; setation of both endopod segments are same on both sides, segment 1 naked, 2 and 3 with 1 and 6 setae respectively; exopod 1 of left leg with 1 marginal spine and 1 glandular pore, segment 2 with 1 middle spine and 2 glandular pores, segment 3 with 1 middle spine 2 glandular pores, and 2 terminal spines, one 2.5 times longer than the other. Exopod segment of right leg with 1 marginal spine and 2 glandular pores.Segment 2 with 1 distal spine and 2 glandular pores, the inner margin of the segment is extended inside like a conical protrusion, exopod segment 3 with a long and serrated proximal spine, 2 terminal spines, one 3 times longer than the other and 2 glandular pores.             

FEMALE:  Not recorded from the Indian Ocean.

Additional  remarks :                 First record from the Indian Ocean (Saraladevi, 1977). Some of the characters of the specimen agree with Park (1960). The most noticeable change is in the frontal region of the head. Two distinct papillae are seen on the head region of the present specimen. In addition to that the endopod    of maxillule in the specimen bears 4 setae while that of Park’s specimen has only 3 setae. First inner lobe carries 6 setae including 2 spiniform setae whereas Park’s specimen has 7 setae including 2 spiniform setae. Park’s specimen has 2 acute spines on the inner margin of the inner tooth of mandible. These 2 spines are not well developed in the present specimen.               
Even though some morphological variations are noticed in the present specimen it agrees with Park’s description in some other characters. Hence it is identified as Haloptilus fertilis male. These differences may be due to the ecophenotypic variations.       

Female not recorded.
