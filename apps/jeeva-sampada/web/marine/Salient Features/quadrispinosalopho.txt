SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      According to Wolfenden (1911) this species differs from Lophothrix frontalis Giesbrecht in the shape of the prosome and frontal crest and in the structure of the 5th pair of feet. The difference in the shape of the prosome is extremely slight.    In female the proportional lengths of the cephalothorax and abdomen are as 66 to 15, so that the abdomen is contained 4.4 times in length of the anterior region of the body.    
The abdomen consists of four segments.     
The mouth-parts and swimming legs agree closely with the corresponding appendages in Lophothrix frontalis, but there are distinct differences in the number and arrangement of the spines on the surfaces to the swimming legs.    
The 5th pair of legs bear, four spines instead of three.
