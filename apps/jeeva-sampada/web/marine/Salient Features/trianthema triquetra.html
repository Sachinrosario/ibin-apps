Salient Features :
It is a small, difussed, much branched prosrate herb.
Leaves 0.3 0.7 cm long, opposite or alternate, sub acute, fleshy, covered with papillae, petiole short, dilated and slightly sheathing at the base.
Flowers sessile, several in an axils, not enclosed in the sheath of the petiole.
Stamens 5, style simple.
Fruits completely enclosed in the calyx tube, 2 seeded, blackish brown.
