Morphological:   

Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1

Body elongate, moderately deep and slightly compressed with upper profile more convex than lower. Upper jaw very broad at end (with very broad supra maxilla) extending to below posterior mid point of pupil, teeth minute in a broad band in both jaws gillrackers (excluding rudiments )decreasing slightly in number with growth, 6-9 upper, 18-20 lower and 24- 29 total on first gill arch at sizes 2-7 cm fork length in specimens larger than about 20 cm fork length, total gillrackers excluding rudiments 22-26. Dorsal fin with 7 spines (first spine minute or missing in large fish) followed by 1 spines and 27-33 soft rays ;anal fin with 2 detached spines (these of dorsal fin lob about 1.3-1.6 times longer than the pectoral fin and 18-22 % of fork length ; anal fin base contained 1.5-1.6 times in second dorsal fin base; pelvic fins longer than pectoral fins. No scutes caudal peduncle groves present . First pterygiophore of anal fin straight in specimens larger than about 10 cm fork length. Vertebrae 10+14.

Colour
In fresh fish variable, brown or olivaceous to bluish green above, sides and belly lighter, some times with brassy or lavender reflections; the nuchal bar often persistent in adults, and a faint amber lateral stripe extending backward from eye frequently present; spinous dorsal fin dusky second dorsal fin dark, anal fin mostly of lobe white and a few white spots along fin base; pectoral fins entirely dark, or dark with the margins dusky pelvic fins white ventrally and latterly with a dark dorsal surface, or sometimes entirely dark ;caudal fin dark with a light narrow posterior margin juveniles (from 2-18 cm fork length) with a dark nuchal bar extending from eye to dorsal fin origin, 6 dark body bars each with a light narrow irregular area through their centre vertically that do not extend in to the membranes of the second dorsal and anal fins and a seventh bar at the end of caudal peduncle ; tip of anal fin white; pectoral, pelvic caudal fins dusky .
