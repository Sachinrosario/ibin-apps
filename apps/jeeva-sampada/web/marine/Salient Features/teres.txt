Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum arborescent 1 to 1.5 m in height forming thickets 1 to 2 mm in diameter.  Main branches 1-2 mm thick, subdividing, upper branchlets 20 to 30 cm long average 1 cm thick.  Axial corallites 2-3 mm thick, about 2 mm exsert, wall very fragile and porous.  Opening of the axials about 1. 5 mm in diameter with very well developed primary cycle of septa, generally fuse deep down in the axial fossa.  Secondaries scarecely visible.  Radials corallites mostly immersed all over the branches, about 1 mm diameter, generally empty without any trace of septation.  However at the top of the branchlets a few projecting labellate radials 1 to 1.5 mm broad and about 2 mm long are seen with only the directives of the primary septa developed.  Wall of the radial corallites strongly costate.  Surface coenenchyme spongy with weak echinulations.  Living coral brown with white axials.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
