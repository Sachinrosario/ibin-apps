Salient features:
Morphological:

  Head somewhat squarish, rostrum small, lateral corners prominent; eyes large. Seventh peraeon segment slightly produced postero-laterally. Pleon segments distinct, segments gradually increasing in length backwards.
  First antenna short and slender, peduncle not differentiated from the flagellum, first three segments represent the peduncle and the last ten the flagellum. Second antenna long, peduncle distinct, four segmented, flagellum composed of fourteen small segments. Maxilliped four jointed, second segment produced and reaching two-thirds the length of the apical segment. Maxilla narrow and distally serrated. Mandible with incisor process long and narrow, third segment of palp expanded and with a comb of setae.
  First peraeopod stout, merus long and cylindrical, propodus with a basal projection, dactylus long and slender, unguis very small. Second peraeopod with small triangular fifth segment, sixth elongate oblong, with five or six large compound spines. Seventh leg considerably elongate, fifth segment not underriding sixt, dactylus with a secondary unguis.
  Telson approximately as long as pleon, apically rounded, statocyst large and median. Exopod or uropod folding over endopod, free segment of the latter slightly longer than broad.
  Body white, with prominent branched chromatophores forming distinct patches.
