SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony  branching at 2/57 winding towards the right. The five-verical rows of the stem branches are irregular and dichotomous. The branches are strongly pointed at the angle of 700 at the base and 400 at the distal end. The number of the internodes of the main branch is 4 or 5 measuring 11-27 mm long between them. The upper part contains 1 or 2 polyps on every internode. Branches are 4 -13 mostly with 6 or 7 polyps on every internode, which stand 1.5 – 6 mm from each other. The polyps with folded tentacles are less than 1.6 mm long with stretched ones 2 -2.5 mm. The coenenchyma with eight distinct rows of 7, up to 0.42 mm long. Their surface is filled with warts standing at wide intervals.  Another deeper layer of tissues at the polyp base are smooth and thin up to 0.25 mm. Nematozooids are absent. The stem bark is calcareous up to 0.18 mm long at the base, 0.35 mm at the center and 0.44 mm long at the main branches. 
		Spicules: The spicules of the bark are highly warted. 

Colour	:	The colony is yellowish in colour.
