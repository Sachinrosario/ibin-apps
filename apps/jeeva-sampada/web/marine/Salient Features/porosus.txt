SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Snout longer and marked by two prominent longitudinal ridges 
extending forwards infront of each eye. Bopny portions of the dorsal 
scutes in contact with one another in a transverse series. No post-occipital shields
Seventeen to ninteen teeth on each side, four teeth in each premaxillary boine in the 
adult. Fingers webbed at the base. A serrated fringe along the outerside of the limbs.

Diagnostic characteristics: Young pale with large black spots or crossbars; adults olive dark.


Feeding Habit: Carnivorous

Main Food: Juveniles eat crustaceans, insects and small fish
and adults take an increasing proportion of large vcertebrates.

Additional Remarks:
The saltwater or Estuarine crocodile inhabits the mouths of muddy rivers and 
canals near the sea and has often found several miles out at the sea.
It ascends rivers but is not known to peneterate beyond tidal limits. It
spends considerable time on land amidst a thick mangrove forest.
This crocodile is the largest marine reptile in existence and ferocious in
disposition.It is little wonder that this crocodile turns an occasional 
man killer and most of the attacks on humans in India are contributed 
to this crocodile. But it is only adult pressed by hunger and in a changed 
mood that attacks huge mammals, and humans.
The female lays from 50 to 60 eggs and build a nests of mound for them.
The female keeps a constant vigil on the nests till the hatchings emerge
after a period of incubation lasting 75 to 106 days. She reponds even to the 
calling of the hatchings and carries them to water.
