Salient Features:
Morphological:

Male- The body is elongate oblong, with subparallel sides. The cephalon is much shorter than the first peraeon segment, peraeon segments one and two are equal and subequal to the seventh. The coxal plates are firmly united with trhe peraeon segments. The 7th peraeon segment has a shaoow postero median concavity. The pleon is almost as long as the first peraeon segment and its hind botder has a median incision similar to that on the 7th peraeon segment. The telson is a broad-based triangle, its dorsal surface has a pair of submedian bulgings studded with tubercles, the tubercles neat the periphery of the bulgings are larger and fall into ill defined rows. The apical foramen of the telson is deep and anteriorly widens into a semicircular transverse foramen with a hairy border, the apices of the telson diverge slightly.

The peduncle of the first antenna is stout, the third peduncular segment is long, the flagellum is 8 to 10 segmented with one oifactory setae. The 5th peduncular segment of the second antenna is the longest, the flagellum is 13 to 15 segmented. The epistome is longer than broad, with a rounded apex, the arms are long, comparitively narrow and apically slightly curved outwards and enclose more than half of the upper lip. The incisor process of the mandibl;e is bidentate, the accessory lobe is 2 to 3 dentate, the spine row is formed of three or four stiff pectinate spines, the molar process is large and toothed. The second anmd third segments of the palp of the mandible carry a comb of pectinate setae. The outer lobe of the first maxilla has four large pointed spinesand four slightly bent barbed spines, the inner lobe has four pectinate setae and a spinule. The inner lobe of the second maxilla has six large setae, the middle lobe has four stout spines, and the outer lobe has three spines. The maxilliped is normal, the second and third segments of the palp are promnently produced.

The anterior peraeopods are comparitively stout, the inner bordre of segments three to six is covered with a thick matting of fine hairs, each segment has a large distal spine, the outer border of segments four to six is spiny. The posterior peraeopods are long and slender. The male appendix on the seventh peraeon segment consists of a pair of long slender processes fused very near their base. The second pleopod has three coupling hooks on the peduncle, the stylet is slightly longer than the endopod and apically acute. The exopod of the third pleopod is one-segmented. The outer border of both rami of the fourth pleopod carries a few short setae, the endopod has neither an apical lobe nor a seta. The exopod of the fifth pleopod has three spiny thickened areas. The uropods clearly overreach the apexd of the telson, their rami are subsimilar and highly flattened with a crenate border carrying short stiff setae.
Length 4.0mm.
Female - The body is elongate oblong and narrows towards both ends. The cephalon is longer than the first peraeon segment which is subequal in length to the seventh peraeon segment. The pleon is slightly longer than the seventh peraeon segment. The telson is broader than long, with a pair of contigous dorsal bulgings. The apical formen is shallow and semicircular.

The mouth parts are not metamorphosed. The pereopods do not have the close matting of hairs seen in the male. The uropods are short and do not reach the apex of the telson, the rami are comparitively very naroow and totally different from those of the male.

Additional Remarks:
Dynamenella Quilonensis belonbgs to a group of species characterised by a transversely widened telsonic formen. This species closely resembles Dynamenlla Seabricula (Heller), Dynamenella Performata (Moore) and Dynamenella Platura nobili. But the tuberculate bosses on the telson easily distinguish D. Quionensis from their species.

The holotype male and the allotype female are deposited in the indian museum, Kolkatta.
