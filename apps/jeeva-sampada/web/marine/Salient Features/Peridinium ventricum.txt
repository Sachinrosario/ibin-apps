SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Biconical cells. Epitheca shorter and broader, with straight sides. Hypotheca narrower, however longer, antapically by the longitudinal furrow emarginated.

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle without wing, strongly sinistral, end displaced about a furrow width. Apical pore slit like.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 51-54µ; breadth: 42-47µ.
