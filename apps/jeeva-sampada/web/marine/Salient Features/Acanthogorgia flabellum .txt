SALIENT FEATURES		

Description		:	Colony flabellate and reticulate, attaining a height of about 12	cm. and a breadth of 7 cm. Branches and main stem about the	same diameter. Calyces unevenly distributed, fairly thickly 	implanted on the sides of the branches. The individual calyces are 
			very small, attaining a height of but 1 mm and  dome-shaped. The 
			calyx walls are covered with spicules, many of which are 	branched or triradiate arranged en chevron. The calyces are high 
			and the polyps are retractile. 
			Spicules: The spicules are small warty, more or less curved 
			spindles with crosses and triradiate. 
 
Colour			:	The colony is golden brown in colour.
