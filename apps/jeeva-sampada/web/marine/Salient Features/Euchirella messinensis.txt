
SALIENT FEATURES:   
Diagnostic characteristics:        

Female: Prosome and pedigerous segment 1 fused, head rounded, without crest. Genital segment left side with sac-shaped protuberance on dorsal wall covering most of urosomal segment 2. Antenna 2 endopod one quarter length of exopod joint 2 with 5+4 setae. Posterior surface of leg 4 basipod 1 with two unequal spines, longest reaching articulation with basipod 2; one of these spines may be absent.     

Male: Prosome with small, more or less triangular crest.  Left leg 5 exopod joint 3 does not reach first thickening on right leg endopod. (Vervoort 1952e, Sars 1925)

Additional remarks:      
The male that Vervoort (1949, 1952e) and Park (1976b) attribute to E.messinensis has leg 5 slightly different from that figured by Claus (1863), Giesbrecht (1892) and Sars (1925). They figure E.messinensis with the exopod of left leg 5 extending at least to the first thickening on the right leg endopod. 

Maximum length (cm) (male/ female/ unsexed)                                               Female:  4.42-6.20 mm                                                          
Male   :   2.8-5.46 mm.
