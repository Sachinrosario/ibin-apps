SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells with rounded sides, with elongated apical horn and two long slender antapical spines placed apart.

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle slightly dextral, not excavated with narrow lists supported by spines. Intercalary striae absent or very narrow.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 45-55m.
