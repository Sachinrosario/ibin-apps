SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Live zoarium purplish brown in colour, but preserved ones faded to translucent brown in hue. Colonies grow to small tufts. Zooecia arranged biserially. Bifurcation of type 4 of Harmer, 1923 and 1926. No spines or avicularia. The outer distal angle of the zooecia pointed. Opesia occupy the whole front. Large, sub-globular ovicells attached to the inner distal portion in a slanting position to the zooecia. Diagnostic characters: The absence of avicularia is an important character which gives specific distinction for this species and in his key to British species of Bugula Ryland refers B. neretina as the only species without avicularia coming under the genus Bugula.
Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: Ryland found the average length of zooecia to be 745µ whereas here it is 890. Thornely (1905, 1907 and 1912) recorded this species from Palk Bay, Gulf of Mannar and from Ye, Burma. In all the collections she noticed specimens with avicularia. The specimens collected from Cochin harbour during the months of March and April contained ovicells. Settlement on glass and wooden panels during early February shows that the breeding season in this area is different from what Austin and Crisp (Ryland, 1960) have observed at Shoreham. The presence of this form at the Cochin Harbour in fair numbers was noticed only during March and April of 1965, and this form was absent from the test panels exposed during 1963 and 1964. It is not improbable that the sudden appearance of this species here might be through the agency of the large number of ships that visit the harbour.
