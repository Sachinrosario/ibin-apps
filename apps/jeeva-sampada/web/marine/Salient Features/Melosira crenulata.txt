SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves with finely punctate disc, puncta scattered, margins showing numerous well-marked denticulations.

Diagnostic characteristics: Frustules much longer than broad, showing on the margin the valves of slightly marked sulcus, striate, striae generally somewhat oblique.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter 16-30m.
