Descriptive characters:  The following details are based on a specimen from Minicoy Island.  Corallum laxly branching with a greater spread of 20 cm.  Branches rounded at the base, expanding towards the distal ends.  Verrucae characteristic of the genes is ascending and appressed, 2 to 3 mm long, about 2 mm thick; arranged in longitudinal rows; adjacent ones touching to 3 mm apart.  Calices polygonal on the top of the branches, about 1 mm in greater diameter, rounded towards the proximal parts of the branches.  In older calices two cycles of septa with prominent directives are visible.  The septa starts little below the summit of the wall.  Columella projecting – surface coenenchyme spiny.

The species has narrower branches than P. verrocosa and better developed septa.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Filter feeder. Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.  Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae:  Planula.  Planktonic
