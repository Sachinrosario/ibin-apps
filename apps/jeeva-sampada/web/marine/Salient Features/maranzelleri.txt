Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum dichotomously branching.  Axial corallites not always present.  Corallites alternate, distichous. Diameter of branches about 5 mm.  Diameter of the calices 3.5 mm.  Openings of the calices facing in different directions.  Septa in three cycles.  The terteries slightly smaller than the secondaries.  Third cycle unite to the second at the mid depth of the corallite.  Primaries unite to the columella straight.  Columella moderately developed, spongy.  Surface of the branches with ridges bearing plate-like granulations.  This species appears to different from E. amphelioides in smaller tertiary septa, slightly smaller calices which faces in all directions and less developed columella.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
