SALIENT FEATURES:             Ref No: 31, 103

 

Morphological: Integument thick, entire body densely pubescent; rostrum usually uptilted,

straight or upcurved, reaching distal half of 2nd antennular segment or little beyond, armed with 7 to 11 dorsal teeth; longitudinal suture short, ending anterior to hepatic spine; pterygostomian angle blunt or sharp; abdomen with a small median tubercle on 2nd segment and a mid–dorsal crest on last 4 segments; telson armed with 3 or 4 pairs of small movable lateral spines subequal in size; no basial spine on 3rd maxilliped; a small ischial spine present on 1st pereopod; epipod present on 1st to 3rd pereopods; 5th pereopod usually well exceeding antennular peduncle. Petasma with broad, wing–like distolateral projections, directed laterally and curved dorsoventrally; distomedian projections small, curved ventrally. Anterior plate of thelycum concave anteriorly, with a middle groove posteriorly and a bluntly pointed anterior margin; posterior plate notched anteromedially; coxae of 4th pereopods often with a small projection (coxal plate ) densely fringed with setae.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Telson armed with 3 or 4 pairs of small movable lateral spines

subequal in size; epipod present on first 3 pereopods; petasma with broad wing-like distolateral projections directed laterally and curved dorsoventrally; anterior margin of anterior plate of thelycum bluntly pointed; 4th pereopod with a coxal plate fringed with setae.

 

 Colour:Body pink to reddish brown, pereopods whitish.

 

 

Feeding habit:                 Ref. No: 14

   

 Main food:    Crustaceans remains (70%) 

         Feeding type:    Carnivorous bottom feeder.

Eggs and larvae:                  Ref. No:55,136

 

  Characteristics: Three larval stages (Mysis I to III ) encountered in plankton collections and

assigned to this species ( George & Paulinose, 1973 ) measured 3.2 to 4.8 mm in total length. Mysis I  (Fig.2) has the following diagnostic features: Rostrum with a single tooth; no hepatic spine on carapace; minute supraorbital spine, distinct pterygostomian spine and antennal spine present; 3rd abdominal somite with a slight dorsal hump–like carination; telson spine formula 8+8; pleopods in the form of rudimentary buds. In Mysis III the pleopods are well developed, segmented and uniramous.

 

 

  Abundance:  The larvae have been noticed in small numbers in the shelf waters off the southwest

                   coast of India during February-April.
