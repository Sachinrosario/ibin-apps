Salient Features:
Morphological:

Male: Body elongate ovate. Head more or less like a broad based triangle; eyes large and black, with large ocelli and with a pair of large horns placed at right angles to the surface of the head in front of the eyes. Peraeon segments on to six subequal in length, seventh slightly shorter third segment broadest; segments five and six with two pairfs of lateral teeth, outer large and inner small, seventh segment with a single pair of teeth projecting considerably beyond the posterior border of the segment; dorsal surface of peraeon segments minutely setose. Coxal plates latge and projecting beyond the posterior border of the respective segment, their external margin carrying fairly long setae, borders indistinctly crenulate; coxal plates four to seven with strong oblique ridges. Pleon segments as broad as peraeon first segment partially hidden by the seventh peraeon segment, fourth segment considerably overlapping the fifth laterally, segments two to four each with a large,blunt spine placed in the centere opf each half and outer to the spine the posterior border of each segment is lobed or crenulate and the border in between the dpines cut into broad lobes. Telson proximally broader than fifth pleon segment, its lateral borders bisinuopus, postero-lateral borders straight and meeting at a median angle, apex with twelve spines.

First peduncular segment of first antenna vertically expanded into a large curveds lobe extending beyond the anterior border of the head, flagellum eleven jointed. Second antenna reaching the posterior border of the third peraeon segment, flagellum 20-23 jointed. Frontal lamina oval, broader than long, with the postero-median part angular. Maxilliped clearly seven joined, first segment minute, second very long, third slightly produced internally, segments 4 to 5 somewhat broader than the third fifth broadest sixth segment internally produced, seventh small; inner distal part of segments three to five with a strong teeth, fifth segment with a long and two spines and a seta and the 7th with five setae. First maxilla falcate, with a slender curved unguis, inner lobe small. Mandibular palp stout, basal segment stout but short, second segment long and setose, third elongate ovate, with a comb of setae.

First peraeopod stout but short, fourth segment with fout blunt spines, fifth slightly immersed, sixth cylindrical, seventh with a long slightly curved unguis. Seventh peraeopod slender, strongly setose and spiny. Exopod of uropod narrow and elongated, slightly longer than the endopod, with eight outer and four inner spines; endopod rounded, with fourteen spines.

Body greyish white, dorsal surface with uniformally scattered chromatophores; some of them on the telson and uropods brick red.

Female: Generally like the male but showing the following differences:- Body lkess setose, horns on the head very small and the spines on the peraeon segments much smaller; margin of pleon segments entire but segments three and four with a row of small teeth on the lateral aspects of the dorsal side; basal lobe of first antenna not much expanded and the flagellum of the second antenna only sparsely hairy.

Body white, with evenly distributed dark chromatophores.

Additional Remarks:
Specimen of Corallana nodosa from Veli lake showed certain features which have not been observed. The most important of thse are the pronounced sexual dimorphism and the sculpturing of the pleon. The basal projection of the antennular peduncle is vertical and the flagellum of the second antenna is feathery in the male. The cephalic horns are also much larger in the male.
This species is found in laterite rocks in Akathuuri lake (Kerala). It is a strong carnivore.

Size and age: Length of male: 10.0mm, Female: 8.0mm
