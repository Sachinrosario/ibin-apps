SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells with convex valves, areolated with a clear rosette. Chamber openings clear. Radial and secondary spiral systems of areolae are present.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Small spinulae in a ring behind the margin of the valve, 1 to 2 in 10m. Valve edge narrow and striated.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 196m.
