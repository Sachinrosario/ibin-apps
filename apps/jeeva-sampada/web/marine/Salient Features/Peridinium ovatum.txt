SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells lens shaped. Apical horn short. Girdle rarely circular; mostly clearly dextral, flat or hollow, margin broadly winged.

Diagnostic characteristics: Both antapical processes with or without wings, more or less long, placed at the end of margin of sulcus or placed isolated.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 56-62µ; breadth: 57-84µ.
