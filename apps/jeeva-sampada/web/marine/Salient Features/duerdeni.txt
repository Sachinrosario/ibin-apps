Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive, calices 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter, rounded or oval with 12 to 18 septa.  Larger and smaller septa alternating, then former reaching a syliform columella.  Inter corallite wall not much raised but solid.  Septa confluent over the wall.  Septal sides, granular.  Major and minor septa only slightly differentiated.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
