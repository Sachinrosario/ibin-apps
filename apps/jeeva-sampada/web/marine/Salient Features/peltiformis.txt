Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  “One explanate specimen 0.5 cm in spread and 8 mm in thickness shows no marked difference from BERNARD’s type in the British Museum (Nat. Hist) (No. 1886-12-9-284).  An epitheca is visible at the underside which stops 1.7 cm. from the growing edge.  Wherever the corallum is free from epitheca, minute calices can be recognized.  The calices in the upperside are closely placed, deep seated, about 0.5 mm in diameter.  Septa 12, primaries often meet over a columella.  The tubercles are low, uniform in size, looking like grains, filling the intercalicinal area.  There is a set of tubercles around the calices, but they remain free from each other without undergoing fusion.  Corallum highly porous” (Scheer & Pillai, 1974).

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
