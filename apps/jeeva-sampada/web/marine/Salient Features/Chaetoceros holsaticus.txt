SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming straight chains. Aperture large.

Diagnostic characteristics: Resting spore formed one in each cell. Valve of resting spore arched and spinous.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 26.5µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
