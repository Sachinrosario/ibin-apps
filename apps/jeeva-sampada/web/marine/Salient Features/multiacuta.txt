Descriptive characters:  The following details of this species is based on two specimens from Nicobar Islands (Scheer & Pillai, 1974).  Corallum cespito-corymbose, branchlets slender, 5 to 6 mm thick, 2.5 to 3.5 cm high with the apices tapering to a long axial corallite, 12 to 14 mm long with an average thickness of 2.5 mm  (According to Nemenzo the axials of the types are upto 26 mm exsert).  The axials have a few nariform bud corallites.  Lower down the radials are nariform and tubular.  They are wide apart.  Very few in numbers.  Older ones attain the form of tubular corallites.  Nariform radials 2 to 2.5 mm thick, outer wall a little thickened, ascending.  On the axials they are still smaller.  The tubular corallites are 4 to 7 mm long and 2.5 thick.  At the basal parts of the branches the wall is thickened so as to appear bursifor.  Outer wall of the radials placed at 450 to the branch surface, with the plane of aperture perpendicular to the branch surface.  Both the axials and the radials have only the primary cycle of septa developed.  A few radials are subimmersed.  Corallum spongy or weakly echinulate, rather dense in section.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
