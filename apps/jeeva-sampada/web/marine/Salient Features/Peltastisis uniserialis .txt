SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony  delicate with unbranched stem growing from a calcareous, lobulated and  flattened base, attaining a length of 6.2 cm. At the base about 1.1cm is devoid of polyps. The basal internode measures 2 mm, the second is 2.9 mm, third and succeeding ones are 3 mm. The horny nodes are very short, about 0.5 mm and much narrower than the calcareous ones. The polyps are uniserial and about 1.7 mm apart from the summit to summit. The individual calyces are club-shaped or conical, 1 mm in height on their abaxial side inclined towards the branch so that the adaxial side is almost contiguous with it. The margin is free from thorns. The calyx walls are armed with irregular imbricating series of oval scale-like plates with ctenate edges and surfaces thickly emplanted with rounded points.  There are 7 plates on each abaxial row and 2-3 on the adaxial rows. The operculum is strong, primnoid type with eight flaps, each flap consisting of a single scale-like spicule. Between each pair of adjacent polyps there is swelling of the coenenchyma of the branch or stem, the swellings alternating regularly with the polyps and containing the ova. The coenenchyma contains a layer of rather heavy tuberculate spindles arranged longitudinally. 
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are regular covered with sharp and rounded ends (Fig. a, b & c)). The calyx walls are with similar points with ctenate border (Fig. d) and the operculum with triangular scales (Fig. e).
 


Colour	:	The colony is white and calyces light brown.
