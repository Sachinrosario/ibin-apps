SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium bushy, whitish in hue. Zooecia arranged biserially, an internode usually containing eight zooecia. Zooecia oblong, with slightly widened distal end. Opesia occupy three-fourth of the front. Frontal avicularia, not represented in all the zooecia, placed on a raised portion on the lateral outer angle (occasionally inner angle also) of the proximal regions of the zooecia. Mandible directs obliquely distally. Lateral avicularia present, rather small and hence do not give the sides a conspicuous serration. Mandibles distinctly bent distally. Vibracula represented in all zooecia. The furrows of the vibracula slightly arched. Setae long and always longer than the zooecia. Single axial vibraculum. Ovicells large, widely opened and perforated, perforations not placed at the tip of tubercles. Rhizoidal chambers small and point of origin of the rhizoid circular.Diagnostic characters: The present specimen differs from S. ferox in the absence of spines as well as giant avicularia (frontal). From S. diadema the forms under consideration can be distinguished by the absence of spines, which in S. diadema are typically five in number, and by the absence of tubular pores (pores of ovicells, placed on tubular projections).Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite.  Descriptive characters:   The frontal avicularia in the present form are placed slightly raised from the gymnocyst. The direction of the mandible in the present form, which is obliquely distalwards, is different from what Waters has noticed. He found them to be "directed diagonally downwards." The mandibles of the lateral avicularia are of a bent nature.
Of the three specimens of Osburn without scutum, the present specimens show resemblance to S. profundis. Nevertheless, well developed vibracula, frontal avicularia and well developed zooecia found in the present material are wanting in S. profundis.
