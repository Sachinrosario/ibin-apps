Salient Features :
It is tall; stout tufted, perennial, 30-60 cm glabrous herbs with facicled branches.
Leaves are 10 to 25 cm long; convolute, rarely flat, very narrowly linear, glaucous, glabrous, strongly 5 nerved.
Spikelets 1.2 to 1.5 cm long, flat, sessile or shortly pedicellate, smooth white or creamy
white.
