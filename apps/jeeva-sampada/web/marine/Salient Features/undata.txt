Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum formed of vertical or horizontal fronts with contorted perpheral parts.  The surface is covered with papillae, 3 to 4 mm high and wide that may run together to form ridges, 2-4 mm high and width.  Corallites small, restricted to the valleys formed by the ridges.  Corallites 0.4 to 0.6 mm in diameter; all immersed.  Septa poorly developed, first cycle visible, second cycle poorly developed.  The coenostem composed of uniform spines under the lens.

Living coral purple pink or brown.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The inclusion is based on specimens collected by author.  It is the first record.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
