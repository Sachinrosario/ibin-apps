SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Body elongate ovate, the dorsal surface appears warty due to the presence of large tubercles mixed with small ones of various sizes, cephalon comparatively short and narrow with transverse row of 5-6 tubercles. Peraeon segments one to four are almost equal to one another. Each peraeon segments with a transverse ridge, posterior border of segments beaded. The third peraeon segments has two rows, an anterior and posterior. Large longitudinally elongate tubercles along the outer border of the coxal plate. The sutures on the pleon segments are clearly visible. Posterior border of pleon with a row of six tubercles and 2 oblique lateral rows of smaller ones. The posterior pleon has row of six tubercles, of which the central pair is larger than the others. Telson triangular in shape and almost equal in length and breadth, the distal part broadly rounded. The border is crenulate, thickened and curved upwards. The dorsal side of the telson has three pairs of longitudinal rows of tubercles, the outer row consisting of three tubercles, the middle row of four and inner sub median pair of five large tubercles followed by about four slightly smaller ones. The median row stops a little short of the apex of the telson.

  First antenna comparatively slender first segment of peduncle stout, surface granular, third segment slender, flagellum 14 segmented. Second antenna stout and hairy, peduncular segments successively, increasing in length; 14-segmented flagellum, proximal 9-segments with a thick bunch of hairs.

  Epistome broad throughout, apex angular and posterior arms very short, exposed surface with large pustules.

Mandible comparatively weak, two lobed. The upper incisor process conical and tridentate and the lower bidentate. The right mandible with 5 and left with 8 middle spines. First maxilla with a hirsute outer lobe; barbed spine on one side, distal border with 12 spines, except 2 barbed. Inner lobe hairy internally and row of setae externally. Outer lobe with 3 stout pectinate and 2 small plumose setae. Second maxilla with 3 elongate triangular overlapping lobes. The inner lobes not much larger than the other lobes. Maxilliped with stout sympod, inner plate broad and triangular; stout sympod, segments 2 and 3 slightly produced, fifth segments longer than fourth.

  First peraeopod with sub equal basis and ischium, triangular carpus. Propodus with a barbed spine. On the border of the segments 3-6 bushy hairs present. Seventh peraeopod basis longer than ischium segments 2-5 expanded, merus with 8-10 stout setae, carpus with spines, and long propodus.

  Pleopods 1-3 sub similar, peduncle with 3 coupling hooks. Male stylet on second pleopod much longer than endopod. Fourth pleopod with apical lobe of endopod directed backward, hairy. Exopod of fifth pleopod with 5 spiny thickened areas and is produced into a spiny process. Slightly elongated penial process. Rami of uropods sub equal. The endopod of the uropod has 4 large and a few small tubercles on its dorsal side, its border thickened and the apex broad and rounded, exopod bears 5 teeth in addition to the apical tooth. Ventral side of the exopod has a median longitudinal ridge formed of a row of small tubercles.

Main food: Plankton, detritus fungus.

Distribution:
Africa, Suez, Egypt, New south Wales, SriLanka, India.

Additional remarks:
  The number and arrangements of tubercles on the telson show slight variation. This is evidently due to the difference in size. In large specimens tubercles merge to produce prominent warts. In small specimens, the tubercles are distinct.

  The incisor process of the mandible is weak and the spines on the outer lobe of the first maxilla pointed and barbed.
  
Ecological notes:
  S. walkeri is typically marine and is usually found under stones, in crevices or in empty barnacle shells. The morphology of the mouthparts shows that this species is not a true borer. The incisor process of the mandibles in this species is comparatively weaker and nonchitinised than that of the other true woodborers.

Size and age: 8-10m in length.
