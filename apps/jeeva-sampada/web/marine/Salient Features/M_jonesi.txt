Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive, composed of closely set massive, inverted cones arranged in several tiers.  Greater diameter of the colony as measured in the field, 45 cm total height 30 cm.  The holotype preserved which is only a part, is 26 cm in greater spread and 18 cm in total height.  The cones have a narrow, cylindrical base 1 to 1.5 cm thick from which the columns expand towards the top to 4 to 5 cm.  The tops of adjacent cones in the same tier fise to firm a level plat-form from which another set of cones arise and fuse at the tops to form the second plat-form.  Four such layers could be made out.  The top layer plat-form bears numerous irregular or digitiform protuberances which are under various stages of fusion from where another set of cones are destined to develop.

Calices at the sides of the column 0.35 to 0.45 mm in diameter, fuse with the surface, irregular in outline, about 1 mm apart.  At the top of the cones they are larger, 0.6 to 0.75 mm in diameter, close together, not well cut out from the surrounding coenenchyme.  Primary septa prominent, directives exsert.  Second cycle incomplete, rarely the full compliment of six is seen.  The coenenchyme at the sides of the cones mostly glabrous with traces of foveolations at certain places.  At the top of the cones the coenenchyme rises into prominent papillae 1 to 2 mm thick and so much in height.  Two to three papillae often fuse to form low ridges sometimes enclosing a calyx.  The coenenchyme under the lens reveals fine echinulations at the sides of the cones, but the papillae look highly frosted.  Section shows a highly porous reticulum.  In spint of the massive appearance of the colony the coral is very light.  Living coral dull-green.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
