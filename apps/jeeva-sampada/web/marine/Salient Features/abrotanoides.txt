Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of tufted semiarborescent thick clumps.  Main branches 3 to 4 cm thick, dividing repeatedly forming upper digitiform branchlets 1.5 to 2 cm thick with blut tips.  Axial corallites 3 to 3.5 mm thick, 2 mm exsert with thickened wall and rounded opening.  Septa in axilas 12, primaries prominent.  Radial corallites tubular, labellate and immersed.  Many radials are proliferous and are 4 to 6 mm long and 2 to 2.5 mm thick.  Non proliferous radials tubular, but the inner wall less developed than the outer, 4-5 mm long.  Smaller radials are found mixed, some which are labellate and others immersed.  Radials spreading 60 to 80 degrees to the long axis of the branch surface.  Radial corallites have two cycles of septa.  Corallite wall porous in young dense in older ones.

Living coral dull brown with white axials.

The species was once fairly common in the lagoon of all Lakshadweep Islands.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
