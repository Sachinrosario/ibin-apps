Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum is tufts of slender, subdividing branches.  Main branches less than 1 cm thick at the base, lower part often dead.  Tips of branches tapering or sometimes expanded and palmate.  Total height of clumps in Gulf of Mannar upto 15 cm.  Calices small, but conspicuous, 0.35 to 0.5 mm. in diameter, about 1 mm. apart well cut out from the currounding coenenchyme by solid thecal wall.  Septa in two cycles, primaries well developed, extend to the half radius circle of the fossa.  Directive septa very large.  Second cycle incomplete, 3 to 4 per calyx.

Surface coenenchyme reticulate and porous.  In order parts of the corallum sometimes faint foveolatuons are seen.  Section of the branches shows a central loose laminate reticulum and an outer thin semi solid layer.  Living corallum dull brown.

This species is very common in the Islands of Gulf of Mannar along the Mandapam Peninsula where they are found mixed with Montipora divaricata.  It is essentially a lagoon form in Gulf of Mannar.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
