Salient Features:
Morphological:

Male: Body strongly convex and conspicously hirsute, especially in the posterior half. First peraeon segment much longer than others, segements 2 to 7 subequal; dorsal side of posterior segments with aa posterior submarginal transverse row of tubrecles; posterior margin also tubercular, each segment thus having a double row of conspicous tubercles. 6th and 7th segments with a pair ofd large submedian tubercles, seventh with an additional large pair. Coxal plates completely united with the pareon segments and their outer border with longer hairs. Pleon with three to four lateral sutures and a row of large tubercles; posterior border with a pair of high submedian boses and two smaller ones, each with a pencil of long setae. Telson charecteristically sculptured, with strong ridges, large spines and smal;l tubercles and two concavitiers at the anterior part to receive the bosses of the pleon.

Starting from these concavities and running backwards are two high setose ridges, which end in a pair of large double spines with long setae. the two ridges are seperated by a broad median groove. Outer to the double spins there is another small spine which also carries long hairs. Apex of telson trifid, median lobe for exceeding tip of lateral lobes, dorsal side of the medan process with a large rounded boss and a row of setae along its border, its tip bifurcated into two pointed curved spines.

Epistome broad with a conspicous construction in the middle of its lateral borders; apex produced into a small apically blunt process, exposed surface with a number of large tubercles; arms comparitively short and the upper lip projecting beyond the arms. Anntennae mouth parts and paraeopods agree with the generic definition. Male appendix on 7th peraeon segment consisting a pair of long process with small spines on the outer border. Male stylet on secon pleopod very long, twice as long as endopod, proximaaly stout and spiny, distally narrow with small spinules.Exopod of 4th pleopod externally setose, apex with one long seta, endopod with a prominent apical lobe, carrying a spine seta. Exopod of 5th pleopod externally setose, distal part with 4 to 5 spiny thickened areas and distal border of endopod hairy. Rami of uropods subsimilar, exopod ending in a strong outer spine, apex of endopod obliquely truncated, surface of both rami with long hairs.
Body dark grey and the base of the setae dark brown.

Female:  Cephalon slightly shorter than first peraeon segment eyes large, dark brown and laterally placed. First peraeon segment large, much longer than all the others; coxal plates small and sparsely setose. Pleon slightly longer than posterior border without tubercles. Telson with two large bosses surmounted by a small conical tubercle; apex trifid, median lobe broad and distaly forming a broad based triangle, slightly over reaching the apex of the lateral lobes; borders sparsely setose.

Arms of epistome narrower than in the male, outer surface with a number of long plumose setae visible beyond the anterior border of cephalon tubercles abscent; upper lip projecting slightly beyond the arms and its distal border practically without hairs. Mouth parts strongly metamorphsed. Peraeopods similar to those of male but less spiny. Endopod of uropod cylindrical and strongly setose, its distal border rounded; distal border of exopod produced into three blunt teeth, of which the outer is stouter. Bordres of uropods carry long setae.

Body pale grey with charecteristic vernicular markings on peraeon segments.

Additional remarks:

Extreme sexual dimorphism makes the identification of the two sexes of the same species very difficult.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size Recorded 10.2mm
