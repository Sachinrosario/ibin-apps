
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics: -                     

FEMALE: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Prosome with a high median crest. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment produced posteriorly into a narrow tip, but with no spines. Rostral spines equal in length with the depth of the notch. Abdomen 4-segmented. Genital segment almost as long as the combined length of the remaining 3 segments. Ventral side of the genital segment slightly swollen downwards. First antenna 24 segmented, extends up to the tip of the last abdominal segment. In 5th leg , terminal joint slightly concave along the inner margin. Subapical spine 3 times as long as the terminal joint and with the setation. Apical spine short and stout.              

MALE : Line of fusion between the 4th and 5th pedigerous segment clearly visible. Abdomen 5 segmented. First antenna reaches upto the tip of the third abdominal segment, 22 segment. Fifth leg distinct in the following characters. Second joint of the basipod  of the right foot  with a strong tooth along the proximal end of the inner margin, distal inner margin is swollen. Exopod 2-jointed, 1st joint sickle-shaped, 2nd joint lamelliform. Endopod slightly longer than the 2nd joint of the basipod. In the left food 2nd joint of the exopod with 2 curved spines at the tip.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                         Male:  3.65mm                                             
Female: 3.6mm		Ref. No.:   T.C.Gopalakrishnan, 1982
