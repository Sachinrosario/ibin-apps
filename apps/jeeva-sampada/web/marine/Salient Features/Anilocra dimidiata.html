SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Body comparatively broad, symmetrical or asymmetrically twisted to one side. Cephalon roughly triangular, as broad as long, anterior border sub truncate; rostrum prominent and bent downwards, apex angular. Anterior border of first peraeon segment faintly double convex, lateral parts excavated to accommodate the eyes. Fifth and sixth peraeon segments equal in length and breadth, longer than all the other segments, seventh postero-laterally produced to some extend, partially covering the first pleon segment. First four pairs of coxal plates short, slightly oval, last two slender and long. Fifth pleon segment only slightly projecting over telson. Telson slightly longer than broad, distally rounded, median carina indistinct.

First antenna geniculate, eight jointed, second slender, ten jointed. Dactylus of anterior peraeopods with a single nodule. Rami of uropods sub equal in size, endopod very slightly overreaching the apex of telson.

Body uniformly dark grey, due to the presence of closely arranged dark chromatophores.

Size and age: Length: 31.0 mm.
