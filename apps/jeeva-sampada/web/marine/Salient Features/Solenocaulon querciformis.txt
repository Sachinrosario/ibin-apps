SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:Colony  dendritic and flabellate, 23.5 cm in length. The stem and all branches except the ends of the twigs are strongly flattened, the larger branches being coarsely and deeply furrowed lengthwise. The stem measures 9.5 mm X 7 mm in section. The main stem gives of two other large branches, which are greately expanded laterally, and also at the bases of the branchlets, and bear a series of unequal grooves and furrows at the front and back. The main branch is 9 mm X 6 mm in section near its base. After giving off these main branches the stem pursues an undulating course, giving off irregular lateral branches and breaking up at its distal ends into a tuft of branchlets which redivide until branching of the 5th order are attained, the whole forming a club of aggregating brachlets. 
		The calyces are conical, measures 1.5mm in height and about the same in diameter. The often slightly inclined towards the distal ends of the twigs. The polyps are partially retractile and most of them rest with their very heavy collarets on the calyx margin. The collaret is composed of 8 to 10 rows of transverse spindles, the upper 2 or 3 rows curve, so that they form an en chevron arrangement on the tentacle bases. The stem shows a comparatively thin coenenchyma and the axis is composed of very slender rod like spindles with distant thorny points immersed in a horny matrix.
		Spicules: Those of the coenenchyma of the stem are small, rounded (Fig.a) disc-like (Fig.b) or compact irregular forms with prominent and irregularly disposed verrucae (Fig.c) Those of the axis are slender, rod-like forms (Fig.d) which are smooth  on the greater part of the surface bearing distant thorny points and longitudinally disposed, forming a felted mass immersed in a horny matrix (Fig.e).
 
Colour		:	Colony is  slight yellowish brown.
