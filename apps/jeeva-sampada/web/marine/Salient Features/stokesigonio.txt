SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum pulvinate, tending to become hemispherical.  Endges closely followed by an epitheca.  Corallites polygonal penta or hexagonal, opening rounded when the wall is much thickened.  Larger calices 4 to 5 mm long, 4 to 5 mm deep, smaller one intercalated.  Some calices with double the number of usual septa is sometimes present.  Wall highly porous, upto 1 mm thick, generally 0.5 mm.  Septa 24, very narrow at the top of the wall, braoder below; edges entate, sides granular, perforate.  Columella formed of concentrically arranged synapticuale, filling the bottom of the calyx.  Pali not prominent.

The polyps are found fully expanded during day time.  They are more or less 4 to 5 cm above the skeleton when fully expanded.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
