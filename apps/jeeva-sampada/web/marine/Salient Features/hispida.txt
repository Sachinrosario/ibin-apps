Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum initially encrusting, sometimes layer over layer.  The upper surface bears several digitiform or crooked branches which undergo fusion among themselves.  Most of them has a central worm tube.  Branches upto 10 or 15 cm long and 1 to 3 cm thick.  The distal parts were often found to be a little tapering and bent.  Calices very conspicuous, 0.75 mm in diameter, close together or upto 2 mm apart.  A solid thecal wall is present.  Calices level or slightly projecting due to the fusion of the surrounding tubercles.  Septa in two cycles, primaries often meet over a rudimentary columella at the centre of the fossa.  In some of the larger calices, in specimens collected at Mandapam upto 14 septa are seen.  Tubercles slender, frosted, 1 to 2 mm in height, 6 to 8 of them closely placed around the wall forming a tube at the proximal parts with the distal ends free.  The tubercles at the interstitial coenenchyme smaller than those around the calices.  On branches sometimes tubercles fuse together and run together to for low ridges.  Living coral in Gulf of Mannar dull green or brown.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
