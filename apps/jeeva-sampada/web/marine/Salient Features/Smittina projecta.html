SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zooecia in regular longitudinal lines, elongate, distinct, slightly convex, with many minute tubercles. Marginal pores and areolae small. Spines typically 2. peristomes raised proximally as a definite lower lip. Some avicularia immensely enlarged, spatulate, covering the entire zooecium or most of it; the mandible sometimes with 3-4 distal spikes. Small avicularia lateral, oral, often rare. Similar avicularia, occasionally numerous, sometimes developed from the marginal areolae. Ovicells large, globose, the ectooecium extensive; its inner edge raised, projecting as a thin semi-circular ridge. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: Thornelys specimens are recognizable by its gigantic spatulate avicularia, the projecting semi-circualr edge of the ectooecium of the ovicells. The ovicells are generally numerous.
The gigantic avicularia are enlarged oval avicularia. If they do not reach a preceding peristome, they remain oval. If they are longer, they continue to expand, the form of their distal end depending on their relation to a preceding zooecium. In those reaching a single peristome, the end is truncate or even concave, but when they are situated so as to end between two zooecia, they commonly have a median acute end. The ovicells are large, globose, not immersed. The fertile zooecia may have two spike-like projections, one with its edge raised, the other formed by the lower lip of the peristome.
