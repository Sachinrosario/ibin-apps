Descriptive characters:   
The following details are based on a specimen form Port Blair Andamans:  Length 20 cm, maximum width 7.5 cm, almost flat with the axial furrow extending the entire length of the elongated disc.  Septa close together, 14 to 15 per cm length of the edge of the disc.  Every fourth or fifth septum is broader than the intervening ones.  Septal edges dentate, teeth uniform in size, about 1 mm high and broad, average 2 mm apart.  Underside of the disc marked by concentric lines representing the lines of growth.  A scar of attachment visible.  Costal spines low, slender, sometimes secondarily frosted, smaller and thinner at the centre of the disc.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
