SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:        
Female:  The lateral angles of the last pedigerous segment are pointed. The genital segment is symmetrical. The 2nd and 3rd abdominal segments are about equal in length.     The furca is symmetrical. The furcal style is longer than 3 times as long as its width. The anterior antennae, when reflexed, extend beyond the end of the furcal style by the last 2 segments.     The inner marginal spine on the 2nd segment of exopodite of the 5th foot is slightly longer than the3rd segment. There is a notch on the inner margin of the 1st segment of exopodite of the 5th foot.

Size and age: Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     Female:  1.5 – 1.9 mm
