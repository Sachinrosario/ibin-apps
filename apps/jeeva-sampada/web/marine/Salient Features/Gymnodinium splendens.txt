SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small to medium sized pleomorphic cell, typically pentagonal in shape with a broadly conical epitheca and a bilobed hypotheca. Epi and hypotheca almost equal.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Cingulum displaced 1 X Cingulum width. Sulcus does not extend onto the epitheca but deeply notches hypotheca. Apical groove present. Chloroplasts present.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 40-75mm.

Biochemical aspects: Extracts of axenic cultures had kinetin activity.
