Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum initially encrusting with short digitiform branches 1.5 to 2.5 cm in thickness and 4 to 7 cm in height, remaining single or in groups of 2 to 3.  The branches taper towards the tip, terminating on an axial corallite 2.5 to 3.5 mm in diameter and about 1 mm exsert.  The axials have the primary cycle of septa well developed, almost meeting deep-down in the fossa.  The second cycle is also mostly present but they are very small.  Radial corallites crowded, labellate, spreading at right angle, about 1 mm in length and 1 to 1.5 mm in diameter.  They are still smaller at the older parts of the branches and finally immersed.  Only the directives of the primary cycle of septa visible.  Outer wall striated and echinulate.  Surface coenenchyme reticulate and echinulate, porous in section.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The details of the axial and radial corallites of this species is exactly the same as that of A. hebes and the present author at first reported a small specimen from Port Blair (described above) as A. hebes (Pillai, 1969).  But more specimens were later obtained form Nicobar.  This species which seems to be a well defined one, differs from A. hebes only in growth form.
