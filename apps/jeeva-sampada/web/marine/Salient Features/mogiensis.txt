SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological: 
Postlarva I. a length of 9.1mm-10.5 mm. Rostrum bears 3 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth. Supraorbital spines and ptrygostomian spines absent; hepatic spine very prominent. The anteroinferior and posteroinferior margins of carapace are feebly serrate. Antennule with short and equal flagella; antennal scale with a strong terminal spine. Mandibular palp 2-segmented. Dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 3-6, and those on 3rd & 6th are longer. Thoracic legs with setose exopods; 1-3 are chelate. Pleura of abdominal segments 1-4 serrated and  tips pointed; pleopods uniramous. Telson with a median spine with 7 pairs of ( 4 apical and 3 marginal ) spines.

Diagnostic characteristics:-Rostrum short; hepatic spine very small; supraorbital spines absent; the antero-inferior margin of carapace denticulate. A dorsal carina is present on abdominal segments 2-4; presence of dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 3-6, those on  3 & 6 are the longest,almost double the size of others. Pleura of ab.segs. 1-4  serrate and a median spine is present in the telson. 

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: pelagic zone

Main food: Plankton, organic matter with detritus,sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous
Additional Remarks:

Size and age: Postlarva.9.4mm-10.5mm

Mysis II: 7.6mm to 8.2mm

Eggs and larvae:                                                                 

Eggs unknown.

LARVAL STAGES:

Mysis II.length,7.4mm to 8.2mm.Rostrum bears 2 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth on a crest.Rostrum extends beyond the 2nd segment of the antennular peduncle,tip pointed and slightly curved downward. Supraorbital and pterygostomian spines minute or absent. Antero-inferior margin of carapace serrate. Antennule with 2 unsegmented flagella of equal length,,inner with long terminal seta and outer with 2 long and  one short terminal setae. Antenna with endopod segmented and scale with a long terminal spine. The mandibular palp unsegmented and tip club- shaped. All thoracic legs bear exopods.Abdomen with a distinct dorsal carina. Dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 3-6; pleura of ab.seg.1-4 serrate with pointed tip. Uropods well developed,exopod of uropod without  a marginal, distal spine. Telson with a spine formula of 7+1+7

Characteristics: the epigastric tooth on a crest; absence of supraorbital spines; a dorsomedian carina present on abdominal segments 2-4 ; the pterygostomian spine is absent; the absence of dorsolateral spines on ab.segs; pleura of ab.segs.1-4 serrate and their tips pointed and telson with a median spine and 7 pairs of spines. 

Abundance: supports minor fisheries(Holthuis 1980)
