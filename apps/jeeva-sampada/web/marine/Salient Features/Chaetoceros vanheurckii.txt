SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming straight chains, apical axis 24-58µ. Valves slightly constricted in the middle. Apertures narrow, elliptic.

Diagnostic characteristics: Setae more or less curved towards one end of the chain, slightly spinous. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 24-58µ.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
