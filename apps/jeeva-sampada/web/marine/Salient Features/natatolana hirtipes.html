  TYPICAL CIROLANID WITH A SMOOTH BODY WITHOUT ORNAMENTATION HEAD WITH A NARROW SESSILE FRONTAL LAMINA AND FLAT CLYPEUS ANTENNULES ALWAYS VERY SHORT . AN TENNAL PEDUNCLE WITH ARTICLES 6-5 SUBEQUAL IN LENGTH : PERAEOPODS 5-7 WITH BASIS MARKEDLY PLATTENED AND PROVIDED WITH LONG NATATORY SETAE PLEDPOD WITH SMALL LOBE ON OUTER MARGINS OF PEDUNCLE , ALL SIMILAR , ALL SETIGEROUS EXCEPT FOR ENDOPOD 5,APPENDIX MASCULINA ARISING BASALLY
  PLENITE 5 OVERLAPPED BY PLEONITE 4, TELSON ANDUROPODS WITH NUMEROUS SETAE , AMONGS T WHICH ARE SET SPINES
  THE FRONT OF THE HEAD IS BISINUATE , WITH A SHORT ROSTRAL POINT WHICH JUST NEETS THE FRONTAL LAMINA, AND SEPPERATES THE FIRST ANTENNA , FROM THE HIND MARGIN OF THE EYES , A VERY FINE IMPRESSED LINE , MORE OR LESS PUNCTATE , IS CONTINUES ACROSS THE HEAD , THE GREATEST WIDTH OF SECOND JOINT OF PERAIPOD 7 IS IN THE DISTAL THIRD OF ITS LENGTH
