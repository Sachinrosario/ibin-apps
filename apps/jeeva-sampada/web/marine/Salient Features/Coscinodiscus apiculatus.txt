SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells with flat valves. Central area small with a rosette. Large areolation of almost same size 4 in 10m around the rosette and 3 in 10µ near the margin.

Diagnostic characteristics: Chamber openings clear. Radial and secondary spiral series present. Valve margin small, striated, striae 6 in 10µ. Two small indistinct asymmetrical processes present.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 217m.
