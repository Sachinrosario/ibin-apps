SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium attached to the substratum by delicate and translucent rootlets. Main stem covered with cuticle. Alternating angulations present at the nodes. Two branches arise from an internode, a third one when present represented as rootlets. Zooecia arranged alternatingly on one side of the branch. Sub-cylindrical zooecia constricted at the point of attachment with the main stem. Tentacles eight in number. Pleated collar short. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters: The present form agreed closely with the figures and description of Vesicularia papuensis given by Harmer (1915). Harmer feels that the constriction at the point of attachment of the zooecia is an indication of the deciduous nature. The presence of scars noticed in certain regions of the stem in the present specimen probably indicated that zooecia sometimes might fall off from the stem.
