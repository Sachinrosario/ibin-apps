SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells with valves depressed in the middle. Valve areolated, areolae in radial rows. Areolae polygonal, almost all of same size 3 to 4 in 10m, slightly smaller at the margin.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Chamber openings clear. Outer membrane clearly punctate, punctae 25 in 10m. Asymmetrical small processes present.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 200-210m.

Biochemical aspects: Obligate Phototroph, which requires vitamin B12 (Lactobacillus type).
