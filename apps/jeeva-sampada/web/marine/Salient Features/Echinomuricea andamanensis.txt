SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony 90 mm in height and 80 mm in maximum diameter. The branching is irregular. And in some the branches arise almost perpendicular. The axis is horny and comparatively hard though flexible. The coenenchyma is fairly thick so that the branches have an almost uniform diameter throughout, excepting at the tips, which are swollen and culb-shaped. The polyps are disposed irregularly over the whole coenenchyma. The verrucae are not prominent but give the surface an undulating contour. The anthocodiae are almost completely retractile.		
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are (1) A thick conical rugose spine from one end of which warty projections arise. (2) Warty clubs. (3)  Irregular spindles.

Colour	:	Colony is light brown  in colour and the spicules colourless.
