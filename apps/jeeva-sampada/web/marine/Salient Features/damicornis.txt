Descriptive characters:  Corallum caespitose, ramose, hemispherical in outline.  Colonies may attain a size of upto 25 cm in greater diameter.  Lower part often dead.  Branches cylindrical at the base, expand towards the top and 1 to 1.5 cm broad at the apex.  Verrucae large, 2 to 3 mm.  Calices circular at the lower parts of the branches, polygonal at the distal ends.  Septa and columella poorly developed.  Surface of coenechyme echinulate.  Diameter of corallites more or less 1 mm.

Living coral dull brown or yellowish brown with pinky patches.

Remarks:  This is the most common Indo-Pacific species found in almost all reef habitats and is very opportunistic species to settle.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food: Plankton.  Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae:  Planula.  Planktonic.
