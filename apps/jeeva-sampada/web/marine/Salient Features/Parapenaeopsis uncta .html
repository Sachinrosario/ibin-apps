SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No:103

 

Morphological:  Rostrum armed with 9 to 11 dorsal teeth; in females and young males sigmoid-

shaped, toothless on distal 1/4 to 1/3 and styliform, usually reaching 3rd antennular article or beyond; in large males, straight, not reaching beyond 2nd antennular article; epigastric tooth present, but small in males;  postrostral crest grooved, almost reaching posterior margin of carapace; longitudinal suture reaching 3/4 of carapace length; telson unarmed; antennular flagella 0.45 to 0.60 times length of carapace; epipod and basial spine present on 1st and 2nd pereopods except in males in which basial spine is very small or absent on 2nd pereopod. Distolateral projections of petasma simply tapering to end, each with a long dorsomedian spine-like process; distomedian projections very small. Anterior plate of thelycum wide and short, with curved anterior margin and with 2 longitudinal ridges, medially fused with the quadrate posterior plate.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Epipod and basial spine present on 1st  and 2nd pereopods except in

males in which basial spine is very small or absent on 2nd pereopod; distolateral projections of petasma simply tapering to end, each with a long dorsomedian spine- like process; anterior plate of thelycum wide and short, with curved anterior margin and with 2 longitudinal ridges, medially fused with posterior plate.

 

            Colour:  Ground colour of body pale yellow, carapace with a  large dorsoposterior   dark                        brown or reddish patch.       

 

 

Sex attributes:

      Descriptive characters:   In females rostrum is sigmoid-shaped, while in large males it is

          straight.
