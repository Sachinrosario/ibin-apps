SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallet blade and stalk of almost equal length. The calcareous portion of the blade is broadly rounded and extended distally by a dark reddish-brown or black, periostracum, which is characteristically, four pointed. This periostracal cap has a long inner cup-like projection set in a shallower outer one, the distal margin deeply V-shaped, and the outer face more so than the inner. In very young specimens only the lateral projections are developed. Shell similar to that of Teredo but the posterior slope is well developed and may be broader than long.

Additional remarks: Indo Pacific species, tropical to subtropical in destination.
