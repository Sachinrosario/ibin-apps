SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Colonies form thin layers over the glass panels appearing as pale whitish circular patches. Quincuncially arranged zooecia, very slightly calcified. The distal region of the preceding zooecium slightly over arch the proximal part of the succeeding one. Gymnocyst absent. Cryptocyst vestigial. Operculum chitinised. Cyphonautes seems to be present. Ancestrula single.

Diagnostic characteristics: The less calcified zooecia and the vestigial nature of the cryptocyst make the identification of this form rather difficult. The simplicity of the zooecial structure, the absence of spines of any kind, gymnocyst as well as cryptocystal spines separates this from E. crustulenta.
Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: The arrangement of the first zooecium developed from the ancestrula is very similar to that found in E. crustulenta collected from Cochin. The operculum is well chitinised and could be distinguished as distinct brown structures on dry specimens.
Meristic characteristics:
Feeding habit:
Main food: Plankton
Feeding type: Suspension feeder
Additional remarks: A very interesting association has been noticed between this species and a folliculinid species. Several folliculinids are seen in close association with the zooecial walls, especially at the corners.
