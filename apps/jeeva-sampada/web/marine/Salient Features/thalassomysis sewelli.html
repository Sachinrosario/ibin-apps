Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics: Genus: Thalassomysis
Carapace without rostral plate. Antennal scale setose all round without terminal spine. First thoracic limb with both exopodite and epipodite. Telson very long and narrowly pointed, apex entire, lateral margin armed with numerous spines on distal part, no plumose setae at apex.

Thalassomysis sewelli
Carapace covering whole of thorax, anterior border semicircular, fully exposing eyes and base of antennae. Eyes small, flattened, cornea almost ventral, dorsal inner part of stalk with papilla. Antennal scale overreaching antennular peduncle, about six times as long as broad, slightly narrowed in middle, with long slender setae all round, apex broadly rounded. Third segment of antennal peduncle with backward extension. Endopods of thoraci limbs long and slender, sixth segment divided into three subsegments, first much longer than other two combined. Telson six times as long as broad, narrowly triangular and very long, longer than endopod of uropod, distal two third of lateral margin armed with about thirty long spines, apex narrowly truncate with pair of spines. Uropod without spines.
