SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Round to cylindrical cells with weakly developed valve mantel and convex disc. Cell wall areolated, areolae in pervalvar as well as in two series crossing one another. 
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Centre of the disc not differentiated, umbilicus absent.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 26-41m in diameter.
