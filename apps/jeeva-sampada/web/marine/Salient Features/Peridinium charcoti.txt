SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Girdle slightly excavated and flattened to the body. The epitheca is flattened at base on which is set abruptly a zone some what inflated.

Diagnostic characteristics: The two-antapical spines very robust long and slightly parallel, winged and each implanted on an antapical horn.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 60-67.5µ. Diameter: 56-62µ.
