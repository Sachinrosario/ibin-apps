SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony subflabellate, branched in one plane, branches dividing 
dichotomously and without any sign of fusion. Colony measured 13 x 12.9 cm. Diameter of
 stalk 5 mm and branches 1.5 to 3.5 mm.

			Calyces borne on all sides with a tendency to get arranged 
laterally in older parts and spirally in actively growing parts; globular in shape, 
size 0.7 x 1.3 mm (height x diameter). Polyps retractile completely.

			The skeletal arrangement tallies well with that of the type.

			Spicules : (1) Discs. Robust, rounded to sole-shaped or even 
top shaped. Tubercles on disc with minute spine like structures. Size upto 0.84 x 0.75 mm. 
Spicules cover general body surface and on calyces they may be smaller (Fig.a) (2) Spindles
 and crosses may be smaller (Fig.b & c). 

Colour		:	Colony greyish brown when dry, axis dark brown and spicules colourless.
