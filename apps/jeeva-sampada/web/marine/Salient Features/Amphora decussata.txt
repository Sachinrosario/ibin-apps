SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Thin elliptical frustules with truncate ends. Zone with numerous divisions, 10-12 in 10µ, very finely transversely striate. Raphe close to the ventral margin.

Diagnostic characteristics: Dorsal side with oblique striae 15-18 in 10µ turned in opposite directions. Punctate the punctae being slightly elongated.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 78-99.5µ long; 28-50µ broad (girdle view).
