SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with slightly convex valves, numerous cells joined together by their spines to form chains. Areolae at the base of valve mantel small 7 in 10µ, towards the disc increase in size 4 in 10µ and in the centre of the disc very large and hardly visible.

Diagnostic characteristics: Spines numerous arranged in a ring and enlarged at the base.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 53-112µ.
