Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum disc-shaped, rounded, flat or slightly arched at the underside.  Axial fossa 1 to 2.5 cm depending on the diameter of the disc.  Disc perforate.  A few major septa highly broad and project into the axial fossa.  These parts of the septa are generally entire without any well developed teeth.  Teeth on the outer portions of the septa small, uniform average 1 tooth in one mm of the septal edge.  Septa unequal in height.  Coaste correspond to septa, larger and smaller.  Costal spines getting smaller towards the peripheral parts of the disc, 1 to 1.5 mm high and thick at the base; sometimes swollen at the base and two to five undergo fusion and frosted.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
