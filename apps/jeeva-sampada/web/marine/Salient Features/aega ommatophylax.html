SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:  Ref No:  BARNARD, 1936
  A CHARACTERISTIC FEATURE OF A. OMMATOPHYLAX IS THE PRESENCE OF PROCESSES ON THE HEAD AND VENTRAL SIDE OF FIRST PERAEON SEGMENT RESMBLING THE 3-PRONGED ARRANGEMENT HORNS IN THE MALES OF SOME COPINE BEETLES. THE ROSTRAL PROCESS, WHICH MEASURES AS MUCH AS 2.0mm, IS EXPANDED AT THE APEX AT ITS BASE IS A SMALL ACUTE ROSTRAL POINT WHICH MEETS THE FRONTAL LAMIND PROCESSED ON 1ST PERAEON SEGMENT MAY MEASURE 2.75 mm.
  EYES ARE CONTINUOUS ACROSS THE HEAD, BUT SOME OF THE OCEELLI IN THE MIDDLE LINE ARE UNPIGMENTED, CAUSING AN IRREGULAR WHITE LINE.
  THE CULTRIFORM PROCESS ON FIFTH SOINTS OF PERAEOPODS 2 to 3IS PRESENT ON BOTH RIGHT AND LEFT SIDES.THE SECOND JOINTS OF PERAEOPODS 4- 7 ARE KEELED IN MIDDLE OF OUTER SURFACE.
