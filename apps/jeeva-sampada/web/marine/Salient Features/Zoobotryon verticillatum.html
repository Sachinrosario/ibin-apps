SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium light greenish in hue, branching irregular and hence the zoarium forms a tangled mass. Rounded stolons large and semi-transparent. Zooecia arranged bilaterally on either side of the stolon, but may occur in an irregular crowded fashion. Elongated ovoid zooecia show variations in size, the place of attachment of the zooecia to the stolon, rounded and narrow, the distal portion being blunt and squarish in shape. Tentacles seem to be eight in number. A gizzard present. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:   Since the colonies contained zooids of various stages of development and because of the transparency and good fixation of the material it was possible to make certain general observations (only external) on the developmental stages of the zooids. The development of the zooid started as a small protuberance on the stolonal wall, and this papillate structure grew and attained the shape of a small rounded bud-like structure, but this bud was not constricted from the stolon and hence in external appearance it looked like a swelling of the stolon. The primordium of the polypide was to be found inside the bud which had attained a length of 30 to 40 and the polypide rudiment was oval in shape and appeared to be heavily granulated. This was followed by the differentiation of the polypide rudiment with 6 to 8 rather small anterior segments the primordial of the tentacles. As the bud developed further the rudiment of the polypide grew and attained a U-shaped condition.
  Buds occupying the sides of the stolons showed different stages of development and this feature indicated that the development of the opposite buds need not coincide despite their bilateral arrangement.
