SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics: -                 

FEMALE: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segment fused; prosome with a high median crest, last pedigerous segment produced posteriorly into a triangular expansion terminating into a sharp pointed spine on either side; rostrum bifid at the tip. Urosome  4 segmented. Genital segment swollen ventrally, ventral posterior margin overlapping the following segment. Postero-lateral margins of the genital segment furnished with spines, which are absent on the dorsal and ventral sides. Antennule 23 segmented, reaches upto the posterior tip of the genital segment. Leg 5 asymmetrical; apical spines short, subapical spines long with 2 rows of teeth that of the left side thicker than that of the right side.                 

MALE: General appearance similar to female. Urosome 5 segmented. First antenna with 20 segments when the fused 8th to 12th segments are counted as one. Fifth leg as illustrated in the figure. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                          Male:   4.57mm   
Female:  4.2mm                                       				T.C.Gopalakrishnan, 1982
