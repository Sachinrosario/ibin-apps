Descriptive characters:  
The following are the details of a specimen obtained from Andamans:  Corallum encrusting, 4 to 6 mm thick at the broken edges.  Underside marked by alternately thick and thin coatae.  Calices on the upper side, 2 to 4 mm in diameter (i.e. distance between adjacent columella centers).  Ambulacra flat.  There are 15 to 18 septa around a calyx, laternating in size, the larger ones much exsert to 1 mm around the periphery of the calyx.  Septal edges entire, sides highly spinulose.  Seven to eight septa reach the columella.  Columella an upright style or compressed twisted lamina.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
