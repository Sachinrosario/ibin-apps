SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:  Encrusting colonies brownish in hue.  Zooecia arranged in longitudinal rows.  Frontal membrane covers the entire surface.  Operculum membraniporine.  Gymnocyst wanting.  Tuberculated cryptocyst extend around the opesia, proximally they invade three-fourth of the opesia, the median process extending from the centre. Opesia trifoliate.  Median process convex and raised distally giving it the appearance of a polypide tube.  Cryptocyst only moderately developed at the lateral portions.  The opesiules little elongated and extend towards the sides of the median process.  Ovicell not noticed.
Diagnostic characters:  
Sex attributes:  Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: Ovicells were noticed by Harmer whereas they were not noticed in the material collected by Menon.  The presence of spinules on the lateral edges of the cryptocyst is not of common occurrence.  The zooecia are found to be arranged in distinct longitudinal rows.  The median processes are not symmetrically placed in some zooecia. Harmer has observed that the size of the zooecia varies and the strongly developed proximal part of the cryptocyst which is produced into a median process is "provided with spinules on its lateral edges, occasionally wanting (in some of the zooecia of the colony)".  Harmer has noticed 'a pair of larger spinules in the position of the cardelles' (Osburn, 1950).

Additional remarks:   The record of this species from a depth of 138m by Menon extends the bathymetric distribution of this species to a sufficiently great depth.  Harmer could collect the material only from 0-52m.
