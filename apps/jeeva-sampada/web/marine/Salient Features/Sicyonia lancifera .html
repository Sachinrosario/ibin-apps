SALIENT FEATURES:          Ref No: 48, 49, 52, 83, 111, 113.

 

Morphological: Rostrum reaching middle to end of 3rd segment of antennular peduncle, distally

upcurved and ending with trifurcate tip, lateral carinae well-elevated on both sides, armed with 8 dorsal (including 4-5 postrostral ) and 1 ventral teeth; hepatic spine strong; abdominal somites with remarkably developed and well sulcated dorsomedian carinae, which on 6th somite sharply pointed as a tooth at posterior end; pleurae of 1st & 2nd somites unispinose, those of 3rd to 5th somites armed with 3 spines each; telson with a pair of strong fixed spines subapically . Petasma more or less flattened, subrectangular, provided with 2 pairs of distolateral horns directed anteriorly and laterally; a pair of smaller distomedian processes pointing inwardly. Appendix masculina unsegmented. Anterior plate of thelycum somewhat square-shaped, moderately concave and narrowing anteriorly as a sharp apical spine.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics: Carapace with 4 to 5 postrostral teeth; pleurae of 1st and 2nd

abdominal sometes unispinose, those of 3rd to 5th somites armed with 3 spines each; petasma provided with 2 pairs of distolateral horns directed anteriorly and laterally; anterior plate of thelycum somewhat square-shaped, moderately concave and narrowing anteriorly as a sharp apical spine.

 Colour: Ground colour of body light yellowish brown.

                   

 

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:  Male has the specific type of petasma on 1st pair of pleopods, and

appendix masculine on endopod of 2nd pair of pleopods. Female has the specific  

type of thelycum on the sternum of last two thoracic somites.
