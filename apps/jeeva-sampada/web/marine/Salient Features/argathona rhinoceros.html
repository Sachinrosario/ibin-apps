Salient features :
MorphologIcal:
Body oblong and comparatively stout, dorsal surface smooth. Cephalon produced into a prominent
curved rostrum, an important character of the species. First peraeon segment longest, seventh shortest, the latter considerably arched. Coxal plates very well developed, all produced beyond the posterior border of
the segments, their apices pointed and curved outwards; seventh coxal plate reaching the third
All the pleon segments distinct, fourth laterally over- lapping fifth. Apex of telson rounded, with six spines.
First antenna slightly shorter than peduncle of second, flagellum twelve segmented. Second antenna reaching the posterior border of third peraeon segment or slightly overreaching it; flagellum twenty five jointed. Frontal lamina pentagonal, slightly bulged basally. Cutting edge of mandible straight, molar membranous. First maxilla with a small tooth at the base of the unguis, inner lobe broad and apically truncate, with a small inner apical spine. Maxilliped short, but stout, six jointed, with prominent barbed setae.

Peraeopods less spiny than those of A.normani. endopod of uropod distally semicircular, exopod almost as long as endopod.
Body white, with minute uniformly distributed dark chromatophores.
Size and Age : Length 10.0 mm
