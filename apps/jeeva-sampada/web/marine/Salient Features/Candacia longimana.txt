
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 
  
Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female:   The genital segment is symmetrical.  The anal segment and the furca are short.  The anterior antennae consists of 24 segments, of which the proximal 7 segments are thickened.
     The 1st marginal spine of the 3rd segment of anterior maxillipede is slightly thinner and shorter than the 2nd.  The terminal spine of the 3rd segment of exopodite of the 3rd foot is much shorter than the interval between 2 last marginal spines. 
     The 5th pair of feet is symmetrical.  The 3rd segment terminates into 3 small spines which are about equal in length.

     Male:  The lateral angles of the last pedigerous segment are asymmetrical. The right side of the genital segment are produced into blunt process.  The right anterior antenna consists of a grasping organ.
     The 5th pair of feet is asymmetrical. The right foot is modified into forceps.
