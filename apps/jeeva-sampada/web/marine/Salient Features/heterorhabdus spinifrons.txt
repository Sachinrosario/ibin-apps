SALIENT FEATURES:.
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:       
Female: Cephalothorax, 2.33 mm; urosome, 1.16mm. The frontal margin of the head produced into a small spine. The 1st antenna exceeds the end of the furca by distal 2 segments.       

In the 5th pair of legs the inner marginal spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod is about as long as the 3rd segment of the exopod; the endspine about 1/3 the length of the 3rd segment of the exopod.           

Male: Cephalothorax, 2.05 mm; urosome, 0.90 mm.     .

In the right 5th leg the 2nd basal segment has rather a small process on the 2nd segment of the exopod furnished with  small spine at the apex: the 3rd segment of the exopod is long, about twice as long as the 2nd segment of the exopod; the sub-apical spine on the 3rd segment of the exopod short. In the 5th leg the outer edge spine of the 2nd segment of the exopod larger than that of the 1st segment of the exopod; the 3rd segment terminates into a long spine; the proximal part of the spine not thickened.
