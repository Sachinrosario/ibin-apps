SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics: The length of the head is approximately half its height and half the length of the pereon . The cone of the antennal gland extends to the lower margin of the head or projects beyond it. The outer lobes of the maxillipeds are short and broad bear a mall number of setae.
The 2nd segment of pereopods I projects sharply forward in the proximal part of the anterior margin and the distal part of the segment is much narrower than the proximal; the 5th segment is twice longer than wide and a armed with one spine in the middle and two spines in the distal part of the posterior margin; the 6th segment has one spine in the middle of the anterior margin. Pereopods II are thin; the 2nd segment is six times longer than wide; the distal process of the 5th segment is 1/3 the length of the 6th segment. Pereopods III-IV are very thin. Pereopods V-VII are much stronger; the 2nd segment is broadly oval; the 4th and 5th segments do not bear long spines. The triangular telson is somewhat longer than its width at the base; in females it is 5/6 , in males ½ the length of the basipodite of uropods III.
