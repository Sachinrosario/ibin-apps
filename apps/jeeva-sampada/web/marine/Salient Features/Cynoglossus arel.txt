Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

 Body flat and elongate, with dorsal and anal fins joint to caudal fin.
Eyes on left side of body, with a smallscaly space between them; snout obtusely pointed, rostral hook short, corner of mouth reaching or almost reaching beyond lower eye, about midway between gill opening and tip of snout.  Two lateral lines on eyed side but none on blind side.   Scales ctenoid on eyed side but cycloid on blind side, large 7 to 9 scale rows between lateral lines on eyed side.  

Colour:- Eyed side uniformally brown, with a dark patch on gill cover, blind side white.
