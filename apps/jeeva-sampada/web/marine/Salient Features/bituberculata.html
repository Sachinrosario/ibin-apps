SALIENT FEATURES:

“First peraeon segment with a broad V-shaped groove. Fifth pleon segment with the median portion slightly elevated, lateral crests prominent. Telson nearly circular, lateral crests prominent, without tubercles, their margin broadly crenate. Proximal median part of telson raised, with two low tubercles joined by an arcuate carina. Distal border with long setae mixed with small ones, tubercles and spine-like setae absent”.

“Flagellum of both antennae 4 segmented. Palp of mandible reduced to a seta. Seatal row composed of a bundle of 5 sub-similar sparsely dentate setae. Lacinioid seta, like the setae of the setal row, bent at right angles in the middle and bifid beyond. Second segment of sympod of maxilliped broadening distalwards, inner lobe apically truncate with 4 simple and 4 pectinate setae. Epipod clavate, very slightly over reaching the second segment of sympod, broadest slightly behind the tip, border hairy and setose”.

“First peraeopod with the two customary spines, secondary unguis fairly bifid, upper border of merus dentate. Merus of sixth leg with 2 and carpus with 4 large spines; merus of seventh leg with 3 and carpus with 5 spines”.

“Peduncle of uropod stout, roughly as long as endopod, exopod half as long as endopod, with a prominent apical claw”(Pillai, 1961).

Main food: Plankton, alga, detritus.

Distribution:
Quilon, Kerala (Pillai 1957) this species infests the holdfast of Sargassam.

Remarks:
  Reported only from the type locality, in Kerala. It resembles L. (P) segnoides very closely but the sculpturing of the pleon and telson is different. In L. (P) segnoides the fifth pleon segment bears a V-shaped median ridge and a pair of diverging ones on the telson. In L. (P) bituberculata, the pleon segment has no sculpturing and the telson has a pair of tubercles connected by an arcuate ridge (Pillai, 1961).

Size and age: 4-6mm long.
