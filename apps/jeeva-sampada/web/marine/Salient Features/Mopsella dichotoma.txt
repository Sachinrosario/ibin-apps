SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony is fan-shaped or reticulate. Stem and branches are circular in cross-section. The nodes do not shoot up externally, the internodes are 4-6 mm at the base, 6-8 mm long at the ends. The polyps stand irregularly on both sides and on the surface and are absent on the opposite side, which is longitudinally striped. The polyps are 1 mm high, 1.25 mm broad. The bark tissues are leaf and thorn calyces as well as triangular forms, while usual; spindles are rare and represent small, bent forms provided with warted belts
		Spicules. The coenenchyma contains spindles of 0.12 mm length, with thorny clubs, 4 edged spindles, single and double shaped spicules of 0.36 mm in length. The polyps have warted spindles of 0.12 mm length. 

Colour		:	The entire colony is wax-red or yellow, nodes brown and the   
                                                   internodes strong yellow.
