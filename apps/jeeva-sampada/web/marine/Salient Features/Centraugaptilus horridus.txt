SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female:    Cephalothorax 7.76 mm; urosome 1.69 mm. The cephalothorax very robust and hirsuite. The cephalic segment valuted anteriorly. The abdomen 3-jointed. The genital segment about as long as wide. The furcal rami  as long as wide. 
     The 1st antenna reaches back to the posterior margin  of the 3rd pedigerous segment. The 2nd antenna has the exopod and endopod of about equal lengths. The mandible has the exopod about 1.7 times as long as the endopod. The other mouth parts and the swimming legs resemble those of  C. rattrayi (T. Scott).
 
     Male:  Cephalothorax, 7.39 mm ; urosome 1.94 mm. General appearance as in the female. The grasping antenna on the left side; the segments 18-21 and the segments 22-23 are fused.
     In the 5th pair of legs the 2nd basal segment of the right leg is furnished with short hairs on the proximal inner margin.

Additional  remarks:
     The species is easily recognised by its large size. According to Sewell the species exhibits a considerable variation in size. Farran’s female specimen measured 10 mm; Jespersen’s 7.5 mm; Esterly’s specimen from the Northeast Pacific measured 6.68 mm; Sewell’s specimen from the Arabian Sea measured 6 to 7 mm in the female.
