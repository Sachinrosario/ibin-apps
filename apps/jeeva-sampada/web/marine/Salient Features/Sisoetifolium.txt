SALIENT FEATURES OF Syringodium isoetifolium (Asch.) Dandy 1939

Leaves: Leaf tubular, narrowed at base and pointed at the apex 

Inflorescence: Flowers in terminal cymes, growing upto 29 cm long 

Perianth: Anthers and ovaries are protected by reduced leaves; no organised tepals 

Ovary: 2, ovoid, one style with bifid stigma 

Fruit: Ellipsoid with hard pericarp 

Flowering and fruiting: Throughout the year
