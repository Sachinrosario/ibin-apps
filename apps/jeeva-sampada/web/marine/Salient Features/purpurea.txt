SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting to submassive.  Corallites polygonal, calices polygonal or oval.  Wall thin or thickened to 3 mm with a middle groove.  Corallite wall level or slightly raised up.  Length of calices 5 to 8 mm width 4 to 7 mm depth 2-3 mm.  Septa range from 40 to 60 depending of the size of the calices.  Septa exsert, exsert ends stop at the groove, edges with 4-6 teeth, sides granular.  In oval calices the first two cycles of septa are larger than the rest.  10 to 20 septa reach the columella.  Columella composed of several vertical papillae, elongated in the direction of the long axis of the calyx.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
