SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Shell sub-globular with the anterior slope provided with a deep sinus. Posterior slope narrow.
			Pallet-blade is longer than wide. Distal margin on inner face nearly straight, margins of two cups on the outer face deeply ‘U’-shaped. Basal cup usually clearly visible, much shorter on outer face.

Additional remarks: This species occurs in fair numbers in the mangrove forests adjoining the mouth of the Talapady River opening into the Arabian sea about 15 km south of old Manglore port and the Nethravathy-Gurpur estuaries. Mangrove vegetation includes such typical genera as Rhizophora and Avicennia along with other mangrove shrubs. Salinity varies from almost fresh water during the monsoon to near marine conditions during the pre-monsoon period. Shells and pallets have been collected from drift wood (not from fixed structures) cast ashore on Lakshadweep Archipelago.
