SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:        

Carapace smooth in early zoea and rostrum toothed on both sides in postlarvae; carapace with an epigastric tooth; a pair of dorsolateral spines present on abdominal somite 5 from 1st zoea to zoea 10; prominent supraorbital and branchiostegal spines present in  2nd stage zoea to 10th zoea; pterygostomian spines always present;pereopods 1-3 biramous, 1 and 2 chelate; pereopod 5 uniramous and appear before the 4th. It is also uniramous and appear very lately; pereopod 5 is very long and terminal segment forming a pointed claw; pereopod  4  dvelops as biramous buds in zoea 6; telson long and parallel-sided, bearing 8 spines. Pleopods appear as uniramous buds and later becomes biramous in zoea 10; seta are mostly plumose.     
In the postlarvae the carapace bears only the branchiostegal and  antennal spines; pereopods devoid of exopods; telson with sharply-pointed tip bearing only 4 spines and 2 setae,anterior  2 pairs of lateral spines movable and have shifted towards dorsal aspect of telson.
Diagnostic characteristics:         Rostrum with dorsal and ventral teeth; carapace with a branchiostegal spine; pereopod 1-2 chelate; pereopod 5 appears before 4th pereopod, rather the delayed development of 4th leg; a paired dorsolateral spine present on 5th abdominal somite.

Sex attributes: Separate (Heterosexual).

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.
Main food:  Plankton, detritus with  sand and mud, small crustaceans.
Feeding type: Omnivorous, turns to be cannibalistic.

Size and age:  
Zoea I-X: length 2.10mm- 6.07mm.
Postlarvae: I-III: length 6.50mm-6.90mm 

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                             Zoea I: length 1.92mm-2.10mmCarapace smooth; anterolateral edge produced into small pterygostomian spine; rostrum slender and pointed; pereopods 1-2 appear as small,biramous buds; abdomen 6-segmented; telson not separated from abdominal segment 6; telson broad,concave posteriorly,carrying 7 setose spines on either side.

Zoea II: length 2.14mm-2.36mmCarapace with prominent supraorbital and branchiostegal spines; eyes stalked; pereopod 1-2 developed; terminal claw of pereopod 1 conspicuous; 6th abdominal somite with a pair of dorsolateral spines; telson with an inner,non-setose spine each in the middle.

Zoea III: length 2.33mm-2.62mmRostrum with epigastric tooth; carapace with well defined supraorbital, branchiostegal and pterygostomian spines; biramous buds of pereopod 3 and uniramous bud of pereopod 5 developed; propodus of pereopod 1 and 2 bear 3 spines each; telson less broad,more concave and bears 8 pairs of spines; uropods biramous and endopods without setae.

Zoea IV: length 2.35mm-2.70mm.Pereopods 3 and 5 developed; rostrum with 2 dorsal teeth; pereopod 5 uniramous,4 -segmented and terminal segment ends in a claw; endopod of uropod with plumose setae.

Zoea V: length 2.70mm-2.93mmRostrum with 2 dorsal teeth; biramous buds of pereopod 4 developed; telson rectangular, 3 marginal and 5 terminal spines.

Zoea VI: length 3.12mm-4.08mmPereopod 4,fully developed and biramous; endopod 4 -segmented and ending in a claw; telson narrowed at end.

Zoea VII: length 3.85mm-4.43mmRostral teeth serrated on the ventral surface; uniramous buds of pleopods developed; telson narrow posteriorly with 10 spines,outermost ones the longest and conspicuous.

Zoea VIII: length 3.93mm-5.10mmOuter flagellum of antennule subdivided into 2 on inner side; 4 setae at joint of propodus and dactylus of pereopod 1-4; budsof pleopods biramous and non-setose; tip of telson pointed.

Zoea IX: length 4.67mm-5.88mm.Pereopods 1 and 2 chelate; exopod of pleopods setose; exopods of pleopods 2,3,and 4 with 2,2,and 3 plumose setae respectively; telson tip pointed with 8 pairs of spines, the 4th pair being the longest.

Zoea X: length 5.68mm-6.07mmRostrum with characteristic double curve,appendix interna on pleopods 2-5; chelae of legs 1 and 2 are developed; exopod of uropod with a spine.

Postlarva I: length 6.02mm-6.50mm.Carapace with antennal and branchiostegal spines; no supraorbital spines present; Rostrum with 9 dorsal and 1 ventral teeth; pereopods devoid of exopods; endopod 5-segmented; legs 1 and 2 chelate,latter stout but leg 5 the longest; the paired dorsolateral spines on abdominal somite 5 is absent; distolateral margin of exopod of uropod with a movable spine, in addition to the fixed one; telson with sharply pointed tip,bearing only 4 spines and 2 setae; anterior 2 pairs of lateral spines movable and have shifted towards dorsal side of telson.
