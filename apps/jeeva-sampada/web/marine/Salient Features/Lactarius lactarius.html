SALIENT FEATURES              Ref. No. 18

Morphological:

Body oblong, strongly compressed, slightly deeper than length of head,
upper and lower profiles similar. Mouth large and oblique, with prominent
lower jaw; a pair of sharp canine teeth at front of each jaw. Two dorsal fins,
the first with 7 to 8 spines. Caudal fin forked. Scales moderate sized,
cycloid, very easily shed.      Ref.No.18

Diagnostic characters:

Body oblong and strongly compressed. Head large, about 2.5 times in standard length. Mouth rather large and oblique with prominent lower jaw. Teeth small in jaws, a pair of small sharp canine teeth in front of each jaw. Two dorsal fins. The first dorsal with 7-8 spines. Anal fin with three spines and with 25-28 soft rays, its base longer than that of the second dorsal fin. Pectoral fin long and pointed. Pelvic fin just below the pectoral fin base. Caudal fin fortified. Scales medium typed, cycloid, very easily shed.

Colour: Body silvery gray, with blue iridescence above and silvery white below; a dusky spot on upper part of operculum. Fins pale yellow, caudal fin sometimes with dusky margins.            Ref.No.18

Season:    Dec. to April and July to September off Mangalore
Fecundity:    9000-79000 eggs     Ref. No.13
Comment:
The fish attains maturity at 135 mm. Maturation in whitefish is a
continuous process and the fish may release eggs in batches over a long period.  Fishes in different stages of maturity occur over a number of months, thus suggesting absence of specific periodicity of spawning. Considering the occurrence of ripe and spent fishes, it appears that peak spawning is during December-April and during the south west monsoon months of July to September off Mangalore. The sex ratio (M:F) at Mangalore was 1: 0.2-1.1                  Ref. No.13


Studies at Waltair along the east coast indicated that 50% of fishes mature
at 167mm and those above 188 mm were mature. Fecundity varied from
18,000 to 104,000 eggs (Average 56,218 eggs). The peak period of
spawning observed was from February to April. Young fish measuring less
than 50 mm were observed in the inshore waters during April to June. The
fish probably inhabits the inshore waters for spawning purpose. The male to
female ratio was 2:1.             Ref.No.1
