SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Wading birds; Bill long slender; legs long with tibia partly base; wings long and pointed.

Diagnostic characteristics: Conciously broad based and black long bill which is swollen at the tip. Supercilium broad and white.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike. Breeding population have refocus coloured on the head, neck and breast.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Forages in mud flats for aquatic invertebrates.

Main Food: Molluscs, crustaceans and worms.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Breeds in Central Asia
