Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Branching, tufted, branches sometimes coalascent.  Colonies attached, sometimes free-lying.  Tips of branches digitiform or with crooked branchlets, tips of branches upto 2.5 cm bread 3 to 7 mm thick, base narrower.  Calices more or less 1 mm in diameter, flush with the surface, no thecal wall, arranged in short rows parallel to the long axis of the branches.  Septa 6 to 10 per calyx, a large clud-shaped one alternating with thinner one, sometimes bifurcating at the periphery of the calyx before merging with the coenenchyme.  Edges of septa with 4 to 6 serrations.  Axial fossa conspicuous with a central styliform compressed columella.  Under the lens the surface looks fine grained.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
