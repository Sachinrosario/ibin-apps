SALIENT FEATURES:         Ref No:31,103

 

Morphological:   Rostrum armed with 7 to 9 dorsal teeth; sigmoid shaped, toothless on distal

third to half, upcurved and exceeding antennular peduncle in females; curving downward, unarmed portion lacking and reaching only to middle of 2nd antennular segment in large males; both shapes found in young males; epigastric tooth small, absent in large males; postrostral crest almost reaching to posterior margin of carapace, feebly grooved or flat; longitudinal suture generally reaching 3/4 of carapace length; antennular flagella 0.4 to 0.6 times length of carapace; epipod and basial spine present on 1st and 2nd pereopods; basis of 3rd pereopod and telson unarmed. Petasma with long, rabbit ear-shaped distomedian projections, deeply concave ventrally; distolateral projections short, directed anterolaterally; proximolateral lobes very large, and curved dorsally. Thelycum with anterior plate distally rounded and broadly articulating with posterior plate, the latter with a median tubercle bearing a tuft of long setae (hairs).

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Epipod and basial spine present on 1st and 2nd pereopods; telson

unarmed; Petasma with long, rabbit ear-shaped distomedian projections, deeply concave ventrally; distolateral projections short, directed anterolaterally; thelycum with anterior plate distally rounded and broadly articulating with posterior plate, the latter with a median tubercle bearing a tuft of long hairs.

        Colour: Body pale with wide, dark brown- almost black- transverse bands; carapace dark  

               brown dorsally, except for a white band about its middle; bands on abdomen reaching    

               ventrolateral margin; uropods with a very wide, dark brown middle transverse band.

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:   In females rostrum is sigmoid-shaped, distally toothless and

  upcurved; while in large males it curves downward without unarmed distal portion.
