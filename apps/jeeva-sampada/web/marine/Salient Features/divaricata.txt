
Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum ramose main branches repeatedly dividing, 10 to 25 cm in height.  Branches slender or irregularly swollen.  Ultimate branches digitiform, tapering or expanding and palmate with or without coalascens.  Specimens from shallow waters have irregularly swollen branches and may be upto 5 cm thick at the base.  Calices conspicuous, 0.75 to 0.9 mm in diameter, a diameter to 1 mm apart deep.  Septa in two cycles, primaries larger than the secondaries.  Both the cycles more or less complete.  Fossa looks deep and open.  Surface coenechyme glabrous or with traces of foeveolations, especially at the older parts of the corallum where it swells up sometimes around the calices.  Under the lens, it displays a smooth reticulum with upward spinnules or plates.  Surface more or less dense.

Living coral dull brown to greenish-gray.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
