SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony is 90 mm in height, and 55 mm in breadth. The branching is in one plane and no trace of anastomosis. The axis is thin and soft. The coenenchyma is thick.  The verrucae are densely packed, 1mm in diameter and 0.5 mm in height and dome-shaped. They are densely spiculose.
		
		
Spicules: The spicules (1) Long spindles with rugose warts, some of these even bearing short warty branches, (2) Short, thick spindles with rugose warts; (3) Spiny, conical needles, from the base of which arise from two to five warty branches; (4) Rough needles, giving origin to irregular, spiny, almost foliaceous expansions. 


Colour	:	Colony is  brick-red,  the anthocodiae are white and the  spicules are pale red in colour.
