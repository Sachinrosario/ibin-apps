SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:  

Post-larva:   Length. 8.0m

The carapace is devoid of spines except the hepatic spine . The rostrum extends beyond the first segment of antennular peduncle and bears 6 dorsal teeth ,3 of them are  borne on the carapace,which is a characteristic feature of this genus. Rostrum has a prominent  lateral carina on either  side.

Antennule with 2 short flagella ,peduncle stout and 3-segmented. The tip of the antennal  scale squarish in shape with numerous setae,and a terminal spine. The 3rd maxilliped and the pereopods are without exopodites. The branchiae on the maxillipeds and legs have developed as 2 rows of small protuberances on their outer sides.

Dorsomedian spines absent and a dorsal carina present on through out the abdominal segments. There is a small median spine present on the 1st abdominal segment,antero-dorsally,directed anteriorward.Ventromedian spines are present on 1-5 abdominal segments,the one on the 5th slightly reduced. The 6th ab.seg.is 1 ½ times longer than the 5th. Uropods well developed; exopod of uropod without a terminal spine. Tip of telson is pointed ,ending in a short spine with 3 pairs of short marginal spines.. Stout setae are also present. 

Diagnostic characteristics:  The postrostral carina  bearing 3 dorsal  teeth. The pleura of first 2 abdominal segments  unispinose and  3rd and 4th trispinos. The short spine on the 1stabdominal segment directed anteriorwards.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: pelagic zone

Main food:  plankton, detritus with  sand and mud.Feeding type: omnivorous

Additonal remarks: The larva of this genus is very rare and very  seldom occurs in plankton.

Size and age:  Protozoea III measures 2.8 mm and post larva –8mm. 

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs unknown.

Protozoea III

Rostrum very short, carapace without  supraorbital spines. Antennular peduncle very long  with 4 segments; terminal segment  has 3  long setae, almost equal in length and 2 short setae.

Dorsomedian spines present on  first 5 abdominl segments,increasing in length progressively. Dorsolateral spines  present on 5th & 6th abdominal segments. Telson is forked and each one bears 7  setae.

Post larva: length 8mm.

Rostrum extends to the tip of 1st segment of antennular peduncle; bears 6 dorsal teeth,3 of which are borne on the carapace; a prominent lateral carina present on both sides of the rostrum; a short hepatic spine on carapace; mandible has a stout,2-segmented palp bearing setae; no dorsomedian spines on abdomen but a  strong dorsal carina present throughout the abdomen; ventromedian spines on 1st 5 ab.somites; a short dorsal spine on 1st ab.somite directed anteriorward; pereopods without exopods; exopod of uropod without a distal marginal spine; tip of telson pointed  ending in a short spine; 3 pairs of marginal spines and a no.of setae present.

Characteristics: rostral teeth borne on carapace; lateral carina on rostrum; anteriorly directed dorsomedian spine on1st abdominal somite; a strong dorsal carina  present through out the abdomen.

Abundance:  nil
