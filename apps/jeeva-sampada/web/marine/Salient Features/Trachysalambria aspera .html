SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No:5,111,141,168

 

Morphological: Rostrum exceeding tip of first segment of antennular peduncle, straight, uptilted,

armed with 9-10 dorsal teeth; longitudinal suture on carapace indistinct; transverse suture prominent; pterygostomian angle blunt, spine present; dorsomedian carina of abdomen commencing at anterior 1/5 of 3rd somite; telson armed with 1  pair of subapical and 2 pairs of minute lateral spines; first pereopod without ischial spine; epipods present on first 3 pereopods; 5th pereopod exceeding tip of antennular peduncle. Distolateral projection of petasma straight with a more or less round tip, and directed laterally. Anterior margin of thelycum forming triangular or semicircular shape with very weakly elevated tubercles at its base.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:    Telson armed with 1 pair of subapical and 2 pairs of minute lateral

spines; epipods present on first 3 pereopods; distolateral projection of petasma with round tip, straight and directed laterally; anterior margin of thelycum forming triangular or semicircular shape.
