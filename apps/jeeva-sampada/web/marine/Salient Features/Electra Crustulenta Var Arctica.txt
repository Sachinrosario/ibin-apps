SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:  Colonies form thin encrusting irregular patches over the substratum.  Zooecia long and elliptical with thick margins and separated by furrows.  Gymnocyst well developed showing calcareous striations with a broad calcareous tubercle and a chitinous spine connected to the tubercle.  Cryptocyst evanescent, vestigial with granulations and minute tubercles projecting into the opesia.  Apertural field slightly reduced by the cryptocyst, opesia oval.  Operculum membraniporine with thick chitinous margins.  Avicularia and ovicells absent.  
Diagnostic characteristics:
Sex attributes:  Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: The spine has a very broad calcareous base which is continuous with the gymnocyst.  The chitinous spine arises from this base and could easily be distinguished by the presence of a constriction.    The zooecia is with two opercula, two polypides and two proximal spines.  Such duplication of polypides and opercula occurs in zooecia which precede the bifurcation during colony formation.  Operculum in the specimens collected by Menon (1967) were membraniporine with thick margins contrary to what Borg and Mawatari had noticed in their materials.
