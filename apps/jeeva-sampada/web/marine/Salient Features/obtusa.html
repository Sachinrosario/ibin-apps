SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Paddle-shaped pallets, blade somewhat rectangular composed of compactly packed indistinct segments with a pustulose incrustation, which is thin, usually light brown and finely papillose. Stalk extends through the blade.

    Siphons united except at the tip, gills blade like, branchial groove well developed, labial palps attached, stomach elongate, caecum moderate, intestine not looping around the style-sac, anal canal open, heart median, auricles deeply pigmented, heart/body length 0.1, gill/body length 0.5, visceral mass/body length 0.24, heart/gill length 0.3 (Turner, 1966).

Feeding habit: Wood.

Main food: Feeding on cellulose supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks: Distribution- Indo-Pacific, tropical having collected from Madagascar, Indonesia, Christmas Islands, Philippine Islands Bismarck Archipelago.
