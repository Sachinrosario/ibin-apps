Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics:
Body rather robust. Carapace short, posteriorly exposing last two thoracic somites. Frontal border of carapace armed with eight to nine pairs of sharp spines on either side of rostral spine. Eyes of moderate size, cornea divided into two pigmentary portions, lateral portion larger than anterior one. Antennal scale oblong, curved, four times as long as broad with strong outer spine. Third to eighth thoracic endopods with three carpopropodal segments, fifth and sixth joints armed with numerous peculiar setae. Posterior and lateral border of all abdominal segments spiny, spines strong and stout. Pleopods in male well developed and biramous, endopod of first pair rudimentary; distal setae not modified. Telson equal in length and breadth with four to six lateral spines and two distal long setae. Endopod of uropod with single spine near statocyst.
