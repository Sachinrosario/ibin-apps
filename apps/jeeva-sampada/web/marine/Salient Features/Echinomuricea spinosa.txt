SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony is 65 mm in height and 70 mm in breadth. The branching is to one plane. The axis is horny, slender and black; the surface is very smooth  and glistening. The coenenchyma is moderately thick, the polyps are disposed over the  whole surface, the verrucae are almost hemispherical with a diameter of 0.75 to 1 mm.  There is a distinct cellaret of 3-4 rows of curved spindles, and extending from this to the base of the tentacles are eight groups of spindles considerably bent at the proximal end.
		
		
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are (a) slightly toothed spine (b) Club-shaped forms (c) warty spindles.

Colour		:	Colony is light brown  in colour and the spicules colourless.
