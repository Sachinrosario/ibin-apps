SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:
Colony flabellate and moderately reticulate, 13 cm in height and 7.5 cm in spread. The branching are dichotomous and the forkings are U-shaped. Many of the smaller branches are frequently girdled by a small parasitic form that looks like checkered belts or bands tightly compressing the coenenchyma. The calyces are mainly lateral and anterior in position, and are so low as to be barely visible		
		The individual calyces are very low and rounded domes. They are very small, averaging scarcely more than 1 mm in diameter. They are filled with spindles and thorny clubs. The polyps are very small, completely retracted. The collaret have spindles en chevron in arrangement.
		Spicules. There are large spiny spindles often spinulate and spiny club forms are also seen. 

Colour		:	The entire colony is lemon yellow and axis is dark pink.
