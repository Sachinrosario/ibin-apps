SALIENT FEATURES   

 

Description: Colony flabellate and reticulate measuring 15.5 cm. in height and 12 cm. in width. The main stem is straight for the greater part of its length, slightly flattened, its greater diameter being 4mm. It gives off a pair of opposite branches 2.6 cm. from its base and a number of other lateral branches above this, some of which are alternate and calyces are thickly distributed on all sides of the stem and branches, are often 0.5 mm apart. The individual calyces are verruciform or dome-shaped, a typical one measuring 0.8 mm in height and 1.4 mm in diameter. Its walls are studded with blunt points projecting upward and outward, and a circlet of such points surrounds the margin. The polyps are completely retractile. The collaret and operculum are composed of very slender and delicate spindles.  

Spicules: The spicules are multiradiate forms predominating. The rays being longer and smoother than the others.

 

Colour   :  The colony is dull dark red and the spicules are bright scarlet.
