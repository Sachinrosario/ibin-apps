SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Lens shaped, weakly silicified frustules with rounded ends forming long slender straight chains. Space between the cells longer than the cells.

Diagnostic characteristics: Marginal spines run parallel to the axis of the chain. No visible structure on the valve.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter of cells: 10-15µ.

Biochemical aspects: Obligate Phototroph, which requires vitamin B12 for growth. Produces extracellular products like vitamins and biotin Escherechia coli type. Extracellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides. Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
