SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum branching branches proliferous, 10 to 12 mm thick at the base, branchlets curved, coalascent, 5 to 7 mm thick, upto 5 cm long.  Monticules at the older parts of the branches conical, spreading, but running together to form long ridges at the younger parts of the corallum.  Septa alternately thick and thin, edges irregularly dentate, those of the opposite ridges uniting at the valley forming a continuous solid columella.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
