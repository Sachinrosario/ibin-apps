SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves narrowly naviculoid, gradually narrowing from the central part to the ends, which are sickle-shaped. Raphe in the middle slightly excentric near the ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Central nodule large rounded to rhombic. Striae 18-20 in 10m, oblique striae crossed at an angle of 700.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 111-159m, width 17-28m.
