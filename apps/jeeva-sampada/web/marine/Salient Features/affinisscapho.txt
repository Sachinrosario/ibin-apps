
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:         

Female:  Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segment fused. Prosome with a low median crest. Rostrum with 2 long slender filaments. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment produced into triangular expansions, which end in blunt points. Urosome 4-segmented. Genital segment swollen downwards at the proximal one-third. Fifth leg 3-jointed. Terminal joint with 4 spines. Inner marginal spine, which is the longest, has a row of spinules on the outer margin. The two outer marginal spines are small.
