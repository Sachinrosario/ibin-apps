SALIENT FEATURES OF Halodule pinifolia (Miki) Hartog 1964 

Leaves: Measure about 1 - 6 cm long and 1- 5 mm width, linear with entire margin and with 3 prominent midribs, lateral ribs form lateral teeth on leaf apex 

Inflorescence: Two flowers enclosed by leaf sheaths 

Tepals: Not well organised

Ovary: Ovoid with filiform terminal styles and pushed subterminal or lateral in fruits 

Fruit: Globose, seen in pairs with persistent lateral styles; seed coat hard with ornamentations 

Flowering and fruiting: February 


Reports on bioactivity: 
	Antiviral activity (Premnathan et al., 1992)
	Antibacterial activity (Balasubramanian et al., 2000)
