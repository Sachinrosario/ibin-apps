Salient Features :
An evergreen tree, 250-400 cm tall, with straight or crooked trunk and dull gray to grayish-white or grayish-brown bark.
Leaves broadly ovate to ovate-lanceolate, glabrous and fleshy.
Flowers greenish-yellow, in axillary and terminal 4-20 cm long panicles.
Drupes 0.2-0.3 cm across, spherical, fleshy, pink to deep purple.
Industries:
Widely used in cosmetic industries. Seeds contain about 30 percent lauric acid (oil) on dry wt. basis . Stem provided fire wood, and when young it is used as tooth brush by natives.
Health:
Leaves and fruits are used in bile troubles, rheumatism and root bark is applied on blister.
Nutrition:
It is favorite fodder for camel.
R & D:
Nil.
