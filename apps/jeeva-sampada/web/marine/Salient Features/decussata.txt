Descriptive characters:  The following details of this species based on a study of several specimens from Gulf of Mannar as well as Dana types is P. lata and P. crassa in USNM which are synonyms.

Corallum bifacial, fronds 10 to 15 cm broad, 7 to 12 mm thick, growing edges mostly entire that is not much divided.  Often the fronds are linked by transverse connections.  Calices close together, arranged in transverse rows, distance between adjacent calices within a row 1.5 to 2 mm, distance between adjacent tows 2 to 3 mm.  Ambulacra flat.  Septa 12 to 16 around a calyx, confluent over the ambulacra, only slightly alternating in size.  Septal edges entire, sides granular.  Carinae generally not developed at the older parts of the fronds though it is seen at the growing parts, placed transverse to the rows of calices.  Columella a compressed, twisted lamella

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
