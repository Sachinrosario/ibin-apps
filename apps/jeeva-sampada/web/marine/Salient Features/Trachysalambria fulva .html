SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No:31

 

Morphological:  Body densely pubescent; rostrum reaching to or almost to tip of 2nd segment of

antennular peduncle, dorsally straight and lower border upcurved, armed with 7-10 (usually 8-9 ) dorsal teeth; longitudinal suture on carapace short, sometimes indistinct; a small transverse suture present; pterygostomian angle blunt; dorsomedian carina of abdomen commencing at anterior 1/3 of 4th somite; telson with a pair of large subapical spines and 2 pairs of minute lateral spines; 1st pereopod with a small ischial spine; 3rd and 5th pereopods reaching to or beyond tip of antennular peduncle; epipod present on 3rd pereopod only. Distolateral projections of petasma directed laterally, their width 1.1. to 1.2 times length of petasma . Anterior plate of thelycum semicircular and flat or concave; no coxal plate on 4th pereopod bearing setae.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Rostrum straight, usually with 8-9 teeth; 3rd and 5th pereopods

reaching to or beyond tip of antennular peduncle; telson with 1 pair of subapical and 2 pairs of minute lateral spines; anterior plate of thelycum semicircular.

 

Colour: Body from mid to light yellowish brown.
