SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  The following description is based on two small coralla obtained from the lagoon of Minicoy; Greater diameters 8 and 5.5 cm respectively.  Attached.  Foliaceous, growing edges slightly turned up.  Thickness at the growing edges 2 to 3 mm.  The central calyx is about 10 mm wide.  The secondary calices arranged concentrically around the mother clayx the arrangement loosing the regularity at the periphery of the corallum.  Distance between adjacent centres 5 mm.  Septo-costae alternating in height, larger ones 1 mm exsert slopping to the fossa.  12 to 15 septo costae can be seen around a calyx, generally 12.  Edges of septa toothed, sides granular.  Columella rudimentary, often a single style.  Under side of the corallum perforate.  Costal spines more prominent at the peripheral parts of the fllium.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
