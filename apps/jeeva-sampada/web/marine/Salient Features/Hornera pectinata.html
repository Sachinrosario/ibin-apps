SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium light purplish in hue, arising from an expanded base. Branching irregular. Zooecia arranged in transverse rows of two to four tubules. Rim of the peristome thin with delicate serrations. Strongly developed sulci. Pores elongated. Ridges between sulci more or less regular. No ovicell noticed.
Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:   A comparison of the material with the figures and description of H. pectinata given by Osburn (1953) showed that the form collected by Menon could be assigned to it. Absence of ovicells made a correct judgment difficult. Osburn states about this aspect "ovicells are not developed in any of our specimens, and in their absence positive identification is impossible". But Osburn has further remarked thus: "However, the zoarial characters given by Johnston (1897, p. 61) in his amplification of Busk's description of H. pectinata apply very well to our specimens".
