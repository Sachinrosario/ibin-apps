 SALIENT FEATURES:							Ref No:103, 212

Morphological:     Body densely pubescent; rostrum usually straight directed forward or slightly
 upward, reaching to or almost to tip of antennular peduncle and armed with 7 – 8 dorsal 
teeth, penultimate tooth generally anterior to orbital margin of carapace; stridulating 
organ present on carapace, consisting of 5 – 8 very strong ridges on a wide, straight band 
near middle on posterior branchiostegite; mid–dorsal carina on 3rd abdominal somite 
broadly sulcate. Petasma asymmetrical; right distoventral projection shorter than left and 
tipped with one long and another minute spines; left distoventral projection triangular 
apically bearing 4 very long filiform; processes on outer margin and 8 - 9 smaller finger – like processes on median margin; inner intermediate strip broadly quadrangular, distomedian lobule slightly shorter, but much broader distally than inner intermediate strip. Thelycal plate subquadrate with rounded corners, anterior margin entire, slightly wider than long; intermediate plate broadly trapezoidal, much wider than long, flat or with a shallow median groove; coxal plates of 4th pereopods smaller than thelycal plate


Diagnostic characteristics:    Carapace with stridulating organ consisting of 5 – 8 very strong 
ridges on a straight band, mid–dorsal carina on 3rd abdominal somite broadly sulcate, right distoventral projection of petasma shorter than left, tip of left distoventral projection triangular and bearing 4 very long processes on outer margin and 8 –9 finger–like processes on median margin apically; anterior margin of thelycal plate entire.

        Colour:   Body white to reddish-brown, with red to dark brown mottlings.
