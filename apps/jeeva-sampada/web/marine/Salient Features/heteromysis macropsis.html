Diagnostic characteristics:
Carapace produced into broadly triangular rostrum. Eyes large, cornea small. Endopod of third thoracic limb massive, ischium and merus with row of small setae on inferior border, dactylus short, claw long and falcate. Endopods four to eight with five tarsal segments. Telson triangular, slightly longer than broad, distal half of lateral border with ten pairs of spines, lobes of telson with two pairs of spines, outer pair much longer than inner, cleft one fifth length of telson with five pairs of spines. Endopod of uropod with eight spines.
