SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Pentagonal circular or slightly ascendant, excavated. Rather deep sulcus, which forms clearly an antapical notch, shallow, of uniform width. Sculpture generally is alveolar fine.

Diagnostic characteristics: The suture of the epitheca and hypotheca are wide striated.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 55-80µ. Diameter: 53-69µ.
