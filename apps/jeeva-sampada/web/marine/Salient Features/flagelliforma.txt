SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:      
Carapace produced in front into short triangular apically rounded rostrum. Mouth parts and thoracic appendages as for the genus. First pleopod of male with single jointed endopod and five to six jointed exopod; second pleopod with two jointed endopod and eight to nine jointed exopod; third pleopod with rudimentary endopod and three jointed exopod, third joint terminating in three spines; one long stout barbed spine, one short smooth spine and one slender lash like barbed spine. Fourth and fifth pleopods similar with single jointed exopod and endopod. First pleopod in female biramous, second to fifth pleopods rudimentary. Telson with nine well spaced spines along lateral margin, last two long, penultimate spine longer; telsonic sinus with       18-20 pairs of spines. Endopod of uropod with 6-8 widely spaced sharp spines on inner margin. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Adult male and female: 6.8 - 7 mm
