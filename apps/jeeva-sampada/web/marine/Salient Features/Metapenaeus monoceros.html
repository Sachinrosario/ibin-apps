SALIENT FEATURES:               Ref No:103

 

Morphological:   Body almost entirely pubescent; rostrum armed with 9 to 12 teeth along entire

dorsal margin, straight, reaching as far as, or beyond, tip of antennular peduncle; postrostral carina reaching posterior margin of carapace or nearly so; adrostral carina ending behind second rostral tooth; adrostral sulcus behind epigastric tooth; branchiocardiac carina sinuous, reaching posterior extension of hepatic spine; telson armed only with spinules; ischial spine on 1st pereopod small; merus of 5th pereopod in adult males with a proximal notch followed by a long, inwardly curved spiniform process and a row of tubercles. Distomedian projections of petasma convoluted, greatly swollen, bulbiform, directed anterolaterally and concealing distolateral projections in ventral view. In females, anterior plate of thelycum long and deeply grooved; lateral plates with strongly raised lateral margins forming two longitudinal crests.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum long, straight and armed with teeth on entire dorsal margin; branchiocardiac carina reaching posterior extension of hepatic spine; merus of 5th pereopod in adult males with a proximal notch followed by a long spiniform process; distomedian projections of petasma convoluted, greatly swollen, and directed anterolaterally; anterior thelycal plate long, tongue-like and deeply grooved.

 

        Colour:   Body pink, green-greyish or whitish with brown specks; distal part of uropods Purple-blue.

 

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:    In adult males, merus of 5th pereopod is provided with a proximal

notch followed by a long, inwardly curved spiniform process and a row of tubercles.

Eggs and larvae:                Ref. No:56,59,110,159

 

  Characteristics:    Eggs are opaque and spherical having a diameter of 0.28 mm. The embryo

hatches out in 15–17 hours and passes through 6 nauplius, 3 protozoea and 6 mysis substages before reaching postlarva. The last mysis stage measures 2.91 to 3.46 mm total length and has a long rostrum with 2 dorsal teeth, small hepatic spines, 2- segmented pleopods and posteriorly convex telson bearing 7 + 7 setae on distal portion. First postlarval stage measures 2.9 to 3.15 mm total length, and is characterized by a short rostrum with 3 dorsal teeth, functional pleopods bearing plumose setae and posteriorly convex telson bearing 3 pairs of lateral setae and 4 pairs of distal setae. Entire ventral aspect of body brownish due to presence of large number of reddish–brown chomatophores.

 

Abundance:    Protozoea, mysis and postlarval stages have been reported from the inshore waters of

                  the west coast in varying densities. They were found in maximum abundance in surface    

                  plankton at 10-20 m depth between Karwar and Goa.

   Locality: Throughout the Indian coasts in relatively deeper waters. Off Cochin, at 50-60 m depth.

   Season: Breeds throughout the year. Peaks Feb-Apr on N.W. coast, Jul-Aug & Nov-Dec on
