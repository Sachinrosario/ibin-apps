Morphological:

Diagnostic characteristics:
Body robust and spiny. Carapace anteriorly produced into broadly triangular apically blunt rostrum. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Thoracic endopods three to eight with three carpopropodal segments, dactylus with long slender nail. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male three segmented, second and third segments short and carrying long barbed setae. Telson broad, flattened, linguiform, proximal widened part with one to two pairs of short spines, distal two third with twenty six to thirty spines arranged in series with longer spines alternating with two to three small spines, distal border rounded with six large flattened spines. Uropods over reaching telson; endopod with four to five spines below statocyst.
