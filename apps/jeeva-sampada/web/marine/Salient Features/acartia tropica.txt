SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:       

 Female: Cephalosome nearly as long as metasome. Armature on posterior corner of metasome similar to that of Acartia sinjiensis (Posterior corner of metasome with 1-row of spines on each side), but spines at ventralmost locus nearly as large as those at dorsalmost locus, each locus with 0-3 (usually 1 or 2) spines. Ur I with 9-18spines on posterodorsal margin; these spines not grouped at loci but evenly distributed; ventral surface with several rows of very minute hair-like spinules. Ur2 with 6-10 postero-dorsal spines; medial 2 spines larger than lateral ones and larger than spines on Ur1. Ur3 with ventrolateral hairs. Caudal rami as long as Ur2 and Ur3 combined. RL/RW 1.9-2.2.     

Rostral filaments straight or slightly curved. A1 with 1 row of spinules on each ventral surface of 9th to 12th segments. Second segment of P5, 1.6 times as long as wide. Terminal spine almost straight, narrow, 3 times longer than 2nd segment; basal swelling ca.2 times as long as wide. Lateral plumose seta shorter than terminal spine.     

Male: Cephalosome as long as metasome with 11-12 spines on posterodorsal and posterolateral margins; lateral spines not smaller than dorsal ones. Ventral surface with spines at 2 loci on each side; each locus with 1-3 spines, most posterior locus located on border of segment. Ur3 and Ur4 with 4-8 and 4-6 posterodorsal spines, respectively, sometimes 4 loci can be distinguished on both segments; these spines usually larger than those on Ur2, Ur5 with ventrolateral hairs. RL/Rw 1.1-1.3.     


Segment sizes of P5 similar to those of Acartia sinjiensis. Inner projection of 3rd segment of right P5 not twisted and not bilobed (sometimes with slight medial identation). Inner large spine of terminal segment of left P5 terminally curved, hook-like, nearly as long as segment.
