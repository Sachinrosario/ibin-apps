
SALIENT  FEATURES :

Diagnostic characteristics:              Female:  Prosome and pedigerous segment 1 and pedigerous segments 4 and 5 fused. Urosome 3-segmented, genital segment of a distinctive shape with the widest part anterior to its middle length; caudal rami longer than wide. Antenna 1 extends beyond the caudal rami by 2 segments. Swimming leg 1 with a 1- segmented endopod and a 2-segmented exopod; endopods of legs 3 and 42-segmented; leg 5 with 2 free segments in addition to a common basal segment, leg terminated by 1 seta but without spinules.            Male:   Prosome and pedigerous segment 1 separated by constriction, pedigerous segments 4 and 5 fused. Urosome 5-segmented, genital aperture on the right, caudal rami slightly longer than wide terminated by 3 setae of equal size. Antenna 1 not entire segments 1 and 2 also 4 and 5 appear to be fused. Antenna 2 with a short terminal segment on the endopod which is devoid of terminal setae evident in the female. Maxilla 1 is very reduced but with a remnant of inner lobe 1. Maxilla 2 is a very small  knob. Leg 5 extends well beyond the caudal rami; left leg not extending beyond the first segment of the right leg; both the first segment of the right leg; both legs terminated by a spine.

 
Additional  remarks:        In some specimens the female maxilliped  has a row of long stiff spines on the inner border. 
Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                        :                   
Female :  0.50-0.57mm                                              
Male    :   0.42-0.44mm
Ref. No:Bradford – Grieve, 1994
