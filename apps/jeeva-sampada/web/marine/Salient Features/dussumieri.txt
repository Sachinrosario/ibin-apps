SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  Snout blunt, squarish; two internasals; 1 pre- and 2 
postuculars; temporals 1 +2 ; 8 suprlabials, 4th touching the eye ; 
anterior pair of chin-shields (genials) larger than the posterior pair
in touch with 5 lower labials. Body stout, scales in 27 rows; 
ventrals 144-150; 34-39 ; anal divided.

Diagnostic characteristics: Like other members of the genus Enhydris, 
this water snake is also characterised by the pattern of longitudinal
stripes on the dorsum and a spotted lower surface. The snake is brown
above, with three blackish longitudinal stripes - one vertebral and
two dorso - lateral. The outer three scales rows are whitish, spotted 
with brown and with a whitish border above; the ventrals are whitish
the outer edges of which are spotted with brown and also a median line
of brownish spots.

Feeding Habits: Piscivorous
Main food : Fish, crustacea

Additional Remarks: The Dussumier's Smooth watersnake seems to be 
extremely rare. So much so even the origin of the type specimen is in
doubt. Smith(1943) says " the type of dussimieri is a female and it
was said to have from Bengal". As one might expect it, practically
nothing is known of the natural history of this rare watersnake.

Size and Age:
Maximum length(cm): (unsexed) 67 cm
