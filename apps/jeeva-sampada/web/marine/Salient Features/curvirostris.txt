SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 

Postlarva I: Rostrum with less number of  teeth and advances the eyes. Epigastricspine prominent; hepatic spine not present; antennal part is only angular; pterygostomian spine reduced; dorsomedian spines  on abdominal segment 4 minute and  on 5-6 small. Abdominal segment 3 slightly carinate dorsally. Antennule short; all thoracic legs bearing exopodites. Pleopods uniramous; exopod of uropod slightly longer than telson. Telson bearing 8+8 spines.

Diagnostic characters: The presence of a long rostrum, a small dorsal organ in the protozoeae; presence of a labral spine in mysis II & III; the absence of hepatic spine\ in all stages and the telson with 8 pairs of telsonic spines are some of the important diagnostic characters.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: mainly on planktonic organisms.

Main food: copepods,diatoms,algae,fish,mussel flesh & debris.

Feeding type: omnivorous

Additional remarks: This species forms a small portion of the prawn fishery.They enter into the estuaries and even to less saline waters in river systems. A good number of them were collected from the Chaliyar river,Kozhikode(Paulinose,unpublished work).This species has a wide range of distribution in the Indian Ocean. It is observed that protozoeal stages were from offshore waters and mysis from shallower regions. The adults of this species are known to occur in littoral waters.

Size and Age: Postlarva.7.0mm; Mysis I-III 4mm-5mm; Protozoeae.4mm-5.6mm.

Longevity (y) (wild) :  1-2 ½ years. 

Eggs and larvae:  2 protozoeal and 3 mysis stages are described and figures given.
Protozoea II. measuring about 3.0-4.0mm in size. Rostrum pointed,long extending just beyond  the 2nd segment of antennular peduncle. Carapace free of spines, a small dorsal organ present ; eyes on stalks. Antennular peduncle 3- segmented,terminal segment bearing 2 long and a short seta . Antennal scale multisegmented; endopod2-segmented. Mandible slightly asymmetrical,incisor & molar gap lodging 5 setae in the right mandible.and only 1 in the left mandible.Exopod of maxillule bearing 4 setae,endopod with 3 segments. The terminal segment bearing 5 setae. Abdomen segmented; telson furcated,each ramus with 7 seate.

Protozoea III. 5.6mm in length. Rostrum and setation as in previous stage.Endopod of antenna bears1+2+4 setae. Mandible with an additional seta. Maxillipeds developed with exopods. First 5 abdominal segments bear dorsomedian spines,and dorsolateralspines on 5&6 ab.segs.Sixth segment has a pair of ventrolateral spines. Uropods separated from the telson ; telson with 2 rami,notch wider,,each ramusbearing 8 setae.

Mysis I. a length of 3.5 to 4.00 mm. Rostrum with a short dorsal tooth.,tip in front of eyes, no epigastric or hepatic present. Antennal spine prominent; supraorbitals and pterygostomian spines weak; labral spine absent. Antennular peduncle 3- segmented with 2 unsegmented flagella. Basal segment bears a large,long spine. Antenna with short,unsegmented endopod. Mandibles asymmetrical (left md.with 7 denticular setae and right md.with 3), no palp present. Dorsomedian spines present only  on 5-6 abdominal segments.The ventrolaterals on the 6th ab.seg.persist. A short dorsal carina present on  abdominal segments 3-4. Thoracic legs with exopodites; pleopods absent. Telson parallel- sided, bearing 8 pairs of  setae, 4th from the outer , the longest

Mysis II. length 4.0 to 4.5mm. Rostrum with 2 dorsal teeth + the epigastric as small indentations. Spines on carapace and abdomen remain unchanged. A labral spine present. Mandible with a short , unsegmented palp. Maxillipeds and legs with exopods; pleopods appear as small,uniramous  buds. Telson not changed.

Mysis III. length of 4.5 –5.0 mm. Rostrum bears 2 prominent dorsal teeth +epigastric;.spines on carapace and abdomen remain same; mandibular palp with2 segments. Pleopods uniramous,without setae. Telson remains the same.

Abundance: nil
