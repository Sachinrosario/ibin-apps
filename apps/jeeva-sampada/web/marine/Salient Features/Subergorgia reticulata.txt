SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Subergorgiidae with reticulated body pattern, colonies often divide in the same plane, colonies often divide in the same plane (fan-shaped growth) or in 
different planes. Meshes of the reticulum 3-5 mm in size in older parts, while towards the grwoing tips they may become slightly larger, about 5-7 mm; meshes polygonal in shape. 
Branches originating from the stalk are clearly discernible up to the edge of the colony in
older specimens.

			
Cortex does not peel off easily; medullar region with fused spicules.

			
Calyces inconspicuous and arranged only on one side of the colony, diameter from 0.2 to 0.5 mm and may be contiguous. Sunken groove, so characteristic in the order species of the genus, is never present in this species.

			
Spicules consist of disc and belted spindles in the cortex and sinuous, smooth spindles in the medulla. (1) Disc spindles. Button-like, like 0.03 - 0.05 mm (Fig. a) (2) Belted spindles. Size up to 0.134 x 0.033 mm (Fig.b) (3) Smooth spicules of medulla. Rarely with annulations, may fuse to form a compact structure; size up to 0.18 x 0.020 mm (Fig.c).

Colour	:	Light pink when alive, may turn white after death; spicules colourless.

Size Attained	:	Specimens measuring up to 60 cm (height) and 40 cm. (width) are common in the Gulf of Mannar.
