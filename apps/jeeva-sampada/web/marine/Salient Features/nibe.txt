 SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref.No. 1
Morphological:   A small species  oblong  and rather compressed.

Diagnostic characteristics : mouth terminal,  the upper jaw (maxilla) slightly less than half head;  teeth in villiform , differentiated  in size in both jaws, the outer series in upper jaw  and inner  series in lower jaw  enlarged ;  no canine  teeth.  Gillrakers   9 to 12 on lower arm of  first arch. Dorsal fin with  10 spines, followed by deep notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 27 to 32 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7  soft rays, the second spine slender.  Pectoral fins long nearly  equal to body depth. Scales cycloid on head. weakly   ctenoid on body. swim bladder carrot – shaped , with about  24 to 30  pairs of arborescent  appendages.

Colour :Back  dark grey, shading to white  on belly; Linings of mouth, gill  chamber  and body  on black .  Spinous dorsal  fin with  scattered  melanophores.
