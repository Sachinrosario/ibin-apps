SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells forming long chains, at times, about 546m long, and weakly silicified.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valves slightly concave without any visible sculptures; chromatophores many lobed discs with each disc showing a pyrenoid.

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter32-64m.
