SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics: 
Genus : Petalophthalmus W. Suhm     Carapace short, leaving last two or three thoracic somites uncovered dorsally, rostrum short, acute, flanked by small denticle. Eyes with or without visual elements, with or without papilla. Antennule long and slender. Antennal scale narrowly lanceolate and setose all round. Mandibular palp enormously developed, long, prehensile and armed with strong spines. Maxilla with lobes from second and third joints smaller than usual, setiferous expansion of lobe from second joint absent, lobe from third joint with bare indication of division into two. First thoracic limb robust without exopod, epipod present, ischium with prominent lobe tipped with prominent spine. Second thoracic limb without exopod., ischium with large lobe. Pleopod of female uniramous, of male biramous and natatory. Telson large, quadrangular, apex truncate with barbed spines, with or without plumose setae, lateral margins armed with many spines. Exopod of uropod two segmented, endopod without spines.Petalophthalmus armiger     Body elongated, eyes flattened, leaf-like and without visual elements. Antennal scale longer than basal segment of antennule, antennal peduncle shorter than scale. Second segment of mandibular palp long and margin armed with strong spines, third segment slender, more than half as long as second and armed with nine strong spines. First and second thoracic endopods as for the genus. Thoracic endopods three to eight long and slender, carpopropodus undivided. First pleopod of male with unsegmented endopod and many segmented exopod. Pleopods two to five with many segmented exopod and endopod. Telson quadrangular, apex emarginated with rounded angles, armed with short median spine, five pairs of long barbed spines and five pairs of spinules, lateral borders armed throughout with 40-50 small spines. Exopod of uropod longer than endopod with three spines on outer border.
