Salient Features :
An erect, much branched, leafless shrub.
Stem and branches pale yellow, not drying black, the joint produced in to 2 long triangular points which takes the place of leaves, and are woolly within.
Flowers in erect spikes, bracteoles, nearly as broadly as long, elliptic or ovovate, rounded at the top, very thick.
Seed horizontal, flattened, orbicular.


Industries:
It is extensively used for the manufacture of crude sodium carbonate.
Health:
Nil
Nutrition:
The plant used as fodder for camels, tender phylloclades are used as salad.
R & D:
Considered as cash crop halophyte.
