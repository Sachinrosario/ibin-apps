SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref No: 103,139,140,184

 

Morphological:   Rostrum straight or slightly sinuous (occasionally convex basally, straight

anteriorly ) and long, almost reaching to, or exceeding distal margin of antennular peduncle, armed with 12 to 16 (usually 12-14) dorsal teeth; anteroventral corner of carapace almost rectangular; cervical and hepatic crests and grooves well defined; hepatic spine situated at bout same level as that of antennal spine; branchiocardiac carina with anterior end very close to hepatic sulcus; telson armed with a pair of fixed lateral spines and 3 pairs of small movable spines. Petasma symmetrical, with distal part of ventral costae curving abruptly dorsomedially and ending in short, relatively narrow processes; ribs of dorsolateral lobules terminating proximally in semicircular or subcircular processes. Anterior plate of thelycum subsemicircular to trilobed; posterior plate with anterior border broadly arched on each side of posteromedian projection of anterior plate, and strongly inclinded posterolaterally, its median ridge sometimes reduced to a posterior tubercle.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Rostrum long and straight or slightly sinuous, armed with 12-16

dorsal teeth; hepatic spine on carapace situated at about same level as antennal spine; telson armed with a pair of fixed lateral spines and 3 pairs of small movable spines; petasma symmetrical.

 

        Colour: Red or dark brown with reddish tint.

 

 

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:  Ventral antennular flagellum in male is semicircular on proximal                                

part  and straight distally with a knob at junction between  the two parts. In female, ventral flagellum is straight and tapering to filiform distal part.
