Description:

Trees, upto 30 m tall with pyramidal pyramidal crowns and many 
ascending branches ; stem-base supported by numerous branched stilt 
roots.  Leaves 10-20 x 5-8 cm, elliptic- oblong, coriaceous, entire, 
acute or apiculate at apex, cuneate at base.  Flowers 10-12 mmacross , 
sessile, white or cream-coloured, in pairs of upper leafaxils ; calyx
4- lobed, concave, accrscent, reflexed in  fruit ; corolla 4-lobed , 
lobes fleshy, glabrous, caducous ; stamens 12,4 episepalous paired, 4 
epipetalous,  Hypocotyle 30-50 cm long, cylindrical, clavate.Flowering 
and fruiting during May-September.

Salient features:

Commercial use:	
·	Its wood forms a potential source of tannin and finds 
	application in plywood, adhesive, dye bark, cellophane, rayon, 
	Ferro-alloys, cellulose acetate industries and as substitute for 
	petroleum coke for calcium carbide, besides being used as fuel 
	–wood and in medicines ; its leaves are used a s fodder.
·	It has C3 photosynthesis and can be used for fuel wood and for 
	cnstruction timber (Aronson 1989:71). [also see Mepham 1983; 
	Mepham and Mepahm 1985]
