SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 1
Morphological: Fairly small species with a fairly deep body,

Diagnostic characteristics : Snout rounded, mouth inferior, maxilla reaching to middle of eye, a single, well developed barbell on chin, rostral pores 3, marginal pores 5, mental pores 5, the median pore at the tip of mental barbell; teeth differentiated into large and small in upper jaw, lower jaw with a tadpole shaped impression, of which the “ tail” is bent sharply, tapering not reaching the ventral edge. Dorsal fin with 10 spines, followed by a deep notch, second part of fin with one spine and 27 to 29 soft rays ; pectoral fins moderate , about ¾ of head length ; caudal fin wedge shaped . Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 rays, second anal spine strong , Scales cycloid on snout, ctenoid elsewhere ; scales present on base of anal fin swim bladder oblong, without appendages;

Colour : Body with about 9 sinous brown bands; a dark blotch at axilla, first part of dorsal fin black ; a black band along the length of second dorsal and anal fins; pelvic fins black
