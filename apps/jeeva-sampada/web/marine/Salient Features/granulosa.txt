Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  A specimen collected from the Manauli Island is having a greater spread of 13 cm, with a maximum thickness of 8 mm.  Growing edges not free, 1 to 2 mm thick; an epitheca present.  On the surface there are many barnacles which have rendered the surface un-even.  Calices irregular in pitline, not well cut out from the coenenchyme by means of a wall; 0.6 to 0.7 mm in diameter close together or up to 3 mm apart.  First cycle of septa well developed, crowded in appearance, secondaries smaller, 2 to 6 in different calices.

The surface of the coral shows a velvety sheen as pointed out by Bernard.  Under the lens the surface reveals small vertical plates with a row of two to five spinules in between which rounded pores are seen.  Transverse section shows a lower solid layer and an upper reticular layer.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The species is found attached to massive Porites and Faviids.  It has dwindled due to quarrying of reefs.
