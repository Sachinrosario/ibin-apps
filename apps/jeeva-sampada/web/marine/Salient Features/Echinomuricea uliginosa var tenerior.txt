Salient Features


Description:
Colony is branched in one plane and anastomoses finely.  The axis is horny and measures 0.61mm long. The anthocodiae are completely retractile.  There is an elevated conical operculum composed of eight groups of three spindles and a collaret of two or three rows of transversely arranged curved spicules.

Spicules:  The spicules of the coenenchyma are (a.) Smooth spine with heavy warted spicules (b) Bent spicules with irregular warts.

Colour :  Colony is white and the spicules colourless.
