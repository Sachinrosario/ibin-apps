SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:                                 Diagnostic characteristics:          Rostrum with long slender filaments. Pedigerous segments 4 and 5 incompletely separated. Antenna 1 long. Basipod 2 of swimming leg 1 with an inner – edge seta. Well–developed ornamentation on the posterior surfaces of the swimming legs, all or some of the outer edges of the exopods of legs 2-4 are serrated, distal outer margin of the endopods end in a small spine. Female leg 5 absent or rudimentary, male leg 5 present only on the left (Tanaka 1956b).        

Female: The prosome is evenly round in lateral view and the line of separation of the prosome from pedigerous segment 1 is very slight; antenna 1 exceeds the end of the caudal rami by the 3 or 4 terminal segments, segments 19-22 are each no longer than segment 25,; the distal toothed outer border  of exopod 3 of swimming leg 4 is about 80 % of the length of the proximal part of the toothed border. (Giesbrecht 1892; Tanaka 1956b)

Additional remarks:        Males are very difficult to tell apart. Tanaka (1956b) summarizes data on proportions of the body from Sewell (1929) and gives his opinion on the identity of the specimens off Japan.
