Descriptive characters:  
Description of the holotype: Massive, very heavy (the type was 35 cm in diameter spread and 25 cm in height, as measured in the field) the surface rising into irregular hillocks resembling some species of massive Porites.  Calices small, inconspicuous, pin-hole-like, irregular in outline, rounded or elongated.  A well formed thecal wall not visible.  Calices at the top parts of the corallum 0.3 to 0.6 mm in diameter, lower down only 0.25 to 0.35 mm in diameter.  Distance between adjacent ones 1 to 2 mm.  Rarely calices are seen in irregular longitudinal rows.  Septa in two cycles, primaries meet at the centre of the fossa over a rudimentary columella.  Secondaries, spiny 4 to 6 per calyx.  Surface coenenchyme  form prominent ramparts around the calices.  The foveolations is more procounced on the gibbosities than on the level parts of the corallum.  Under the lens the coenenchymal surface reveals a fine reticulum with crowded vertical plated with secondary spinnulations.  Section of the coral appears solid with a compact reticulum of vertical and horizontal bars.  Living coral lilac in colour.
Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Hitherto known only by the type.
