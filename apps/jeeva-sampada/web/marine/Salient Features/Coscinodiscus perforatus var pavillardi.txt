SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc-shaped cells with slightly convex valves areolated with a central rosette. Areolae around the rosette 3-5 in 10m, forwards the margin 3-4 in 10m. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Interstitial meshes few and not present before all radii. Valve margins striated, striae 6 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 145-170m.
