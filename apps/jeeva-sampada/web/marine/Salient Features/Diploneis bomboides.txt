SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves constricted in the middle, with broadly elliptical segments, 22 µ in width of constriction. Central Nodule Square, longitudinal ribs parallel.

Diagnostic characteristics: Longitudinal canal radiate, coarse 7-8 in 10µ crossed by several undulating longitudinal costae on each side of the medium line about6 in 10µ.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 95 µ, width 35 µ.
