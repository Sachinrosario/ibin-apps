Diagnostic characteristics:
Genus: Dactylamblyops Holt and Tattersall
Diagnosis: Anterior margin of carapace rounded with or without rostral projection. Antennal peduncle short, third segment may overlap second, outer border of scale naked, usually terminating in long spine. Eyes set close together but separate , visual elements imperfect, papilla may be present on dorsal side. Thoracic endopods three to eight slender, carpus separated from propodus by oblique articulation. Propodus two segmented. Pleopods of male biramous and natatory with some modification of setae of fourth pair. Telson linguiform, entire, tapering to narrowly rounded apex, distal two thirds of lateral border armed with evenly graduated close set of spines, distal border with one or two small median spines. Endopod of uropod with one to four spines near statocyst.

Dactylolamblyops murrayi
Body slender. Frontal plate conically produced, apically blunt and reaching beyond eyes. Eyes small with long ocular process projecting well beyond cornea. Antennal scale long and narrow, extending beyond antennular peduncle by third of its length, outer margin terminating in spine, reaching apex of scale. Telson elongate-triangular, twice as long as broad, distal half armed with about twenty eight rather closely set spines increasing in size to apex, apex without setae. Exopod of uropod twice as long as telson, endopod with single spine near statocyst.
