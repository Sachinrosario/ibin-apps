Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body fairly elongate, sub cylindrical in cross-section;  belly rounded but with a low keel of scutes. Head moderate, about 4 times in standard length; gill openings with 2 fleshy outgrowths; gillrakers 31 to 33 on lower limb of first gill arch. Dorsal fin origin at mid-point of body or a little nearer to snout; 8 pelvic fin rays; anal fin origin well behind dorsal fin base. Pre-dorsal scales forming  single (median) series.
	Colour: back blue/green, flanks silvery and without spots; dorsal fin black (or dusky when fully extended)
