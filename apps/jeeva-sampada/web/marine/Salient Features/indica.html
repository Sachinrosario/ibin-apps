SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Female body long and parallel sided. In the male, body elongate, and the coxal plates more projecting than in female. First peraeon segment twice longer than second; 2-4 segments sub equal. Fifth pleon segment slightly longer than the first four combined. Peraeon and pleon with comparatively long plumose setae. Adjacent areas of carinae concave, lateral part of the segment with an oblique setose ridge.

Telson not tuberculate, with a pair of; lateral setose ridges. The border of telson bears a sub marginal row of tubercles in the female. Dorsal surface of telson in male with 6 conical tubercles, 2 sub median pairs, and a lateral pair of smaller tubercles; each tubercle with a seta by its side. The tubercles present only in males are connected by faint carinae. Telson with spinules, and distal border with short spine setae.

Second antenna with a 5-segmented flagellum. Incisor process of right mandible with ridges and the left with file-like teeth. Mandibular palp normal. Laciniod seta with the apex smooth or very minutely spiny, setal row composed of 10 setae successively increasing in length. Incisor process of left mandible with 2 simple setae. Maxilliped with a stout palp, epipodite elongate, 3 times as long as broad, the borders hairy.

Peraeopods with prominent tubercles the entire surface covered by crescentic rows of microscopic setules. The first leg with 2 barbed spines on propodus, secondary unguis prominently bifid. Sixth leg merus with 3 and carpus with 5 spines. Merus of seventh leg with a row of 7 spines and a single lower spine, carpus with 7 large spines.

Uropod peduncle as long as endopod, exopod bears a prominent claw.


Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi, wood.

Distribution:
Mandapam, Madras (Becker & Kampf 1959) Lakshadweep.

Remarks:
  This species exhibits conspicuous sexual dimorphism. The male L. (L) indica very closely resembles L. (L) quadripunctata in the structure of peraepods.

  Sexual dimorphism distinguishes it from other species.

Size and age: 3-5mm in length.
