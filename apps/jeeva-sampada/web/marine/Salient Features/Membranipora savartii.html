SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting vincularian, zooecia arranged in very regular longitudinal rows. The zooecium from which bifurcation takes place, comparatively bigger than the rest. Zooecia short and wide with slightly curved and raised distal portion. Gymnocyst absent. Cryptocyst thick and well calcified, with small denticles projecting from the lateral edges. A large and conspicuous proximal denticle projects into the opesium, bearing small conical spines at its edge. Opesia deep and oval divided into two lateral compartments proximally by the large proximal denticle.
