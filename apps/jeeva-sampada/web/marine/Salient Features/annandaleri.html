SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Body elongate oblong, almost parallel sided. Cephalon with a small antero-median rostrum, eyes small. Peraeon segments sub equal, peraeonites 2-4 with transverse ridge. Ridges very prominent on the fifth peraeonites. The sixth and seventh peraeonite ridges are brocken up into 4 pairs of transversely elongated tubercles. Composite pleon with 3 lateral sutures, a pair of sub median tubercles. Telson slightly bulged and dorsal surface, distal border semi circular, bears 2 pairs of large sub median tubercles arranged longitudinally, followed by a median tubercle, this arrangement of tubercle is flanked by a longitudinal row of three tubercles on both sides, apex semicircular.
  
  First antenna with 3-jointed peduncle: first two segments short, distal one long. Flagellum 10 segmented, margin of segments bear cluster of aesthetases. Second antenna comparatively long, peduncle five jointed, flagellum 16-17 segmented with tufts of setae on their distal superior margin, Epistome slightly longer than broad, apex triangular, apical and lateral margins setose, arms slightly broad embracing more than one half of the upper lip.

  Mandible with a well developed bidentate incisor process-lacinia mobilis, molar stout, palp 3-segmented, distal two segments with pectinate setae. First maxilla of two lobes , outer lobe very hairy, distal border with 11 strong spines, outer five, blunt, stout and not barbed, middle four with barbs on inner side only and the inner most two barbed on both sides. Inner lobes with stiff hairs and with three stout setae distally and two small setules at the base of these, inner margin slightly hairy. Inner lobe of second maxilla quadrate in shape with long plumose setae latero-distally, middle and outer lobes elongate triangular with slender setae, exposed surface of all three lobes with stiff hairs. Maxilliped stout, long and narrow, slightly bent at the distal tip, apex and lateral margins armed with barbed spines. Palp stout, 5-segmented, terminal segment longest, long hairs on the margins of the last four articles.

  All pleopods ambulatory. Peraeopods 1-3 long, slender almost similar in structure. First peraeopod with basis as long as ischium, merus with long setae, carpus triangular with two spines on its infero distal margin, propodus broadest in the middle, the lower distal spine stout, with a few large subsidiary spines, distal superior margin of the propodus with a tuft of long setae, dactylus biunguiculate. Peraeopods 2 and 3 more setose and spiny and the carpus longer. Peraeopods 4 and 5 short and stout, superior lateral margin of basis setose, margin of carpus and propodus setose, dactylus short stout biunguiculate. Peraeopods 6 and 7 more slender and longer than peraeopods 4 and 5. Lateral margin setose, infero-lateral margin of merus, carpus and propodus bear slender spines amidst setae, dactylus short, biunguiculate.

  Pleopods 1-3 with triangular endopod and exopod subtruncate apically. Second pleopod of male bears masculine on the endopod, apex rounded with tiny spinules on lateral margins. Exopod of pleopod 4 two joited with three thickened spiny areas, endopod fleshy, pleated. Uropod firmly attached to the telson, endopod immovable, exopod movable with five dentate setose outer margins.

Main food: Plankton, detritus and fungi in timber.
  
Ecological notes:
  S. annandalei abundant in the estuarine regions, but can occasionally be found in typically marine environment as well. This species is abundant in the back water systems of Kerala and is a true timber bore causing considerable damage. It also causes serious damage to living mangrove trees.

Size and age: 8-12m in length.
