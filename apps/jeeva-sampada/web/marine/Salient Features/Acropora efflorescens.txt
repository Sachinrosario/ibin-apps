Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum vasiform, plate like, formed by the fusion of the main branches.  Periphery of the corallum with free branches.  Underside generally devoid of branches but possesses, nariform, tubular and subimmersed corallites; the larger tubular ones upto 4 mm long and 2 mm thick, many are appressed.  The upper side with numerous branchlets, better called proliferations, 1 to 1.5 cm long 4 to 6 mm thick, getting longer upto 3 cm at the growing edges.  Distance between adjacent branchlets 5 to 10 mm.  Apices slightly tapering; generally a little curved.

Axial corallites about 2 mm in diameter, 2 to 4 mm exsert, wall thin but not very porous, costate.  Primary cycle of septa well developed, secondaries weak but the cycle mostly complete.  Radial corallites tubular but the inner wall slightly less developed than the outer, ascending at 450; larger radials 4 mm long, 1.5 to 2 mm thick.  Septa six.  Surface coenenchyme reticulate and echinulate.

Remarks:  Acropora efflorescenes is regarded as a synonym of A. hyacinthus by some Australian workers.  However, A. efflorescenes differs from hyacinthus in having a plate like corallum and tubular corallites.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
