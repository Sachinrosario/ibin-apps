SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum thick, encrusting or submassive with a epitheca at the growing edge.  Corallite at the top of the corallum, polygonal calices oval or rounded, fully developed ones average 2.5 mm in diameter.  Wall thickened though porous.  At the basal parts of the corallum the corallites and calices are polygonal, slightly larger than at the top, average 3 mm in length.  Smaller calices are found intercalated among the larger ones.  The first two cycles of septa fully represented.  Third cycle spiny, often incomplete.  Primaries very broad, highly exsert, often to the level of the wall.  Pali on the primaries very prominent, thick, bulged at the top.  Septa and pali highly frosted.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
