SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells. Valves without radial elevations. Ground membrane areolated, areolae 6 in 10m, at the centre irregularly arranged. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Three distinct processes on the valve, knob-like, placed equally apart. A number of pore canals a little within the border.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 74-114m.
