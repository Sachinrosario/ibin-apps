Additional remarks:
     These specimens originally identified as P. aurivilli  by Sewell (USNM 102081) are closely related to P. copactus. However, several distinct differences of male leg 5 indicate separate species status: i) Right basipod 1 with acutely pointed bifid process. ii) Endopod larger, more widely spaced points. iii) Outer spine on right basipod 2 with pointed apex not recurved. iv) Right exopod 1 shorter (reaching the proximal knob of exopod 3), distal tip simple with an undulating hyaline process (not straight). v) Exopod 3 and outer spine of right exopod 2 shorter. vi) Left basipod 2 groove with coarser spinules at inner margin. vii) Left exopod 1 outer spine stouter and slightly curved. vii) Left exopod 2 hyaline process more circular.
