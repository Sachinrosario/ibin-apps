SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: More or less rectangular valves with broadly rounded ends. Central nodule clear. Furrows broad, gradually tapering from the middle and crossed by faint prolongations of the costae.

Diagnostic characteristics: Costae 12 in 10 µ, with alternating alveoli which are more or less quadrate; alveoli 12-15 in 10 µ.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 57 µ Long and 25µ broad.
