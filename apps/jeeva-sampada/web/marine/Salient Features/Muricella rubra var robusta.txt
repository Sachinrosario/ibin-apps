SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 The colony is 90 mm in height and 40 mm. in breadth. The branching  is sparse and is in one plane. The verrucae are alternate and are considerably elongated in one direction of the axis, being flattened in the plane of ramification. The coenenchyma is thin and has larger spicules projecting beyond the surface. The polyps are contracted. They are densely covered with transparent spicules arranged en chevron .
		
Spicules : The spicules are all warty and spiny.
 
Colour	:	Colony is purplish-red or bright red with white polyps.
