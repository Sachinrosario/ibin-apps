Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum convex, underside highly arched.  Greater basal diameter 9 cm, height 5.5 cm.  Axial fossa 1.5 cm long, obstructed by the projecting major septa.  Every fourth septum stands 4 to 5 mm above the level of the intervening subsidiary septa.  Septal dentition irregular, those of the major septa 1 to 2 mm broad and high, those of the higher cycles in the form of serrations.  Columella spongy.  Spines on costae sharp, sometimes curved, bifurcated, 1 to 3 mm long and more or less 1 mm thick at the base, often adjacent ones fusing to form clusters.  At the central part of the disc spines get shorter and obsolete.  Disc perforated at the periphery.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  Illustrated in Indian postal stamp 2001.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
