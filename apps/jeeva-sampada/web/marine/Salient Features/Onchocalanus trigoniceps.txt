SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:    

Diagnostic characteristics:      

Female: The cephalon and 1st pedigerous segments are partly fused, the line of separation being visible dorsally; the 4th and 5th pedigerous segments are separated by a line that is clearly visible. The rostrum is composed of 2 stout spinous processes directed vertically downwards and slightly backwards. The forehead is prominent but without any trace of a crest. The posterior pedigerous margins are prolonged backwards in a triangular flap, at the apex of which is a short spinous projection . A number of small pores, passing through the exoskeleton, are present on the cephalon and the pedigerous segments. The genital segment of the abdomen is produced ventrally, and on either side of the genital orifice is a flap that is sharply pointed at its anterior end.  The genital orifice is surrounded by an area that is profusely covered with short hairs. The posterior segments are hairy.     The appendages agree very closely with the description and figures given by Sars.      

Male: The cephalothorax resembles that of the female; the forehead is prominent and the rostrum is composed of two spines, each without any terminal filament. In the abdomen the 1st and 2nd segments are separate, and the 4th and 5th are fused. The segments are covered with short hairs.     The 1st antenna reaches back beyond the furcal rami. The 2nd antenna and mouth parts agree closely with those of the adult female. In the 2nd maxilla the setae arising from the distal part of the appendage are modified into sensory organs, as in the adult female.     The swimming legs also resemble those of the female.      
The 5th legs are each composed of four segments, instead of three as in the female. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Female: 7.33 mm
Male: 5.77 mm
