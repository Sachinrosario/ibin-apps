SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 The larger is 80 mm. in height and mm. in maximum breadth, and consists of a long main stem from which lateral branches arise. The smaller is 25 mm. in height and 50 mm. in breadth; the central main stem gives origin to five lateral branches. The axis is cylindrical, horny, soft and flexible.  The coenenchyma is very thick; in the younger portions it is thicker than the diameter of the axis.  The polyp display a tendency to a bilateral arrangement, The verrucae are large and conical; they are about 2 mm. in diameter at the base and over 1 mm. in height.  The spicules are arranged longitudinally in eight sheaves, which pass, by gradual transitions into the coenenchymal amature. The anthocodiae are completely retractile. The crown consists of three of four of curved spindles, and each point is made up of least three pairs of bent spindles arranged en chevron.
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are red spiny or slightly warty spindles and of the polyps are spiny. 

Colour	: Colony is dark scarlet in colour  and the spicules are colourless.
