SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Head long, narrow, flat, distinct from rather slender neck; snout blunt; head shields entire; nostrils superior, nasals in contact with one another; one or 2 pre and 2 or 3 postoculars; two (sometimes three) anterior temporals and 2-4 posterior temporals; 6 to 8 supralabials, 4th and 5th below the eye, usually separated from it by suboculars; 9 to 13 lower labials, first five much larger than others; chin shilds (genials) in 3 pairs, the anterior part of which are usually distinct but separated from one another by small scales. Body short, stoutish, marketedly compressed laterally, covered with smooth, hexagonal or quandrangular juxtaposed scales; scale rows at midbody 47-63; ventrals 264-406, divided by a median longitudinal fissure and hence poorly differentiated for most of length rendering an accurate count impossible ; preanals 3 to 5, moderately enlarged.

Diagnostic characteritics:

The colouration of the sea snake is much variable, ranging from one sea to another.  It is normally lemon yellow to cream above and paler below, with a brown dorsal stripe which extends the entire length of the body The head is black above, with a little dark pigment on the throat. Tail banded with black and white, or with vermicular markings.(see 'additional remarks' for more details about the colouration,


Descriptive characters: 

Males with minute tubercles in the lowest rows of scales of the body.

Additional remarks:

The yellow bellied Sea Snake , the most widely distributed of all the sea snakes, is a strongly pelagic species and expectedly with the greatest range of any living snake, being found from the east coast of Africa, across the Indian and Pacafic oceans,to the west coast of Mexico, Central and northern South America, and from Tasmania to Southern Siberia. It has been met with hundreds of miles from land and inhabits purely salt water and is not found in estuaries. Although it is a rapid and graceful swimmer, this sea snake is quite helpless on land, moving under stress and breathing with difficulty. It is reported that the gravid females retire to rmote reefs and isolated island to give birth.

The head, in both colour forms, is black above, the upper lip being yellow. The tail may be barred with black and white.
