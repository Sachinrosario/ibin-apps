SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Erect, foliaceous, bilaminar, with undulating lobes or more definite branches. Zooecia elongate, moderately convex septal lines not very distinct. Pores not numerous, in deep pits, with no definite marginal areolae. Primary orifice deeply sunk, more or less sinuate. Avicularia (suboral) in the peristome, usually small, occasionally enlarge; (large) vicarious, spatulate, sometimes wanting; (small) round, frontal, extremely numerous in the older state. Ovicells with a median sieve-like area. Opercula generally longer than wide, sinuate.
Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: The characteristic perforated frontal area of the ovicells is sometimes bounded by a longitudinal imperforate ridge on either side. It may be relatively narrow or wider. The pores of the frontal area are moderately numerous. Their size, shape and orientation are variable. Some are large, elongate and much widened distally. Others are smaller and shorter, sometimes with the distal end expanded and almost circular. The majority has a distal orientation, but this may be reversed.

The suboral avicularium is more or less transverse, forming a slight projection into the peristome; one lateral edge being at a higher elevation than the other, Some especially on fertile zoecia, are large. Their mandibles are longer than wide, and the proximal, as well as the distal end, may form a cusp altering the form of the peristome. In older parts small around avicularia are numerous; and since they are raised, the frontal surface is correspondingly irregular.
