SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1
	
Body deep and compressed; belly with a sharp keel of scutes. Top of head with 6 to 11 fronto-parietal striae; mouth inferior, snout projecting, lower jaw flared outward along its edge. Dorsal fin with last ray filamentous. Hind edges of scales tooted; a double series of overlapping pre-dorsal scales.

Colour: back blue/green or gray, flanks silvery, dark sot behind gill opening.
