Salient Features :
It is perennial, erect or basally geniculate from decumbent, woody, creeping base.
Leaves 2.5 to 7 cm long, close-set, distichous, erecto-patent, narrow and almost terete due to involute margins.
Panicles 2.5 to 10 cm long, elongate, subspiciform, interrupted.
Spikelets 0.2 to 0.25 cm long.
