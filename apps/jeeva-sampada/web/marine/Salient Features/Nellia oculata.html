SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Highly branched colonies reaching a length of nearly 20mm. Attachment to the substratum affected by tufts of rootlets. The length of the internode greater distally and lesser proximally. The distal region of the preceding zooecium slightly overarch the proximal portion of the succeeding one. The frontal membrane occupies the entire front. The mural rim thin and raised and this gives the zooecium the shape of a boat. Operculum large and occupies the distal region. Gymnocyst well developed proximally and bears two triangular avicularia each bearing two pores one of which is bridged by a small bar. Non-granulated cryptocyst present, slightly extensive proximally. Opesia smaller than the aperture, ovicells not evident in any of the colonies in the present collection.Diagnostic characters:Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:   These specimens agree well with the figure and description of Nellia oculata Busk, given by Harmer (1926). However, in the present specimens, none was found bearing ovicells. The internodes of the present form have 10 zooecia in each face of which 2 zooecia occupying the nodal region are invariably small.
