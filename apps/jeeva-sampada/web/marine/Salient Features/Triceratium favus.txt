SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Box like cells with three cornered valvar plane and short Pervalvar axis. Sides of valve convex and corner rounded. Blunt processes are present at the corners.

Diagnostic characteristics: Cell membrane areolate and strongly sculptured. Primary membrane punctate punctae 12 in 10µ. Girdle band areolate punctate and chamber openings clear.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Sides of valves measuring 96-166µ.
