SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Flat valves, areolae in linear arrangement, uniform in size, about 10-11 in 10µ. A circle of fine areolae inside the margin.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surrounded by a broad flat margin, radially striate.

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 19-31µ.
