SALIENT FEATURES:

Flagellum of second antenna with four articles. Epipod of maxilliped short not reaching the articulation of first article of palp with endopod; semi-triangulate in shape, three time as long as wide; apex acute. Mandibular palp with one article. Fifth somite of pleon with an anteriorly located depressed, triangulate area on mid-line followed by a mid-longitudinal carina. Pleotelson with a large, elevated, median tubercule at proximal end. Posterior margin of pleotelson lacking tubercles; spike-like setae present. Lateral crests, lack tubercles. Laciniod seta of right mandible a simple, apically spinulate seta (Menzies, 1967).

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi, wood.

Distribution:
Ponape, Caroline Islands, Pacific Ocean (Menzies 1957) India (Andaman and Nicobar Islands) Ganapati and Rao (1960).

Remarks:
  In the ornamentation of the pleotelsonic region this species resembles the algal borers, L. (P.) segnis (Chilton) and L. (P.) segnoides Menzies, which similarly exhibit a reduction in the number of articles of the mandibular palp.

Size and age: 3-5mm long.
