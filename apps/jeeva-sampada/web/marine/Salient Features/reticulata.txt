Descriptive characters:  
The following is the details of a specimen collected from the lagoon of Minicoy:
Corallum prostrate, total length 18 cm, width 11 cm.  Main branches 7 to 8 mm thick, reticulately coalascent with wide spaces in between.  Underside of the branches with a few tubular and proliferous corallites, 1.5 mm in diameter and 2 to 6 mm long.  The branchlets on the upper side 1 to 2 cm long, 4 to 6 mm thick.  Axial corallites about 2 mm in diameter 2 to 3 mm exsert.  The radials are labellate and scending on the branches and on the upperside of the main divisions.  Many are prominent immersed ones.  In the old radials only the directives of the primary cycle can be seen, while the younger calices look empty.  Surface coenenchyme reticualte, dense in section.  Living coral brown with blue tips.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
