SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:       

Male:  Anterior end and postero – lateral corners of last metasome segment rounded, each with three backwardly directed spines clearly visible in lateral view. Chela formed by the processes on the right exopod of fifth leg elongate.      

Female:  General shape as in male.  Urosome three segmented, genital segment with numerous spinules, its right posterior corner with small flap like projection carrying small teeth; second segment with a ventral protuberance.  Spine on second exopod segment of fifth leg shorter than third segment with a characteristic curve.        

Size and age: Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     Female:  1.0 – 1.2 mm     Male   :   1.0 mm

Ref. No.:Kasturirangan, 1963
