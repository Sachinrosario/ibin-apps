SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells longitudinally stretched, dorsiventrally flattened, all processes long and slender. Girdle oblique to sulcus, scarcely excavated. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Longitudinal axis often considerably oblique displaced. Sulcus generally with winged lists on both sides.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 118-300µ.
