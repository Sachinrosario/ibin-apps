SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Prism shaped cells with strongly rounded ends and three-cornered valvar plane. Valve margin wavy and membrane finely punctate.

Diagnostic characteristics: A circle of short spines on the valve surface and a siliceous hollow spine at the centre of the valve.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Sides of the valve measuring 46-132µ.

Biochemical aspects: Phototroph, which requires vitamin B12 (Escherischia coli type). Requires NADH as electron donor and stimulated by magnesium sulphate. Ref: Eppley et. al. 1969.
