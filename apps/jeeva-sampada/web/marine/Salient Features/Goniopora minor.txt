Descriptive characters:  
The following is a description of a specimen collected from the lagoon of Minicoy Atoll:  Corallum submassive.  Calices polygonal, 2 to 2.5 mm in diameter, 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter, 1.5 to 2 mm deep.  The first two cycles of septa well developed, the third cycle rudimentary and are of varying numbers, scarcely the full compliment is seen.  The third, when present fuse to the sides of the adjacent secondaries.  Twelve septa reach the columella.  Columella occupies most of the bottom of the calyx.  Six pali present, one on each of the primaries.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
