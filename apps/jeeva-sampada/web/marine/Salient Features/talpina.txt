Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum oval, Fungia like.  Calicinal side convex.  Axial furrow divided into prominent secondary calices.  Lateral calices close-set, numerous conspicuously defined.  Septa upto 24 around a lateral calyx, alternating in height and thickness.  Septal edges dentate, sides granular.  Underside perforate.  Costae covered by spines.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
