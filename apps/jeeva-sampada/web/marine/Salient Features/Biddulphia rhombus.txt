SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Rhombic elliptic valves, processes horn shaped twisted. Valve plain convex, slopping near the margin wall of the valve strongly siliceous, areolated throughout.

Diagnostic characteristics: Areolae arranged quincunx short spine attached to the margin. Areolae 11 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Length 75µ, width 35µ.
