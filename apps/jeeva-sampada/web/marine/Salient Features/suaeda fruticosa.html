Salient Features :
It is commonly known as saltwort
Shrubby usully erect, much branched.
Stem pale, glabrous.
Leaves fleshy, sub sessile, linear, oblong or elliptic.
Flowers hermaphrodite, axillary, solitary, bracteoles membranous, ovate, acute entire denticulate margins.
Seeds black when ripe.
