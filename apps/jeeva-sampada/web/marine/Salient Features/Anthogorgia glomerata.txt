
SALIENT FEATURES		

Description		:Colony is branched in one plane and anastomoses freely; it measures 6 cm in height and 9 cm at the broadest part. It consists of short portions of two main branches 3:5 cm apart. The polyps are very prominent, and are 0.75 mm in height and 1.25 mm in diameter. The operculum is formed from the top of the verruca and consists of eight triangles that meet and close over the retracted anthocodia.  		
		
Spicules: The spicules are spindles covered with tuberculated or branching warts. They are straight, slightly curved or twisted 

Colour		:	Colony creamy-white.
