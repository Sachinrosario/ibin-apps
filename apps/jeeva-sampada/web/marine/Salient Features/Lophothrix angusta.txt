SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:       
Female: Prosome and 1st pedigerous segment fused; 4th and 5th pedigerous segment separate. Prosome with a low median crest. Postero-lateral corners of last pedigerous segment pointed. Below the pointed tip there is a deep notch on either side. Urosome 4-segmented. Genital segment as long as the total length of the 2nd and 3rd urosomal segments and with a slight ventral swelling. First antenna 23-segmented, reaches up to the posterior end of the genital segment. Fifth leg with 3 spines on the distal joint.
