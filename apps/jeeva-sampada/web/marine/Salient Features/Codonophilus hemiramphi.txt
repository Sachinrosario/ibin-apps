SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Female: Body long, broadest at the sixth peraeon segment. Cephalon perfectly triangular, lateral borders slightly sinuous in front of the eyes; dorsal side convex. First peraeon segment roughly squarish, considerably longer than others, twice as long as second, its anterior margin concave and antero-lateral corners produced beyond the eyes; these prolongations are apically acute when viewed from the sides; anterior half of lateral border raised and ridge like, dorsal surface in between the ridges concave. In the middle of the segment the two ridges curve towards the dorso-median line thus incompletely dividing the segment into two halves. Segments two to five successively shorter, sixth and seventh very short, seventh segment shortest. Coxal plates large, none of them reaching beyond the posterior border of the respective segment. Pleon considerably immersed in peraeon, first segment narrow, overlapped by the seventh peraeon segment; segments two to five very broad, broader than seventh peraeon segment. Telson almost twice as broad as long, distal border very slightly concave at middle. Pleon with a broad medio-dorsal ridge, telson with a faint median ridge.

First antenna moderately flat, seven jointed, second eight jointed, slender and slightly longer than first. Incisor process of mandible acute, molar triangular and setose, first segment of palp stout and the third very small. Border of maxilliped spiny, spines extending on to the upper surface, distal segment with two hooks. Inner lobe of second maxilla with eight to ten teeth, outer with three teeth. Basis of posterior peraeopods highly expanded and carinate. Uropods not overreaching telson, exopod slightly broader than endopod.

Body cream yellow, with uniform grayish tint, anterior part of body ash grey, the colour becoming lighter towards the hind end; lateral parts of peraeon with impressed vermicular lines, more pronounced on the anterior segments.

Male: Body elongate and parallel sided. Cephalon almost triangular, anteriorly rounded and dorsally convex. First peraeon segment as long as fourth, indistinctly trisinuous anteriorly. Coxal plates sub equal in length and breadth, anterior ones slightly broader behind and posterior ones crescentic. Pleon as broad as fourth peraeon segment, very slightly immersed in peraeon. Telson semicircular. Uropods slightly overreaching the apex of telson.
 
	First antenna stouter than second, seven jointed, apical segment with long sensory setae. Second antenna eight jointed, with scattered short setae. Mouth parts less spiny than in females; second maxilla with two spines on inner lobe and three on outer. Basis of posterior peraeopods not carinate. Endopod of uropod with a few long setae.

	Body yellowish white, with scattered dark chromatophores, more concentrated on the cephalon, posterior border of the peraeon segments and the dorsal side of the pleon, pleon ash grey. 

First larva: Body like that of the second larva of Codonophilus imbricatus.  Cephalon anteriorly acuminate, eyes very large. Peraeon segments nearly sub equal, seventh short. Pleon narrower than peraeon, first segment longest. First antenna stout but not flattened, seven jointed, second antenna nine jointed. Spines on peraeopods visible inside the larval skin. Uropods and telson without setae, their position indicated by small marginal knobs.

Size and age: Length of female: 26.0 mm; male: 9.5 mm.
