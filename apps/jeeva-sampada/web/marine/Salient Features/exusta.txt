SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting, edges thin.  Coenosteum dense.  Corallites 3-3.5 mm in diameter, 1 to 2 mm exsert, 2-5 mm apart, more or less 1 mm deep.  Calices rounded or oval.  Septa in three cycles, subequal, but in a few cases the primaries are larger than the others.  Septa thin, interseptal loculi twice the thickness of the septum.  Septa scarcely exsert, upper part with entire edges, below 3-4 teetch.  Septal sides granular.  First tow cycles of septa reach the columella.  Columella prominent, almost fill the bottom of the cup, with 8-10 upright rods merging with the lower septal teeth.  Costae thicker than septa, round topped extend to the naked eye but under the lens reveals small soines representing the course of the coaste.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
