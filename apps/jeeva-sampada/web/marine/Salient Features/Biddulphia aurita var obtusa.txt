SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells oblong in girdle view and bipolar in valve view. Pervalvar axis usually greater than the apical axis.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface covered with puncta arranged in radiating lines. Spines usually absent but, cornuted process present.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Length: 60-80µ, width 30-40µ.
