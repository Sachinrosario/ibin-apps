Morphological: Stolonate, creeping zoaria forming patches over the substratum. Zooecia arise from very short lateral internodes of the stolon. Pedicels long and slender with length ranging from 400 to nearly 1000 and an average breadth of 30. The point of origin of the zooecia proper from the pedicel, demarcated by the presence of an enlarged, transversely wrinkled portion. The chitinous ectocyst at certain instances occupy a very small proximal part of the otherwise membranous front. Tentacles seem to be twelve in numberDiagnostic characters: . Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:  The flattened frontal end of the zooecia is described as completely membraneous in T. pedicellata whereas in some of the present specimens, the proximal end of the membraneous front was occupied by the chitinous ectocyst. Osburn observed zooecia with a total length of 3.50mm but in the present form, the length was 1.8mm in most cases even though some zooecia had been noticed with a length of 2mm.
  The shape of the developing bud was oval but during growth the proximal portion enlarges. The shape of the bud was different from that of Triticella boeckii. The occurrence of this form, which had hitherto been recorded only from cooler waters, in a typical tropical habitat is interesting.Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: Main food: Plankton / detritus
