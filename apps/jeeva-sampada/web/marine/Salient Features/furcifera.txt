SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallet highly variable largely calcareous. Blade with a transverse ridge at widest point, outer face of pallet blade slightly convex and the distal end deeply indented in the form of U or V, inner face flat with the distal end having a shallower excavation. A thin, light, yellowish brown or brownish, black periostracum which closely adheres to the distal aspect of the blade is present.

	Shell typical of the genus, anterior slope small marked by closely packed denticulated ridges.

	Siphons separate, gills blade like, branchial groove well developed, labial palps not free, stomach elongate, caecum cylindrical, intestine not looping over style sac, anal canal open, heart median with no or light brown pigmentation for auricles, larviparous, heart/body length 0.1, gill/body length 0.6, visceral mass/body length 0.3, heart/gill length 0.2 (Turner, 1966).

Descriptive characters: Protandrous hermaphrodite exhibits rhythmical sexuality. Breeds throughout the year. Attains maturity when 20 days old.

Feeding habit: Boring into wood, destroying it and draws the nourishment.

Main food: Wood supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks:
		This is a very important and dominant wood-boring mollusk along the coast of India. It occurs in most of the harbours in India attacking wooden water front structures, it also occurs on wooden stakes and other water fron t structures in the backwaters of Kerala. This species readily settles on test panels exposed at Cochin harbour during the hot, highly saline pre-monsoon period. One of the serious impediments for the culture of the oysters around Tuticorin is the massive infestation by this species. It also occurs in the piles of the turtle pens in places such as Tondi and Adirampatnam on the east coast of India.
	
	According to Karande and Pendsey (1969) T. furcifera breeds throughout the year in Bombay Harbour. It attains sexual maturity when it is barely 20 days old and may liberate larvae after 55-60 days in the laboratory tanks if fed on nannoplankton.
