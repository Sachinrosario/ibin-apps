SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells. Flat valves with minute spines along the border. Valve areolated, areolae in three or more systems.

Diagnostic characteristics: Areolar size becoming smaller towards the border. In the centre, about 12 in 10m and towards the border, 15 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 16m.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides. Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
