Salient Features:
Morphological:
Female :

Form oval, two and one half times longer than greatest width. Surface finely and rather sparsely punctate. Cephalon much wider than medial length including frontal process which is apically truncate. Eyes dorso-lateral widely separated. First antennae reaching to the hinder margin of first peraeon segment; third article of the peduncle shorter than the first or the second which are subequal in length ; flagellum composed of 16 articles, the first very short. Second antenna almost reaching to the hind margin of fourth peraeon segment ; the last article of peduncle about one-third longer than the penultimate ; flagellum composed ot 22 articles. Frontal lamina large in form of an inverted triangle with apex beneath extreme anterior apex of the clypeus. Outer lobe of first maxillae capped with ten spines most of which are enticulate ; inner lobe with three plumose spines. Basipodite of maxillipeds as long as first three articles of palp together. First, fifth and sixth peraeon segments subequal in length, a little longer than any of the other segments, which are subequal in length. Coxal plates each with a well marked oblique furrow, plates of second and third segments subquadrate not reaching beyond posterior angles of their segments;remaining plates, sub triangular reaching beyond hinder angles of their respective segments, those of seventh segment extending to the middle of length of lateral margin of fourth pleon segment. Greater part of the first pleon segment concealed beneath last peraeon segment; second segment a little longer than third, which is slightly longer than fourth; fifth distinctly longer than fourth segment, telsonic segment subtriangular, with sides rounded; dorsum with a pair of large foveae near base; apex with two short spines. Uropods reaching beyond apex of pleon, protopod with a spine at middle of outer margin and two marginal spines on the ventral side near outer posterior angle; inner process reaching to about level of middle of length of endopod, with plumose hairs on inner margin; endopod shorter than, and more than half as wide again as, exopod, with outer emarginated; intero-posterior margins of endopod and inner margin of exopod provided with a row of short stout spines in addition to the plumose hairs; exopod suboval , narrowly truncate apically dotted with tiny spinules towards and on outer margin. Dactyli of peraeopods bi-ungulate; first peraeopods with six blunt and three acute spines on inner margin of the merus, one acute spine on the inner margin of carpus and five on that of propodus, basos of seventh peraeopods not greatly expanded; ischium three-fourths as long again as merus which is subequal in length to carpus.
Colour in alcohol Dorsum yellowish, with a broad median brown marking and a narrower lateral brown shripe; anterior part of the head and posterior part of telson yellowish.

Diagnostic characteristics:
Two conspicuous on the dorsalsurface of Telson, and the emarginationof the outer edge of the endopod of the uropods.

Feeding habit : Predator

Additional remarks:
According to Richardson (1913) Excirolana Orientalis is very Ravenous attacking small fisches first devouring eyes, then Gills then working into body cavity all of this is done inside of ten minutes.
