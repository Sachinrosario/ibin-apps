SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallet-blade triangular to conical, hollow stalks, periostracum thin, yellow, and not extending as border; stalk short, irregular and knobby at proximal end.

Feeding habit: Feeding on the wood into which it bores.

Main food: Cellulose.

Feeding type: Supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks: In the Philippine Island specimens were recovered from 28 fathoms.
