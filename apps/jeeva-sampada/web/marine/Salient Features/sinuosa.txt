Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum meandroid, valleys upto 3 cm wide, very deep.  Major septa 8-10 apart with occasional smaller ones in between.  Septa 10 to 12 mm exsert, exsert ends only slightly arched or vertical.  Major septa about 12 mm broad, edges and sides smooth, those of the opposites sides almost meet at the middle bottom of the valley.  They often turn right or left before fusing each other.  Columella absent.  Endotheca vesicular.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
