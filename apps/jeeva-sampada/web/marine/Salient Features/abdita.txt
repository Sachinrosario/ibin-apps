Descriptive characters:  This species is fairly common in Gulf of Mannar.  Larger colonies in Manauli and Hare Island were 70 to 80 cm in greater spread.  Surface rises into hillocks.  Three coralla collected from Pamban (Palk Bay) had foliaceous branches.  Corallites polygonal, generally 12 to 15 mm in length, 4 to 6 mm deep, one side of the corallites wall slightly more elevated than the other.  Septa close together, 40 to 70 per calyx depending on the size of which 18 to 20 reach the columella.  Paliform lobes present on the major septa.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3.   Building Blocks.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
