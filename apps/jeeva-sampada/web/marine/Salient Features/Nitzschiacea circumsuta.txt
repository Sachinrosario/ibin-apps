SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Lanceolate elliptic valves with slightly acute apices. Marginal keel broad and strongly marked. Keel puncta large, sub-rectangular, 3-5 in 10µ.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface with longitudinal fold and faint transverse striae. Striae finely punctate, 25-30 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Length 160-176µ, width: 54-56µ.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides
