Salient Features:
Morphological:

Body stout and cylindrical, two and a half times as long as broad. Head small, twice as broad as long and half as long as firs peraeon segment. First peraeon segment longer than all the others, two to seben subequal to one another in length and breadth, with transverse rows of small teeth, one row on the second segment, two each on segments three to five and three each on the sixth and seventh. Coxal plates prominent, last broadly triangular and reaching beyond the second free pleon segment. First pleon segment completely hidden, fourth laterally overlappingf the fifth; posterior border of each segment with a row of large teeth, the odd tooth in the middle of each segment larger. Telson a broad based triangle, with a pair of large submedian teeth at the base, followed by two longitudinal rows of denticles reaching the tip of the telson, these two rows giving the telson a distinct bicarinate appearence; apical spines eight in number.

Anterior border of frontal lamina broadly rounded. Peduncle of first antenna three jointed and the whole appendage scarcely reaching the tip of the fourth peduncular segment of secon antenna. Second antenna slightly over reaching the anterior border of third peraeon segment. Second segment of mandibular palp with stout plumose setae. Outer lobe of first maxilla with thirteen spines and inner with three feathery setae.

Fourth segment of first peraeopod with four curved blunt teeth, fifth segment immersed in the fourth, dactylus with a secondary unguis; seventh peraeopod only maderaltely spiny.

Second pleopod of male with oblong rami, stylet slightly longer than endopod and spiny along the inner distal border, apex pointed and with two small setae; protopodite with six to seven coupling hooks. Vasa defferentia opening on small rounded mammiform papillae situated slightly apart from each other.

Body light yellow with aslightl greenish tint.

Additional Remarks:

Pillai(1958,1967) created Cirolana Bicarinata based on two male specimens collected from kayamkulam lake. C. Bicarinata differs from all known species of cirolana in the presence of up to 3 rows  of posterior submarginal tubercles on the paraeon segments , while all the other species under this genus have only a single row. The similarities of C. bicarinata with C. prustulosa hale are striking, the only important difference between the two is the presence of three rows of tubercles on the pereon segments of C. bicarinata. C. prustulosa was created based on a single slightly multi lateral specimen, hence the number of row of tubercles is not very clear. If more than one row of tubercles are present, it is probable that bicarinate may proove to be synonymous with C. Prutulosa. However in a later paper (Pillai,1967), has syninimised this species with C. Fluviatilis.
