Descriptive characters:  Corallum semi-arborescent with slender branches.  Corallites arranged in alternate and opposite pairs.  Greater diameter of the corallites 2 mm (Bourne) to 5 mm (Horst), circular or elliptical in out-line.  Depth of calices 1 to 2 mm.  Septa in four cycles, the fourth incomplete.  Primaries most exsert, forming a crown of six points at the thecal rim.  Third cycle of septa larger than the second.  Columella prominent, projecting, trabecular, about 1 mm in diameter.  Costae extend to the base of the corallites, subequal, granular.  Septal edges entire or minutely serrated.  Intercostals furrows perforated.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
