SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological:
Rostrum very short; carapace without or with minute supraorbital and pterygostomian spines; antero-inferior margin of carapace serrated; abdominal somite 5 and sometimes 4 too with a pair of dorsomedian spines. Antennular peduncle curved and antennal scale unsegmented in stage I; the endopod is a simple pointed rod; exopods of maxillipeds with 3 apical setae; pereopods 3-5 with protopods not expanded; pereopod 5 or even 4 , without exopods; there may be 8-10 sub- stages.
The paired dorsomedian spines of abdominal somite 5 is lost in postlarval stages; the pleopods biramous; telson sulcate- like in adult with 2 pairs of distinct dorsal spines and posterior margin with 3 pairs of spines.

Diagnostic characteristics:
Rostrum minute without teeth; carapace without hepatic spine and the antero-inferior margin serrated; abdominal somite 5 with a pair of dorsomedian spines in larval stages; telson with 3 pairs of terminal and 2 pairs of lateral spines,on the plate.
Sex attributes: Separate (heterosexual)

LARVAL STAGES:
Zoea I: length 1.75mm
Rostrum absent; carapace with a small dorsomedian hump behind eyes; eyes sessile; antero-inferior margin of carapace serrated with 3 minute denticles; pterygostomian sharp; antennular peduncle unsegmented,outer ramus with 3 aesthatascs and a plumose seta; and a long ,plumose terminal seta in the place of inner ramus. Antennal scale unsegmented with 9 inner and 2 outer setae; endopod short,terminating in a stiff seta; mandible without palp; maxillae and maxillipeds of usual form; only 2 pereopods present, 1st one as a tiny uniramous bud and 2nd as a small biramous bud; abdomen
5- segmented and 5th with a paired dorsolateral spines; telson triangular, not separated from the last ab.segment,slightly notched in the middle, bearing 7+7 setae.
Zoea II: length 1.95mm
Rostrum short; eyes stalked, suprorbital spines present; maxillipeds 2-3 with endopods 5 segments; pereopod 1 functional and biramous; telson with 8+8 processes.
Zoea III: length 2.00mm
Carapace with pterygostomian spines and antero-inferior margins serrated; antennular peduncle 3- segmented, basal with a spine; antennal scale with a short terminal spine; pereopod 2 functional,endopod 4- segmented, distal segment with 3 setae; telson processes reduced to 7+7. Uropods separated, exopod functional with 6 setae and endopods small budlike.
Zoea IV: length 2.15mm
Antennal scale with 12 setae; pereopod 2,5-segmented with 1,0,0,3,3 setae distalwards; pereopod 3 biramous, endopod unsegmented, budlike; exopod with 4 terminal setae; telson rectangular,setae reduced to 5+5; uropods well developed, scale with a spine.
Zoea V: length 2.25mm
Antennal scale with 15+1 marginal setae; endopod reduced; telson processes remain 5+5 terminal and 2 pairs on lateral margin.
Zoea VI: length 3.25mm
Antennule characteristically bent outwards,both rami almost equal; antennal scale with a small spine at the base and outer margin with 3 small hairlike setae.pereopod 5 uniramous; telson becomes more narrow.
Zoea VII: length 3.75mm
Pereopod 1 on right shows traces of chelation,left simple; leg 2 minutely chelate; leg 4 long and p5 uniramous; 5 pairs of pleopod buds present.
Zoea VIII: length 4.40mm
Inner ramus of antennule 2- segmented; endopod of antenna slightly shorter than scale,4segmented and flagellar; setae on scale increased to 40; chelae more developed; telson more elongated,narrowing posteriorly; pleopods as biramous buds.
Zoea IX: length 5.15mm
Chela more developed on leg 2; pleopods with setae; telson tip slightly rounded with 6+6 setae terminally and 2 dorsal (lateral) on either side.
Zoea X: length 5.75mm
More setae on appendages; pereopod-1 on left is still not chelate; telson and uropods remain same.
Postlarva: length 5.90mm
Rostrum bifid with a pair of setae; carapace with a broad projection- like antennal spine; serration of antero-inferior margin lost; a small spine present on stylocerite; merus of p3-4 devoid of spines; mandible with molar process only; P2 both chelate but unequal; dorsomedian spine on somite 5 lost,that of the pleura produced into a spine; telson with 3 pairs of terminal and 2 pairs of distinct dorsal spines.

Characteristics.
Rostrum minute; antero-inferior margin of carapace serrate; P1 only right side chelate,left simple; large size of supraorbital,branchiostegal and abdominal spines (Gurney 1937a)

Abundance: nil.
