SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body moderately deep (depth in front of dorsal fin about  2.9 to 3.3 times in standard length), with a convex dorsal profile and a blunt snout. Eye a snout, backward projecting spine just below it.  Dorsal fin with 10 spines and 9 soft rays, continuous, unnotched; anal fin with 3 spines (the 2nd equal to or greeter than the 3rd) and 7 soft  rays ; pectoral fins with 2 unbranched and 14 branched rays. Predorsal scales (on top of head ) beginning at level middle of eyes; 4 or 5 transverse rows of scales on cheek; lower limb of preopercle unscaled.
Colour : 

overall silvery-gray with numerous black or dark brown spots (one per scale) on sides and spots over lateral line forming 2 or 3 narrow brown or black longitudinal strips with white spaces in between; a pearly white band from below eye to upper edge of pectoral base; fins blue-gray to whitish.
