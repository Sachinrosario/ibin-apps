SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branched in one plane. The polyps are mostly lateral in position, alternate or sub-opposite, but young forms occur among the more mature verrucae. The calyces are 1 mm in height. The branches and twigs are occupied by two diverging almost opposite polyps. The verrucae are cylindrical and have rough surface. The coenenchyma contains 0.3 mm long warted and leaf-like spindles.
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are warted, club-shaped with irregular ends and with star forms with 4 or more rays.
 
 Colour		:	The colony is reddish and white polyps.
