Descriptive characters:  This foliaceous species was very common in Gulf of Mannar and Palk of Bay, where it cuts plat-forms upto 3 meters in diameter.  Some of the specimens collected from the reef crest in Lakshadweep which is exposed at times have a spreading flat coralla with the folia closely packed rather than the usual flower like arrangement.  Thickness of the fronds very from 5 to 7 mm and the larger diameter of the individual coralla range from 50 to 70 cm.  The height of tubercles and the elevation of the calicinal fossa vary in different specimens.  It is rather unnecessary to go into the details of this species.  Bernard (1897) has given a detailed description also discussing the skeletal variations.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The species is no more abundant as it used to be in Gulf of Mannar due to indiscriminate quarrying of corals

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
