 SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref No:  65, 143, 144,214.

Morphological:   Body  with velvet texture. Rostrum slightly elevated and straight, reaching to or 
slightly surpassing second segment of antennular peduncle, armed dorsally with 8 to 9 teeth; carapace with stridulating organ consisting of 6 to 11 ridges on a rather wide and moderately curved band on posterior branhiostegite; 3rd to 6th abdominal somite strongly carinated, carina on 3rd somite broadly sulcate; inner uropod usually exceeding tip of telson. Petasma asymmetrical, right distoventral projection shorter than left and possessing a number of dorsally bent spinules; left  distoventral projection broadly swollen,  toe-like, with a semicircular arrangement of                 blunt to sharp spinules; inner intermediate strip short and cylindrical; outer intermediate strip longer than inner strip, broad and curved; distomedian lobule triangular and long. Thelycal plate subrectangular, as long as wide and carrying a minute anteromedian projection, anterior edge minutely mucronate


Diagnostic characteristics:   Carapace with stridulating organ consisting of 6 to 11 ridges on a 
wide and moderately curved band, mid-dorsal carina on 3rd abdominal somite broadly sulcate; right distoventral projection of petasma shorter than left, left distoventral projection broadly swollen, toe-like with a semicircular arrangement of spinules; anterior edge of thelycal plate minutely mucronate.

 Colour:    Body pinkish with irregular dark brown or dark pink mottlings.

   Maximum length(cm)(male/female/unsexed): Male:8.5, Female: 10.5		   Ref. No:60, 70.
