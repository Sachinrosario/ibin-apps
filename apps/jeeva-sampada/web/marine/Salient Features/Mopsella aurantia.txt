SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony flabellate. The main branches are significantly thicker than the branchlets. The end branches go off in short right angle. The nodes protrude in an angle at the main branches mainly. The internodes are 15 mm long on an average, somewhat shorter at the base. The poyps stand exclusively on a surface, closely crowded on the besides. The diameter is 0.8 mm. The cortex consists of horizontally laid thin spindles, which have high but rounded thorns in the center, and over that conversing, much thicker spindles with very high thorns which are pointed diagonally upward at the upper ends and outwards. The somewhat bent tentacles tissue also strongly thorned and broad. Calyces and barks are abundantly provided with leafy clubs, are having round shape and baour 0.1 mm long. The numerous leaves of every leafy club are somewhat notched at the top, their root part is very short and thickly warted. In addition to this, there are single heavily thorned spindles. The nodes 0.1 mm long stretched smooth is orange- red or white with orange coloured red or whitish polyps           

Spindles are shapely curved in the center with large thorns
		
 
Colour		:	The entire colony is orange -red colour
