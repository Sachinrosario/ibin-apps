Descriptive characters:  The following a general description of this species based on many specimens including the types studied.

Corallum vasiform, often pedicellate, main branches spreading, subdividing, reticulately coalascent, 8 to 10 mm thick.  Underside with verruciform or nariform corallites or with prominent tubular ones in the form of ramiculii (as in A. conferta and A. armata).  Upper side with small branchlets, single or in groups of 2 to 5, 1 to 3 cm long and 4 to 6 mm thick.  They are arcuate and the tips are tapering.  Distance between adjacent apices of branchlets 5 to 10 mm.  Axial corallites 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter 1 to 2 mm exsert, wall very thin and porous.  Opening oval or rounded with six prominent primary and 2 to 6 spiny secondaries.  Radial corallites labellate, ascending, lip thin and styliform or a little broadened and cresent shape (as in forma conferta), 2 to 3 mm long, 1 to 1.5 mm broad.  Primary cycle of septa well developed, second cycle scarcely seen.  Wall porous at first getting thicker and compact later, striated with weak achinulations at the basal.
Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
