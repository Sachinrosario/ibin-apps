SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Coarse zoaria. Projecting marginal avicularia give a distinct serration for the branches. Spines not noticed. Nearly half of the front occupied by the opesia. Scutum small, partially covering the opesium, arise from the inner distal portion of the zooecium. Cryptocyst moderately developed. Frontal avicularia present, with disto-laterally directed long mandibles. Marginal avicularia long, the rostra aquiline. Vibraculum with a short seta, which usually bends and overarches the front. Axial vibracula two in number. Ovicells with a long slit like fenestrae. The opesia of the outer zooecia at the branchings traversed proximally (median?) by joints. Diagnostic characters: Harmer (1926) has stated that the distinguishing features of this species from Scrupocellaria maderensis are the scutum not filling the opesia, absence of a truncate distal border and the smaller number of spines.
Narrow long and oblique vibracular groove similar to that in S. maderensis distinguishes this species from S. scabra.   Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite.  Descriptive characters: The present material does not possess spines. Harmer found the replacing of the normal lateral avicularia by large marginal ones, but this feature was not common, and is not noticed in the case of present specimens. In this connection, it is remarkable to recall the remark of Waters (1897) that "most colonies have no frontal avicularium, and in those that have it is only found in some zooecia". Waters feels that S. delilii is a southern form being represented in the Mediterranean and Maderia.
