SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 
POST LARVAE:Size ranges from 8.4-16.0mm. Rostrum bears 2 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth; tip pointed,extends beyond the antennular peduncle; a pair of supraorbital spines present; hepatic spine placed slightly in front of the epigastric tooth and pterygostomian spine is placed in the anteroinferior angle of carapace. Mandibular palp 2-segmented, asymmetrical; thoracic legs with exopods.

Dorsomedian spine on the 3rd abdominal segment long and borne on a crest as in P.longipes and that on the 5th reduced. Ventromedian spines restricted to last thoracic and  1st 2 abdominal segments; paired dorsolateral spines on the  5th seg.absent. Abdominal pleura form a single spine,ventrally. First pleopod without an endopod,others biramous. Telson narrow and tapers at posterior end  and has a median spine with a spine formula of (9+1+9). 

Diagnostic characteristics:-The presence of a prominent sub-supraorbital spines in protozoea III; less no.of rostral teeth; pterygostomian placed at the anteroinferior angle of carapace; dorsomedian spine on 3rd abdominal segment without crest and ventromedian spines on last thoracic and 1st 2 abdominal segments.                        

Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: pelagic zone Main food: planktonic organisms, organic matters with sand and mud.Feeding type: omnivorous  The larvae and postlarvae of this species are distributed widely in the Arabian sea through Red sea and east off Java and Andaman Islands in the Bay of Bengal.They were mainly caught in Feb.&,Mar. and Sept.&Nov. Most of the specimens were damaged and hence,the figures of the whole animal could not be shown. 

Size and age:  Postlarvae.8.4mm-16.0mm                       Mysis:  4.9mm-6.5mm        

Protozoea. 3.0mm        


Eggs and larvae: 
Eggs unknown.
LARVAL STAGES:                                               

Protozoea III: length 3.0mm.Rostrum long with a bifid supra orbital spine,inner long and prominent; 4 terminal setae present on the distal segment of the endopod of maxillule.

Mysis II: length.5.0-6.5mm.Rostrum long bearing 2 dorsal + epigastric tooth; subsupraorbital spine vanished,a small labral spine present; hepatic present and the pterygostomian situated at the anteroinferior margin of carapace.Mandible unsegmented and palp without setae; pereopods with exopods; dorsomedian spines present on ab.somites 3-6 and 3rd has no crest; a paired dorsolateral present on somite 5; ventromedian spines on last thoracic and first 4 abdominal somites; abdominal pleura serrate ventrally; pleopods in bud form; telson parallel sided and posterior end straight with a median spine and 5 pairs of terminal spines,making the spine formula 9+1+9,the 4th being the longest.

Characteristics: Strong supraorbital spine; paired dorsolateral spines on abdominal somite 5; pterygostomian spine in the anteroinferior margin of carapace.Abundance: Nil
