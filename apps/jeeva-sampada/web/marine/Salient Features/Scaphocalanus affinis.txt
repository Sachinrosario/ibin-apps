SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Carapace in male produced into low triangular rostrum apically acute; in female much more produced. Eyes large and sub globular. Antennal scale much shorter than antennular peduncle, distal lobe short. Pseudobranchial lobes spirally twisted; pleopods not modified. Telson reaching beyond articulation of exopod, base with two pairs of spines, more than half of lateral margin with spines regularly distributed and increasing in length, and at distal one fourth with distinct arrangement into series; apex with pair of long spines, three small median spines and pair of plumose setae. Exopod of uropod slightly longer than endopod, with three to five or rarely six to seven spines; spines on endopod proximally in series, distally not in series.


Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Male 7- 7.5 mm,  Female 6- 6.5 mm.
