Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body elongate, the depth  3.7 to 4.3 times in standard length . Chin with 2 slender  barbells  which usually fall to reach rear margin of  pre opercle,  their length 1.45 to 1.9   times in head;  maxilla reaching slightly posterior  to front edge of eye ;  villiform  teeth in bands in jaw , on vomer and  palantines ; gill rakers on first arch 21 to 25;  First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first spine minute. Pectoral rays 12 to 14; Pelvic  fins  nearly as long as pectorals. Lateral line  scales 28 to 32; 4 ½ vertical rows of scales in space between dorsal fins; 10 or 11 vertical scale rows  along upper part of caudal peduncle.  

Colour:-  A dark reddish brown to blackish  stripe passing from front to front of snout through eye along upper side of body to above midbase of caudal fins; head and body above stripe brownish to greenish grey, flecked with dark reddish brown or blackish, below white with rows of dark dots; barbells yellow; first dorsal fin whitish with two large irregular dark blotches, one in distal part and one at base; second dorsal fin with three irregular dark stripes; upper lobe of caudal fin with 4 to 6 blackish cross bands, the lower lobe with 5 to 7.
