SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological:
Rostrum broad basally,tapering acutely and reaching slightly beyond eyes; a distinct dorsomedian hump on carapace,anteriorly; body somewhat flattened. Size bigger than other genera of alpheids,eyes are of peculiar shape and size; rostrum reaching beyond eyes in stage 3; the pterygostomian angle not produced; abdomen 6- segmented, telson and uropods separated; pereopod 5 is very long and uniramous; dactylus styliform,pointed and there is always a wide gap left between pereopod 4 and 5; pereopod 4 develops at a later stage; telson quadrangular in shape,bearing 7+7 processes in stages 1&3,whereas in stage 2 the formula is 8+8. Uropods biramous and endopods without any seta.

Diagnostic characteristics:
Rostrum short but broad basally; dorsomedian hump anteriorly on carapace; 5th pereopod longest,uniramous with styliform dactylus.

Eggs:
Caridean prawns generally carry their eggs (berried females) with their body and they hatch out into an advanced larval stage,the Zoea. The eggs are dark greenish in color; with a diameter of 0.2-0.4mm.

LARVAL STAGES:
Zoea I: length 2.1mm
Body somewhat flattened; rostrum broad basally,tapering acutely and reaching beyond eyes; eyes sessile; a distinct median dorsal hump present anteriorly on carapace; pereopod buds present; abdomen 5-segmented and telson rounded; antennular peduncle 2-segmented, basal one longer with 5 small outer and a long, plumose inner seta; 2nd segment short with 4 short distal setae at base of inner ramus and a single outer seta. Both rami well developed, inner terminating into a long
spine-like seta and a shorter,slender seta on it; outer at its apex 1 broad and 4 slender aesthetascs and 1 seta; endopod of antenna large,tapering into an acute point with a small seta near apex; scale elongated, slender, parellel-sided, bearing 10 setae and 1 seta on the outer margin. Mandible with incisor and molar processes,former with a large tooth and 3 tubercle-like small teeth and molar with 2 blunt teeth-like projections at the two corners. Maxillipeds with exopods and endopods; pereopod buds present; first 4 pairs biramous,3-4 small, 5th long and uniramous; telson narrow and elongate,almost rounded posteriorly with a shallow median notch,constricted slightly above the setal portion with 7+7 processes. The 5th process is the longest.

Zoea II: length 2.15mm
Eyes stalked; antennular peduncle incompletely 3-segmented; 1st seg. with 3+1, 2nd 1 and 3rd with 5 short setae; inner ramus with a long ,plumose and a short,plain seta; outer ramus with 2 broad + 3 slender aesthetascs and a short inner seta. Mandible,both incisor and molar processes with blunt, tubercle-like teeth; pereopod buds increased in size,not segmented; telson with a median notch, with 8+8 processes, the innermost being the smallest.

Zoea III: length 2.2mm.
Rostrum extends beyond the eyes; carapace smooth,pterygostomian angle not produced; 6-segmented abdomen with telson and uropods separated from it; antennal scale without outer seta and with a minute spine;14 marginal setae present. Mandible well developed,incisor with a sharp,broad tooth and 3 additional blunt,long teeth; molar with 4-5 teeth, 1st 2 pairs of pereopods become functional; endopods 4-segmented,tip ending in a point; exopods with 4 terminal +2 subterminal, long,plumose setae; pereopods 3 and 4 still small,biramous buds; pereopod 5, 3-segmented with its tip produced into the usual long, styliform process. Telson quadrangular,median notch persists, number of processes reduced to 7+7. Uropods biramous,exopod with 6 plumose setae and endopods bud-like without setae.

Characteristics:
Rostrum short and broad basally; dorsal tubercle anteriorly,on carapace; no spines on carapace; pereopod 5 unusually long and uniramous with styliform dactylus. There exists a clearcut gap between pereopods 4 and 5; telson quadrangular with
7+7 processes.
