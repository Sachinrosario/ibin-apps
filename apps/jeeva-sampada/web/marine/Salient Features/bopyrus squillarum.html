Additional remarks:    SPECIMES OF BOPYRUS SQUILLARUM STUDIED BYCHOPRA [1923, 1930] WERE DESCRED AS A NEW VARIETY BIMACULATUS. HOWEVER, A SINGLE FEMALE AND MALE FROM PALAEMON TENUIPES COLLECTED BY SAVANT AND KEWAL RAMANI (1964)WAS IDENTIFIED AS B. SQUILLARUM DUE TO THE ABSENCE OF CHARACFERS TYPICAL OF THE VARIETY SUBSEQUENTLY , DESHMUKH(1984) FOUND THAT 1.78% OF NEMATOPALAEMON TENUIPES FROM THE CATCH IN MUMBAI WERE INFECTED BY THIS BOPYRID.
  MUCH CONFUSION EXISTS IN THE SYNONYMY OF BOPYRUS SQUILLARUM OWING TO ERRONEOUS HYPOTHESIS OF EARLIER AUTHORS THAT ONE EPICARID CANNOT INFEST MORE THAN ONE SPECIES OF HOST CHOPRA (1928) HAS COMMENTED ON ITS SYNONYMY AND TRIED TO CLEAR THE CONFUSION.
