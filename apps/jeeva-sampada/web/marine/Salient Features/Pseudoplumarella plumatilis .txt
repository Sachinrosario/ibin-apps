SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branching in one plane, pinnate with short branches, which regularly alternate. Short branches are unbranched up to 38 mm long. The polyps are up to 1 mm long. The calyces are relatively big and stand in 4-5 longitudinal rows. The outer surfaces of the main branches are covered by net shaped strips of scales. The polyps are contracted. They are densely covered with transparent spicules arranged en chevron.
		Spicules: The spicules of the polyps are 0.3 mm long, fine closely covered with warts. Those of the coenenchyma are 0.27 mm long spindles.
