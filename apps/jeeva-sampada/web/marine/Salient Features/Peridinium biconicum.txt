SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Hypotheca in the shape of a cone and it terminates in a point towards the bottom in the same manner as epitheca in reverse. 

Diagnostic characteristics: The body is divided into two symmetrical parts in the equatorial plane. Girdle circular and excavated with a slight notch to the ventral side. Sulcus is broad and short, rounded at its base.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 60µ.
