Diagnostic characteristics:
Carapace produced infront into triangular plate with acutely pointed apex. Eyes large. Antennal scale long and narrow, about 13 times as long as broad, carpopropodus of thoracic endopods three to eight three jointed, terminating in long stout nail. Pleopods in male biramous. Exopod of fourth pleopod with stout plumose seta on last three segments. Telson gradually narrowing for three fourth of its length, then widening and terminating in two broad lobes, cleft ovate with two setae, margin of cleft unarmed, lateral margin of telson armed throughout, spines becoming shorter to narrowest part of telson and then becoming longer. Endopod of uropod with prominent spine on dorsal surface of statocyst, inner margin armed with dense row of spines grouped into series.
