SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium encrusting, often more or less hemispherical. Zooecia erect, the large orifices central, rounded by tubercles or massive conical or truncate projections. Orifices large, deeply sunk, subcircular or slightly wider proximally; the condyles not far behind the middle. Avicularia munute, circular, numerous, on various parts of the colony; or large, spatulate, probably vicarious. Ovicells inconspicuous. Opercula with well marked marginal sclerties; the occlusors inserted near the distal end.
Diagnostic characters:
Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters: C. turrita differs greatly in appearance from a typical encrusting species, the zooecia, even those of the marginal zone, being erect, their vertical length exceeding the diameter of the frontal surface. A young colony expands slightly fro its attached base. The outlines of young zooecia are distinct frontally, with shallow marginal valley, and about 5-6 calcified conical processes, small or large, commonly surround the orifice in a regular ring. The frontal surface later becomes thickened, forming wide bars separating the orifices, which are deeply situated at the bottom of vertical tubular spaces in the general thickening. In the older state the regular arrangement of the conical processes is obscured.
A young colony may be so different from the older state that it might be taken for another species. That an elongate membranous opening is later replaced by a calcified porous surface, and the comparison of the tow specimens results in the conclusion that the present species is referable to Cigclisula.
The small avicularai described by SMITT are constantly present, often raised,but they vary in number and position. The large spatulate avicularia occur in all the Siboga specimens, sometimes only on the short stalk of young ones. The distal part of the rostrum is surrounded by a thin raised rim.
