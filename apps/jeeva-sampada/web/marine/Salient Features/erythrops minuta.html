Morphological:

Diagnostic characteristics: Genus Erythrops G. O. Sars
General form slender and delicate. Antennule markedly more robust in male than in female. Antennal scale with outer margin entire or coarsely serrate, without setae, terminating in strong spine, no distal suture. Eyes short, well developed, compressed dorsoventrally and pyriform. Third to eighth thoracic endopods long and slender. Carpus separated from propodus by very oblique articulation. Last four pairs of pleopods of male well developed and biramous. Telson very short, entire, lateral margins naked or spiny, apex truncated and armed with two pairs of stout spines and pair of median plumose setae. Inner margin of endopod smooth or spiny.

Erythrops minuta Hansen
Body spiny. Anterior border of carapace convex without definite rostrum. Eyes small, antennal scale about four times as long as broad, outer distal spine long, overreaching apex of scale, outer border of scale with three teeth. Telson broader than long, distal border more than one third total width, with two pairs of spines and pair of plumose setae, in female inner pair of spines longer; lateral border throughout spiny.
