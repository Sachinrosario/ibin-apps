Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of calcareous vertical plates, 1 to 5 cm thick, 10 to 20 cm high.  Plates undergo fusion linked by vertical laminae in the form of honey-comb fashion.  Gastrozooids are large.  Dactylozooids arranged around the larger gastrozooids.  

Reproduction by medusae.

Colour in living condition yellowish-brown.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  

Main food:  Plankton.   

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The species was found common in lagoons of almost all Lakshadweep islands.  Not recorded from Gulf of Mannar or Gulf of Kutch, hither to.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
