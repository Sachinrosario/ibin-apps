Description	:	Colony  branching dichotomous and irregular. The polyps stand vertically and surround the branch with their basal part. 2 or 3 polyps are found at the internodes.  
		Spicules: The polyp sclerites are stretched or slightly bent up to 0.8 mm long, finely warted spindles or irregular flat sticks are also found. The spicules of the tentacles are longitudinally arranged. The axis is hard and brittle. 

Colour	:	The axis brown and the polyp is white in colour.
