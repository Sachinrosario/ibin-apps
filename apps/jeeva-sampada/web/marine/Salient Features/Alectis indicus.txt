SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells depressed, cake shaped, with one conspicuous antapical spine on the right outside the list of sulcus. Epitheca with sigmoid sides and suddenly adnate apical horn. Hypotheca bread loaf like.

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle dextral, ends slightly displaced, running almost around, with smooth winged lists.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 40m; breadth: 64m.
