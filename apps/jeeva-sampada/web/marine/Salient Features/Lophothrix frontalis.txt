
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:              

Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Prosome with a median crest. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment narrowly rounded. Rostrum deeply notched, with very small points at the tip. Urosome 4- segmented. Genital segment with a small ventral swelling. Urosomal segments 1to3 with fine teeth on the posterior margin. First antenna with 23 free segments reaches up to the tip of the urosome. Fifth pair of legs 3 – jointed, distal joint with 3 spines in some specimens and with 4 spines on the right leg in some specimens. Three inner spines with spinules on the outer margin and the outer spine with spinules on outer and inner margins.  

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                         Female: 5.3 mm.                                                Ref. No.:                  T.C.Gopalakrishnan, 1982
