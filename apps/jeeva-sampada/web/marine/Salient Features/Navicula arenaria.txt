SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Lanceolate valves, narrow with sub-acute apices. Axial area narrow indistinct. Polar nodules small, central small, rounded and ill defined.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface striate, striae in the middle transverse or slightly convergent towards the apices. Striae 9-10 in 10m.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 50-60m, width 9-10m.
