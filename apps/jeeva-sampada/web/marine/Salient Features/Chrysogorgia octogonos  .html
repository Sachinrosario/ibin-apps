SALIENT FEATURES 

 

 

Description: Colony  branching at 1/47  winding towards the right. The stem branches are short and not spread in one plane and with 4 or 5 successive internodes of 2 ½ to 6 ½ or terminal up to 9 mm long. The stem internodes are 3 mm long; the branches are up right, polyps arise at the end of the internodes of the branches, mostly one at each branch pointed diagonally. The total length including the tentacles is 2.5 mm. The polyps consist of three calcareous folded layers, lying transverse and appears roof-tiles-like. The nematozooids are absent.  

Spicules: The scales of the polyps are with sharp ends and those of the bark are stick-shaped, smaller and thinner.

 

Colour :  The colony is pale yellowish to brown in colour.
