SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No:18,48,83,184

 

Morphological:   Rostrum slightly tilted upward, dorsally convex, overreaching eye in female

and slightly shorter in male, armed with 7-8 dorsal teeth ; carapace with well developed

branchiostegal spine situated submarginally, longitudinal and transverse sutures present, the former extending to posterior border; 6th abdominal somite more than twice length of 5th; telson much shorter than inner uropod; 5th leg reaching slightly beyond middle of antennal scale. Petasma with distal petaloid appendages less intricated with lobules; distolateral edge of ventral lobule of lateral lobe pointed and recurved outward, spine on distal fringe of dorsal lobule of lateral lobe well developed. Thelycum made up of  a semicircular anterior plate, a median concavity bounded by lateral bosses or knobs and a sub-circular posterior plate on last thoracic sternite with a median conical tubercle.

 

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:    Carapace with well developed branchiostegal spine situated

submarginally, 6th abdominal somite more than twice length of 5th, 5th pereopod not reaching tip of antennal scale.

 

 

            Colour:   Body light pink with reddish eye.

Eggs and larvae:               Ref. No: 55,136

 

  Characteristics:   Mysis stage encountered in plankton collections measured 7.2 mm total length. It

has the following distinguishing characters (Fig.2 ): Rostrum very long, extending far beyond eye and 2nd segment of antennular peduncle, armed with 5 dorsal teeth; carapace with hepatic, supraorbital and branchiostegal spines; abdomen with prominent and triangular dorsal spine on 3rd somite and shorter ones on 4th and 5th somites, ventral spines on 1st to 3rd somite, pleurae with pointed spines, 5th and 6th somites with lateral spines; pleopods present as buds.

 

 

  Abundance:  Developmental stages of the species have been observed in the plankton from

           shelf– waters off the southwest and east coasts of India.
