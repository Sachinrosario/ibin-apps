SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Body stout and hispid. Carapace produced in front into broad triangular rostral plate with pointed apex; in female rostral plate narrow and more produced. Fifth segment of endopod of second thoracic limb in male distally expanded and distal border cut into many sharp small teeth. Exopod of third pleopod of male nine segmented, ninth segment with 3 stout naked spines, eighth segment armed on each distal corner with barbed spinose seta, seventh segment with barbed seta on inner distal corner and one slender naked seta on outer distal corner. Lateral border of telson and cleft armed with spines. Endopod of uropod reaching tip of telson, exopod shorter, spines on telson and inner uropod carry secondary spinules. 

Additional  remarks:       According to Panampunnayil (1999) in specimens collected from the Andaman Sea, the distal border of the inner expansion of the merus of the second thoracic endopod in male is cut into eighteen sharp small teeth as against seven to nine in Pillai specimens. The terminal segment of the exopod of the third male pleopod ends in two spines while in Pillai’s specimens the terminal segment ends in three stout spines.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)

Adult male : 5-6 mm
Female       : 4.5-5 mm
