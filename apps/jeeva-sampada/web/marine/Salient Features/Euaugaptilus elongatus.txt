SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 


Diagnostic characteristics: 
      The proportional   lengths  of the anterior  and posterior  regions  of the body  are as  70 to 30. 
     The Ist   antenna  overreaches the tip   of the furcal ramus by the last  five or six segments.
     In the  2nd antenna the endopod  and exopod  are of approximately equal length. The exopod  is composed  of eight    segments ;   the 3 rd segment appears  to be devoid  of a seta.
     The mandible  possesses  a well- developed  palp ,  the two rami being  of approximately equal  length. The biting  ramus  is well developed, but  the  biting  edge  is much more  oblique  than in  either  nodifrons or  indicus ;  the teeth  are arranged  in three  groups, possessing  2,2 and  3 teeth  respectively.
     The Ist maxilla  agrees with the  description  given  by Farran (1906), but on the  right  side  the  2nd  basal segment bears 4, instead of 3, setae.
     In both  the 2nd maxilla and the maxilliped the distal setae are fringed  with large “buttons”, and in each seta one  can trace a gradual transition  from hairs, through small spines to “buttons”.
     In  the Ist  leg the 2nd  basal segment  bears   a retroverted seta near the distal  outer angle.
     In the 5th  leg  the 2nd  basal segment  bears an extremely long,  delicate seta, the total  length of which  is about  twice the length of the exopod.
