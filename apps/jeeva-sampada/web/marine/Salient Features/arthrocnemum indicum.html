Salient Features :
It is perennial, prostrate, 5-30 cm. tall succulent, diffused, green halophyte.
Phylloclade reddish in colour with short inter node fleshy, calvete, thick, spongy, highly branched with 2-toothed at the tip.
Flowers minute, pink, bisexual and 2-3 together present in cylindrical blunt 1.5 2.0 cm. long spike.
Seeds are brownish, trigonous, with hard seed coat.

Soil data for (1:2) extracts

Soil salinity : 12.5 to 48.5 dS.m-1
Soil pH : 7.8 to 8.3
E S P : 10.9 to 28.4
S A R : 9.2 to 27.7

Health:
Plant alexipharmic, Ash of plant used in scorpion sting.
Nutrition:
Its tender phylloclades are used as pickles by natives of Gujarat.
