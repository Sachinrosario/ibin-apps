SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 Diagnostic characteristics:        Female: Prosome moderately produced and triangular anteriorly; shape of metasome as that of A. centrura. Relative lengths of prosome and urosome 79.5:20.5%. Posterior corners of fifth pedigerous segment rounded, each side with 1 medium sized lateral spine and 1 or 2 very small dorsal spines. Relative lengths of urosome segments and caudal rami 41.7:20.8:12.5:25%. First segment of urosome without spines, second segment with 2 large dorsal spines.          

In leg 5, lateral seta subequal to 3rd segment including terminal spine; 3rd segment with bulbous base and a pectinate terminal spine, terminal spine comparatively short and stout.     Male: Metasome shape as in female, but lateral and dorsal spines smaller than those in female. Prosome of urosome segments and caudal rami 12.5:30:22.5:5:15:15%. Ur. I small, bilobed, lateral margins having few hairs laterally in concavity of surface. Ur. II with 1 pair of lateral and 1 pair of dorsal spines distally, latter pair larger. Ur. III with 1 pair of still larger spines. Ur. IV bears a pair of small spinules. Anal segment without prominent knobs. Caudal rami nearly twice as long as wide and setae slightly more divergent than in A. centrura.          

Right leg 5 with strong spine on inner posterior side of 2nd segment; 3rd segment broad with inner lobe of quadrilateral shape with shelf-like projection on it and carrying a spinule on distal margin; 4th segment long with irregular margins and 2 small spines, 1 on inner middle region and the other terminally. Left leg with 3 segments; 1st segment broad, 2nd segment rather small, 3rd segment with fringed inner proximal margin, long leaf-like process with small basal spine at about midlength and apex with long spine and curved process.

Additional  remarks:     Acartia centrura and Acartia bowmani coexist and their trophic adaptations are similar. However, the absence of specimens with the specific features of the two species overlapping is evidence that this two species are reproductively isolated.
