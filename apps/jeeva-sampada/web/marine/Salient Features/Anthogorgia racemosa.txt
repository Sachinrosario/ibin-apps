SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony is 110 mm in height and 80 mm in breadth. The branching is confined to one plane; the branches arise very indefinitely and are markedly sinuous. There is no anastomosis. The axis is horny and dark brown at the base, but becomes almost pale yellow in the twigs. The diameter of the main stem is over 4 mm, that of the twigs about 1 mm. The coenenchyma is thin and allows the axis to shine through. It is densely covered by an armature of warty spindles. On the main stem and larger branches the polyps occur all round at considerable intervals, but on the twigs they are disposed mainly on the lateral surfaces. When expanded the verrucae are cylindrical and are about 3 mm in height and 1 mm in diameter; they are capable of retraction to a height of 1 mm. On the verrucae the spicules overlap and form a network that is surmounted by eight triangular teeth, each composed of two spicules enclosing an acute angle. Abundant spicules are found on the aboral surface of the tentacles.
		
Spicules: The spicules of coenenchyma are warty spindles and the tentacles are spiny spindles.
