Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting or nodular, sometimes free lying.  Surface with several modular protuberances some of which may form short stunded branches.  Gastrozooids and dactylozooids small, less than 0.3 mm in diameter.  Colour greenish yellow.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.  

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  Though several species of Millepora are described from the Indo-Pacific the identification is mainly based on the growth form.  The genus can inflict itching and irritation on soft human skin by their nematocyst hence the common name fire coral. 

Egg and larvae:Medusae.  Planktonic
