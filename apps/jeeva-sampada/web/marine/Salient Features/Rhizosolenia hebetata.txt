SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Longitudinally drawn out cells with intercalary bands, scale-like, pointed towards the apex, punctate, punctae 18-24 in 10m.

Diagnostic characteristics: Process long hollow at the base ending in a long spine.

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 6-14m and length 224-560m..

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
