SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Medium sized cell body with large list extensions. Margin of left sulcal list characteristically sub quadrate without posterior lobes but with a straight posterior margin.

Diagnostic characteristics: Left sulcal list extends beyond mid point of cell in lateral view. Peripheral areas of both sulcal and cingular lists can be reticulate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
