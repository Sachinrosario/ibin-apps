SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 Colony is delicate, branching in one plane. It is 100 mm in height, and 120mm in breadth. The branches are long delicate and slender. Anastomoses occur in several places. The axis is very soft.  The coenenchyma is very thin, and in the older branches the axis is distinctly visible. The verrucae are small and densely crowded, especially on the smaller branches; they are very spinose.  
		
		
Spicules: (1) Long, slender, curved spiny needles; (2) Irregular forms  (3) Short, thick, smooth, conical needles, giving off root-like processes with rugose warts. 

Colour		:	Colony and the spicules are  pale yellow in colour.
