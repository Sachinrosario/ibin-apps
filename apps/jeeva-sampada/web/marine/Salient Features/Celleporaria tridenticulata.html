SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium encrusting; zooecia often in few layers. Orifice semicircular; proximal denticles 3, stout, symmetrically arranged. Spines commonly present, 2, less often 3, long, jointed at the base. Suboral rostra median, short or long, narrow and cylindrical; the avicularium at their free end; other forms of rostra and suboral avicularia not present. Frontal avicularia generally few or wanting; sometimes wide, relatively short, spatulate, the largest ones much widened distally. Ovicells mostly with deficient frontal wall, their opening pyriform, widest distally, facing frontally; rarely with a complete frontal wall, the opening then facing proximally.
Diagnostic characters: . The species is recognizable by its tridenticulate orifices and by the terminal position of its suboral avicularia.
  C. tridenticulata is one of the species in which there is an umbonula-like development of the compensation sac. The absence of a basal sclerite, the operculum being thus continuous with the frontal membrane, is a notable feature of zooecia thus constructed.
Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: The range of variation in the specimens is slight. In fertile zooecia, these may occur on or just inside the proximal edge of the peristome. The rostrum may be vertical, very short and inconspicuous, leaving the orifice completely exposed. In other specimens, it is long and narrow, overarching the orifice.
  The frontal avicularia are sometimes gigantic, often narrow proximally, widened distally. The opercula are semi-circular, with a sub marginal sclerite thickened laterally, but with no trace of a basal sclerite.
  The ovicells are smooth and imperforate, sometimes widely open, the frontal part of their wall being slight or even wanting.
