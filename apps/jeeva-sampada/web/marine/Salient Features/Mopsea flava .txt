SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony  flabellate, 26 cm in height and 17 cm broad and 36 cm above its main stem. The diameter of main stem is 5.5 mm and the lateral twig is 2 mm. The stem and main branches have even surfaces, there being no  swelling nor constrictions at the nodes. Proximally the horny nodes are longer than the calcareous internodes, the former being 2.7 mm long and the latter 2 mm. The polyps are small and parallel. The branch spring from the calcareous internodes. The polyps are small, and are emplanted on all sides of the larger branches, but tend to be lateral on thesmaller twigs. The calyces are small and shaped like a truncated cone or dome-shaped verrcae measuring 1.3 mm in height and 1.1 mm in diameter. The calyx walls are covered with a mosaic of scale-like spicules with ctenate edges. The polyp is completely retractile. Few spicules appear on the dorsal surface of the tentacles.	
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are small, oval or irregular scles, which are densely covered with relatively large tubercles. 

Colour	:	The colony is light yellow.
