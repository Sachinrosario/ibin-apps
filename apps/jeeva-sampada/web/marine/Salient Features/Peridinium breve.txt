SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells short, ovoid in shape, thick walled sides, somewhat rounded. Apical horn short. Epitheca conical. Hypotheca semi-circular slightly flattened posteriorly. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle dextral, slightly excavated, with lists supported by spines. Sulcus broad, with inconspicuous lists. Intercalary areas clear, striated.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 30-75µ; breadth often greater than length.
