SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Carapace broadly rounded in front without definite rostrum. Eyes large, cornea wider than stalk. Antennal scale as long as antennular peduncle. Carpopropodus undivided. Telson short, linguiform, not reaching articulation of outer uropod, basal part with three stout spines, apex broadly rounded and armed with pair of long stout spines and three median setae. Exopod longer than endopod, more than half of outer margin of proximal segment armed with 12 spines; endopod with spines arranged in series. 

Male unknown

Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Adult Female:  9.3 mm.
