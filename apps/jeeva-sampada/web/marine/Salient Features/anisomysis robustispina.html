Diagnostic characteristics:
Body short and smooth. Carapace anteriorly produced into low apically rounded triangular rostrum. Antennal scale just over reaching antennular peduncle. Second segment of mandibular palp broad and without denticles. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male extending to tip of telson and armature as usual for the genus. Telson equally broad at base and distal end and depressed along lateral two thirds. Distal half of lateral margin armed with five pairs of short spines, distal border with small but distinct median emargination lodging pair of short spines, distal border on either side of sinus armed with three long stout spines, outer two pairs curved inwards and inner pair curved outwards. Uropods long and narrow.
