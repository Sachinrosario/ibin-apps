SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves bacilliform, slightly convex in the middle of valve margin, ends obtuse-round.

Diagnostic characteristics: Striae coarse, radiate in the middle turn convergent at the ends, 10 in 10m. Axial area narrow, central nodule slightly large, raphe oblique.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 220m, width 35m.
