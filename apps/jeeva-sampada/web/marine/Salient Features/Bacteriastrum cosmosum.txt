
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells forming long chains with wide aperture. Inner setae with short basal part perpendicular to chain axis.

Diagnostic characteristics: Anterior terminal setae curved and directed towards the posterior, with spirally arranged spines. End valves with a deep furrow.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter of cells 9-19m.
