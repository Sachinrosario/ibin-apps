SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Dendroid with distichous or dichotomous gemmation leaving the present calyx compressed.  The calicular openings tend to face the same direction.  Calices little projecting or immersed, circular, 4 mm in diameter shallow.  Septa in six systems and three cycles.  Third cycle of septa unite to the second near the columella.  Septa subequal, elegantly notched at the wall, but not exsert.  They are subequal, very narrow, two cycles unite to the columella.  Columella deep-serated, spongy crisp.  Primary septa directly join the columella.  Costae in the form of subequal, scabrous vermiculate ridges, traversing the entite corallum (Alock, 1892).

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Rare

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
