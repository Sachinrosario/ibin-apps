Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum in the form of thick plate growing layer over layer, upper most layer 1.5 cm thick.  Surface rises into irregular gibbosities.  Edges free underside with small calices.  An epitheca follows the growing edge.  Calices very conspicuous and deep, well marked by a thick wall, 0.7 to 0.8 mm in diameter about 1 mm apart.  Septa in two cycles, primaries meet over a rudimentary columella.

Surface coenenchyme foveolate and papillate.  Papillae run together to form low ridges the valleys of which enclose the calices.  Individual papilla almost absent.  In many cases the ridges enclose single calyx, forming funnel-shaped depressions.  Thickness of ridges 1.5 mm to 2 mm,  in height about 2 mm.  Surface under the lens reveal a close set reticulum.  Section shows a reticulum with upward and downward trabeculae.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
