Female: The adult female is markedly asymmetrical, the axis of the body being inclined strongly to right or left according as the parasite is a left or a right Bopyrid. The colour of the animal is uniformly white, and some time the thorax has a pinkish tinge. The usual colour markings are for the most part absent, but on the lateral margins on the head there is a characteristic colour patch, one on each side. This patch some time extends forward so as to enclose the head anteriorly. Except for these two spots of dark brown pigment the dorsal surface of the animal is unpigmented. On the ventral surface of the oostegites are covered with spots of brown pigment. Lateral margins of abdomen have also some irregular patches of colour
The head is turned to towards the shorter side of the body, and is produced in front into a large broadly rounded anterior lamina. The antero lateral angles are not very prominent. The posterior margin of the head, as is common in the genus, is partly fused with the first thoracic segment and is not clearly seen. A pair of small eyes of conspicuous dark colour is placed in the anterior part of the head
The thoracic segments are distinct and their lines of demarcation are well defined. The first segment in the mid dorsal region seems to be more than half as long as the second. The ovarian bosses are not distinctly seen, but can some times be made out on the longer side of the body as oval structures occupying about the anterior three-fourths of the margins. The epimera occupy the usual position and are somewhat poorly developed.
The pleon is proportionately large and is almost as long as broad. The segments of which it is formed are fused in the middle of the dorsal surface but the boundaries are clearly seen on the margin. The segments are distinct on both sides. The posterior extremities of the fifth segment extend considerably beyond the posterior limits of the sixth and are pointed. The posterior margin of the sixth segment is sometime entire, sometime notched.
The head appendages are extremely reduced and are not easily seen. The posterior lamina has the usual median lobe and the secondary lamellae; the former has an almost straight margin, and the later are of about the same size and are pointed at the tips. The maxiliped is rectangular and is 1 ½ times longer than broad. The palp is long and prominent, situated a little away from the antero-internal angele. The anterior margin is rounded and is provided with five of six thick setae. Almost as long as the palp itself. The thich muscles at the base of the anterior segment of the maxipilled are well developed.

Those of the first pair consist of the usual two protions; the anterior is a little longer than broad and its anterior martin is almost straight with a slight concavity in the middle; the posterior is prolonged into a short blunt lobe at its infero-internal angle. The internal crest is well developd and entire. The remaining oosgtegites are rectangular and in all except the fifth the lengis is 1 ½ times the breadth. The fifth pair encloses the incubatory cavity posteriorly, and each carries a small number of hairs along the posterios border. The thoracic legs have the usual structure, and a boss is present on the basal segment of each. The external maregins of merus and carpus are beset with small hairs; the dactylus is large and forms a strong claw with the propodus.

Each pleopod is a small plate like triangular structure with an acute apex pointing inwards. The pleopods of the opposite sides do not meet in the middle lines. Urpopoda are altogether absent.
