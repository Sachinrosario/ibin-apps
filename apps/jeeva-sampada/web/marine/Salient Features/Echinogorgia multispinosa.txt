SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony evenly branched in a and sinuous. The main stem  branches mostly at right angles. The individual calyces are dome-shaped verrucae within which the polyps are of complete retraction. The coenenchyma are extraordinarily shaped, 0.8 mm long.
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are warted leaf-clubs of 0.4 mm long with irregular, scale-like spindles. There are four radiant star like spicules also.
 
Colour		:	The colony is light pinkish brown, the coenenchyma darker than calyces. The axis is dark brown and the spicules colorless.
