Morphological: Zoaria with erect zooids which may be arranged uniserially or biserially growing into sac-like expansions from a basal encrusting portion which spreads over the substratum in sponge-like fashion. Colour of the colonies brown with a greenish tinge when fresh becoming paler in material preserved in formalin. The erect portion develops from the center of the encrusting part of the colony. The shape of the zooids vary considerably in the different situations. Zooecia of the encrusting region usually somewhat rectangular in outline becoming much more elongate and sometimes becoming hexagonal. Zooecia of the distal region of the flabellate portion (figs. c and d). The proximal part of the erect portion (fig. e) long and more or less tubular. Inter-zooecial walls thicker in the encrusting zooids and thinner in the erect ones. The frontal wall thin, translucent and even. Aperture placed at eh distal end of the zooecia giving the appearance of being placed at the tip of a papilla when the tentacle sheath is not completely withdrawn. In the uniserial and biserial regions of the colony the aperture is placed at the tip of a small papilla (fig. e) marked with two or three wrinkles. The lophophore is placed at an angle with the frontal membrane, bearing sixteen tentacles. The pharynx, as well as the region of the first and second constrictions are broad. The caecal part brownish with greenish tinge. The pleated collar funnel-shaped. Parietal muscles consist of about eleven pairs.

Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:  The present specimen showed similarities to Alcyonidium erectum of Silen (1942) and Mawatari (1953) but showed all the above three morphological peculiarities in the one and the same colony. In this connection, the present form appears to be unique. The zooecia of the proximal part of the erect portion connecting the encrusting part, possessed small papillae with apertures at their tips. These zooecia showed similarity to A. mamillatum Alder. Silen's (1942) observations were based on a specimen, which was the "torn off tip-end of a zoarium". The uniserial or biserial arrangement of the zooids in the proximal part of the erect portion in the present forms was also of special interest. The nature of the pleated collar which was funnel shaped in present specimens was not mentioned by the above authors. The number of tentacles also showed variation. Silen's material shows twenty tentacles, whereas that of Mawatari twenty-two, and in the present form, the number was only sixteen. The disposition of the lophophore was same as described by Mawatari (1953). The shape of the zooecia was more or less same in the encrusting portion of the colonies, whereas it was irregular in the erect portion, which might be owing to the peculiar mode of growth.
