SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting.  Corallites oval or slightly compressed, not projecting, 10 to 14 mm long, 9-11 broad, 3-4 mm deep.  The interior and peritheca of the corallites look highly blistery.  Septa 28 to 38 of which 12 to 16 reach the columella.  Septa exsert, exsert ends arched, lower one third broader, edges with 4-6 teeth, sides granular. Coatae conspicuous, fuse at the middle of the intercorallite area or remain opposed.  Columella trabecular, poorly developed.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
