
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:        

Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment round, slightly indented, but not as much as in Amallothrix indica. Rostrum deeply notched with 2 short slender filaments. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment slightly swollen downwards. First antenna 23- segmented, reaches up to the posterior margin of the 2nd abdominal segment. In 5th leg terminal joint with one stout short outer marginal spine, an apical spine which is twice as long As the outer marginal spine and a long inner marginal spine which is more than 4 times the length of the apical spine. Inner marginal spine with spinnules on its outer margin.
