SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:  
Post Larvae: I&II. length ranges from 8.4mm to 9.1mm. Rostrum long, extending beyond the antennular peduncle, and without  crest. Rostrum bears 3 or 4 dorsal teeth and an epigastric tooth; no ventral setae present. Carapace with a small supraorbital and antennal spines; hepatic spine present; anteroinferior margin of carapace feebly serrate. Antennal scale broad with a strong terminal spine. Mandibular palp with 2 segments,without setae and shows asymmetry. Maxillule with 3-segmented endopod ,each bearing 2,2,setae and terminal segment 5 setae. Maxillipeds and pereopods with exopods, bearing setae.Dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 3-6; those on 5 & 6 equal and the longest. Paired dorsolateral spines present on ab.segs.5 & 6; 6th segment also bears a pair of posteroventral spines. Pleura of abdomen faintly serrate. Pleopods with setose exopods. Telson tapers with a median spine and a spine formula of 7+1+7, the 4th   pair, being the longest.

Diagnostic characteristics:  Comparitively a long rostrum than in M.mogiensis and M. andamanensis; only the anteroinferior margin of carapace is serrate; supraorbital spines small;,hepatic spine prominent. Antennal spine large; dorsomedian spines on ab.segs.5 & 6 are longer than others. Pleura of abdominal segments 1-5 serrate and rounded in shape.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: pelagic zone Main food: plankton-  diatoms,algae,copepods,polychaetes etc.

Feeding type: detritus,organic matter with sand and mud; omnivorous.

Additonal remarks:The adults of this  species was reported for the first time from the east coast of India,off  Visakapatnam by George and Muthu(1968). The larval stages were not recorded earlier from Indian waters.

Size and age: Postlarvae I & II: 8.4mm to 9.1mm 

Longevity (y) (wild) :  1 ½ to 3 years.
