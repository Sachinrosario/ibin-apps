Characters:  Corallum composed of thick stout flattened branches.  They do not undergo fusion.  Main branches rounded.  Pores are situated on the lateral side of the branches.  According to Klunzinger (loc. cit) the gastropores are about 0.5 mm in diameter, the dactylopores minute 0.2 mm in diameter.  The dactylopores are arranged on either side of the gastropores.  Colour blue or violet.  The colonies may grow up to 30 cm in height.  Reproduction by planulae larvae.  


Additional remarks:  The inclusion of this genus in the present account is based on a small fragment of the coral shown to this author by Dr. Hassen manuku of Lakshadweep.  He also informed (personal comm.) that he obtained the sample from Kadamat from a tourist who collected it from more or less 10 m deep.  But for this there is no record of the genus from the Indian coral fauna.  The identification of the species is tentative.  The identity of the species can be established only by examination of more samples.  Scheer and etal (1986) reported D. nitida from Maldives from a depth of 20 m.  


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
