Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      

Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Posterolateral corners of the last pedigerous segment rounded and slightly indented. Abdomen 4-segmented. Genital segment slightly swollen below. First antenna reaches up to the posterior end of the abdomen. Fifth leg oval in outline, plate–like, with a strong inner marginal spine and an apical spine which is smaller.
