SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:        Rostrum  long and extends beyond eye; dorsal spine present in postlarva I and ventral rostral spines from postlarva VII onwards; carapace with antero-orbital and pterygostomian spnes;  a strong hepatic spine  always present; mandible with 2-segmented palp; dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 4 -6;  a pair of posterolateral spines present on abdominal segments 4 -6; a median ventral spine also present on somite 6; pereopods 1-3 chelate with vestigial exopods; uropods well developed with setae; telson with 8 pairs of spines.

Diagnostic characteristics:-Rostrum long; rostral teeth appear in late postlarvae; pereopods 1-3 chelate; exopods vestigial; dorsomedian spines on abdomen only on 5 and 6 somites; an additional pair of posterolateral spines on somite 4 of abdomen; 6th somite with a paired posterolateral,and ventrolateral and a median ventral spine; telson with 8+8 spines.

Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.Main food:   plankton, small crustaceans,copepods,polychaetes;organic matter with sand and mud.Feeding type: omnivorous.Additonal remarks:The 3 species of prawns, P.merguiensis,P.semisulcatus and P.monodon are more or less similar in characteristics; they occur more along the east coast of India.

Size and age:   Protozoea I-III-0.96-2.6mm in length.Mysis I-III-3.4mm-4.2mm

Postlarvae-4.8-10mm             

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed)    240mm                          

Longevity (y) (wild) :    1-3 years.                     

Eggs and larvae:

Eggs: Spherical,yellowish brown in color, somewhat translucent; from 0.27-0.28mm diameter. Nauplii I-VI: from 0.30-0.50mm; body pyriform and bears the typical ,3 pairs of appendages; caudal end round with 2 spines. The characters are shown by figures; the caudal seta increases to 7+7 in nauplius VI; the setae are plumose.

Protozoea I-III: size 0.96-2.6mm; in Protozoea I the body is clearly divided into 2 parts, anterior part covered with the loose fitting carapace; the narrow posterior part is divided into a 6-segmented thorax and an unsegmented abdomen; ocellus is present; eyes compound but sessile; mandible is greatly enlarged; the antennule is uniramous,endopod with 3 segments,basal subdivided into 5 sub- segments and no setae; the distal segment has 2 lateral and 3 terminal setae. The maxillae and maxillipeds appear; 3rd mxp. in the form of a small bud; caudal furca wide with 7+7 setae.The main changes in  Prot.II-eyes stalked,rostrum  and subsupraorbital spine are present; abdomen segmented; telson unchanged. In Prot.III, the sub- supraorbital spine absent; dorsomedian spines present on somites 1-5; paired dorsolaterals on 5&6; telson is separated; telson with an additional spine, 8+8 spines.

Mysis I: 3.4mm; rostrum long,extends beyond tip of eye; supraorbital,pterygostomian and hepatic spines present; dorsomedian spines on abdominal somites 1-3 disappear; on 4 -6 reduced and with dorsolateral spines; an anal(ventral median) spine also present on somite 6; mandible with incisor and molar processes, without palp; 3rd maxilliped longer than others; pereopods 1-3 chelate and others developed; uropods well developed with telson bearing 7 terminal and 1 lateral spines (8+8).

Mysis II: 4.0mm; spines on carapace and abdomen remain same; pleopod buds appear; chelae prominent on legs 1-3; mandibular palp as a small protuberance; telson with 2 lateral and 6 terminal spines.

Mysis III: 4.2mm; hepatic spine prominent; pleopods 2 -segmented; mandibular palp has a constriction; telson with 3 lateral and 5 terminal spines.Postlarva I: 5.8mm in length.Rostrum long with a dorsal spine; supraorbital and hepatic spines on carapace; pleopods with setae; mandibular palp 2-segmented with setae; abdominal segment4 also develops a pair of dorsolateral spines; exopods on legs vestigial; telson more elongated with same spine formula,8+8.

Characteristics: long rostrum with dorsal and ventral spines(late postlarvae);presence of paired dorsolateral spines on abdominal somites 4 -6; an additionalpair of ventrolaterals on 6th somite; a median ventral spine on somite 6; 

Abundance: It contributes to a fishery only in the middle region of both east and west coasts.
