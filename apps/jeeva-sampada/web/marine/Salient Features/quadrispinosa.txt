SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  

Diagnostic characteritics:  
Body slender. Frontal plate in both sexes considerably produced with apically blunt apex. Carpus and propodus distinct. Pseudobranchial lobes spirally twisted; pleopods not modified. Telson reaching articulation of exopod, basal part wide with two pairs of strong spines, apex rounded with three small spines and two pairs of large spines, outer pair longer than inner, lateral border with spines on distal one third increasing in length and arranged in irregular indistinct series. Exopod of uropod clearly over reaching endopod, proximal segment with six to nine spines spread over less than half the border, inner margin  of endopod  with serially arranged spines. 

Size and age

Maximum length:  Adult male 7- 8.5 mmAdult female 7- 7.5 mm
