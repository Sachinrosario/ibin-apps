SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Wading bird but often prefers cheer area. Body ablong, bill longer and straight.

Diagnostic characteristics: Dark brown above, dull white below. Bill basal olive green, terminal one third blackish.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike, female slightly larger.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Solitary, feeds during dusk or dawn.

Main Food: Aquatic invertebrates.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder
