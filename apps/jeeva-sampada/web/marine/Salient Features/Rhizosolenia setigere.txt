SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Rod shaped cells, cylindrical, conical valves, slightly oblique. Apical process long, hollow to some distance and ending in a long spine.

Diagnostic characteristics: Intercalary bands scale like, punctae 18 rows in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 8.3m; length: up to 518m.

Biochemical aspects: Phototroph, which requires vitamin B12 for growth. Mammalian type. Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
