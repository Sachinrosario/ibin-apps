Morphological:    Body irregularly pubescent; rostrum long, straight, reaching tip of antennular
peduncle, armed with 9 to 11 teeth along entire dorsal margin; postrostral carina extending to 8/9 of carapace; adrostral sulcus ending behind epigastric tooth;  branchiocardiac carina absent; mid-dorsal carina on abdomen commencing from middle of 4th somite; telson armed with 3 pairs of movable marginal spines; 1st pereopod with an ischial spine. boss close to posterior edge of last thoracic sternite present.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum armed with teeth on entire dorsal margin; branchiocardiac 
carina absent on carapace; telson armed with 3 pairs of movable marginal spines; 1st pereopod with ischial spine; thelycum with median boss close to posterior edge of last thoracic sternite.
