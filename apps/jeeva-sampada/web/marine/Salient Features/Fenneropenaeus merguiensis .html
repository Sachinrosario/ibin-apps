SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No: 103, 111  

 

Morphological:      Rostrum long and slender, surpassing or almost reaching tip of antennular

peduncle; armed with 6 to 9  teeth on dorsal, and 3 to 6 teeth on ventral margins; its blade high, broadly triangular in shape (not so in juvenile and young specimens). Carapace with postrostral crest ending distinctly before posterior margin; adrostral carina and sulcus not reaching as far as epigastric tooth, sulcus shallow; gastro-frontal and hepatic carinae absent; gastro-orbital carina extending over middle third to half of distance between hepatic spine and orbital margin. Abdomen carinated dorsally from posterior half to two- third  of fourth somite. Telson unarmed. Dactyl of third maxilliped in adult male half (0.5 to 0.6 times)  as long as propodus, tuft of hairs of propodus reaching half to two-third of dactyl. Petasma in males with distomedian projections overhanging distal margin of costae; free border of ventral  coastae serrate near apex; outer

surface of lateral lobs with several rows of minute tubercles.      

 

                                                                                         Diagnostic characteristics:    

Rostrum long and slender, bearing 6-9 teeth on dorsal and 3-6 teeth on ventral margins; adrostral carina and sulcus on carapace not reaching as far as epigastric tooth;

gastro-orbital carina extending over middle third to half of distance between hepatic spine and orbital margin; dactyl of third maxilliped in adult male half as long as propodus; free border of ventral costae of petasma serrate near apex.

 

Colour: Ground body colour creamy yellow with minute brown speckles.

                             Eggs and larvae:              Ref. No. 146, 116

 

     Characteristics:  Eggs are spherical, white and translucent, and measure 0.23 mm in diameter. The embryo develops and hatches out in about 18 to 24 hours. Five naupliar, three protozoeal and three mysis stages comprise the larval stages which are completed in about 13 days. The first stages of nauplius, protozoea and mysis measure respectively 0.32mm , 0.96mm and 3.4mm in total length.  The first postlarva is 5.8mm in total length and has the telson bearing 8 pairs of spines on sides and distal margin. In advanced stages, chromatophores are present from base to distal end of telson; outer rami of uropods carry no chromatophores and inner rami contain 3-4 chromatophores in the middle region on medial aspect.
