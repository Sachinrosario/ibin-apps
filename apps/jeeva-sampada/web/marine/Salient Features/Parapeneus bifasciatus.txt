SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body not elongate (for a mulled), the depth 2.8 to 3.2 times in standard length. Chin with 2 slender barbells which usually do not reach rear margin of preopercle, their length 1.6 to 1.9 times in head, Snout short, 1.8 to 2.4 times in head; lips thick and fleshy; mouth small, the maxilla reaching about ½ to 2/3 distance to front edge of eye; a single row of wall-spaced stout conical teeth in jaws; no teeth on roof of mouth; gillrakers on first arch 35 to 41. First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first spine small; the third and fourth longest, contained 1.4 to 1.6 times in head of adults; pectoral fin rays 15 to 17 (usually 16). Lateral line scales 28 to 30; 2 or 3 vertical rows of scales in space between dorsal fins; 8 vertical rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour : 
ground colour varying from yellowish grey to brownish red, the centers of the scales paler, shading to whitish ventrally, with a board saddle-like dark bar centred below anterior part of first dorsal fin, another below posterior part of second dorsal fin, and often a third faint one caudal peduncle; a dark area usually surrounding and extending dorsally from eye, sometimes containing irregular pale bluish lines; second dorsal, anal and caudal fins may have faint banding. The dark saddle-like bars of small individuals much evident; deeper water individuals more red than those from the shallows.
