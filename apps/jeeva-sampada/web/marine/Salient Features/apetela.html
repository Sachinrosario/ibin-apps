Discription :

Trees, upto 30 m tall ,  crown large, branches many. Drooping, bark thin , light-brown, irregularly fisured : buttresses not prominent ;pneumaphores 60-150  cm.long, arising form horizontal roots and corky , forked twice or thrice, associated with anchor roots and nutrition roots.  Leaves 4-10 x 2.3cm., thick, narrowly elliptic oblong, tapering towards apex, attenuate at base.  Flowers 1.5-2 cm across, apetalous, cream-colored , arranged in axillary , 3 –flowered dichasial r 7-flowered cymes from the branch axils ; calyx 4-merous, reflexed; petals absent ; stamens many ; stigma large, umbrella-shaped . Fruit 2.2.5 cm  across , a globose berry sealed on the flatened  calyx-tube .  

 

Notes:

The species is easily identified from the other two species of the genus by its               narrowly elliptic leaves , apetalous flowers, 4-merous calyx and  large umbrella-              shaped stigma.      

 

Salient Features:

Commercial use

        It may be use for timber, fuel wood, and as a fodder for some animals (Aronson 1989:73). [also see Correll and Correll 1982; Mepham and Mepham 1985] Its stem is used for paper pulp, matches, and as poles ; leaves as fodder ; its fruits are used as vegetable.
