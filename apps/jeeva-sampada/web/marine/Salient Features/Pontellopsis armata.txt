SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      

Female: Cephalon broadly rounded anteriorly; separation between cephalon and pedigerous segment I distinct; posterior corners of pedigerous segment V produced into acutely pointed symmetrical lobes; three blue coloured blotches present mid-dorsally on prosomal segments II, III and IV; urosome two-segmented; segments covered with hirsute setae; genital segment with a posteriorly directed spine ahead of genital orifice; the segment with a broad, postero-dorsal swelling on its right side; anal segment symmetrical, distally overlapping caudal rami dorsally; caudal rami symmetrical; 5th leg: Re stout and turned inwards with three minute marginal spines and three apical spines of which middle one is longest; tip of inner apical spine curved; Ri bifid apically.     

Male: Cephalosome resembles that of female; posterior pedigerous segment V margin highly modified, left side produced into an acutely pointed lobe reaching urosome III; right side bluntly rounded bearing a slender, elongated curved organ extending to basal margin of caudal rami; marginal hairs present between this organ and base of urosome insertion; urosome of five segments; genital segment broader than long and sparsely hirsute; urosome III with astout finger-like projection on its right margin carrying a group of small spinnules at tip;left margin of segments not projecting but boardered with small dense short spinules; right antenna I: geniculate,with small denticulations on segment 17 and 18, segment 13 with a stout spine at its distal tip; segment 19-21 with a row of 10-12 radially arranged spines towards its proximal part; 5th leg: Right leg chelate; basipod 2 medially broad, and with two suequal setae; hand squared with short thumb, carrying an elongated seta at its base; finger thinned and slightly bent medially and with two short setae at proximal inner part and one distal seta; a short conical spine present at outer nasal margin of finger; left leg: terminal segment with two outer marginal spines and two subequal spines apically, of which outer one is longest; subterminal segment with a disto-lateral spine; inner margin of terminal segment profusely hirsute. 

Additional  remarks:     Wilson (1950) drew attention to the discrepancies in the figures of this species by Giesbrecht (1892) which had not shown the marginal hairs and the position of the attachment of the flagellar organ on the right posterior margin of the pedigerous segment V correctly. The flagellar organ of the male distinguishes P. armata from the other species.  

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult female: 2.36-2.51 (av. 2.40) mm     
Adult male: 1.90-2.11 (av. 1.98) mm
