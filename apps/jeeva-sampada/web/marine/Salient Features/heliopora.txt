SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive, surface level.  Corallites rounded, shallow, level, about 2 mm in depth.  Diameter 8 to 10 mm.  Intercorallite wall 2-3 thick, a meadian groove present.  Total number of septa 30 to 32, younger calices with 10 to 12 septa.  Large and small septa join the columella.  Primary septa about 2 mm exsert and about 1 mm thick at the wall.  Septal edges dentate, sides granular.  Costae continuous or stopping at the middle of the wall.  Columella trabecular, circular in outline, about 3 mm in diameter.  Pali not visible.  Living coral light chocolate.

The specimen was very common in Minicoy in sixties.  Now rare.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
