SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:             

FEMALE:  Stage IV:  Prosome somewhat rounded. Anterior tooth of bifurcated mandible with 6 small spines and the posterior tooth with 1 small additional spine. Exopod of maxillule elongated and furnished with 8 setae; endopod 1 segmented and carries 6 setae; 1st inner lobe with 7 setae 2 of which are spiniform; 2nd and 3rd inner lobes with 2 and 3 small setae. Maxilla without spine on 5th and 6th lobes. Maxillule elongated and furnished with long setae. Legs1-4 more or less similar in morphology, having two-segmented endopod and exopod. Coxa and basis of 1st leg with 1 seta each; endopod 1and 2 with 1 and 7 setae respectively; exopod 1 with 1 inner seta and 1 outer spine; segment 2 with 4 internal setae, 3 external spines and one long terminal spine. Leg 2 same as the first leg except for the absence of the seta on the basis. Coxa of leg 3 wth a small seta; basis without seta; endopod 1 carries 1 inner seta and endopod 2 with 8 setae; exopod 1 with 1 inner seta and 1 outer spine, segment 2 with 5 inner setae, 3 small outer spines and 1 long terminal spine. Coxa and basis of leg 4 with one seta; endopod segment 1 with 1 and 2 with 7 setae; 1st exopod segment with1 inner seta and 1 outer spine, segment 2 with 5 inner seta, 3 outer spines and 1 long terminal spine. Leg 5 is very small, coxa without seta; basis with a long seta; endopod and exopod with a single segment; endopod with 5 setae, exopod with 3 outer spines, 1 terminal spine and 3 inner setae; urosome 3 segmented. 

Additional  remarks:                  The structure of mandible, maxillule and maxilla agrees well with the characters of adult described by Sewell (1947).  Male not recorded.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                        Stage IV Female: 2.02-2.07 mm.                    K. Saraladevi, 1977
