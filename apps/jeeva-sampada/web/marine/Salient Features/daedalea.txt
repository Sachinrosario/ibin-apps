Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive larger colonies upto 6 cm in greater spread, subhemispherical or spreading.  Surface with meandering valleys.  They range form monostomodaeal fully circumscribed calices to sinuous valleys upto 12 cm long.  Width of valleys 5 to 7 mm, depth 4 to 6 mm.  Collines perforated at the upper parts thicker towards the basal part sometimes with endothecal deposition.  Septa 12 to 18 per cm length of colline of which 10 to 12 are larger than others and reach the columella.  Septa exsert.  Exsert ends continuous over the collines, narrower at first, getting broader towards columella.  Septa edges with 4-7 teeth, the last one vertical simulating a palus.  Septal sides granular.  Columella trabecular, sometimes broadened at the ends of the valleys.  Columella centres not distinct.  Living coral yellowish-brown.  Polyps are found partly expanded during sunny days.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3.  Building block.  Curio

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
