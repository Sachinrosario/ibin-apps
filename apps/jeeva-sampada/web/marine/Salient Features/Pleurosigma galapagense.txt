
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Scarcely sigmoid valves, lanceolate, are tapering from the middle to the sub-acute ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Raphe slightly sigmoid. Transverse striae 18 in 10m and oblique striae 15 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 74.5-140m long: 14-28m broad.
