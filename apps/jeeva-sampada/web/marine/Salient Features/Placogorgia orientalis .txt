SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony irregularly branched. The polyp stands exclusively sideways and alternate in a row. The calyces are short and conical, 1 mm high and their operculum consists of each 2 converging spindles, resting on 4 transversal rows of spindles. The coenenchyma is thin and distributed evenly. The anthocodiae are almost completely retractile.		
		Spicules: The spicules of the calyx are scale-like. The coenenchyma consists of leaf-like, club-like and heavily thorned spindles.

Colour			:	Colony is light brown  in colour and the spicules 
				colourless.
