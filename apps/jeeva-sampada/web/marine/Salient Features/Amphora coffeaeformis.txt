SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules narrowly elliptical, ends truncate. Connecting zone with 10-16 longitudinal ornaments of fine striae in 10µ.

Diagnostic characteristics: Raphe approximated to the ventral margin. Striae slightly radiate on the dorsal side.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 20-46µ, width: 6-8m.

Biochemical aspects: Show heterotrophic dark growth on glucose and acetate. Phototroph requiring vitamin Thiamin for growth. Escherischia coli type.
