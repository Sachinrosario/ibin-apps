SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:           
Anterior border of carapace rounded. Eye plates separate and quadrate with few distolateral ommatidia. Mouth parts and thoracic limbs as for the genus. Eighth thoracic endopod much longer. Pleopods biramous, increasing in size posteriorly. Telson short, linguiform, whole border except very near base armed with spines (number of spines increasing with size of animal), median spine flanked by pair of short spines and lateral group of three spines, inner pair shortest, uropods longer than telson, protopod distally trifid and armed with row of closely packed spines on inner margin; exopod with thirty two spines on outer border of basal segment. Female unknown.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Adult male: 6-8.1 mm
