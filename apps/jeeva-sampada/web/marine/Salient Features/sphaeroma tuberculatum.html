Ref No: Purushotham & Rao
Diagnostic characteristics:
Body with transverse ribs on all the thoracic segments; lateral sides of epistome parallel;
Pleon with two prominent round submedian tubercles on the dorsal surface; convex anterior part of telson with two submedian longitudinal rows of four tubercles and two lateral longitudinal rows of tubercles.


Feeding habitat: Browsing on wood
Main food: primary film and algal growth on wood surface.

Additional remarks:
Few specimens of Sphaerpma tuberculatum were collected from George (1963) from Tuticorin and there is no record of this species subsequently .No information is available on its biology and ecology.
