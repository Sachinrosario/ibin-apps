SALIENT FEATURES                                                        Ref. No. 18

 

Morphological:

 

Body oblong, strongly compressed, slightly deeper than length of head,

upper and lower profiles similar. Mouth large and oblique, with prominent

lower jaw; a pair of sharp canine teeth at front of each jaw. Two dorsal fins,

the first with 7 to 8 spines. Caudal fin forked. Scales moderate sized,

cycloid, very easily shed.                        Ref.No.18

 

Diagnostic characters:

 

Body oblong and strongly compressed. Head large, about 2.5 times in standard length. Mouth rather large and oblique with prominent lower jaw. Teeth small in jaws, a pair of small sharp canine teeth in front of each jaw. Two dorsal fins. The first dorsal with 7-8 spines.  Anal fin with three spines and with 25-28 soft rays, its base longer than that of the second dorsal fin. Pectoral fin long and pointed. Pelvic fin just below the pectoral fin base. Caudal fin fortified. Scales medium typed, cycloid, very easily shed.

 

Colour: Body silvery gray, with blue iridescence  above and silvery white below; a dusky spot on upper part of operculum. Fins pale yellow, caudal fin sometimes with dusky margins.                                          Ref.No.18

 

 

Sex attributes: No sexual dimorphism

Main food:                                                   Ref.No. 1,2,5,6,12,13,19

Crustaceans and teleosts are the chief constituents of food. Acetes indicus  was the important item among crustaceans and Stolephorus spp. Among fishes.

Teleostean fishes especially anchovies, among crustaceans Acetes spp.  

Acetes occurred in 72% of the juveniles, and  in 28% of adults. With

increase in age the fish becomes piscivorous.

 

        The following items were observed as food at Waltair                Ref.No. 1

Comments:

 

At Visakhapatnam - The age of fish having a modal size of 80-90mm could  

be regarded as 6 months old and at the end of one year may attain a size of  

160 to 180mm.                                                                 Ref.No.1

                                                                         

At Karwar - Attains 90 mm in six months.  The juveniles with a modal size  range of 65 to 95 mm show a growth rate of 15 mm per month, till they attain a size about 125 mm. Thereafter the fish shows a lesser growth rate of 6.9 mm per month.                                                                                                                                                                        Ref.No.16

 

At Karwar, the fish has been found to attain a size of 150mm total length at the end of first year, and 270 mm by 2nd year. Fish in the 3rd year of life are few in the commercial catches, the fishery being mainly based on 0 and 1+ year old fishes.                                                                                                                                                                        Ref.No.13
