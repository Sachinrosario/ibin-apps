SALIENT FEATURES		

Description		: Colony branching is bushy and not in one plane. Several stems can shoot off from one common base. The end of the branches are swollen or club-shaped.  The polyps are found on all sides are retractile and have no calyces. The axis is thick at the base and thin at the branches. 

Spicules: The spicules of the calyx are small, warted and club-shaped. 
 
Colour	: The colony is dirty grayish white with light brownish spots and axis dark brown in colour.
