SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with more or less slightly convex valves, depressed in the middle. Cells bound in chains.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valves with a crown of spines. In the center of each valve, a spine-like pore canal present. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 14-39m.
