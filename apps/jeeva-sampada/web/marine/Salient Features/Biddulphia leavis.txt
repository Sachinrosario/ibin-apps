SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells in chains or solitary. Valves sub circular. Valve surface almost flat and covered with fine puncta arranged in radiating or slightly curved surface.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve mantle deep, occupying 1/3 the prevalvar length. Valve surface near the margin furnished with numerous spinulae.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter of valve 40µ, 80µ, prevalvar axis 60µ-180µ.
