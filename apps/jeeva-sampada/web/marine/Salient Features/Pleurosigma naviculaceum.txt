SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Sigmoid naviculoid valves, rostrate at the ends not very evident. Central nodule large, rounded striae in the middle of the valve evident and curved.

Diagnostic characteristics: Oblique/ transversal striae 18-20/ 14-16 in 10m, finer at the ends. Oblique striae crossed at an angle of about 600.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 78-176m, width 13-28m.
