SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:     
Rostrum devoid of teeth; a small tubercle-like projection present at its base; carapace with pterygostomian, supraorbital and branchiostegal spines; pereopod 3 developed (zoea IV); rostrum develops dorsal teeth in later zoeae(zoea VII),rostrum bears 2 dorsal teeth; the paired dorsolateral spines on the 5th abdominal segment is absent in this species,throughout the zoeal stages; uniramous buds of pleopods developed; uropods with exopod and endopods; exopod with marginal, distal spine; telson tapers posteriorly, convex at distal region, bearing 3 pairs of lateral and 5 pairs of terminal spines.

Diagnostic characteristics: The rostrum reduces its length in advanced stages unlike other species; the paired dorsolateral spines on abdominal somite 5 never present. Details on   the post larvae are not available as the rearing experiment stopped with Zoea VII.

Feed on planktonic organisms in Pelagic zone 
Main food: Artemia nauplii,living and dead  organic matters; detritus with  sand and mud.
Feeding type: Carnivorous, cannibalism shown.
Additonal remarks: Rearing experiment failed to produce larvae after Zoea VII.

Size and age: 
Zoea I-VII: length 2.62mm to 5.26mm.

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            
Eggs: Cariid prawns carry the eggs with the females and not shed into the sea. They hatch out at an advanced (zoeal) stage.

LARVAL STAGES:Zoea I: length 2.62mmRostrum slender,pointed without tooth; carapace smooth, anterolateral angles produced to form pterygostomian spines; eyes large and sessile; pereopods 1 and 2 present as biramous buds; abdomen 6-segmented, telson not demarcated from last abdominal segment; broad, concave posteriorly, bearing 7 spines on each side,the 2 pairs of outer spines plumose only on inner side and others both sides.Zoea II: length 2.46mmRostrum short of eyes,a small papilla develops anteriorly on the carapace; supraorbital and pterygostomian spines present; eyes stalked; legs 1 and 2 developed; telson with 8+8 spines.Zoea III: length 2.95mmBiramous buds of P3 developed; telson separated from the last abdominal segment; uropods developed; telson bears 8 pairs of spines, the innermost and outermost on either side non plumose.Zoea IV: length 3.19mmRostrum devoid of tooth; short,small tubercle persists; carapace with supraorbital, pterygostomian and branchiostegal spines; pereopod 3 developed; uropods with exopods and endopods; telson narrower, bearing 1 lateral and 5 terminal spines each, inner and outer 2 spines non plumose.Zoea V: length 3.21mm.Rostrum very short; pereopod 4 appears, 5 well developed and uniramous; telson rectangular with 3 lateral and 5 terminal spines,of the 5, the inner and outer non plumose; lateral spines non plumose.Zoea VI: length 3.83mm.Rostrum shortened to become triangular, with a dorsal tooth; papilla exists on  carapace; all pereopods developed; P5 uniramous; telson not changed.Zoea VII: length 5.26mmRostrum with 2 dorsal teeth; uniramous buds of pleopods developed; telson tapers towards the posterior end, convex at distal end with 3 pairs of lateral and 5 pairs of terminal spines.
