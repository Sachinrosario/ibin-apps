SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony strictly flabellate and reticulate, base lacking. The height of the specimen is 26.5 cm and the spread is about 19 cm. The main stem keeps its identity throughout as rudely geniculate course, is round in section and has a diameter near base if 7.5 cm. From this main stem arise a few larger branches roughly alternating, and many smaller twigs. These anastomose very extensively, forming a close network the meshes of which vary greatly in size. The smaller twigs, forming the mesh, are round in section and have a rather uniform diameter of about 1.5 mm. The network extends clear to the periphery of the colony and the tips pf t he free twigs are not appreciably enlarged at the ends. The calyces are small and re distributed on all sides of the branches, but appear to be absent from the main stem and proximal parts of the larger branches. They are with evenly distributed, averaging about 1 mm from mouth to mouth.
		
		The individual calyces are very low dome-shaped verrucae, a typical one measuring about 0.7 mm in diameter and showing eight distinct marginal lobes. The calyx walls are filled with minute round or oval spicules. When the polyp is entirely retracted the calyx is completely included. The polyps are minute, disk-shaped when retracted, and have their infolded tentacles armed with a dorsal series of longitudinal disposed spindles. A cross section of branch shows  a moderate coenenchyma and an axis composed of agglutinated spindles with smooth surfaces and a horny investment which coalesces freely with others. Water-vascular canals do not penetrate the axis, these being confined to a circlet in the coenenchyma.
		Spicules: There are two sharply distinguished forms (1) Long often bent spindles with smooth surfaces and horny investment which coalesces to form the axis and (2) Spindles varying from nearly round or oval forms to the typical terete spindle, and having their surfaces ornamented by regular verrucae in well defined whorls. A few double wheels are also seen. 

Colour		:	The entire colony is a dull grayish brown.
