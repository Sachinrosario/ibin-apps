Salient features:
Morphological:
	
	Body smooth, regularly widering to the fifth peraeon segment. Head very slightly longer than broad, about two thirds as long as the first peraeon segment; rostrum small, antero-lateral corners produced; eyes well developed. Peraeon segment one narrowest , two to four subequal in length, seventh shortest. Pleon short, as long as sixth peraeon segment, sutures visible, last segment produced at its postero-median part and with a faint incision.
	First antenna shorter than second, penduncle three jointed, first segment large, about twice as long as broad, rest of the appendage sharply bent outwards; flagellum five jointed. First antenna of male longer than second, penduncle three jointed, flagellum eight jointed and stouter than the penduncle; each segment carrying a bunch of long hairs. Second antenna similar in both sexes, second segment of penduncle large, rest of the appendage bent outwards; flagellum two segmented in female and three segmented iin male, segmentation indistinct.
	Maxilliped four jointed, second segment stout, fourth minute and clearly marked off from the third, with several apical setae. First maxilla long and slender, distally serrated serrations directed backwards. Mandible with incisor process long, palp well developed, segments one and three equal, second large, third carrying a comb of setae.
	First peraeopod with second and third segments almost equal in length, fourth dorsally produced, sixth roughly oval, with a prominent basal expansion overhanging the tip of the roughly triangular fifth segment, seventh segment long and falcate, reaching the tip of fifth segment, unguis very short; male with the palm of sixth segment more densely setose. Peraeopods two and three subequal, segments two and three elongated, fourth small, fifth slightly under riding sixth, perfectly triangular, sixth segment elongate ovate, with eight spines on the palm, seventh segment slender and stopping short of the fifth when closed. Posterior peraeopods slender and long, fifth segment long and not underriding sixth, sixth elongate cylindrical, with two basal and one apical spine on lower border, seventh segment shorter than sixth.
	Endopod of uropod as long as telson, the free segment slightly broader than long and its dorsal side setose; exopod oval and slightly shorter than the endopod, margin crenulate.
	Body white, with branched chromatophores on the dorsal side of head and peraeon segments. Peraeon segments with a dark transverse line situated anteriorly in the early segments and becoming successively more and more posterior on the succeeding segments and on the last segment close to the posterior border; male with a medio-dorsal longitudinal line.
	  
Additional remarks:

	Sexual dimorphism in Paranthura plumose is very pronounced especially in the antenna first two peraeopods and in the telson. The first antenna is very prominently hairy and is quite different from that of any other species so far recorded.
	Holotype female and allotype male are in the Indian Museum, Kolkatta.
