SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small to medium sized obovate cell that is broadest postmedian. Periflagellar area with eight plates; anterior flange attached to plate. Each valve with about 50-80 marginal pores and about 60-100 evenly spaced pores on the valve surface.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Both valves are anteriorly indented, but the right valve has a shallow V-shaped or triangular excavation with the protruding flange.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Biochemical aspects: Two naturally occurring ester derivatives named diol esters were isolated.
