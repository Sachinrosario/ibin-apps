Salient features :
Morphological :
Female :

Body perfectly oval, slightly longer than broad, segments symmetrical except for the coxal plates. Eyes absent. Cephalon roughly oval, very slightly broader than long, anterior border rounded and the posterior
convex at the middle. Frontal lamina half as long as the cephalon, anterior border irregularly crenate with a
median incision, extending beyond the lateral margins of the cephalon. Posterior lamina with two digitate lateral
processes. All the thoracic segments distinct. First five pairs of ovarian bosses distinct; coxal plates very .
well developed on one side, first pair overlapping the frontal lamina and the cephalon and the succeeding ones overlapping. First two on the deformed side well developed and the rest, except the last two, reduced to minute processes. Pleon six jointed, segments one to five well developed and almost symmetrical, with large lateral plates. Sixth segment minute and with lateral plates; the lateral plates only sparsely tuberculated. Ventral surface of pleon wrinkled. Five pairs of biramous pleopods present, rami subequal and distally pointed, surface tuberculated.
Uropods uniramous, tubercular and similar to the pleopods but shorter and more slender.
First antenna three jointed, segments one and two very large, third minute. Second antenna five jointed, segments one and two large, three to five slender. Maxiilliped with a large apically curved digitiform palp. Posterior lobe triangular, with a blunt apex.. First oostegite with a large apically rounded posterior prolongation, inner crest moderately high and cut into irregularly rounded lobes but without digitiform processes as in Epipenaeon and Apopenaeon.
Basis of peraeopods with a large scaly boss, more prominent on the posterior legs. Segments four to six
of each leg with their lower border serrated and the lower part covered with scales. Propodus distally bulged, forming a prominent lobe against which the dactylus closes.
,
Male : Body slightly more than twice as long as broad, without any pigmentation. Eyes present. Cephalon roughly oval, with the antero-median part concave. Thoracic segments subequal in length and breadth, segments deeply incised. Abdomen slightly broader than long, with the antero-lateral corners somewhat pronounced.
First antenna three and the second four jointed, both with tufts of setae. First peraeopod with spines and setae on the inferior border of segments four to six, dactylus reaching the tip of the fifth segment and with a row of spines on its inferior margin.
In one specimen the postero-lateral borders of the abdomen is straight and without the sinuosity below the lateral corners.

Additional Remarks

Despite its distinct characters like possession of larger, rounded lateral plates of the pleon, strongly frilled ventral surface of the pleon, uropods not being apically acuminate, anterior border of the cephalon of male having a distinct concavity and with pleural lamellae rounded and entire which separate his specimens from other closely related species of the genus Parapenaeon, Pillai 1954, 1958 did not give it a specific name.
