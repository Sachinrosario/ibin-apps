SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca conical with a small knob at apical end, hypotheca almost hemispherical in outline in dorsal and ventral view. Cells slightly compressed dorso ventrally. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle circular, excavated, no displacement of ends. Sulcus wide, winged, does not reach antapical end.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
