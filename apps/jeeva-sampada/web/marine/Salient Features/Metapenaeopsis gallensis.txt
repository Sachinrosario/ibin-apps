 SALIENT FEATURES:								Ref No:114

Morphological:   Rostrum straight, reaching middle of 2nd antennular segment in female and near 
to end of basal segment in male, armed with 7 dorsal teeth; carapace with well defined antennal, hapatic and pterygostomian spines, no stridulating organ, entirely tomentose; 3rd to 6th abdominal somites strongly carinated mid-dorsally, carina on 3rd somite deeply sulcate, postero-median margin of 4th and 5th somites excised, that of 6th with a prominent spine; telson longer than 6th abdominal somite, not quite reaching tips of uropods, armed with 3 pairs of movable spines and a pair of fixed spines. Distomedian lobule of petasma broad distally and shorter than distoventral flap. Thelycal plate broader than long with rounded anterolateral corners; a pair of well developed auriculate platelets standing up from sternal surface behind thelycal plates, anterior edge of anterior sternal plate between 5th pair of pereopods straight with a broad triangular spine at antero-lateral corners. Coxa of 4th pereopods small, rounded and with a brush of setae facing platelets.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum armed with 7 dorsal teeth, carapace entirely tomentose and 
without stridulating ridges, carina on 3rd abdominal somite deeply sulcate, distomedian lobule of petasma broad distally and shorter than distoventral flap, anterior edge of anterior sternal plate between 5th pair of pereopods straight with broad triangular spine at antero-lateral corners.

      Colour: Body covered with reddish brown mottlings.


Size and age:
   Maximum length(cm)(male/female/unsexed): Male: 5.0, Female: 6.5		   Ref. No:114
