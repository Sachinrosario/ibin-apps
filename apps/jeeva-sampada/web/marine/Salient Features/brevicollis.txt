Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum corymbose with an expanding base.  Large colonies 30 to 40 cm in spread, 20 to 30 cm in height.  Main branches 1 to 2 cm thick, subdividing.  Peripheral branches prostrate with rare fusion among themselves.  Ultimate branches 3 to 6 cm long, 7 to 10 mm thick, apices not tapering, often with a few proliferations.  Axial corallites 2 to 2.5 mm in diameter, rarely upto 3 mm, 1 to 1.5 mm exsert.  Wall moderately thickened, porous reticulate; opening rounded, small.  Septa in two cycles, the primaries meet at the centre of the fossa.  The radial corallites show wide variation in size and form in different specimens.  They are scale-like or nariform ascending to spreading, lips thin or highly thickened, mostly labellate and very closely arranged.  Radials 1 to 2 mm in length and width in different specimens.  When the lips are thickened the outer wall spreads to right angles.  The nariform ones often bears one or two buds.  At the lower parts of the branchlets and on the main divisions majority of the radials are immersed.  Wall striated and echinulate, texture porous in young, compact in old ones.  Surface coenenchyme striated and echinulate.  Living coral dull green to bluish with pink axials which makes the coral very conspicuous even from a fair distance in the field.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
