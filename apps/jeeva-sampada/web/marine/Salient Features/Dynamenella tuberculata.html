Salient features:
Morphological:
Female.
Body elongate oblong, narrowing towards both Cephalon longer than first peraeon segment. First
segment equal to seventh in length, others sub- equal. Pleon slightly longer than seventh peraeon segment. .Telson broader than long, with two dorsal bulgings, devoid of tubercles. Apical foramen semi- circular and very shallow.

Mouth parts not metamorphosed. Peraeopods without bushy hairs. Uropods short, not reaching beyond telsonic
apex, rami narrower than in the male, borders setose, exopod smaller than endopod. Brood pouch internal and brood lamellae overlapping.

Body white, with a greyish tint due to the presence of dark chromatophores, arranged in sub marginal posterior rows on peraeon and pleon segments.

Male:
Body elongate oblong and parallel sided. Cephalon short, shorter than first peraeon segment. Peraeon segments one and two equal, subequal to seventh,segments three to six subequal. Coxal plates firmly united with peraeon segments, posteriorly rounded, with short hairs along the border. Seventh peraeon segmentwith a sharp median incision on its posterior border and a pair of submedian tubercles in the middle of the dorsal side. Pleon almost as long as first peraeon segment, lateral parts produced, backwards, posterior border with an incision similar to that on seventh peraeon segment and also a similar pair of submedian tubercles; the lateral pleon sutures are clearly visible. Telson a
broad based triangle, dorsal side with two longitudinally elongated bulgings with a number of tubercles and separated by a median groove, the tubercles at the periphery of the bulgings are larger and arranged in definite rows. Telsonic slit narrow but deep, anteriorly widening into a perfectly semicircular transverse foramen with hairy margin, median lobe absent. The distal part of the foramen is wide since the apices of the telson diverge.

First antenna with first segment of peduncle large, third slender and long, flagellum eight to ten jointed and with long olfactory setae; second antenna with fifth peduncular segment longest, flagellum thirteen to fifteen jointed. Epistome longer than broad, anterior part broad, apex rounded, arms long and narrow, apically slightly curved outwards and enclosing more than half the upper lip. Incisor part of mandible bidentate, accessory plate two to three dentate, spine row consisting of three to four stiff pectinate spines, molar large and strongly toothed; second and third segments of palp with a comb of pectinate setae. Outer lobe or first maxilla with four large blunt spines and four sharp bent barbed spines, inner lobe with four barbed setae and a spinule. Inner lobe or second maxilla with seven to eight large setae, innermost longest, middle ones with very long barbs, outer two large, with two to three long barbs; middle lobe
with four stout spines and outer lobe with three spines. Maxilliped normal, second and third segments well lobed.

Anterior peraeopods stout, with a thick matting of hairs on inner border of segments three to six, each with a large apical spine, outer border of segments four to six spiny. Posterior peraeopods slender and long, with several seta.e on superior border of segments two and three. Malle appendix on seventh segment consisting of a pair of long slender processes fused at the base. Second pleopod with three hooks on peduncle, male stylet slightly longer than endopod, proximally broad and apically acute. Exopod of third pleopod single jointed. Outer border of rami of fourth pleopod with a few setae, endopod without apical lobe or setae. Exopod of fifth pleopod with three thickened spiny areas. Uropods clearly overreaching the apex telson, rami subsimilar setae, rami spreading out like a fan when the animal swims.
Body white, with slightly branched chromatophores forming distinct patterns on the peraeon.

Additional remarks:
Dynamenella tuberculate very closely resembles Dynamenella quilonensis Pillai. However, the presence of two sub-median tubercles on the deorsal side of peraeon segment seven and on the pleon clearly separates this from D.Quilonensis.
