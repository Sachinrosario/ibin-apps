Additional remarks:  ACCORDING TO PILLAI (1965) ALL SPECIS INCLUDED UNDER THE GENES CASSIDINA SHOULD COME UNDER CASSIDINIDEA, AS THE DESCRIPTION OF THE LATTER GENUS IS BASED ON CASSIDINA OVALIS(SAY) SUBSEQUENTLY, SPECIES UNDER CASSIDINIDEA WERE TRANSFERRED TO THE GENUS DIES AS RECTANCULAR SHAPE OF EPEISTOME, OBSERVED IN THEM, IS CONSIDERED A UNIQUE GENERIC CHARACTER OF THE LATTER . ACCORDINGLY, PILLAI(1965) REFERRED TO CHILTON'S C.PULCHRA A DIES PULCHARA(ACTUALLY REFERRED AS DIES PULCHE-PROBABLY A PRINTNGMISTAKE) HOWEVER, CHILTOR(1924) CLEARY STATES THAT IN HIS SPECIMENS EPITOME IS TRIANGULAR. HENCE, AS STATED BY PILLAI(1965),C.PULCHIRA SHOULD NOT BE INCLUDED UNDER DIES. MOREOVER, SPECIMENTS OF THE GENUS DIES ARE SMALL HARDILY EXCEEDING 3 to 4 mm, WHEREAS CHILTOR(1924) GIVE THE LENGTH OF C. PULCHRA AS 20 mm (PRINTERE'S MISTAKE, NOT KNOWN). PROBABLY A REEXAMINATION OF C PULCHRA IS NECESSARY TO ASCERTAIN ITS POSITION.
