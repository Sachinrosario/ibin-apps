SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1

Body oblong and somewhat compressed. . Snout projecting, mouth moderately large, with small teeth; upper lip absent, lower lip well developed; eyes large (eye diameter 3.0 to 3.8  times in head length), with adipose tissue. Pectoral fins in two parts, upper part with almost all rays branched, lower part with 6 free filamentous rays, of which the upper 2 are the longest, reaching to tip of pelvic fin; caudal fin forked with lobes equal. Scales small ctenoid (rough to touch).
	
Colour : 

golden olive  above , silvery below; fins yellowish with black spots; inner side of gill cover pigmented with black; a large blotch at beginning of lateral line.
