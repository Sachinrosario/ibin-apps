SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: A snipe with slightly slender and longer bill.



Diagnostic characteristics: Obliterated colour. Two long horizontal stripes on the upper scapular. Opens up tail during flight.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Micro-invertebrates are obtained by probing into soft mud with the slender and long bill.

Main Food: Worms and insects.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Nesting season in Kashmir and Himalayas in May/June. Eggs, yellowish stone colour.
