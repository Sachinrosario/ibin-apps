Eggs and larvae: Ref . No.: Characteristics:
The body is thin, transparent and flattened with a dagger shaped caudal portion. Eyes are symmetrical and black. The lower jaw is prominent and projects beyond the upper. The intestinal loop is circular and placed in the middle of the body cavity. Two filamentous rays at the anterior end of the dorsal fin fold, supported by the first interneural spine, whose distal ends are split. Dark brown pigment patches are seen on the auditory vesicles, at the roof of the skull above the optic lobes of the brain and on the dorsal border of the swim bladder. There are 9 patches of similar pigments along the dorsal body wall ; of the 9 patches, the posterior most is the biggest and seems to be characteristic of the group. Black pigment spots are present on almost all the caudal myotomes as also on the ventral aspect of the abdomen. There are 110-119 dorsal fin rays and 85-98 anal fin rays.
