SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Colony light brownish in colour with less calcified, biserially arranged zooecia. Division dichotomous and branching of type 3 of Harmer 1923 and 1926. Long zooecia with slightly narrowing proximal region. Opesia usually occupy nearly three fourth of the frontal area, but may invade the proximal region also. The distal outer margin provided with two short unjointed spines of which the inner one is smaller, and the distal inner angle with a spinule which may be absent in a few zooecia. Avicularium small, placed at the distal half of the zooecium, length as much as or less than the width of the zooecium, the length of the avicularia never exceeding the width of the zooecia. The avicularian beak down-curved and less in length than the head. No ovicell was noticed. Ancestrula spineless and with distally facing opesia.
Diagnostic characters: The shape of the ancestrular opesia certainly assigns this species to the plumosa group. It resembles Bugula mollis Harmer to a great extent but distinguishes itself by the absence of distinct joints at the base of the branches.
Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: This species is referable to Bugula plumosa (Palls) given by Ryland (1960). However, certain variations are noticed. The colour of the specimens noticed by Ryland was buffish orange but here it is light brown. Ryland's specimens did not possess spines at the distal inner angles whereas in the present form a short unjointed spine is present in a few zooecia. Ryland noticed that the length of the avicularium was less than the width of the zooecium, but in the majority of cases of the present material the length of the avicularia is equal to the width of the zooecia. Presence of additional ancestrular spines is an unusual occurrence (Ryland, 1966).
