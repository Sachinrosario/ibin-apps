Salient Features:
Morphological:

Pleonite 5 dorsomedially with 2 longitudinal carinae of very low elevation which converge posteriorly; with prominent postero-trancsverse ridge bearing 7-10 long setae. Pleotelson with two pairs of antero-medial puncta, posterior pair closer together than anterior pair. Pleonite 5 0.75 times as long as pleotelson. Dorsal surface pleotelson composed of scales bearing central spike. Dorsal row of laterally compressed tubercles extend from lateral crests to posterior margin of pleotelson; some tubercles with row of tiny spikes at apex. Posterior margin of pleotelson fringed with long- sheathed setae, without stout setae or scale spikes.

Anntenna 1 with 4 flagellar articles; second article with about 7 aesthetascs. Flagellum of antenna 2 with 4 articles. Mandibular incisors with rasp and file. Lacinia mobilis of right mandible short, curved,with row of small teeth. Epipod ofd maxilliped broad, oval, 2.5 times as long as wide, reaching past palp articulatrion; epipod lacking simple true setae.

Secondary unguines of preopod 1 bifid. Ventral comb seta on merus of pereopods 6 and 7 and carpus of pereopods 2,3,5,6 and 7. Uropod peduncle with small lateral tubercles; endopod 1.1 times as long as peduncle; endopod with apical row of simple setae extending laterally.

Pleopod 2 with short plumose setae up to .3 times length of exopod. Appendix masculina short, reaching endopod tip, articulatingf distal to midlength of endopod. Endopod of pleopod 5 posterior to exopod, triangular, similar length to exdopod of pleopod 2; peduncle of pleopod 5 woth simple seta laterally.

Additional Characters: Sometimes pereonite 6 to pleonite 4 with transverse row of dorsal tubercles, most prominent on pleonites 3 and 4 with tuberculate row near posterior margins, row become more centrally located on more anterior somites. Exopod of uropod with two lateral tubercles. Body length up to 2.6 mm. Colour in alchohol pale yellow, with dark reticulate pigment.

Diagnostic Characteristics:
Articulation of endopod of pleopod 5 posterior to exopod, Endopod triangular, epipod triangular, Epipode of maxilleped broad and oval; pleotelson with two pairs of tubercles dorsally; claw of exopod of uropod well developed.

Feeding Habit: Bores into wood and eats the sawdust.

Main Food: Wood Cellulose.

Additional Remarks:
The subparallel longitudinal carinae on pleonite 5 are indistinct, and in many cases not detectable. The hind  pair of tubercleson the pleotelson vary in the degree with which they are seperated and in some specimens they are found joined together (Although still distinct tubercles). The apical teeth on the lacinia mobilis of the right mandible are difficult to detect with light microscope and so may have been overlooked by Rao and Ganapathi(1969), who described lacinilia mobilis as pointed, smooth and curved. These authors also overlooked the prescence of a row of modified tubercles on the postrior margin of pleotelson and also the rather prominent tubercles on 6thperaeon segment to 5th pleon segment.

L. Anadamanensis is most closely similar to L.Pfefferi but differs from the latter mainly in the sculpturing on the pleotelson and pleon 5, presence of dorsal rows of tubercles on 6th peraeon segments to 4th pleon segment, comparitively short uropod peduncle comparitively large claw on exopod of uropod, and in lacking a median ridge on the uropod peduncle.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size Recorded 2.6mm.
