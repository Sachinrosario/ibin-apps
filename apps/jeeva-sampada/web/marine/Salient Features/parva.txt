SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteristics:        Body slender, frontal plate small with rounded tip. Cornea much narrower than eyestalk. Antennal scale, shorter than peduncle. Labrum with long frontal spiniform process. Third thoracic endopod extremely elongated, twice the length of fourth, distal part of ischium carrying three strong smooth spines; dactylus extremely reduced. Thoracic endopods four to eight with long slender dactylus. Pleopods without modified setae; pseudobranchial lobes spirally twisted. Telson reaching articulation of exopod, 2 ½ times as long as broad at base, steadily narrowing towards apex; lateral border with 2-3 spines near base and 6-9 spines on distal half; apex broad with three pairs of spines, three small median spines and pair of plumose setae; middle pair of spines twice as long as outer pair. In female lateral border of telson with fewer spines, apex broader, outer and middle pair of apical spines sub-equal. Uropods narrow, endopod with dense row of spines; exopod shorter than endopod with 3-6 marginal spines.

Size and age :

Maximum length  :   Male:  4.4-7 mm;   Female:   4.4-6 mm

Geographical Location:         Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, Andaman Islands, East Indies, Malaysia, Great Barrier Reef, Java, Philippines, Gilbert Islands, South China Sea, Indonesia.
