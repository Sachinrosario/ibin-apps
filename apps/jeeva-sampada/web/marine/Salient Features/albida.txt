SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref.No. 1,9
Morphological:    A fairly large species, with an oblong  body. 
Diagnostic characteristics :snout rounded and projecting  slightly beyond  tip of upper jaw; mouth terminal or slightly inferior, a pair of very small, tapering barbels  on chin.   Teeth differentiated  into large and small, especially in upper jaw, the large ones forming  the outer  series; no canine teeth;  Gillrakers  on  lower limb  of first arch 7 to 9., with several toothed  plates  below. Dorsal fin with 9 to 10 spines, followed by deep notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 23 to 26 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7  soft rays, the second spine  long and strong, up to half of head  length . Pectoral fins long, about  ¾ of head length;  Caudal fin bluntly  rhomboid in adults.  Scales cycloid onfront part of  head, elsewhere   ctenoid  swim bladder carrot – shaped , with about  17  to 19  pairs of   appendages, one posterior  simple,  the rest  arborescent the anterior most sending  some of its  twig lets  into head.
Colour :Back  grey, belly silvery ;faint oblique  lines along scale rows; spinous  part of dorsal  black, upper margin of second  part of dorsal fin dusky.
