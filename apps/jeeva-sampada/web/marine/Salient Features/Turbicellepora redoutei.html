Peristome slightly everted. Sub-oral avicularium single or rarely double, laterally placed at the proximal side of the orifice, often fuse with the peristome. Spatulate large avicularia present. Ovicell globose and perforated. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters: Turbicellepora is the new genus created by Ryland to include species of Schismopora. The history of the genus Schismopora is confusing. Even regarding the genotype, authors differ. Thus, Harmer (1957) has stated that the genotype is Cellepora costata which, however, he has rejected and selected a new species Schismopora redoutei (Aud.).
  The irregular arrangement of the zooecia together with frontal budding make it impossible to state with clarity the nature of arrangement of the zooecia. But it is to be noticed that the irregular arrangement of the zooecia is a character of this genus. The oriented nature of the zooecia at the rim of the colony was noticed here also.
  The sinus in the present specimen was small and rounded. The orifice rather small, and Harmer (1957) states that the "smallest orifices belong to the minute zoaria". In the present material the zoaria were multi-laminar, the dimension of the two colonies had not exceeded 2sq. cm.
