SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Female: Body regularly widening to the fifth peraeon segment. Cephalon triangular and slightly constricted in front of the eyes; eyes small, very well developed. Anterior border of first peraeon segment slightly convex, antero-lateral corners produced into blunt processes. First peraeon segment longest and fifth broadest, seventh segment short and arched. Dorso-median aspect of posterior border of segments one to five slightly bulged, each with a faint longitudinal median carina. Coxal plates large and roughly oblong. Pleon considerably immersed in peraeon, its median part raised to form a broad longitudinal ridge, first two pleon segments narrow, their lateral parts hidden by the seventh peraeon segment. Telson about one and a half times as broad as long, its distal border slightly concave at the middle; a conspicuous dorso-median carina present.
 
First antenna slightly flattened, seven jointed, contiguous at base; second antenna eight jointed, widely separated at base. Apex of maxilliped with three spines; inner distal lobe of second maxilla with five spines, outer lobe with two; cutting edge of mandible truncate, with a sharp tooth, palp long, second segment of palp longest. Basis and ischium of anterior peraeopods large, merus internally expanded; basis of posterior peraeopods expanded and carinate. Uropods as long as telson, rami short, exopod slightly longer than endopod.

Body white, with a dark grey tint due to the presence of closely packed chromatophores, the intensity of the colour decreasing towards the hind end.

A single non-ovigerous female in the present collection shows considerable difference from the ovigerous specimens. Cephalon abruptly constricted in front of the eyes, exactly as figured by Kale (op. cit.). First peraeon segment roughly squarish, with the antero lateral corners forming a truncate lobe. In dorsal view, the coxal plates appear better developed than in ordinary females. Pleon a little more immersed in peraeon and uropods reaching slightly beyond the apex of telson. Exoskeleton comparatively hard.

Male: Cephalon roughly semicircular, with a small median point; eyes small, well developed and wide apart. Antenna not flattened, first three segments of first antenna large, upper border spiny, seven jointed; second antenna also seven jointed and comparatively small. Peraeon parallel sided, segments sub equal. Pleon considerably narrower than peraeon and only slightly immersed; first segment narrow, others sub equal in length and breadth. Telson slightly broader than long. Uropods over reaching the telson, endopod of uropod with minute setae along its border. Basis of posterior peraeopods carinate. First segment of mandibular palp slightly longer than the second.
 
	Body white, with profusely branched chromatophores.

Second larva: Cephalon semicircular, eyes large. First antenna stout, with long sensory setae on the last five segments; second antenna slender. First peraeon segment long, about three times as long as second, its antero-lateral corners acute; seventh segment very small. Pleon as long as peraeon excluding its first segment. Telson rounded, as long as broad. Rami of uropods sub equal, slightly overreaching the apex of telson, margin prominently setose. Anterior peraeopods with one long spine on the fifth segment and two on the sixth segment in addition to several small spines, dactylus with a row of six spines.

	Body white, with profusely branched chromatophores.

Additional remarks: Specimens from Thiruvananthapuram differ slightly from the description given by Hale (1926). The antero-lateral corners of the first peraeon segment do not overreach the eyes and the uropods do not extend beyond the telson.

Size and age: Length of female: 21.0 mm; male: 7.0 mm.
