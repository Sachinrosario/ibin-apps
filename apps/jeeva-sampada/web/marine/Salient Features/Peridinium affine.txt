SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca is conical. Hypotheca is rounded with two diverging winged spines; a third left and at times, one other right.

Diagnostic characteristics: The sulcus is wide, open behind. The sculpture is in the form of punctae and scattered.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length without spines: 44-59µ. Diameter: 46-61µ.
