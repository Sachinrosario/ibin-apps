SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:        


Female:  The lateral angles of the last pedigerous segment are rounded. The 2nd abdominal segment has a pair of knobs on the lateral sides. Those knobs are furnished with many hook – like spinules. The furca is symmetrical.      The anterior antennae, when reflexed, extend beyond the end of the furca by about the last 5 segments.     The inner marginal spine on the 2nd segment exopodite of the 5th foot not reaches to the end of the 3rd segment.     

Male:  The teminal claw of the grasping foot is longer than the thumb, and has a triangulated process on the inner margin.

Size and age: 
Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     Female:  1.8 mm     
Male   :   1.8 mm
