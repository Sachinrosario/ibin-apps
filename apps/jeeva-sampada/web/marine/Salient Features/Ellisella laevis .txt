SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony unbranched, 18 cm in height. The basal part of the stem is devoid of calyces and is 2.2 cm in diameter 5 cm from its base the colony seems to have been forked and one of the branches broken off short. Above this point the stem is bare for 2.6 cm and but 1.2 mm in diameter across the polyferous portion. The calyces are very irregularly but rather closely scattered on all sides of the stem with a tendency toward a more compact arrangement on the sides. The individual calyces are regularly hemispherical or dome-shaped, varying greatly in size. One of the larger one is 1.7 mm in height and 2.3 mm in diameter at its base. They are so completely closed by the strong contraction of the polyps that the openings are seen with difficulty, but appear to be somewhat inclined toward the distal end of the colony. The distal end of the polyp body is filled with a mosaic of spicules.
		Spicules:  The spicules are very densely tuberculated double headed with the girdles very narrow and often obliterated so as to produce on oval form comparative covered with very closely aggregated verrucae. Spindles are very rarely seen. 


Colour	:	The colony is coral red and calyces are dusk pink. Axis and polyps are light yellow in colour.
