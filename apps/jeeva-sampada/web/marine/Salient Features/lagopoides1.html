GRASS, perennial; FLOWERS in globose clusters, dioecious although monoecious populations occur.  Aeluropus lagopoides (L.) Trin. var. lagopoides has widely spaced leaves that are neither strongly distichous nor rigid. This latter quality may provide an opportunity to develop a forage variety.  Aeluropus lagopoides (L.) Trin. var. mesopotamica (Nab.) Bor in Rech. (1964) has closely spaced leaves that are strongly rigid, nerved and distichous    

 

Commercial use It has C4 photosynthesis and has some limited use as a forage due      to the 'stiff' character of the leaf blades. Potential exists to      develop softer  palatable cultivars.   mentions a putative      hybrid between Al littoralis and

     A. lagopoides where two populations were growing mixed on      Dareene Island,Saudi Arabia. The type locality is India, Burmann.  Industries
