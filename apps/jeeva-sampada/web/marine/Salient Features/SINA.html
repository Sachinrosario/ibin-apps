SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 7,8
Morphological: Fairly small species,
Diagnostic characteristics : snout rounded but not projecting , not greatly swollen,
mouth large, a little inferior, the upper jaw extending to below hind margin of pupil and about 2/5 of head length, lower jaw not quite ½ of head length; rostral pores 5, marginal pore 5; mental pores in 3 pairs, the anterior close together in a pit or joined by a groove ;teeth in narrow bands, moderately differentiated into large and small in both jaws, the large ones forming the outer series in upper jaw, the inner posterior series in the lower; enlarged teeth in both jaw widely spaced; no canines; gillrakers slender, and finely toothed , 13 to 15 a lower limb of first arch.. Dorsal fin with 9 or 10 spines, followed by deep notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 26 to 29 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 soft rays, the second spine moderately long, about 1/3 of head length. Pectoral fin moderate , about ¾ of head length. Caudal fin rhomboid. Scales cycloid on head, elsewhere ctenoid; lateral line scales reaching to tip of caudal fin. Gas bladder hammer shaped , with about 12 to 17 pairs of arborescent appendages , the first entering head and sending a palmate branch to front of pectoral arch.
Colour : No distinctive colouration, a steel blue blotch on opercle. Upper 2/3 of first dorsal dark grey.
