SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Bill long, slender and curved at the tremina; wings long, pointed; tail short to medium.



Diagnostic characteristics: Whitish supercilium and crown stripe, dark eye atipe and slightly shorter bill distinguisher whimbrels from curlews.




Sex attributes:


Descriptive characters: Sexes almost alike; sometimes females are smaller and dull coloured.



Meristic characteristics:





Feeding habit: Gregarious, probes into soft mud in tidal flats and mangrove lined creeks.







Main Food: Small crabs (particularly fiddlers) and other.


Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder





Additional remarks: Extralimital
