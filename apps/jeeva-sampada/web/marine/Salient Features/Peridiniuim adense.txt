SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Anterior and posterior of cells rounded, asymmetrical. Girdle (transverse furrow) strongly twisted leftwards, displaced by a width equal to that of furrow, furrow hollowed out. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus (longitudinal furrow) widened out below. Surface smooth.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 42µ; breadth 38µ.
