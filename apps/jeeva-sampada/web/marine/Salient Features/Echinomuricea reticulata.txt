SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony 180-200 mm in length and 110 to 160 mm in breadth. There is abundant anastomosis; the whole colony forms an open network with almost rectangular meshes.  The polyps stand perpendicularly from the stem and branches; and are disposed closely over the whole coenenchyma. The verrucae are cylindrical, truncated terminally, and measure 0.5 mm in height and 1 mm in diameter. The anthocodiae are almost completely retractile
		
		
Spicules: Sharp smooth spines, Tetra-radiate forms, Irregular clubs with numerous short warty arms. 

Colour			:	Colony is light brown  in colour.
