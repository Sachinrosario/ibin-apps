SALIENT FEATURES:             Ref No: 103

 

Morphological:  Carapace smooth; rostrum armed with 5 to 8 teeth on dorsal, and 2 to 4 teeth on

ventral margin; adrostral carina and sulcus extending beyond epigastric tooth; postrostral carina almost reaching posterior margin of carapace, with a distinct median groove; gastro frontal carina absent; antennal carina ending above posterior third of hepatic carina; gastro-orbital carina extending over posterior 2/3 of distance between hepatic spine and orbital margin; hepatic carina sloping anteroventrally; 5th pereopod with exopod; petasma with distomedian projections reaching as far as costae, outer surface of lateral lobes minutely tuberculate; thelycum with lateral plates, their median margins forming tumid lips, anterior process concave, posterior process convex and partly inserted between lateral plates.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum armed with 5-8 teeth on dorsal and 2-4 teeth on ventral

margin, adrostral carina and sulcus extending beyond epigastric tooth, gastrofrontal carina absent, gastro-orbital carina extending over posterior 2/3 of distance between hepatic spine and orbital margin, hepatic carina sloping anteroventrally, 5th pereopod with exopod.

 

Colour: Body pale brown or greenish, carapace with 2 yellow – cream dorsal transverse bands, abdomen with brown grey and pale yellow dorsal transverse

 

Feeding habit:                 Ref. No: 105

   

 Main food:   Small and large crustaceans, polychaetes, fish items and vegetable matter.

         Feeding type:  Carnivorous bottom feeder.

Eggs and larvae:                  Ref. No: 44, 116

 

  Characteristics:   Eggs are spherical, measuring 0.27 to 0.29 mm in diameter.  The embryo after

hatching out passes through 6 nauplius, 3 protozoea and 3 mysis stages before transforming into postlarva. The last mysis stage, which is 4.43 mm in mean total length, has the rostrum extending beyond eye, sometimes with a rudimentary rostral tooth, supra-orbital, hepatic and pterygostomian spines on carapace, two segmented pleopods, and telson with 1 lateral and 7 terminal setae on either side of median cleft. The postlarvae found in brackishwater areas have telson with chromatophores throughout its length and 8 pairs of spines on sides and distal margin, and distal half of inner and outer rami of uropods with numerous chromatophores.
