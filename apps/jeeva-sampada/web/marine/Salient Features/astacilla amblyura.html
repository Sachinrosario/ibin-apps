Morphological :

The surface of body rough with numerous minute tubercles, short furrows and convexities. Cephalon when viewed from above, deeply concave anteriorly, antero-lateral sides produced forwards into large apically narrowd processes, with the large black eyes slightly projecting on the sides of the Cephalon; a pair of large tubercles between the eyes and a similar pair slightly behind separated by a deep traverse furrow also present. First peraeon segment separated from the cephalon and subequal to second and third. Fourth segment very long, almost twice as long as the body in front of it, with two dorsal projections near the posterior margin in addition to the prominent dorso-median hump. Segments five to seven equal to one another, with the dorso-median part high. Pleon completely united with the telson, demarcated by a prominent lateral constriction and a dorsal transverse furroe. Telson dorsally convex, eith two pairs of lateral tubercles, the area in between is squarish and the area beyond a broad based triangle.

First antenna reaching slightly beyond the second segment of second antenna. First segment of peduncle stout and oval, second and third segments narrow. Flagellum as long as second and third peduncular segments combined with five sensory setae. Second antenna as long as the body, fourth peduncular segment with a prominent basal tooth. Flagellum three jointed, with a strong apical spine, first segment with a discontinous row of strong teeth arranged in groups and displaced to one side.

Upper lip bilobed and prominently hairy. Maxilliped typical, with five joted palp. Incisor part of mandible three lobed, first two lobes tridentate and the third bidentate, two separated median setae also present, molar rounded and smooth. Outer lobe of first maxilla with ten spines and inner lobe with three large barbed and feathery setae and a small sharp spine. Inner lobe of second maxilla broad, other lobes comparatively narrow.

Peraeopods of the usual type. Dactylus of first peraeopod with row of spines on the lower border, ending in large eta. Peraeopods two to four without the long distal seta observed by Stebbing, the distal seta is almost similat to the onebelow, but with a slight basal bulge. Peraeopods five to seven stout, thickly hairy, dactylus stout and distallt produced, with a large unguis.
Lateral ramus of the uropod slightly longer than broad, margin hairy, with two small apical setae. Secondary ramus twice as long as broad and with two long apical setae. Peduncle with a row of plumose setae at the outer distal border.

Body uniformly brown in alcohol.
The only character which distinguishes Astacilla amblyura from other species of the genus is the longer second antenna with a tooth at the base of the fourth peduncular segment.
