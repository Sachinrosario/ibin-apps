SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body fairly elongate, sub cylindrical in cross-section;  belly rounded but with a low keel of scutes. Head moderate, about 41/2 times in standard length; gill openings with 2 fleshy outgrowths; gillrakers 33 to 43 on lower limb of first arch. Dorsal fin origin nearer to snout than to caudal fin base; 8 pelvic fin rays;  anal fin origin well behind dorsal fin base. Pre-dorsal scales 
Colour: back blue/green, flanks silvery  with a series of 10  to 20 green/gold spots (turning black in preserved material)
