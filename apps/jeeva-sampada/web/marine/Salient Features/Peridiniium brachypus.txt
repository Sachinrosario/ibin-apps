SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca similar to that of Peridinium elegans, F: granulatum. Hypotheca shows both horns very short, thin, with short spine. 

Diagnostic characteristics: The horns are the continuation of the head-like prominence of the hypotheca, over which ventrally the sulcus with winged margin runs.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 100-130µ; breadth: 97-110µ.
