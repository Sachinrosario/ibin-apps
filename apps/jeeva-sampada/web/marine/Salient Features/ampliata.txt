Descriptive characters:  Corallum foliaceous, the largest colony measured 60 cm in greater spread in the field.  Folia folded with ridges and furrows, between which is perforated.  The calicinal side rises into humby prominences 5 to 6 mm high.  Valleys long and sinuous, width 1.5 to 2 mm.  Collines acute or rounded average 2 mm thick, depth of valleys about 2-3 mm.  Septa continuous over the collines, slightly exsert, edges dentate.  There are 16-18 septa per cm length of colline of which 10 to 12 are larger than the rest.  Septal edges dentate, the last tooth the largest; septal sides granular.  Major septa reach to the centre of the valley.  Columella continuous, lamellate, centre well differentiated.  Coastae visible at the non-calicinal side.  Coastae spiny.

Living coral yellwish white.  Polyps not found expanded during day time.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
