Salient Features:

Morphological:
Body oblong, dorsal surface glazed like porcelain, with a closely grained appearance when observed under the microscope. Cephalon short, eyes dark brown, situated on
large lobes deeply immersed in first peraeon segment, with a short process at the base of each eye and a concave impressed line on the first peraeon segment connecting the
two processes. First peraeon segment longest, its postero-- lateral parts perfectly rounded; segments two to five subequal, sixth and seventh slightly longer. Coxal plates completely fused with peraeon segments, anterior ones pointed, last two rounded. Pleon short, with two lateral sutures. Telson triangular, apex drawn out into a long narrow tube; lateral parts expanded and bent downwards to form a tube opening by a crescentic aperture on the ventral side of the apex of the telson. Dorsal side of of hairs or tubercles.
Episome somewhat semicircular, apex forming broad based triangle; arms slender, enclosing two thirds the upper lip. First antenna stout, peduncle large, flage-
llum seven jointed; second antenna with last two segments of peduncle subequal, flagellum nine jointed. Incisor , part of mandible produced, three lobed; molar small,with three to four inferior setae, spine row consisting or three week spines; third segment of palp with a row of setae. Distal part of maxilliped with three teeth and several setae, segments of palp prominently lobed. Outer lobe of first maxilla with nine spines, of which five are large and blunt and the rest pointed and barbed; inner lobe with four setae and a spine. Inner lobe of second
maxilla with eight large strongly pectinate setae, middle lobe with four strong spines, outer with four spines, of three are large and one small.

First peraeopod stout, third segment with two large outer setae, fourth externally produced, with three setae, fourth, fifth and sixth segments each with a lower distal barbed spine; second peraeopod longer but more slender than first; seventh peraeopod long and slender, third segment with three setae, fourth with three and fifth with two setae and a strong barbed spine. Peduncle of third pleopod with three hooks, exopod two jointed; endopod of fourth pleopod with a narrow apical lobe carrying a stiff seta, exopod of fifth two jointed, with two jointed, with two spiny thickened areas.

Uropods lamellar, exopod ablong, endopod longer than exopod and reaching the apex of the telson.

Body white with scattered dark spots.

Additional Remarks:
Cymodocella porcellana differs from all the known species under this genus in the total absence of tubercles on the peraeon and pleon segments.

Size and Age :
Length : 5.0 mm
