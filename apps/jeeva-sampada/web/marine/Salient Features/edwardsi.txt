Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum attached, unifacialm foliaceous, margin entire or folded.  Thickness about 1 cm.  The central corallite visible; with many secondary corallites.  Septa short, radiating from centre to centre.  Septa alternating.  Septal margin with several, small very irregular granules.  Columella spongy or papillose.  Underside of the corallum imperforate.  Numerous thin, low, rounded costae present.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Additional remarks:
This species and genus is reported herein for the first time from Indian region, and is based on a collection by Dr. G. Venkataraman of S.Z.I., Chennai from South Andamans.  This species is similar to Podabacia.


IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
