Diagnostic characteristics:
Carapace produced in front into apically rounded broad based triangular rostrum. Eyes large, prominent, double faceted, dorsal zone smaller than ventral zone. Antennal scale narrow, over reaching antennular peduncle. Third thoracic endopod stout, carpus and propodus distinct, dactylus ending in long claw. Eighth thoracic endopod slender, carpopropodus not divided, dactylus small and ending in long slender seta. Exopod of fourth male pleopod three segmented, third segment ending in two stout spines, inner spine shorter and armed with long barbs in middle, outer spine longer and barbed only on outerside. Telson with swollen base steadily narrowing to distal third, further on sides parellel, distal two third of lateral margins armed with five pairs of short sharp spines increasing in length distalwards; narrowed distal border of telson with two spines.
