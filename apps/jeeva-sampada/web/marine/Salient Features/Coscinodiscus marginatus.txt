SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Almost flat cells, black in colour. Very striking, strongly silicified valves, areolated, large more or less areolae of the same size about 3 in 10m but very near the edge 5 in 10m.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: No central area or rosette. Inner chamber openings clear. Border of valve heavily striated.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 46-135m.
