Diagnostic characteristics:

Male: Cephalon distinct from pedigerous segment I; lateral cephalic hooks present; rostral eye lenses rather small; dorsal eye lenses not well developed; Pedigerous segment V posteriorly produced into a symmetrical short spine; urosome five segmented; urosome III larger than rest. Right antenna I: geniculate; segment 17 produced at its dorsal surface as a low crest terminating in a ashort spine distally; segment 18 with a denticulated plate, extending to three-fourth of its dorsal margin; fusion segment 19-21 with two dorsal toothed plates, each armed with fine spinous teeth; distally, segment with a falcate spine. Leg 5: right leg 5 chelate; hand with two subequal spines and a proximal hood-like projection; finger distally club-shaped and with four spinous setae along inner margin; left leg 5: with short basipod 2; terminal segment elongate, with one spine and two processes distally; an additional serrated spine at about two-thirds of its distance along its outer surface; a tuft of small hairs present along its inner margin; subterminal segment elongate and with a conspicuous spine at outer distal margin.

Additional remarks:
The species has been recorded from coastal and brackish water environments. Its original description was based on single male and partly on a damaged female obtained from Chittagong. Sewell (1932) described Copepodite III and discussed the growth stages in detail.
