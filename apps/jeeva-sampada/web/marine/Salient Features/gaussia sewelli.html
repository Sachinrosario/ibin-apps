Additional remarks:
The first record of the genus Gaussia from the Indian Ocean was that of Sewell (1913). There is an erroneous statement in Wolfenden (1911) that Gaussia was obtained in Gardiners collection from the Indian Ocean. The drawings of Sewell (1932) are clear enough to recognize them as identical with specimens obtained during the present study from the northern areas of the Indian Ocean.
