Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:   
     Cephalon distinct from pedigerous segment 1 and rounded anteriorly, rostrum with two long filaments, slightly developed; pedigerous segments IV and V fused, posterior angles of latter produced asymmetrically into outwardly directed spines, left spine reaching to posterior two-third of the genital segment being longest; an additional spine present on either side of dorsolateral region indicative of fusion between pedigerous segments IV and V. Urosome four-segmented. 
     Genital segment longest, asymmetrical and with lateral swellings; anteriorly at right lateral margin it is produced externally into a recurved peg-like structure. 
    Leg 5: asymmetrical, with basipod 2 of both legs produced internally, endopod structure of left side asymmetrically bifid, whereas that of right leg is bluntly rounded; exopod 1 with length : width ratio = 3:1 and with coarse inner margin serrations and a distal outer spine; outer margin of the segment beyond the distal spine provided with 4 to 6 spinules; distal exopod segment with three subequal spines, inner distal spine foliaceous, middle one bearing a small spine near its base and a third long spine on outer margin of segment; proximal outer margin of terminal segment with a few scattered setae.

Additional remarks:
    The new species shows apparent affinities to P. clevei, an insular tropical species described from Malay Archipelago than to any other species of this genus. However it can be distinguished from P. clevei by  1) body proportions, 2) asymmetrical posterior corners of pedigerous segment  V, 3) modification of genital segment, 4) the possession of the highly elongated caudal setae, and 5) the nature and ornamentation of the constituting parts of the fifth pair of legs in both sexes.
