
SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum hemispherical, generally 15 to 20 cm in greater spread.  Corallites circular or oval, projecting upto 5 cm, 6 to 10 mm in diameter, 4 – 5 mm deep.  Peritheca vesicular.  Septa range from 30 to 40 in different calices depending on the size.  Adjacent corallites 1 to 4 mm apart.  Septa exsert with arched ends.  Upper two-thirds of the septa narrower than the lower one-third.  Eight to 14 septa reach the columella, others turn towards the sides of the immediate lower cycles.  Edges of septa dentate.  Costae correspond to septa, those of the adjacent corallites meet at the middle of the intercorallite area over a transverse ridge.  Edges of costae with teeth.  Columella formed of the fused ends of major septa.  Upper part of the broader end of the septa simulate a palus.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3.  Building blocks.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
