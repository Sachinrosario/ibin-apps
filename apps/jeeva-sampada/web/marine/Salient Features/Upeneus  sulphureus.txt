SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body moderately elongate, the depth 3.2 to 3.6 times in standard length. Chin with 2 slender barbels which usually reach to or extend beyond rear margin of preoperole, their length 1.2 to 2.0 times in head; maxilla reaching clearly posterior to front edge of eye (on some specimens to below center of eye); villiform teeth in jaws, on vomer and palatines 
( roof of mouth); gillrakers on first arch 26 to 32. First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first spine minute pectoral fin rays 15 or 17 (usually 16); pelvic fins . Lateral line scales 34 to 37; 51 vertical scales rows in space between dorsal fins; 12 or 13 vertical rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.
	Colour : back greenish, mottled with bronze, silvery, white on side with 2 narrow yellow stripes, the uppermost at level of eye most prominent, the lowermost ending in axel of pectoral fin; ventral part of body pale yellow; barbells whitish  to pale yellow; dorsal fins with 3 yellowish brown stripes, the outermost of first dorsal fin notably darker; caudal fin without dark bends.
