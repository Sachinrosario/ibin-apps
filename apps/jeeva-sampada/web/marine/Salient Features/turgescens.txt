Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum entrusting 20 cm in greater spread with a maximum thickness of 10 mm.  Edges very thin, followed by an epitheca.  Calices small, 0.5 to 0.6 mm in diameter, look like deep pin holes with a solid thecal wall.  Septa in two cycles, primaries more enlarged than the secondaries.  Surface of the coenenchyme swells up around the calices forming low ramparts.  Under the lens it revels a reticulum with minute echinulations with coastal striations around the wall.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
