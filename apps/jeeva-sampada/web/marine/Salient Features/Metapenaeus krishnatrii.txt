
SALIENT FEATURES:								Ref No:167

Morphological:    Integument pubescent on dorsum of entire length of carapace and all 
abdominal somites in female, and in hepatic fossa, postocular sulcus and 4th to 6th abdominal somites in male; rostrum almost straight reaching near to distal end of 2nd segment to tip of 3rd segment of antennular peduncle, and armed with 7 to 8 teeth on dorsal margin; postrostral carina reaching 9/10 carapace; adrostral carina ending between epigastric and penultimate tooth, adrostral sulcus extending well behind epigastric tooth; branchiocardiac sulcus distinct, not quite reaching posterior margin of carapace; a minute orbital tooth present; 1st pereopod with a sharp ischial spine about 1/3 size of basial spine. Distomedian lobes with bluntly triangular apices with median margins parallel to each other, barely projecting beyond anterior margin of distolateral projections. Anterior plate of thelycum tongue-like with raised lateral margins parallel; lateral plates flat, bounded posteriorly by two transverse anteroventrally directed protuberances bearing tufts of setae.


Diagnostic characteristics:    A medium sized species in which distomedian lobes of petasma are 
broadly triangular with apices barely projecting beyond anterior margin of distolateral projections; anterior plate of thelycum tongue-like with raised parallel lateral margins, and lateral plates of thelycum flat, bounded posteriorly by two transverse protuberances bearing tufts of setae.
