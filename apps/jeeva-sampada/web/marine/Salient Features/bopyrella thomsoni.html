SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:

Female:

Body elongate ovate and slightly asymmetrical, head completely fused with the first thoracic segment but its postero-lateral borders visible to some extent. All the thoracic segments distinct throughout ; ovarian bosses faintly visible on the first four segments and the epimera are present but poorly developed. Abdomen slightly asymmetrical and completely fused, the lateral and posterior borders forming a continous unbroken border, pleural lamellae totally absent.

Antennae reduced, maxilliped with well developed palp carrying sixteen setae, twelve on the inner and four on the outer side. Anterior margin of first oostegite deeply concave but the finger shaped process of the internal crest not prominent. Eyes are present in the form of very faint dark spots.

Male :

Thoracic segments show slight increase in breadth posterior wards .first antenna is three-segmented and the second two-segmented.

Additional Remarks:

Choprs (1927) observed about 15 setae on the palp of maxilliped, of which eight are arranged along the inner margin, whereras Pillai (1958) sixteen setae on the palp, twelve on the iner side and four on the outer. Again, Chopra 1927 recorded slight traces of the first two pleopods in the male, but according to Pillai 1958, Pleopodes were absent.

Specimens show variations in size and those from Krusadai are much larger (length 14.5 mm) than the Andaman materials (length 10.5 mm). Bonnieri type materials was only 2.5 mm long and Pillai 1958 from quilon 9.7 mm.
