SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elliptical valves with rostrate, capitate apices. Valve surface striate, striae coarse, finely lineate, radiate in the middle and transverse at the extreme ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Opposite the central area, the striae are alternately long and short, 12 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 45-50m, width 15m.
