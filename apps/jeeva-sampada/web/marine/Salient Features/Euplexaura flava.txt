SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branching is in one plane and pinnate. The main stem is flattened. The polyps shoot from low calyces and they are retractile. The axis is cornuculated. 

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are straight and crooked and some are ball-shaped spindles.
 
Colour	:	The colony is grayish black in colour.
