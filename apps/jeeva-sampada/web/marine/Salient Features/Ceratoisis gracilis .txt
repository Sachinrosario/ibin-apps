SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony  unbranched, thin and stiff. The internodes are smooth and hollow averaging 10 mm long. The polyps leave a small longitudinal strip of the stem free, and are biseriallly arranged and turned to one side. They are mostly pointed and diagonal at the distal end and adaxially bent. They are thin at the center and thick at the distal ends measuring 3-5 mm long. The polyps are pointed longitudinally, are strong, needle-like, smooth, 8 lying in 7 rows of 4 mm long. The free ends are tipped; the basal ends are rounded and strongly toothed.
		Spicules: The spicules of the polyps are small, broad, irregular, flat sticks of 0.12 mm long. Flat thorn-like spicules with rounded ends are also found. The tentacles are with small spicules. The bark contains isolated, bigger and smaller, flat-thorned spicules.
 
Colour	:	The colony is white and polyps are reddish brown.
