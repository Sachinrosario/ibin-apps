Salient features:
Morphological:

  Description- The pleon anteriorly is narrower, and the telsonic apex is feebly excavate. Eyes distinct. The first peraeon segment has been ijured, and has been abnormally regenerated, its hind margine strongly arcuate, and medianly sunk into a hole in the 2nd segment. Side-plates ageing with Miers description, and the later description of those of dubia by Nierstrasz.

  Antennae 1 and 2 respectively 7- and 8-jointed. Mandible with palp greatly enlarged, 1st joint transverse, projecting inwards as a rounded lobe, 2nd joint stout but longer than broad, 3rd slender, trete-Maxilliped with 1st joint of palp very broad, 2nd joint inserted subapically and scarcely projecting beyond apical margine of 1st joint (unless forcibly extended).

  Peraeopods 1-3, 2nd joint keeled on anterior (outer)margin inner distal corner produced as a subacute lobe, which is easily pliable like one of the pleopods, and (as preserved) is folded up against the prosterior surface of the joint, 3rd joint stringly keeled on inner margin, dactylus not noticeably geniculate and without any thickening on outer margin (such as is present in indica, periophthalni and dubia).

  Peraeopods 4-7, 2nd joint keeled on anterior outer margin, with a less conspicuous keel on the posterior outer margin (neither keel prominent on peraepod 4, but becoming more prominent on the other peraeopods), inner margin of 3rd joint keeled but not so shrply as in the anterior peraeopods, one minute spinule on inner apex of 4th joint, 2 on 5th, and 3 on inner margin of the 6th joint (all easily overlooked).

  Pleopodes with small notch on outer margin of outer ramus, but the surtre between the two joints barely traceable; no persistent stylet on pleopod 2; inner ramus of pleopod 5 with 2-3 large longitudinal folds or pleats.

   Outer ramus of uropod a little longer than inner ramus, apicallysubacute.

  Colour (as preserved) dull olivaceous or plumbeous; both antennae, mandibular palp, all peraeopod, basal portions of the brood-lamrllae, and the pleopods (especially the first pair) closely speckled with dark grey stellate and dendritic pigment; eyes black.

  Embryos 2.75mm in length, yellowish, with black eyes and a dark stripe on each lateral margin of the peraeon and pleon, and continued on to outer rami of uropods. Rostrum very short, consisted in front of eyes. Telson longer than its basal width, apex rounded, with plumose setae. Inner ramus of uropod oval, apically rounded, extending beyond telsonic apex, outer remus narrow, elongate, twice as long as inner, apex acute with one spine, inner margins of both rami with plumose setae.

  Peraeopod 1, 5th and 6th joints unarmed or with 1-2 very obscure spinules on inner margin of 6th, 3 blunt denticleson inner margin near base of dactylus. Peraeopods 2 and 3, one spinule on inner apex of 5th joint, 3 on inner margin of 6th, 4-5 spiniform denticles, well spaced on dactylus. Peraeopods 4-6 (7 of course not yet developed), one spine on inner apex of 5th joint, 3-4 strong spines on inner margin of 6th, and 4 blunt denticles on dactylus.

Addition remarks:

  Renocila ovata is provisionally kept under the genus Renocila, until such time when more specimens available for comparison to assign it its correct systematic position.
