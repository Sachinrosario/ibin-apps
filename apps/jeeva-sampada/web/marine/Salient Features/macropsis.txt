SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        Rostral process broadly arched, dorsomedian nodules present. Antennal sympod armed with four spines, first and second short, third longest, fourth slightly shorter than third. Thoracic endopods three to seven with four carpopropodal segments. Endopod of eighth limb of male three segmented and longer than basal segment of exopod, second segment short with four long setae, third segment long, in female endopod two segmented and short. Telson narrow with twelve to fifteen long spines, outer pair of distal spines longer than inner armed with blunt barbs. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)    

Adult male: 7.8 mm    
Female      : 8.9 mm.
