SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony  branching in one plane and the thinner and branchlets are turned towards the same side of this plane. Branching is dichotomous. The internodes are 4-5 mm long and often bent. The polyps are absent on the under surface of the stronger branches and sometimes irregularly spread in 1 or 2 and 3 or 4 on a internode measuring 1.6 mm long lie in 8 longitudinal rows of 7 and are rounded in cross section. Sometimes they are also broad oval, with rounded wnds and their surface is wakly warted. The polyp bases are free from warted spicules. The nematozooids are absent.  
		Spicules: The scales of the bark are irregular, thin, and fully smooth with 0.2 mm in length.

Colour	:	The axis brownish yellowish in colour.
