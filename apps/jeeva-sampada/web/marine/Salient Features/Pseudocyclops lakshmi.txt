SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:   
     Female: Prosome oval in dorsal view, cephalosome separate from first pedigerous somite, fourth and fifth pedigerous somites completely separate, posterior corner of fifth pedigerous somite smoothly rounded, reaching to one-third length of genital double-somite. Rostrum pointed, triangular, with a pair of minute sensilla. Urosome four segmented, distal margins of first 2 somites lamellar. Caudal ramus with serrate posterior margin diorso-medially and one bluntly pointed process ventromedially and with one dorsal, 4 terminal and one outer subterminal setae.
     Basis of leg 5 with outer subterminal setae on posterior surface: endopod three segmented, first segment having a small outer spinule, first and second segments with inner terminal setae, third segment having one inner, one outer and two terminal setae; exopod three segmented, third segment with 4 flanged spines and four inner setae. 
    Male:  Urosome 5 segmented, covered with minute prominences as in the female. Caudal ramus with serrate dorsomedial margin and ventromedial blunt process. 
     Leg 5: Right leg:  Coxa and intercoxal sclerite fused; basis separate from coax with one seta on posterior surface; endopod 1 – segmented with small terminal spinules and minute surface spinules usually without terminal seta; exopod 2 segmented first segment with stout flanged terminal spine, second segment with three processes.
     Left leg: Coxa incompletely fused with basis on both surfaces; basis covered with minute surface prominences. Endopod one segmented with numerous surface prominences and 5 plumose terminal setae. Exopod two segmented.
 
     
Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Female: 0.86-0.95 mm
