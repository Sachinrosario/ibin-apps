SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:             
Immature Female: The specimen is in the 5th copepodite stage. General appearance as in the female. The abdomen consists of 4 segments. The segments and furca are in the proportional lengths as 13:12:12:23:40=100. The furcal rami nearly equal in length to the combined length of the first 3 abdominal segments, and 6 times as long as wide. The 1st antenna exceeds the distal margin of the furca by terminal 3 segments. The 5th leg with 2-jointed exopod and endopod.            

Male: Cephalothorax, 4.32m; urosome, 3.12mm. The cephalothorax ovate in outline. The head has an acute triangular lateral hook on each side. The rostrum consists of an oval base to which 2 stout spines are attached.       

The urosomal segments and furca are in the proportional lengths at 11:16:14:11:14:34=100. The furcal rami are equal to half the length of the urosomal segments taken together, and about 6 times as long as it is wide at the proximal.      

The right 1st antenna reaches back to the posterior margin of the anal segment. The mouth parts and swimming legs resemble that of  L.wolfendini.        

In the 5th pair of legs the 2nd basl segment has, beside 2 strong teeth, 4 small ones on the inner distal margin; according to Sewell these small teeth vary in number from a single to 7; the 2nd  basal of the right leg produced internally and has 9 small teeth on the inner distal edge.
