Description of a specimen form Krusadai Island:  Corallum covered an area of about 50 cm.  The edges expanding without any upright branches.  Branches at the central part of the colony crowded clusters with a maximum height of 27 cm.  Lower parts of the branches dead.  Branches about 1 cm thick at the base repeatedly dividing, coalascent with sharp edges.  A few are thick and obtuse at the tip exactly resembling Pavona venosa var. arbuscula.  Calices display wide range of variation.  Those at the expanding basal parts are polygonal, single or in series of two to three.  Single corallites 2 to 4 mm long, 2 to 3 mm deep with a few smaller ones.  There are 24 to 40 septa in a calyx, septa alternating in size, either continuous over the wall, or those of the opposite sides meeting at the top of the wall.  The calices on the branches are superficial, 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter, with 20 to 24 septa, half of which meet the columella.  On the obtuse tips the calices resemble those at the expanding base and run together with continuous wall.  Septal sides granular, edges entire.  Columella poorly developed, when present styliform.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  The study of the specimen described above shows that P. venosa var. arbuscula Umbgrove from East Indies is only based on obtuse branches of P. divaricata.  Tichoserisobtusata Quelch, 1886 is based on a worn out specimen.  Wells’ (1936) of the type of T. obtusata has many similarities with some portions of the expanding base of the present specimen.  It is very likely that T. obtusata is based on a gragment of P. divaricata.  Crossland (1952) T. obtusata to P. varians but it seems to be more related to P. divaricata than P. varians.  The reference of P. divaricata to P. cactus by some author is errenous.  It is likely that P. divaricata is the same as P. venosa (Ehrenberg) and in this case the first available name is venosa for the species.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.
