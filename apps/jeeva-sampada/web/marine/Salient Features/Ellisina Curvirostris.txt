SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:  Encrusting.  Zooecia very delicate, shallow, in not very distinct longitudinal lines, separated by grooves, the gymnocyst wanting or small.  Frontal membrane oval, 2 small spines, one on each side of the orifice, at the proximal corners of the ovicells in fertile zooecia.  Cryptocyst narrow.  Avicularia at the proximal end of a kenozooecium, the rostrum asymmetrical, considerably curved and denticulate at the frontal edge.  Mandible triangular proximally, acicular distally.  Ovicell hyperstomial, prominent, globose, its surface frosted, usually with a slight mucro in the middle line of the oral arch; not closed by the operculum.

Sex attributes:  Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: The avicularium is at the proximal end of a kenozooecium; or the supposed kenozooecium, the existence of which has been specially emphasized by Hincks (1880), Waters (1898) and Harmer (1928).  Waters has stated that the rosette plates were 8 lateral and 1 distal.
