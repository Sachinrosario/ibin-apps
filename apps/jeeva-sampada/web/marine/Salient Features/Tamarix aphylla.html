SALIENT FEATURES:

Commercial useIt has C3 photosynthesis (Aronson 1989:72). The Seri Indian name 'Hoco hapéc' means 'wood what-is-planted' (cultivated tree)
This tree makes a good shade tree, but is a phraeatophyte in that it consumes a lot of water. The high evapotranspiration rate can substantially cool the air passing through the tree. My wife and I once backed a pickup truck with a camper shell onto the downwind side of a tree to receive the cool air. The summer desert temperature was approximately 110°F and the temperature in the truck was below 80°F (N.P. Yensen per. obs.).
The tree may be propagated by placing cuttings in water until they root (a few days) and then plant directly into the ground It can be used for fire wood, carpernty, furnature and boxes, and as wind breaks for soil stabalization (Lewin <halophyte.ref?a=Lewin&d=1957> and Reibenbach 1957; Aronson <halophyte.ref?a=Aronson&d=1989> 1989:73)
