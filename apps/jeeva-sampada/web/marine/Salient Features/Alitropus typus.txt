SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Male: Body comparatively long and slender, about three times as long as broad. Head broadly triangular, with the anteromedian part slightly produced; eyes large. First peraeon segment narrowest, segments one to four successively increasing in length; anterolateral corners of first segment slightly produced forwards, postero-lateral corners of all segments pointed; peraeon segments five to seven abruptly longer than the fourth, sixth segment longer and broader than all the others. Coxal plates well developed, produced but not exceeding the posterior border of the respective segment, each with a row of outer setae. All the five pleon segments clearly visible, abruptly narrower than the peraeon and somewhat immersed in it; lateral parts of segments one to four drawn out into acute processes; fifth segment narrow. Telson semicircular, distal border perfectly rounded, broadly triangular with a sub truncate apex in very large specimens.

First antenna as long as the peduncle of second, third peduncular segment slender and as long as the first two segments combined, flagellum six jointed, with a stout apical seta. Second antenna reaching slightly beyond the second peraeon segment, flagellum thirteen jointed.

Outer border of of maxilliped hairy, apex carrying three teeth, placed apart from one another. First maxilla with a short apical tooth and two elongated inner spines. Inner lobe of second maxilla with three spines, margin of outer lobe serrated. Second segment of mandibular palp very large, with a comb of stout pectinate setae, third segment comparatively small, with a single apical; seta. Fifth segment of first peraeopod slightly immersed in fourth, sixth cylindrical and not expanded as in Rocinela, dactylus stout and falcate. Posterior peraeopod long and slender, lower border of segments strongly spiny; upper distal angle of segments three to five with a row of stout branched spine and long setae. Peduncle of second pleopod with six hooks, stylet shorter than endopod and with a small apical spine; exopod with a series of spots as observed hy Chilton in Rocinela simplex. Peduncle of uropods much produced, reaching more than two thirds the length of the endopod; endopod apically rounded, with seven spines, exopod narrow and long with seven spines, shorter than endopod.

Body dark grey, with profusely branched chromatophores forming characteristic pattern.

Female: Body broader than that of male, about twice as long as broad. First peraeon segment as long as head; segments five to seven not as long as in male; postero-lateral corners of segments not acute, those of the first segment rounded. Coxal plates comparatively broad, with their apices perfectly rounded. Pleon very short, as broad as peraeon, segments one to four equal in length and breadth. Colour of body same as in the male.

Juvenile: Very much like the female with the following differences. First peraeon segment longest and the seventh shortest; pleon narrower than peraeon and slightly immersed; telson longer than broad, apical margin perfectly rounded. Colour as in the male but paler.

Sex attributes: Development of eggs and embryos take place within the brood pouch and incubation period is about 42 days.	

Main food: Juveniles mainly feed on blood and mucous from the host fishes.

Additional remarks: Sexual dimorphism is pronounced. Though adults lead a free-swimming life, juveniles as soon as released from the marsupium attack fishes immediately and lead a parasitic life on their gills, buccal cavity or opercular region. They suck the blood from the host with the help of the piercing type of mouthparts. Thus they cause immense damage to the fishes. Biocides like Folithion, Hinosan and Malathion are found to be very effective in controlling these parasites even in small dozes that are harmless to fishes. (Nair and Nair, 1986). Anabas testudinius is found to be the most severely attacked hosts while Puntius sarana subnasutes is comparatively less infested. A drastic fall in protein, glycogen and lipid has been observed in the infested fish (Nair et al., 1981).
	
Size and age: Length of male: 13.0mm, Female: 15.0mm.
