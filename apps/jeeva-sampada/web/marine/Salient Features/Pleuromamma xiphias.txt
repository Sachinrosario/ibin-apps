SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 


Diagnostic characteristics: 
     Female: Resembles Pleuromamma abdominalis. Differs clearly in the out line of the head apex, which in front of the rostral base continues into a process bent slightly down ward.
     Male: Prosome apex, like wise with process; the latter, however, is directed forward, not downward. All other features as the preceding species.
Size and age: 
Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
     Female: 3.5-5.87 mm
     Male    : 4.0-6.42mm.
Ref. No.: K.A.Brodskii, 1950
