SALIENT  FEATURES :

Diagnostic characteristics:      Carapace:- Length 1.1 to 1.3mm and 1.1 to 1.5mm in male and female respectively. Elongated shape narrowing anteriorly.     Mandible:-  Toothed edge of coxale with 11 teeth. Distal tooth list with 2 large and about 14 smaller teeth. Proximal tooth list about 20 teeth, 2 or 3 posterior ones are larger.     Copulatory limb has a curved tip  

Additional  remarks:       P. elegens closely resembles to P. discophora. Though not as numerically abundant as many other species, has been reported by most of the previous authors and it is known to have a very wide distribution
