SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony (incomplete), flabellate and reticulate, 12.5 cm long. Main stem to first branch 3 cm long. Second horny node 4.5 mm broad and 4 mm in length. Calcareous internodes 2.1 mm board and 4.5 mm long. The stem forks at the fifth nod. The branches are compressed laterally; branching is partly dichotomous and partly alternate, all branches springing from the horny joints and antomosing through the joining of the ultimate twigs. Calyces are on three sides of the colony, except on the distal twigs. Individual calyces are low domes when the polyps are retractile and truncated cones when the partly expanded. They are rather regularly spaced. The calyx walls and coenenchyma are packed with foliceous clubs or Blattkeulen. The polyps are heavily speculated with a strong collaret of bent tuberculate spicules. Above these ach tentacle base is provided with two spicules, whose ends meet forming an upward directed point. Dorsal surfaces of the tentacles are covered with longitudinal tuberculate spindles.

Colour		:	The entire colony is brick red or light scarlet, polyps yellow and  
     			the axis deep crimson in colour.
