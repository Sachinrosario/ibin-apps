SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Very small cells forming chains.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Chromatophores two, large and disc-shaped.

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 2.5m, Length: 26-29m.

Reproduction: Resting spore in globular with a cylindrical neck.

Main Ref: Hargrover, 1990.
