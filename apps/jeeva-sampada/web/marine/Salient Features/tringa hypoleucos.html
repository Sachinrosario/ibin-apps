SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Slender, fusi form body with horizontal stance. Legs moderately long and greenish in colour.

Diagnostic characteristics: Olive green upper half and white coloured plumage in the lower half. Supercilium passes through eye.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Feeds along the sea shore by picking marine micro-invertebrates.

Main Food: Marine micro molluscs, crustaceans and insects.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Nesting in Kashmir, Garwal and Kumaon in May/June. Eggs yellowish buff blocked with reddish brown.

Size and age: 500
