SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Sides of epitheca more or less concave to convex. Slender sturdy horns and circular girdle. Sulcus with lists supported by spines. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Theca sculpture marked reticulations with spines at the junction of the reticulations. Intercalary striae broad.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 80-84µ; breadth 56µ.
