SALIENT FEATURES :

Morphological : 
Ref No: cookson, 1991

Pleonite 5 dorsomedially with anterior pair of nodes, followed by carinae which converge posteriorly to single node. Pleotelson with anterior dorsomedial punctum followed posteriorly by pair of puncta, each puncta followed posteriorly by carina. Pleonite5 0.5 times as long as pleotelson. Dorsal surface of pleotelson composed of scales, some with posterior row of thin spikes. Dorsal row of tubercles follows entire perimeter of pleotelson; posterior margin with 4 large stout seate between which are long-sheathed setae, lacking scale spikes.
Antenna 1 with 4 flagellar articles; second article with about 5 aesthetascs. flagellum of antenna 2 with 5 articles. Mandibular incisora with rasp and file. Lacinia mobilis of right mandible curved, bifid, with apical teeth, epipod of maxilliped triangular, 2.9 times as long as wide, not reaching palp articulation; epipod with simple true setae.
Seconday unguis of pereopod 1 bifid. Ventral comb seta on merus of pereopod 7 and carpus of pereopods 2,3,6 and 7. Uropod peduncle with prominent blunt lateral tubercles; endopod with 3 tuberscles near midlength, endopod 0.9 times as long as peduncle.
Pleopod 2 with plumose setae up to 0.6 times length of exopod. Appendix masculina reaching beyond endopod tip, articulating near mid-length of endopod. Endopod of pleopod 5 anterior to exopod , circular, 0.8 times as long as endopod of pleopod 2 ; peduncle of pleopod 5 with comb seta laterally.
Colour in alcohol pale yellow, with dark reticulate pigment.

Diagostic characteristics:
Flagellum of antenna 1 with 4 articles and that of antenna 2 with five articles; Epipod of maxilliped elongate triangular with a blunt apex not reaching the base of the palp, borders sparsely hairy. Mandibular palp 3-segmented lacinia mobilis of rigtht mandible curved, bifid with aptical teeth. Pleonite 5 dorsomedially with anterior pair of modules, followed by carinae which coverge posteriorly to single mode. Pleopod 1 with three coupling hooks; Endopod of Pleopod 5 circular but produced proxomedially. Outer margin of the peduncle of uropod dentate with prominent blunt tubercles. Telson roughly semicircular, proximal part of its lateral borders straight. Dorsal side of Telson with three prominent conical tubercles arranged in the form of a triangle, each followed posteriorly by a carina. A row of small tubercles or anterior margin of telson. Lateral crest very prominent. Dorsal row of tubercles follows the entire perimeter of pleotelson.

Feeding Habit: Bores into wood and consumes the saw dust.
Main food : Wood Cellulose

Additional Remarks :
L.Tripuctata is a very rare species at Chennai and is not all important as a borer destructive to timber.Elsewhere it is known for its ability to bore into even timber treated with wood preservative chemicals such as CCA,CCB,TBTO and CREOSOTE (Menzies 1957; Cookson and Barnacle 1985; Gambetta ET AL. 1988). It has also been reported atttacking red mangrove wood in Belize (Kensley and Schotte, 1987).
