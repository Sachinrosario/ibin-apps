
SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 Colony is branched in one plane and anastomoses Colony branched in one plane. It measures about 19 cm in height and about 5 cm. in breadth. It consists of a main stem measuring 19 cm long and 4.5 mm in diameter. The verrucae are large, measuring 1 mm in height and 1.5 cm in diameter.  The anthocodiae are completely retractile.  There is an elevated conical operculum  composed of eight groups of three spindles and a cellaret of two or three rows of  transversely arranged curved spicules.  The axis is horny and measures 1.75 mm in diameter at the base.	
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are (a.) Smooth spine with irregularly warted spicules (b)  Bent spicules with irregular warts.

Colour			:	Colony pinkish-red, axis brown and the spicules 
				colourless.
