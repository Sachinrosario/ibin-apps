SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Similar to Peridinium sphaeroidea Abe but differs from it in the antapical spines being somewhat more short and absence of the wings besides markings in the border of sulcus.

Diagnostic characteristics: At the apex of epitheca a slightly projecting button is present. Cell globular, ovoid.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 60µ; width: 56µ.
