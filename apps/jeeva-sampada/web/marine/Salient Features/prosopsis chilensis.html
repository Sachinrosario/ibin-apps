Salient Features :
Large shrubs or small trees.
Leaves bipinnate; pinnae 4-6 cm long; leaflets 0.4 to 1.2 cm x 0.2 to 0.3 cm, sessile, oblong, glabrous.
Flowers yellow, in 2-8 cm long, axillary, pendent spikes, pods 10-15 x 0.9-1.2 cm, pandent, and slightly curved.  

Soil data for (1:2) extracts

Soil salinity : 20.3 to 51 dS.m-1
Soil pH : 7.5 to 8.3
E S P : 21.5 to 56.2
S A R : 19.5 to 45.2

Industries:
It is a good source of wood, charcoal, gum and honey.
Health:
Nil.
Nutrition:
The pods are good fodder for goats.
R & D:
Huge plantation of this species checks soil erosion.
