SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body fusiform, compressed; belly with a sharp keel of of scutes from isthmus (between gill openings on underside of head ) to anus. Tip of snout at level of eye centre; maxilla produced, reaching to or almost to, base of first pectoral fin ray; gillrakers usually 13 to 16 on lower limb of first arch, their serrae uneven but not clumped. Anal fin long, usually with 31 to 36 branched rays.

Colour:

 back blue/green, flanks silvery; a black venulose area behind gill opening, with a golden area before it. Dorsal in lemon yellow with a dusky tip, , caudal fin lemon yellow with upper, lower and hind edges black; anal fin clear.
