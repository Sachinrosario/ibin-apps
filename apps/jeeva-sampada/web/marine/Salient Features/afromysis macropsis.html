Genus: Afromysis Zimmer
Frontal plate conical and apically blunt. Antennal scale lanceolate, setose all round and with distinct suture. Labrum with sharp frontal spine. Second segment of palp of second maxilla considerably produced, inner border armed with closely arranged row of long and short spines. Thoracic endopods with two propodal segments. Pleopods in male well developed and biramous, exopod of fourth pleopod longer than endopod and armed with one to three spiny setae. Telson distally cleft, lateral border with two groups of spines separated by a gap, cleft with a pair of plumose setae and with or without spines. Inner border of endopod of uropod through out spiny, spines grouped into irregular series.
Afromysis macropsis
Body long and slender. Eyes elongated, cornea wider than stalk. Antennal scale shorter than antennular peduncle. Exopod of fourth pleopod in male very much elongated, last three segments with long barbed setae. Lateral margin of telson with three proximal spines and about twenty distal spines, most distal ones blunt, cleft one third length of telson and with pair of plumose setae. Endopod of uropod with dense row of about fifty spines on inner margin, varying in length, all spines blunt except distal few.
