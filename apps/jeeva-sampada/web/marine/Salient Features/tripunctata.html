SALIENT FEATURES:

Flagellum of second antenna with five articles. Epipod of maxilliped triangular, border sparsely hairy. Mandibular palp normal and 3-segmented. Fifth pleon segment with three tubercles, lateral crest of pleotelson very prominent and conspicuously tuberculate. Telson roughly semicircular. On the dorsal side 3 prominent conical tubercles, a median proximal tubercle followed by a short faint carina. Lateral crest tuberculate. Border of telson setose. Mandible normal, with 3-segmented palp, lacinoid seta of right mandible abruptly curved and distally bearing two prominent spinulate projections. Epipod of maxilliped elongate triangular, apex blunt, border hairy. Peduncle of uropod as long as endopod; outer border irregularly dentate, outer middle part with a few teeth.

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi on wood, wood into which it bores.

Distribution:
Atlantic and Pacific coasts of America, Mediteranian (Pillai 1961), India (Tamil Nadu) (Becker and Kampf 1959).

Remarks:
  Menzies describes this species as a temperate, tropical species. This is very destructive borer species along the coasts of U.S.A.

Size and age: 3-5mm in length.
