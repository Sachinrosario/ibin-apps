SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:     

Diagnostic characteristics:  
        Female:   The prosome and the 1st pedigerous segment are completely fused but traces of the line of fusion of the 4th and 5th pedigerous segment can be detected.  The posterior thoracic margin is rounded. 
     The proportional lengths of the cephalothorax and abdomen are as 88 to 47, so that the abdomen is rather more than half the length of the anterior region.  The urosome consists of 4 segments. The 1st segment is crossed on its dorsal aspect by a thickened ridge of chitin; the 1st, 2nd and 3rd segments are all armed across their dorsal aspect with rows of fine spines running along the posterior margins.
     The 1st antenna reaches to a little beyond the posterior margin of the 1st perdigerous segment. The 10th, 11th and 12th segments are very short but the lines of division between them can be clearly made out. The 2nd, 4th, 6th and 8th segments are armed with a single spine each.
     The 2nd antenna and mouthparts closely resemble those of V. oligarthra.  
     The 1st pair of swimming legs shows distinct trace of division between the 1st and 2nd segments of the exopod and each part is armed with a marginal spine. The 2nd pair of legs shows also a line of demarcation between the 1st and 2nd segments of the exopod and similar traces of line of separation between the 1st and 2nd segments of the endopod can equally be detected.  The 2nd basal segment is armed on its outer margin with a small spine.
     In the 3rd leg the proximal segments of the exopod are completely fused but the endopod consists of three segments, the division between the 1st and 2nd segments being quite clearly marked.
     In the 4th pair of legs the two proximal segments of the exopod are incompletely fused and again the endopod consists of three segments.         

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
 Female: 4.0 mm
