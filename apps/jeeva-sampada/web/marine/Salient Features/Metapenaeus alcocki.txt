SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref No:53

Morphological:     Body more tomentose than glabrous; rostrum long, distal half slightly curved, 
extending beyond tip of antennular peduncle, armed along entire dorsal margin with 10 teeth including epigastric; postrostral carina reaching near to posterior margin of carapace; adrostral carina ending before epigastric tooth, sulcus behind it; branchiocardiac carina distinct, reaching posterior extension of hepatic spine; mid-dorsal carination on abdomen commencing from 4th somite; ischial spine present on 1st pereopod, strong and large; telson with minute spinules on lateral margins. Distolateral projections of petasma spout-like, distomedian projections directed anterolaterally overlying distolateral projections, its distal end broad and trilobed. Anterior plate of thelycum tongue–like, medially grooved, wider posteriorly, its posterior extension bound laterally by a pair of oval bosses; lateral plates without raised ridges and each with a posterior concave inwardly curved plate meeting each other in the middle.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Body more tomentose than glabrous; rostrum reaching beyond tip of 
antennular peduncle; mid-dorsal carination on abdomen commencing from 4th somite; a strong and large ischial spine on 1st pereopod; distomedian projections of petasma directed anterolaterally; posterior extension of anterior plate of thelycum bound laterally by a pair of oval bosses, plates without raised ridges.
