
SALIENT FEATURES:                   
Diagnostic characteristics:           

Female: This species can be very easily identified by its characteristic robust nature. Prosome fused with the 1st pedigerous segment. Fourth and 5th pedigerous segments separate. Postero-lateral corners of the last thoracic segment produced into rounded lappets. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment with a shovel- like process on the ventral side. First antenna 19-segmented, reaches up to the posterior end of the last pedigerous segment. Fifth pair of legs absent.         

Male: In general appearance like the female. In 5th leg joints are very stout and massive. It has its characteristic shape.
