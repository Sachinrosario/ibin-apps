SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium with a small encrusting base erect and with irregular branches. Zooecia lageniform, slightly bent peristome. Front a tremocyst with large tremopores. A pair of minute avicularia occupy the rim of the peristome, latero-medianly. Position of the avicularia demarcated by two pointed calcareous prolongations, placed on the external phase of the avicularia. No ovicell noticed.Diagnostic characters:   
Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:   The dimensions of the zooecia which Osburn thinks to be of some importance are more or less equal to those observed by Osburn in his material; even though the width of the zoecium is less here in the present specimen. The peristome here is very short. Osburn (1952) states "The peristomial tubes vary in length, occasionally as long as the zooecial body but usually much shorter". The costae of the peristomes are not at all clear and may be even absent.
  A comparison of the present form with the figure of L. spinulosa given by Robertson (1908) considered synonymous to L. punctulata by Osburn (1952) has revealed some interesting features. The figure given by Robertson shows well-developed spines, and lateral avicularia placed on elevated peduncles. These structures are totally absent in the present form and are not shown in the figures given by Osburn (1952). These features may not be of much specific importance.
