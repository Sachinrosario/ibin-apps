SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules constricted in the middle and twisted in a 'figure of eight" pattern. Valves linear, with acute apices. Axial area raised to form a sigmoid keel enclosing the raphe. 
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface striate, striae finely lineate, keel coarsely punctate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 90-100m. Breadth: 87m.
