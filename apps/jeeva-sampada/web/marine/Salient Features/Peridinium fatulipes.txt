SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells markedly broadened. Sides of epitheca and hypotheca concave. Epitheca ends ventrally in a long conical apical horn; hypotheca ends in two long horns diverging at the apex decorated with ring and reticulate like sculpturing.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus deep, with winged lists on both sides not projecting below.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 150-250µ; breadth: 100-170µ.
