SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells in ventral view quadratical. Longitudinal axis diagonal. Girdle sinistral, ¼ girdle width displaced, excavated. Antapical section between the short horns is girdle width deep, ½ girdle width broad, round.

Diagnostic characteristics: Horns carry two tiny spines.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 83µ. breadth: 80µ.
