Diagnostic characteristics:
Body slender and smooth. Carapace anteriorly produced into broad triangular rostral plate with bluntly pointed apex. Eyes large. Antennal scale long and narrow, ten times as long as broad, far over reaching antennular peduncle. Second segment of mandibular palp narrow, inner margin with seven tooth-like prolongations. Carpopropodus undivided, dactylus with long slender nail. Fourth pleopod of male extending to middle of telson and as usual for the genus. Telson strongly constricted at two third distance from base, distal border forked, lateral margin armed with twelve spines, lobes of telson with three pairs of stout spines, sinus shallow and armed with two pairs of spines. Uropods long and narrow.
