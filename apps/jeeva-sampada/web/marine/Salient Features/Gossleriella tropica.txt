SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells with flat valves. Border of the valve with a ring of spines 28-57m long of which several are striking owing to thicker silicification. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Strong spines bifid and swollen at the base and between two strong spines are situated, weaker spines.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 182-196m.
