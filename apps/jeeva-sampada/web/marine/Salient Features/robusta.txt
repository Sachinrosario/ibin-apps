SALIENT FEATURES:


Diagnostic characteristics:                 Body stout. Carapace produced into low triangular apically blunt rostrum, in both sexes.Eyes large and globular. Antennal scale reaching tip of antennular peduncle in male, shorter in female. Second thoracic endopod stout, carpopropodus distally excavated. Thoracic endopods three to eight with carpus and propodus distinct. Pseudobranchial lobes of pleopods simple and bilobed; pleopods not modified. Telson wedge shaped, four times as long as broad, steadily narrowing to apex, lateral borders throughout spiny, first three spines large, others in series of two to four; apex with pair of long spines, three small spines and pair of plumose setae. Exopod of uropod slightly over reaching endopod, more than half of outer border of basal segment armed with nine spines; inner border of endopod with closely packed spines arranged in series. 

Size and age

Maximum length:       9.4 - 9.6mm
