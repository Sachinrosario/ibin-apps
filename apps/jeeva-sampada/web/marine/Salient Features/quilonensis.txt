SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  
Rostral process triangular, longer in female. Antennal scale broader in female, not reaching distal border of antennular peduncle. Thoracic endopods comparatively stout. Pseudobranchial lobes not spirally twisted, pleopods not modified. Telson two and a  half times as long as broad with distinct constriction beyond base, apex broadly rounded with three pairs of long sub equal spines and three small median spines; lateral border with fourteen spines, two proximal spines separated from third by longer interval , distal spines falling into indistinct series. Exopod of uropod  slightly  over reaching endopod , basal  segment with four spines in male and three in female ; endopod with long  spines   alternating with groups  of one to four small spines.

Maxikmum length:  Male and female -10mm
