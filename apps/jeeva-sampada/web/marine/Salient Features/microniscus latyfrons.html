SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:

The carapcae of this form in having the anterior region depressed so that the front edge is flatter.
Appendages The 1st antenna Fig 3 is biramous and short. The protopodite appears to be two-jointed, the proximal joint being very large and stout. The second joint is about half its size, but longer and three times as stout as the endopodite. The exopodite is single jointed but twice as long as endopodite. The endo-podite is single jointed, slender and ends in pair of finger-like processes. The 2nd antenna Fig.4 is four articled and three times as long as the 1st , uniformly thin and bears two small pointed processes at its tip.

The mouth parts Fig.6 are similar to those of Micronicus acartii and Micronicus cusalanii as regards their arrangement and structure. The difference in the lengths of the joints of the 1st maxilla and 2nd may however be mentioned.

There are seven pairs of biramous pleopods. The expodite has a small spinous process at its outer edge and bears five setae. The endopodite has three setae fig.7.

There is a pair of biramous uropod. The protopodite is two-jointed, cylindrical. The endopodite is single jointed and bears a stout spine half as long as the base. The endopodite is distinguished by a small slender accessory soine. The exopodite also consists of a basal piece and a spine and the basal portion is as long as the entire endopodite and is proportionately stouter. The spine is also more than twice the length of the spine of the endopodite.

Additional remarks:

Microniscus latifrons was described by Gnanamuthu and Krishnaswamy (1948) based on specimen, collected from the cephalothorax of the copepods, Acartia erythraea and Acrocalanus longicornis. These authors could nor ascetain whether the specimen were made, female or larvae and hence they were provisionally described as new species.
