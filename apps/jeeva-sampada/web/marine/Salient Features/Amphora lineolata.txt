SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Hyaline, weakly silicified frustules. Rectangular elliptical with slightly convex sides in the girdle view. Raphe with straight branches, which run back from the central nodule dorsal ward.

Diagnostic characteristics: Intercalary bands numerous, 10 in 10µ. Axial area narrow, central area absent. Transapical striae very slightly radial 18-21 in 10µ finely punctate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 66-93µ long; 31.5-52.5µ broad (girdle view).

Biochemical aspects: Obligate Phototroph, which requires vitamin B12 for growth.
