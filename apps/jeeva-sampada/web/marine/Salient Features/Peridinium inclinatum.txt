SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elliptical body, wide or Globose with a very small abrupt neck slightly ventral; sulcus very narrow and shallow slightly excavated and with no depression at its Antapical end.

Diagnostic characteristics: The left spine is sometimes shorter, may have a short pseudospine. There is no dorso-ventral flattening.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 54.5-62.5µ, Diameter: 54-57.5µ.
