SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca and hypotheca nearly equal. Girdle central, not displaced; provided with lists strengthened by fine spines.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus not reaching to the centre of the hypotheca, with a conspicuous but narrow wing like list on the left.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 28-56µ.
