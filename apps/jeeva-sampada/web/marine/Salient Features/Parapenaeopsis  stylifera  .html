SALIENT FEATURES:         Ref No:103,144,161

 

Morphological:   Rostrum sigmoidal, toothless on at least distal half, armed with 6 to 8 dorsal

teeth at base, strongly upcurved and much over-reaching tip of antennular peduncle, shorter in some males; epigastric tooth present; postrostral crest almost reaching posterior margin of carapace; longitudinal suture reaching 2/3 of carapace length; telson armed with 1 or 2 pairs of subapical fixed spines (proximal pair much smaller when present); antennular flagella slightly longer than carapace; epipod and basial spine present on first and second pereopods; basis of 3rd pereopod unarmed. Distolateral projections of petasma slender, horn-like and straight, directed anterolaterally, with ventro-external openings; distomedian projections small and curved ventrally. Anterior plate of thelycum square and concave, with a slender stem-like posterior process; posterior plate deeply notched anteromedially.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum toothless at least on distal half; telson armed with 1 or 2

pairs of subapical fixed spines; epipod and basial spine present on 1st and 2nd pereopods; distolateral projections of petasma slender, horn-like, straight and directed antero-laterally; anterior plate of thelycum square and concave.

 

        Colour: Body grey, sometimes grey-reddish or brownish.

 

 

Feeding habit:                   Ref. No:98

   

 Main food:  Stomach contents of the species showed remains of crustaceans such as

            copepods, cirripeds, mysids, amphipods and larval decapods; minute gastropods,

            bivalves and foraminiferans.

         Feeding type:  Omnivorous bottom feeder

Eggs and larvae:               Ref. No:123

 

Characteristics: At species level, eggs are opaque and spherical measuring 0.35 to 0.38 mm in

diameter, with a wide perivitelline space. The embryo hatches out in 15 to 16 hours and passes through 6 nauplius, 3 protozoea and 7 mysis substages before transforming into postlarva. The last mysis stage is 3.7 to 4.1 mm total length and  characterized by having a short rostrum reaching middle of eye, 2 rostral teeth, no hepatic spine, long two-segmented pleopods without setae, and a telson with its distal margin slightly convex and bearing 8 pairs of setae.

 

              In the first postlarva, an epigastric tooth also develops on the rostrum, hepatic spine is

present, and posterior margin of telson is tapering and ending in a prominent median

spine which is longer than the 4 pairs of telsonic setae present on either side of it. The

pleopods become setose. 

   

   

Abundance:  Eggs, larvae and postlarvae of the species have been noticed in the inshore plankton at

  Cochin. While eggs and nauplii occurred in October and February, protozoea and mysis stages were observed almost throughout the year except during  June to August. They were most abundant during October-December and February-April.  Postlarvae were generally scarce in the inshore waters at this center (Rao, 1972).

SPAWNING INFORMATION:             Main Ref:157

   Locality:       Within about 20 m depth in the sea throughout the range of distribution.

   Season:         Peak spawning Nov-January & Apl on SW coast, Nov-Dec on SE coast.

   Fecundity:    39,500 to 2,36,000 eggs at 7-12 cm size.

   Comment:     The species breeds throughout the year with peak spawning varying from place to                            place and from year to year (Rao, 1970).
