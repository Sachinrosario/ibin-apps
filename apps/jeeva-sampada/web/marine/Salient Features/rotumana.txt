SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive (15 x 8 cm in diameter and 3.5 cm in thickness) with thin edges.  As epitheca is present.  Corallites polygonal, walls fuses, intercorallite groove absent; wall 2 to 3 mm thick, flat at the top.  Calices oval or slightly distorted.  10 to 13 mm long, 8 to 10 mm broad, 4 to 6 mm deep.  Septa 28 to 32 including 5 to 6 rudimentaries; alternating in thickness and height, wide apart, 1.5 to 2 mm exsert; exsert part vertical, upto 1 mm thick.  Septa almost of the same thickness thought their length.  They are 2 to 3 mm broad at the top, narrower below, almost vertically descending.  There is a deep cleft a the lower part of the septum, above which there is a conspicuous paliform lobe.  Exsert part of septa stopping at the middle of the wall or fusing with the opposite ones.  Generally a broad septum fuses with a narrow one of the opposite side.  Top of septa has a frosted appearance owing to the presence of large number of twisted lamellae with prominent granulations.  This is a notable feature of the specimen.  The septal edges are mostly entire, below resembling that of Galaxea.  15 to 16 reach the columella.  Columella situated below paliform lobes, 2 to 3 mm in diameter, poorly developed, composed of twisted trabeculae by the fusion of the septal ends.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Fission appears to be marginal, unequal.  The notable feature of this specimen are the thickened septa with almost entire edges and conspicuous ring of paliform lobes.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
