SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No.1, 2

Body extremely elongate and strongly compressed, ribbon-like, tapering to a point (caudal tapering part very long), Snout long, contained  2 to 2.5 times  in head length, month very large with a dermal flap at tip of each jaw, 2 or 3 (mostly 3) fangs with barbs  and 2 small forward directed canine teeth present in upper jaw, anteriormost fang very long, coming out through a small slit on ventral side of lower   jaw (when mouth is tightly closed) a pair of fangs (usually without barbs) present at tip of lower jaw ; eye small,  its diameter contained about 7 to 9 times in head length and slightly longer than suborbital space, lower hind margin of gill cover concave., A single, long base, dorsal fin with 3 or 4 spines and 110 to 120 soft rays anal fin reduced to small spinules (about 75) breaking through skin, the anteriormost fairly long, its origin situated beneath 36th dorsal fin ray pectoral fins slightly shorter than snout, with spine and 10 soft rays;  pelvic and caudal fins caudal fins absent. Lateral line nearer to ventral contour than to dorsal contour of body.

Colour:  

In fresh specimens, body steely blue, with metallic reflections tapering part white. Margin of anus pale;  usually, margin of dorsal fin membrane white top of both jaws black;  inside of opercle and anterior part of shoulder girdle, pale black.

Feeding habit: 
Main food: 								Ref . No. 2
Feeds on a wide variety of small fishes and crustaceans (chiefly on prawns and species of Setipinna, Anchoviella, Trichiurus and on harpadon nehereus, in Hooghly estuaries).

Feeding type:  Carnivorous
