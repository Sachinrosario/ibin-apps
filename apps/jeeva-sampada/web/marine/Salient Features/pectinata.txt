Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting, explanate, tending to become massive.  Corallites and calices polygonal, wall fused 1 to 3 mm thick in different cases.  The size of the calices shows bewildering variations in different coral.  They range from 5-7 mm in length 4-6 mm in width and upto 6 mm in depth or they may be upto 12 mm in length, 9 mm wide and upto 8 mm deep.  Septa vary from 30 to 40 depending on the size of the corallite.  Septa only slightly exsert, exsert parts continuous over the wall, narrow at first, broader below.  Septal edges dentate, sides spinulose.  The last septal tooth form a prominent palus in major septa.  13 to 16 septa reach the columella, others turn to the sides of the major ones.  Columella trabecular, 1 to 3 mm in diameter.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
