Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum prostrate, main divisions flat at the underside forming broad plates.  The underside has to branchlets.  The peripheral edges with several branchlets that are prostrate.  The upper branchlets widely spaced, 3 to 7 cm long 10 to 12 mm thick.  Branchlets curved with tapering apices.  Axial corallites 2.5 mm in diameter, conical, 2 to 3.5 mm exsert.  Septa poorly developed with rudimentary first cycle.  Radial corallites uniform in size, nariform, slightly appressed, outer wall thickened and curved up.  Opening oval.  Radials 4 to 5 mm in length.  Subimmersed and immersed corallites few.  Septa in radials poorly developed. 


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
