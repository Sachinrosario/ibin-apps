Diagnostic characteristics:
Body surface spiny. Front margin of carapace produced into triangular rostral plate, apex obtusely rounded. Eyes large, eye stalk with dorsal ocular papilla. Antennal scale lanceolate, setose all round, over reaching antennular peduncle. Thoracic endopods with three carpopropodal segments. Pleopods in male well developed and biramous, exopod of fourth pleopod elongated, with single spinose seta on antepenultimate and penultimate joints, ultimate joint terminating in two short setae. Telson more than half as long as uropod, tapering, cleft at apex, cleft one fourth total length with pair of setae, lobes of cleft narrow and terminating in large spine, lateral border of telson with eight to eleven spines. Endopod of uropod with 13-23 spines, proximal part carrying set of small spines and distal part with four large claw like spines, spines decreasing in length towards tip of endopod.
