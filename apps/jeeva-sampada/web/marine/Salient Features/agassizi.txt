
SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:           S. agassizi is very close to S. aestiva; it  differs from the later only in some structural details.         The body is smooth with a very thin, finely faceted transparent integument.  The head is short  and high, without a rostrum.  The eye cups are large and well demarcated; their anterior margin projects forward behind the frons; the width of the commissure between the lower and upper cups varies; the bottom of the cups in freshly  caught specimens has a dull metallic sheen.    The structure of the appendages varies and evidently has no characters that distinguish this species from S. aestiva.  The exception is the structure of the  distal  segment  of the flagellum of antennae I; the inner distal angles of the 1st  and 2nd segments are drawn forward’ the 3rd segment is conical, rarely narrows distally and is nearly two times longer than the 2nd segment. 

Sex attributes:Dimorphic
Male: 1st antenna well developed , female: 1st antenna reduced.
