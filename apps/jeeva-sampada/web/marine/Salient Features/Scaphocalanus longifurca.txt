
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Diagnostic characteristics:                 

Female:  Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segment fused. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment angular and rounded. Urosome 4-segmented. Genital segment slightly swollen below at the orifice at about the middle. First antenna 22-segmented and reaches up to the posterior end of the 4th urosomal segment. Fifth leg 2 jointed with 2 spines on the terminal joint which are provided with spinules.          

Male:  Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segment fused.  Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment rounded. In 5th leg endopod of the left leg characteristically sickle-shaped, but not curved.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                        Female : 1.5 mm                                          Male     :  1.45 mm
