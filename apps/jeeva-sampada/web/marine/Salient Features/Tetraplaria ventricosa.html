Morphological: Internodes consisting of 1-10 or 11 pairs of zooecia, which are convex, separated by distinctive sutural lines; often pyriform, narrower proximally. Surface with dep conical pits, each with a pore. Orifices with large rounded sinus. Ovicells developed on both members of a pair of zooecia; limited by sutural lines; the fertile orifices larger than the others. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:    It consists basally of a nearly circular encrusting portion, about 2 mm. In diameter; composed of slightly convex porous units resembling short zooecia. Most of these are kenozooecia, but one at least has a normal sinuate orifice.

The occurrence of two zooecia in an internode was given as a generic character of Diploecium by Kirkpatrick who showed an internode consisting of two pairs. His type material in the British Museum consists of the more proximal part of a colony in which the internodes are mostly bi-zooecial, although one or two of the more distal ones have a s many as 5 pairs. The ovicells characteristics of T. ventricosa are represented.
In the other specimens here considered the number of zooecia in an internode becomes more numerous distally; the largest number being 11 pairs.

Multiporous terminal rosette-plates. The connecting chitinous tubes of the internodes are longer than the portion visible in a dry specimen. When decalcified they are seen to extend, with a diminished diameter, into the zooecia, which are generally narrow proximally; each reaching its predecessor of the same row. The first pair of zooecia of an internode seem to be always completely united laterally, so that the second pair do not extend between them to the joint, which is formed across a pair of zooecia, the proximal parts of which reman in the preceding internode.Meristic characteristics:
