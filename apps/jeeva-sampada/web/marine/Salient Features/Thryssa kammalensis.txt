SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body fusiform, fairly  strongly compressed;  belly keeled with abot 8 pre- pelvic and 5 post- pelvic scutes.  Snout  fairly prominent, bluntly pointed; maxilla short, reaching only a little beyond gill-opening, not to pectoral – fin base; mouth not strongly oblique. Gillrakers on lower arm of first arch 27 to 29. Dorsal  fin with 13 or 14 rays; a small, sharp spine before dorsal fin. Anal fin with about 34 rays. 

Colour:

 back  brown, flanks  silvery;  dark   venulose area on shoulder occasionally present.
