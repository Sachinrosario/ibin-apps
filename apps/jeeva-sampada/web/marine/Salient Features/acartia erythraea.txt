SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        Female: The rostral filaments are very stout. The lateral angles of the last pedigerous segment are produced into the pointed processes, and with an accessory spine. The genital segment is twice as long as the following segment, and has 2 dorsal spines, which are longer than those of the latter. The furcal style is hairy,  and about as long as its width.      

The anterior antennae reach to the end of the furca, and have the spines on the proximal segments.     The middle segment of the 5th leg is longer than 2 times as long as its width. The terminal claw is curved. Male fifth leg as figured.     

Size and age: Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)Female: 1.4 mm

Ref. No.:T. Mori, 1964.
