Morphological:  Head small, neck not distinct; 1 pre - and 1 (rarely 2), postoculars; a single larger anterior temporal followed by another as large; 6-7 supralabials, the 3rd and 4th touching the eye; 4 infralabials in contact with the genials, both pairs of which are well - developed, the posterior pair usually separated by scales.  Body stout, long, slender anteriorly and much compressed posteriorly, covered with imbricate scales throughout, smooth or with a cental keel, in 29-37 rows on the midbody ventrals 300-338, distinct throught, feeby enlarged, each one with two keels; preannals moderately enlarged.

Diagnostic characteritics:  The extremely eccentric ontour of the snake - the very small head nd slender; long whip - like neck, with the body becoming much thicker farther back - will easily establish the identity of the species.  The colouration is much variable; the young which are brightly coloured, are black or bluish - black, with from 35 to 55 bright yellow or whitish bars that encircle the body as rings on the rear part of the body.  Head with a curved yellow mark on the top.  The markings fade with age and the adult is more or less of a uniform greyish or bluish clolur above and yellowish below, without tdrace of crossbars seen in the young.

Additional remarks:  The estuarine sea snake, H.obscura, as its common name suggests, is exceedingly common in the Chilka Lake, Orissa, the waters of which vary in salinity at different depths and in different portions of the lake.  Murthy (1988, 1995) has, in the course of faunistic investigations of the Chilka, has picked up specimens of the snake from the course of faunistic investigations of the Chilka, has picked up specimens picked up are devoid of the encrustations of Hydroids and barnacles which are characteristic of the ocean - going sea snakes.  Annadale (1915) has rightly stated the this species is maily, if not exclusively , an estuarine snake.  Fishermen of the Chilka Lake have no apparent fear for this snake which is certainly docile than the beaked sea snake, also found in the waters of the lake.

Besides the eastern coastal belt of India, the range of thie species includes the coasts of Myammar, Malay Peninsula and Straits of Malacca.
