SALIENT FEATURES OF Halodule wrightii Asch., 1868

Leaves: 5-12 cm long, lamina linear, narrowed at base, covered; leaf margin entire, nerves ending in lateral teeth at the leaf tip, teeth bidentate 

Inflorescence: Male flowers 2, subequal; female flowers sessile and enclosed by leafy sheaths 

Perianth: Absent Ovary: Ovoid, with upto 2.8 cm long styles 

Fruit: Globose or ovoid, mostly found in pairs with persistent lateral styles 

Flowering and fruiting: March-July
