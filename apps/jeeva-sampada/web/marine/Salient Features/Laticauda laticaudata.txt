SALIENT FEATURES :

Morphological : Head shields entire; nostrils lateral; no azygous preforntal shield separating the internasals or preforntals; 1 pre and 2 postoculars; 7 supralabials, the 3rd and 4th touching the eye; emporals 1 (posteriror) and 2 (anterior); 5 lower labials in contact with the chin-shields, both pairs of which are broadly well developed and in good contact with each other.

Body subcylindrical, only slightly compressed, of nearly uniform diameter throughout; scales smooth and overlapping throughout, in 19 rows at midbody; ventrals 225-243, those on the belly wide and being about four times as broad as long, sometimes with a median keel at least on the posterior half of the body; anal divided; caudals 38-47 (males), 30-45 (females).

Diagnostic characteritics:  

This sea snake is light or dark bluish - grey above, with numerous broad, black to blackish -  brown rings on the body; the bands, which are separated by spaces of nearlyequal width, may be incomplete on the belly. The head is black, with a curved yellow mark on the top, the colour sometimes extending forwards to cover the entire snout and downwards, crossing over te eye to the upper lip; an elongated yellow patch below the lower jaw.Thr belly is yellow.




Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Piscivorous

Main food:  Fish

 

Additional remarks:  

The black-banded sea snake ranges from the Bay of Bengal and the seas south of Japan to the coast of Australia and islands of Oceania. Rare in the Oriental region.
Recent studies indicate that this species prefers to live around the rocky sae coasts. It is said to be commonly met with in harbours and bays also.
