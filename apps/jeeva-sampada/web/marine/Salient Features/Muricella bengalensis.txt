SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 Colony  flabellate and the branches are alternately arranged in rows. The polyps are in steep spiral pr irregularly arranged. The calyces are blunt, club-shaped with longitudinally arranged spindles, which form the projecting tips at the edge. The operculum is composed of converging spindles. The collaret spindles, arranged en chevron over the tentacles in one or two rows.
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are irregular and tuberculated

Colour		:	Colony is rose red in colour.
