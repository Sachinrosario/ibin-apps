SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells rounded or pyriform; epitheca produced into a more or less long apical horn, hypotheca almost semicircular. Girdle slightly dextral, generally broadly winged, excavated. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Two strong processes surrounded by more or less broad serrated fins.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: (without process) 39-88µ. Breadth: 22-40µ.
