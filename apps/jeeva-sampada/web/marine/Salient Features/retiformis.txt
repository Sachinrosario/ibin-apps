SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive.  Corallites and calices polygonal, average 4 mm in length more or less 1 mm deep.  Intercorallite wall fused, 1 to 1.5 mm thick.  Septa 18 to 22 of which 7-11 reach the columella.  Larger septa with prominent paliform lobes.  Columella spongy, sometimes compact.

Living coral was found golden yellow in Palk Bay, while in Minicoy they are dull brown.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3,  Building blocks.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
