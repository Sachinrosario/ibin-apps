SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming short chains attached by their horns or single elliptical lanceolate in valve view.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve horns slender and directed outwards. Two long straight spines on each valve placed equally apart from the horns.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Apical axis of cells 26-79µ.
