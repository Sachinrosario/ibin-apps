SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:          
Rostrum  long,slender, a small, knob-like papilla present on carapace; carapace  with a small pterygostomian spine; orbital angle is round; pereopods 2 and 3 developed; legs 1-3 bear reduced exopods; uniramous buds of legs 4 and 5 more enlarged; pleopods uniramous; telson rectangular with 7+7 spines (zoea II); and 8+8 in zoea III. Rostrum with 1 dorsal tooth in Postlarva I to 9/10 dorsal and 24/27 ventral teeth in the juvenile. Rostrum tip slightly curved upwards; longer than carapace; antennal spine present; telson bears 4 pairs of spines dorsally and 4 pairs distally,outermost on either side short and non plumose.      

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone

Main food: Algae,small copepods and other planktonic animals.

Feeding type: Omnivorous

Additonal remarks:A sword-like rostrum present in the Juvenile.

Size and age: 

Zoeae I-VI: length 1.40mm- 2.68mm
Postlarva I: length 2.96mm
Juvenile: length 14.40mm

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs: hatch out as Zoea.
Zoea I: length 1.54mmEyes large and sessile, fused with the carapace; rostrum small, unarmed and decurved at tip; anterolateral angles of carapace slightly produced; abdomen 6-segmented,3 pairs of maxillipeds present; telson with 7 pairs of spines.Zoea II: length 1.58mmEyes stalked; carapace with a pterygostomian spine; 1st pair of pereopods present as biramous buds; telson with 8 pairs of spines.Zoea III: length 2.07mmPereopods developed,1-3 biramous and 4-5 uniramous; telson separated from abdomen; uropods well developed; telson broad posteriorly, with 8+8 spines.Zoea IV: length 2.22mmRostrum long, slender and a small knob-like papilla present at its base; uniramous buds of pleopods developed.Zoea V: length 2.64mmPereopod 5 fully developed; pleopod buds become biramous without setae; telson rectangular with 3 pairs of short spines on lateral margin and 5 pairs of plumose setae terminally.Zoea VI: length 2.68mmRostrum with a single dorsal tooth; dorsal papilla exists; pereopods 1-2 with developing chelae. Exopod of uropod with 14 plumose setae and 1 spine and endopod with 11 plumose setae.Postlarva I: length 2.96mmRostrum with 3 dorsal teeth; carapace with antennal and pterygostomian spines;legs 1-2 chelate; endopod of leg 3 ends in a claw-like spine; legs 4 and 5 equal and uniramous; telson with 2 pairs of short spines on lateral margin and 4 pairs terminally. As the postlarva advances to the juvenile, the number of rostral teeth is increased to 9/10 dorsal and 24/27 ventral teeth; tip of rostrum slightly upcurved; only antennal spine present,the anterolateral margin of carapace is round; dactylus of pereopod 5 with 23 spines,the spines branched; propodus 4 times as long as dactylus; telson with 4 pairs of dorsal spines, tip narrow and convex in shape carrying 4 pairs of spines.Characteristics: Carapace bears a sword-like rostrum. The pereopod buds develop only in Zoea II; the 4th pereopod without an exopod; the pleopod buds develop only in the 4th Zoea.
