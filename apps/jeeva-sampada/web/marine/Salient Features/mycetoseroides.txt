Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Colonies are encrusting or laminar unifacial with a surface of small irregular folds.  Corallites are crowded between the folds except towards the periphery where they are aligned in rows parallel to the margins.  They are outwardly inclined.  Septo-costae are fine and even.  Living coral brown and green, usually dark colours if in shade.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
