SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical straight cells. Tapering valves. Spines slightly constricted at the base, then broadened and then drawn out into a long hair-like process.

Diagnostic characteristics: No visible structure on the valve or girdle. Chromatophore numerous and disc shaped.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Width: 42-51m.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
