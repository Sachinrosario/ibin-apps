Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum cespitose or corymbose; branches upright, obtuse cylindrical to short conical, 1.0 to 2.5 cm thick, rarely less than 1.5 cm.  Axial corallites 2.6 mm in diameter usually between 2.5 to 4 mm, hemispherical, sometimes slightly exsert.  Radial corallites usually thick walled, spreading from 450 to 900, 1.5 to 2.5 mm in diameter, inner wall weak or developed; aperture normal to axis of corallites, ablique, dimidiate, or even gutter-shaped.  Septa usually well-developed.  Coenenchyme spongy to evenly echinulate; walls of radials striato-echinulate to echinulate (Wells, 1954, p. 426).

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
