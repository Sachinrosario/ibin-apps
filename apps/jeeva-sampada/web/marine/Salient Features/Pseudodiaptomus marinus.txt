Diagnostic characteristics:   
     Female: Cephalon and pedigerous segment 1 fused; pedigerous segment V produced posteriorly into sharp spines directed slightly outwards; genital segment with slight lateral swelling; small patches of fine hairs scattered over lateral and ventral sides of segment; urosome I to III with triangular spikes on their postero-dorsal margins; urosome IV with serrated shield on disto-lateral corners; antenna 1 of 21 segments; leg 5 with basipod 2 bearing two small spinules on outer angle and a seta on posterior surface; exopod 1 bears a spine at distal outer margin; terminal segment with a spine on outer distal angle, and is produced into a medially serrated curved spiniform process; terminal spine bears a short serrated spine near its base. 
    Male: General characteristics resemble that of female; pedigerous segment V posterior corners produced into acuminate spines; urosome II to IV with triangular spikes; urosome V with a serrated shield at its distal outer corners; caudal rami symmetrical; antenna 1 with 21 segments; 4 large spines on segments 10-13 of which that on segment 12 is recurved at its tip and has a secondary spine as in P. ardjuna; segment 14-16 each with a slender spine; segment 18 with a denticulated plate carrying fine teeth; leg 5; right leg with basipod 2 carrying spinules on its outer margin, a posterior seta and a forked endopod; endopod with one slender pointed ramus and one shorter stouter ramus which ends in three points; exopod 1 bears a few spinules on its inner and outer margins and on its outer distal corner a ‘Y’ shaped spine with a subsidiary spinule in the fork; exopod 2 bears a long straight spine on distal part of its outer margin and a small bunch of scattered spiules anterior to it; exopod 3 sickle-shaped and bears a blunt process at its mid-length; left leg: with basipod 2 carrying a few spinules on its outer margin, a posterior seta and a long club-shaped naked endopod; endopod 1 bears a spine on its outer distal angle; exopod 2-3 elongate, truncate, distally bearing a short terminal spine and an outer marginal spine opposite to the tip of endopod; margin between these two spines fringed with numerous spinules; inner margin of terminal segment is straight and bears two short spinules.

Additional remarks:
  Since its original description, this species had been recorded from Japanese waters (Brodsky, 1956; Tanaka, 1966); 51?48’N, 174?21’E (Chiba, 1956); brackish waters of Hawaii (Jones, 1966) and from Indian Ocean (Mauritius Islands: Grindley and Grice, 1969). The present isolated occurrence of this species in the Andaman Sea is interesting as it considerably extends its known distributional range and fills in a gap.
