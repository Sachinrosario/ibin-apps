SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Adult female:
Body elongate ovate with the anterior part narrow. Dorsal side convex and the ventral more or less flat. Cephalon triangular, anteriorly truncated and some-what excavated ventrally. Hypopharynx conical and directed forwards. Antenna very much reduced and appear like two stiff spine like backwardly directed processes, placed on either side of hypopharynx. Peraeon large and without any trace of segmentation, dorso-median part pigmented. A single pair of large brood lamellae present, these lamellae overlapping ventrally and their free edge setose. Posteriorly they are produced into two large lamellar plates covering the pleon and reaching slightly beyond its tip. Peraeopods five pairs, small, prehensile and crowded on either side of the cephalic mass. Starting from the base of the legs are found four pairs of overlapping plate like structures, with setose margin as in the brood lamellae. Pleon four segmented, first three segments laterally drawn out into narrow processes, probably representing the coxal plates, seen in Bopyrids. Fourth segment apically bifid. Attached to the ventral side of the third segment is a pair of biramous lamellar structures, resembling pleopods. Free margin of the abdominal segments ciliated. The entire peraeon forms a spaceous brood pouch filled with large ova.

Body white, with two dark spots slightly behind the anterior border, one in the posterior part of the peraeon and one on the first pleon segment. The dorso-median part of the thorax with a broad brownish patch.

Immature female:

Body rather elongated and clearly segmented dorsally, in very young specimens the body is about three times as long as broad and segment throughout. Legs not so closely crowded as in the adult. Last pleon segment cleft in the middle and with distinctly biramous uropods. Border of the pleon and uropods with minute spines and the dorsal side with a gew dark chromatophores.

Male :

Body elongated and distinctly segmented. Cephalon large and with a large setose hood like lobe. Buccal mass triangular and produced beyond the anterior border of the cephalon. The antennae well developed and six jointed. Peraeon consisting of seven large, subequal segments, each with a median transverse ridge and a pair of prehensile peraeopods. Peraeopods progressively increasing in length from the first to the seventh. Pleon, elongated six jointed and regularly narrowing to the blunt apex. The last segment with two small setae, uropods and pleopods absent.

Body white, with dark patches on the cephalon, first and lst thoracic segments and the abdomen.

Additional Remarks:

The female parasite remains attached to the abdomen of the host, Rhopalophthalmus egregus and the male is found attached to the posterior extension of the brood lamellae of the female.

The genus notophryxus has only a single pair of brood lamellae, but the antennae, tips of mandibles and the maxillipods form rather flatterned lobes simulations lamellae. The thin cilated plates at the base of the legs in N.abdominalis are probably the flattened mouth parts. On the other hand if they are brood lamellae, there are a total of five pairs. Moreover the segments of the pleon in n.abdominalis are laterally produced and there is at least one pair of pleopods. According to Pillai (1958) these differences appear fundamental and probably , it may be necessary to create a new genus for accomadating this species.
