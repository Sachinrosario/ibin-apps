SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:     Rostrum longer than eye but falls short of the 1 st segment of  antennular peduncle; carapace with supraorbital,pterygostomian and hepatic spines; antennal scale          very narrow and long, longer than the endopod; dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 4 -6; posterolaterals on 5 &6; exopods of legs rudimentary; a small, but distinct exopod is present (Racek 1970); telson with 8+8 spines.  

Diagnostic characteristics: Long rostrum with dorsal and ventral teeth(in PL.VI onward); a sharp hepatic spine on carapace; telson with 8+8 spines (from Prtozoea III onward).

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.                        

Main food:   plankton, small crustaceans,debris with organic matters.

Feeding type: omnivorous. 

Additonal remarks:  The protozoeal substages of P.monodon,P..semisulcatus and P.merguiensis are very difficult to distinguish one from the other. In P.monodon the antennal scale and peduncle are of same length,so also,in the uropods and telson. In P.semisulcatus the antennal scale extends beyond the peduncle and the uropods surpass the telson; 6th abdominal somite is shorter than that in P.monodon in postlarval stages. In P.merguiensis rostrum has a crest, short, reaching the middle of eye; antennal scale more than peduncle; uropods shorter than telson. A small antennal spine present in P.monodon and P.semisulcatus.

Size and Age:

Protozoea I-III: 0.96-2.47mm. in length.

Mysis I-III: 3.15-4.43mm

Postlarva: 4.82mm

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed)    27cms.                           

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs: Spherical in shape, 0.29mm in diameter.Nauplii I-VI: size from 0.30mm-0.49mm; pyriform,unsegmented body with 3 pairs of cephalic appendages. Ocellus present; antennule(AI),endopod with 1+2+1 or 2+2+1 setae; setae nonplumose.In nauplius II setae become plumose; furcal seta 1 +1 in Nauplii I& II and increases to 7+7 in Nauplius VI(N6).

Protozoea I-III: size 0.96-2.47mm; carapace round; ocellus present; thorax 6 segmented; abdomen unsegmented in Protozoea I; buds of maxillae and maxillipeds develop in P2; eyes become stalked; subsupraorbital spines present;  telson with 7+7 spines; in P3 sub- supraorbital absent,telson develops 8 spines on each caudal furcum.

Mysis I: 3.15mm; rostrum without dorsal tooth and extends beyond eye; supraorbital,pterygostomian and hepatic spines on carapace;legs developed and functional; spine on abdomen remain same; legs 1-3 identical,endopods unsegmented; telson with 8, 1lateral and 7 terminal spines each; chelae develop and exopods longer.

Mysis II: 3.50mm; rostrum without tooth; reaches beyond or the tip of eye; mandible with an unsegmented palp; telson with 2 lateral and 6 terminal spines(8+8).

Mysis III: 4.43mm; rostrum with a small dorsal tooth; mandible with segmented palp; antennal scale is longer than endopod in all stages; pleopods segmented; telson with 3 lateral and 5 terminal spines.Postlarva: 4.82mm; rostrum longer than eye; one dorsal tooth; spines on carapace and abdomen remain same; mandibular palp with setae; legs 1-3 with reduced exopods; 5th pereopod with vestigial exopod; pleopods setose and functional; telson with 8+8 telsonic proceses.

Characteristics:3rd leg cheleped reaching beyond antennal scale by half of the chela.
