SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branching is in one plane. The polyps stand on 8 sides and are 0.8 mm high, 0.6 mm broad. The operculum is conical. The thin bark shows very rough surface.		
		
Spicules:  Not available.

Colour		:	Colony is light brown  in colour and the spicules colourless.
