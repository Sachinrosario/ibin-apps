SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:   
                                                         
Juvenile Stage: 22.2mm in length.Rostrum becomes short, with 9 dorsal teeth and epigastric,extends just beyond the eyes. Antennal and hepatic spines well developed; no pterygostomian spine  present. Dorsomedian spine on abdominal somite 3 becomes short and borne on a small crest. The median spine on  4 -5 abdominal segments very much reduced. The ventromedian spines present on the last 2 thoracic  and 1- 4 abdominal segments, short and blunt. Pereopods without exopods; ischial and basial spines present on 1st pereopod; pleopods well-developed; first pleopod without an endopod. Telson tridentate with a median acicular spine,a pair of fused posterolateral spines and 3 pairs of  marginal spines         
Post larvae: I,III &IV. length 12.0mm-26.3mm. Rostrum long,bearing 6+1 dorsal teeth in  postlarva I,to 9+1 in postlarva IV. Pterygostomian spine becomes reduced or absent. Hepatic spine becomes prominenet;pleura of abdominal segments 1-5  form curved spines, that on the 5th curved forwards. Telson with a median spine, median cleft vanished and 10 pairs of spines present on telson. The ventromedian spines on last 2 thoracic and  first 4 ab.segs become reduced.
Diagnostic characteristics:The absence of a pterygostomian spine; presence of prominent hepatic and antennal spines; the median spine on a crest on 3rd abdominal segment and a long median spine in the telson with 10 pairs of  posterior spines.  

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.
Main food: Live and dead planktonic organisms
Feeding type: Omnivorous

Additional remarks: This species is considered to be a deepwater  form and there were no record of this  species from southeast off Somalia and Muscat. Therefore,the species extends its distribution in the western , northern and northeastern Arabian sea (Paulinose,1979).

Size and Age:

Juvenile.22.2mm in length
Postlarvae I,III&IV: length 12.0-26.3mm
Protozoea III: length 3.5mm   

LARVAL STAGES:

Protozoea III:  3.5mm in length.Rostrum extends beyond the antennular peduncle; carapace oval; 2 pairs of supraorbital spines present,outer long and inner short. Dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 1-5 more slender and longer than in P. investigatoris. The paired dorsolateral spines  on abdominal segment 5 very prominent. Telson notched,with 8 pairs of spines with no median spine.Mysis.V: length 6.3mm.Rostrum bears 5 dorsal teeth and extends beyond the antennular peduncle; epigastric spine is lacking; a small pterygostomian spine present. Mandible without palp; maxillule without exopod, endopod bears 5 setae on the terminal segment.Thoracic legs with exopods.Dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 3-6, that of 3rd borne on a crest. Ventromedian spines present on last thoracic and 1st 2 abdominal segments; no dorsolateral spine on the 5th segment. Pleopods as small  buds. Exopod of uropod  has no spine. Telson with a median cleft and 6 terminal spines on each ramus; spine formula is 10+10,no median spine present.Characteristics: The pterygostomian spine  absent in late postlarvae; more of rostral  and telsonic spines in this species; a long dorsomedian spine on 3rd ab.seg.; A median spine in the telson,and tridentate in juvenile stage. Generally the larvae of Parapenaeus spp.are larger than the same stage larva of other genera. The development also shows a variety of variations in different stages.
