SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony flabellate, the largest being a fragment 6.2 cm long and above 4 cm in spread. Stem and branches round.. Main stem 2.1 mm in diameter and 1.5 cm long to first branch. The first complete node is 4 mm long and 3 mm in diameter, and a distal one is 1.2 mm long. The proximal internode is 1 cm long and 2 mm in diameter, while a distal internode is 1.4 cm long and 8 mm in diameter. The branching is irregularly dichotomous and the furcations are Y-shaped rather than U-shaped. The calyces are almostly lateral leaving a broad bare space on the back of the colony and a narrow one, sometimes invaded by calyces, on the front. There is often a tendency to form a zigzag row on the side of the branch.
		The individual calyces are dome shaped verrucae, often hemispherical when the polyps are completely retractile. They are somewhat closely approximated on the sides of the branches and are rendered conspicuous by their dark red colour in contrast with the dull yellow of the coenenchyma. A typical calyx measures 0.7 mm in height and 1.3 mm in diameter at the base. Their walls are filled with coarse tuberculate spindles that sometimes tend to an en chevron arrangement around the margin and otherwise are horizontal or irregularly disposed. The polyps are retractile and have a strong collaret composed of about three horizontal rows of red tuberculate spindles, above these other spindles are arranged en chevron over each tentacle base, forming a series of 8 points above the collaret. The reminder of the dorsal surface of the tentacles bear longitudinal yellow spindles.
		Spicules. These are mostly rather stout terete spindles, sometimes assuming an oval outline, and with definite whorls of prominent tubercles on the more slender spindles and with irregularly placed tubercles on the stouter ones. There are also a number of clubs, most of which are tuberculate rather than horny. Many of the spindles are curved.

Colour		:The entire colony is red and yellow , the coenenchyma is dull yellow, owing to the red axis showing through the yellow layer of spicules. The calyces are dark dull red with a few superficial yellow spicules. The polyps are red and yellow.
