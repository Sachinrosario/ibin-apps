SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small cells. Valves areolated, areolae 10 in 10m, arranged more or less in bundles, but not very clear. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Marginal spines present. Margin broad, cells dark under the microscope.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter of cells 29-53m.
