SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:    POST LARVAL STAGES:
Postlarva I. length 10.5 to 13.8mm. Rostrum bears 5 dorsal teeth and  an epigastric tooth; labral  and hepatic spines prominent; pterygostomian spine placed far behind the antero-inferior angle of carapace.Antennal scale very narrow with segmented endopod; mandibular palp with 2  segments and no setae; shows asymmetry in the number of denticular setae.Thoracic legs with exopods. Dorsomedian spines on abdominal segment 3, the longest and 4th slightly reduced than in mysis IV; ventral margin of each pleuron of ab.segs.1-5 form a spine each and that of the 5th pointed  and bent forward. Pleopods with  small exopods and endopods, exopods bearing setae. Exopod of uropods having more setae on the outer margin,towards the base. Telson  tapers towards the tip and with a median spine; the spine formula is 8+1+8.

Postlarva II.The rostrum is longer with an additional dorsal tooth. Mandibular parts become harder; dorsal spines on abdomen become reduced; no endopod on  1stpleopod; exopods with more  setae. Telson narrow and median spine longer than the terminal ones; spine formula unchanged.

Diagnostic characteristics:-The position of the pterygostomian spine(far behind the anteroinferior angle of carapace); presence of a labral spine and  dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 3-6;  that on the 3rd the longest; presence of ventromedian spines on the last thoracic and the 1st 2 abdominal segments ; and  the presence of  paired  dorsolateral spines on abdominal segments 5 and 6. 

Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit:  feeds on plankton.

Main food:  live and dead planktonic organisms; detritus with sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous

Additonal remarks:      This species is supposed to be a deepwater form and recorded from a depth of 1005 m(George and Paulinose1973).

Size and age: Postlarvae I&II: 10.5 mm to 14.5 mm.

Eggs and larvae:
Eggs not known.
LARVAL STAGES:

Protozoea II. Body length 1.7mm. Rostrum long,extends beyond 2nd segment of antennular peduncle,tip pointed,downcurved. Carapace without spines; a pair of supraorbital spines present. Antennule uniramous,endopod bearing 3 terminal setae. Antenna with exopod and segmented endopod; telson with a notch and each ramus bearing 7 setae.

Protozoea III. length 2.4mm-3.5mm. Carapace and rostrum same as in Protozoea II; rudiments  of pereopods present. First 5 abdominal segments  bear  dorsomedian spines; segments 5 and 6 also with a pair of dorsolateral spines. Abdominal segment 6 more  than twice the length of 5th and bears a pair of ventrolateral spines .Telsonic cleft becomes shallow,with no addition of spines.

Mysis I. length 3.7 mm. Rostrum long with a dorsal tooth+ epigastric. A pair of supraorbitals,hepatic and pterygostomian spines present; labral spine well developed. Antennule with 2 unsegmented flagella ; inner shorter and outer with a terminal seta; endopod of antenna  unsegmented. Mandibular palp is in the form of a small knob. Maxillipeds and thoracic legs with exopods.Dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 3-6; 5&6 with a pair of dorsolaterals and 6th again with a pair of ventrolaterals. Ventrmedian spines on last thoracic and  1st 2 abdominal segments. Pleopods not developed. Telson with  an    additional pair of  setae,8+8 terminal spines.

Mysis II. length  4.7 mm.Rostrum with 2 dorsal + epigastric tooth. Mandibular palp unsegmented; spines of abdomen not changed. Pleopods absent and telsonic notch reduced,spines remain 8+8.

Mysis III.length 6.0mm-6.5 mm.Rostrum long with 3 dorsal+epigastric; supraorbitals present; hepatic and pterygostomian spines more developed; labral spine prominent. Antennule with segmented flagella, bearing setae; antenna with a long narrow scale with numerous setae at the inner side and a terminal spine. Mandibular palp still unsegmented. Pereopods with exopods; dorsomedian spines on abdomen remain unchanged; uropods well developed; exopod with a terminal spine. Telson remains the same 8+8 spines and the cleft completely vanished.

Mysis IV. Length 7.0mm-8.7mm.The rostral tip pointed and tilted upwards. Mandibular palp shows sign of division; abdominal pleura form a spine lateroventrally. Pleopods become longer. Telson with a small median spine,8+1+8spines.

Characteristics: Rostrum long; a paired supraorbitals and hepatic spines present; pterygostomian spine is placed well behind the anteroinferior angle of carapace. Dorsomedian spine on abdominal segment 3, the longest. Ventromedian spines  on last  thoracic and 1st 2 abdominal segments; labral spine present. Abdominal segments 5 and 6 with a pair of dorsolateral spines and 6th with a pair of ventrolateral spines too.Telson with a median spine and 8 pairs of spines(4 terminal spines, 3 marginal and a posterolateral spine). The spine formula is 8+1+8.

Abundance: nil
