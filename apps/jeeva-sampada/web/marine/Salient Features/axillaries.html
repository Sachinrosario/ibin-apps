SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 1,8
Morphological: A medium sized species with a fairly deep body.
Diagnostic characteristics : Snout rounded; mouth terminal and oblique; rostral pores 3, marginal pores 5,the outer pair in a feeble notch; mental pores in 3 pairs, Teeth differentiated into large and small in both jaw, the large teeth not canine like forming the outer series in upper jaw and the inner series in lower jaw. Gillrakers slender and finely denticulate, 19 to 23 on lower limb of first arch. Dorsal fin with 9 or 10 spines, followed by deep notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 26 to 29 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 soft rays, Caudal fin rhomboid. Scales cycloid on head and nape, elsewhere ctenoid . swim bladder carrot shaped, with a pair of short, horn like simple appendages at its broad anterior end and entering head.

Colour : Grey / green on back, flanks yellow/ silver; a black blotch on pectoral fin axil; spinous part of dorsal fin black.
