Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of digitiform branches.  Growth form similar to A. humilis.  Sometimes corymbose.  Axial corallites 2 to 3 mm in diameter slightly exsert.  Radial corallites, tubular, dimidiate with flabellate spreading ones intercalated.  Larger radials 1 to 1.5 mm in diameter.  Septa in two cycles.

Living coral pale brown with blue axials.

The species differs from A. humulis in having smaller radials smaller axials and relatively thinner branches.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
