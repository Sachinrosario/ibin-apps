
SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological: 
  
Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female:  The genital segment  has a spine on the right side, but rarely has spines on both sides. The anterior antennae consists of 23 segments, of which the proximal 6 segments are thickened. 
     The 1st marginal spine of the 3rd segment of the 1st maxilliped is slightly shorter than the 2nd. The endopodite of the 1st foot has 2 segments. The terminal spine of the 3rd segment of exopodite of the 3rd foot is bent, and much shorter than the interval between 2 last marginal spines.
     The 5th pair of feet is symmetrical. The terminal segment has an outer marginal spine on seta, and a large inner marginal spine. The apex terminates 2 spines.
  
    Male: The lateral angles of the last thoracic segments are asymmetrical. The right side is more slender than the left, and curved inwardly. The 1st abdominal segment has a large process on the right side.
     The right anterior antenna consists of a grasping organ. The preceding 2 segments of the knee-like articulation are roughly denticulated. The terminal section consist of 5 or 6 segments.
     The 5th pair of feet is asymmetrical. The right foot makes a forceps.

Additional  remarks:
    Mori has described Candacia bicornuta which resembles Candacia curta. But the characters of the latter which is distributed near Japan, are slightly different from the description by Giesbrecht, and rather identical with the description of the former, except the spines on both sides of the genital segment of the female. So Mori identified C. cornuta  with C. curta.
