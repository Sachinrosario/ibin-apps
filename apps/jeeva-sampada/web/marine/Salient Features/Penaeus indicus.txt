SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:    Post-larva: I:  Length. 5.56mm.The rostrum long, dorsal teeth present; ventral tooth present in postlarva V onwards (juveniles); carapace with well developed supraorbital and hepatic spines; pterygostomian spines also present; dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 4 -6,some times on 3rd too; posterolateral spines on 5 and 6 ab.segs.; a pair of minute  ventrolateral spines and a hooked, prominent  posteromedian ventral spine present on seg. 6; thoracic legs with vestigial exopods;  pleopods well developed, setose and uniramous;  uropods with endo and exopods with equal length; an externodistal spine prominent on exopod; telson is always with 8+8 spines.

Diagnostic characteristics:-Long rostrum; long body; 6th ab.seg. more than twice the length of  5th segment; rostrum with dorsal and ventral teeth; telson with 8+8 spines;  dorsomedian spines present on 4 -6 abdominal segments and 6th with a pair of dorsolateral spines.   

Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: pelagic zone. 

Main food:  plankton, detritus; organic matter with  sand and mud.

Feeding type:omnivorous

Additonal remarks: considering the commercial value and importance, the size andthe tolerance to the low salinity ,they are largely grown in brackish water areas(chemmeen kettu).

Size and age: Protozoea III- post larva I measures 2.8 mm- 5.56mm.

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed)  200 mm. 

Longevity (y) (wild) :  2-31/2 years.      

Eggs and larvae:

Eggs: Spherical in shape,opaque with narrow perivitelline space; dia.0.25-0.27mm; yolkmass 0.22-0.24mm.

Nauplii I-VI: length from 0.30mm-0.48mm. 3 pairs of anterior appendages present; 4th seta on the exopod of  antenna bifurcate at tip; mandible with 3 setae each on endo and exopods; the furcal setae become 7+7 in nauplius VI. Protozoea I: 0.8mm.Carapace round anteriorly; frontal organs present as protuberances; ocellus persists; carapace with segmented thorax and unsegmened abdomen; maxillae and maxillipeds appear.

Protozoea II: 1.52mm. Carapace with a pair of strong supraorbital spines,subdivided at its base; rostrum long and eyes stalked; frontal organs absent; abdomen with median spines on somites 1-5; a pair of dorsolaterals on 5-6 somites; telson with 8+8 setae.

Protozoea III: 2.69mm.Rostrum long and straight; sub-supraorbital spines absent; biramous buds of maxillipeds and legs appear; telson demarcated from abdomen; exopod of uropod slightly longer; spine present at tip; telson unchanged.

Mysis I: 3.36mm.Rostrum long extends beyond eyes; supraorbitals and hepatic prominent; mandible asymmetrical (Paulinose 1973); maxillipeds and pereopods biramous; more setae on uropods.Rostrum pointed; spines on carapace remain; legs 1-3 chelate; pleopods uniramous, non-functional; telson more truncate with 8+8 spines.

Mysis III: 4.17mmRostrum extends beyond eyes; one dorsal and an epigastric tooth present; hepatic strong; mandible with unsegmented palp; abdominal spines remain; pleopods 2-sgmented; still non-functional.

PostlarvaI: 5.03mm.Rostrum with 2 dorsal + epigastric tooth ; small dorsomedian spine on ab.segs.4-6; legs with small exopods; endopods 5-segmented; telson  spines remain same; ventral teeth appear on rostrum, after 5th postlarva.

Mysis II: 3.58mm.

Characteristics: A pair of subsupraorbital spines present in prtozoea II; long rostrum; very strong hepatic spine; dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 4 -6; 6th somite is more than twice the length of 5th; pleopods uniramous; telson  with 8+8 spines.

Abundance: Abundant in the southwest,and east coasts of India; 3rd abundant species in the prawn fishery.
