Descriptive characters:  
Description of a specimen from Krusadai Island:  Corallum cespitose from an encrusting base; greater spread 30 cm.  Branches numerous, single or in groups of 3 to 4 rather digitiform, 4 to 5 cm long, 10 to 12 mm thick, apices tapering with a few proliferations.  Peripheral branches oblique.  Axial corallites 3 mm in diameter at the top, slightly more at the base; 1 to 1.5 mm exsert, wall thick and porous; opening rounded.  Septa 12, primaries larger than the secondaries, reach to the centre of fossa.  Radial corallites tubular, a few bearing buds.  The proliferous ones 7 to 9 mm long 2 to 3.5 mm thick, non-proliferous ones 7 to 9 mm long and 2 mm thick, inner wall less developed than the outer.  A few subimmersed and immersed corallites are found scattered among the projecting radials.  Projecting radials spreading to 450.  In young radials only the directive of the first cycle of septa are seen, in old radials the first cycle is fully represented.  Wall of the radials porous, outer wall striated with plate-like echinulations.  Surface coenenchyme reticualte and echinulate.

In two specimens obtained from Port Blair, Andamans some of the branches are upto 4.5 cm thick at the base.

Living coral in Gulf of Mannar brown with white tips.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
