SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca conical, in the girdle region, slightly convex, transformed into a cone like horn. Girdle notched, slightly sinistral. Hypotheca slightly concave.

Diagnostic characteristics: Posterior horns each carry a smooth, long, solid process.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 130µ; Breadth 120µ,  Diameter: 37µ.
