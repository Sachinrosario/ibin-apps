Salient Features :
Annual, more or less erect, much branched, branches rather slender, opposite.
Stem fleshy, usually jointed.
Flowers bisexual, sunken in the cavity of the joints, spikes 1-2 inches.
Seeds pale-brown.
This is a salt tolerant species and salts are accumulated till the death of the plant.
Industries:
Nil.
Health:
Ash used in itch and considered as abortifacient.
Nutrition:
Pickles, salad and cattle feed.
R & D:
Some times uses as pot herbs. Promising cash crop for saline wastelands.
