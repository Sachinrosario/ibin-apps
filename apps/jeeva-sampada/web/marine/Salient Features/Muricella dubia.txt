SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colonies subflabellate and branched in one plane; branches 
simple or divide dichotomously without any trace of fusion. The basal attachment zone
 is retained in both specimens. Size of the first specimen, 3 x 2 cm and of the other, 
5.8 x 3 cm.

		Calyces are borne on both sides as also along the front and back portion 
of the stem, and branches, they are globular in shape with a size of 0.6 x 1 mm-
 (height x diameter), and covered with warty spindles which are irregularly placed. 
Polyps retractile completely, operculum is usually sunk and is made of longitudinal 
spindles arranged En chevron over the tentacular base. Diameter of the stalk is 0.75 mm 
and that of branches about 0.5 mm.

Spicules	:	(1) Warty spindles, tuberculated, short and stout; one end broader
 than the other in some. Size upto 0.35 x 0.16 mm (Fig.a). (2) Smaller spindles (Fig.b) and 
crosses are also common in both specimens (Fig.c).

Colour	:	Colony light brown, axis dark brown fading to light brown in branches, 
spicules colourless.
