Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum cespito-corymbose, peripheral branches prostrate.  Branchlets 2 to 7 cm long about 1 cm thick.  Apices of branches taper to a large conical axial corallite 2.5 to 4 mm in diameter 2-3 mm exsert.  Radial corallites nariform or tubular, ascending, thick, opening small.  Outer wall narrow beaked.  Larger radials 5 to 6 mm long about 2 mm thick, arranged in longitudinal rows along the log axis of the branches.  Surface coenenchyme finely echinulate.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio.
