
SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characters:     

Female :    The forehead is produced in small spinous projection.  The posterior thoracic margins are bluntly rounded.  The 1st antenna overreaches the tip of the furcal ramus by about the last two segments.  The proximal segments are provided with long plumose setae.  In the 2nd antenna the exopod is less than half the length of the endopod.  It appears to be composed of only 6 segments, of which the 1st is nearly equal in length to the rest of the appendage.  The mandible is of the same general type as that of H. chierchia.  In the 1st maxilla the biting ramus tapers towards the distal end and bears only two stout serrated teeth and 4 delicate setae.  The 2nd inner lobe is small and rounded and bears a single long seta.  The 3rd inner lobe bears 3 setae.  The 2nd basal segment bears 5 setae, and the endopod is much reduced and forms a small rounded lobe bearing only two setae.  The endopod is long and comparatively slender and bears 10 setae, of which the proximal 6 are well developed and the distal 4 are small and delicate. The outer lobe bears 3 very delicate proximal setae and 6 well-developed distal ones.        
The 2nd maxilla resembles that of H. ornatus.  The 1st lobe bears 3 setae and is produced in a small pointer process.  Lobes 2, 3 and 4 each bear 2 setae ; the 5th lobe bears 1 seta and a stout serrated spine.  Lobe 6 bears a stout serrated spine and 2 setae.       
The swimming legs resemble those of other members of the genus.  In the 5th leg the 2nd basal segment bears externally a delicate seta that reaches the distal end of the exopod.  

Male not recorded.
