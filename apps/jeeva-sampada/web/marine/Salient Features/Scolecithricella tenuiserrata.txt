
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:            
Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment angular. Abdomen 4-segmented. Genital segment not swollen below. First antenna 19- segmented, reaches up to the tip of the genital segment. Fifth leg 1-jointed. As against the plate like structure of the female 5th leg in the genera, in this case the joints are slightly cylindrical. One small apical spine and a long inner marginal spine on the terminal joint. In male 5th leg endopod of right leg is very small. Distal segment of exopod is transformed into a short spine. Endopod of the left leg slightly smaller than the 1st joint of the exopod. Exopod 3-segmented. Second segment with small hairs at the distal end. Third segment very small.
