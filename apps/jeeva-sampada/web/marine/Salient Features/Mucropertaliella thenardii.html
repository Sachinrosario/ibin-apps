SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium small, encrusting. Zooecia delicate, long, convex, with inconspicuous septal lines. Spines generally 2-6, long. Slender. Orifice more or less circular, or wider than long. Lyrula narrow, bidentate, its sinuses wider than long, the lateral denticles recurved. Mucro unusually variable; long, cylindrical and simple, or with a lateral branch near its free end or more basally, on one or both sides; the branches sometimes with secondary branches. Lateral oral avicularia often on short raised columns. A large spatulate avicularium sometimes found, in connection with the mucro. Ovicells smaller than in most species of the genus, the lip considerably raised above the level of the operculum.Diagnostic characters: .
Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: The range of variation of this species is very great. In the Siboga specimens, the suboral mucro is nearly always simple.
  The avicularia are wide, median and vertical with ascending rostrum in the specimens of Thornely and Kirkpatrick. They have numerous minute pores as in other species, the only difference being their smaller size. According to Robertson (1921) they are rather wider and her Petralia laccadivensis is clearly a synonym of Audouins species.
