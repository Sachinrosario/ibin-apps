SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear-elliptical valves with broadly rounded poles and slightly drawn in sides in the middle. Raphe straight, narrow. Central nodule quadrate, wavy at the sides with well-developed hours. Furrows narrow.

Diagnostic characteristics: Transapical costae very well developed, robust, swollen at the tip, 6 in 10m.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 60-74m long and 23-28m broad.
