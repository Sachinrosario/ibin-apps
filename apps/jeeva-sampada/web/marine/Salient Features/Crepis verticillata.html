SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Small zoarium whitish in hue when dry. Zooecium creeping with long tubular proximal portion and oval distal expansion usually associated with another single zooecium where the tubular part may be shortened or suppressed. Margin of the oval part occupied by the frontal membrane elevated. Operculum membraniporine. Depressed and extensive cryptocyst. Avicularia absent.
Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: Harmer has given great importance to such characters as the verticillate arrangement of the zooecia, the raised distal ends, the connected oblique orientation of the frontal membrane and the suppression of the tubular proximal portion of the lateral zooecia. In the present material only one lateral zooecium is present and rarely the lateral zooecia has less developed proximal tubular portions. The lateral zooecium always occupies a position slightly above its partner. In this respect, this form clearly distinguishes itself from C. longipes. Apart from the occurrence of a solitary partner, the form under consideration agrees in all important details with the figures and description of C. verticillata Harmer.   Meristic characteristics:
