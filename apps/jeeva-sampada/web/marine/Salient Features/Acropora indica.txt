Descriptive characters:  Corallum cespito-corymbose from abroad or pedicellate base.  Generally colonies around Rameswaram, 10 to 20 cm in greater spread.  Total height including the base 10 to 15 cm.  Several branches arise from the base, 10 to 12 mm thick which divides two or three times; ultimate branchlets 2 to 3 cm long about 1 cm thick with suddenly tapering spices.  Generally two or three small proliferations destined to become branchlets are also seen.  Axial corallites 2.5 to 3 mm in diameter, 2 mm exsert; wall porous and costulate; opening rounded, large; primary cycle of septa well developed with the directives fusing each other deep down in the fossa.  The radials are of different size and shapes.  They are nariform, half-tubular labellate and immersed.  Some of the larger ones are proliferous.  Non-proliferous radials 1 to 1.5 mm in diameter 1.5 to 2 mm long, spreading, sometimes at right angles to the plane of the branch surface within aval opening.  Labellate corallites average 1 mm in length and width.  Towards the basal parts of the branches majority of the radials are immersed.  Wall of the radials costulate, rather dense.  In some cases the larger radials are arranged in longitudinal rows.  In old radials all the primaries are seen, while in young ones only the directives are seen.  Surface coenenchyme at the basal parts of the corallite-wall, with palate-like echinulations.  Coenenchyme porous in section.  Living coral brown with white axial corallites.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
