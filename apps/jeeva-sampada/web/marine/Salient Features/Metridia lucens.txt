
SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 


Diagnostic characteristics:   
      Female: Dorsal side of prosome more convex than in M. longa and M. okhotensis. Posterior corners of last thoracic segment with small acute apex, latter visible both dorsally and laterally. Abdomen slightly less than half-length of cephalothorax. Genital segment slightly shorter than caudal rami. Fifth pair of legs 3- jointed, some times with defined fourth joint (in specimens of the Atlantic coast of America); distal joint with 3 bristles, inner bristle clearly longer than the rest; same joint bearing rather long outer spine; distal margin of second joint with similar spine.

     Male: Distal joint of right leg larger than corresponding joint of left leg and not narrowed distally as in M. longa. Left leg with one large spine on inner side of fourth joint. Right leg 4- jointed.
