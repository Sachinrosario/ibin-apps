SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:   Zooecia large, produced into a cylindrical, imperforate, peristome, with a more or less everted margin. Frontal shield with large foramina, each surrounded by a short tubular wall, produced unilaterally into a sharp, triangular spike. Operculum weak without basal sclerite. Avicularia and ovicells not formed. Diagnostic characters:   Teuchopora verrucosa (Canu & Bassler, 1924) shows many resemblances to E. tuberculata. But in T. verrucosa, the zooecia are very large, their frontal walls commonly have small pores thus differing from those of Exechonella.
Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:   The characteristic features of this species are illustrated in the Siboga material. The characteristic frontal spikes are clearly visible in the specimen, some placed so as to conceal the foramina. The two genera Teuchopora and Exechonella may possibly prove to belong to the same major group; in which case, Harmer is of the opinion that the structure of Teuchopora have been derived from that of Exechonella and not vice versa.Meristic characteristics:
