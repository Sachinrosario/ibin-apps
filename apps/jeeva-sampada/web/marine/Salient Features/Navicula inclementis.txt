SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves rhombic with rounded apices. Polar nodules, small, sub-apiculate, slightly removed from the valve apices.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Striae radiate throughout, about 11 in 30m near the apices, 7 in 10m near the middle.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 55-62m, width 14-18m.
