SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells rhombic dorsiventrally flattened somewhat. Girdle circular, excavated. Intercalary plates two.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus more or less of uniform width, margin without wings, reaches Antapical end. No Antapical spines.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 37µ; Breadth: 34µ.
