SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: A stout bird with strong straight bill which is swollen at the tip.



Diagnostic characteristics: Mottled brown, black and white feathers. Bill long and slightly swollen at the tip. A blackish line running from the base of bill to eye.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Forager.

Main Food: Worms, grubs, seeds and shoots of plants.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Though not a strict coast dwelling bird, it is often seen in the plantations adjoining the coast.
