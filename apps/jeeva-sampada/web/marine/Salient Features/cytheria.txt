Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Colonies vasiform, pedicellate, main branches 1 to 1.5 cm thick, spreading, reticulate, coalascent with wide interspaces.  Branches at the upper side of the corallum in clumps of 3-5, 1 to 3 cm long, 3 to 6 mm thick, arcuate with tapering tips.  Axial corallites average, 2 mm thick and exsert, wall fragile, porous, opening rounded.  Septa six.  Radials, labellate, appressed, ascending, lips thin, septa six, 2 to 4 mm long, average 2 mm wide.  Coenosteum reticulate.

Living coral dull brown with white axials.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  Following Wells (1954) Pillai (1986) treated this species with A. hyacinthus.  However, many Australian workers, recently separated these two species and is followed here.  It differs from hyacinthis in larger branchlets with larger radials.  

This species formed large colonies upto about a meter in Palk bay in early sixties.  However, at present it is rare due to heavy destruction.
