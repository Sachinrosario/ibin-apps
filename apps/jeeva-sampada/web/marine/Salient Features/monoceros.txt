SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:          Rostrum short,extends to ¾ the eyes; bears 3 dorsal teeth and a small epigastric tooth; the antennal scale reaches beyond the 2nd peduncular segment of antennule; pterygostomian and hepatic spines present; dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments 5 and 6 only. Antennal spine strong; telson and uropods of equal length; telsonic spines 7+7 and 1st pair of thoracic legs with an ischial and basial spine.

Meristic characteristics:
Feeding habit: feed on plankton,algae and living & dead  organic matters.Main food:  plankton, detritus with  sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous.Additonal remarks: 

Size and age:  Protozoea I- III measure from 0.8mm – 2.9mm.

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed)-  130mm.                            

Longevity (y) (wild) : 2-3 years. 

Eggs and larvae: 

Eggs: Spherical and brownish in appearance; opaque and demersal; a diameter of 0.22mm; embryonic development after 15-17 hours.      6 Naupliar stages, ranging  size from 0.26-0.41mm.

Body  pear-shaped,unsegmented; anterior region broad and a median eye ocellus present and posterior end round, bearing a pair of furcal setae; 3 pairs of appendages present. Antennule-uniramous; antenna and mandible biramous as in all other cases. The 4th inner seta of the exopod of antenna is bifid at its tip, in 2nd nauplius and prevails throughout following stages. In later stages setae become plumose; furcal setae increases in number as the stage advances from 1pair to 7 pairs in nauplius VI.
Protozoea:size range 0.8-2.9mm.It is universally agreed to have 3 protozoeal stages; carapace round anteriorly with median eye; antennule 3 segmented; basal segment subdivided into 5 segments. Mandible without palp,incisor and molar parts separated; maxillule with exopod like a small knob,bearing 4 long plumose setae; telson with 7+7 spines.

Protozoea II: bears dorsomedian spines on abdominal segmrnts 1-5; rostrum pointed; supraorbital spine present; eyes stalked and telson with 7+7 spines.

Protozoea III: abdominal segment 5 with a pair of dorsolateral spines; uropods biramous; telson spines unchanged.Mysis stages: size 2.4 -3.5mm.In Mysis I the rostrum is pointed,extends beyond the eyes; no dorsal tooth; antennal and pterygostomian spines present; dorsomedian spines on 5-6 abdominal segments; 6th segment with a prominent ventrmedian spine .

In mysis II,rostrum with a dorsal tooth; telson rectangular,median cleft persists; thoracic legs with exopods.

MysisIII: rostrum with 2 dorsal teeth; a small hepatic spine present; pleopod buds appear,unsegmented; developing chela seen in legs 1-3; telson cleft more reduced,with 7+7 spines.

Mysis IV: this stage has not much change,as these are reared in the laboratory,stages may be more in development; normally there are only 3 mysis stages.

Mysis V: the pleopods are segmented; telson tip truncate.

Mysis VI: pleopods elongated,uniramous; mandible with a segmented palp,bearing setae; legs fully developed; exopod of uropod bears an exteno-distal spine and 16 plumose sete. Telson spines unchanged.

Postlarva I: size 3.02mm. Rostrum very much reduced in length,with 3 dorsal teeth; hepatic prominent; legs 1-5 with reduced exopods,without setae; pleopods uniramous,functional with plumose setae; dorsomedian spines reduced to a minute one on the 6th ab.seg.; exopods absent on legs 5-6; spine formula of telson unchanged.  

Characteristics:Rostrum short; legs with reduced exopods or absent on 4 -5 legs; dorsomedian spinespresent only on ab.seg.6; telson carries7 setae from last nauplius to post larva. Reduction in the length of rostrum, certain spines and exopods of legs.

Abundance: One of the most important commercially exploited shrimps along the coasts of India; also fished in large quantities from backwaters and also from traditional  paddy-cum-prawn fishery in Kerala.
