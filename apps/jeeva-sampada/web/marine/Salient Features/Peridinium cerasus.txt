SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells almost globular. Girdle dextral, not excavated, with narrow lists supported by spines. Sulcus narrow. Two fine antapical spines, in individual cells variable.

Diagnostic characteristics: Intercalary striae often present. Membrane surface is always robustly punctate or pitted.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 54-65µ. Transdiameter with girdle: 30-58µ.
