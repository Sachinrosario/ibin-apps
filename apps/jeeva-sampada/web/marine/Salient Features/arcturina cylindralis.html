SALIENT FEATURES :

Morphological : Body cylindrical, strongly bent between the fourth and fifth peraeon segments, moderately hairy and without tubercles. Cephalon large, clearly separated from the first peraeon segment. Anterior border of cephalon rounded and the antero-lateral corners very slightly produced. Peraeon segments one to three partially united into a large composite segment, its ventral edge expanded and produced ventralwards, posteriorly broader. Fourth peraeon segment slightly longer than the body in front of it, anteriorly broad and posteriorly constricted. Ventral border faintly serrated, lateral sides with an indistinct longitudinal groove. Peraeon segments five to seven subequal, each with a prominent dorsal ridge. Pleon three jointed shorter than telson, telson elongate triangular with a prominent constriction at its base.

First antenna short reaching the distal border of third penduncular segment of second antenna flagellum as long as peduncle with four groups of sensory setae. Second antenna slightly more than half as long as body prominently hairy fourth and fifth penduncular segments equal in length flagellum three jointed third segment long ending in a spine like prolongation. Lower border of flagellum with a single row of sharp spines.

Cutting edge of mandible strong and produced with three subsidiary teeth, accessory plate bidentate, spine row composed of two spines; molar broad and well developed. Innerlobe of first maxilla with three plumose setae and a strong spine, outer lobe with eleven sharp spines in two rows, its outer border thickly hairy. Inner plate of maxilliped large, with a single strong coupling hook, distal border truncate and slightly concave, with an inner seta and an outer ooth. Third segment of palp longest, terminal segment well developed and setose.

Second segment of first peraeopod longest, its inferior border hairy, fourth segment short but dorsally expanded sixth segment as long as third seventh well developed with long unguis; sixth segment with an inner row of long strongly pectinate setae succesiive increasing in length diastalwards. Peraepods two and three identical segments three to six flattenend almostequal in length seventh segment small with three long setae. Fourth peraeopod similar to third but more slender. Peraeopods five to seven comparitvely very long fifth peraeopod longer than sixth and seventh; inner border of segments five and six of seventh peraeopod expanded the expansion of sixth segment with a long apically blunt spine seventh segment long and with a distinct unguis. Uropods elongate oblong distal segment rounded outer border with dense seae concealed segment small with a single apical seta.

Body slightly grayish with balck chromatophores.


Additional remarks:

Arcturina cylindralis differs from Arcturina rhomboidalis and Arcturina hexagonalis Barnard in the shape of the fourth peraeon segment which is nearly cylindrical. The first three peraeon segments are united into a composite segment. The seventh peraeopod has a characteristic expansion and the first peraeopod has several prominent pectinate setae.

Size and Age : Length : 6.0 mm
