Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  The corallum is massive or columnar.  The gibbosites fusing to form large colonies.  The calices are 1.25 to 1.5 mm in length, polygonal.  Septa narrow at first later broadening to descent down.  Septal edges generally devoid of any teeth.  Outer synapticular ring close to the wall, inner surrounding a columella tangle.  The pali of the lateral pairs of septa very prominent, those of the dorsal directive and ventral triplet suppressed.  Septal ends united to the columella be radii.  

The species is the ecological equvalent of P. solida in the Pacific.  The major difference is in the lack of septal denticles and in the prominence of the pali.  The growth form is also different.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
