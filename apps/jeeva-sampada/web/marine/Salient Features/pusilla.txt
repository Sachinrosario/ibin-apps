SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  

Genus Pseudanchialina   Hansen

Diagnosis :  Carapace covering the whole of the thorax,  frontal plate large, hind border feebly concave.  Eyes well developed.  Antennular peduncle of male thicker than in female.  Antennal scale small, rapidly broadening distalwards, outer distal spine long, terminal suture present, setae very long.  Labrum anteriorly produced into a spine.  Endopod of thoracic limbs slender, sixth segment divided into a few subsegments.  First abdominal segment in female with lateral wings, sixth segment longer than the combined length of the two previous segments.  Pleopods in female simple, third pair in male as in Gastrosaccus, with long peduncle.  Uropods slender, with long setae, exopod not longer than endopod, its outer margin naked, with a susapical spine.  Telson as long as or longer than uropod, with a few evenly spaced lateral spines, apex incised, with two rows of spine, extreme lateral spines very long.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Pseudanchialina pusilla           

General form small. Carapace in female with large broadly rounded frontal plate; in male narrower. Antennal scale small, less than three times as long as broad, rapidly broadening distalwards, outer margin naked ending in spine, terminal suture present , setae very long ;  antennal sympod produced into sharp inner distal spine. Anterior spine like prolongation of labrum with secondary spinules. Endopods of thoracic limbs three to eight slender, sixth segment divided into few sub-segments ; dactylus minute and without claw. In male first, fourth and fifth pleopods rudimentary. Second pair of pleopods with very short endopod; exopod two segmented terminating in two long setae. Exopod of third pleopod long, two segmented reaching beyond middle of sixth abdominal segment, with three long apical setae. Telson less than three times as long as broad at base, lateral border with 7-9 spines, apex incised each lobe with 5-6 spines, extreme lateral spine very long. Uropod slender, with long setae ; exopod slightly longer than endopod, outer margin naked with sub apical spine.
