Salient Features:
Morphological:

Diagnostic Characteristics:
Body elongate ovate, narrowing towards both ends. First peraeon segment slightly longer than secondand subequal to fifth and sixth. Third pleon segment considerably produced, reaching beyond the base of the uropods. Posterior border of peraeon segments two to seven with small spines, fifteen on fifth, eleven on sixth and thirteen on seventh; the spines on the other segments not distinct. Pleon segments two to four each with five teeth, fifth with three; the odd median tooth on each segment slightly larger. Telson perfectly triangular, apex narrow and rounded and with eight spines.

Frontal lamina narrow and elongate, antero-lateral sides straight, meeting to form an acute angle. Fourth segment oif first peraeopod with six blunt teeth, fifth immersed, sixth with four sharp spines. Endopod of uropod perfectly rounded and with twelve spines, exopod distally narrowed and with four inner and eight outer spines. Appendix masculina on the seventh segment like a pair of mammiform papillae placed close together and appearing as a bilobed bulge

Colour of body very characteristic, with a broad median dark area widening distally; laterally the chromatophores form reticulate patterns with clear yellow areas in between; telson dorsally black; general colour dark grey.

Sex Attributes:
Development of fertilized eggs takes place in the brood pouch. Number of young ones in a brood varies from 5 to 15.

Feeding Habit:  Carnivorous, Voraceous feederi.

Main Food:  Feeds on living and dead organisms

Additional Remarks:
The species is esseentially eurvhalin, Tolerating wide ranges of salinity variants and are capable of  living in waters with Low Oxygen content.

Cirolana Willeyi is a swift moving isopod and are very common on submerged timber Very often it is found in burrows of wood borers and are likely to be mistaken as a wood-borer. It avoids light and are found hiding in burrows, crevices and empty shells of fouling organisms. The species is important as natural enemy of wood borers and feeds on shipworms and sphaeromatids also. It is also a predator of slow moving organisms like small crabs, prawn etc. In Akathumuri Lake, it is found on laterite rocks.

Size and age: Maximum Length: 10.0 mm.
