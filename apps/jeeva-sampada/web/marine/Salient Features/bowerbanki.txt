SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Coralla encrusting.  Corallites polygonal or distorted, 30 to 35 mm long and 25 to 30 mm broad, 8-12 mm deep; monostomodeal, wall sometimes fused.  Total number of septa range from 60 to 72 of which 15-18 reach columella.  Septa exsert, slightly swollen at the wall, edges with 4 to 6 teetch, the upper tooth about 1 mm long and thick.  An epitheca is often present at the growing edge of the corallum.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
