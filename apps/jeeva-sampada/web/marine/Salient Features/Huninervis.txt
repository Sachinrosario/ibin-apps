SALIENT FEATURES OF Halodule uninervis (Forsk.) Asch. 1882

Leaves: Shoots upto 30cm long and erect, having 2-4 leaves in each branch, leaf linear, narrowed at base with sheath, margin entire, nerves 3, midrib conspicuous and lateral ribs inconspicuous, ending in well developed lateral teeth at leaf apex, teeth tridentate 

Inflorescence: Male flowers 2, subequal, female flowers sessile and enclosed in leaf sheaths Perianth: Absent 

Ovary: Ovoid with 2-3 (8cm long) slender, smooth, terminal styles

Fruit: Subglobose to ovoid, usually in pairs with persistent styles

Flowering and fruiting: June-July
