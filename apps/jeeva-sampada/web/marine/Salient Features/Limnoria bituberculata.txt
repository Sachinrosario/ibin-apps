SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :
Ref No: Pillai, 1961

First  peraeon segment with a broad V-shaped groove. Fifth pleon segment with the median portion slightly elevated, lateral crests prominent. Telson nearly circular, lateral crests prominent, without tubercles, their margin broadly crenate. Proximal median part of telson raised, with 2 low tubercles joined by an arcuate carina. Distal border with long setae mixed with small ones, tubercles and spine like setae absent.

Flagellum of both antennae 4 segmented. Palp of mandible reduced to a seta. Setal row composed of bundle of 5 subsimilsr sparsely dentate setae. Lacinioid seta like the steae of the setal row, bent at right angles in the middle and bifid beyond. Second segment of sympod of maxilliped broadening distalwards, inner lobe apically truncate, with 4 simple and 4 pectinate setae. Epipod clavate, very slighttly overreaching the second segment of sympod, broadest slightly behinf the tip, border hairy and setose.

First peraeopod with the 2 customary spines, secondary unguis faintly bifid, upper border of merus dentate. Merus of sixth leg with 2 and carpus with 4 large spines; merus of seventh leg with 3 and carpus with 5 spines.

Peduncle of uropod stout, roughly as long as endopod, exopod half as long as endopod, with a 
prominent apical claw.

Feeding Habit :
boring into the holdfasts of sea-weeds

Main food : Cellulose

Size and Age : Maximum size recorded 2.5mm
