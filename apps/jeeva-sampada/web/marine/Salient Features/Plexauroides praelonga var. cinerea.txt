SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Specimen, a part of a large colony, measuring 19.5 x 4.5 cm 
(height x width); branching sparse and wide apart, branches arranged in the plane of colony except two branchlets which were arranged at right angles to the plane of colony; diameter of main branch 2 mm.

			
Coenenchyme denuded off at places, but wherever intact covered with epizoic animals. Spicules colourless, typical of varirety.

			
Spicules : (1) Leaf-clubs. With one main leaf, with or without lateral leaves; both main and lateral leaves serrated at their margins. Basal part of spicule with 2 to 5 root-like tuberculated structures (Fig.a). (2) Spindled. Size upto 0.4 x 0.065 mm (Fig.b) (3) Multiradiates (Fig.c).

Colour	:	Colony dirty gray when dry, axis black and spicules colourless.
