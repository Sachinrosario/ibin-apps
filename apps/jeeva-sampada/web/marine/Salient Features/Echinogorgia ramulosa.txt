SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branching in plane. The branches and the twigs stand alternatively in right angles. The ends of the branches are thickened. The operculum is highly conical and consists of each 3 spindles of 0.26 mm in length. The calyx walls are arranged tile-like contour. The polyps are disposed irregularly over the whole coenenchyma. The verrucae are not prominent but give the surface an undulating contour. The anthocodiae are almost completely retractile.		
		Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are thorned and heavily warted and disc-shaped of 0.3 mm in length and 0.2 mm in breadth.

Colour			:	Colony is light brown  in colour and the spicules 
				colourless.
