SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Body elongate and parallel sided, becoming slightly broader behind. Cephalon with its anterior border concave and antero-lateral sinous. Eyes subdorsal and dark with well developed ocelli. Peraeon segments sybequal in length, third longest. Segments one to three bent forwards, fourth straight and segments five to seven bent backwards. Coxal plates well developed one to three triangular and five to seven rounded. Telson elongate-ovate, posterior border very short and sub-truncate, with the margin double convex.

First antenna very short, first segment stout, flagellum seven to eight jointed, with long sensory setae. Second antenna very long, peduncle five jointed, first three segments togther equal to fifth. Third segment with a small scale , twice as long as broad and quarters as long as body. Upper lip semicircular, prominently hairy. Maxilliped with a single coupling hook, first three segments of palp considerably expanded. Outer lobe of first maxilla with eight to ten large, barbed spines and inner lobe with four stout setae. Inner lobe of second maxilla oblong or elliptic and thickly hairy. Incisor process of mandible demarcated by a prominent constriction, bilobed and each lobe four dentate. Middle row formed of six long spine like setae. Molar produced and well toothed, with two or four inferior setae. Palp well developed, with subequal segments. Lower lip clearly bilobed, the lobes slightly overlapping in the middle.

First peraeopod subchelate carpus short and triangular propodus very large and widening towards the apex, with two very large spines on the distal lower border, dactylus very long and slender, almost reaching the base of fifth segment. Dactylus with a number of small hairs on the lower border and a few long setae near the apex slightly swollen in front of the base and thence progressively narrowing and ending in an abruptly narrow apex. Peraeopods two to seven similar, typically as in the genus, carpus produced at the distal part of its dorsal border, dactylus short and biunguiculate. First pleopd with peduncle completely united rami fused with peduncle, terminating in acute spine like processes and with a row of sharp slightly curved spines on the free border. Stylet on second pleopod inserted at the middle of the inner border and slightly overreaching the tip of the ramus distal border of the ramus with three plumose setae. Uropods well developed, longer than telson, endopod longer than exopod and thrice as long as peduncle.
Body white, turning white in spirit.

Additional Remarks:
The size and nature of thr first gnathopod clearly distinguish Bagatus longimannus from other species of the genus.

Size and Age :
