Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics:
General body form very slender, thorax greatly elongated between first and second thoracic limbs, displaying long bare region of body ventrally. Carapace very short and without rostral plate; anterior margin transeverse with four symmetrically placed blunt spines. Posterior margin of first five abdominal segments with three pairs of sharp spines. Eyes long, carried on long slender stalk. Antennal scale reduced to conical spine. Second thoracic endopod large and prehensile. Thoracic limbs extremely long and slender with carpus and propopdus distinct. Pleopods in male well developed and biramous, modified setae absent. Telson small, one and a half times as long as broad, apex bilobed, each lobe tipped with short spine; lateral margin unarmed. Uropods long with subequal rami.
