SALIENT FEATURES	


Description	:	Colony unbranched. Axis calcareous, thin, hair-like measuring 18 cm. in length. The coenenchyma on the axis is thick; the spicules composing it are arranged side by side, and are intercalated with one another. The polyps are placed on the stem, at distances of about 6 mm. apart. They are arranged in a uniserial manner on the axis, and measure about 3:5 mm. in height by 1 mm. in width; the body portion of each polyp is covered with broad, spindle - shaped spicules, which are larger and denser on the portion of the body nearer the axis; at the base of the tentacles, and extending for a little way down the body of the polyp, the spicules are arranged in eight rows, which on the tentacles being inverted, become folded over the oral cavity and form an eight-rayed quasioperculum
		Spicules: The spicules of the polyp are large, irregular or dendate in structure. 
Colour	:	The stem is  yellowish white in colour.
