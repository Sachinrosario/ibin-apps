Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting thick.  Corallites with distinct margins, projecting to 1 mm, wall thin, separated by narrow intercorallite areas.  Calices oval or distorted, 3.5 – 5 mm in diameter, mode 4 mm, depth about 2 mm, funnel shaped, when seen from above.  There are 24-28 septa, but in a large calyx 5 mm in diameter there are 40 septa.  Septa exsert, arched, either stopping at the middle of the intercorallite area or sometimes confluent between adjacent walls.  Septal edges serrated, the serrations becoming larger at the lower half of the septum.  12 to 13 septa reach the columella, others turns towards the major ones.  Columella poorly developed, composed of a crowded cluster of papillae, merging with the larger septal serrations.  Washed and dried coral black, but the exset parts of septa white.  The genus is rare.  Living coral chocate brown.  

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
