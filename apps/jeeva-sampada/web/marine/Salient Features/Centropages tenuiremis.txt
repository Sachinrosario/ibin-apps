SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics: -         
Female:  Posterior corners of the last pedigerous segment produced into long, sharp, asymmetrical spines; left spine directed obliquely backward and laterally, right spine directed backward; spines projecting beyond distal margin of genital segment. Genital segment smooth, without armature, except for ventral hook, only slightly inflated laterally in the distal part. Caudal rami symmetrical, 2.5 times longer than anal segment, with short symmetrical bristles. Fifth pair of legs asymmetrical; right exopodite 2-jointed, inner process of proximal joint more than 2/3 the length of the joint, covered with tubercles and denticles, basally curved and directed distally; left exopodite 3-jointed, inner side of second joint bearing process directed obliquely inward and distally , process of left exopodite half as long as that of right exopodite.  Second joints of right and left endopodites covered with thin short bristles.    
Male:  Last thoracic segment with asymmetrical processes, left one larger than the right.  Caudal rami long, 3 ¼ times longer than maximum width (in distal part). Cheliform process of right leg with shorter rami than in C. mcmurrichi.
.
Additional  remarks :       Neritic species of shallow waters and bays found sporadically in large numbers in warm waters.
