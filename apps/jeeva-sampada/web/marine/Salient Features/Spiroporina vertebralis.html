SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Branches with an axial row of cylindrical kenozooecia, the main ones stout, with alternating whorls of 13 zooecia or fewer. Zooecia not distinctly outlined, with numerous small pores, often slit-like and narrower than the interspaces. Peristomes short, their edge typically bearing a pair of minute avicularia proximally, their rostra acute, directed inwards; less often with a distal avicularium. Frontal avicularia of the same kind but generally larger, mostly at the lateral junctions of two zooecia; directed proximally, others, with an inconstant orientation, sometimes found elsewhere. Spiramen distant from the secondary orifice. Ovicells elongate, deeply immersed sometimes visible as swellings on the distal side of the peristomes. Opercula sinuate. Diagnostic characters:    Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:   The colonies of S. vertebralis are often very large, as much as 5.8 cm long, and much branched. They show a considerable amount of variability in the width of their branches. Old ones are thick, with as many as 13 zooecia in a whorl; but in thinner branches the number is often reduced to 4-6; and abnormally developed processes may consist of only 1or 2 zooecia.
  A transverse section of a branch shows a large axial canal being surrounded by radiating septa, which divide the zooecia from one another.
The spiramen of S. vertebralis appears to be developed as a median sinus on the proximal border of the secondary orifice.
The avicularia typically occur at the lateral junction of two zooecia, on the proximal side of the spiramen; and are horizontal, raised; the rostra long, narrow, with a proximal orientation. These avicularia are often shorter or wanting, and a few smaller avicularia, with inconstant orientation, may be found on other parts of the frontal surface.
The ovicells are sometimes distinguishable as slight inflations on the distal side of the peristomes, but they are generally completely immersed. The primary orifice and the operculum have a distinct narrow sinus.
