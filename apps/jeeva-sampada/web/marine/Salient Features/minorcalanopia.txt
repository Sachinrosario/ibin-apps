SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:         

FEMALE: Cephalon enlarged medially, maximum width of body at pedigerous segment-II; posterior corners of pedigerous segment-V are pointed and posteriorly produced; rostrum bulged at base and tapers to tip which end in pointed processes; urosome two – segmented; genital segment swollen on either margins; urosome-II 1.5 times longer than the genital segment; caudal rami twice as long as broad; 5th leg: symmetrical, Re one segmented, terminates distally in one longer inner spine and  one shorter outer spine; former with distal half finely setose; another short spine present on outer median margin of terminal segment.        

MALE:  Prosome resembles that of female; lateral margin of pedigerous segment –V with a small setule one on either side; Urosome five – segmented; anal lamina crescentic; caudal rami 2.5 times longer than broad; right antenna – 1 : geniculate, with segment 18 carrying fine villiform serrations along its dorsal margin; fusion segment 19 – 21 with a spinous seta at its dorsal midmargin; 5th leg : Right leg: basipod 2 swollen towards its proximal half; palm – like joint of Re 1 invaginated along its inner margin and with a seta; thumb short and naked; claw spoon shaped and provided with two inner marginal setae; left leg basipod 2 considerably swollen at its proximal end on inner margin, the bulged portion is produced into a small tooth – like spine; a small seta present at distal inner margin of segment; Re 1 more than 4 times longer than broad and with a disto – lateral spine; terminal segment ends in two subequal spines, outer one of which is long and thick; a small seta present on dorsal mid – margin of segment.  

Additional remarks:        
This species is very closely allied to C.aurivillii and C.Americana. It is well distributed in the coastal and oceanic waters of the Indian Seas and always occurs in good numbers in plankton collections.
