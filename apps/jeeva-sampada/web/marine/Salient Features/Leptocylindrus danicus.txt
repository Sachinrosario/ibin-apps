SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells forming long chains.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: No structure visible on the valve. Chromatophores numerous and disc shaped.

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 3-17m, Length: 9-89m.

Reproduction: Resting spore consists of two unequal valves.

Main Ref: Hargrover, 1990.
