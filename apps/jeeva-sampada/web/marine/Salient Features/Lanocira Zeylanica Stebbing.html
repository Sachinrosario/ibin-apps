Salient feature:
Morphological:

  Body elongate oblong, with the posterior half densely setose. Head as long as the first peraeon segment, with a distinct submarginal groove as observed by Stebbing. Peraeon segments subequal iin length and breadth; coxal plates, except the last two not dorsally visible, seventh with an acute posterior prolongation. Pleon segments one and two concealed by the seventh peraeon segment. Telson triangular, lateral sides bisinuous, apex with six spines.
  First antenna as long as penduncle of second, contiguous at the base, flagellum five jointed; flagellum of second antenna fifteen jointed. Frontal lamina broader than long, similar to the of L.gardineri.
  First peraeopod as in L.gardineri, but the fifth segment minute and scarcely visible; seventh peraeopod comparatively stout, but the setae shorter than those of L.gardineri and longer than those of L.rotundicauda.
  Mouth parts as in L.gardineri.

Additional remarks:

  Presence of a submarginal line on the cephalon, comparatively ill developed coxal plates, the very short fifth segment of the first peraeopod and the considerably more setose body distinguish Lanocira zeylanica from L.gardimeni and L.rotundicauda.
