SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting.  Surface rising to hillocks.  Corallites and calices polygonal, penta or hexagonal, 5-6 mm ling and 4-5 mm broad, 2-3 deep.  Wall fused, thinner at the summit than at the base.  Total number of septa 20 to 24, alternating in size, very little exsert, continuous over the wall.  Lower part of the septa broader than the upper two-thirds.  Septal edges dentate, sides granular.  Eight to 12 septa reach the columella.  Columella trabecular, 1-2 in diameter.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
