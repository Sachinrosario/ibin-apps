SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with almost round valves. Form chains. Apertures narrow and elliptical. Setae with small spines arranged spirally.

Diagnostic characteristics: Resting spores with strongly curved primary valve, spinous on the upper part and with numerous needle like processes at the margin.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 16-29µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
