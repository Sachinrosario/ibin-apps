SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony 120 mm in height and 110 mm in breadth. It is branched in one plane and attached to a piece of coral. The verrucae occur mainly in three sides and are more crowded on the twigs than on older portions. They are nearly cylindrical and the anthocodiae are almost completely retracted throughout the whole colony. The twigs terminate with two almost opposite polyps, between which there is a small conical projection.  
		
Spicules: The spicules are curved.

 Colour		:	The colony is dull brown in colour .
