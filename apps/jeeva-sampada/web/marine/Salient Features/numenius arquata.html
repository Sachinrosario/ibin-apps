SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: A large wading bird with a down wardly curved long dark brown bill. Long legs bluish grey.

Diagnostic characteristics: Large downwardly curved bil. Plumage streaked buff and brown, abdomen whitish streaked in black.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Forages on the mud flats.

Main Food: Fiddler crabs, small crustacean and insects.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Nesting season is in April to June in Europe. Not known to breed in India.
