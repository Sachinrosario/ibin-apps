
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      

Female: Prosome 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segment fused. Postero- lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment narrowly rounded and notched. In 5th leg joints are slightly cylindrical. Terminal segment with 2 approximately equal spines, which are with fine spinnules on the outer margin. 

Additional remarks:      The similarities between S. tropica and S. beata (Tanaka, 1962) are so obvious that the existence of the latter as a distinct species becomes doubtful. It can be considered as a synonym of S. tropica.
