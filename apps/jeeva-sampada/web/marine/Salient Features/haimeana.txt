Descriptive characters:  The specimens were examined one from Minicoy and a second from Kiltan Island.  Both are free unattached in the form of oval masses.  Corallum massive or encrusting.  Corallites polygonal, single rarely in short series, enclosed between well developed, acute low collines.  Distance between the tops of adjacent collines 3 to 5 mm.  Axial fossa 1 mm in diameter.  12 septa teach the columella but at the summit of the collines upto 40 septa can be around an axial fossa.  Septal edges with 6 to 8 frostal teeth, septal sides granular.  Interseptal loculi narrower than the septum.  Columella a single style surrounded by 6 to 8 paliform lobes of equal height.

P. haimeana belongs to the subgenus Plesioseris differing form typical Psammocora in the possession of low, acute collines enclosing single or series of calices.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
