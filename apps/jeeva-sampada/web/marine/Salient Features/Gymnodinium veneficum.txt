SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: More or less oval cells, not as slightly flattened. Girdle shows a spiral, which is, displaced 1-2 girdle widths. The sulcus is slightly sigmoid being deflected in the intercingular region, with a short protrusion on to the episome.

Diagnostic characteristics: 4 chloroplasts with pyrenoids. The nucleus is located in the central part of the hyposome.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 9-18µm, width: 7-14mm.

Biochemical aspect: Carotenoid fucoxanthin is present instead of peidinin. Toxin produced is an exotoxin.
