SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elliptical valves with broadly rounded poles and strongly convex sides. Central nodule more as less well developed, small rounded and quadrate. Horns robust.

Diagnostic characteristics: Furrows lanceolate, diminishing in breadth from the middle towards the poles. Transapical costae 9 in 10m radial, with alternating double rows of alveoli.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 58m long and 35m broad.
