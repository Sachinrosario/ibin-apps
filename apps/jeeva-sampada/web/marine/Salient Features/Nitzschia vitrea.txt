SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear rectangular cells with somewhat parallel sides and rounded corners in girdle view.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves linear, slightly constricted in the middle with rounded ends. Kiel punctae 7-8 in 10µ. Transapical striae 21-24 in 10µ, fine.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: 28-140µ long; 4µ broad.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides.
