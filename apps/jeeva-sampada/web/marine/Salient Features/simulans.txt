SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:                General form slender. Hind border of carapace with six filaments and pair of notches, a similar notch on the outer border opposite the inner notch. Anterior margin of labrum transverse and armed with one stout median spine flanked by one short spine on each side. Endopod of third pleopods of male eight segmented; exopod eight segmented, last segment with three distal barbed spines, a grooved median process and two small spines.  Lateral border of telson with eight spines. with eight spines, first large others slightly increasing in length. Endopod of uropod with four equidistant spines.

              
Size and age

Maxikmum length:              Adult male and female: 10-12 mm
