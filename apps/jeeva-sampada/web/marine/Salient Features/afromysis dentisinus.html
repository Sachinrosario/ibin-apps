Morphological:

Diagnostic characteristics:
Closely resembling A. macropsis in general shape, but smaller and more slende. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male terminating in one long barbed seta. Telson steadily narrowing, basal part with one spine, distal two-third of lateral margin with 12-19 spines regularly increasing in length, each lobe of telson carrying three spines, middle spine longest; cleft with pair of long setae and 2-3 pairs of denticles in male and one pair in female. Endopod of uropod with row of closely packed spines, spines on proximal half blunt and that on distal half sharp.
