SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  flabellate and reticulate, 11 cm in height and about 6.5 cm in diameter. The stem and branches are not appreciably flattened and are without evident median grooves. The main stem grows from an encrusting base and is 2 mm in diameter and ***mm long to the first branch. This branch extends outwards and then upward, bears a number of lateral branches, one of which is compound, and is connected with the rest of he colony through several anastomoses. Almost immediately above the first branch the main stem is rather abruptly bent to one side and gives off numerous lateral branches which are irregularly spaced and often compound and anastomose through their branchlets with other braches, forming a loose and irregular network of very delicate texture. Many of the branches are not more than 0.5 mm in diameter, while the larger branches are scarcely 1 mm thick. The calyces are irregularly but rather thickly distributed on all sides of the terminal twigs, and on three sides of the others. They average a little more than 1 mm apart from summit to summit.
		The individual calyces are small but prominent for the genus, dome-shaped, averaging about 0.8 mm in height and 1.5 mm in diameter at the base. The margins are surrounded by eight lobes and the walls are provided with eight rather indefinite longitudinal ribs which are also seen 
		
		in the general coenenchyma as rude longitudinal corrugations resembling the bark of a tree. There is superficial layer of small oval spicules and minute double heads in the calyx alls, beneath which are larger regular spindles. The polyps are completely retracted and much flattened or disk-shaped in retaraction. They are provided with rather weak collaret with the spindles arranged en chevron on tentacle bases. There are rather narrow longitudinal bands of spindles on the dorsal surfaces of the tentacles. The axis is the typical one as described for the genus.

		Spicules: These differ materially from the other forms in this genus. There is superficial incomplete layer of quite small double heads or dumb-shaped forms under which is a much thicker layer of comparatively large spindles, often curved, with thick-set verrucae arranged regular whorls. 

Colour		:	Colony is a very light grayish brown.
