SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                       

Female :  As in the generic description with the following additional characters.  Dorsal surface of the body moderately strongly arched, anterior head rounded.  Antenna 1 extends beyond the caudal rami by its last 5 segments.  Outer distal border of exopod segment 3 of leg 4 with very small numerous teeth.

Male :  As in the family and generic descriptions with the following additional characters.  Exopod segment 3 of swimming leg 4 similar to that of the female.  Leg 5-5 segmented on the left.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed )                         Ref. No.:Females  1.00 – 1.26 mm                                                            Male  :   0.95 – 1.25 mm			Bradford-Grieve, 1994
