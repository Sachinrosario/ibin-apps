SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Armored, laterally compressed cells with conspicuous cingular list. Anterior cingular list broadly funnel shaped extending beyond the epitheca, which is relatively short, at times barely detectable.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Hypotheca exceeds epitheca. Cingulum distinctly premedian. Sulcal lists prominent, resembling wings and supported by ribs.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 54-94mm, dorso ventral depth: 46-60mm.
