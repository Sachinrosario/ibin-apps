SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Closely resembles H. flagelliforma except in the armature of the telson. Lateral margin of telson with eleven pairs of spines, spines longer and more closely arranged, last two pairs longer sub equal. In female telson steadily narrowing distally, lateral margin with 14-15 pairs of spines. Endopod of uropod with 6-7 well spaced sharp spines.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult male    : 8 mm     
Adult female : 7.5 mm
