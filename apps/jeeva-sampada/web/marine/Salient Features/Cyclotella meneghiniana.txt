SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules discoid in valve view, rectangular and undulated in girdle view.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Margin well defined, coarsely striated and the striae wedge shaped. Striae 8-10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 11-30m.

Biochemical aspects: The production of male gametes and auxospores appear to be induced by changes in salinity (Schuttz & Trainer, 1970) or by artificial seawater lacking sodium chloride (Schutz & Trainer 1968). Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
