SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Grey partridge like bird. In winter the plumage of forehead and lores white speckled with black. Summer breeding plumage is black above, profusely bored with silvery grey or pearly white feathers.







Diagnostic characteristics: In flight a large black oval patch underwing; white rump and tail and a whitish wing bar is conspicuous.






Sex attributes:


Descriptive characters: Gregarious, usually in flocks of more than 20 birds.




Meristic characteristics:





Feeding habit: Gregarious feeder.







Main Food: Insects and molluscs



Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder






Additional remarks: Does not breed in India
