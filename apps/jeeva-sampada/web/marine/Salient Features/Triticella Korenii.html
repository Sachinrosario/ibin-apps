Morphological: Colonies consist of brown branching stola creeping over the substrata and the zooecia arise solitarily from short internodes of the stola. Zooecia with long, slender pedicel connected to the stola by simple movable joints. Frontal surface entirely membranous. At the point where the pedicel joins the zooecium proper, it becomes slightly wrinkled. Zooecia are elongate and elliptical. Pleated collar funnel-shaped. Tentacles twenty four in number.
Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters: The form distinguishes itself from Triticella elongata (Osburn) by the comparatively long pedicel, which has got an average length of 1150; and by the reduced length of the zooecium proper. It differs from T. pedicellata (Alder) in the simple nature of the joints. The zooecia in the present arose from slightly expanded portions of the stolon. Many regenerating zooecia were noticed. In the regenerating zooecium the pedicel assumed a wrinkled nature at the point of regeneration even though the zooecium proper developed away from the area of regeneration, the regenerating point being marked by the presence of a small dilation. Silen (1935) has given a detailed account of the various specific characters of this interesting species.
