SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Elliptical valves with large central nodule, quadrate horns clear. Narrow furrows scarcely broad in the middle and slightly dilated around the central nodule.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Slightly radial costae, 12 in 10 µ, with alternating double rows of alveoli visible.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 16.5µ Long; 10µ broad.
