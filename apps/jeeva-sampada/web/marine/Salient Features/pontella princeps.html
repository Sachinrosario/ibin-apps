Diagnostic characteristics:
Female: Cephalic side hooks prominent; rostarl lenses and dorsal eye lenses moderately developed; ventral eye lens conspicuous; cephalon and pedigerous segment I separated; pedigerous segment IV and V distinct; posterior margin of pedigerous segment V produced sa two asymmetrical flaps on either side of urosome; terminating in acute spines, that on left side reaching distal end of genital segment; urosome of two segments, genital segment on its right dorsal side with a conspicuous process followed by four sub-equal, short conical outgrowths; left lateral margin of genital segment concave at middle, due to two lateral projections; ventrally segment with three subequal stout spines on right side, middle one being longer; genital flap conspicuous; anal segment asymmetrically bifid posteriorly, left lobe being larger; caudal rami asymmetrical, right ramus longer and broader; Leg 5 : external rami stout and well developed, twice as long as internal rami; both bifid asymmetrically at apex, outer spine of bifurcation being small; three minute outer marginal spines present on external rami which are scarcely visible in low magnification.

Male: Prosome resembles that of female; dorsal eye lenses are more conspicuous and rostral lens well developed; pedigerous segment V ending in pointed lobes, directed posterad; urosome of five segments, genital segment with a lobe on its left proximal margin; caudal rami longer than broad, right ramus slightly larger than left; Right Antenna I: geniculate, segment 14 with an elongated spine, with a curved flagellum at its tip; a short, stout spine present on outer dorsal margin of segment 15; two large toothed plates each on segment 17 and 18 and two other smaller toothed plates on fusion segment 19-21 present; teeth on the former are coarse and denticulate, while those on latter villiform, a falcate spine present on distal margin of segment 19-21; segments 22-25 completely fused; Right leg 5: with a chela; finger of chela with three subequal setae on its inner margin, a short bent spine-like projection present at base of distal fold of finger; hand enlarged at middle in the form of curved, triangular structure; inner margin of hand with a bluntly rounded process followed by a seta at base and tooth-like pointed process at base of thumb; left leg: distally ending in two subequal processes, each expanded along tips; longer one of these processes is spatulate and with crenulated margin; shorter process truncate distally and with short seta towards its base; distal segment with an elongated spiniform proximal outer seta; two tufts of marginal hairs present at inner margin of distal segment.
