SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Chains straight and stiff. Valve corners touching those of adjacent cell. Apertures slit-like to broadly lanceolate.

Diagnostic characteristics: Terminal setae thicker than the others, initially divergent, then bent, becoming parallel to chain axis.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
