Descriptive characters:  The following details of this species are based on a specimen form Minicoy Atoll collected by the author in 1968.  It is cespitose.  Greater spread 12 cm.  Branches about 10 mm in thickness, 5 to 6 cm long with a few slender branchlets.  Axial corralites conical, 3 to 4 mm in diameter 1 to 2 mm exsert.  Septa 12.  Radial corallites half-tubular to narriform very little spreading, 2 to 3 mm long and 1.5 to 2 mm thick.  Some of the radials are proliferous and tubular with a rounded aperture, and may be upto 5 mm in length.  The lower parts of the branches are supplied with verruciform and immersed corallites some of which are found scattered among the prominent radials.  The radials are not much crowded.  Surface coenenchyme and the wall of the radials echinulate.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
