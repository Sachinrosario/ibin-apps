SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Female: Body long and perfectly parallel sided. Head a broad based triangle, slightly broader than long, anterior border perfectly rounded; eyes fairly well developed. Peraeon segments sub equal to one another, fourth slightly longer than al the others. Antero-lateral corners of first segment produced forwards as small triangular processes; postero-lateral corners of all segments rounded and not produced; last peraeon segment slightly arched.  Pleon abruptly narrower than peraeon, first segment very narrow, others as broad as seventh peraeon segment. Telson almost semicircular, posterior border rounded.
 
First antenna comparatively stout but not flat, eight jointed; second antenna slender, eight jointed. Apical segment of maxillipid spiny and with three stout hooks; inner lobe of second maxilla with two hooks, outer lobe with two stout hooks, border serrated. Incisor part of mandible blunt, with a membranous lamina representing the molar; palp three jointed, first segment stout, third small and with five setae. Anterior peraeopods with fourth and fifth segments short, together equal to the third segment in length, dactylus strongly curved. Posterior legs long fourth segment longer than fifth, dactylus strongly falcate. Uropods with membranous, flattened rami, exopod longer than endopod, reaching the tip of the telson.

Body reddish brown, dark red anteriorly.

Additional remarks: Three isopod specimens collected by Pillai (1958) from an unknown host, showed slight similarity to the genus Codonophilus; but unlike other species under this genus; the first antennae are not flattened. At the same time they do not show any resemblance to ant other known genera and hence Pillai (1958) included them under Codonophilus provincially. 

Size and age: Length 13.0 mm.
