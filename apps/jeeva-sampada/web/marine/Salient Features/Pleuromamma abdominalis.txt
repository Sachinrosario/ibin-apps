SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 


Diagnostic characteristics: 
     Female: Pigment spot usually on left side, rarely on right side. Proximal joints of 1st antenna bearing several small and two large denticles (on first and second joints)- one straight, the other incurvate. The denticles of this species often vary in size and direction. Fifth pair of legs 4 - jointed (with three free joints); distal joint bearing three apical bristles of very unequal length and two thin spines.

     Male: Pigment spot, genital aperture, and denticles of inner margin of endopodite  of 2nd pair  of legs situated on left side. Right antennule geniculate. Proximal joints of antennule denticulate. Abdomen sharply asymmetrical, with long, thick bundles of bristles. Left leg of 5th pair with wide distal joint.
Size and age:
Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
     Female:  2.4-4.36 mm
     Male    :  2.68-4.3 mm
Ref. No.: K.A.Brodskii, 1950
