SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body elongate, the depth 3.3 to 3.9 times in standard length. Chin with a pair of slender barbells which reach beyond rear margin of pre opercle, their length 1.2 to 1.6 times in head; snout 2.1 to 2.6 times in head; mouth small, the maxilla not reaching front edge of eye; villiform teeth in a band in jaws; no teeth on roof of mouth; qillrakers on first arch 32 to 36. First dorsal fin with 8 spines very small; pectoral fin rays 16 or 17. Lateral line scales 35 to 38; 5 rows of scales between dorsal fins and 11 or 12 rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour:
 whitish to pink with a yellow stripe edged in pale blue on body at level of upper end of gill opening: no black spot within lateral yellow stripe on body; barbells white to yellow; fins yellow.
