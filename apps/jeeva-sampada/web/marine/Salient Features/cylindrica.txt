SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of blunt: ended columns which may subdivide.  Surface with meandroid valleys.  Distance between tops of adjacent.  Collines 3-4 mm; depth of valleys about 3 mm.  Many calices are circumscribed.  Top of collines acute or thickened.  Septa subequal; exsert part of septa either continuous over the wall or opposing each other.  Septal edges serrated, sides granular.  Columella solid with upwardly projecting tubercles.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
