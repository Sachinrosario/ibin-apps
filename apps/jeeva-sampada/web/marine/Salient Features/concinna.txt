Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum disc-shaped.  Upper surface slightly convex as in F. repanda.  Underside concave.  Septal teeth uniform.  About 1 mm in height and width.  Costae become faint towards the central part of the disc.  Costal teeth small, the larger ones upto 1 mm high and thick; larger ones byfercating.  Disc imperperate.   

This species differs from F. repanda in having an imperperate corallum against the perperate disc of the repanda.  Otherwise the general characters are more or less the same and this too may be one and the same.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
