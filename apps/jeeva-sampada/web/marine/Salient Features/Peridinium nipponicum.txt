SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Body ellipsoidal, epitheca slightly conical, towards the clear apical knob somewhat tapering. Girdle dextral, ends displaced up to a furrow width.

Diagnostic characteristics: Two slender Antapical processes, left smaller bent towards sulcal wing.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 40µ; Breadth: 33-36µ.
