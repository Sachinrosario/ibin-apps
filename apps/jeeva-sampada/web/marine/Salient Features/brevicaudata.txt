SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:   Body robust. Rostral process short and broadly rounded. Eyes fairly large. Antennal scale reaching tip of antennular peduncle in female, slightly shorter in male. Carpopropodus undivided. Pseudobranchial lobes spirally twisted; pleopods not modified. Telson short, 1 ½ times as long as broad, proximal part of lateral border with 2-3 spines, apex broadly truncated or slightly emarginated, distal portion of lateral margin and apex together bearing 5-6 pairs of spines, inner most pair of spines longest, centre of apex with three small spines and pair of plumose setae. Uropods twice as long as telson, endopod shorter with 10-12 spines gradually increasing in length; proximal segment of exopod with five spines.                

The shape and armature of the telson and endopod of uropod are very characteristic. 

Size and age

Maximum length:  Male and Female (adult) 4  – 6mm

Geographical Location:   Kilakarai and Pamban,(Gulf of Mannar);  Red Sea,  Gulf of Aquaba;  the Suez Canal, Madagascar and  Arabian Gulf.
