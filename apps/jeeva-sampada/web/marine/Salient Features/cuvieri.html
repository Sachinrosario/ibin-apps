SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 4,5
Morphological : A fairly slender species,

Diagnostic characteristics
      Snout a little longer than eye diameter, mouth large and terminal lower jaw projecting; rostral pores absent, marginal pore 5, mental pore usually indistinct; teeth in a single series in lower jaw ; 1 or 2 pair of strong canines in upper jaw and 1 pair at tip of lower jaw ; gillrakers on lower limb of first arch 12 to 17, with some toothed plates anteriorly. Dorsal fin with 10 spines followed by a notch , second part of the fin with 1 spine and 29 to 32 soft rays; anal fin with 2 spine and 7 or 8 soft rays, the second spine short and weak, its base beginning behind middle of soft part of dorsal fin, caudal fin rhomboid, but with tip pointed. Scale cycloid on head, elsewhere ctenoid, lateral line scales reaching to tip of caudal fin. Swimbladder carrot shaped, with about 28 pairs of arborescent appendages. somewhat swollen at their bases; none entering the head.

Colour : brown / pink above , yellow/ gold or silver on flanks; spinous part of dorsal fin edged in grey/black, soft part of dorsal fin and anal fin edged in grey; pectoral and pelvic fins yellow.

Feeding habit:
          
Main food:   Feeds on small fishes & invertebrates.    Ref. No. 6
    At Veraval Acetes spp appears to be the chief food item followed by other prawns and young fish. Among prawns P. stylifera, and solenocera spp were the dominant food items. Bregmaceros spp. C. dussumieri, ribbon fish and N. japonicus were also observed in the stomachs of O. cuvieri.

Feeding type: Carnivorous
