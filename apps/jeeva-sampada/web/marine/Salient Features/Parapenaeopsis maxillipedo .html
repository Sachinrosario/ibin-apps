SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No:49,103,144,

 

Morphological:   Rostrum straight, tip uptilted, generally toothless on distal ¼, reaching from

middle of 2nd to distal margin of 3rd antennular article and armed with 8 to 11 (usually 9 or 10) dorsal teeth; epigastric tooth present; postrostral crest reaching, or almost reaching posterior margin of carapace; longitudinal suture reaching 1/3 to ½ of carapace length; antennular flagella 0.35 to 0.55 times the length of carapace; epipod present on 1st and 2nd pereopods; basial spine present on first 3 pairs of pereopods; telson unarmed. Petasma with long and slender, horn-like distolateral projections, diverging proximally and curving inward distally, without small dorsal spiniform processes; distomedian projections extremely small. Anterior plate of thelycum subquadrate, posteriorly depressed and medially fused to posterior plate, the latter with a  pair of lateral depressions and a median boss; a median tuft of long setae(hairs) behind thelycum.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Longitudinal suture reaching 1/3 to ½ of carapace length; epipod

present on 1st and 2nd pereopods; basial spine present on first 3 pairs of pereopods; petasma with long and slender, horn-like distolateral projections without small dorsal spiniform processes; anterior plate of thelycum subquadrate, posteriorly depressed and medially fused to posterior plate; a median tuft of long setae (hairs) behind thelycum.

 

        Colour: Usually grey, sometimes pale brown, abdomen with dorsal transverse dark bands
