
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:       

Female:   Body small; cephalic spine small, curved ventrally. Spines on posterolateral metasomal borders fine, pointing straight posteriorly, reaching almost to posterior margin of genital segment. Urosome one-quarter length of mettasome. Antenna 1 slightly longer than metasome. Basipod 1 of maxilliped with distinct, broadly based lamella. Exopod of leg 1 two–jointed with two spines on outer edge. Basipod 1 of leg 4 with row of about 11 fine bristles.

Additional remarks:       Generally the posterior metasomal points diverge slightly in south –west Pacific specimens, but in some specimens these points were directed straight posteriorly. Legs 1 and 4 are always characteristic of G. minor.
