SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells with numerous intercalary bands in two series. Apical process short and straight. Base slightly enlarged with lateral wings at the base.

Diagnostic characteristics: Cell wall strong, clearly sculptured. Intercalary bands coarsely punctate-striated, striae running from. Central line in a fan like manner to sides, 15 in 10 m; punctae 30 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 38-44m.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
