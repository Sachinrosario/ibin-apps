Salient Features :
It is a diffused branched, woody, perennial under shrub.
Stem prostrate, much branched, smooth, yellowish.
Leaves numerous, elliptic-oblong or linear-obovate, rigid, glabrous.
Flower hermaphrodite, axillary in dense, globose, many flower clusters, forming dense spikes leafless in the upper part, stigma 3.
Seeds-shinning black having beak with curved embryo.
Industries:
It is used to produce impure sodium carbonate.
Health:
Nil.
Nutrition:
Eaten as vegetable and in preparation of snacks.
R & D:
Act as soil binder. Potential as cash crop on saline wastelands.
