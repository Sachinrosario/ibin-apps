SALIENT FEATURES :

MORPHOLOGICAL
Female:
In general shape and colouration, Bopyrina Cochinensis resembles B.Kossmanni.
The anterior lamina of head is not developed to as great an extent as it is in B.Kossmanni, and the posterior margin forms a sharper curve. A pair of eyes is present.
The thoracic segments are distinct throughout their length and the margins of the first four are each divided into two parts. The ovarian bosses are indistinct; those on the longer side of the body seem to be oval outline, while those on the other are more or less rounded. The epimera are poorly developed and occupy the usual position.
The lines of demarcation between the abdominal segments are not visible on the dorsal surface. The greatest length of the abdomen considerably exceeds the greatest breadth. The segments are distinctly seen along the shorter margin of the abdomen; on the other side they are somewhat fused. The margins of the fifth segment do not extend beyond the sixth, but the latter forms the posterior extremity of the body. The posterior margin of the sixth segment is entire and regularly rounded.
The maxilliped is like that of the preceding species but the palp is shorter and carries only four or five setae.
The shape of the firsst oostegite is somewhat different, and the posterior lobe is shorter and slightly more pointed. The internal crest is well developed and entire throughout. The fifth incubatory plate extends well backwards and covers a part of the abdomen.
The four pairs of uniramous pleopods are moderately large; the fifth pair and the uropods absent.

Male :
The male is somewhat shorter abdomen is almost as long as broad and tapers rapidly posteriorly,, so that the last segment is many times less broad than the first. The segments, especially, the first four, are distinct on the sides, though they are considerably fused dorsally. The sixth segment does not carry any anal spines, nor are there any pleopods or uropods.

Diagnostic Characteristics:
Abdomen of female longer than broad abdominal segments fused dorsally but more or less distinct on both sides, fifth abdominal segment doesnot extend beyond sixth, abdomen of male almost as long as broad nad tapering greatly, breadth near its posterior extremity is many times less than that at anterior; most abdominal segments almost distinct both dorsally and laterally; terminal segment not finger like; anal spines absent.
Length of female : 3.6 mm
Breadth of female : 2.5 mm
