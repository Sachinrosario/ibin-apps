SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 
Rostrum with 3 dorsal teeth (1 epigastric + 2 carapace spines); supra orbital, antennal and pterygostomian spines present in zoeal stages; antennal and branchiostegal spines developed in the post larval stages; rostrum with 9 dorsal and 2 ventral spines; rostrum long, tip ventrally serrated; carapace with epigastric and antennal spines; rostrum hooked ventrally at tip; abdominal segment 5 with a pair of dorsolateral spines; pereopods 1 and 2 chelate, 2nd leg chela prominent and larger; telson narrow with 8+8 spines in Zoea I, to 3+3 in Zoea X, tip is rounded. Pereopods with rudimentary exopods which are absent in the Juvenile. Exopod of uropod with accessory subapical spines. Telson with 2 pairs of dorsal spines, tip acute; posterior margin with 2 pairs of spines, second pair about 7 times the 1st and also a pair of feathery setae present in between inner pairs of spines,(6+6). 

Diagnostic characteristics:Rostrum tip hooked, 3 epigastric teeth, a pair of dorsolateral spines on abdominal segment 5 (in mysis stages). Presence of an antennal and branchiostegal spines on carapace, pereopods 1-4 with rudimentary exopods and 5th without exopods; all vestigial or absent in Postlarvae and Juvenile.   

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.
Main food:  Plankton,(Artemia nauplii), detritus with  sand and mud.
Feeding type: Carnivorous and cannibalistic.

Size and age: 
Zoeae I-XII: length 2.3mm-6.5mm                      
Postlarva: length 7.1mm                      
Juvenile: length 7.55mm 

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            
Eggs: Being carried with the female body and hatch out as Zoea.

LARVAL STAGES:
Zoea I: length 2.35mmRostrum long, surpasses antennal scale; tip ventrally serrated; carapace smooth; eyes sessile; abdomen 5-segmented; antennale scale with 5 distal segments and 9+1 outer setae; mandible slightly asymmetrical; without palp; maxillipeds 1-3 biramous; pereopods 1-2 present as biramous buds; telson triangular with 7+7 setae.Zoea II: length 2.65mmCarapace with an epigastric and antennal spines; rostrum hooked ventrally at tip; eyes stalked; abdominal somite 5 with a pair of dorsolateral spines; pereopods 1-2 functional, endopod 5-segmented; telson spines increased to 8+8 pairs.Zoea III: length 3.10mmCarapace with 2 epigastric teeth; pereopod 3 present as biramous buds; exopods of uropods become functional; telson elongated slightly and spines reduced to 7+7.Zoea IV: length 3.35mmCarapace with an additional tooth (3 epigastric); pterygostomian angle produced; pereopod 3 functional, endopod 5-segmented; pereopod 5 present as small uniramous bud; telson rectangular,spines reduced to 5+5; uropods developed, exopod longer than telson.Zoea V: length 3.5mmCarapace with a minute pterygostomian spine; pereopods 4 and 5 unsegmented; 5 uniramous; telsonic spines reduced to 4+4.Zoea VI: length 3.75mmPereopod 5 functional, endopod 5-segmented, terminal segment ending in a long spine; telson with 3+3 processes.Zoea VII: length 4.25mmRostrum with dorsal, basal setae; developing chela on pereopod 1; P4 well developed, endopod 5 segmented; pleopods appear as small buds; telson tip narrowed.Zoea VIII: length 4.8mmCarapace with antennal and pterygostomian spines; with 3 dorsal teeth; rostrum tip hooked; pereopods 1 and 2 chelate; P5 longer; pleopods as biramous buds; telson narrow with 3+3 spines.Zoea XI: length 5.10mmPleopods with 3-4 stumpy setae; uropods longer, overreaching posterior margin of telson.Zoea X: length 5.35mmEndopod of antenna overreaching  scale; dorsolateral spines on abdominal somite 5 long and strong; telson same, tip more rounded.Postlarva: length 7.10mmRostrum slightly upturned at tip; 7 dorsal teeth present and 1/3rd of rostrum is free of teeth; ventral margin with 2 teeth; carapace with antennal and branchiostegal spines;Mandible without palp; 1st 4 pereopods bear reduced exopods; paired dorsolateral spines on abdominal somite 5 is lost in this stage; pleopods biramous with setae; telson with 2 pairs of dorsal spines, tip acute and posterior margin with 2 pairs of spines, 2nd pair about 7 times the 1st; a pair of feathery setae present in between the inner pairs of spines; uropods longer than telson.Juvenile: length 7.55mmRostral teeth 9/2; pereopods without exopods; chelae narrower; pleopods setose.Characteristics: Rostrum with dorsal and ventral teeth,tip slightl upturned; antennal and branchiostegal spines present; 3 epigastric teeth and a paired dorsolateral spines on ab.seg.5 in mysis stages; uropods longer than telson.

Locality:
