SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:   
     Female:  The genital segment is asymmetrical. However the pedigerous 5 bears a saw. The genital segment is prolonged into a sheath which overlaps a part of Ab3. This overlapping is more expressed on the right side, and its posterior margin bears a row of spines which extends into a group of bigger and scattered spines on the right angle of the sheath. On the left side of the genital segment there is no trace of a ‘low cup-shaped swelling. Ab3 and Ab4 bear a row of spines on the posterior margin of the upper side. Ab5 bears on the dorsolateral angles finely separated shields overlapping a small portion of the furcal branches. P1 Re bears a saw, the outer marginal spines of Re1 and Re3 are not serrate. Ri3 bears only 3 Si. The terminal spine of P5 has the same length as the spiniform process of Re2, it bears a blade-like seta. Some females have a yellow double patch in Ab5.

     Male: Ab5 bears a similar shield as in female and in the Ab5 there is ayellow patch. In addition there are some more yellow patches in the body. Prosome and pedigerous segment 1 are separated. No spines on the pedigerous segments. P4 Ri3 with 3 Si.
