Salient features:
Morphological:
Female :
Very similar to forma typical except that the outer rami of the pleopods are more elongated and the last abdominal segments more deeply notched posteriorly.
Male:
Male differs from that of forma typical in several characters. The thoracic segments taper considerably towards the sides and thus give the body an appearance of being loosely articulated. The breadth of the animal in the type species is as much as half the length and the body has thus a stout appearance but in the variety the breadth is only about onefourth the length. The thoracic legs perhaps as result of attenuatuin of the body appear to be much longer than those of the forma typical. The abdomen is proportionately longer and tapers posteriorly as in the type species but unlike that of the latter doesnot end in a point. The posterior margin is more or less truncate and has a slight concavity about its middle. The segments are fused together but the lateral undulations which in the forma typical indistinctly mark off the anterior one or two segments are better seen. Further there appear to be some faint lines in correlation with these undulations which seem to mark off these segments.

The pleural Lamellae are of the normal type and are almost as long as the thoracic legs. There are no pleopods or uropods.
Diagnostic Characteristics:
Very similar to Hemiarthrus Filiformis. But last abdominal segment of female deeply notched posteriorly and male about four times longer than broad.
Feeding habit : Parasitic
