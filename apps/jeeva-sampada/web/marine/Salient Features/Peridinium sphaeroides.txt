SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells spherical, little more high than broad. The girdle dextral slightly overhanging not excavated. Its border wing projects very little.

Diagnostic characteristics: The sulcal processes two marginal fins, which terminate as spines in the shape of wings incurved.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 80 to 90µ, diameter: 72-88µ.
