SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Circular frustules. Areolated valves, areolae close together, square, pentagonal or hexagonal in outline, radially arranged, size slightly diminish from the periphery to the centre, 6-9 in 10µ.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Pseudoraphe very narrow in the center and slightly dilated at the poles.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Diameter: 18-45µ.
