Salient Features :
It is perennial , shrubby, much branched, 45-75 cm tall.
Leaves elliptic oblong, nearly orbicular or somewhat obovate
0.5 1.2 cm, fleshy, glabrous, white or glaucous beneath.
Male flowers inaxillaries clusters or short spikes, utricle membranous, enclosd in persistent bracteoles.
Seeds sub-orbicular, compresses, pale brown smooth.

Industries:
Recently perennial Atriplex species have been considered as a possible biomass crop plants for energy production.

Nutrition:
The plant is used as forage.

R & D:
It is used to reduce the speed of the wind, water and soil erosion. Salt tolerance and reclamation of saline wastelands.
