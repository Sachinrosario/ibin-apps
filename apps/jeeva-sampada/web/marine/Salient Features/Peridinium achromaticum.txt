SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small cells, more or less pentagonal with clear socket. Girdle very prominent. Antapical spines small without neck or apical horn. Epitheca and hypotheca of same length. Epitheca conical. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Outline of apical pores prominent but asymmetrical. Hypotheca convex or sinous. Cingulum broad, very much deep.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 33.5-40µ (without spines).
