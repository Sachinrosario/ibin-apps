SALIENT  FEATURES :

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                       
Characteristics:        
  Larval body thin, transparent.  Anterior portion of the alimentary canal runs slightly slanting downwards towards the ventral aspect and makes an  elliptical coil at the posterior end of the abdominal cavity. The antero-posterior axis of the liver is longer than the dorso-ventral axis in early stages. A pigmented diverticulum hangs down just in the front of the first spine on the urohyal in the early stages which shrivels up growth progresses.  Brownish black pigment is present at the base of the appendage which disappears along with the appendage. Spines appear late on urohyal and posterior basipterygial  processes.  Spines are found distributed  near to the baseosts and along the fin rays.   The dorsal and anal fin rays ranges from 82-93 and 68-70 respectively.  There are 10+28-30 vertebrae including urostyle.


























Meristic characteristics : Dorsal fin rays 82-93, Anal fin rays 68-70, Vertebrae 10+28-30
