SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear-oblong valves, tri-undulate, with apices rostrate-capitate.

Diagnostic characteristics: Striae gradually shortened towards the central nodule, the median ones very radiate, the terminal convergent, 1-14 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 40-60m, width 10-12m.
