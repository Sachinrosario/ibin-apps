Salient Features :
It is a prostrate somewhat sub-succulent herb.
Stem more or less annular, glabrous much branched.
Leaves sub fleshy, obliquely opposite unequal the larger, broadly obovate, rounded and often apicular at the apex cunate at the base, glabrous.
Stamens 10 or more, style 1.
Capsules small, almost concealed in the petioler lid truncate.
Seeds many, dull black, reinform.
