SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Girdle strongly sinistral with deep sulcus, which at the terminal of the antapical cone is cut out, and two usually unequal apices result, of which the right one is generally longer.

Diagnostic characteristics: Surface lightly punctate.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 70-100µ; Diameter: 47-85µ.
