SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells somewhat rhombic in outline, with rounded sides and a conical apex. Girdle right handed, excavated, with narrow lists. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Second anterior intercalary almost square and very small. Sulcus broadened towards posterior and ending in two small spines.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 18-45µ.
