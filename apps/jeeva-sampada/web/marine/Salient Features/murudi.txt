SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        General form robust. Carapace anteriorly produced into broad triangular rostrum, dorsomedian nodules present. Antennal sympod with two long and two short spines. Third to fourth thoracic endopods with three to four carpopropodal segments. Eighth endopod three segmented in male, second segment short with five long plumose setae, third segment cylindrical bend at right angles. When straightened shorter than basal segment of exopod in female, endopod unsegmented, straight and shorter than basal segment of exopod. Lateral margin of telson with sixteen slender spines; inner pair of distal spines longer than outer pair.  

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)    

Adult male or female: 8.5 – 9.3  mm
