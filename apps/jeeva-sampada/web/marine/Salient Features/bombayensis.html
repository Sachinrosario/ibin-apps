SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Body about 4 times as long as broad. Head transversely oblong. First peraeon segment with an arcuate groove. Peraeon 2-7 with arrow of setae at the posterior border. Pleon with a setose lateral ridge, that on the fifth segment prominent and tubercular. Dorsal surface of telson and pleon without tubercles or ridges. Telson almost rounded and without tubercles and ridges on the dorsal side. Distal border with a row of tubercles and border setose, sub marginal dorsal side with a few stiff setules.

The third segment of flagellum of the antennule with stout aesthete, second antenna with 5-segmented flagellum. Incisor process of mandible with file-like ridges and teeth, 3-segmented palp; laciniod seta small, apically curved and feebly spiny. Maxilliped slender and long, epipodite lanceolite, not reaching the base of palp. First peraeopod with two barbed spines on propodus, lower border of ischium, merus and carpus tubercular. Merus of seventh leg with the outer distal angle produced and carrying a row of 5 stout barbed spines, carpus with a stout, lower inner tooth and a distal row of 6 strongly barbed spines. Peduncle of uropod slightly longer than endopod, endopod about twice the length of exopod, exopod with a distinct claw. (Pillai, 1961).

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi, wood.

Remarks:
  This species resembles many other species in the nature of the epipod of the maxilliped. The absence of tubercles on the dorsal side of the pleon and telson of this species is characteristic.

However L. bombayensis and L. tripunctata resemble very closely (Pillai 1961). The two species agree in the nature of the epipod of the mandible, in the highly tubercular lateral crests of the fifth pleon segment and telson and even in the presence of a large aesthete on the antennule

Size and age: 3-5mm in length.
