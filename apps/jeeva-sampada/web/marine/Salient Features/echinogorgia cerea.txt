SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branched in a plane, without diagonal leaf-like nerves. The main branch is laterally in almost right angles. The end of the branches are swollen. The coenenchyma is fairly thick so that the branches have an almost uniform diameter throughout, excepting at the tips, which are swollen and club-shaped. The polyps are disposed irregularly over the whole coenenchyma. The verrucae are not prominent but give the surface an undulating contour. The anthocodiae are almost completely retractile.
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are thorny clubs and with big size spindles.  

Colour			:	Colony is yellow brown  in colour.
