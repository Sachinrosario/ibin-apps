SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum tending to become massive.  Corallites 3-4 mm in diameter, 3-4 mm in height, adjacent ones 3-4 mm apart.  Calices circular or oval, 1-1.5 mm deep.  Septa in 2 to 3 cycles, the third almost complete.  In some cases upto 28 septa are visible.  Septa exsert ends vertical, edges entire, sides granular.  Twelve septa reach the columella.  Columella solid looking.  Costae correspond to septa extend to the base of the corallites.  Coenosteum vesicular with blisters.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  Veron & Pichon (1979) considered Galaxea clavus (Dana) and G. lamarcki as synonym of G. astreata and is followed here.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
