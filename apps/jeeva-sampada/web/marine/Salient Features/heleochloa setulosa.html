Salient Features :
It is a rigid, perennial, 15 to 25 cm high grass occurring in salt mashes.
It is densely gustigates from wood inclined base.
Leaves are 7.5 to 15 cm long, involute, rigid, coriaceous glabrous.
Inflorescence is a spike like solitary, spikelets densely imbricate 0.2 to 0.25 cm long.
Seeds are small round with a thin brown seed coat.
