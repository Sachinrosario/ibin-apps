Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Main branches horizontal, coalascent, some times the fusion is complete enough to form plate-like corallum.  Under side of the branches and the plates with numerous tubular corallites, 4 to 15 mm long, pressed to the general plane with minute pore like openings.  Branchlets on the upper side ascending, either single or groups of 3 to 4 with tapering tips.  Some of them bear proliferations.  Apices on an average 15 mm apart.

Axial corallites generally 2 mm, rarely upto 3.5 mm, to 2 mm exsert, cylindrical, opening rounded, about 1 mm in diameter.  Septa six, deep-seated, second cycle not developed.  Radial corallites, ascending, beaked-nariform, 3 to 4 mm long (In Dana’s spressa (Non Ehrenberg) the radials are not more than 2.5 mm USNM No. 264), average 1.5 mm thick.  A few of them become tubular and proliferous and are upto 8 mm long.  Radials become shorter and finally immersed at the proximal parts of the branches.  Radials have only the first cycle of septa, the directives often meeting each other. Wall of the corallites striated, echinulate at the base.  Surface coenenchyme echinulate and reticulate, porous in section.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
