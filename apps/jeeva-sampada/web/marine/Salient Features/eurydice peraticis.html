Salient Features:
Morphological:

Antennule of medium length fig3a not reaching beyond the eye, fourth peduncular article longest, just shorter than combined length of articles 2 and 3. Flagellum short with combined length of flagellar articles equal to length of peduncular articl 4.
Antenna with fourth peduncular article subequal to combined length of peduncular articles 2 and 3 (fig 3b.) article 3 bearing 9 stout curved spines on its outer margin. Antennal flagellum with 13 or 14 articles reaching to the middle of the seventh peraeon segment.
Thoracic epimera with small setae present on posterior borders of epimera 6 and 7, hind margins terminating in a moderately well developed point (fig.3 c).
Peraeopod 1 (fig 3d.) with terminal spine less than half the length of the opposing dactyl as is the usual case for Eurydice, propodus bearing 5 blunt spines.
Peraeopod 7 elongate (fig 3c) with articles 2,4 and 5 subequal bind margins setose. Spines on anterior border of propodus arranged in 3 groups of 3.
Telson (fig,3f) broader than long with hind margin approximately one third width of telson. Hind margin almost straight somewhat intenden bearing two pairs of small articulated spines and 13 plumose setae.
Uropods large (fig.3f) with both exopod and endopod produced beyond hind margin of Telson.
Appendix masculine (fig.3g) straight and broad with rounded tip, shorter than length of pleopod2.
Colour in formalin: whitw with black and browm chromatophores on dorsal surface of the body only

Female:
Female similar to male.
