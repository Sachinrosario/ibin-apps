SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Large cell with rounded epitheca and apical horn. Slightly directed to the right. Typical diagonal straight posterior body margin that makes proximal end of left horn wider than that of the right horn.

Diagnostic characteristics: Left horn curves and is almost immediately directed anteriorly. Right horn bends and is directed to the right. Hypothecal horns with open ends.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 316µm.
