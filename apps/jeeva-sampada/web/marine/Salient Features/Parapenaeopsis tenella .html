SALIENT FEATURES:         Ref No:31,111,113,196

 

Morphological:   Rostrum almost straight, uptilted in females, armed with 6 to 8 teeth along

entire dorsal margin, exceeding tip of antennular pedunlcle in females and reaching 2nd segment of peduncle in males; epigastric tooth absent; postrostral crest broad, indistinct posteriorly, ending at middle of carapace; longitudinal suture reaching 2/3 of carapace length; epipods absent on 1st and 2nd pereopods; telson generally unarmed, some times with 2 pairs of minute lateral spines on distal part (Muthu, 1968). Distolateral projections of petasma slender, tapering and directed proximolaterally, distance between tips of projections 4/5 of length of patasma; lateral lobes expanded laterally into elongate wing-like (keeled) projections. Anterior plate of thelycum 1.5 times as wide as long, with a bluntly V-shaped posterior process enclosed in a shallow depression of posterior plate.

 

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum without epigastric tooth; epipods absent on 1st and 2nd

pereopods; distolateral projections of petasma slender, tapering and directed proximolaterally, distance between tips of same about 4/5 of length of petasma; anterior plate of thelycum 1.5 times as wide as long, with a bluntly V-shaped posterior process enclosed in a shallow depression of posterior plate.

        Colour:    Body creamy white with red, black and dark brown chromatophores distributed    

                   on sides of carapace, posterior margins of abdominal segments and tips of telson and    

                   uropods.
