SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref No: 103, 111	

Morphological:  Carapace very slightly pubescent. Rostrum short and straight, not exceeding 
distal margin of first antennular segment, and armed with 6 to 9 (usually 8 or 9) dorsal teeth;  postrostral carina ending near posterior margin of carapace; postorbital sulcus deep; hepatic spine present; mid-dorsal carina on 4th and 5th abdominal somites distinct, but not ending in a posterior spine on each segment; eye stalk not surpassing first antennular segment; antennular flagella longer than carapace; propodus and dactyl of 5th pereopod very long and slender. Petasma with distolateral projections directed forward, narrowing from base to tip, with inner surface deeply concave and with an indentation at tip. Thelycum closed, its anterior plate longue-like, with rounded anterior and posterior ends; lateral plates bar-shaped enclosing posterior half of anterior plate and delimitating with posterior sternal plate a seminal receptacle.


Diagnostic characteristics: Rostrum short and straight, not exceeding distal margin of first
antennular segment, armed with only dorsal teeth; postrostral carina ending near posterior margin of carapace; hepatic spine present; eye stalk not surpassing first antennular segment; propodus and dactyl of 5th pereopod very long and slender.

Colour:   Pink to  reddish pink.


Feeding habit:
Main food:. Crustacean remains (copepods & decapods), debris and Foraminifera (Aravindakshan & Karbhari, 1988) 

Feeding type: Omnivorous
