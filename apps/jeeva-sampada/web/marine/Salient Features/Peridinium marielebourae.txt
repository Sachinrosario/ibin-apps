
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells small and flattened dorsiventrally. Girdle circular or slightly sinistral excavated with or without winged lists supported by spines. Sulcus reaches well into posterior end. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Hypotheca with two hollow horns each having terminal spines.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 50-55µ.
