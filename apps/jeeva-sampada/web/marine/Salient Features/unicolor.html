Diagnostic characteristics:

Body robust and fusiform. Gill-rakers 1 on upper arm and 11 or 12 on lower arm of the first gill arch. Two dorsal fins, almost contiguous, first dorsal with 13-14 spines. Second dorsal with 3 spines and 10 soft rays, followed by 7 finlets. Anal fin with 3 or 4 spines and 10 soft rays, followed by 6 finlets. Lateral line prominent, curved over pectoral fin, wavy posteriorly.

Colour: Body dark blue on back and along sides above the level of pectoral fins, silvery on belly. Spinous dorsal fin bluish green, other fins and fin lets dusky blue, except distal parts of soft dorsal and anal fins which are white.
