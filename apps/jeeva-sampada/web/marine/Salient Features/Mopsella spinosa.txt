SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony reticulate, the branches radiates in radius shaped from the base and are repeatedly divided dichotomous. The furcations are “U-shaped Main branches do not emerge. The nodes are swolllen. The internodes are 8-10 mm long. The thin branches which are circular in cross section are many times bound by diagonal leaf nerves. The polyps shoot from circular calyces and leaving only a narrow strip free. The polyps are armed with 8 transverse spindles and covering upto 0.28 mm long. Spindles are shapely curved in the centre with large thorns
		Spicules. The most characteristic forms are remarkable Blattkeulen  ( Fig a, b & c) and with irregular spicules (Fig.d).
 
Colour		:	The entire colony is wax -red colour or dull yellowish brown, 
                                                   Polyps yellow and axis blood-red colour.
