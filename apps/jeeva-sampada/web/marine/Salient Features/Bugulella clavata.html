SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Erect zoarium? Greyish white. Zooecia long and sub-truncate, with an arched distal end, tapering proximally. Aperture long and ovate, occupying more than three fourth of the front. Frontal membrane translucent. Semilunar operculum membraniporine. Cryptocyst wanting. Two avicularia directed laterally and articulated with round tubercles, occupy the distal lateral angles. The shape of the avicularia very much like that of Bugula. Rostrum down-curved. Mandible with a bend at the tip. Spines absent. No ovicells noticed.

Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: The present material shows very close resemblance to the figure and description given by Hincks. A detailed examination of the limited material available with him showed that the organisation was very simple, probably approaching that of Aetea. Here the fragments are erect and the daughter zooids arise directly from the parent zooids without the intervention of stola. The boat shaped zooecia of B. clavata resemble those of Beania and Bugula and the shape of the avicularia of this species is somewhat similar to those of Beania and Bugula. Branching take place from the sides of the zooecia. The point of origin of a branch is demarcated by circular thickening. Meristic characteristics:
