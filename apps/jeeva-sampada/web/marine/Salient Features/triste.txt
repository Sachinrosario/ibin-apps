Salient Features:
Morphological:

Body is oval shaped with the thoracic segments showing an increase in breadth towards the posterior end the broadest one being the last thoracic segment. The lateral border of thorax is fringed with minute hairs. The cephalon os triboiled anteriorily with an obscure tubercle in the middle of the head (distinctly visible in dried specimens) and the anterior third is distinguished from the posterior part by a submarginal groove. Eyes are lateral, large and black, being deeply sunk in cup like antero lateral extension of the first thoracic segment.

The first thoracic segment is the longest. Segments 2-5 are subequal, bearing transverse ridges which are granulate in the last two segments. Pleon possess a pair of stout conical submedian tubercles and two expanded backwardly directed lateral plates, fitting into the antero-lateral depressions of telson. The dorso-meridian part of the telson ios convex with two submedian tubercles, more prominent than those on the pleon. Distal half of telson is triangular with a slightly rounded apex, the border being raised upwards. Lateral border of telson is devoid of hairs.

The flagellum of the antennule(Fig. 2)is fifteen segmented. The apically directed anterior extremity of epistome (Fig. 4) is blunt with obtusely rounded bulge just above the base of the postero laterallyu extending arms which enclose more than half the upper lip. The edges of these are arms hairy. The insicor processor of the nandible chitinized, red and bidenate in the left and indistinctively tridenate in the right one. The middle spines are nine all barbed at the tip. The molar projection is distinctly toothed. The palp is three articulated with a cylindrical hairy just joined rounded second joint and a triangular pointed third joint. The endopodite of maxillule half broad as the exopodite carries at the distal apex one short and three long pulmose setae, the border bieng devoid of hairs. The exopodite has six spines distally, the outer margin being hairy. The endopodite of maxilla is subquadrangular with pectinate setae. The two lobes of the expodite arre ovate and subequal. The outer lobe bears hairs at its outer margin. Both the lobes bear at their distal end setae of unequal length; Maxpilled with a five joint palp, long sympod and triangular inner plate, very much setose.

The first thoracic leg(Fig.9) has a basis as long as the ischium, the whole of the anterior border bearing long netotary setae; merus with a construction at its proximal end and half as long as ischium; carpus triangular bearing a spine at the distal end propodus armed with a stout spine at the distal tip and distal half of anterior border fringed with hairs dactylus with spine and unique stout pointed and curved. Seventh thoracic leg. (Fig.10) with basis and ischium robust and cylindrical; merus with the distal anterior tip produced into a triangular lobe, overlapping the proximal antrior border of carpus, which broadens distally and with serrated spines at the distal free edge; propodus narrows towards the dactylus; unguis is curved. The anterior and the posterior margin of propodus are fringed with densely arranged hairs. The anterior three pleopods (Fig.11) have an endopod which widens distally and bears long pulmose setae. The expod narrows from base and bears at its apex at a pulmose setae. The endopod of pleopod four (Fig.12) has nine transverse respiratory folds and the expod has a suture line at distal half and the entries outer border bears small spines. Rami of uropods (Fig.13) subequal in length,outer border of exopodent into eight pointed teeth, including the apical. The outer and inner margin of exopod and also the ouiter margin of endopod bear small hairs of uniform length.

Color: The formalin preserved specimens are brownish with black eyes. 

Diagnostic Characteristics:

Typical isopod; epistome triangular as long as broad with an angular apex; seventh peraeon segment without tubercles; exopod of uropod with 8-9 teeth pleon and with a pair of conspicious submedian tubercles; Telsonic apex angular and slightly raised.

Sex Attributes: Embryo and larval development completed within an internal brood pouch.

Feeding Habit: Browsing on Wood Suface

Main Food:  Primary Film and algal growth on wood surface.

Additional Remarks: 
S. Triste shows close resemblence to S.Terebrans neverthless, Spheoroma triste can be easily distinguished from S. Terbrans by the presence of large number of teeth on the outer border of the exopod of the uropod.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size Recorded 12.0 mm
