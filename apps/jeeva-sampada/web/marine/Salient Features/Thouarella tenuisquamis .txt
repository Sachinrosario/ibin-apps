SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	The colonies are branched in one plane. The branches arise at right angles bent to one side, leaving the opposite sides free. The secondary branches measures 1.8 cm long equally distributed. They are unbranched. The polyps arranged in 7-8 pairs in 1cm above the branch. Near the base of the main branch the polyps are arranged densely. 
		Spicules: The spicules are smaller and fine of 0.25 mm in breadth and of equal height. They are also warted, pointed, 0.26 mm broad, 0.55 mm long and triangular in shape.  The spicules of the bark are keel-shaped.
