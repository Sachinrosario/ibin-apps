SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony (incomplete) flabellate and irregular. Almost 2 cm height from its base, the main stem gives origin to two opposite branches. The individual calyces are verruciform to conical in shape. They are about 1 mm high and 2 mm broad at the base. The calyx walls are armed with oval warty spindles sometimes disk-shaped.  The polyps are retractile. The collaret and operculum are composed of three spindles in the usual position.
		
Spicules: The spicules of the stem are longitudinally disposed. Heavy, very warty spindles predominate, which are occasionally flattened (Fig. a & b). 

Colour :	Colony is grayish, the axis is dark brown and the spicules are colourless.
