SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body fairly elongate, sub cylindrical in cross-section;  belly rounded and smooth without scutes. (except plate-like pelvic scute). Mouth small, snout pointed; a small vertical slit in upper half of adipose  tissue covering eyes; branchiostegal rays numerous (12 to 19) gillrakers 19 to 26 on lower limb of first arch. Dorsal fin origin behind mid-point of body; pelvic fins below dorsal fin base; anal fin very short. Hind portion of scales with fine horizontal striae 
Colour: back iridescent blue with a shiny gold/brass line below. (quickly fading after death), flanks silvery;  caudal fin with a dark hind margin.
