SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves ovate, cup costae 2.5-3 in 10µ. Inside and outside cup costae with striae, 12-14 in 10µ.

Diagnostic characteristics: Axial area navicular, with a circle of puncta at its margin, 15-16 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Length 54-68µ, width 32-47µ.
