Descriptive characters: The corallum semiarborescent, fruticose thick branches more or less 1 cm thick and 5 to 7.5 cm long.  Tips of branches scarcely tapering, obtuse.  Axial corallites 3 to 4 mm in diameter, slightly exsert, wall very thick.  Septa 12.  Radial corallites close together much crowded, labellate, gutter-shaped, spreading, 1.5 to 2.5 mm broad and about 1 mm in length.  Radials generally have only the first cycle of septa.

Remarks:  The present author examined the types of A. hebes (USNM. 287) A. exigua (USNM. 288) and A. aspera (USNM 285) along with a good suit of specimens in the Smithsonian collection labeled A. exigua, A. mani, A. hebes or A. aspera reported by the authors like, Vaughan (1918) Hoffmeister (1926); Faustino (1927) and Wells (1950).  A. exigua is only a thinner stemmed form of A. hebes and has no separate status.  The present author fully endorse the view of Crossland (1952) that A. aspera is the same as A. hebes, the so called differences are only minor skeletal variations.  There are many specimens from Philippines in USNM labeled A. mani.  Some of these specimens have very thin arborescent corallum.  The radials are some what ascending and nariform, but others are similar to A. hebes.  A specimen from Cocos-Keeling Islands (No. 44315) labeled A. hebes and so reported by Wells (1950) reveals no marked difference from specimens labeled A. manni from Philippines.  I take manni to the synonymy of A. hebes.

It is very common species in the lagoons of all Islands in Lakshadweep.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
