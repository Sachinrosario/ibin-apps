SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branching is in one plane and the terminals are flattened. The polyps stand 2 mm distances apart and 0.7 mm high, without calyx and with a crown of small 0.06 mm spindles. 
			
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma long and belted. Few small rounded spicules are also found. 

Colour	:	The colony is yellowish brown and axis is blackish brown.
