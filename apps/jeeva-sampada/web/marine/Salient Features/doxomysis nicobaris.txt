SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological: Diagnostic characteristics:      Body smooth. Frontal plate triangular with pointed apex. Eyes large, cornea divided into two pigmentary parts with ocelli free part in between. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Endopod of maxilla armed with ten strong spines and two plumose setae. Lateral border of telson with six spines on distal one third, apices of lobes of telson with five stout spines, apical cleft one third length of telson and armed with ten to thirteen pairs of spines and two median setae. Endopod of uropod twentyfour , small and large sharp spines arranged irregularly along inner border.
