
SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Columnar, columns thick, obtuse at the top, sometimes divided.  Living zone often confined to the upper parts of the columns.  Corallites polygonal, wall slightly thickened thus giving a rounded appearance to the calices.  Calices 4 to 5 mm in diameter, 1 to 2 mm deep.  Thickness of the wall 0.5 to 1 mm.  Septa in three complete cycles, the third fusing to the second.  Septal edges with 5 o 7 teeth; septal sides granular.  Columella trabecular, composed of concentric layers of synapticulae.  Shallower calices six pali very prominent, in deeper ones they less developed.  The development of pali shows much variation in different parts of the same corallum.  In some cases a second cycle of pali may be visible.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
