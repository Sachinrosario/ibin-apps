SALIENT FEATURES:							Ref No:52, 103

Morphological:   Body almost entirely hairless; rostrum reaching posterior margin of 2nd to distal
margin of 3rd antennular segment, armed with 5 to 7 dorsal teeth, toothless on little less than its distal half, rostral crest high; carapace with postrostral carina not reaching posterior margin, adrostral carina and sulcus reaching as far as second rostral tooth, brachiocardiac carina feeble, not reaching middle of carapace; telson armed only with spinules, 1 or 2 distal pairs larger; 1st pereopod with a small ischial spine; basial spine on 3rd pereopod of male simple; merus of 5th pereopod in adult male with a proximal notch followed by a keel-shaped tubercle. Distomedian projections of petasma with a long and slender apical filament on each ; distolateral projections directed anterolaterally. Thelycum with white conjoined pads in impregnated females; anterior plate large square-shaped and grooved medially; lateral plates boomerang-shaped and enclosing two pear-shaped plates.


Diagnostic characteristics:     Posterior part of rostrum with distinctly elevated crest, distal half 
toothless; basial spine on 3rd pereopod of male simple; distomedian projections of petasma with long and slender apical filaments; anterior plate of thelycum large square-shaped.

       Colour:  Body flesh coloured with sparsely distributed brown dots; distal part of telson and 
         uropods brown to rusty red.

Feeding habit:									Ref. No:65, 150
    
 Main food: Crustaceans (copepods, remains of penaeids) molluscan remains, 
  	       foraminiferans, echiurids, fish remains, polychaetes, algae and detritus.
 Feeding type:  Omnivorous bottom feeder.

 Additional remarks:  More feeding activity during daytime.

Eggs and larvae:								 Ref. No:153

  Characteristics:   Fertilized eggs are spherical measuring 0.28 to 0.29 mm in diameter. The embryo
hatches out in 9 hours after spawning and passes  through 6 naupliar and 3 protozoeal substages before transforming into mysis stage. The first mysis substage measures 1.96 to 1.99 mm total length, and is characterised by carapace with rostrum falling short of anterior margin of eye, toothless, well developed antennal and pterygostomial spines, distinct supraorbital spines; 5th and 6th abdominal segments with dorsal spines and telson with a deep cleft and 7+7 setae.
