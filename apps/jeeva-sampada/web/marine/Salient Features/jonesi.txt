SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:    Body rather stout, abdomen steadily narrowing backwards.                             
Diagnostic characteristics: -                     Anterior border of carapace produced into broad triangular apically blunt rostrum. Eyes small, cornea narrower than eye. Labrum with short frontal process. Thoracic endopods stout, fifth endopod very long and stout reaching beyond antennular peduncle when bent forwards. Pleopods not modified, pseudobranchial lobes spirally coiled.  Telson broad and linguiform with broadly rounded apex, lateral border with 28-32 spines in male and 19-24 spines in female, falling into definite series, basal widened part with 2-3 spines ; apex armed with pair of long spines, three small median spines and two plumose setae. Exopod of uropod longer with four spines; endopod with 19-23 spines in male and 15-18 spines in females, spines regularly increasing  in size from first to last.     

In female, carapce with prominent protuberance in front   of cervical sulcus.      

The stout fifth thoracic endopod and the short frontal processes of the labrum easily distinguish this species.

Adult male and female -       5.8-8.8mm
