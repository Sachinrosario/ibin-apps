SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:  

Diagnostic characteristics:              Closely allied to P. pusilla and differ from it as follows. Frontal plate of carapace considerably narrower. Eyes larger, stalk increasing in width distal wards. Exopod of second pleopod of male single jointed and apical seta shorter than in P. pusilla. Telson more than three times as long as on broad, lateral borders with four to five spines, telsonic sinus with 4-6 pairs of spines. Endopod of uropod clearly over reaching exopod and with single spine on middle inner border.           

Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Adult male and female : 2.9 – 3.2 mm.
