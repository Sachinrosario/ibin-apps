SALIENT FEATURES:                  Ref No:49, 103

 

Morphological:  Body almost entirely pubescent, some times partly or completely hairless;

rostrum slightly sinuous reaching proximal to distal margin of 3rd antennular segment or exceeding it, armed with 8 to 11 teeth along entire dorsal margin; carapace with postrostral carina generally ending near posterior margin, adrostral  carina, ending behind 2nd rostral tooth, adrostral sulcus ending a little behind epigastric tooth; branchiocardiac carina slightly sinuous and reaching posterior extension of hepatic spine; telson armed with spinules; 1st pereopod with small or without ischial spine, merus of 5th pereopod in adult males with a proximal notch followed by a twisted, keeled tubercle. Distomedian projections of petasma crescent-shaped leaning on distolateral projections and concealing them partly or completely; distolateral projections directed anterolaterally. Thelycum with anterior plate deeply grooved longitudinally and considerably wider posteriorly; posterior transverse ridge with 2 anterolateral rounded projections partly covering lateral plates.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Branchiocardiac carina on carapace reaching posterior extension of

hepatic spine; distomedian projections of petasma crescent –shaped; anterior plate of thelycum longitudinally grooved and wider posteriorly.

 

         Colour:   Body translucent bluish green mottled with chromatophores. Uropods tipped with

    conspicuous green.

 

Feeding habit:                 Ref. No:174, 217

   

 Main food:     Vegetable matter, small crustaceans (copepods & ostracods), polychaetes,    

                        echiurids, molluscs and fish remains.

         Feeding type: Omnivorous feeder, larger prawns showing preference to a molluscan diet.

Eggs and larvae:                  Ref. No:56, 59, 122, 136

 

  Characteristics:   Eggs are opaque and spherical measuring 0.25 to 0.27 mm in diameter. The

embryo hatches out in 15 to 17 hours and passes through 6 nauplius, 3 protozoea and 5 mysis sub-stages, followed by 2 intermediate stages, before transforming into postlarva.  The last mysis stage has a short rostrum hardly reaching half eye, 2 dorsal spines, 2-segmented pleopods and a posteriorly convex telson bearing 7 +7 setae on distal portion. Postlarvae measure 3.2 to 3.9 mm total length, with the rostrum short, sharply pointed bearing 3 dorsal spines, telson with 7 pairs of spines on sides and distal margin, and characteristically a single chromatophore at tip of each uropod ramus.

 

 

Abundance:     Larvae of the species have been observed in the inshore waters throughout the west

                    coast of India, with peak abundance between Goa and Bombay.
