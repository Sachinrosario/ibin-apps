SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branching on all sides and bushy in appearance. The branches shoot from the main stem, single or in two or four branchlets. Branching is dichotomous. The internodes are longitudinal; the polyps are arranged single in 2 rows at the branches and alternate at 3-5 mm long. The tentacles arranged in 3 or 4 longitudinal rows and the pinnnulae are also filled with longitudinally with flat, warted spicules.  
		Spicules: The spicules of the polyps are fine and thorned and irregularly arranged. The bark contains isolated spicules of 0.18 mm long.
