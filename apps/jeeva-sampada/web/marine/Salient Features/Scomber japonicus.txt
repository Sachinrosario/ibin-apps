SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
	
Body elongate and rounded, caudal peduncle slim. Snout pointed, front and hind margins of eye covered by an adipose eyelid; teeth in upper and lower jaws small and conical; teeth also present on vomer and palatines (roof of mouth); gillrakers 25 to 35 on lower limb of fist arch, usually, 29 to 33 in the area. Two widely separated dorsal fins the first with 9 or 10 spines; anal spine fairly stiff and strong; 5 dorsal and 5 anal finlets; a single small flap (Interpelvic process) between pelvic fins. Scales behind head and around pectoral fins larger and more conspicuous than those covering rest of body, but no well developed corselet. Two small keels on each side of caudal peduncle (at base of caudal fin lobes), but no central keel between them. Swimbladder present.
	

Colour : back steel-blue crossed by faint wavy lines; lower sides and belly silvery-yellow with numerous dusky, rounded blotches.
