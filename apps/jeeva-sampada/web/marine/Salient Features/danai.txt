Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  The largest in the Indian Museum is nearly 28 cm in greater diameter and the smallest (No. 2846/3) is 7.5 cm in diameter.  The underside is all concave with a highly convex upperside.  Axial fossa very short (a specimen 28 cm in diameter has an axial fossa about 3 cm long).  Major septa broad, standing more than 5 mm above the higher cycles.  They are highly exsert around the axial fossa.  The septal edges within the fossa entire.  Dentition on the septa septa uniform, 6 to 7 teeth per cm length of septum.  Septal sides almost smooth.  Costae correspond to septa, those of the major septa about 2 mm thick and high.  Costal spines blunt or pointed at the tips, larger ones upto 3 mm thick at the base, swollen.  Spines become shorter at the centre of the disc.  Disc perforated.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
