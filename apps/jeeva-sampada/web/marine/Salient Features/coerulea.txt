Descriptive characters:  The genus like Tubipora is monotypic. 

The corallum exhibit wide range of variation in growth form.  It is either with digitiform thick branches or commonly of flabellate coalascent wide plates.  Sometimes encrusting.  Surface the large and small pores.  Ten to 16 pseudo sept is present.  Surface coenenchyme (peritheca) with fine granulations.

Living coral so also dired skeleton blue.

Meristic characteristics:

Main food:  Plankton.   

Additional remarks:  The species was growing is large thickets on the lagoon reef and inner lagoon of Minicoy.  It is common throughout Lakshadweep.  Not recorded from Gulf of Mannar.


Egg and larvae: Planktonic
