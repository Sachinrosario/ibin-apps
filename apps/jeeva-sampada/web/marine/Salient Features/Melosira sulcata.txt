SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Long, closely fitting chains with disc shaped cells. Bowl shaped valves with short mantle, constricted at the base. 
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Flat disc with strong cell wall and papilla-like structures at the border of the valve. Ground membrane of valve with rows of pores. Numerous small disc shaped chromatophores.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Valves 11-30m in diameter and 5-8m in height.
