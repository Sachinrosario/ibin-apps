Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of thick vertical plates 3 to 8 cm broad and 3 to 4 cm thick (based on specimens from Lakshadweep).  Large colonies about half a metre in spread and 30 to 40 cm in total height.  The lower parts are often dead.  The corallites at the growing edges much crowded, touching each other and often fused at the sides, very little exsert 1 wall average 1 mm thick, 4 to 4 mm in diameter.  Septa 12 all well developed.  Corallites at the sides of the plates half tubular, tubular or nariform, inner wall less developed than the outer, outer thickened.  Corallites ascending apressed or spreading to 600.  length 2 to 4 mm average 2.5 mm diameter 2 to 3 mm.  Directive septa prominent, laterals of the first cycle poorly developed.  Secondaries generally not represented.  In some specimens some of the radials are proliferous and form a rosette over the ridges formed by the folding of the plates (as in cuneata).  The wall of the radials with prominent plate-like echinulations.  Corallum almost dense in section.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
