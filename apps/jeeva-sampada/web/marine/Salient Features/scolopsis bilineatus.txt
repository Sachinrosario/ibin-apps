SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body moderately deep (depth in front of dorsal fin 2.5 to 3  times in standard length), with a convex dorsal profile and a pointed snout. A snout, backward projecting spine just below eye;  preceded by a small, antrorse (forward-pointing) spine.  Dorsal fin with 10 spines and 9 soft rays, continuous, unnotched; anal fin with 3 spines (the 2nd equal to or greeter than the 3rd) and 7 soft  rays ; pectoral fins with 2 unbranched and 15 branched rays. Pre dorsal scales (on top of head ) beginning in front of eyes, reaching on snout to a line connecting anterior nostrills; 4 or 5 transverse rows of scales on cheek; lower limb of preopercle with 1 or 2 rows of small scales.

Colour : 

generally olive to pale gray; a pair of distinctive narrow oblique stripes from eye and rear corner of mouth to base of first soft dorsal rays; also a pair of short yellow stripe from upper portion of eye to area below anterior dorsal spines; a ehitish patch just below base of posterior half of soft dorsal dorsal rays; spinous part of dorsal fin yellow, soft part with a black  patch covering most of anterior portion, remainder of fin white or transparent; caudal fin mainly transparent; anal fin black on anterior half and white or transparent on remaining section; pelvic and pectoral fins whitish.
