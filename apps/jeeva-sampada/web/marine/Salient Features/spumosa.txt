Descriptive characters:  A specimen from Manauli Island in Gulf of Mannar has a greater spread of 30 cm with a total height of 16 cm.  Upper surface has thick digitiform or columnar branches all fusing to form a solid mass.  A few branches are slender having a central worm tube.  The entire surface bears large number of knobs formed by the coenenchymal elevations carrying calices.  Calices conspicuous, but a well formed thecal wall is generally absent.  Diameter of the calices 0.6 to 0.8 mm average 0.75 mm, a diameter to 1 mm apart.  First cycle of septa prominent, the directives often meet each other.  Secondaries of varying numbers, the cycle rarely complete.

The coenenchyme between the knobs and gibbosities is generally smooth and possesses calices.  The papillae are better developed on the knobs, they are round-topped or pointed, about 1 mm in height and thickness, occupying almost the entire inter-calicular areas.  Under the lens the smooth regions reveals a flaky reticulum.

Living corallum yellowish brown.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic 

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
