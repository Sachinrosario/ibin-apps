SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 
Rostrum pointed, extends to the middle of 2nd  segment of antennular peduncle; a tubercle with a spine present at the base of rostrum; supraorbital, antennal and pterygostomian spines present; behind the pterygostomian there are 2 lateral spines; eyes large and borne on elongated peduncle; a short spine present on the eye stalk; pereopod 2 and 5 developed; P2 is seen as biramous buds; the propodus of P5 flattened much like a paddle; a short pair of dorsolateral spines present on abdominal somite 5; telson triangular,broad,posteriorly, carrying 7+7 spines, innermost being nonplumose; uropod biramous; legs P3 and P4 developed at a later stage. Pereopods1 and 2 are chelate. Pereopod 5 without exopod. Telson with 2 pairs of lateral and6 pairs of terminal spines. In farther advanced stages the terminal spines reduced to5 pairs.Diagnostic characteristics: Rostrum with dorsal and ventral teeth; carapace with lateral carinae. The delayed development of pereopods 3 and 4; larger chela on P2; telson pointed with 2 lateral and 5 terminal spines.

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.
Main food: Plankton, nauplii of brine shrimp, Artemia( zoeal stages); detritus with  sand and mud, worms, mussels and small crustaceans.
Feeding type: Omnivorous; occasionally cannibalistic.

Size and age: 
Zoeae I-IX: length 2.23mm-11.39mm.
Psotlarva I: length 11.14mm 

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs:Eggs are carried with their body (berried) and hatch out directly into a Zoea.
Zoea I:  length 2.23mmCarapace simple,eyes sessile; mandible asymmetrical; posterior margin of abdominal somite 5 with a spine dorsally.Zoea II: length 2.75mmCarapace with supraorbital spine; peduncle of A1 unsegmented; antennal scale distally with 3 segments; P1 as biramous bud.Zoea III: lengt 2.82mmEye-stalk becomes elongated with a spine on it; antennal spine on carapace; P1,P2 present,biramous and P5 uniramous; telson separated from abdomen; telson with 7pairs of spines, the innermost non plumose and short; uropods with exo and endopods.Zoea IV: length 3.53mmCarapace with grooves, a tubercle with a spine at base of rostrum; suprorbital, antennal and pterygostomian spines present; 2 lateral spines seen behind the pterygostomian (anteroinferior margin) spine; eyes on an elongated peduncle, a projection developed  at its distal part; P2 and P5 developed well and P3 seen as biramous buds; the propodus of leg 5 very much flattened,like a paddle. Telson with a pair of lateral and 7 pairs of distal spines.Zoea V: length 3.68mmCarapace without the lateral spines at the anteroinferior margin; biramous buds of P4 seen; P5 elongated, carpus 4 short setae and 1 spine; telson rectangular with 2 pairs of lateral and 6 pairs of terminal spines of which the 2nd and 4th the longest.Zoea VI: length 4.0mmThis stage is characterized by the presence of 1 dorsal tooth on rostrum and the development of P4.Zoea VII: lengh 5.0mmPleopods appear as uniramous buds; telson pointed with 2 pairs of laterals and 5 pairs of terminal spines.Zoea VIII: length 9.11mmRostrum with 3-4 dorsal teeth; eyes very prominent, stalk with a spine, and as long as  the A1 peduncle; pleopods biramous and exopod with a short seta. Telson same.Zoea IX: length 11.39mmRostrum with 9 dorsal teeth; suprorbital spine becomes short; P1 and P2 chelate; pleopods biramous with setae; telson tapering and carries 3 pairs of spines and 2 pairs of bristle-like setae, inner pair plumose and outermost spine very short.Postlarva I: length 11.4mmRostrum with 12 dorsal and 6 ventral teeth; carapace with antennal and pterygostomian spines; eyes large,peduncle short and devoid of spine; mandibles much elongated, only molar processes prominent; exopods on pereopods 1-4 rudimentary and on P5 absent; telson tapers towards tip and ends in a median point, on either side of which 2 spines are present, outer small and placed slightly above the inner spine; large plumose setae present all along the margin of telson.
