Morphological:
Diagnostic characteristics:
Body slender and smooth, carapace anteriorly produced into broad triangular rostrum with obtusely pointed apex. Eyes large and well developed. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Second segment of mandibular palp without denticles. Thoracic endopods three to eight with unsegmented carpopropodus, dactylus ending in long slender nail. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male extending to distal end of telson, three segmented, third segment terminating in two stiff spines, inner spine slender and naked, outer spine stout with bunch of strong barbs in middle, a basal segment clearly demarcated on outer spine. Telson deeply cleft distally, cleft about 0.45 length of telson, wide and round at bottom, margin of cleft unarmed; lateral margin slightly concave and armed with 5-6 well spaced spines, lobes of telson narrow and terminating into single spine. Uropods long and slender, endopod with single spine on inner ventral region of statocyst.
