SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Body almost round, apical part somewhat tapered gently, still remaining convex always with an abrupt knick passing on to apical horn. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle slightly dextral. Sulcus slightly narrowing towards end, beset with two tiny spines at terminus.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 40µ; breadth: 38µ.
