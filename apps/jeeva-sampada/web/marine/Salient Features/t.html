SALIENT FEATURES:

 

Morphological:

Body laterally compressed, ovate, short, with small deciduous scales, eyes lateral, gill opening moderately wide, lateral line complete, mouth small, jaws with single series of teeth, esophageal teeth present, dorsal fin large and single, spinous portion embedded in the skin. Caudal forked.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:

5-10 blade like spines in front of dorsal and anal fins; lower jaw slightly protruding, gill opening a vertical slit, dorsal  and anal fin falcate; caudal deeply forked, lower lob longer; scales cycloid and deciduous; coloration upper surface gray, sides and head silvery tending to white on abdomen, small black dots  all over. The young are dark in colour with vertical fins almost black and the lower lob of the caudal longer than in adult.

                                                                                                     Ref: No.21

Feeding habits: Feeds on macro-zooplankton

 

Main food: Thaliaceans and hydromedusae. Young fishes have been found to feed on  

                  copepods and small crustaceans (Paracalanus, Oithona, Acartia, Ostracods,

                 amphipods, Lucifer, etc). Molluscan larvae and filamentous algae also have

                  been noticed. There are fluctuations in the occurrence of various food items

                 which appeared to be related to the abundance of food items in the

                 environment.

                

            Mostly digested material and gelatinous substance with occasional occurrence

                of copepods and amphipods. Young ones of Apogon sp., Myctophum sp and          

                Nematopalaemon tenuipes.

 

            Most of the earlier workers consider copepods a very important part of the diet

                                                                           Ref.No. 13 ,18,19

               

            Copepods 3.87-19.2% and semidigested matter 67-90%              Ref. No. 14

            Many workers recorded Salps, medusae and ctenophores             Ref. No.24

 

 

           Food of silver pomfrets collected from the pair trawling operations in the Palk

             Bay during February-April 1982 consisted  mostly of crustaceans apart from

            larval bivalves, gastropods and polychaetes.                                     Ref.No 22

 

Feeding type :                 Carnivore
