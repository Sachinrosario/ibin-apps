SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:    
Rostrum long and extends beyond the 3rd segment of the antennular peduncle; bears 2 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth; carapace bears an antennal, pterygostomian and hepatic spines; thoracic legs 1-3 chelate and all legs with exopods; long dorsomedian spines present on 2-6 abdominal somites and the spine on 2nd the longest; posteriorwardly curved ventromedian spines present on 1-5 abdominal somites; paired dorsolaterals present on ab.somites 5-6; the 6th somite is more than double the length of somite 5 and also bearing 5 pairs of ventrolateral spines, with an additional pair of posteroventral spines.Pleopods  uniramous; uropods well developed with telson; the exopod of uropod bears a strong spine at the tip and 7 spines on its outer margin; telson with a wide gap,bearing a pair of long posterolateral spines and 3 pairs of lateral spines one of which is placed in the middle of the telson plate and 14-16 pairs of terminal setae.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum long; presence of a hepatic spine; long dorsomedian spines on abdominal somites 2-6; ventromedian spines on somites 1-5,curved posteriorward; the 6th somite with 5 pairs of ventrolateral spines; telson V- shaped with 14 -17 pairs of inner setae and a  pair of long fused posterolateral spines, with 3 marginal spines; pleopods uniramous and legs with exopods.

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.
Main food:  Plankton, detritus with  sand and mud.
Feeding type: Omnivorous

Additonal remarks: 
The larvae of Aristaeomorpha and Funchalia woodwardi are having very similar characters. Ventromedian spines present in both; 6th abdominal somite bearing  rows of ventrolateral spines; telson also similar in shape and structure.
Size and age: Mysis III: 10mm.

Mysis III: length 10mm.Rostrum long and toothed; 2 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth; carapace with antennal, pterygostomian and hepatic spines;  all thoracic legs with exopodites; legs 1-3 chelate; long, posteriorly curved dorsomedian spines present on abdominal somites 2-6; ventromedian spines on first 5 abdominal somites;  6th somite bearing 5 rows of ventrlateral spines and a pair of posterolateral spine; pleopods uniramous; telson with a wide gap, with a pair of long posterolateral spines and 3 pairs of marginal spines,the proximal pair being placed in the middle of the telson plate.
