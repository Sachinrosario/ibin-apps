SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallet blade thick and heavy like an elongated triangle or goblet shaped, flattened on the inner face and strongly convex on outer face, broadening distally where the concave border is caped by a zone of brownish periostracum. Distal border of calcareous portion of blade irregularly truncate. Extent and colour of periostracum variable, stalk stout tapering, shorter than the blade normally.

	Shell higher than broad, anterior slope small, posterior slope short internally overlapping the disc.
