SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony is almost fan-shaped. Stem and branches are circular in cross-section. Diagonal leaf-nerves seem to be absent or are very rare. The internodes are 6-8 mm long. The polyps stand sideways and are not close; their calyces are small; only 0.5 mm high, 0.7 mm in diameter; their walls is filled with thorny clubs, the tips of which project over the surface. The retractile polyps bear a ring of horizontal spindles, over that each 2 or more converging spindles, which continue longitudinal spicules the dorsal central line of the tentacles. The bark tissues are leaf and thorn calyces as well as triangular forms, while usual; spindles are rare and represent small, bent forms provided with warted belts
		Spicules. There are large triangular (Fig. a), irregular (Fig.) and club-shaped spindles (Fig.). 

Colour		:	The entire colony is lemon yellow and axis is dark pink.
