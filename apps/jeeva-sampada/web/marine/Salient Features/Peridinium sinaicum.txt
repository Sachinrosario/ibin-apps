SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small rounded cells. Epitheca somewhat conical with clearly demarcated apical horn. Girdle not as slightly dextral to one half-furrow width.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcal wings connected with the process. Right process free, separated away from the sulcus.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 28-31m.
