SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves broadly rod shaped, sides of the valves nearly parallel, ends slightly sigmoid. Raphe obliquely straight, near ends excentric.

Diagnostic characteristics: Transversal striae at their middle part sub radial, 16/16 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 75-118µ, width 11-14m.
