Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum caespitose to caespito corymbose, tufted with a broad base.  Tips of branches tapering with crowded corallites.  Some branches have bottle-brush appearance.  Axial corallites rounded, 2.5 to 4.5 mm, wall thick.  Radial corallites of different size, thick and shapes, those on the branchlets are up to 5 mm long, nariform, those on the lower sides of the branches smaller 1 to 2 mm.  Septa subequal, both the cycles are well developed.  Wall of the radials with spinnules, surface coenostem reticulate.

According to Veron and Wallae (loc cit) “Living colonies are uniform grey, cream or purple in colour”.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The growth form is more or less that of A. humilis but the branches are more subdividing with papering apices.  The axials are thinner and the radials are shorter in L. lutkeni.

This is the first published record of this species from Indian region and is based on collections made by the present author from Wandoor Marine Park.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
