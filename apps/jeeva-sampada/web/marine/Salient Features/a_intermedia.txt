Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum arborescent, main branches 1 to 2 cm thick at the base, subdividing, upper branchlets more or less 1 cm thick; 5 to 8 cm long with tapering apices.  Axial corallites average 2.5 mm at the wall, 2 mm exsert, wall thick and costalate.  Opening rounded.  Primary cycle of septa prominent, usually unite at the centre of the axial fossa; secondaries smaller, rarely the cycle is complete.

Radial corallites are of different sizes and shapes.  Larger radials tubular with the inner wall slightly less developed than the outer.  At the upper half of the branches the radials area ascending, but lower down they are all placed at right angle to the branch surface.  Larger radials 2 to 3 mm in length, 1.75 to 2 mm thick, getting smaller towards the basal parts of the branches the smaller radials are fully tubular with a circular opening.  Two cycles of septa present in older radials.  Wall porous, striated and echinulate.  Surface coenenchyme reticulate with weak echinulations.

Living coral brown with white axial corallites.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
