SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Body stout. Carapace with pointed rostrum. Eyes large, cornea wider than stalk. Antennal scale little shorter than antennular peduncle and in female extending to distal margin. Carpopropodus undivided. Pseudobranchial rami spirally coiled. Exopod of third pleopod and both rami of fourth pleopod with modified setae. Telson three times as long as broad, steadily narrowing to apex, basal part with three large spines, distal two third with closely set spines arranged in series; apex with pair of long spines, three small median spines and pair of plumose setae. Exopod of uropod longer than endopod, outer margin of proximal segment with 14-18 spines; endopod with serially arranged spines.


Size and age

Maxikmum length:     Male and female -  7-8.6 mm
