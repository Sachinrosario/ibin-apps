SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells globular with a conspicuous, but low apical horn. Girdle not displaced; provided with narrow lists, spines absent. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus reaching nearly to the centre of the hypotheca and expanding posteriorly. Theca with fine pores apart.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 40-56µ.
