SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No: 103

 

Morphological:  Rostrum reaching middle to ¾ of eye, convex on ventral margin and carries 6 to

9 dorsal teeth; postrostral crest markedly elevated and laminose; reaching posterior margin of carapace and interrupted by a notch just ahead of cervical groove; the latter is deep and reaching or almost reaching dorsal midline; postorbital spine present; suprahepatic and branchiostegal spines absent; pterygostomian angle broadly rounded and unarmed; hepatic crest curved downward anteriorly, with a sharp bending near its anterior end delimiting a round loop just behind frontal margin of carapace; branchiocardiac crest oblique, its anterior part curving ventrally; telson trifurcate, with pair of fixed distolateral spines; antennular flagellum as long as carapace including rostrum and tapers abruptly towards rounded apex; 5th pereopod with a coxal spine

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:    Rostrum reaching middle to ¾ of eye, postrostral crest markedly

elevated and laminose and interrupted by a notch just ahead of cervical groove; suprahepatic spine absent, telson trifurcate with a pair of distolateral spines, 5th   pereopod with a coxal spine.

 

        Colour:    Body, pereopods and pleopods red; antennae banded dark red and white,

        uropods predominantly dark red.

 

Sex attributes:

 Descriptive characters:   In the petasma of adult male, the notch separating dorsal and ventral lobes of medial segments is located  closer to ends of lateral segments than to distal end of petasma when viewed from ventral side.  Distal segment of appendix masculina elongated and triangular.  In the thelycum of female, pairs of hairy protuberances are located between coxopodites of 4th and 5th pereopods.
