SALIENT FEATURES

Morphological : Spindle shaped cells with more or less pointed ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Long chains, the ends of cells lying pressed to each other for a short distance. Striate 12 in 10µ

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 
Length : 50 – 131 µ
Breadth : 3.5 – 5 µ

Biochemical aspects : Phototroph which requires Vitamin B12.
Extracellular carbohydrates are polysaccharider.
