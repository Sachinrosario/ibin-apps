Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum branching, 10 to 15 cm in total height with a greater spread of 20 cm.  Branches subdividing, corallites mono to tristomodaeal, the former condition being most common.  Single corallites 15-20 mm in greater diameter, valleys upto 40 mm in length, depth about 15 mm, rarely upto 20 mm.  Wall thin and fragile.  Septa little exsert, vertical at first, broader below, 13-17 per cm length of the wall of which 4-5 about 6 mm broad.  Septal edges entire, side granular.  Columella absent.  Costae of the major septa prominent, extend to the base of the branches, those of the subsidiary septa obsolete.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio.
