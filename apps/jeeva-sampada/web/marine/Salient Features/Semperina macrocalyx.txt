SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony incomplete, consisting of and erect stem wit short scattered branches. Length 13.5 cm. The stem and branches are round, the former 3 mm in diameter. The first branch arises 2.6 cm from the basal end of the stem and is simple. The calyces are irregularly distributed and branches and on all sides of the distal parts of the colony. The individual calyces are long, tubular and project at right angles from the branches measuring 1.8 mm in height and 2 mm in diameter. The polyps are retractile. The calyx walls filled with thorny spindles arranged en cheveron.
		Spicules: All are slender spindles (fig. a, b and c) differing mainly in the number of thorn-like points scattered over their surfaces. 

Colour		:	The colony is light yellowish brown.
