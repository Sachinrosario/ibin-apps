Salient Features :
It is a rigid herb, densely tall up to 60-150 cm.
Leaves are long, solid, pointed at their end.
Stem erect, terat, striate.
Flowers in decompounds, cymes with erect branches, floret bracts, ovate mucronate, the margins broadly membranous. Sepal pale green with membranous margins, lanceolate, acute, petals slightly shorter obtuse.
Stamens 6, filament dilated and connate at the base.
Millions of minute seeds produced per plant.

Industries:
