Salient features:
Morphological:

Female Surface without pubescence or setae except on hind part of telson, and on outer ramus f uropod; a transverse series of 4 tiny pits on hind margin of head and each of the peraeon segments, from which arise minutetufts of setules; two similar pits on process of fourth pleon segment. Head and first pereon segment closely punctate, the punctae not covering the very slightly raised vermiculate markings on the dorso-lateral parts of first segment. Segments 2-7 gramlate, the gramles on segment two more or less aggregated to form two slightly raised ridges, one on anterior, the other on posterior margin of the segment; these ridges are more distinct in segments 307.

  Pleon segment 4 with a broad apically squarely truncate, conical projection, not reaching quite to end of telson. This projection has a shallow media-dorsal groove, and a similar groove on each lateral margin; ventrally near the end there is a semicircular ridge which projects laterally like a small conical tooth.
  
Telson strongly convex proximally, with 2 conical bosses which fit in front of the lateral projections of the process of the 4th segment when the later is depressed; distally the telson is depressed, with 2 longitudinally oval perforations; apical margin with squarish median lobe. Whole surface of pleon and telson, except a bare area adjoining the insertion of the uropods; strongly granulate.

  Peraeopod 1, 3rd joint with 1 spine on outer distal margin, 4th, 5th and 6th joints with 5,3,4 spines respectively on inner margins.

   Pleopod, 2, stylet half as long again as inner ramus. Penial processes on 7th sternite elongate, extending to end of outer ramus of pleopod 1 (in situ).

  Urood, peduncle with upper surface shallowly punctate and with 3 low knobs near hinge, outer ramus granulate and setose, apically truncate, with 2 conical knobs.

   Ovigerous o+ (with modified mouth-parts)- surface much smoother, punctate but with scarcely any granulation. The setiferous pits as in , the pair on the 4th pleon segment being ituated at the apices of very low and inconspicuous bosses. Two bosses, more conspicuous, on telson, the bluntly pointed apex of which overhangs the apical notch.
  Spines on peraeopod 1 as in . Uropods as in figure 14, sparsely setose.
  The smallest juv. Resembles the adult (except of course as regards the mouth-parts).

  Immature resembles the , but the surface is more granulate, the knobs on 4th pleon segment and telson are more prominent, and the outer ramus of uropod is shorter and broader, especially in the 10 and 11mm. Specimens. In theses latter the penial processes extend to end of peduncle of pleopod 1.


Additional remarks: Thomson (1879) in his original description of cilicaex canaliculata (naming this species as Nesea camiculata) did not notice the pair of perforations in the telson.
  The broad square process of the fourth pleon segment and the peculiar perforations in the telson are enough to separate this species from all other of the genus. When the animal is completely rolled up, the epistome and peduncles of first antennae fit closely against the lower surface of the telsornic margin; and the two perforation allow the access of water to the branchial pleopods.
