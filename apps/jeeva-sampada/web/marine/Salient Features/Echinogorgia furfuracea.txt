SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony flabellate measuring 10.5 cm. in height and 8.5 cm. in diameter. The main stem is erect and sinuous. The main stem is slightly flattened basally with 3 mm in diameter and 2 mm in thickness. The individual calyces are dome-shaped verrucae within which the polps are of complete retraction.
		
Spicules: The spicules consist of basal part composed of several ray-like densely tuberculate points radiating from a common centre. Above this centre arises a cluster of smooth, more or less convoluted, leaf-like expansions enclosing a central space. Each of these expansions is heavily keeled, the keel being most conspicuous part and having its free edge finely dentate. The central space is by the bases to which the keels are attached. There are relatively few triradiate forms, crosses, double etc., and a few ordinary spindles and which are all warty and spiny.
 
Colour			:	The colony is light pinkish brown, the coenenchyma darker than calyces. The axis is dark brown and the spicules colorless.
