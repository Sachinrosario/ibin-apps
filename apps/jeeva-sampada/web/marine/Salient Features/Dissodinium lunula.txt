SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Unarmored, small gmnodinoid reproductive stage, with various cystic life stages including stage I, which is spherical, and stage V, which is broadly crescent, shaped.

Diagnostic characteristics: Cystic stages non-motile, usually found floating. Chloroplasts present.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 73µm. Length: 137µm.
