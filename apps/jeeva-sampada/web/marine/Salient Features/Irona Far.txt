SALIENT FEATURES :
MORPHOLOGICAL :
Ref No: Pillai, 1958

Female : Body slightly asymmetrical, cephalon triangular, its anterior border rounded, prominently constricted in front of the eyes; eyes prominent and reniform. First perseon segment longest, its anterior border well arched, antero-lateral angles produced into large blunt lobes; segments two to four subequal, fiveand six shorter, seventh shortest; postero-lateral angles of peraeon segments two to seven angular, those of first rounded; seventh segment considerably overlapping the lateral parts of pleon. Coxal plates very large and projecting much beyond the peraeon segments; anterior plates comparitively narrow and long, posterior short but broad and tha last posteriorly rounded and overreaching the postero-lateral corners of seventh paeraeon segment. Pleon very short but broader than peraeon, very well immersed; first segment completely and the second partially hidden. Telson as broad as pleon, very large,and perfectly semicircular, with a moderately pronounced median longitudinal carina.

First antenna stout, eight jointed, third segment drawn out. Second antenna slender, nine jointed, longer than first. Palp of mandible well developed, first segment very large, third comparitvely small. Uropods not reaching the apex of telson, rami long and narrow and exopod longer than endopod.

Body white, with a light yellowish tint on the dorsal side. There is a very narrow transverse strip pf pigment along the posteriorborder of the first peraeon segment. In the succeeding segments it expands more and more till it occupies nearly the entire posterior half of the seventh segment. Dorsal side of sixth segment of peraeopods, usually visible in the dorsal view, also dark brown.

Male.
	Male very much like the female in generalshape of body. Peraeon somewhat parallel sided, third segment slightly broadre than the others and the coxal plates moderately developed. Pleon longer than in the female, first segment slightly broader than peraeon. Telson as broad as pleon, almost as long as broad. First antenna eight joined, faintly demarcated into a three jointed peduncle and a five jointed flagellum. Flagellar segments with dorsal setae and ventral sensory hairs; second antenna eight jointed, with scattered setae.Anteriorperaeopods without spines. Uropods slender and long, outer ramus one and a half times as long as the inner.
	Colour of body similar to that of female, pigmentation less intense.

First Larva :
Body Slender and nearly parallel sided. Cephalon twice as broad as long, with an antero-median point. Eyes large and well developed. Peraeon slightly longer than pleon, segments equal in length and breadth, seventh segment short. Telson longer than broad. First antenna stout, eight jointed, second antenna slender, nine to ten jointed. Sixth segment of anterior peraeopods not expanded, with three small teeth. Exopod of uropod considerably longer than endopod, both without setae, but with knobs along the borders.

Diagnostic Characteristics :

Coral plates thick and narrow; legs slender; pleon broader than Peraeon, well immersed; telson broader than Pleon, semicircular; uropods not overreaching telson.

Additional Remarks ::
Holotype (female) and allotype(male) are in the Indian Museum Kolkatta and paratypes in the Zoological Laboratory, University pof Madras, Chennai.

Size and Age :  Length of female 20.0 to 25.0mm; Male 12.5 to 16.0mm.
