SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony branches scanty and lateral in a plane. The long and equally thick branches gives off at right angles bending upwards. The main stem and branches are flattened in one plane. The basal portion is sterile. The polyps are arranged sideways in each 1 or 2 longitudinal rows. The water vascular system consists of 2 longitudinal canals having a broad opening.
		Spicules:  The calyces contain 0.07-0.09 mm long double spindles with closely warted ends. The thick bark is reinforced with 0.04-0.07 mm long club shaped with closely warted spindles. 



Colour	:	The colony is yellowish red  in colour.
