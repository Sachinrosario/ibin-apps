SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting, tending to become massive.  Corallites polygonal, wall fused, 3-9 mm thick.  Calices 10-25 mm in greater length, 10 to 15 mm deep.  Septa range from 30 to 50 depending on the size of the corallites.  Septa exsert and swollen at the wall, swollen part hollow, edges strongly dentate, upper 2 to 3 teeth swollen and vertical.  About 15 septa fuse to the columella others turn towards and fuse to the sides of the major ones.  Columella trabecular with vertical papillose projections.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
