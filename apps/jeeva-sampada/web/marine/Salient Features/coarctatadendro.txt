Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum tufted, radiating from a central stem.  Corallites 10 to 12 mm in diameter, 10 mm deep, upto 15 mm in height.  Septa in four complete cycles, in larger corallites with a few of the fifth.  Primaries and secondaries larger than others, not exsert.  Septa at the wall narrower than within the calyx, vertically descending.  Edges of septa entire or microscopically serrated in higher cycles.  Columella spongy, convex, circular or elongated.  Costae specimen is represented by a single corallite with two buds.  A third specimen is present in the collections of the Biological Station of the Krusadai Island.  It has central stem 28 mm thick from 11 corallites radiate.  The colony has a total height of 8 cm.  The larger corallites are 17 to 19 mm in diameter with 95 to 105 septa.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
