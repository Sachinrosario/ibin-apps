SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells with broadly elliptic valvar plane. Two hyaline eyes of 11.5m in diameter opposite each other. Valves sculptured with strong radial ribs, which become faint towards the centre. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves radially striated, striae 24 in 10 m. Central area hyaline, more or less oblong with round corners.

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Long axis 46m, short axis 41.5m.
