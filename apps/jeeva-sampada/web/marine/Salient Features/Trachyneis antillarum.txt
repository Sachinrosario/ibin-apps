SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear- elliptical valves with obtuse ends. Raphe excentric, axial area more or less broad, irregularly linear and unilateral.

Diagnostic characteristics: Transverse striae in radial rows, alveolate, 9-12 rows in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 89.5-114m long; 33-35.5m broad.
