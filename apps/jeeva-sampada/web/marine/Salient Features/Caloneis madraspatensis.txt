SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear elliptical valves with slight transapical contraction of the border at the centre and blunt, boat, like rounded poles. Straight raphe.

Diagnostic characteristics: Axial area small, lanceolate, dilating into a large elliptical central area, which has on either side of the nodule a crescent, shaped figure, in which the striae continue faintly. Striae 15 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
