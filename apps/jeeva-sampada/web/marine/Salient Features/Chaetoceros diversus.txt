
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming short straight chains. Aperture very small.

Diagnostic characteristics: Setae, some hair like; others thicker tubular and spinous. Terminal setae thin and hair like.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 5-8µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
