SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body moderately deep (depth in front of dorsal fin about  2.6 to 3.3 times in standard length), with a convex dorsal profile and a pointedss snout. Eye medium-sized, a snout, backward-projecting spine just below it.  Dorsal fin with 10 spines and 9 soft rays, continuous, unnotched; anal fin with 3 spines (the 2nd stouter and shorter than the 3rd) and 7 soft  rays ; pectoral fins with 2 unbranched and 16 branched rays. Predorsal scales (on top of head ), reaching forward to level of posterior nostrils; 4 or 5 transverse rows of scales on check; lower limb of preopercle with 1 or 2 rows of small scales.

Colour :  

Overall pale gray with an elongate blackish patch on upper side beginning below the 7th or 8th dorsal fin spine, its upper portion intersected by the lateral line; fins generally pale gray.
