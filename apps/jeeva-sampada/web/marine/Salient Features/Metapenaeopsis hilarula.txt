 SALIENT FEATURES:							Ref No:35,41,113

Morphological:   Rostrum straight, reaching just below or up to middle of second segment of 
antennular peduncle, armed with 8-9 dorsal teeth; carapace with well defined antennal, hepatic and pterygostomian spines, no stridulating organ; mid-dorsal carina of 3rd abdominal somite non-sulcate, depth of 6th somite about half its length; telson falling short of tip of inner uropods, non-sulcate, terminating in sharp spine and armed with 3 pairs of movable and a distal pair of fixed spines. Tooth on lower antennular flagellum in males farther from its base. Distomedian lobule of petasma fairly large with a broad distal end almost reaching tip of distoventral flap. A pair of large, lamellar and distolaterally pointed teeth or platelets in posterior region of thelycal plate; anterior border of sternum between 5th pair of pereopods in females with 2 small lateral protuberances; seminal vesicles distally broad. Anterior margin of coxal plate of 4th pereopods prominently conical. 


Diagnostic characteristics: Carapace without stridulating organ, mid-dorsal carina of 3rd 
abdominal somite non-sulcate, tooth on lower antennular flagellum in males farther from its base, distomedian lobule of petasma large with a broad distal end almost reaching tip of distoventral flap, anterior border of sternum between 5th pair of pereopods in females with 2 small lateral protuberances.

                   Colour:  Body sparsely mottled with dark brownish red, pleopods dark red, distal    
                    half of uropods dark brownish red.
