SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Medium sized with characteristic posterior fingerlike process. Cells occur in pairs, dorsally attached. Dorsal contour is gradually curved, whereas the ventral margin in lateral profile is generally straight along the main body.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: The posterior process varies in length and shape and the left sulcal list extends the length of the main body. Surface with areolations.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
