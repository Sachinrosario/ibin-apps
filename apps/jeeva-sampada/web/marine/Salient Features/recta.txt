Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Massive, meandroid, uninterrupted valleys upto 18 cm long, 2 to 3 cm wide, 1 to 1.5 cm deep.  Collines 3 to 6 mm thick.  Septa 7 to 12 per cm length of colline, of which 4 or five are highly thickened (1 mm).  Septa highly exsert, exsert ends swollen, stop at the middle of the colline, edges dentate, upper two or three teetch 3-4 mm long and 1 mm thick.  Septa thinner within the valleys, turn right or left unite to the columella.  Columella centres well defined, adjacent centres linked by toothed lamellae.  Polyps partly expanded during day brown with a brilliant green oral disc.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
