Descriptive characters:  
Description of a specimen form Mincoy:  Corallum shrubby arborescent with a narrow base, total height 16 cm.  Greater spread 21 cm.  Main branches about 2 cm thick, branches 1 to 1.5 cm thick at the base.  They taper to a conical axial corallite 4 to 5 mm thick and 3 to 4 mm exsert.  Wall of the axials highly thickened, margin rounded, dense and echinulate.  Opening average 0.5 mm in diameter.  Septa in two cycles, both well-developed, primaries meet at the centre of the axial fossa.  Radial corallites tubular, bursiform, thick-walled, spreading but not to right angles, 3 to 4 mm long, 3 mm thick with a rounded opening.  A few are proliferous and ar upto 6 mm long.  Scattered among the dominant radials a few shorter and subimmersed corallites are seen.  Truly immersed corallites are wanting.  Septa in two cycles.  Surface and wall echinulate.  Corallum dense in section.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  The shrubby arborescent corallum with very thick conical axial corallites and mostly uniform-sized thick-walled or bursiform radial corallites with rounded apertures easily distinguishes this species.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
