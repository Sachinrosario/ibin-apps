SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca and hypotheca slightly concave, between the posterior horns a deep indentation present.

Diagnostic characteristics: Posterior horns on the inside convex, carrying a small outwardly directed process.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 58µ; Diameter: 55µ; Length of indentation: 15µ.
