SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Flat cells forming ribbon like chains. Weakly silicified. Apertures slit like, closed in the middle by round valves.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves with a rudimentary central knob and punctate in the margin.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 50-78µ in length.
