Diagnostic characteristics:
Genus: Gibberythrops
Eyes normal, not depressed dorsoventrally. Thorax without sternal processes between pair of thoracic legs. Telson elongate, triangular, armed on narrow apex with pair of long spines and pair of median setae. Lateral margin of telson with small spines on distal half of margin. Pseudobranchial processes on endopods of male pleopod slender.

Gibberythrops acanthura
Cephalothorax infront of cervical sulcus highly swollen. Anterior margin of carapace broadly rounded without definite rostrum. Eyes small. Antennal scale with long distal lobe but not reaching tip of antennualr peduncle. Carpopropodus of thoracic endopods three to eight three segmented. Pleopods of male biramous and not modified. Telson one and a half times as long as broad and rapidly narrowing towards apex, apex armed with pair of long spines and pair of plumose; lateral border distally armed with four pairs of spines. Endopod of uropod much shorter than exopod with 1-2 spines below statocyst; statocyst large.
