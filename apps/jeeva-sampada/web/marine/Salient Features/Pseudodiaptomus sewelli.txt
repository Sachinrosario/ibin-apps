Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:   
     Female:  There is a pair of black dots in the middle of the anal segment. In some samples there occurs a blue pigmentation. P5 of the female beras only one hair on the outer margin. Furca: St2 is extremely long and whip-like, twice bent in the middle. 
      
     Male: As in the female there is a pair of black dots in the anal segment. Spikes are on the whole posterior margin of Ab2, Ab3 and Ab4 and additional spinules on the ventral aspect of Ab2, Ab3 and Ab4. Leg 5 posterior view: Right leg, basipod 1 with small bifid process at medial margin. Outer spine of basipod 2 short with seta, endopod base set with fine short hairs and spinules. Exopod 1 short, but produced into large distolateral spine, plumose on medial margin, with spiniform basal process. Exopod 2 with 3 knobs along inner medial margin and 2 setae; outer spine serrate, shorter than exopod 3, with proximal spiniform projection and similar to exopod 3. Left leg, exopod 2 with proximal ear-shaped process, 4 setae, small projection at distomedial corner and hyaline membrane with 2 broad, shallow indentations. Leg 5, anterior: Right leg, endopod with proximal 2 points triangular, first widest and largest, 1 fine hair between second and curved terminal point. Left leg, basipod 1 with cluster of large spatulate projections along distal suture line. Exopod 2 with unusual proximal process fringed with short hairs.

Additional remarks:
     This species, collected from Indian waters by Wellershaus (1969)and Pillai (1980), differs from Sewell’s drawings of the male leg 5 in that the right basipod 1 has a small bifid process at distal margin. 
    The noteworthy features of the male leg 5 which separate it from the other species in the trihamatus-type group are: i) Endopod proximal point large and triangular with recurved distal projection. ii) Outer spine of right exopod 2 heavily serrate with 1 basal spiniform projection. iii) Hyaline process on left exopod 2 only slightly indented as compared to P. bispinosus, P. incisus or P. trihamatus.
