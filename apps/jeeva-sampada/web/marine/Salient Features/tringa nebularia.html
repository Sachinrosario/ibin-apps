SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Largest among Indian sand pipes. Stocky with long stout bill.

Diagnostic characteristics: Upper part of body grey, fore necks and underparts white in non-breeding plumage.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Picks food while running in a straight line.

Main Food: Marine micro-invertebrates, shore insects.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Nests in northern hemisphere.
