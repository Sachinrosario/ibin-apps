SALIENT FEATURES:							Ref No:103,168

Morphological:    Dorsal carapace and some areas on last abdominal segments pubescent, body 
sometimes hairless; rostrum nearly straight, long, reaching  proximal to distal margin of
third antennular  segment or exceeding it, armed with 9 to 12 teeth along entire dorsal margin; postrostral carina not reaching posterior margin of carapace; adrostral carina ending well behind 2nd rostral tooth, adrostral sulcus behind epigastric tooth; branchiocardiac carina reaching at least to middle of carapace; telson armed only with spinules; ischial spine on 1st pereopod generally small. Distomedian projections of petasma directed anterolaterally, leaf-like, with a distinct longitudinal groove. Anterior plate of thelycum tongue-like, wider anteriorly with deeply emarginate border, and longitudinally grooved; lateral margins of lateral plates raised and curved inward posteriorly.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Carapace and abdomen partly pubescent; rostrum long, straight and 
toothed on entire dorsal margin; branchiocardiac carina reaching base of hepatic spine; merus of 5th pereopod in males with only a tubercle; anterior plate of thelycum in females wider anteriorly and longitudinally grooved, lateral margins of lateral plates raised and curved inward posteriorly.

        Colour: Body and legs pale green to pinkish, sometimes grey-greenish


Sex attributes:
       Descriptive characters:   In adult males, merus of 5th pereopod is provided with proximal 
                    notch, followed by a keeled tubercle.
