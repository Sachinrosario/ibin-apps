Salient Features:
Morphological:

Body two and a half to three times as long as broad. Head slightly shorter than the first pereon segment; first peraeon segment longest, second shortest. First and second pleon segments concealed by the seventh peraeon segment; segments three to five subequal in length, fifth slightly loner than the rest. Telson triangular, sides straight and apex narrow and rounded. Peraeon segments four to seven with a row of teeth along the poosterior border, seventh segment with thirteen teeth. Pleon segemnts three to five also with a row of teeth, eleven on fourth segment and five on fifth, median tooth on each segment larger. Telson with two submedianb teeth at the base followed by two parallel rows of three to four denticles forming a pair of short ridges.

Anterior border of frontal lamina broadly rounded. Third segment of first peraeopod with two blunt teeth, fourth segment with four and fifth with a large curved blunt distal tooth, fifth segment not immersed, sixth with three sharp spines. Seventh leg typical of the genus. Apex of telson with eight spines, endopod of uropod distaly rounded and with twelve spines, exopod narrow, with four inner and seven to eight outer spines.

Colour of body semi transparent lemon-Yellow, with a slight greenish tint. Insome specimens a medio-dorsal greenish line is also visible.


Additional Remarks:
In a later paper, pillai (1967) synonymised his earlier species, C. bicarinata (Pillai,1954) under fluviatilis

Size and Age: Length- 9.0mm.
