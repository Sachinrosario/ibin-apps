SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 Rostrum short,with 4 dorsal teeth+ the epigastric tooth; tip pointed and slightly tilted upwards; supraorbital and hepatic spines absent;pterygostomian spine well developed and the anteroinferior margin of carapace serrated forming 4 -7 spinules, becomes rounded in the postlarva; the antennal scale broad with numerous setae at its inner margin distally, and a thick carina present laterally on the outer margin with a lateral spine.the posterior end of 6th abdominal segment carinate and ends in a spine; a short ventromedian spine present on the 4th thoracic segment. Pereopods with exopods. Pleopods uniramous,bearing setae; telson with a long median spine,3 pairs of lateral and 4 pairs of terminal spines.

Diagnostic characteristics:Include relatively a short rostrum, presence of exopods on all legs; absence of a paired dorsolateral spines on the 5th abdominal somite; and presence of an acicular medianspine in the telson.

Sex attributes: Heterosexual.

Descriptive characters:      The genus Trachypenaeopsis has close resemblance to the genera Atypopenaeus and Penaeopsis.However, the present genus can be distinguished from others by the following characters-a short rostrum,dorsomedian spines on abdominal somites 4 and 5 and by the absence of paired dorsolaterals on 5th somite.

Meristic characteristics:
Feeding habit: pelagic zoneMain food: plankton, algae,organic matters,detritus with sand and mud.

Feeding type: omnivorous

Additional remarks:      Only 2 specimens were available for study,out of 1927 zooplankton samples collected during the IIOE,which shows its scarcity and rarity.

Size and age: 10mm

Eggs and larvae:  

Eggs unknown.

Mysis III: length.4.9mm.Rostrum bears 3 dorsal and an epigastric tooth; only pterygostomian spine ; anteroinferior margin serrated to form 4-7 spinules; mandibular palp unsegmented; maxillipeds and pereopods with exopodites; dorsomedian spines on 5 and 6 abdominal somites; pleopods uniramous; telson with a median,3lateral and 4 terminal spines(7+1+7).

Postlarva: length.6.7mm.Rostrum with an additional tooth; pterygostomian absent,a hepatic and antennal spine present; anteroinferior margin of carapace round; mandibular palp 2-segmented; a dorsal carina on the posterior end of 6th abdominal somite which ends in a short spine; A short ventromedian spine present on the 7th thoracic segment; pleopods uniramous bearing setae. Telson unchanged.

Abundance: nil
