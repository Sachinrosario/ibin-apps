SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Zooecia large, convex, marginal valleys distinct, a median longitudinal area commonly imperforate. Orifices semicircular, condyles and peristome wanting. Avicularia generally absent, rarely large, vicarious. Ovicells smooth or slightly granular.
Diagnostic characters:
Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters: A. cecilii is nearly always encrusting, The halves of the proximal edge of the orifice are generally produced medianly into points which constrict the sinus. This is typically long and narrow, but it may be short. Its articulate character is the most distinctive feature of the genus.

Pores are commonly wanting in a medium suboral area. Ortmann distinguished a variety mediolaevis in which this area was larger than usual. Lepralia perugiana Heller (Adriatic) was figured as having a small central umbo, which is also present in British specimens (HINCKS, 1880). Levinsen described the ectooecium of the ovicells as membranous; and this is confirmed by the Japanese specimen 40 B of Siboga collections.
