SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
body flat and elongate, with dorsal and anal fins joined to caudal fin.  Eyes on left side of body, with a narrow space between them; snout rounded, rostral hook very short, corner of mouth not reaching beyond lower eye, a little nearer to tip of snout than to gill opening.  Two lateral lines on eyed side, none on the blind side.  Scales ctenoid on both sides of body, 15 to 19 scale rows between lateral lines on eyed side. 

Colour:-  Eyed side yellow/brown, with very distinct irregular dark brown blotches, often forming irregular cross bands; some rays of  dorsal and anal fins dashed with dark brown.
