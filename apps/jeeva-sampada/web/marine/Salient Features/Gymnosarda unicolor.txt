SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
	
Body elongate and moderately compressed. Mouth fairly large, upper jaw reaching to mind of eye 14 to 31 large, conical teeth on upper jaw, 10 to 24 on lower jaw; 2 patches of teeth on upper surface of tongue; 11 to 14 gillrakers on first gill arch. Dorsal fins close together, the first (spiny) long (13 to 15 spines) its border almost straight, the second followed by 6 or 7 finiets; anal fin followed by 6 finiets; pectoral fins with 25 to 28 rays; interpelvic process large and single. Later line strongly undulating. Body naked posterior to corselet except for the lateral line, dorsal fin base, and caudal keel; caudal peduncle slender, with a well developed lateral keel between 2 smaller keels on each side.

Colour : back and upper sides brilliant blue-black, lower sides and belly silvery; no line, spots or other markings on body; anterior tip of first dorsal fin dark; other fins grayish.
