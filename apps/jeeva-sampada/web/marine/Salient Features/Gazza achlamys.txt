SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:
Diagnostic characters: 
Mouth pointing forward when protracted and the presence of caniniform teeth in both jaws. Greatest body depth 1.98-2.20 times in standard length. Scales on body absent, anterior to a line from origin of soft dorsal fin to behind pectoral fin bases and thence to origin of the anal fin, which dintinguishes it from the similar species Gazza minuta in which  scales are present on the whole body except absent from breast ahead of a line from base of pectoral fin to origin of anal fin. It is also distinguishable from G.minuta  by its relatively bigger eye (eye diameter 2.0 times in interorbital width  vs 1.0-1.2 times) 
                                      Ref no: 8
Sex attributes: 
Sexes externally distinguishable by the external appearance of the light organs.(Please see the heading ‘Structure of light organs’)        Ref no: 7

Descriptive characters: 
Body oval and somewhat compressed, rather deep; greatest body depth 1.98 to 2.20 times in standard length. Dorsal and  ventral profiles equally convex. The dorsal profile shows a slight concavity over the front border of the eye. Snout pointed. Mouth large, lips broad and thick. Mouth when protracted forms a horizontal tube. Gape of mouth oblique and opposite the middle of the eye. Mandibles almost straight, ascending with an angle of 50-60o. A band of small villiform  teeth  on each side and a pair of symphysial canines on the upper jaw, lower jaw having a series of teeth on the sides, getting bigger when going 
forward, with a pair of large canine teeth at the symphysis, with a gap between them to receive the upper canines. Two small supraorbital spines present opposite the front border of the eye. Pre-operculum with its lower margin finely serrated. Lateral line convex from the origin and runs parallel to the dorsal profile extending posteriorly up to the base of the caudal fin. Ventrals with an axillary scale and tip of the ventrals does not reach the origin of the annals. Caudal deeply forked.

Colour:
Body silvery, back grayish, with dark irregular marks or cycles, extending to little beyond lateral line, which often  disappear on preservation. Membrane of the spinous dorsal black in its distal portion. Snout tip dotted grey. Edge of soft dorsal also grey. Inner side of the pectoral fin dotted black and dark pigment spots present along the edges of the ventral half of the gill opening, covered by the opercular flap. Caudal dusky at its posterior margin. Minute black dots all over the ventral half of the body.
