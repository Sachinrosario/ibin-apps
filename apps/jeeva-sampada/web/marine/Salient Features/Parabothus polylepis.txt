SALIENT  FEATURES :
Eggs and larvae:                                                                                           
    Characteristics:      The larval body thin, transparent, laterally compressed almost uniformly broad, leaf like.  The dorsal and ventral body wall runs almost parallel except towards the caudal region.   Mouth small, less than the diameter of the eye, jaws almost equal with numerous teeth. The alimentary canal runs parallel to notochord to the level of 7th vertical segment forms an elliptical coil placed at the posterior end of the abdominal cavity. The larvae can be distinguished from others in the broad leaf-like body which is thin, in the presence of elongate dorsal fin ray at the anterior end of the dorsal fin, first tiny dorsal ray, urohyal in front of the cleithra, long asymmetircal left pelvic fin radial with anterior three rays in advance of the right fin, and in the posterior basipterygial processes extending to the ascending loop of the intestinal coil, the characteristic bend in the intestinal loop towards its middle, late differentiation of pelvic fin rays and the meristics. There are 92-94 dorsal and 72-73 anal fin rays and 10 + 29 – 31 vertebrae including urostyle.
