SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells connected by a thin mucilage strand and forming chains of 4 to 8 or rarely more. Valves convex, about 40m in diameter; very weakly silicified.

Diagnostic characteristics: Structure on valve not visible in water mounts.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 40m.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides. Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
