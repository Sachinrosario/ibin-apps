Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body slender, sub cylindrical in cross-section;  belly rounded,   with  4 to 6  small, needle – like scutes between pectoral  and pelvic fin bases. Maxilla tip pointed.  Reaching onto pre – opercle;  isthmus (between gill openings on underside of head) not reaching to hind border of gill membrane, leaving exposed a small (white or silver) diamond-shaped bony urohyal plate (easily visible without lens); 3 unbranched rays in dorsal and anal fin origin behind dorsal fin rays.

Colour :
