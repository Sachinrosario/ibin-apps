SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Zoarium dull white in colour. Zooecia alternate, radiating from a median point and separated by broad shallow grooves. Front convex with 6 to 7 pairs of costae joining in the median line. Lacuna present, placed in the centre of distinct polygonal calcareous cells. Usually ten in number. The size of the polygonal thickening increases laterally. Orifice small, rounded with slightly drawn out lateral sides. Ovicells present with a median thickening, non-porous. Diagnostic characters: The nature of the pericyst and the increased number of the lacunae distinguishes this species from the other species of the genus Reginella Jullien.
Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:   In the non-porous nature of the ovicell, this form differs from Reginella mattoidea Osburn, with which it shows resemblance.
The presence of a thickening in the median line of the ovicell is a feature not mentioned by Osburn in his text, even though his figures show these clearly. No carina is noticed in the present specimen. In this respect and in the slightly increased number of the lacunae in some specimens (Osburn could observe only 10) the present form differs from R. mattoidea. The porous nature of the ovicell probably is not of much specific importance since Osburn (1950) has not stated anything in particular about this aspect in his key to species of Reginella.Meristic characteristics:
