Diagnostic characteristics:
Female: Body narrowed ans short; prosome a little more than 3 times length of urosome; cephalon anteriorly rounded with a pair of lateral hooks; rostrum slender; dorsal eye lenses as in L. stylifera; separation between pedigerous segment IV and V obscure; asymmetry of posterior corners of pedigerous segment V more pronounced, left lobe distinctly longer and reaches to middle of genital segment; urosome 3-segmented, anal segment exceedingly short; genital segment asymmetrical in dorsal view, its left margin smoothly curved while right margin bears two distinct lobes, posterior lobe carrying a triangular stout spine projecting perpendicular to segment at its apex; urosome II produced into a pair of subequal robust ventrally directed spines at its right lateral margin; urosome II extends posteriorly over anal segment and produced into three subequal spines at its right posterior margin; caudal rami asymmetrical; caudal setae as in female stylifera; antenna I and other cephalic appendages resembles those of L. stylifera. Leg 5: External rami asymmetrical; left external rami slightly longer; external rami appears more straight when compared to that of L. stylifera, where it is perceptibly curved inwards; internal rami symmetrical, nearly half length of external rami and is bifid at its tip.

Male: Cephalon narrowed as in female, but dorsal eye lenses large and placed close together; postero-lateral corners of pedigerous segment V produced asymmetrically left corner ending in an acute spine while right margin being drawn out into a squared lobe carrying three subequal, stout projecting spines; tip of inner spine reaching to middle of urosome II; urosome of five segments, left lateral margin of urosome I produced into a lobe as in L. stylifera, but with a spine directed posterad, nearly half length of urosome II, originating from right distal corner of urosome I; caudal rami as in female. Antenna I: right antenna I geniculate; segment 17 with a spine like seta; segments 18 and fusion segments 19 21 with dorsal toothed plates as in L. stylifera; segment 22 with a disto-dorsal serrated spine which is nearly onefourth length of segment. Leg 5: ovate, stout and stocky; right P5 with a chela, thumb of chela short, blunt and hardly 25% of length of claw and with a basal seta; hand with two lamellae on its inner margin and with a distal inner seta; claw more pointed than that of L. Stylifera; inner margin of claw lacking in pronounced lamelliform ridges found in L. stylifera, and it is with one terminal and two inner marginal setae; left leg: terminal segment with a well developed inner marginal protuberance; distally segment with two blunt lamelliform structures crowned with spinnules, a curved stout spine and with two flagelliform setae with papillated tips; subterminal segment twilce longer than terminal segment and with a disto-lateral spine.
