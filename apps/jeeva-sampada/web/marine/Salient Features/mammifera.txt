Salient Features:
Morphological:

Male:  The body is elongate oblong. The dorsal surface is smooth and the lateral borders are sparsely hairy. The cephlon is as long as the first peraeon segment. The seventh paraeon segment has a pair of submedian setose tubercles on the posterior border. The pleon is as long as the first peaeon segment its hind border has two large submedian setose tubercles and a pair of slightly smaller lateral ones. The telson is almost triangular, its dorsal side has two large mammiform bosses, each surmounted by a large triangular tooth carrying a bunch of long hairs. The outer edge of teeth is continued forwards as a setose ridge giving the telson a bicarinate appearence. The apex of telson is trifid the lateral lobes are apically pointed, the median lobe is distally triangular and reaches clearly beyond the lateral lobes. 

The epistome is longer than broad and apically triangular, its posterior arms are comparatively narrow. The male appendix on the seventh paraeon is in the form of two stout but shoprt processes placed closed near each other. The male stylet on the second pleopod reaches slightly beyond the tip of the endopod and carries a long pulmose apical seta; the inner border of stylet is hairy. The endopod of fifth pleopod is apically subtruncate. The uropods over reach the apex of the telson, their rami are subequal and drawn out into spine like apices. Length: 7.0 mm

Female:  The female is slightly larger than the male and is devoid of tubercles on the seventh peraeon segment and the pleon. The bosses on the telson are comparitively low and without the tubercle in the male. The apex of the telson is triboiled but the median lobe is apically truncate and does not reach beyond the lateral lobes. The exopod of the uropod is smaller than the endopod,  The former has a spine like apex and the latter is apically rounded. The mouth parts are metamorphosed. Length 8.0 mm.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size Recorded  male 7.0mm Female 8.0mm
