SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Zoaria light brownish in colour. Irregular hexagonal zooecia separated by distinct zooecial walls. The size of the zooecia shows great variations. Orifices placed on small, rounded raised papillae, near the distal portion on the ventral wall. 16 tentacles. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:  A study of the available literature shows that variations occur in the nature of the zoaria, size of the zooecia, nature of papillae and number of tentacles.
  The zoaria of these specimens are comparatively thick, Harmer (1915) observed that the specimens he got from Torres strait were very thin and therefore, peculiar.
  The orifices in the present material were placed at the tips of the papillae, even though Silen (1942) did not observe these in his specimens, and evidently he included this species under the category of the genus Alcyonidium where "the aperture is not raised". Both Harmer (1915) and Osburn (1953) found raised papillae in their specimens. In the species Alcyonidium mammillatum the occurrence of raised papillae is a character of importance. But in that species, the raised papilla was very large, when compared to the size of the zooecia. In the present specimens, certain zooecia of same colonies did not bear raised papillae at all. And this finding supports Osburn's statement that "it is possible that the presence or absence of the oral papillae may be due to the degree of retraction of the tentacle sheath".
  The variations in tentacle number are considerable. Thus Harmer (1915) noticed the tentacle number to be 12, Hincks 15-18, Siberman 16-20 while Silen counted 20. Hassall in his original description has given the tentacle number as 20, Marcus (1941) and Rogick (1949) as 16. Osburn noticed the tentacle number varying from 15-17, he failed to notice 20 tentacles in any of his specimens. In the present specimen, the tentacle number was 16. So it is quite probable that great variations in tentacle number exist in this species.Meristic characteristics:
