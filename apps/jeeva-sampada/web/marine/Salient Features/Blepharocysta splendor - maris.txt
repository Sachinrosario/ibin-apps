
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Armored, rotund, spheroidal to compressed. Sutures often difficult to determine. Trichocyst pores at plate borders prominent. Cingulum not composed of plates, rather represented by a homologous cingular area where transverse flagellum undulates.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcal area typically with left and right lists. Cell body without elongate or obtrusive apical process. Cells phagocytizing other protists.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Width: 43µm.
