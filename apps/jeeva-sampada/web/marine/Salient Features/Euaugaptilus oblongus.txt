SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics:  
      Female:   Cephalothorax, 4.75 mm; urosome 1.31 mm. The cephalothorax oblong ovate.  The cephalic segment as long as the pedigerous segments taken together.  The lateral corner of the last pedigerous segment broadly rounded. The rostrum consists of 2 short prominences.
     The urosome 3-jointed. The genital segment produced below, bears fine hairs on the apex of the swelling.  The furcal rami about as long as wide. 
     The 1st antenna 25-jointed, exceeds the end of the furca by last 2 segments. The 2nd antenna has the endopod 1.5 times as long as the exopod which is composed of 7 free segments.  The mandible has the exopod slightly longer than the endopod; the biting part has 3 teeth; the exopod has 5 marginal setae; the endopod has 6 setae on the distal segment.  The 1st maxilla has 9 setae on the outer lobe, 2 on the exopod, 2 on the 3rd inner lobe, a single seta on the 2nd inner lobe, 9 spines on the 1st inner lobe. The 2nd maxilla and maxilliped as figured by Sars.
     In the 5th pair of legs the inner marginal spine on the 2nd segment of the exopod more than half the length of the 3rd segment of the exopod.

Feeding type : 

Additional  remarks:
      Sar’s specimen is larger in size measuring 7.40 mm. Sewell’s specimen from the Arabian Sea measured 6.15 mm and has s single seta on the 2nd basal segment of the 1st maxilla, whereas, the Tanaka’s specimen and Sar’s specimens have each 2 setae on the same segment.
