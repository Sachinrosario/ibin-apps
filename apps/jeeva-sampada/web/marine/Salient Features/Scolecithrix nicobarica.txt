
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:              

Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment rounded and slightly indented. Abdomen 4-segmented. Genital segment not swollen below. First antenna is 20-segmented, reaches beyond the last pedigerous segment. Fifth pair of legs totally absent.            

Male: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. First antenna 18- jointed. In 5th leg endopod of left leg very small, exopod 3- jointed, the distal joint of which carries a thin lamellous plate. The distal segment of the exopod of the right leg very slender.
