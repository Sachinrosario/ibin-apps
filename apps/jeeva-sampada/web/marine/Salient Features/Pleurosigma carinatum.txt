SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves convex, linear-lanceolate and acute at the ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Raphe on elevated keel, diagonal in the center and closely following the margins. Striae 21-24 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 53-60m long, 8m broad.
