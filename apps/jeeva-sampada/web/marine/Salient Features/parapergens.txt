
SALIENT  FEATURES :

Diagnostic characteristics:                 
Female: Urosome relatively shorter. Rostrum in lateral view usually short, thick,and curved ventroposteriorly. Ventral profile of the genital segment in lateral view somewhat undulant. Genital segment more than 1.5 times as long as urosome segment 3. Ventral lobe of seminal receptacle in lateral view large, visible anterior to the base of its dorsal lobe; dorsal lobe bulb shaped, constricted in the region of attachment to yhe ventral lobe; dorsal and ventral lobes conspicuous in ventral view. Leg 3 with basipod 2 spiniform processes 2 and 3 usually not tapered uniformly from their bases.Leg 3 with form and spacing of basipod 2 spiniform processes 2and 3. Segment 2 of leg 5 more than 2.0 tomes as long as segment 1.               
Male: Rostrum in lateral view knob-like and protruding ventrally. Caudal ramus less than 1.6 times as long as wide. Leg 3 with basipod 2 spiniform processes 2 and 3 not tapered uniformly from their bases. Longer ramus of leg 5 and genital pore on left; left leg 5 longer than urosome segments 1-4 of variable thickness, often very slender in lateral view; right leg 5, 2- or 3- segmented, distal segments usually reduced in size.

Additional  remarks:  This species belong to Frost and Fleminger’s (1968) Group III.
