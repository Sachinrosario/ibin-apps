SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:       

Diagnostic characteristics:   
Genus: Rhopalophthalmus Illig.     

Carapace short with pair of post-orbital spines continuing backwards as dorsal keels. Pleura of first abdominal segment in male produced into pair of lobes. Antennal scale with strong outer distal spine,  sympod armed with two or more spines. Thoracic endopods three to seven with varying number of carpopropodal segments, eighth endopod reduced in size and showing pronounced sexual dimorphism. Pleopods in male biramous, exopod of second pleopod long and armend at distal end with modified setae. Telson linguiform with pronounced waist beyond base, distal half of lateral border armed with strong spines regularly increasing in length, distal border armend with two pairs of long barbed spines. Rami of uropods two-segmented, endopod with spine below statocyst. Rhopalophthalmus chilkensis     Rostral prolongation absent, carapace without dorsomedian tubercles. Antennal sympod with three very long subsimilar spines. Thoracic endopods three to seven with three to four subsegments. Endopod of eighth thoracic limb in male three segmented, third segment bent at right angle to second, latter with group of five to six long setae, whole limb when straightened overreaching basal segment of exopod; endopod in female slender and shorter than basal segment of exopod with indistinct articulation and seta in middle. Lateral borders of telson with fifteen to sixteen spines, apical spines long, inner pair longer than outer.  

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult male: 11.4 mm     
Adult female: 9.5-10 mm
