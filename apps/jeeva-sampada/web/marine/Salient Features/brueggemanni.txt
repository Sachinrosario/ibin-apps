Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum arborescent, branches lax with blunt apices.  Sometimes the tips are truncated.  Main branches 1.5 to 8 cm thick at the base.  Axial corallites 2 to 8 mm in diameter, sometimes there may be a cluster of axials.  Radial corallites tubular, appressed, small, openings circular.  In both the radials and the axi also two cycles of septa can be seen.  Coenosteum with spinnules.

The living coral is pale brown to pale green.
Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  This is the first report of the species from India, and is based on specimens collected by the author form Wandoor Mahatma Gandhi Park.

The species according to Veron (1986) groups with Acropora palifera in corallites characters though is having arborescent corallum.
