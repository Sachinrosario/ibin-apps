SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small species with low cake shaped body depressed; girdle slightly dextral, lists moderate. Sulcus narrow, near girdle very wide, circular or antapical surface, lists narrow. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Apical horn long, slender. Antapical horns not distinct but two long hair like spines present.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Height: 15µ; breadth: 55µ; Apical horn: 20µ, antapical spines: 20µ.
