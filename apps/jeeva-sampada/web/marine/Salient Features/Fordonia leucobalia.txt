SALIENT FEATURES :

Morphological : Head not distinct from neck, covered with large shields; nostril in the nasal; nasals separated by the internasal;frontal much broader than supraocular; normally without loreal but rarely with a small loreal; 1 pre and 1 or 2 postoculars; 1-2 anterior temporarls, irregular in size and shape;5 supralabials,3rd touching the eye,5th longest; chin-shields in 2 pairs, subquadrangular in shape, the anterior pair in touch with the 3rd and 4th labials.]

body not elongate, rather stout, covered with smooth scales, in 25-29 rows at midbody; ventrals well developed, 138-156, the last 1-2 sometimes divided. Tail short; subcaudals paired.

DIAGNOSTIC CHARACTERISTICS:

This mangrove snake is greyish or brownish above and is whitish or yellowish below. The young are spotted with black.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding Habit : Carnivorous
Main Food : Marine invertbrates, fish.


Additional Remarks :

The white-bellied mangrove Snake, through said to be common around the coasts of India (especially in the mangroves), seems to be rare and not much is known of this snake. It is said to inhabit the crab holes, subsisting chiefly on the marine crabs that live in the holes.

Besides the coasts of Bengal, this snake is found in the tidal rivers and around the coasts of Myanmar, Cochin - China, and the Indo-Australian Archipelago to Northern Australia.

Like most other freshwater snakes which are equally at home in brackishwater, this mangrove snake feeds on fish and crabs. It brings forth live young.

SIZE AND AGE :

Maximum length (cm) male 68 cm
Average Length (cm) 94 cm
