Salient features:
Morphological:

	Head a broad based, anteriorly rounded triangle. Peraeon segments one to three short and subequal, four to seven longer and equal; coxal plates large and projecting beyond the lateral border of the segments. Pleon narrower than peraeon, telson broader than long, apex perfectly rounded and with six spines, dorsal side with very few setae. 
	First antenna shorter than the penduncle of second, flagellum five to six jointed; flagellum of second antenna thirteen jointed. Frontal lamina twice as broad as long. Pentagonal. Cutting edge of mandible without an accessory lobe. First maxilla similar to that of L.gardineri. Second maxilla with two apical setae, outer twice as long as inner; maxilliped club shaped, second segment large and third narrow, four to six successively broader.
	First peraeopod like that of L.gardineri, fourth segment with its lower distal part produced in front of the fifth. Seventh peraeopod stout, with fewe spines and setae.
	Body white without chromatophores.

Additional remarks:

	Broader frontal lamina, larger coxal plates and absence of an accessory lobe on the mandible and characters which distinguish Lanocira rotundicanda from L.gardineri.

Size and age: Length : 6.0mm.
