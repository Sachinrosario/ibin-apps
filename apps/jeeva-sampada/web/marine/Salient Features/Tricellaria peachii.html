SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium whitish in hue. Internodes long with 4 to 6 zooecia. Long zooecium with narrow more or less cylindrical proximal regions. Opesia occupy less than half of the front. The external lateral side of the zooecium produced into a flank. Two external and one internal spines. Scutum, frontal and lateral avicularia wanting. Bifurcation of type 11 of Harmer (1923). The joints traverse the opesia of the outer zooecia. Radicles arise from the external aspect of the proximal region of the zooecia.
  Diagnostic characters: In the absence of scuta, lateral and frontal avicularia of the present form differs from all species of Tricellaria hitherto described. The nature of bifurcation is also peculiar.    Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  
Descriptive characters:   Harmer (1923) remarks that this species "appears to represent the extreme amount of loss of structures which typically occur in the genus, as shown by the disappearance of the frontal and marginal avicularia and the rostrum". The proximal portion of the zooecia C and D at the bifurcation more or less borders the axillary zooecium, which is undoubtedly different from the other three types of bifurcation noted in this genus viz., 9, 10 and 12. Perhaps this might have prompted Verril (1879) to select this species as the genotype of Bugulopsis. Nevertheless, Harmer (1923) feels that "the mode of bifurcation is that of Tricellaria".
