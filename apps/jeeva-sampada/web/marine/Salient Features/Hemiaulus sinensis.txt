SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Flat cells with broadly elliptical valve plane, forming long chains by attachment with the processes of adjoining cells.

Diagnostic characteristics: Cell wall areolated and strongly silicified. Areolae in radial rows, at the centre of the valve about 6 in 10µ, near margin 9 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Apical axis measuring 23-38µ.
