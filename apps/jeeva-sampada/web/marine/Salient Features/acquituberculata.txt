Descriptive characters:  The following are the details of this species based on a specimen from St. of Malacca (Pillai and Scheer, 1974).  Corallum frondose, in the form of thick plates.  Growing edges thin, getting thicker towards the older parts.  Underside of the folium with numerous verruciform or hemispherical projections 2 to 4 mm in diameter and height.  The tips of these verruciform projections bear small calices with a set of rudimentary septa.  Calices on the upperside numerous, openings directed upward or slightly oblique, average 1 mm in diameter.  Primary cycle of septa well developed, secondaries in full complement, but smaller than the primaries.  The tubercles much crowded, forming prominent warts all over the coralla bearing 2 to 3 calices.  In between the warts single tubercles are seen.  The tubercles on the warts round-topped, average 2 mm in height, shorter in between the warts.

A foliaceous corallum with several crowded tubercle and calicle bearing warts at the upperside, and verruciform projections at the lowerside are the characteristic feature of this species.
