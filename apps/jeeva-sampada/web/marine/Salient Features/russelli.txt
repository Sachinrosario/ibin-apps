SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref. No. 7
Morphological:    A fairly small  species, with an oblong  body. 
Diagnostic characteristics :snout rounded and projecting  slightly beyond  tip of upper jaw; mouth  inferior, upper jaw less than  half of head length  a single barbell on chin, rostral pores 3, marginal pores 5,  median mental pore at the base of the solid , pointed mental barbell; teeth not  well differentiated  into large and small, the large one not widely spaced  and forming an outer series in upper jaw;    no canine teeth;  Gillrakers  on  lower limb  of first arch 8 to 10,  Dorsal fin with  10 spines, followed by deep notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 25 to 28 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7  soft rays, the second spine  strong  and a little less than half of head length,. Pectoral fins long,  Caudal fin  rhomboid.  Scales cycloid on front part of  head  and   lower parts of dorsal  and anal fins  , elsewhere   ctenoid ;l swim bladder carrot – shaped , with about  14  to 17  pairs of arborescent   appendages, the first  entering  the  head, the last bifid and parallel  to tubular   end  of bladder. Lateral line scales  reaching  to tip of caudal fin.

Colour :Back  grey, shading to white  on  belly ; a dark brown broad band on nape; opercle with a deep  blue blotch; upper edge of spiny part of dorsal fin dark .
