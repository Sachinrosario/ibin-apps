SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped strongly silicified cells, slightly convex. Central area small with scattered areolae in series, divided into structures-sectors by hyaline radial rays running from the centre to the margin to different lengths.

Diagnostic characteristics: Areolae 9 in 10 m, round in lower magnification and polygonal in higher magnification. A hyaline eye present. Valve margin finely striated. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 33-61m.
