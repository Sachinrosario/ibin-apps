Salient Features :
It is annual 30-45 cm tall grass, clums slender.
Stem tufted; leaf blades mostly basal, crowded, narrow entire and often convolute; leaf sheath smooth, somewhat loose, ligules hairs.
Inflorescence pyramidal; rachis slender, spreading, almost at right angle from the axis; spikelets spindle shaped, 1-flowred.
Fruit grains pericap, loose.
