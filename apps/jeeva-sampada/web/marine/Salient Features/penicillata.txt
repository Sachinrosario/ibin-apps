SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Carapace produced into apically rounded rostrum with anteromedian point. Eyes large, cornea wider than stalk. Dorsal surface of the basal segment and inner surface of second and third segments of antennular peduncle with dense matting of hairs. Inner distal part of fifth segment of second endopod produced into blunt process. Carpus of third thoracic endopod very long, twice as long on combined length of propodal segments. Exopod of third pleopod of male elaborately modified. Telson with short, sparse spinulation, uropods not reaching apex of telson. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)

Adult male : 9.2 mm
Female       : 6.5-7 mm
