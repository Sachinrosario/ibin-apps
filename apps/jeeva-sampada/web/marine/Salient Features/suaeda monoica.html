Salient Features :
It is small erect, annual bush; branches numerous, ascending, marked with prominent leaf scars, pale glabrous.
Leaves sessile, linear, obtuse or sub-acute, some time apiculate, bright green.
Flowers polygamous, in slender lax spikes, the cluster distant each in the axil of a short leaf; bracteoles minute, ovate, acute, entire.
Stigma 2-5 short.
Seeds vertical, ovoid, smooth, shinning black.
