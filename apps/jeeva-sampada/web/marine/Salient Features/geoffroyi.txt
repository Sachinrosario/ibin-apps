SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum with a narrow or broad base of attachment.  Broader towards the top, flabellate with constrictions.  An epitheca usually visible.  Valleys 10 to 15 mm wide, upto 25 mm deep.  Septa closely placed, exsert (2 mm or so); Edges dentate.  Pali present in larger ones.  Costae extend towards the side, edges granular.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
