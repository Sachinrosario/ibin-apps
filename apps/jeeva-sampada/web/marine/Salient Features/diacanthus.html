SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 5,6.
Morphological: A large species.
Diagnostic characteristics : Pointed snout and a big, nearly horizontal and terminal mouth. Teeth differentiated into large and small in both jaws; rostral pores 3, marginal pores 5, mental pores in 3 pairs, the first pair close to the symphysis and joined by a semicircular groove, no canine teeth, Gillrakers 7 to 8 in lower limb of the first arch. Dorsal fin with 9 to 10 spines, followed by low notch, second part of the fin with one spine and 22 to 24 soft rays. Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 soft rays, the second spine rather strong, Caudal fin rhomboid. Scales cycloid on snout and below eyes, elsewhere ctenoid; lateral line scales reaching to tip of caudal fin .
Gas bladder carrot shaped with 16 to 20 pairs of arborescent, appendages , the first branching on transverse septum but not entering head, the last two small and simple, the rest branching , but without dorsal limbs.

Colour :5 dark bars along back and many small black spots on top of head, upper half of body and caudal fin; pectoral, pelvic, anal and lower part of caudal fins black.in large fishes the 5 bars and the smaller spots are absent.
