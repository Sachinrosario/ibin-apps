SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:                   Body robust. Rostrum low and triangular. Propodus of thoracic limb with distinct partition. Pseudobranchial lobes nearly straight. Endopod of fourth pleopod of male with long modified setae. Telson three times as long as broad with closely set spines arranged in distinct series; apex with pair of long spines. Uropods slender, rami sub-equal; exopod with 9-10 spines occupying more than half the border.

Meristic characteristics :

Additional  remarks :        In the specimens collected by Panampunnayil from the Andaman Sea all the modified setae on the endopod of fourth pleopod of male are secondarily armed with fine hairs and spinules.  In all the earlier publications the modified setae are described as naked.     This species can be readily recognized by the robust body, characteristically modified setae of the fourth pleopod of the male and the spinulation of the telson and uropods.  It is a rare species and has never been found  in large numbers,  usually not more than two or three in a collection.

Size and age :

Adult male and female  -    8-11mm

Geographical Location:    Tropical Indo-Pacific including Arabian Sea to the Red Sea, Bay of Bengal, Seychelles, South China, Philippines, Micronesia, East coast of America.
