SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Oblong- elliptic valves, with straight, parallel sides and rostrate apices. Raphe straight, with the terminal fissures hooked in the same direction.

Diagnostic characteristics: Striae strongly radiate, 9 in 10m. At the ends of the striae the puncta become confluent and elongate towards the raphe.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 50-80m.
