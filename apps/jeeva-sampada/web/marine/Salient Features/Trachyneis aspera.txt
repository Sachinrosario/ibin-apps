SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear lanceolate valves with obtuse ends. Axial area broad, stauroid, truncate, not reaching the sides.

Diagnostic characteristics: Transapical striae alveolate, 10 in 10m. Longitudinal striae very fine, 24 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 52-224m long and 10.5-21.5m broad.
