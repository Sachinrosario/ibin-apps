SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Carapace slightly produced in front without distinct rostral projection. Antennal scale in male shorter than antennular peduncle, in female as long as peduncle. Labrum with short frontal spine. Carpopropodus undivided. Pseudobranchial lobes spirally coiled; pleopods not modified. Telson short, not reaching articulation of outer uropod; basal part with three spines, distal half of lateral margin with 12-14 spines regularly increasing in length; apex broadly rounded with three small median spines and pair of setae; in male innermost pair of apical spines longer than outer pair ; in female just the reverse. Endopod of uropod with 15-21 spines in male and 19-27 spines in female; spines increasing regularly in size; exopod longer than endopod with 3-5 spines on outer border.


Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Male and female -  5- 6.6mm.
