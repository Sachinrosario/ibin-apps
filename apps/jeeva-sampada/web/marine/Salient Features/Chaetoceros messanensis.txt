
SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming long straight chains. The corners of adjacent cells touch each other. Apertures round to elliptical. Thin bristles. Setae robust, spinous and spines arranged spirally.

Diagnostic characteristics: End bristles diverging, unlike each other. Chromatophore is a single plate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 12-39µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
