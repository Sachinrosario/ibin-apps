Morphlogical:                Ref.No:1,2,7,8,10

  Diagnostic characteristics:

Fusiform compressed body, its depth 2.8 to 3.0 times in standard length; belly sharp with keeled scutes. Dorsal fin origin slightly before mid point of body, anal fin base short and lying far behind dorsal fin base. Lower gill rakers 47 to 62 (40-67 as per Ref. No. 9). Scales deciduous.Anterior scales perforated and fimbriated at posterior margin. Posterior scale with 4 to 5 vertical striae, interrupted at the centre of the scale. Pre-dorsal median ridge covered by adjacent sides of two longitudinal rows of scales. Pelvic fin with 8 rays, its origin below anterior part of the dorsal fin base. Anal fin with 16-19 rays.

    Colour: Back blue/green, flanks silvery

Salient features
Eggs and larvae:         Ref.No. 11
  Characteristics:

Eggs: The planktonic eggs are 1.1 to 1.2 m in diameter and enclosed in a
double membrane with a large perivitelline space. The yolky part is about 0.94 mm with an oil globule of 0.1 mm in diameter.

    Larvae: The just hatched larvae is 2.3 mm long with 37 preanal and three
post anal myotomes. A two-day old larva is 5.6 mm long having its eyes black, yolk completely absorbed, caudal fin rays clearly seen and brownish pigment cells along the lower side of the myotomes
