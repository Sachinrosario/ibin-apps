SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum encrusting and plocoid.  Corallites projective, base narrower than the top, height upto 30 mm.  Diameter of calices varies from 10-12 mm; depth upto 17 mm; shorter calices shallower.  Adult corallites with 1 or 2 buds.  Septa in five cycles; the last cycle rudimentary.  Septa not exsert, narrower at the wall than below. Higher cycles undergo fusion.  Columella well developed, concave, or flat.  Costae extend to the base of the corallites; edges with small granules.  Adjacent ones linked by transverse trabeculae.  Corallite wall porous.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
