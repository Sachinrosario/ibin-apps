SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:  Ref.No.

Postlarva. Rostrum bears 3 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth ; extends slightly beyond  the eyes Hepatic and pterygostomian spines persist. Antennule with stylocerite. Antennal scale  narrow,parallel-sided,with a terminal spine.Thoracic legs with exopodites; dorsomedian spines present on 3-6 abdominal  segments; abdominal segments 5 & 6 with an additional pair of dorsolateral spines. Pleopods uniramous; endopod with a seta. Telson tapers towards the tip and  the spine formula is  7+1+7 ( a median ,3 posterior and 4 marginal spines). The 4th spine is the longest.

Diagnostic characters.Predominently a short rostrum; the absence of longitudinal and transverse sutures on carapace; a dorsal carina present on carapace;  carination  of the abdominal sements and the presence of exopodites on all the thoracic legs.

Sex attributes: Heterosexual. 

Descriptive characters :Kubo (1949) included Atypopenaeus and Trchypenaeopsis along with Metapenaeus and Penaeopsis in a single group based on the similarity of the thelycum of these genera. De Man(1911) remarked that Trachypenaeopsis and Atypopenaeus are closely related to each other. However,there are characters which distinguish one from the other.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit : pelagic feeders on plankton.

Main food: plankton and detritus

Feeding type:omnivorous

Size and age: 4.0-4.3 mm.-6.3mm

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs:  unknown.
Characteristics:2 mysis stages  are available.Mysis I. Specimen has a size length of 4.0-4.2mm.  Rostrum bears a dorsal tooth and an epigastric spine; extends just beyond the eyes. Carapace with a minute pterygostomian and a small hepatic spine; anteroinferior margin is  slightly serrated. Mandibular palp short and unsegmented; dorsomedian spines present on 3-6 abdominal segments,and a pair of dorsolateral spines on 5th segment. Thoracic legs with exopodites;no pleopods present. Exopod of uropod with a spine at the externodistal margin. Telson squarish with a deep cleft, without a  median spine; spineformula is 7+7.    

Mysis II. Specimen has  a size length of 4.3 mm. Rostrum has 2 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth. Hepatic and pterygostomian spines become more prominent on carapace. The serration at the anteroinferior margin is reduced; spines on abdominal segments remain same. Pleopods appear just as small  buds. Telson cleft reduced and a median spine is implanted in it; spine formula becomes 7+1+7.

Postlarva: length 6.3mmRostrum with 3 dorsal and epigastric tooth; hepatic and pterygostomian persist; antennal scale narrow,parallel sided with a terminal spine; legs with exopodites; dorsomedian spines on abdominal somites 3-6 and paired dorsolaterals on 5 and 6; pleopods uniramous with a single seta; telson tapers at tip with a median, 3 marginal and 4 posterior spines.
