SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

 Body flat and elongate,  with dorsal and anal fins joined  to caudal  fin. Eyes on left  side of  body,  with a small scaly  space between  them; snout rounded, rostral hook short,  corner of mouth reaching beyond lower eye, nearer to gill opening than to tip of snout.  Then two lateral lines on eyed side and two on blind side.  Scales ctenoid  (rough to touch) on eyed side but cycloid on blind side; 13 to 16 scales raw between lateral line on eyed side.  

Colour:-  Eyed side brown with an irregular dark blotchs on gill cover, blind side white.
