Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	



	Body oblong and somewhat compressed. . Snout projecting, mouth moderately large, with small teeth; upper lip absent, lower lip well developed; eyes moderate in size (eye diameter 4.5times in head length), with adipose tissue. Pectoral fins in two parts, upper part with unbranched  rays; lower part with 6 free filamentous rays, of which the upper 2 are the longest, reaching to tip of pelvic fin; caudal fin forked with lobes equal. Scales small ctenoid (rough to touch).

Colour : 
golden, pectoral fins deep black, anal fin with a black margin, pelvic fins dark in the middle.
