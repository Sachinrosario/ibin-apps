SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum foliaceous, similar to M. foliosa.  Colonies upto 50 cm in greater spread and develop platforms 2 to 3 m wide.  The edges of fronds may get contorted with the underside bearing buds and miniature colonies.  Corallites circular, slightly projecting to 2 mm or less, 3 to 4 mm in diameter, about 2 mm deep.  Septa in three cycles rarely with a few of the fourth, total septa upto 28.  Septa exsert, exsert parts vertical and with a deep notch.  Septal edges with frosted teetch.  Upto 18 septa reach the columella.  Columella trabecular.  Coenosteum dense.  Costae form ridges with a single row of spines, run between the corallites.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
