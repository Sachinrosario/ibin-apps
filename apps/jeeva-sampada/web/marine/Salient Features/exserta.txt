Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum closely encrusting over a dead Porites.  Maximum thickness at the broken edge 3 cm; greater spread 8 cm.  A few polychaetes tubes are found attached on the coral.  Calices shallow, rather ill-defined, polygonal or rounded, 0.6 to 0.9 mm in diameter.  The wall is composed of erect, stout flakes united by zig-zag threads which actually represent the exsert ends of septa standing vertical.  Generally all the septa are present, but rarely one or two of the second cycle are missing giving wide interseptal spaces.  The laterals of the ventral triplet do not form a trident.  A prominent septal denticle visible between the palus and the exsert ends of the septa.  Sepal sides smooth.  Outer synapticular ring near the wall, inner below the crown of pali, surrounding a deep axial fossa.  Pali five, one of each of the fused lateral pairs of septa and the outer on the ventral directive.  Pali subequal, frosted, stand below the level of the septal denticle.  Columella usually absent.  Section of the corallum shows vertical and horiyontal elements.  Living coral brownish green.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Additional remarks:  The general characters of this species is similar to P. lichen.  However, two features, viz, the size of the calices and the nature of the wall seem to differ from P. lichen conspicuously.  It is likely that P. exserta is an extreme skeletal variant of P. lichen.
Known only by type.  Rare.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
