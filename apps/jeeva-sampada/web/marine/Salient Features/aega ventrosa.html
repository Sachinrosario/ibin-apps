SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:  Ref No:  BARNARD, 1936
  Integument with rather numerous punctae. Eyes separated by a space equal to basal width of rostral point, which curves down between bases of Ist antennae to overlap anterior margin of frontal lamiaa. The transverse keel on the latteris entire (not bilobed)
  Antenna I extending to end of Ist peracon segment, 3rd peduncular joint very slightly longer than 4th , flagellum ca. 20-jointed (15 in juv).
  Side plates with oblique, curved keel; side- plates on segments 2-5 with postero- inferior corner quadrate with short sharp point, those on segments 6 and 7 more produced and more acute.
  Peraeopods 1-3, 6 th joint subequal to 4th plus 5th joints, and distinctly longer than 3rd ; on inner apex of 5th joint a slightly larger and stouter one. Inner margin of 4 th joint slightly concave and without armature.
