 SALIENT FEATURES:							Ref No:31, 52, 213

Morphological:   Rostrum straight, reaching tip of 2nd segment of antennular peduncle, armed 
with 10 dorsal teeth ; carapace densely pubescent, possessing well defined antennal, hepatic and pterygostomian spines, hepatic sulcus descending almost vertically to ventral edge of branchiostegite, no stridulating organ; 5th and 6th abdominal somites dorsally carinated, depth of 6th somite 2/3 its length; telson shorter than inner uropod, armed with 3 pairs of movable and a distal pair of fixed spines.  Petasma asymmetrical, right distoventral projection large and flap like, left distoventral projection vestigial, right and left distodorsal lobules large, distomedian lobule bifid with minute distal setae.  Appendix masculine two-segmented, distal segment 1/8 length of proximal segment.  Thelycum with a single median acute spine on transverse plate between coxae of 4th pereopods, posterior part consisting of 2 subrectangular plates with deep median groove.


Diagnostic characteristics:    Rostrum armed with 10 dorsal teeth, hepatic sulcus on carapace 
descending almost vertically reaching to ventral edge of branchiostegite, no dorsal carination on abdomen anterior to 5th somite, appendix masculina two segmented.

   Maximum length(cm)(male/female/unsexed): Male : 3.9, Female :6.4		   Ref. No: 70
