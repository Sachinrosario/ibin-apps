SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Very large cells with three-cornered valves possessing a central, straight, hollow spine. Membrane finely punctate.

Diagnostic characteristics: No circle of small spines on valve. Valve margin wavy, giving the appearance of many longitudinal lines in girdle view.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Sides of the valve measuring 110-148µ.
