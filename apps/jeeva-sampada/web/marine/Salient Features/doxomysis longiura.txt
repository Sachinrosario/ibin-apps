SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological: Diagnostic characteristics:     Body spiny. Rostrum moderately produced. Eyes stout. Antennal scale not reaching tip of antennular peduncle. Distal border of endopod of maxilla armed with nine apically flattened spines and three plumose setae. Exopod of fourth pleopod seven segmented, fifth segment with long barbed seta, sixth with short barbed seta. Lateral border pf telson with sixteen pairs of spines with wide gap between first two spines, lobes of telson carrying three blunt spines, cleft 1/3 length of telson and armed with eleven pairs of strong spines and pair of plumose setae. Endopod of uropod with row of closely arranged spines, spines up to middle apically flattened, further on flattened spines alternate with short pointed spines and towards tip all spines sharp.
