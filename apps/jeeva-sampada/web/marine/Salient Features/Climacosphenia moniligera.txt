SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Frustules are short-branched mucilage stalks, epiphytic and colony forming, cuneate, narrow with upper margin rounded at the angles, base truncate. Septa two, with numerous foramina, which are rectangular.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valves Clavate, rounded at the apex, elongated below, transversed longitudinally by two parallel lines. Striated, striae on the valves 21 in 10µ. Finely punctate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Valves 98-308µ long, 25µ broad at the top and 7-10µ at the base.
