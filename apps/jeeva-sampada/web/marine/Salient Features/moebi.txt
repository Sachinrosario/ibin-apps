SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:     
Small size; rostrum with 2 or 3 dorsal teeth; carapace with antennal and hepatic spines; exopods on pereopods reduced without seta or absent;  legs 1-3 chelate; carpus of 2nd and 3rd pereopods longer than pereopod 1; pleopods functional with setae; mandibular palp 2-sgmented with plumose setae; incisor and molar parts have no teeth; telson convex and bearing 7 pairs of spines (3 lateral and 4 terminal).
Diagnostic characters:The 4th seta of the exopod of antenna is bifurcated at tip from Nauplius IIto Nauplius VI.

Feeding habit: Pelagic zone.
Main food: Live and dead planktonic organisms, organic matter with sand and mud.
Feeding type: Omnivorous

Size and Age:

Nauplii I-VI: length. 0.26mm to 0.36mm
Protozoeae I-III: length. 0.78mm to 1.35mm
Mysis I to III: length. 2.07mm to 2.31mm
Intermediate stage: length. 2.31mm
Postlarva I: length. 2.80mm.

Eggs:Opaque with very wide perivitelline space; diameter varies from 0.50-0.54mm.Yolk mass  from 0.27mm to 0.33mm.LARVAL STAGES:Nauplii I-VI: length 0.26-0.36mmPear-shaped,unsegmented body; anterior region broad bearing an ocellus; posterior region rounded bearing a pair of furcal setae; 3 pairs of cephalic appendages,of which the 1st pair uniramous,the antennule; inner margin bearing 3 setae, proximal 1 small,outer distal margin with 1 long seta and apex with 2 long setae; antennal exopod with 5 long  setae at its inner and distal margin; endopod with 2 long setae at apex,3 short setae,one at distolateral and 2 on inner margin. Mandible biramous, each ramus bearing 3 long setae. In 2nd stage nauplius all setae become plumose,the4th inner,lateral seta on exopod of antenna bifid; the number of setae increase to 7+7 on the caudal furca of nauplius VI.In N4 setae elongate,exopod of antenna with 6 long setae and 2 spike-like setae; basal swelling of mandible developed. In N5 antennule bearing 3 setae apically,2 of same length,and  antennal exopod with 8 setae,7 long and plumose; 4 pairs of limb buds developed posterior to mandibles. N6, body elongate,frontal organs seen. Carapace demarcated; furcal lobes 7+7 setae; buds of 1st 2 maxillae and 3 maxillipeds appear; cutting edge visisble in mandibles.Protozoea I: length.0.78mm. Carapace rounded anteriorly with a median notch, ocellus persists, eyes sessile, frontal horn present; thorax segmented and abdomen unsegmented and telson fused.Antennule still uniramous,peduncle 3- segmented, the basal segment sub-segmented into 5, 2nd segment bears 4 setae at its inner margin and distal segment with 2 aesthetes and 3 setae. A2 biramous,endopod 2-segmented,distal with 5 setae and proximal with 1+2+3 setae on inner margin; exopod 10-segmented with 10 setae and 2 short setae on outer margin; mandibles asymmetrical without palp; 3rd maxilliped not fully developed.Protozoea II: length 1.10-1.52mm.Rostrum short; eyes stalked; a pair of supraorbital spines present; abdomen segmented into 5, 6th fused with telson. Caudal furca with 7+7 spines; other appendages have little change.Protozoea III: length 1.30-1.40mm. Abdominal somites demarcated, telson distinct with a wide notch,bearing  7+7 spines. Dorsomedian spines present on 1-5 abdominal somites and paired dorsolaterals on somites 5 & 6. Uropods developed, biramous, exopod bearing 6 short, non-plumose setae distally.Segmentation of the distal segment of antennule disappears; mandibles bearing teeth,(6 on left and 3 on right).Mysis I: length.2.1mmAppears to be a shrimp; rostrum extends beyond the eyes, devoid of tooth; carapace bears only pterygostomian spine; the dorsomedian spines of abdomen present only on somites 5 and 6; a ventral median spine present on somite 6; pleopod buds not clear and telson truncate with 7+7 spines.Mysis II: length.2.2mm.Rostrum short and not reaching eye; no dorsal teeth; carapace with antennal and pterygostomian spines; mandible with longer palp, unsegmented and without setae; pereopods 1-3 partly chelate, endopod segmented and identical; legs 4-5 identical and endopod longer than exopods; pleopod buds present; uropods with exopod and endopod, the former bears a terminal spine and a movable spine inner to it ,longer than the outer spine; endopod bears more of setae; telson parellel-sided, notch minimized, bearing 7+7 spines.Mysis III: length.2.3mmRotrum short,with 1 or 2 dorsal teeth; a small hepatic appers in addition to the existing antennal and pterygostomian spines; dorsomedian spines on abdominal somites 5 and 6; pleopod buds longer; mandibular palp long and devoid of setae; exopods of mxps. Shorter than endopods; legs 1-3 chelate; exopod as long as first 3 segments of endopod; telson tip narrow.Postlarva I: length.2.8mmRostrum with 2 dorsal + epigastric tooth; carapace with antennal and hepatic spines; pereopods 1-3 chelate; 4 -5 without exopods or vestigial without setae; mandibular palp 2- segmented,flat,with plumose setae and incisor and molar processes have lost their teeth; telson convex posteriorly,bearing 3 lateral and 4 terminal spines on either side..Characteristics: Rostrum short, prominent hepatic spine present; dorsomedian spine on abdomen present only on 6th somite,or a very minute one on the 5th too. Telson always with 7pairs of spines.Abundance: less abundant in the inshore waters of Karwar,N.Karnataka.
