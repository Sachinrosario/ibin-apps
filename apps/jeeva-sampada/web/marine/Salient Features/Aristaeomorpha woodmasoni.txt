Morphological:  Rostrum about 2/3 the length of carapace in adult females and slightly longer than carapace in juveniles of both sexes, curved upwards towards apex and armed dorsally with 10-13 teeth; much shorter and armed with 4-6 dorsal teeth in adult males; no ventral teeth. Carapace with well marked hepatic and pterygostomian spines;  length of pterygostomian region about 2.5 times its greatest  breadth. Dorsal carinae of 3rd to 6th abdominal somites end in sharp spines, carinae on 3rd somite present only on posterior half.  Telson reaching tip of endopod of uropod and armed with 4 spinules on either side.  Outer antennular flagellum shorter than first segment of antennular peduncle.  Pereopods lacking exopods; ischial and meral spines absent; 4th and 5th pereopods slender, their dactyli measuring half or more than half the length of their respective propodii.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Rostrum long, bearing 10-13 teeth(usually 10-11) dorsally in adult females, much shorter in adult males, no ventral teeth;  length of pterygostomian region of carapace about 2.5 times its greatest breadth;  telson reaching tip of endopod of uropod; dactyli of last two pairs of pereopods measuring half or more than half the length of their respective propodii.
 	Colour:  Body pinkish throughout.

Sex attributes: 

       Descriptive characters:     The species shows distinct variations in size, shape and dentition of rostrum between sexes, besides full development of petasma and appendix masculina in males and thelycum in females.  In females the rostrum remains long on adult hood and carries usually 10-11 dorsal teeth. In adult males the rostrum becomes very short, hardly surpassing end of basal segment of antennular peduncle and retains only 4-6 dorsal teeth.
