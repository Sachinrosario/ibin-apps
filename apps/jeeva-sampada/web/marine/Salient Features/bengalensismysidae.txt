Diagnostic characteristics:      
Genus Haplostylus Kossman          This genus agrees with the genus Gastrosaccus  in all respects except in the form of the third pleopod of the male. In Gastrosaccus  the endopod of the third pleopod is multiarticulate but in Haplostylus the enopod is rudimentary and reduced to a small oblong segment.     
Haplostylus bengalensis          Front margin of the carapce produced into small triangular rostral plate with acute apex; posterior margin without any lobes. First pleopod in male with single jointed endopod and six jointed exopod. Second pleopod with five to six jointed endopod and nine jointed exopod. Third pleopod with short conical endopod and four jointed exopod, exopod terminating in a seta, a stumpy process, and a long club shaped process ending in a seta; articulation between third and fourth joints oblique; fourth pleopod with two jointed exopod and single jointed endopod; fifth pleopod with three jointed exopod and single jointed endopod. Telson with twelve to fourteen  spines along lateral border, last two pairs very long and subequal. Inner uropod with five widely spaced sharp spines.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Adult male and female: 5.5-6 mm
