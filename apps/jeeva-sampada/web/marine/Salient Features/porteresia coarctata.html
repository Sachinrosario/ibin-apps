Salient Features :
Clums standing, erect from a stout creeping rhizomes or rootstock, branched and polished, nodes swollen.
Leaf blades margin unequal, midrib obscure, coriaceous, spinulose, serrate, leaf sheath long, undulately retuculate, polished; ligules very short.
Inflorescence panicles, spiciform, branched, few-flowered; rachis branched, trigonous, stiff, and quite smooth; pedicels deviate at top and contracted.
Spikelets laxly imbricate, rigidly cartaceous.
Fruits caryopsis, cylindrical, longitudinally striated.
