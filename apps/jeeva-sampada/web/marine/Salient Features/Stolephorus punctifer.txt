SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body slender, sub cylindrical in cross-section; belly rounded, with 3 to 6 small, needle-like scutes between pectoral and pelvic fin bases. Maxilla short and bluntly rounded posteriorly, scarcely projection beyond 2nd supramaxilla; isthmus (junction of gill openings on underside of head ) not reaching to hind border of gill membrane, leaving exposed a small (white or silver) diamond-shaped bony urohyal plate (easily visible without lens). Only 2 unbranched fin rays in dorsal and anal fins; anal fin origin behind dorsal fin base.

Colour :  Light transparent  fleshy  brown,  with a silver  stripe down  flanks.
