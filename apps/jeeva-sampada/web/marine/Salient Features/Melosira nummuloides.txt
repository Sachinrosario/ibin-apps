SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells in short chains. Globose frustule often elongated with slightly flattened ends. Valves hemispherical, not constricted in valve mantle. 
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface furnished with fine puncta arranged in lines radiating from a small hyaline central area, sub central keel or puncta or collar.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter and 28m-34m.

Biochemical aspects: Autotrophic. After ten years maintenance in a medium containing B12, thiamine and biotin, they grow without vitamins through nine months of transfer.
