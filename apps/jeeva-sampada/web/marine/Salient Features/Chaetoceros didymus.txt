SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming straight chains. Transapical axis shorter than apical axis. Setae arising from the corners of the adjacent cells crossing farther out. Chromatophores two, plate like.

Diagnostic characteristics: A semicircular knob or protuberance present in the middle of the valve.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 24-39µ in length.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
