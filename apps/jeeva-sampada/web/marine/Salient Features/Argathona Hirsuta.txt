Salient features:
Morphological:

	Body elongate ablong, broadest at the middle and evenly narrowing towards both ends. Head very short and narrow, a little more than twice as broad as long, about half as long as first peraeon segment; eyes comparatively large and lateral in position. Fourth peraeon segment longest and broadest. Dorsal surface of body with small hairs, more numerous towards the posterior half of each segment. Coxal plates very well developed and projecting considerably beyond the lateral borders of the segments, with long hairs. Pleon short, first segment partially hidden by the peraeon, lateral parts of fourth segment overhanging fifth and reaching beyond the base of the uropods; posterior margin of each segment feebly crenulate and with a submarginal row of short setae. Telson triangular, apex somewhat rounded, with six spines; dorsal side carrying a number of short stiff brown setae very much like those of A.normani but less numerous.
	First antenna short, with a two jointed penduncle and ten jointed flagellum, flagellum with long setae. Second antenna reaching the posterior border of fourth peraeon segment, fourth and fifth segments of penduncle subequal, flagellum twenty eight jointed.
	Mouth parts normal. Tip of the outer lobe of first maxilla well curved and without accessory teeth, inner lobe apically truncate and without spines. Second maxilla with three to four short apical hairs. Palp of mandible stout, second segment with a row of setae in the distal half of the upper border, third segment with a comb of setae followed by two long barbed apical setae.
	Second segment of first peraeopod long, fifth segment very short, dactylus with a small secondary unguis. Posterior peraeopods prominently spiny. Male stylet considerably longer than endopod and apically drawn out into an outwardly curved spine. Endopod of uropod perfectly rounded, with ten spines, exopod elongate oblong, with seven spines.
	Body steel blue.
