SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Colonies thin with glistening zooecia. The size of the zooecia vary considerably ranging from 360 to 400 in length and 210 to 250 in breadth. Tremocyst with numerous small pores. The presence of small prominences in between the pores give the tremocyst a granulated appearance. An umbo not present proximal to the aperture. The aperture typically rounded with an arcuate posture. Cardelles present. Thin operculum with two transparent regions at the proximal part. Ovicell hyperstomial and porous, slightly embedded in the succeeding zooecium. The pores restricted to the front, the sides thickly calcified. Avicularia absent.Diagnostic characters: .
Sex attributes: HermaphroditeDescriptive characters:  Slight differences in structure are noticed in the present material from Schizoporella linearis var. inarmata described by Osburn. The sinus of the forms described, by Osburn is between 'U' and 'V' - shaped but here it is slightly arcuate. Strong condyles are not evident in the present form, even though the cardelles are represented in all. The size of the zooecium is much smaller than in the specimens studied by both Robertson (1908) and Osburn (1952). The presence of two small transparent portions at the proximal region of the operculum is yet another feature which is not mentioned by Robertson (1908). Similarly the absence of umbo in the Indian material is also noteworthy. The habitat of this species on the wooden and glass test panels used for the study of the fouling is also of interest since the previous records are from depths five to fifty fathoms.Meristic characteristics:Feeding habit: Main food: Plankton / detritus
