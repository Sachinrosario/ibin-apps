SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: White zoarium. Absence of well developed lateral avicularia gives the sides of the branches a non-serrated appearance. Nearly three-fourth of the front occupied by the opesia. Scuta and spines wanting. Frontal avicularia rare, small and quite inconspicuous. Cryptocyst smooth and vestigial. Vibracula small, distinguishable from the frontal side of zooecia, setae as long as or a little longer than the zooecia. Vibracular groove slightly bent. Axial vibraculum single. No ovicell noticed. Chitinous joints traverse the median portion of the opesia of the outer zooecia.
Diagnostic characters: The most important character of specific importance, viz. the absence of scuta helps to distinguish this species from many other species of Scrupocellaria with scuta.
The absence of frontal avicularia is an important character by which the present material is distinguished from S. ferox.Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite.  
Descriptive characters:   The remarkable differences from S. diadema are the absence of spines and giant avicularia. Harmer has noticed five spines, but according to him, great variability occurs in this species. For instance, "scutum typically present, extremely variable, rarely completely absent, sometimes wanting in many of the zooecia". Regarding the presence of frontal avicularia also he remarks that this can be absent in many zooecia. He did notice giant avicularia on the axillary zooecia. This material resembles to a remarkable degree the specimens Harmer collected from one of the Siboga stations. Meristic characteristics:
Feeding habit: Main food: Plankton
Feeding type: Suspension feeder
