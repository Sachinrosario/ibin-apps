SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        Carapace with dorso median nodules, rostrum wide and obtuse. Antennal sympod armed with five short spines arrange in the form of cone. Endopod of thoracic limb three to seven with five to six carpopodial segments. Endopod of eighth limb in male stout, as long as basal segment of exopod, two segmented, basal segment armed with seven long setae; in female two segmented and straight, basal segment with one seta. Lateral border of telson with fourteen slender spines, outer pair of distal spines longer than inner 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)    

Adult male and female: 15-17 mm.
