Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum arborescent, subdividing branches a little flattened at the lower parts.  Main divisions 20 to 30 cm in height.  Corallites 6-8 mm in diameter, 7-8 mm in height, 6-7 mm deep, generally braoder at the top than at the base.  Septa in three cycles, tertiaries very narrow.  The first two cycles reach the columella.  There is no fusion of septa.  Primaries larger than the second cycle.  Columella poorly developed.  Corallites wall thin and porous.  Coenosteum costate, costae with transverse connections.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
