Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of vertical flabellate thick plate (The entire colony was 40 cm to 60 broad and 30 cm high) arising form a solid base.  Clumps 15 cm broad, lower portion dead.  Tip of branches either single or divided.  Calices polygonal, excavated.  1 to 1.5 mm long, 1 mm deep.  Wall thin, wavy with frosted twisted mural denticles.  A single septal denticle between the palus and the wall.  Septa begin a little below the summit of the wall.  Ventral triplet do not form a trident.  Two rings of synapticulae.  Pali poorly developed; generally five, one each on the lateral pairs of septa and the fifth on the ventral directive.  Columella thin, compresed – style joined to the fused ends of septa by radii.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The calicular characters are almost similar to that of Porites solida.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
