SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :


Female : Body more or less globular anteriorly broad and regularly narrowing towards the posterior end. Segmentation of body clear on the dorsal side more conspicuous in the middle region. Lateral parts of the body curved upwards so that the dorsal side appears concave. Brood pouch large. Cephalon large and transversely elongated, with a median longitudinal groove. Peraeon segments separated from eachother by deep grooves. Dorso-median part of each segment raised to form a broad ridge. First segment almost hidden under th cephalon, only a small area of the postero-median part exposed. Segments two to four subequal in length and breadth, five to seven shorte and narrower. Sixth segment with a large dorsal boss which on closer scrutiny is seen o be formed of three independent bosses, contiguous at the base but slightly diverging distally. The boss on the seventh segment, simple, large and slightly displaced to one side. First four pairs of ovarian bosses distinct. Pleon six segmented and bent upwards, first five with well developed, long and apically acute lateral plates, their surface prominently tuberculated; the successive lateral plates overlapping and confined to the dorsal side of the body, the anterior most plate reaching the posterior border of the second thoracic segment.
Pleopods biramous and prominently tuberculated. Uropods uniramous consisting of two lineae lamellae with only a few faint tubercles on the lower side.


Antenna and antennules hidden under the anterior oostegites. Frontal lamina narrow, its antero-median part visible from above. Posterior lamina broadly rounded and with two acuminate, ciliated and membranous processes, of which the outer is slightly larger. Palp of maxilliped linguiform and bent inwards. First oostegite roughly oblong, with ciliated margin, posterior process with only a moderately developed prolongation.

First segment of anterior peraeopods stout and with spiny inferior border, fourth, fifth and sixth segments with a few small soines. First segment of posterior peraeopods with a double boss and its inferior half with scaly surface.

Body uniformly straw yellow, grooves brownish yellow, cephalon and the bosses on peraeon of a lighter shade.
