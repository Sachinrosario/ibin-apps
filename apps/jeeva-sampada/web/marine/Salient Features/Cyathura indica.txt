SALIENT FEATURES

Morphological								Ref  No. Pillai , 1958

	Body comparatively stout; head as long as broad, rostrum prominent and eyes well developed. Peraeon segments slightly broader than head, third segment longest and seventh shortest. Pleon segments coalesced, except on the sides. 

	Antennae comparatively short, flagellum of first three segmented and that of second composed of a single composite segment with a comb of setae. Mouthparts as in C. Carinata, but the cutting edge of the mandible not expanded. First peraeopod stout, fifth segment apically blunt and very slightly projecting, sixth and seventh segments like those of C.Pusilla. Second peraeopod stout, fifth segment clearly under riding sixth, sixth segment with a very well developed, serrated apical spine. Seventh leg long, fifth segment only very slightly under riding sixth, lower border of sixth segment serrate and with three apical spines.
	
	Telson perfectly linguiform. Apical segment of endopod of uropod very slightly longer than broad, with a few very long setae; expod broadly oval, very slightly folding over endopod.

	Body white, with dark branched chromatophores forming distinct patches behind the eyes and the medidorsal part of the peraeon and pleon segments; a longitudinal streak on the telson and the endopod of the uropod.

Additional Remarks

	In some specimen , the flagellum of the first antenna is not brush like and the manditrular falls in well developed with the apical segment small, unlike the original description of the species by Barnard (1925)
Size and age : maximum length : 5.7 mm
