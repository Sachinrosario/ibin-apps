Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

A very small, slender species; belly rounded, with 5 to 8 unkeeled plate-like scutes before pelvic fin bases, but none behind. Dorsal fin at about mid-point of body; pelvic fin bases just before dorsal fin origin; anal fin well behind dorsal fin base.
Colour : 
back fleshy yellow; a faintly dark midlateral line, and  a tapering elongate silver area (peritoneum) from pectoral fin bases to anus; a series of black spots at anal  fin base and three fine black lines on caudal peduncle. Caudal fin faint yellow at base, outer rays clear, Inner rays lined of dotted with black.
