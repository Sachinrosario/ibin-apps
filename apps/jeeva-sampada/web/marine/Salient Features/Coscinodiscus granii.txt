SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Rounded valves and areolated. Middle areolae larger than the rest forming a sort of rosette. Areolae around rosette 4 in 10m.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Covering membrane is poroid and chamber openings are clear. Two asymmetrical pore canals at the margin. Wedge shaped cells in girdle view.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 153-182m.
