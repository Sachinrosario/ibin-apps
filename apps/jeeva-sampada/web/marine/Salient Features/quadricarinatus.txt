Salient Features:
Morphological:

Male - The body is perfectly ovate and dorso-ventrally flattened, with the margin fronged with fine hairs interspersed with short setae. The cephalon is one third to one half as long as broad and the area between the eyes is somewhat rectangular. The dorsal surface of the cephalon has a pair of submedian posterior submarginal tubercles. The eyes are small and place on distinct ete lobes placed well inside the lateral border and sunk deep into the first peraeon segment. The anterp-median part of the cephalon is faintly trilobed and the rostral process is hardly indicated. The first peraeon segment is the longest, the second is subequal in length to the sixth, segments three to five are nearly equal. Each peraeon segment has four nearly equidistant tubercles near the hind border. The coxal plates are large but completely fused with the peraeon segment and the line of fusion is indicated by a shallow longitudanal depression. The pleon is composed of a single segment without lateral surfaces, and like the peraeon segments, has four tubercles. The telson is broadly triangular, its antero-lateral parts are drawn out and overlap the base of the uropods, its distal border is very short and slightly concave. The proximal median part of the telson istumid and, like the segments of the body, carries four tubercles. The inner pair of tubercles is followed by two parallel longitudinal rows of smaller tubercles forming rwo prominent ridges. The tubrcles on the body are continued forwards as faint ridges so that the four longitudinal rows thus formed give the dorsal surface of the animal a quadricarianate appearance which has suggested the specific name.

The first two segments of the peduncle of the first antenna are large and visible in dorsal view, the third segment is slender, the flagellum is six-segmented. The 4th and 5th peduncular segments of the second antenna are subequal, the flagellum is eight-segmented, the first three flagellar segments are long and stout. The epistome is squarish or slightly recatngualr with the upper lip sunk into a fairly deep depression on its hind border, the anterior border of the epistome is straight or slightly convex and hairy; in dorsal view the epistome is visible as a triangular lobe projecting between the bases of the first antennae. The incisor process of the mandible is five spines, the molar process is large and toothed. The first segment of the palp of the mandible is long, the second has four setae and the third a row of five pectinate setae. The outer lobe of the first maxilla has nine teethand the inner lobe four spine setae. The lobes of the second maxilla are subsimilar, the inner border of the inner lobe is hairy and its distal border has five setae, each of the other two lobes has four spines. The apex of the maxilliped is hairy and setose, the segments of the palpare slightly are slightly produced inwards.

The fourth segment of the first peraeopod is dorsally expanded and carries two setae, the fifth segment is small, the sixth is stout and carries a barbed spine on the inner side, the dactylus is biunguiculate. The seventh peraeopod is long and slender, the inner margin of segments three to six is covered with bushy hairs. The first pleopod is small its peduncle carries three coupling hooks, the endopod is narrow and more than three times as long as broad, the exopod is longer than the endopod. The second pleopodhas a very long stylet which is more than twice as long as the endopod and characteristically folded near its base, the margin of the stylet is spiny and its apex is pointed. The exopod of the third pleopod is two-segmented, that of the fifth has three thickened areas. The endopod of the uropod is fused with the peduncle to form a large lamina closely fitting on either side of the telson thus preserving the oval outline of the body, the exopod is small and roughly ovate, less than one fourth the length of the endopod and lodged in an excavation on the endopod. 

The body is dark brown in clour. 
The female is similar to the male but slightly larger and darker in colour. The mouth parts are not metamophosed.

Additional Remarks:
The holotype male is kept in the indian museum, Kolkatta.
The rectangular epistome is a distinct character of the genus Dies. The quadridentate dorsal surface of the body readily distingushes this species from others under the genus Dies.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size Recorded  4.0mm.
