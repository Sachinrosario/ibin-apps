SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body moderately elongate, the depth 3.3 to 3.7 times in standard length. Chin with 2 very long slender barbells, reaching or nearly reaching  posterior end of head; about  1.65 to 1.75 times in head; mouth small, the maxilla reaching about 3/5 distance to front edge of eye; a single series of well-spaced, stout, conical  teeth in jaws; no teeth on roof of mouth; gillrakers on first arch 33 to 39. First  dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first small, the third longest, contained about 1.6 times in head; last dorsal and anal rays elongate, about 1.5 times longer than preceding rays; pectoral rays 15 or 16 (usually 16). Lateral line scales 27 or 28; 2 vertical scale rows in space between dorsal fins; 8 or 9 vertical scale rows along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour :

 reddish brown, paler ventrally (sometimes pale magenta or lavender) and posteriorly, with a dark brown stripe passing from front of snout, through eye, across upper end of gill openings, and ending beneath rear base of second dorsal fin; a roundish black spot  (usually larger than eye) on posterior third of caudal peduncle, the lateral line passing through its lower third; a vertical pale blue line or spot on scale of body below dark stripe, and pale blue lines margin of dark stripe on snout; basal third of second dorsal fin blackish, with a narrow extension to posterior tip of fin; lateral edge of pelvic fins broadly blackish.
