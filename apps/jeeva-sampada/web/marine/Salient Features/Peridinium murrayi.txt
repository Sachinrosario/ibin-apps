SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cell body height and width about same. Apical horn long rising steeply from the epitheca end blunt, probably open. Posterior horns long diverging markedly from body, ends pointed.

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle sinistral, ends deeply excavated winged with lists. Sulcus slightly inclined.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 125-250µ; Diameter: 75-110µ.
