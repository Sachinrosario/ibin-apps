Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum foliaceoar with thinner edges.  Maximum thickness 6 mm.  Corallites nariform, i.e. projecting on one side of the wall, inner wall not elevated, inclined towards the growing edges.  Corallites 9-11 mm in diameter, projecting wall upto 8 mm in height.  Septa 20 to 24, larger and smaller one alternating, thickened at the wall, larger ones reaching to the columella.  Edges of septa with 3-4 small teetch.  Costae correspond to septa, continuous from corallite to corallite with rounded granulations.  Columella formed of twisted trabeculae.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3 Curio. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
