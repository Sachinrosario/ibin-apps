
SALIENT FEATURES:						Ref No:49,52,103,144

Morphological:  Rostrum slightly curved downward, reaching or exceeding distal margin of 
basal antennular segment, armed with 6-7 dorsal teeth; carapace with distinct postrostral crest, no branchiostegal spine, no trace of hepatic crest, longitudinal and transverse sutures present, the former extending almost to posterior border ; antennular flagella shorter than carapace, 5th pereopod exceeding antennal scale by length of dactyl. Petasma with distolateral lobes spout-like and as long as distomedian ones. Thelycum with semicircular anterior plate articulating to intermediate plate and with a median groove which continues on intermediate plate; the latter is broad, quadrate and continuous to posterior sternal plate.

Diagnostic characteristics:   Carapace without branchiostegal spine, 5th pereopod exceeding 
                          antennal scale by length of dactyl.

Colour: Body pink to whitish, speckled with pink chromatophores,; a red spot on 
              distomedian  part of outer uropods.

Feeding habit:										Ref. No: 14
    
 Main food:    Crustaceans remains (60%), molluscan remains, foraminiferans, sand grains, 
       debris.
         Feeding type: Carnivorous bottom feeder.
