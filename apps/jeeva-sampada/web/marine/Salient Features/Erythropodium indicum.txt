SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony erect. The stem is branched following polyps bear eight lobed calyces with 1 mm in diameter.
		Spicules:  The spicules of the polyps and the coenenchyma are mostly warted with club-shaped spindles of 0.08 mm length. 

Colour		:	The colony is sealing-wax red and the polyps are yellow in 
			colour.
