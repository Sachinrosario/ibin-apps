SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:       
Male:  Length of cephalothorax 7.05 mm, abdomen: 3.00 mm.     The cephalothorax elongate ovate ; the lateral corner of the last pedigerous segment rounded. The rostral filaments slender.     The abdomen 5 – jointed. The furcal rami symmetrical, 1.6 times as long as wide ; the inner distal margin of the ramus carries a small dorsal seta.     

The right 1st antenna extends at least to the end of the furca. The 2nd antenna as in Heterorhabdus, but the exopod is smaller than the endopod. In the mandible the tooth plate as that of Heterorhabdus, but the ventral tooth is not so strong.  The 1st maxilla has 6 setae on the endopod.  The 2nd maxilla is provided with each a strong spine on the 4th and 5th lobes ; these spines are furnished with denticles along the posterior margin.     

In the right 5th leg the inner marginal process of the 2nd segment of the exopod rather small and simple ; the inner margin of the 2nd basal segment has a rounded process furnished with a brush of hair – like spines.       

Female:  Body elongated. Proximal lobes of maxilla 2 not well defined.  Exopod 3 of pereiopod 5 longer than exopod 1 and exopod 2.

Additional  remarks:      The female specimen from the Indian Seas measured 9.21 mm. The specimen reported by Sars from the Atlantic measured 10.30 mm.
