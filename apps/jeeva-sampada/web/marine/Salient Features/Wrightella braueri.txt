SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony is flabellate. Branching is alternate and partly dichotomous. The branches are uniformly thin. The internodes are 15 to 18 mm long and the nodes are not prominent. The polyps stands sideways, almost alternatively in rows and are armed with heavily warted with long spindles (0.16 mm), of which the collaret composed of four transverse rows , above which two pairs of similar spindles spring up as eight pointed double rows meeting at the distal ends. 
		Spicules:  The calyces and the general coenenchyma are heavily with leafy-club shaped spicules. Besides, these there are number of ordinary spindles, clubs, bent spindles, and thorned and variously branched forms. The nodes contain 0.08 to 0.9 mm long, thin, smooth spindles occasionally with protuberance at the centre. 

Colour		:	The entire colony is whitish violet and the axis is bright red.
