SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony flabellate and reticulate 19.5 cm long and a spread of 12.5 cm . The main stem is nearly round, the horny joints (internodes) being 5 mm in diameter and the calcareous nodes 3.5 mm in diameter. The branches are borne on the internodes and are typically alternate and lateral in position from its basal 6.5 cm the stem gives off occasional irregular branchlets, above this point the stem soon gives off a reticulate mass of branches and branchlets which are dichotomous. Most of the branchlets terminate in U-shaped bifurcations on the margins of the fan. The calyces are lateral in position forming a close-set row on each side of the braches and twigs. The individual calyces are small; dome shaped verrucae, less than 0.5 mm in height and 1 mm in diameter. The polyps are minute but show a very different collaret composed on one or two rows of bent spindles and a pseudo-operculum of similar spindles arranged en cheveon basally and disposed longitudinally on distal parts of the tentacles; the whole forming a symmetrical rosette when viewed from above.
		Spicules: Those of the axis are small, smooth, bar-like forms aggregated together into a felted mass, which is less dense in the horny and denser in the calcareous nodes. The spicuels of the coenenchyma are tuberculated spindles and spiny clubs. The spindles immensely preponderate over the clubs in number. 

		
Colour		:	The colony is light grayish and spicules are colorless..
