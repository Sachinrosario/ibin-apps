SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  flabellate and not reticulate, 8.5 cm in height and 5.5 cm in diameter. The stem and branches are not appreciably flattened and are without evident median grooves. The main stem 1.9 mm in diameter and 1.8 cm long to first branch. The remainder of the main stem bears seven lateral branchlets, two of which are compound. The ultimate twigs are about 1 mm in diameter. The calyces are regularly lateral and alternate.
		The individual calyces are subconical in shape, a typical one measuring 1.2 mm in height ad 1.9 mm in diameter at the base. The calyx walls are filled with heavy spindles, most of which are placed vertically. These differ from the oval forms that are found in the calyx.
		The polyps are completely retractile. The collaret is delicate, consisting of but one or two rows of slender encircling spindles, above which other spindles are arranged en chevron over the tentacle base.
		Spicules: Spindle shaped and slender in the coenenchyma (Fig.a, b,c & d) .Spicules of the axis (Fig.e) are smooth and are joined by cross connections into a sort of mesh.

Colour		:	Colony is a light reddish brown.
