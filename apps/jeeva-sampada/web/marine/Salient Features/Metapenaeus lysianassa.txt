 SALIENT FEATURES:							Ref No:83,103

Morphological:   Body almost entirely pubescent; rostrum very short, reaching as far as middle 
of  first antennular segment and armed with 6 or 7 teeth along entire dorsal margin, rostral crest high; postrostral carina ending near posterior margin of carapace; adrostral carina and sulcus reaching as far as 3rd rostral tooth; barnchicardiac sulcus not reaching to middle of carapace; telson armed only with spinules; ischial spine on 1st pereopod absent or minute; in adult males, merus of 5th pereopod with a proximal notch followed by a large, slightly arcuate triangular tooth. Distolateral projections of petasma bifurcate distally; distomedian projections with a minute filament on their median margins. Anterior and lateral plates of thelycum subequal in size, anterior plate tongue-like and grooved, posterior plates suboval with thick median margins; impregnated specimens with white conjoined pads on thelycum.


Diagnostic characteristics:    Rostrum very short reaching middle of first antennular segment and 
toothed along entire dorsal margin; rostral crest high; adrostral carina and sulcus extending to 3rd rostral tooth; filament on median margins of distomedian projections of petasma minute; anterior plate of thelycum tongue-like and grooved, posterior plates suboval.

        Colour: Body, pereopods and pleopods pale yellow with grey specks.


Sex attributes:
       Descriptive characters:   In adult males, merus of 5th pereopod is provided with a proximal 
notch followed by a large, slightly arcuate triangular tooth. Impregnated  females                 have white conjoined pads on thelycum.
