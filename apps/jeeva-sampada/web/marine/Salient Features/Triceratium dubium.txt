SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Rhombic lanceolate valves with three, four, and five sided or irregularly shaped. In side view two angles each with a stout horn like process and the other two angles with short blunt processes.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve surface strongly sculptured, areolated irregularly, areolae 6 in 10µ. Valve margin striated. Girdle band areolate, areolae 12 in 10µ arranged in rows.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 21-34µ.
