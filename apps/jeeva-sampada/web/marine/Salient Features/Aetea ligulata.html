SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zoarium spread over the substratum by means of stolons. Erect zooecia arise from the expanded bases. The stalk of the zooecium corrugated, very different from the annulations found in Aetea anguina. The head portion smooth. The basal expansion from which the zooecia arise shows vague punctuations. Branching at right angles from the expanded base. Stolons also show indistinct corrugations. Frontal membrane thin and translucent.

Diagnostic characteristics: The corrugations are of specific value since they have been noticed in all portions of the colony.
Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: The nature of branching is at right angles which showed certain differences from the figure given by Osburn (1950, Ref. No. 230). The colony is white in colour.
