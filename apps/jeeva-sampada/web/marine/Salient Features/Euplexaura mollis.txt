SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	: Colony branching is in one plane and numerous diagonal branchlets appear from the main stem. The polyps are small and stand 1 mm apart. The operculum is low. The coenenchyma is evenly thick with smooth upper surface. 
			
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are small and large belted, warted spindles with clubs are also found. 
 
Colour	:	The colony is brown to ash-black in colour.
