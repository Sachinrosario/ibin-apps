SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum subhemispherical.  Corallites level or projecting to 2 mm, diameter 12 to 24 mm, 5-10 mm deep.  Intercorallite wall 3-4 mm thick, vesicular.  Calices circular, oval or distorted with 40 to 80 septa depending on the size.  Septa exsert, exsert ends stopping at the middle of the intercorallite area.  Septa narrower at the top than the lower one-third; edges dentate, fifteen to 20 septa reach the columella, others unite to the sides of the immediate lower cycles.  Columella trabecular, 3-6 mm in diameter.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3.  Building blocks.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
