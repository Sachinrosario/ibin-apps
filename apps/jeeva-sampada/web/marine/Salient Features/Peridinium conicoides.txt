SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells rhombic in ventral view with slightly convex sides. Girdle almost circular, excavated with fine lists. Hypotheca ends in two short hollow horns. Longitudinal furrow broadening posteriorly, on the left making a twist just below the girdle, sometimes with two small spines near it. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Outline of the cell from above and below almost circular.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter of the cell: 45-60µ.
