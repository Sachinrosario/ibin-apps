SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Head long, narrow and mobile; snout pointed; nostril
nearer to the orbit than to the end of the snout; neck long. Ear-opening
conspicuous. Eye with a round pupil; eyelids well developed. Tounge
long, slender, deeplky forked and retracticle into a sheath at the base,
as in snakes. Scales on top of the head small and polygonal, while those
on the back are granular; the venrtral scales are quadrangular and
smooth, arranged in from 90 to 110 transverse series. limbs well developed
digits moderately elongate, and with sharp claws. Tail long, not 
fragile, strongly compressed.

Diagnostic Characteristics: Size large. Tounge snake-like. Body heavy. Adults
usuallyolive or brownish aboive, with dark spots and yellowish below, uniform
or spotted darker, more prominent on the throat. Young dull orange to brown
above, with 10-13 narrow yellow to cream bars alternating with dark
spots or bars; top of head with light spots or bars; a more or less distinct
temporal streak; underside whitish; tail with alternating light and
dark bands except for light tip.

Feeding habit: Carnivorous
Main food: Fish, crabs, birds, rats eggs of birds/reptiles and 
rotting flesh.

Additional Remarks: The common monitor is found in the pains, hills
and round the coasts and estuaries throught India. Despite the massive
body, the monitor is surprisingly agile. It can climb trees, swim deep rivers, 
run over the ground or dig a burrow with a remarkable speed, if purused.
The monitor swallows the prey whole.

The female, depending on the size lays from 8 to 30 white oval and soft
shelled eggs in termite mounds and such other secretive places. Incubation
takes 8 to 9 months.

If concerned, the monitor rises high on its body, hisses loudly and lashes
out with all its tail in readiness to defend itself.

The monitor lizard is hunted for its prized skin, delicious eggs, wholesome
mat and the so called "medicinal oil" extracted from the animal's 
visceral fat. The monitor lizard is endangered and is strictly protected.
