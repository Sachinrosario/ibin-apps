SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Disc shaped cells with undulating valves. Valve with six sectors of the same size. Central area hexagonal. The raised sectors possess a short blunt process in the middle near the margin. The surface strongly areolated and punctate. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Depressed sectors without process; areolae not so prominent, instead a weakly differentiated network of lines, punctate.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter 34-53m.
