SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells forming straight chains. Tiny spine at the centre of the valve, aperture elliptical.

Diagnostic characteristics: Setae arising from the inner valve surface, base of the setae short. Chromatophores numerous disc shaped and distributed in the cell and setae.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Diameter: 32-50µ.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
