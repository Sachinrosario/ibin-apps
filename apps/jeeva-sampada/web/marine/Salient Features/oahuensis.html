SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallets feather shaped, weak and without stalks. Pallets are symmetrical; stalk extends through the blade, best seen when viewed through bright-transmitted light. Baled consists of very closely packed segments which are nearly fused and covered with pale brown non adherent periostracum, Distal portion of the stalk at the point where it enters into the blade is also covered with periostracum. No sign of segmented nature could be seen in the periostracum. Outer face of the blade convex and inner face concave.

Feeding habit: Feeding on the wood into which it bores.

Main food: Cellulose supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks: Typically an estuarine species. Original description is from the mouth of the Kalhi Stream, Oahu, Hawaii. Turner (1966) has suggested that this species may probably be a young Nausitora.
