Descriptive characters:  A specimen from the Andaman Islands reported by Matthai (1924) under the specific name L. digitata is preserved in the Indian Museum Calcutta.  It has a highly branching corallum with narrow terminal branches 3 to 5 mm thick.  The calicinal side is folded and concave, while the non-calicinal side is costate.  The calices are wide apart.  Septo-costae alternating in size.  Columella centers formed of twisted trabeculae.

A few specimens obtained from the west coast of India from a depth of 30 m had some of the branches curled upto form cylindrical tubes resembling L. tubilifera.  There are 20 to 22 septa around a calyx.  

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
