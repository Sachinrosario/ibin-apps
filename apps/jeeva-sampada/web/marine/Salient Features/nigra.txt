Descriptive characters:  Corallum primarily encrusting, later becoming pulvinate.  Larger colonies upto 50 cm in greater spread.  Living layer 1 to 3 cm thick.  Calices at the growing edges and lower parts of the corallum neatly polygonal, 3 mm in length and width, flush with the surface.  Wall very thin.  At the upper parts and top of the corallum the calices become rounded, 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter.  Wall thickened, septa 24.  In shallow calices they are straight.  It look like a row of broad, frosted granules extending from the wall to the centre of the axial fossa.  The denticles become shorter towards the centre of the fossa.  Six to 6 such denticles are present on the primaries and secondaries.  In the rounded, deeper calices the septa at first descent down little from the top of the wall and then extends towards the columella.  Interseptal loculi wider than the thickness of the septa or they are equal in width.  Septal sides granular.  Columella an upright single style, joined to the septal ends by radii.  Fusion of septa not visible.  Palar formula typical Gonioporid pattern.  

Living coral sepia ink in colour which is retained even after washing and cleaning in fresh water.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Raw material for CaCO3. 

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
