SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics: 
     Female: Pigment spot on right side. Proximal joints of 1st antenna bearing small low denticles. Fifth pair of legs 2 – jointed (with one free joint); distal joint with three terminal spines and two thin outer spines.  

     Male:  Pigment spot on right side. Left antennule geniculate. Proximal joint of endopodite of 2nd pair of legs bearing denticles on right side only. Distal joint of right leg of 5th pair elongate, slightly less widened than in the preceding forms; penultimate joint of same leg bearing curved process with double row of blunt tubercles
Size and age: 
Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
     Female: 1.60-2.55 mm
     Male    :  1.51-2.25 mm
Ref. No.: K.A. Brodskii, 1950
