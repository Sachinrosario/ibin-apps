SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Zooecia vary greatly in size, based on the rate of development. Striated ectocyst often covered with mud. The stolon-like proximal portion represents the adnate part, and the erect portion, the peristome. From the base arise stolon-like portions often four or more in number, and give rise to cylindrical peristomes with squarish orifices. Distal end of fertile zooecia often with embryos. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes:   HermaphroditeDescriptive characters: The zooids are longer and in the same colony zooecia of different sizes are noticed. Mature zooids possess two or three embryos at their tips. Very long zooids are noticed, longer than 1mm usually vary from 1.2mm to 1.5mm. Empty zooids which are shorter and the ectocyst deep brownish in hue have also been noticed. Harmer (1915) has stated that in some of the oldest zooecia, evidenced by the larger amount of mud in their ectocyst, are, not longer than many of the young individuals. This indicates that on degeneration of the polypide the zooecia may shorten considerably, partly by contraction and probably in part by the loss of the distal end of the peristome. Regenerated zooecia similar to those examined by Harmer (1915) have been noticed in the present collection.
