SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Delicate zoarium with biserially arranged zooecia. Branching of type 4 Fig. 46a of Harmer (1923, 1926). Zooecia short, narrow in the proximal region, broadening distally. Opesia occupy the entire front. Spines wanting. Avicularia present, placed at the proximal end of the gymnocyst, with rounded head and tubular basis. Rostra long and moderately hooked. No ovicells noticed. Radicles emerge from the median part of the dorsal region. Diagnostic characters: This form differs from B. robusta to which it seems to be allied, in the more fragile nature of the branches, the absence of dorsal median calcareous lines and the strongly marked septal lines.Sex attributes:   Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: The present material consists of immature zoaria only as evidenced by the absence of ovicells. Except for the absence of ovicells, all other characters of Bugula crosslandi are clearly represented in the specimens. The avicularium is placed at the proximal end of the zooecium on a cylindrical thickening, which is placed at the outer portion of the proximal region, in close contact with the distal part of the preceding zooecium. Most of the species of Bugula have their avicularia placed at the distal half of the zooecium. Due importance have been given to this character by Harmer (1926).
  Nothing can be mentioned about the nature of ovicells in the present specimens. Ovicells according to Hastings (1939) are pedunculate. Meristic characteristics:
Feeding habit: Main food: Plankton
Feeding type: Suspension feeder
