SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Lanceolate valves with scarcely drawn out ends. Raphe less valve with robust transapical ribs, perpendicular to the middle line, crossed by delicate longitudinal ribs. Pseudoraphe long and linear.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve with thread like raphe. Axial area narrow, widened in the middle with a small cross band in the centre. Transapical striae radial, 18-10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: 35-41m long and 13-17m broad.
