SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Slightly convex cells. Ovate valves. Excentric middle field. Sector lines of the middle are unbranched. Hyaline rays 7 to 8, 1.5m wide, one slightly narrower reaching margin of the valve. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Rays slightly curved. Border segments areolated in three lines system, areolae about 15 in 10m.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Long axis 38-61m, short axis 32-54m.
