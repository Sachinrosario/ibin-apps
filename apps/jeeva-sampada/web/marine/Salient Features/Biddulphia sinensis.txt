SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Large cylindrical, weakly silicified cells elliptical lanceolate in valve view with pervalvar axis elongated.

Diagnostic characteristics: Two thin horns at the corners of the valve and near each other, a long thin spine. Membrane finely areolate; areolae in rows on the girdle.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis measuring 120-196µ.
