SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 Colony slender and flabellate, 16.5 cm in height and with a spread of 7 cm. The main stem is 2.4 cm above its bass and the colony is denuded of calyces. The calyces are irregularly distributed and the distance varying from 1 to 2 mm. There are no terminal polyps. The individual calyces are sub-cylindrical in shape measuring 0.75 mm in height to the very prominent operculum with a diameter of 1.5 mm. The operculum is a high dome shaped and armed with spindles. The coenenchyma is rather thin, but the spindles are spaced so that the inner layer of spindles is often exposed.
		
Spicules: The spicules are  warty.

Colour		:	Colony is light silvery brown or grayish and the axis is dark 
			brown in colour.
