Morphological:   Colonies erect, jointed, attached to the substratum by rootlets arising as bunches from the proximal region of the colony. Zoarium deep brown in colour when alive, light brownish in hue when preserved in alcohol. Zooecia arranged in fours around a central axis of the jointed branches. The distal portion of the preceding zooecia overarches the proximal portion of the succeeding one. Lateral zooecial margins raised. Gymnocyst extensive proximally. Cryptocyst vestigial. Spines present, numerous. A proximal long spine, placed medianly rarely bifurcate, two small ones placed on the disto-lateral margin located nearly parallel to the proximo-lateral corners of the operculum. Occasionally, two small and fragile branched spines disposed on the proximal side of the opesia, overarching it. Operculum membraniporine. Joints of the branches very much like the joints in N. oculata. No ovicells noticed. Diagnostic characters: Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:   Nature of the zoarium no doubt helps in the inclusion of the present form under Cellularina. The arrangement of the zooecia and the nature of the joints assign the present form to the family Farciminariidae, but it seems that no genus described under this family can as such receive this form.
A study of the literature shows that the present material may be tentatively assigned to the genus Nellia. But the characters of the zoarium do not even support a tentative assignment of the form under any of the species of the above genus. In the presence of spines and in the absence of sessile avicularia this form differs from all species of Nellia hitherto described. However, more detailed study is required before any attempt is made towards the creation of a new genus to receive the present species.
