SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 The branching is confined to one plane and is very irregular. The branches arise at varying angles and bend in diverse directions. The coenenchyma is fairly thin. The axis horny and the calyces are 0.5 mm high and 0.75 mm broad. The polyps are fully retracted and they are dome shaped. The collaret consists of two or three rows of slightly interlocking, curved spindles, and standing upon this are the eight points, each formed of about four curved spindles arranged "en chevron". 
		
Spicules: The spicules are all warty and spiny spindles, straight, curved, or slightly S-shaped, 

Colour		:	Colony is bright red to orange-red in colour.
