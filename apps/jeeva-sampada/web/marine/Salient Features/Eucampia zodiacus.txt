SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Flat cells united into spirally twisted chains by blunt processes. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve concave in the middle. Intercalary bands difficult to make out. Valve punctate. Punctae 15 rows in 10µ. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 44-60µ in length.
