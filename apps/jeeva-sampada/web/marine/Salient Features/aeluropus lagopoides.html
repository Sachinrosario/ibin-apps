Salient Features :
It is perennial rigid, tufted grass, much branched, with long rigid, convolute leaves (0.4 to 2.5 cm), which are generally coverd with small crystals of the salt secreted trhough glands.
Stem is runner densely tufted, crowded ona woody rootstock.
Spikelet short simple racemes.
Seeds are small, size ovoid, oblong, dorsally compressed and brown in color.
It is only species of the genus occurring in arid parts of India.

Soil data for (1:2) extracts

Soil salinity : 10.10 dS.m-1
Soil pH : 7.8 to 8.2
E S P : 18.2 to 32.3
S A R : 15.1 to 33.3

Nutrition:
This halophytic grass is used as forgae for live stock (fooder and hay)

R & D:
It is helpful in reclamation of saline soild patches due to its sand binding quality.
