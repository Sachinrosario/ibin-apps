Descriptive characters:  The following details are based on Scheer & Pillai (1974).  Corallum caespitose, repeatedly dividing, branchlets with occasional coalascens.  Main branches about 1 cm thick, branchlets 3-4 mm thick evenly narrow or up to 8 cm if thickened.  Calices 0.5 to 0.6 mm in diameter, a diameter apart.  Primary cycle of septa very prominent, the directives fuse each other to form a complete partition of the calyx.  Second cycle of cycle rarely complete.  On the distal parts of the branchlets the calicular wall rise to form a distinct hood at the upper side.  Surface of coenenchyme ehinulate.

The species is known from the Indian waters only from the Ganges Harbour of Nicobar Islands, but should occur through out Andaman Nicobar Islands. 

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
