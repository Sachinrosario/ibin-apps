SALIENT FEATURES:
Diagnostic characteristics:       

Female: Prosome with out crest. Genital segment asymmetrical, knob- like protrusion on left posterior margin, right side indented. Leg 4 basipod 1 with two long spines (Grice 1962, Vervoort 1949).      

Male: Prosome with out crest. Maxilla 1 endopod and basal joint 2 with five setae. Left leg 5-exopod reaching right leg endopod first thickening, terminal exopod joint of right leg with 35 semicircular teeth (Sewell 1947).

Additional remarks:       
The two males from the south –west Pacific are identical to those described by Sewell (1947) except  that the terminal   exopod joint of the right leg has 31 semicircular teeth.
