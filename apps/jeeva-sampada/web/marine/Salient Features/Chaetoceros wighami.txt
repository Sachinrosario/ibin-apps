SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Tender cells forming chains. Cells oblong with sharp corners in broad girdle view. The corners of neighbouring cells touch each other enclosing a narrow slit like aperture.

Diagnostic characteristics: Setae thin and fragile. Inner ones perpendicular to the chain axis, end setae parallel to the chain axis. Chromatophore plate like. 

Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 10-18µ.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
