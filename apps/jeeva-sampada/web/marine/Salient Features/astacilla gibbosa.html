SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:

Body comparatively stout but short, surface smooth. In dorsal view the anterior border of cephalon with a minute median point. Antero-lateral corners produced apically narrowed prolongations. Eyes large and bulging. Dorsal side with a pair of anterior and a slightly larger pair of posterior submedian bosses, the two pairs of bosses separated by a deep froove, the whole structure resembling that of A.amblyura but more pronounced. First peraeon segment partially united with cephalon, dorsal and ventral regions completely free, dorsal side with a large median boss. Second and third segments smaller, each with a postero-dorsal boss. Fourth segment large, with a very large dorsal hump, situated in the anterior half. Segments five to seven narrower and subsimilar. Pleon united with telson but the areas of fusion indicated by faint grooves. Telson roughly pentagonal, with a very prominent tubercleon the postero-lateral corner, beyond which it appears as a broad based triangle.

First antenna comparatively stout, reaching the second segment of second antenna. First segment large and rounded, flagellum longer than segments two and three combined. Second antenna reaching the base of the pleon, fourth and fifth peduncular segments subequal in length ; flagellum very short, three jointed and much narrower than the last peduncular segment. Apical flagellar segment with four equidistant large teeth along the inferior border. Upper lip bilobed and prominently hairy. Maxilliped typical. Outer lobe of first maxilla strongly arched, with ten large spines in two rows, inner lobe with three strong setae and a small spine. Second maxilla typical. The bilobed incisor part of mandible with the upper lobe three toothed and the lower teo toothed, middle setae three, molar well developed, with three inferior setae. Lower lip formed of two diverging setose lobes, over-lapping in the middle.

First peraeopod moderately setose, fifth segment with a row of very prominently serrated spines. Dactylus with a very strong apical seta and two slightly smaller ones on either side. Second and fourth legs without a pronounced dactylus, fifth to seventh with a very small secondary unguis.

Uropods as long as telson or slightly longer. Basal part narrow and the distal lobe slightly longer than broad, with hairy margin and a small apical seta. Secondary ramus short, with two apical setae. Peduncle of second pleopod of male with two inner spines, endopod longer but narrower than exopod, male stylet stout and longer than endopod, with minute spines on its inner border.
Body white in alcohol.

Size and Age
Length 10.0 mm
