SALIENT FEATURES:       Ref No:20,48,52,83,113.

 

Morphological:   Rostrum short, horizontal, reaching about middle of eye, upper margin almost

straight and armed with 7-11 teeth including epigastric, ventral profile convex; postrostral crest present upto  a little in front of middle of carapace; cervical groove deep, reaching almost to end of postrostral crest, does not indent carapace mid-dorsally; telson trifurcate; antennular flagella about 1.3 times as long as carapace including rostrum; outer lobe of uropod without spine on posterolateral angle; 4th pereopods reaching beyond distal tips of antennal scales by atleast entire dactyli

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Postrostral carina absent on posterior half of carapace, cervical

groove does not indent the carapace mid-dorsally, outer lobe of uropod without spine on posterolateral angle.

 

 

 

Sex attributes:

 

Descriptive characters:   In male, petasma is fringed with numerous prominent setae along

distal 2/5 of lateral margins, process–a and process–b present.  Appendix masculina is provided with a tuft of long wavy hairs near anterior base of spur-like process of proximal piece.

 

        In female, thelycum has a quadrangular area in between 5th pereopods with a median elevation covered with hook-like spines; two oval plates bearing bunches of setae present in front of anterior border of quadrangular area..
