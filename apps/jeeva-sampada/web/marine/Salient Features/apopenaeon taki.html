SALIENT FEATURES :
Morphological :

Female :

Body slightly longer than broad, somewhat pear shaped, slightly assymetrical. Coxal plates on one side very much degenerated. Head somewhat oval, two thirds as long as broad; anterior border perfectly rounded and the posterior slightly produced. Frontal lamina very broad, with irregularly crenate anterior and lateral borders extending beyond the lateral borders of the head. Posterior lamina with two pairs of highly branched processes, the branches are slender, with two smaller bunches on either side. The ovarian bosses on the first four segments very prominent. Coxal plates well developed and overlapping, those on the left side reduced. Sixth and seventh coxal plates large. Brood pouch open at the center. Abdomen six jointed, sixth segment very small and hidden by the lateral plates. Pleural lamellae well developed, symmetrical, large and foliaceous and prominently tuberculated.

First antenna three and the second five jointed, latter twice as long as the former. Palp of maxilliped large, apex narrow and in the present specimen bent downwards, posterior lobe apically truncate. Inner crest of first oostegite very high and covered by long digitiform processes which are less numerous than in Epipenaeon. Peraeopods with a high rectangular or slightly oblong boss on the basis. Uropods biramous, stout and apically blunt, resembling the pleopods. Pleopods biramous, not reaching beyond the pleural lamellae, prominently tuberculated. Lower surface of pleon segments wrinkled.

Additional Remarks:
Compared to the Japanes specimens the female collected from Alleppey by Pillai (1954, 1958) had larger coxal plates and well developed pleural lamellae. The tuberculation of the pleopods and the pleural lamellae is less intense, though the individual tubercles are larger than in Shinos (1950) specimens.
