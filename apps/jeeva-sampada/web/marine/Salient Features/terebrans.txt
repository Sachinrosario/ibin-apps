Salient Features:

Morphological:
Body elongate oblong, broadest at the sixth segment. Posterior half of dorsal side coarsely granular, each granule carrying a  bunch of long hairs. Border of segments hairy.

Anterior border oof cephalon feebly trilobed, second peraeon segment shortest and fourth longes. Segments 3-7 with a transverse ridge placed in the anterior half on segment 3 but shifting successively backwards on the succeding segments and becoming posterior submarginal on the seventh segment. Peraeon segments 5-7 with 4 equidistant posterior submarginal tubercles, each carrying a bunch of long stiff hairs; submedian pair slightly longer than the lateral; some specimens with 4 indistinct tubercles on the fourth segment also.

Composite Pleon segment equal to the seventh peraeon segment, with 4  large tubercles as on the peraeon. Lateral parts with n3 oblique sutures.

Telson a broad based triangle; proximal median part bulged, apex angular and slightly curved upwards. Proximal region  with 4 tuberceles similar to those on the pleon and peraeon. Dorsal side of pleon and telson with pustules of various sizes, surmounted by hairs, giving the body a coarsely granular appearence. The successive tubercles on the peraeon, pleon and telson fall into 4 equidistant longitudinal rows.

First antenna with 3 segmented peduncle and 9-10 segmented flagellum. First Peduncular segment stout, third slender and long second equal in length and breadth; all the segments with sensory setae. Flagellar segments, except the first, with simple and olfactorysetae. Second antenna stouter than first, fifth segment of peduncula longest, anterior border of peduncle hairy; flagellum 12-13 segmented, each segment with a bunch of setae.

Epistome triangular, slightly broader than long, anterior part subtruncate; arms short but stout, embracing nearly half of the upper  lip; exposed surface in large specimens granular.

Mandible with a strong dark brown, bidentate incisor process; molar cylindrical and produced, with file like arrangement of teeth; middle part of cutting edge with 5 spines on the left and 6 on the right . Palp 3 segmented , first segment long and hairy, second cylindrical and withy a row of pectinate setae, third triangular and with about 17 comb like setae regularly increasing in length distalwards. 

First maxilla with distal part of both lobes hairy, exposed surface of outer lobe with a stout, barbed spine distal border with 13 teeth, 10 large and barbed and 3 small and simple. Inner lobe with 3 stout spines, with stiff hairs and strong barbs and 2 simple spine setae and a small spinule.

Second maxilla with 3 elongate triangular, overlapping lobes, inner lobes larger and placed at a lower level and its inner border with a row of stout setae, each seta with a thick matting of long hairs, distal part of each seta with small barbs. Distal two-thirds of inner margin of middle and outer lobes with long pectinate setae, outer lobe conspicously hairy.

Maxilliped with a stout sympod, inner lobe elongate triangular, palp stout; inner border of segments not produced, with a thick matting of long hairs, inner border of third segment hairy, fifth segment slightly longer than the fourth.

Lower lip bulged at the middle deeply bilobed proximal outer part spiny, distal part of each lobe narrow and prominently hairy.

Peraeopods 1-3 subsimilar, long and frail. First peraeopod with ischium about one and a half time as long as basis, merus half as long as ischium, carpus triangular; upper border of ischium and merus witha a double row of long plumose setae. Outer distal part of propodus hairy, inner distal angle with a stout spine having a double row of teeth and a trigger like spinule; ventral side with a sout , curved and barbed distal spine. Inferior border of dactylus irregularly spiny, unguis and secondary unguis slightly falcate. Peraeopods 2 and 3  without spines on propodus. Peraeopods 4-7 having the same structure, prominently hairy and successively increasing in length. Seventh peaeopod with basis longer than ischium upper part of carpus with 4 stout barbed spines and the uppper part with a transverse row of 10 similar spines. Dactylus with a spiny lower border.

Pleopods 1-3 subsimilar. Peduncle with 3 coupling hooks. Expod of third pleopod without even a trace of a division. Endopod of pleopods 4 and 5 with transverse folds that of fourth with a blunt apical lobe directed inwards , without setae. Exopod of fifth plopod 2 segmented basal segment with 1 and distal segment with 3 spiny thickened areas.

Outer border of exopod of uropod cut into 4 or 5 teeth in addition to the tooth like apex.

Body dirty brown or grey, border of segments lighter, with scattered chrome yellow patches.

Diagnostic Characteristics:

Typical isopods apex of epistome subtruncate; Seventh peraeon segment with 4 tubercles with setal tufts; Four prominent tubercles with setal tufts on telson arranged in a transverse row; Apex ofd teslson angular

Sex Attributes: Embryo and larval development completed within an internal brood pouch.

Feeding Habit: Browsing on Wood Suface

Main Food:  Primary film and algal growth on wood surface, especially green algae.

Additional remarks:

S. terebans is highly gregorious and hence is predominent and destructive borer in the backwaters and mangroves along indian coasts. With heavy attack the intredial portion of jetty piles will assume an - hour glass shape due to locallised destruction. It infests live mangrove vegetation in very high intensity.

S. Terebans is a continous breeder and occurs on various timber structures in Kochi Harbour area almost throught the year, But incidents on fresh panels takes place during september to may. Abscence of fresh water attack during may to august may be due to strong tidal current and high turbidity, which inhibit the movements of young ones.

Studies on slinity tolerence indicated that S. Terebrans can withstand wide range of salinity from as high as 50% to as low as .5% for over 16 days. Boring activity increased towards the monsoon season and high salinity seems to inhibit their tunneling activity. Within the safety of the burrow in moist wood, this borer can toleratearial exposure for several days.

A just released juvenile matures and produes a new brood in 6 moinths Embryomic and larval developmentare completed in an internalk brood pouch. The fertilised eggs hatch out in about 17 to 20 days.
