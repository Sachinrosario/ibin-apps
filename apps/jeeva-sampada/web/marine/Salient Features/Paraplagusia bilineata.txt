SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	

Body flat and elongate, with dorsal and anal fins joined to caudal fin.  Eyes on left side of body, with a scaly space between them; snout rounded, rostral hook long and reaching beyond lower eye; corner of mouth not reaching beyond lower eye, nearer to gill opening than to tip of snout; mouth fringed with tentacles. Two lateral lines on eyed side of body but none on blind side.  Scales ctenoid (rough to touch) on both sides of body

Colour: eyed side brown sometimes spotted or marbled with darker patches, blind side tinged with yellow.
