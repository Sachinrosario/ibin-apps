Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Arborescent, large colonies 30 to 40 cm in total height.  Main branches stout, 3 to 4 cm thick at the base, repeatedly dividing, top most branches 1 to 1.5 cm thick, 4 to 7 cm long.  Apices obtuse or only slightly tapering.  Axial corallites very thick, average 4 mm in diameter, but may be only 3 mm.  Wall porous, costulate, aperture rounded or oval.  Septa in two complete cycle.  Radial corallites variable in size and shape.  They are much crowded, half-tubular, labellate and subimmersed, ascending or a little spreading sometimes the outer lips at right angles.  Larger radials 2.5 to 3 mm long, 1.5 to 2.5 mm thick at the lips.  Shorter half-tubular and labellate ones 1.5 to 2 mm long and broad.  Two cycles of septa are prominent.  Wall moderately porous in young, getting dense in older ones.  A few larger corallites are proliferous.  Surface reticulate, spongy with weak echinulations.  Living corals at southeast India brown with white tips.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  The species formed once large thickets in Gulf of Mannar.  At present not very common.  The axial and radial corallites have similarity to A. humilis but differs in its arborescent corallum.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
