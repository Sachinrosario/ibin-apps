SALIENT FEATURES:

Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive, or expanding and attached.  The corallites are cerioid, polygonal, 4 to 5.5 mm in length.  Septa mostly 24 to 26, rarely upto 32.  No columella.  In one of the specimens (X2: 551-14) the calices at the underside are oval, or rounded, with the intercorallite areas thickened upto 2 mm.  Septa not confluent form calyx to calyx, stop at the top of the theca.  These corallites resemble those figured by VAUGHAN (1918) in his plate 58.


Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

IMPORTANCE: Reef builder.  Source of CaCO3  Curio.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
