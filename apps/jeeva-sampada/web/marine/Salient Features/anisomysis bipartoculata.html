Diagnostic characteristics:
Carapace large, much wider than abdomen, anterior margin broadly triangular with rounded apex. Eyes large, cornea divided into two unequal pigmentary parts. Antennal scale six times as long as broad and over reaching antennular peduncle. Second segment of mandibular palp without protuberances. Carpopropodus of thoracic endopods three to eight two segmented. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male reaching middle of outer uropod; armature as usual for the genus. Telson slightly narrowed beyond basal one third, apex broadly rounded and distal three fifth of margin armed with 20-24 short spines, apical spines with pair of blunt processes at base.
