SALIENT FEATURES   

 

Description: Colony flabellate, 6.7 cm in height and 3 cm in spread. Calyces are mainly lateral and their distance between them is 2 to 4.5 mm.  The individual calyces vary in shape from sub conical to nearly tubular. Some are dome-shaped, in complete retraction of polyps. A typical one measures 1.2 mm in height and 1.5 mm in diameter. The polyps are completely retractile. The acorn-shaped operculum rising well above it. The spindles do not cover the tentacles and the operculum flaps leaving a rosette-shaped bare space when viewed from above.

Spicules: The spicules are in the shape of stars and multiradiate forms and resemble greatly a collar-button.

 

Colour : Colony is light brown  in colour, the axis white and the

                        spicules colourless.
