Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum shrubby arborescent, with numerous branchlets all around the branches bearing numerous proliferations.  Main branches stoutm about 2 cm thick at the base, branchlets tapering to a large axial corallite.  Axials 3.5 to 4.5 mm thick, about 3 to 5 mm exsert; wall thick and porous, outer side costulate.  Opening rounded, 1 mm diameter with two cycles of well developed septa.  Radials both proliferous and non-proliferous the former upto 1 cm length with two to three smaller buds.  Non-proliferous corallites tubo-nariform, appressed, ascending with the inner wall less developed than the outer.  Radials 2 to 3 mm long with mostly rounded apertures.  Smaller radials found mixed along with a few immersed radials.  Septa six in radials.  Outer wall reticualte and echinulate.

Living coral brown with white tips.  The species is rare.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
