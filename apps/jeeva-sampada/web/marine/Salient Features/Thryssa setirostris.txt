Morphological:   
 Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body fusiform, moderately compressed; belly with a sharp keel of scutes from isthmus (between gill openings on underside of head) to anus. Tip of snout at level of eye centre; maxilla very long, reaching to base of pelvic fin and beyond; gillrakers 10 to 12on lower limb of first gill arch. Anal fin moderately long, usually with 32 to 35 branched rays.
	Colour; 
back blue/green or brown, flanks silvery; dark venulose area behind gill opening. Dorsal and caudal fine faint yellow, with dusky margins.
