SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological: Diagnostic characteristics: Diagnosis:     Frontal plate angular at the anteromedian part.  Thoracic limbs three to eight with six carpopodal segments.  Fourth pleopod of male long reaching beyond tip of telson, endopod very small, only half length of first segment of exopod;   third segment of exopod with two spines inner shorter and barbed, outer long and spirally twisted.  Telson with four lateral spines, narrowed part of telson one third total length armed with 50-55 teeth.
