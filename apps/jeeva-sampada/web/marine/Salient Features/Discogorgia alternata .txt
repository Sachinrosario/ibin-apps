SALIENT FEATURES		
Description	:	Colony flabellate, 16 cm in height and 14 cm in width. The stem is 6 mm in diameter near its base. The main stem gives off irregularly alternate branches until it attains a height of 6 cm. The calyces are distributed evenly over the stem and branches. The individual calyces are low truncated cones, 1 mm in height and 2 mm in diameter at the base. Their walls are filled with the overlapping jagged edges of flat, disc-like plates and the margin is as surrounded by points from similar spicules. The calyces are acorn-shaped tentacular portions of the polyps resting on the well-marked collaret. The opercular covering of each tentacular consist of two long bent spindles divergent at their proximal ends and convergent at their distal ends.		
		Spicules: The spicules are of different forms such as, foliaceous discs with branched and tuberculate scales (Fig. b). The coenenchyma are filled with warty, branched discs-like spindles (Fig.a). 

Colour		:	Colony is white, stem and branches grayish  in colour and the 
			spicules are colourless.
