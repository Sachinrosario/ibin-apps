SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting. Zoarium forms a unilaminar flat, glistening white encrustation over the substratum. Zooecia arranged in longitudinal rows, separated by thin mural rims which are denticulate. Zooecia elliptical, but often vary from normalcy. Gymnocyst wanting. Cryptocyst well developed, extending moderately, laterally and distally. Proximal region of the cryptocyst extend into the aperture as a flat, some what coarse, horizontal lamina, raised at the distal end and provided with spines and spinules. Two comparatively long spines occupy the latero-distal corners of the horizontal lamina. Two small spines are present on the lateral side of the aperture, at the position of the cardelles. Opesia partly trifoliate, the opesiules small, their proximal and lateral sides bordered by the cryptocyst with spinules. Avicularia and ovicells not noticed.Diagnostic characters:
Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite
Descriptive characters: Osburn has stated that "this species appear to be rather close to C. levinseni (Harmer, 1926) but that species is evidently larger (Harmer merely states zooecia large) and appears to lack the horizontal spinules of the process and the opesiular borders" (Osburn, 1950). It seems probable that the spinules on the horizontal lamina and the mural rim are very characteristic of this species. The length of the zooecia ranges from 420-520ì and width from 310-375 ì. “Subglobular tubercles" at the proximal corner of the specimens may or may not be present.  Meristic characteristics:
