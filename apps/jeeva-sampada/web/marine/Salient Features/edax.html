SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallets paddle-shaped, ovately broad with convex outer and concave inner face, stalk short extending through the length of the blade, outer distal end of the blade has an irregular thumb nail-like depression, which in some cases get filled with calcareous deposits and may became indistinct, periostracal cover light-brownish in hue.

  Shell moderately large, anterior slope sculptured with widely spaced finely denticulated ridges, its posterior part by closely spaced and coarsely denticulated ridges, dorsal knob large, shelf well developed in older specimens, posterior slope small.

Main food: Cellulose materials supplemented by plankton.

Feeding type: Cellulose. It is capable of feeding and surviving on plankton alone.

Additional remarks:
This species is common to occur throughout the Indo- Pacific. It occurs in marine conditions in estuarine situations as well as in the mangroves. According to Rayner (1974) “ It is well adapted to a diet of both wood and phytoplankton having well developed gills, food grooves and labial palps as well as a large caecum. Turner (1966) believes that the species may be able to obtain enough nutrition from plankton alone to survive as specimens have been found with their tubes completely calcified at the boring end even through more wood was available. N. edax is reported to be abundant in the mangroves of the Godavari.
