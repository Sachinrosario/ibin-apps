Descriptive characters:  Corallum composed of stout palmate branches 15 to 20 cm in height.  Verrucae all over the branches.  Verrucae more or less uniform in length and thickness, 3 to 5 cm high and thick top rounded.  Calices about 1 mm in diameter, circular, moderately deep with two well developed cycles of septa.  Columella styliform.  Surface coenenchyme spiny.

Remarks:  This species differs from P. verrucosa in having very well developed two cycles of septa and a prominent styliform columella.

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
