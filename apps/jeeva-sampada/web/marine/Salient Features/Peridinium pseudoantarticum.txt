SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Large species, rhomboidal, girdle descending in shape. Cingular much inclined. Sculpture alveolar with strong modules at the notch of the reticulae.

Diagnostic characteristics: The alveolar sculpture often becomes very vague when perforations are the only visible things. Lists of cingulars well developed.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 142-145µ, Diameter: 89-92µ.
