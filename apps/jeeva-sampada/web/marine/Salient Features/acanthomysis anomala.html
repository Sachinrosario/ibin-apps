Diagnostic characteristics:
Carapace produced into short apically rounded rostrum. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Thoracic endopods and fourth pleopod of male as for the genus. Telson elongate triangular, proximal part of lateral border with one to two pairs of spines, distal part with fifteen spines increasing in length distalwards, but some spines short at irregular intervals, distal border of telson with two pairs of spines, outer pair stout and twice as long as inner. Endopod of uropod with two to three spines below statocyst.
