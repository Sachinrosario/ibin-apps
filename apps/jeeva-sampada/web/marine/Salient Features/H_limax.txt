Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum disc-shaped, elongated, thick or this, extremities, tapering or rounded.  An axial fossa extends the entire length of the corallum or stops short of ends.  The fossa is broken up into several secondary calices by the fusion of septa from either side, with or without a columella.  On the disc secondary calices are present, which look like as if formed by the breaking up of septa.  Septa alternating in thickness, edges minutely dentate, sides granular.  Aboral side perforate.  Costae thin supplied with small spines, may or may not extend to the centre of the disc.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
