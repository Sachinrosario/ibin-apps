Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics : 
									Ref. No. 3
Body elongate  and tubular; head and caudal  peduncle somewhat  depressed. Several rows of teeth visible in both jaws even when mouth is closed; 2  series of  teeth present on palate  ( roof  of mouth ), outer series in 2  rows anteriorly;  vomer  usually toothless.  Dorsal fin rays  usually 12 ( 11 to 12 ), longest  dorsal ray about  3 ½    times as long as last rays;  pectoral fin rays 14 or 15, longest ray nearly as longest pelvic ray and extending to about dorsal fin origin;  Pelvic fin  ray and extending to about dorsal fin origin; pelvic fin rays  sub equal in length. Pectoral  fin long and pointed. Lateral line scales about 45 to  52;  3 ½ to 4 ½  scale  rows above lateral line.  

Colour :

Back and sides brown, belly pale or silvery. Eight to 10 bars often visible along lateral line. Distal  parts of dorsal, pectoral and particularly  caudal fin dusky. 
Second dorsal fin ray and upper caudal fin ray with a series of about 8 dark spots; fins otherwise without markings.

Main food: 

Stomach  contents  of S.undosquamis  observed   from  Mumbai  waters contained teleost fishes as the major food items (68 %)  followed by cephalopods (27%)  and crustaceans  (3.5%). And in Karnataka Fishes (Stolephorus spp. Saurida spp. Nemipterus  Spp. platycephalus, Leioghanthus and  Cynoglossus )  followed by cephalopods  (Loligo ) and crustaceans ( Parapeneopsis stylifera, Soloenocera sp ) were the major food items.

Feeding type: Cannibalistic,  Carnivorous
