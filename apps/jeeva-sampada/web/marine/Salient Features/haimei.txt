Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum caespitose, tufted arborescent or prostrate.  Axial corallites cylindrical 2-3 mm thick and long (Klunzinger, 1879).  Two cycles of septa usually visible.  Tips of branches usually tapering.  Radials tubular, inner wall thinner than the outer, they are placed at different angles to the branch suface upto 900.  Both cycles of septa are well developed.  Intermixed are found shorter and subimmersed corallites at the lower part of the branches.

The branches are 1 to 2 cm thick and branchlets 8-10 mm thick.

Living coral pale brown.  It is found common in Palk Bay.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
