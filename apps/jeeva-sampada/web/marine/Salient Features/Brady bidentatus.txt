SALIENT FEATURES:Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:       

Female: Cephalothorax, 2.06 mm; urosome, 0.95 mm. The cephalothorax ovate. The prosome separates from the 1st thoracic segment; the 4th thoracic segment fused with the 5th. The last thoracic segment asymmetrical; the right side is more elongated and extends to the distal margin of the genital segment; the lateral distal margin of the segment obliquely truncate. The rostrum short and pointed.      

The urosome 4-jointed, the segments and furca are in the proportional lengths as 38:15:13:19:15=100. The genital segment longer than wide; the lateral margins of the segment parallel. The furcal rami about 1,3 times as wide as long.     

The 1st antenna 24-jointed, extends about to the middle of the last thoracic segment.      

Male: Cephalothorax, 2.00 mm; urosome, 0.88 mm. The last thoracic segment symmetrical. The lateral corner of the last thoracic segment symmetrical, rounded with a minute process on the posterior margin.      

In the 5th pair of legs the distal segment of the left leg swollen near the inner proximal margin and has a tooth-like process on the distal end; a curved claw arises from the outer distal margin of the segment which is about half the length of the segment. In the right leg the 3rd segment has a characteristic club-shaped process on the inner proximal margin.

Additional remarks: The form described and figured by Giesbrecht under the name bidentatus  slightly differs from that described by A. Scott in the proportional length and in the shape of the last pedigerous segment. According to A. Scott Giesbrecht’s specimen is identical with Phyllopus giesbrechti A. Scott taken from the Malay Archipelago. The male 5th pair of legs of bidentatus resembles that of impar but differs from the latter in having a small distal tooth and a short curved claw on the distal segment of the 5th leg.
