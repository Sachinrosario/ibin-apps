SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Epitheca and hypotheca cone shaped. Girdle sinistral, end displaced 1-2 furrow width. Sulcal lists terminate as teeth in the inner angle of the posterior horns.

Diagnostic characteristics: Surface finely reticulate with thin shell.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 110-120µ. Breadth: 75-80µ.
