SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small cells, reniform in lateral view with anterior cingular funnel; circular cingular chamber appear fused to the prominent left sulcal list.

Diagnostic characteristics: Left sulcal list sac likes and directed dorsally; sub ovoid U-shaped space midpoint to ventral on posterior margin of cell body without surface reticulations and rib spurs.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.
