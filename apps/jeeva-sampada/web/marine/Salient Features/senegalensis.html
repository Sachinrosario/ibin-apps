SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallets heavy and almost entirely of calcareous material with a thin film of periostracum adhering at the outer face, blade elongately oval in outline with a short stalk. Poaterior slope (auricle) of the valves large and usually flaring. In the specimen of the present collection the posterior slope is small.

Feeding habit: Wood borer.

Main food: Cellulose in wood.

Additional remarks:
    This is reported as a variable species with striking differences occurring with age. Monod (1952) discussed and illustrated the variation occurring in the pallets of senegalensis and showed that petitii Recluz is the young form. Rancruel has since shown that specimens growing in areas of low salinity and in uncrowded conditions remain the petitii form throughout life. Thus it appears that petitii is a young and ecologic form of senegalensis Blainville (Turner, 1966).
