Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum elongated, disc-shaped, sometimes underside slightly arched.  Axial fossa short with a trabecular columella.  Septa unequal in height at the margin of the disc, edges wavy, finely dentate, sides granular.  Tentacular lobes not prominent sometimes poorly developed.  Costae low, subequal, extend to the centre of the disc, covered with small granular.  Disc perforated at the periphery.

The species belongs to Pleuractis.  It differs from F. scutaria mainly in the absence of tentacular lobes.  Further the corallum is more elongated and flattened.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
