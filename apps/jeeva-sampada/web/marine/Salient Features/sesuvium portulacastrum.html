Salient Features :
Herbaceous, stem prostrate, succulent, much branched, red glabrous.
Leaves opposite, linear, fleshy, glabrous, petiole short, much dilated.
Flowers axillaries, solitary, pedicels about equaling the calyx.
Ovary usually 3-5 celled.
Fruits dru scarious, seeds numerous, black, glabrous, smooth.

Health:
