SALIENT FEATURES:         Ref No: 52, 64, 103, 197

 

Morphological: Body densely pubescent. Rostrum reaching to distal half of second antennular    article, generally strongly upcurved in females, straight in males, armed with 8 to 10 (rarely 8) dorsal teeth; hepatic and cervical grooves discernible; longitudinal suture short; pterygostomian angle bluntly pointed; mid-dorsal crest on last 4 abdominal segments; telson armed with 4 pairs of minute lateral, movable spines, the penultimate pair the largest; epipod present on first 3 pereopods; 5th  pereopod reaching only to 3rd antennular article (or nearly so). Petasma with long, horn-like distolateral projections directed laterally, their tips slightly curving forward; distomedian projections small, curved ventrally. Anterior plate of thelycum with strongly raised anterior and lateral margins, distally pointed; posterior plate also with strongly raised lateral margins, the whole making a widely concave armchair-like structure.

 

Diagnostic characteristics: Epipods present on 1st to 3rd pereopods; petasma with long, horn-like distolateral projections directed laterally, their tips slightly curving forward; anterior plate of thelycum with strongly raised anterior and lateral margins, distally pointed, posterior plate also with strongly raised lateral margins, the whole making a widely concave armchair-like structure.

 

 Colour: Body yellowish white to pale pink with dark yellow patches; uropods yellowish  with grey or dark-brown on middle and margins.

 

 

Size and age:

   Maximum length(cm)(male/female/unsexed): Males 5.5; Females 8.8
