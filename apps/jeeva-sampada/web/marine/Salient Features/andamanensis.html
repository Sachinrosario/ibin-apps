SALIENT FEATURES:

Flagellum of second antenna with 4 articles. Epipod of maxilliped slightly over reaches the articulation of the palp of the endopod, it is two and a half to three times longer than broad and is broadest behind the apex. Mandibular palp 3-segmented. Dorsal surface of pleotelson with prominent tubercles; fifth pleon segment with two sub median curved ridges. The lateral crests are well raised, with a row of thick long hairs. Lacinioid seta has the apex pointed and smooth and curved in the form of a beak. The epipod of the uropod is slightly longer than the peduncle, the exopod unguiform, about as long as the endopod.

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi, wood.

Distribution:
India; Chatham Islands (Andamans) (Rao & Ganapati 1969).

Remarks:
  With two pairs of sub-median tubercles on the dorsal surface of the pleotelson, the species resembles L. (L.) quadripunctata but differs markedly in lacking the X shaped carina on the fifth pleon segment, spike-like bristles on the dorsal surface of the posterior margin of the pleotelson and in the nature of the epipod of the maxilliped.

In the general shape of the body, the general structure of the mouth parts especially that of the maxillipeds and even in the finer structure of the peraeopods L. andamanensis closely resembles L. pfefferi. However, both forms can be easily distinguished form one another by the nature of the ornamentation of the fifth pleon segment and the pleotelson.

Size and age: 3-5mm long.
