SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Small to medium sized cell. In lateral view, body almost round with concave ventral and dorsal edges.

Diagnostic characteristics: The left sulcal list extends from greater than one-half to almost three-fourths the length of the hypotheca.

Feeding habit: Phagocytic species, feeding on the loricated and non-loricated ciliates by piercing the prey cell with a feeding tube (Hensen, 1991, Inove et. al. 1993).

Size and age: Length: 42-50mm, dorso ventral depth: 36-43mm.
