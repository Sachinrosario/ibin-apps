
SALIENT FEATURES:								Ref No:224

Morphological: Body predominantly glabrous; rostrum armed with 8 to 9 teeth on dorsal margin, 
straight and with a small crest, extending slightly beyond tip of antennular peduncle; postrostral carina reaching near posterior margin of carapace; adrostral carina ending near epigastric, sulcus extending to middle of carapace; branchiocardiac carina reaching posterior extension of hepatic spine, sulcus distinct; mid-dorsal carination on abdomen commencing from 4th somite; telson with very minute dorsolateral spinules; ischial spine present on 1st pereopod; merus of 5th pereopod in adult males with a shallow notch and feeble tooth. Distomedian lobes of petasma transversely placed with proximal end narrow and distal end broad. In females, anterior plate of thelycum tongue shaped and wider posteriorly; posterior plate concave, glabrous and transversely cut into two unequal segments.


Diagnostic characteristics:   Body predominantly glabrous; mid-dorsal carination on abdomen 
commencing from 4th somite; ischial spine present on 1st pereopod; merus of 5th pereopod in adult males with a shallow notch and feeble tooth; posterior plate of thelycum in female concave, glabrous and transversely cut into two unequal segments with no clusters of setae between them.


        Colour:  Body light brown with speckling of minute greenish blue spots on abdomen; tips 
          of  uropods greenish blue.


Sex attributes:
