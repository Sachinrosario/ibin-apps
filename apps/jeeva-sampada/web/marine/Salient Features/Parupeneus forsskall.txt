Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body moderately elongate, the depth 3.6 to 3.7 times in standard length. Chin with 2 very  slender barbells which usually extend slight posterior to rear margin of preopercle, their length 1.55 to 1.7 times in head; snout moderately long its  length 1.6 to 1.8 times in head; mouth small, the maxilla extending only slightly more than half distance to front edge of eye; a single row of well-spaced stout conical teeth in jaws ; no teeth on roof of mouth; gillrakers on first arch 26 to 32. First dorsal fin with 8 spines, the first small, the third longest contained 1.5 to 1.7 times in head; last dorsal end anal rays prolonged, 1.2 to 1.3 times longer than preceding rays; pectoral fin rays 15 or 16.  . Lateral line scales 27 to 28; 3 vertical rows of scales in space between dorsal fins; 9 or 10 vertical rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour  : 

a black stripe from front of snout through eye, along upper side of body, ending below rear base of second dorsal fin; a round black spot about as large as eye on posterior third of caudal  peduncle above and adjacent to later line; head an body above stripe yellow white caudal fin yellow longitudinally streaked with pale blue; second dorsal and anal fins pale yellowish, narrowly and faintly striped with pale blue.
