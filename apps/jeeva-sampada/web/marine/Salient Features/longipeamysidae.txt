SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics: 
Genus : Spelaeomysis  Caroli      Body depressed.  Penultimate segment of thorax with forwardly directed lobe.  Eyes reduced to single or pair of plates without visual elements or with few ommatidia. Antennular flagella longer or shorter than body.  Mandibles with well developed incisor and molar processes;  palp large and with pectinate spines on outer margin of second and third segments.  Maxillule with two segmented reflexed palp bearing long distal seta. Maxilla with inner margin of endopod expanded.       
Endopod of first thoracic limb with well-developed endites, exopod reduced to small unsegmented plate, epipod large.  Endopod of second thoracic limb stout, exopod long and segmented. Thoracic limbs 3-8 subequal or eight very long.  Pleopods similar in both sexes, increasing in size from first to to fifth, biramous with one segmented exopod.  Chitinous lamella present on posteroventral margins of abdominal segments three to five.  Telson triangular or linguuiform with partly or fully spiny border, distal border with or without pair of setae.  Uropod with backward prolongation of sympod armed with spines, exopod with lateral suture, endopod without statocyst.Spelaeomysis longipes.     
Anterior border of carapace convex. Eyes reduced to single rectangular plate with out visual elements.  Thoracic endopods 3-5 of male with pectinate setae on segment three to five.  Eighth thoracic endopod much longer than seventh.  Telson short, linguiform lateral borders armed through out with thirteen spines, apex with long spine flanked by pair of plumose setae.  Exopod of uropod with ten spines on outer border of basal segment.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     
Length : 5.6 mm.
