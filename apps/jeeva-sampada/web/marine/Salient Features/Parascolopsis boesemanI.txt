SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   

Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body moderately deep (depth in front of dorsal fin about 2.8 times in standard length), with a convex dorsal profile and short snout. Eye large, suborbital margin finely serrate; opercle with a broad membranous margin; gillrakers on 1 st arch 9 or 10.  Dorsal fin with 10 spines and 9 soft rays, continuous, unnotched; anal fin with 3 spines (the 2 nd  and 3rd  about same size) and 7 soft  rays ; pectoral fins with 2 unbranched and 14 branched  rays. Predorsal scales (on top of head ), reaching forward to level of poterior nostrills; 5 transverse rows of scales on check; lower limb of preopercle unscaled.

Colour : 

Overall rosy-yellow; 4 light rosy saddles on body, of which 1 is located on peduncle; a blood-red blotch on dorsal fin between 7th and 10th spines(faded in preserved specimens); 1 light greenish-orange longitudinal stripes below lateral line; caudal and dorsal fins silvery-yellow.
