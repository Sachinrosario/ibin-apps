SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Solitary or loose chains. Adjacent cells touch each other by the processes. Cells straight. Richelia intracellularis, a blue green alga often found inside the cell.

Diagnostic characteristics: Bands distinct with tapering ends. Valves convex or rounded. External process tube long and curved.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extra cellular organic acids are glycolate.
