SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Large drum shaped cells with slightly convex valves, thin walled and hyaline. Slender areolation with well-differentiated rosette of large meshes. Surrounding at the centre and 12 in 10m near the margin.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Chamber openings indistinct. Radial and secondary series regular.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 210-420m.
