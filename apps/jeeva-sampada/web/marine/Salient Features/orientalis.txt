Salient features:
Morphological:

Body slender, head very short, almost as long as broad, rostrum prominent, reaching slightly beyond the equally prominent lateral corners. Peraeon segments one and two comparitively narrow and long, subequal in length; segments three to seven broader but considerably shorter than second, slightly longer than the last two peraeon segments combined; segments subequal in length and breadth.

First antenna with peduncle three jointed, flagellum six jointed but differentiation into peduncle and flagellum indistinct. Second antenna with peduncle four jointed and stout, fourth segment almost twice as long as broad; flagellum six to seven jointed and clearly demarvated from the peduncle, segments one and two long; secondary flagellum two to five jointed, distal segment of peduncle with two long feathery setae; very much resembling the secondary flagellum but unsegmented.

Mouth parts are described by Barnard for X. brevitelson.

First peraeopod robust and subchelate, fifth segment apicallyblunt, its lower distal border forming rounded lobes, sixth segment elongate ovate, with two prominent blunt teeth on lower border; dactylus long, reaching a little short of the tip of fifth segment. Second peraeopod subequal to first in size, subchelate, sixth segment apically narrow, its lower border somewhat concave and excavate, dactylus stout unguis reaching the basal prolongation of sixth segment. Third peraeopod seubequal to second, fourth and fifth segments produced into triangular processes with setae, sixth segment oval, dactylus elongate triangular. Peraeopods four to seven short and slender, segments four and five with an inner pointed spine, fifth segment slightly underriding sixth, sixth triangular and seventh almost as long as sixth.

Pleopods similar. Telson narrow, very shortone and a half times as long as broad and half as long as endopod of uropod, distal border subtruncate, with four setae, middle pair of setae longer. Endopod of uropod longer but narrower than exopod, with a median inner construction, carrying four stiff spine setae. Exopod broad and lamellar, completely folding over telson and overlapping each other in the median line; distal part of rami with long setae.

Colour of body in spirit light brown.
Size and Age: Length: 3.1 mm.
