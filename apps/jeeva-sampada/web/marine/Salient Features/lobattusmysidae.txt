SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Body stout and hispid. Carapace large, anterior margin produced in front  into broad triangular rostral plate, apex pointed and bent downwards like hook. In female narrower and more produced. First segment of antennular peduncle in male with large mid dorsal hairy lobe extending to middle of third segment. Third segment of mandibular plap in male with peculiarly modified terminal and sub-terminal setae. Second thoracic endopod in male with large second segment, fifth segment internally produced into oblong rounded process. In female second thoracic endopod normal. Third to eighth thoracic endopods similar in form. Exopod of third pleopod in male fourteen segmented , ninth, eleventh and twelfth segments externally produced into lobes, twelfth segment as long as preceding three segments combined together, inner distal angle with one long spine and one mid dorsal spine; thirteenth segment short with one thick granulated spine on outer distal corner and one slender spine on inner distal corner; fourteenth segment terminating in two long and one short stout spines. Lateral margin of telson except one fifth of basal part armed with spines. Endopod of uropod over reaching telson, longer than exopod. Spines on telson and inner uropod with secondary spinules.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Adult male : 8-8.2 mm
Adult female: 5.7-6.3 mm
