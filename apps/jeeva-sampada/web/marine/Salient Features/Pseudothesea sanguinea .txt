SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony flabellate, 5 cm. in height, and with a spread of 3.6 cm. The main stem straight and without branches pointing 1.7 cm high from its base. The calyces are rather evenly distributed on all sides of the branches, that they are more closely approximated on the distal parts of the colony. They are 1 mm. to 1.5-mm apart. The individual calyces are very low verrucae or very short cylinders. Their margins are 1.1 mm. in diameter, and are armed with a distinct circlet of blunt but prominent points. The calyx walls are so low that this crown of points is about all that rises above the level of the coenechyma. The polyps are completely retractile, and when retracted are disc-shaped the shallowness of the calyces. The operculum and collaret are not well developed.
		Spicules: The spicules are unilateral with jagged points larger and stouter (Fig.b). The spindles are in the shape croses, stars and triradiate forms (Fig a). 

Colour		:	Colony is light brown  in colour, the axis white and the 
			spicules colourless.
