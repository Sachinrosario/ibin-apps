SALIENT FEATURES:

Description	:	Colonies subflabellate, branches divide dichotomously; 
diameter of branches may vary from 3-4 mm.  Calyces evenly distributed on all sides
 or may be irregular, spiral or in rows at places; they may be conical or truncate with 
a height of 1.5 mm and diameter of 2 mm at the base.  Older parts may be devoid of calyces.
  Polyps retractile.
	
	Cortex thick and granular; very thin in older parts.  Spicules are : 
(1) Spindles.  Size up to 0.084 X 0.04 mm (Fig. a).  (2) Dumbbells. Size, 
0.063 X 0.04 mm (Fig. b) (3) Crosses (Fig. c).
	
Colour	:	Colony yellow, axis pale white spindles are light yellow and dumbbells
 colourless.


Size attained	:	Only bits could be collected.
