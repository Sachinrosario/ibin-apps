
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics: 
           
Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Line of fusion in both cases visible. Postero-lateral corners of the last pedigerous segment broadly rounded. Rostrum deeply notched and filaments are shorter than the notch. Abdomen 4 – segmented. Genital segment not swollen below. First antenna 23- segmented, extends up to the tip of the last pedigerous segment. Fifth leg 1- jointed, plate-like. Apical spine and inner marginal spine are short and stout, inner marginal spine the largest.        

Male: In general appearance like the female. In 5th leg right endopod is very short, terminal segment of the exopod is slightly shorter than the 1st joint of the exopod, 2nd and 3rd segments of the exopod with fine hairs.  

Maximum length (cm) (male/ female/ unsexed)                                               Female:1.35 mm
