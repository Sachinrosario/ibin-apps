SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	The height of the specimen is one meter and 5.9 cm in The main stem and branches are strongly compressed laterally, but the smaller banes are round. The nodes are indicated externally by annular swellings, but they are almost obliterated internally in the larger branches. The polyps are thickly distributed on three sides of the branches. The branching is usually dichotomous, but sometimes lateral.
		Spicules: The spicules are mostly small oval spindles with proportionally large densely crowded verrucae. The hard internodes are made up of an agglutinated mass of rod-like spicules. The coenenchyma contains a number of warty clubs, and there are also wary spindles, probably from the polyps.


Colour		:	The entire colony is dark brick red and axis is darker in colour
