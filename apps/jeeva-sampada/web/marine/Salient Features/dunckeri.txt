SALIENT FEATURES:

Genus Gastrosaccus Norman

Diagnosis : Carapace forming a small triangular rostrum, hind border deeply emarginate, with filaments of   reflected lappets  or entire.  Eyes small.  Antennal scale short, outer margin terminating in a spine.  Labrum anteriorly produced into a spine.  Thoracic endopods three to eight with several carpopropodal segments.  First abdominal segment of female with expanded pleural plates.  Pleopod one in female  uniramous biramous,  remaining pleopods uniramous, short and unjointed. Pleopods in male biramous, exopod of third very long.  Telson long, with armed apical cleft, lateral border throughout armed.  Exopod of uropod externally armed with spines, inner border of endopod with sharp spines.Diagnostic characteristics:   Gastrosaccus dunckeri               

General form slender. Posterior border of carapace with pair of large forwardly directed lobes variable in size and shape. Endopod of third pleopod of male 8 – 9 segmented; exopod four segmented, fourth segment with two stout spines, one longer and with few strong barbs. First pleopod in female uniramous Telson with 13 – 21 irregularly long and short spines with long gap between first two and last two spines, last lateral spine stouter and longer; lateral margin with definite constriction beyond penultimate spine; telsonic sinus wide with 15 pairs of closely packed spines. Endopod of uropod with 19 – 22 spines in male and 15 – 19 spines in female.

Additional remarks:  According to Pillai, in specimens collected from the inshore waters of the Kerala coast, the spines arming the telsonic sinus are very long and the distal ones are bent towards the median line and he has stated that this peculiarity has never been observed in offshore specimens.  This is not true in the case of specimens studied by Panampunnayil.  According the Panampunnayil in specimens collected from the shallow coastal waters of Maharashtra and South Gujarat the spines in the telsonic sinus are straight and not bent inwards.
