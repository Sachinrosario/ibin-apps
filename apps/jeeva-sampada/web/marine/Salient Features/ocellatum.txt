Salient Features:
Morphological:

Generic diagnosis: Platybranchiate sphaeromatid with body deeply valuated, not depressed. Cephalosome not set posteriorly into first pereonite. Coxal plates of pereonites 2-7 seperated from tergites by suture. All coxal plates directed ventrally not expanded. Pleonal tergite with two minute, posteriorly plated incisions. Lateral margins of pleonal tergite entire, unbroken by sutures or incisions. Pleotelson with rounded apex lacking groove, notch or projection. Both uropodal rami unreduced and separate. Pereopods all ambulatory, increasingin size posteriorly. Penes separate to base. All pleopodal rami lacking complete articulations. Mouthparts of ovigerous female unmetam0orphosed. Marsupial lamellae absent, broad carried in pouch formed by two opposing ventral pockets.

Adult Female
Sexual differentation, other than primary sexual characteristics, not marked. Females smaller than males and with more slender pereopods. Pleopod 2 as in male but without appendix masculina and with internal margin of endopod straight(Fig. 3C). In ovigerous females, the brood is incubated in a chamber formed by opposing pockets; one anterior with its posteriorly directed mouth situated between the third pereopoods, and one posterior with its anteriorly directed mouth situated between the fourth pereopods. Average brood size appeats to be only five embryos.

Sex Attributes: Average Five embryos in one breed.

Feeding Habit: Herbivorous and probably omnivorous diet.

Main Food: Organic particles deposited on the upper parts of the beach by waves.

Additional Remarks:
Holotype and Allotype are deposited in british museum of natural history.
This species is very abundant along Goa coast and densites from 440 to 4760 individuals per sq. m. have been recorded.

Size and Age:  Maximum Size Recorded Adult Male: 3.12mm. Ovigerous Female: 2.34mm.
