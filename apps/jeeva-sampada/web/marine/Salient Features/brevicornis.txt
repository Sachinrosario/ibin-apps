SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:    Rostrum falls short of the eye; no dorsal tooth; antennal and pterygostomian spines present; also a pair of small supraorbital spines  and a hepatic spine which is not present in earlier stages, present. Dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 5-6; lateral spines absent on seg.5 and 6; a prominent ventromedian spine present on the 6th ab.seg.; pleopods uniramous; telson with 7+7 spines; median deep cleft present.

Sex attributes: Separate, (Heterosexual).

Descriptive characters: All species of the Genus Metapenaeus are having 7+7 spinesin the telson; smaller in size;  very few spines on carapace.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  pelagic zone.Main food:  plankton, detritus with  sand and mud,algae, small crustaceans etc.

Feeding type: omnivorous.

Additonal remarks: Mostly found more abundant in the northern region, both on the west and east coasts.

Size and age:  Nauplii:-0.27mm-0.39mmProtozoeae: 0.86-1.46mm .Mysis: 1.97mm; 

Maximum length (cm)  (male/ female/ unsexed)   125mm 

Longevity (y) (wild) : 2-3 years.                       

Eggs and larvae: 
Eggs: Oval and irregular; becomes spherical; measure 0.28-0.3mm in diameter; yolk mass alone  is 0.20-0.21mm.

Nauplii I-VI: a size of 0.27-0.39mm; all stages bear the 3 pairs of cephalic appendages; naupliar eye present; antennule uniramous and A2 and Md. biramous; each ramus of mandible bears 3 long setae distally; furcal setae 1+1.Setae become plumose in 2nd stage; the 4th seta of A2 is bifurcate at the tip; the no. of setae on the telsonic furca increases as the stage advances,7+7 setae in Nauplius VI. 

Protozoea I: size 0.86mm. no rostrum; eyes sessile; mandible asymmetrical; telson with 7+7 setae.

Protozoea II: size 1.14mm; rostrum short,pointed and eyes stalked; minute supraorbital spines present; antennule distal segment carrying 1 aesthetes and 4 setae, of which one is long; abdomen 6-segmented; telson not demarcated,,carries 7+7 setae.

Protozoea III: thoracic legs as biramous buds; dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments1-5; 5th segment also with a pair of postero-lateral spines; telson separated from abdomen; caudal ramus with 7 setae; uropods well developed with setae.

Mysis I : size 1.97mm; rostrum without tooth; falls short of eye; antennal and pterygostomian  spines well developed; small,distinct supraorbitals present; small dorsomedian spines present on segments 5-6; also a pair of ventromedian on the 6th somite; pleopods not appear; telson cleft deep and 7+7 setae present.

Characteristics: The presence of supraorbital spines  seem to be characteristic of Metapenaeus brevicornis.

Abundance: the larvae are more abundant in the northern region of Indian Ocean,both in the west and east coasts.
