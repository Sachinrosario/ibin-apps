SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony branching in a plane. The polyps are widespread and cylindrical and about 2.25 mm in height and 1 mm broad. The coenenchyma is filled with irregularly arranged spindles. The operculum consists of 2-3 transversal rows of spindles, on which 8 pairs of converging ones rise. The tentacle tissues are up to 0.2 mm long. The thick bark contains 1.8 mm long irregularly arranged spindles. 
		
Spicules: The coenenchyma and calyces are filled with heavy spindles that are verrucae and not in definite whorls.  

Colour	:	Colony is whitish, axis brown at the base and golden yellow at the branches.
