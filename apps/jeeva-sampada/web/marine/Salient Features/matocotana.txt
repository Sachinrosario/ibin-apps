SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Shell globular, thin, anterior slope broad, bearing stout denticles, umbonal-ventral sulcus rough marked by transverse wrinkles, disc about as wide as umbonal-ventral sulcus but may be completely eroded, posterior slope small or absent probably by erosion and when present separated from the disc by a constriction, apophysis long and twisted extending from the under side of the strong umbonal knob.
	
	Pallets paddle-shaped with blade wider than long, the calcareous portion of the blade composed of a broad basal cup into which is inserted an inner second medially divided cup. This resembles the horn portion of the hoofs of a sheep. The stalk is long and rough.
Soft parts: 
		Sipon separate, gills broad and flat, branchial groove well developed, labial palps not free, stomach globular, caecum small, intestine looping over style-sac, anal canal closed, heart anterior with lightly pigmented auricles, heart/body length 0.37, gill/body length 0.46, visceral mass/body length 0.37, heart/gill length 0.8 (Turner, 1966).

Feeding habit: Wood into which it bores. 

Main food: Cellulose supplemented by plankton.
