SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells globulas, apical pare on a small prominense. Girdle central, not displaced slightly indented, provided with conspicuous lists strengthened by minute spines.

Diagnostic characteristics: Sulcus not reaching to the centre of the hypotheca, provided with a conspicuous wing like list on the left.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameters : 22-28µ.
