SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1
	
Body fusiform, moderately deep, compressed; belly with a distinct keel of scutes. Mouth small, 2nd supramaxilla rectangular;  gill opening smoothly rounded. Dorsal fin at about mid-point of body; pelvic fins below dorsal fin origin, 7 pelvic fin rays; anal fin short, its origin behind dorsal fin base.

Colour :

 back pale grey, a double pigment line along back, from head to caudal fin base; flanks with a diffuse silver band, belly pale.
