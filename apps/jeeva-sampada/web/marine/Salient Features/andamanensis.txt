SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:     Postlarva I. 17 specimens were available.Length ranges from 8.4 -9.5mm. Rostrum bears 3 dorsal teeth + epigastric tooth. Hepatic spine present;pterygostomian reduced with no distinct spine.The denticulate nature of the anteroinferior margin of carapace persists. Mandibular palp 2-segmented,bearing a terminal seta. First 3 pairs of thoracic legs chelate; all of them having exopods with setae. Dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments slightly reduced; segment 5 with a pair of small dorsolateral spines.Pleopods uniramous,exopods bearing setae. Telson becomes narrow and the median cleft disappear; a median spine present. The spine formula becomes 7+1+7.

Diagnostic characters: rostrum comparatively short with a dorsal crest.Supraorbital spine prominent,hepatic small and a pterygostomian spine  present. Antennal angle is angular,no spine; dorsolateral spines on 5th & 6th ab.segs.present.  Abdominal pleura1-5 serrate and 1st 3 pointed.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: pelagic zone Main food:  diatoms,copepods,debris and other excrement removed from water.

Feeding type: omnivorous.Additonal remarks: The larvae and postlarvae are caught from same areas from where the adults of this species are commonly caught. 31 specimens were collected from 26 stations which itself shows its less abundance,but wide distribution.

Size and age: Postlarva I. A length of  8.4mm to 9.5mm

Mysis I.   5.6mm to 7.3mm

Mysis II. 7.4mm to 8.2mm .

Longevity (y) (wild) : 1 ½ - 3 years.      

Eggs and larvae:                                                                                            

Eggs not known.

Larvalstages: 

Mysis I. a length of 5.6mm to 7.3 mm. Rostrum with 1+1 teeth on a crest,tip pointed and downcurved. Supraorbital,hepatic and pterygostomian spines present. Antero-inferior and posteroinferior  margins of carapace serrate. Antennular peduncle 3-segmented with 2 unjointed flagella. Antennal endopod unsegmented and scale with inner marginal setae and terminal spine not present. Mandibular palp short and unsegmented. Maxillipeds and pereopods have segmented endopods and setose exopods; legs 1-3 developing chelae.Dorsomedian spines present on abdominal segments 3-6, those on 3&6 the longest.5th abdominal segment has a pair of dorsolateral spines. Pleura of abdominal segments 1-4 serrate; pleopods appear as small buds. Telson with a deep,median cleft with no median spine and 7 pairs of spines.

Mysis II. A length of 7.4mm to 8.2mm. Rostrum has an additional tooth and 3 ventral setae. Supraorbital and antennal spines reducing while hepatic becomes more prominent. Mandibular palp still unsegmented and longer. Antennal scale with a long terminal spine on its outer margin. Dorsomedian spines on abdominal segments remain same; legs with exopods and pleopods uniramous. Telson cleft slightly reduced and  a short median spine is implanted in it. The spine formula being 7+1+7.

Characteristics:Abundance: there may be a potential fishery for M.andamanensis on the  west coast of India (Kurien and Sebstian 1976).
