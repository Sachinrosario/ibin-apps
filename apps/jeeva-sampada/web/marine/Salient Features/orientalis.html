Diagnostic characteristics:
  Body fusiform, completely covered with fine scales posterior to the corselet, caudal keels naked. Jaw teeth conical; palatines with a strong row of teeth, no teeth on vomer and tongue. Two dorsal fins, almost contiguous. Inter-pelvic process with double flap. Prominent keel between two smaller keels on each side of caudal peduncle. Swim bladder absent. Severel (5-10) stripes along the back, ranging from horizontal to oblique in orientation.

Meristic characters:

Two dorsal fins, First dorsal fin 17-19 spines, dorsal fin lets 7-9 and anal finlets 6-7. Pectoral with 23-25 soft rays., Gill rakers 8-13 on first gill arch.
Inter-pelvic process paired; body covered with minute scales, anterior corselet with modified scales; keel on each side of the caudal peduncle between two smaller keels.
