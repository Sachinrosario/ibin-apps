Descriptive characters:  Corallum arborescent with a broad base.  Branches tangled, coalascent with tufted or dendroid excrescenses.  Branches thin 6 to 8 mm thick upto 7 mm long, branches 3 to 4 mm in diameter.  Corallites circular or slightly oval, 2 mm in diameter.  Distance between corallites 3-5 mm.  Septa ten, equal, prominent, strongly exsert, vertically descending from the top of the corallite wall.  Septa unite with the prominent styliform columella.  Interseptal loculli clear and distinct.  Coenenchyme solid with prominent spinnules.

Remarks:  This is the first published record of this species; though Pillai has listed it as Madracis sp.  The identification is rather tentative.  The species reported from Gulf of Mannar can very well be a hitherto undescribed species.  Needs more study.  It is a rare and deep water form.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.
