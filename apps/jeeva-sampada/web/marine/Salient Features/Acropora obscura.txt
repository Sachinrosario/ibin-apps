Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum prostrate, main branches 1 to 1.5 cm thick, branchlets on both upper and lower sides of main branches.  Lower branchlets pressed to the general plane of the main divisions; 2-4 cm long and 4-8 mm thick.  Upper branchlets single or in groups of 3 to 4; three to 5 cm long, 6 to 12 mm thick, apices blunt.  Axials 2.5 to 3 mm thick, 1-2 mm exsert with 12 septa.  Prominent radials nariform, ascending, 2 to 3 mm long, 2 mm wide, larger ones proliferous.  The nariform corallites are confined to the upper parts of the branchlets, lower down they are all immersed.  First cycle of septa well developed in the radials second cycle poorly developed.  Wall of the radials reticulate and echinulate.  Surface coenenchyme strongly echinulate.

Living coral dull brown with pink axial corallites.

To the date only a single specimen, other than, the type of Brook from Rameswarm is collected by the present author from Plak Bay Mandapam.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
