SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells Compressed in the vertical axis and twisted obliquely, hence left side of the hypotheca larger than the right side and longer than the left. Apex quite excentric vertically shifted. Anterior intercalaries very dissimilar, the first small, the second very large. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Girdle circular excavated, with winged lists. Left side of sulcus wall with wing. Lower end demarcated by the sulcus.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 36-60µ; Diameter: 45-75µ.
