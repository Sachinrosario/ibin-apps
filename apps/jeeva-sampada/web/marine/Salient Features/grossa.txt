SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological: 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Body clumsy. Carapace large, frontal plate in female large, anteriorly produced into triangular acuminated rostral part; in male proportionately smaller, rostral plate more acuminated with lateral margin more concave. Second thoracic endopod in male with long second segment, fifth segment considerably widened and internally produced into long oblong apically rounded process; sixth segment broad. In female second thoracic endopod normal. Pseudobranchial lamellae of male bilobbed, outer lobe very large. Exopod of third male pleopod complicate with several spines, some developed in most aberrant way, terminal processs bifurcate near base with one ramii short, other extremely long and curved; last two segments with extremely thick, distally hook shaped body along outer lower side. Endopod of uropod over reaching telson, longer than exopod.

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)

Adult male : 9.2 mm
Female       : 6.5-7 mm
