Salient Features:

Body elongate oblong, sides parellel,peraeon segments subequal,each, except the first, with a transverse ridge. The ridges on segments 2-4 indistinct, on fifth very prominent, those on sixth and seventh broken up into 4 pairs of transversely elongated tuberceles, tuberceles on seventh segment more prominent, submedian pair larger.

Compositre pleon with a proximal sutureextending its entire width, lateral parts with 2 oblique sutures : a pair of large submedian tuberceles and sometimes a smaller lateral pair also present.

Telson almost one and a half times as broad as long, distal border semicircular Dorsal side proximally bulged and with 2 pairs of submedian tuberceles followed by a median tubercle and flanked by 3 tuberceles falling into a longitudanal row on either side. The median tubercle  generally with 3 small tubercles on either side. In large specimens the telsonic border is proximally ridge like and distally crenulate and slightly curved upwards.

First antenna with first pedencular segment stout, Its surface scaly; second segment rounded, with parellel grooves, third slightly shorter than the first 2 combined; all the segments with the sesory setae. Flagellum 9-10 segmented, first segment longest. Second antenna comparitively long, first pedencular segment stout, fifth longest, anterior border of all segments hairy flagellum 17 segmented. 
Epistome slightly longer than broad, embarasing more than half opf the upper lip, apex triangular and acute.

Mandible with a broad blunt incisor process, indistinctively bidenate, molar stout, middle spines 8 on the right and 6 on the left. First segment of palp long and hairy, second subcylindrical and with 16 pectinate setae, third elongate triangular, with a row of 18 pectinate setae increasing in length distalwards. 

First maxilla with outer lobe very much hairy, the spine of the outer surface barbed on one side, distal border with 11 stong spines, of which outer are 4-5 are stout, blunt and without barbs,middle 5 with barbs on the inner side, the 2 inner spines barbed on both sides. Inner lobe with a patch of hairs on the inner border, distal border with 3 stout setae, each with long stiff hairs and stout barbs and 2-4 simple setae.
Inner lobe of the second maxilla rectangular or quadrate, quite different from that of the S. terebans and S. walkeri; middle and outer lobes elongate triangular; exposed surface of all the 3 lobes with short stiff hairs; outer border of outer lobe with long hairs. The setae bordering the lobes as in S. terebans.

Maxpilled stout, sympod short and stout, inner plate apically very narrow, particularly when viewed under a cover glass. Palp stout, segments very slightly lobed lower border of third segment sparsely hairy, forth segment comparitively broad, fifth long. The whole appendage more hairy than that of s. terebans.

First peraeopod with basis as long as ischium, proximal part of lower border and the whole of the upper border hairy, ischium about 4 times as long as broad, merus half the length of ischium , carpus triangular propodus broadest in the middle the lower distal spine stout with a few large subsidiary spines. Ventral spine large and barbed. Dactylus with,lower border spiny, unguis nearly straight. Seventh paraepod with the upper distal part of merus produced and carrying a row of 4 large spines; all the spines with strong barbs. Propodus long and slendor, dactylus short, with a slightly curved unguis. The border of all the segments, except the distal half of propodus with a thick matting of hairs.

Pleopods as in S. terebans. Endopod of forth with a very prominent apical lobe carrying a single apical seta. Exopod of fifth pleopod with 5 spiny thickenings that on proximal segment with a long seta. Second pleopod of male with the stylet inserted slightly below the base of the   endopod, regularly narrowing to the rounded apex,borders  spiny. Penial process almost hemispherical, placed close together.

Diagonistic characteristics:

Typical isopodes epistome longer than broad,seventh peraeon segment with transverse elongated. Tiberceles; middle part of telson with five prominent tubercles arranged in the form of a 'v' submedian pair of tubercules on telson similar distal border of telson rounded, but not raised 3 small tubercles as a lateral longitudanal row on the telson on either side.

Sex attributes:

Embryomc and larvel development completed within an internal brood pouch.

Feeding Habit:  Browsing on wood surface.

Main food:  Primary film and algal growth on wood surface.

Additional remarks:  Together with s. Terebans, S. Annandalei also brings about extensive damage to under water timber structures in various estuaries along the indian coasts. They are abundantly found in  mangrove habitat also, where they destroy live vegetation.
S. Annandalei is also present in timber structures throught the year, But like S. terbans fresh attacks is restricted to october to may panels exposed in kochi waters from may to september arte totaly devoid of this borer. This species also can withstand aerial exposure for several days within the moist wood.

Size and Age:  Maximum sixe recorded : 14.7 mm
