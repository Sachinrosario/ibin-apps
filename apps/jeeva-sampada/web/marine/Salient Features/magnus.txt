SALIENT  FEATURES :

Morphological:                                 
Diagnostic characteristics:     

FEMALE:  forma major      
The proportional lengths of the cephalothorax and abdomen are as 76 to 24.      
The prosome is somewhat bluntly rounded and is provided with a linear crest.  The posterior thoracic margin is produced backwards in a wing, the apex of which is still further produced in a narrow rounded prominence.     The 1st segment of antenna 1 bears a transerverse row of delicate spinules near the distal margin.The proximal segments from 3 to 14 or 15 are slightly dilated, the chitin is thickened along both anterior and posterior borders; from segment 16 to the end chitin appears to be thickened along lines running parallel to the margins.     In the swimming legs 2, 3 and 4 in addition to the groups of large spinules on the posterior aspect of the several segments of the rami, there are patches of very small spinules on the anterior aspect of the limbs, and also on the 2nd basal segment in the 3rd and 4th legs.      The 5th leg agrees with the description of previous authors.
