SALIENT FEATURES	

Description	:	Colony  flabellate, 15 mm in height and a spread of 20 mm. The main stem arises from a disc-like base and contains one short, unbranched internode of 1.2 mm in length. The other internodes bear branches, except the distal one, which is very slender and short. These internodes are 3.5 mm and 2.9 mm long.  All branches arise form the calcareous internodes. The axis is faintly grooved, hollow and the polyps are biserial, alternate and 2 mm apart. The individual calyces are club-shaped, directed outward basally, and curving upward, or towards the branch termination, distally measuring 1.6 mm in height and 1 mm in diameter.  The calyx walls are covered with numerous small scales transversely disposed but in numerous rows both vertical and transverse.
		Spicules: The spicules are narrow scales with curved and ctenate borders. 

Colour	:	The colony is white.
