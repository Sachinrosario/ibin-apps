SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:        Body shape and prosome – urosome ratio in both sexes as in Acartia southwelli. Posterior corners of pediger 5 round, this and urosome somites without ornamentation. Proportion of urosome segments  - female: 46:19:13:22 = 100, male: 11:29:23:8:12:17 = 100. Details of mouthparts and legs 1-4 as in Acarti southwelli except that cutting edge of mandible differs.      

Apart from the total length and the structure of the mandible gnathobase, the new species differs from A. southwelli in some details of the female and male fifth legs. Female: basis swollen medially, terminal segment smoothly drawn out without suture at midlength. Male: inner lobe of right second exopod segment more rounded, outer process of the bifurcated left terminal segment less produced and the spine flanking the membrneous sheath only slightly curved compared with A. southwelli.

Size and age: Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)Total length 

Male: 0.78 to 0.83 mm (av. 0.80 mm)                    Female: 0.85 to 0.91 mm (av. 0.89 mm)
