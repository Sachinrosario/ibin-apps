Diagnostic characteristics:
Carapace without nodules and produced in front into short triangular rostrum with bluntly pointed apex. Eyes large. Antennal scale narrowly oval, four times as long as broad, over reaching antennular peduncle. Telson as long as last abdominal segment, apex quadrate with four pairs of long stout spines, inner pair nearly one third length of telson, lateral margin armed with ten spines, distributed along whole border, distally more crowded. Endopod of uropod with comb of ten spines in region of statocyst.
