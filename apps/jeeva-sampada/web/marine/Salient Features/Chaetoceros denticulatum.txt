SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cylindrical cells forming long chains with vertically rhombic small apertures. Base of setae directed almost vertically with a small tooth on the inner side. Setae spinous and striated.

Diagnostic characteristics: A very small spine present at the centre of the valve.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis 21-32µ.

Biochemical aspects: Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
