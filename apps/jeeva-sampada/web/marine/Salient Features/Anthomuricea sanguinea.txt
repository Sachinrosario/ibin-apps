SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony flabellate with a height of 8 cm., and a diameter of 4.9 cm main stem is straight, the first branch being given off 1.8 cm. from the base. A number of lateral branches are given off irregularly. The calyces are regularly alternate, and are inclined toward the front and to the distal ends of the branches, where pair of large calyces form a forked termination axis ending in a point between the calyces. The distance between the calyces is regularly 2.4 mm. The individual calyces are truly cylindrical in shape, sometimes attaining a height 2 mm. Their diameter is about 2 mm. The walls are filled with slender spindles which vertically disposed, but tend to the en chevron. The polyps are completely retractile.
		 Spicules: These are nearly all very slender spindles, often curving or sinuous, and sometimes attaining a length of 2.2 mm. Some of these are the most slender tuberculate spindles (Fig. a & b). 

Colour	:	The color of the colony and of the spicules is a deep crimson.
