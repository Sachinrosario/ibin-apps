SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Very much flattened in the apical and Antapical regions. Neck very long, about 12 to 15µ and inserted abruptly on the apical region. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Two Antapical spines, scarcely winged are about 15µ.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Transverse diameter: 50µ, Longitudinal diameter: 25µ.
