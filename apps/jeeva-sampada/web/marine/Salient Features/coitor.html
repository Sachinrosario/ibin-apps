SALIENT FEATURES:            Ref.No. 1
Morphological: Fairly small species.


Diagnostic characteristics : swollen snout projecting well beyond lower jaw; mouth inferior; upper jaw reaching to below middle of eye; no barbell on chin; Teeth differentiated into large and small in upper jaw, not, or but little, in lower jaw, the large forming outer series in upper jaw; no canine teeth. Lower gill rakers 10 to 13, with one or two minute stumps occasionally in front. Swim bladder hammer shaped, with 11 to 13 pair of arborescent appendages. the first entering head. Dorsal fin with 10 spines, followed by a low notch, second part of the fin with one spin and 26 to 28 soft rays; pectoral fin moderate, ¾ of head length . Anal fin with 2 spines and 7 to 8 soft rays, the second spine strong, caudal fin rhomboid or rounded at tip. Scales cycloid on operculam and lower parts of head, elsewhere ctenoid ; lateral line scales reaching to tip of caudal fin.

Colour : light golden yellow with a light purple / blue sheen; spinous part of dorsal fin with dusky to black border; soft part of dorsal, anal and caudal fins with a dull green / grey border.
