SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	 Colony flabellate, 9.8 cm in height and with a spread of 9.6 cm. The calyces are conical in shape when lateral and tubular when terminal. The former measure about 1.3 mm in height and 1.7 mm in diameter at the base. They attain a length of 2.8 mm and a diameter of 1.5 mm. The calyx walls are filled with regular spindles irregularly placed. The polyps are retractile and are armed with vertical spindles. The collaret is formed of a single circlet of spindles, while the operculum is in the form of an eight-pointed star. It is composed of relatively heavy spindles arranged en chevron on the basal parts of the tentacles.
		
Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma are stout and tuberculated

Colour	:	Colony is dark scarlet in colour  and the spicules are colourless.
