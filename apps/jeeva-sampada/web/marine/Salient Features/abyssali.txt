
SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      

Female: Prosome and 1st, 4th and 5th pedigerous segments fused. Posterolateral corners of the last pedigerous segment broadly rounded. Abdomen 4- segmented. Genital segment not swollen below. First antenna 22- segmented, reaches up to the caudal furca. Fifth leg single jointed, plate- like. Apical spine and inner marginal spine short and stout, of the two inner marginal spines is the largest.

Maximum length (cm) (male/ female/ unsexed)                                               Female:   1.8  mm.                                                        
Ref. No.:     T.C.Gopalakrishnan, 1982
