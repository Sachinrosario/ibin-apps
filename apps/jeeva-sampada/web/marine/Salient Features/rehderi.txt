SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallets spoon shaped with a broadly oval blade and short stalk. Outer face of the blade ribbed with radiating longitudinal rows of ridges and troughs. These ribs protrude slightly at the distal end of the blade, which is entirely of calcareous material with no trace of periostracum. Valves small and nearly resemble those of the genus Teredora.

Main food: Cellulose of wood.

Feeding type: Supplemented by plankton.

Additional remarks:
	This species invades fishing craft off the coast of Madras. It is a pelagic species commonly found in driftwood and very rarely in fixed wooden objects.
