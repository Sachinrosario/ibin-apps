SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Linear valves gradually attenuate to the rounded ends. Transapical striae robust, 9 in 10µ. Cell wall porous and appear as small openings.
 
Diagnostic characteristics: Valves with three longitudinal ribs. Outer membrane finely areolate-punctate. Between two ribs the double series of areolae.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: 140-300µ long and 18-20µ broad.

Biochemical aspects: Extra cellular carbohydrates are polysaccharides. Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides.
