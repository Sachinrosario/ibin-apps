SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells globular to lens shaped. Girdle median, not displaced. Lists without spines. Sulcus not reaching to antapical end, with large winged list on the left.

Diagnostic characteristics: Apical plate with a more or less diamond shaped first apical which touches the first and seventh precingulars and the second and fourth apicals.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Diameter: 40-90µ.
