SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:      Carapace anteriorly produced into apically rounded conical rostrum, posterior border with inwardly directed short lobes. Third pleopod of male with four-jointed exopod, fourth segment apically armed with one barbed stout spine, one stout smooth spine, one stumpy process and one knob-like process. Fourth and fifth pleopod with single jointed endopod and four jointed exopod. Lateral margin of telson with 8-9 stout spines, terminal spine longer than subterminal spine with bases closely adjacent to each other, cleft with 10-11 spinules on each margin. Endopod of uropod with five widely spaced stout spines. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)     

Adult male    : 5.6 - 6 mm     
Adult female : 6 - 6.5 mm
