MALE
  BODY ELONGATED , TWICE AS LONG AS BROAD . HEAD SMALL, DISTINCT FROM THORAX EYES EXTREMELY REDUCED . ANTENNULES THREE SEGMENTED. THORACIC SOMITES DISTINCT WITH DEEPLY NOTCHED LATERAL MARGINS , FIRST TWO PAIRS OF LATERAL MARGINS BEING DIRECTED ANTERIORALY, WHILE THE OUTER ONES EXTENDS LATERALLY . ABDOMINAL SOMITES COMPLETELY FUSED TO FORM A SUB TRIANGULAR STRUCTURE NO TRACE OF PLEOPODS OR UROPODS.
