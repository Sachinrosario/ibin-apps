SALIENT FEATURES:           Ref No: 103, 184.

 

Morphological:  Rostrum horizontal or slightly ascendant, reaching to about distal margin of eye,

armed with 6 to 8 dorsal teeth; postrostral crest elevated and laminose, reaching posterior margin of carapace and interrupted by a notch just in front of  cervical groove; cervical groove deep, reaching to or almost to dorsal midline; postorbital and suprahepatic spines present; branchiostegal and pterygostomian spines absent; hepatic crest curved ventrally on its anterior part, with a sharp bending near its anterior end; branchiocardiac sulcus very distinct  and L-shaped with one of its limbs almost parallel with postrostral carina and the other (anterior one) turning ventrally at right angle; telson with a pair of fixed distal lateral spines (trifurcate); 5th pereopod with a coxal spine.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics:  Rostrum reaching to about distal margin of eye, postrostral crest

elevated and laminose, suprahepatic spine present, branchiocardiac sulcus very distinct and L-shaped, telson trifurcate, 5th pereopod with a coxal spine.

 

Colour:    Body light pink, antennular flagella banded red and white.

 

 

Sex attributes:

       Descriptive characters:   In male, the ventromedian lobule of petasma projects beyond tips of

dorsomedian and dorsolateral lobules, armed antero-laterally with 20-28 spinules, ventral costa with strongly recurved distal part bearing 16-25 short spines. Appendix masculina elongate with rounded apex. In female, posterior part of last thoracic sternite distinctly quadrangular, anterior part with two pairs of setose protuberances.
