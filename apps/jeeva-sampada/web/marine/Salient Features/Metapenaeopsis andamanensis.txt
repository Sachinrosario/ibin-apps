Morphological:     Rostrum extending to or surpassing tip of antennular peduncle and armed dorsally with 7-9 teeth; carapace with well defined antennal, hepatic and pterygostomian spines, no stridulating organ;3rd to 6th abdominal somites strongly carinated, 4th to 6th sometimes with blunt subcarinae ; telson slightly shorter than inner uropod, terminating in sharp spine and armed with 3 pairs of movable and a distal pair of fixed spines; lower antennular flagella 0.8 to 1.1 times length of peduncle , stylocerite extending slightly beyond eye; 3rd maxilliped with a well developed basial spine, 1st pereopod with basial and ischial spines, 2nd pereopod with a small basial spine. Petasma asymmetrical, about ½ as long as carapace. Thelycum with the median plate extending posteriorly and occupying about half of that plate, with a shallow longitudinal depression in central region; its posterior margin free and recurved with angular lateral corners.
