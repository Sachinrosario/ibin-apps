SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	Colony flabellate measuring 8 cm in height. The main stem branches 3 cm above its base. The individual calyces are verruciform or dome-shaped, 1.5 mm high and 2 mm broad on the bases. The polyps are entirely retractile, the margin closing in around them so as to have but a small aperture. The calyx walls are covered with small oval scales or plates, which have a darker centre and a lighter margin. They are imbricated near the margins. The operculum is of the same type as in the last species, and the collaret is well marked where it divides into two approximately equal parts.
		Spicules: The spicules are thick warty disc having star-shaped, multiradiate or cruciform spicules and are also dumb-bell-shaped. 

Colour		:	Colony is dark purplish brown, with a vinaceous tinge and the 
			spicules colourless.
