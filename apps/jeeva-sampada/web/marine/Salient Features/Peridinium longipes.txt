SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Long neck and large symmetrical Antapical curved spines. Fins divergent and well developed. Well-marked dorso-ventral flattening. Apical horn conical, truncated with slight median dilation and variable apical dilations.

Diagnostic characteristics: The epitheca and hypotheca form a rather abrupt angle when they meet.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 81-104µ, width: 38-41.5µ.
