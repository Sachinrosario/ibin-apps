SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Wading birds of small size; wings pointed, squinty tail. Bill along and slightly curved upwards.



Diagnostic characteristics: In flight the broad black band across end of white tail, and a white bound along the trailing end of wings are diagnostic.


Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters: Sexes are almost alike.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit: Keeps probing the mud flats with its slightly up curved bill.

Main Food: Worms, mollusks, crabs and insects.

Feeding type: Suspension/impingement feeder: Impingement feeder

Additional remarks: Nesting habits unknows.
