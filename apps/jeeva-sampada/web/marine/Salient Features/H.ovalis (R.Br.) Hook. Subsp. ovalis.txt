SALIENT FEATURES OF Halophila ovalis (R.Br.) Hook.f. subsp. ovalis    

Leaves: Paired at each node with long petiole; a leaf with petiole may measure upto 4 - 12 cm

Inflorescence: Flowers solitary, axillary, covered by two spathesPerianth: Tepals 3, broad elliptic

Ovary: Ellipsoid in shape, 1-celled with pointed apex

Fruit: Ovoid to ellipsoid with 18-27 seeds

Seeds: Globose, white in colour when young but brown when mature

Flowering and fruiting: Throughout the year


Salinity:
	Less tolerant to hyper salinity and more tolerant only to lower salinity


Commercial Use:            
	Occasionally plants are used as manure in coconut and other plantations            
	Local fishermen in Cuddalore O.T. apply the leaf paste mixed with turmeric to cure various skin diseases, burns, boils, etc.


Reports on bioactivity: 
	Antibacterial activity (Balasubramanian et al., 2000)
