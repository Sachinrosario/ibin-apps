SALIENT FEATURES:
Morphological: Encrusting zoarium distinctly brownish in hue. Zooecia more or less hexagonal with conspicuous mural rims. Cryptocyst strongly tuberculated, extends well up to the distal end of the front. Opesia trifoliate with more or less distinct opesiules. Large and symmetrical avicularia. Distinct rostrum wanting. Cordiform mandible with lateral membranous expansions extending nearly three-fourth of the mandibular rachis. Rostrum with extensive cryptocyst, denticulate posteriorly. Ovicells not noticed.Diagnostic characters:   Sex attributes: Hermaphrodite  Descriptive characters:   These specimens more or less resemble the figures and description of Smittipora abyssicola given by Harmer (1926). The differences noticed are in the nature of the cryptocyst which in the present form is nearly concave and lacks the angular depression. The avicularia occur in large numbers and the cryptocyst of the avicularian rostrum is with denticles only on the proximal half. Further, the shape of the lateral membranous portion of the avicularian mandible is not the same as that shown by Harmer. The irregular teeth sculpturing the cutting edge of the rachis of the mandibles are absent in the present material. It is assumed that these differences are just ecotypical in character and are therefore, not of much importance from a specific stand point.
