SALIENT FEATURES OF Thalassia hemprichii (Ehrenb.) Asch. 

Leaves: 3-7 in each shoot, measuring up to 15.5 cm in length and 1.2 cm in width, leaf blade linear, sometimes leaf tips show some serration

Inflorescence: Flowers single, covered by spathe

Perianth: Tepals 3, elliptic in shape

Fruit: Globose, rough coated, showing 3 distinct ridges, bursting into 8-13 irregular valves

Seeds: Globose, white in colour when young but brown when mature

Flowering and fruiting: Throughout the year


Commercial Use:     
	Nutrition: Hypotensive diuretic (H.S. Garg, 1993)


Reports on bioactivity: 
	Antiviral activity (Naqvi et al., 1980)
