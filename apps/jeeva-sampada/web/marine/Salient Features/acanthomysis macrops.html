Morphological:

Diagnostic characteristics:
Body slender and smooth. Carapace anteriorly produced into broad triangular rostral plate. Antennal scale overreaching antennular peduncle. Eyes large, cornea broader than stalk. Thoracic endopods with three carpopropodal segments, dactylus slender with long nail. Exopod of fourth pleopod of male three segmented, last two segments with long barbed seta. Telson with swollen base, constricted at about middle and further linguiform with broadly rounded apex; basal expanded part with five pairs of spines, lateral borders of distal half with ten pairs of spines regularly increasing in length; distal border armed with four to five pairs of large spines regularly decreasing in length towards middle. Uropods clearly overreaching telson, endopod with row of eleven to sixteen spines successively increasing in length.
