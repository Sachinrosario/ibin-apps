Description	:	Colony branches in one plane. The polyps are arranged lateral in position leaving the opposite side completely free. The calyces are short and blunt, 0.5 mm high and they are reinforced with a crown of 4 transverse rows of spicules, on which 2 are converging and the 3rd one is pointed towards the base of the tentacle.
		Spicules: The spicules are long spindles with leaf-like clubs. The spicules of the axis are bent or straight with numerous warts often forked at the ends..
