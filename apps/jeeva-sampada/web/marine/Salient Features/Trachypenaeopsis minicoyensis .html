SALIENT FEATURES:             Ref No: 198

 

Morphological: Rostrum straight, pointed, armed with 6 to 7 dorsal teeth, about ¼ length of

carapace, tip slightly raised above dorsal profile, reaching about middle of 1st segment of antennular peduncle; adrostral carina short, reaching level of epigastric tooth; cervical groove well defined; hepatic groove  sigmodial; antennal spine stout; supraorbital and hepatic spines smaller; pterygostomian angle rounded; upper antennular flagellum shorter than lower, and half length of carapace; 1st maxilliped well developed, endopod 5-jointed, epipod elongate and broad unfurcate; abdomen dorsally carinated on 5th and 6th  somites; telson armed with 3 pairs of lateral movable spines, last pair with articulating tips, distal end terminating in a long median spine which is broad at middle. Petasma with 3 pairs of distal projections, the distomedian, distolateral and proximolateral; distomedian and proximolateral projections longer than broad, the former directed anteriorly and the latter diverging laterally; distolateral projections short, tips pointed, about ½ length of proximolateral projections. Thelycum with anterior plate conical, as long as wide at base; lateral plates concave towards inner aspect and with convex outer surface.

 

 

Diagnostic characteristics: First maxilliped well developed with endites and unfurcate epipod;

telson armed with 3 pairs of lateral movable spines, last pair with articulating tips; petasma with 3 pairs of distal projections, distolateral projections between distomedian and proximolateral ones shortest with pointed tips; thelycum with anterior plate conical and as long as wide at base

 

Colour: Body cream coloured.
