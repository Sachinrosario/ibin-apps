SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells forming long, straight chains with apertures of varying sizes. Setae springing from the corners with a very short basal part.

Diagnostic characteristics: Terminal setae thicker and shorter than the others. Setae four sided, punctate areolate, punctae of neighbouring faces alternating with each other's.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes water dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Apical axis cells 16-58µ in lengths.

Biochemical aspects: Phototroph, which requires vitamin B12 (Mammalian type). Extracellular nitrogenous substances are amino acids and peptides. Extracellular organic acids are glycolate.
