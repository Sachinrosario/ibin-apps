SALIENT FEATURES:

Flagellum of second antenna with four articles. Epipod of maxilliped just reaching the articulation of the first article of palp with endopod, epipod spatulate, widest near middle, two and three times along as wide. Mandibular palp with three articles. Fifth somite of pleon with a conspicuous hump on midline near distal border. In females the hump is markedly reduced to only a mid-longitudinal carina. Pleotelson lacking ornamentation but the lateral crests of the male particularly are much elevated and extend outwards over the cup-shaped part of the pleotelson. Posterior margin of pleotelson tuberculate, spike-like setae lacking. Lacinioid setae of right mandible with two apical spines (Menzies, 1957).

Main food: Plankton, detritus, fungi, wood.

Distribution:
South Pacific Ocean (Menzies, 1957), Chatham Islands (Andamans) (Ganapati and Rao 1960).

Remarks:
  The peduncle of the uropods of this species is markedly compressed and tuberculations are spine-like. This characteristic and the cup-like nature of the pleotelson distinguish, this species from all others known to date.

Size and age: 3-5mm long.
