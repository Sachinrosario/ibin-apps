SALIENT FEATURES

Description	:	Colony  flabellate, 23.5 cm in height. The stem and branches are flattened. The main stem having cross section of 6mm X 4 mm. 1.3 cm above its proximal end it gives off a simple undivided branchlet 14.8 cm long and 1.3 cm higher it bifurcates. All the stem and branches are transversed by distinct median grooves on one side and most of them on two opposite sides. The calyces are in lateral in position, in two or three interrupted rows on each side of the branch.
		The individual calyces are completely included, leaving little indication of their presence except in the very fine 8-rayed slits surrounding the calyx mouth. These slits are like sharp cuts radiating from the center. The polyps are completely retractile, very small, and the dorsal surfaces of the retracted tentacles show a heavy armature f longitudinally placed red spindles showing conspicuously against the yellow substances of the tentacles. 
		
Spicules: The spicules of the axis form an agglutinated mass of tortuous bodies that unite in a sort of network or mesh (Fig.a & g). The coenenchyma is packed with oval or disc-shaped forms which have their surfaces thickly crowded with coarse verrucae (Fig. b, c, e, f & g).

 Colour	:	Colony is a yellowish red or terracotta, mouths of the calyces yellow and the opercular spindles are crimson.
