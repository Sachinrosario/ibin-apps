SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological:

Diagnostic characters: A black blotch on upper part of spinous dorsal, Mouth when  

                                       protruded forms a tube  directed forwards.      

           

Sex attributes: Undistinguishable externally

 

Descriptive characters: Body compressed and deep, anterior part of dorsal profile more strongly convex than the anterior part of ventral profile, dorsal profile with a notch above eye, Supraoccipital concavity twice as long as broad, snout blunt and shorter than eye diameter. Gape of mouth below lower border of eye. Maxilla reaching the front border of eye. Mandibles slightly concave. Interorbital space bounded by two ridges , which continue posteriorly as the outer edges of the nuchal spine. The median portion of the nuchal spine is elevated. Minute villiform teeth in each jaw, in a single row. Lower margin of pre-operculum finely serrrated. Scales small and deciduous, absent on head, but are distinctly present on the chest. Dorsal and anal spines strong and laterally compressed. 3rd and 4th  dorsal spines and 3rd anal spine serrated anteriorly for about half length from base. Lateral line prominent and convex from the beginning , but less convex than the dorsal profile. It extends beyond the end of the soft dorsal and anal fins, but stops short of the base of the caudal. Ventral fin shorter than the pectoral fin with a very strong spine and an  axillary scale reaching very near the origin of anal fin.

 

Meristic Characters:D.VIII,16; A.III,14; P.18-30; V.1,5; C.24.            Ref no (7,8,18).

 

Feeding Habit:

 

Main food: Detritus and plankton

Feeding type: Mostly bottom feeder on small crustaceans, foraminiferans,  bivalves and  detritus , but also feeds in the column on phytoplankton                

The main food items were found to be

1. Diatoms- Fragilaria, Nitzschia, Coscinodiscus, Pleurosigma

2. Copepods- Forms a main portion of the food in April , July, August

3. Cladocerans-  July – October , with maximum occurrence in August.

4. Polychaetes  - September and October

Stray numbers of decapod larvae, fish eggs and larval bivalves were also recorded in stomach contents.

In juvenile fishes of 3-4 cm length, copepods and phytoplanktonic organisms constitute the main food.
