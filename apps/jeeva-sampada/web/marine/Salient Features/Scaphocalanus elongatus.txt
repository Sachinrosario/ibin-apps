SALIENT  FEATURES :
Morphological:                                 

Diagnostic characteristics:              

The forehead bears a linear crest. The posterior pedigerous margin is produced backwards and ends in a pointed process.  The Prosome and 1st pedigerous segment are fused, as also are the 4th and 5th pedigerous segments. The furcal ramus is about twice as long as wide.     
The 5th pair of legs show certain differences from the usual condition, the terminal segment carries only two spines, one on the inner margin and a second at the distal extremity, and the inner border of the segment is produced in a rounded lobe. 

Maximum length (cm)  (male / female/ unsexed)
Female total length: 2.55 - 2.92 mm
