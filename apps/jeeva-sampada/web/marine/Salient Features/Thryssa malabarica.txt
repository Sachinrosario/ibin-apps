SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body fusiform, rather  strongly compressed; belly with a sharp keel of scutes from isthmus ( between  gill openings on underside of head ) to anus . Tip of snout above level of eye centre; Maxilla short, reaching to gill opening or just beyond ; gillrakers usually 17-19 on lower limb of first  arch  Anal  fin long . usually with 35-38 branched rays.
 
Colour :

Back blue/ green or brown, with no dark median bands or lines, flanks silvery ; a black enulose area behind gill opening; gill arches end inside of gill cover yellow or golden. Dorsal and caudal fins yellow with narrow dusky margins; anal fin deep yellow at base, margin milky white, the two areas separated by a black band
