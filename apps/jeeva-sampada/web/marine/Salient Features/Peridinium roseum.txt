SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Cells rounded to rhombic in ventral view, tapering gradually above to a short apical horn. Girdle right handed. Sulcus broadening below the left margin bearing a small wing, which is not connected with the spines.

Diagnostic characteristics: Two spines without conspicuous wings are situated a short way from the end of the longitudinal furrow.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 50-58µ without spines.
