Diagnostic characteristics:
Rostral prolongation short. Eye stalks fully exposed, corneal portion large. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Middle segment of palp of mandible broad and without dentiicles. Carpopropodus of thoracic enopods three segmented. Fourth pleopod of male reaching tip of telson. Exopod three segmented, third segment with two stiff spines, inner spine slender and naked, outer stout, with bunch of strong barbs in middle. Telson roughly triangular, lateral borders nearly straight but converging distally, distal border truncate with very small median depression and with three pairs of spines on either side, spines successively increasing in length outwards. Less than half of lateral borders armed with nine pairs of sharp spines.
