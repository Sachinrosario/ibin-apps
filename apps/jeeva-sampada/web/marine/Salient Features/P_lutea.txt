Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum massive, surface undulate.  Calices polygonal, shallow, 1 to 1.25 mm in diameter.  Wall thin, zig-zag or straight thickened at the lower parts of the corallum than at the top.  Mural denticles roughly double the number of septa.  The ventral triplet of the septa form a trident by the fusion.  Septa thin, interseptal loculi wider than the septa.  A single denticle between the palus and the wall, which generally stands below the level of the pali.  Two rings of synapticular present the outer apposed to the wall.  Pali eight, those of the lateral pairs of septa larger than others.  Columella a single style, compressed in the plane of the ventral directives, stands below the level of pali of the lateral septa.  Septal ends jointed to the columella by radii.


Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Feeding type:

Additional remarks:  An important reef builder and fairly common.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
