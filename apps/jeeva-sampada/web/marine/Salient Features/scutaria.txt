Sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Corallum highly convex, heavy, rarely young stages flat and thick.  The length of the axials fossa range form one-third to half of the basal diameter.  Septa thin, often wavy, edges with 30 to 40 serrations per cm length.  Tentacular lobes very well developed and amarked feature.  Septal sides granular.  A scar of attachment is visible at the underside in many specimens.  Disc perforated, perforations slit-like, numerous at the periphery.  Costal, spines thin, conical, smooth or with granulations, 10 to 12 per cm length of coastae.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.


Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
