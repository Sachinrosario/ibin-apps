SALIENT FEATURES:						
Morphological:   
 
Diagnostic characteristics : 						Ref. No. :1	
Body moderately elongate, the depth 3.6 to 3.7 times in standard length. Chin with 2 slender barbells which do not reach rear margin of preopercle, their length 1.55 to2.1 times in head; maxilla reaching  to or slightly posterior to front edge of eye; villiform teeth in jaws, on vomer and palatines (roof of mouth); gillrakers on first arch 27 to 31, First dorsal fin with 8 spine, the first spine minute; pectoral rays 15 to 18 (usually 16); pelvic fins about 2/3 length of pectoral fins.  Lateral line scales 33 to 36; 51/2 vertical rows of scales in space between dorsal fins; 12 or 13 vertical rows of scales along upper part of caudal peduncle.

Colour : 

a golden yellow stripe about as broad as pupil passing from eye along upper side of body to upper caudal base; head and body above stripe silvery pink to greenish, silvery white below; barbells whitish to pink ; first dorsal fin with 3 or 4 orangish strips (including tip) second dorsal fin with 2 or 3 orangish strips (including tip), upper lobe of caudal fin with 6 or 7 transverse orangish  bands; power lobe of caudal fin longitudinally streaked with orangish and whitish.
