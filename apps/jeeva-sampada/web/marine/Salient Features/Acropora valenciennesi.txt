sex attributes:

Descriptive characters:  Arborescent with several stout branches arising from a base which undergoes repeated divisions.  Main branches 1.8 cm thick, gradually tapering to a large axial coralite.  Height of the type specimen 18 cm.  Axial corallites 4 to 4.5 mm.  in diameter, 2.5 mm exsert, will thick and porous; septa in two cycles.  Radial corallites tubular and half-tubular spreading, sometimes nearly at right angles.  Larger tubular radials upto 4 mm long 2 mm thick; with shorter ones in between.  Truly immersed corallites are absent.  Some of the larger radials bear buds.  First cycle of the septa well developed in the radials.  Corallum fragile, surface spongy-reticulate.

Meristic characteristics:

Feeding habit:  Filter feeder.  Catching food by tentacles.

Main food:  Plankton.   Extracellular products of symbiotic algae.

Egg and larvae: Planula.  Planktonic
