SALIENT FEATURES:

Morphological: Pallet consists of a basal calcareous portion white in colour and naked at the proximal portion. The calcareous base is capped with prominent brown to nearly black periostracal cap, which can be easily removed from the base. The distal end of the calcareous base is conical and extends for some distance into the periostracal cap. This portion covered with periostracum bears concentric growth lines. Distal margin of the periostracal cap straight, broadly curved or may have lateral horns. It may even be solid or with a shallow cavity.

Feeding habit: Feeds on wood into which it bores.

Main food: Cellulose supplemented by plankton.
