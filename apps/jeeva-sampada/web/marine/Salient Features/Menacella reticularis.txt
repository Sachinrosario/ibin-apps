SALIENT FEATURES		

Description	:	The colony is 20 cm high and about 17 cm in diameter; is strictly flabellate and reticulate slightly. The axis of the main stem is 3.5 cm in diameter. The average between branches is about 4.8 mm. The calyces are irregularly distributed on all branches. The individual calyces are verruciform or dome-shaped when the polyps are retracted, 0.8 mm in height and 1.5 mm diameter. The walls are filled with warty spindles most of which tend to be in a horizontal position.

Spicules: The spicules of the coenenchyma and the calyces are warty. 

 Colour		:	Colony creamy-white and the spicules are  colourless.
