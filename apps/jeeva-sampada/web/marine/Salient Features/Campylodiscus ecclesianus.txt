SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves sub circular, central area sub circular, crossed densely by longitudinal and transversal rows of coarse striae, surrounded by two circular of coarse puncta.

Diagnostic characteristics: Valve margin with two layers of radially short costae in alternative arrangements 4.5 in 10µ, fine puncta indistinct between costae.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and characteristics: Diameter 67µ.
