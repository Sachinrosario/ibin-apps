SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Valves sigmoidly naviculoid, protracted in rostrated ends in medium length at the ends. Raphe on the middle line, or sub-excentric near the ends.

Diagnostic characteristics: Striae fine, transverse striae more evident than the longitudinal, 23/23 in 10µ.
 
Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length 91-97µ and width 15-18m.
