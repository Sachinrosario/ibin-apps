DIAGNOSTIC CHARACTERISTICS

The short sea snake is generally greenish or yellowish-olive above, shading to white 
or pale yellow	on sides and is whitish below. The dorsal pattern comprises of some
43-55 narrow, dark, sometimes blck, bars tapering to a point on sides; the bands ofter 
alternate to form a zigzag band. The head is blackish in the young, olive and greyish 
in the adult, with or without a yellow curved mark on the top. Smoth (1943) examined
an adult specimen from Trivandrum, which was dark grey and almost uniform in colour.

SEX ATTRIBUTES

Male short sea snakes occasionally with short spines on lowest row of scales.

FEEDING HABIT : Piscivorous
MAIN FOOD : Fish

ADDITIONAL REMARKS:

The short sea snake,though restricted in distribution being found from the shores of Arabia 
to the West coast of Peninsular India and SriLanka (Smith 1943) is, however , not as rare as 
is believed on the east coast. Murthy (!977) and Srinivasan (2000) recorded this species 
from the shores of Bay of Bengal. Wall (1909) also stated that the species is common on 
the Malabar and Coramandel coasts of India. Smith (1943), probably because of the paucity 
of material at his disposal , didnot include the east cosat of India in the range of this sea 
sanke with certainity.
