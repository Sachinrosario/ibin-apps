Morphological:   
Diagnostic characteristics : 
Body elongate  and fusiform, with  smooth unkeeled body. Mouth  terminal, with many, sharp, small teeth; upper  jaw long,  extending  well behind eye; lower jaw projecting  slightly. Dorsal fin with 20to 25 soft rays set near midpoint   of body. Anal  fin with 18 to 20 rays, its origin well behind last dorsal fin ray. Pelvic fin  base below middle of dorsal – fin base.  Scales very small, about 100 along lateral line.  

Colour :

Back of body  blue / green,  flanks silvery; fins yellowish.
