SALIENT FEATURES:

Diagnostic characteritics:  Body slender, hind border of carapace with 8 to 10 filaments and a pair of notches. Labrum with one stout median spine flanked by one short spine on each side. Endopod of third pleopod of male seven segmented, and exopod five segmented, third segment with one stout naked spine and one flattened groved process on distal margin; fourth segment with one stout naked spine; fifth segment terminating in two stout spines, one shorter and naked, other long and stout and carrying nine strong barbs. First pleopod in female biramous, exopod small and ovate ; endopod single jointed armed with many plumose setae.  Lateral border of telson with 10 –15 spines, first and last longer than others. Endopod of uropod with four equidistant spines.


Additional remarks:  The very  characteristic shape of the labrum has not been observed by previous authors  and is evidently a variable character within the genus.

Size and age

Maxikmum length:  Male and female –   10-15 mm
