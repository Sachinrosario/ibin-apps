SALIENT FEATURES :

Morphological :

Diagnostic characteristics :
Female : Prosome and pedigerous segment 1 indistinctly separate. Pedigerous segment 4 and 5 separate. Posterolateral metasomal borders rounded. Urosome 1/3 length of metasome. Urosomal segments very hairy genital segment symmetrical. Antenna 2 endopods 2/3 length of exopod. Maxilla 1 endopod with 16 setae. Basipod 1 of leg 4 with 8-13 strong teeth on posterior surface.


Male : Posterior metasomal border rounden and without spines, pedigerous segments 4 and 5 separate. Leg 5 like that of P.notacantha, but differs in proportional lengths and shapes of various joints, both basipod 2 distal borders level.

Size and Age:

Female : 5.25-6.45 mm
Male : 4.8 mm

Reference No; Bradford and Jillett, 1980.
