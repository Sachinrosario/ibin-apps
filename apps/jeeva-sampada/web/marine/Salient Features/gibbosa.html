  Diagnostic characteristics:
    Body fusiform, slightly compressed, its depth 22 to 31% of standard length; belly sharp with keeled scutes, pre-pelvic 17 to 19, post-pelvic usually 15 (sometimes 14 or 16). Pseudobranch short, flat; gillrakers on lower arm of the first arch 38-65. Dorsal fin with 13 to 16 branched rays, its origin slightly before midpoint of body. Anal fin with 14 to 18 branched rays, lying far behind dorsalfin. Pelvicfin with 8 rays, its origin below anterior part of the dorsal-fin base. Scales cycloid, thin and deciduous; anterior scales with few perforations and fimbriated at the posterior margin, posterior scale with 4 to 5 striae, interrupted at centre of scale; pre-dorsal median ridge covered by adjacent sides of two longitudinal rows of scales.

    Colour: Blue-green above, silvery on flanks, separated by a narrow yellow
horizontal line; black spot at bases of anterior dorsal rays,
