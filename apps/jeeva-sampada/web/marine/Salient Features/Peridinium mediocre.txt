SALIENT FEATURES: 

Morphological: Rounded, smaller species. The epitheca relatively more high, having the posterior border rectilinear. 

Diagnostic characteristics: Posterior sulcal plate forms a sort of posterior wing. The apical neck is short, antapical horns absent, but with sharp long spines.

Additional remarks: Utilizes dissolved inorganic nutrients.

Size and age: Length: 56-62µ. Diameter: 56-58µ
