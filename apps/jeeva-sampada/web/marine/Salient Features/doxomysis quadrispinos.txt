SALIENT  FEATURES :Morphological: Diagnostic characteristics:     Body spiny. Carapace produced into low triangular acute rostrum. Eyes large. Antennal scale over reaching antennular peduncle. Distal border of endopod of maxilla armed with three long plumose setae and eight strong spines. Exopod of fourth pleopod six or seven segmented, penultimate and antipenultimate segments with stout barbed setae. Telson twice as long as broad, steadily narrowing towards tip, apical sinus deeep and armed with nine pairs of spines and pair of plumose setae; distal half of lateral border armed with eight pairs of similar well spaced spines, telsonic lobe with four blunt large spines. Inner border of endopod of uropod armed with fifteen spines generally increasing in length distalwards.
