Descriptive characters:

       Body shape                                                         

                                        Lateral                         : short and / or deep.

                                        Cross section               : Oval

                                        Dorsal head profile      : more or less straight

                                        Type of eyes                 : more or less normal

                                        Type of mouth/snout     : more or less normal

                                        Position of mouth          : Tetminal

                        Diagnosis  :   Large adults recognized by the rounded tail with pale margin. Has narrow dark bands and lacks peduncular spot that distinguishes this species form similar ones (Ref.12). Similar to A. vaigiensis and A. sexfasciatus but has more black bars (6-7 versus 5).
