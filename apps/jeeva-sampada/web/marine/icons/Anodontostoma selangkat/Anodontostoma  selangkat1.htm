Descriptive characters:

       Body shape                                                         

                                        Lateral                         : fusiform / normal

                                        Cross section               : compressed

                                        Dorsal head profile      : more or less straight

                                        Type of eyes                 : more or less normal

                                        Type of mouth/snout     : more or less normal

                                        Position of mouth          : sub-terminal/inferior

                        Diagnosis  : Body depth increasing with size of fish. Second supra-maxilla a mere splint. Lower gill rakers 100 to 166; longest gill rakers on lower part of arch less than corresponding gill filaments. Teeth on hind edge of scales wider than gaps between them; a median series of pre-dorsal scales. A large dark mark behind gill opening, followed by longitudinal streaks along flank.
