 Climate

 

Tropical; 41°N - 18°S.

Back to Top

 Environment
 


 Demersal; amphidromous; brackish; marine; depth range - 70 m.

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Indo-West Pacific: Persian Gulf to the Philippines, north to Korea and southern Japan, south to northern Australia. Possibly in the Red Sea and eastern and southern Africa.

Vision

               

                            Max.Size        : 65.0 cm WD (male/unsexed; Ref. 12).

                            Max.Age        :  

         Biology : Occurs inshore and offshore, from the intertidal to at least 70 m depth     (Ref. 9). May enter brackish water. Feeds on worms, crustaceans, snails, and bony fishes (Ref. 14). Ovoviviparous (Ref. 13). With litters of up to 4 young. Size at birth about 17 cm WD (Ref. 10).
