Biology         

Occurs inshore and offshore, from the intertidal to at least 70 m depth     (Ref. 9). May enter brackish water. Feeds on worms, crustaceans, snails, and bony fishes (Ref. 14). Ovoviviparous (Ref. 13). With litters of up to 4 young. Size at birth about 17 cm WD (Ref. 10)
