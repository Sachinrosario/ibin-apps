Taxonomic Details

Scientific Name: Stichodactyla  helianthus 

Original bionem: Actinia   helianthus 

Common Name: Caribbean sun anemone, Sun anemone

Valid Name: Stichodactyla helianthus (Ellis, 1768) 

Synonym:  questionable synonymy Actinia anemone, Actinia helianthus ,Actinia Anemone ,Actinia Helianthus ,Discosoma anemone ,Discosoma helianthus ,Discosoma Helianthus, Discosoma Anemone ,Stoichactis anemone ,Stoichactis helianthus ,Stichodactyla helianthus .

Distribution

 Mumbai suburbs, Western India      

Other countries:Australia, Queensland, Torres strait, Great Barrier Reef, Indonesia, Japan, Micronesia, federated states of, New caledonia, Papua new guinea, Solomon islands .

 Ecosystem:  found in Symbiotic association with zooxanthellae Amphiprion sebae.    

 Depth:

Location:

  • Latitude:15.50

  • Longitude:73.80  

Key Identification Features: Thenaria (Endomyaria) with well developed pedal disc. Column usually with verrucae. Sphincter diffuse to circumscribed, usually not very strong. Tentacles short, wart-like or longer, never very long. From all or some of the endocoels the tentacles arise in radial series; sometimes there is more than one row per endocoel, sometimes only one; rarely the radial arrangement is indistinct or absent. Only one tentacle communicates with each exocoel. Longitudinal muscles of tentacles and radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal. Pairs of perfect mesenteries numerous, all the stronger ones, with or without the directives, fertile. Retractors weaker or stronger, always diffuse. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic p-mastigophors.

Museum Record: Natrual History Museum, Smithsonian Institute, Survey of India. Date of observation: No later than 1981
