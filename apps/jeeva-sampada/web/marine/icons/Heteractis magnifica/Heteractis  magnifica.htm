Distribution

Other Countries:French Polynesia to East Africa, and Australia to the Ryukyu Islands.  

Ecosystem: Zooxanthella  symbionts   

Depth: 0 – 30 m     

Location:

  • Latitude:-4.00  Longitude:73.10
  • Latitude: -4.66 Longitude: 55.45  

Key Identification Features: Heteractis magnifica rarely lives on branching coral. Its blunt tentacles and brightly coloured column are distinctive. Oral disc  1 m diameter (although commonly 300-500 mm), flat to gently undulating, densely covered with finger-like tentacles ( 75 mm long) that hardly taper to blunt or slightly swollen end. May irritate human skin and raise welts. Tentacles approach mouth to within 20-30 mm; central oral disc yellow, brown, or green, often raised so that mouth sits on a cone. Column with longitudinal rows of translucent verrucae same colour as column or slightly lighter or darker. Animal capable of almost complete contraction so that only a tuft of tentacles is visible in center. Stoichactiidae with well developed pedal disc. Column usually with verrucae. Sphincter diffuse to circumscribed, usually not very strong. Tentacles short, wart-like or longer. From all or some of the endocoels the tentacles arise in radial series; sometimes there is more than one row per endocoel, sometimes only one; rarely the radial arrangement is indistinct or absent. Only one tentacle communicates with each exocoel. Longitudinal muscles of tentacles and radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal. Pairs of perfect mesenteries numerous, all the stronger ones, with or without the directives, fertile. Retractors weaker or stronger, always diffuse. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic p-mastigophors.

 

Museum Record: National Natural History Museum,Leiden, Smithsonian Museum ,California Academy of Sciences . Date of Observation: No later than 1981
