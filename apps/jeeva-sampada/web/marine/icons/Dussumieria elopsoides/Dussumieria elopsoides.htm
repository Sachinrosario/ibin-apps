Distribution

Indo-Pacific: Suez and western Indian Ocean (the Persian Gulf to Mombasa; possibly to Madagascar) to China, the Arafura Sea (Ref. 14) and to about Solomon Islands. Previous records of Dussumieria acuta from northeastern Indian Ocean would refer to this species (Ref.15).

 Countries      

Australia, Bahrain, Bangladesh, Brunei Darsm, Cambodia, Caroline I., China Main, Cyprus, Djibouti, Egypt, Eritrea, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Japan, Jordan, Kenya, Kuwait, Lebanon, Malaysia, Maldives, Marshall Is, Micronesia, Myanmar, Oman, Pakistan, Palau,Papua N Guin, Philippines, Qatar, Ryukyu Is., Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Solomon Is., Somalia,Sri Lanka Sudan, Syria, Taiwan, Thailand, Tonga, Turkey, Untd Arab Em, Viet Nam, Yemen.
