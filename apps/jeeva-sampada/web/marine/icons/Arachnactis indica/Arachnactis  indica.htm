Distribution

Tamil Nadu, Madras, from plankton one-half to one mile off the shore .

Ecosystem:

Depth: 0 – 3 m

Location:

  • Latitude:

  • Longitude:

Key Identification Features: Spirularia with craspedonemes on the mesenteries, cnidorhagae are absent. Ceriantharia lacking p-mastigophores in the cnidom, holotrichs are present. Cnidorhagae or craspedonemes may be present on the mesenteries, acontioids are always absent. Lateral mesenteries arranged in quartets.
