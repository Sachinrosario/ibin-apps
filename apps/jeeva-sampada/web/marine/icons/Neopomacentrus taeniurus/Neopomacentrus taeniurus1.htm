 Climate

 

Tropical; 23 - 28°C; 19°N - 21°S .

 Environment
 

Demersal; freshwater; brackish; marine ; depth range 0 - 3 m .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Indo-West Pacific: East Africa to Indonesia and the Solomon Islands, north to the Philippines, south to northern Australia and Vanuatu. Reported from Taiwan (Ref. 12).                                                                                                       

Countries

                        China Main, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Kenya,Mozambique, New Caledonia, Palau, Papua N Guin, Philippines, Ryukyu Is., Seychelles, Solomon Is., Somalia, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Tanzania, Vanuatu.

Vision            

Maximum sensitivity: 492.7 (nm)             

                          Max.Size    :  10.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 3)

                           Max.Age     :

                          Biology   : Inhabits mangroves, estuaries, lower reaches of freshwater streams, and harbors with freshwater discharge. Also found in pure fresh water, but always within a few kilometers of the sea (Ref. 5). Spawning may take place in both brackish and fresh waters (Ref. 5)
