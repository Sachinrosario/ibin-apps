 Climate

 

 Subtropical; 32°N - 22°S.

Back to Top

 Environment
 


 Reef-associated; amphidromous; brackish; marine ; depth range 10 - 49 m.

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Indo-West Pacific: temperate and tropical waters from India to northern Australia, New Guinea, Indonesia, Philippines, Taiwan and Japan.

                                                                                                        

Countries

                        Amer Samoa, Australia, Bangladesh, Brunei Darsm, Cambodia, Chagos Is., East Timor, Fiji, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Iran, Japan, Kiribati, Madagascar, Malaysia, Marshall Is, Mozambique, Myanmar, N Marianas, Nauru, New Caledonia, Oman, Pakistan, Palau, Papua N Guin, Philippines, Reunion, Ryukyu Is., Samoa, Singapore, Solomon Is., Somalia, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, Tuvalu, US Minor Is, Viet Nam, Wallis Fut.

Descriptive characters:

       Body shape                                                         

                                        Lateral                         : short and / or deep

                                        Cross section               : compressed

                                        Dorsal head profile      : clearly concave

                                        Type of eyes                 : more or less normal

                                        Type of mouth/snout     : more or less normal

                                        Position of mouth          : terminal

                        Diagnosis  : Color generally silvery with greenish tinge above. Pectoral fins long and pointed. Similar to D. longimana but differs in having 4 - 11 vertical gray spots on the upper half of the sides, and generally 8 dorsal spines (against none (spots) and generally 9 spines in D. longimana).

Vision      

                          Max.Size    : 50.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 4)

                           Max.Age     :  

                            Biology : Occurs in various inshore habitats: sand or mud bottoms, reefs, estuaries and harbors (Ref. 12). Found near coral and rock reefs at temperatures ranging from 26° to 29°C. Feeds on invertebrates and benthic fishes (Ref. 14). Generally marketed fresh (Ref. 15).
