Ttropical; 30°N - 32°S .

 Environment
 

Rreef-associated; brackish; marine ; depth range 3 - 36 m .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                  Indo-Pacific: Red Sea and Algoa Bay, South Africa (Ref. 13) to Rapa and Ducie islands, north to southern Japan, south to Perth, New South Wales. Likely at Seychelles (Ref. 35). Eastern Pacific: Gulf of California to Ecuador (Ref. 36). Eastern Mediterranean: off the coast of Shiqmona, first Lessepsian immigrant of Family Scaridae (Ref. 37).

Vision                         

 Maximum sensitivity: 484.6 (nm)  

                          Max.Size    :  90.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 42)

                           Max.Age     : 13 years

                          Biology   :Inhabits lagoon and seaward reefs (Ref. 39), in slopes and drop-offs (Ref. 43). Males common in atolls where they live mainly around the inner and outer edges of barrier reefs at depths of about 30 ft; females prefer deeper habitat (Ref. 44). Small juveniles inshore on algae reef habitat. Enters silty, murky environments (Ref. 13). Adults solitary (but may sometimes occur in small groups), juveniles in groups (Ref.43, 45 ). Feeds by scraping algae from rocks and corals (Ref. 36). A protogynous hermaphrodite (Ref. 46). Caught with nets and other types of artisanal gear. Marketed fresh (Ref. 42).
