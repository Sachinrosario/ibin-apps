Distribution

Indo-West Pacific: Gulf of Aden north to the Persian Gulf, then eastward to the Andaman Sea, South China Sea and the Philippines, and north to southern tip of Korea. Single record from South Africa (Ref. 3259).

Diagnosis               :   Dark bluish dorsally, silvery below (Ref. 3259). A dark spot behind gill opening. Belly with 17 to 20 (usually 18) + 9 to 13 (usually 11), total 28 to 32 (usually 30) scutes. Anterior arm of pre-operculum with the third infra-orbital bone immediately above it, no fleshy gap between; lower jaw strongly flared outward. Pectoral axillary scale present; hind edge of scales distinctly toothed.

Biology    

Known from estuaries and coastal areas, occasionally ascending into the upper reaches of the tidal zone (Ref. 13). A filter-feeder. Marketed fresh, dried-salted or boiled. Made into fish balls. Very bony.

Eggs                                      

Place of development :  buoyant (pelagic)

Shape of egg           : spherical

Attributes              : smooth  

Additional Characters : Oil globules are concentrated near the tail. Yolk is round and not segmented. Perivitelline space is narrow. Egg hatches after 55 hours. Just prior to hatching, pigment spots appear on the dorsal side of thte embryo.

Remarks: At 9 mm, dorsal fin has 8 rays; anal fin has about 15 rays. Pigmentation: 12 black pigment spots on each side of the larva; one black spot in front of the opercular opening, 2 on the posterior side of the pectoral fin, 6 in the foregut region, and 2 in the midgut and one above the anus.
