 Climate

 

Tropical; 15°N - 19°S .

Back to Top

 Environment
 

Demersal; non-migratory; marine; depth range 12 - 70 m .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                  Western Pacific: including the South China Sea, Strait of Malacca,    southern coasts of Sumatra and Java, northern Australia, and the Gulf of Papua, to the Solomon Islands. This species has previously been confused with Nemipterus mesoprion.

                                                                                                        

Countries

                        Australia, Brunei Darsm, Cambodia, East Timor, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Papua N Guin, Philippines, Ryukyu Is., Singapore, Solomon Is., Taiwan, Thailand, Viet Nam., Yemen.
