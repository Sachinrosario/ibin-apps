Distribution

Location:

  • Latitude:19.97

Key Identification Features: Thenaria (Acontiaria) whose acontia are provided with basitrichs and microbasic p-mastigophors. Uppermost part of column with longitudinal muscles. Cinclides in the middle of the body, margin tentaculate. Sphincter mesogloeal, but very weak, so that the upper part of the body is not able to cover the tentacles. Mesenteries not divisible into macro- and microcnemes. Six, rarely eight, pairs of mesenteries perfect and fertile.

 Museum Record: Zoological  Survey of India . Date of Observation: No later than 1967
