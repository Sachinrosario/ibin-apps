Distribution

Indo-Pacific: East Africa, including Somalia (Ref. 2). Also reported from the Andaman Islands, India and Indonesia (Ref. 7). There are a few records from brackish and fresh waters. Recorded from the Mekong delta (Ref. 8).

Countries    

Andaman Is. , Cambodia , India , Indonesia , Somalia , Tanzania .

Miscellaneous Information

 

Found in estuaries (Ref. 12). Recorded from Tamil Nadu coast, Kerala, Goa and back waters under the name as Gobius malabaricus (Ref. 12), questionably a synonym of Stenogobius gymnopomus (Ref. 5). Critically endangered (Ref. 13).
