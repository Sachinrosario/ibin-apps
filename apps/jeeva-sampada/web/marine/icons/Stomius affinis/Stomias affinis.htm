Ecological Habitat     

 

                                                                                                                                                                  

Distribution

Eastern Atlantic: Mauritania south to Angola . Also across the Atlantic between 0 and 20°N, extending to 35°N and 39°S in the western part ( USA to Argentina ). Also, Indian and Pacific oceans. Circumglobal in warm waters (Ref. 8).

Countries    

  Angola, Anguilla, Antigua Barb, Argentina, Aruba, Ascension I., Australia, Barbados, Benin, Br Virgin Is, Brazil, Cameroon, Cape Verde, Cayman Is, Congo Dem Rep, Congo Rep, Cote d'Ivoire, Cuba, Curaçao I., Dominica, Dominican Rp, Eq Guinea, Fr Guiana, Gabon, Gambia, Ghana, Grenada, Guadeloupe, Guinea, GuineaBissau, Haiti, India, Jamaica, Japan, Liberia, Martinique, Mauritania, Mexico, Montserrat, NethAntilles, Nigeria, Papua N Guin, Philippines, Puerto Rico, Saint Lucia, Sao Tome Prn, Senegal, Sierra Leone, St Kitts Nev, St Vincent, Suriname, Taiwan, Togo, Trinidad Tob, Uruguay, US Virgin Is, USA.
