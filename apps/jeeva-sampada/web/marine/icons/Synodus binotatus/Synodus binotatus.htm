Distribution

 Indo-Pacific: Gulf of Aden and East Africa to the Hawaiian and Gambier islands, north to the Ogasawara Islands, south to the Great Barrier Reef.

Biology      

Occurs in coral reefs (Ref. 4). Generally solitary and typically rests on hard surfaces, occasionally with a head down position on a steep slope (Ref. 8). Sold fresh in markets
