Vision

               Maximum sensitivity: 482 (nm), Confidence interval: 0.4 (nm).

                            Max.Size        : 250 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 19),

                                                      Max. Published weight: 83.0 kg (Ref. 20).

                            Max.Age        :  * D.N.A

         Biology :    An oceanic, epipelagic species frequently solitary or forming small loose aggregations rather than compact schools. Feeds on fishes and squids. An important sport fish in some areas (Ref. 20). Marketed fresh, salted or spice-cured slices of meat; also frozen (Ref. 21). Flesh of very good quality (Ref. 22)
