Distribution

Indo-West Pacific: Somalia (Ref. 15) and Tanzania to Durban, South Africa (Ref. 16) east to Pakistan, India, Sri Lanka and southeast Asia to New Guinea, Australia, and the Philippines. Reported from southern China (Ref. 6).

Countries    

 

  Australia, China Main, Fiji, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Kenya, Madagascar, Maldives, Mauritius, Micronesia, Mozambique, New Caledonia, Oman, Pakistan, Palau, Papua N Guin, Philippines, Reunion, Seychelles, Somalia, South Africa, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Tanzania.

Biology     

 Inhabit coastal waters and estuaries (Ref. 20). Larvae and early juveniles are found in mangroves (Ref. 21). Feed mainly on small fishes (Ref. 16) and crustaceans (Ref. 21). Oviparous (Ref. 22). Eggs may be found attached to objects in the water by tendrils on the egg's surface (Ref. 22). Marketed fresh (Ref. 17)

Remarks: Upper and lower jaws are mildly elongated forming a beak-like structure. The lower jaw is slightly longer than the upper jaw. Minute teeth are seen on both jaws. The dorsal and anal fins are located at the posterior part of the body. Dorsal fin rays 18. Anal fin rays 24. Dorsal fin originates above the 6th anal fin ray. Caudal fin is subtruncated and marginal. Pre-anal finfold is present. Pectoral fin rays 10. Ventral fin buds emerge mid-way between the eye and the caudal base. Pigmentation: Prominent longitudinal rows of pigments about 5-8 are present.
