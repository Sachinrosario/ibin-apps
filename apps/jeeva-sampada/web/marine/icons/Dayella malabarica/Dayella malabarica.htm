Striking features     : 

 Body shape                                                         

          Lateral           : fusiform / normal

                Cross section  :  oval

Dorsal head profile   :  more or less straight

Type of eyes            : more or less normal

Type of mouth/snout:  more or less normal

Position of mouth     :  terminal

Diagnosis               : With 1 to 4 unkeeled and irregular scutes without vertical arms; pelvic scute with vertical arms (not W-shaped, cf. Spratelloides, Dussumieria); no post-pelvic scutes. Snout pointed, lower jaw a little prominent, small but distinct teeth in each jaw; second supra-maxilla long; gill rakers on hind face of third epibranchial (cf. absent in Ehirava fluviatilis).
