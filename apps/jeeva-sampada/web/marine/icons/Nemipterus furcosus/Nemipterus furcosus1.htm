 Climate

 

Tropical; 34°N - 26°S .

 Environment
 

Reef-associated; non-migratory; marine; depth range 8 - 110 m .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Indian Ocean: Maldives (Ref. 13), Gulf of Mannar, Sri Lanka, Andaman Sea, Strait of Malacca and northwestern Australia. Western Pacific: southern Japan to northeastern Australia, Most authors have incorrectly applied the name Nemipterus peronii to this species.

Descriptive characters:

       Body shape                                                         

                                        Lateral                         : fusiform / normal

                                        Cross section               :  oval

                                        Dorsal head profile      : clearly convex

                                        Type of eyes                 : more or less normal

                                        Type of mouth/snout     : more or less normal

                                        Position of mouth          : terminal

                        Diagnosis  : Suborbital spine absent. Preopercle with 3 transverse scale rows. Pectoral fins moderately long, reaching to or just short of level of anus. Pelvic fins moderately long, reaching to or just short of level anus. A line drawn up from posterior edge of suborbital reaching the dorsal profile at about the origin of dorsal fin. Females predominate at small sizes while males dominate the larger size classes. Maybe a sequential hermaphrodite. Axillary scale present. Color: Upper body iridescent pink, silvery white below. Lower margin of caudal fin white.

Vision                       

                          Max.Size    : 24.0 cm SL (male/unsexed; Ref. 1)

                           Max.Age     :  

                          Biology   : Inhabits sandy and muddy bottoms; also in sheltered estuaries (Ref. 1). Feeds mainly on crustaceans and small fishes. Principal catch in the Taiwanese pair-trawl fishery off the coast of northwestern Australia. Good food fish (Ref. 14)
