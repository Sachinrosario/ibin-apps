Vision              

             Maximum sensitivity  : 496.1 (nm), Confidence interval : 0.2 (nm)

                          Max.Size    : 120 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 22)

                          Max. published weight : 15.0 kg (Ref. 23)                                                   

                           Max.Age     :  

                          Biology   :Occurs in deep lagoon and seaward reefs where they feed by rooting for crustaceans and other invertebrates in the sand. Also feeds on small fishes. Small juveniles live among the tentacles of jellyfish (Ref.24). Forms schools. It is noted for its behavior to closely swim around sharks and other large fish, but also following divers (Ref. 25) to gain protection from likely predators (Ref. 24). Marketed fresh and salted or dried (Ref. 13). Juveniles are used in the aquarium trade.
