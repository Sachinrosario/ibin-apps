Distribution

Bengal, Chilika lake, Orissa

Ecosystem:

Depth: 1.83 m -3.66 m

Location:

  • Latitude:19.74

  • Longitude:85.37

Key Identification Features: Mena with no distinct external differentiation of capitulum, scapus and physa. Aboral end rounded, physa-like, at least sometimes without stinging warts. Other parts of the column, except the region of the upper sphincter, with longitudinal rows of stinging warts, which in the lower part are more scattered. 2 mesogloeal sphincters, the one close to the base of the tentacles, the other in the upper region of the column. 2 siphonoglyphs and 2 pairs of directives. 6 pairs of macrocnemes, at least 8 mesenteries fertile, microcnemes weak. Retractors of the macrocnemes strong, restricted. Parietal muscles very weak.

Museum Record: Indian Museum ,Calcutta. Date of observation: No later than 1915

External Anatomy

Actinopharynx: Two siphonoglyphs and two pairs of directives.    

Nematocysts:

Mesenteries: Six pairs of macrocnemes, at least eight mesenteries fertile, microcnemes weak.

Retractor muscles: Retractors of the macrocnemes strong, restricted.  

Parietal muscles: Parietal muscles very weak.

Sphincter muscles: Two mesogloeal sphincters, the one close to the base of the tentacles, the other in the upper region of the column.

Oral disk:

Column: No distinct external differentiation of capitulum, scapus and physa. Aboral end rounded, physa-like, at least sometimes without stinging warts. Other parts of the column, except the region of the upper sphincter, with longitudinal rows of stinging warts,

Tentacles: Tentacles 12 or more, in the contracted state short, but stout and cylindrical.
