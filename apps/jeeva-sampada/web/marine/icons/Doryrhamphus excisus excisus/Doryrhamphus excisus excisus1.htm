Vision      

                          Max.Size    :   7.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 17)       

                           Max.Age     :  

                            Biology : Uncommon, cryptic species (Ref. 18), prefers crevices in rocks and corals and areas beneath ledges (Ref.  19). Occurs in lagoon and seaward reefs to a depth of 45 m or more (Ref. 17). Often seen in pairs. Uses its tubelike snout to ingest small crustaceans and plankton (Ref. 19). Ovoviviparous (Ref. 16). The male carries the eggs in a brood pouch which is found under the tail (Ref. 16)
