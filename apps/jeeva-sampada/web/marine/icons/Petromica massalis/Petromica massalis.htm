 Identifying Characteristics:
 Articulated siliceous desma megascleres, with or without free spicules (Order Lithistida is polyphyletic). Desmas classified according to number of secondarily silicified rays(crepis), from one (monocrepidial) to four (tetracrepidial); many species also with secondary skeletons composed of free spicules indicating phylogenetic relationships (in this sense most orders of living sponges have desma-bearing representatives and possession of desmas is interpreted as a primitive feature. Family diagnosis :Encrusting to massive growth forms; rigid skeleton with tetracrepidial desmas(tetraclones); free spicules consist of tracts of styles and tylostyles dispersed in choanosome; special ectosomal spicules absent; microscleres absent.
