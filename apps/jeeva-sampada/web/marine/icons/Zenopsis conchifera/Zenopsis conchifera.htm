Distribution

Western Indian Ocean: Somalia (Ref. 13) and India to South Africa, but not in the Red Sea. Eastern Atlantic: Bay of Biscay to South Africa. Western Atlantic: Sable Island, Canada to northern North Carolina, USA (Ref. 6) to northern Argentina (Ref. 14). Reported from Indonesia (Ref. 15) but not in the Pacific Ocean.

Countries      

Angola, Argentina, Aruba, Benin, Brazil, Cameroon, Can Maritims, Canada, Cape Verde, Colombia, Congo Dem Rep, Congo Rep, Cote d'Ivoire, Curaçao I.,Eq Guinea, Fr Guiana, France, Gabon, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, GuineaBissau, Guyana, India, Indonesia, Kenya, Liberia, Madagascar, Madeira Is., Mauritania, Morocco, Mozambique, Namibia, Nigeria, Portugal, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, Spain, Suriname, Togo, Trinidad Tob, Uruguay, USA, Venezuela, West Sahara,Yemen.
