Distribution

 Indo-West Pacific: widespread throughout the Indian Ocean from Natal, South Africa northward and from Japan south to Australia and east to Fiji.

Countries

Biology      

Found near the surface in coastal waters (Ref. 13). Found in small schools. Feeds on fishes (Ref. 14). Dorsal and anal fins may carry toxins (Ref. 15). Generally marketed fresh, may be dried or salted (Ref. 16)
