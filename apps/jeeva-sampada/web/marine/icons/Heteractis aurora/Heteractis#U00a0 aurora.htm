Distribution

Arabian Sea, Indian Ocean (Seychelles) Bay of Bengal.

Other Countries: Indonesia, Papua New guinea, Marshall island, Egypt, East Africa, Tanzania, Western Australia, Torres Strait, Japan ,Gulf of Oman, Seychelles.

Ecosystem: Column buried in sand, base weakly attached to coral debris or stones; Symbiotic

Depth: 3m - 40 m

Location:

Latitude:24.91 Longitude: 57.58,

Latitude: -5.75  Longitude: 53.30

Latitude:15.00 Longitude: 88.00

Key Identification Features: Beaded tentacles . Lines on the oral disc traveling down towards the mouth. Up to two inch tentacles with characteristic beaded appearance. With apparent mouth opening and often with radiating appearance from center. Stalk may be mottled or solid orange or red. Live attached to solid structure, able to retract completely beneath the substrate. Stoichactiidae with well developed pedal disc. Column usually with verrucae. Sphincter diffuse to circumscribed, usually not very strong. Tentacles short, wart-like or longer, never very long. From all or some of the endocoels the tentacles arise in radial series; sometimes there is more than one row per endocoel, sometimes only one; rarely the radial arrangement is indistinct or absent. Only one tentacle communicates with each exocoel. Longitudinal muscles of tentacles and radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal. Pairs of perfect mesenteries numerous, all the stronger ones, with or without the directives, fertile. Retractors weaker or stronger, always diffuse. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic p-mastigophors.

 Museum Record: Western Australian Museum, Humboldt University Museum, Natural History Museum,Leiden

Mesenteries: Pairs of perfect mesenteries numerous, all the stronger ones, with or without the directives, fertile.

Retractor muscles: weaker or stronger, always diffuse.

Parietal muscles:

Sphincter muscles: Sphincter diffuse to circumscribed, usually not very strong.

Oral disk: radial muscles ectodermal . eleven inch disc width, with characteristic beaded appearance. With apparent mouth opening and often with radiating appearance from center.

Column:Column with more or less distinct verrucae, which, however, may be absent; able to retract completely beneath the substrate.

Tentacles: Tentacles short, wart-like or longer, never very long. Only one tentacle communicates with each exocoel. Up to two inch tentacles.

Pedal Disk: Well developed.

Miscellaneous Information

Act as Host anemone to clown fishes; found in algal-coral zone.

Sea anemones of the species Heteractis aurora and H. malu seldom contain sexually mature pairs of fish; we term them "nursery anemones”.
