 Identifying Characteristics:

Cup-shaped, club-shaped, spherical, plate-like or fan-shaped or conical growth forms; rigid skeleton composed of monocrepid desmas, and free skeleton with tracts of monoaxonid megascleres (styles or oxeas) in choanosome; special ectosomal skeleton absent; microscleres absen; .with oxeas, without special ectosomal spicules.
