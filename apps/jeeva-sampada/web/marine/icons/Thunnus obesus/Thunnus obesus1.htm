 Distribution

                  Atlantic, Indian and Pacific: in tropical and subtropical waters. Absent in the Mediterranean. Highly migratory species, Annex I of the 1982 Convention on the Law of the Sea (Ref. 14).

The first dorsal fin is deep yellow, the second dorsal and anal fins are light yellow, the finlets are bright yellow edged with black.

Vision                       

                          Max.Size    : 250 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 15)

                                                 Max. Published Weight: 210.0 kg (Ref. 16)

                           Max.Age     : 11 years

                          Biology   : Occur in areas where water temperatures range from 13°-29°C, but the optimum is between 17° and 22°C. Variation in occurrence is closely related to seasonal and climatic changes in surface temperature and thermocline. Juveniles and small adults school at the surface in mono-species groups or mixed with other tunas, may be associated with floating objects. Adults stay in deeper waters (Ref. 1). Eggs and larvae are pelagic (Ref. 17). Feed on a wide variety of fishes, cephalopods and crustaceans during the day and at night (Ref.  18). Meat is highly prized and processed into sashimi in Japan. Marketed mainly canned or frozen (Ref. 19), but also sold fresh (Ref. 18).

Reproduction

                                  Mode                             :  dioecism

                                  Fertilization                   :   external

                                 Spawning frequency       :  Throughout the year, but peaking once

                                 Reproductive guild         :  nonguarders, open water/substratum egg scatterers

  Description of life cycle and mating behavior : Are multiple spawners that may spawn every 1 or 2 days over several months (Ref. 27 ). They spawn over periods of the full moon (Ref. 17). Spawn throughout the year in tropical waters (Ref. 17).
