Taxonomic Details

Scientific Name: Stichodactyla tapetum

Common Name: mini carpet anemone

Valid Name: Stichodactyla tapetum (Hemprich and Ehrenberg in Ehrenberg, 1834)

Key Identification Features: Very hardy and Prolific.that has sticky tentacles .It can form colonies of numerous individual polyps approximately 5 cm in diameter. Can reproduce by fission. Stichodactlya tapetum also has a form that may grow to 30 cm in diameter. Nyantheae [sic] with basilar muscles. Aboral end flattened and usually abherent [sic], distinctly differentiated from the column. Bodywall of variable appearance, sometimes divisible into different regions; often with verrucae, marginal spherules or pseudospherules, vesicles or other protuberances. Sphincter usually endodermal or mesogloeal, but sometimes there is no sphincter. Tentacles and mesenteries usually numerous, the former cyclically or radially arranged. Mesenteries rarely differentiated into macro- and microcnemes. Retractors weak or strong, rarely circumscribed. Acontia present or absent.
