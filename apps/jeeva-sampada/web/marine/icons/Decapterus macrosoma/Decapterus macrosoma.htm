Distribution

 

Indo-West Pacific: East Africa (no verified records from the Red Sea) to Malaysia, Indonesia and the Arafura Sea. Eastern Pacific: Gulf of California to Peru, including the Galapagos Islands (Ref. 12).

Biology      

 Found in rivers, rivulets, canals, drains, ponds and lakes (Ref. 15). Rarely found in the sea (Ref. 16). Inhabits medium to large rivers, flooded fields and mainly stagnant waters including sluggish flowing canals of the lower Mekong (Ref. 17). Most common in areas with floating plants or rooted aquatics that reach the surface (Ref. 18). Larvae and early juveniles are sometimes found in the upper reaches of mangroves during the wet season (Ref. 19). A live-bearer species. Feed on worms, crustaceans and insects (Ref. 20). Rarely taken in commercial catches, although it may be found in subsistence catches. Not seen in markets (Ref. 18)

Larvae    

Striking feature:  Lower jaw largely protruding. Dorsal fin origin behind anal fin. Caudal fin almost round and mildly forked. Pigmentation: Starts as a line from the middle of the lateral side above the pelvic fin and runs posteriorly up to the caudal base. A mild blotch at the base of the caudal fin. A prominent red streak at the base of the dorsal fin occupying two thirds of the length horizontally.
