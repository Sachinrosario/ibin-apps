Descriptive characters:

       Body shape                                                         

                                        Lateral                         : short and / or deep

                                        Cross section               : compressed

                                        Dorsal head profile      : more or less straight

                                        Type of eyes                 : more or less normal

                                        Position of mouth          : terminal

                        Diagnosis  : Species of Diagramma differ from Plectorhinchus by having a much longer and narrower caudal peduncle and fewer dorsal fin spines (Ref. 11). Juveniles yellow or white with broad black stripes and anterior part of dorsal fin tail. Adults become plain grey or spotted. The Queensland fish are plain grey (Ref. 12).

Vision      

                             Max.Size    : 100.0 cm FL (male/unsexed; Ref. 13); max. published weight: 6,300 g (Ref. 14).                           

                            Max.Age        :  

                            Biology : Typically found on open muddy or silty substrates in protected bays or estuaries (Ref. 12). Also in shallow coastal areas and coral reefs (Ref. 15). Juveniles are found in weedy areas (Ref. 4). Feeds on benthic invertebrates and fishes (Ref. 15). Reportedly ciguatoxic in some areas (Ref. 4). Marketed fresh and frozen (Ref. 16) .
