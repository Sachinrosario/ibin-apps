Distribution

 

Circumglobal. Western Atlantic: Nova Scotia, Canada and Bermuda to approximately Pernambuco, Brazil. Appears to be absent from the Gulf of Mexico (Ref. 12). Eastern Atlantic: St. Helena, Ascension, Cape Verde, and Gulf of Guinea (Ref. 9); Azores and Madeira (Ref. 13). Indian Ocean: Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, Seychelles, Mascarenes, South Africa, and Sri Lanka (Ref. 11). Eastern Pacific: Gulf of California and Revillagigedo Island to Ecuador (Ref. 7).

Biology      

 Prefers clear oceanic waters, frequently around islands (Ref. 21). Sometimes near the surface, but generally caught between 40 and 200 m depth (Ref. 7). Forms schools (Ref. 4). Usually seen as fast moving schools along the reef edges near deep water (Ref. 22). Feeds mainly on zooplankton (Ref. 7). Marketed fresh and salted or dried (Ref. 7)
