Distribution

Secretariat Foreshore , Chaupatty, Cuffe Parade,Goa , Cabo de Rona, Ratnagiri, Malvan (Maharashtra), 24 paraganas Dist.(West Bengal )

Other countries: China, Vietnam, South China sea  

Ecosystem: Muddy sand of littoral zone.

Depth: 0 m

Location: 

  • Latitude:19.00

  • Longitude:72.80

Key Identification Features: Paracondylactis with very elongate body and narrow pedal disc. Column smooth, sometimes with nematocysts collected in groups (rarely with verrucae or vesicles? in the upper part). At the margin an annulus of perforated pseudospherules. Sphincter diffuse. Tentacles hexamerously arranged, up to 96, rather short. Longitudinal muscles of tentacles and radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal. 2 well developed siphonoglyphs very prolonged aborally. Mesenteries somewhat more numerous distally than proximally, hexamerously arranged, all or almost all perfect. 2 pairs of directives. Retractors strong but diffuse, parietobasilar muscles distinct. All mesenteries with or without the directives fertile. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic p-mastigophors

 Museum Record: Leiden Natural History Museum, Riksmuseet Natural History museum. Date of Observation: No later than 1989.
