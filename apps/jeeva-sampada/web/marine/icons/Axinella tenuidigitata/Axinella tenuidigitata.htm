 Identifying Characteristics:
viviparous sexual reproduction. Microscleres typically absent, microscleres diverse (meniscoid, oxeote,toxote, spheres) but never asterose, with main skeleton composed of criss-cross (family Halichondriidae) of monaxonic megascleres (styles, oxeas, strongyles), usually with more organization at surface, or sometimes condensed into an axial skeleton and a plumose or plumo-reticulate extra-axial skeleton(family Axinellidae).

Chemicals Extracted
3-debromohanishin methyl ester from an Indian specimen of Axinella tenuidigitata. Four imidazo-azolo-imidazole alkaloids, axinellamines A-D, have been isolated from an Australian marine sponge, Axinella sp. These compounds contain a unique perhydrocyclopenta-imidazo-azolo-imidazole carbon skeleton. Three of these compounds had bactericidal activity against Helicobacter pylori.
