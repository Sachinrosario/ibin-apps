 Identifying Characteristics:

Charecterised by 2 kinds of tylostyles, It is a red to red-orange sponge with two types of tylostyles, long and slender tylostyles, and short ensiform tylostyles. Both species have been found exclusively excavating corals of the genus Pocillopora. The boring activity of C. mucronata produces a network of reticulate galleries with irregularly spherical or ovoid chambers 1.14×0.67 mm in diameter.( Bautista-Guerrero E. et al.,2006). Cliona mucronata is characterized by 2 kinds of tylostyles.

  General Biology

Skeletal Structure: Erosion patterns: Erosion chambers small indiameter (0.9-2.5 mm Bu 501; 0.6-1.5 Phil 16)and ovoid, often with main axis parallel to substrate surface and connected to papillae by short papillar channels (< 1 mm long)

Spongin fibres:

Spicules: thin mucronate tylostyles regularly arranged in a palisade with tips toward exterior. Only tips of thin tylostyles visible in intact papillae Around chamber walls a layer of mucronate tylostyles is present. The spicules are arranged side by side with their heads toward chambers walls and tips toward center. This organization also present in foramina connecting chambers .Double layer of mucronate  tylostyles sometimes observed.

Megascelera: Distribution and Shape: Spirasters are very abundant.
