Biology     

   A schooling species occurring in coastal waters and which appears to enter at least the estuarine parts of rivers and to tolerate brackish water. Feeds most likely on zooplankton, but more data needed. In Manila Bay , the Philippines , it migrates out into deeper and more saline water to spawn, returning inshore immediately thereafter. Processed into nuoc-man (fish pickle) in Indo-China (Ref. 17). Used as bait in the tuna fishery in the South Pacific, although said to be fragile

Miscellaneous Information

 Found along coastal waters, entering estuarine parts of rivers (Ref. 24). Fairly common in the Chilka lake in Orissa and the Hooghly estuary where it is of minor interest to fisheries (Ref. 24); and Godavari estuary (Ref. 25).
