Ecological Habitat    

 

                                                                                                                                                                  

Distribution

Indo-West Pacific: Bay of Bengal south to Penang; Thailand south to Java and southern Kalimantan, but no certain records from the Philippines, Sulawesi or Papua New Guinea. Records of this species from about Hong Kong northward refer to Setipinna tenuifilis.

 

Countries    

Bangladesh, Brunei Darsm, China Main, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Papua N Guin, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, Viet Nam.

Diagnosis               :

Belly with 20 to 29 + 9 to 14 = 32 to 40 keeled scutes from isthmus to anus. Lower gill rakers with the serrae distinctly clumped. Pectoral filament long, reaching to base of 23rd to last anal fin ray. Scales present on dorsal and anal fins.
