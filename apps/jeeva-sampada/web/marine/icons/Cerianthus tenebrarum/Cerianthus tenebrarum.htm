Cerianthidae in which the mesenteries of the second couple (adjacent to the directives) are long, reaching almost to the aboral end of the column , and fertile. Tube anemones are characteristically slender smooth cones topped with two sets of dissimilar tentacles. The outer fringing tentacles are particularly well armed with stinging cells. The shorter (less than two inch) inner labial or oral tentacles assist in food gathering and manipulation.
