Biology      

 Inhabits coastal waters with sandy substrates (Ref. 2); a shallow-living species, benthic in adults; only species found in oceanic islands (Ref. 18). Solitary, well-camouflaged and slow-moving (Ref.  4). Feeds on crustaceans, clams, and small fish. May be caught using ring nets (Ref. 19)
