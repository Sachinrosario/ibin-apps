 Identifying Characteristics:
Massive-amorphous to subspherical; ectosomal skeleton with smooth, thick, flaky crust of paratangentially arranged, relatively thin spicules; choanosomal skeleton with spicules strewn in confusion, occasionally grouped in spongin-enforced tracts, directed mainly parallel or oblique to the surface; choanosome with poor collagen; many species with aerophobic pigments (modified from vanSoest et al. 1990, Diaz et al., 1993). Megascleres are oxeas of various sizes, sometimes with their points modified to strongyles or styloids, but typically centrangulate; extra-axial region is cavernous without any obvious skeletal tracts.

  General Biology

Skeletal Structure: ectosomal skeleton, choanosomal skeleton with spicules strewn in confusion.

Spongin fibres: occasionally grouped in spongin-enforced tracts, directed mainly parallel or oblique to the surface.

Spicules: paratangentially arranged, relatively thin spicules; spicules often angular or doubly bent; spicules often angular or doubly bent; most species show aerophobic reaction when collected  (generally unstable ).
