 Identifying Characteristics:

encrusting or cushion shaped specimens, usually considered as characteristic of hard bottoms, hymedesmoid choanosomal skeleton composed of a basal spongin fibres lying on substrate, with ascending non-anastomosing fibre nodes ("microcionid" skeleton), and erect principal, echinating and auxiliary spicules perpendicular to substrate.
