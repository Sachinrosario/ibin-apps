 Climate

 

Tropical; 30°N - 30°S.

Back to Top

 Environment
 


 Reef-associated; non-migratory; marine ; depth range 1 - 55 m.

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Indo-Pacific: Red Sea and East Africa to the Line and Pitcairn islands, north to southern Japan, south to Sydney, Australia. Not found in the Hawaiian and Marquesan islands.

Descriptive characters:

       Body shape                                                         

                                        Lateral                         : short and / or deep

                                        Cross section               : compressed

                                        Dorsal head profile      : clearly convex

                                        Type of eyes                 : more or less normal

                                        Type of mouth/snout     : more or less normal

                                        Position of mouth          : terminal

                        Diagnosis  : Juveniles overall black with scale centers bluish; white blotch on forehead and upper sides; all fins black except the transparent pectoral and outer portion of soft dorsal rays. Geographic and behavioral color of adults variable; no spot on forehead; spot on upper sides very reduced; head and fins normally black; scales with black margins. Margins of preorbital, suborbital and preoperculum finely serrated. .

Brain

                          Body weight (W; g)    : 97.3

                          Brain weight (B; mg)  : 251

                          Encephalization coefficient (B/W)  : 2.580

 

Vision

               Maximum sensitivity: 494.4 (nm).                         

                            Max.Size        : 11.0 cm SL (male/unsexed; Ref. 10).

                            Max.Age        :  

                            Biology : Inhabits coral and rocky reefs, juveniles often commensal with large sea anemones, sea urchins, or small coral heads (Ref.  2). Occurs in small to large aggregations. Stomach contents include algae, copepods, and other planktonic crustaceans.

                                                                                                            Back to Top

Reproduction

                                  Mode                             : dioecism

                                  Fertilization                   : external

                                 Spawning frequency       :        

                                 Reproductive guild         : guarders, nesters.

  Description of life cycle and mating behavior :  Courtship characterized by male 'signal-jumping'. Captive pair reported to spawn thrice monthly with a total of 17 over a 7-month period (Ref. 19).

                                                                                                                Back to Top

Eggs

                                Place of development     : in open nest

                                Shape of egg                   : ovoid

                                Attributes                        : 

                                Additional Characters    : Eggs laid on dead coral branches, bare rock, man-made substrata (cables or shipwrecks); eggs guarded & cared for by males during incubation lasting for about 3 days at 26°-28°C or 4 days at 24°C. Newly hatched fry measure slightly under 2 mm TL & presumed to be pelagic for the first few weeks.
