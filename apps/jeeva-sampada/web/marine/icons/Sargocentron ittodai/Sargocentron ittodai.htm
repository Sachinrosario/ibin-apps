Distribution

Indo-Pacific: Red Sea and Natal, South Africa (Ref. 5) to the Marquesan Islands, north to southern Japan and the Ogasawara Islands, south to New South Wales, Australia.

Descriptive characters:

Striking features     :  none 

 Body shape                                                         

          Lateral           :   fusiform / normal

        Cross section  :  compressed

 

Dorsal head profile   :  clearly convex

Type of eyes            :    more or less normal

Type of mouth/snout:     more or less normal

Position of mouth     :  terminal

Diagnosis               : Body with red and white stripes following the scale rows, the red stripes slightly narrower than the white ones (Ref. 5) (red stripes usually broader for Taiwan and Japan specimens); lateral line scales for northern Philippines, Taiwan and Japan specimens 44-49; 5 oblique rows of scales on cheek; body depth 2.8-3.1 in SL; head length 2.8-3.1 in SL; short snout, 3.65-4.15 in head length; interorbital width 4.3-4.65 in head length; maxilla extending from below front of pupil to below center of eye, upper jaw length 2.6-2.9 in head length; premaxillary groove reaching about a vertical at anterior edge of orbit; rounded anterior end of nasal bone; edge of small nasal fossa without spine; median edge of nasal bone (premaxillary groove margin) without spinule; upper edge of suborbital bones below the eye weakly serrated and without lateral spine; short preopercular spine, about 1/3 orbit diameter, 5.5-8.2 in head diameter; 4th or 5th longest dorsal spines, 1.7-2.2 in head length; third anal spine long, 1.15-1.4 in head length (Ref. 3).
