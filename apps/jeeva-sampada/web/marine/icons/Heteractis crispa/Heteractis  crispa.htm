Mesenteries: Pairs of perfect mesenteries numerous, all the stronger ones, with or without the directives, fertile.

Retractor muscles: Weaker or stronger, always diffuse .

Parietal muscles:

Sphincter muscles: Sphincter diffuse to circumscribed, usually not very strong.

Oral disk: radial muscles ectodermal

Column: Column with more or less distinct verrucae, which, however, may be absent; able to retract completely beneath the substrate.

Tentacles: Tentacles short, wart-like or longer, never very long. Only one tentacle communicates with each exocoel. Has numerous long, tapering tentacles that end in points characteristic of Heteractis crispa.
