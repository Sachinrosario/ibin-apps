 Tropical; 24°N - 10°S .

 Environment
 


 Pelagic; anadromous; freshwater; brackish; marine ; depth range 0 - 50 m.

 Distribution

                    Indo-Pacific: Indian Ocean (Bombay to Bay of Bengal and Andaman coast of Thailand), Java Sea  (off Java, Singapore). Sarawak, `Cochinchina' and Macao specimens (Ref. 13) should be rechecked.

                                                                                                        

Countries

                        Bangladesh, Brunei Darsm, Cambodia, China Main, India, Indonesia, Iran, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Pakistan, Singapore, Sri Lanka,  Thailand, Viet Nam.

Descriptive characters:

       Body shape                                                         

                                        Lateral                         : fusiform / normal

                                        Cross section               : compressed

                                        Dorsal head profile      : more or less straight

                                        Type of eyes                 : more or less normal

                                        Type of mouth/snout     : more or less normal

                                        Position of mouth          : superior

                        Diagnosis  : Body rather deep, belly with 19 to 23 + 8 to 12, total 28 to 35 (usually 30 to 34) scutes. Eye large, lower jaw strongly projecting. Dorsal fin origin near midpoint of body; anal fin origin below hind part of dorsal fin base. Swim bladder with a single long tube passing back down right side of body above anal fin base. No toothed hypo-maxilla.

Vision                       

                          Max.Size    : 28.0 cm SL (male/unsexed; Ref. 15)                       

                           Max.Age     :  

                          Biology   :  An inshore species, but apparently occurs also in rivers (Ganges at Allahabad and rivers of eastern Uttar Pradesh, but not in Velar estuary). Ascends into the upper end of the tidal zone (Ref. 15). Feeds on fishes, crustaceans, amphipods, occasionally polychaetes, tunicates and small amounts of algae and diatoms.

Miscellaneous Information

Found mainly along east coast of India (Ref. 17). Common in the Ganges at Allahabad and in other rivers of eastern Uttar Pradesh (Ref. 1).
