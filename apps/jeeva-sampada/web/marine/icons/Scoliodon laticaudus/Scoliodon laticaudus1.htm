Vision                         

                          Max.Size    :  100.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 4)

                           Max.Age     : 6 years

                          Biology   : Found on rocky substrates of coastal waters and lower reaches of tropical rivers (Ref. 10). It is uncertain, however, if this species can live in perfectly fresh water for extended periods (Ref. 10). Forms large schools (Ref. 10) . Feeds on small bony fishes, shrimps and cuttlefish (Ref. 10). Viviparous (Ref. 18). Utilized fresh for human consumption; processed into fishmeal and used as bait for other sharks and bony fishes (Ref. 10). Maximum sizes up to 120 cm unconfirmed (Ref. 10)

Reproduction

                                  Mode                             : dioecism

                                  Fertilization                   :  internal (oviduct)

                                 Spawning frequency       : 

                                 Reproductive guild         :   bearers,  internal live bearers.

  Description of life cycle and mating behavior : Viviparous, with an unusual columnar placenta (Ref. 10). Maternal and foetal placenta comprises the entire placenta  (Ref. 21). Transplacental nutrient transfer may be hemotrophic (Ref. 21). Litter size varies from 5 to 14 (Ref. 14). Size at birth about 13 to 15 cm TL (Ref. 14). Distinct pairing with embrace (Ref. 22).

Miscellaneous Information

            Found along west and southeast coast (Ref. 23). Fairly common in the lower reaches of the Hooghly estuary and also in the Chilka lake, Orissa (Ref. 5 , 24 ). Taken by the poorer section. Used as poultry food in dry powdered condition (Ref. 25).
