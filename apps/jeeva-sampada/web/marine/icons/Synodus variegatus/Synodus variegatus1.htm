Environment
 

Reef-associated; depth range 4 - 91 m .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Indo-Pacific: Red Sea and East Africa to the Hawaiian, Line, Marquesan, and Ducie islands, north to the Ryukyu Islands, south to the Lord Howe, Kermadec and Rapa islands. Reported from New Zealand (Ref. 14).

                        Diagnosis : Adults variable from grey to red (Ref. 17). On some individuals, the hourglass- or saddle-shaped markings along its side may be obliterated by an almost solid dark red band (Ref. 16). Large individuals are often thick-bodied, giving a hump-backed appearance (Ref. 16).

                          Encephalization coefficient (B/W)  : 1.434

Vision                       

                          Max.Size    : 40.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 18)

                           Max.Age     :

                          Biology   : Inhabits deep lagoon and seaward reefs to depths over 40 m (Ref. 19); sometimes found on sandy bottoms concealing itself in the sand. Prefers to rest on hard surfaces and frequently occurs in pairs (Ref. 20). Feeds on small fishes by seizing these from passing schools. Caught with various types of artisanal gear; sold fresh or dried salted in markets.
