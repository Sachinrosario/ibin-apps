 Distribution

                                                        Indo-West Pacific: coastal waters from Pakistan to Sri Lanka; east coast of India, Taiwan Island, Okinawa in Japan, the Philippines, Papua New Guinea and Australia. Records from South Africa, as Caranx kalla, are based on misidentifications of Alepes djedaba.

                                                                                                                

Countries

                       Australia, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Iran, Japan, Kuwait, Pakistan, Papua N Guin, Philippines, Ryukyu Is., Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Viet Nam .

Eco-system

                   Ecosystems (confined to India):                

                                  Arabian Sea     (Ref. 7), Indian Ocean    (Ref. 11).                       

.                  Ecological Habitat:   

                                  Intertidal and Marine  

                     Occurrences :

                                  Known from Malabar, Kerala and Portonovo coast (Ref.17). Found in Godavari                                   estuary (Ref.18).   

                Resilience:    

                                High, minimum population doubling time less than 15 months (k >0.30).
