Taxonomic Details

Scientific Name: Ecionemia acervus Bowerbank,(1864)                                

 Common Name: Black pillow or massive grey sponge.

 Accepted Name: Ancorina acervus (Bowerbank)   

 Taxon author(s):                                                                                              

Synonym: Ancorina novaezealandiae Dendy, 1924,Ancorina simplex Lendenfeld, 1897,Ecionemia agglutinans Thiele, 1899,,Ecionemia amboinensis Lendenfeld, 1903,Ecionemia carteri Dendy, 1905,Ecionemia cinerea Thiele, 1900,Ecionemia cribrosa Thiele, 1900,Ecionemia nigrescens Thiele, 1900,Ecionemia rotunda Sollas, 1888,,Stelletta bacillifera Carter, 1887,Stelletta lobata Kieschnick, 1896,Stelletta truncata Kieschnick, 1896,Thalassomora nigra Lendenfeld, 1888

Shape: not uniform in shape, some being regularly oval,

Surface characteristics: Massive or thickly encrusting sponges without a distinct cortex. Triaenes of different types, and large oxeas as megascleres. Microscleres include spiny microrhabds in addition to euasters. Microrhabds usually form a dermal layer.
