 Identifying Characteristics:

Ball-shaped with regularly spaced rounded crater-like pits between which the surface is hispid, bright yellow Paratetilla bacca (Selenka, 1864). Globular sponges with hairy-bristly surface, provided with porocalices. Skeleton radiate, with bundles of oxeas originating from a central focus and running radially to the surface. Spicules protriaenes, anatriaenes, calthrops-like triaenes, oxeas, sigmaspires. So far only a single wide-spread Indo-West Pacific species is reliably recorded .The genus Paratetilla has the megascleres that are short-shafted triaenes with equal-sized rays (calthrops). N.B. these may have bifid cladi but are not to be confused with long-shafted amphiclads .
