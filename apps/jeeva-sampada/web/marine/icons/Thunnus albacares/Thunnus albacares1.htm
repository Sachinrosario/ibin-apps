Fish with very long second dorsal fin and anal fin, which in some may reach well over 20% of the FL.                           Biology   : An oceanic species occurring above and below the thermoclines. They school primarily by size, either in monospecific or multi-species groups. Larger fish frequently school with porpoises, also associated with floating debris and other objects. Feed on fishes, crustaceans and squids. It is sensitive to low concentrations of oxygen and therefore is not usually caught below 250 m in the tropics (Ref.28, 29). Peak spawning occurs during the summer, in batches (Ref. 30). Encircling nets are employed to catch schools near the surface (Ref. 31). Marketed mainly frozen and canned (Ref. 30), but also fresh (Ref. 31) and smoked (Ref.32). Highly valued for sashimi (Ref. 33).

Reproduction

                                  Mode                             :  dioecism

                                  Fertilization                   :   external

                                 Spawning frequency       :  Throughout the year, but peaking once

                                 Reproductive guild         :  nonguarders, open water/substratum egg scatterers

  Description of life cycle and mating behavior : Spawn throughout the tropical and equatorial waters of the major oceans (Ref. 1). At higher latitudes, spawning is seasonal, with peaks in summer; may continue throughout the year at lower latitudes (Ref. 1). Yellowfin tuna are multiple spawners, ie they spawn every few days over the spawning period (Ref. 1). Eggs and sperm are released into the water for fertilisation (Ref. 1).

Remarks:

 Has firm meat with a large flake. Steaks are excellent broiled or grilled. Slices can be brined and hot smoked. Can also be baked. (Ref. 32 ). Light colored meat of the yellowfin has a pinkish tinge and dense consistency. The dark meat unlike the light has a friable structure. When boiled, light meat becomes gray, with a dense, somewhat hard consistency. In taste, light meat resembles chicken and has a distinct acid flavor. Dark meat has low taste qualities. (Ref. 37)
