Biology      

Found over sandy and muddy bottoms, sometimes in estuaries and near rocky reefs (Ref. 7). Feeds on bottom-living invertebrates and fishes. Ovoviviparous, gestation period about 4 months and 4-7 young are produced. Wings marketed smoked, dried-salted, and also used for fishmeal and oil. Harmful to shellfish banks; dangerous to bathers and fishers due to its poisonous spine. Feeds on bottom fishes, crustaceans and mollusks. Barbed poison spine is a modified denticle that can be 35cm long, shed occasionally and replaced (Ref. 4)
