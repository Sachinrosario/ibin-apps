Biology      

  Inhabits coastal waters, including estuaries and rivers (Ref. 12). Young fish frequent estuaries and also ascend rivers and coastal creeks (Ref. 13). Feeds on algae, diatoms, detritus, and crustaceans (Ref.8). Caught during the spawning period (Ref. 8). Other methods include the use of stakenets, pouch nets, and barrier nets (Ref. 8)
