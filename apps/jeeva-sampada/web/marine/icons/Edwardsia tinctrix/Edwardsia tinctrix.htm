Distribution

 Chilika lake, Orissa. Cuffe Parade, Mumbai , South 24 Paraganas (Kakdwip),West Bengal

Ecosystem: estuaries, brackish

Depth: 0 - 4.8 m

Location:

  • Latitude:19.74 , 18° 54’ N.

  • Longitude:85.37 ’ °S , 72° 49’ S.

Key Identification Features: Athenaria with elongate, vermiform body usually divisible at least into two regions, a long scapus provided with a cuticle and a short upper scapulus. Often there is also a rounded, naked physa at the aboral end and a very short, thin capitulum immediately below the tentacles. No sphincter or acontia. Mesenteries divisible into 8 macro- and at least 4 microcnemes. Of the macrocnemes there are two pairs of directives and four lateral mesenteries, two on each side, whose retractors face the ventral directives. Retractors diffuse to strongly restricted. Parietal muscles always distinct.

 Museum Record: Indian Museum, Calcutta. Date of observation: no later than 1915

External Anatomy

Actinopharynx: A weak ventral siphonoglyph.    

Nematocysts: Nematocysts discharging their threads through papillae on column.

Mesenteries:

Retractor muscles: Retractors very strong, diffuse to restricted reniform,  

Parietal muscles: Parietal muscles well developed.

Sphincter muscles: No sphincter or acontia.

Oral disk:

Column: Column protuberances; column shape vermiform. Column slender, lacks rows of tubercles. Physa with cinclides.

Tentacles: Tentacles 16-36, arranged in three cycles, always with four in the primary cycle, these are stouter and sometimes shorter than the other tentacles.
