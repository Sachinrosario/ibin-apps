 Identifying Characteristics:
2-3 categories of asters distributed on both surface and choanosome, or only present in choanosome; cortex well-developed. Typically with asterose microscleres (but sometimes lost), Growth forms either encrusting to massive, or more specialised with spherical body and long inhalant and exhalant tubes at opposite ends (the latter with stellate, spicular, funnel-shaped end); megascleres long-shafted triaenes (with shaft directed inwards and clads on surface) and oxea ,megascleres; microscleres euasters and microrhabds, without sterrasters or amphiasters.

Chemicals Extracted
 Strain KMM 1922 of Bacillus subtilis, a producer of the antifungal iturin peptide antibiotic bacillomycin D, was isolated from a specimen of the Kuril sponge Stelletta validissima. The peptide was shown to exhibit a pH-dependent cytotoxic activity.
