Key Identification Features:Body divisible into physa, scapus, scapulus and capitulum; the aboral part, physa, short, ampullaceous, without nemathybomes (Spheroid invaginations of the columnar ectoderm into the mesogloea). Scapus long with batteries of nematocysts (nemathybomes) sunk in the mesogloea. Tentacles at least 12, shorter or longer, the inner shorter than the outer ones. A weak ventral siphonoglyph. 8 perfect mesenteries (macrocnemes) and at least 4 imperfect, very weak ones in the most distal part of the body. Retractors very strong, diffuse to restricted reniform, parietal muscles well developed. Only the 8 perfect mesenteries fertile and with filaments. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic b- and p-mastigophors.

Museum Record: Museum record: Bombay Natural history Society, British Museum Natural History. Date of Observation: No later than 1990.

Mesenteries: Has 8 perfect mesenteries (macrocnemes) and at least 4 imperfect, very weak ones in the most distal part of the body. . Only the 8 perfect mesenteries fertile and with filaments.

Retractor muscles: Retractors very strong, diffuse to restricted reniform,

Parietal muscles: Parietal muscles well developed.

Sphincter muscles: No sphincter or acontia.

Oral disk:

Column: Column slender, lacks rows of tubercles. Physa with cinclides.

Tentacles 16-36, arranged in three cycles, always with four in the primary cycle, these are stouter and sometimes shorter than the other tentacles
