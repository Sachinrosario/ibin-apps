 Distribution

                    Indo-Pacific: East Africa to Rapa and Ducie islands, north to southern Japan and the Hawaiian Islands, south to the Lord Howe Island. Eastern Pacific: southern Gulf of California to Peru (Ref. 8).

Vision    

Maximum sensitivity: 492 (nm)  

Confidence interval: 0.2 (nm)                   

                          Max.Size    : 23.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 12)

                          Max. Age :

                          Biology   : Inhabits turbid inner lagoons, reef flats, and clear seaward rocky and coral reefs (Ref. 6). Generally encountered in small groups of 2 or 3 individuals (Ref. 8). Feeds on small encrusting animals (Ref. 12). Size of metamorphosis from postlarva to juvenile is 7.5 cm (Ref. 8). Popular aquarium fish but rarely survives in tanks (Ref. 6)
