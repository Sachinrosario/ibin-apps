Descriptive characters:

Striking features     :   

 Body shape                                                         

          Lateral           :  fusiform / normal

        Cross section  :  oval

Dorsal head profile :  clearly convex

Type of eyes          :   more or less normal 

Type of mouth/snout:  more or less normal

Position of mouth     : terminal

Diagnosis               : Head scales reaching to anterior nostrils. Lower limb  preopercle scaly. Antrorse (forward-directed) suborbital spine present beneath eye. Pelvic fins long, reaching to or just beyond level of anus. Axillary scale present. Color: Variable, usually brownish with a reddish-purple tinge. A broad white vertical bar from top of head onto opercle. Opercular membrane blood-red. Fins greyish with a tinge of red.

Biology      

    

Benthic in inshore waters, usually on sand or mud bottoms close to reefs (Ref. 13), as well as offshore areas (Ref. 14). Seen solitary, but may school deep (Ref. 4). Feeds on benthic organisms (Ref. 16). Marketed fresh, salted, smoked or processed into fish balls (Ref. 15)
