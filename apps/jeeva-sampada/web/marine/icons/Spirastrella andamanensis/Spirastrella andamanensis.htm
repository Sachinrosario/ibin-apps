 Identifying Characteristics:
Hadromerida with limestone-excavating capability, having tylostyles as principal megascleres, in some genera accompanied by oxeas or styloid modifications. Microscleres may be absent entirely or in some specimens or populations. If present, they include spirasters, amphiasters, microxeas, microrhabds, or raphides; some spirasters display secondary branching of spines, microrhabds may be smooth or microspined, straight, bent, or spiral.

  General Biology

Skeletal Structure:

Spongin fibres: tylostyles (subtylostyles or styles), rarely oxeas as megascleres.

Spicules:The megascleres are placed in a somewhat hymedesmoid arrangement in the choanosome, sometimes clustered in more or less well defined bouquets.

Megascelera: Distribution and Shape: Megascleres are absent from the ectosome. The streptasters form a cortical well defined layer. They can also be widespread in the choanosome and/or concentrated in a basal layer.

Axons:   

          Actines (rays or points of growth):large.

Chemicals Extracted
Demospongiae has been found to contain two new and exceptionally active cell (human cancers growth inhibitors , named spongistatin 2 and spongistatin 3. A bright colored Southwest Indian Ocean sponge Spirastrella spinispirulifera has also been found to contain related human cancer cell growth inhibitors named spongistatin 4 and spongistatin 6. A method of treating human cancer cells with spongistatin 1, spongistatin 2, spongistatin 3, spongistatin 4 and spongistatin 6 has been found.

Spongiostatin: Macrocyclic lactones isolated from the sponge Spirastrella spinispirulifera
