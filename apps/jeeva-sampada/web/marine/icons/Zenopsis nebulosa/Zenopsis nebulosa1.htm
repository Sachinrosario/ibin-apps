 Climate

 

Deep-water; 45°N - 47°S.

Back to Top

Environment

 

 Pelagic; brackish; marine .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                      Indo-Pacific: Japan, northwest shelf of Australia to Broken Bay in New South Wales, New Zealand (Ref. 6), and elsewhere in the region. Eastern Pacific: off central and southern California, USA (Ref. 7); and on the seamounts of the Nazca Ridge in the Peru area (Ref. 8).

Countries

                        Australia, Chile, Hawaii, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea D P Rp, Korea Rep, New Caledonia, New Zealand, Peru, Taiwan, USA, Viet Nam

Vision   

                          Max.Size    : 70.0 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 9).

                          Max. published weight: 3,000 g (Ref. 10).

                          Max. Age :

                          Biology   : Occurs in deeper trawling grounds of the continental shelf and slope, close to the sea bed. Nothing is known about larval development or early life history for this species, and no studies on ageing and growth rates have been undertaken. Caught by Japanese trawlers during winter (Ref. 11).
