 Identifying Characteristics:
Microscleres typically absent, with main skeleton composed of criss-cross (family Halichondriidae) of monaxonic megascleres (styles, oxeas, strongyles), usually with more organization at surface, tangential reticulation composed of thick bundles of oxeotes often with blunt terminations , forming intercrossing bundles, occasionally stylotes, "echinated" by small styles ;fibre system poorly developed or absent . choanosome is halichondroid, without any compression of the axial region;

  General Biology

Skeletal Structure: sometimes condensed into an axial skeleton and a plumose or plumo-reticulate extra-axial skeleton.
