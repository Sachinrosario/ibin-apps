Taxonomic Details

Distribution

 Gujarat ,Okha , Mithapur coast, Gulf of Mannar, off Tuticorin, Arabian sea, Okha-Dwaraka area  .

Other Countries: Australia, Queensland, Indonesia, Japan ,Japan, Honshu, Okinawa Ryukyu Islands, Mozambique, New caledonia, Papua new guinea,Saudi arabia, Seychelles, Vietnam, Fiji, Red sea.   

Ecosystem: found in Symbiotic association with zooxanthellae, Amphiprion sebae. Small puddle on the limestone reef .

Depth: 0 m

Location:

  • Latitude:22.42 , 8.80 , 22.42

  • Longitude:69.02 , 78.26 , 69.02 

Key Identification Features: Color of this anemone is variable, but often light brown, green, gray, and variegated with white streaking across the disk. Lives in sand in which it can completely retract when disturbed. Thenaria (Endomyaria) with well developed pedal disc. Column usually with verrucae, have larger columns. Sphincter diffuse to circumscribed, usually not very strong. Tentacles short, wart-like or longer, never very long. Variably folded disc surface, open near surface. Has a tentacle-free central area. Very small tentacles with narrow stalks, globose ends; often vary in color. Lives in sand in which it can completely retract when disturbed. From all or some of the endocoels ,the tentacles arise in radial series; sometimes there is more than one row per endocoel, sometimes only one; rarely the radial arrangement is indistinct or absent. Only one tentacle communicates with each exocoel. Longitudinal muscles of tentacles and radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal. Pairs of perfect mesenteries numerous, all the stronger ones, with or without the directives, fertile. Retractors weaker or stronger, always diffuse. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic p-mastigophors.

Museum Record: Natural History Museum, Leiden.South Africa Museum, California Academy of Sciences. Date of observation: No later than 1893,Discosoma Haddoni. No later than 1981, Stichodactyla haddoni

Mesenteries: Pairs of perfect mesenteries numerous, all the stronger ones, with or without the directives, fertile

Retractor muscles:Retractors weaker or stronger, always diffuse.

Parietal muscles:

Sphincter muscles: Sphincter diffuse to circumscribed, usually not very strong.

Oral disk:Radial muscles ectodermal. oral disc diameter 130 mm. Regular folds less than 10 inches (25 cm) across. Variably folded disc surface, open near surface. Has a tentacle-free central area.

Column: Column Small, non-adhesive verrucae on uppermost column are same colour as column or light rose to purple. Have larger columns.

Tentacles: Isolated tentacles with their inflated apices, slightly magnified, are represented in lateral view, (thus showing their columnar stalks) near the top and bottom margins of the plate. The fish, Amphiprion bicinctus, in this illustration (having only two white bands) is a commensal with Discosoma Haddoni. Tentacles short, wart-like or longer, never very long. Tentacles arise in radial series. Very small tentacles with narrow stalks, globose ends; Only one tentacle communicates with each exocoel. Longitudinal muscles ectodermal. tentacles are short and compact. Tentacles sticky to touch, may adhere to human skin so strongly that they pull off the anemone;   

Pedal Disk: well developed pedal disc.
