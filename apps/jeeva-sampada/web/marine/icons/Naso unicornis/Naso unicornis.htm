Distribution

Biology      

Inhabits channels, moats, lagoon and seaward reefs with strong surge. Typically occurring in small groups. Juveniles in shallow protected bays and harbours (Ref. 1). Mainly diurnal, it feeds on coarse leafy brown algae like Sargassum. Pair-spawning has been observed.
