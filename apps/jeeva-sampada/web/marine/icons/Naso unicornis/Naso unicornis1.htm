 Climate

 

Tropical; 26 – 29°C; 35°N - 33°S.

Back to Top

 Environment
 

Reef-associated; marine; depth range 1 – 180 m .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Indo-Pacific: Red Sea and East Africa (Ref. 10) to the Hawaiian, Marquesas and Tuamoto islands, north to southern Japan, south to Lord Howe and Rapa islands. Presence in Somalia to be confirmed (Ref. 12).

Brain

                          Body weight (W; g)    : 340

                          Brain weight (B; mg)  : 665

                          Encephalization coefficient (B/W)  : 1.956

 

Vision                      

                     Maximum sensitivity: 492 (nm)  

                          Max.Size    :  70.0 cm FL (male/unsexed; Ref. 14)

                           Max.Age     :  

                          Biology   :  Inhabits channels, moats, lagoon and seaward reefs with strong surge. Typically occurring in small groups. Juveniles in shallow protected bays and harbours (Ref. 1). Mainly diurnal, it feeds on coarse leafy brown algae like Sargassum. Pair-spawning has been observed.
