Distribution

Indo-West Pacific: Gulf of Oman, the Arabian Sea (Ref. 3), the Arafura Sea (Ref. 5) and the continental shelf of northern and northwestern Australia (Ref. 1). Probably occurs in other localities in the Eastern Indian Ocean and the Western Central Pacific (Ref. 6).

Countries   

Australia, India, Indonesia, Oman, Papua N Guin, Tanzania
