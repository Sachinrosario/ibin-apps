Distribution

Depth: 0 m

Location:

  • Latitude:25’ 07’ N

Key Identification Features: Bunodosoma with well-developed pedal disc. The whole or almost the whole column provided with rounded uniform vesicles forming weak nematocyst-batteries. A ring of marginal spherules in the fosse, rarely absent. Atrichs in the marginal spherules very numerous, spirocysts and basitrichs fewer, the latter short. Sphincter well developed, more or less circumscribed. Tentacles and mesenteries hexamerously arranged. As a rule 2 siphonoglyphs and 2 pairs of directives, rarely several siphonoglyphs not connected with directives. Perfect pairs of mesenteries usually numerous. All stronger mesenteries, sometimes except the directives, fertile. More mesenteries at the limbus than at the margin. Cnidom: spirocysts, atrichs, basitrichs, microbasic p-mastigophors.

 

Museum Record: National Natural history Museum, Leiden

Miscellaneous Information

The potassium channel toxin secreted by the sea anemone Bunodosoma granulifera (BgK) is a 37-amino-acid peptide containing three disulfide bridges.
