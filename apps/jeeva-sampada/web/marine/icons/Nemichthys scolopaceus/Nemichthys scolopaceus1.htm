 Environment
 

Bathypelagic; marine ; depth range 91 - 2000 m .

Back to Top

 Distribution

                   Worldwide in tropical and temperate seas. Western Atlantic: Nova Scotia, Canada and northern Gulf of Mexico to Brazil. Eastern Atlantic: Spain to South Africa, including western Mediterranean (Ref. 20); reported from Iceland (Ref. 21). Regularly found in the Skagerrak (Ref. 22). Northwest Pacific: Japan (Ref. 23) and Arafura Sea (Ref. 24). Eastern Pacific: Alaska (Ref.23) to Chile (Ref. 25), including the Gulf of California (Ref. 26).

                        Diagnosis  : Jaws long (Ref. 30). Posterior end of body narrow, ending as a long filament (Ref. 30). Dorsal fin with about 350 rays; caudal fin not recognizable; anal fin with about 320 (Ref. 4). Dark brown or grey in color, often darker below (Ref. 31); anal fin and tips of pectoral fins almost black (Ref. 4).

Vision                       

                          Max.Size    : 130 cm TL (male/unsexed; Ref. 27)

                           Max.Age     :  

                          Biology   : Occur in midwater, usually below 400 m and occasionally in shallow water in the northern part of its range. Feed on crustaceans while swimming with its mouth open (Ref. 29). Oviparous, with planktonic leptocephali (Ref. 26). Degenerative changes in males and females suggest semelparity (Ref.26)
