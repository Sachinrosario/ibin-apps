Distribution

Worldwide in tropical and temperate seas. Western Atlantic: Nova Scotia, Canada and northern Gulf of Mexico to Brazil. Eastern Atlantic: Spain to South Africa, including western Mediterranean (Ref. 20); reported from Iceland (Ref. 21). Regularly found in the Skagerrak (Ref. 22). Northwest Pacific: Japan (Ref. 23) and Arafura Sea (Ref. 24). Eastern Pacific: Alaska (Ref.23) to Chile (Ref. 25), including the Gulf of California (Ref. 26).

Diagnosis                : Jaws long (Ref. 30). Posterior end of body narrow, ending as a long filament (Ref. 30). Biology      

Occur in midwater, usually below 400 m and occasionally in shallow water in the northern part of its range. Feed on crustaceans while swimming with its mouth open (Ref. 29). Oviparous, with planktonic leptocephali (Ref. 26). Degenerative changes in males and females suggest semelparity (Ref.26)
