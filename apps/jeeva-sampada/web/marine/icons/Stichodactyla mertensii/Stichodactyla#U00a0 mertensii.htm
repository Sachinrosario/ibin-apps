Distribution

Indian Ocean, Maldives,Seychelles.

Other countries: Micronesia and Melanesia to East Africa, and Australia to the Ryukyu Islands

 Ecosystem: Found on reef slopes and basalt slopes 

Depth:

Location:

  • Latitude:- 5.82 , -4.65 , -7.00

  • Longitude: 55.37,55.50, 52.72

Key Identification Features: Stichodactylidae with  Folded disc exceeding a meter in diameter in some specimens. Lives on hard surfaces that it covers closely. Small pedal disc, striated by disc-spreading verrucae of  orange or magenta color. Short tentacles (1cm) of uniform color. non-adhesive tentacles club-shaped to finger-like -- all may be short (10-20 mm long), or some (in patches) very long (to 50 mm or more). Have narrow columns of grey to white color, with longitudinal rows of verrucae pigmented magenta or orange (which appear purplish at depth)

 Museum Record: California Academy of Sciences,Natural History Museum, Leiden. Date of Observation:No later than 1981,in Maldives. No later than 1992-93 ,in Seychelles

Oral disk: Flat, generally not seen, covered with tentacles.

Column: Small, anchored in crevice in rock. Tan to white (unlike H. magnificas). Verucae :Longitudinal rows are colored magenta or orange, adhere to rock to keep disc open.

Tentacles: Short, finger-like, uniform color, not sticky. Tentacles, of uniform diameter, blunt-ended or pointed: short ones all colour of the oral disc, sometimes with narrow white basal portion; long ones may be white-, yellow-, or green-ended. Tan oral disc almost entirely covered with tentacles; yellow or greenish tentacle-free oral area 20-50 mm diameter. (see image). Symbiotic fishes may be melanistic.

Pedal Disk: Small pedal disc frequently attached in crevice into which animal can retract (although not rapidly).
