Key Identification Features: Metridiidae with well developed base. Column devided [sic] into scapus and capitulum, the former ending above in a decided collar which is especially distinct in older individuals. The capitulum lies above the collar and is extensive. Cinclides scattered below the collar. Sphincter in the uppermost part of the scapus, mesogloeal. Tentacles short, numerous, their longitudinal muscles ectodermal. Radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal to meso-ectodermal. Oral disc circular in the young, but deeply and conspicuously lobed in the adult. Siphonoglyphs variable in number, usually one or two. Perfect mesenteries very variable in number, typically 6 pairs but often more. The six primary pairs sterile in typical individuals. Retractors diffuse, sometimes short and broad in sections. Acontia well developed, their nematocysts microbasic b-mastigophors and microbasic amastigophors, the latter sometimes absent in older individuals. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic b- and p-mastigophors, microbasic amastigophors.

 Museum Record: Zoological Survey of India. No later than 1855.
