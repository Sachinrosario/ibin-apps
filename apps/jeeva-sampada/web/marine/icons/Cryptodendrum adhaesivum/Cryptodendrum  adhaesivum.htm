 Taxonomic Details

Scientific Name: Cryptodendrum  adhaesivum

Original bionem: Cryptodendrum   adhaesivum 

Common Name: adhesive sea anemone , Pizza Anemone (Robert M. Fenner)

Distribution

Seychelles (Alphonse, Platte, St. François), Maldives (Malé Atoll, Hulele Island) Indian Ocean.

Other Countries: Red Sea, Egypt, Koseir, French Polynesia, Tuamotu Archipelago, Gambier Islands, Lagoon , Marquesas Islands, Philippines (Mindanao Island) ,     USA, Line Islands, Palmyra Atoll ,Indonesia, (Belitung) ,Israel,

Location:

  • Latitude:4 °35 S, 3° 15 N,

  • Longitude:55 °40 E, 73° 00 E

Key Identification Features:Large flattened anemone, with very short (5 mm) and tightly packed tentacles, extremely sticky. The disc tentacles, slightly branching (often with glove like expansions) are clearly different from the edge tentacles (bulb shaped). Highly variable colour, brown to green at the center, sometimes striped, usually different on the border (can be white, pink, purple, green, brown). Diameter 30 cm. Thalassianthidae with broad pedal disc. Upper part of column with small verrucae. Sphincter weak, to very weak, more or less circumscribed. Margin somewhat crenulated. Wide, irregularly folded oral disc. Tentacles of two kinds, partly short, dendritic, partly spherical. At the margin there is a single row of dendritic, exocoelic tentacles, inside those a continuous, broad band of globular nematospheres. The inner greater part of the oral disc is occupied by short branched endocoelic tentacles radially arranged. Longitudinal muscles of tentacles and radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal. Two well developed siphonoglyphs. Pairs of mesenteries numerous, at least those of the three first cycles perfect. All stronger mesenteries except the directives, fertile. More mesenteries at the margin than at the base. Retractors diffuse, well-developed, band-like. Parietobasilar muscles weak, basilar muscles distinct. Cnidom: spirocysts, basitrichs, microbasic p-mastigophors.

 Museum Record: University of Kansas Natural History Museum, National Natural history Museum, Leiden, Humbolt university museum. Date of observation :no later than 1981.

Mesenteries:Pairs of mesenteries numerous, at least those of the three first cycles perfect. All stronger mesenteries except the directives, fertile. More mesenteries at the margin than at the base.

Retractor muscles: Retractors diffuse, well-developed,band like.

Parietal muscles: Parietobasilar muscles weak, basilar muscles distinct.

Sphincter muscles:Sphincter weak, to very weak, more or less circumscribed.          

Oral disk: Wide, irregularly folded oral disc. The inner greater part of the oral disc is occupied by short branched endocoelic tentacles radially arranged. radial muscles of oral disc ectodermal.

Column: Upper part of column with small verrucae. Margin somewhat crenulated.

Tentacles: Tentacles of two kinds, partly short, dendritic, partly spherical. At the margin there is a single row of dendritic, exocoelic tentacles, inside those a continuous, broad band of globular nematospheres. Longitudinal muscles of tentacles ectodermal.

Pedal Disk: Broad pedal disc
