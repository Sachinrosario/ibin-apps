Salient features:
Morphological:

Female. Body more or less flattened dorso-ventrally, with the dorsal side slightly convex and the ventral concave. Anterior part apparently broader. Cephalon slightly bent downwards, somewhat elongated transversely; anterior margin convex and the posterior concave. In dorsal view, the cephalon appears as a triangular lobe wedged in between the highly produced lateral lobes of the peraeon, with a pair of dark pigmented areas very much like a pair of eyes. Buccal mass conical and directed forwards, with a small four jointed antenna on each side. Peraeon very large, with the lateral parts produced forwards into two large rounded lobes reaching far beyond the cephalon. Posteriorly there are two narroe lobes, extending up t the middle of the pleon. Peraeopods five pairs, small, prehensile, crowded together on either side of the buccal mass, in longitudinal rows. There is a pair of brood lamellae which posterioly rows. There is a pair of brood lamellae which posteriorly reaches the middle of the pleon. Peraeon is a large brood pouch, in which the comparatively small eggs are arranged in symmetrical patterns, when viewed from outside. Pleon short, four segmented, with the apical segment small and distally forked, wedged in between the posterior prolongations of the brood lamellae and the peraeon. Surface of abdomen setose all over. Pleopods absent.

  Body transparent, with the egg mass opaque.

Additional remarks:
  The disposition of the eggs, arranged in symmetrical patterns, in the brood pouch, is characteristic to Prodajus gastrosacci and is a specific distinguishing feature. Presence of only a single paint of brood lamellae also separates Pegastrosacci from other species of this genus.
