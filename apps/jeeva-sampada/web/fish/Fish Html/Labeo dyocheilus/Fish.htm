Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT: Warm  Water


Distribution :Doon valley, Kashmir, Poonch, Assam, all along the Himalaya, Mahanadi and Damodar rivers.

 

Back to Top

MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES: Body elongate. Snout conical, projecting beyond mouth, with a distinct lateral lobe. Eyes small, not visible from underside of head, the diameter 6 to 9 times in head.Mouth wide and inferior; lips thick, not fringed; lower lip with a fold which is interrupted and joined to isthmus by a narrow bridge. Barbels one short maxillary pair. Dorsal fin inserted equidistant .

Miscellaneous Information

This carp is a medium-sized fish and grows to 90 cm. In north Bengal it seldom grows beyond 11 cm. It is a common species in the Himalaya up to Sikkim and in Assam.
