MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES: Body subcylindrical, somewhat flattened on undersurface, its depth 3.5 to 4.5 times in standard length. Head short and bluntly pointed, its length 3.6 to, 4.3 times in standard length; snout rounded and smooth, its tip marked off by a deep transverse groove; transverse lobe at tip and sides of snout in front of nostrils covered with horny tubercles. Mouth wide; mental disc small but well developed. Barbels two pairs, shorter than eye-diameter. Dorsal fin inserted nearer tip of snout than to base of caudal fin. Pectoral fins longer than head. Caudal fin deeply emarginate. Scales moderate-size; lateral line with 31 to 34 scales; pre dorsal scales 8 to 10; breast and belly scaled but scales on breast much reduced. Distance of vent from anal fm 4 to 5.7 times in inter-distance between pelvic fin origin and anal fin base. .
