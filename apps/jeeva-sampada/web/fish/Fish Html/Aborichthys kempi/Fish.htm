Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT: Cold Water


Distribution : Abor and Garo hills, Meghalaya.

Back to Top

MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES:Body elongate and strongly compressed, of uniform depth, its depth 6.4 to 8 times in standard length. Eyes small, its diameter 6 to 8 times in head length. Mouth semicircular; lips moderately fleshy, upper lip continuous, lower interrupted in middle. Dentiform process well-developed. Barbels three short pairs, much longer than eye-diameter. Dorsal fin inserted nearer base of caudal fin than to snout-tip. Scales minute and embedded; lateral line incomplete, terminating before dorsal fin. Vent situated distinctly nearer to base of caudal fin than to snout-tip.

Miscellaneous Information

 In life, body striped, from gill-opening to above anal fin, with 18 to 21 dark brown transverse bands, inclined backwards, on a yellowish-white background, but not extending to ventral surface of body; an irregular blotch on upper surface of base of caudal fin. Dorsal fin banded with dark brown spots; caudal fin banded with two broad black conscentic curves, one

This species which attains a length of 8 cm SL
