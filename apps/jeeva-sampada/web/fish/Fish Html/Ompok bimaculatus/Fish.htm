HABIT & HABITAT: Warm Water


Distribution :Fresh waters of Kashmir, Eastern Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Manipur, Assam, West Bengal.

Back to Top

MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES:Body elongate and strongly compressed. Eyes moderate, its lower border below level of cleft of mouth. Mouth large and oblique; teeth in villiform bands in jaws; vomerine teeth in two oval patches. Barbels two pairs; maxillary barbels :long and extend to or slightly beyond anal fin origin, the mandibular pair very short. Anal fin long, inserted well behind dorsal fin.
