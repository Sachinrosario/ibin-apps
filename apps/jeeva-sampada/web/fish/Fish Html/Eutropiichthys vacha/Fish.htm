HABIT & HABITAT:Warm Water


Distribution : Freshwaters of East Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Darjeeling, Calcutta and Orissa.

Back to Top

MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES: Body elongate and compressed. Mouth large, its cleft extends to about posterior border of orbit; teeth villiform in broad bands on jaws; vomero-palatine band of teeth pyriform in shape, considerably wider than maxillary band and often so close to it that the two are indistinguishable from each other. Barbels four pairs, of varying lengths but are generally longer in young species than in adults; maxillary barbels extend slightly beyond orbit, nasal barbels rarely extend to hind edge {or slightly beyond) of head, but the mandibular ones (which arise on a transverse line across chin) are rather shorter. Dorsal fin short, inserted slightly in advance of pelvic fins (in Indian specimens), or opposite to or slightly behind origin of pelvic fins (in Burmese specimens); dorsal spine finely serrated on its posterior border. Branchiostegal rays 11.

Economic Importance

Uses: This elegant catfish is an excellent table-fish, grows to about 35 cm in length and attains 1.35 kg of weight.It rises readily to the fly and affords a good sport. The Bachacha is a voracious feeder on other smaller fish and insects. It is a surface feeder. In ordinary tanks this fish may prove very destructive to other small fish and, therefore, its culture is not recommended (Tilak and Sharma, 1982: 124).

Miscellaneous Information

 It is caught in large numbers from rivers and canals in northern India. It is easily acclimatised to lakes, large tanks and bheels. The best fishing season is from March to November when the mahseer fishing is out of question.The name Batchwa is usually applied to two species by anglers. Eutropiichthys vacha, usually called the Bachcha, is an elegant fish .
