HABIT & HABITAT: Cold Water


Distribution : Garo hills, Meghalaya.

Back to Top

MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES:Body greatly elongate with a compressed tail, the dorsal and ventral profiles almost parallel and horizontal, its depth 6.4 to 7.1 times in standard length. Eyes small, its diameter 6.1 to 7.2 times in head length. Mouth semicircular; lips fleshy, lower lip interrupted in middle. Dentiform process moderately developed. Barbels three pairs, well-developed, of equal length. Dorsal fin inserted nearer to tip of snout than to caudal fin base. Scales small, more prominent posteriorly, absent on breast; lateral line incomplete, terminating slightly behind tip of pectoral fin. Vent almost equidistant between snout-tip and base of caudal fin.

Miscellaneous Information

In life, upper surface of head marked with short black lines anastomosing with one another; body marked with 30 to 35 black forked-shaped bands, inclined backwards, narrower than interspaces, the bands broader above, narrower below and do not extend to ventral surface; a black spot at upper end of base of caudal fin. Dorsal fin banded with two or three rows


This species attains a length of3.8 cm
