HABIT & HABITAT: Warm Brakish Water


Distribution :Calcutta & Port Blair

Back to Top

MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES:Body very elongate, cylindrical anteriorly and compressed posteriorly. Head subcylindrical; eyes minute. Mouth vertical; chin with three pairs of short barbels. Teeth in bands on both jaws, about 5 canines on each side of upper, and about 4 canines on each side of lower jaw. Dorsal and anal fins densely enveloped in skin.
