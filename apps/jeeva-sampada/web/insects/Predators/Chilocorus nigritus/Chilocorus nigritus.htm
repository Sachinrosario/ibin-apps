 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The body of Chilocorus nigritus is shining black; mouth parts dark brown, eyes black pronotum, venter of thorax and legs brownish, abdominal terga dark brown; hind wings grey. Body is medium sized, rounded, dorsum strongly convex, widest in the middle of the body. Head about 0.36 times as wide as body; frons coarsely punctate; inner ocular margin straight; anterior margin of clypeus concave. Antenna eight segmented, slightly clavate. Pronotum 0.54 times as wide as the body, lateral lobes of pronotum strongly developed and anteriorly directed, discally sparsely punctate, laterally densely punctate with pale coloured setae arising from the punctures. Scutellum elongate, triangular, lateral margins longer than the basal margin, punctures coarse, more numerous with white setae. Epipluera very broad; prosternal process long. Coxal line shallowly curved and reaching anterior margin; femoral line gently curved, extends almost to the lateral margin.

Male genitalia: Sipho rather long,  thin and moderately curved, siphonal capsule with distict outer process and an inner protruberance; apex of sipho expanded. Tegmen relatively broad, lateral lobe shorter than the median lobe and narrower, parllel sided.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca sac-like with a finger-like projection anteriorly.

Measurements: Body is 4.0 mm long, 3.7 mm wide (4).
