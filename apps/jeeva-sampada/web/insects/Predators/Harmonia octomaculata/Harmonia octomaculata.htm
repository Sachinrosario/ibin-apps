 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The colouration of Harmonia octomaculata is variable; head black or pale orange with a posterior black spot; pronotum orange or reddish brown with a median black spot or without spot or with three or four spots; scutellum black; elytra including epipleura reddish brown with a variable number of black spots. Commisural line either black or brown, in paler specimens it is concolourous as the elytra; legs and venter either reddish brown or blackish with apex of front femora and tebiae dark brown, in darker specimens, a spot on the meso sternum and another on the anterior lateral corner of abdomen whitish.

Body oval, longer than broad, moderately convex and medium sized. head about 0.2 times as wide as the body, slightly wider than half of pronotum; frons shagreened, very finely punctate; interocular margin strongly divergent apically. Antenna 11 segmented, the terminal segment 1.5 times as broad as the last but one segment. Pronotum 0.36 times as wide the body, subangulate at the interior corners; surface of pronotum shagreened and finely and densely punctate. Scutellum broad triangular, the basal margin longer than the lateral margins. Elytra coarsely and sparsely punctate. Coxal line slightly curved and almost reaching the anterior margin; femoral line angulate.

Male genitalia: Sipho short, basally strongly curved, apical 0.5 straight; apex with a membranous projection having a digitae end; inner process of siphonal capsule hooked, outer process broadened. Apophysis of the ninth abdominal segment abruptly broadened at caudal end.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca broadely V-shaped, of uniform width.

Measurements: Body is 6.61 mm long, 4.90 mm wide (1).
