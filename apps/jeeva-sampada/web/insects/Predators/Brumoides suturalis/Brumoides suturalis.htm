 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The head and thorax of Three striped lady beetle is reddish brown, eyes black; pronotum yellowish brown; scutellum black; elytra yellow; commissural line except the apical 0.14 length, broadly black, a broad longitudinal stripe on each elytron black; venter black to dark brown; legs dark brown with pale tarsi.

Body oval, longer than broad, moderately convex above; head 0.35 times as long as wide as the body, sparsely fine punctate; from each punctation a hair arises; frons widened posteriorly or the inner ocular margins convergent anteriorly; apical margin of clypeus concave; pronotum 0.63 times as wide as the body with anteriorly produced lateral lobes; surface sparsely, finely punctate, hind margin broadly rounded. Scutellum triangular, lateral margins longer than basal margin. Elytra finely, sparsely, punctate, a submarginal row of punctures on lateral aspects more prominent; epipleuron with recurved pale hair. Coxal line complete.

Male genitalia: Sipho strongly curved in basal 0.33, apical 0.66 rather straight, broadened apically; siphonal capsule with inner process slender, long quadrate, outer process stout, quadrate. Tegmen with median lobe half as long as lateral lobes; lateral lobes parallel sided. Apophysis of ninth segment caudally bifid.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca strongly curved, robust with a W-shaped inner margin.

Measurements: Body is 2.9 mm long, 2.2 mm wide (3).
