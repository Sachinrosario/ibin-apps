Miscellaneous Information

The consequences of storage at different temperatures on the fecundity and longevity of Sticholotis madagassa. Newly emerged adults were collected and kept in plastic containers with folded paper strips and honey + agar medium for food. Progeny production declined with increased storage period. Storage at 5, 10, 15 and 26°C caused 81.2, 53.1, 0.29 and 0.29% mortality, resp., after 7 days; 90.0, 84.2, 0.29 and 26.1% mortality after 15 days; 90.0, 90.0, 12.8 and 90.0% mortality after 60 days and 90.0, 90.0, 81.2 and 90.0% mortality after 90 days. Storage at 15°C had relatively little effect on fecundity (2).
