 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for about 1/5 distance from vertex to antennal base, dark brown, bare, except three fairly broad vertical stripes of white pubescence. Vertical triangle elongate, the sides more than twice as long as length of base at vertex, back, with short black hairs on posterior half upto anterior ocellus; portion anterior to ocellar prominence whitish pollinose. Ocelli deep red, place almost in the verticle triangle. frontal triangle and face light yellow, with short, fairly dense concolourous hairs; a narrow pale brown median stripe on face from oral tubercle to halfway between it and antennal base, beyond which it continues as a much thinner, insignificant greyish streak. Face in profile sloping gradually upwards on to indistinct facial tubercle at 1/3 distance from antennal base to oral tubercle. Oral cavity more than three times as long as wide with dark brown edges. cheeks very small, dark brown, with fairly long, yellow white hairs arising from their border with eye-margin. occiput slightly concave, black, dusted entirely with white pollen, bearing short stout hairs throghout these being a little longer towards the middle on each side. asntennae three segmented, first two short, equal in length, third very elongate, more than twice length of firat two together, orange-brown, completely dark brown on dorsal surface; arista bare, arising from centre of third antennal segment, pale brownish orange, little more than half as long as third segment.

Thorax with mesonotum qudrate, shining black with bluish reflections, densely punctulate, a pair of greyish stripes almost united at centre of anterior margin divering and narrowing thence towards posterior margin but not reaching it; a pair of whitish transverse oval spots placed at tip of suture on each side, separated from median stripes by their half length. whole of dorsum with fine, short, yellowish pubescence which is very long on the humeral and posterior corners, longest on the former. Pleura entirely shining black; mesopleuron and sternopleuron entirely covered with long white hairs; posterior part of mesopleuron with thin, sparse, longish, yellowish hairs; popsterior margin with 10-16 serrated teeth, which in profile have hooked pointed teeth at their tips which are pale brown.

Wings clear, hyaline, trichose; stigma and base very pale yellow, veins dark brown; squamae dirty yellowish-white, leathery, with some tufts of fairly longish, concolourus hairs on hind margin. Halteres lemon-yellow.

Legs with coxae dark brown; femora light brown, yellowish at tips, more so on fore and mid pairs; hind pair with an indistinct, sometimes clear, darkish brown ring about the middle; tibiae yellowish on posterior and light brown, claws black; pubescence short, yellow except on dorsal part of brown portion of hind femora, where they are a little longer, thinner and brownish.

Abdomen ovate, gradually tapering towards the subtruncate apex; tergite 1 light brown with a transverse ridge across whole tergite towards the subtruncate apex; tergite 1 light brown with a transeverse ridge across whole tergite towards its base, anterior margin sinuate, fairly emarginate in middle; a knob-like protuberance in each posterior corner of tergite which is shining black, this colour extending forwards at the sides as a patch upto transverse ridges; anterior margin of tergite for 1/3 its total width pale yellow, shining, devoid of punctuation or pubescence; posterior 2/3 of tergite strongly punctulate with short, suberect, dark brown hairs; tergite 2 similar to basal 2/3 of first, but with lateral margin entirely shining black and all hairs subdepressed; tergite 3 similar to foregoing, but with a pair of pale yellow transverse subdepressed vittae placed on anterior margin, thier inner ends broadly rounded and not meeting each other, with concolorous hairs; tergite 4 similar to preceding, but vittae distinctly whitish, not pale yellow, with whitish pubescence and attaining lateral margins of tergite; tergite 5 asymmetrical, subtruncate at tip, vittate border, less depressed and with their inner ends closely appressed but not actually touching, entire tergite with longish, shining white suberect pubescence which extends on the lateral margins upto base of tergite 3; tergite 6 just visible from top, bulbous, tucked under preceding tergite, light brown with whitish pollen and entirely covered with shortish, fine, yellowish-white hairs.

Terminalia fairly large; tergite 9 large with posterior margin oblique, making tergite trapezoidal, covered with minute spicules; the posteroventral lobe with a distict hook at tip and posterior margin parallel to anterior, posterodorsal angle with many long hairs; sternite 9 irregular in shape; superior lobe with a long hairs; sternite 9 irregular in shape; superior lobe with a long curved spine at posterodorsal corner and covered with shortish hairs.

Female :Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space more than twice as wide at antennal base than at vertex. Frons shining black with a pair of white pollinose spots midway between ocellar prominence and antennal bases separated by a short distance in the middle and contiguous to eye margins, continuing along margin towards antennal base, gradually narrowing. Rest as in male (1).
