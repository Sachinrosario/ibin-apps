 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
A large and distinctive hoverfly with three pairs of white lunules on the abdomen. It is a common visitor to flowers in gardens, meadows and waste ground in summer. It doesn't usually survive in winters (though this may change with global warming) and arrives as a migrant and then breeds here every summer, so numbers vary from year to year (2).

Size : Adults are 1.27 cm in length.
Food :Adults: nectar and/or pollen. Larvae: aphids (1).
