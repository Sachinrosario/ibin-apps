Family: Syrphidae

Order: Diptera

 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for about half distance from vertex to frons, brownish-red, bare; facets almost uniform. slightly smaller on lower half. Vertical triangle elongate, sides twice as long as base, black, yellow-dusted behind posterior ocelli, hairs long, erect, forwardly directed at tip, yellow on posterior and black on anterior half. Frons and face yellow with concolourus tomentum and covered with fairly long yellowish hairs which are wholly black near antennal bases; face in profile with prominent tubercle, bare at tip and shining. Upper part of cheeks as rest of face, lower portion near mouth border bare, shining, orangish. Oral cavity a little more than twice as long as wide, pale yellow, wider on posterior half. Antennal prominence dark greyish posteriorly, dull orange on actual bases of antennae, shining, bare. Antennae deep orange dark brown dorsally on third segment, first segment about twice second in length, both with stiff, short black hairs on anterodorsal region, third segment oval, twice as long as first together; arista bare, brownish orange proximally,  black distally, as long as second and third antennal segments together. Occiput covered with white tomentum, a complete ring of stout white hairs, except behind vertex where they are yellow.

Thorax with mesonotum greyish black, shining with coppery or greenish reflections, some yellow tomentum own humerus base of sutures, dorsum covered with dense, fairly long pile of yellowish hairs on lateral borders, posterior calli yellow with concolourous hairs. Pleura as in mesonotum almost wholly covered with long yellow hairs, upper parts of sternal pleuron with similar hairs, rest bear. Scutellum greyish yellow, finally punctulate as mesonotum, covered wholly with long black hairs except on lateral margins and ventral side of hind margin where they are yellow.

Wings clear, trichose, stigma and base of subcostal cell greyish yellow, squamae pale yellowish white darker on hind margin with long yellow hairs on the same halters deep yellow.

Legs deep yellow; coxae, tarsi, anterior half of hind femora and hind tibiae brown; pubescence yellow except on dorsal and lateral parts of hind femora, tebiae and tarsi; claws black distally, yellow proximally.

Abdomen elongate-oval, widest at junction of tergites 2 and 3; tergite 1 shining black with bluish reflections, yellow on basal half laterally; tergite 2 brownish-black with two large irregularly oval yellow spots, confluent in the middle and reaching sides at sub-basal region; tergite 3 brownish-black with a very broad yellow and almost in the middle of segment its width two thirds two thirds length of segment; tergite 4 yellow on basal half, brownish black on apical half, brownish black on apical half; tergite 5 brownish orange. pubescence on lateral margins short, black from tip to anterior half of tergite 2, rest with longer yewllow hairs; hairs ion dorsum mixed yellow and black, but mostly concolorous with the ground colour. sides of abdomen distinctly margined.

Terminalia not very large; cercus extending caudad beyond level of posteriormost point of fusion of surstylar apodemes, with about 12-15 long hairs and several shorter ones; tergite 9 with very minute hairs covering it almost totally; surstylus short and broad, produced strongly posteroventral emargination; lingula fairly short and broad, gradually tapering to a truncate point, hairless; superior lobe directed caudad, broad basally, compressed towards apex, slightly narrowed towards base, rounded apically, anterior part of lower margin with a distinct tooth; aedeagal base dorsally fused, broadly rounded and extended ventrad, deeply notched apically; distal portion of aedegus shortish, swollen just beyond base, narrowed in middle and expanded apically wiht some short apicules on ventral surface.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space 21/2 times wider at level of antennal bases than at vertex. Vertex black on sides and a little in front of ocellar prominence, thence produced into a thin mid-dorsal line which diverges to form a black triangle below antennal prominence. Antennal prominence with two dark brown oblique spots below base of each antenna, rest of frons with whitish tomentum and with short black hairs.

Abdomen with tergite 4 yellow, with subapical brownish-black band one-third as wide as length of segments; tergite 5 yellow with an arcuate, wide brownish black band, convex and touching basal margin in middle, anterior half of tergite yellow (1).
