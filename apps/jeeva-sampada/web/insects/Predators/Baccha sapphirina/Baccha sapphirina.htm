 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for half distance from vertex to frons, bare, brownish red; facets almost uniform. Vertical traingle elongate, shining black with bluish reflections, sides twice as long as base. Ocelli pale orange, Placed at the apex with sparse greyish hairs directed towards at tip. Frons, face and cheeks shining black with bluish reflections and white tomentum along margins; frons and faace bare in centre, sparsely covered elsewhere with shortest white hairs which are greyish and longer on upper part of frons; face in profiles with tubercle prominent. Oral cavity pale, three times longer than wide, sinuate in middle. Antennal prominences shining black; antennae orange-yellow, first two segments subequal with few short black hairs on top; third segment slightly longer than first two together, brownish dorsally; arista orange yellow, bare, black at tip, as long as third antennal segment. Occiput white tomentose with a ring of sparse white hairs.

Thorax with mesonotum shining black with greenish reflections, finely punctulate, covered with short, greyish, suberect hairs which are longest on humerus; a collar of longish, stouter whitish hairs on anterior margin near occiput. Pleura shining black with spiracle yellowish, hairs longest, white. Scutellum similar to mesonotum with ventrally directed longest white hairs on apical margin.

Wings hyaline, trichose; stigma and subcostal cell down to base dark brown; a brownish spot at tip of submarginal cells below stigma infused with brown in some examples. Squame dirty white, with whitish marginal hairs. Halteres lemon-yellow.

Legs brownish orange; narrow ring of brown in centre of fore and mid tibiae; nearly whole of hind femora and apical half of hind tibiae dark brown; all tarsi dark brown; except nearly all of first segment.

Abdomen shining black, slender widest at base, thence narrowing, maximally at joint of second and third segments, then gradually widening till the tip; sides with short white hairs, shortest from tip to middle of tergite 3, slightly longer from there to middle of segment and longest thereon to base; tergite 1 with bluish reflections. whole of dorsum with mixed subdepressed, short black and white hairs.

Terminalia small; tergite 9 subquadrate, a little emarginate in middle of dorsal and ventral margins, with shortish hairs; syrstylus broadly oval, widest at base, thence narrowing into a posteroventral lobe with some stout, short, spine-like hairs; Sternite 9 with few short hairs at popsteroventral corner; superior lobe hairless; aedegus bifid.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space three times as wide as level of antennae as at vertex. Vertex upto little after apical ocellus wholly shining black, narrow strip there on meeting broader median shining black parts surrounding antennal prominence, again narrowed from base of antennae to tip of oral cavity. Rest of face and frons with close white tomentum.

Legs wholly yellow; with all tarsi brownish; apical half of hind femora and tibiae dark brown. Abdomen similar to male except for median band on tergite 3 with bluish reflections.
