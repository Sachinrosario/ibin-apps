 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The body of Rodalia fumida is uniformly reddish brown; eyes black. Body oval, slightly longer than broad, widest at the base of pronotum, moderately convex, pubescent and medium sized. Head 0.32 times as wide as the body, very finely densely punctate, inner ocular margin curved. Antenna eight segmented, pedicel slightly withdrawn into large scape. Pronotum 0.62 times as wide as the body, with well developed lateral lobes, surface finely, densely, punctate, posterior margin broadly V-shaped with lateral margins lower than the basal margins. Elytra finely, densely, punctate; epipleura moderately developed. Coxal line straight and complete; femoral line curved and complete.

Male genitalia: Sipho very strongly curved, pointed apically; siphonal tube with well developed processes, inner process longer than the outer. Tegmen slender, elongate; median lobe slightly pointed apically and slightly shorter than the apical lobes. Apophysis of the ninth abdominal segment very slender.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca bulbous, with a narrow and short base.

Measurements: Body is 4.3 mm long, 2.8 mm wide (1).
