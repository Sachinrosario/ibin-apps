 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The head, pronotum and elytra of Curinus coeruleus is shining dark blue; in certain light, purplish reflection can be seen. Labrum black, lateral lobes of pronotum orange; legs and abdominal sterna brown. Body is round, very strongly convex above, glabrous, medium sized. Head 0.27 times as wide as the body, finely punctate, antero-lateral areas slightly depressed, black setae arising from these punctures; inner ocular margin straight. Antenna 11 segmented. Pronotum 0.6 times as wide as the body, sparsely punctate, posterior margin broadly V-shaped. Scutellum triangular, basal margin slightly shorter than the lateral margins. Elytra finely, sparsely, punctate, shagreened, a few punctures before lateral edge prominent in the basal 0.67 area; epipleura well developed. Coxal line gently curved, almost reaching the anterior margin, femoral line strongly curved and slightly angulate.

Male genitalia: Sipho strongly curved at basal 0.33, apical 0.67 almost straight, distal end slightly swollen before apex; outer process of siphonal capsule weakly developed. Tegmen slender, median lobe shorter than the lateral lobes, lateral lobes constricted in basal 0.33. Apophysis of the ninth abdominal segment caudally bifid.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca slightly curved, basaly swollen and distally finger like.

Measurements: Body is 5.6 mm long, 5.0 mm wide (1).
