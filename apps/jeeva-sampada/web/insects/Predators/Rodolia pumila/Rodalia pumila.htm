 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The body of Rodalia pumila is uniformly reddish brown, pubescence grey; mouth parts dark brown; compound eyes black; venter and legs brown. Body oval, broadest at the base of elytra, medium sized, pubescent. Head, frons finely densely punctate; inner ocular margin almost straight. Antenna eight segmented clavate, basal segment stout and twice as long as the second segment prothorax finely, densely, punctate, lateral margins projected anteriorly. Scutellum large triangular, lateral margins slightly longer than the basal margin. Elytra finely, densely, punctate; epipleura modertely developed. coxal line slightly curved and reaching the anterior margin, femoral line curved and complete.

Male genitalia: Sipho long curved at basal 0.25; inner process of siphonal capsule long, outer process not well developed. Median lobe of tegmen slightly longer than the lateral lobes.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca strongly curved U-shaped, distal end slightly longer than the proximal end.

Measurements: Body is 3.5 mm long, 3.0 mm wide (1).
