 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for slightly less than half distance from vertex to frons, brownish red, bare; facets uniform in size, a little larger on upper part. Vertical triangle almost equilateral. Shining black with three pale orange ocelli placed at the apex; hairs yellow and short except on ocellar triangle where they are black and longer, all with tips forwardly projecting. Frons, face and cheeks bright, almost greenish-yellow with irregular greyish discolouration in some examples, bare, except for a few short yellowish hairs at apex of frontal triangle; face in profile with distinct tubercle, shining at tip. Oral cavity 21/2 times longer than wide, yellow. Antennal prominence pale yellow with two small, some times indistinct black spots placed medially between and a little above base of antennae. Antennae orange, third segment darker, first two segments subequal with short, stiff black hairs on anterodorsal corner, third segment broadly oval, a little longer than first two together; arista brown, as long as second and third segments together, tip black, bare. Occiput greyish with white tomentum, fairly emarginate laterally, hairs thinner and yellowish on upper and white, stouter on lower halves.

Thorax with mesonotum longer than wide, oblong, shining brownish-black with two thin darker stripes on each side of mid-dorsal line, laterqal margin with a fairly broad yellow stripe, broadest at anterior margin, narrowing towards posterior margin which it reaches; dorsum punctulate, the punctures not impressed, covered with short, fairly dense yellowish hairs. Pleura shining black with most of the upper sclerites yellow; hairs sparse and yellow. Scutellum bright yellow, covered with longish yellow hairs and a few black hairs in the centre of posterior margin. Wings hyaline, trichose; stigma and base yellowish; squamae very small with few marginal hairs. Halteres yellow.

Legs yellow; tips of tibiae and all tarsi orange. Hairs yellow except on mid and hind femora, tibiae and tarsi dorsally; mid femora on inner side at distal end with long black hairs. Claws black yellow proximally, black distally.

Abdomen with tergites 1 shinig black except at sides where it is greyish yellow; tergite 2 shining black with a median broad yellow transverse band reaching sides and a little produced in the middle; tergites 3 to 5 yellow with darker apical margins. Abdomen slender, narrow than thorax, widest at the base of tergite 5; narrow and constricted at joint of tergites 2 and 3. Pubescence on margin short and yellow except on basal half of tergite 2 where they are longer. Pubescence on dorsum yellow and subdepressed except along mid dorsal line and along apical margins of tergites where they are black.

Terminalia very large; tergite 9 large, subquadrate and covered with minute hairs; cerci very small, almost indistict; surstylus also large and complex, with a posterodorsal projection bearing a very long tuft of hairs; a longer posteroventral projection truncate apically with a few fairly long hairs and bearing small spicules basally in two rows on each margin; sternite 9 irregular and complex in shape with some small setae at posteroventral area and a narrow projection bearing hairs posterodorsally; superior lobe elongate with a narrow subacute apex and the whole bearing numerous short spicules.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, width of the interocular space at level of antennal bases about twice that of vertex; antennal prominence; rest of frons and face greenish-yellow.

Thorax with two greyish broad stripes on mesonotum separated by a thin black mid-dorsal line, distinct. Pleura more yellow than black.

Abdomen with first two tergites similar to male; tergites 3 and 4 also black with a median yellow stripe placed more towards base; tergites 5 and 6 black with a pair of basal yellow bands narrowing in centre but not confluent; tergite 7 just visible, yellowish. More black hairs on abdomen than in male. Abdomen less constricted and almost subparallel and uniform in width than in male. Last three segments graduated (1).
