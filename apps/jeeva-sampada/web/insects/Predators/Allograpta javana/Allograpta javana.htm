 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for half distance from vertex to frons, bare, brownish red; factes larger on upper side. Vertical triangle black, elongates, sides more than twice length of base; ocelli red, placed at tip of verticle triangle; hairs greyish yellow, their tips directed strongly forwards. Frons, face and cheeks bright lemon-yellow, covered sparsely with yellowish hairs, longest on frons; a broad, shining black median stripe from lower part of facial tubercle up to base of antanne; face in profile with tubercle prominent, directed slightly ventral; lower part of cheeks blackish. Oral cavity a little more than twice as long as broad, borders yellow. Upper side of antennal prominence with a large, black shining spot; antennal prominence dull orange; antennae bright orange, dorsum of third segment dark brown; first two segments subequal with short black, stiff bristles on anterodorsal corner; third segment about as long as first two together; arista orange, bare, black at tip, as long as second and third segments together. occiput very narrow on upper half, dirty grey with short yellow hairs, broader on lower half, white tomentose, with longer and stouter clean white hairs.

Thorax with mesonotum shining black, with greenish cupreous reflections and a fairly broad yellow stripe on lateral margin; dorsum with shortest yellow fairly dense hairs, longest on humerus; pubescence on hind 1/4 dorsum dark grey, not yellow; no hairs on anterior most portion of mesonotum. Pleura shining black except for five yellow spots; posterior spiracles also yellow; pubescence rahher sparse, yellow. Scutellum yellow, brownish in centre, covered fairly densely with longish black hairs, longest on posterior margin.

Wings with base dark brown, stigma yellow; membrane clear, trichose; second spurious vein in anal cell; squamae greyish with marginal hairs yellowish - grey. Halteres orange.

Legs with coxae brownish; fore and hind femora and tibiae bright yellow; all tarsi brown; hind femora yellow, apical third black; hind tibiae black with a median, fairly broad yellow ring. Claws black distally, yellow proximally. Pubescence yellow, black on upper side of tarsi, near tip of fore femora, lower half of mid femora, lower side of hind femora, apical half of same on upper side, dorsal and lateral surfaces of hind tibiae.

Abdomen with sides subparallel, widest at junction of tergites 2 and 3, a little constricted at joint of tergites 3 and 4; tergite 1 yellow, blackish medially; tergite 2 shining black with a pair of transeverse, almost oblong-ovate yellow spots, their inner ends rounded, not meeting in centre, outer ends confluent with lateral margins; tergites 3 and 4 shining black with arcuate yellow bands across middle of segment but more towards base in centre, meeting lateral margins; tergite five yellow with black spots on posterior and anterior corners and a median black stripes; anterior border narrowly yellow.

Terminalia with tergite 9 subquadrate with a blunt projection at the anterodorsal corner, minutely haired; surstylus short, broadly oval with posteroventral angle subacute with longish hairs on the anterior half; sternite 9 with truncate posterior margin haired; superior lobe subtriangular with posterior angles slightly projected outwards; a few small hairs at basal margin; distal portion of aedeagus very short, subcylindrical, membraneous apically, bare.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space three times as wide at level of antennae than at vertex. Vertex and frons for a short distance after anterior ocellus shining black, thence produced into a median stripe, narrowly at first, gradually diverging until bases of antennae; rest of frons, face and cheeks bright yellow; median stripe on face less black, more greyish, shining; hairs on frons yellow, erect shortest, shorter just above antennae and black.

Abdomen parallel sided, gradually narrowing from tergite 4 to tip; colouring as in male, but black parts more brownish; median stripe on tergite 5 contiguous with spots on anterior corners; tergite 6 just visible, brownish black, pubescence consisting of short, subdepressed black hairs except on tergite 1 and basal two-thirds of tergite 2. On sides consisting of very short black hairs from tip to apical third of tergite 2; rest with yellow longer hairs, longest on posterior corners of tergite 2 (1).
