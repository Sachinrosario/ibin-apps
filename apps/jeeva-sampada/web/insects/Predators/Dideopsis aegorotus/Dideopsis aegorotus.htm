 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for half the distance between vertex and frons, dark brown; facets uniform, slightly larger on upper half, bare. vertical triangle elongate, 21/2 times as long as wide at base, black. Ocelli reddish, small, placed at anterior tip of vertical triangle. Vertical triangle with short, suberect black hairs. Frontal triangle black upto level of antennae, covered with longish, erect black hairs. Face white with concolourous dense tomentum and white hairs, median stripe black, broad, 1/3 as wide as width of face; cheeks dark brown, almost devoid of hairs face in profile with tubercle prominent. Antennae brownish orange, first two segments equal in length, with shortest, stiff black hairs; third segment 21/2 times as long as first two together, dark brown dorsally; arista dark brown, bare as long as third antennal segment. Oral cavity 21/2 to 3 times as long as wide, with pale borders. Occiput black, with lower part white pollinose, the whole with stiff white hairs which are distinctly yellow behind vertex.

Thorax with mesonotum shining black, with faint brownish borders and suture, finely punctulate, covered with shortest black hairs, lateral borders with brownish yellow hairs; humerus with long, golden yellow pubescence; a dense collar of long, stiff, erect golden yellow hairs on anterior margin. Pleura black with white tomentum on mesopleura and sternopleura, anterior mesopleuron with dense, long yellow hairs on upper hind border and white on rest; posterior mesopleuron and hypopleuron with brownih hairs; upper half of sternopleuron with whitish hairs. Scutellum yellow, covered entirely with longish, black, suberect hairs, longer on hind border.

Wings clear, trichose, with a conspicuous, broad, oblique, dark brown stripes across middle of wing from costal to anal margins, occupying middle 1/3 of wings; base of wing and stigma brownish. Halteres yellow.

Legs with coxae dark brown; fore and mid femora yellow, basal 1/3 blackish brown; frons and mid tibiae yellow; tarsi blackish brown except base of first segment which is yellow; hind legs completely blackish brown. Pubescence concolourous with ground colour, tarsi with short, stiff black hairs, claws black distally, yellow proximally.

Abdomen elongate oval, broad in the middle, tapering at the both ends; tergite 1 black; tergite 2 black with a pair of irregularly yellow oval spots placed obliquely from hind corners, their inner ends rounded, not contiguous; tergite 3 with basal half yellowish, apical half black; tergite 4 similar to preceding but black area more extensive, anterior margin narrowly paler; tergite 5 black. Pubescence almost completely black except on basal parts of tergite 2 and 3.

Terminalia with tergite 9 broadly quadrate, slightly narrower and produced anteriorly, covered with minute hairs; surstylus broad, short, broadly rounded apically, with many fairly long hairs; sternites 9 narrowed posteriorly and anteriorly, broad in middle; superior lobe broad basally, subsemicircular in profile but with apex produced into a curved, strong, tooth like projection; aedeagus with a posterodorsal proceess in middle, the distal tip membraneous, fairly compressed.

Female: similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space 1/4 as wide at vertex as at level of antennae. Vertex and frons shining black with voilet reflections and with a whitish dusted spot on each side on eye margin just above antennal prominence. frons with short, black erect hairs. Facial tubercle not as prominent as in male. Thorax with scutellum having more yellowish hairs than in male. Abdomen with yellow spots on tergite 2 contiguous on inner ends (1).
