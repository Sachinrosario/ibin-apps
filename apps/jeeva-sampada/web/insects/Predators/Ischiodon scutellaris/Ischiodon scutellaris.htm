 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The Ischiodon scutellaris's eyes are glabrous. Antenna with segment 3 only twice as long as broad. Abdomen flat or slightly convex dorsally; slightly, but distinctly margined on tergites 2 to 5. Lateral mesonotal margin bright yellow, distinctly demarcated from dark dorsum. Lower lobe of squama bare above. Anterior flat portion of mesopleuron bare, with only microscopic hairs and not with long hairs. Metasternum bare. Sternopleural hair patches separated posteriorly. Abdominal tip in ventral view symmetrical in female, asymmetrical in male (1).

Common Hover Flies may sometimes confused with bees or wasps because of their mimic colour. Their bodies are medium to slender. On their abdomen there are the yellow-black wasps pattern and the narrow waist mimic pattern. Common Hover Flies visit flowers as bees and wasps. They are usually seen hovering or resting on flowers. The flies feed on nectar and are the pollinators of plants as well. Common Hover Fly rests on stick during the evening. This Hover fly hovering among the hibiscus plants searching for aphids. Their larvae are the predator of aphids. After mating, female Common Hover Fly look for the good site to lay her eggs. Usually somewhere near the aphids feeding place. Aphid-eating hover fly larvae are legless and maggot-like. They are creamy-white to brown in colour, the above picture shows the larvae is searching its prey in a aphids colony. They pupate on the stem of plants which is dark brown in colour. They turn into adults in about a week. Adults look for mate and start the new life cycle (2).

Adult (Male/Female): The adults are yellow and black in colour look like bees or a wasps. They have the typical fly head with large pair of red compound eyes and very short antenna.

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for less than half the distance from vertex to frons, brownish red, bare, facets larger just behind frons. Vertical triangle elongate, balck, sides little more than twice length of base; portion on vertex behind basal ocelli yellow dusted, rest shining black; hairs short, erect, yellow behind ocelli, black in front and on ocellar prominence. Frons, face and cheeks yellow, shining, brighter on frons, with short, sparse, yellow hairs, darker on frons; face in profile with prominent tubercle directed a little ventral, oral cavity over as long as broad, pale yellow. Antennal prominence bare, shining; antennae deep orange, first two segmnets subequal, first a little langer, with short, black, stiff bristles on anterodorsal region; third segment brownish

above 21/2 times as long as first two together; arista bare, orange, black at tip, 2/3 as long as third antennal segment. occiput with white tomentum and fringed with shortish stiff white hairs which are golden yellow just behind vertex.

Thorax with mesonotum black, broadly yellow on lateral margins, colour not reaching posterior corners, finely punctulate and covered with shortish, rather sparse yellow hairs which are longer and darker on side margins. Pleura shining black, hind half of posterior mesopleuron white; sternopleuron with an oval white spot on upper part of posterior border, perpendicular to mesopleural spot and contiguous with it; spiracle above hind coxa also white; hairs white on white portions, pale yellow elsewhere. Scutellum yellow, little darkened with brown in centre; hairs shortish, golden yellow.

Wings clear, membrane a little wrinkled, sparsely trichose; stigma and base barely yellow with a second spurious vein below anterior margin of anal cell; squamae dirty yellow, brighter on hind margin, with yellow hairs on the same. halters lemon yellow.

Legs yellow; fairly broad, subapical brownish ring on hind femur, similar but indistinct median ring on hind tibia; tips of all tarsi blackish, rest of hind tarsi and tibiae brownish-yellow; pubescence yellow except on dorsum of hind tibiae and tarsi.

Abdomen elongate oval, widest at middle of tergite 4; tergite 1 shining black, yellow at sides; tergite 2 shining brownish-black with a pair of median horizontal yellow spots, narrower and rounded at inner ends but not confluent, outer ends meeting side margins; tergites 3 and 4 brownish-black with a slightly curved broad yellow band half as wide as length of segment, laterally reaching margins, not attaining hind margin in centre; tergite 5 and 6 wholly brownish-yellow. pubescence on lateral margins short and black exept on tergite 1 and 2 where they are longer and yellow; pubescence on dorsum concolourous, black on tergite 5 but yellow on tergite 6; sides of abdomen margined.

Terminalia large; tergite 9 normal in shape; surstylus elongate, directed caudad, compressed, slightly broadened beyond base with ventral side straight, dorsal side gradually curved and serrate, apex acute and somewhat thorn-shaped, whole of lateral surface except basal 1/3 covered with shortish, stiff uniform hairs; sternite 9 bare except for some short pile near apex of ventral margin, smaller than tergite 9 and broadly quadrate in shape; superior lobe elongate, broad basally, tapering irregularly to rounded apex, base ending ventrolaterally in a blunt, dark tubercle and dorsolaterally in a spine; aedeagus with distal tip strongly sclerotized, with a subcylindrical, slightly curved, truncate anteroventral process at mid length, apex consisting of two divergent compressed plates with tip serrate.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes separated, interocular space little more than twice as wide at level of antennal base than at vertex; frons black for a short distance after front ocellus and then gradually narrowing into a median stripe, slowly diverging again to reach base of antennal prominence; hind part of antennal prominence as in male.

Abdomen as in male; yellow band on tergite 4 attaining hind margin in centre; anterior margin narrowly on tergite 4,5,6 and 7; rest black on tergites 5, 6 and 7 except at posterior side corners; tergites 5, 6 and 7 telescoped, graduated; tergite 8 black, except for orange basal median stripe little less than 1/3 as wide as segment, fading out in middle (3).
