 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Male: Head with eyes contiguous for about 1/5 distance from vertex to antennal base, dark brown, bare, except three fairly broad vertical stripes of white pubescence. Vertical triangle elongate, the sides more than twice as long as length of base at vertex, back, with short black hairs on posterior half upto anterior ocellus; portion anterior to ocellar prominence whitish pollinose. Ocelli deep red, place almost in the verticle triangle. frontal triangle and face light yellow, with short, fairly dense concolourous hairs; a narrow pale brown median stripe on face from oral tubercle to halfway between it and antennal base, beyond which it continues as a much thinner, insignificant greyish streak. Face in profile sloping gradually upwards on to indistinct facial tubercle at 1/3 distance from antennal base to oral tubercle. Oral cavity more than three times as long as wide with dark brown edges. cheeks small, black, with shorter hairs arising from their border with eye-margin. Hairs on occiput uniform thruoghout, occiput slightly concave, black, dusted entirely with white pollen, bearing short stout hairs throghout these being a little longer towards the middle on each side. antennae three segmented, first two short, equal in length, third very elongate, more than twice length of first two together, completely darker dorsal surface; arista bare, arising from centre of third antennal segment, pale brownish orange, little more than half as long as third segment.

Thorax with median pair of stripes contiguous at centre of anterior margin, tips not reaching anterior margin; sutural transverse spots not oval. Scutellar teeth with terminal hooks not light brown but distinctly black. Pleura entirely shining black; mesopleuron and sternopleuron entirely covered with long white hairs; posterior part of mesopleuron with thin, sparse, longish, yellowish hairs; popsterior margin with 10-16 serrated teeth, which in profile have hooked pointed teeth at their tips which are pale brown.

Wings clear, hyaline, trichose; stigma and base very pale yellow, veins dark brown; squamae dirty yellowish-white, leathery, with some tufts of fairly longish, concolourus hairs on hind margin. Halteres lemon-yellow.

Legs with coxae dark brown, fore and mid femora light brown, apical 1/3 pale yellow; hind femora with a complete pitchy brown to black, broad median ring, light brown at base, apical 1/4 pale yellow; tibiae sometimes darker; tarsi light brown on fore and mid pairs, hind tarsi with first segment thickened, dark brown; fourth and fifth segments light brown. Claws black; pubescence on legs yellow, sparse and thin except on hind tibiae and tarsi where they are thicker and denser.

Abdomen quadrangular, distinctly truncate posteriorly; tergite 1 completely black on basal 2/3, strongly punctulate, apical 1/3 shining, devoid of punctulation. Light reddish-brown with sinuate anterior margin where it is light brown; centre of anterior margin with a black triangular spot with apex directed distal; black on lateral sides attaining anterior margin; pubescence similar to tergite 1, longest on shoulders; tergite 3 black, punctulate except on anterior margin which is shining; posterior margin light brown; vittate border, less depressed and with their inner ends closely appressed but not actually touching, entire tergite with longish, white suberect pubescence which extends on the lateral margins upto base of tergite 3. Tergite 4 black with white vittae; tergite 5 black, posterior margin truncate, a pair of oblique white spots, pubescence fairly long, white as on lateral edges of whole abdomen; tergite 6 as in serratus, light brown to black, with pubescence similar to preceding segment.

Terminalia larger, tergite 9 almost oblong in shape with opposite sides subparallel except for a concavity for receiving the cercus, densely haired; posteroventral lobe broader, without apical hook; superior lobe without long curved spine.

Female: Similar to male but with the following differences. Head with eyes not contiguous but separated.

Abdomen with tergite 5 with posterior 1/3 slightly raised; sixth and succeeding tergites gradually tapering, not bulbous as in male (1).
