 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The head of lady beetle is pale yellow colour; dorsal colouration vary variable. In paler specimens, pronotum yellow with a median half-moon-shaped marking connected with posterior marginal stripe, black. Sometimes these areas are extensively black; each elytron with three transverse irregular spots; commissural line black. Scutellum black. Body is medium sized, elongate oval, dorsum relatively strongly convex and globrous. Head 0.3 times as wide as body; slightly narrower than half pronotum, shagreened and very finely punctate. Antenna 11 segmented, clavate, scape twice as long as the pedicel, broadened anteriorly. Pronotum 0.59 times as wide as the body, shagreened and very finely, densely, punctate. Scutellum triangular, slightly wider than long, finely and sparsely punctured. Punctuation on elytra distictly coarser and sparser than on pronotum. Coxal line almost straight and reaching the anterior margin; femoral line almost reaching the posterior margin and the external margin of sternum.

Male genitalia: Sipho small, slender, distally thread-like; siphonal capsule well developed, inner process quadrate and outer process pointed. Median lobe of tegmen as long as the lateral lobes. Caudal end of apophyses of the ninth abdominal segment with a rounded margin.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca short, stout and slightly curved; base broader.

Measurements: Body is 5.02 mm long, 4.6 mm wide (4).

The lady beetles catch slow-moving prey. When disturbed, they fly off. Both the adults and larvae prey on small planthopper adults, nymphs, and eggs. They can consume 5 to 10 prey daily. Development from egg to adult takes 6 to 10 weeks. A single beetle can lay as many as 150 to 200 eggs (2).

Adult (Male/Female):

The Menochilus sexmaculatus is a black-spotted lady beetle. The long and narrow black band at the pronotal base is connected to the transverse oval black discal spot by a short and narrow longitudinal constriction or line. Each elytron has two wavy black bands and a rounded black sub-apical spot and a narrow black band is present on both anterior edges of the elytron (2).
