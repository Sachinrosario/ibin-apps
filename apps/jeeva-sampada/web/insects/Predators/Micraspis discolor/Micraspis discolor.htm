 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
The head of Micraspis discolor is yellow often with a large median black spot; compound eyes black; mouth parts brown; pronotunm yellow laterally, medially black; scutellum black; elytra orange brown with black comminssural line, anterior 0.25 much wider, lateral margin fiscous; front legs yellowish brown; mid and hind coxae black; venter black.

Body elongate oval, dorsum convex, globrous and medium sized. Head 0.36 times as wide as the body, finely, densely, pitted; inner ocular margin curved. Antenna 11 segmented long, last three segments gradually enlarging. Elytra finely punctate, punctures dense compared to pronotum; epipleura well developed. coxal line almost straight and complete; femoral line straight and incomplete.

Male genitalia: Sipho curved at basal 0.33, apical 0.67 nearly straight; siphonal capsule well developed, siphonal tip spatulate with hooked process. Tegmen long, the lateral lobes longer than the median lobes.
