 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The head of Micraspis cardoni is yellowish brown; compound eyes black; mouth parts dark brown; prontum orange yellow with two median discal spots and posterior marginal transverse band (medially constricted) black; scutellum orange yellow with black margins; elytra orange yellow with black commissural line, two black J-shaped stripes; lateral margins narrowly piceous; venter and legs brown. Body round, dorsally convex, globrous and medium sized. head finely, sparsely, punctate. Antenna 11 segmented. Pronotum 0.55 times as wide as the body, finely, sparsely, punctate, lateral lobes projected anteriorly. Scutellum triangular. Elytra finely, sparsely, punctate, epipleura well developed. Coxal line slightly curved and complete; femoral line straight and almost reaching the lateral margin.

Male genitalia: Sipho slightly curved in anterior 0.25; siphonal capsule well developed; inner proicess slightly curved inwards, apex of sipho with bilobed membranous structure, lateral lobes of tegmen longer than the median lobe, median lobe flattened, much broadened apically.

Female genitalia: Spermatheca broad basally and apically narrow, finger like.

Measurements: Body is 4 mm long, 3.7 mm wide (1).
