 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The female head and antenna mostly black, clava yellow; dorsum of thorax dark brown or black, mesopleuron yellow. Head about 2.5 times as wide as fronto-vertex; scape about twice as long as wide; pedicel slightly longer than first funicle segment; sub-marginal vein about 5-6 times as long as marginal and post marginal veins together about one half longer than stigmal vein (3).
