 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Description. Female: Length, approx. 1.9mm. Colour about as in H. eytelweinii, but F5 and F6 white; mid femur and tibia yellowish brown, paler than hind legs; fore wing infuscation as in Fig. 16, the basal infuscate and setose area relatively larger.

Structurally very similar to eytelwenii otherwise details as in: figures and the following characters: Mandible with two teeth and a short truncation (Fig. 17). Pedicel nearly 3.0x as long as broad; funicle segments relatively shorter.

Miscellaneous Information

Holotype: ♀ (on slide under 4 coverslips): INDIA: Uttar Pradesh: Aligarh, xi.1980, M. Rayat. The holotype in Hayat collection pending deposition in some museum.

Comments: H. formosus, sp. nov. appears related to H. mexicanus Tim­berlake (1919), but differs in body, colour and relative dimensions of the funi­cle segments as noted in the key (1).
