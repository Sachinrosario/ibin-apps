 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female: Yellow, wings hyaline; eyes, ocelli, red.

Antenna: Scape 21/2 times pedicel, funicle 1 ring-like, funicle 2 triangular; club about double scape, linear sensilla undiscernable, 11 sensilla present on nontype.

Thorax: Mesonotum with 3x2 setae, sculpture subhexagonal to elongate, scutellum with 2x2 setae and placode sensillum anteriorly lateral of posterior setae.

Legs: Leg 1, Femur some what longer than tebia, basitarsus double any other tarsal segment; leg2, femur somewhat shorter than tibia, basitarsus longer than twice any other segment, spur half basitarsus; leg 3, femur shorter than tibia, basitarsus equals tarsi 2 and 3 combined, spur equals 2/5 basitarsus.

Wings: Forewing about 2.5 times longer than wide, width 5.6 times longest marginal fringe, marginal almost double stigmal, area posterior to submarginal with no setae, wing with 8 cilia, marginal with 3 prominent setae, wing disc excluding cilia, and circummarginal setae, covered with about 90 setae averaging 13.8 per 6.4 square millimeters; Hindwing, about 6 times longer than wide, width 1.2 times longest fringe.

Abdomen: Tip protrudes 60µ beyond cercal plates, of sub abdominal setae 2 large, each surrounded by 4 smaller ones visible.

Ovipositor length subequal to antennal club:

Measurements: Antenna ( µ length/width) radicle 42/17, scape 125/30, pedicel 50/25, funicle I ring-like 3/10, funicle II triangular 6/12, club 240/35, mesoscutum 120µ long, scutellum 90 µ long; leg I coxa 80, trochanter 50, femur 170, tebia 145, tarsus I 60, II 35, III 30, IV 30, spur 45 µ long; leg 3 coxa 140, trochanter 60, femur 200, tebia 235, tarsus I 80, II 50, III 30, IV 35, spur 30µ  long; Forewing long 550, wide 265, longest fringe 50, marginal 90, stigma 50 µ ; Hindwing long 490, wide 80, longest fringe 65, marginal 320 µ , ovipositor 220 µ long.

Male: Similar to Female except for: Antenna dominated by extensive club, some what over 5 times scape.

Wings: Forewing about 2.3 times longer than wide, width 4.4 times marginal fringe, marginal somewhat longer than stigmal, 7 cilia present, setal density similar to females; Hindwing 6.6 times longer than wide, width equals longest fringe

Abdomen: Allotype dissected beyond recognition; Aedegus with hook like digiti (2).
