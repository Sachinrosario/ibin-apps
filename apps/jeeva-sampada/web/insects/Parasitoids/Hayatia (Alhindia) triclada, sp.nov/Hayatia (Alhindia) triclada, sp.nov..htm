 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
Male: Length, 0.55 mm-0.65 mm. Body pallid ( Becomes white after clearing in KOH and mounting in balsam), except as follows; occiput from foramen downwards brown; pronotum anterior to collar, prosternum. propleuron, meseforamen downwards brown; pronotum anterior to collar, prosternum. propleuron, mesepisternum in ventral half or so, mesepimeron in dorsal half, metapleuron, sides of propodeum, dark brown; mid lobe of mesoscutum except sides, posterior fourth or so and a median strip. brownish; gaster with alternating cross-bands of dark brown and pallid on T I and T II; rest of terga, except apex of T VII, dark brown; in a distended gaster, T I to T III with dark brown cross bands alternating with pallid bands; the red like structure supporting the genetalia, dark brown. Antennal radicle and scape pallid; pedicel, funicle and clava brownish. Wings hyaline to subhyaline; fore wing with submarginal vein, parastigma and stigma, a small appended to proximal end of parastigma and another appended to stigmal vein, brown. Legs pallid to nearly white, with hind coxae except apices and third tarsal segments of all tarsi brown.

Frontovertex with transverse reticulations and with 8 long, hyaline setae ( 4 around vertexo-occipital margin, 2 behind anterior ocellus and 2 on frons); ocellar triangle with apical angle strongly obtuse; posterior ocellar line about 2x of ocello-ocular line. Antennae; scape subequal in length to pedicel, anellus and funicle segment combined, or to third segment of clava. Thorasic dorsum with setae.Fore wing, 2.68x-2.78x as long as broad;marginal fringe 3.16-3.2x as long as width of disc. Gaster about 1.55 x as long as thorax. Phallobase: mid tibia in holotype, 50:70 (1).
