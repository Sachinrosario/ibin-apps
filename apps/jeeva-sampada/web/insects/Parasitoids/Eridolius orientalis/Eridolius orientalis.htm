 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Diagnosis: Close to E. clauseni in having lower tooth of mandibles longer than the upper, petiole with basolateral flanges, tergite 1 rugose, and tergite 2 coriaceo-rugose basally. Distinguished from that species by its smaller size (6-8 mm. long) and by having the face and lower part of mesopleurum shallowly and sparsely punctate. The fore and middle coxae and trochanters are lighter in colour than the rest of fore leg.

Male and female: Face shiny and with scattered punctures. Punctures saperated from each other by more than 2.0x their diameter, crowded below just anterior scrobes. Frons with small close punctures, but sparser than clauseni. Clypeus convex and largely smooth and shiny. Malar space 0.6x the basal width of mandible. Interocellar distance 0.5-0.56x the ocellocular distance. Hypostomal carina at same level as occipital carina at their point of junction.

Mesoscutum convex, shiny, and with minute punctures. Notauli deeply impressed anteriorly and extending to middle of mesoscutum. Punctures on mesopleurum less coarsely and extending to middle of mesoscutum. Punctures on mesopleuron less coarsely punctate that in clauseni. Propodeal carinae usually less pronounced. Areola wider, 1.5-2.0x as long as wide, sometimes as wide as long. second lateral area as in Figs. 7 and 10, comparatively longer than in clauseni.

Tergite 1 and 2 more coarsely rugose (Figs. 8, 9, 11). Dorsolateral carina of tergite 1 stouter and reaching almost to apex of tergite. Ovipositor stout, decurved.

Colour: The body, clypeus and face is black, mandibles, tegula, fore and middle coxae and trochanters and apices of tergites, yellow. Fore and middle femora, tebiae and tarsi yellowish-brown. Sometimes middle tibia marked with browanish black. Hind coxa and femur reddish-brown, trochanters yellow to yellowish-brown, and tarsus brownish to brownish-black. Female sub-genital plate black with yellow border.

Length: Body 6-8 mm; fore wing 5.5-7.5 mm.

Holotype: Female, INDIA: Himachal Pradesh: Dalhousie, 2132 m., 17. VIII. 1971, Joseph Coll. (Gupta). Paratypes: 4 males and 13 females.  INDIA: Himachal Pradesh: Dalhousie, 2131 m., 3 females, 15.
