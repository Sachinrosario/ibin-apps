 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
Female; Length, holotype, ca 0.98 mm. Body and legs pale yellow, except as follows; malar space and mouth margin pale brown; anterior fifth or so of mesoscutum, scuto-axillar sutures, tegulae in distal half and posterior margin of scutellum, metanotum and propodeum completely brown; gaster especially last 2-3 terga and sides narrowly of these terga, pale infuscate brown; last segment of tarsi pale brown; tips of mandibles reddish brown. Antennal radicle yellow; scape yellow brown, dorsally brownish; pedicel brownish, dorsally dark brown; funicle and clava dark brown.

Head densely setose; vertex with fine strigose reticulations; frons appear smooth; malar space with fine longitudinally drawn-out cells; eyes anteriorly converging so that the width of face anteriorly is much less than width of frontovertex at anterior ocellus; eyes densely setose, setae pale

each seta as long as a facet.

Antenna with ventral length of pedicel nearly as long as F1 and 2 combined; F1 slightly longer than broad, other segments slightly broader than long. In the paratype, F1 only 1.3x as long as F2 and F2-5 quadratic.

Relative measurement; Head frontal width-31.75; head frontal weight-25.5; frontovertex width at anterior ocellus-19; eye length - 16.5; malar space-8.5, Antennal scape length-11.5; pedicel length-5.5; funicle length-19; claval length-13.

Thorax with mesoscutum and scutellum with very fine reticulations, not clearly visible at 400x; scutellum with atleast 30 pairs of setae; sides of propodeum with numerous setae; setae on thorasic dorsum pale. Linea calva interrupted by 2-3 setae.

Relative measurements: Thorax length-37, Mesoscutum length-16, scutellum length-15, Fore wing length-70, Venation length from base-40, marginal fringe length-5 (1).
