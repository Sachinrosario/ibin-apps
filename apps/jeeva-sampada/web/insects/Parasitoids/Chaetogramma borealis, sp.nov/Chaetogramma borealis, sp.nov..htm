 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
Female; Length, holotype, 0.74 mm. Head with frontovertex pallid white, malar space and mouth margin brown; occiput from level of foramen downwards dark brown; pronotum except medially, propleuron, anterior and posterior margins of mesopleuron in upper two-thirds, largely metapleuron and two narrow longitudianal bands on mid lobe of mesoscutum, dark brown; rest of thoracic dorsum pallid white; gaster ( slightly distended in holotype), with the dark brown bands on T1-V alternating with narrow yellow bands, posterior half of TVI and whole TVII. Antennae pale brownish yellow, with dorsal margin of scape and basal half of pedicel dark brown. Mandibles in distal half reddish brown. Wings hyaline; fore wing with a strong infuscation under all of marginal vein and parastigma to posterior margin, subhyaline below aubmarginal vein; parastigma to postrior margin, subhyaline below submarginal vein; parastigma, distal half or so of marginal vein and stigmal vein, dark brown. Legs pallid to white, except as follows; fore coxae at base on anterior surface, mid femora, hind coxae, hind femora and hind tibiae

except bases and apices, dark brown; fore femora, fore and mid tibiae except bases and apices, pale brown; third tarsal segment of all legs brown; hind tarsal segments 1 and 2 pale brown; rest of leg parts pallid to white.

Head: Frontovertex with transversely elongate reticulations and with about 18 strong setae; eyes with transparent setae, each not longer than diameter of a facet; malar space less than half eye length; the distance between a torulus and mouth margin about twice the distance between a torulus and eye margin.

Thorax with setation and relative dimensions of sclerites; in the holotype the postmarginal vein is rather distinct. Holotype; fore wing length, width, marginal fringe length and length of veins; 52:25:4:21.5: hind wing length, width, marginal fringe length. 40:4:4 (1).
