 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
Female: length, 0.67mm. Head brown with frontovertex white; thorax brown; gaster dorsum with broad brownish bands alternating with narrow pallid bands on terga 1-5, TI - TV); T VI and T VII completely brownish. Antennae pallid. Fore wings infuscate from base up to level of stigmal vein, beyond that subhyaline or very lightly infuscate; veins dark brown. Hind wings subhyaline, submarginal vein brown. legs largely dark brown; coxae, trochanters, fore and mid femora except epical fourths or so, hind femora, hind tibiae except distal fourths or so, dark brown; fore and mid tibiae indistinctly pale brown; third tarsal segment of all legs pale brown; rest of leg parts pallid.

Head: Frontovertex with long and strong setae; eyes with transparent setae, each seta about as long as diameter of a facet. Mandibles with 2 teeth and a truncation. Maxillary palps unsegmented.

Thorax: Thorax with mid lobe of mesoscutum about 2.0x as long as scutellum; setae on tergites as follows; pronotum 2+1+1+2; mid lobe, 1+1; each side lobe, 1; each axilla, 1; scutellum, 1+1; propodeum with one seta anterior to each spiracles. fore wing length, 72.5; fore wing width, 28; length venation,29; marginal fringe length,12. hind wing length,65;width 3.5; marginal range, 13; hind wing disc with a single line of dorsal setae and two lines of ventral setae. Legs long and slender (1).
