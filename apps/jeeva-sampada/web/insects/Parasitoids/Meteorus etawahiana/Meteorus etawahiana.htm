 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female: Body length, 4.3 mm; fore wing-3.5 mm.

Colour: Yellowish brown; mandibles, legs including coxae. Second metastomal tergite and ovipositor yellowish; maxillary palp and labial palp yellow; metanotum, ovipositor sheaths and first metasomal tergite brown; stemmaticum black; ocelli transperent; eyes grayish: wing veins brownish yellow.

Head: Width of head in dorsal view 1.65x its length; occipital carina complete, dorso-medially shortly interrupted; OOL; POL; AOL; QOD= 5:5:3:4: length of eye in  dorsal view 1.35x its width and 2.11x temple; eyes distinctly convergent; vertex almost 2x as wide as long. Smooth, setose; frons 2.7x as wide as long. nearly punctate, antennal sockets depressed, somewhat aciculate between antennal sockets; face 1.44x as wide as long, nearly strigose and sparsely setose; tentorial pit not deep. intertentorial line 4.5x tentorio-ocular line; clypeus distinctly convex, raised, 1.42x as wide as long, somewhat strigose; malar space 0.66x basal width of mandible; mandibles moderately large, twisted. antennal segments 29, scape 1.5x as long as wide, pedicel as long as wide, F1as long as F2. Length of F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F11, F12, F17, F18, F26 and F27 3.6x. 3.3x, 2.8x 3x, 2x and 3x their width respectively; apical flagellomere pointed.

Mesosoma: Length of mesosoma almost 2.0x its height and 1.77x its width; pronotal side antero-dorsally largely coarsely crenulate, ventrally somewhat punctate with hairs; precoxal sulcus wide, reticulate rugose; mesopleuron dorsally crenulate, remaining punctate with hairs; notauli deep, broad, anteriorly crenulate, ventrally somewhat punctate with hairs; precoxal sulcus wide, reticulate rugose; mesoscutum nearly smooth and sparsely setose; scutellar sulcus wide, deep with one median longitudinal carina and two weal lateral carinae; scutellum convex, nearly smooth; side of scutellum reticulate rugose; medio-posterior depression oval shaped with one median carina; metanotum crenulate; propodeum entirely coarsely reticulate with hairs.

Wings: Fore wing and pterostigma 2.8x as long as wide: length of vein 1-R1 1.28x length of pterostigma; vein SR1+3-SR slightly curved, r arising just middle of the pterostigma; r:2-SR:SR1+3-SR=4: 10: 40; m-cu antefurcal: cu-a postfurcal; hind wing 3.33x as long as wide; 1-M:1 rm: 2-SC+R = 8:11:4.

Legs: Hind coxa nearly rugose, lengths of hind femur, tebia and basitarsus 5.5x, 11x and 8.4x their widths respectively; length of hind tibial spurs 0.33x and 0.28x hind basitarsus.

Metasoma: Length of metasoma 4.5x its height and 3x its width; length of first metasomal tergite 1.71x its apical width, apical width 3.5x its basal width, gradually widened from base to apex, dorsope present, spiracles behind the middle of first metasomal tergite, its surface longitudinally striate, basally smooth; second tergite long with a brownish patch in middle; remaining tergites smooth and shiny; ovipositor sheaths 0.89x length of vein C+SC+R of fore wing; ovipositor shorter than first metasomal tergite, slightly curved downwards.

Male: Similar to female except antennae 30 segmented; pterostigma 2.14x as long as wide; antenna longer than female; pronotal side more setose than female and length of male 3.8mm (1).
