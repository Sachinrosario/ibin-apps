 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

The body of Encyrtus noyesi sp.nov. is light yellow. Head light yellow; frontovertex with fine squamiform reticulate sculpture, scattered with small, shallow punctures of diameter about one-third the diameter of median ocellus, spaced from each other by about diameter of median ocellus, with small pale setae; facial area below frontovertex comparitively more whitish, antennal prominence with indistinct melting reticulate sculpture, with fine punctures bearing small whitish setae; malar space with longitudianal reticulate sculpture and with shallow irregular punctures; malar sculcus indistinct but a faint line sharply diverging posteriorly present; eyes chocolate brown, asetose; ocelli red; antennae yellowish, with black setae; scape dorsally at distal half with faint brown, pedicel and flagellum on inner side darker, distal tip of pedicel and funicle with 3-5 stouter black setae, other setae small and thinner; distal segment of the club dark brown on outer side also; tip of mandible slightly reddish brown; maxillary and labial palpi light yellow. Thorax dorsally light yellow as frontovertex, laterally and ventrally whitish; pronotum with silvery white setae, one or two black setae on the lateral corners; mesoscutum with silvery white setae on most of the area and increasing in length posteriorly, anterior and lateral sides with two or three rows of black, small setae, posterior corners with a long black setae; axillae with 14-18 setae but those on lateral three-fourths black; scutellum tip with a tuft of long stout setae, malar space with a well developed longitudinal carina, usually bearing long black setae. Sculpture on the mesoscutum is similar to that on frontovertex but that on scutellum is more prominent; dorsally metanotum and propodeum and antero lateral corners of gaster whitish; propodeum with very faint microsculpture; legs totally whitish only pretarasi darker; wings hyaline, fore wing having brownish tinge, humeral palte black. Gaster dorsally yellowish and suffused with light brown; X-tergum with four dull, long setae with black bases.

Head: In frontal view 1.25x as wide as long; 1.94x as wide as frontovertex; frontal facial carina not sharp; dorsal tips of toruli on the line joining lower margins of eyes, torulus 2.0x as long as wide, torulo-eye distance 1.3x, torulo-moth distance 1.2x and intertorular distance 1.7x as long as torular length; mandible 2.0x as wide as long; ocelli arranged in slightly obtuse angle triangle, frontovertex 2.0x as wide as POL; POL abaout 5.0x, OOL 1.6 and OCL 1.1x as long as the diameter of median ocellus. In profile 1.76x as long as high, eye 1.33x as long as wide;  malar space shorter than eye length, 0.47x the head length. Antenna, scape 3.67x as long as wide; pedicel 1.72 x as long as wide; length width dimensions of funicle segments; F1, F2, F3, F4 and F6; club 1.52x as long as wide.

Thorax: Pronotum 6.1x as wide as long; mesoscutum 2.58x as wide as long, axillae narrowly meeting each other, 0.4x as long as scutellum; scutellum slightly shorter than wide, its posterior end smoothly curving down to the level of propodeum. Fore wing 2.33x as long as wide; costal cell 7.2x as long as wide, marginal , postmarginal and stigmal veins in following proportions; 60;161:143. Middle leg tebial spur longer tan basitarsus.

Gaster:0.75x as long as thorax wider than long; ovipositor 0.59x gaster length, I-valvifer triangular 1.9x as high as wide, II valvifer triangular 1.26x as long as its length beyond the articulation with I-valvifer, III-valvula fused with II valvifer; quadrate plate triangular 2x as long as wide.

Male: Length 1.26mm, Head with frontovertex deep yellow, sculpture punctate reticulate with black setae; below dorsal ends of scrobes containing with  white setae; below eye on the lateral sides a black spot which meets the lower proximity of black patch on the postocciput; eyes black, ocelli dark red. Antenna brown except an oblique white patch on scape running diagonally across its dorsoproximal and ventro distal ends; mandibles yellowish with reddish margins, palpi yellow. Thorax with pronotum black except lateral ends which are yellow; Anterior one -third of mesoscutum with the black patch, axillae with inner one third portion and posterior inclined side blackish ( inclined valve with silvery colour setae) metanotum  black, propodeum medially dark brown, lateral portions lighter, rest of dorsum with yellowish orange; sculpture on mesoscutum longitudinally striate reticulate, more so on lateral sides, axillae and central portion of scutellum with reticulate punctate, laterad one third of scutellum on either sides with longitudinal striate reticulate scullpture, setae on dorsum black, sub apex of scutellum with sparse setae representing brush. Wings hyaline. Legs mainly of white except one-fourth of dorso-posterior margin of femur, dorsal margin of tibiae of fore leg; coxae, anterior one third of femur, dorsal margin of tibia and pre tarsus of middle leg; and dorsal margin of femur, tibia and tarsus of hind leg, black. gaster dorsally black, ventrally white.

Head: In frontal view 1.18x as wide as long; 1.63x wide as frontovertex; lower margins of toruli on the line joining lower ends of eyes; torulus 2.0x as long as wide, torulo-eye distance slightly longer than, intertorular distance and torulo-mouth distance each 1.17x as long as torular length; scrobes not meeting dorsally; reaching halfway between torulo-median ocellus distance. In dorsal view 2.17x as wide as long, posterior margin deeply concave and sharp; oceli in obtuse angle triangle, apical angle about 1300 POL 2.86x, OOL 0.84x and OCL 0.34x as long as diameter on median ocellus. In profile 0.58x as high as long; eye 1.18x as long as wide; malar space 0.62x as long as eye length. Antenna with scape 3.0x and pedicel 1.17x as long as wide; length width dimensions of funicle segments are; F1 1.71x, F21.89x, F3-F5 each 2.42x, F62.15x and club 3.69x as long as wide.

Thorax: Pronotum 11.8x and mesoscutum 1.92x as wide as long; axillae 0.34x as long as wide. Fore wing 2.0x, costal cell 8.0x, as long as wide: stigmal vein 1.3x as long as marginal vein, post marginal vein longer than stigmal but its distal end not demacrated. Hind wing 2.75x as long as wide, 1.57x as long as vein length.

Gasater: 1.24x as long as wide, shorter than thorax (1)
