 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Diagnosis: Upper tooth of mandible a little wider and longer than the lower. Petiole with a short basolateral flange over each glymma. Female subgenital plate reddish-brown. Abdomen beyond tergite 1 reddish-brown. Tergite 1 slender, elongate. Tergite 1 slender, elongate. Tergites 1 and 2 subpolished.

Female: Upper tooth of mandible a little wider and longer than the lower. Face shiny and with a few sparse shallow punctures. Clypeus largely smooth and shiny. Frons with minute scattered punctures. Malar space 0.5x the ocellocular distance. Occipital and hypostomal carinae normal, not raised at their junction.

Mesoscutum convex and subpolished, notauli weakly impressed anteriorly. mesopleurum shiny, with shallow punctures separated from each other by more than their diameters. Metapleurum largely smooth and shiny. Propodeum convex and largely subpolished, with a few regulosities in apical half. Basal area and areola weakly separated. Areola about 2.0x as long as wide, parallel sided. Costulae strong. Second lateral area longer than wide.

Tergite 1 about 1.78x as long as its apical width, gradually widened to apex, largely subpolished but with some scattered rugulosities, its basolateral flanges weak, its dorsomedian carinae distinct up to 0.66 the length of tergite, its dorsolateral carina distinct up to apex. Tergite 2 subpolished. Other tergites smooth. Female sub genital plate weakly convex, hairy and pointed apically. ovipositor sheaths elongately triangular.

Colour: Black with abdomen beyond tergite 1 reddish-brown. face, clypeus ( except epistomal groove), mandible except teeth, palpi, malar space, tegula, wing bases, and all trochanters, yellow. Coxae black, fore coxa yellow apically. Fore and middle femora, tebiae and tarsi, and hind tibia and tarsus light brown. Hind femur black. Abdominal venter and female subgenital plate reddish brown, Ovipositor sheaths blackish apically.

Male : Unknown (1).

Length: Body 6.5 mm; fore wing 5.5 mm (1).

Miscellaneous Information

Holotype: Female, INDIA: Kasmir: pahalgam, 7200 ft., 29.VI.1966, V. K. & S. Gupta, coll. No. 220 (GUPTA).

Etymology: The name of this species is derived from the Greek aithos = reddish-brown + gaster = abdomen (1).
