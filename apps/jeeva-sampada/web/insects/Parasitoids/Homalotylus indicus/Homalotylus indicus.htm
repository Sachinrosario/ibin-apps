Miscellaneous Information

Comments. N. indica: The holotype was in alcohol in a vital. It is now mounted on a slide. Agarwal’s (1966) record of only 3 females as paratypes is erroneous. We found in a vial 2 females and 2 males labeled as paratypes of this species, and one specimen is dissected and parts mounted on 11 (eleven) slides. This species is also a paratype as Agarwal drew figures from these slide mounted parts.

P. nipaecocci: The type are not found in IARI, New Delhi, but we examined a specimen (from the collections of The Natural History Museum, London) labeled as this species is Subba Rao’s hand writing and agreeing with the original data. This specimen may actually be a part of the original material. It agrees with the type of indicus in all respects, and, therefore, nipae cocci is placed in synonymy with indicus Agarwal.

E. Shafii: The holotype is on two slides. It is a typical specimen of indicus, and hence the above synonymy.

H. inidcus is a distinctive species, characterized by the shorter funicle segments and dusky yellow colour of the pedicel and flagellum (1).
