 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female: Length, 0.83- 1.04 mm (Holotype, 0.92 mm).

Body dark brown; mesoscutum dark green with bronze lustre; scutellum dark brown with bronze violet; antenna dark brown; wings hyaline; legs, including coxae, dark brown; fore tibiae, tarsi, basal third of hind femur and tarsi brownish yellow; apex of mid femur, tebia, spur and tarsi pale.

Frontovertex 0.59x of head width, 1.1 x of scape length, with reticulate sculpture; mandibles with middle tooth long. Clava unsegmented, with oblique truncation, truncate part half length of clava. Relative measurements (from holotype on card): head dorsal width, 27-; frontovertex width -16; distance between posterior ocellus-8; distance between anterior ocellus to posterior ocellus-4; distance between posterior ocellus to occipital margin-1; distance between posterior ocellus to eye margin-2; eye length - 14; malar space length-10; scape length-14; pedicel length -5; funicle length-25; clava length-10; scape length-14; pedicel length-5; funicle length-25; clava length-10. From slide mounted paratype: head frontal length (height),43; head frontal width-46; frontovertex width-26; mouth fossa width-17.

Mesoscutum and scutellum with reticulate sculpture. Fore wing about as long as broad. Hind wing 3.4x as long as broad. Relative measurements (From holotype): mesoscutum length-15; scutellum length-18.5; (from paratype): mesoscutum length-15; scutellum length-18.5; thorax length (width)- 55 (59); Mesoscutum length (width), 23 (44.5); scutellum length (width)- 28(29); fore wing length (width)- 125 (61); marginal fringe length-2.5; hind wing length (width)-89 (26); marginal fringe length-3. Ovipositor not exserted. Relative measurements: ovipositor length-28; second valvifer length-13.5; third valvula length-14; tebia length-44; mid spur length-11; mid basitarsus length-10.5; distance between propodeal spiracles-35.

Male: Length, 0.86-0.93 mm. Similar to female except in antenna and genitalia. Relative measurements: Scape length (width)- 21 (4); pedicel length (width)- 54.5

(58.5); mesoscutum length (width)- 22 (47); scutellum length (width)-31 (30); propodeum length-2; distance between propodeal spiracles-36; fore wing length (width)- 130 (64); hind wing length (width)-92 (29); mid tibia length-46; mid spur length-11; mid basitarsus length-8.
