 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female: Body length, 3.0 mm.

Head ; Rectangular in dorsal view with sparse white pilosity including eyes; vertex and temple indistinctly punctate; frons deeply concave and transversely striate laterally, smooth and shiny elsewhere with a median longitudinal carina extending upto the depression of frons; face 1.5x wider than high, indistinctly punctate with somewhat faint longitudinal striations laterally; clypeus almost smooth and shiny, epidstomal suture distinct and straight; malar suture present, some what as long as basal width of mandible; antennae shorter than the body.

Mesosoma; About 2x as long as wide; scutum about as long as wide, distinctly punctate, punctuations denser, confluent and longitudinally striate in posterior course of notauli; Scutellum smooth and shiny except for few indistict punctures; sides of pronotum smooth and shiny with a deep crenulate furrow, dividing into post dorsal and postero ventral arms; mesopleuron distinctly punctate antero-laterally the punctures becoming denser and transversely striated below epicnemial ridge, smooth and shiny posteriorly along the side of pleural suture; propodeum with faint transverse and oblique rugosities postero-laterally, medially finely punctate, arcola V shaped, open anteriorly in a sculptured depression, transeverse carina absent.

Wings: Fore wing sparsely setose basally, densely and evenly setose apically, stigma 2.7x longer than wide and as long as 1-R1; r slightly longer than inside width of pterostigma, 1.25x longer than wide; venal lobe of hind wing slightly concave subapically, basally with long hairs while apically hairless.

Legs: Hind coxae large reaching upto T3, indistinctly punctate with hairs, hind tibia 1.2x longer than hind femur, hind basitarsus 2.0x longer than outer tibial spur and 4.0x longer than inner tibial spur.

Metasoma: T1 about 1.3x longer than wide at apex, slightly barrel shape, longitudinally rugulose punctate over posterior 0.6 of tergite, postero-medial depression reaching upto the posterior 0.6 of tergite; T2 virtually smooth, 3.5x wider than long, with strongly convex hind margin; hypopygium about as long as ovipositor sheaths, desclerotized, folded and striated medially acuminate apically; ovipositor sheaths broad, hairy, pointed apically and 0.75x longer than hind tibia  (1).

Colour: Black except the following: palpi, labrum. basal half of hind tibia, basal tarsal segments, mid legs beyond the basal femur, fore legs beyond coxe, venation and stigma beyond the central area are yellowish; tibial spurs whitish; ocelli, apical half of hind tibia, hind basitarsus, fore femur basally, vein 1-R1 of fore wing. R1 of hind wing yellowish brown; wings hyaline and stigma medially colorless.

Male: same as female, except in length 2.5mm; antennae distinctly larger than female.
