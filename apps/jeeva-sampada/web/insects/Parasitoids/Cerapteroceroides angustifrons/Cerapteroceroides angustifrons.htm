Comments: Both of these species were reared from the same host (Fig. 20). There are no variations, except the size, among the individuals of C. latifrons (1.40 ± 0.019 mm) and C. angustifrons (1.53± f).038 mm). Some males also emerged in the same container but it is difficult to match them with a particular female. The males could be assigned only after breeding experiments are carried out.

Type species : Telegraphus mculipennis Ratzeburg by monotype.

Cerepetrocerus belongs to the tribe Cerapterocerini. Under it eight species are known from Afrotropical Austrlasian, Holarctic and Oriental regions. From India only one species, C. virens, Agarwal was  known.

This genus is close Cerapteroceroides and is distinguished on the basis of characters mentioned under the preceding genus. A new species is described below.

Some important literature on Cerapterocerus are : Girault (1915), Ghan and Fagan (1923), Ishii (1928), Agarwal (1963), Tachikawa (1963), Gordh and Trjapitzin (1981), Noyes and Hayat (1984) in Subba Rao and Hayat (1986) and Trjapitzin in Medvedev (1987).
