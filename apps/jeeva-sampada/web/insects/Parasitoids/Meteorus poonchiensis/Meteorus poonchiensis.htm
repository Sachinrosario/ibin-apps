 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female: Body length, 3.6 mm; fore wing-3.25 mm.

Colour: Black; scape, pedicel, F1-F11,F18-F30, mandibles, metasoma beyond peteole yellowish brown

Yellowish brown; mandibles, legs including coxae. Second metastomal tergite and ovipositor yellowish; maxillary palp and labial palp yellow; metanotum, ovipositor sheaths and first metasomal tergite brown; stemmaticum black; ocelli transparent; eyes grayish: wing veins brownish yellow.

Head: Width of head  in dorsal view 1.35x its length; occipital carina complete; OOL: POL: AOL: QOD = 6;5:3: 2.5; length of eye in dorsal view 1.36x its width and 2.14x temple; eyes slightly convergent: vertex 2.5x as wide as long, sparsely punctate, largely setose; frons almost 3x as wide as long, punctate with hairs; face 2x as wide as long, punctate with hairs; tentorial pit deep, intertentorial line 2.66x tentero-ocular line; clypeus slightly convex, 2x as wide as long, nearly smooth; malar space almost equal to basal width of mandible; mandibles large, stout and slightly twisted; antennal segments 32, scape and pedicel almost as long as wide, length of F1 as long as F2, Length of F1- F2, F3- F4, F5- F7, F8- F13, F14- F17, F18- F29 and F30 4.5x, 4x, 3.5x, 3x, 2x, 1.5x and 4x their width respectively, apical flagellomere blunt.

Mesosoma: Length of mesosoma almost 2x its height; pronotal sides largely coarsely crenulate, only ventrally smooth and sparsely setose; precoxal sulcus wide and distinctly crenulate; mesopleuron antero-dorsally crenulate, depression with ruge and foveolae, remaining largely smooth; notauli deep, broad, anteriorly crenulate, posteriorly reticulate coriaceous; mesoscutum nearly smooth, sparsely setose; scutellum slightly convex, smooth; scutellar sulcus wide, deep with one median longitudinal carina and two weak lateral carinae; sides of scutellum reticulate; medio-posterior depression small, transverse with longitudinal carina; metanotum crenulate; propodeum reticulate and sparsely setose.

Wings: Fore wing 2.7x as long as wide; pterostigma 3.22x as long as wide; length of vein 1-R1, 1.39x length of pterostigma; vein SRI slightly curved; r arising from middle of pterostigma, 0.44x width of pterostigma; r: 2-SR: SRI=4: 10: 40: 1-CU1; 2-CU1: 3-CU1 = 3:15:5; m-cu antefurcal; cu-a postfurcal; hind wing 3.46x as long as wide; 1-M: 1 rm: 2-SC+R= 8:9:4.

Legs: Hind coxa punctate with hairs; lengths of hind femur, tibia and basitarsus 6x, 10.88x and 10x their widths respectively; length of hind tibial spurs 0.2x and 0.25x hind basitarsus; tarsal claw simple.

Metasoma: Length of metasoma 2.55x its width and 3.83x its height; length of first metasomal tergite almost 2x its apical width, apical width, apical width 2.5x its basal width, gradually widened from base to apex; dorsope present: spiracles behind the middle of first metasomal tergite, its surface longitudinally striate and medially rugose; remaining tergites smooth; ovipositor sheaths about 0.9x length of vein C+SC+R of fore wing; ovipositor shorter than first metasomal tergite, slightly curved downwards (1).
