 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female: The Length of female Brachymeria gigantica is 24 mm. Black; tegulae brownish-yellow with the basal margin blakish-brown. Fore coxa and tronchanter black; femur black except the apex which is yellow; tebia yellow with a pale brownish coloration on the inner ventral side in the middle. Mid coxa and trochanter black; femur black except at the apex where it is yellow; tebia yellow with a pale brownish coloration on the inner ventral side at middle. Hind coxa, trochanter and femur black, except the apex of femur where it is yellow; tebia yellow with a pale brownish coloration on the inner ventral side middle. Hind coxa, trochanter and femur black, except the apex of femur where it is yellow; tebia yellow except at the base where it is black, this blackish coloration extending to the ventral carina. Pubescence dirty-white and dense.

Head width a little less than that of thorax, a little more than one and one-third as wide as its own length; surface of head with reticulate shallow and shiny, its maximum width occupying over three-fourths the interocular space, reaching the front ocellus. Length of scrobe a little more than twice its width; median ocellus equal in size to the lateral ocellus; the distance between median and lateral ocelli hardly a little more than three-fifths the interocellar distance; interocellar space two and a half times the interocular distance; interocular distance a little less than three times ocellocular distance. Width of clypeus about six times its median length; width of labrum hardly a little more than two times its median length; width of ocellar area three-fourths the interocular space; interocellar distance about twice the maximum diameter of the lateral ocellus. Frons without pre-orbital carinae; postorbital carina present and becomes obsolescent before reaching the genotemporal margin. Interantennal projection moderately developed. Lateral ridges of scrobe produced in front of the antennal toruli; area below scrobe not smooth but with punctures; height of malar space about one -fourth the height of eye; front genal angle slightly obtuse. Antenna rather slender; space not exceeding the front ocellus, distinctly longer than the segments four to six combined, slightly contracted after the middle; pedicel about one and one third as long as thick; ring-segment length about one fourth its width; fourth segment longer than any other funicular segments; fifth slightly shorter than the fourth segment; sixth and seventh equal; eighth and ninth subequal and shorter than the segments six and seven; tenth shorter than ninth; slightly increasing in thickness from fourth to the sixth segment; segments seven to ten subequal; club hardly a little more than twice the tenth segment and a little more than twice its own width.

Thorax: Provided with reticulate, rounded, umbilicate and close pits, interspaces of pits narrow and rugose; mesoscutum about one and two fifths as wide as long; scutellum abaout one and one fifths as long as wide, high in profile, strongly, almost perpendicularly declined posteriorly, apical margin widely explanate, deeply emarginate and bilobed with dense pubescence. Fore wing about two and one fourth times as long as its greatest width, pubescence sparse in the basal one-fourth and close in the rest of the wing membrane; marginal vein distinctly maore than half of the submarginal; post marginal a little more than one fourth the marginal and a little more than twice the stigmal. Hind coxa, on ventral side with the distinct punctures and pubescence, its dorsal side smooth with sparse pubescence. Hind femur a little more than one and three-fourths as long as wide; its outer side with distinct punctures and pubescence, inner side without a tooth and with fewer punctures and pubescence, and provided along its outer ventral margin with a row of 13 black teeth.

Abdomen: Distinctly longer than the pronotum, mesonotum and scutellum combined; highest in the middle; about one and two thirds as long as its width. First tergite finely shagreened and with a few punctures and pubescence on the latero-distal sides. Second tergite with deep punctures in the baso-dorsal area except in the middle where there are only very few pits; its lateral sides with deep punctures in the baso-dorsal sides. Second tergite with deep punctures in baso-dorsal area except in the middle where there are only very few pits; its lateral sides with deep punctures and pubescence, the remaining areas with fine microsculpture, sixth tergite with a median narrow longitudinal ridge and bearing 9 to 10 transverse rows of reticulate pits, each row with several pits and interspaces between the pits rugose. Ovipositor sheaths slightly or not visible from above.

Male: Length 6.45 mm; the male resembles the female in all features except that the length of its abdomen is almost equal to the thorax. Sixth tergite with seven transeverse rows of reticulate pits, each row with several pits and the interspaces between the pits and inside of the pits rugose (3).
