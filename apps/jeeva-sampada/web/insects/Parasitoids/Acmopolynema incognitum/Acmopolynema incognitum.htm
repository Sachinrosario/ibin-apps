Miscellaneous Information

Maidliella incognita Narayanan, Subba Rao & Raur, 1960 : 889. Holotype ♀, India: Delhi (IARI).

Polynema (Maidliella) incognita (Narayanan et al.): Narayanan & Subba Rao, 1961: 667. Acmopolynema incognita (Narayanan et al.): Subba B-ao & Hayat, 1983: 131, catalogue.

Subba Rao & Hayat, 1986: 180. catalogue. Subba Rao, 1989: 154, female, Mudigere, key. Hayat. 1992: 65, female, Aligarh.

This species can be recognized on the basis of the original description and the key characters given here; hence it is not described. Illustrations of the antenna and fore wing were given by Narayanan et al. (1960) and Subba Rao & Hayat (1983)(1).
