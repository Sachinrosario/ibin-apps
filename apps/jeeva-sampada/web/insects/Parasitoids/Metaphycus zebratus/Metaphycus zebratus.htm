 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
Diagnosis: Female. Frontovertex 0.2 slightly less than 0.2 of head width and 2.75-3.0x as long as wide; F1-4 subequal:F5 larger than F6, both with longitudinal sensilla. Mesoscutum with nearly complete notaular lines. Fore wing with linea clava interrupted by 2-3 lines of setae. Ovipositor only slightly longer than or subequal in length to middle tibial spur.

Body yellow to pale yellow, with occiput above foramen, mouth margin, a larger patch on pronotum, anterior margin of mesoscutum, notaular lines, sides of metanotum, propodeum mesad of spiracles, and dorsum of gaster largely, brown to dark brown. Radicle, scape with a band on outer surface above, and a larger patch or band on inner surface on the expanded part, basal 0.33-0.66 of pedicel, F1-2 usually incompletely and nearly whole of clava brown to dark brown, rest white. Fore wing with a faint infuscation in middle. legs yellow, with two brownish bands on all tibiae.

Male: Similar to female except as follows: head about 3.5x of frontovertex width; frontovertex about 2.0x as long as wide; scape narrow, about 3.0x as long as wide and with a dark streak in upper third (1).
