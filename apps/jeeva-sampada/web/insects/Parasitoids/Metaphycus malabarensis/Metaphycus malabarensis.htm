 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Diagnosis: Female. Head crushed in holotype, but appearantly frontovertex less than 0.25 of head width; ocellar triangle possibley right angled, lateral oceli slightly less than one ocellus diameter from eye margin. Linea calva of fore wing interrupted by 3 lines of setae. Ovipositor shorter than middle tebia; third valvulae short, less than 0.2x the second valvifer and nearly 0.5x the middle tibial spur. Head yellow with dark patch on occiput above foramen; thorax lemon yellow. with three long brown bands one in middle of dorsum reaching from anterior margin of mesoscutum to nearly the apex of scutellum, and one on each sides reaching up to axilla; sides of metanotum, brown; gastor including ovipositor sheaths yellow, with brown cross-bands on terga iv-vi. Radicle brown; scape except basal 0.25 and apical 0.25, slightly less than basal 0.5 of pedicel, clava with first segment and proximal half or so of second segment, dark brown; F1-4 brown, but not as dark as the patch on scape, rest of antenna yellow. legs yellow, front and middle tibiae immaculate(1).
