 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour
Diagnosis: Female. Frontovertex about 0.25x the head width and slightly less than 2.0x as long as wide; ocellar triangle acute. Scape about 3.0x as long as wide, pedicel shorter than F1 and F2 combined; all funicle segments quadrate to slightly longer than wide and with longitudinal sensilla. Linea calva of fore wing interrupted by 2-4 lines of setae. Ovipositor slightly shorter than middle tebia, third valvula about 0.25x the length of second valvifer and slightly longer than 0.5 of middle tibial spur.

Body yellow to pale yellowish brown, with occiput above foramen, centre of pronotum, anterior margin of mesoscutum on sides, propodeum mesad of spiracles and dorsum of gaster, brown to dark brown. Radicle at least partly, a patch each on outer and inner face of scape, basal 0.66 of pedicel. F1-4 and basal two segments of clava, dark brown. Wings hyaline. Legs yellow, with two incomplete brown bands each on all tibiae.

Male; Similar to female except for a slender scape, uniformly coloured brown flagellum and broader frontovertex (1).
