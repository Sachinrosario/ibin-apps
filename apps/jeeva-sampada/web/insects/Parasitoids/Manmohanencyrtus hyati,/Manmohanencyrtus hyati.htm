 Biology/ Ecology / Behaviour

Female: General coloration, head and thorax yellowish with dark infusions; lateral areas of mesoscutum smooth, metalic green; scutellum metallic green; gaster dark brown; fore and middle legs largely yellow with some dark areas, hind legs darker; antenna black.

Body normal; head in frontal view slightly longer than wide; scrobes shallow but sharply margined dorsally and sublaterally; frontovertex smoothly rounded; mandibles tridentate; maxillary and labial palpi four and three segmented, respectively; oceli in an acute angle triangle. Antenna inserted at oral margin, highly foliaceously expanded; scape slightly longer than wide, dorsally shelved; ventral margin rounded; pedicel very small, conical, as long as F1 medially but narrower; funicle six segmented, expanded wider than scape and club; club expanded, obliquely truncate, not as wide as funicle, small incomplete suture present on dorsal side- a condition between bisegmented and solid club. Thorax moderately convex, mesoscutum with lateral smooth metallic flat portions edged from the median narrow, sculptured, setose portion; axille narrowly separated from each other; scutellum convex with its distal tip at level of propodeum, smooth, metallic flat portions edged from the median keel; scutellar tuft absent; propodeum short; mesopleuron not touching gaster. Fore wing infuscate brown throughout, incised at base of marginal vein, long setae present at basal 0.66; post marginal vein longer than stigmal. Middle tebial spur as long as basitarsus. Gaster blunt; ovipositor not projecting, III-valvulae free; hypopygium reaching tip of gaster.

Structure: Head in frontal view slightly longer than wide; 6.0x as wide as frontovertex at level of median ocelus; eye more than 2.0x as long as wide (400:187), its lower margins not reaching toruli, toruli 0.5 its own length below eye; toruli 2.0x as long as wide (100:50), dorsally converging towards centre of antennal prominence;

 torulo-eye and inter-torulaar distance equal to length of torulus; scrobes shallow, meeting dorsally; distance between toruli and dorsal ridge of scrobe (375:112). Head in dorsal view 1.66x as wide as long (525:312); ocelli arranged in an acute-angle triangle; COL about 2.0x as long as POL (75:37); lateral ocelli touching eyes. Head in profile view about 2.0x as long as high (287); eye 1.66x as long as wide (400:245); malar space slightly longer than 0.66 the length of eye (150:400). Mandibles tridentate, dorsal one the shortest and middle the longest. Antenna with scape 1.44x as long as wide, dorsal shelf slightly wider than 0.25 its width (87:337); pedicel very short and triangular in out line, as long as first funicle segment; pedicel plus flagellum (712) more than 2.0x as long as scape width; funicle wider than club.

Thorax: pronotum, 5.0x as wide as long; mesoscutum less than 2.0x as long (462:237);  posterior margin slightly conical , slightly separating axillae, middle piece (between two lateral smooth mirror like areas) (93) about 0.2x its total width; scutellum pointed at tip, longer than wide (275:237); propodeum narrow (37) about 0.14x scutellum length. Fore wing 2.84x as long as wide (1100:387); marginal, post marginal and stigmal veins in the ratio of 1:3:6:3:5 (37:137:131). Hind wing 3.5x as long as wide (800:225); more than 1.5x its vein length (487). Middle leg tibial spur as long as basitarsus.

Gaster: Blunt at tip, as long as thorax; subgenital plate reaching tip of gaster: Fused IX and X terga of abdomen with paratergites prominent and stripe-like; ovipositor not exserted, III-valvulae free.

Coloration: Head and thorax light brown, with yellow suffusions; gaster dark brown.

Head brown, frontovertex and scrobes yellowish; vertex brown with yellowish green metallic reflections, area behind eye and just below it with yellowish-green and bluish reflections; stripe along dorsal ridged margin of scrobes brown and with bluish green reflections; scrobes golden yellow, interantennal prominence bluish green to violet; head with very fine squamous punctate sculpture, punctures shallow and small (about 0.33 the diameter of median ocellus), with pale setae; eyes gray, setose, setae small and pale; oceli red; mandibles brown with long dark setae; maxillary and labial palpi yellowish, tip of maxillary palpi brownish. Antenna dark brown, greatly expanded with golden reflections, ventral margin of scape on inner side with violet reflections.

Thorax dorsally yellowish, its corners dark; lateral dark brown metallic patch separated by an edge from the yellowish-brown middle area; lateral patches with greenish-blue to blue tinge, glabrous with a row of setae along its periphery; middle portion of mesoscutum fine squamously sculptured with pale and small setae; axillae yellowish, similarly sculptured as middle portion of mesoscutum, raised dorsal surface posteriorly inclined, posterior steep side with 8-9 long ridges; scutellum similarly strongly metallic and mirror like as two lateral patches on the mesoscutum and with same coloration, longitudinally keeled in the middle with a row of widely separated setae along the margins and another row in the middle running across the width; propodeum lighter in the middle; laterally, propodeum and mesopleuron light blue, rest of thorax dark brown. Wings infuscated, dark brown, fading apically, veins and setae dark brown; hind wing hyaline, vein and setae dark brown. Fore leg yellowish except dorsal margin of femur, whole tebia and basitarsus, whioch are dark brown; hind leg dark brown, except tarsi all the pretarsi slightly darker. Gaster dark brown, reflecting brick red to golden, violet on the sides, pale on the ventral sides; setae brown (1).
