Chemistry:

·        Lapachol isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        An iridoid glycoside – specioside – isolated from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Two novel 1(17)-methyl anthraquinones, sterequinone-A and -D, their biogenetic precursors sterequinone-B, -C, and a new naphthoquinone sterequinone-E along with a known naphthoquinone, sterekunthal-B, have been isolated from the petroleum ether extract of stem bark of Stereospermum personatum. Their structures were established using spectroscopic methods. (Kumar et al., 2003).

Pharmacology:

·        This invention relates to a pharmaceutical composition comprising an effective amount of (-)-secoisolariciresinol (I) together with or associated with an additive useful as an antioxidant and a process for isolating the from the S. personatum.  (Rao et al., 2002) CA. 2002, 137: 389126.

Ethnobotany:

Medicine for stomachache, vomit (bark, Borthakur, 1976).
