Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:                                                                                     It is glabrous erect herb, 6-18 in. high, with opposite leaves and small flower heads. It is found in Himalayas and many parts of the country. As a weed in cultivated and waste lands, along stream side, forests, gardens and roadsides.
Biotic Zone: Himalaya, Konkan plain, Tropics. It is distributed in countries America, India, Tropical Africa. In India it is distributed in Assam, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh and in districts wise it is distributed in Allahabad, Bangalore, Chamba, Chamoli, Chamrajnagar, Chikmaglore, Coimbatore, Dharmapuri, Hassan, Hoshangabad, Jowai, Kangra, Kinnaur, Kodagu, Kolar, Kullu, Lahaul, Spiti, Madurai, Mysore, Nilgiri, Pulni, Raigad , Salem, Satara, Simla, Sirohi, Surguja, Trichurapalli, Tumkur, Uttar Kannada,

Availability :  
Status :  Wild. Annual.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES :
It is glabrous, erect herb, 6-18 in. high, with leaves Opposite, simple, lightly dentate, lance-ovate, pointed at tip and small flower heads,. mostly branched at base.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY :
Flowers:4-5 small white ray petals surrounding yellow disk flowers. - Flowers in heads: ray -florets white, with a short ligule, a marked tube, female. Disk-florets yellow, tubular. Short-peduncled inflorescence axillary or terminal on forked branches, solitary on pedicels with small glands. Involucral bracts on 2 rows, green, glabrous.
Fruit : Central achenes circa 1 mm, ovate, black, covered with short white bristles, pappus as long as the fruit, with 8-20 silvery and cut-margined scales. Marginal achenes smooth, 3-blunt-angled, without pappus.
