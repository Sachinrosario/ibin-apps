Non-polar crude fractions (Ho - 0 and Ho - 1) of Psoralea corylifolia seeds was administered orally to untreated and experimental rachitic rats. In the biological screening of the fractions, Ho - 1, an elution with n-hexane-Et acetate by column chromatography over silica gel of the acetone extract, untreated rats showed significant elevation of the serum inorganic phosphorus and revealed histomorphometrically a significant increase in bone calcification. When Ho - 0, an elution with n-hexane, and Ho - 1 were administered to the rachtic rats fed with vitamin D - free, low phosphorus diet, they not only increased significantly the concentration of inorganic phosphorus in serum, but also significantly promoted bone calcification. Administration of 30 mg/kg of Ho - 1 resulted in marked decrease of osteoid volume and improvement of hyperosteoides in rachtic rats, these results suggested that Ho - 0 and Ho - 1 are useful remedy for bone fracture, osteomalacia, osteoporosis and related conditions. (Miura et al, 1996). C.A. 1996, 125, 49196g.
A gas chromatography method was established for determination of contents of active compound psoralea and isoprosalen in fructus Psoralea. The stationary phase was 3% SE30 with flame ionization detector (FID) and 6,7-dimethoxy coumarin as internal standard. The recoveries of psoralen were 0.05 - 1.17% in sample of 11 areas and isopsoralen 0.16 - 1.20%. (Yao et al, 1996). C.A. 1997, 126, 50878h.
Neo-psoralen (fig. 23), was isolated from Psoralea corylifolia. Its structure was elucidated on the basis of IR, UV, HRMS and NMR methods. Isopsoralen, psoralen, psoralidin were also isolated from this plant. (Peng et al, 1996). C.A. 1997, 126, 303679q.
An ethanol extract of Psoralea corylifolia caused strong DNA polymerase inhibition in a whole cell bioassay specific for inhibitors of DNA replication enzymes. Bioassay-directed purification of the active compounds led to the isolation of the new compound corylifolin (1) and the known compound bakuchiol (2) as DNA polymerase inhibitors. On the basis of the structures of 1 and 2, resveratrol was tested and found to be active as a DNA polymerase inhibitor in this bioassay, neobavaisoflavone was isolated as a DNA polymerase inhibitor daidzein as a DNA polymerase and topoisomerase II inhibitor, and bakuchicin as a topoisomerase II inhibitor. (Sun et al, 1998). MAPA 9901-0168.
Psoralen and isopsoralen were extracted from Psoralea corylifolia with modified supercritical carbon dioxide. The supercritical fluid chromatographic system consists of a NH2 column with carbon dioxide containing 5% methanol and UV 247 nm detection. The optimized column temperature and pressure were 40°C and 27.6 Mpa, respectively. The recoveries of psoralen and isopsoralen are 96.9% and 95.1% respectively. (Lu et al, 1999). MAPA 9904-2819.
A method based on SFE and CGC was developed for qualitative and quantitative analysis of major components in Psoralea corylifolia. The recoveries of psoralen are 96.93% (RSD 1.87%) and 98.43% (RSD 2.06%) respectively. Compared with conventional methods, the SFE as an alternative technique for the simple preparation will be most useful in the quality control. (Chen et al, 2000). C.A. 2000, 132, 284312v.
The isolation as a guest molecule is selectively included by 1,1,6,6-tetraphenylhexa-2,4-diyne-1,6-diol in inclusion crystal and removed by acetone from the inclusion compound and total yield is 0.18%.isopsoralen is determined by UV, IR, 1HNMR and MS and its inclusion compound is determined by means of IR, Powder XRD and single crystal XRD. The isolation effect is analyzed by GC/MS. The method is simple and selective for isolation of isopsoralen from crude extract of Psoralea corylifolia. (Guo et al, 2000). MAPA 2001-03-1662.

Pharmacology: 

Essential oil from fruits used internally as tonic and as aphrodisiac against impotency and externally for treatment of leucoderma, psoriasis and leprosy. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.
Seed extract possessed anthelmintic activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)e.
Psoralen administered orally for 2.5 months at 1,2 and 4 times human dose of 30 mg/day affected detoxication mechanism in rabbits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)f.
Toxic effects of psoralen, isopsoralen and imporatorin, used in the treatment of leucoderma, were studied on rat at daily dose of 2.5 mg/75g for 60 days. Psoralen and imporatorin alone or mixed, had no effect on growth or weight, but postmortem revealed congestion and enlargement of liver and weight of spleen was doubled. Hyalinsation of lung alveoli was observed in one rat and haemorrhage from mouth, nose and anus in two others. A mixture of psoralen, isopsoralen and imperatorin caused hypertrophy of liver, kidney and spleen. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)g.
Extract of seeds containing psoralen and angelicin showed skin photosensitising activity in guinea pigs. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)l.
Corylifolinin exhibited vasodialator activity and inhibitory effect on HeLa cells. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)i.
Psoralen used in treatment of uterine haemorrhage. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)j.
Ethanol extract of fruits exhibited cytotoxicity against L929 cell culture. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)g.
Petrol extract of roots inhibited feeding of 48th instar larvae of Spodoptera litura. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)h.
Bakuchiol exhibited irreversible cytotoxicity against L929 cell culturein short time. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)i.
It also exhibited cytotoxicity against cultured human cancer cell lines viz. A549, SK-OV-3, SK-MEL-2, XF498 and HCT15. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)j.
Powder of Psoralea corylifolia for treatment of odontopathy such as in dental carries and periodontal disease. (Wang, 1995). C.A. 1996, 124, 156075t.
Oral compound containing extracts of Psoralea corylifolia for controlling S. mutans related dental caries. (Oshino and Eguchi, 1996). C.A. 1996, 125, 177045u.
Antifertility activity of Psoralea corylifolia has been reviewed. Coumestan and diosgenin are the active constituents of these two estrogenic plants. (Shah et al, 1997). MAPA 9803-1598.
Psoralea corylifolia with other plant material showed results in infantile atopical dermatitis. (Zanchi, 1998). MAPA 9903-1620.
The active fraction from Psoralea corylifolia seeds was found to be cytotoxic to murine ascitic tumor cells, transformed human lymphocytes and KB cells in culture. Active fraction significantly increased the life span of EAC ascitic tumor bearing mice and showed lesser activity against solid tumor as compared to ascitic tumor. (Latha and Panikkar, 1998). MAPA 9902-0997.
The ether extract of Psoralea corylifolia has showed the antioxidant activity. The pure compound psoralidin was obtained. (Wei and Zhou, 2000). C.A. 2000, 133, 163327r.
Psoralea corylifolia seed extract has been reported to stimulate the immune system in mice. Administration of the seed extract was also found to inhibit EAC ascitic tumor growth and stimulate natural killer cell activity, antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, antibody forming cells and the antibody complement-mediated cytotoxicity during tumor development. (Latha et al, 2000). MAPA 2000-05-2864.


Ethnobotany: 

Medicine for itch (Seed, Saxena and Vyas, 1983); Leprosy (Seed, Saxena and Vyas, 1983; Saxena and Vyas, 1986); Leucoderma (Seed, Singh and Pandey, 1980; Shah et al, 1981); Psoriasis (Seed, Saxena and Vyas, 1986); Skin disease (Seed, Malhotra and Moorthy, 1973; Singh and Pandey, 1980; Shah et al, 1981; Fruit, Sebastian and Bhandari, 1984); Veterinary injuries, skin disease (Seed, Shah et al, 1983)

Pharmaceutical and Toxicology:

A cytotoxic coumestan derivative, psoralidin, was isolated from the seed of Psoralea corylifolia. (Yang et al, 1996). C.A. 1996, 125, 265331q.
