HABIT & HABITAT
Trees grows upto 8-14 mts tall and found to occur in deciduous and dry evergreen forests, occasionally along roadsides and sometimes planted in gardens. Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Orissa, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Goa, Daman & Diu, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala. Pakistan, Myanmar, Sri lanka.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Trees, 8-14 m tall; bark rough, grey. Leaves alternate, 10-30 cm long; leaflets opposite or alternate, ovate-oblong or obovate-oblong, 1.5-15 x 0.5-5.5 cm, rounded or emarginate at apex, entire at margin, glabrescent above, softly tomentose beneath and on midrib above; petiolules 2-5 mm long, tomentose.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Flowers white, 4-5 mm long. Sepals 5, ovate, 3.0-3.5 mm long, obtuse at apex, hairy on outer surfaces. Petals 5, lanceolate, 3-4 mm long, clawed, margin ciliate with wooly scales above the claw. Stamens 8. Drupes ovoid, 1.0-1.5 x 1.5 cm, yellowish-brown, 3-lobed, rusty pubescent. Seeds 5-7 mm in diam. It flowers between October - January.
