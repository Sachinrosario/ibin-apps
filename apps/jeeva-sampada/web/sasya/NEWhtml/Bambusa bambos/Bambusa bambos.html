DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herbs. Culms 6-20 m tall, 5-10 cm in diam., erect, branched; culm sheaths 20-30 x 3.5 cm, rounded in the upper part except the apiculate tip with dark brown hairs on the dorsal surface. Leaves linear to linear-lanceolate, 15.5-19.5 x 1.0-2.0 cm, apex stiff, base rounded, glabrous or hairy on back; auricles with bristles; ligules truncate, long ciliate. Inflorescence of enormous, leafless, compound panicles.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Spikelets lanceolate, 1.2-2.5 x 0.5 cm, 3-6 flowered; rachilla hairy. Lemma lanceolate, c 1 cm long, glabrous. Pelea keels ciliate. Lodicules 3, fimbriate, c 2 mm long. Cryopsis oblong, beaked by tyle bases, grooved on one side . 

 Inflorescence: An enomorous panicle often occupying the whole stem, branchlets bearing the loose clusters of  pale, sub-erect, lanceolate, acute, glabros spikelets ; Flower:  Bracteate, many nerved, empty, floral glumes 3-7, the upper most 1-3 males or neuter, palea 2-keeled, lodicules 3, ; Androecium:  Stamens 3, free, anther yellow, obtuse; Gynoecium: Ovary oblong or obovoid, with hairy tip, styles short, stigma 2-3. ; Fruit: Grain oblong, beaked by style bases ;


Floral and seed biology:
Once in life-time (100 years) often during September to May. Flowering & Fruiting : Feburary-April.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.   
 
Genetic variability:
The plant has reported chromosome number 2n= 72.
