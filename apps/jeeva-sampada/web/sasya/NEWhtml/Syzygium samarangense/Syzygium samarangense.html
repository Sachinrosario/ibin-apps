DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A tree, 5-15 tall, with low-branched trunk, 25-30 cm. in diam. and widely branched crown. Leaves elliptic-obtuse 10-25 cm. x 5-12 cm. in diam. terminal or axillary, lax panicles; fruits white or depressed,pyriform, turbinate,flattened at the endm 3.5-5.0 cm.long and 4.5-5.5 cm.in diam., juicy and seedless; seeds 0.5-0.8 cm.in diam.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY

Breeding System :

Phenology : 
The flowering occurs during   January- March  and in some places  it will be from  April- June. 

 Pollinators:
Cross-pollination.Pollination is done by honeybees,houseflies and wind.
Seed Biology :

Seed Germination:
Seeds sown fresh usually show a high percentage of germination within two ot three weeks.

Cultivation:
It can be propogated vegetatively by layering,grafting on rootstock of rose-apple or budding onthe root of the same species.

MAJOR PESTS & DISEASES

Pests:The major pests are :
Leaf eating caterpillar:The insect infects the leaves and defoliate the trees.Treatment with rogor and malathion has proves effective.
Bark-eating caterpillars:The larva feeds on the live bark tissues,shelters under the covering os silken webbing during night.
Diseases
Glomerelia cingulata causes leaf spots and fruit rot. Affected leaf shows scattered spots light brown or reddish brown in colour.The affected fruits rot and shrivel.It may be controlled with fungicide like dithane Z-78 at 0.02 per cent or Borseauz mixture.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Fruit.

Uses

1. It is cultivated in many parts of India for its edible fruit.

Harvesting Practices
The seeding plant start bearing after 8-10 yrs of planting while grafted ones after 4-5 years.The fruit ripens in the month of Jun-Jul.The main characteristics of ripe fruit at full size is deep purple or black colour.
