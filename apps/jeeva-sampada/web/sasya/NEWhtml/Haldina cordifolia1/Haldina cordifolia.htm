Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A large tree found mainly in moist deciduous forests. Suitable for tropical semi- humid conditions and for red soils and black soils. A major fuelwood species found in Bundelkhand region comprising parts of Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh, found throughout the state of Bihar, primary forests at an altitude of 500- 1200 m in Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, forests in hilly tracts in Rajasthan and also in open forests of Goa, Daman - Diu and Deccan regions.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Uses:
Considered to be a good fuelwood. Wood is suitable for internal fittments, used for structural purposes, for making furniture, bobbins, battery separators, cheaper carving, forage for honey bees, decorative & ornamental purposes, shoe lasts, cheaper toilet brushes, printing blocks, musical instruments, fibre board, penholders, picture & slate frames, tool handles, toys, and wooden flooring.

Parts Used: Bark and leaves.

Miscellaneous Information

 Bark girdling is dangerous and may cause death. 

Wood: Wood is deep yellow to brownish yellow. Wood is fine compact- textured, wood parenchyma is diffused aggregate, growth rings exists but are not so distinct, exhibit fluorescence. Average unit weight at 12 per cent moisture content ranges from 480- 785 kg/cu. m. and average around 689 kg/ cu. m.
