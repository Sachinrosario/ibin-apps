 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
A diffuse shrub found in hilly regions of Eastern Bengal, ascending to 4,000 feet on the Khasia mountains, also in Malay peninsula, and in South-Western India, in the Southern Konkan, the Nilgiri Hills, the mountains of Travancore, and in Ceylon.
Habitat: Cultivated fields and Plains, Evergreen forest, Forests, Hilly regions, Mixed forests, Outskirts, Plains, Primary forest in shaded places, Primary forests, Shola, Shrubby forest, Stream and Thickets.
BioticZone: Deccan, Deccan Plateau, Eastern ghats, Goa ghats and Western Ghats.
Availability :  
Misc: Attikan(B.R.Hills), Bordabri, Dalgado, Hollong, Honey falls, Horsleykonda, Jowai, Kambakam hills, Khasia, Madgol hills, Madras Presidency, Narsapur Reserve Forest, Salkumar, Tekkumalai and Tiger falls.
Country: India, Malay Peninsula, and Tenasserim.
Zone: Central, East, North East, South, South west and Westzone.
State: Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Goa Daman Diu, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Meghalaya, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal.
District: Bastar, Chamrajnagar, Chengalpattu, Chingleput, Chittoor, Coimbatore, Dharmapuri, Goa, Jowai, Khasi hills, Madras, Madurai, Medak, Mysore, Nilgiri, North Arcot, Pulni, Ramnathapuram, Salem, South Arcot, Thanjavur, Tirap, Tiruchi, Tirunelveli, Trichurapalli and Visakhapatnam.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Root, Stems, Leaves, Green Fruits

Uses

  1. The green fruits are used in curries and the ripe ones eaten.
  2. Leaves made into decoction in conjunction with certain aromatics used in cases of diarrhoea and lientery.
  3. The tough stems are employed for binding fences instead of ropes.
  4. The root yields a dye.

Harvesting Practices

Produce and Products
Yellow dye

Chemistry
The root bark contains the colouring principles,morindone and morindin. It contains in addition, a considerable amount of rubichloric acid and small quantities of a trihydroxymethyl anthraquinone methyl ether,morindanigrin, 1,2-dihydroxy-6-methyl anthraquinone, a derivative of dimethyl anthraquinone, and a hydroxy-methyl anthraquinone carboxylic acid.
