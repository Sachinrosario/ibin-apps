DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual, monoecious, climber, soft-hairy; stems angular. Leaves ovate-reniform, 10-25 x 11.5 cm, deeply cordate at base, dentate, hispid beneath, scabrous above, tendril bifid. REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Large yellow, monoecious, all solitary, without bract ; Male flower:  peduncle 5-15 cm long; probracts foliaceous; calyx tube densely villous; stamens 3, filaments hispid, anthers sub-trilobate.Calyx tube campanulate, lobes 5, serrate, petals 5, nearly separate, obovate, Stamens 3, inserted at the mouth of corolla tube, anthers inserted, one celled ; Female flower: staminodes 3, ovary triplacentiferous, hairy.Calyx tube campanulate, lobes 5, serrate, petals 5, nearly separate, obovate, ovary oblong, densely hairy, style thick with 3-flexusus stigma, ovule numerous, horizontal ; Fruit:  Fruits cylindrical to ellipsoid, green with white pruinose when young, 30-45 cm long. Large, fleshy, oblong, pubescent,; Seed:  Seeds oblong, white & many, compressed, margined . Flowering & Fruiting : June- October.Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by animals.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:
Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant can be grown successfully on all types of soils ranging from sandy to clayey. Medium soil like loams with plenty of organic matter are most suitable with pH ranging from 5.5-6.4.

Nutrient requirements: During ploughing field is well manured with 20-25 tonnes /ha of farm yard manure or compost. Application of phosphate and potassium fertilizers is beneficial for plant.

Water management:  In light soil light irrigation is given after sowing and in heavy soil irrigation is preffered before sowing.                                     

Harvesting period / Method: Plant start fruiting in 80-120 days after sowing and fruits are harvested when fully mature.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: In Ayurveda system of medicine the fruit is laxative. It is used in diuretic, tonic. It also cures stangury, urinary discharges, urinary caliculi, biliousness, diseases of blood, removes foul taste of mouth. It is also used as tonic of heart. Decoction of unripe fruit is given for respiratory troubles. In Unani system of medicine fruit has a bad taste. It enriches blood and tonic. Seeds are used in dry cough, fever, urethral discharges, biliousness and brain tonic. Seed oil is good for brain, liver and syphilis.


Chemistry:
Detection of b-sitosterol, lupeol, n-triacontanol, mannitol, arginine, aspartic acid, glutamic acid, asparagine, glutamine, proline, hydroxyproline, isoleucine, cysteine, L-leucine, glucose and rhamnose in fruits by TLC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

Isomultiflorenol acetate isolated as major constituent of wax coating on fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Isolation of lupeol, b-sitosterol and their acetates from ripe fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

A protease has been purified from the sarcocarp of Benincasa hispida by two steps of chromatography. It’s Mr was estimated by SDS-PAGE to be about 67.000. (Uchikoba et al, 1998).


Pharmacology:
Skin lightening cosmetics containing Benincasa hispida essence as melanin formation inhibition. (Fuji et al, 1994). C.A. 1996, 125, 67209t.

The methanol extract of wax gourd the fruit of Benincasa hispida was found to show inhibitory activity on histamine released from rat exudates cells induced by antigen-antibody reaction. (Yoshizumi et al, 1998).

Benincasa hispida is recommended for the management of pepetic ulcers. Therefore, anti-ulcerogenic activity different extracts of Benincasa hispida (fresh juice, supernatant and residue fraction of centrifuged juice, alcoholic and petroleum ether extract) were studied in aspirin plus restraint, swimming stress, indomethacin plus histamine and serotonin-induced ulcers in rats and mice. The oral feeding of different doses of the extract significantly reduced the ulcer index produced by various ulcerogens. The anti-ulcerogenic effect was dose-dependent in stress induced model of ulcer and not in the other models. B. hispida probably has a CNS component in prevention of stress induced ulceration. Chronic toxicity studies carried out for 3 months revealed no deletrious effect of fresh juice of B. hispida on various hematological and biochemical parameters studied. Thus extract of B. hispida may be considered to be a drug of natural origin possessing antiulcer activity. (Grover et al, 2001).
