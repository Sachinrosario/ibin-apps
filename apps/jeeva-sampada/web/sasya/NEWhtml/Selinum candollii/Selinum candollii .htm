DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Perennial herbs; stem erect, stout, branched; leaves 3-pinnate, pinnae Rancealate to oval, pinnatipartite to pinnatiseet, glabrous above, hairy on the nerves beneth, leaf bases sheething; involucral bracts long, linear. White margined, umbel base pubescent, rays long,. minutely pubescent; flowers white, calyx teeth linear; fruits long, oblong to subquadrate, ridges winged, dorsal furrows 1-vittate, lateral 1-2-vittate.
