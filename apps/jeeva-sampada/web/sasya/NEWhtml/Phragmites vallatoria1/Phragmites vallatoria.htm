HABIT & HABITAT

Perennial 1-3 m tall grass, found growing along lake margins and roadsides.
India, Australia, Burma, Sri-Lanka, South East Asia.

Availability : Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Punjab, Sikkim, Tamilnadu Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Perennials Culms tufted, terete, stout, 1-3 m tall, erect with creeping woody rhizome, stout, branched; nodes glabrous. Leaves: sheaths terete, 8-15 cm long, glabrous, adhering to the culms; ligule a fringe of stiff hairs; blades flat, lanceolate, 25-50 X 0.8-2.8 cm, glabrous; apex acuminate. Panicles lax, pyramidal, 15-50 cm long, bearded at base; rachis angular, grooved; branches capillary; pedicels capillary. Spikelets linear-lanceolate, 9-15 cm long, 3-6 flowered; rachilla with long silky hairs. Lower glume membranous, oblong-lanceolate, 2.7-3.5 mm long, 1-nerved; apex acute. Upper glume membranous, oblong-lanceolate, 3.8-5 mm long, 3 nerved; apex acute. Lower lemma empty, oblong-lanceolate, 8-9.5 mm long, 3-nerved; apex acute. Upper lemma thinly membranous, oblong-lanceolate, 7-9 mm long, 3-nerved; apex acute. Palea hyaline, oblong, 3.2-3.8 mm long, 2-keeled; apex acute. Lodicules 2. Stamens 3; anthers 2.5-2.8 mm long. Grain cylindric.
