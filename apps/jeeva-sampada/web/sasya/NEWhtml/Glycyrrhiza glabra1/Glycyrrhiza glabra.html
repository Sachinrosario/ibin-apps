Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Root is sweet, refrigerant, tonic, aphrodisiac, alexeteric, diuretic; good for eye;   improves taste; lessens hiccough, vomiting; heals ulcers, wounds; improves voice; purifies blood; used in leprosy, anemia; abdominal pains, epilesy (Ayurveda). The root relieves thirst, cough, vomiting, asthma, bronchistis, abdominal colic, headache; cures eye troubles, unhealthy humours, ulcers. Leaves are used for scalds of the head, and in foul perspiration of the armpits (Unani).
