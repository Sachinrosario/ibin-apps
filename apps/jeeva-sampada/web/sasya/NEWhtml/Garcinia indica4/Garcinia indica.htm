Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
The fruits is taken with water and sugar and taken as drink (Arora & Pandey, 1996)

Traditionally, the fruits are used for preparing syrups, as an acidulant in cookery and in preparation of certain medicines for piles, pain, heart diseases etc. Kokum beverage prepared from the syrup or fresh fruits is a refreshing drink popular during summer months. This beverage is popular in certain parts of Karnataka, Goa and Maharashtra.

The rind of the fruit is rich in acidic component and considerable quantity of red and yellow pigments. The red pigments are water soluble while the yellow pigment is soluble in non-polar solvents like texane. This paper describes preparation of product such as Kokum concentrate, syrup and the beneficial health effects of the acid component in the products.

Kokum Fruit Liquid Concentrate and Syrup

Kokum fruit concentrate is a liquid rich in the acid component (more than 25%) [(-) Hydroxy citric acid] and red anthocyanin pigments. In the preparation of the concentrate, the raw material used can be either the fresh fruit or the dried kokum rind. The fruit material is sliced into small pieces and extracted with water. The extract is centrifuged and vacuum concentrated to obtain a dark red coloured viscous liquid possessing the characteristic pleasing flavour. The concentrate can be used as a source of red colour is foods and beverages similar to the natural anthocyanins

Kokum syrup is prepared by mixing purified sugar syrup or any other natural/synthetic sweetener and kokum concentrate is suitable proportions along with preservative for long shelf life. A ready-to-drink still orcarbonated beverage is prepared by diluting the concentrate with calculated amounts of water or carbonated water respectively. ( Anthony 1997)
