DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
   Inflorescence: Lax  racemes ; Flower: Bracteate, pedicellate, complete, iregular ; Calyx: Sepals 5, pubescent outside; Corolla:  Petals 4, spurs present; Androecium: Hypogynous, anthers adnate, dehiscing laterally ; Gynoecium:  Ovary superior, ovulaes many, style simple; Fruit: Follicles 2, glabrous; Seed: Many .

Floral and seed biology:

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.  
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome numbers  -16, 24,32, 48.
