Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Large evergreen tree with straight cylindrical stem. It is often found in mixture with other trees in the evergreen forests of western ghats from North Kanara southwards, up to an altitude of 3,000 ft.


Availability : Very large quantities are available from the east zone particularly from Assam.


Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

Floral and seed biology:

Seedling Germination: Natural regeneration is generally adequate though its tendency is to occur in large irregular diffuse patches. Where the top canopy is partially opened and mother trees are present, abundant natural regeneration of the species occurs. Where regeneration is natural, rapid development can be assisted by providing light from below by felling trees in the intermediate canopy and by selective tending; later the trees in the topmost canopy may have to be removed.

The seeds lose their viability rapidly. When fresh seeds are employed about 40 per cent of them germinate. Sowing of ripe seeds collected soon after they fell to the ground has given good results. Basket planting and naked planting of nursery seedlings have also proved satisfactory. Experiments in Wynaad (Madras) indicate that it does best under conditions of clear felling with no cover crop and without burn. Weeding is essential for success. Shading minimises nursery casualties.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel: The wood classed among excellent fuel woods. 

Timber used in house construction, railway carriages, ship-building and for masts and spars. In the Kolar gold fields, used for beans and planks for underground work. Suitable for railway sleepers after preservative treatment.


Parts Used: Bark

Miscellaneous Information

 Wood: Sapwood and heartwood fairly well demarcated. Sapwood dirty white to greyish to pale yellowish-brown, heartwood pale red to reddish-brown, sometimes with orange tinge darkening to red on exposure occasionally with reddish streaks, often with gummy exudation on the end surface. Moderately hard; moderately heavy to heavy. Mostly moderately heavy. Average unit weight at 12 per cent moisture content 755 kg/m3. Specific gravity 0.59 – 0.91 air dry. Straight to interlocked-grained, coarse textured.
