Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Occurring sparsely in the dry regions of northern India (rare), and commonly throughout the Deccan Peninsula, and in the Andaman Islands. The plant is parasitized by Cassytha filiformis Linn. 


Availability :

North-eastern India, occurring mainly in the Nilgiri hills, Karnataka.


Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild, Cultivated

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
It is a much-branched, armed shrub or woody climber, with oblong, elliptic or obovate leaves, white flowers, and globose, deep purple, fleshy edible berry
