DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Inflorescence: Axillary terminal panicles ; Flower: Bracteate, bracteolate, hermaphrodite ; Calyx: Sepals 4-5, cupular ; Corolla: Petals 4-5, imbricate in bud ;Androecium:  Stamens numerous, filaments united, anthers dithecous; Gynoecium: Multicapellary, ovary superior, placentation axile, style free, stigma terminal ; Fruit: Small, rounded, bright orange with rind; Seed:White, oblong . Flowering & Fruiting: June-September: 
Floral and seed biology:

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
 
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome numbers 18, 36.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: It prefers well drained sandy loam.

Nutrient requirements: Plant requires farmyard manure and nitrogenous fertilizers during planting and fruiting.

Water management:  Plant requires periodic irrigation after 7-10 days.                 

Harvesting period / Method: Fruits are harvested when they are fully mature turns to orange in colour.

Pharmacology:

Polysaccharides containing galacturonic acid showed anti-allergic activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)h.

Effects of quercetin, taxifolin, nobiletin and tangeretin were studied on in vitro growth of human squamous cell carcinoma HTB 43. Cell cultures were treated with each flavonoid (2.0 to 8.0 mg/ml) for 3 to 7 days. Nobiletin and tangeretin markedly inhibited cell growth at all concentrations tested on days 5 and 7. Inhibition was 70 to 72% at 8.0 mg/m1 on day 3, whereas on day 5 it was 61 to 88% at 2.0 to 4.0 mg/ml. Quercetin and taxifolin exhibited no inhibition at any concentration tested. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)i.

Tangeretin and nobiletin markedly inhibited proliferation of squamous cell carcinoma (HTB-43) and gliosarcoma (9L) cell line at 2.0 to 8.0 m g/ml. Quercetin had no effect on 9L cell growth at these concentrations, whereas at 8.0 mg/ml it inhibited HTB43 cell growth. Taxifolin inhibited HTB 43 cell growth slightly 80 mg/ml and moderately t 2.0 to 8.0 mg/ml. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)j.
