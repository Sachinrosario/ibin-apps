Chemistry:  

(-)sulforaphene from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).
b-sitosterol isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
A new growth inhibitor - raphanusol A (fig 1) - isolated from seedling and characterised as 1b,4-di-O-(4-hydroxy-3,5-dimethoxycinnamoyl)gentiobiose. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
2-thioxothiazolidine-4-carboxylic acid isolated from seedling and its structure established. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
Malic acid esters of p-coumaric, ferulic and caffeic acids from leaves and malic acid ester of sinapic acid from cotyledons. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.
1,2-disinapolylglucose (fig 2) isolated from cotyledons and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.
Isolation of stearic acid and b-sitosterol from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
2-O-(p-coumaryl)-, 2-O-caffeoyl- and 2-O-feruloyl-malates isolated from leaves and inflorescences. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
Sinapine, a hypotensive compound, identified in seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.
Seven 13-hydroxygibberellins - GA1, 3-epi-GA1, GA8, GA17, GA19, GA20 and GA77 (new, fig 3) - together with four non-13-hydroxygibberellins - GA9, GA24, 12b-hydroxy-GA24 and GA25 - isolated from mature seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
A new b-carboline derivative (fig 4) isolated from roots and characterised as 1-(2'-purrolidinethion-3'-yl)-1,2,3,4-tetrahydro-b-carboline-3-carboxylic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
Castasterone and brassinolide identified in seeds by GC-MS. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
Isolation of trans-4-(methylthio)-3-butenyl glucosinolate from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
Identification of 28-homotesterone and teasterone in seeds by GC-MS. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.

Pharmacology: 2-thioxothiazolidine-4-carboxylic acid inhibited growth of hypocotyl sections and intact hypocotyls of letture seedlings at concentration less than 3 mg/l. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.
a purified basic protein, homologus to nonspecific lipid transfer proteins, from seeds showed antifungal activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)f.
2-thioxy-3-pyrrolidine carbaldehyde, a major thiolactane compound generated from the pungent principle of radish in an aqueous medium showed antimicrobial activity. (Hiroki et al, 1997). C.A. 1998, 128, 215445w.
Raphnus sativus showed antioxidant activity. (Kim et al, 1997). C.A. 1998, 128, 26742e.


Ethnobotany: Medicine for acidity (Leaf, Shah, 1984); Rashes, ringworm (Seed, Purohit et al, 1985); Skin eruption (Seed, Saxena and Vyas, 1986); Edible as vegetable (Leaf Shekhawat and Anand, 1984; Shah, 1984; 207; Root, Shekhawat and Anand, 1984; Shah, 1984; Lakshmanan et al, 1986; Fruit, Shekhawat and Anand, 1984; Shah, 1984)
