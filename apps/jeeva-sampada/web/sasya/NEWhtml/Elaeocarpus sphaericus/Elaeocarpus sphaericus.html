REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Racemose; Flower: Pedicellate, complete, hermaphrodite, regular; Calyx: Sepals 4-5, valvate, oblong, acuminate, pubescent outside; Corolla: Petals as many as sepals, laciniate, inserted round the base of thickened round torus, ciliolate ; Androceium: Stamens about 40 in groups opposite each petal, filaments very short, anthers linear, one valve tipped at the apex with a small tuft of glistening hair ; Gynoecium: Ovary pilose, 5-celled, ovules 2 in each cell, style longer than stamens, subulate, entire; Fruit: Drupe globose or somewhat obovoid, purple, stone tubercled, 5-celled; Seed: 5, pendulous, flat;   .

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting time: January-February. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by   wind, birds or animals.

Chemistry:
Absolute configurations assigned to seven isomeric alkaloids – (-) isoelaecarpiline (fig. 1a), (+)elaeocorpiline (fig. 1b)and alkaloids I – V (fig. 1c – 1f) isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Isolation of elaeocarpidine, (±) elaeocarpine and (±) isoelaeocarpine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Structure elucidation of new alkaloid – rudrakine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Saponification of seed oil yielded palmitic (51.7%), isopalmitic (tr) and linoleic (tr) acids. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Elaeocarpidine  synthesized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

Elaeocarpine and isoelaeocarpine synthesized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
