REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   Inflorescence: Cymes from the long peducles from the crown rom root stock, appearing after the leaves that have fallen, 3-many flowered; Flower: Bracts at fork ovate-lanceolate, monoecious, combined in an inflorescence of many male florets surrounding female floret ; Male Flower: a stalked stamen without floral envelop ; Female Flower: Ovary 3-celled on ultimately exerted stalk in the center of greenish involucre, ovules solitary in each cell, style 3, free; Fruit: Capsule of 3 two valved compressed cocci with styles combined in the middle ; Seed: Broadly ovoid, subacute, smooth. 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : April – May  . 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
