Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A leafy closely adpressed lichen of temperate and alpine regions of India. It grows on bark of trees, rocks and on soil along with mosses. The species prefers the decaying wood logs and siliceous rocks in Western Himalaya and Bhutan. It is abundant in Europe, North America and East Africa.

Restricted distribution in Himalayas

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
The plant forms loose, grey patches, 10 cm in diameter on the substratum. The lobes forming a rosette or irregularly spreading and hollow inside. The powdery soredia develops from the rupture of the underside.
Economic potential
Inhibition of Tyrosinase activity: Both mycobiont and photobiont of Hypogymnia physodes separated in culture exhibit the inhibition of Tyrosinase activity. The mycobiont showed a stronger inhibition rate (55.3%) than the photobiont (22.4%).
The extract of tissue culture of Hypogymnia physodes inhibits growth of gram positive bacteria Escherchia coli and Pseudomonas aeruginosa.
