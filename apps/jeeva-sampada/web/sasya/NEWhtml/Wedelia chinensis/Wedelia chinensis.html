HABIT & HABITAT
 Procumbent or ascending herb, rooting at lower nodes. It is found in Assam, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stem reddish, glabrous. Leaves spathulate-oblanceolate, 1.5-5.5 x 0.5-1.5 cm, acute at apex, entire or feebly serrate or slightly inrolled along margin, trinerved, shortly appressed hairy on both the surfaces, subpetiolate. Heads yellow, solitary on terminal peduncles, 5-7 mm across; peduncles 3-7 cm long. Involucral bracts in two-seriate; outer oblong, 4.5-7.5 x 1.5-2.5 mm, acute or obtuse at the apex, appressed pubescent; inner lanceolate, 4.5-6.5 x 1.0-2.0 mm. Ray florets few; corolla 5.5-7.5 x 2.5-4.0 mm. Achenes dark brown, 3.5-5.0 x 2-3 mm, glabrous. Pappus a minute, irregularly margined, withered cup of maturity.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Leaves are considered as tonic and it is used in cough, cephalagia, skin diseases and alopecia. Decoction of plant is used as a deobstruent and given in uterine hoemorrhage and menorrhagia.
