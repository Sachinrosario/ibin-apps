DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Small trees or shrubs. Leaves broadly ovate or suborbicular, 5-19 x 4-15 cm, acute to acuminate at apex, obliquely cordate or rounded at base, crenate at margin, scabrous above, tomentose beneath; petioles up to 1.8 cm long.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers in axillary, umbellate cymes; peduncles up to 3.5 cm long; pedicels up to 1 cm long. Sepals oblong-lanceolate or oblanceolate, 6-12 x 2-3 mm, tomentose. Petals oblong-obovate oe linear-oblong, 3-7 x 1.5-3 mm, obtuse. Stamens numerous. Drupes subglobose, 7-12 mm across, obscurely lobed, red or purple, pilose.
