 Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 Deciduous, spreading tree, up to 35 m tall with crooked trunk. The tree is found in forests along the base of the Himalayas from Nepal to Bhutan and north Bengal extending southwards to the dry hilly regions of Bihar, Orissa and Madhya Pradesh ascending up to 3,000 ft. Common in deciduous forests.
BioticZone: Chota Nagpur,Himalaya, Peninsular India and Upper Gangetic plain,

 Availability : 
Misc: Bettiah and surrounding areas,Khallari,Lamni,Martaban,Mergui, Pegu and Taurenga,
Country: Bhutan, Borneo, Burma, India, Java, Nepal and Thailand,
Zone: Central, East, Islands, North, North East and North West.
State: Andaman & Nicobar, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Orissa, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal.
District: Bankura, Bilaspur, Champaran, Mandla, Midnapur, Purulia and Raipur.
Status :  Wild.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES : Deciduous, spreading tree, up to 35 m tall with crooked trunk. Bark light- coloured, smooth. Leaves obovate, acute at apex, gradually narrowing at base, spinulose denticulate silky above, tomentose beneath when young, subentire to slightly dentate and pubescent or somewhat hairy beneath in adult. 20-40 x 12-20 cm with 30-45 pairs of close strong secondary nerves; petiole 1.5-4 cm long.Flowers 10-12 cm in diam, solitary, at the end of short, leafless side-branches. Peduncle to 4cm long, with a number of bracts. Sepals elliptic, 3-4 x 2-3 cm. Petals bright yellow, obovate-lanceolate,6.5-8 cm x 4.5-5.5cm. Stamens in 2 distinct groups, outer ones many, to 1.2 cm long, stright in bud, inner ones yellowish white, to in number, 2-2.5 cm long with their apex reflexed in bud. carpels 10-12, with to 2cm long styles. Fruit indehiscent, nearly globular, yellow or 0rang, 3.5-4cm in diam. including the enlarged fleshy sepals. Carpels to 2.5 x 1cm, 1 to few-seeded. Seeds exarillate, embedded in transparent slime.(4)

Other Uses

  1. The wood is light brown in colour and moderately hard and heavy (44-45 Ib./cu. ft.). It is mostly used as fuel.
  2. Ash made from wood and mixed with clay is used locally for making frir resisting resistant crockery. A paste made from bark , is given for thrush and weak gums.
