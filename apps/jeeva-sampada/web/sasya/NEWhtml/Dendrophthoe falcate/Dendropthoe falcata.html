Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
Strospeside (fig. 1a), m.p. 253°C, neritaloside, m.p. 135°C and odoroside F (fig. 1b), m.p. 233°C, isolated from leaves of plant growing on Nerium oleander. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

b-Amyrin acetate, b-sitosterol, stigmasterol, oleaolic acid and its methyl ester acetate from stems. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

(+)catechin and leucocyanidin obtained from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

Quercetin and gallic, ellagic and chebulinic acids isolated from plant growing on Terminalia tomentosa. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

Quercitrin isolated as major constituent from plant growing on different host plants. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).


Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany:

Medicine for abortifacient (Stem, Tarafder, 1985); Antifertility (Stem bark, Mudgal and Pal, 1980; Leaf + Stem, Goel et al 1984; Whole plant, Tarafder; 1983; Sharma, 1981); Brain tonic (Stem, Jain, 1965); Headache (Stem, Goel and Mudgal, 1988); Impotency (Whole plant, Jain, 1965); Paralysis (Stem, Chaudhuri et al, 1975; Leaf, Goel and Mudgal, 1988); Symbolic worn as armlet removes fever (Stem, Jain, 1971).
