HABIT & HABITAT: 
A small tree distributed throughout India, from Garhwal eastwards to Assam and southwards to Kerala, it is also found in Andaman Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES 
A handsome shrub or a small tree with tough branches, sometimes developing aerial roots. Leaves 2-3 pinnate, leaflets elliptic-oblong, flowers in corymbose cymes, whitish, berries depressed-globose, purple black.

  2. Leaves are used as green manure.

Other Uses 
  1. The large pith of stem and branches is often used as a substitute for Elder pith (from Sambucus spp) in biological laboratories. 
  2. The roots are used in diarrhoea, colic, dysentery and as sudorific. 
  3. The leaves are roasted and applied to the head in vertigo.
