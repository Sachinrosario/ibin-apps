Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A large tree found in Temperate Himalayan regions. Suitable for dry valleys. Cultivated in the North-West Himalayas at altitudes of 900 - 3,700 m., also planted in avenues, especially in Kashmir.


Availability :


Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A large tree with a narrow pyramidal crown and viscid buds. Bark grey, rough, deeply furrowed in old trees. Leaves ovate-rhomboid. Flowers in catkins, up to 10 cm long.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

 

Breeding system and varietal improvement:

Phenology:

It rarely flowers in India.


Pollinators:

Floral and seed biology:

Seedling Germination: It is raised from stem or root cuttings, and also from root suckers, which are produced mostly when the tree is cut down.


MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:

Ganoderma lucidum (Leyss.) Karst. – root rot

Corticium salmonicolor Berk. & Br. – stem canker followed by twig blight and die-back

Botryodiplodia palmarum (Cke.) Petrak & Syd. – stem canker

Cercospora populina Ell. & Ev. – leaf spot

Septoria populi Desm. – premature defoliation
