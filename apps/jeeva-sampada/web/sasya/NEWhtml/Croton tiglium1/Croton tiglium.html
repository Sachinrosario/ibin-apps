DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Small evergreen tree. Young parts sparsely stellate hairy. Leaves ovate or elliptic-oblong, acute or acuminate at apex, somewhat oblique at base, serrate at margin, glabrous, usually rounded, biglandular at base. Flowers in racemes. Male flowers: sepals bearded at the apex; petals woolly; stamens 15-15. Female flowers: sepals sparsely hairy at base within; petals 0. Ovary stellately hispid. Capsules obovoid, whitish, obtusely trigonous. Seeds obliquely oblong, globose, pale brownish.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flower: yellowish green, solitary or fascicled in the axils of minute bracts on long erect racemes, males in the upper part of raceme and female on lower part  ; Male flower: pedicellate, segments obtuse, petals elliptic lanceolate, stamens 15-20, inflexed in the bud, filaments hairy ; Female flower: Pedicellate, sepals more acute than males, petals ovate with dense wooly margins, styles subdivided into long slender branches ; Fruit: Capsule white terinately ovoid, obtusely trigonous ; Seed: Ellipsoid, rounded and quite smooth on the back . Flowering & Fruiting :  October- December. 
Floral and seed biology:
Fl. & Fr.: Throughout the year.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: In Ayurveda fruits and seeds are purgative. Seed-oil is purgative and carminative. It is also useful in disease of abdomen, mental troubles, convulsions, fever, insanity, inflammations, bronchitis. Seeds having bitter taste, causing a burning sensation, cathartic, expectorant, emetic; good in sore eyes, excessive phlegm, leucoderma. Oil is cathartic, tonic. It removes pus and bad matter from the body. Root is used as abortifacient.
Chemistry:
Isolation, structure and semisynthesis of new highly active tumor-enhancing compound C-3 (fig. 1) from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

Plant extract used in folk medicine for treating cancer, showed activity against P-388 lymphocytic leukaemia in mice. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

b-sitosterol from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Active principle – phorbol-12-tiglate-13-decanoate (fig. 2) – isolated from oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

Eleven short chain phorbol ester (I-XI) isolated from oil and their structures elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).
