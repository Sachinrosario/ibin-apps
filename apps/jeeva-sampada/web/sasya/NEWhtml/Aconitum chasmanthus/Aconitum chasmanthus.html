Availability :  

Conservation/ Threat Status : 
Plant is rare in  wild. Usually found in alpine and sub alpine zones of the western Himalayas.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   Inflorescence:In long narrow racemes ; Flower: Hermaphrodite, regular, complete; Calyx: Sepals 5, petaliod, posterior vaulted, the rest flat, 2 anterior narrow; Corolla: Petals 5, 2 posterior clawed, limb hooded and enclosed in helmet ;Androecium: Stamens many, anthers adnate, dehiscing laterally; Gynoecium: Multicarpellary, free, 1-celled, stigma simple ; Fruit: Follicles contiguous or slightly divergent at the tip; Seed: Seeds unequal, 3-winged . Flowering & Fruiting : Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: The plant is propagated through seeds. It may be stratified and sown in  spring.

Soil type: Thrives well in heavy clay and calcareous soils.

Nutrient requirements: Grows well in soil rich in humus.

Water management: It prefers moist conditions and grows well both in sun and semi shade conditions.

Chemistry:
  • The five alkaloids which have been isolated from the methanolic extract of the root A. heterophyllum are Base –A, C26H43O6N, m.p. 105- 107 ° ; Base –B C25H41O6N, m. p. 90-91 ° , Base –C, C34H47O9N, m.p. 181-182 ° , also isolated in a low melting form, m.p. 165-167 ° ; Base-D, C36H40O9N, m.p. 160-161 ° , and Base-E, C34H47O10N, m.p. 189-191 ° . Structure of Base-C and D which were proposed to chasmaconitine and chasmanthine respectively (Fig. 1a&b).    (Achmatowicz and Marion 1965).
  •  The alkaloid chasmanine, C25H41O6N isolated from root of A. chasmanthus (Fig. 2) contain four methoxyl and two hydroxyl groups as well as an imino-ethyl (Actimatowicz et al.,1965).
  •   The diterpene alkaloid chasmanine, having a 1 b -methoxy-substituent was shown to have a 1 a -methoxy group by x-ray crystallography this proves the structure of diterpene alkaloids Chasmanine (Fig. 3) (Pelletier et al., 1976).
  •   The crystal structure of chasmanthinine, C36H49NO9 a C19 nor diterpenoid alkaloid which was isolated from the roots of A. chasmanthus. Stap of ex Holmes contain independent molecules separated by normal vander waals distances. The mean bond distances are: N-Csp3 1.468 (11) Csp3 – Csp3 1.54(2), Csp2 – Csp2 1.471 (2), O-Csp3 1.428 (18), O-Csp2 1.338(2) and C=0 1.205 (4) A. The fused-ring system contain one boat, ½-chair, two chair and two envelope conformations. Crystallography data are given. (Masood et al., 1998). C.A. 1998, 129, 134682u.
  •   The molecular structures of two new C19 nor diterpenoid alkaloids, 14-O-benzoyl-8-methoxy bikhaconine [(1 a , 6 a , 14 a , 16 b )-20-ethyl 13hydroxy-1, 6, 8,16-Tetramethoxy-4 methoxy methyl aconitan-14-yl benzoate] and 14-O- benzoyl-8-ethoxybikhaconine [(1 a , 6 a , 14 a , 16 b )-8-ethoxy-20-ethyl-13-hydroxy-1, 6, 16-trimethoxy-4-methoxy methyl aconitan-14-yl benzoate], isolated for the first time from the roots of Acoitum chasmanthus. (Masood, et al., 1998), C.A. 1998, 129, 214091m.
  •   The crystal structure of indaconitine hemiacetonitrile solvate, [1 a , 6 a , 14 a (E), 16 b ]-2O-ethyl-16, 16-trimethoxy-4-methoxy methyl-3, 13-dihydroxyaconitane-8, 14-diyl 8-acetate 14-benzoate hemiacetonitrile solvate, C34 H47NO10 0.5 C2H3N, a C19 norditerpenoid alkaloid isolated from the roots of Aconitum chasmanthus.The molecular dimensions are as expected. The fused ring system contains one boat 1\2-chair, two chair and two envelope conformations. The crystal structure is established by an inter- molecular H bond between indaconitine and MeCN, with an O—N distance of 3.036(6) A ° . An hydroxyl group is also involved in an intermolecular interaction with a methoxy group with an O-O separation of 2.643 (5) A ° . crystallography data are given.   (Masood et al., 1999), C.A. 1999, 130, 146484k.
