Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

It is submerged aquatic herb with erect emergent inflorescence. It grows in brackish water or backwater areas. The depth of water varies from 1-4 feet. During monsoon (June-October) the backwater area gets flooded with fresh water whereas in winter and summer (November-May) the fresh water is pumped out by saline backwater of sea.
Availability :

Along coastal belt. Kerala, Maharashtra and Tail Nadu.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Endemic, Rare
It is critically endangered, rooted submerged hydrophyte with emergent inflorescence. Among the Indian species of Aponogeton, it is distinct from all others in its brackish water habitat, submerged leaves and seeds with 4-6 linear tortuous appendages.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Monoecious, tuberiferous, perennial, submerged aquatic herbs, ca 90 cm tall. Rootstock tuberous; tubers ca 4 x 2 cm, elongated, often narrow towards lower end; roots slender, fibrous, from the top of the tuber. Leaves: submerged, petiolate; petiole upto 30 cm long, sheathing at the base; lamina ca 55 x 4.2 cm, flat or slightly undulate, base narrowly tapering, apex narrowly tapering, obtuse or acute, main nerve prominent with 6 parallel nerves on either side. Spathe 1.5-6 cm, membranous, attenuate, cauducous, enclosing young inflorescence. Inflorescence: peduncles ca 65 cm long, thickened towards spike, cylindrical; spike ca 25 cm long, simple, white-lilac, densely flowered, sturdy, flowers arranged in all directions. Flowers: tepals 2, persistent, ca 2.4 x 2.5 mm, concave, obovate, obtuse, 1-nerved, often wider than long, white-lilac. Stamens 6, included; filament ca 1.5 mm long, narrow towards apex, yellow; anther two celled, pale yellow; pollen yellow, ca 18.30 µ across. Carpels 3, ca 1.4 mm long; stigma decurrent; ovary ca 0.7 x 0.7 mm, ovules 1-2. Follicles 0.9-1.3 x 0.5-0.9 cm, beaked, often spiculate. Seeds 0.8 x 0.3 cm, tapering towards both ends; embryo obovate, tuberous, crowned with 4-6 linear, tortuous appendages, upto 5 mm long, obtuse or subacute.
