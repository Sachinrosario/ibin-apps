Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A genus of glabrous succulent herbs with swollen roots containing sugars, commonly called Beets, found in Europe and the Mediterranean region and cultivated in various parts of the world. The cultivated beets include sugar beet, garden beet, leafbeet, chard or swiss chard and mangels.

 

Availability :

Sugar beet is commonly cultivated in areas between 35 and 60°N. With the increased demand for sugar, its cultivation has now been extended to the sub-temperate and sub-tropical regions. In India the promising areas for its cultivation are Punjab, Maharashtra, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Rajajthan, Tamil nadu, (Bhavanisagar and Coimbatore), and Karnataka(Belgaum).

Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/Cultivated

Breeding system and varietal improvement:

No detailed nomenclatural work is available in literature regarding the placement of all these types of beets either within the species, B,vulgaris Linn. Or in different species. Basically, these cultivated beets fall into two groups: (I) the Cila group (B.vulgaris var. cicla Linn.) including the beets grown as leafy vegetables, viz, leaf beet or spinach beet, and chard or swiss chard; and (II) the Crassa group [B.vulgaris var. crass (Alef) J .Helm]including the types grown chefly for their roots, used as a vegetable (garden beet or beet root,). Miscellaneous Information

A pharmaceutical preparation containing fat-soluble root extract is administered orally or parenterally to treat vascular and metabolic disorders in humans. The root juice exhibited anti-microbial activity against Salmonella typhi and Micrococcus pyogenes. The plant contains alkaloids of which betaine is a mild diuretic and emmenagogue. Infusion and decoction of the root are administered in stomach and bowel complaints.
