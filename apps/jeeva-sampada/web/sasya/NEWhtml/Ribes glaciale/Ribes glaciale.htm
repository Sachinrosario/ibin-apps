 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT : 
Shrubs up to 5m height, occasional in forest margins and Rhododendron forests between 2500 - 4000 m altitude.
Found in Himalaya (Kasmir to Bhutan), South Tibet and West China.
Habitat: Forests and Shrubberies.
BioticZone: Alpine Himalaya, Himalaya and Temperate Himalayas,

Availability : 
Misc: Afghanistan to S.W.China, Bhuna-Bistola, Hemkund, Kashmir to Bhutan and Way to Khankelia.
Country: India and Tibet.
Zone: Central and North.
State: Himachal Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.

Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Dioecious shrubs up to 5m high, branches woody silvery - greyish. Leaves 2 - 5 cm long, broadly ovate, shallowly 3 - lobed, middle lobe elongate, cordate at base, dentate at margins, acute at apex, puberulous above, glandular - pubescent beneath; petioles up to 2cm long. Racemes glandular pubes cent. Flowers dull purplish; bracts elliptic and glandular - ciliate.   Male flowers: Calyx lobes ca 4 mm long, linear oblong, acute. Petals not seen. Stamens ca 1 mm long. Berries globose, glabrous, red.
