HABIT & HABITAT
 Tree, 10-15 m high, deciduous; It is found in West Bengal, Tripura, Orissa, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka and Maharashtra.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Bark brown, corky hard wood. Leaves paripinnate; leaflets 7–10, elliptic-oblong, 7-10 x 3.5-5.0 cm, abruptly caudate-acuminate at apex, acute or rounded at base. Flowers in terminal panicles, yellowish with purple tinge at the throat. Sepals unequal, shortly lobed. Corolla bell-shaped, sparsely pubescent. Stamens 4. Capsules 4 – 50 cm long, spirally twisted, 4 angled. Seeds winged.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Bark is used as antibacterial and antitubercular. Root & bark is used against typhoid. Decoction of root is useful in asthma, cough and excessive thirst and leaves are used in chronic dyspepsis. Leaf juice boiled with oil is used in the diseases of ear, teeth and in rheumatism.
