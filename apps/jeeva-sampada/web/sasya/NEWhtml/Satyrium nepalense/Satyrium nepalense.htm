DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Tuberous, terrestrial herbs, 17-53 cm high with inflorescence. Tubers one or two, ovoid-oblong, 2.5-4.0 x 0.7-1.2 cm. stem short with one or two sheaths below. Leaves 2, rarely more, subradical, oblong, ovate-lanceolate, fleshy, often complicate, sheathing at base, obtuse-acute, 4.0-16.5 x 1.1-6.7 cm. inflorescence terminal, 10-30 cm long, dense. Scape covered by large foliaceous bracts, progressively smaller upwards. Flowers pink, fragrant, non-resupinate. Bracts longer than ovary, foliaceous, deflexed, ca 1.9 x 0.5 cm. Ovary ca 7 mm long with pedicel. Perianth reflexed over the ovary. Dorsal sepal ca 6.0 x 1.5 mm, oblanceolate-spathulate, obtuse. Lateral sepals ca 5.5 x 2.5-3.0 mm, oblong, thick, acute with a prominent submedian longitudinal nerve, pellucid gland dotted. Lateral petals ca 5.5 x 1.2 mm, as long as lateral sepals, more or less twisted at the mouth of the lip, narrowly lanceolate, obtuse, 3-nerved, median nerve keeled. Lip very conspicuous, globose, ca 4 mm across, hooded, held on the distal side with a narrow reflexed median rim and a prominent dorsal ridge without, 2-spured. Spur 1.1 cm long, held closely over the ovary, filiform, longer than ovary.
