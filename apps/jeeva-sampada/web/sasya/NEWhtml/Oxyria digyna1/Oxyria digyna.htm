HABIT & HABITAT
 Is a glabrous, succulent herb, found in the Himalayas from Kashmir to Sikkim, in the alpine region at altitudes of 3,000-6,000 m. A genus of perennial herbs distributed in the alpine and arctic regions of the northern hemisphere, Grazing grounds, Hill slopes and Open slopes.
One species occurs in India.
 BioticZone: Alpine Himalaya and Temperate regions,

Misc: Gothing, Jumma area and Pakistan to S.W.China,

State: Himachal Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.

Distributed in Alpine Himalaya from Kashmir to Sikkim, 10,000-17,500 ft altitute, Mountains of Europe, N Asia and America & in Arctic regions.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Herb with rounded, cordate or reniform leaves, which are mostly radical, and panicles of greenish or reddish flowers. Flowers in panicled racemes, bisexual. Sepals 4, Stamens 6, Ovary compressed, styles 2, short, stigmas fimbriate. Nut biconvex, 2-winged. Fruit 4-6 mm diameter.
