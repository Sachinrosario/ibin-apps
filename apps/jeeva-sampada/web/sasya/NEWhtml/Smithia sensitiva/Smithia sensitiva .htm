Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A much-branched, diffused, procumbent annual, 90 cm. high, distributed throughout India, up to altitude of 900 m. and in the Andaman and Nicobar especially in the moist areas. It is distributed to Madagascar, Abyssinia, Java and China.

It is also found along Stream side, Cultivated fields and Plains, Ditches, Gardens and Roadsides, grass land, Hedges of fields and gardens, Moist places, Moist shady places, on moist pasture lands, Open areas, Open forest, Open marshy lowlands, Plains, Rice fields, Swampy tracts, Waste land, Water-logged areas and Wetlands.

BioticZone: Himalaya, Konkan plain, Tropics, West Coast and Western Ghats.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves paripinnate leaflets 4-12, rarely up to 20, oblong; flowers yellow, in simple racemes in the axils of upper leaves; flattened, 4-6 jointed, densely papillate.
