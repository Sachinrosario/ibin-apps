HABIT & HABITAT :
A large, more or less climbing shrub, with drooping branches; branchlets and under side of leaves with soft tawny tomentum.
It can be seen in Forests, Hilly parts, Hilly region, Primary & Secondary forests, Secondary forests, stream, waste land and it is also recorded in biotic zones like Deccan, Kanara, Konkan and North Circars.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A large, more or less climbing shrub, with drooping branches; branchlets and under side of leaves with soft tawny tomentum. Leaves short petiolate, elliptic or obovate, 2-6 inch long; nerves 8-12 pair, with prominent transverse veins at right angles; stipules broad - lanceolate. Flowers monoecious, yellow- tomentose, subsessile, crowdedin lateral heads, in the axils of leaves or bracts, generally arranged in terminal paniculate spikes. Broad lanceolate tomentose braceoles between the flowers. Calyx-segments long-triangular, persistent and considerably enlarged in fruit. Drupes oblong-ovoid, often 2-seeded, nearly 1/2 inch long, black when ripe.
