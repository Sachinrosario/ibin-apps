HABIT & HABITAT:
 Stout undershrub, found to occur in Tropical and subtropical Himalaya from Kashmir ( 700-1000 m) to Bihar, Assam, Meghalaya, Deccan Peninsula, Konkan to Travancore. Grow in waste places, road sides and among bushes, usually in shades.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stout undershrub. Leaves variable, lower leaves larger, lanceolate, ovate-oblong or broadly ovate, acute or acuminate at apex, rounded at base, lobed or shallowly cordate, dentate at margin, coriaceous, glabrous, dark green above, minutely hairy beneath, bi-glandular, irregularly sinuate, sometimes 3-5 lobed. Petioles sometimes as long as blade; stipules of 2 glands. Inflorescence axillary racemes or short panicles. Flowers unisexual, males or with a few females towards base. Male calyx segments almost glabrous, suborbicular, membranous, finely mottled, disc of 6 glands, stamens 12-20. Female calyx-segments ovate-lanceolate, pubescent. Capsules ovoid.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Axillary racemes or contracted panicles; Male Flower:Calyx globose, 4-5 partite, glabrous or slightly pubescent, segments suborbicular, concave, imbricate, membranous, finely mottled, disk of 6 glands, Stamens about 20  ; Female flower: Sepals ovate lanceolate, pubescent, not accresent in fruit, disk thin entire, ovary hairy, styles thick, 2 partite, dull red, stigmatic surface smooth  ; Fruit: Capsules ovoid, usually hairy, of 3 two valved, crustaceous cocci; Seed: ellipsoid, quite smooth, mottled. Flowering & Fruiting: December- January.Flowers are pollinated by insects.Seeds are dispersed by wind.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: In Ayurveda roots are pungent, heating, purgative, anthelmintic, diuretic, alexiteric, cathartic. It is used in used in dropsy, anasarca, pains, skin diseases, diseases of abodomen, piles, wounds, enlarged spleen, itching, inflammations, anaemia, leucoderma and jaundice. Seeds are drastic purgative, in overdose acro-narcotic poison, externally used as stimulant and rubefacient. The oil is a powerful hydrogogue, cathartic, useful for external application in rhueumatism. Leaf-decoction is useful in asthma.
Chemistry:

·         New phorbol esters – montanin (fig. 1), 12-deoxyphorbol-13-palmitate (fig. 2), baliospermin (fig. 3), 12-deoxy-5b-hydroxyphorbol-13-myristate (fig. 4)and 12-deoxy-16-hydroxyphorbol-13-palmitate (fig. 5)- isolated from roots; structures determined by spectral studies. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

·         Montanine synthesized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

PHARMACOGNOSY

 Pharmacognostic study of stem, leaf and root. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.


Pharmacology:

·         All the five phorbol esters showed ED50 0.06-3.4mg/ml against Lymphocytic leukaemia. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

TOXICOLOGY

 ·         Techniques used to detoxicify Baliospermum montanum (root, leaves and seeds) are enumerated. (Shanvaskhan et al, 1997).
