Uses:
Chemistry:

·        Isolation of wedelolactone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

·        Isolation of a new benzofuran-norwedelic acid-from leaves and its structure elucidation; norwedelolactone also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        A new bisdesmosidic saponin (I) isolated from fresh leaves and charecterised as b-D-glucopyranosyl 3-O-b-D-xylopyranosyl 3-O-b-D-xylopyranosyl (1®2)-b-D-glucuronopyranosyl-olean-12-en-28-oate; ginsenoside Ro also obtained. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        A sesquiterpene lactone, wedelolactone, and a new compound, norwedelic acid [5, 6-dihydroxy-2-(2’, 4’, 6’-trihydroxyphenyl) benzofuran-3-carboxylic acid] were isolated from fresh leaves of Wedelia calendulacea. (Nguyen, et. al., 1998).

·        A triterpene, b-amyrin (I), has been isolated from dry leaves of Wedelia calendulacea Less. (Nguyen, et. al., 1998).


Pharmacology:

·        Effect of coumestans isolated from the leaves of Wedelia calendulacea was evaluated in paracetamol induced liver damage. The increased serum enzyme levels by paracetamol induction were significantly lowered due to coumestans treatment. Coumestans of Wedelia calendulacea afforded a significant protective action in the alleviation of paracetamol induced hepatocellular injury. (Emmanuel, et. al., 2001).
