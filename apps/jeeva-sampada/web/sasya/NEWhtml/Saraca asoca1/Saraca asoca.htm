Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A medium sized tree, grows in evergreen forests upto an elevation of about 750 m.
It prefers a semi shady and sheltered situation but is nevertheless very hardy and easy of culture.
Availability :

Throughout India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A medium sized handsome evergreen tree upto 9 m in height with numerous spreading and drooping branches; leaves pinnate, 30-60 cm long having 2-3 pairs of lanceolate leaflets; flowers orange or orange-yellow in dense corymbs, very fragrant; fruits flat black pods, leathery, compressed; seeds 4-8 per pod, ellipsoid, oblong and compressed. The bark is dark brown to grey or black with a warty surface, fresh cut ends pale yellowish red. The entire cut surface turn reddish on exposure to air. An indigenous evergreen slow-growing small tree, held sacred. The young leaves clustered and drooping as in Brownea and Amherstia.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:


Breeding system and varietal improvement:
Propagated from seeds.

Phenology: On the stem and branches are borne the flowers, which are slightly scented in dense erect panicles, 7 to 15 in diam., changing in colour from yellowish orange to orange scarlet. The flush of bloom is from February to May but the tree bears flowers intermittently throughout the year.
