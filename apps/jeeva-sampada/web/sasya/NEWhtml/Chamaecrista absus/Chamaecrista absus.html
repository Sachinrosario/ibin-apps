HABIT & HABITAT
 Annual, erect, 12-60 cm tall herb, viscous hairy; it is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Stem and leaves covered with bristly viscous hairs. Leaf-rachis (including petiole) 1-5 cm long; leaflets two pairs, elliptic or obovate, 0.8-3.0 x 0.5-1.5 cm, mucronate at apex, oblique at base, gland-dotted and pubescent on both surfaces; glands between both pairs of leaflets, sessile, flattened, ligulate; petiole 2.5-3.5 cm long, viscous bristly; petiolules 1-2 mm long. Stipules linear, 3-5 mm long, acute-acuminate, 1-nerved, recurved, persistent. Flowers reddish yellow, 4-12 mm long in terminal or leaf- opposed raceme; bracts broadly ovate, c 2 x 1.5 mm, acuminate; bracteoles 2, about half as long as bracts, linear-subulate. Sepals 5, oblong to narrowly ovate, 3-4 mm long, obtuse, membranous, glandular hairy on the back. Petals 5, broadly obovate, 4-7 mm long, pink or white. Stamens 4 or 5. Ovary sessile, densely bristly; style glabrous; stigma fan-shaped, ciliate. Pods strap-shaped, 2.0-4.5 x 0.5-0.6 cm, flat, oblique, copmpressed, clothed with  bristly hairs. Seeds 4-6, ovoid or rhomboid, 3.5-5.0 x 2.5-4.0 mm, black, shining.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Leaves are used as purgative and remedy for cough, asthma and bronchitis. The leaf-powder or paste is applied to heal venereal ulcers, to cure haemorrhoids, ringworm, pain, headache and leucoderma. Seeds are useful in ulcers, eye diseases, piles, bronchitis and pain. The baked kernels are used to cure purulent opthalmia and conjunctivitis and as a collyrium to strengthen the sight. In Unani system of medicine seeds are bitter and used in eye diseases, syphilitic ulcers and leucoderma.
