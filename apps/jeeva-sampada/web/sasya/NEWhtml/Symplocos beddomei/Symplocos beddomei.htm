Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A moderate to large, evergreen tree found in the western ghats from Konkan to Coorg at altitudes between 600 and 1,200 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A moderate to large, evergreen tree with a shady head. Bark often marked with white patches. Leaves crowded at the ends of the branches, subcoriacrous, elliptic-oblong, acute or acuminate, glandular-serrate, crenate or subentire, glabrous on both sides, polished and shining above, base narrowed; nerves 6-10 pairs.

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers sessile or shortly pedicelled, white, fragrant (having the odor of hawthorn), in pubescent spikes or racemes, each flower embraced in bud by a large broadly ovate acute foliaceous hairy and ciliate reticulately veined deciduous bract. Bracteoles 2, foliaceous, ovate, acuminate, hairy, ciliate. Calyx glabrous, lobes 5, ovate, subacute. Corolla divided to the base, the segments scarcely connate. Petals oblong, obtuse, either 5 or in one series or 6,7 or even 8 in two series. Filaments united in bundles attached to the very base of the petals. Style thick, with a ring of hairs and orange-coloured glands at the base. Fruit ovoid-oblong, dark-blue (becoming dirty yellow when dry), surrounded by the distinct calyx lobes.
