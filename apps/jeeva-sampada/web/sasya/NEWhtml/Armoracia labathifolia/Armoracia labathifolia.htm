Chemistry
It is the richest plant source of the enzyme paroxidose which is used for analytical purposes in many biochemical and clinical tests as a molecular probe in rheumatoid arthrihy and in enzyme immunoassays. The two man produrtion and overall yield of the enzyme can be enhanced by 2 steps hairy root culture method. The plant contains isothyrosinalis, the ether extract of the root isothiocyanates may irritate mucous membrane upon contact or inhilation. Ingestion of a large amount can cause bloody vomiting and diarrhoa. The plant extract inhibits the cholinesterase enzyme activity.

Other Uses

1.The plant can be used to decontamination of water polluted by Phenolic compounds.

2. The flavor of the Horseradish Root (Armoracia lapathifolia) is so bitter that it brings tears to the eyes. Horseradish is also an excellent diuretic, and is good for digestion problems. Herbalists combine horseradish and honey for coughs and asthma treatments. Externally, it can be used to alleviate the pain and stiffness caused by rheumatism.

3.There are many benefits of using Horseradish. Horseradish is a good expectorant, soothing for respiratory problems, and may help relieve rheumatism by stimulating blood flow to inflamed joints.

4.Horseradish can be used both orally or externally. For relief from a bad cold, mix grated Horseradish Root with honey and warm water, or take 1 tablet up to 3 times daily. To make a poultice, add cornstarch to fresh horseradish, and apply to the affected areas in a gauze bandage. When taking Horseradish orally, it is best to use the fresh product if it is available at your grocery store or herb store. Do not take large quantities at one time; it could cause diarrhea and excessive sweating.
