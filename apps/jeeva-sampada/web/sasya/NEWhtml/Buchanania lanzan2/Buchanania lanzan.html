DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Deciduous trees, 12-15 m tall, with rough bark. Leaves alternate, oblong to elliptic-oblong, 4-19 x 2-10 cm, apex obtuse, margin entire, coriaceous, tomentose beneath, pubescent above; petiole swollen, 1.5-2.0 cm long, pubescent. Flowers in terminal pubescent panicles, 4 mm across, greenish white, sessile, highly villous. Calyx lobes 5, ovate, pubescent, persistent. Petals 5, elliptic, 3 x 2 mm, pubescent. Stamens 10. Drupes globose or lenticular, 2 cm in diam., 2-valved.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Terminal and axillary pyramidal ferrugineo-pillosepanicles  ; Flower:  Small, sessile, greenish white, hermaphrodite, bracts small, cadaucous ; Calyx: Calyx-lobes short, broadly ovate, ciliate, persistent, imbricate ; Corolla: Petals 5, ovate-oblong, imbricate, sub-acute ;Androecium: Stamens 10, inserted at the base of the disk, a little shorter than the petals, filaments flattened, anther about as long as filaments ; Gynoecium: Carpels 5-6, Ovaries 1,perfect, conical, villous, the other 4 reduced to cylindrical filaments, Style short, stigma truncate; Fruit: Drupes, obliquely lentiform, black, stone hard, 2-valved; Seed: Gibbous acute at one end . 

Floral and seed biology:
It flowers between November to March. Flowering & Fruiting : January-March .
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by birds or animals.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
Fruits are edible. It tastes somewhat like Pistacio nuts and used in sweet-meats. Plants is used in bronchitis, cholera, cosunption, cough, dysuria, fever, inflammation of gums and scorpion bite. Stem and bark are also useful in burns, cuts, skin diseases, snake bite and wounds. Roots are useful in veneral complaints.
Chemistry:
Isolation of kaempferol and its 7-O-glucoside, gallic acid, quercetin, its 3-O-rhamnoglucoside and 7-O-rhamnoside from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).


Pharmacology:
Feeding of Buchanania lanzan significantly stimulated both CMI and humoral immunity in BALB/c mice as evidenced by the enhancement of MMI, HA titres, and PFC counts. The observation provides scientific basis for feeding Buchanania lanzan to mothers after child birth and to invalids with a relatively poor immune status.(Puri et al .,2000)


Ethnobotany:
