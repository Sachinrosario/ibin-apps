HABIT & HABITAT :
The variety var. cinnamomum (Wall.) Hook. f. is a small tree with smaller leaves and white or pale-rose or purple-spotted flowers, found in Nepal and North Bengal. The leaves (from Darjeeling) contain friedelin, epifriedelinol, ursolic acid, quercetin, and two minor unidentified components.
 Habitat: Forests, Hills, Primary forest in shaded places, Shrubberies and Temperate forests.
BioticZone: Himalaya, Hindukush himalaya, Temperate Himalayas and Western himalayas.

Availability :  
Soil: Acidic soils.
Misc: All along the Himalaya up to Arunachal pradesh, Amritgang valley, Kashmir to Bhutan, Khasia hills, Kukinakhal, Nilgiri hills, Ootacamund, Pakistan to S.E.Tibet, Pulney, Simla hills, Temperate regions, Thadlaskein, Near Jowai and Travancore.
Country: Burma, India, Myanmar, Sri Lanka and Tibet.
Zone: Central, North, North East and Southzone.
State: Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Kerala, Manipur, Meghalaya, Tamil Nadu and Uttar Pradesh.
District: Chamba, Chamoli, Jowai, Kangra, Khasi hills, Kinnaur, Kullu, Nilgiri, Pulney, Shimla and Simla.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
An evergreen, much-branched tree, up to 14 m. in height and 2-4 m. in girth, found in the Himalayas from Kashmir to Bhutan and in the hills of Assam and Manipur at altitudes of 1,200-4,000 m. Bark reddish brown, soft, rough, exfoliating in thin flakes, 2-5 cm. thick; leaves lanceolate or oblong, 10-20 cm. x 3-6 cm., crowded towards the ends of branches, petiole covered with white scales when young; flowers showy, in shades.

Chemistry
Leaves of var. nilagiricum contain ursolic acid, aamvrin, friedelin, epifriedelinol, a new triterpenoid named' campanulin (C50H50O, m.p. 200-02°; different from the polyfructosan campanulin isolated from the roots of Campanula punctata Lam.), quercetin, and hvperoside (3-D-galactoside of quercetin). Bark contains taraxerol, ursolic acid acetate, betulinic acid (4%) and ; leuco-pelargonidin. Flowers contain ursolic acid, quercitrin and resin.
The pigments present in the flowers are cyanidin-3-galactoside and cyanidin-3-arabinoside.
