DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
It is a broad-spreading, a deciduous evergreen tree reaches a height of 35 to 45 feet, branches are droopy, leaves; green, even-bipinnately compound, incised, serrate, ovate, oblong, Flowers are bright yellow, in panicles up to 18 inches long, very showy in the fall. Fruit; Bright pink capsules with papery texture oval and elongated.

Cultivation:

Flamegold is a tough tree which should be grown in full sun on any well-drained soil, in sand or heavy clay, and should receive moderate watering. The tree becomes leggy and thin in partial shade. When well-established, Chinese flame tree will tolerate air pollution, heat, and drought. Tolerant of alkaline soil pH, it does well in many parts of the Deep South where soils are often calcareous.
Propagation is by seed, which will germinate within six to eight days.


MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:
Pests: Scale insects

Diseases: Mushroom root rot, Canker, Verticillium wilt

Miscellaneous Information

 Trees need to be trained when young to develop a strong branch structure. Spacing main branches apart along a central trunk is crucial to developing a durable tree. Remove any double or multiple trunks which develop, especially if they have embedded or pinched bark in the branch crotch.
