 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
 Is a rigid, tall, erect or decumbent herb with ovate-lanceolate or oblong leaves and whitish flowers, distributed in Nepal and Sikkim and in the Khasi, Naga, and Cachar hills, and Assam, ascendinj up to an altitude of about 2,000 m. in Assam.
Habitat: Gardens and Roadsides,
