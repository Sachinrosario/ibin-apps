DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   Flower: Minute on the inner walls of the fleshy receptacles which is sessile or  in pair, axillary, globose, or pendulous, with broad basal bracts 3, coriaceous ; Male flower: Few sessile, globose, near the mouth of receptacles, sepals 3, lanceolate, stamens 1, anther single on filament ; Gall and Fertile flower: sepals 3, lanceolate, gall ovary smooth, obovoid, style in both elongate, stigma clavate; Fruit: Achenes minute, tubercled, mucilagenous ; Seed: Pendulous . 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : May-June . 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
 
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome number 2n=26.
