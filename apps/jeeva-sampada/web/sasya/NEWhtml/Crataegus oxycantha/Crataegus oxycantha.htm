Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

An ornamental tree in humid west Himalayan region, both swampy and dry ground, 1200-1800 m. Occurs in north-western Himalayas from Indus to Ravi, at altitudes of 6,000 - 9,000 ft. the plant does not thrive in Indian plains.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Wood used as fuel.

Wood: Wood is yellowish grey in colour, close and even-grained, smooth, durable, hard and heavy (wt. 46-56 lb/c. ft.).

Used for axe handles and walking sticks. It is suitable for engraving and is considered to be as good as boxwood for this purpose.

Other uses:

Fruits - Liquid extracts of the fruits is medicinally useful.

Food - Hawthorn marmalade has a high vitamin C content (up to 1.5 mg/g) and serves as a valuable source of the vitamin. The beverage similar to tea, made from the rind and pip, is also rich in vitamin.

Fruits - Fruits are edible. The liquid extract of the fruit is cardiac tonic.
