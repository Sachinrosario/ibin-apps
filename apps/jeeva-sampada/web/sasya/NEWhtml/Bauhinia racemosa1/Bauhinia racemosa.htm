HABIT & HABITAT
A shruby scandent with white flowers, cultivated throughout india. Fairly common in the deciduous forests. The species is heavily subjected to human interference, thus growing as small tree only. 

Distributed in Bahraich( UP), in Sub-Himalyan tracts from Ravi eastwards upto 1500 mtrs, in Oudh, Bengal, Western India, Central India, South India , Burma and Ceylon.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Small tree, 3-7 m high, leaves connate, lobes obtuse, shallow cordate at base, pubscent especially on the nerves beneath; flowers white with yellow stinge, 0.5-1 cm long; calyx spathaceous; pods hanging with a long stalk, flat, turgid. Flowers in terminal or leaf-opposed racemes 2-5 inches long; rachis densely pubescent; buds pointed; pedicles 1/8-3/16 inch long; bracts beneath the pedicles linear acute. Calyx pubescent; tube very short; limb1/4-1/3 inch long spathaceous, at length reflexed. Petals narrowly ablanceolate, acute, 3/8-7/16 inches long, white or pale yellow. Stamens 10, all fertile; filaments densely hairy at the base. Ovary pubescent; stigma sessile, somewhat falcate, glabrous, turgid not or scarcely veined. Seeds 12-20, oblong compressed, rounded at the apex and are black in colour.

Other Uses

1.Plant is considered sacred for performance of puja. 

2.Leaves used for the preparation of 'bidi' wrappers. 

3.The fruit is used in cough, asthma and diarrhea and the bark is astringent in dysentery. 

4.Used in interior constructions , ploughs , bedstead legs, carts , axles , yokes and turnery works.
