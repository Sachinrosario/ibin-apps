DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Large evergreen tree with large sapwood, whitish, very hard; red heart wood; weight about 60 lb per cubic foot. Flowers are small and fragrant. Berries are small and oval, yellowish when ripe.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used: 
Berries, Seeds, Wood, Green Fruit, Bark, 

Uses

  1. The seeds yield a kind of oil used for cooking, for burning and for medicine and also employed by painters.
  2. The tree yields pogada gum.
  3. The bark is used for dyeing shades of brown and also used as tan.
  4. Unripe fruit is astringent and useful masticatory for fixing loose teeth. The decoction of bark is also astringent and useful for gorgle in diseases of the gums and teeth.
  5. Flowers are used to prepare lotion for sores and wounds.
  6. A snuff made from the dried and powdered flowers is used in a disease called ahwah, which is common in Bengal.
  7. The bruised seeds are used in constipation of children
  8. The pulp of ripe fruit is sweetish; eaten by the poorer natives and astringent; used in curing chronic dysentery and also to relieve headache.

Other Uses
  1. Sapwood reddish brown, heartwood dark red, very hard, close and evenly grained, durable and polishes well, seasons well, diseases resistive.
  2. The fragrant flowers of which are used in Indian perfumery.
