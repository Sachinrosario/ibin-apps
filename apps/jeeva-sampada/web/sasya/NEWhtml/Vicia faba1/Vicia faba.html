·        Extracts seeds of faba bean and broad bean were prepared using 80% acetone. Five fractions were separated from each extract on a Sephadex LH-20 column, using methanol as the eluate. The antioxidant activity of fraction was investigated in a b-carotene –linoleate model system. For individual fractions, UV spectra were recorded their antioxidant activity was determined by TLC. Individual fractions contained several phenolic compounds as noted by TLC. Absorption maxima from UV spectra showed that flavonoids were the main phenolic compounds in separated fractions. Spots on the plates sprayed with a solution of b-carotene and linoleic acid indicated that many of these compounds could act as natural antioxidants. (Amarowicz, et. al., 1996). CA. 126:88499y.

·        ABA disrupted cortical microtubules in guard cells but not in epidermal cells, with concomitant closure of stomata. Other plant growth regulators did not disrupt the microtubules in guard cells. Thus disruption of microtubules seems to be a specific effect of ABA in guard cells but its physiological significance remains obscure. (Jiang, et. al., 1996).

·        Lipids and fatty acid in guard-cell protoplasts from Vicia faba leaves. Guard cell and mesophyll cell protoplasts were isolated from Vicia faba leaves and thin lipids and fatty acid quantitatively analyzed. All the glycerolipids and fatty acids that occurred in mesophyll cell protoplasts were found in guard cell protoplasts on a total fatty acid basis, levels of chloroplast lipids (monogalactosyldiacylsglycerol, digalactosyldiacylglycerol and sulpha quinovosycdiacylglycerol) and phosphatidylgycerol were lower and their constituent fatty acids were more saturated in guard-cell protoplasts than in mesophyll cellones. (Sakaki, et. al., 1996).

·        The hydroxycinnamic acid esters enhanced peroxidase dependent oxidation. Of DOPA and DOPA suppresses their oxidation. The degree of the enhancement was roughly correlated with rates of the oxidation of hydroxycinnamic acid esters. Since no mechanism of oxidation is given for the 3, dihydroxy phenylalanine (DOPA) in leaves of Vicia faba, the author hypthesized its oxidation by radicals of hydroxy- cinnamic acid esters (Takahama, Umeo, 1998). (CA 130: 322 979m). 

·        Vicia faba hulls (Broad Bean Giza 2), (Leguminosae) showed to contains 18 flavonoid glycosides, 3 aglycons and 2 phenolic acids. They were isolated from the 80% aqueous ethanolic extract after its fractional extraction using di-Et ether and Et acetate. The structure of the isolated compounds was identified through chromatographic, chemical and spectral analysis. The antibacterial effects of these extract and some of the isolated compounds were studied by using the agar-diffusion, technique on six different microorganisms. (Bacteria). (Micheal H.N., 1998). CA   131: 331766n.

·        Vicia faba lectins specifically trigger IL-8 production by the human colon carcinoma and Inc Caco-2-Cultured Caco-2 cells were stimulated with Vicia faba lectins. The production of IL-1, Il-6, IL-8 and MCP-1 was extracted by ELISA and RT-PCR. (Rodriguez-Juan, et. al., 2000).

·        The seed lipid contents were found to be between 2.30 and 3.91%. The fatty acid compositions of 8 different species were determined by gas chromatography of the Me esters of their fatty acids. The seed oils of Vicia species contain palmitic and stearic acids as the major component fatty acids among the saturated acids with small amounts of myristic, pentadecannoic, arachidic and behenic acids. The major unsaturated fatty acids found in the seed oils were oleic, linoleic and linoenic acids.    (Akpinar, N. 2001).

Pharmacology:

·        Wyerone was active against a number of phytopathogens and dermatophytes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1970-1979).

·        Effects of hulls of Vicia faba L. tannins on the apparent ileal and fecal digestibility of nutrients and the excretion of endogenous protein in ileal digesta and feces of pigs was studied and vs concluded that condensed tannins in faba beans interact with both dietary and endogenous proteins in the digestive tract of pigs. This reduces the true digestibility of dietary protein and increases the excretion of endogenously secreted proteins.  Tannins from faba beans show some prefernce to interact with proteins with a high content of proline and histidine. (Jansman, et. al., 1995).

·        The effect of the extract obtained and of chemically pure tannins on the absorption of glucose, methionine and water in the small intestine of rat was determined using gut perfusion and everted sacs method. Significantly lowering of glucose and methionine absorption took place when perfusin fluid was supplemented with analogical amount of chemically pure tannins. (Zdunczyk, et. al., 1996). MAPA 9703-1601.

·        Dopamine (DA) is known to increase diuresis and natriuresis through its action on renal dopaminergic receptors. Augmentation of intra-renal DA concentration by enhancement of its in situ production is greatly dependent on the availability of its precursor L-DOPA to the sites of its renal decarboxylation. Vicia faba is a ubiquitous plant rich in easily absorbable L-DOPA. Following ingestion of 40g freshly chopped Vf containing 120-130 mg of L-DOPA, plasma L-DOPA and urinary sodium and DA exeretion increased significantly. The DA/Cre ratio reached a maximum level (280±58microg/g) 60 minutes after Vf ingestion. The use of Vf is suggested in treating conditions such as hypertention, heart failure, renal failure and liver cirrhosis in which natriuresis and diuresis are medically beneficial. (Vered, et. al., 1997).

·        Vicia faba consumption of cooked/raw field beans by young men lowered the blood serum glucose; insulin triacylglycerols total cholesterol, LDL-cholesterol, and very– low-d. lipoprotein (VLDL) - cholesterol. The legume intake also increased glucagons and high-d. lipoprotein cholestrol levels. Neither cortisol nor thyroid hormone values changed significantly. The hypocholesterolemic effect of field bean intake may depend on a concomitant increase in blood serum ducagon and decrease in insulin values. The more market redction in the triacylglycerol and VLDL-cholesterol concentrations in subjects who consumed raw field beans indicates a corparticipation of their thermo labile components. (Fruhbeck, et. al., 1997).

·        Naturally occurring amino acid L-3, 4-dihydroxyphenylalanine (L-dopa), was isolated from seedling of Vicia faba beans. It is used as the most efficacious drug treatment of Parkinson's disease (PD). D, L-dopa exerted significant effects on glucose metabolism (causing marked hyperglycemia) and on arterial blood pressure. In the animals and human body L-dopa was enzymatically converted to dopamine (DA), the first biologically active amine in the biosynthetic chain of tissue catecholamines. L-Dopamine dopa reversed the reserpine-induced state of "tranquilisation" and that its decarboxylation product (DA) occurred in high amounts in animal and human brain, with a preferential localization in the basal gangalia. L-dopa's strong therapeutic effect in patients with PD was demonstrated. In 1967, the chronic high-dose oral L-dopa regimen was successfully introduced into clinical practice. L-dopa use in PD is a classic example of a brain neurotransmitter replacement therapy. DA replacement potential of L-dopa may not be its sole action of interest, as suggested by recent evidence that L-dopa may also have its own biological activity in the CNS, independent of DA. (Hornykiewicz O, 2002).

·        A new trypsin-chymotrypsin inhibitor, with an N-terminal sequence showing some differences from the previously reported trypsin-chymotrypsin inhibitor, was isolated from the broad bean Vicia faba. The inhibitor was a peptide with a molecular mass of 13 kDa. It exerted antifungal activity toward Mycosphaerella arachidicola and Physalospora piricola. The trypsin -chymotrypsin inhibitor elicited a mitogenic response from mouse splenocytes and inhibited the activity of human immunodeficiency virus-1 reverse transcriptase. (Ye XY and Ng TB, 2002).

Pharmaceutical and Toxicology:

·        Groups of male and female rats were fed acctylated faba bean globulin (ac VFG). The subchronic toxicity study revealed growth retardation in both sexes at the 80 g kg-1 acVFG. Histopathology changes and decreases in the absolute and relative weight of the uterus were found in all groups fed acVFG. Therefore a no-obsd-effect level was not derivable. Feeding females 80 gkg-1 acVFG resulted in diminished pre-implantation losses but increased post-implantation losses in the fertility study. (Schmandke et. al., 2000).

Miscellaneous Information

·        Lambs were fed Bakla based rations for one year and two metabolism trails were conducted (winter and summer). The gross efficiency of crude protein (CP) and metabolizable energy was significantly higher at early stage of growth in winter season and it can be concluded that Bakla can replace partial or entire CP of concentrate mixture. (Fulpagara, 1996). MAPA 9704-2106.

·        Vicia faba meal proteins were supplemented with black cumin (N. sativa) cake protein (protein content 27%) and their amino acid components as well as their biological effects on growing rats were investigated. Compared with sole corn or bean meal protein, substitution of 25% corn or bean meal protein with N. sativa cake protein in the diet remarkably raised the growth rate and led to higher levels of rat total blood serum lipids and triglycerides. The supplemented diet also increased the blood serum total proteins, albumins, globulins, and the activity of blood serum phosphatases and transaminas (Gaby, AMAI, 1998). CA 129: 342889q.
