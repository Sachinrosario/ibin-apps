DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Shrubs; branches, undersurface of leaves and inflorescence rusty tomentose. Leaves 3-foliolate, 5-16 cm long; leaflets elliptic to oblong-obovate, 4-16 x 2.5-5.0 cm, apex blunt, base cuneate, shallowly rounded-toothed in the upper half of margin. It is also occurs in Nepal.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Flowers in axillary and terminal panicles. Male flowers 2-3 mm in diam., pedicellate. Calyx lobes ovate. Petals linear-oblong, 1 x 0.5 mm. Drupes globose, 0.3-0.4 cm in diam., epicarp glabrous, wrinkled, indehiscent. It flowers and fruits between May-August.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
 Fruits are edible, when mixed with salt taste like tamarind. The juice is having vermifuge property. Dried leaves and either mixed with or substituted for tobacco. The wood is used for fence-post and as fuel. It is suitable for turnery and also for small tool-handles. This species produces profusely root suckers and can be successfully chosen for afforestation programme.


Chemistry:
