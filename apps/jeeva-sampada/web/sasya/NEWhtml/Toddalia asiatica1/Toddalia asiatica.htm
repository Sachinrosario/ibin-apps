DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 A very variable rambling, prickly, sarmentose shrub. In the plains, particularly in dry situations, the plant assumes the form of a low shrub with smaller and  narrower leaflets.
Roots woody with c. 2mm. thick bark, consisting of a soft, yellow, corky erternal layer, longitudinally wrinkled, leaves digitately trifoliate, leaflets oblong, elliptic or obovate, glanduler, flowers white or greenish yellow, in axillary cymes, polygamous, fruits fleshy, globose, orange- coloured, 7-12 mm. in diam, seeds reniform.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Leaves, fruits, roots and root bark. 

Uses

  1. When the plant  properly tended forms a pretty hedge with clusters of edible fruits.
  2. The ripe berries are as pengent as black pepper and are pickled.
  3. In Philippins, the fruits are used to flavour the food. 
  4. The leaves from Philippines yield 0.08% of an essential oil with the odour of camphor and lemongress.
  5. The root bark yields a yellow dye.

Chemistry
The root bark contains alkaloids: cheleythrine ( toddaline; m.p. 204 -050).
Dihydrochelerythrine (toddalinine; C21 H19 O2N; m.p. 1650).Chelerythrine acetone adduct (C24 H23O5N;m.p.193-940, present as an artefact).skimmianine, and 7,8-dime-thoxy-2',3'-methylene   dioxy-1,2- benzophenanthridine (C20H15O4N; m.p. 220-210). Other constituents reported include an oil with cinnamon like odour, fatty oil (3%), resin (7%), diosmin, toddalolactone (C16H20O)6, acceleatin, pimpininllin, isopimpinellin and an artefact (C16H19O5Cl; m.p.1530).

Other Uses
  1. The root bark is aromatic and pungentand is credited with diaphoretic, stomachic and antipyretic properties.
  2.  It is considerd to be a potent antimalarial drug and is said to have shown both antipyretic and antiperiodic effect.
  3.  The root bark is also used in cases of diarrhoea and constitutional debility during convalescence.
  4.  An infusion of the fresh bark is a stimulating tonic and carminative.
  5.  The alcoholic extracts of leaves and roots have been found to possess anti bacterial properties.
  6.  The juice of the flowers is sometimes applied to the stings of wasps, for immediate relief.
