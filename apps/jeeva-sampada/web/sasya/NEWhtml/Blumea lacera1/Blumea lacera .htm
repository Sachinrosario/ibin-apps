HABIT & HABITAT
An erect annual, with a strong odour of turpentine distributed throughout the plains of India, ascending to 2,000' ; also in Ceylon, China and Malaya.Occurs in shady places and also found along slopes of ghats, along wayside, Decidious forest, dense forest, Forest edges, forest reserves, Gardens, Herb, Hilly regions, hilly slopes, Maize fields, Moist places, open Areas, Plains, rice fields, river banks, river-stream-canal banks, Road sides, Sea coast, Shady habitat, Sugar cane fields, tea gardens, Undergrowth forests, waste land, Wet habitat, Wheat fields.

It's BioticZone is Deccan Plateau, Kanara, Konkan, Konkan plain, Palaeotropics, Plains, Tropics, West Coast, Western Ghats.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
An erect, strong smelling herb with thin very variable leaves. Leaves oval or obovate, dentate or serrate. Heads in short axillary cymes or terminal, spiciform panicle.

Chemistry
Plant contains a diester of coniferyl alcohol,acetylenic compounds, a thiophene derivative,serial parts gave campesterol,hentriacontane,hentriacontanol,alpha-amyrin and its acetate and beta-sitosterol,leaves contain alpha-fenchone and flavonoids-5-hydroxy-3,6,7,-pentamethoxyflavone.
