REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
Chemistry:
Isolation of berberine, berberrubine, jatrorrhizine, thalifendine, palmatine and N,N-dimethyl-lindcarpine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

Berlambine, dihydroberlambine and noroxyhydrastinine isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

New protoberberine alkaloids – oxypalmatine (fig. 1), (-)8-oxotetrahydrothalifendine (fig. 2), (-)8-oxo-isocorypalmine (fig. 3a) and either (-)8-oxothaicanine (fig. 3b) or (-)8-oxo-3-hydroxy-2,4,8,10-tetramethoxyberberine (fig. 3c) isolated from stems of Thai plant; berberine and (-)8-oxocanthine also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
