Chemistry:

·        Aristolochic acid, m.p. 283° isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

·        Pin-2-en-8-ol (fig. 1) and its acetyl derivatives isolated from essential oil of aerial parts along with b-caryophyllene oxide, linalool and bornyl acetate. Volatile fraction from underground parts contained calarene,maaliol and 1(10)-aristolen-2-one. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

·        A new aristolochic acid derivative (I) (fig. 2a) and aristolochic acids I (fig. 2b), II (fig. 2c), III (fig. 2d) and IV (fig. 2e) isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

·        Fatty acids, polyprenols and methyl, ethyl, iso-butyl and phytyl esters of fatty acids obtained from nonvolatile fraction of root extract and aerial parts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
