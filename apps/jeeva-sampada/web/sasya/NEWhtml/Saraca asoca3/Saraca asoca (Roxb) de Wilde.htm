Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT;:
Saraca asoca is a small evergreen tree commonly found in India. It occurs almost throughout India up to an altitude of 750 m, in the central and the eastern Himalayas and in the Khasi, Garo and Lushai hills.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
A small evergreen tree, 6-9 metre in height. Bark with warty surface, dark brown to gray or almost black. Leaves are paripinnate, 15-20 cm long, leaflets 6-12, rigidly subcoriaceous, oblong or oblong - lanceolate. Flowers borne in dense axillary corymbs, very fragrant, orange or orange-yellow, finally turning Vermillion - red. Pods, flat, oblong, woody. Seeds 4-8, ellipsoid - oblong, compressed. The leaves are paripinnate and the leaflets 6-12, oblong and rigidly sub-coriaceous. The flowers are orange or orange-yellow, eventually turning vermillion, very fragrant, in dense axillary corymbs; the pods, flat, leathery, the seeds, 4-8, ellipsoid-oblong and compressed.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Parts Used:
Seeds, Flowers, Bark

Uses:
Seeds used as a diuretic. Flowers are useful in blood dysentery. Stem - bark is used to cure colic, dysentery, dyspepsia, piles, ulcers and uterine troubles, in particular menorrhagia due to uterine fibroids, leucorrhoea and menstrual pain.

Other uses:
The bark has a stimulating effect on the endometrium and ovarian tissue, is useful in menorrhagia due to uterine fibroids, in leucorrhea and in internal bleeding, where ergot is indicated. It is well established for its effectiveness in menorrhagia and dysmenorrhea. It also has a stimulatory effect on the ovarian tissue and may produce an oestrogen-like effect that enhances the repair of the endometrium and stops bleeding.
 Healthcare Properties:

1.     Abdominal discomfort: Boil the bark of Saraca asoca with cow milk. Add some powdered sugar candy to it. Filter it with a cloth. Take this orally once a day with honey for 21 days.

2.     Cut wound: Grind the bark of Saraca asoca and make a paste. Warm this paste. Apply this paste over the wound once in a day for two days.

3.     Hysteria: Boil the bark of Saraca asoca in an earthen put. Extract juice from it. Take drink this juice twice daily for a week.

4.     Leucorrhoea: Boil the bark of Saraca asoca with cow milk. Add some powdered sugar candy to it. Filter it with a cloth. Take this orally once a day with honey for 21 days.

5.    Menstrual disorder: Grind together the bark of Saraca asoca with two numbers of black pepper and 2 g latex of Salmalia malabarica. Mix this paste with a small quantity of water that has been used for washing rice. Take this orally in empty stomach with honey once a day in the morning for three days (Take only when menstrual starts).
