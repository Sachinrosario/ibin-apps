Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
An unarmed, dioecious, large shrub or a small tree 8 m. high, distributed throughout India, ascending up to an altitude of 2,000 m., and in the Car Nicobar Island. It is also found along slopes of ghats, deciduous forest, edges of forest, forest edges, Forests, Forests & Forest borders, forests and wastelands, Hill slopes, Open areas, Paddy fields, Plains, Road sides, Sandy regions, Semi evergreen forest, Undergrowth, waste land and wet mixed forests.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Bark smooth, thin, rusty or reddish brown; leaves very variable, elliptic, ovate, obovate or orbicular, 2.5-7.5 cm. xl.6-4.5 cm.; flowers minute, greenish yellow, scented,in axillary clusters, male numerous; female whitish, gene-rally few; berries globose, 2 types; 3-4 mm. with dry pericarp; 8 mm. with fleshy, white pericarp; seeds 3-6 in a fruit, punctate.

Chemistry
 Several alkaloids have been reported to occur in different parts of the plant. Securinine (m.p 142-143°) has been isolated as the main alkaloid from the plant along with viroseciirinine ( m.p. 139-140°). Stem and root barks contain 0.4-0.6 and 1-0 per cent total alkaloids respectively, and one of the alkaloid isolated is flueggeinc ( m.p. 228°); roo bark is also reported to contain the alkaloid virosim ( m.p. 135-136°). Other alkaloids reported to occur in the plant arc : hordenine ( m.p.. 117.5°), nor-securinine ( m.p. 81-82°), an uriidentified base ( m.p. 135-136°) from the roots and viroallo-sccurinine ( m.p. 135-37°) front the leaves. Bergenin (m.p. 138^59°) is present in the leaves

Other Uses
  1. Bark contains 8-9 per cent tannins, and is used for tanning; the bark is also used to dye matting black.
  2. Extracts of stem bark, when injected subcutaneously in mice, caused 60 per cent mortality. Root is reported to possess analgesic and aphrodisiac properties
