DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stems flexous, furnished with numerous opposite pairs of green root-like pectinate organs. Submerged leaves to 3 cm long; floating ones 2-6 cm in diam., broader than long, entire in lower part, irregularly inciso-dentate-serrate in upper part,  reddish and densely pubescent  or villous beneath; petiole  8-15 cm long, hairy or wooly; stipules minute. 
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Flowers bisexual, solitary, axillary, 4 merous. Calyx pubescent; segments 4;  lanceolate, acute, 2 of which   turning to thorns in fruits. Petals 4, white, obovate, crenulate, about twice as long as calyx-segments. Stamens 4. Drupe somewhat urn-shaped, 2-2.5 cm long and broad, with a short beak in centre at apex and a flattened very sharp thorn at either side; shell bone-hard; thorns barbed at apex. It flowers and fruits between August to December.
