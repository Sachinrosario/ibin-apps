HABIT & HABITAT
Erect or suberect or ascending, annual, aromatic herbs, 15-20 cm high, hairy. Stem slender, robust, 4-angled, branching from the base; branches 4-angled, grooved, found almost throughout India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Erect or suberect or ascending, annual, aromatic herbs, 15-20 cm high, hairy. Stem slender, robust, 4-angled, branching from the base; branches 4-angled, grooved. Leaves ovate, broadly ovate or sub-orbicular, 0.5-7.0 x 0.5-3.5 cm, acute or obtuse at apex, cordate at base, crenate along margin, pubescent on both surfaces; petioles slender, 0.5-2.0 cm long, hairy. Flowers bluish-purple, arranged in long, stalked axillary cymes; bracts setaceous. Calyx 3.5-4.0 mm long, oblique, ribbed, villous, lobes 5; 3 upper lobes deltoid; 2 lower filiform, smaller. Corolla violet white or bluish-purple, bilabiate; lower lobes with 2-fid. Stamens 4, slightly exceeding from the upper lip of corolla. Nutlets oblong-elliptic, 1x 1 mm, light-brown with white spots.
