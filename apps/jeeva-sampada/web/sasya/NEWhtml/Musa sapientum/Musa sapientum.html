DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Pseudostems, 2.4-4.5 m high. Leaves oblong  1.2-1.8 m long. Spikes soon decurved and finally drooping, 90 cm or more long with very large ovate deep red or dull purplish bracts, lower 15-20 cm long and deciduous, upper often forming a clib.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Lower bracts with numerous 2 seriate female or bisexual greenish-yellow flowers c 3.8 cm long, above these bracts contain male flowers only or the terminal ones empty. Connate part of the perianth 5-toothed. Fruits oblong, 3-gonous in the wild form, c 7.5 cm long with very astringent scent. Seeds rugose.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda the root is acrid, tonic. It is used in ear pain, menstrual disorders, blood diseases, diabetes, leprosy. Stem juice is astringent to bowels, anti-dysenteric and used in thrist, urinary discharges, leprosy, ear, blood, vaginal and uterus diseases. Flowers are used in bronchitis. Unripe fruit is useful in leprosy, burning sensation, vaginal and urinal discharges. In Unani leaves are good for scabies. Root juice is anthelmintic. Fruits used in dry bronchitis, sore throat and kidney troubles (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
