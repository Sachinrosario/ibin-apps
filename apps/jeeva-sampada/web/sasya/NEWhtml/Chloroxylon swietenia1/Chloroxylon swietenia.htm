Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Found abundantly in Madhya Pradesh, dry deciduous forests of Maharashtra & Karnataka, Peninsular India, and in Konkan region. A moderate size tree attains the height about 30-40 ft. this is one of the associates of Shorea tumbyggaia. Suitable for successful plantation in shallow black cotton soils.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel:
Amongst the good fuelwoods of India.
Wood:
Wood is hard and tough, Porous, shows characteristic luster due to presence of a special physical property of cell walls to reflect light. 2 growth rings are present, parenchyma is well developed arranged in a continuous line, visible to naked eye, wood shows ripple marks on tangential surface, does not show any variation in specific gravity due to change in the rate of growth. Average weight of wood is 960 kg per cu. m. at 12% moisture content, average durability of wood is around 39 months.
Used as decorative and ornamental timber. Used for making agricultural implements, axles for carts and carriages, brushes, oil mills, golf clubs, furniture & veneers.
