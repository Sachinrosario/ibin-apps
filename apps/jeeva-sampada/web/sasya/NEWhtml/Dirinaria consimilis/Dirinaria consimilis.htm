Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

This foliose lichen grows on trees abundantly, sometimes on boulders in open places.
Availability :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Thallus foliose, suborbicular, patches up to 8 cm in diam., glaucous grey to pale grey in colour; laciniae radiating, 1 - 2 mm wide, subpinnately divided, sorediate; soralia farinose; under side black with tubercle like rhizinae. Apothecia laminal with black disc; ascospores bicelled, brown, 12-21 x 5-7 µm.
