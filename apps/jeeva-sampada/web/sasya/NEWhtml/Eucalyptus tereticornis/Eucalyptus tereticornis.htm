Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Tree. Indigenous to Australia. Occurs in dry areas of Rajasthan and Peninsular India (dry tropical)

Tropical to subtropical, semi-arid to arid, with annual rainfall as low as 400 mm, though the ideal range is between 800 and 1500 mm. Adapted to wide range of temperatures between 400 C and 00 C with frequent frosting, on altitudes up to 1000 m. Deep, well drained, light, neutral or slightly acidic soils are best suited but also adapted to a variety of poor soils. Strong light demander. Tolerates mild frost and light fires. Drought sensitive. Annual water requirement of 1200 mm. Occurs in hill station of India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Large, evergreen, straight tree with smooth bark and thin, evergreen foliage. Attains a height of 45 m, with straight trunk up to 1-2 m in diameter and erect growth habit.

Floral and seed biology:

Seed Germination & Propagation: Grown by entire transplanting. For raising plantations of Eucalyptus, seedling stock is raised in the nursery. Seeds are sown broadcast in boxes or beds during September/ October or February/March. Germination takes place in one to two weeks. Seedlings are pricked out in polythene containers when they are 5-10 cm high. The plants are watered regularly. Polythene bags are shifted from time to time to prevent plants taking roots in the soil. Nursery seedlings are outplanted with the onset of the summer monsoon. Transplanting is completed early to enable seedlings to establish during the rains. Seedlings in the polythene bags are generally to establish during rains. Seedlings in polythene bags are generally used for planting though naked-root planting is practised in some states. Before planting in pits, an insecticide is generally added to soil to protect seedlings against termite damage to which the tree is susceptible.

Plant has coppicing ability.



MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:

Cylindrocladium scoparium Morg. – seedling blight in Goa and Dehra Dun (U.P.)

Cylindrocladium quinqueseptatum Boedijn & Reitsma – seedling blight in Dehra Dun and Kerala.

Cercospora eucalypti Cke. & Mass. – leaf rust

Phytophthora cinnamomi Rands – root rot

Ganoderma lucidum – root rot

Corticium salmonicolor B & Br. – pink disease

Alternaria alternata (Fr.) Keissler – leaf spot

Mosaic virus – bushy growth

Little leaf – stunting and reduction in leaf size

Leaf crinckle – upward curling of leaves

Termites attack saplings.

Susceptible to snout beetle and mole cricket.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Fuel:extensively used as fuel. Good firewood as it is heavy and burns slowly. Calorific value: 4,880 kcal/kg.

Gives good quality charcoal. Calorific value: 7,550 kcal/kg.

Wood: Wood, with sp. gr. 0.75 or more. Annual yield is 22-25 m3/ ha.

Suitable for transmission poles, packing cases, crates, boxes, beams, columns, poles and posts. Also used as timber as well as for furniture.

Newsprint and rayon grade pulp of good quality. Wood employed for making railway-sleepers and for ship building and flooring.
