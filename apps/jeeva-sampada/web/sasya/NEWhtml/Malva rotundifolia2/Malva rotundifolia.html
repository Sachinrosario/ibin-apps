 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
It is herbaceous plant, INDIA: Throughout Himalayas in temperate forests between 2000 and 3000 m. Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal  (Darjeeling), Rajasthan (Jaipur; Ramniwas garden), Himachal Pradesh: Chamba, Kangra, Kinnaur, Kulu, Lahuland spiti. BASHAHR HIMALAYAS: Between Sangia and Rancham (alt.  2600 m.), between Shalabag and Chora (alt. 2200m), Kilba; Pangivillage; Roghi (alt. 2000 m); Sangla (alt.2750 m), Taranda (alt. 2185 m); Urni (alt.2300-2800 m); Wangto (alt.  1500 m.). Afghanistan, Pakistan, Europe, Nepal, Bhutan , Mayanmar, Australia.
