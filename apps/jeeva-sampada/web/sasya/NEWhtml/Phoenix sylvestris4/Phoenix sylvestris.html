DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Robust palm, without root suckers. Trunk 8-15 m high, rough with persistent bases of leaf-sheaths. Leaves 3.0-4.5 m long, greyish-green; pinnules numerous, densely fascicled, 12.5-44.5 x 1.5-3.0 cm, rigid, ensiform, cannaliculate, spinous-pointed; petioles compressed towards the apex and bearing a few short spines at bases.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Male flowers: spadix 55-85 cm long, erect; spathes wooly; spikes numerous towards the apex; peduncles in fascicles, simple. Female flowers: spadix and spathe same as in male; spikes arranged in distant group; fruiting spadix ca 90 cm long. Drupes oblong-ellipsoid, orange-yellow, 2.5-3.0 cm long. Seeds pale brown, rounded at ends.
Floral and seed biology:
It flowers between January-October.
