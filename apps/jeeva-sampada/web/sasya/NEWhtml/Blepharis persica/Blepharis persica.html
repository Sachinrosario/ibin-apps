DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Inflorescence: Terminal spikes; Flower:  Sessile, bracteate, bracteolate, complete, hermaphrodite; Calyx:  4-partite nearly  to the base, segments unequal in opposite pairs, one of the larger pair emarginate, the inner 2 segments narrower and much shorter; Corolla: 2-lipped, tube rigid, upper lip sub-obsolete, lower lip spreading nearly flat, oblong, with 3-5 rounded lobes, blue ;Androecium: Stamens 4, didynamous, filaments thick, rigid those of lower stamens produced beyond the attachments of anthers, anthers with 1 large cell small ; Gynoecium: Ovary 2-celled, ovules 2 in each cell, style linear, stigma shortly 2-fid ; Fruit: Capsule oblong, scarecely contracted at the base, 2-4 seeded; Seed: Compressed, orbicular, echinate, supported on rigid retinacula.


   
Floral and seed biology:

Flowering & Fruiting : September-October. 

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
