 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
An erect shrub  up to 3 m. high ,found in the Western  Himalayas from Kumaon to Kashmir at altitudes of 2,300-3,500 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
  It is a perennial shrub. Leaves ovate-cordate,3-5 lobed, racemes elongate,  pendulous, flowers greenish yellow, berries red, smooth.
