DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Stem branches with numerous roots, internodes 2-14 cm long and 2-3 mm in diam., slightly ridged, glabrous. Latex milky-white. Leaves opposite, decussate, lamina 2.0-7.9 x 1.2-3 cm, elliptic, rarely ovate or orbicular, obtuse or subacute at apex, narrowed or truncate at base, waxy margin, thick, fleshy, glabrous on both sides, lateral veins 5-7 pairs, petioles terete, 2-8 mm long and 1.5-2 mm in diam.

Flowers lateral, in drooping umbels, many flowered; peduncles stout, terete, arising from in between petioles, 3-11 mm long, ca 1.5 mm in diam., glabrous; bracts minute, ca 1 mm long, margin ciliolate, caducous; pedicels terete, 1.2-2 cm long, ca up to 1 mm in diam., glabrous. Calyx 5-lobed, divided up to base, lobes ca 2 x 1.5 mm, ovate, apex obtuse or rounded, margin ciliate, glabrous, glandular within. Corolla rotate, ca 1 cm across; lobes united up to ca 1 mm, five, ca 5 x 3 mm, ovate-oblong, apex acute, glabrous without, appressedly puberulous within. Corona staminal, uniseriate, 5-lobed, spreading, adnate below to the staminal-column, free at tips, ca 3 x 2 mm, ovate, concave on upper side, inner margin produced into Sharpe point in the centre, articulated. Stamens five, ca 1.5 mm long; pollinia five, pollen masses solitary in each anther cell, yellow, waxy, ca 561.6 x 216 mm attached by light-brown caudicles ca 100.8 mm long, ca 43.2 mm wide to dark-brown corpuscle ca 216 mm long with tail and ca 115.2 mm wide. Gynostegium ca 2 mm long.
