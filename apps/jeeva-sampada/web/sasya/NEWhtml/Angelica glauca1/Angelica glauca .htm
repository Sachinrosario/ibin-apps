 Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A genus of aromatic herbs, including about 70 species, distributed in the north temperate regions and New Zealand. Two species occur in India.

It can be seen in the Western Himalayan region from  Kashmir to Simla,Uttar Pradesh between 1800 and 3,700 m.

Chemistry
The herb contains iso-imperatorin, pragolarin, tert-O-methyl loxypeucedanin and a novel dimeric-lactone-angelide and ligustilide; essential oil from  roots comprises of lactones as well as mono and sesquiterpenes.
