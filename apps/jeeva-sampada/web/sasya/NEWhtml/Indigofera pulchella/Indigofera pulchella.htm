Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 A large shrub, 4-6 ft. high, found growing gregariously in moist evergreen forests throughout the Himalayan tract and on hills up to 5,000 ft.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves short-petioled: leaflets 13-21, thinly hairy, pale green above and glaucous below; flowers large. numerous, bright red to rose pink, in shore peduncled racemes ; pods straight, 1-2 in. long, with broad sutures and 8-12 seeds. Flowers about 5 inch long, bright pink, in dense racemes 2-4 inch long. Bracts boat shaped, long pointed, silky hairy, exceeding the flower buds. Pod 1-1.7 inches long, straight, glabrous.
