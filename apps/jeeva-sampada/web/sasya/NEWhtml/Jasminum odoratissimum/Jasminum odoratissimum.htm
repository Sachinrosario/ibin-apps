Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
An erect glabrous shrub native of Maderia and canary islands, with stiff, terete or slightly angular branches and yellow, fragrant flowers is grown in some gardens in India.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used :
Flower

Uses:-
1. The plant is cultivated in formosa for the flowers which are reported to be used for flavouring tea.

2. The flowers retain, even when when dry, their natural perfume which combines which fragrance of jasmine, jonquil and orange blossoms.

Chemistry
They yield on extraction a reddish brown absolute(0.116%). The absolute contains linalool, d-linalyl acetate, benzyl alcohol, benzyl acetate. methyl anthranilate, indole and sesquiterpene or diterpene alcohol; jasmone is absent.
