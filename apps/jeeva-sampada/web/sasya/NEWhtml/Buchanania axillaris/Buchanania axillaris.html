HABIT & HABITAT
Common in open scrub and dry-wet deciduous forests of the plains. Assam, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, TamilNadu and Kerala, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh. It also occurs in Srilanka.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Trees, 12-15 m tall, glabrous throughout. Leaves elliptic-oblong, 4-13 x 1.5-4.5 cm, margin entire glabrous; petiole 0.5-2.0 cm long. Flowers in axillary and terminal panicles, bisexual, 3-4 mm across; pedicels short. Sepals ovate, glabrous. Petals white, oblong, 5 x 3 mm, bent downward. Stamens 10. Fruits drupes, globular, 1.2 x 0.4 cm.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Inflorescence:  Branched panicles; Flower: Complete, hermaphrodite, samll, white, regular ; Calyx: Calyx lobes semi-orbicular, persistent, imbricate ; Corolla: Petals oblong at the length recurved imbricate ;Androecium: Stamens 8-10, inserted at the base of disk, filaments subulate, anthers 2-celled; Gynoecium:  Carpels 5-6, seated on the cavity of the disk, one fertile pilose, the others imperfect, ovule one, pendulous from basal funicle, style short and stigma truncate; Fruit: Drupe obliquely globose, slightly compressed ; Seed: Gibbous acute at one end. Flowering & Fruiting : January-March .Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by birds or animals.
