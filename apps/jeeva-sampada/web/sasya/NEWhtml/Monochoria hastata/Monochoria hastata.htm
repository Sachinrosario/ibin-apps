HABIT & HABITAT:
 A perennial herb with elongate, creeping, spongy rootstock found in the margins of tanks and ponds, swamps, ditches, marshes, rice fields and sometimes in brackish water, almost throughout India. the vegetative stems often long and robust. Radical leaves with with sheath broadened at the bas: petiole 30- 90 cm. : leaf blade triangular or triangular-ovate, 5-15 x 3-15 cm. base sagitate to hostat, apex acute to aciminateA small genus of rhizomatous aquatic herbs distributed from North-East Africa to Manchuria and southward to South Australia. Two species occur in India.
Habitat: Banks of rivers, Tanks,canals, Ditches, Margins of fresh water pools, Ponds and wet rice fields, Marshes, Plains, Ponds & Tanks, Pools, Rice-fields and Tanks-ponds-canals.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves long-petioled, sagittate, hastate or cordate.  Flowering stems are erect or obliquely so, 50-90 cm: leaf petiole 7-10 cm. . Inflorescence erect or suberect, remaining so after anthesis, subumbellate to shortly racemose, 10-40 flowered: peduncle distinctly shorter than associated leaf petiole. Pedicels 1-3 cm. Perianth segments bluish with green median, vein and 1-3 cm. Perianth segments bluish with green median vein andreddish blotch, ovate, 1-1.6 cm. Larger stamens : anther 5.3-6.5 mm. Smaller stamens; filaments filiform; anthers 3-4 mm. Style densely and shortly spreading hairy at apex. Flowers purplish-blue or violet blue dotted with red, in sub- umbellate racemes ; capsules small, ellipsoid, sub- globose or oblong.
