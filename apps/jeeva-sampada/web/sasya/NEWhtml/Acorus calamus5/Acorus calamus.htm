DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Herbs, perennial; rhizome prostrate, covered with scales; leaves sessile, deeply channeled, linear–lanceolate; flowers bisexual, small, crowded in pseudo- terminal spadix; fruits berries.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Rhizomes are used in preparation of soft drinks. (Gaur., 1999.)

It is also used in manufacture of aromatic vinegar, and for flavouring drinks, beer, zin, etc. (Watt, 1908.)
