DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
This plant is a stout and woody climber. The leaves are oblong-ovate or sub-obovate, 15 to 25 centimeters long, 8 to 15 centimeters wide, tapering to a point at the apex, rounded or heart-shaped at the base, smooth or hairy on both surfaces, dotted and streaked with cystoliths. The stipules are large, rusty-brown, and smooth. The male heads are about 6 millimeters in diameter, in broad, short, peduncled, dichotomous cymes. There are 3 to 4 stamens. The female heads are about 25 millimeters in diameter, occurring in rounded, concave, deciduous bracts. The flowers are sweet scented.
