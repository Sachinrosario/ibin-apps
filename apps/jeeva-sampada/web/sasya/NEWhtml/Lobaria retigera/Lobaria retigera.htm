Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A foliose lichen grows on moss covered trees and rocks in moist, humid and open places.
Availability :

Out side India, it is known from tropical and subtropical regions of Japan, Nepal, South Africa, Australia and North America .In India, it is distributed in following states.
Arunachal Pradesh : Debang valley district, Punli; Athuli-Gunli foot track; Riyali-Kronli; Lower Subansiri district, Sorobasti;Pange forest; Talle valley; old zero road; Lohit district, Hayuliang-Chipru;Yatang -Koibang;West Siang district, Payum-Gassang;Yapik; Bulli-Shikar,Upper Subansiri district,Taliah- Nacheo;West Kameng district, Sessa; Shergaon; Bomdila;Tawang district,Lumla. Assam: 3 miles from Jomai to Jorain.Himachal Pradesh:Parbati river valley, Just above Pulga Rest House. Manipur:Korong district, Kongpokpi Mission hospital; Mao.Meghalaya : Mamphlong. Nagaland : Kohima - Dzukou valley, Japho hills, Tening village; Mon- Chenkhao- Minakshu village track at 5 km point; Phek- Near Dzulhami, Wazhio-Ziphu road, at 13 km point; Tuensang district, on way between Chingthang - Konyak village; Saramati hills;New Yakar; Wokha district, Mt. Tiyei Peak ; Zunheboto district, Satakha road, at 15 km point. Sikkim : Near Karponang. Tamil Nadu : Nilgiri hills, Pykara forest range, Shola near Pykara; Palni hills, Shambaganur to Periakulam via short route.Uttaranchal :Almora district, Takula ridge on way to Pindari glacier; Chamoli district, on way to Vyas Shikhar; Nainital district, NainiTal, on the way of Naina Peak; Pithoragarh district, Askote , Dhanlake; Uttarkashi district, Sayanachatti and Hanuman chatti; Tehri district, Dhanalti.West Bengal : Darjeeling district, Sandakhpoo.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Thallus 5- 30 cm across; lobes imbricate, scrobiculate, yellowish grey to brownish, reticulately ridged, isidiate; isidia on ridges or on cracks, globular, simple to coralloid branched withage; lower surface bullate; tomentose; tomenta yellowish, netted; rhizinae blackish or bluish. Apothecia present in ridges, brown, 2-3mm wide; spores colourless, transversely 3-septate, 25-35 x 5-9 µm; picnidia, soredia absent.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
A common foliose lichen, easily available in good amount and can be utilized for its antibiotic activity as the other species of the same genus exhibit antitumour activity. (Takahashi et al. 1974).
