Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Wild in the Western Ghats from Maharashtra to Kerala. Grows mostly on the western slopes in deep ravines under the shade where there are sufficient soil depth. Although in the field the palm appears as a single stemmed, it actually part of the large spread out colony of erect shoots developed from underground stolon. This palm prefers to grow in shade, therefore a very good ornamental palm for growing in the green houses.
Availability :

Western Ghats, Malabar, South India.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild, endemic to India

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Stoloniferous pinnate leaved, monoecious palm. Stem slender, erect, to 8 m long, to 5 cm in daim. near base; leaves about 1.5 m long; leaflets many, sessile, elongate, broadly linear to falcate, acuminate or praemorsed, 5-7 nerved, to 60 cm long, about 3 cm broad at middle; light green in colour; uppermost leaflets jointed. Inflorescence infrafoliar; prophyll simple, boat shaped, caducous; peduncle about 4 cm long; flower branches simple, mostly 5-8 in number, densely covered with flower clusters. Male flowers pinkish; sepals 3, subulate, almost same length of petals; petals 3, ovate-cordate, stamens numerous; filaments short; pistillode absent. In female flowers sepals and petals 3 each, reniform; staminodes 6, clavate, tips pointed. Ripe fruits ovoid-ellipsoid 1.5 cm 1 x cm, conical at stigmatic region; seed ovoid; endosperm ruminate.
