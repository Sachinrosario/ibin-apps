 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
A viney plant occurring throughout India except in dry places. These will be found in bushes, hedges, hills, river banks, waste land.
It's Biotizone is Himalayan region.

Availability :  
Misc: Corbett National Park, Mount Abu, Village of Katraj ghats,
It is also found in the countries like Africa, Australia, Burma, Ceylon, China, India, Malaya, Malayasia, Mauritius, Philippines, Sri Lanka, In India it is found in the states like Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Orissa, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal and the zonal regions are Central, East, North, North East, South and West zone.
District: Belgaum, Bilaspur, Damoh, Dharmapuri, Jabalpur, Pune, Sagar, Salem, Sirohi, South Arcot, Tiruchi.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Root large, tuberous, stems very slender, glabrous, often spotted with darker green, internodes very long; 3-5 inch ovate-rotundate in outline, very deeply cordate at base, cut nearly to base in to 5 lanceolate or linear acute, coarsely serrate segment, the two basal ones deeply pedate, glabrous, thin, the upper surface slightly rough with minute scattered scales, petiole 1- 1.5 inch. Flowers in clusters of 3-6, on short peduncles; Calyx segmented, linear, filiform; corolla segmented, oval oblong, acute, pubescent; fruit globose, over 3/4 inch diameter, smooth, bluish-green, with broad, white, vertical stripes; seeds gibbous at the sides, with a prominent raised band running round the edge.
