HABIT & HABITAT:
Erect or ascending herb, 30-85 cm high, it is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh at alt. 8000–12,000 ft. Common.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Glabrous or sparsely pilose, profusely branched in lower half; rootstock up to 12 mm in diam.; branches slender, densely foliate, 5.5-35 cm long. Leaves alternate to opposite, ovate-elliptic, ovate-lanceolate or lanceolate, 15-35 x 3.5-23 mm, acute at apex, truncate, attenuate or cordate at base, subentire along margin, densely pubescent or pilose on both surfaces; petioles 2-14 mm long, slender, sparsely to densely pilose. Flower solitary, terminal; pedicels 2.0-18.5 cm long, glabrous or sparsely pilose. Hypanthia broadly subglobose, 3.0-3.5 x 7-9 mm, glabrous or sparsely pilose. Calyx perigynous; teeth oblong, 1.3-1.9 x 0.5-1.0 cm, acute or obtuse at apex, entire, rarely ciliate towards the apex. Corolla blue, epigynous, broadly campanulate; tubes 13-20 cm long; lobes broadly triangular, 6-7 x 9-11 mm, acute.Ovary semi-inferior; style 8-10 mm long; stigmatic lobes 2.0-2.5 mm long. Capsules broadly subglobose or ovoid. Seeds narrowly ellipsoid.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Terminal racemes ; Flower: Bracteate, pedicellate ; Calyx: Calyx lobes minutely pillose; Corolla:  Widened upwards, silky blue, shortly 3-lobed ; Androecium:  Stamens 5, free, inserted on the margin of epigynous disc, filaments dilated at the base ; Gynoecium: Ovary at first inferior, turbinate with short conic, superior vertex, 3-celled, style cylindric, stigma lobed; Fruit: Capsule fleshy beaked ; Seed:  Many slightly compressed, brown, ellipsoid. Flowering & Fruiting :Flowers are pollinated by insects.Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

UTILIZATION:
Plants are used in rheumatism.


Chemistry:
Two alkaloids condonopsine and condonopsinine were isolated from the chloroform extract of roots of Codnopsis clematidea and their structures were established by a correlation of spectral characteristics and chemical transformations. (Aripova, 1996). MAPA 1998, 9802-0971.
