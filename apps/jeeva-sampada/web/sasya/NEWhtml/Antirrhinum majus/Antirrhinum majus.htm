Name : Antirrhinum majus

Family : Scrophulariaceae

Vernacular Name : Snapdragon


Back to Top

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Uusually grown as an annual both in the planis and in the hills. 

Snapdragons are a great favorite of the horticulturists for their gorgeous and mildly scented flowers in varying shades of magenta, scarlet, crimson, mauve, pink, rose, apricot, orange, white, yellow, ets.

Availability :

Plenty

Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/Cultivated

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: 
An erect, simple or branched annual-perennial herb, up to 1.8m in height, . Leaves ovate-elliptic to oblong; flowers white, red or variegated, in many flowered, terminal rademes.

Miscellaneous Information

 An infusion of the plant possess cardiotonic, hypotensive and sedative properties. In Europe, the leaves are applied in poultices to tumours and ulcers.
