REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Long pedunculate racemes arranged in few flowered distant whorls along a slender rachis; Flower: Small, pedicellate, bracteolate, complete, regular ; Perianth:  Usually white, perianth tube much narrowed above the ovule, the peltoid poriton funnel shaped, lobes of the limb 2-fid ;Androecium: Stamens 2 or 3 , not exerted, filaments capillary, unequal, anthers dorsifixed, didynamous; Gynoecium:Ovary 1-celled, free, ovule solitary, basal, erect, oblique, stipitate, style filiform, stigma peltate ; Fruit: Clavate, furnished with large semi-globose glands round the crown ; Seed: with adherent testa. Flowering & Fruiting : August-December. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
