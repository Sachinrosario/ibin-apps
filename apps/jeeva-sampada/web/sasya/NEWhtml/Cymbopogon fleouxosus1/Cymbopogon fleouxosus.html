DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Inflorescence spathate panicle, decompound, branches flexuosus or drooping, Spatheole 10-20 mm long, glabrous, peduncle very slender and ciliate in upper part. Raceme epinastically deflexed, 15-17 mm long, slender; Flower long, slender, sessile spikelets 4.5 mm long, lower glume lanceolate and subchartaceous, 0.8 mm wide, acuminate, narrowly winged, flat open with 3 wrinkles on the back,3-nerve; Pedicillate spikelets 4 mm long, ower glume narrowly lanceolate, acuminate, upper glume    boat shaped, 1nerved, lower florets empty; lemma lanceolate, hyaline scale, 2-keeled,ciliate on laps, narrow, hyaline apex shortly bilobed, lower florets empty; emma lanceolate, hyaline scale,2-keeled,ciliate  flaps narrow, hyaline apex shortly bilobed with awn 1.2 cm  long, excurrent from notch; Androecium stamens 3 in whorls odd stamen being anterior, filaments  free and delicate, anthers basifixed, dithecous, introrse and opening usually by longitudinal slits; Gynoecium monocarpellary with superior unilocular ovary having single anatropous ovule adnate to adaxial side of  ovary, placentation basal, styles two, stigmas plumose; Fruit caryopsis; One seeded with thin ovary wall adheres to it; Plants are highly cross pollinated by wind; Seeds are dispersed by wind.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds and vegetatively by splitting the   culms.

Soil type: Plant is adapted to wide range of soils but well drained sandy loam soil supports good growth of plant. Although it prefers neutral soils, it is also cultivated in moderately alkaline soil with pH up to 8.5 and marginal and sub-marginal soils of poor fertility.

Nutrient requirements: Plant responds well to fertilizer application, a mixture of 30-kg/ha nitrogen, 30 kg/ha phosphorous and 30-kg/ha potash is recommended at the time of planting.  Micronutrient application found beneficial.

Water management: In areas with well-distributed rainfall, no irrigation is required deficient areas required 3-5 irrigations.

Harvesting period / Method: The first harvest of lemongrass is obtained months 4-6 after planting and subsequent harvests are obtained at an interval of 3-4 months depending upon fertility of the soil, humidity and temperature.
