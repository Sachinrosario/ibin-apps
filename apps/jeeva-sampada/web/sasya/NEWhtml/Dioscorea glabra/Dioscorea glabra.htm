 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
A climber with stems twining to the right, armed with prickles towards the base, unarmed and smooth above. This species occurs in Assam, Bengal, Bihar, Orissa, and in Andaman and Nicobar Islands; it is also met with in central and eastern Himalayas in Nepal, Darjeeling, Abor hills and Khasi hills. It does not extend southwards below Godavari river. Six varieties have been recognized, of which var. vera occurs in India. The tubers are eaten in Andaman Islands and Khasi hills, but are not much liked as they become gluey when cooked. A form found in Khasi and Abor hills is said to be of good edible quality.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
  Here the leaves of the plant are elliptic, ovate or ovate Bulbils absent. Tubers 1, 2 or many, long stalked and buried deep in the ground; skin earth coloured with a few rootlets; flesh white and edible. Male spikes clustered, spreading upto 4 cm long. perianth segments ovate. seeds winged all round.
