Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 A small, deciduous tree, 9-12 m in height, with spreading and drooping branches, introduced in to India from Myanmar as an ornamental; found in parts of Punjab, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharastra, West Bengal, Tripura, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, suitable for avenues.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves 25-30 cm long. leaflets8-20 pairs, oblong, membranous, pubescent below; flowers pink or red, scented, in short racemes on leafless branches; pods cylindric, black, glabrous, 35-45 cm long.

Chemistry
Analysis of the seeds gave the following results (dry basis); crude protein-12.9; ether extract-3.5; N-free extract-75.8; crude fibre-4.5; ash-3.3%; calcium-133; phosphorus-178; iron-12.67; niacin-9.03; and ascorbic acid5.42 mg/100 g. The essential amino aicd composition of the protein is ; (g/16 gN); histidine-2.5; lysine-4.4; methionine-1.1; leucine and isoleucine-6.1; phenylalanine-3.7; threonine-1.3; tyrosien-0.68; tryptophan-0.7; and valine-3.6. The stem bak contains tannina (15%) and non tannins (8%) and 1-hydroxy-3,8-dimethoxy-2-methylanthraquinone, 5-hydroxy-6,7,3',4',5'-pentamethoxyflavanone-5-0-alpha-L-hamnopyranoside, quercetagetin-3,6-dimethyl ether and a flavanone glycoside, which on hydrolysis, gave 5,7-dihydroxy-4'-methoxyflavanone and rhamnose. The leaves contain chrysophanol, rhein, physcion and kaempferol.

1.The wood is very hard and well suited for wheels and tool handles. The plant has been triesd as a shade plant in plantations.
