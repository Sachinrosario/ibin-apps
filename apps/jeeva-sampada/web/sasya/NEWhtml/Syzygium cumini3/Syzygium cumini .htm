Taxonomic Details

Synonyms: Syzygium jambolana Lamk., Eugenia jambolana Lam.
Family: Myrtaceae
Local Name:

Tamil : Jambu, Naval
Hindi: Jamun
English : Jaman, Java plum

HABIT & HABITAT

Large trees; mostly planted along the avenues.
Availability :

Cultivated throughout India and run wild in some places.
Conservation/ Threat Status : 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Evergreen trees; leaves lanceolate, elliptic-oblong or broadly ovate-elliptic, entire, acuminate, subacute or obtuse, narrowed at the base; petioles long; flowers greenish-white, in 3-flowered trichotomous cymes, arranged lateral, long panicles, calyx campanulate, contracted into a pseudostalk; limb 4-lobed; petals orbicular calyptrate; fruits oblong or ovoid-oblong, dark-purple, juicy, 1-seeded.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Fruits are used for fermentation of rice beer.,( Jain 91) 
Wine is prepared from Ripe fruit pulp. (Islamik & Jha, 2001)

Parts Used:  


Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products: The Squash is prepared as specified in the Fruit product order (1955).Syrup is prepared by CFTRI, Mysore method (anon 1990). Sugar is added to raise the juice T.S.S. to 45 and 70 Brix for squash and syrup, respectively. The squash is prepared by the addition of sodium benzoate at 600 mg/kg of finished product respectively whereas the syrup is heat processed. The squash and syrup are filled in 200 ml sterilized glass bottles and sealed, labeled and stored.
