DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Inflorescence axillary umbellate cymes; Flower bracteate, pedicillate, small and caducous, complete, actinomorphic, hermaphrodite, penta or tetramerous ,hypogynous and cyclic; Calyx sepals 5 or 4, gamosepalous, valvate; Corolla petals 5 or 4, polypetalous, imbricate, white, coriaceous, and with dotted glands; Androecium stamens indefinite, polyadelphous, dithecous  dorsifixed, introrse, pointed at the apex; Gynoecium multicarpellary, syncarpous, ovary superior, multilocular with generally one ovule in each locule, placentation axile, a nectariferous annular disc is present below the ovary, style stout and stigma capitate; Fruit hesperidium. 
Floral and seed  biology:

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal: 
Plant are cross-pollinated by insects or sometimes self pollinated. Animals and human agencies disperse seeds.
Genetic variability:
Plant is diploid with chromosome number 2n=18.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated both by seeds and by vegetative means.

Soil type:   Plant thrives well in deep,loose well-aerated soils devoid of any hard pan of calcium carbonate in rooting zone with the ideal soil pH 5.5-7.5.

Nutrient requirements: Young and pre-bearing trees require applications of nitrogen to provide proper balance between growth and production. Addition of farmyard manure in the pits prior to planting is essential.

Water management: Plant requires irrigation in areas where annual rainfall is less than 890 mm. The plant is sensitive to water logging during growth period.

Harvesting period / Method: Fruits remains fresh for several weeks on the tree without any deterioration after attaining maturity which facilitates harvesting according to one’s choice and market demand.
