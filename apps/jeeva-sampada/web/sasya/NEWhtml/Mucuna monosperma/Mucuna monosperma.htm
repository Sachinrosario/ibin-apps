HABIT & HABITAT
A large woody perennial twiner found from Nepal eastwards to Khasia hills, Deccan Peninsula and Andaman Islands.
Habitat: Deccan hills, Hill forests, Moist deciduous forest, Reserve forests and Semi evergreen forest.
BioticZone: Alpine Western Himalaya, Deccan Plain, Eastern Himalayas, Khasia hills, Konkan, Tropical Eastern Himalayas, Tropics, Western Ghats and Western peninsular India.
Availability :  
Misc: Anekad, Begur, Chittagong, Hills of mysore, In banks of streams, Kagemanegiri forests,  Kesave, Kalanaddi, Khasi hills, Mahaballeshwar, Muthodi forests,  Kesave, N. Circars, Paschimavahini, Samulcotta, Vaniampuzha, Nilambur and  Wynaad. 
Country: Bangladesh, Burma, India and Sri Lanka. 
Zone: East, North, North East, North West, South, South West and Westzone.
State: Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Goa Daman Diu, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Orissa, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu and Tripura. 
District: Cannanore, Chikmaglore, Coimbatore, Hassan, Kodagu, Malappuram, Mysore, Satara, Shimoga, Tiruchchirappalli, Tiruchi, Trichurapalli and Uttar Kannada.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves trifoliolate: leaflets ovate-oblong or elliptic; flowers in axillary corymbose racemes, purple ; pods orbicular, plaited, winged, densely covered with reddish or dark brown, deciduous, irritant bristles : Pod is nearly circular, hairy, slightly compressed, dark brown. Seed is  solitary about an inch in diameter ear shaped, flat, the entire convex margin is occupied by the hilum.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Entire plant, Seeds,

Uses
  1. The seeds are restorative and are sometimes consumed as a vegetable. 
  2. Seeds are used as an expectorant in cough and asthma, also in affections of the tongue. Externally they are said to be sedative.

Harvesting Practices
The seed crop is ready for harvest in c. 6 months from sowing.
