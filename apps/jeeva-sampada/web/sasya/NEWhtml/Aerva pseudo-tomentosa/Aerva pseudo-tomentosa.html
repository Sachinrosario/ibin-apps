HABIT & HABITAT
Much branched, woody undershrubs, densely wooly tomentose, up to 1.5 m high,  it is found to occur in Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Gujarat and Rajasthan

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stem erect, obscurely furrowed, often glabrous or shinning. Leaves alternate, linear-lanceolate, 5-8 x 0.3-0.6 cm, subobtuse or acute at apex, attenuate at base, entire at margin, generally densely tomentose on the dorsal surface and glabrous on upper surface, midrib and lateral nerves prominent below, sessile or subsessile. Flowers unisexual, dioecious, white or pinkish, simple or compound, 1.0-2.5 cm long cylindrical spikes; bracts broadly ovate, hyaline, acute, red. Perianth segments 5; outer 2 segments linear-oblong, glabrous and remaining 3 lanceolate, densely tomentose on dorsal surface, all apiculate. Stamens 5. Pistillode shortly stipitate.
