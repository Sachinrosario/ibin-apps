DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A stem less rhizomatous herb of the ZINGIBERACEAE Family rhizome, branched with bright orange to yellow within. Leaves are large, elongated and borne at the top of the non-woody underground stem, with overlapping petioles. Leaves are light green, 30-40 cm long and 8-12 cm wide with thin ellipse-shaped or elongated lance-shaped blades. The play yellow cylindrical inflorescence 10-15 by 6-8 cm develops in the centre of the leaves. It consists of curved bracts, each with at least yellow flowers, except in the upper part, where the bracts are white or pink. A stamen with shoot filament, broad and constricted at the apex is found in the floret. The another is usually sparred at the base. The ovaries consists of three locules each containing two ovules. The capsules are ellipsoid seeds are rare.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Antifungal Atnifeedant, Antiviral, Insecticide, Repellent, Nematicide.

Parts Used: Rhizomes and leaves


Harvesting Practices: Matured plants are harvested for rhizome and leaf extraction.


Produce and Products:


Chemistry: 2- Hydroxy-methyly anthraquinone, 4-Hydroxy bisabola-2-10-dien-9-one, Caffeiu acid, Dehydro curdione, Ferouloyl, Turmerin, Turmerone, Turmeronol, Zingiberene.

Processing Techniques: Steam distilled leaf oil and Rhizome powder are used.

Miscellaneous Information

 To prevent stomach disorders, threeto five ml of fresh juice is taken regularly on a empty stomach. Hot water extract of dried rhizome is taken orally for slow lactation, to regulate fat metabolism for diabetes, dropsy jaundice, and liver diseases.
