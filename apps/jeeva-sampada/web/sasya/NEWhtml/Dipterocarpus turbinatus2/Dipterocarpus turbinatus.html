REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
 Inflorescence:  Axillary 3-8 flowered raceme; Flower: Large, complete, regular, hermaphrodite, white; Calyx: Tube obconic, hoary-puberulous, the mouth contracted in fruit, the two enlarged lobes oblong, obtuse, strongly 3-5 nerved and reticulately veined; Corolla: Petals white, tinged with red, obtuse, puberulous without, twisted in bud; Androceium: Stamens numerous, filaments yellow, linear, subulate, anthers linear, connective acuminate; Gynoecium: Ovary 3-celled, ovules 2 in each cell, style filiform; Fruit: Nut –like, subglobose, pubescent; Seed: Adnate to the base of pericarp;

Chemistry:
  Dipterocarpol from wood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

Hydroxydammarenone II, dammerenediol II, betulonic acid, asiatic acid, 3,23-O-isopropylidene asiatic acid and madasiatic acid isolated from oleoresin of plant from Yunnan . (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

a-cadinene, d-elemene, a-gurjunene and g-muurolene identified by GC-MS in oleoresin essential oil from Yunnan  plant; composition of oleoresin essential oils collected in dry and rainy seasons found to be different. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

CIMAP:
The sesquiterpenes of the oleoresin essential oils of Dipterocarpus turbinatus and D. retusus were analysed by GC and GC/MS/DS for relative contents and seasonal variations. The main compds. in the oleoresin essential oil of D. turbinatus and δ- elemene, α- gurjunene, γ- muurolene and α- cadinene. The main compds. in oleoresin essential oil of D. retusus are allo- aromadendrene and α- humulene, and the allo- aromadendrene relative content is the highest. Seasonal variations differed for δ-elemene between the oleoresin essential oil collected in rainy season of D. turbinatus. The compn. of the oleoresin essential oil of D. turbinatus was not affected by season. The oleoresin essential oil of D. turbinatus was not affected by the season. The oleoresin essential oil both D. turbinatus and D. retusus had antibacterial activity1.
