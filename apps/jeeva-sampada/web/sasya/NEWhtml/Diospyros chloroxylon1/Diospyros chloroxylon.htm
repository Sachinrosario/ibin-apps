HABIT & HABITAT
A large shrub or a small spreading tree, distributed in many parts of central and southern India extending north to Orissa, Chanda and Nasik. It prefers laterite and sandstone hills and black cotton soils.
In general it is seen in the dry areas of coastal zone, dry deciduous forest, dry evergreen forests, forests, open forest, Owk forest and waste land.
Biotic Zone: Deccan and Deccan Plateau.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 A small tree or a large shrub. Branches thorny. leaves elliptic, obovate or elliptic-oblong, minutely hairy above, covered with brownish hairs below. The fruits are globular, of the size of a large pea, and contain 2-3 seeds.
