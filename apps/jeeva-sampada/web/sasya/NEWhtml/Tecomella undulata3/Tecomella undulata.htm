HABIT & HABITAT

Small tree, this species is found in plains on coarse sandy ground. it is distributed in Arabia, Pakistan (Baluchistan, Sind), India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A medium sized tree. Branches drooping, glabrous, minutely stellately hairy when young. Leaves simple, 5-12 x 1.5-2.5 cm, usually subopposite, oblong, apex obtuse, emarginated, margins entire, more or less undulating, slightly cuneate at base; midrib prominent below; petioles 1-2 cm long, terete, glabrous. Inflorescence usually in few-flowered cymes, arranged in racemose manner, terminating short, lateral branches; pedicels 1-2 cm long, terete, minutely stellately hairy, pedicel-bases persistent. Calyx 8-9 mm long, campanulate, green or yellow; lobes broadly ovate, obtuse mucronate, often recurved, with few black dots outside. Corona 5-7 cm long, yellow or orange-yellow, campanulate; lobes 5, subequal. Stamens exerted; filaments glabrous, anterior 30-35 mm long, posterior 25 mm long. Ovary on a yellow annular disc; styles upto 4 cm long, glabrous; stigma bi-lamillate, elliptic, 3/5 mm long. Fruit a capsule, 15-35 cm x 8-10 mm, curved, linear, oblong, smooth. Seeds winged, 2-2.5 cm x 9-10 mm; wing thin, narrow at the apex of the seed, absent at base.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
ORNAMENTAL FLOWERING TREE:Tecomella undulata is a small tree, having loose spreading branches and narrow undulating leaves. It is attractive in March and April when it bears recemes of large, erect, yellow or orange coloured flowers, which are attached close to the younger part of the stem. Endemic to our country.The colour of the flower is variable from yellow to deep orange. The plants in full blooms/flowers give a gorgeous sight.

Parts Used:

Leaves, flowers, bark, wood, seeds.

Chemistry:

Other uses:

The plant is very useful locally. Cattle and goats eat the leaves; the bark is used in indigenous medicines; the wood is used in furniture; since it is soft, tough and durable. It takes a good polish. The wood is also used for Persian wheels, furniture, lacquered toys; seeds are used against abscess.
