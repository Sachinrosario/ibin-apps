 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
Usually a weed of wet places (Stone, 1970).  In Hawai'i, "naturalized in wet sites, beach parks, urban sites, cultivated fields, and along roadsides, 0-50 m"  (Wagner et al., 1999).  In Fiji, "a naturalized adventive, occurring near sea level in wet places in the open, in swamps and pastures, along roadsides, and often abundantly in rice fields" (Smith, 1979).
Availability :  
Ceylon.
India:Andhra Pradesh,Arunachal Pradesh,Assam,Bihar,Diu & Daman,Goa,Gujarath,Karnataka,Kerala,Himachal Pradesh,Maharashtra,Madhya Pradesh,Meghalaya,Nagaland,Rajasthan,Tamil Nadu,Uttar Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Annuals, tufted, glabrous; culms up to 50 cm tall, sharply trigonal; leaves shorter than culm; bracteal leaves (some of them) longer than inflorescence; inflorescence umbellate, of rays to 5 cm long; spikes subglobose, dense, about 1-1.3 cm in diameter; spikelets with reddish markings on light brown; glumes rounded at apex; achenes trigonal
