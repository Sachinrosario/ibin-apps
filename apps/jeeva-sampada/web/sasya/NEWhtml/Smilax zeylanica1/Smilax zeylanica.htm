DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Extensive climber. Stem sub-cylindrical, green or yellowish-green, armed with small prickles. Leaves alternate, simple, braodly ovate or orbicular, rounded or sub-cordate at base, undulate, acuminate, 5-7-served, up to 25 x 15 cm; sheathing at base; tendril long, slender, connate at base with the petiole. Inflorescence an umbel, greenish-white,dioecious, bracts ovate, acute. Perianth 3+3, outer linear, obtuse, reflected, broad, inner small, ovate-oblong. Stamens 6, fertile, free; staminodes 3-6 in female. Ovary 3-celled, 3-gonous;ovoles 1 or rarely 2 in each cell; style short, stimgas 3, recurved. Fruit a globose berry, red when ripe.
