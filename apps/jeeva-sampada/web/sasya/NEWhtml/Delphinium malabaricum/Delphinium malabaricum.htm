Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Grows on grassy slopes at highest elevations in Hills and Slopes.
It has dark-bright blue flowers of great ornamental potential

Availability : Maharashtra


Conservation/ Threat Status : Endemic to Maharashtra, Rare.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Perennial herbs with woody cylindrical rhizome; stems 30-100 cm high, slender, glabrous to retrorse pubescent below, pale to bright yellowish strigose in inflorescence, simple or few-branched above, remotely few-leaved. Lower petioles 10-25 cm long, glabrous to somewhat pubescent, strongly sheathed at base; cauline petioles reduced, upper very short. Leaf blades somewhat reniform, 3-10 cm across, glabrous or nearly so above, with few to many stiff hairs along margin and the veins beneath, 5- to7-fid for up to half their length; segments broadly cuneate-obovate, mostly bifid, then coarsely toothed with broad entire or dentate teeth with mucronate tips; middle cauline leaves more deeply divided with entire or 3-toothed segments. Inflorescence consisting of 1-few, rather densely several flowered racemes; lower bracts leafy, trifid, 2-4 cm long; upper bracts linear, entire, 6-10 mm long; pedicels divergent, 5-20 mm long, strigose; bracteoles subulate, placed near the base or middle of pedicels. Sepals bright blue with a somewhat subterminal spot, strigose to subgabrous; upper sepal ovate, 10-14 x 5-7 mm; spur subconic, obtuse, 7-12 x 3-4 mm; lateral sepals elliptic-obovate, truncate-rounded at apex, 13-16 x 7-9 mm; lower sepals narrowly obovate, 11-13 x 5-7 mm. Upper petals slightly oblique, obtusely bilobed at apex, ca 10 mm long, glabrous; spur ca 7 mm long; lower petals rounded to oblong, bilobed up to base, ca 7 x 5 mm, short-ciliate and strigulose; claw ca 5 mm long. Stamens 6-7 mm long, ciliate; anthers bluish, rounded, les than 1 mm long. Follicles strigulose or glabrous, divergent, ca 1 cm long with papillose scaly covering or rugulose and without scaly covering.
