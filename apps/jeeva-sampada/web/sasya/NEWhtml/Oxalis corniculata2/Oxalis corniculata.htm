Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
Chemistry: Vitexin, its 2''-O-b-D-glucoside and isovitexin isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
Isolation of 2-heptenal, 2-pentylfuran and trans-phytol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
From the n-hexane extract a mixture of higher fatty acid esters (C38H76O2, C29H58O2, C30H60O2), 1-octacosanol and b-sitosterol were isolated. The ethylacetate extract contained an alkaloid and a flavonoid (isovitexin). (Ahmad et al., 1996)

Pharmacology: Alcoholic extract of leaf showed complete inhibition of Staphylococcus typhi, S. amerus, S. albus and S. citrus at 6.5 mg/ml. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).


Ethnobotany: Medicine for appetite (Leaf, Shah et al, 1981); Cooling (Whole plant, Karnick et al, 1981); Cough (Leaf, Rao, 1981; Whole plant, Tarafder and Chaudhuri, 1981); Cuts (Leaf, Saxena and Vyas, 1983; Shah and Joshi, 1971); Diarrhoea (Whole plant, Singh and Pandey, 1980); Digestion (Leaf, Shah et al, 1981); Dysentery (Leaf, Saxena and Vyas, 1983; Janardhanan, 1963; Rao, 1981; Whole plant, Kharkongor and Joseph, 1981; Rajwar, 1983); Epilepsy (Whole plant, Lakshamanan and Sankaranarayanan, 1988); Eye complaints (Leaf, Bhall et al, 1982; Whole plant, Megoneitso and Rao, 1983); Fever (Leaf, Janardhanan, 1963; Whole plant, Singh and Pandey, 1980; Rajwar, 1983); Insect bite (Leaf, Shah and Joshi, 1971); Jaundice (Whole plant, Singh et al, 1980); Rickets (Leaf, Maheshwari et al, 1980; Maheshwari et al, 1981) Scurvy (Whole plant, Das et al, 1983; Rajwar, 1983); Skin disease (Leaf, Bhall et al, 1982); Snake bite (Whole plant, Rao, 1981; Kharkongor and Joseph, 1981); Stomachache (Leaf, Maheshwari and Singh, 1984; Das et al, 1983); swelling (Leaf, Shah and Joshi, 1971); Wart (Leaf, Purohit et al, 1985; Whole plant, Rajwar, 1983) Edible (Fruit, Pal, 1984; Leaf, Maheshwari and Singh, 1984; Lakshmanan et al, 1986); Acidic (Leaf, Shah, 1984); Salad (Leaf, Datar and Vartak, 1975)
