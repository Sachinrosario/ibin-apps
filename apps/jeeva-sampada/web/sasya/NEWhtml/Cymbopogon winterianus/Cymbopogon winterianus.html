REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Floral and seed  biology:
Inflorescence spathate panicle, decompound, large about 1 m long with racemes arranged along zig-zag axis, spatheole 20   mm long, glabrous, raceme 20mm; rachis, internodes and pedicle pilose along margins, glabrous or puberulous along the  back; Flower  sessile spikelets 5 mm long, lower glume oblong- lanceolate, usually flat narrowly winged, 3-nerved,awn  5 mm long when present, pedicillate spikelets 5 mm long; lower glume lanceolate,7 nerved; Androecium stamens 3 in whorls ,odd stamen being anterior, filaments free and delicate, anthers introrse basifixed,and dithecous, opening   usually by longitudinal slits; Gynoecium monocarpellary with superior unilocular ovary having single anatropous ovule.    adnate to adaxial side of ovary, placentation basal, styles two ,stigmas plumose ; Fruit caryopsis; Seed one seeded with   thin   ovary wall adheres to it.  

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal: 
Plants are highly cross pollinated and Seeds are dispersed by wind.
Genetic variability:
The cultivated form of plant is diploid (2n=20). Four strains of citronella java; “Java –I,” “Java-II, Guatemala and Burma strains have been identified chiefly based on their sources of availability. These strains show several variations in economic traits like plant height, number of tillers, number of leaves per tiller, leaf length and breadth.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated vegetatively by splitting the culms.

Soil type: Although plant can adapt to various types of soils, light loam soils, which are rich in organic matter and are well drained are ideal for cultivation. Plant can tolerate pH from 5.8-8.0.

Nutrient requirements: Plant responds well to application. In average soil 20 kg nitrogen, 40 kg P205 and 40 kg K2O /ha basal dose at the time of planting. Neem cake, fertilizer, coated urea and urea super granules are found better.

Water management: Plant requires no irrigation in areas with well-distributed rainfall. In areas where long periods of drought exists,   4-10 irrigations are required depending upon the period of drought.  

Harvesting period / Method: First harvest of plant is obtained 4-6 months of planting, subsequent harvests is done 3-4 Months during 1st   year and 4-5 months in subsequent years.
