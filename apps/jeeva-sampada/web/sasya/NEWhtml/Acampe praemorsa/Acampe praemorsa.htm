Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Almost throughout the country, common orchid in South India.
Common

Terrestrial herb with yellow flowers, speckled with red spots. Orchid, Flowers, Ornamental.

This epiphytic orchid is seen in clumps on tree branches and sometimes among crevices of rocks. Common in forest regions from coast to hills up to 1,000 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Epiphytes. Stem about 1-1.5 cm thick, sheathed; sheaths brown, woody, longitudinally striated. Leaves thick, coriaceous, channeled, 12-30 x 1.5-3 cm, oblong, entire, emarginated with 2 unequal rounded lobes. Peduncle 1.5-6 cm long, rarely branched; racemes compact, corymbose. Flowers clustered at the apex of the peduncle pedicellate, bracteate. Bracts minute, ovate-oblong, acute, persistent, brown. Sepals 8 x 5 mm similar, coriaceous, entire, creamy-yellow with dark brownish-red irregular transverse bands; lateral sepals ovate, obtuse, rarely subretuse; dorsal sepal ovate-oblong, obtuse, mucronulate. Lip 7.5 mm long, 5 mm broad when spread out, creamy-yellow with 3-4 small narrow, red lines arranged in the form of a cross on the broad midlobe; fleshy, saccate at the base, obscurely 3-lobed; lateral lobes small, erect, subentire; midlobe dilated beyond the lateral lobes, deflexed, obovate-suborbicular, acute or mucronulate, margin irregularly waved. Column 3 x 3 mm short, stout, with 2 erect, minute horns produced on top from the sides of the column; broadly margined with deep brownished. Anther 2 x 2 mm triangular-conical; pollinia 2, waxy, yellow, globose, with a narrow, linear-oblong, translucent caudicle and a small, oblong gland. Stigmatic surface broadly oblong, large, margined with brownish-red. Ovary with pedicel 1 x 0.4 cm yellow, twisted. Capsules 6-7 x 0.6-0.8 cm, cigar-shaped, longitudinally ribbed.
