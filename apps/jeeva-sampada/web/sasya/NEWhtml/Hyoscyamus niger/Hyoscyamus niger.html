Chemistry:

Synthesis of scopoline via trans-6,7-dihydroxytropine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Rutin isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Hyoscyamine-N-oxide isolated from roots, leaves and stems. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Improved method of isolation of scopolamine from alkaloid mixture. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Total alkaloids (0.13%) from aerial parts contained hyoscyamine, hyoscine, skimmianine, apohyoscine, apoatropine, tropine and a- and b-belladonines. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

Ratio of atropine and scopolamine in tropine alkaloids of leaf estimated as 2:1. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

Three withanolide class steroids were isolated from the seeds of Hyoscyamus niger. The new compound was elucidated as 16a-acetoxyhyoscyamilactol (fig. 1) on the basis of spectroscopic properties and X-ray crystallography analysis. (Ma et al, 1999). C.A. 1999, 131, 297603v.

A simple HPTLC method is used for separation and estimation of tropane alkaloid hyoscyamine and hyoscine is described. (Gupta et al, 199). C.A. 1999, 131, 254454e.


Pharmacology:

Scopolamine administered i.p. protected guinea pig against bronchospasm produced by inhalation of histamine spray. It had antihistaminic activity equal to that of atropine but was almost inactive even at high dose against odema of rat paw injected with kaolin suspension. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Scopolamine HBr at 1-50 mg/kg had antihistaminic effects in gunea pig. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.

Evaluation of modulations in heart rate variability caused by a composition of herbal extract of Hyoscyamus nigers with other plant extracts. (Cysarz et al, 2000). C.A. 2000, 133, 129701y.

 


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for astringent (Seed, Gupta et al, 1981); Hysteria, muscle pain (Leaf + Flower, Gaur et al, 1983); Sedative (Leaf, Gupta et al, 1981); Toothache (Seed, Koelz, 1979); Vermifuge (Whole plant, Shah, 1982); Whooping cough (Leaf, Gaur et al, 1983; Gupta et al, 1981); Symbolically auspicious (Leaf, Seed, Koelz, 1979) .
