Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
·         Isolation and structure of a pyridine alkaloid – buchananine (fig. 1). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

·         Synthesis of Buchananine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·         Sarmentogenin and sarmentocymarin isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

·         Isolation of a new glycoside – buchanin (fig. 2a) from roots and its characterization as sarverogenin-3b-O-a-L-oleandropyranoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

·         Cryptanoside A (buchanin) as major compound and cryptanoside B as minor compound isolated from leaves; root afforded cryptanoside C along with germanicol docosanoate; cryptanosides B and C characterized as isosarverogenin-3-O-a-L-oleandroside and sarverogenin-3-O-[b-D-glucopyranosyl-(1à4)-a-L-oleandroside] respectively; cryptanoside D (isosarverogenin-3-O-[b-D-glucopyranosyl-(1à4)-a-L-oleandroside also isolated as tetra-acetate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

·         Isolation of anew cardenolide – cryptosin (fig. 2b) from leaves and its crystal structure determination. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.

·         Determination of 9-oxo-cis-12-octadecenoic acid (45.9%) in seed oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).


Pharmacology:
·         The chloroform and alcohol extracts of the seeds, roots, leaves and stems of cryptolepis buchanani were found to be active against Staphylococcus citreus and Shigella flexneri from 1.25 mg/ml and aboved in agar dilution methods. Cryptanoside A and C exhibited activity against Stem,. Citreus  at 2.0 and 025 mg/ml reswpectively, while only cryptanoside C showed activity against S. flexneri at 2.0 mg/ml. (Vasanth et al, 1997) MAPA 1998, 9803, 1684.


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for abdominal pain (Root, Joshi et al, 1980); Anasarca, ascites, body ache, cholera, dropsy, dysentery (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Cuts (Latex, Saxena et al, 1981); Fissures on sole (Latex, Apparantham and Chelladurai, 1986); fits (Root, Jain et al, 1973); Intermittent fever (Root, Tarafder, 1986); Lactation (Root, Jain, 1963; Saxena et al, 1981); Rheumatism (Root, Mudgal and pal, 1980; Latex, Jain et al, 1973; 127); Rickets in children (Whole plant, Sudhakar and Rolla, 1985; Sharma et al, 1979); Snake-bite; sores (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Stomachache (Root, Srivastava and Varma, 1981); Stop bleed (Floss on Seed, Shah et al, 1983); Syphilis, venereal disease (Whole plant, Jain and De, 1966; Root, Jain and De, 1966); Edible drink (Stem juice, Chaudhuri, 1977)
