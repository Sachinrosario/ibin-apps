Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Leaves, Seeds, flowers.

Uses

1.The juice of this plant is known as to be a violent purgative. It is stimulant and laxative used for cure of ringworm and mixed with chloride of ammonium, to cure dandruff.

2.The dried leaves and seeds are aromatic and astringent, and used in native practice in diarrhoea and dysentery of children along with butter milk. 

3.The fresh plant applied to wounds by Arabs, and the leaves and seeds given by the Tamuls in cases of worms.
