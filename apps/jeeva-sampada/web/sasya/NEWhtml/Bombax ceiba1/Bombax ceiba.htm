DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Gigantic tree reaching up to 40m in height, with a straight buttressed trunk and wide spreading branches in whorls. Leaves digitate, leaflets entire, glabrous, long petiolul.ed. fruit a 5-valved capsule, woody lined with white silky hairs inside. Seeds many.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
Flowers are many, present at the ends of the branches. Calyx 3-lobed green. Petals 5, tomentose outside. Stamens numerous, arranged in 5-6 bundles; in 5 phalanges of 12 opposite the petals, with 10 thin ones behind and 5 large central double ones alternate with the petals. Ovary 5-celled; stigmas 5.
