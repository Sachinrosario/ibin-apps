DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Terminal, sessile or shortly pedunculate umbellate cymes; Flower: White, green or pale yellow; Perianth: Persistent, silky, without densely hairy, lobes rounded ovate, equaling ;Androecium: Stamens 10, anthers sub-sessile, oblong, connective broad ; Gynoecium: Ovary subsessile, villous, 2-celled, stigma large ; Fruit: Capsule compressed, oblanceolate, hard and woody ; Seed: Many. Flowering & Fruiting : Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

Chemistry:

A sesquiterpenoid – agarospirol (fig. 1)– isolated from oil from plant and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

Liriodenine (fig. 2) isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Isolation and structure of new sesquiterpene – agarol (fig. 3) and gmelofuran. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Jinkohol (fig. 5) isolated from heartwood and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Syntheses of agaospirol and hinesol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.

Isolation and characterization of aquillochin (fig. 4). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.

Isolation of a new chromone – 2-[2(4’-methoxyphenyl)ethyl]chromone (I) (fig. 6a). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Another chromone – 6-methoxy-2 [2(4’-methoxyphenyl)ethyl]chromone (II) (fig. 6b) isolated and characterized by 13C-NMR spectroscopy. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

New chromenes – AH1, AH2, AH3, AH4, AH5 and AH6 – isolated, structure of AH1 found to be same as that of agarotetrol (fig. 7), isolated earlier. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

AH2 assigned structure (5S,6R,7R,8S)2-(2- phenylethyl)-5e’,6e,7e,8e’-tetrahydroxy-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrochromene on basis of NMR and CD studies and named isoagarotetrol (fig. 8); AH3, AH4, AH5 and AH6 (fig. 9) characterised as 6-hydroxy-2(2(2-phenyl-ethyl)chromone, 6-methoxy-2(2-phenylethyl)chromone, 6-methoxy-2[2-(3’-methoxy-phenyl)ethyl]chromone and 6,7-dimethoxy-2-(2-phenylethyl)chromone respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.

A new phenylethylchromone – AH1A-separated as acetyl derivative from crude AH1 whereas two other chromones – AH2 and AH2b obtained from the crude AH2. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)e.

Isolation and characterization of new biphenylethylchromones – AH10 (fig. 12a) and AH11 (fig. 13). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)f.

Structure and characterization of new bi and tri phenylethyl chromones – AH15 (fig. 15) and AH18 (fig. 16). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)g.

Further new chromones –AH7 (fig. 10), AH8, AH9 (fig. 11)– from agar wood and their characterisaion. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)g.

Isolation and structure elucidation of AH12 (fig. 14a), AH13 (fig. 14b) and AH14 (fig. 12b). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)h.

Two new tri-2-(phenylethyl)chromones – AH19a and AH19b (fig. 17)– isolated from agarwood and their stereostructures determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Isolation of AH17 (fig. 18a), AH20 (fig. 19b) and AH23 (fig. 18b); structures of AH17 and AH23 elucidated as 5a,6b,7b-trihydroxy-8a-methoxy-2-(2-phenylethyl)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrochromone and 5a,6b,7b,8a-tetrahydroxy-2-(2-(2’-hydroxyphenyl)ethyl]-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrochromone respectively; AH20 found to be a trimer formed by ether linkage between 2-(2-phenylethyl)chromone and two mols of agarotetrol at C5,8’ and C6,5’. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

A new dioxan-linked bis-2(2-phenylethyl)chromone – AH21 (fig. 20)– isolated and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.

Isolation of three new sesquiterpenes – (-)guaia-1(10),11-dien-15-al (I) (fig. 22), (-)selina-3,11-dien-9-one (II) (fig. 23) and (+)selina-3,11-dien-9-one (III) (fig. 24) from agar \wood and their structure determination. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.

Two new phenylethyl chromone derivatives isolated and characterized as (5S,6R,7S,8R,7’R)7’-hydroxy-isoagarotetrol and its 7’(S) – isomer. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.

(-)guaia-1(10),11-dien-15,2-olide and (-)2a-hydroxyguaia-1(10),11-dien-15-oic acid synthesized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)f.

six new nosesquiterpenoids – IV (fig. 25), V (fig. 26), (S)4a-methyl-2-(1-methylethyl)-1,2,3,4,4a,5,6,7-octahydronaphthalene, (2R,4aS)4a-methyl-2-(1-methylethylidene)-1, 2, 3, 4, 4a, 5, 6, 7-octahydronaphthalene, (1R,6S,9R)6, 10,10-trimethyl-11-oxatricyclo(7.2.1.0)dodecane and (1R,2R,6S,9R)6,10,10-trimethyl-11-oxatricyclo(7.2.1.0)dodecan-2-ol isolated from agar wood oil and their structures established. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)g.two new sesquiterpene aldehydes – (-)selina-3,11-dien-14-al (VI) (fig. 27) and (+)selina 4,11-dien-14-al (VII) (fig. 29)- three new sesquiterpene carboxylic acids – (-)selina-3,11-dien-14-oic acid (VIII), (-)selina-4,11-dien14-oic acid (IX) and 9-hydroxyselina-4,11-dien-14-oic acid (X) – isolated as methyl esters, and a new nosesquiterpene ketone-(+)1,5-epoxynor-ketoguaiene (fig. 21) was isolated from agarwood and their structures determined. Pharmacology:

The aqueous extract of A. agallocha stems inhibits the immediate hypersensitivity reaction by inhibition of histamine release from mast cells. (Kim et al; 1997) MAPA, 1998, 9801-0184.
