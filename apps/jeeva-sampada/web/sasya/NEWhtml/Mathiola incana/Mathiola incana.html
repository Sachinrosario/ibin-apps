UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:

Dactylin (fig. 1) (0.4%) from seeds, characterised as 5,7-dihydroxy-3’-methoxy-flavone-3,4’-digloucoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

3-arabinosylrhamnosido-7-rhamnoside of kaempferol from petals. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Kaempferol-7-rhamnoside, kaempferol-7-glucoside, kaempfer-3-rhamnogluco-7-rhamnoside and kaempferol-3-rhamnoarabino-7-rhamnoside were isolated from inflorescences. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).


Pharmacology:
Beneficial effect of dietary M. incana oil in reducing cholesterol levels and increasing (n-3) fatty acid levels in the plasma.  This new, terrestrial plant source of  (n-3) fatty acids could replace marine oils and thereby contribute beneficially to the human diet. (Yaniv et al, 1999) C.A.1999, 130:181873
