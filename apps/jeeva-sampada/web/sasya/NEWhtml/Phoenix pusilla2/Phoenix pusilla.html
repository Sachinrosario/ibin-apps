DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Shrubs. Stem very short, stoloniferous, thickly clothed with old leaf-sheaths. Leaves pinnate; leaflets subopposite, sword-shaped, much pointed, rigid, smooth, pale green.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Spathes axillary, concave inside, splitting longitudinally, laethery, smooth. Spadix erect, 20-30 cm long, much-branched; branches simple, spreading. Male flowers: calyx small, slightly 3-toothed; petals 3, oblong, rigid, white. Female flowers: calyx same as male flowers; petals 3, orbicular, concave. Berry 1.2 cm long, dull purple black. Seeds cartilaginous.
Floral and seed biology:
It flowers between January - August.
