HABIT & HABITAT:
A small tree with smooth brown bark and thorny branches, and distributed throughout the Mountainous regions of the South India. It is commonly found in coastal areas, dry deciduous forest, Dry Forest, Forests, Owk forest, Plains, Scrub forests and slopes.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
The wood, when first exposed, is pale yellow to yellowish-white, but gradually becomes light brownish-yellow. It is smooth, lustrous and heavy. Usually it has a fine texture and is straight-grained.
Anatomically, the wood is characterized by very small vessels encircled by parenchyma and and extensive tracts of short, fine, libriform fibres, containing yellow gummy deposits and starch grains.

Chemistry
Root bark gave acridone,alkaloids,atalaphylline,N-methylatalaphylline,N-methylbicycloatalaphylline,atalaphyl-lidine and atalaphyllinine,limonoids-atalatin,dehydratalantin,atalantolide and cyclopiatalantin and isomers.Leaves contain friedelin,epifriedelinol,sitosterol,stigmasterol and campesterol,Essential oil from leaves consist chiefly of sabinene.

Other Uses:
  1. The berries yield an oil which is used externally in rheumatism.
  2. The timber is useful for cabinet-work and furniture. Since the wood is strong and shock resistant, it is recommended for camp-furniture. It may be tried for making articles of turnery.
