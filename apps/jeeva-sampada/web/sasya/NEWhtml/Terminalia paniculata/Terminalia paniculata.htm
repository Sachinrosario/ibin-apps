Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A large deciduous tree attaining 30 m height and 4 m girth, found mainly in semi- evergreen moist forest, secondary moist deciduous forests and tropical moist deciduous forest of Western Ghats, in the deciduous forests in Tamilnadu, in Gujrat, Goa- Damn- Diu, Karnataka, Kerala, and southwards to Cape comorin. Extends upto 600m in Kerala, sea level to 1050 m in the Western Ghats and 1200m elsewhere. Average life of the tree is around 90 months (ranges from 60 to 119 months)

Availability : It is a native of areas with parent rock of crystalline series, either overlain or not with laterite. It is found mostly in the valleys and lower slopes preferring moist situations.

Floral and seed biology:

Seed Germination & Propagation:
Germinability of seeds is low, but if properly stored, remain viable for 5 months. Soaking in cold water does not improve germination, which takes about 2 months to complete. It reproduces freely in nature. Artificial propagation- Direct sowing is not satisfactory due to high %age of infertile seeds. Growth rate is slow, attaining a diameter of 2.5-5.5 cm in 10 years, 18-30 cm in 50 years. On laterite soils it is advantageous to plant species under light shad rather than in open. Stump planting is considered better.



MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:

A few fungi attack the plant while weevils reduce seed fertility.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel:
It is amongst the good fuelwood of India.
Wood:
Wood is very hard and heavy specific gravity is 0.75 and wood weight ranges 655 - 900 kg per cu. m. (average 800 kg per cu. m.) at 12% moisture content, heartwood moderately durable and requires mild preservative treatment. Wood is durable under cover. Average wood-durability ranges from 55 - 62 months.
Wood excellent for construction works, for building doors, planks and rafters, furniture, agricultural implements and plywood, used for making motor lorry and bus bodies, railway carriages and wagons, poles, wooden flooring. Suitable for fire-proofing.
