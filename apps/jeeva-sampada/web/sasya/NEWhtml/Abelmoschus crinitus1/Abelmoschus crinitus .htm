HABIT & HABITAT:
A bristly herb found in sub - Himalayan tract, Kashmir Eastwards. It generally prefers the Deciduous forest, Ditches, Forests margins, Fringes of forests, grass land, Hill slopes, moist deciduous forest and waste lands,
Biotic Zone: Chota Nagpur and North West Himalayas,


Availability :  
Misc: Bagaycha forest, Bagaycha forest (Kushalgarh), Chilpi, Corbett National Park, Helang, Hundrugagh, Korba, Kushalgarh, Palamau and Sitanadi
Country: Asia, Burma, China, India, Malesia, Pakistan and Philippines,
Zone: Central, East, North, North East, South, South east and West zones,
State: Bihar, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Meghalaya,Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal.
District: Balaghat, Banswara, Bastar, Bilaspur, Chamoli, Champaran, Garhwal, Jhalawar, Khasi hills, Mandla, Purulia, Raigarh, Raipur, Rajnandgaon, Simla, Surguja and Uttar Kannada,

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Perennial herbs. 0.5-2 m tall, the whole plant with yellow, long, scabrous hairs, hairs 5-6 mm. Stipule filiform, 1.5-3 cm, densely yellow long hirsute; petiole 4-12 cm, densely hirsute with long hairs; leaf blade various forms, that on the lower part of stem orbicular, ca. 9 cm in diam., 5-lobed, that on the middle part of the stem cordate, and that on the upper part of the stem sagittate, 4-15 cm, hirsute with long hairs on abaxial and adaxial surfaces, sparsely hispid or stellate hispid with long hairs on veins. Inflorescence terminate or axillary, 3-9 flowers in a raceme. Pedicels 1-1.5 cm, densely hirsute with yellow and long hairs; bracteoles 10-20, filiform, 10-35 ´ 1-2 mm, densely hirsute with yellow and long hairs. Calyx spathaceous, longer than bracteoles, densely hirsute with yellow and long hairs. Corolla yellow, ca. 13 cm in diam., petals 5-8 cm. Staminal column ca. 2 cm. Style branches 5; stigma flat. Capsule subglobose, ca. 3 cm in diam., 3-4 cm long, densely hirsute with yellow and long hairs. Seeds numerous, reniform, verrucose.
