Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
Tree occurring in sub-tropical Himalayas from Nepal to Sikkim and in the hills of Assam at altitudes of 3 000-7,000 ft. in India. It is also found in the other countries like China, India, Java and Nepal.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A small or medium-sized tree, 2-3 ft in girth with 10-15 ft. clear bole, not infrequently crooked, It bears pinnate leaves, greenish white flowers and red capsules containing dark brown compressed seeds. It is cultivated on. A small scale in north Bengal for fodder purposes. The wood is white and soft.

Other Uses

  1. The wood is white when first exposed, turning greyish or brownish with age, lustrous, light (sp. gr., 0.27; wt., 17 Ib./cu. ft.), straight-grained ;and medium-textured. Green conversion and open-stacking of converted material under cover or kiln-seasoning are recommended. The wood is soft and fibrous, easy to saw and work, takes a good finish and lasts fairly well under cover. It is used locally for posts and tea boxes. It is suitable for ceiling boards, partitions, and match splints and boxes.
  2. They are also used in dysentery.
  3. The plant is used as an antipyretic in Indo-China and a lamp oil is prepared from the seeds.
