HABIT & HABITAT
Large shrubs or small trees, 3-8 m tall occurs throughout India. In dry deciduous forests up to 1500 m as an undergrowth. Pakistan, Sri lanka  Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, S. China, S.E. Asia and Australia.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Large shrubs or small trees, 3-8 m tall. Stems terete, branchlets rough with scattered stellate hairs. Leaves broadly ovate-orbicular, elliptic-obovate, 10-23 x 11-17 cm, shortly acuminate at apex, cordate at base, serrate or crenate-serrate at margin, sparsely scabrous to tomentose, especially on lower surfaces, main nerves 3-7; petioles 1.0-2.5 cm long, pubescent; stipules subulate, c 1 cm long, deciduous
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers 8-9 flowered axillary, c 8 cm long, solitary or in clustered cymes, reddish turning purplish-grey. Calyx 2 cm long, gibbous, laterally compressed, somewhat 2-lipped, and densely stellate-hairy. Petals 4-5 cm long, reflexed, 2 lower shorter and broader than the 3 upper ones, claws winged. Follicles cylindrical, 4-8 x 0.5-1.0 cm, spirally twisted with a apical beak, up to 1 cm long, stellate-tomentose. Seeds many, angular, 2 mm long, wrinkled, tomentose.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: Roots are used in abortifacient, antigalactagogue, asthma, astringent, cuts, dumulcent, diabetes, expectorant, sciatica, antidote to snake bite, stomachache and wounds. Fruits are also used in anthelmintic, cold, colic, dysentery, ear complaints, fever, gastrointestinal complaints, rickets of babies, sores and stomachache. Leaves are useful in ecezema.


Chemistry:
Diosgenin isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

A new ester isolated from leaves and charcterised as tertraatriacontanyl tetratriacontanoate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Cucurbitacin B and isocucurbitacin B identified in roots by HPLC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

A new compound methyl 3b,27-diacetoxylup-20(29)-en-28-oate, isolated along with isorin which was characterized as methyl 3-oxo-27-acetoxyolean-12-en-28-oate; betulinic acid, oleanolic acid, daucosterol and b-sitosterol also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

A new flavone 5,8-dihydroxy-7,4’-dimethoxy flavone has been isolated along with trifolin and hibifolin from the leaves of Helicteres isora. (Ramesh and Yuvarajan, 1995). C.A.  1995, 123, 251397n.

Three new compounds, 4’-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl rosmarinic acid, 4,4’-O-di-b-D-glucopyranosyl rosmarinic acid and 2R-O-(4’-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl Catteoyl)-3-(4-hydroxyphenyl) lactic acid named as 4-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl isorinic acid were isolated together with rosmarinic cid from fruit of Helicteres isora. (Satake et al, 1999). C.A. 2000, 132, 2104b.

From aqueous extract of Helicteres isora, six new neolignans helicterins A – F were isolated. Helicterins A – F are dimeric (7.5’, 8.2’)-neolignans withbicyclo (2.2.2) octane C – frame work and showed mild inhibitory activity against reverse transcriptase from arian myeloblastoris virus. (Tezuka et al, 2000). C.A. 2001, 134, 83502z.

Five flavonoid glucuronides were obtained from the fruit of Helicteres isora, three of which were previously unknown compounds: isoscutellarein 4'-methyl ether 8-O-beta-D-glucuronide 6"-n-butyl ester. isoscutellarein 4'-methyl ether 8-O-beta-D-glucuronide 2", 4"-disulfate and isoscutellarein 8-O-beta-D-glucuronide 2",4"-disulfate. The structures were determined on the basis of spectroscopy and hydrolysis experiments. (kamiya et al, 2001)

Pharmacology:

Cucurbitacin B and isocucurbitacin B exhibited cytotoxic activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

The antispasmodic activity was checked in vitro on guinea-pig ileum against three spasmogens, acetylcholine, histamine and barium chloride. The IC(50) for each was determined. The activity was compared with standard antispasmodic agents, atropine and diphenhydramine hydrochloride. The activity was also studied in vivo by observing the gastrointestinal motility in mice using the marker technique and the ED(50) was calculated. Acute toxicity studies were conducted on mice using the method of Weil and the LD(50) was determined. (Pohocha and Grampurohit, 2001).

4,4’-O-bdi-b-D-glucopyranosyl rosmarinic acid had greater scavenging activity against superoxide anion produced with xanthne and xanthine oxidase (XOD) than rosmarinic acid. (Satake et al, 1999). C.A. 2000, 132, 2104b.

Ethanolic extract of H. isora root caused significant reduction in plasma glucose, triglyceride and insulin levels at 300 mg/kg dose after 9 days of administration to insulin resistant and diabetic C57BL/KsJdb/db mice. In normoglycemic and mildly hypertriglyceridemic Swiss albino mice, the extract also showed significant reduction in plasma triglyceride and insulin levels, without affecting plasma glucose level. An ethanolic extract showed activity distinctly different from glybenclamide and acarbose but similar to troglitazone in these models. In high fat fed hamster model, the extract showed significant reduction in plasma lipid levels. In order to identify the active pharmacophore, the ethanolic extract was further subjected to sequential partitioning with low, medium and high polarity solvents, which yielded a semipurified fraction having both euglycemic and lipid-lowering activity. (Chakrabarti et al, 2002).
