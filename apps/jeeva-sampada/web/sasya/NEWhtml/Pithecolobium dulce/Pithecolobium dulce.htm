Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

A small to medium-sized evergreen tree. Native to a vast region that extends from Pacific slopes of Mexico and southern California through all of Central America to Colombia and Venezuela. Occurs in tropical medium rainfall areas of Indo-Gangetic Plains and the Peninsula (moist tropical), dry areas of Rajasthan and Peninsular India (dry tropical).

This species is suitable for most dry regions. It is drought resistant and in low rainfall areas develops an extensive root system.

It has a fast growth rate. Hardy tree, suitable for growing on boundaries and on marginal lands, grows even on waste and denuded lands, pure sandy soils and on sea coast with roots in brackish or salt water. Withstand heat and shade, drought resistant.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A small to medium-sized, evergreen, spiny tree that grows up to 20 m or more in height. It has a broad crown (to 30 m across) and a short bole (to 1 m thick). Bark smooth, grey with yellowish white lenticels. Leaves bipinnate, pinnae 2; pinnules 2 in each pinna, obliquely ovate-oblong, 1-4 cm long. At the base of each leaf is normally found a pair of short, sharp spines, though some specimens are spineless. Flowers in dense heads, small, white. Pods curved or twisted, constricted, reddish brown. Seeds 6-10, shining black, enveloped in pink to whitish, pulpy aril.

Floral and seed biology:

Seedling Germination: Grown by direct sowing. It reproduces easily by seeds or cuttings. It fruits at an early age. Seed may be stored for about 6 months (though it must then be protected from insects.) Seed treatment is not necessary. Germination takes only 1 or 2 days.

 

MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:

Tree is affected by leaf spot diseases (Phyllosticta inga-dulcis Died. and Colletotrichum spp.) And a number of defoliating and boring insect pests. It has also been recorded as a host of lac insect.

 

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used: Whole tree, Seeds and leaves.

Wood an excellent fuel . In parts of India, it is planted and harvested as fuel for brick kilns. Yields firewood. Wood used for general construction, packing cases, fence posts, cart-building and agricultural implements.


Harvesting Practices:

It has a fast growth rate. It coppices vigorously and can withstand any amount of pruning.

 

Produce and Products:

Oils, gums and Tannins.

Chemistry:

Calorific value of wood - 5,177- 5,580 cal.

Processing Techniques:

 

Other uses:

Shade, hedges and ornamental use - Mostly grown for hedges and fuel. With regular trimming it makes dense, almost impenetrable, thorny hedges that keep out live stock and form useful shelterbelts. Grown as a shade or roadside tree and for ornament. This makes a good highway tree.

Food - Seeds eaten raw or in curries. Pods are harvested in Mexico, Cuba and Thailand and customarily sold on roadside stands. They contain a thick, sweetish, but also acidic pulp that is usually white, but sometimes red. It is eaten raw or made into a drink similar to lemonade. Fruits are edible. Pulp is made into lemonade.

Forage - Leaves used as fodder for sheep, goats, horses and cattle. Pods used as fodder for cattle, sheep, goats and other livestock. Hedge clippings are often gathered for animal feed. The plants withstand heavy browsing.

Seed – Seeds contain a greenish oil (20 per cent), which, after refining and bleaching, can be used for food or making soap. Presscake residue is rich in protein (30 per cent) and may be used as stockfeed. Aril of the seed is sweet and edible.

Miscellaneous – Extract from bark is used for tanning, in Philippines, which produces light coloured leather.

Flowers are visited by bees and yield good quality honey.

The wounded bark exudes a mucilagenous gum somewhat like gum arabic.
Bark - Bark yields tanin.

Miscellaneous Information

Once introduced, it may hold an area firmly. In Tamil Nadu, India, repeated attempts to replace this plant with other species have failed, because the trees are often top heavy and shallow rooted, in heavy windstorms branches may break off or the whole tree may topple.

Cultivated, shade tree, also planted for thorny hedge.
Tree is a nitrogen fixer.

Wood: Reddish brown, somewhat lustrous, straight to irregular-grained, rather fine-textured, hard, heavy, 641 kg/ c m., strong but brittle. Difficult to cut.
