DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Erect, annual or perennial, up to 75 cm tall, much branched, woody herbs or undershrubs, glabrous or with scattered stellate hairy. Leaves highly variable, ovate to oblong, often rhomboid or lanceolate, wedge-shaped, 3-10 x 1-5 cm, sharp at apex, bidentate-biserrate at margin, glabrous or sparsely stellate hairy above, wooly beneath; petiole 3-6 mm long; stipules linear, longer than petiole, 0.5-1.2 cm long.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers axillary, solitary or in 2-7 in clusters on short branchlets, 1.5-2 cm across. Pedicels jointed above middle, 5-7 mm long, accrescent. Calyx bell-shaped, 6-7 mm long, free upto above the middle, prominantly 10-nerved, 5-lobed, lobes triangular to ovate, pointed at apex. Corolla yellow to pale orange; lobes oblique, usually shallow notched at apex, wedge-shaped at base. Mericarps 6-12, muticous, 2.5-3.5 x 2-2.5 mm with 2 short awns; awns  stellate hairy  or glabrous. Seeds c 2 mm, across, flattened reniform, dark brown or black, glabrous.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Whole plant is used in gonorrhoea. Roots are used in asthma, bile, boils, child birth, cuts and bruises, rheumatism, heart ailments, tuberculosis and tonic. Leaves are also use in dysentery, spermatorrhoea and wounds.
NBPGR:  Roots- febrifuge; root bark-antiparalytic, in sciatica; plant-in spermatorrhoea; seeds- aphrodisiac, antigonorrhoeic, in colic. Also useful in blood diseases, bleeding piles, diseases of the throat, phthisis and insanity. A decoction of the root with ginger is given by hindu physicians, in intermittent fever. The powdered root bark is given with milk and sugar for the relief of frequent mictorition and to leucorrhoea. In disease of nervous system the root is used a longer in combination with other medicines.


Chemistry:
Roots contain alkaloids-ephedrine; β-phenethylamine and Ψ-ephedrine; carboxylated tryptamines-δ(+)-Nb-methyltryptophan methyl ether and hypaphorine; quinazoline alkaloids-rasicinone, vasicine and vasicinol. In addition, choline and betaine have also been obtained. Alkaloids present in roots are also present in stems and leaves but in different amounts. Ephedrine and Ψ-ephedrme are the major alkaloids in the aerial parts. Isolation of b-phenethylamine, N-methyl-b-phenethylamine, S-(+) Nb-methyltryptophan methyl ester, vasicinol, vasicinone, vasicine, choline, hypaphorine methyl ester, hypaphorine and betaine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

Pharmacology:Crude extract of root (5 and 10kg) produced sedative effect and significant potentiation of pentobarbitone sleeping time in mice. Extract did not produce any change in normal temperature of mice, but 10g/kg dose showed significant decrease in rectal temperature of pyretic rats 2 and 3hr after administration. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).
The methanol extracts from the leaves of Sida rhombifolia, exhibited cytotoxic activity against the 60 human cell lines tested. (Muanza et al, 1995). C.A. 1996, 123, 102210
