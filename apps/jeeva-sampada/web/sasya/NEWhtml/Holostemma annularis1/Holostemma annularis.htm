HABIT & HABITAT
 A handsome, extensive, laticiferous twining shrub found in most parts of India , ascending up to an altitude of 6,000 ft.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves opposite, ovate or triangular, acute or acuminate, deeply cordate, midrib glandular at the base. Flowers in axillary umbellate cymes, purplish inside and silvery white or pinkish outside, fragrant, fleshy, follicles thick, cylindrical, bluntly pointed.

Other Uses

1.The bark yields a fiber reported to be suitable for cordage and paper-making.

2.The plant exudes latex  which on drying yields an elastic residue.

3.It is grown in gardens for its pretty flowers and may be trained over trellis work.

4.The roots are reported to possess cooling, alterative, tonic and laxative properties. Made into a paste, they are applied in ophthalmic and orchids.

5. They are also used in diabetes, gonorrhea, coughs and stomach-ache.
