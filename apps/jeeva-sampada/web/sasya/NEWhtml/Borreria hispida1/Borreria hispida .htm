HABIT & HABITAT
It is a procumbent herb distributed from the Himalayas at Simla, eastwards to Assam, and southwards to Ceylon.
A genus consisting of 100 spp. of annual or perennial herbs, distributed in tropical, countries. In india, it is represented by 3 or 4 app. Several species are medicinal. Found on the bunds of rice fields,coast sands,Cultivated fields and Plains,Dry Open areas,drylands in interior,hills.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Erect or diffuse annual herb; branches quadrangular, grooved, leaves ovate, obovate, scabrid, pubescent. The plant is a procumbent, branched, hairy or rough herb 10 to 14 cm in length. The branches are greenish or purplish, ascending, stout, 4-angled. The leaves are ovate and pointed or rounded at the tip. Flowers pale pink, The fruit is hairy capsule, oblong-ellipsoid. The seeds are oblong, granulate, opaque, usually variable.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
The flower are 4-6 and occur in a whorl in the axils of the leaves. The calyx-teeth are linear lanceolate. The corolla is pale blue or white, and is 5-10 mm in length.
