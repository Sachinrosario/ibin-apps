HABIT & HABITAT
Straggling shrubs, 2-5 m tall and found in dry and arid regions. Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Straggling shrubs, 2-5 m tall; young branches tomentose, with spines; spines paired, one straight, slender and very sharp, nearly as long as the leaf stalk, other much shorter, hooked, bent downwards. Leaves ovate, elliptic or orbicular, 1-2.5 x 0.5-1.8 cm, apex acute or obtuse, base oblique or rounded, margin serrate, tomentose on both surfaces, basally 3-nerved.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers 4-5 mm across, in short, axillary, compact 10-20 flowered cymes. Calyx lobes deltoid, glabrous within, hairy without. Petals obovate-spathulate.  Drupes globose, c 8 mm in diam., woody, glabrous, black red and shining when ripe. Seeds 1 or 2, compressed.
