Chemistry:

·        Patuletin (quercetagetin-6-methylether), mp. 2610 and spinacetin (quercetagetin-3’, 6-dimethylether), mp.2350, isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

·        6-hydroxymethyllumazine isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

·        Spinasaponins A and B isolated from roots and characterized as 3-O-[b-D-glucopyranosyl [(1-3)-b-D-glucuronopyranosyl]-3b-hydroxyolean-12-en-28-oic acid and corresponding derivative of hederagenin respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

·        A new eudesmenolide -7a-hydroxyeudesm-4-en-6,12-olide (I) (7-hydroxyfrullanolide)-isolated from flowers and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Its isomer -7b-hydroxyeudesm-4-en-6, 12-olide (II)-and a new dihydrolactone (III) also isolated and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Isolation of a new sesquiterpene acid-2-hydroxycostic acid – and its structure elucidation; b-eudesmol and ilicic acid also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Isolation of an anthelmintic agent -24(S) 24-ethylcholesta-5, 22-dien-3b-O-b-D-glucoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Isolation of three new eudesmanolides (I, II and III) from flowers and their characterization as 11a, 13-dihydro-3a, 7a-dihydroxyfrullanolide, 11a, 13-dihydro-7a, 13-dihydroxyfrullanolide and 11a, 13-dihydro-7a-hydroxy-13-methoxyfrullanolide respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Two more eudesmanolides (IV and V) isolated and their structures established. Cryptomeridiol and 4-epicryptomeridiol also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Flowers afforded two cyclopeptide alkaloids (VI and VII) whose tentative structures proposed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        A novel isoflavone glycoside, 5,4'-dimethoxy-3'-prenylbiochanin 7-O-b-D-galactoside was isolated from the leaves of S. indicus. (Yadava et al., 1999)

·        Three new eudesmanoids have been isolated from Sphaeranthus indicus and their structures were established as 11alpha, 13-dihydro-3alpha, 7alpha-dihydroxy-4,5-epoxy-6beta,7-eudesmanolide (1), 11alpha,13-dihydro-7alpha-acetoxy-3beta-hydroxy-6beta,7-eudesm-4-enolide (2a) and 3-keto-beta-eudesmol (3) by comparison of spectral data with those of other 7alpha-hydroxyeudesmanolides.(Pujar et. al., 2000)

Pharmacology:

·        7a-Hydroxyeudesm-4-en-6, 12-olide exhibited antimicrobial acivity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·         Sphaeranthus indicus shows anti-inflammatory activity by suppressing the capacity of P. acnes-induced ROS and pro-inflammatory cytokines, the two important inflammatory mediators in acne pathogenesis. (Jain et al., 2003)
