HABIT & HABITAT

In the outer Himalayas and the sub-Himalayan tract from the Ganges eastwards, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Central India, Deccan and on the west coast in Konkan and N. Kanara. Also recorded from the Lushai hills in Mizoram. The tree usually occurs in dry, rocky forests and is often associated with Bosellia serrata. It is useful for reclothing base, rocky land.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A moderate-sized, deciduous tree, upto 15m. in height.
Bark grayish, white or reddish, smooth, peeling off in papery flake.
Leaves crowded at the ends of the branches, palmately-5-lobed, 20-30 cm diameter.
Follicles 4-6, ovoid oblong, c.2.5cm diameter., coriaceous, red, covered with stinging hairs.
Seeds 3-6, oblong, black.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Bark, timber, seed, leaves


Harvesting Practices:
Gum - the gum exudes naturally from the bark throughout the year, but most of it is generally produced by tapping or blazing by stripping off the bark. Tapping is done from April to June, before the rains, and them from Oct. to Jan, after the rains. In general, trees with reddish bark and those growing on the hill slopes are reported to yield more gum.

Produce and Products:
Bark - Gum : (Indian Tragacanth) or gum karaya; used as thickening agent in the preparation of printing pastes for textile industry; used in pharmaceuticals, medicinal preparation such as lozenges, emulsions, lotions, sprays and pastes, in denture-fixature powders.

Used in paper industry as a pulp-binder; used in leather industry, in food, baking and diary industry - used in dressing, spreads, preparation of ground meat.

Timber - toys, guitars, inferior packing - cases, boarding, cement-barrels, cheap match boxes, splints, low-grade pencils, for moulded picture and stale frames.

Seed - roasted seeds eaten.

Bark - fiber - cordages, indigenous water-bottles.
In Bastar (MP), pounded bark given to women to facilitate delivery.
Leaves - fodder, rich in Vit. A.
Seed - glue from seed protein used as an adhesive in making plywood.

Processing Techniques:
Gum - after collection, the larger lumps of gum are broken into smaller pieces and the gum in this underground and unprocessed state occur in commerce.
