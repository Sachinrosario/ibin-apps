HABIT & HABITAT
 An erect herb, smooth, 30-60 cm high, stem angular, succulent, sometimes reddish. It found in Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan.
Availability :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves ovate to oblong or triangular-hastate, obtuse or acute at apex, alternate, smooth on both surfaces; lower leaves long petiolate. Male flowers:  glomerate spike, sessile, numerous; calyx 4-partite; stamens 4. Female flowers: sessile, axillary; calyx two tipped, projecting horn growing into spine during seed ripening; styles generally 4. Fruit capsular, 1-valved, 1-celled, crowded with small persistent calyx.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Ayurveda: The plant is sweet; useful in blood and brain diseases, asthma and leprosy. In Unani system of medicine leaves are used in urinary concretions, inflammation of the lungs and the bowels, pain in the joints, sore throat, cold and sneezing, sore-eyes, ring worms, scabes, lecucoderma and vomiting. Seeds are used in fever, leucorrhoea, urinary discharges, brain and heart diseases.
