DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Cormatous, dioecious herbs; corm 4-5 x 2.5-3 cm, globose. Leaf solitary; petiole stout, mottled and banded with red and brown, 40-60 x 0.5-1.2 cm, sheathed; blade radiatisect, leaflets 5-11, whorled, 17-30 x 5-10 cm, dark green above, paler below, base tapering, broadly lanceolate, oblong; midrib stout, blade tip cauda 3-4 cm long. Peduncle shorter than petiole, stout, mottled and banded, 15-25 x 0.5-0.7 cm. Spathe 18-20 x 3.5-4 cm, dark green, with longitudinal white striae; tube shorter than limb, cylindrical, 4-5.5 x 1.8-2.2 cm; limb 6-7 x 3-4cm, dark green, dialated at the base, becomes horizontal, slightly decurved, lanceolate, acuminate, tip 3-5.5 cm, long. Spadix shorter than spathe, 7-8.5 cm long, pale green, smooth; appendage with a rounded warty tip. Spadix unisexual. Male Spadix up to 8.5 cm long, 2.5-3.5 cm portion occupied by anthers; 2-6 lobed, sessile and scattered; appendage 2.5-3.8 cm long, pale green, smooth with a round warty tip; neuters absent. Female Spadix up to 8.5 cm long; ovaries many, up to 3.8 cm portion, dark green, densely crowded; 4 ovules in each ovary; neuters 7-8 above the female flowers; appendage 4 x 0.5 cm, pale green with round warty tip. Fruit berry 3-4 seeded.
