HABIT & HABITAT

Succulent herb found growing in dry region.
India, Burma and Sri Lanka.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Stems usually dichotomously branched; branches green with brownish streaks, quadrangular, angles rounded; internodes 2.5-8 mm long and 2-8 mm broad, fleshy, glabrous. Latex watery. Leaves reduced to scale like structure, opposite, decussate, sessile, 1.5-3 x 1-1.5 mm, deltoid-ovate, acute at apex, ciliate on either side at the base, caduceus leaving upwardly directed protuberance on the stem. Flowers axillary at upper nodes, solitary; pedicels cylindrical, 2-4 mm long, glabrous; bracts ca 1.2 x 0.4 mm, triangular, apex acute, glabrous. Calyx 5-lobed, divided up to base, lobes ca 1 x 1.5 mm, lanceolate, apex acute, glandular within, glabrous. Corolla rotate, yellowish with purplish striations on lower half and dark purple on upper half on inner side; lobes five, 7-8 x 1.5-2 mm, lanceolate, spathulate, rounded-apiculate, expanded near base, divided ¾ of the way down, margin folded back along the middle line, fringed; fringes 1-2 mm long, spindle shaped, purple. Corona staminal, biseriate; outer five lobed, lobes divided into five deeply cleft with two long filaments ca 1 mm long divergent teeth; inner five lobed, adnate to the base of outer corona, ca 2 mm long and ca 1 mm wide, apex obtuse, incumbent at the tip over anthers. Stamens five; ca 2 mm long; pollinia five, pollen masses solitary in each anther cell, yellow, waxy, 259.2-273.6 x 360 mm, lens shaped, with pellucid layer attached by light-brown caudicles ca 100.8 mm long, ca 57.6-72 mm wide to a dark-brown corpuscle ca 187.2 mm long and ca 57.6 mm wide. Gynostegium ca 2 mm long. Follicles paired, 7.5-10 x 0.3-0.6 cm, linear, tapering from base to apex, pointed tip, slightly curved, glabrous; seeds many, 7-8 x 3-4 mm, oblong, base rounded, dark-brown with light brown margin ca 1 mm wide; coma silky white, ca 2-4 cm long.
