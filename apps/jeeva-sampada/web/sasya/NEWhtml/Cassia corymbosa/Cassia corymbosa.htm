HABIT & HABITAT                                                                                
One of autumn's standout shrubs. Extremely rich buttercup-yellow flowers. flowers smoother this rapidly growing evergreen from March through to May.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
it's a show-stopper when in flower and for the rest of the year it has prominent mid-green-hint-of-blue lobed leaves on a bush that grows to around shape 2.5m tall.Easy to grow in a sunny position and a well-worked freely draining soil. It'll grow quickly to around 2.5m tall and is best pruned to half size when flowering has finished, if left it will look gangly after a few years. Hardy to extremely cold weather, but it will benefit from shelter from harsh winds in early years. Bright autumn colour in gardens at a time of year when colour's a little scarce. A mid-border plant that contrasts well with foliage from larger tress and shrubs behind. OK for patio pots when young, in cold areas it's great in a conservatory too.
