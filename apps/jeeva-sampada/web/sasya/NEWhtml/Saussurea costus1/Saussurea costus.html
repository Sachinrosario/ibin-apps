DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Herbs, 1.0-2.5 m tall, simple, pubescent. Basal leaves with petioles 55-88 cm long; terminal lobe sometimes c 30 cm across, irregularly toothed, membranous, scabrulous above, glabrate beneath; petioles lobately winged; cauline leaves half amplexicaul at base, 15-30 cm long, sessile or with short petiole.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Heads subglobose, axillary or in terminal clusters of 2-5, 2.5-3.5 cm across. Involucral bracts purple, ovate-lanceolate. Receptacles bristles very long. Corolla 2 cm long, tubular, dark blue or purple or almost black. Stamens free. Anther-tails fringed. Pappus hairy, 1.7 cm long, brown, all feathery. Achenes curved, compressed, c 8 mm.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plants is cultivated for the roots which are used in perfume industry, incense and medicine. Root is hot, bitter, sweetish, pungent; fattening, aphrodisiac, alterative. It improves the complexion, cures leucoderrma, erysipelas, itching, ringworm, diseases of blood, bronchitis, vomiting, scabies and also used in epilepsy, headache, hysteria (Ayurveda).
BSI:
The root is hot, fattening, aphrodisiac, alterative, alexipharmic, carminative, anti-spasmodic, analgesic, emmenagogue, tonic, brain stimulative, stomachic, antiseptic, disinfectant, narcotic, cardiac stimulent, expectorent, diuretic, depressant, depurative, bronchodilator, insecticidal and prophylactic.

Root is used in the treatment of the leucoderma, erysipelas, itching, ringworm, scabies, blood diseases, cephalagia, bronchitis, vomiting, epilepsy, headache, cholera, hysteria, liver and kidney diseases, deafness, joint and chest pain, paralysis, asthma, dropsy, cough, inflammation, opthalmia, old fever, typhoid, ulcer, worms in wounds and persistent hiccough. It controls attack of the bronchial asthma of vagotonic type, improves complexion, turns gray hair black (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
