DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Anuual herb with deeply grooved stem. Leaves are lanceolate, base always narrowed into the petiole. Whorls of flower lax or dense; panicle leafy to the top. Fruiting perianths are yellow brown when ripe. The fruit varies greatly in size & number and length of the spines or the valves.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used: 
Seeds, Plant, Leaves.

Uses:
1.Seeds are used as an aphrodisiac (Yunani), used to cure gleet. Tonic of seeds is tonic to the loins; remove pain from the back and the lumber region.
2.Plant is a good refrigerant.
3.The leaves are applied to burns.
