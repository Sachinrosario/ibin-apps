HABIT & HABITAT
Erect herb, sparingly branched, strongly aromatic. It is found in almost throughout India. Cultivated, often grow as weed in cultivated fields, roadsides etc.
Availability :  
Throughout India and Cultivated in Indian gardens.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
  Leaves opposite, decussate or upper ones alternate, pinnate; leaflets lanceolate-oblong, 1.5-4.5 x 0.5-1.0 cm, acute at apex, cuneate at base, remotely dentate at margin; petioles up to 5 cm long. Heads heterogamous, solitary, terminal on 3.5-15.0 cm long peduncles, 6-8 cm across. Receptacles convex. Involucral bracts 1-seriate, connate, glandular. Florets orange-yellow. Ray florets female, ligulate, 1-2 cm long. Disk floret bisexual, tubular. 5-toothed. Achenes blackish, 7.5-9.5 mm long, scabrous on angles. Pappus scales united, 3.5-4.0 mm long, unequal.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Leaves are used as an application to boils and carbuncles. Leaf juice is given in earache. Flowers are used in diseases of eye, unhealthy ulcers and internally to purify blood. Flower juice is given as a remedy for bleeding piles.
Parts used: Herb-antirheumatic, in cold, bronchitis; root-laxative; flower, depurative, febrifuge, astringent, carminative, in diseases of eye, bleeding piles, unhealthy ulcers, fits, scabies, belching, liver complaints, teeth troubles, leaf-vermifuge, diuretic, in kidney troubles, muscular pain, earache, ophthalmia, applied to boils, carbuncles.
