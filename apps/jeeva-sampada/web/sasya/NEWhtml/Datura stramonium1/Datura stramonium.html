Chemistry:
CIMAP:   The contents of proline in both plants increased progressively with the rise of salinization level. However, the contents of other free amino acids were variably affected. They are generally lowered with the rise of salinization level except for stem, where they were somewhat elevated. The total alkaloid content as well as the contents of the various alkaloid fractions in D. stramonium generally increased with the rise of salinization, regardless of the organ analysed, but in roots these contents did not exibit any general pattern1; changes in growth and chem. compn. such as saccharides, protein, free amino acids, alkaloids and mineral of fruits of salinized Datura stramonium were studied. The fresh and dry matter was mostly increased with the rise of salinization level. Generally, the insol. and total saccharides, insol. protein, free amino acids other than protein and total alkaloid contents increased significantly with the rise of salinization level. However, the sol. saccharides, sol. and total protein , proline contents decreased with the rise of salinization level. Mineral compn. also exhibited significantly change, but the dominant feature was the progressively increased of sodium content with the rise of salinity level2.
