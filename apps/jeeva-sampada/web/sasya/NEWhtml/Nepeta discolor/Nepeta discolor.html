HABIT & HABITAT:
 Decumbent or procumbent herbs, c 20-26 cm long. Stem 4-angled with short diffuse branches covered with minute hairs, found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Sikkim.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Decumbent or procumbent herbs, c 20-26 cm long. Stem 4-angled with short diffuse branches , covered with minute hairs. Leaves simple, ovate-cordate, 0.5-1.0 x 0.2-0.6 cm, obtuse at apex, broad at base, prominently serrate along margin, pubescent on both surfaces; petioles slender, 0.1-0.6 cm long, pubescent. Flowers in terminal verticels, numerous. Nutlets ellipsoid, smooth, dark brown.
