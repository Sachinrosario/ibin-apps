Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

Common in mixed dry deciduous forests. Throughout India except J & K; and Arunachal Pradesh. The tree is very typical of open grasslands, where it is frequently gregarious as in the most arid regions. It grows in water logged fluctuation, on black cotton soils, eve on saline, alkaline, swampy badly drained soils and on baren lands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A deciduous tree with a crooked trunk, upto 15m in height and 1.6-2.0m in girth;
Back bluish grey or light brown;
Leaves long petioled, 3 foliate, leaflets coriaceous, broadly obovate from a cuneate ordeltoid base, glabrescent above, densely finely silky below;
Flower buds dark brown, flowers bright orange, red, sometimes yellow, in 15 cm long racemes on bare branches;
Pods pendulous, silky-tomentose, 10-13 cm long containing one seed at its apex;
Seeds flat, semiform, 3.3-3.8 cm x 8.2-2.5 cm.

Floral and seed biology:

Seed Biology :
Seedling germination : It reproduces by seeds and root-suckers. The seeds germinate early in the rainy season. Artificial regeneration can be done both by direct sowing and transplanting from the nursery. The tree can be raised by line sowings, both irrigated and unirrigated with or without other field crops.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Flower - used during holi, dye yellow, cotton, sola articles, woolen carpets, astringent, diuretc, depurative, aphrodisiac, tonic properties as an emmenagogue, as poultrice in orchitis, to reduce swellings, for bruises and sprains, effective in leprosy, leucorrhoea, gawk; decoction given in diarrhoea and to peripheral women.

Aq. Extract - for colouring food stuffs.

Seed - have apparent and rubefacient properties, as vermifuge in veterinary medicine, freshly powdered seeds against.

Acaris infection; paste of seeds with lemon-juice as a cure for ringworm and herpes, decoction of seed is given in gravel.

Seed oil - Moodooga Oil or Kino - free oil - soap making seed meal blended with tannin urea formation mixture for form a tannin adhesive for plywood many act.

Wood - well-curb, water-scoops, rough packing-cases, cheap board-wood, for structural work.

Paper pulp - Braille paper, wrapping paper, newsprint.

Produce and Products:

Butea gm or Bengal Kino - astringent, given for diarrhoea, dysentery-pthisis, haemorrhage. Solution of it applied to bruises, erysipetalous, inflammations and ringworm. Infusion is employed as local application in leucorrhoea as subs for genuine gum kino from Pterocarpus marsupium Roxb.

For dyeing and tanning.

Bark - astringent, bitter, pungent, alternative, aphrodisiac and anthelmintic properties; useful in tumors, bleeding piles and ulcers; decoction in cold, cough, fever, various forms of hemorrhages, in menstrual disorders, preparation of tonics and elixis, may be anti-asthmatic.

Roots - in elephantiasis, night blindness and other defects of sight, cause temporary sterility in women, used as an aphrodisiac, analgesic and anthelmintic. Applied in sprue, piles, ulcers, tumors and diopsy.

Root fiber - country brushes, cordage, slow matches, for caulking boats.

Leaves - fodder, have astringent, tonic, diuretic and aphrodisiac properties, to cure boils, pimples and tumours haemorrhoids and externally given in flatulent colic, worms, rules. Shoot apex used by the Kani tribal women by Kerala to prevent contraceptions.

Leaves for platters, cups, native umbrellas, wrapping.
