HABIT & HABITAT
A large, rambling or erect herb or under shrub ,Climber over bushes, Up to 1.8 m. high, distributed all over India, ascending up to an altitude of c. 3,100 m. in the Himalaya.

Along slopes of ghats, cultivated in gardens, Dry Hills, evergreen forest, Forest hedges, Forests, Gardens, Hill forests, hills, moist deciduous forest, Mountains, Near streams, Potato fields, Rice fields, Road sides, Scrub forest, Secondary forest, Sugar cane fields, Tea gardens, tea/coffee/cardomom garden, Upper ghats, waste fields, waste land and Wetlands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves very variable, oblong-lanceolate to elliptic flowers white or pink or purplish red, in cyrnose, corymbose inflorescence. Ovary trigonous; style trfid above. Nuts pulpy & dull black.Perennial herbs or subshrubs; stems scandent or prostrate, with ascending lateral branches, usually glabrous. Leaves broadly ovate to ovate-oblong, (4-) 8-12 cm long, 3-6 cm wide, glabrous or with a few fleshy hairs on nerves of lower surface, apex acuminate, base truncate-cuneate, petioles 1.5-2 cm long, ocreae membranous, 10-20 mm long, glabrous, apex obliquely truncate. Flowers 10-20 in terminal, subglobose spikes, these cymose or paniculate, peduncles usually sparsely glandular pubescent; tepals white or rarely somewhat pinkish, 3-4 mm long, enlarging and becoming fleshy and dark purple or bluish in fruit. Nuts dull black, trigonous, 3-4 mm long
