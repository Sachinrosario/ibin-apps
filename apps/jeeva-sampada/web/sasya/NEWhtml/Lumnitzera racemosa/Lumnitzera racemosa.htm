Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Small handsome tree frequent in muddy water or sandy elevated fringe areas in the estuarine and back water mangroves, often in association with Ceriops tagal or forming pure stands. In India, this species occurs in the mangrove swamps along both the coasts from Sunderbans downwards and up to Maharashtra, but is not reported from Kutch and Saurashtra areas. It occurs in the tidal forests in Andaman and Nicobar Islands. Its geographical distribution extends from tropical E. Africa to N. Australia and Polynesia through India, Sri Lanka and S. E. Asia.


Availability : Tidal shores of India and Ceylon, Malaya.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A tall glabrous shrub or small tree. Leaves sessile, fleshy, crowded at the ends of the branches, oblanceolate or obovate, glabrous, shallowly crenate or entire, rounded or emarginate at the apex, tapering much towards the base; main nerves 3-5 pairs, indistinct. Flowers sessile, in short lax axillary spikes. Calyx tube fusiform, glabrous, teeth obtuse, ciliolate; bracteoles 2, adnate to the calyx tube, ovate, acute, one of them higher up the tube than the other. Petals white, oblong, subacute. Stamens 10, almost equalling the petals, the alternate filaments shorter. Fruit ovoid, glabrous, longitudinally striate when dry, crowned by the persistent calyx tube. White flowers are borne throughout the tree.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used: Bark

Uses:

Wood used as fuel. Wood used for piles, posts, wharves, decks, canoes, ship building, bridges, general construction, flooring, sleepers, furniture, cabinet work, axles of carts and tool handles. Wood used for extracting tannins. Useful as timber.

Miscellaneous Information

It is a mangrove. This tree is easily identified in the field from its reddish-brown bark of the stem, thick oblanceolate or obovate leaves crowding at the ends of branch-lets, white flowers and woody compressed fruits. Mangroves with seed dispersed fruit.

Wood: Brownish grey with rose like odour when fresh, hard, heavy, 54 lb./ c ft., fine- and straight grained, fine textured. 0.6% ash. Durable.
