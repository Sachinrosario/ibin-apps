 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used: 
Juice, Flower-buds, Plant.

Uses

  1. The milky juicy is used in Sind as an external application to tumours and swellings.
  2. Flower-buds are sweet, and are eaten raw or cooked, as a vegetable.
  3. The plant is used as fodder for goats and camels.
  4. Dry shrub is used as fuel.
