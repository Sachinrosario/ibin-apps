DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Erect; stem 4-angled, appressed pubescent. Leaves ovate, elliptic or oblong-lanceolate, 4-14 x 1.0-3.0 cm diam., acute to acuminate at apex, ciliate at margin, lateral nerves 6-8 pairs, tomentose. Flowers in terminal and axillary short, capituliform spikes; bracts lanceolate, 1.0-1.5 x 0.3 cm, acute at apex, membranous; bracteoles lanceolate,  c 1.0-2.0 cm long, ciliate at margin. Calyx nearly divided up to base; two outer segments obovate to ovate-oblong, 1.5-2.5 x 0.3-0.7 cm, membranous, ciliate, mucronate; inner two lanceolate, 0.6-0.8 x 0.2-0.3 cm, acuminate at apex, ciliate at margin. Corolla deep purple or pinkish purple, 3-4 cm across, softly pubescent outside; tube 4.0 cm long; lobes ovate-oblong, c 0.6-0.8 cm long, obtuse. Capsules ellipsoid, 1.0-1.8 x 0.2-0.4 cm, glabrous, 4-seeded. Seeds brown, silky hairy.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Axillary and terminal dense ovate spikes ; Flower: Bracteate, bracteolate, hermaphrodite, complete, regular; Calyx:  Calyx hairy at the base, outer sepals membranous, whitish, broadly lanceolate, long-acuminate, mucronate with sub-spinous bristle-tipped teeth on the, margin, the opposite sepals similar but running out into the marginal spines and raised reticulate venetion between the nerves ; Corolla:  Pubescent outside, blue, obovate-oblong, rounded; Androecium: Stamens 2, fertile with oblong 2-celled anthers, usually 2-staminodes occasionally containing a little pollen also present often a rudimentary; Gynoecium:  Ovary 2-ceeled, ovules 2 in each cell, style long, stigma bifid; Fruit: Capsule ellipsoid, acute at both the ends, 4-seeded; Seed: Orbicular, compressed, silky-hairy.

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : December-March. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
The plant is bitter, pungent. It is useful in ulcer, skin diseases, leucoderma, pain, itching, inflammation, bronchitis and diseases of teeth. The roots are used by the santal as remedy for cough.
