HABIT & HABITAT
A soboliferous palm of the river estuaries and tidal forests of the Subderbans, Chittagong, Burma and the Andamans.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Nypa is a palm with large pinnate leaves and an underground or submerged trunk. It can be found growing in brackish water at the mouths of major rivers. Both male and female flowers are yellow; the malein thick spikes, the female in a large head. The fruit grows on a thick stalk and is the size of a basketball.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Leaves, Fruit, Seeds, Fronds.

Uses

  1. The leaves are used for thatching houses, also for matting
  2. The inside of the fruit when young is edible; a Toddy is obtained from the spathe.
  3. Hats and Cigar cases are made from the fronds.
  4. The seeds might be used for vegetable ivory.
  5. The foliage when burnt yields a supply of salt which is used for various purposes.
