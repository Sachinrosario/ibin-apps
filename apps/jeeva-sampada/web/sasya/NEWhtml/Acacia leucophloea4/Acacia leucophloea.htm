DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

- Moderate sized, deciduous tree upto 3 m. in ht.
- Leaves bipinnate with spines.
- Flowers in yellow heads arranged in terminal panicles 30m long.
- Pod black, 10-20 cm long, narrow, legulate with 10-20 seeds.
- Bark is light yellowish grey to nearly white outside and light red inside, smooth and exfoliates in irregular scales.

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Bark


Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:
Bark fiber - for fishing nets and coarse cordage; preparation of spirits from palm juice and sugar, clarifying and flavouring agent; bark is bitter, astringent and cooling, and used in bronchitis and biliousness.
Wood - agriculural implements, oil mills, carts and cart wheels, for twinery, fuel.
Gum - used in indigenous medicine, demulcent, used as an emulsifying agent.
Young pods and seeds eaten.
Bark and seeds mixed with flour at time of scarcity.
