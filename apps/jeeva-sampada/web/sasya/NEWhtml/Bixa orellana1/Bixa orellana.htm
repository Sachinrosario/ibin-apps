Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Small handsome evergreen tree, native of tropical America, now widespread in tropics. Cultivated throughout India (for dye as well as an ornamental) and found wild especially in W. Ghats.
Availability :

Almost throughout India.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Common

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small, evergreen tree, 4-6 m tall. Leaves ovate, 7.5-18 x 5-12 cm, subcordate at base, acute or acuminate, glabrous or nearly so on both surfaces; petioles 4-8 cm long. flowers 3-5 cm across, in terminal, short panicles; pedicels stout, pubescent. Sepals broadly ovate or orbicular, 8-10 mm across. Petals white or pink, obovate, 1.2-1.5 cm long. Stamens many; anthers dehiscing by apical pores. Capsules ovoid, clothed with long soft prickles, reddish-brown, 2-valved. Seeds red, trigonous.
A large herb or small tree producing pink flowers, followed by spectacular scarlet seed capsules in large attractive bunches. Red Annota dye is produced from the seeds.

Other uses:
It is a wonder tree commonly called as lipstick tree. The seeds of the plants are covered with a bright orange to red pulp, from which a commercial colour material is extracted.
The seeds, which are dry, hard, bright red and have a characteristic smell are considered good for dyeing, they form 30-40 % of the total weight of fruits. The surrounding of the seeds contains a lipid dye that is soluble in grease and yields a bright yellow dye. The dye is obtained by macerating the pulp and brushing the seeds in hot water till it forms a suspension of dye. The remains of the seeds are removed. The liquid containing the dye is allowed to ferment for 7 to 8 days after which the dye settles down at the bottom. The dye known as annatto settles and it is dried and cut into cakes. The yield is 4.8 % to 6 % by weight of seeds. Another method of extraction is, the seeds are crushed and are extracted with soda ash solution. The extract is acidified and adding common salt and boiling coagulate the colouring matter. The coagulum is filter-pressed, washed and dried (Anon, 1985)
The seeds are boiled in a vessel of water with a small amount of turmeric paste and lime along with the yarn for three days. The yarn is then boiled in the solution washed off and dried. Repetition of the process deepens the colours. Treatments with copper sulphate improve the fastness for light. The quantity of dyes to be used is 50 % of the weight of the material to dyes. It is used for dyeing silk of various shades of yellow and orange; but colour is not permanent and is not much used.
The predominant use of annatto in the developed market is in the from of extracts by food industry.
The traditional major colouring application have been in hard cheese, butter, other dairy products and in margarine, fish products, salad dressings, confectionery, bakery products, ice creams, beverages, and snacks. It is also used for colouring silk, wool, cotton and other textiles. It was also used by milkmen to give a yellowish tinge to their butter, hence it was also known by the name of butter seed. The principal colouring agent is "bixin", which accounts for about 10 % of the dye and considered as non-carcinogenic.
The exclusion of many artificial colours in the Western market from the list permitted additives has further increased its value. The plant can be propagated by cutting of elite material possessing, a good combination of high seed yield and seed pigment contents available.
