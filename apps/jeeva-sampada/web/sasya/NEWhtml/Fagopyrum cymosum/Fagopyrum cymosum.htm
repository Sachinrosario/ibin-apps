Other Biological Details


HABIT & HABITAT: 
This species is believed to be the progenitor of cultivated buckwheats. It is a vigorous- growing perennial found commonly in he temperate Himalayas from Kashmir to Sikkim, at an altitude of 1215 to 3335 m. The plants are tall with perennial root- stocks.
BioticZone: Temperate Himalayas,
Misc: Khasia hills.
Country: India.
Zone: North East,
State: Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya and Nagaland.

Availability : 
Distributed in Temparate Himalaya 5,000-10,000 ft altitide, Khasia hills, 4,000-5,000 ft and in Yunnan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
The leaves are large, long – stalked and triangular; angles acute or obtuse, upper usually narrow and amplexicaule; petiole long and slender. The pedicelled white flowes are borne on long recurved branches of peduncled terminal and axillary cymes. The seed is ovate and triangular, more than twice as long as the persistant perianth, larger than that of cultivated species, 7-8 mm long 6 –7 mm wide. In the north western hills, this species is locally called ‘phaphru’ or ‘khurfa’, in Assam as ‘jarain’.
