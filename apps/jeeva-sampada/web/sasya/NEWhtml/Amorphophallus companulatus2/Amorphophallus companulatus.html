DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Inflorescence:  Cylindric, dense-flowered, male and female contiguous; Male inflorescence:  subturbinate, anther densely crowded, pale-yellow, pollen golden-yellow, Stamen 2-4, anthers sessile, cells oblong, opening by apical pore; Female inflorescence: Ovaries sessile, densely crowded, depressed globose, placentation parietal, style stout, ascending, purple, stigma large, 2-3 lobed ; Fruit: Berries red, obovoid, ; Seed: 2-3, large, obovoid. 

Floral and seed biology:

Flowering & Fruiting: May-June. 

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:

Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by birds.

GERMPLASM CHARACTERIZATION:

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:
Propagation:  Plant is propagated through seeds and vegetatively by corms.

Soil Type: Plant requires well drained loams, sandy loams, alluvial light  soils.

Irrigation: Plant is irrigated frequently, given once in week when there is no rains.One given immediately after planting, second three days after first. As crop matures lighter irrigations are required till the corm gets mature.

Nutrient requirements:

Harvesting: The crop maturein the period of 7-8 months after planting. Yellowing and drooping of the leaves are sign of maturity of the crop.

Miscellaneous Information
Other information:


Tubers of Amorphophallus campanulatus, was screened for inhibitory activities against amylase, trypsin and chymotrypsin. Amorphophallus tubers possessed antiprotease activity and partial retention of inhibition was also observed in the case of amylase inhibitor in Amorphophallus. (Prathibha et al, 1995).
