HABIT & HABITAT
A variable, aromatic, prostrate, evergreen shrub,10-25 cm. high, found in the Himalayas from Kashmir to Nepal at altitudes of 1,500-4,500 m.; also reported to be grown in the gardens of western India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES : 
A variable, aromatic, prostrate, evergreen shrub,10-25 cm. It is one of the most effective ground-covers, forming dense mats .Rootstock woody; leaves small, elliptic or oblong-ovate or lanceolate, 6-15 mm. long, sub sessile, glandular; flowers polygamous, in whorls, males larger; nut lets smooth.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used :
plant shoots leaves & seeds.

Uses

  1. The shoots are employed for flavoring 
  2. A non- alcoholic beverage is reported to be prepared from the plant shoots, leaves.

Harvesting Practices
Yield: In Himachal Pradesh, about 3,700 kg. herb is reported to be collected per year from Bias and Kangra valleys. Generally, it is collected during July-August.

Produce and Products
The leaves and floral tops yield a volatile oil, known as OIL OF WILD THYME. The yield of oil from fresh plants is c. 0.27 per cent, and from the dried plants up to 0.60 per cent. Three flavonoids, viz. scutellarein heteroside, luteolin-7-glucoside and apigenin, have been isolated. The floral tops and leaves yielded 0.75 per cent of ursolic acid. The nectar is rich in ascorbic acid (222 mg./lOO g.)

Chemistry
The oil is a pale-yellow liquid, with an agreeable odour, reminiscent of thyme, lemon and geranium. A sample of oil distilled from wild herb, growing in Punjab and Kashmir (yield 0.5%), had the following physico-chemical characteristics : sp. gr300/300:9269; MD >-4.8°; fr§, 1.4968; phenolic content (mainly carvacrol), 52.7%. In addition to carvacrol (isomer of thymol), the oil contained p-cymew (17%), y-terpinene and other terpenes (8%), terpenic alcohols (5%) and zmgiberene and other sesquiterpenes (4 %). The phenolic content of oil, distilled in Calabria, was found to be much lower (24%). An oil from Provence (France) contained 50-55 per cent carvacrol, 2-4 per cent thymol, and 1.5-3.0 per cent borneol. A chromatographic examination of the oil, obtained by steam-distillation of the fresh herb from Germany, revealed the presence of geraniol and its acetate, terpineol and its acetate, citronellal, borneol and its acetate, linalool and its acetate, nerolidol citral, cmeol, carvacrol, thymol, neryl acetate citro-nellol, esters of higher fatty acids and camphene How-ever, a recent investigation has shown that oil samples from plants growing wild in Kumaun (Uttar Pradesh) had mainly phenols, consisting predominantly of thymol oils distilled from non-flowering and flowering herbs(yield, 0.92 and 1.00%, respectively, on semi-dry wt.) had the following characteristics, respectively sp. gr.27-50,0.9387, 0.9503; n; 1.4823,-; acid val, 4 94'-; ester val., 18.64, -; and phenols, 52.00 (7007 of which is thymol), 59.47% (comprising 984% thymol)

Other Uses
  1. The plant is bitter, and is considered to possess antispasmodic, antiseptic, expectorant, carminative, anthelmintic and stimulant properties due to its content of volatile oil. 
  2. The leaves and floral shoots are employed the treatment of several diseases, such as suppression of urine and menstruation, catarrh, and convulsive and whooping-coughs; they are also used as a sedative in radiculalgia and epilepsy. 
  3. An infusion of the herb is stated to be useful in the treatment of itch and eruptions on fhe skin. The seeds are given as a vermifuge. The oil is also applied in toothache.

Economical Potential to the country

The oil has not attained any commercial importance because it contains very little thymol (but for Kumaun samples) the valuable constituent of the oil from T. vulgaris (q.v.). Carvacrol does not have the same delicacy as thymol and also does not crystallize. Since the oil has an agreeable odour, it could possibly be used as a cheap flavoring agent. It is also applied in toothache. Ethanolic extract of the herb is An ingredient of some of the hair-and scalp-lotions.
