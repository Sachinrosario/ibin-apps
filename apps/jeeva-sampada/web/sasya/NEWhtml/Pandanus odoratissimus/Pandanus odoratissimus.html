DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Shrubs, up to 6m high. Stem supported by aerial roots. Leaves ensiform, linear-lanceolate, 90-150 cm long, apex caudate acuminate, glaucous-green, marginal spines pointing forward, those on the midrib pointing  forwward and backward.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Male flowers : spadix with numerous, subsessile, cylindric spikes, 5-10 x 2.5-3.7 cm enclosed in long, white, fragrant, caudate- acuminate spathes. Female flowers: spadix solitary, c 5 cm in diam. Syncarpium oblong or globose, 15-25 x 15-25 cm, yellow or red. Drupes numerous, each consisting of 5-12 carpels.March - December.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In  Ayurveda  leaves are used in strangury and tumours, flowers are pungent; anthers are used in pruritus. Fruits are useful in urinary discharges. In Unani system of medicine leaves are useful in leprosy, small pox, syphlis, scabies, leucoderma, heart and brain diseases. Anthers are used in earche, headache, leucoderma, blood diseases. Oil cools and strengthen brain (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
