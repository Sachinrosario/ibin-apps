Taxonomic Details
Correct Name : Myrsine africana Linn.                                                               
Family :  Myrsinaceae
Synonyms : Myrsine glabra, Gaertn.; M. scabra, Gaertn
Common Names :
English: Cape Myrtle, African Boxwood
Punjab: Bebrang, Kakhum, Kokhuri, Gugul.

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:                                                    
 Evergreen shrub found in Afghanistan, the salt range and the outer Himalaya from Kashmir to Nepal, at altitudes from 1,000 to 8,500 feet.
Habitat: Forests, Open slopes, Salt Ranges and Shrubberies.        
BioticZone: Himalaya and West Temperate Himalaya.
Alitude : 0-8500 m                                                                            

Availability :                                                                                                        
Misc: Afghanistan to C.Nepal, Bagrigad - Wan and Kashmir to Nepal.
Country: Afghanistan, Africa, China and India.
Zone: Central and Northzone.
State: Himachal Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.
District: Chamba, Chamoli, Kinnaur, Kullu, Mandi and Simla.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A small evergreen shrub with white moderately hard wood which weighs 49 lb per cubic foot.Slow-growing, upright, leafy shrub to 4 ft (1.2 m) tall by 30 in (75 cm) wide. The aromatic foliage is 3/4 in (18 mm) long and glossy dark green, and the berries are 1/4 in (6 mm) across and pale blue in color.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used: 
Fruit, wood.

Uses

  1. The fruit is a powerful cathartic vermifuge and is also used in dropsy and colic.
  2. The gum is warm remedy and is considerably employed as a remedy for dysmenorrhoea.
  3. The fruit is said to be eaten by the poorer.
  4. The plant has been recommended as suitable for hedges.
  5. It is said to be alternative and is use with Liquorice for strengthening the body and preventing the effects of age.
