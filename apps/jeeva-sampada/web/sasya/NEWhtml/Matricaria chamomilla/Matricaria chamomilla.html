REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed  biology:
Inflorescence racemose ; Flowers arranged in small heads and in clusters ,head rather large nearly 2.5 cm across a terminal peduncles with 10-12 truncate white rays surrounding the central mound of tiny yellow flowers which are embedded in hollow conical receptacle, bracteate, hermaphrodite, pentamerous, epigynous ; Calyx represented by pappus; Corolla gamopetalous; Androecium stamens 5, epipetalous, alternating with corolla lobes, filaments free but anthers usually connate into the tube around the style, anthers dithecous, introrse and opening by longitudinal slits; Gynoecium bicarpellary, syncarpous, ovary inferior, unilocular with single ovule in each locule, stigma bifid; Fruit light coloured very small achenes with 3-5 ribs and no border at the top without pappus. 

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal: 
Plants are cross-pollinated by variety of insects and sometimes self-pollination is also seen. Seeds are dispersed by wind
Genetic variability:
 Plant is diploid with diploid chromosome number 2n=18.Tetraploids have also been produced which have larger flowers. Considerable variation have been reported among the strains regarding the height of the plant. Yield of flower and oil contents.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation:   Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: It prefers moist, moderately heavy soil that is well drained and rich in humus, however it can also be cultivated in medium loam and light soils. It can tolerate swide range of pH and can be cultivated profitably in moderately alkaline soils with high salt concentrations.

Nutrient requirements: Nitrogen has been found to give good response and trace elements like molybdenum and boron have been found to affect flower yield and oil contents.

Water management:   It grows under moist conditions and when cultivated as winter crop require 3-4 irrigations in between December-March. When cultivated in alkaline soils, the number of irrigations will be more in compare to normal soil.  

Harvesting period / Method: Flowers are harvested when fully open during April if crop is planted during November-December and in temperate areas it starts from August-September and continues up to October.
