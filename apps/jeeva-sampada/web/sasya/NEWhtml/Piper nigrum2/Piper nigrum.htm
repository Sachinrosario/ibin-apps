Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

 

South-west Indian, and tropical countries. Probably originated in the hills of south-west India. Also cultivated in warm and moist regions of the country.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A branching, clombing perennial shrub, mostly fond cultivated in the hot and moist parts of India, Ceylon and other tropical countries. Branches stout, trailing and rooting at the nodes; leaves entire, 12.5-17.5 by 5.0-12,5 cm.,very variable in breadth, sometimes glaucous beneath, base acute rounded or cordate, equal or unequal; flowers minute in spikes, usually dioecious, but often the female bears 2 anthers, and the male, a pistillode; fruiting spikes very variable in length and robustness, rachis glabrous; fruits* ovoid or globose, bright red when ripe; seeds usually globose, testa thin, albumin hard.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Repellent, Anti feedant, Insecticidal.

Insecticidal activity is due to the amides, pipericide, 
dihydropipericide and guineensine. Crude extract of the fruit has high insecticidal activity against Callasobruchus chinensis due to the action of these three amides. Other amides, such as pellitorine exhibit moderate activity moderate acitvity. These amides act as neutrotoxins. (Gbewonyo., 1993).

A black peper, is katu tikta, ushnaveerya, laghu, controls vitiated sleshma and vata; anthelmintic; useful i heart disease and as appetizer.

MISCELLANEOUS :
Dried fruits, known in the market as black pepper, are used as aromatic, stimulant and stomachic, in the treament of dyspepsia, flatulence, malarial fever, paraplegia and arthritis. They also find local application for sore-throat, piles and skin diseases.
