DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
A large evergreen tree Bark grey to dark brown, scaly, leaves clustered near ends of branches, linear-lanceolate, 7.5-12.5 cm x 2.5-4.5 cm, tapering towards base, glabrous when mature; flowers in dense clusters near ends of branches, many, small; calyx rusty pubescent; corolla tubular, fleshy, pale yellow;  aromatic, cauducous; berries ovoid, yellow when ripe; seeds usually 1-2 compressed, yellow when ripe and shining.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Bark-antidiabetics, astringent, emollient in gum troubles, tonsillitis, stomach ache of horses, bone fracture of cattle; root and bark in ulcers; flowers-tonic, fish-poison, demulcent, laxative, stimulant, anthelmintic, antidote for snake-bite, bechic, in eye diseases; bronchitis; seed oil-galactogenic, anticephalalgic, emetic, in skin diseases, piles, pneumonia, cold, bark, seed oil and gum-antirheumatic.

Pharmacology:

·        Analgesic effect of aqueous and alcoholic extract of Madhuka longifolia and elucidate its probable mechanism of action. Graded doses of both aqueous and alcoholic extract of M.longifolia (4.0 to 64.0 mg/kg, i.m. X 3 days) produced dose dependent analgesic effect in all the three nociceptive methods carried out either in rats or mice. The analgesic effect exhibited by both the extracts was not antagonized by naloxone in rats only. The analgesic effect exhibited by both aqueous and alcoholic extracts does not mediate through opioid receptors. (Chandra, 2001)

Miscellaneous Information

·         Inhibition of larval emergence from the egg-sacs of cyst nematodes was observed when they were dipped in extract of mahua for 24 and 48 hours. These organic amendments were also found to decrease the cyst population and increase the plant growth, characters. (Sobita and Gupta, 1995). MAPA 9702-0975.

·         Mahua oil 1% was tested against nymphs of Citrus blackfly. It exhibited low efficacy. (Katole et al, 1996). MAPA 9702-0963.

·         Mahua oil cakes gave higher biomass production than the nematicides and VAM + nematode treatments. The nematode population was much reduced in the oil cake treatments than the VAM + nematode treatments. The phosphorus content was high in the oil cake treatments than the nematicidal treatment. (Sankaranarayanan and Sundarbabu, 1997). MAPA 9803-1706.
