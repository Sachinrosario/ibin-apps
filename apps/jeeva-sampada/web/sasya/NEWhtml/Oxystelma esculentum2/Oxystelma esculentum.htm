DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Stems terete, internodes slender, 1.6-12 cm long and 0.5-1.5 mm in diam., glabrous. Latex milky-white. Leaves opposite, decussate, lamina 0.5-8.2 x 0.1-0.7 cm, oblong-linear to linear-lanceolate, acutely acuminate at apex, rounded or subacute at base, glabrous on both sides, ca 0.5 mm in diam.

Flowers lateral, in drooping cymes, usually two flowered; peduncles terete, 2.8-5.4 cm long, 1-1.5 mm in diam., glabrous; bracts 1.5-2 x 0.5 mm, linear-lanceolate, apex acute, margin ciliate, glabrous, deciduous; pedicels filiform, terete, 8-20 mm long, ca up to 1 mm in diam., glabrous. Calyx 5-lobed, divided nearly up to base, lobes 2.5-3.5 x 1.5 mm, ovate-lanceolate, copular, apex acute, glabrous, glandular within. Corolla sub-campanulate or saucer-shaped, 1.2-1.6 cm across; corolla-tube 1-1.1 cm long, papery, densely pubescent ring at the base, ca 2.5 cm wide; lobes five 6-7 x 7.5-10 mm, deltoid or triangular, apex acute, margin ciliate, glabrous. Corona of two types, outer corolline, uniseriate, fleshy, cup shaped, arising from the base of corolla-tube, ca 2 mm high, ca 6 mm across, pubescent at the base, undulate at apex; inner staminal, five lobed, erect, adnate to back of staminal column, ca 5 x 2 mm, ovate-lanceolate, apex acute, tip keeled incurved, basely inflated. Stamens five, ca 5 mm long; pollinia five, pollen masses solitary in each anther cell, yellow waxy, ca 1137.6 x 295.2 mm attached by light-brown caudicles ca 302.4 mm long and ca 244.8 mm wide. Gynostegium ca 5 mm long. Follicles usually single, ca 7.4 x 1.6 cm, ovoid-lanceolate, curved tapering to the point at apex; seeds many, ca 3 x 2 mm, ovate or sub-orbicular, light-brown margin; ca 0.5 mm wide; coma silky dull-white, 2 cm long.
