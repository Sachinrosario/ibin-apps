Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Native of eastern Mediterranean region. Plant introduced 400 years back in Kashmir. Majestic tree cultivated in Western Himalayas. Cultivated chiefly in North West Himalayas from Sutlej westwards at altitudes of 1,200-2,400 m. Tree preferes moist deep well-drained soil and grows best along springs or streams. It is quite frost hardy.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Short trunk and a round spreading crown. Bark greyish, exfoliating in flakes. Very large deeply cut, pointed, palmately lobed, triangular leaves. Leaves turn golden yellow to flaming red in late autumn. Flowers monoecious, unisexual, greenish long, stalked, in globular clusters. Fruits densely closed with long fine hair. Seeds are downy.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:


Breeding system and varietal improvement:

Phenology:

 Fruiting is during June - July.

Leaf fall in October - November. New leaves appear in April. Flowering takes place in April - May.


Pollinators:


Floral and seed biology:

Seed Germination & Propagation: Propagation by cutting, layering, nursery-raised seedlings. Cuttings are planted about 10-12 cm deep. Seed propagation is also common. But cuttings are preferable.

Seeds, mixed with sand, loam and water and spread in well-raised nursery beds of fine soil containing considerable sand. The year-old seedlings are preferred for transplanting. Larger plants can also be transplantred with difficulty.
