Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Large, handsome, evergreen tree found in tropical regions with high rainfall areas of Western Ghats, West Bengal and Assam (wet tropical). Tree occurs in the outer Himalayas from Nepal eastwards to Assam, Khasi hills and Burma, ascending to 3,000 ft., sometimes to 5,000 ft. Suitable for growing on open lands. Epiphytic in earlier stages. Aerial roots numerous, often buttressed and fluted in the trunk. Their depth several feet while their thickness only few inches. Copious adventitious roots from the branches which grow downwards and enter the soil.

It is particularly common in damp valleys and on the sides of gorges and moist hill slopes. It is found typically in warm steamy localities, though it extends to altitudes where frost is not uncommon.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Large, handsome, glabrous, evergreen usually epiphytic tree with greyish or reddish brown, fairly smooth but scurfy bark. Dense crown of dark green foliage and spreading branches, aerial roots numerous, often much buttressed and fluted in the trunk. Leaves large coriaceous, elliptic, shining closely parallel-nerved with prominent midrib, 5-10" long, with pink stipules reaching sometimes 6" long.

Seed Germination & Propagation:

The tree may be propagated either by seeds or by cuttings and layers. Propagation by transplanting nursery-raised seedlings is preferred, as plants so growns are found to be more hardy and to produce aerial roots prolifically.

The time for sowing in Assam is June-September, during the rains. Germiantion takes a week or two and seedlings, when 2-3 in. high, are pricked out c. 1 ft. X 1 ft. in nursery lines, on raised and well drained beds. When 1-2 ft. high, seedlings are transplanted in stockaded nurseries (as protection against deer and wild elephants) and left until the plants atatin a height of 10-12 ft. (about 3 years) beforeplanting on mounds, with a spacing of 30-50 ft. each way. In Assam, plantations are formed by clearing lines in existing forests and planting seedlings or rooted cuttings at intervals.

Grown by entire transplanting or by branch cuttings.

Other uses:

Tapping - Stem and main branches are tapped for latex, which is made into rubber. But resin content is high and the rubber hardens on aging. It may be used in admixture with Hevea rubber for the preparation of soles and other articles in which block rubber is used.

Fodder - Tree is considered to be a good source of fodder.

Miscellaneous Information

Plant starts its life as an epiphyte, germinating on the branches of the tall forest trees, grows slowly at first, but soon sends down aerial roots, and when these have taken root, begins to make rapid progress. Eventually the tree stands on its own and attains a height of 29-58 m.

Figs are eagerly sought after by birds and the bulk of them are devoured on the tree. Trees suffer damage from elephants and deer. Large tree with buttresses, usually grown as juvenile in houses as an ornamental plant.
It is an important source of rubber.
