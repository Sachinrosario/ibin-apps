DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves elliptic-lanceo- late or vateoblong, regularly or irregularly serrate- dentate ; flowers white, fragrant, in axillary racemes ; fruit a small globose berry, c. 3.0 mm. diam., pinkish white, succulent when ripe.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Leaves ,Fruits , Stem, Bark.

Uses
  1. The leaves of the plant are used in curries in North Kanara. 
  2. The fruits are edible and are considered anti hermitic.

Other Uses
  1. Both leaves and fruits are used as fish poison ; 
  2. Extracts of branchlets, leaves, bark and stem exhibit insecticidal activity. 
  3. The wood of the plant is soft; it is used for house posts and as fuel.
