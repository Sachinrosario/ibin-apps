Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Semideciduous tree occurs in Andaman Islands, but not very common. It also occurs in eastern part of India particularly in Assam.


Availability : Very large quantities are available from the east zone particularly from Assam.


Conservation/ Threat Status :Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Semideciduous tree. Bark dark grey, rather rough, flaking off irregularly; inner bark light brownish-yellow.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel: Wood an excellent fuel. 

Wood used for constructional purposes. Also used for heavy packing cases and for rough furniture. It can be used for railway sleepers after treatment. It yields light timber. It absorbs preservatives. Much used for slippers and heavy construction.

 

Parts Used: Bark


Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:

Wood yields a large amount of oleo-resin and Gurjam balasam.


Chemistry:

Calorific value of the wood : sapwood - 5,237 cal., 9,430 Btu; heartwood - 5,284 cal., 9,513 Btu.

Processing Techniques:


Other uses:

Tree is a source of Gurjum balsam - used in medicine.

Miscellaneous Information

 Wood: Sapwood and heartwood fairly well demarcated. Sapwood dirty white to greyish to pale yellowish-brown, heartwood pale red to reddish-brown, sometimes with orange tinge darkening to red on exposure occasionally with reddish streaks, often with gummy exudation on the end surface. Moderately hard; moderately heavy to heavy. Mostly moderately heavy. Average unit weight at 12 per cent moisture content 755 kg/m3. Specific gravity 0.59 – 0.91 air dry. Straight to interlocked-grained, coarse textured.
