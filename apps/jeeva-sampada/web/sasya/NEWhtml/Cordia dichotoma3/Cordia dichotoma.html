UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
5,7-Dimethoxytaxifolin-3-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside (fig. 1) isolated from roots and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

b-sitosterol from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Fruit kernel contained protein (31.5) and oil (46.3%); mixture of palmitic (15.85), stearic (6.5), archidic (1.85), behenic (0.95), oleic and linoleic acids (67.65) and other fatty acids (7.47%) also found in kernels. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

Hesperetin-7-rhamnoside obtained from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

Lup-20(29)-ene-3-O-b-D-maltoside (fig. 2) isolated from roots and its structure elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.

New 3’,5-dihydroxy-4’-methoxy-flavanone-7-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside (fig. 3), m.p. 80°C, allantoin and b-sitosterol isolated from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)f.

Taxifolin-3-rhamnoside isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)g.

Tyaxifolin-3,5-dirhamnoside from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

3’,4’,5,7-tetrahydroxyflavoanone-3-O-b-D-xylopyranoside isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Lupa-20(29)-ene-3-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside isolated from root and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Glucose, xylose, rhamnose, glucuronic acid, arabinose, lactose, fructose, glycine, leucine, glutamic acid, cystine, alanine, threonine, aspartic acid and proline isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.

Macrophylline isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

b-sitosterol and a-linolenic, palmitic, linoleic and oleic acids identified in leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.


Pharmacology:
Tropical preparations for skin care or treatment contain extracts of Cordia dichotoma. The extracts showed rough skin-preventing, anti inflammatory, wound healing and other activities. (Nakada and Inoe, 1996). C.A. 1996, 125, 256789t.


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for anasarca (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Antihelminthic (Seed, Negi et al, 1979); Chest infection; lung disease (Fruit, Bhalla et al, 1982; Leaf, Sebastian and Bhandari, 1984); Cholera (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); cold (Leaf, Saxena and Vyas, 1983; Singh and Maheshwari, 1983; Maheshwari et al, 1980; Maheshwari et al, 1981; Chattopadhyay, 1982); Colic (Leaf, Sebastian and Bhandari, 1984); Constipation (Fruit, Roy and Chaturvedi, 1986); Cough (Leaf, Saxena and Vyas, 1983; Singh and Maheshwari, 1983; Maheshwari et al, 1980; Maheshwari et al, 1981; Chattopadhyay, 1982); Dropsy, dysentery, dyspepsia (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Headache (Leaf, Das et al, 1983); Jaundice (Leaf, Hemadri and Rao, 1984); Stomachache (Fruit, Sudhakar and Rolla, 1985; Leaf, Sebastian and Bhandari, 1984); On ulcer (Leaf, Das et al, 1983; Rao and Chaturvedi, 1986); Urinary disease (Fruit, Sudhakar and Rolla, 1985); Urticaria (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Edible (Fruit, Maheshwari and Singh, 1984; Das et al, 1983; Jain, 1963; Jain, 1963; Singh and Maheshwari, 1985; Negi et al, 1979; Maheshwari et al, 1981; Leaf, Jain, 1963); Vegetable (Fruit, Shah, 1984; Singh and Singh, 1981; Fruit, Jain, 1964; Jain, 1963; Sen et al, 1984; Nagar, 1985; Datar and Vartak, 1975; Flower, Singh and Maheshwari, 1985)
