HABIT & HABITAT
 A small genus of trees and shrubs, distributed in tropical Africa and south-east Asia to Australia. One species is found in India , in Sub Himalayan tracts, covering East zone, (Northern Districts, West Bengal )

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 A large shrub or a small tree with dark grey warty bark. Young parts rusty pubescent. Leaves pinnate, large, 1-2 ft. long; leaflets 8-12, short-stalked, elliptic or ovate-oblong, 2.5-7 in. long, often acuminate. Flowers pinkish or brownish in colour, sweet-scented, borne in clusters or in elongated spike-like racemes arranged in panicles. Fruits red to black, borne in big bunches, astringent and edible. The plant occurs in north-east India, Deccan Peninsula and in Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

Other Uses    

1.The wood is grey with pinkish brown heartwood and alternate wavy concentric bands of soft and hard texture, strong and durable. It does not split on drying. It is used for rice pounders and tool handles in Java.

2.An astringent decoction made from the roots is given in fevers and a decoction of the seeds is used for whooping cough.
