 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
A spreading or twining herbaceous annual, found almost throughout India ascending up to 4000 ft., usually in hedges, grasslands, waste places, bushes, fields and sea coast.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves deeply palmately 5-9 lobed, hairy; flowers funnel-shapel, white or pink, in axillary cymes ; capsules-ovoid, papery, glabrous; seeds 4, minutely velvety.

Chemistry
In nutritive value, the plant compares flavourable with legumes, being rich in protein, calcium and phosphorus. Analysis of the green feed and hay gave the following values.Green feed: crude protien,12.27; fat, 3.00; N-free extr., 48.21; fibre 2.23; ash, 10.29; calcium, 0.917; and Phosphorus 0.479%. Feeding trails on farm animals showed positive balances for nitrogen, calcium and Phosphorus.

Other Uses

1. The herb is used in the treatment of boils and carbuncles, and as an antidote to dog bites. 

2. The root is used as a purgative and is reported to contain a resin.
