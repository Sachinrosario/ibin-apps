 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
A large erect shrub with robust branches, found in western temperate Himalayan regions of Kashmir at altitudes of 1,000-2,000 m. This is a composite species of blackberries split up by some authors into a very large number of species. This is said to be a characteristic shrub of wastelands in Kashmir.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES :
Stems arched with short stout prickles; leaves with white stellate tomentum beneath; flowers pink; fruits small, with many black, more or less hemispherical fleshy drupes.
