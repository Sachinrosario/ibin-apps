DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
It is a climber.Finely pubescent,prostrate herb with ascending branches upto 45 cm high. Leaves alternate,simple,lower broadly ovate,upper ovate lanceolate,petiolate, serrate, acute,upto 2.5x1.3 cm; stipulous narrow ,acumilate. Flowers in axillary clusters or in cymose panicles; brachets narrow ,acuminate. Calyx 5, cupilla,lobe to the middle;lobes narrow,acuminate, upto 5 mm  long. Corolla 5, purplish coneate at base, lanceolate, acuminate, upto 8 mm long. Stamens 10. Fused into a staminal column at base, purplish; anthers ovoid . Ovary 5 celled peri;ovule solitary in each cell; styles cuneate at base, free above; stigma simple.Fruit a capsule globose,bristled,glochidiate; seeds black sligthly rugose ,ovoid.
