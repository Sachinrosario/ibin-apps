DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual herbs. Culms erect, 60-100 cm tall, tufted, fistular. Leaves linear-lanceolate, 12-50 x 0.5-1.2 cm, acute, membranous, minutely scaberulous on nerves above and along margins, glabous below
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Panicles 10-20 cm long. Spikelets ovate-oblong, 8-12 x 3 mm, laterally compressed, pale green. Glumes reduced. Lower lemma empty, scale like, 1-nerved; upper lemma 4-6 mm long, chartaceous, 5-nerved; awn c 5 cm long, retrosely barbed. Palea acute, 3-nerved. Caryopsis variable, red or dirty white.
