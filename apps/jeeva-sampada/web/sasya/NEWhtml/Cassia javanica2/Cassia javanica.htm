Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 A beautiful, small or medium-sized tree with widely spreading almost horizontal branches and showy blossoms; native to Java, introduced in to Indian gardens, found mainly in and around Central India; also planted for shade.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Bark smooth, brownish grey; leaves paripinnate, 15-30 cm, with crescent shaped 1.2-1.8 cm long stipules, leaflets 8-14 pairs, oblong, flowers bright rose or pink, fading to white, witj red sepals, in elongated bracteate corymbs; pods cylindrical, dark brown, smooth, 45-60 cm long, flesh dry.

Chemistry
Analysis of the seeds gave; moisture-7.8; protein-15.6; pentosan-10.5; and water-sol mucilage-25.3%; They contain an oil with the following fatty acid composition; palmitic-19.5; stearic-5.4; oleic-18.9; and linoleic-47.1%. The seeds contain chrysophanol, physcion, two new anthraquinones, 1,5-dihydroxy-4, 7-dimethoxy-2-methylanthraquinone-3-0-alpha-L-( - ) rhamnopyranoside (C23H24O11) and 1,3,6,7,8-pentahydroxy-4-methoxy-2-methylanthraquinone (C16H12O5) and a galactomannan. Presence of ( - )-epiafzelechin, piceatannol, chrysophanol, ceryl alcohol, hentriacontane, cerotic acid, beta-sitosterol and its D-glucoside is reported in the wood. The bark flowers, pods and leaves contain 17.9, 28.4, 24.5 and 10.6% respectively. The root bark contains quercetin and two new leucanthocyanins, leucocyanidin-3-0-alpha-L-rhamnopyranoside (C21H24O11) and leucopeonidin-3-0-alpha-L-(-)-rhamnopyranoside (C22H26O11). The leaves contain a new flavone rhamnoside, javanin (C22H22O10) besides kaempferol, its 3-methyl and 7-methyl ethers, kaempferol-3-rhamnoside, kaempferol-glucosyl-rhamnopyranoside, quercetin, beta-sitosterol, its seters, alpha and beta -amyrins, beta-amyrin palmitate, leucocyanidin, leucopelargonidin, rimuene, butyrospermone etc.

Other Uses

1.The wood is beautiful and used in Java for the construction of houses.

2.The seeds may be useful as a source of industrial gum. The seeds are also used in leis.

3.Medicinal Uses: The pods are used medicinally as a substitute for C. fistula. They are used as purgative. The saline extract of seeds showed complete haemagglutinating activity against the red blood cells of fowl. The extract also showed hemolytic reaction.
