Chemistry:
Auroxanthin, mutatoxanthin, phydalein, zeaxanthin and its cis-isomer, b-cryptoxanthin and b-carotene from calyx. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Glycoalkaloids detected in seeds but alkaloids were absent in fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

3a-tiglyoxytropane isolated from root. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Physalin A, mp. 266°C and physalin B isolated from var. francheti and their structures proposed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.

Structure of physalin A (fig. 1) and physalin B (fig. 2) elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Crystal structure of physalin A. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Tiglodine (3.0%), 3a-tigloyloxytropane (33.0%), cuscohygrine (20.0%) and tropine isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

A new withanolide – physalactone (fig. 3) isolated and characterized as 4b,17,20(R)-trihydroxy-3-methoxy-1-oxo-5b,6b-epoxy-22(R)with a-8(14), 24-dienolide. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

Reinvestigation showed physoxanthin to be identical to a-crytpoxanthin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.

Vitanolide and physalactone were isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

(3R, 3’R)- and (3S, 3’S)- and (3R, 3’S; meso)-zeaxanthin synthesized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

isolation and characterization of physalin L (fig. 4) along with physalins E and F. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

A cycloheptane bearing an amino and three hydroxyl groups was isolated from the roots of Physalis alkekengi var. francheti, along with calystegins A3, A5, B1, B2, and B3. By 1H- and 13C-NMR spectra, combined with extensive decoupling expts., 1H-13C COSY, and NOE enhancements, the structure of this cycloheptane was detd. to be 1b-amino-2a,3b,5b-trihydroxycycloheptane.  This compd. may be a precursor of calystegin A5 in the biosynthetic pathway or may occur from the enzymic redn. of the 5-aminocycloheptanone derived from the 1,5-bridge scission in calystegin A5. (Asano et al, 1996) C.A. 1996,125:81810.

steroidal constituent named physalin T (3) was isolated from the aqueous extract of Physalis alkekengi var. francheti. Based on 1H and 13C NMR spectral studies the structure was assigned as 2,3-dihydrophysalin D, i.e., 5alpha,6beta-dihydroxy-2,3,5,6-tetrahydrophysalin B,(Kawai et al, 2001)

The complete amino acid sequence of [2Fe-2S] ferredoxin from Physalis alkekengi var. francheti has been determined by automated Edman degradation of the entire Cm-protein and of the peptides obtained by trypsin and endoproteinase Asp-N digestions. This ferredoxin exhibited ten, ten, and nine differences respectively in the amino acid sequence, when compared with the ferredoxins of Datura stramonium, D. metel, and D. arborea, but 21-28 differences for other angiosperms, and 34-37 differences for fern and horsetails (Mino and Yasuda, 1998)
