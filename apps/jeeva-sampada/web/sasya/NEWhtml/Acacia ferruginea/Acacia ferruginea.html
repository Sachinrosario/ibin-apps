 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
A medium-sized tree found in Gujarat, parts of Andhra Pradesh, and moderately common in the Deccan Maharashtra, Karnataka and the Eastern slopes of Western Ghats, scattered in thorny forests.


Availability :  
Found in Gujarat, Berar and N. Circars and is fairly common in Deccan, Konkan/Carnatic and the eastern slopes of the Western Ghats. It is scattered in thorny forests. In Tamil Nadu, it is found in the districts of Coimbatore, Kanyakumari, Madurai, Nilgiri, South Arcot, Tirunelveli, Dharmapuri, Salem and Tiruchinapalli.

Status : Found in plains, forests & slopes.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Deciduous tree, 3.6-4.2 m in height. Bark cracking into pieces, wood olive-brown, very hard, branchlets  glabrous, armed with prickles. Leaves alternate, bipinnate,  7-10 cm long, petiole 2-3 cm with a gland near the middle, rachis terate, glabrous with glands opposite to the uppermost pinnae, stipular  thorns short, erect or hooked, pinnae 6cm long, 6-8 pairs, leaflets 15-25 pairs, oblong, glaucous, base oblique, margin sparsely ciliate, apex sub-acute, nerves prominent; flowers creamy-white in dense spikes, axillary, solitary or in pairs, peduncle 2-2.5 cm, bract small, caduceus, bracteolate, calyx 5-lobed, up to 1 mm, stamens numerous, 4 mm, basally connate, ovate stipulate, grey, lanceolate, glabrous, apex obtuse, nerved;  pods strap-shaped,  7.5-12.5 cm long, stalked, reticulately veined, glabrous, indehiscent, the upper suture narrowly winged; seeds 4-6, gray.

Other Uses
  1. Wood : This is very brown and very hard, used for agricultural implements and in wooden chariots. Wood is also used as posts and beams.
  2. In Ayurveda the astringent bark is considered useful for dysentery, itching, leucoderma, stomatitis, and diseases of the blood. Decoction of bark with that of the tamarind tree and a few other trees is used as gargle for sore mouth. Also used in curing itching, leucoderma, ‘kapha’ and ‘vatha’, ulcers and blood diseases. 
  3. In Unani medicine, the extract of the leaves is considered astringent, styptic and tonic; it is used to enrich blood and in the treatment of eye diseases, diarrhea, dysentery, gonorrhea and scalds and is thought to be beneficial  to the alimentary and urinary tracts. 
  4. Foliage : Leaves used in religious celebrations like Dussehra and as fodder for goats and cattle. Used also for treating diseases like diarrhoea, dysentery, gonorrhoea, burns and eye ailments. 
  5. Pods are eaten by goats.
