HABIT & HABITAT
Tubers depressed-globose, 20-25 cm in diam., dark brown. It is cultivated largely throughout the plains of India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves 30-90 cm broad; segments spreading, simple or forked; petiole stout, 60-9 cm long; leaflets obovate or oblong, 5.0-12.0 cm long, acute at apex. Peduncles short, stout, elongating in fruits. Spathes campanulate, 15-25 cm broad, greenish-pink externally with pale ocellated blotches, base purple within. Spadices as long as spathes. Male inflorescence sub-turbinate, 6.0-7.5 x 2-5 cm; female inflorescence c 7.5 cm long or more, c 6.2 cm in diam. Berries red, 2-3 seeded, obovoid.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Tubers are dry, acrid, and pungent. It increases both appetite and taste. It is useful in stomachic, constipation, piles, enlargement of spleen, tumours, asthma, bronchitis, vomiting, abdominal pain, blood diseases, and elephantiasis. It also causes itching sensation.
