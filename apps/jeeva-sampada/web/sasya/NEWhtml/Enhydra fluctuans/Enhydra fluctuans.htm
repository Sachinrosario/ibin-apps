Chemistry
The dry plant yields an essential oil (0.21%),stigmasterol (0.05%) and a minute quantity of a form. The air-dry commercial drug contains :moisture, 7.6; aq. extr, 22.6; ale. extr, 6.6; ash 17.8; silica, 7.4; and KC1, 1.5%. A major part (74-82%) of the total silica is soluble in water.The drug contains 3-methoxy pyridine, nicotine, palustrine, thymine, dimethyl sulphone,iso-quercitrin (quercitin 3 lucoside,C21H20O12.4H2O; mp220-21°), galuteolin (luteolin 5-glucoside,C21H20O11.3H2O ; m.p., 260-63°), equisetrin (campherol 7-diglucoside) equisetonin, acdnitic acid and lignin-like substances. Equisetonin is a light yellow amorphous saponin, yielding on hydrolysis, equisetogenin fructose and arabinose.
