HABIT & HABITAT
 Tree, 5-30 m tall, glabrous; It is found in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim, Assam, Orissa, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Tree, 5-30 m tall, glabrous; oleoresin aromatic; bark smooth, ash-coloured, exfoliating large, white flakes. Leaves variable, opposite, linear-lanceolate or oblong-lanceolate, acute or acuminate at apex, young ones red, shining above, glaucous or pruinose beneath, generally covered with waxy white powder beneath. Flowers axillary or terminal, white, 2.5-7.5 cm in diam., very fragrant. Sepals 4, orbicular. Petals 4, white with purple veins, obovate or obcordate. Stamens numerous, forming a yellow globose mass. Fruits 2.5-3.0 cm long, surrounded by enlarged sepals; pericarp semi-woody, 2-valved. Seeds 1-4, angular, smooth, chestnut- brown.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine the plant is used in fever, biliousness, scabies, skin eruptions, tumours, headache, blood and heart troubles, cough, hiccough, vomiting, dysentery and for bleeding piles. In Unani system of medicine the plant is used in asthma. It cures ulcers and piles.
