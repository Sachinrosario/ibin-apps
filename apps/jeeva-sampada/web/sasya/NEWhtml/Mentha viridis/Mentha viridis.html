HABIT & HABITAT
Perennial, pungent, herbs, 0.3-0.9 m high with aerial leafy stolons, found in Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan. Cultivated in gardens.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial, pungent, herbs, 0.3-0.9 m high with aerial leafy stolons. Leaves smooth, lanceolate-oblong, coarsely dentate, smooth above, glandular blow. Flowers dull violet, tinged with white, in loose, cylindrical, slender spikes.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:  

Floral and seed  biology:
Flowers are sharply pointed, long and narrow, actinomorphic, perfect, hermaphrodite and hypogynous; Calyx teeth are  hirsute; or glabrous, persistent, composed of 5 sepals which shows various degree of union; Corolla about 3mm long, gamopetalous, differentiated into tube and limb, bilipped; Androecium stamens 4,didynamous,inserted in the corolla  tube, filaments  free, anthers dithecous, introrse, dehisces lengthwise; Gynoecium bicarpellary, syncarpous, ovary superior, deeply four lobed, bilocular, two ovules in each locule, placentation. axile, style gynobasic, stigma bifid; Fruit four on seeded  nutlets. 

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal: 
Plants are pollinated by insects and persistent calyx help in dispersion of nutlets.
Genetic variability:
Chromosome number is 2n=48 has originated from hybridization between M. longifolia and M. sauaveolens.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through underground stems called stolons or runners.

Soil type: Plant can be cultivated in wide variety of soils but sandy loam soil which are rich in organic matter and are well drained as well as muck soil .It thrives well in neutral pH .

Nutrient management:-   Plant respond well to the application of fertilizers in soil of average fertility nitrogen at the rate of   20 kg/ha, potassium 40 kg/ha and phosphorous 60 kg/ha   is applied as the basal dose at the time of planting.

Water management: It is water-loving plant and requires frequent irrigations. In north India, crop requires 8-10 irrigations from January to June and 3-4 irrigations in Sept. and Oct.

 Harvesting period / Method: Plant is harvested just before flowering   initiation starts. Harvesting is done in clear sunny days.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:  Decoction of leaves is given in fever and brochitis and also used as lotion in aphthae. The herb is considered stimulant, carminative and antispasmodic.
