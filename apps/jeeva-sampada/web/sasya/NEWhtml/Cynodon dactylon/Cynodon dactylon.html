DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Weak perennial herbs. Culms terete, 5-40 cm tall, ascending, stoloniferous, rooting at nodes. Leaves narrow, linear, glabrous, glaucous; sheaths compressed, glabrous; ligules ciliate.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Inflorescence of 2-6 spikes, erect, spreadind; rachis slender. Spikelets sessile, 1.5-2.5 mm long, compressed, 1-flowered; lower glume narrowly oblong, 1.0-1.5 mm long, membranous, acute; upper glume 1.5-2.0 mm long. Lemma 2.0-2.5 mm long, acute, membranous, 3-nerved, silky pubescent on keels. Caryopsis obovoid, 1.2-1.5 mm long, turgid. It flowers throughout the year.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant juice is astringent and is used as an application to fresh cuts and wounds. It is also used in chronic diarrhoea and dysentery. The plant is diuretic and used in dropsy and anasarca. It is useful in catarrhal opthalmia.


Chemistry:
CIMAP: 
“Midiron” and “U3” bermudagrass were exposed to conditions known to induce cold accimilation [eg., 8/4˚ C (day/ night temp., 10 h photoperiod, 250 μmol m-2s-1) photosynthetic photon flux d. (PPFD)]. Tissues (leaves, crowns and roots) were harvested and stored frozen until processed. Polar member lipids were isolated by TLC, and fatty acids (FA) were sept. and quantified by gas chromatog. Different organs from the same plant responded differently  to low temp. Crowns showed the most dynamic changes in, total FA content and compn. and were the focus of this study. Overall, greater than 95% of the total FA content was accounted for by four FA species: palmatic acid (16: 0), stearic acid (18: 0), linoleic acid (18: 2) and linoleac acid (18: 3). Midiron (relatively cold-tolerant) responded more rapidly and to a greater extent than did U3 (relatively cold sensitive) as illustrated by nearly four-fold increase of unsatd. FA: satd. FA ratio for midron vs. U3, and by the significant difference between the double bond index of the two genotypes. Thus, specific desaturase enzymes (eg., W-3 and W-6) are of fundamental importance in controlling membrane lipid/FA compn. in response to low temp. and ultemately in avoiding the winter damage suffererd by bermudagrass along its northern boundry of adaptation1.
Isolation of 2-(3'-methoxy-4'-hydroxyphenyl)propionic acid, 3-methoxy-4-hydroxy- benzoic acid, 4-hydroxybenzoic acid, 2-(4'-hydroxyphenyl)propionic acid, phytol, sitosterol and stigmasterol acetate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
