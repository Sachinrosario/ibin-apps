Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
It is found in open areas and shady places and also on Khasia and Nilghiri mountains.
BioticZone: Eastern Plateau, Himalaya, Peninsular India and Western Ghats.

State: Arunachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Meghalaya and Tamil Nadu.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
It is a robust form and bears a large strawberry, globose in form but inclined to be conoidal in the Nilgiris and flattened in the Khasia hills; is of a pale pinkish-white colour.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY

 Breeding System :
The flowers are hermaphrodite (have both male and female organs).

Phenology :
The flowering takes place in early summer (01).

Pollinators:
This is known to be pollinated by Insects.

Seed Biology :

Seed Germination:
Seed - sow early spring in a greenhouse. The seed can take 4 weeks or more to germinate. The seedlings are very small and slow-growing at first, but then grow rapidly. Prick them out into individual pots when they are large enough to handle and plant them out during the summer. Division of runners, preferably done in July/August in order to allow the plants to become established for the following years crop [200].
