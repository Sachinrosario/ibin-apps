HABIT & HABITAT:
 Perennial herb with a woody rootstock. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat. Pakistan, Nepal, China.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Stems many from the rootstock, 12-30 cm high, rigidly flexous, scabrid. Leaves sessile, oblong or ovate-oblong, 3-5 cm long, obtuse at apex, rounded at base, denticulate along margin, often scbrulous above. Heads yellow, terminal, 1 or few, hemispheric, 1.2-3.5 cm in diam. Involucral bracts few; outer leafy, inner linear or linear-lanceolate, acute, sometimes acuminate, rigid, pubescent. Ray florets c 7.5 cm long; ligules shorter. Achenes slender, up to 2.5 mm long, silky. Pappus palereddish.
