DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Inflorescence: Consisting of open paired, outward coiled branches with flowers on the upper side which open progressively from the base to the apex of each branch;  Calyx: Sepals 5, fused in to tube below, tipple with purple; Corolla: Tube without scales on the throat but ring of hairs at the base, 5-lobed,  Androecium: Stamens 5 attached to the corolla tube ; Gynoecium: Ovary superior, with 2 fused carpels with one ovule in one chamber, style simple; Fruit: 4 nutlets; 

Floral and seed biology:

Flowering & Fruiting: May-July. 


Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:

Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

·         Two caffeic acid tetramers (I fig. 6a and II fig. 6b) isolated from roots, shown to have enantiomeric1,2-dihydro-6,7-dihydroxy-1-(3’,4’-dihydroxyphenyl) naphthalene-2,3-dicarboxylic acid (caffeic and dimmer) moieties linked to two molecules of 3-(3’,4’-dihydroxyphenyl)-(R)-lactic acid (I) was found to be identical with rabdosiin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

·         Isolation of deoxyshikonin, b,b-dimethylacrylshikonin, acetylshikonin, tetracrylshikonin, shikonin and b-hydroxyisovaleryl shikonin from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

·         Three phenolics, arnebinol, skikonofuran and de-O-methyllasiodiplodin isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.

·         Isolation of two quinines- arnebinone and arnebifuranone- from roots and their structure elucidation. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.

·         Isolation of tormentic and 2a-hydroxyursolic acids. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.

·         O7- and O9- angeloyl retronecines isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)f.

·         Four anticomplementary polysaccharide-LR-2IId-1a, LR-2IId-1b,LR-2Ild-3a and LR-2IId-5a- consisting mainly of mannose, galactose, glucose and glucuronic acid in addition to rhamnose, fucose and arabinose isolated from the acidic polysaccharide fraction (LR-2) of roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)g.

·         Rabdosin is isolated from M. euchroma. (Myazaki and Kobayshi, 1995).

·         Monosodium and monopotassium salts of isomeric caffeic acid tetramers were isolated from A. euchroma as anti-HIV agents. Mixtures of potassium and disodium salts of a caffeic acid and tetramer and dipotassium and potassium-sodium salts of a caffeic acid tetramer glucoside were also isolated from the active fraction. (Kashiwada et al, 1995). MAPA 1995, 9505, 2976.

·         Detailed pharmacogonostical study of the root of Arnebia euchroma. The botanical and physicochemical parameters of both samples were quite similar through variations were observed in the number of vessels per unit area and percentage of arnebins. (Khatoon and Mehrotra, 2000).



Pharmacology:

·         Plant showed antipyretic and antimicrobial properties. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.

·         Acetylshikonin inhibited histamine- induced capillary permeability of rat paw oedema and cotton granuloma. It also showed anti-inflammatory activity in adrenalectomised rats. LD50 of acetylshikonin in mice was 22.75 mg/kg, i.p. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)g.

·         Shikonofurans B and C and de-O-methyllasioplodin exhibited prostaglandin biosynthesis inhibitory activity and it was greater than that of arnebinol and arnebinone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)h.

·         Arnebinol, shikonofuran and de-O-methyllasiodiplodin inhibited prostaglandin biosynthesis in vitro. Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)h.

·         Acetylshikonin, tetracrylshikonin, b,b-demethylacrylshikonin and shikonin inhibited aggregation of wached rabbit platelets caused by collagen, arachidonic acid, platelets-activating factor, HDP or thrombin, with IC50, 2.1mM, 2.8mM, 4.2mM and 10.7mM respectively. In rat aorta acetylshikonin and shikonin inhibited high potassium and norepinephrine- induced contraction while b,b- dimethylacrylshikonin and tetracrylshikonin potentiated phasic contraction caused by norepinphrine. Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)i.

·         Three compounds the mixtures of monosodium, monopotassium and disodium, dipotassium salts of caffeic acid tetramer glucosides possessed anti-HIV activity with EC50 values of 2.84 and 1.5 mg/ml. (Kashiwada et al, 1995). MAPA 1995, 9505, 2976.

·         Arnebia euchroma roots 5-14 with other contents was used in the oral liquids for treatment of psoriasis. (Lu et al, 1994). C.A. 1994, 123, 17896c.

·         Petroleum ether (60-80°C) extract of A. euchroma roots, was found active against K. penumoniae, S. aureus and B. subtilis while methanolic extract was effective against S. aureus and B. subtilis only. (Kaith and Kaur, 1995). MAPA 1995, 9504, 2399.

·         The petroleum ether, chloroform, alcoholic and aqueous extracts of Arnebia euchroma roots (500 mg/kg, orally each) were found to exhibit maximal edema inhibition (61.2%, 45%, 27.5% and 60% respectively) against carrageenin-induced rat-paw edema at 300min interval. (Kaith et al, 1996). MAPA 1997, 9705, 2837.).
