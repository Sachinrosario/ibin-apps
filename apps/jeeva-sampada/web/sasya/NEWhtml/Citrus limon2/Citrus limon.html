DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Large spiny shrubs, up to 3m high; branchlets glabrous; bark green. Leaves unifoliate; leaflet ovate or elliptic-oblong, 5.5-11.5 x 2-5 cm, subacute or obtuse at apex, obtuse to rounded at base, margin glandular-serrulate; petiole narrowly winged. 


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Flowers in axillary condensed racemes, bisexual, white, shortly pedicellate. Calyx urceolate; sepals suborbicular, glabrous. Petals ovate-oblong, 15-20 x 3-5 mm, obtuse, coriaceous, glabrous. Fruits ovoid-oblong or oblate, up to ca 6 cm across, yellow, mamillate; rind thin; pulp abundant, acid. Seeds ovoid, 5-10 x 5-6 mm, acute, small. It flowers between March and June.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
 Fruits are used in the preparation of lemonade, squash and sherbet. Fresh fruits are also used in various culinary preparations. Citric acid, pectin and lemon oil is by-product of the lemonade. Lemon oil is an effective remedy for scurvy, rheumatism, dysentery and diarrhoea. Frits are pickled. They are rich source of vitamin C, vitamin B1 and carotene.
