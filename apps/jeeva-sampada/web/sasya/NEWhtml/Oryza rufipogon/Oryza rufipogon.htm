HABIT & HABITAT
An annual growing in water or swampy places of Assam, West Bengal, Chota Nagpur, Madhya Pradesh, Orissa and eastern parts of Andhra Pradesh. Under this species are included the major pool of the wild rice populations which are often growing as a weed in the fields of cultivated rice on margins of tanks. It is widely distributed in tropics and subtropics of Asia, America and north Australia; its sporadic occurrence in West AB also known.  Generally it is found in Ditches, Low-lying areas, Marshy Areas, Marshy habitats, Marshy places, Ponds, pools, Reserve forests, Rice fields, Swampy places-ditches-canals, Weed in rice field and Wetlands.
