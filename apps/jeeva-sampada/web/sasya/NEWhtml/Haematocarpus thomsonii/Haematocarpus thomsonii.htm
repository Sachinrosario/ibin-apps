HABIT & HABITAT
 A large woody climber found in Sikkim and Khasi hills, up to an altitude of 3,000-4,000 ft.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves 3-6 in. long and 1.5-2.5 in. wide, Fruits smooth, sub-oblique, drupacious, 1.5-2 in. long and 1-1.3 in. wide, borne solitarily or in bunches of 2 to 4, on stout woody racemes up to 15 in. long.
