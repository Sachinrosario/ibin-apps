 Taxonomic Details
Correct Name : Commelina nudiflora                                                                 
Family : Commelinaceae  
Synonyms : Commelina diffusa
Common Names :
Marati:Gandolgi.
English:commelina, dayflower, wandering Jew, spiderwort.

 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
Can form dense mats that choke out other vegetation. In Hawai`i, "often forming a conspicuous part of ground cover in mesic valleys to disturbed mesic and wet forest and other disturbed sites, 5-1,220 m" (Wagner et al., 1999).  In Fiji, "from near sea level to 600 m in wet places in forest and in the open, on sunny stream banks, along trails, in cleared areas and in damp pastures, and along roadsides"  (Smith, 1979). To 1,500 m elevation in Papua New Guinea.
Availability : 
Goa,Karnataka,Kerala,Maharashtra,Tamil Nadu,Ceylon,Singapore.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Creeping herb producing short erect branches; leaves suboblong, up to 8 cm long and 4 cm wide, glabrous; spathe boat-shaped, to nearly 4 cm long; flowers bright blue, or rarely white" (Stone, 1970)."Annual herbs with fibrous root system; stems at first erect, later decumbent, rooting at the nodes, up to 4 dm long, glabrous or nearly so.  Leaves narrowly to broadly lanceolate, 3.5-11 cm long, 0.8-2 cm wide, upper surface glabrous to sparsely scabrous, lower surface glabrous, apex acute to acuminate, base rounded to cuneate, sheath 1-2 cm long, ciliate on the upper edge (hairs translucent or whitish), otherwise glabrous to sparsely puberulent.  Hypogeous flowers not present, spathes 1.5-2 cm long, margins not connate at base, gradually tapering to the acute or obtuse apex, peduncles usually exserted from spathe; petals blue, the upper 2 reniform, the lower one smaller, ca 6 mm long, saccate.  Seeds 5, the surface raised-reticulate
