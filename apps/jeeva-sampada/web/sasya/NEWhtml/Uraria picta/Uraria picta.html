HABIT & HABITAT
Perennial suffruticose herbs or undershrub, 40-80 cm tall, hairy. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Assam, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Daman & Diu, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
  Leaves imparipinnate, lower leaves 1-3 foliate, upper ones 5-9 foliate, gradually narrow or rhomboid at base, blotched with white and glabrous above, finely reticulate and pubscent beneath, 10-20 cm long, usually with yellowish or purplish green bands along nerves. Flowers in raceme, c 15 cm long, clothed with short hooked bristles, abruptly recurved at tip, violets, purple or red. Pods 8-10 mm long, glabrous, lead coloured, joints 3-6. Seeds shining white.
