HABIT & HABITAT
Twining, perennial herbs; roots many, fleshy. It is found throughout the plains of India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Stem elongate, glabrous or pubescent. Leaves ovate or elliptic- oblong, 4.5-9.5 x 2-5 cm, acute or acuminate at apex, usually cordate at base, glabrous above, more or less pubescent beneath; petioles 0.5-1.2 cm long. Flowers in umbellate cymes. Calyx coarsely hairy outside, divided almost to the base; segments lanceolate, 2.5 mm long, acute. Corolla greenish yellow or greenish purple, 5-6 mm long; lobes oblong, c 4 mm long, acute; corona gibbous below, abruptly narrowed at the apex. Follicles 7-10 cm long, tapering to a fine point at the apex, finely striate, glabrous. Seeds broadly ovate, 6-8 mm long; coma 2 cm long.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Dried leaves are emetic, diaphoretic and expectorent. It is useful in dysentery, catarrh and other affections in which ipecacuanha has been employed.


Chemistry:

·        Structure of tylophorine as 2,3,6,7-tetramethoxyphenanthro [9,10:6’,7’]indolizidine confirmed by synthesis. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1960- 1969)a

·        Structure of tylophorinine confirmed by synthesis. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1960- 1969)b

·        a-amyrin, tylophorine, kaemferol and quercetin from leaves. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1960- 1969)c

·        Three alkaloids A,B and C isolated; alkaloids B and C characterised as desmethyl- tylophorine and desmethyltylophorinine respectively. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)a

·        Crystal structure of tylophorinidine and relative stereochemistry of tylophorinine and tylophorinidine. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)b

·        (+)septicine and ( + )isotylocrebrine also isolated from fresh leaves. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)c

·        dehydro- tylophorine, anhydrodehydrotylophorinine and anhydrodehydrotylophorinidine isolated. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)d

·        Stereochemistry of tylophorinine and tylophorinidine . (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)e

·        Absolute configuration of tylophorine. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)f

·        Tylophorine and tylophorinine content in leaves is a function of plant growth phase and is hirghest during flowering period. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)g

·        g-fagarine and skimmianine isolated from roots and aerial parts. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)h

·        Synthesis of septicine and tylophorine. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1980- 1984)a

·        Tylophorinicine isolated from roots and characterized as 14-hydroxytylophorine (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1980- 1984)b

·        Enantio-selective synthesis of (+) tylophorine. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1985- 1989)a

·        Synthesis of (-)tylophorine (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1985- 1989)b

·        Isolation and structure determination of new alkaloids -tyloindicines A, B. C  and E in addition (+) 14-hydroxyisotylocrebrine, 4,6-desdimethylisotylocrebrine. Tylophorine. 6,demethyltylophorine, tylophorinidine and 5-hydroxy-O-methyltylophorinidine. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1990- 1994)a

·         Tetratriacontanol, octacosanyl octacosanoate, a- amyrin, stigmasterol and b-sitosterol isolated. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1990- 1994)b

·         Septicine and  tylophorine synthesized. . (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1990- 1994)c

·        Five new dihydrophenanthroindolizine alkaloids -tyloindicines F, G, H, I and J -and a benzocyclopent-substituted phenanthrene -tyloindane -isolated from aerial parts and their structures established: tylophorine also obtained . (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1990- 1994)d

Pharmacology:

·        Alcoholic extract and total alkaloids produced CNS depression in near lethal dose. Both produced myocardial depression and fall of blood pressure in relatively large doses. Extract produced non-specific relaxation of smooth muscles such as ileum, uterus and tracheal ring chain of guinea pigs, rats and dogs and antagonised contractile effects of histamine and acetylcholine. Total alkaloids (10 mg/kg, p.o.) prolonged the survival rate of skin homografts in rats. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)i.

·        Tylophorine caused CNS depression characterized by ptosis, sedation and decreased motor activity. it also potentiated pentobarbital sleeping time and effect of subanalgesic doses of morphine in rats. it also showed anti-inflammatory effects in rat hind paw oedema, granuloma pouch and cotton pellet  implantation tests. It had no cardiovascular effects in frogs or dogs . (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970- 1979)j

·        Tylophorine inhibited systemic anaphylaxis and primary and secondary responses of adjuvant-induced arthritis in rats. It showed significant inhibition of mast cell rupture induced   by diazoxide probably by interfering with production or degradation of c-AMP. (Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1980- 1984)c

·        Tylophora indica is used by the gounda tribals in Tripattur to treat victims of poisonous snakes cobara viper and Krait. (Masilamani et al  1995).9504-2690.

·        Tylophora indica is formed to be useful in curing timer both malignant as well as benign. ( Karnick et al., 1997) MAPA.9706-3437.

·        An antitumor activity screening study of tyloindicines, indicine (G, H and I) was carried out. These compounds exhibited up to 1000 folds range of different sensitivity phenanthroindolizidine alkaloid showed  an interesting of cytotoxic activity. (Ali et al., 2001).


Ethnobotany:

Medicine for asthma (Leaf , Sudhakar and Rolla, 1985); backache (Leaf + stem, Saxena, 1981); on wounds (whole plant, Chaudhuri et al., 1977).
