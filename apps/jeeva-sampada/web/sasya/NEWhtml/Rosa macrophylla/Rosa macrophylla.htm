 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT : 
An erect, deciduous shrub, up to 4.5 m. high, found in the temperate Himalayas from Punjab to Sikkim at altitudes of 1,200-3,600 m.
Habitat: Forests, Hill slopes and Shrubberies.
BioticZone: Hindukush himalaya and Temperate Himalayas.

Availability : 
Misc: Bhuna-Bistola, Deodi (Nanda devi), Ghangaria, Himtoli (Nanda devi), Milam glacier (Nanda devi) and Pakistan to S.W.China.
Country: India.
Zone: Central and North zone.
State: Himachal Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.
District: Chamba, Chamoli, Kinnaur, Kullu, Lahaul Spiti and Simla.
Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Flowers solitary or in corymbose clusters, 5-8 cm. across, pink, sweet-scented; fruits red and ovoid, c. 4 -5 cm. long. there are said to be two forms, one with prickles and the other lacking them.
