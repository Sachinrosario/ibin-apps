HABIT & HABITAT

A stout shrub occuring in the hilly parts of south India, Gujrat, Western Ghats, Deccan, Konkan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

shrub; branches stout, as thick as a goose-quill. Leaves opposite coriaceous, 5-8 by 1.5-2.5 in., linear-oblong, acuminate, entire or sinuate-subserrate, base acute; main nerves 15-20 pairs, spreading slender; petioles stout, eglandular.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers in axillary 1-sexual robust spikes.

Male Flowers: Spikes solitary, 4-5 in. long; rachis very stout; bracts small, broad, fleshy; bracteoles large, embracing the unexpanded flower. Sepals 3, membranous, with a broad cordate base, unequal, orbicular, irregularly toothed, the point of insertion small.

Female Flowers: Not seen.

Fruit immature 2/3 in. in diam., on a very short axillary raceme, 3-lobed, truncate at top; pedicels short, stout; style short, persistent.
