REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
   Inflorescence: Much branched very large panicles; Flower: Polygamous, complete, regular ; Calyx: Campanulate, 5-fid, valvate, clawed within ample, teeth ovate, pubescent; Corolla: Petals linear, inserted between the calyx teeth ;Androecium: Stamens 10, inserted on the tube of calyx, filaments hairy at the base ; Gynoecium: Ovary ovoid, narrowing into hairy style, stigma capitate, 5-lobed ; Fruit: Drupe black ; Seed: 1 .

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting :May-June. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.  
Genetic variability:

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:

Amentoflavone isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

A new alcohol-6-propyltetradecan-7-ol-isolated from leaves and stem bark and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Isolation of a- and b-amyrins, butyro-spermol and dammaradienol from resin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

Isolation and structure determination of new macrocyclic biphenyl ether – garuganin I (fig. 1a) from leaves and stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

Gargunin III (fig. 1b) isolated and its structure elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.

Stem bark extract showed coagulatory and spasmolytic activities. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Isolation of octanedioic acid-2-ethyl-bis(2-ethylhexyl)ester from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

A new euphane-type alcohol isolated from stem bark and characterised as 13a,14b,17a-lanosta-7,24-dien-1b,3b-diol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.

Z-isomer of garugamblin 1 synthesised which spontaneously isomerised to E isomer (garugamblin 1). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.

Three new diaryl heptanoids -garuganin IV (fig. 2), garuganin V (fig. 3) and garuganin VI (fig. 4) isolated from stem bark and their structures elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.

Isolation and structure determination of 13a,14b,17a-lanosta- 7 ,24-dien-3b, 11b,16a-triol from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.


Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for asthma, astringent (Leaf, Shah et al, 1981); Diabetes (Bark, Saxena et al, 1981); Dysentery (Fruit, Roy and Chaturvedi, 1986); Eye disease (Bark, Sebastian and Bhandari, 1984); Skin disease, venereal disease (Root, Megoneitso and Rao, 1983); Wounds (Bark, Saxena et al, 1981); Edible (Fruit, Shah, 1984; Maji and Sikdar, 1982; Bhandari, 1974; Vartak, 1981; Pal and Banerjee, 1971; Bhargava, 1981); Dye black (Bark, Vishnu Mittre et al, 1984; Borthakur, 1981); Fish poison (Bark, Joshi, 1986) .
