DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Stem slender, dichotomously branched, sub angula, glabrous, brown, fleshy, fibrous, with 4-winged internodes and a leaf opposed, persistent tendril at some nodes, aerial roots developing during the rainy season, leafless when old, 5-15 cm long; leaves cordate, broadly ovate or reniform, crenate-serrate, some times 3-7 lobed, glabrous, 2.5-7.5 cm x 3-9 cm; flowers small, greenish white, in short umbellate cymes; berries obovoid or globose, succulant, very acrid, pea sized, 1-seeded.

Chemistry
 Analysis of the air dried plant gave: moisture-13.1; protein-12.8; fat and wax-1.0; fibre-15.6; carbohydrates-36.6; mucilage and pectins-1.2 and ash-18.2%. The ash contains mostly carbonates and a to a smaller extent phosphates of sodium, potassium, magnesium and calcium.
The stem sontains two unsymmetric tetracyclic triterpenoids, onocer-7-ene-3alpha, 21 beta-diol (C30H52O2, mp 200-02°) and onocer-7-ene-3beta, 21 alpha-diol (C30H52O2, mp 233-34°) and two steroidal principles I ( C27H45O, mp 249-52°) and II (C23H41O, mp 136-38°). Presence of beta-sitosterol, gamma-amyrin and gamma-amyrone is also reported.

Other Uses

  1. The ash of the plant is used as a substitute for baking powder. The leaf extract shows anti-fungal activity. The stem an root yield a strong flbre.
  2. Folk Medicine: The stem is useful in piles and its juice is beneficial as an alternative in scurvey and irregular menstruation and in diseases of the ear and the nose bleeding. A paste of the stem is given in asthmaand may be useful for muscular pains, burns and wounds, bites of poisonous insects and for saddle sores of horses and camels. The powder of dry shoots is given in digestive troubles. A decoction of the shoots with dry ginger and black pepper is given for body pains. The infusion of the plant is anthelmintic. The extract of the plant exhibits cardiotonic and androgenic property. Alcoholic extract of the stem showed acivity against E. coli Castell. & Chalm. The aqueous extract of the plant given topically or by intra muscular injection hastens fracture healing by reducing the total convalescent period by 33% in experimental rats and dogs. It aids in recovery of the strength of the bones up to 90 % in 6 weeks. The drug has a deifnite influence both on the organic and mineral phase of fracture healing leading to quickest recovery in animals. The drug is useful not only in building up the chemical compositioon of the bone but also in bringing about its functional efficiency.
