Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

In the lower hill-forests of Eastern Himalayas, extending to Nagaland and Manipuar and in the evergreen hill forests of South India.
Availability :

Common found in the evergreen forests of W. ghats, chiefly on the hill slopes and in Sikkim, Duars of W. Bengal and Assam. Cultivated in Coorg and South Kanara district of Karnataka.
Conservation/ Threat Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

- Lofty, narmed, deciduous, buttressed tree, upto 60m in height and 8.1m in girth with a clear bole of 18-45 m.
- Bark fairly smooth, greyish brown.
- Leaves bipinnate with 30cm or more long rachis, leaflets 5-8 pair, oblong, elliptic-lanceolate or oblique.
- Flower scarlet, red or orange in simple, axillary, dense racemes;
- Pods long-stalked, flat, thin, 5-10 seeded.
- Seeds brown, obovate, oblique compressed.
