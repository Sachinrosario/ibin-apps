DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A moderate sized to large spreading tree with ovate, ovate-lanceolate or elliptic, dark green leaves; fruits red when ripe, 1-2 inches in diameter,sub-globose or pyriform, borne in large clusters on short leafless branches emerging from the trunk and the main branches.

Chemistry
The fruit contains the  following values: moisture, 13.6; albuminoids, 7.4; fat,5.6; carbohydrates,40.0,colouring matter,8.5; fibre,17.9;ash, 6.5 ; silica (SiO2),0.25; and phosphorus(P2O3),0.91%.
It is a good shade tree for coffee, Shed leaves afford a valuable mulch. Analysis of the leaves gave (air-dry basis) ; nitrogen, 0.915; phosphorus (P2O5),0.163; and lime (CaO),5.57%.

Other Uses

1. They may be dehydrated, ground into flour and taken with milk and sugar or used for preparing cold jelly.

2. It is astringent and a decoction of it is used as a wash for wounds.

3. The root is used in dysentery.

4. The leaves ground to powder and mixed with honey are given in bilious affections.

5. The fruits are astringent, stomachic and carminative.

6. the milky juice is administered in piles and diarrhoea.
