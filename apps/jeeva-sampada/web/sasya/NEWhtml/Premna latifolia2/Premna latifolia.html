HABIT & HABITAT
 Small tree, up to 8.2 m tall, trunk c 1.2 m girth. It is found to occur in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Assam, Tripura, Meghalaya, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Madhya Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves 5-17 cm long, entire, pubescent beneath or hairy both surfaces when young, often permanently pubescent on the nerves beneath, entire or rarely serrate, blackish on drying, nerves 7 pairs, reticulation distinct, petiole small. Flowers in 3-chotomous, corymbose panicles, 5.0-12.5 cm in diam., pubescent; bracts small, oblong or lanceolate, upper bracts deciduous. Calyx pubescent or strigose, 5-toothed, scarcely 2-lipped. Corolla 4-5 mm long, densely hairy at throat within, 2-lipped, upper lip oblong rounded, entire or emerginate, lower longer with 3-spreading, obtuse or rounded lobes. Drupes 6 mm in diam., depressed globes, seated on the saucer-shaped, sub entire calyx.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Leaves are used as diuretic and dropsy. Milk of the bark of is applied to boils and juice is given to cattle in colic.


Chemistry:

A new aromatic bisnorditerpene - premnolal (fig. 1) isolated and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
Novel diterpenes - nellionol (fig. 2), anhydronellionol (fig. 3a) and 5-dehydronellinol (fig. 3b) isolated from root bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
Four new hydroxy sandaracopimar-15-enes I, II, III and IV (fig. 4) isolated from bark and their structures elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.
Three new hydroxsanndaracopimar-15-encs (I-III, fig. 5a - 5c), a new bisnorditerpene -premnolal- and three new diterpcnes -nellionol, dehydronellionol and anhydronellionol -iso]atcd from root bark along with sandaracopimar-15-en-8b-ol and b-silosterol; structures of new cornpounds elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
New furanoid -2,4-di(3',4'- methylenedioxyphenyloxy)furan (premnaIatin, fig. 11) -isolated from leaves together with b-sitosterol and its glucoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
Confirmation of structures of premnolal, nellionol tetraacctate, the sandaracopimarenol derivatives (IV, fig. 6, V, fig. 7a and VI, fig. 7c) and a sandaracopimarantriol (VII, fig. 8) by 13C-NMR study. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
Two new flavone glycosides isolated from leaves and identified as 5- hydroxy-4'- methoxyflavone- 7-O-arabinopyranosyl rhamnoside and 5-hydroxy-4'- methoxyflavone-7-O-trioside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.
Two iridoids-7-deoxyloganic acid (= bisdesoxydihydromonotropein) and geniposidic acid - isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.
New pimarene -sandaracopimar-15-en-1a,8b,11a-triol (VIII, fig. 9) -isolated from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.
Isolation of new sesquiterpene -premnaspiral (fig. 10b) along with prernnaspirodiene (fig. 10a) from root bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)g.
Absolute configuration of nellionol and 5-dehydronellionol determined by synthesis. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
