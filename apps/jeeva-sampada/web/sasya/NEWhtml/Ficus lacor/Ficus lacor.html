REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   Flower: Minute on the inner walls of the fleshy receptacles which is sessile or  in pair, axillary, globose, or pendulous, with broad basal bracts 3, coriaceous ; Male flower: Few sessile, globose, near the mouth of receptacles, sepals 4, lanceolate, stamens 1, anther broadly ovate, filaments short ; Gall and Fertile flower: sepals 4, lanceolate, style of fertile female flower short, stigma elongate ; Fruit: An enlarged hollow  receptacles, the inner wall studded with achenes ; Seed: Pendulous .
Floral and seed biology:

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:

Chemistry:  

GLC of surface hydrocarbons from fresh leaves showed expected preponderance of odd numbered (C25-33) alkanes which comprised 60.29% of total alkanes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
Leaf was contained nonsaponifiable matter (60.0%) which consisted of hydrocarbons (18.0), alcohols (30.0) and sterols (12.0%); alcohol fraction contained a- and b-amyrins (38.0) and lupeol (35.2%). GLC showed that hydrocarbon fraction contained mainly n-nonacosane (C29, 12.5), n-hentriacontane (C31, 29.5) and n-tritriacontane (C33, 19.1%). Sterols contained of sitosterol (77.6), stigmasterol (11.2) and campesterol (11.2%). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
Pharmacognostic studies of bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
Methyl ricinolate, b-sitosterol, lanosterol, caffeic acid and bergenin isolated from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
