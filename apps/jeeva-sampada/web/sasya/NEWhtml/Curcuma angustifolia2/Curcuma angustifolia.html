DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Rootstocks small, globose; tubers many, oblong, at the end of long fibres. Leaves lanceolate, 15-30 x 5.0-7.5 cm, green; petioles 30-45 cm long.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
  CIMAP:
Inflorescence: Dense vernal spikes; Flower: Bright yellow, Bracts - greenish-white, complete, hermaphrodite; Calyx: Obtusely 3-toothed, split down one side ; Corolla: Lobes oblong, acute, lip broadly ovate-suborbicular, more highly coloured than the lobes, yellow ; Androecium: only 1 perfect rest is reduced to staminodes,, anthers linear 2-celled ; Gynoecium: Ovary 3-celled ,inferior, ovules many, style slemder, stiga entire; Fruit: Capsule glabrous and globose ; Seed: Often arillate. Flowering & Fruiting : November. 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowers pale yellow in 7.5-15.0 x 5 cm long spikes; peduncles 15-30 cm long; flower bracts ovate, 2-2.5 cm long, green; bracts of coma few or many. Corolla tube 1.2-1.3 cm long; upper segments ovate, lateral shorter, oblong. Capsules globose, membranous. June - December.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
 
Genetic variability: The reported chromosome number - 42.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine it is used against leucoderma and blood diseases (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
Chemistry:
Detection of a- and b- pinenes (-)ar-curcumene, (+)camphor, (+)a-terpineol,(+)borneol and zingiberol in rhizome oil by GC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).
