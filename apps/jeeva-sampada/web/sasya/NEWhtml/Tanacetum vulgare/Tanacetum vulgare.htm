Chemistry:  

A new oxygenated sesquiterpene - tanavulgarol (fig. 1) isolated and its structure elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).
Following known lactones germacranolides tatridin A and B and the eudesmanolides santamarin, regnosin, 1-epi-ludovicin C, armefolin, tanacetin, 1b-hydroxyarbusalin and 3a-hydroxyreynosin were isolated along with new eudesmanolide was identified as 3-epi-armefolin. (Todorova and Ognyanov, 1999). C.A. 2000, 133, 235127a.
Effects of the chloroform extract of Tanacetum vulgare and one of its active principles, pathenolides, on experimental gastric ulcer in rats. (Tournier et al, 1999). C.A. 1999, 131, 13725s.
The essential oil from the aerial parts of Tanacetum vulgare was analyzed by GC/MS of the 20 constituents identified, 6 were monoterpenes hydrocarbons (3.9%), 10 oxygenated monterpenes (81.9%), 1 sesquiterpene hydrocarbon (1.7%), 2 phenolic compounds (3.0%) and a long-chain hydrocarbon (1.5%). The oil belongs to the thujone (66.8%) chemotype (+)-10hydroxy-3-thujone was characterized by NMR methods. 9Charles et al, 1999). C.A. 2000, 132, 313402g.
Crispolide, tatridin, tamirin, artemorin acetate abd tanacetol B were identified by HPLC in Tanacetum vulgare. (Belyaev et al, 1996). C.A. 1999, 130, 194365u.
The flavanoids of Tanacetum parthenium and Tanacetum vulgare and their anti-inflammatory properties. (Williams et al., 1999). C.A. 1999, 131, 142071w.
Anti-inflammatory active principles parathenolide and the methoxy flavones jaceosidin eupatorin, chrysoeriol and diosmetin were identified in aerial parts of Tanacetum vulgare. (Schinella et al, 1998). C.A. 1999, 130, 47251a.
Inflorescence accumulated more B, Sigma, V, Co, Cu, Mo, Cd, Pb, Cr and Ni. Leaves contained high levels of Mg, Al, P, Ca, Ti, Mn, Fe, Zn, Ga, Sr, Zr, Ag, Sn, La, Bi whereas stems differed by high level of Ba. (Popov, 1994). C.A. 1995, 123, 165149b.
Isolation and general characterization of polysaccharide as pectic substances and /or acidic arabinogalactans. (Polle et al, 1999). C.A. 2000, 133, 355045u.
