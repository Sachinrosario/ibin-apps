HABIT & HABITAT:
 Is a tufted annual found in temperate sub-tropical and tropical zones. It is seen all along banks of streams-sutis-canals-rivers, Along streams, Cultivated fields and Plains, Ditches, Gardens and Roadsides, grass land, Lakes, Marshy habitats, moist places, Moist situations, Open areas, Plains, Ponds, rice fields, Road sides, semi acquatic, stream, waste land, Water-logged soils and Wetland. With the advent of drought it becomes prostrate and  dies.                                                                                                      
BioticZone: Pantropical, West Coast and Western Ghats ,                   
Soil: It prefers moist clayey soils and Moist soils.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Tufted annual, culms 20-60 cm, tall, erect, ascending or decumbent at base, sometimes rooting at lower nodes, often reddish purple, sometimes swollen. Sheaths compressed, keeled, glabrous. Panicles usually 5-10 or occasionally 15 cm long, green or purple tinged.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
 Racemes several, 1-2 cm long, the lower ones up to 1cm apart but the upper ones crowded, rachis scaberulous. The flowers are hermaphrodite. Anthers about 1mm long, rather broad, pale yellow to reddish purple. Stigmas 0.7-1mm long, reddish-purple to dark purple, sparingly plumose, excerpted near apex of spikelet at anthesis.

Breeding System :
Phenology :
The flowering occurs throughout the year  depending on the regions and climatic conditions.

Pollinators: Flowers are pollinated by Wind.

Seed Biology :
Glumes unequal, hispidulous, ovate, acute: first glume 1.2-1.5 mm long, 3-nerved; second glume 2.5-3mm long, 7-nerved. Sterile lemma like second, with its glabrous ovate palea about 2mm long; fertile lemma about 2mm long, broadly ovate, glabrous, shining, coriaceous, rather indurate, with its included palea similar. Grain 1.7-2 mm long, broadly ovate, flat on one side, whitish.
Seed Germination:
Seed - sow early spring in a greenhouse and only just cover the seed. When they are large enough to handle, prick the seedlings out into individual pots and plant them out into their permanent positions in early summer. A sowing in situ in late spring might also succeed but is unlikely to ripen a crop of seed if the summer is cool and wet.


Cultivation:
Prefers a rich moist soil but should succeed in ordinary garden soil.
