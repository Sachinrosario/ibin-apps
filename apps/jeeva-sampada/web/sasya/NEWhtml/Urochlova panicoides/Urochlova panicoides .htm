 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
Annual species of 25-60 cm of height, with wide leaves and semipostrados stems, that they form a open mass. It grows in the roadside ditches of the highways, alluvial grounds and, like bad grass, in the fields of culture. 
In India it is found on roadsides, fields and pastures almost throughout the plains ; it is also cultivated for the grain and fodder in Gujarat.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
An indigenous, tufted annual, 30-120 cm. tall, with erect or geniculately ascending culms, frequenting.  Nodes slightly pubescent or glabrous; leaves lanceolate to linear-lanceolate from a wide or semi-amplexicaul base, soft, flat, pale or yellowish green, finely hirsute; spikelets 4-5 mm. long forming two regular rows, glumes unequal; caryopsis, rotundatel elliptic, much compressed, yellowish or green.
