DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Bushy shrubs; sap milky; spines simple or forked, straight, 1-3 cm long. Leaves ovate to elliptic, 1.5-4.5 x 1.0-2.5 cm, acute at apex and base, entire at margin, coriaceous. Flowers in terminal or axillary, few-flowered cymes. Calyx lobes c 1.5 mm long. Corolla white. Berries subglobose or somewhat ellipsoid, 6-8 mm long, green to purple, 2-seeded.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Inflorescence: Terminal few flowered corymbose chymes ; Flower: Complete, actinomorphic, regular, hermaphrodite, bracteate, pedicellate; Calyx: 5-partite, rarely glandular within, segments acute; Corolla:  White, salver-shaped, tube cylindric, usually dilated at the top, lobes 5 ;Androecium: Stamens at the top of the tube, included, anthers linear-oblong ; Gynoecium: Ovary 2 celled, ovules 3 in each cell, style filiform, stigma minutely 2-fid; Fruit: Globose berry ; Seed: 2, peltately attached to septum . 
   
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : January-April.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by  wind.
