Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A gregarious, herbaceous shrub growing abundantly near coast, Marshy Areas, Salt marshes and Salt Swamps.
BioticZone: East Coast and Western peninsular India.

Availability :  
Misc: Balsar, Chilka lake, Nausari, Porbandar(Kathiawar).
Country: Ceylon, India and Sri Lanka.
Zone: East, Islands, South and Westzone.
State: Andaman & Nicobar, Andhra Pradesh, Goa Daman Diu, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal,
District: Chengalpattu, Chingleput, Ganjam, Nellore, Ramnathapuram, South Arcot, Tanjaore, Thanjavur and Tirunelveli.
It is found to be distributed in Bengal, Kathiawar, Western peninsula nad Ceylon.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Shrubby, erect and branched. Flowers in spikes. Utricles membranous, ovoid, subacute. Seed is pale brown, hispid with white hairs.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Leaves, Young leaves.

Uses

  1. The ashes are used for the mange and the itch. They are considered as emmenagogue & abortive.
  2. It is source of alkaline earth, sajji, used in medicine & also in the arts.
  3. Leaves & young leaves are used to prepare pickles & in the times of scarcity utilise them as greens.
