HABIT & HABITAT

Large tree found in subtropical regions of East Indian hills and parts of Western Ghats.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A lofty tree with a spreading crown, up to 36 m in height and 1.8-2.4 m in girth. Bark grey, exfoliating in large papery flakes which often overhang, inner cortex reddish brown. Twigs dark purplish brown, strongly speckled white. Leaves ovate-lanceolate, coriaceous, glabrous above, dotted beneath.

 

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Fruiting spikes panicled, 7.5-10.0 cm long. Fruits scale-like with 2-membranous winged margin.
