Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A bark inhabiting lichen species usually grows on trunk of Alnus nepalensis, Cedrus deodara and Pinus sp. In moist situations it occurs rarely on mossy rocks, in open places between an altitude of 900 and 2800m.
Availability :

Outside India, it is distributed in Japan, Nepal, Taiwan and Thailand.In India, it is known from following localities:
Karnataka : Coorg, Bagmandala. Kerala : Thekadi. Maharashtra : Panhala.
Tamil Nadu : Ootacamund on Kottagiri road. Sikkim: East Sikkim, Gangtok, BSI compound; on way between Premlakha and Tenjabir; West Sikkim, near Bakhim.

Conservation/ Threat Status : Scarce

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Thallus foliose,loosely attached to substratum, ca 5 mm across; mineral grey; lobes irregularly branched, ± imbricate, subsrotund, 2-4mm wide upper surface, emaculate, isidiate; isidia laminal, cylindrical-branched; medulla white; lower surface black with dichotomousely branched black rhizinae (Divakar, 2001).
