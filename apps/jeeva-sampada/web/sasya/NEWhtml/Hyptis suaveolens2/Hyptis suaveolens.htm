HABIT & HABITAT
A rigid, sweetly aromatic herb, sometimes attaining a height of 7 ft., found in Deccan Peninsula , north-east India and Andaman and Nicobar Islands .

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves broadly ovate, very variable, 2.5-11 cm. long, sinuate-denticulate, tomentose ; flowers small, blue, in unilateral axillary or terminal clusters often arranged in panicles ; nutlets blackish brown, ovoid, com-pressed. The plant is also grown in gardens.

Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Shoot tops, Leaves

Uses

1.The shoot tops are edible. They are sometimes used for flavoring ; they are also used as an adulterant for patchouli leaves.
2.An infusion of the leaves is taken as a beverage.
3.In Java, the plant is used as cattle feed.
