Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A medium to large size evergreen tree, 15- 24 m tall with a girth of 1-2 m. It is distributed densely in Sunderbans of West Bengal, Tidal forests and Eastern part of India.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Uses:
It is amongst the good fuelwood of India. Used for posts, agricultural implements, wheal work, cart shafts and poles, boat building, tool handles, fence posts, fresh water piles, marine water piles, harbour work, mine works and pit props, oil-well sucker rods, railway keys and brake blocks, tent pegs, slipways and corded tracks in Sundarban region. Wood is a good source of charcoal.

Parts Used: Bark and seeds.

Miscellaneous Information

Wood: Wood diffused porous, growth rings indistinct to distinct, growth rings are 3-6 per cm., texture is even and medium to fine. Wood grain is almost straight to somewhat interlocked. Sapwood is pale - pinkish brown or buff, 5-9 cm wide, it is not sharply defined and sometimes gradually merges into the heartwood. Heartwood deep reddish brown with dark brown or purplish strips, it darkens with age. Wood is very hard, heavy to very heavy, extremely strong, specific gravity ranges from 0.84 to 1.20 (air dry), wood weight is around 993 kg per cu. meter at 12% moisture content, low durable in exposed positions, but durable under cover and in contact with water, average durability is 41 months.
