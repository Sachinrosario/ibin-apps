DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Tree grows to a height of 12-15 m, in India bear fruits in August and September. This massive tree has a thick trunk. Its drooping branches are covered with long leaves. These latter, entire and rather big, are pointed. The small flowers, greenish white, form sparse clusters ; male and female flowers appear on different trees. The fruits are ovoid some 10 cm in diameter with a thick woody rind. Internally they contain 10-16 black seeds embedded in the fruit pulp. Seeds are ovoid, irregular and angular, 1 to 1 1/4 inches long, 1 inch wide, skin smooth, grey, brittle; The seeds account for some 20% of the fruit weight. A typical tree produces 20 kg of seed/annum. The kernels make up 60-70% of the seed weight and contain 63% of pale yellow oil .

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Parts Used:
Seeds and Pulp of the fruit


Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:
True Chaulmoogra oil is extracted from the seeds.


Chemistry:
Gynocardia oil occurs as a solid fat of varying consistence, or as a brownish-yellow oil in warm weather, having a characteristic odour, a somewhat acrid taste, and an acid reaction. Specific gravity, 0.951 at 25% 0.940 at 45°; optical rotation, +52°; melting-point, 22° to 23°; acid value, 23.9; saponification value, 213; iodine value, 103.2. A mixture of 1 mil with 0.5 decimil (0.05 milliliters) of sulphuric acid acquires a reddish-brown colour, changing to olive-green.

Soluble in ether, chloroform, or carbon bisulphide; alcohol dissolves the greater part of the oil, repeated treatment with warm alcohol dissolving the remainder.

Processing Techniques:
The seeds require decortication prior to oil extraction. 
Other uses:
Chaulmoogra oil has traditionally been used for thousands of years in the treatment of leprosy. The expeller cake is a useful manure and is reported to ward off ants and other insect pests. It cannot be used for animal feed due to its toxicity. The oil has been shown to be highly active against fungal plant pathogens including Aspergillus niger and Rhizopus nigricans. Its used to heal chronic skin diseases, wounds and ulcers. Applied locally, it also relieves rheumatism and other pains. In England, it successfully treated starting lung tuberculosis. Chaulmugra oil is used both internally and externally in leprosy, secondary syphilis, rheumatism, scrofula, and in phthisis.
