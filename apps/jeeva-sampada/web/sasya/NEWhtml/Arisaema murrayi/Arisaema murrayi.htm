DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Corm hemispheric, 1-2.5 x 2-2.5 cm. cataphylls broad, oblong lanceolate to linear, mucronate, lowest white with others purplish, 4-26 cm long. Leaf solitary, comming up shortly peduncle but co-existent with the spadix and reaching higher than the spadix. Petiole 9-9.3 x 0.4-1.3 cm, cylindrical, greenish purple or green streaked with purple, sheathing ½ portion of peduncle, blade radiatisect; leaflets 7-9, dark green above paler shining below, sessile. Ovate lanceolate, acuminate or oblong lanceolate, cuneate at the base, with two intramarginal nerves, nerves depressed on the upper surface, prominent on lower surface; margin entire, wavy. Spadix monoecious or dioecious. Peduncle shorter or longer than petiole, 8.5-75 x 0.3-1.1 cm, green or purple, cylindrical. Spathe 5.3-12.5 cm long; tube grass green, 2.1-5 x 1.4-2.5 cm, cylindrical, slightly constricted at the top; limb 3.3-9.7 x 2-5.2 cm, broadly ovate-acuminate, hood like, incurved, sometimes at a right angle, pure white, purple at the throat at junction of tube and limb, veined. Spadix 4.4-9.2 cm, narrowed from base to apex; appendage 2.5-5 cm long, greenish at the base, purple above, slightly exerted, curved, tapering to fine point. Bisexual spadix with female flowers on basal 0.9-1.6 cm portion; ovaries many, in spirals, crowded, sessile; style short; stigma disc shaped, white; interspace 2-4 mm long; male flowers on 1.1-3.3 cm portion above interspace, 2-6 lobed yellowish cream coloured; anthers shortly stalked; neuters absent. Male spadix with basal 1.1-3.2 cm flower bearing potion; anthers 2-4 lobed, opening by a slit. Plants only with female spadix are not found. Fruit berry, 0.7 x 0.8 cm, 2-5 seeded.
