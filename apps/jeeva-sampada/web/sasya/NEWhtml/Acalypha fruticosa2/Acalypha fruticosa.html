DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Strong smelling shrubs, 1.2-1.4 m tall, more or less covered with yellow waxy glands. All young parts greyish puberulous. Leaves ovate to ovate-oblong, obtuse or sub-acute at apex, acute at base, crenate at margin, glabrous beneath, 3-5 nerved; petiole much shorter than blade. Flowers minute, greenish, clustered in sessile spikes arising from leaf scars; female flowers few at base of the spike or in separate axillary spikes; bracts rotundate in bud, broadly ovate at maturity, minutely greyish pubescent without. Ovary pubescent and glandular; style single, arms many, filiform. Capsules tomentose. Seeds smooth.
