Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for abdominal disease, (Part not mentioned, Shah et al, 1981); Earache (Whole plant, Sudhakar and Rolla, 1985); Fever (Leaf, Maheshwari et al, 1986); Gastric disease (Leaf +Stem, Rao and Neogi, 1980; Root, Krishna and Singh, 1987); Stomachache (Leaf + Stem, Rao and Neogi, 1980); Edible (Fruit, Lakashmanan et al, 1986; Singh and Singh, 1980; Ramchandaran and Nair, 1981; Singh et al, 1985; Maheshwari et al, 1986) .
