DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Erect annual herbs, 5-95 cm tall. Leaves linear-lanceolate, 15-30 x 0.3-2.5 cm, minutely scaberulous on both surfaces and along the margin.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Panicles 8-12 cm long, compact. Spikelets ellipsoid, 0.25 cm long; lower glumes ovate, c 0.05 cm long, 1-nerved; upper ovate, c 0.15 cm long, glabrous, rounded.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant is sweet, acrid, fattening, aphrodisiac. Sedative to the gravid uterus. It is useful in burning sensation in healing fractures. It is also used in externally in rheumatism (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).


Chemistry:  

Leaf wax contained a- and b-amyrins, free and as ester. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).
Two new C-glycosylflavones -scoparin-2"-O-xyloside (fig. 1a) and scoparin-2"-O-glucoside (fig. 1b) from leaves and their characterisation; orientin-(6''-O-(E)ferulyl)-2"-O-xyloside (fig. 1c), orientin-(O- (E)ferulyl)-2''-O-glucoside (fig. 1d), vitexin-(O-(E)ferulyl)-2"-O-xyloside (fig. 2a), vitexin-(O-(E)ferulyI)-2"-O-glucoside (fig. 2b) and vitexin-(O-(E)sinapyl)-2"-O-xyloside (fig. 2c) isolated and partly characterised; in addition, apigenin -7-(p-coumaryl)rutinoside, luteolin -7-rutinoside, chrysoeriol-7-glucoside and 7- rutinoside, tricin-7-glucoside and 7-rutinoside, vitexin, its 2"-O-xyloside and 2"-O-glucoside, orientin, its 2"-O-xyloside and 2"-O-g1ucoside, isoorientin and scoparin also obtained. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
Isolation of a new flavone glycoside - setaricin (fig. 4) from leaves and its structure determination. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
8,3'-dimethoxy-5,4'-dihydroxyflavone- 7-glucoside isolated from leaves and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
Leaves afforded a new coumarin - setarin (fig. 5) whose structure elucidated as 4-propenoxycoumarin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
A new polyhydroxylated steroid - setariol (fig. 3) isolated from leaves and characterized as 4, 14,24-destrimethy1- 7,20-dien-cyc1oeuphordane-3a, 14a,26-triol; b-sitosterol, b-amyrin and taraxerone also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.
Two new coumarins have been isolated from the leaves of Setaria italica. Their structures were elucidated as 6,7-dimethoxycoumarin and 5,8-dimethoxycoumarin on the basis of their spectral and chemical analysis. (Yadav and Jain, 1995) CA. 1997,123:280963.
