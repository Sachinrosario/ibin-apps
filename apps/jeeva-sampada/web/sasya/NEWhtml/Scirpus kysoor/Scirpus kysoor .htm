Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A weed common on the margins of tanks & river beds throughout India.It is found in fresh-water swamps, and in newly opened rice lands at low altitudes.  It also occurs in India to Indo-China and Malaya.
BioticZone: Konkan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
is a very coarse, large, erect, glabrous aquatic or marshy herb.  The stems are triangular, 1.5 to 2 meters high or more.  The leaves are few, basal, often half as long as the stem, 1 to 2 centimeters wide, the leaflike bracts subtending the inflorescence, spreading, broad, 30 to 60 centimeters long.  The inflorescence is corymbose, and 10 to 18 centimeters long.  The spikelets are very numerous, brown, ovoid, and 5 to 8 millimeters long.  The nuts are obovoid, trigonous, dark brown or black, and shining.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Roots.

Uses

  1. Roots have astringent properties & are given for diarrhoes and vomiting.
    Kesur is used to remove the taste of medicine from the mouth.
  2. The roots are dug up in large quantities in the cold weather, sliced & eaten uncooked. 
  3. They are sweet & starchy, & are considered cooling and highly nutritious.
