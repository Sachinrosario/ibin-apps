HABIT & HABITAT:
Creeping or prostrate herb with slender,it is found in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Andaman & Nicobar Islands..

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Creeping or prostrate herb with slender, filiform stem; roots arise from nodes, clothed with scattered soft hairs. Leaves orbicular to reniform, 0.5-2.6 x 0.8-3.2 cm, glabrous or slightly hairy. Flowers yellow, 1-3 flowered, axillary. Capsules globose, 0.3 x 0.4 cm, glabrous.
