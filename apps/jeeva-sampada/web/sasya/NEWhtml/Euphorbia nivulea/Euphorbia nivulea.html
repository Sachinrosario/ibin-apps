DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Armed shrub or small free. Stem and branches cylindrical, with pairs of stipular spines from the tubercles. Leaves crowded at the end of the branches, alternate, oblanceolate, obovate-oblong or spathulate, entire, sessile or subsessile, round fleshy, glabrous. Cyathia 3-nate, in short cymes, from axils of fallen leaves, central male and lateral 2 bisexual. Styles connate to the middle, stigma capitate. Cocci compressed, glabrous. Seeds smooth.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Solitary or twin cymes with sessile male appearing first in centre, the two lateral flower 2 sexual , pedicellate; Flower: Bracteoles fimbriate and numerous, combined in an inflorescence of many male florets and  female floret ; Male Flower: Stamens 40 in 5 bundles; Female Flower: Ovary 3-celled on ultimately exerted stalk in the center of  hemispheric and 3-nate involucre, ovules solitary in each cell, style 3, free; Fruit: Capsule broad styles combined in the middle, undivided ; Seed: Ovoid and smooth .

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
 In Ayurveda the plant is useful in biliousness, leprosy, leucorrhoea. The milk is alexiteric, carminative, useful in abdominal troubles, tumours. In Unani the juice is purgative, diuretic, carminative, useful in gonorrhoea, whooping cough, asthma, dropsy, enlargement of spleen, dyspepsia, jaundice, colic, tumours, leprosy, stones in bladder. The fresh milky juice is applied to warts and also used as a rubefacient, embrocating in rheumatism. It is a good alterative in syphilis and a good application in neuralgia. In Konkan it is given as a purge. Mixed with neem oil it is used in rheumatism. Warm juice is a good cure for earache. It is occasionally rubbed over the eyes to remove dimness of sight.
