Chemistry:

·        A new steroidal haemolytic and ichthyotoxic saponin isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

·        Glycosidic fraction from seeds on hydriolysis yielded egonol and a new benzofuran derivative – 2-(3,4-dimethoxyphenyl)-5-(3-hydroxypropyl)-7-methoxybenzofuran (fig. 1). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

·        Myristone, ginnone, n-nonacosane, n-octacosane, triaconatol, b-sitosterol and styracitol isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

·        Isolation of styracitol and sucrose from fruit peels. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

·        A new lignan – styraxin (fig. 2)– isolated and its structure elucidated as 2-exo-(3’,4’-methylenedioxyphenyl)-6-exo-(3’’-methoxy-4’’-hydroxyphenyl)-4-oxo-dioxabicyelo-(3,3,O)-octane. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

·        21-benzoylbarringtogenol C isolated and its structure determined.( Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)

·        Egonoglucoside (fig. 4a), egonolgentiobioside (fig. 4b), egonogentiotrioside (fig. 4c) and benzofuran glycoside (fig. 3) isolated from seeds and their structures determined. .( Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)

Pharmacology:

·        Aqueous and alcoholic extracts showed antitumor activity in vivo but toxic. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.
