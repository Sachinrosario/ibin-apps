Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:  
A medium -sized to large deciduous tree, usually with a short hole and large rounded crown, found throughout the greater part of India up to an altitude of 1, 200 m. it is found in mixed deciduous forests, usually of a somewhat dry type, often growing on rocky and sandy soiland thriving in Deccan trap. It is common throughout central India, Bombay, and Andhra Pradesh; it is also common in drier type of sal forests of in Madhya Padesh.

Altitude Minimum: 0,1300,
This is seen in the Cultivated fields and Plains, Deciduous forest, dry deciduous forest, forests and wastelands, Gardens and Roadsides, Hill forests and Slopes.
Biotic Zone: Western Ghats

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Bark dark coloured, cracked; leaves clustered near ends of bramches, elliptic or elliptic oblong, 7.5-23 cm. by 3.8-11.5 cm., coriaceous, pubescent, when ypung, almost glabrous when mature; flowers in dense pascicles near end of branches, many, small; calyx coriaceous; corolla tubular, fleshy cream coloured, with 1.5 cm long, greenish turning reddish yellow or orange when ripe; seeds 1-4 brown, ovoid, shining, 2.5-3.75 cm. long.
