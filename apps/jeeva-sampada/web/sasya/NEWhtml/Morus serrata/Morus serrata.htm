HABIT & HABITAT:
Morus serrata is the common mulberry of the Himalayas. It is frequently cultivated in hill stations and sometimes planted as a shade tree.
Habitat: Cultivated in gardens, Forests, Near temples, Shrubberies and Villages.
BioticZone: Himalaya, Temperate Himalayas, West Temperate Himalaya and Western himalayas.
Availability : 
Misc: Pakistan to C.Nepal and Sitapur.
Country: India and Nepal.
Zone: Central, North and Westzone.
State: Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh.
District: Chamba, Chamoli, Kinnaur, Kullu and Simla.
Taluk: Joshimath.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A large tree, 18-21 m. high, with a clean cylindrical bole 3.5 m. long, found from Trans-Indus to Kumaon, principally in the inner ranges of Himalayas at alti- tudes of 1,200-2,700 m. Bark reddish or grayish brown, smooth when young, rough with vertical fissures in old trees.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
Leaves broadly ovate-cordate, acuminate, often lobed ; flowers dioecious in spikes: male spikes, 2.5-5.0 cm. long, female much shorter ; fruit long, purple, sweet, edible.
