REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Cymes from the long peducles ,3-many flowered; Flower: Bracts ovate-lanceolate, monoecious, combined in an inflorescence of many male florets and female floret ; Male Flower: a stalked stamen without floral envelop ; Female Flower: Ovary 3-celled on ultimately exerted stalk in the center of axillary and campanulate involucre, ovules solitary in each cell, style 3, short, 2-fid, free; Fruit: Capsule obtusely keeled pubescent, of 3 two valved compressed cocci  ; Seed: Quadrangular, bluntly  pointed numerous furrows . 

Floral and seed biology:

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Throughout the year. Flowers are pollinated by insects.

UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:

Kaempferol, quercetin, its 3-rhamnoside and apigenin-7-glucoside detected by TLC; cosmosiin and rhamnetin-3-galactoside isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

b-amyrin acetate, b-sitosterol, campesterol, stigmasterol and cholesterol isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Rugosins A, B, D, E and G, tellimagrandins I and II, corilagin, geraniin, gallic acid and 1,2,3-tri-O-galloyl-D-glucose isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Three new ellagitannins – prostratins A, B, and C- isolated from leaves and their structures elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

Isolation of b-sitosterol, stigmasterol, cycloart-3b,25-diol, alanin, isoleucine, 2-aminobutyric acid, ornithine hydrochloride and threonine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
