HABIT & HABITAT
Procumbent, hispid herbs found throughout India. Common in open wastelands and prefers gravelly soil.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Procumbent, hispid herbs; branches 10-40 cm long. Leaves obovate, spathulate, oblong or elliptic, 1.0-4.5 x 0.5-1.8 cm, acute or obtuse at apex, narrowed at base, scabrid or ciliate at margin, pubescent or nearly glabrous, subsesile; stipules scarious with a few long thick bristles longer than sheath.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers 2-6 in a whorl within the stipular cup, sessile or subsessile; bracts lanceolate-subulate, hyaline. Calyx hispid, 4 mm long; tube narrowly campanulate, teeth as long as tube, linear-lanceolate, very acutely pointed, reflexed hairy. Corolla pale blue or white, 4-5 mm long; lobes oblong, 1-2 mm long, acute, bristly on back near the tip. Capsules ellipsoid, 3.5-5.0 mm long, rounded at apex, obtuse at base, crowned with calyx teeth. Seeds 2-3 mm long, dark brown or black.

Uses:
 Roots are used in antidotes to snake bites, antifertility, appetite, bleed in child birth, check miscarriage, gum troubles and toothache. Leaves are also useful in eye diseases, scabies, skin diseases, stomach complaints, ulcer and wounds.
