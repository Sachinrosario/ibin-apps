Synonyms : Brassica campestris ssp campestris var agrestis; Brassica colza; Brassica rapa ssp rapa,;
Crucifera rapa .
Common Names :
English: Coleseed, Colza, Cabbage, Navette, Swedish Turnip, Wild Navew .

 Miscellaneous Information
Cabbage early stage will be stored at temperature of 0.0-1.7 degree celcious and relative humidity of 92-95%. At these conditions, it can be stored for 4-6 days.
Cabbage late stage will be stored at temperature of 0.0-1.7 degree celcious and relative humidity of 92-95%. At these conditions, it can be stored for 12 days.
