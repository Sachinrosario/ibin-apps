Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Tree found throughout the Himalayas from Garhwal to Bhutan, up to an altitude of 1,200 m. Occurs conspicuously on Indian dry hills.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small tree, with 1.5-2.0 m long bole, coriaceous, elliptic-lanceolate acuminate, crenulate leaves.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Wood used as fuel. Charcoal is made from the wood.

Wood: Wood is straight grained, coarse and even-textured, light red or reddish-brown and moderately soft. Reported to be durable under cover.

It is used for making household and dairy articles like cups, bowls. Boxes and vessels.

Other uses:

The plant is a source of fibre.

Bark - Bark is medicinally important used against asthama, dysentery, ulcers, haemorrhoids and skin diseases.
