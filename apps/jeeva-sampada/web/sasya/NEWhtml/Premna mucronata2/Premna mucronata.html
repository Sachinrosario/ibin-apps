DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Small tree. Leaves broadly elliptic, ovate, oblong-elliptic or lanceolate, 7-12 x 4-5 cm, abruptly acute or shortly acuminate at apex, cuneate at base, entire, pubescent. Flowers in axillary and terminal corymbose, rustly tomentose. Calyx obscurely bilabiate. Corolla white. Drupes subglobose, 4-5 mm, across, fleshy, hardly verrucose; fruiting calyx c 2.5 x 6.0 mm, appressed pubescent outside.
