 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
A scandent thorny shrub found often in rivers and stream banks in the sub-Himalayan tract Kumaun, upper Gangetic plain, Bihar, Oi (Mayurbhanj), Madhya Pradesh, Northern Camatic, Deccan and western ghats, Banks of Streams, Coastal areas, Common, deciduous forests, dry mixed forests. Forests, Gardens and Roadsides, mixed forests, Open forest, Plains, Ravines, Road sides, Scrub forests and Semi evergreen forest.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Bark deeply cleft vertically ,leaves ovate-oblong, flowers small, white, in axillary racemes, fruit globose or ovoid drupe, yellow, fleshy with a crescent calyx. This climber is considered very destructive to trees, because of its rank growth.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
Flowers small, white, in axillary racemes, fruit globose or ovoid drupe, yellow, fleshy with a crescent calyx.

Other Uses
  1. In Malaya, a decoction of roots is given as a draught. 
  2. The bark is use Santals in anaemia during fevers. 
  3. The wood ( 608.76 kg./cu.m.) is yellowish white, soft and is not put to any industrial purpose.
