DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Coarse, tuberous twiners. Leaves variable; peduncles, pedicels and calyx hairy. Cymes few-many flowered. Corolla 2.8-5.8 cm long; tube 2-4 cm long, depressed inflated at base, funnel-shaped above; lobes 8-18 mm long, broadly oblong or oblong-obovate, hairy inside and along margins. Outer corona of 5 bifid, hairy lobes; inner linear, erect. Pollinia five, pollen masses solitary in each anther cell, yellow, waxy, 403.2-417.6 x 302.4-331.2 mm, with pellucid layer, attached by light-brown caudicels ca 115.2 mm long, ca 72 mm wide to dark-brown corpuscle 403.2-417.6 mm long and 273.6-288 mm wide.

UTILIZATION:
Ceropegia with beautiful fly-trap curiously variegated flowers of attractive architecture. The tubers are edible (Maheshwari & Singh, 1965).
