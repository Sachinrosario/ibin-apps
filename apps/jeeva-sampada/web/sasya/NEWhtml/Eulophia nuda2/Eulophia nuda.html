DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Pseudobulbs round, c 7 cm in dism., smooth. Leaves usually paired, elliptic-lanceolate, 25-40 x 4-8 cm, acuminate at paex, margin entire, narrowed into the long tubular sheath.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers 9-20, in lax racemes, fragrant; scapes 45-60 cm long, erect, stiff; bracts lanceolate, 6-20 mm long, acute; pedicels 2-3 cm long. Sepals linear-oblong, 1.0-2.2 x 0.2-0.5 cm, acute, inserted on the spur of the lip; spur very short, conical, obtuse, purplish green. Petals white, o blong, 1.0-1.6 x 0.4-0.7 cm, obtuse, many nerved; lip oblong, 1.5-2.2 x 1.0-1.6 cm, emarginated at apex, margin waxy, shorter than the sepal, pale green at base, white in the middle, pink at the tip. Column short with a long foot. Capsules fusiform, 3.8 cm long, conspicuously ribbed April - July
CIMAP:
   Inflorescence: Racemose; Flower: Large, green and purple, bracteate, hermaphrodite; Calyx: Free, linear, oblong-acute ; Corolla: Free, shorter, elliptic-oblong, many nerved ;Androecium: Pollinia 2-4 sessile; Gynoecium: Ovary inferior, 1-chambered ; Fruit: Capsule; Seed: Oblong.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 The tuber is an appetizer, hot. It is useful in tuberculosis glands in the neck, tumours and bronchitis (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
Chemistry:
n-Hexacosyl alcohol, a substance, mp. 82° and lupeol isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

Structure of eulophiol (fig. 1). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

Isolation of nudol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

9,10-dihydro-2,5-dimethoxyphenanthrene-1,7-diol isolated from tubers and its crystal structure determined; 9,10-dihydro-4-methoxyphenthrene-2,7-diol, 5,7-dimethoxyphenanthrene-2,6-diol, 1,5,7-trimethoxyphenanthrene-2,6-diol, 4,8,4’,8’-tetramethoxy[1,1’-biphenanthrene]-2,7,2’,7’-tetraol, 4-hydroxybenzyl alcohol also isolated from tubers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

Isolation and structure elucidation of two new phenanthrene derivatives –9,10-dihydro-1-(4’-hydroxybenzyl)-4,7-dimethoxyphenanthrene-2,8-diol (fig. 2a) and 1-(4’-hydroxybenzyl)-4,8-dimethoxyphenanthrene-2,7-diol (fig. 2b); 3,4’-dihydroxy-5,3’,5’-trimethoxybibenzyl and bis(4-hydroxybenzyl) ether also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
