 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
A deciduous shrub 30-45 cm. high with stems up to 1.3cm. diameter; Bark rough, fibrous; Western Himalaya from Kashmir to Kumaon and western Tibet between 2000 and 5000m. It grows commonly and abundantly in north-west Kashmir and covers vast areas of plateau lands and mountain slopes and in Gurez and Kistwar valleys. It is fairly common in Kulu valley and in Lahul and Spiti in Kangra district of Himachal pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Twigs striate, hoary of somewhat white-wooly; Leaves: 1.3-5 cm. long, 2 pinnatisect; segments many, small spreading linear, obtuse, more or less grey.  Petioles slender, up to 2.5 cm. long, much shorter towards the end of the shoots.  Heads homogamous 3-8 flowered.  Oblong or avoid, a little more than 2.5 mm;  Flowers: yellowish involucral bracts linear – oblong with scarious margins.  ; Corolla with a short cylindric tube and narrawly campanulate limb.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Axillary clusters of spike ; Flowers: arranged in small heads, yellowish to reddish heterogamous ,2-3 mm wide,globular,bract linear,oval-acuminate or oval,marginal flower are pistillate and central florets are hermaphrodite; Calyx absent ; Corolla of both the floret are tubular with the top split into 5 lobes in hermaphrodite florets and into 2-3 lobes in the pistillate florets ; Androecium stamens 5,epipetalous, filaments free,anthers usually connate into the tube around the style, anthers dithecous, introrse and opening by longitudinal slits ; Gynoecium bicarpellary, syncarpous, ovary inferior, unilocular with single ovule in each locule, stigma bifid ; Fruit    achene .  Plants are cross pollinated by variety of insects and wind action, sometimes self pollination is also seen. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Dried immature leaves and flower  heads constitute the drug.  It is collected when the flower heads have not fully matured, since the proportion of santonin is said to be maximum just before the flowers begin to open.  The drug is mainly used for expelling thread worms from the stomach.  It is also useful in fevers, dropsy and as a stimulant.
BSI: Seeds are bitter, hot, pungent with a sharp taste. It is stomachic, appetizer, aphrodisiac, anthelmintic. It cures abdominal pain and mucous diarrhoea.

Pharmacology:
Santonin showed significant anti-inflammatory activity in acute inflammation and significantly inhibited granuloma formation. Its antipyretic effect in mice was found to be independent of route of administration. Like morphine, it increased hot plate reaction time of treated mice. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.

Antioxidative activity of Artemisia maritima was studied. (Budincevic et al, 1995). C.A. 1995, 123, 202728a.

The hepatoprotective activity of the aqueous methanolic extract of A. maritima was investigated against acetaminophen and carbon tetrachloride (CCl4) – induced hepatic damage. (Janbaz and Gilani, 1995).


Ethnobotany:
Medicine anthelmitic, antiseptic, on cuts, gastric complaints (Whole plant, Gupta et, 1981); Blood purifier (Whole plant, Baruah and Sarma, 1987); Vegetable (Part not mentioned, Koelz, 1979); Leaf bad conductor of heat, filled in cushions, cover for fermentation pot (leaf, Koelz, 1979)
