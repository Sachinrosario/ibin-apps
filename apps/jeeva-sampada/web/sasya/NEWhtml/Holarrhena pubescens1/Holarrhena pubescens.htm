Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Holarrhena, also called the Ester Tree, is a deciduous shrub-like tree, which is quite common in the hilly tracts of India.
Availability :

Throughout India
Conservation/ Threat Status : Common

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Large shrubs or small trees, 5-10 m tall; bark pale brown; young parts pubescent. Leaves broadly elliptic-ovate, 5-16 x 4-10 cm, obtuse at base, entire, obtuse or acuminate, glabrous above, pubescent on the nerves beneath; petioles 2-3 mm long. Flowers in terminal, corymbose cymes; pedicels slender; bracts small, lanceolate, pubescent and ciliate. Corolla white, puberulous outside; tube 8-12 mm long; lobes oblong, rounded at apex. Stamens included; anthers free. Ovaries distinct; style filiform. Follicles linear, cylindric, 20-30 cm long, black, dotted with white spots. Seeds linear-oblong, 6-8 mm long, tipped with deciduous coma of brown hairs 2-2.5 cm long.An excellent small tree from North-east India, which has been immortalized by Rabindranath Tagore by an exclusive poem on it. Bears in a hot season corymbs of cream sweet scented flowers.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Ornamental and Medicinal shrub.

Parts Used:

Bark, leaves, flowers, seeds
The bark, leaves, flowers and seeds are used in medicinal. As the botanical name correctly suggests, the plant is widely used as a local medicine to cure dysentery and a large number of other ailments.
