Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Erect herb or a low shrubby annual found as a weed in rice fields or coming up gregariously in waste places. Quick growing. Native of tropical and subtropical areas. Grows well in tropics. It is well adapted to different soils, such as alkaline wastelands and wet waterlogged soils. Also tolerant of salinity and waterlogging.

It is highly resistant to drought and grows best in regions of rainfall from 550-1100 m.

Other uses:

Seeds - Seeds contain water-soluble gum producing a smooth, light-coloured, coherent elastic film useful for sizing textiles and paper products and for thickening and stabilizing solutions.

Stem - The stem provides fibre for cordage that has jute-like qualities and is useful for fishing nets, gunny sacks and sails.

Foliage - The foliage is a choice green manure for increasing soil fertility, especially on saline soils. Stem fibres are known as dundee.

Miscellaneous Information

Tree is used to provide wind-breaks, hedges, erosion control, shade and cover for crops. Source of guar.
