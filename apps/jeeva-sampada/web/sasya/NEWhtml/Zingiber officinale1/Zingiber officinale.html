DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Rhizome stout, tuberous, with erect leafy stems 0.6-1.2 m high. Leaves narrow, distichous, linear-lanceolate to lanceolate, 10-25 x 1.5-2.0 cm, acuminate at apex, margin entire, narrowed to a base, sessile.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers greenish with a small dark purples 15-30 cm long; bracts suborbicular, 1.5-2.5 cm long, cuspidate, green. Corolla segments lanceolate, unequal, 1-2 cm long. Lip shorter than corolla segments.
