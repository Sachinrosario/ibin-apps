DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Large, straggling shrubs or small trees, 7.5-12 m tall; spines in pairs on younger branches, one straight, other curved. Leaves broadly elliptic or orbicular, rarely ovate, 2.5-7.5 X 1.5-6.5 cm, apex obtuse with mucro, hairy beneath, basally 3-4-nerved.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers 4-6 mm across, yellowish green in dense, axillary dichotomous cymes. Calyx lobes glabrous within.. Petals 1.4-2 mm long. Drupes globose, 1.8-2.5 cm in dia., woody. Seeds 3, black.
