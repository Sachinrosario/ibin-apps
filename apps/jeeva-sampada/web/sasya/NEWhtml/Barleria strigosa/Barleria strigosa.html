DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herb or undershrub, up to 1 m high; stem and branches terete, fulvous-strigose. Leaves elliptic-lanceolate to broadly ovate, 7.5-20.0 x 4.0-9.5 cm, acuminate at apex, abruptly attenuate at base, entire at margin, membranous, sparsely ciliolulate, main lateral nerves 6-8 pairs; petioles c 1.2-3.8 cm long. Flowers in axillary and terminal, dense, unilateral, strigose spikes; bracts brown, ovate to lanceolate, 1-3 cm long, denticulate at margin, pubescent. Calyx densely strigose; outer segments ovate, 1.5-2.5 cm long, denticulate, ciliate at margin; inner smaller, linear, acute at apex. Corolla blue to pale violet, 3.5-5.0 cm long; lobes obovate-oblong, obtuse. Capsules oblong-ellipsoid, c 2 cm long, apiculate, glabrous, 4-seeded. Seeds flattened, silky hairy.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Terminal strigosely hairy spikes often crowded at the top of branches; Flower: Bracts foliaceous, oblong or oblong lanceolate, bracteoles narrowly linear, hairy on the back ; Calyx:  densely strigosely hairy, outer sepals herbaceous, subequal, elliptic-lanceolate, subacute, strigose with long acute hairs, inner sepals linear, acute, thick, densely clothed with white silky hairs; Corolla:  Blue, the tube paler than the limb, glabrous, tube infundibuliform in the upper part, lobes ovate oblong, obtuse ; Androecium:  Stamens 2 fertile and 2 staminodes, filaments of stamens and  staminode hairy at the very base; Gynoecium:  Ovary 2-ceeled, ovules 2 in each cell, style long, stigma bifid; Fruit: Capsule oblong-ellipsoid, acute, glabrous, 4-seeded; Seed:  Compressed, clothed with silky hairs. Flowering & Fruiting : November-March. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
