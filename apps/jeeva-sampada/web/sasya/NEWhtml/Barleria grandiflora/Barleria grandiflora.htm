Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Much-branched shrubs, unarmed; young branches glabrous. Leaves 13-18 x 3.5-5.5 cm, elliptic-lanceolate, apex acuminate base acutely tapering. Flowers white, solitary axillary or in terminal short spikes; bracts foliaceous. Corolla white; limb 2-lipped. Capsules glandular-pubescent in the upper half. Seeds 4, broadly ellipsoid, subobtuse, compressed.
