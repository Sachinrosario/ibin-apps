HABIT & HABITAT
An erect annual, 30-60 cm. high, with spreading puberulous branches, is found throughout the plains in India. A monotypic genus of annual herbs distributed in tropical Africa and Asia. DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Annual with leaves membranous, ovate, short pointed ; white woolly flowers, very minute, dense axillary spikes ; fruits minute, oblong, seeds dark brown, shining.
