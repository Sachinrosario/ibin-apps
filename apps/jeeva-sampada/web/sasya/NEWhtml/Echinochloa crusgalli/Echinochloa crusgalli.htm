DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES                                                               
Annual herb, up to 30 cm high. Stem slender, glabrous, smooth, leaves linear, narrow at the base, acuminate flat,margins scabrid; sheath panicle; rachis triangular,hairy. Spikelets crowded, ovate-elliptic,acute, awned. Lower glume membranous, broadly ovate,concave,longer then the lower.5-7 nerved.nerves with tubercles on the outer surface. Lemma similar to upper glume, flat, awned, 7-nerved. Palea elliptic,keeled. Stamens 3; anthers oblong. Grain eliptic.2x =36,42,48,54.

Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:Grass.

Uses:

1.A warm-season bunchgrass used as cattle fodder and sometimes cultivated for this purpose. Also suited for ensilage, but not for hay. Usually fed green. Grass also used for reclamation of saline and alkaline areas, especially in Egypt. Grain of some varieties used as food in times of scarcity and sometimes used for adulterating fennel.
