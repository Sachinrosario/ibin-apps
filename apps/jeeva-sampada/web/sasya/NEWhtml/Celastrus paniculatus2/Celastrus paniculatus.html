DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Scandent shrubs, up to 12 m long, without spines. Leaves obovate-orbicular, elliptic to ovate-oblong, 4-15 x 2-10 cm, acute at apex, cuneate at base, dentate at margin, glabrous above, hairy beneath; nerves 5-8 pairs, stalk up to 3 cm long.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flower unisexual, pale green in 6-8 cm long panicles. Calyx lobes 5, suborbicular. Petals 5, oblong to obovate-oblong, up to 3 mm long. Fruits subglobse, c 10 x 8 mm, Capsular, 3-6 seeded. Seeds ellipsoid, smooth, brownish.   

Terminal pyramidal panicles ; Flower: yellowish-greenish or white, unisexual in terminal, pedicillate,bracteolate   ; Calyx: pubescent outside, lobes semi-orbicular, ciliate; Corolla: oblong and rounded at the apex; Male Flower : Stamens inserted on the margins of the disk, filaments short, anthers oblong, rudimentary ovary small, sub-conical, slightly 3-toothed at the apex ; Female Flower:  Ovary globose, narrowed in to short stout style, glabrous, stigma large, 3-lobed,. Stamens inserted at the edge of the disk in the male flower , anthers small, without pollen, ovate, acute or sub-triangular; Fruit:Capsules subglobose, bright yellow, transversaly wrinkled, 3-valved, valves united at the base exposing seeds ; Seed: 1-6, often solitary, ovoid, cinnamon – brown, striate, completely enveloped in scarlet, fleshy aril .

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant are used in anaemia, carb, colic, diarrhoea, fever, syphilis and sores. Roots are also useful in antidote of snake bite and sex disorder. Bark are used in bone fracture, bronchitis and wounds. Fruits are also used in dysentery and skin diseases. Seeds yield oil, which is used in certain disorders of the stomach, bobyache, backache, cough, ecezema, leprosy and rheumatism.
Chemistry:
  • Isolation and crystal structure of malkanguniol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra1970-1979)a.
  • Isolation and structure  elucidation  of  a new sesquiterpene ester- malkangunin and of two new sesquiterpenoid tetra esters-celapanine and celapanigine .( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1970-19790)b.
  • A new triterpene diol- paniculatadiol –isolated along with β-amyrin,β-sitosterol and fattyacids from  seed oil ( Rastogi and  Mehrotra 1970-1997)c.
  • Extraction method of active polyester principle from seed oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1970-1979)d.
  • Zeylasterone and  zeylasteral identified in root bark of SriLankan plant.( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1985-1989)a.
  • Seed contained normal  triglycerides (20.2), polar triglycerides (44.4), polar nonglyceridic ester (23.5) and  nonpolar nonglyceridic esters (11.0) major acid  components on thes fraction  were: palmitic acid 25.1, 42.0,12.7, 58.2, 10.0 and  linolenic acid 3.0, 13.1, 43.0% respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1985-1989)b.
  • Root bark chloroform extract showed significant antimalarial activity against  Plasmodium falciparum in vitro. ( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)a.
  • An activite principle- pristimerin isolated from roots bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)b.
  • In addition , celastrol, zeylasterone and  zeylasteral isolated from  root bark .( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)c.
  • Two  new β-dihydroagarofuran sesquiterpene polyesters- 1b,6a, 8a,12-tetracetoxy-9β-benzoyloxy-β-dihydroagarofueran (I) and -1β,,8a,12-triacetoxy-9a-furancarboxy-β-dihydroagarofuran (II) isolated and  characterised .( Rastogi and Mehrotar 1990-1994)d.
  • Isolation and structure elucidation of another two new sesquiterpene polyester (III and IV) .( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)e.
  • A new sesquiterpenoid isolated from  seed oil and  characterised  as 1β, 6a-diacetoxy-9b-benzoyloxy-8β-cinnamoyloxy-β-dihydroagarogarofuran (V); 1β,6a,8β-triacetoxy-9a-(β-furancarbonyloxy) β-dihhydrogarofuran also isolated .( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)f.
  •  Seed  oil yielded four new β-dihydrograrofuran sesquiterpenoids (V,VII,VIII,IX) whose structure determined.( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)g.
  • 4,4-dimethyl-1,3-dioxan-5-ol isolated  from  seed  oil and its structure  established and  confirmed by synthesis. ( Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)h.

Pharmacology:
