Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
The  Siberian crab apple is a native of the region extending from Siberia to Manchuria and Japan. and southwards to the Himalayas. 
Habitat:    Forests.  
Biotic Zone:    Himalaya and Temperate Himalayas,
Zone:    North, North East and Southzone.
Altitude Minimum:    0-3600m.  
Availability : 
Misc:    Kashmir to Bhutan and Kodaikanal.
Country:    Asia, China, India, Japan, Mongolia, Siberia and Tibet.
State:    Assam, Himachal Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.
District:    Chamba, Kinnaur, Kullu, Lahaul Spiti, Palani, Simla and Sirmaur.  
Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A small round-headed tree with short trunk, up to 14m high, armed with simple or branched spines when old; leaves tomentose or glabrous serrulate; flowers white; fruits sub-glabrous, 1-3 cm. in diam., red, scarlet or yellowish green with a few white spots, borne on long slender pedicels.
