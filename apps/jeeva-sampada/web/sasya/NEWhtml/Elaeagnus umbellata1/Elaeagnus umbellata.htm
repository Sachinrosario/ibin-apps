 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
A much branched decidous shrub, .12 ft or  more in height , The plant is found in temperate Himalayas from Kashmir to Nepal ascending to 10,000 ft. and in Manipur.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Often spiny, with yellowish,brown, partially silvery branches.  Leaves oblong- lanceolate, silvery beneath.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
Fruits Flowers, Seeds.

Uses: 
  1. The fruit is edible and is pickled or eaten in curries.  The flowers are considered cardiac and astringent:  The seeds are used as stimulant in coughs and the expressed oil is used in pulmonary affections.  The wood is white, hard and even- grained, it warps on seasoning.   It is used asfuel (Kirt. & Basu, loc. cit.; Gamble, loc. cit.).

Harvesting Practices

Produce and Products
Oil, Cough medicine .
