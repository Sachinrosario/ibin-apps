Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A foliose lichen grows on twigs of shrubs in shady places between altitudes of 1500 and 1600 m in temperate regions. Sinha, 1994).
Availability :

The species shows restricted distribution in south east Asia. In India it is distributed as follows.
Arunachal Pradesh: West Siang district, Pugging-Gette track; Payum-Gassang foot track, Rego-Mechuka. foot track; West Kameng district, Kalaktang-Denji road; Tirap district, Namdapha, Burmanala; Nagaland: Kohima district, near Kohima Church college; Kohima-Wokha road, at 12 km point. West Bengal: Darjeeling district, Tiger hill (Type locality).

Conservation/ Threat Status : Scarce.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Thallus forms whitish grey, 2-3 cm patches, affixed centrally to the substratum. The suberect, irregularly branched, ecorticate, densely marginally rhizinate thallus lobes are up to 3 mm wide. The subterminal to terminal pedicillate, densely marginally black ciliate apothecia are upto 3mm in diameter. The spores are 36 - 44 x 23 -26 µm in size (Awasthi, 1988).
