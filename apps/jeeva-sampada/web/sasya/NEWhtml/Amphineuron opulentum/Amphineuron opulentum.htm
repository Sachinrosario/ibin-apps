Taxonomic Details

Name: Amphineuron opulentum (Kaulf) Holtt. in Blumea 23 (2): 212-214. 1977; Blumea 19:45.1971.

Family: Thelypteridaceae

Synonyms: Cyclosorus extensus (Moore) Ching.; Aspindium poulentum Kaulf.

Local Name: Ribo, Sal-dakuni

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Rhizome short creeping, stipe slender, erect, slightly rufescent, short hairy in grooves, basal scales ovate to broad lanceolate; lamina upto 1 met. Tall, simple, pinnate; sori confined to loes, rarely on veins, medial, indusiate, indusia thin, shrinked at maturity, bearing marginal glandular hairs and short acicular hairs on surface, sporangia selender stalked, stalks glandular hairy. (Baishya & Rao, 1992.)

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Subsitute for tea, an ingredient for fermenting local drink. (Jain 1991.) Fronds are used as substitute for tea (Saklani & Jain, 1991).  Apong a local drink is an indespensible item in the socio- cultural life of Adis.  The Frond is inverably used in the preparation of yeast cakes ( Das, A.K. 1997).
