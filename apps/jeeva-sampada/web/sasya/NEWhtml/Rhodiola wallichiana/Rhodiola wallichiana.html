DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Stout, erect. perennial, c 10-30 cm long, herb. Rootstock woody, thick. Stem succulant, branching crown like, glabrous branche base with numerous small crowdly leaves, slender, internodes very short, hollow. Leaves alternate, simple linear-lanceolate, 0.6-3.8 x 0.1 cm, acute at apex, attenute at base, entire along margin or distinctly toothed glabrous on both surfaces, lower surface slightly paler than upper sessile. Flowers bisexual in terminal cymose panicle. Calyx tube persistant, c 0.3 cm long; sepals 5, linear 0.2-0.25 cm long, half as long as the petals, acute at apex, glabrous, free corolla yellow or white red; petals 5, triangular or lanceolate, spathulate, 0.5 x 0.1 cm, acuminate at apex, flate and narrow at base. Stamens 8, filaments slender yellowish, c 0.4 cm long, anther bithecous, basifixed, carpels 5, superior, free, each 1 celled; styles as many as carpels; stigma obliquely truncate. Fruit folliclar, narrow, membranous, enclosed by withered calyx and corolla, dehiscence adaxyally. Seeds numerous, minute reticulate, ellipsoid, compressed.
