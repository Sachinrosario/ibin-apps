HABIT & HABITAT

Tree in arid western plains if India. Distributed on the banks of the Ganges at Mayaganj, Bhagalpur, Bihar and Orissa. Also in arid rocky tracks of Rajasthan, Khandesh, Deccan, Bellary and Mysore.

Floral and seed biology:


Seed Germination & Propagation: Tree is propagated through cuttings. Best time for plantation is in July. The plant is propagated by seeds. Seeds are scarified mechanically and kept under running water for 24 hours. One-month-old seedlings are transferred to plastic bags and after 2 years they are planted in field.
Successfully propagated through stem cuttings. The plant is also propagated by air-layering.

Other uses:

Leaves - Leaves are used as fodder.

Gum - Gum is medicinally important.

Oleoresin from the trunk, guggul, is important in local medicine, especially for cure of arthritis. It is also a common adulterant of myrrh, the African Bdellium. The gum resin is astringent, antiseptic, carminative and aphrodisiac.

Miscellaneous Information

 Slow growing but hardy tree on dry rock sites.

Multipurpose tree used in agroforestry and wasteland utilization.

Occasional hedge plant.
The tree is micropropagated through induced axillary branching.
