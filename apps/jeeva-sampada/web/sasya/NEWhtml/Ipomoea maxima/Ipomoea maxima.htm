HABIT & HABITAT:
A herbaceous slender, perennial twiner found almost throughout India in hedges, near streams and tanks.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves ovate-cordate, entire, acute; flowers large, funnel-shaped, pale purple, pink or white with a purple eye, in many-flowered, pedunculate, umbellate or sub umbellate, axillary cymes.
