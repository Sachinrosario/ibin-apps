HABIT & HABITAT:
Perennial, dioecious climber; roots tuberous; stems slender, branched, furrowed, glabrous and shining. It is found almost throughout India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Tendrils elongated, striate, glabrous. Leaves membranous, ovate, 3.5-10.0 x 2.5-8.0 cm, cordate at base, entire or 3-5 lobed, glabrous; lobes triangular, ovate or oblong, distantly denticulate; petioles 1.3-4.5 cm long, pubescent. Male flowers: solitary, 3.8-14.0 cm long; bracts cucullate, enclosing the flower, orbicular-reniform, pubescent on both surfaces, prominently nerved; calyx lobes linear-lanceolate, 3-7 mm long, acute, distant; petals yellow, oblong with black-grey veined on lower surfaces. Female flowers: peduncles as long as those of male flowers, with a small bracts near the base; ovary covered with soft papillae. Fruits ellipsoid, 2.5-6.3 cm long, beaked, with soft spines. Seeds pale yellow, ellipsoid, compressed, corrugated.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:  In Ayurveda system of medicine roots are used in head troubles, urinary calculi. Leaves are used in fever, asthma, bronchitis, hiccough, piles. Fruits cure asthma, leprosy, bronchitis, fever, tumours, urinary discharges, heart troubles and excessive salivation. Seeds are used in treatment of ulcers.
