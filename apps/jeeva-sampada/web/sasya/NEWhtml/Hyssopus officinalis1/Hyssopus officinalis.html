DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Herbs or undershrubs; stems branched below, branches woody, diffuse or erect, tetrangular with short internodes. Leaves simple, sessile or slightly petiolate, oblong-lanceolate, 1.0-2.0 x 0.1-0.4 cm, obtuse at apex, narrowed at base, entire at margin.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
  Flowers bluish-purple, in whorls of 6-15 axillary or terminal spikes. Nutlets narrow, smooth, triquetrous and it flowers and fruits between July to December.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Infusion of leaves is used in coughs, toothache, uterine or vesical affections, and induration of the liver or spleen. Leaves are stimulant, stomachic and useful in hysteria and colic, used as a poultice to eyes. A syrup made from sap of leaves with sugar and honey is used as a vermifuge for roundworms. Decoction of flowers is given in case of asthma and chronic bronchitis.


Chemistry:  

Myrtenol methyl ether (fig. 1a), methyl myrtenate (fig. 1b), cis-pinic acid, (+)2-hydroxyisopinocamphone (fig. 2), cis-pinomic acid along with b-pinene, pinocamphone and isopinocamphone from essential oil; fourteen unidentified compounds also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).
Volatile oil yielded a- and b-pinenes, a-camphcne, car-3-ene, p-cymene and pinocamphone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
Seven glycosides containing 5,4'-dihydroxy-7,3'-dimethoxyflavanone as common aglycone, isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
Pinocamphone (69.1), b-pinene (16.8), camphene (11.5) and pinocampheol (1.1%) identified in essential oil (1.0-1.5%) of aerial parts by GC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).
Determination of isopinocamphone (38.1), pinocarvone (20.3), 1,8-cineole (12.2) and b-pinene (10.2) in volatile oil from aerial parts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

Pharmacology: 

Essential oil (1.6 ml/kg, i.p.) showed convulsant action in rats which was related to presence of terpenic ketones, camphor, thujone, pinocamphone and isopinocamphone; it also exhibited epileptogenic and neurotoxic effects. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
Oil exl1ibited anthelmintic activity against chickcn worms (Ascaridia galli) and antituberculous activity against tuberculosis bacilli. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.
