DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
hrubby, branches densely pubescent, leaflet small linear, oblong 21-31, stipellae abortive bracts exceeding the buds.A low shrub with woody branches clothed with short spreading grey a brownish pubescence. leaves subsessile, 1-3in. long; leaflets usually opposite, membranous, obtuse 1/4-1/2 in. longdull green above, glaucous below, more or less densely pubescent.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY:
Racemes distinctly peduncled , moderately close 1-3in. long; bracts 1/4in. long lanceolate-cuspidate silky; pedicels very short, Calyx 1/12in. densely silky teeth, corolla bright red glabrous, 3/8-1/2in. pod stright, glabrescent 1--1/4in. long 8-12 seeded with very broad sutures.
