DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Erect tree with smooth ash-coloured bark. The main or mid-rib carries a smaller number of minor ribs or pinnae, 4-8 pairs arranged opposite. Leaflets almost stalkless, closely set in from 10 to 20 pairs along the ribs, linear-oblong, somewhat pointed at the apex., smooth.

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers at the end of the branches in raceme. The stalks of the lowest flowers are longest, pedicels are pubescent. Calyx leathery, covered with silky down externally. Sepals oblong, very narrow, sharp pointed. Petals at first are white and then yellow. Upper petal is usually smaller and of a deeper colour than the other. Petals are round in shape, curled at the margins. Stamens pubescent, dark coloured, thickened at the base. Pods smooth, narrowed at both ends, marked with a network of veins. Seeds 4-8.

reeding system and varietal improvement:

Phenology:

Leaves are shed during February - March. Young leaves appear towards the end of May or early in June. Flowers in the hot season or early rains.
