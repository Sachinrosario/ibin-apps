DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Herbs, more or less acaulescent or with subterranean stem reaching upto 60 cm high and producing rosette of leaves from the ground. Leaves erect, crowded, lanceolate, 30-50 cm long, acuminate, thick, succulent, glaucous-green, spinous on the margins.

Inflorescence: Raceme ; Flower:  Bracteate, pedicillate, pendulous, hypogynous, trimerous ; Perianth:   tepals six arranged in two whorls of three each, segments are usually petaloid and two whorls are generally   undifferentiated ; Androecium: Six stamens arranged in two alternate whorls of three   each, anthers   dithecous, basifixed, filaments free ; Gynoecium:  tricarpellary, syncarpous, ovary superior, trilocular with  many  ovules in each locule, placentation axile ; Fruit: Capsule ; Seed: Endospermic with curved embryo. 


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers in simple, erect racemes, yellow or orange, the lower flowers falling off as the raceme elongates. Perianth dull red or reddish yellow, tubular. Capsules cylindrical or ellipsoid-oblong, ca 1.5 x 1.0 cm.
Floral and seed biology:
It flowers December between May. Flowering & Fruiting:  December-March.


Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects
 
Genetic variability:

Plant has chromosome number 2n=14.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant propagates vegetatively by offsets.

Soil type: It grows in wide variety of soil but sandy loams are best suited.

Nutrient requirements:  It does not require  lot of manures and fertilizers but responds well to their application.

Water management:  Plant requires adquate water at the time of planting but  does not requires much water during its growing period.

Harvesting period / Method: Leaves of the plant are harvested , when it becomes mature after four years of planting. Harvesting can be done every 6-8 weeks by removing 3-4 leaves per plant.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant is sweet, bitter, cooling, purgative, alterative, fattening, tonic, aphrodisiac, anthelmintic, alexiteric. It is useful in eye diseases, tumours, enlargement of the spleen, liver complaints, vomiting, fever due to bronchitis, erysipelas, skin diseases, biliousness, asthma, leprosy, jaundice, strangury and ulcer. Flowers are anthelmintic and useful in biliousness and ‘Vata’ (Ayurveda) (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
