Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Small to medium sized fast growing tree grows tropical climate, humid to subhumid climate, in Western Ghats, Karnataka plateau. Grows up to 1200 mts in hills. Found cultivated throughout the plains of India up to an altitude of 1,200 m.

It can grow under widely different conditions. It is recommended suitable for cultivation in regions liable to periodic inundation. It can also be grown under waterlogged conditions and acid soils. Grows in wide range of rainfall (600 - 1000 mm) and temperature (100 - 450 C). Extremely fast growing, light demanding, frost sensitive, somewhat drought resistant.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Soft-wooded, quick-growing, short-lived shrub. Straight, cylindrical, furrowed stem. Light, spreading crown of feathery leaves. Leaves 7.5-15 cm long, paripinnate, leaflets 8-20 pairs, linear-oblong, glabrous, entire, mucronate to acuminate.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers handsome, large, yellow or yellow spotted red to purple or standard petals coloured purple or brown from outside, in 8-10 flowered, lax, axillary racemes. Pods pendulous, long, weak, distinctly torulose, twisted, sharply beaked, each containing about 40 seeds, septate.

Floral and seed biology:

Seed Germination & Propagation: Propagated by direct seed sowing. Best time for planting is July. Rhizobium inoculation is beneficial. Coppices well but looses vigour after 2-3 cycles.

When grown as a shade plant in orchards, it is sown parallel to the row of young orchard plants about a foot from their periphery. As a support to sugarcane, it is planted in equidistant rows at the same time as the sugarcane setts are planted.

 

MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:

Cercospora sesbaniae P. Henn - leaf spots

Uredo sesbaniae P. Henn

Daedalea sp.

Diplodia macrostama Lev.

Erysiphe polygoni DC

Xanthomonas sesbaniae - bacteria causes spots on leaves

Tobacco caterpillars (Prodenia litura F.) eat the foliage. Larvae of Azygophleps bore the main stem. Some insects damage the seeds. Several fungi and bacteria attack the plant.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel:Used as fuel within a year of sowing, but not preferred due to its very light quality. Medium quality fuel. Charcoal obtained from the wood is used for gunpowder. It is employed as fuel to boil jaggery.

Wood: White, light (500 kg/m3) and extremely soft. Density 0.42 - 0.5. Not durable.

Wood makes a good semi-chemical process pulp, that can be mixed up to 20% with long fibre pulp to make cheap papers.

Also yields timber. It is grown in Deccan to furnish poles as substitute for bamboo and for roofing huts. Wood is used to make gunpowder charcoal.

Other uses:

Leaves - Highly palatable fodder. Leaves as green manure.

Gum - Yields clear gum, which is used as substitute for gum arabic in foods and adhesives.

Tender leaves, buds, flowers and pods used as steamed or boiled vegetables. Sesban is classed under famine foods because of its seeds which are rich in protein.

Yields tannin.

Bark yields good fibre which is used for making ropes.

Fodder - Leaves and young branches are lopped for fodder for livestock.

Seeds, leaves, flower oil medicinally valuable.

Miscellaneous Information

Insecticide - Plant yields an insecticide.

Ink - It is also used in making ink.

Grown for ornamental purpose. Extensively used as shade tree for turmeric, tea, sweet orange and mandarin in south-west India and for cotton raised in Tamil Nadu. Planted as soil stabilizer and wind break. Used for agroforestry.

Grown as a hedge in north India and as paddy field-bunds in Tamil Nadu.

It yields annual biomass of 10-25 m3/ha with3-4 years of rotation.

It is grown as a perennial green manure crop. The plant stands lopping well and produces regrowths very quickly. Excellent green manure is obtained when the plants are 60 cm high.

On the basis of the colour of the flowers, plant is classified under three varieties: (i) var. typica Prain with uniformly yellow flowers (ii) var. picta Prain with standard petal externally spotted purple and (iii) var. bicolor W. & A. having the standard petal dark maroon or purple from outside.

Plant is a source of molluscicidal saponins.
It is an important fodder and fibre plant.
Also source of green manure.
Well rooted micropropagated shoots show improved survival when inoculated with Glomus fasciculatum or Glomus macrocarpum.
The plants have been micropropagated.
The plants have been micropropagated via somatic embryogenesis in var. bicolor.
