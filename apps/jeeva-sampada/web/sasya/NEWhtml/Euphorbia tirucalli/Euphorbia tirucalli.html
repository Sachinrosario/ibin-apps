DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Shrubs or small tree. Branches terete, fleshy, articulate, glabrous. Leaves spiral, linear or linear-oblong, obtuse at apex, cuneate at base, entire at margin. Cyathia few, terminal. Involucre cupular, coriaceous; glands 5, circular or more or less angular. Ovary pubescent; styles 3, forked from middle, arms curved. Capsules globose, finely puberulous. Seeds globose, glabrous, dark brown, carunculate.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Cymes from the long peducles ; Flower:Bracts  ovate-lanceolate, monoecious,  ; Male Flower: a stalked stamen without floral envelop ; Female Flower: Ovary 3-celled on ultimately exerted stalk in the center of greenish involucre, ovules solitary in each cell, style 3, free; Fruit: Capsule of 3 two valved  cocci with styles combined in the middle ; Seed: Broadly ovoid and subacute, smooth. Flowering & Fruiting : August- September  .

Floral and seed biology: Fl. & Fr.: July – October.

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.  
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome number 2n=20.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
In Ayurveda the plant is useful in biliousness, leprosy, leucorrhoea. The milk is alexiteric, carminative, useful in abdominal troubles, tumours. In Unani the plant juice is purgative, carminative, useful in gonorrhoea, whooping cough, asthma, dropsy, enlargement of spleen, dyspepsia, Jaundice, colic, tumours, stone in bladder. Fresh milky juice is applied to warts and used as a rubefacient, embrocation in rheumatism. It is good alterative in syphilis and a good application in neuralgea. In Konkan it is given as a purge. Root vesicant, rarely used as emeto-cathartic.


Chemistry:
Hentriacontane, hentriacontanol, b-sitosterol, taraxerol, 3,3’-di-O-methylellagic acid and ellagic acid isolated from stems. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

Taraxerone, tirucallol, sitosterol and 3,4,3’-tri-O-methylellagic acid (fig. 1) isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Structures of four new diterpenes (I – IV, fig. 2a – 2d) isolated from latex. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

A new diterpenoid – euphorbinol (fig. 5), mp. 111°C – isolated from latex and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Cycloeuphornol (fig. 3) isolated and its structure confirmed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Isolation and structure elucidation of a new sterol – euphorbosterol (fig. 4) together with a-amyrin and cycloartenol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Cycloartenol, 24-methylenecycloartenol, b-sitosterol, ingenol triacetate, euphorbol, euphorbol hexacosanoate, 12-deoxy-4b-hydroxyphorbol-13-phenyl-acetate-20-acetate and taraxerone from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.

In addition n-hexacosanol, cyaniding-3,5-diglucoside, tulipanidin-3-rhamnoglucoside, tinyatoxin, 20-O-acetylresiniferonol-9,13,14-orthophenylacetate, 12-deoxyphorbol-13-isobutyrate-20-acetate, 12-deoxyphorbol-13-O-phenylacetate-16-O-a-methylbutyrate-20-acetate isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.

12,20-dideoxyphorbol-13-isobutyrate, 12-deoxy-4b-hydroxyphorbol-13-phenylacetate-20-acetate and euphol isolated from latex. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Glut-5-en-3b-ol and cycloart-23-en-3b,25-diol isolated from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

A new macrocyclic diterpene - tirucalicine (fig. 8) isolated from latex and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

Isolation and characterization of a 31-nortriterpene – cycloeuphordenol (fig. 6b) from latex. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.

A new triterpene – cyclotirucanenol (fig. 6a) isolated and its absolute configuration determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)e.

Euphorginol (fig. 7) isolated from stem bark and its stereostructure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)f.

A new triterpene – euphorcinol – isolated from fresh stem bark and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Stem bark afforded cycloeucalenol and g-taraxasteryl acetate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

Isolation of a new dimmer – euphorbin F – along with euphorbin A. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.

A new ingol ester (I) isolated from latex and characterized as 3,7,12-tri-O-acetyl-8-isovalerylingol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
