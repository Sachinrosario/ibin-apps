HABIT & HABITAT
Small tree or shrub, 1.2-2.4 m, tall and it is found  occur in Jammu & Kashmir, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Branched, bark smooth, reddish brown; branchlets pubescent. Leaves sessile, 5-10 cm long; leaflets 8-12 pairs, slightly overlapping, elliptic-oblong, 2.0-2.5 x 1.0-1.3 cm, rounded-mucronate or emarginated at apex, rounded or obliquely cuneate at base, glabrous or finely downy, dull green above, paler beneath; petioles 1.0-1.5 cm long, terete. Stipules reniform, c 2.5 x 1 cm, ciliate, persistent. Flower in terminal or axillary corymbose raceme; pedicels 1.5-3.5 cm long, bracts ovate-lanceolate to suborbicular, 5-8 mm long, acuminate, puberulous, caducous. Sepals broadly ovate, 9-12 mm long, obtuse, outer ones puberulous. Petals bright yellow, broadly elliptic or obliquely rhomboid or orbicular, 1.5-2.5 cm long, veined. Ovary stipitate, appressed hairy; stigma terminal, punctiform. Pods linear, 4.5 x 12.5 x 0.5-1.5 cm, mucronate, pale brown, deeply depressed between the seeds, transversely veined, pubescent, compressed. Seeds 10-20, obovate-oblong, 5-14 x 4-5 mm; areole linear-oblong or ovate-lanceolate, c 3 x 0.75 mm

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine root is used in thirst, urinary discharges tumours, skin diseses and asthma. Leaves are good for ulcers, leprosy, skin diseases. Flowers are useful in urinary discharges, diabetes and throat troubles. Pods are anthelmintic, emetic and useful in vomiting and urinary discharge. Seeds are useful in chronic purulent opthalmia and conjunctivitis, gout, gonorrhoea, dysentery, cough, asthma and diabetes.
