Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 It is found in open grasslands, chiefly at medium altitudes, ascending to 2,000 meters, in Cagayan, Isabela, Bontoc, Lepanto, Benguet, and Nueva Vizcaya Provinces in Luzon; Semirara; in Culion; and in Mindanao. Occasionally it may be found at sea level (Semirara). It also occurs in India to China and through Malaya to tropical Australia.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES   The stem of katil are 30 to 50 centimeters in height, slender, erect, woody, little branched, and densely hairy. Leaflets are simple, linear-ligulate, and 2.5 to 5 centimeters in length. The flowers are yellow, 1 to 2, and borne in leaf-axils. The pods are oblong, about 2 centimeters in length, and densely hair.

Other Uses 

1.For scrofula, a decoction of the grain, with powdered pepper added, is given for diarrhea.

2. A powder of these seeds is applied to the skin to check cold sweats. A decoction of this grain is given to females during parturition to promote discharge of the lochia. 

3.It is also used in leucorrhoea and menstrual derangements.
