DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Stems twining, branched, internodes ca (2-3) 11-19 cm long and 1.5-3 mm in diam., faintly ridged, glabrous. Latex milky-white. Leaves opposite, decussate, lamina 2.1-15.7 x 1.8-15.5 cm, ovate-oblong, caudate or aristate at apex, cordate with incision up to 1.8 cm deep at base with rounded lobes, more or less pubescent along vein beneath, lateral veins 5-7 pairs, arched; petioles terete, 1-6 cm long and 1-2 mm in diam.

Flowers axillary, in umbellate cymes, many flowered; peduncles terete, 0.6-4 cm long, ca 1.5 mm in diam., glabrous; bracts 1.5-2 x 0.5 mm linear, apex acute, grooved in the center from inner side, glabrous; pedicels terete, 1-4 cm long, ca 1 mm in diam., glabrous. Calyx 5-lobed, divided up to base, lobes 5-7 x 4.5-6 mm, broadly ovate, saucer shaped, apex obtuse, margin papery ciliate, glabrous, glandular within. Corolla rotate, ca 1.5 x 1-1.5 mm, ovate-oblong, apex obtuse, leathery with papery margin, glabrous. Corona staminal, uniseriate, annular ring, arising from the base of the staminal column, 3.5-4 mm high, 7-7.5 mm across, truncate. Stamens five, ca 8 mm long; pollinia five, pollen masses solitary in each anther cell, yellow, waxy, 1987.2-2044.8 x 403.2-417.6 mm attached by light-brown caudicles ca 1195.2 mm long, ca 158.4 mm wide to dark-brown corpuscles ca 734.4 mm long and ca 216 mm wide. Gynostegium ca 11 mm long. Follicles paired or usually solitary, ca 11 x 3.5 cm, ovoid, thick, apex acute, glabrous; seeds many, ca 7.5-8 x 4.5 mm, ovate, base-rounded, flat, brown with light-brown margin; ca 0.5 mm wide; coma silky-white, 2-4 cm long.
