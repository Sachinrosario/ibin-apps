HABIT & HABITAT
Extensive dioecious, climbers; roots tuberous; stems stout, angular, sulcate, glabrous or slightly hairy. It is found in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura, Nagaland, Manipur and Andhra Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Leaves membranous, ovate-cordate, 12-20 x 9-12 cm, acute or acuminate at apex, entire or rarely sublobate, basal lobe rounded. Tendrils 3-fid, elongated, furrowed lengthwise, minutely pubescent. Male flowers in 4-8 flowered raceme, 12-20 cm long; pedicels 1-2 mm long; bracts large, obovate, c 4 cm long, base sheathing, sparsely villose-hirsute; petals papillose; filaments 1mm long, glabrous. Female flowers: subsessile; ovary oblong, sparsely puberulous. Fruit globose, red, and smooth. Seeds subquadrangular, 10-12 x 4-5 mm.
