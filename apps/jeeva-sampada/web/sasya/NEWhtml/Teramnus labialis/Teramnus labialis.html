HABIT & HABITAT
Slender, widely spreading, twining herbs; stem and branches hairy. It is found in Maharastra, Bihar.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
  Leaves 3-foliate, leaflets ovate-elliptic, 1.5-9.0 x 0.5-5.4 cm, appressed hairy beneath. Flowers in axillary and few-flowered terminal racemes, violet-purple, fading to pales blue or white. Pods 4-5 x 0.25-0.3 cm, narrowly compressed, stright or slightly incurved with a short, stout beak bent upwards at more or less right angle, hairy when young, glabrous at maturity. Seeds 2-12, oblong, about 4 mm, smooth, dark brown.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine fruits are used as cooling, astringent, antipyretic, tonic, galactagogue. It is useful in inflammation, blood diseases, gout, fever, bronchitis, thirst, paralysis, rheumatism and nervous affections.
