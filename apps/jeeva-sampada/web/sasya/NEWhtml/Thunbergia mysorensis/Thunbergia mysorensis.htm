HABIT & HABITAT

A large climber, growing in south India in W. Ghats (Nilgiris), S. Canara and Mysore to Travancore and Triunelvelly upto 1000 m, cultivated in the plains throughout India.
Availability : South India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A glabrous twining shrub with slender glabrous often twisted stems. Leaves opposite, ovate-lanceolate or narrowly elliptic-acuminate in shape, 4-6 inch long x 1.25-2 inch broad, broadly cuneate at the base, sinuate, entire or toothed on the margins; basal nerves 3, prominent with conspicuous venation between; petioles 0.5-1.5 inch long. Flowers large, in long pendulous interrupted racemes. Bracteoles spathaceous, enclosing the corolla tube, ovate-oblong, 1 inch long, parallel nerved, margins obscurely lobed or crenulate. Corolla tube purple, 2 inch long, shortly conical below, swelling above and ending in four lobes, the upper lobe erect with reflexed side lobes, the lower lip of 3 subequal spreading lobes. Lobes bright yellow or maroon, spotted with yellow or brown. Stamens 4; filaments hairy at the base; anthers bearded with a short spur at the base of each cell. Ovary immersed in the thick fleshy disk; style long; stigma cup-shaped. Capsules 1.25 inch long, glabrous; seeds rugose.
