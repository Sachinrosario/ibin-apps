REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Inflorescence: Axillary corymbose panicles ; Flower: White, bract foliaceous, hermaphrodite; Calyx: Sepals 4-6, stellately spreading, pubescent outside, glabrous inside ; Corolla: Absent ; Androcoeium: Stamens many, anthers usually adnate, dehiscing laterally, filaments glabrous, connective not produced; Gynoecium: Multicarpellary, ovule 1, style simple; Fruit: Achenes ovoid, compressed, silky villous with long feathery tail; 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : September -November. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
