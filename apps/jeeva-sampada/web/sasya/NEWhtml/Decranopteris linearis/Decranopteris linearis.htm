Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

Found in the hills, almost throughout India, ascending upto 6000 ft.
Availability : The Nilgiris hilly region is protected now since its declaration as the Nilgiri B. Reserve.

Measures proposed - a thorough intensive search be made in the above areas to locate the plants and introduce in the botanical gardens for ex situ conservation.


Conservation/ Threat Status : D. linearis (Burm. f.) under w. var. sesbastiana Panigr. & Dixit.
Vulnerable .

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A handsome fern.
Has a creeping rhizome 8 zig-zag, repeatedly forked strikes bearing pinnae in pairs.
For var. sebastiana Paingr. and Dixit.
Perennial, terrestrial trailing nert.
Lateral branch sys. Is characteristic and forked several times.
Branches at successive forks conspicuously, alternately unequal, successive larger branches forming almost a straight line.
Ultimate branches always with accessory branches, 8-16 x 3-4 cm;
Costules on the ultimate branches, 5-5 mm part.
Segments of lamina tapering from base to apex.
Texture thin but firm, apex acute, margin slightly reflexed when dry, lower surface glabrous and slightly glaucous.
Vein concolorous to lamina, distinctly prominent on the upper surface and slightly so on the lower surface.
