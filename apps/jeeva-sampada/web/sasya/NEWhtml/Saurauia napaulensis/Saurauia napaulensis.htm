HABIT & HABITAT
A deciduous tree, 6-9 m. in height and 30-90 cm. in girth, with a clear bole of 1.5-4.5 m., distributed in the Himalayas from Sutlej eastward at altitudes of 600-. 2,500 m. and in the hills of Assam; it has also been introduced in the hills of South India and other places as an ornamental tree.
This can be seen in the cultivated areas, Cultivated fields and Plains, Edges of Forests & Forests, Forest edges, Forests, Road sides.
BioticZone: Himalaya, Hindukush himalaya and Temperate Himalayas.

Availability :  
Misc: Himachal Pradesh to South West China, Khasi hills, M.V.Road, Namdapha, Mawryngkneng, Mishmi hills and Yercad.
Country: Burma, China, India, Indochina, S.E.Asia and Thailand.
Zone: Central, East, North, North East and South zone.
State: Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal.
District: Chamba, Chamoli, Darjeeling, Dibang Valley, Jalpaiguri, Jowai, Kameng, Lohit, Salem, Siang, Subansiri and Tirap.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves oblong-elliptic, oblanceolate or oblong-lanceolate, 18-38 cm. x 6-10 cm., crowded towards the ends of the branches; flowers pink, in axillary, drooping panicles; berries green, sub-globose or broad-ovoid, 1-1 cm. or slightly more in diam., 4-5-lobed; seeds red-brown.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
Leaves, Fruits, Bark.

Uses

  1. Leaves are lopped for fodder. 
  2. Fruit is sweet and palatable, and is eaten. 
  3. Bark is used as a poultice to extract splinters from the flesh.

Chemistry
On analysis of leaves, following values were obtained (dry wt. basis) : crude protein, 12-25;ether extr., 4-23; N-free extr., 51-65; crude fibre, 18-40;ash, 13-47; calcium, 2-74; and phosphorus, 0-24; dig.crude protein, 1-19; and total dig. nutrients, 34-01%,Kumauni bullocks, when fed with the leaves in c^ -function with wheat-straw and rape cake, showed positive balances for nitrogen and calcium, but a negative balance for phosphorus. The leaves contain 6-5 per cent tannin as gallotannic acid on dry basis.

Other Uses
Wood is light-pink, very soft (sp. gr., 0-37; wt., 400 kg./cu.m.),and spongy. It is used for minor type of interior construction and may be useful for packing-cases.
