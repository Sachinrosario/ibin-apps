Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A leafy lichen known to be growing in open places on different trees and rocks in tropical and temperate regions of India, Nepal and other parts of the world. It prefers trees including Acrocarpus fraxifolius, Cedrus deodara, Cinchona, Cupressus, Erythrina, Pinus roxburghii, P. kesia, Quercus leucotrichophora, Rhododendron and Shorea robusta.

Availability :

Rimelia reticulata is widely distributed throughout the pantropical parts of the world, and is also widely represented in India.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
As spices: The tribals belonging to Baiga, Bhil, Bhilala, Gond, Korka, Muria communities in Central India use the whole thallus as spices. The thallus is dried and powdered and added in pulses, vegetables and meat to enhance the flavour. The species is one of the important ingrediant in the spices available in Saudia Arabia, London and mostly in Indian markets, particularly in grocery shops. The spice ‘Chharila’ sold in the market is a mixture of Parmotrema tinctourm, P. nilgherrense and Parmotrema sancti-angelii.
As dye: It is also one of the potential source of red-brown dye.
