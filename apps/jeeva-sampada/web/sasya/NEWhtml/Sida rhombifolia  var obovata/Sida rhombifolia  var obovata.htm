DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
This is a large leaved plant, the broadly obovate leaves measuring 1 1/2 by 2 inches, hoary beneath, and having the apex coarselt toothed, and the base drawn out or cuneate. Peduncles longer than the petiole, but shorter than the blade.

Other Uses
  1. Leaves and roots are used, piles, gonorrhoea, anti-soud, diuretic, aphrodisiac
  2. Root of these herbs are held in great repute in treatment of rheumatism. Stems abound in mucilage and are employed as demulcents and emollients both for external and internal use.
  3. The herb is also useful in calculous troubles and as a febrifuge with pepper. Mucilage is used for scorpion sting.
