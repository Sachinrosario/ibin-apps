Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Small to moderate sized, crooked, bushy tree with drooping branches. In dry deciduous forests, in sub-Himalayan tracts. Found throughout India, ascending to an altitude of 1,650 m in western Himalayas.The tree is light demander. Though it is affected slightly by frost, it recovers soon.

Availability :

Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small crooked tree with dark scabrous bark; branches numerous, drooping. Bark bluish black, rough, pinkish red inside, turning brown on exposure. Leaves broader than long, divided a little less than ½ way down into 2 rounded lobes, green and glabrous above, white or more or less tomentose beneath, base usually cordate; main nerves 7-9, petioles pubescent.

Back to top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers in terminal or leaf-opposed racemes, rachis densely pubescent, buds pointed; bracts beneath the pedicels linear, acute. Calyx pubescent, tube very short, spathaceous at length reflexed. Petals narrowly oblanceolate, acute, white or pale yellow. Stamens 10, all fertile, filaments densely hairy at the base. Ovary pubescent, stigma sessile. Pods stalked, 6-10, blunt at the apex, tapering at the base, not or scarcely veined. Seeds oblong, compressed, rounded at the apex.

Breeding system and varietal improvement:

Phenology: Flowers March – June.


Pollinators:

Floral and seed biology:

The seeds have a fairly high germinative power and retain their vitality for nearly a year. Propagation is done by line-sowings. The young plants are kept weeded and the soil is loosened from time to time.



MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:

Xanthomonas bauhiniae Padhya et al - leaf parasite

Dendrophthoe falcata

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel: Wood used as fuel. But considered to be bad fuel.

Wood: Wood is brown with darker patches, hard and heavy (wt 736 kg/m3). It is used for ploughs and yokes.

Harvesting Practices:

Produce and Products: gum


Chemistry:

Processing Techniques:

Timber seasons without cracking and should be converted green and open stacked under cover.



Other uses:

Leaves - Used as fodder. They are also used for the manufacture of bidis. The leaves are given with onion for diarrhoea. They are also reported to be anthelmintic.

Bark - It yields a fiber used to make ropes. Also used for torches and slow matches. It is highly astringent and is used in dysentry. It showed significant anti-inflammatory and cholagogue activities.

Plant yields a medicinal gum.

It is useful species for filling blanks in forest plantings and helps in checking soil erosion.
