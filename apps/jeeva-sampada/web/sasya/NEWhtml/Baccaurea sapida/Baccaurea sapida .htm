HABIT & HABITAT
This is a medium-sized tree occurs wild or cultivated in sub-Himalayan tracts, Assam, Burma and the Andamans. It prefers a deep moist soil and wet climate. and also found in Primary forests of India.

Availability :
Zone: North East, South, State: Kerala, Meghalaya, 

District: Jowai,
Status :

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
It is medium sized tree, occurs wild or cultivated in sub-himalayan tracks. The Yellow globose fruits, about an inch in diameter possess a pleasant acid taste, they are not quite whole some. In the Rangoon market it is generally plentiful.

Parts used :
Ripe fruit, flowers, leaves and bark.

Uses:

  1. The pulp in the ripe fruit is delicious.

  2. In Siam, the flowers and leaves also are said to be eaten.

Other Uses :
  1. The soft grayish brown wood is liable to split badly(wt.,ca 42 lb.per c.ft).
