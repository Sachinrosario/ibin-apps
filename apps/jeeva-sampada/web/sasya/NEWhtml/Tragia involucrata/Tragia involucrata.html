DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves alternate, petiolate, variable, ovate, oblong-lanceolate to lanceolate, acute or acuminate at apex, cordate at base, serrate at margin; stipules ovate, auricled, acute. Flowers in terminal and leaf-opposed raceme, males on upper part, females below, apetalous. Male flowers: sepals 3 greenish-yellow, glalorous; stamens 3; pistillode minute. Female flowers: sepals 6, sometimes pinnatifid, accrescent; ovary 3 obed; styles 3, stigma linear. Seeds globose, black, covered with white scales.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Root is aphrodisiac. It is given in fever to cause perspiration and alterative. Root in infusion is applied in ardent fever and itching of skin. In Konkan root paste is employed to extract guinea worm. Mixture of root paste & Ocimum sanctum juice is effective in itchy skin eruptions. In Chota Nagpur root is given in pains in legs and arms. Root is used as medicine in leprosy. Leaf used in preparation of errhine, which is prescribed in cases of headache.
