 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT : 
A small genus of shrubs or under shrubs distributed in the Indo-Malaysian region and eastwards to New Guinea. Six species occur in India.
Habitat: Shaded moist places & Rocky Cliffs in primary forests,

Availability : 
Sikkim, North Bengal, Assam (Cachar) N.E.F.A., and Lushai, Khasi and Aka hills, ascending up to an altitude of 1,000 m.

Misc: Deban forest, Namdapha, Haldibari, Namdapha, Khleihriat to Sonapur, M'Pen Check gate, Namdapha and Sohka.
Country: India,
Zone: North Eastzone.
State: Arunachal Pradesh and Meghalaya.
District: Jowai.
Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Is an erect under shrub, 60-150 cm. high. with broad-elliptic, dentate leaves 12.5-32.5 cm. x3-8-12-5 cm., rose-purple flowers, and small globose, whitish, juicy berries.
