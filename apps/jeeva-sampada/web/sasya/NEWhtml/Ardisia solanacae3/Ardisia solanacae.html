DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Inflorescence:Axillary corymbs; Flower: Hermaphrodite, rosy, complete; Calyx: divided to the base, sepals imbricate, rotund, ovate, densely gland dotted, with ciliate margins; Corolla: Rose-coloured, divided almost to the base, petals ovate-elliptic, acute, mark with dark lines; Androecium: Stamens 5, attached to the base of the corolla, shorter than petals, filaments very short, anthers lanceolate, acute ;Gynoecium: Ovary ovoid, ovules few to many, immersed in globose placenta, style short, stigma minute; Fruit: Depressed, globose, purple-black; Seed: Solitary, globose; 

Floral and seed biology:

Floweing & Fruiting:  April-June. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:

Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.


Genetic variability:
The plant has reported chromosome number 2n= 46.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
Bauerenol, a-amyrin and b-amyrin isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

Alcoholic extract exhibited significant activity against most of the tested Gram-positive and Gram-negative microorganism. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).


Pharmacology:

Ethnobotany:
Medicine for headache (Bark, Jain and De, 1966); Post natal (Root, Saxena et al, 1981); Snake-bite (Bark, Gupta, 1981); Edible vegitable (Leaf, Arora, 1981).
