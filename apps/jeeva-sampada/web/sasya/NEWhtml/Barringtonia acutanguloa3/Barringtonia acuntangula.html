REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Many flowered racemes; Flower: bracteate, fragrant, dark-scarlet, hermaphrodite ; Calyx: Calyx-tube puberulous outside, about as long as the lobes, oblong, rounded, ciliolate; Corolla: Petals 4, imbricate, adnate at the base to the staminal tube;Androecium: Stamens numerous, in several rows, connate at the base into ring, filaments filiform,all antheriferous, anthers small; Gynoecium: Ovary inferior, 2-4 celled, ovules 2-8 in each cell, pendulous, style long filiform and stigma small ; Fruit: Bluntly quadrangular, broadest in the middle, slightly narrowed towards and truncate at each end, crowned by small persistent calyx; Seed: Ovoid, exalbuminous. Flowering & Fruiting : September-October. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by  wind.

Chemistry:
Barringtogenol D (fig. 2.) characterised as 3b,22a,28-trihydroxy-16a, 21a-oxidoolean-12-ene, m.p. 233°, from fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Barringtogenol C (fig. 1.) characterized as 3b,16a,21a,22b,28-pentahydroxyolean-12-ene. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

b- and g--sitosterols, a triterpene alcohol-tanginol (fig. 4), m.p. 283°, -and a triterpene dicarboxylic acid isolated from heartwood; barringtonic acid, m.p. 285°, after acid hydrolysis of alcoholic extractive. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Barringtogenol E (fig. 3) m.p. 221°, [3a,16a,28-trihydroxy-21b,22a-bis(benzoyloxy)-olean-12-ene] from branch wood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.

Barringetogenol B (3b,21b,22a,28-tetrahydroxy-16a-angeloyloxyolean-12-ene). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)e.

2a,3a,19-trihydroxyolean-12-en-23,28-dioic (fig. 5) acid (I) from branch wood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)f.

A new triterpene – barrinic acid (fig. 6)– isolated and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Structure of barrigenic acid (fig. 7) isolated from fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Revised structure of barringtogenol B (fig. 8)(sapogenol A) proposed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

A new trihydroxytriterpene acid – acutangulic acid (fig. 9)– isolated in addition to stigmasterol-b-D-glucoside and barringtogenic acid from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

A new triterpene – tangulic acid (fig. 10)– isolated along with b-amyrin, b-sitosterol, stigmasterol and oleanolic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.

A new triterpenoid glucoside isolated from seeds and characterized as 2a,3b,19a-trihydroxyolean-12-en-23,28-dioic acid-28-O-b-D-glucoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Isolation of shikimic acid, gallic acid, b-sitosterol and its glucoside from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

Three monodesmosidic glucuronide saponins of barringtogenol C – barringtoside A (fig. 11), barringtoside B (fig. 12) and barringtoside C (fig. 13)– isolated from seeds as their methyl esters and their structures established. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
