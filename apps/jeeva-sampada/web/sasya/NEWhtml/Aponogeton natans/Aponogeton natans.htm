HABIT & HABITAT

It grows in temporary ponds, rice fields and marshes.
Availability : Almost throughout the India except in Arid areas and at altitudes above 1300 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Tuber up to 2 cm in diameter. Submerged leaves petiolate; petioles up to ± 5 cm long; blades lanceolate, up to 6.5 cm long, up to 1.5 cm wide, at base and tip cuneate, midrib wide with 2-3 parallel veins on each side. Floating leaves up to 11.5 cm long and 3 cm wide, at base cordate, at tip cuneate, with 5 or 7 secondary veins. Inflorescence a single spike, up to 7 cm long, elongating in fruit; spathe caducous, up to 5 m long. Flowers bisexual, turned in all directions. Perianth segments 2, petal-like, white, pink or purple, spathulate, 1.5-2 mm long, 0.5-1 mm wide. Filaments white; anthers yellow.ovaries 3 follicles with ± 8 seeds, up to 4 mm long, up to 2.25 mm wide, with a very long terminal beak (up to 1 mm). Seeds with a double coat.
