DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Trailing, annual herb; stems angular, scabrous. Leaves ovate-orbicular or reniform, 3.5-7 cm in diam., palmately 5-7 lobed, scabrous on both surfaces, sometimes with soft hairs; lobes dilated at tips or again sub-lobed.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Male Flower: In axillary fascicles of about 2-3, pedunculate; calyx densely hairy, tube narrowly campanulate, teeth subulate; corolla segments hairy outside, often turbinated by hairy apiculation; Stamens 3, filaments shorts, anthers free, oblong, one celled, cells linear, the connective produced into a pappilose appendage; ovary rudimentary, glanduliform, tube constricted above the ovary; Female Flower: calyx densely hairy, tube narrowly campanulate, teeth subulate; corolla segments hairy outside, often turbinated by hairy apiculation; Ovary ellipsoid, covered long bristly hairs, ovules infinite, horizontal style short, stigma three; Fruit: Ellipsoid or turbinate, smooth or with few bristles; Seed: Numerous, narrowly ovoid, compressed. Fruits ovoid, spherical or contorted, 5-15 cm in diam., glabrous or hairy, with green and white strips, yellow when ripe, smooth. Seeds white, oblong.
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : June- August. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
 
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome numbers - 20, 22, 24.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant requires well drained loamy soil.

Nutrient requirements: Require tonnes of farmyard manure at the time of planting and 80 kg/ha nitrogenous fertilizers and 50 kg/ha potassium and phosphorous at growing period give good yield.

Water management: Plant requires good irrigation facilities.                                       

Harvesting period / Method: Fruits are harvested when they are fully mature.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: In Ayurveda system of medicine ripe fruit is cooling, fattening and diuretic. It cures biliousness, insanity and ascites. In Unani system of medicine fruit strengththens the heart, brain and body. It also cures opthalmia, urinary discharges. It causes congestion of eyes. Seeds are diuretic and lachrymatory. It is used in opthalmia, troubles of liver, bronchitis, fever, thrist and burning of throat.

Chemistry:
CIMAP:  Polamines were related to various plant physiology process. The endogenous polyamines content changes during adventitious root formation in cotyledon of Cucumis melo cultured in vitro were studied. Cotyledon contents of putrescine and spermine during the adventitious root formation raised but spermidine kept stable initially & then experienced a decrease and then stable or raised a litter. sol. protein was decreased, and peroxidase activity increased. Addn. of D-arginine, putrescine contents and peroxidase activity were decreased, the adventitious root formation was inhibited but sol. protein had litter change Addn. of MGBG decreased the spermidine & spermine contents but not affect of formation of adventitious roots1.
