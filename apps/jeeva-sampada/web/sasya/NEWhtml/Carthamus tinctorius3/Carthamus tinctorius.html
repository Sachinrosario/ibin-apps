HABIT & HABITAT
 Erect, glabrous herbs, c 45 cm high, thistle like, it is found to be cultivated almost throughout India for extraction of edible oil from seeds and sometimes found as an escape.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Leaves lanceolate, 3.5-11.5 x 1.0-2.5 cm, spinous toothed, stiff, sessile. Heads 1-2 cm across, discoid, terminal. Involucral bracts many, spinous, erect; outer ones leaf-like; inner narrow. Receptacle flat, densely bristly. Flowers orange-red. Corolla tube slender, deeply 5-lobed. Achenes 4-angled, glabrous.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Seeds are used in rheumatism, purgative and diuretic.


Chemistry:
Carthamin (fig. 1) (6-glucosidoxy-2,4,4’,5-tetrahydroxychalcone) and neocarthamin (fig. 2) (5-glucosidoxy-6,7,4’-trihydroxyflavanone) from yellow and ivory white varieties of plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Kaempferol-3-rhamnoglucoside and kaempferol glycoside from ivory white flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Isolation and structure elucidation of tracheloside (2-hydroxyarctiin) (fig. 3). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

A steroid cellobioside (fig. 4) isolated from flowers and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

(Z,Z,Z)1,8,11,14-heptadecatetraene isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

Re-examination of red pigments of flowers showed that carthamin is not a tautomer but a pyridine salt of isocarthamin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

Three acylserotonins isolated from oil-free safflower identified as N-feruloyl-serotonin, N-p-coumaroylserotonin and N-p-coumaroylserotonin-b-D-glucopyranoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.

Polyacetylenes-1-tridecene-3,5,7,9,11-pentayne, (11-Z)trideca-1,11-diene-3,5,7,9-tetrayne, (3Z,11Z)trideca-1,3,5,11-tetraene-7,9-diyne – isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)f.

A glucopyranoside of tracheloside, b-sitosterol, campesterol, glucose, maltose and raffinose isolated form seeds; seed cake contained protein (37.53) and carbohydrate (57.98%). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Three new serotonins – N-feruloylserotonin, N-(p-coumaroyl)serotonin and N(p-coumaroyl)serotoninN-(p-coumaroyl)seratonin-mono-b-D-glucopyranoside – isolated from seeds along with 2-hydroxyarctin, matairesinol mono-b-D-glucopyranoside and acacetin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Safflor yellow B (fig. 5) isolated from petals and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Nonacosane, b-sitosterol,palmitic, myristic and lauric acids isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.

Luteolin and its 7-O-glucoside, b-sitosterol and its glucoside isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

New indole alkaloid serotobenine (fig. 6) isolated from seeds along with N-feruloyltryptamine and N-(p-coumaroyl) serotobenine – isolated from seeds along with N-feruloyltryptamine and N-(p-coumaroyl) tryptamine and its crystal structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

Dipalmitin, linoleic acid, oleic aid and b-sitosterol-3-O-glucoside identified. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

Propanetriol-1-O-glucopyranosyl(4®1)-arabinopyranoside isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Volatile oil of buds, leaves and stems contained 1-pentadecene as major compound; flower oil contained mixture of polyacetylenes. 1,2,3-trimethoxy-5-methylbenzene identified in plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

Luteolin-7-O-glucoside isolated from seedlings. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.

Yellow and red pigments isolated and identified as safflor yellow A (fig. 7) and carthamin respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.

Kaempferol, its 3-glucoside and 3-rutinoside, 6-hydroxykaempferol, quercetin, its 3-glucoside, 7-glucoside and 3-rutinoside isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.

Astragalin, quercetin, rutin and kaempferol, its 3-glucoside, 3,6-diglucoside, 3,6,7-triglucoside and 3-rutinoside-6-glucoside isolated from dried petals. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)f.

Isolation and structure elucidation of a new N-containing quinchalcone glycoside – tinctormine (fig. 9) from safflower. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)g.

Another new quinochalcone C-glycoside-hydroxysafflor yellow A (fig. 8) also isolated along with carthamin, Safflor Yellow B and safflomin C and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)h.

A C31 alkanediol isolated from dried flowers and shown to be (6S,8R)erythro-hentriacontane-6,8-diol (fig. 10); in addition, 6,8-diols of eicosane, tricosane, pentacosane, heptacosane, octacosane, nonacosane, triacontane, dotriacontane, tritriacontane, tetratriacontane and pentatriacontane isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)i.

Carthamin was found to be much more stable to heat at alkaline pH and acidic pH. (Kim et al., 1997) MAPA. 1998,9804: 1656.

(15a,20R)-Dihydroxypregn-4-en-3-one 6'-O-acetyl-20-b-cellobioside and matairesinol 4'-O-b-D-apiofuranosyl(1®2)-b-D-glucopyranoside were isolated from safflower (Carthamus tinctorius L.) oil cake.  The structures were confirmed by spectroscopic methods and X-ray crystal structure anal.  The 1H- and 13C-NMR signals of (15a, 20R)-dihydroxypregn-4-en-3-one were assigned. (Nagatsu et al., 1998) CA. 1999,129:161774.

Antioxidative compounds contained in roasted safflower seeds were investigated.  Six phenolic compds., N-feruloylserotonin, N-(p-coumaroyl) serotonin, matairesinol, 8'-hydroxyarctigenin, acacetin 7-O-b-D-glucoside (tilianine) and acacetin were isolated and identified from the seeds. (Kang et al., 1999) CA. 2000,133:29949.

6-hydroxy-kaempferol-3-O-glucoside, 6-hydroxy-kaempferol-7-O-glucoside, kaempferol-3-O-rutinoside and quercetin-3- O-glucoside were isolated from petals. (Li and Che, 1998) CA. 1999,130:220356.

A novel blue phyto-pigment was isolated from the flower of saffron thistle (C. tinctorius), and purified by column chromatography techniques.  It is dissolved into aqueous organic solvents to show a fine lazulite blue color.  Based on the data from tentative analyses, the newly isolated pigment is named as "LAZULININE". (Saito, 2000) CA. 2001,134:350630.

The flavonoid constituents in fresh florets of the three distinctive cultivars of Carthamus tinctorius L. were purified and identified to investigate flavonoid biosynthesis in the petals. (Kazuma  et al., 2000)CA. 2001,134:2620.

From the aqueous  ethanol extract (AE) of Carthamus tinctorius seeds, a new acacetin diglycoside has been isolated and identified as acacetin 7-O-b-D-apiofuranosyl-(1''' (R) 6'' instead of 6')-O-b-D-glucopyranoside together with previously isolated kaempferol 7-O-b-D-glucopyranoside, acacetin 7-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside and acacetin. (Ahmed et al., 2000) CA. 2000,133:293522.

Cartormin, a novel semi-quinone chalcone sharing a pyrrole ring C-glycoside, was isolated from Carthamus tinctorius L. and its structure was established from various spectral data and a single-crystal X-ray analysis. ( Yin et al., 2000) CA. 2000, 133:30869

Cartorimine, a new cycloheptenone oxide derivative was isolated  from Carthamus tinctorius. (Yin et al., 2000) MAPA. 2001, 05: 2920.

A total of eight flavonoids, including a novel quercetin-7-O-(6''-O-acetyl)-b-D-glucopyranoside and seven known flavonoids, luteolin, quercetin, luteolin 7-O-b-D-glucopyranoside, luteolin-7-O-(6''-O-acetyl)-b-D-glucopyranoside quercetin 7-O-b-D-glucopyranoside, acacetin 7-O-b-D-glucuronide and apigenin-6-C-b-D-glucopyranosyl-8-C-b-D-glucopyranoside, have been isolated from the leaves of the safflower. (Lee et al., 2002). CA. 2002, 137:229312.

Pharmacology:

Pharmacognostical analysis  of flowers of Carthamus tinctorius. (Dey and Das, 1998) MAPA. 1998, 9804: 2089.
Plant showed anti-inflammatory activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.

Polysaccharide (15-35 mg/kg, i.p.) of safflower increased plaque-forming unit cells and spleen weight in mice. Splenic antibody- forming cells were markedly increase  when polysaccharide was given before sensitization of animals whereas antibody-forming cells decreased when given before sensitization. It also suppressed inhibitory effect of immuno-suppressant prednisolone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.

Safflor yellow A (0.55-1.0 g/kg, i.p.) showed significant analgesic action. It markedly inhibited formaldehyde-induced foot swelling, formation of cotton ball granuloma and histamine-stimulated capillary permeability in rats. It greatly enhanced barbital and chloral hydrate-induced CNS inhibition but markedly reduced coramine-induced convulsion and death in mice. The i.p. and oral LD50 in mice were 5.49 and 5.53 g/kg respectively. Thus, it appears to possess analgesic, anti-inflammatory, sedative and anti-convulsant effects. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.

Safflower yellow (SY), a mixture of chalconoid compounds (1.0 to 2.0 g/kg/day, i.g.) lowered blood pressure of spantoaneously hypertensive rats (SHR) from 1.86 to 3.86 K.Pa. Five week after administration of SY, plasma rennin activity and angiotensin II level were diminished in SHR group. Results suggests that decrease of blood pressure my be mediated by reninangiotensin system. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)j.

The triterpene alcohols from flowers were evaluated with respect to their anti-inflammatory activity against 12-O-tetradecanoylphorbol-13-acetate-induced inflammation in mice which showed marked inhibitory activity. (Akihisa et al., 1996).

Seven antioxidative serotonin derivatives were isolated from safflower (Carthamus tinctorius L.) oil cake. Their structures were established as N-[2-(5-hydroxy-1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]ferulamide (1), N-[2-(5-hydroxy-1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]-p-coumaramide (2), N,N'-[2,2'-(5,5'-dihydroxy-4,4'-bi-1H-indol-3,3'-yl)diethyl]- di-p-coumaramide (3), N-[2-[3'-[2-(p-coumaramido)ethyl]-5,5'-dihydroxy- 4,4'-bi-1H-indol-3-yl]ethyl]ferulamide (4), and N,N'-[2,2'-(5,5'-dihydroxy-4,4'-bi-1H-indol-3,3'-yl)diethyl]- diferulamide (5), N-[2-[5-(beta-D-glucosyloxy)-1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]- p-coumaramide (6), and N-[2-[5-(beta-D-glucosyloxy)-1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]ferulamide (7). Antioxidative activities of the compounds were measured by the ferric thiocyanate method and the alpha,alpha-diphenyl-beta-picrylhydrazyl (DPPH) method, and compounds 1-5 were found to have relatively strong antioxidative activity. (Zhang et al., 1997).

New calcium antagonists were isolated from Carthamus tinctorius by extracting with 50% ethanol, and extracting again with EtOAc, are claimed for treatment of cardiovascular diseases. (Che , 1998) CA. 2000,132:231958.

Carthamus tinctorius showed strong tyrosinase inhibitory activity. (Miao et al., 1998) MAPA. 1998, 9805: 2693.

The inhibitory effects of phenolic compounds on 1,1-diphenyl-2-picrylhydrazyl (DPPH) radical and lipid peroxidation induced by H2O2/FeSO4 in rat liver microsomes were determined.  Two serotonins showed more potent DPPH radical scavenging activity, and a stronger inhibitory effect on the lipid peroxidn. than that of a-tocopherol.  In addition, acacetin and matairesinol also considerably inhibited lipid peroxidation. (Kang et al., 1999) CA. 2000,133:29949.

Tyrosinase inhibitory and anti-tumor promoting activities of 14 compounds were isolated from safflower. Catechin and quercetin showed moderate tyrosinase inhibitory activity.  N-(p-Coumaroyl)serotonin and its 5-O-glucoside exhibited weak anti-tyrosinase activity, but potent inhibitory activity on melanine production in the assay.  Serotonin derivatives, 2-hydroxyarctiin, matairesinol glycoside and sesquiterpene glucosides exhibited potent inhibitory activity on phorbol-12-myristate-13-acetate induced Epstein-Barr virus early antigen activation, which is the short term in vitro assay for anti-tumor-promoting activity. (Nagatsu  et al., 2000) CA. 2000, 133:358971.

The inhibitory effects of myricetin and kaempferol components of Carthamus tinctorius against washed rabbit platelet (WRP) aggregation, 5-HT release, and intercellular free calcium concentration elevation induced by platelet activation factor (PAF) were studied. The platelet aggregation induced by PAF was determined with turbidity assay.  The platelet 5-HT release and platelet inner free calcium concentration elevation induced by PAF were measured by OPT fluorescence assay and Fura-2/AM probe fluorescent technique, respectively.  The WRP aggregation and 5-HT release induced by PAF (9.55 x 10-9 M) were both inhibited by Myr and Kae in a concentation dependent manner. (Chen et al., 2001) CA.2002, 137: 210654.

The antioxidative activities of water and MeOH exts. of seed, sprout and flower of safflower were compared in vitro exptl. models.  Antioxidative activity was measured by inhibition against lipid peroxidation of rat brain microsome, and it was shown in the following order; water extract of flower (97.19%) > MeOH ext. of sprout (81.38%) > MeOH extract of flower (80.97%) > water ext. of seed (68.86%) > water ext. of sprout (64.99%) > MeOH ext. of seed (64.95%).  When antioxidative activity was detd. by thiocyanate method, the MeOH and water exts. of seed showed higher activity than other exts.  In DPPH (a,a'-diphenyl-b-picrylhydrazyl) method, the free radical scavenger ctivity of 0.1% MeOH ext. sprout was higher than other exts.  The concentration of total polyphenolic compound in water and MeOH exts. of flower was 12.70% and 8.05%, water and MeOH exts. of seed was 6.96% and 12.34% and water and MeOH exts. of sprout was 8.75 and 5.10%, respectively. (Kim et al., 2000). CA. 2001,135:121357.

6S,8R-Stereochemistry of the C27- and C29-alkane-6,8-diols isolated from flower. (Ukiya et al., 2000)

Carthamus tinctorius showed anti-senility effect. (Zhang et al., 2001) CA. 2002, 135:221214.

Anti-bone resorption properties of the Korean herbal formulation, Honghwain (HHI; Carthamus tinctorius L. seed) was biochemically investigated. On processing bone metabolism, PGE2 accelerated production of IL-1beta in fetal mouse osteoblast and stimulated physiological activation substance, IL-1beta. The novel class of Src tyrosine kinase inhibitors, Herbimycin A (HERB) and HHI reduced COX-2 mRNA levels as well as PGE2 production induced by IL-1beta, TNF-alpha and IL-6. HHI inhibited in vitro and in vivo bone resorption by inhibition of phosphorylation of peptide substrates. HHI dose-dependently reduced the hypercalcemia induced in mice by IL-1beta and partly prevented bone loss and microarchitectural changes in young ovariectomized rats, showing that the protective effect on bone was exerted via the inhibition of bone resorption. These results indicate that the synergy between IL-beta, TNF-alpha, IL-6 on PGE2 production is due to an enhanced gene expression of COX-2 and that tyrosine kinase (s) are involved in the signal transduction of COX-2 in mouse calvarial osteoblasts. Thus, HHI as a possible Src family kinase inhibitor may be useful for the treatment of diseases associated with elevated bone loss. (Yuk et al., 2002).

Flavonoids, luteolin-acetyl-glucoside and quercetin-7-O- (6''-O-acetyl)-b-D-glucopyranoside showed potent antioxidative activities against 2-deoxyribose degradation and lipid peroxidation in rat liver microsomes.  Luteolin, quercetin, and their corresponding glycosides also exhibited strong antioxidative activity, while acacetin glucuronide and apigenin-6, 8-di-C-glucoside were relatively less active. (Lee et al., 2002). CA. 2002, 137:229312.
