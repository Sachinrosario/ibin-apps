Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Large shrub or small spreading tree, distributed in many parts of central and southern India extending north to Orissa, Chanda and Nasik. Prefers laterite and sandstone hills and black cotton soil. Trees produce root suckers.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

Small tree, sometimes spinescent, bark deeply cracked; branchlets tomentose. Leaves alternate, thinly coriaceous, elliptic, obovate or elliptic-oblong, obtuse or subacute, more or less pubescent above, rusty tomentose or fulvous-pubescent beneath; base acute or obtuse; nerves inconspicuous; petioles densely hairy.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Inflorescence densely fulvous-pubescent, flowers white tetramerous.

Male flowers: 4-10 together, subsessile on short hairy peduncle, bracts small, elliptic. Calyx subglobose, densely fulvous hairy, deeply divided, segments 4, rounded, apiculate, ciliate, and glabrous inside. Corolla 4-fid, lobes acute, with a line of hairs on the back of each lobe. Stamens 16, glabrous, in 2 rows, the inner smaller, anthers ovate, acute, as long as the longer filaments.

Female flowers: solitary, sessile, tetramerous. Calyx fulvous-hairy, lobes twice as long as the tube. Corolla as in the male but longer. Staminodes 7-9, glabrous, uniseriate. Ovary glabrous, 8-celled, styles 4, erect, glabrous.

Fruit globose, glabrous, edible, fruiting calyx nearly flat. Seeds 2-3; albumen not ruminate.
