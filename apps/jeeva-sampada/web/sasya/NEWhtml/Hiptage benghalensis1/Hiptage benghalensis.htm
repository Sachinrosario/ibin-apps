Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

Large, handsome, evergreen climbing shrub. found throughout the india and Andaman Islands, chiefly in damp places. It is cultivated in tropics for fragrant flowers.

Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

Availability: Largest supply from the south zone – chiefly Coorg and Kanara forests. Moderate supplies from North zone. Small supplies from central zone.

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Large woody, much branched climbing shrub; young parts silky. Leaves coriaceous, elliptic-oblong, acuminate or acute, entire, glabrous, strongly nerved beneath, base acute; petioles silky-pubescent. Flowers fragrant, white, in erect tomentose racemes about as long as the leaves, bracts lanceolate. Calyx persistent, densely pubescent outside, lobes oblong, obtuse, one with a large oblong gland at the base outside. Petals twice as long as the sepals, orbicular, clawed, fringed on the margin, the uppermost petal broader than the rest. Carpels with a central wing between the two lateral, the inner wing 1 11/2" long and the 2 lateral wings ¾" long. Seed solitary, globose.

Other uses:

Plant cultivated in gardens for its attractive flowers.

Leaves used in cutaneous diseases. Leaf juice possesses insecticidal properties and used as an application in scabies. Plant also used in chronic rheumatism and asthma.

Leaves used as fodder.
