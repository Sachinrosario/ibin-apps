Taxonomic Details

Name : Lasallia pustulata (L.) Mérat
Family - Umbilicariaceae
Basionym - Lichen pustulatus L.
Synonym - Umbilicaria pustulata (L) Hoffm
Common name - Rocktripe, Crotal Clach Aoil Grey Blister (Gilbert, 2001)

Back to Top

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A foliose lichen commonly found growing on nutrient and mineral rich boulders at higher temperate regions. 

Availability :

The species is wide spread in West Britain, E. Britain, Europe, Canary Island, North America and Asia. In India, it is distributed in Himachal Pradesh and Nagaland.


Conservation/ Threat Status : 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: Thallus dark olive buff, attached to the substratum by a central umbilicus, 4-7cm. across, 150-170 thick, isidiate, isidia laminal, scattered to densely crowded and occurring in patches, darker than surface, lower surface grey black, asci monosporous to disporous, pycnidia present mainly in the peripheral zone of upper surface (Wei & Yumei 1993).
