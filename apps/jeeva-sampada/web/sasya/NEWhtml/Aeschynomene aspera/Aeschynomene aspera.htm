HABIT & HABITAT                                                                                
A genus of shrubs or under shrubs, comprising 90 species, distributed in the warmer parts of the world. Two species occur in India. It is plentiful in Bengal and Assam and is also found in South India and Burma. It is usually met with in marshy places, waterlogged situations, and on the margins of tanks or lakes.

Other uses:

  1. The stem consists of a mass of very light, soft pith which has very good insulating properties. It is used extensively in making sun hats worked on wooden or clay moulds and the sola sheets are wound round in layers. The best quality of pith comes from Bengal, and Calcutta is the centre of trade.
  2. The pith is also used for making toys, artificial flowers; models, etc. It may also be used as a substitute for bottle-corks and in the manufacture of swimming jackets and life belts.
