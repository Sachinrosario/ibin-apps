DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
 Flower: Solitary, hermaphrodite, pale rose coloured, complete, pedicillate; Calyx: Sepals 5, deciduous, imbricate, ovate, aristate; Corolla: Petals 5, clawed, spathulate, imbricate, twice as long as sepals ; Androceium: Stamens 10, inserted in the disk, filaments filiform naked, anthers shortly oblong; Gynoecium: Ovary sessile, 5-gonous, 5-celled, narrowed into 5-gonous subulate style, hairy, style simple, tapering; Fruit: Glandular, pubescent, rounded at the base, pyramidal towards the apex, deeply 5-partite, almost to the axis; Seed: Ovoid, smooth, acute, flattened, smooth;      .   
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting time:  October-December. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by   wind, birds or animals.        
 
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome numbers -18, 22.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
Chemistry: Isolation of ceryl alcohol, b-sitosterol and n-triacontanol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.
Chinovic acid, mp. 305°, from hydrolysate of plant extract. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.
Fagogenin, mp. 307°, genin A, mp. 326°, and genin B, mp. 247°. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.
Stigmasterol and campesterol isolated from Egyptian plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.
Harmine, two unidentified compounds and three triterpenoids of oleanane group isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).
Alanine, arginine, glycine, isoleucine, leucine, lysine, phenylalanine, proline, tyrosine and valine isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).
Two new tripterpenoid saponins isolated from aerial parts and characterized as 23,28-di-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl-taraxer-20-en-28-oic acid and 3b,28-di-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl-23-hydroxytaraxer-20-en-28-oic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
Quercetin and kaempferol from leaves and flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
Docosyl docosanoate isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.
Three new bisdesmosidic saponins were isolated from aerial parts of Fagonia cretica. They were characterized as hederagenin-3-O-a-L-arabinopyranosyl,28-O-b-D-glucopyranoside, hederagenin-3-O-b-D-xylopyranoysl(1®2)-a-L-arabinopyranosyl-28-O-b-D-glucopyrnoside and oleanolic acid 3-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl(1®2)-a-L-arabinopyranosyl-28-O-b-D-glucopyranoside. (Melek et al, 1994). C.A. 1995, 122, 128535h.
Four new triterpenoid saponins were isolated and identified from the aerial parts of Fagonia cretica. The structures of the saponins were assigned by spectral analyses (FABMS, 1H, 13C NMR, 1H-1H COSY, TOCSY, HMQC and HMBC spectra) and NOE experiments. (Khalik et al, 2000). MAPA 2000-06-3616.
Three triterpenoid saponins including two new ones were isolated and identified from the aerial parts of Fagonia cretica. The new saponins were characterized as 3-O-(b-D-xylopyranosyl (1®2)a-L-arabinopyranosyl)27-hydroxyoleanolic acid 28-O-(b-D-glucopyranosyl(1®6)b-D-glucopyranosyl)ester and 3b-O-(b-D-xylopyranosyl(1®2)a-L-arabinopyranosyl) olean-12-en-27-al-28-oic acid 28-O-(b-D-glucopyranosyl(1®6)b-D-glycopyranosyl ester. The structures were determined by spectral analyses. (Abdel-Khalik et al, 2001).
