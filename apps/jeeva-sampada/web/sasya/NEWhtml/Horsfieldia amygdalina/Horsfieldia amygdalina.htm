Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 A tall, glabrous tree, c. 65 ft. high, found in Sibsagar, Cachar, North Cachar hills, Lushai hills, Goalpara, Garo hills and the foot of Khasi hills.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves aromatic, 4.0-10.5 in. long and 1.0-4.0 in. broad, entire, elliptic to elliptic-lanceolate or obtuse, glabrous, shining above flowers unisexual; fruits obscurely trigonous, ovoid, 1.3-2.0 in. long; seeds oblong-cylindric, 0.8 in. long with a thin, fleshy, yellowish aril.
