Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Large tree, occurs in temperate Himalayan regions, suitable for growing on river banks, ravines and newly formed soils, useful for soil conservation in landslide areas. More common in western parts on river sides and ravines. Distributed in western Himalayas from Yamuna westwards to Kashmir at altitudes between 900 and 2,700 m.

The tree is always found in moist situations, mainly along the beds and banks of streams and rivers. It springs up gregariously on new land thrown up in beds of rivers, where it forms pure crops, sometimes of considerable extent.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A large deciduous tree, up to 30 m in height and 4 m in girth. Leaves elliptic-ovate, 10-20 cm long. Male spikes 5 cm long in terminal, erect often leafing racemes. Female spikes 2.5-5.0 cm. nuts with narrow thickened margins.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Used as fuelwood.

Wood: The wood is reddish white to light brownish grey, very lustrous, light (sp gr 0.43; av wt 450 kg/cu m) and soft.

It is largely used for house building, where better timber is not available, for bed-steads, country trucks and solid wheels, hooked sticks in rope bridges.Wood used for carpentry work.
