Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A densely branched shrub, rarely erect. Stem supported by aerial roots, frequent along streams.

Availability :

Almost throughout India.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Common 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Much branched shrub or small tree, the branches spreading; trunk with basal prop roots. Leaves ensiform, to 200 x 8 cm, glaucous below, with princles on margins and dorsal midrib. Male inflorescence of c. 9-13 spikes, cream, subtended by bracts. Spikes to 12 cm, composed of many racemose phalangens and many stamens; anthers elongate, apiculate, white pollen grains spinulose. Female inflorescence a solitary, terminal cephilium, pendulous in fruit. Phalanges mostly 5-15 celled, the carpels concentrically arranged, their apices convex, with U- or V-shaped stigmas; style 0. Flowers yellowish, fragrant.
