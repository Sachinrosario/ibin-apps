Taxonomic Details

Name : Aspicilia calcarea (L.) Mudd.
Family - Hymeneliaceae
Basionym - Lichen calcareus L.
Synonym - Lecanora calcarea (L.) sommerf
Common name - English Name - Lime stone Lichen.
Gaelic name - Crotal clach Aoil.

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A crustose lichen forming patch to 20 cm diameter on calcareous rocks in the temperate regions of N. Hemisphere.

Availability :

A very common widespread species in British Isand, Eupore and North America. In India it is widely distributed in temperate regions of Western and northern Himalayas, in Himachal Pradesh, Garhwal and Kumaon Himalayas and Jammu and Kashmir area (Upreti & Chatterjee 2002).
