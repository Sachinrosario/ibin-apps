HABIT & HABITAT:
Twiners, stout; young part and inflorescence densely softly pubescent; sap milky. It is found to occur in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves broadly ovate to orbicular, 7.0-14.5 x 6.5-10.5 cm, abruptly acuminate at apex, cordate, truncate or rounded at base, glandular above; petioles 4.5-6.5 cm long, softly pubescent. Flowers in corymbose cymes; peduncles interpetiolar. Calyx deeply divided; lobes oblong or lanceolate, c 3 mm long, acute. Corolla greenish-yellow, c 10 mm across, hairy outside; lobes oblong, shorter than the tube, obtuse, spreading. Follicles ovoid-lanceolate, 9.5-14.0 cm long, wrinkled, horny. Seeds ovoid or oblong, c 1.2 cm long.

Chemistry:
17b- marsdenin, 17a-marsdenin, D-cymarose, b-D-glucosyl-L-thevetose, cinnamic acid and acetic acid obtained by acid hydrolysis of glycoside mixture from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

A new polyhydroxy pregnane ester- tenasogenin (fig. 4a) isolated from seeds and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984) a   

Hydrolysis of pregnane glycosides of 2-deoxysugars afforded cissogenin (fig. 4b) from mixture of genins(Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b

Tenacigenin A (fig. 1), B (fig. 2)and C (fig. 3)were isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c

Isolation and structure elucidation of new glycoside methyl-4-O (3-o-methyl-6-deoxy-b-D-cymaroside from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d

Deoxysugar glycosides from seeds on acid hydrolysis yielded isodrevogenin-P (fig. 5b), drevogenin Q (fig. 5a), cymarose, aslepobiose, canarose and 3-O-methyl-6-deoxy-D-allose, structure of drevogenin Q was established as 11-O- isovaleryl-12-O-acetyldrevogenin P. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e

Five new glycosides – tenacissisides A, B, C, D and E (fig. 6a-6e)isolated and characterized as 3-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl(1® 4)-3-O- methyl-6-deoxy-b-D-allopyranosyl (1®4)-b-D-oleandropyranosides of tencigenins BI, BII,BIV and BV respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)

Six polyoxypregnanes (I-VI) (fig. 7)  isolated and characterised as 11a- O-tigloyl-12b-O-acetyltenacigenin B, 11a-O- benzoyl-12b-O-acetyl tenacigenin B, 11a-O-[2-methylbutyryl]-12b-O-acetyltenacigenin B, 11a-O-[2-methylbutyryl]- 12b- O-tigloyltenacigenin B, 11a-O-[2-methylbutyryl]-12b-O-benzoyltenacigenin B, and 11a,12b-O-ditigloyl-17b- tenacigeninB respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)

Two new polyoxypregnanes, designated marstenacigenins A and B,along with a known compound dresgenin, were isolated from the mild acid hydrolyzate of the ethanol ext. of the stems of Marsdenia tenacissima.  Their structures were deduced as 12b-cinnamoyldihydrosarcostin and 12b,20-dibenzoyl dihydrosarcostin, respectively. (Qui et al, 1996)


Pharmacology:
Alcoholic extract of root abolished contractions induced by histamine and acetylcholine in guinea pig ileum. It also induced mild CNS depression. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for asthma, cough (Root, Maheshwari and Singh, 1987); fever (Root, Singh and Maheshwari, 1983, Maheshwari et al, 1986); stomachache (Root, Tarafder, 1983); urinary complaints (Root, Maheshwari et al, 1986); Edible (Leaf, Ball, 1967).
