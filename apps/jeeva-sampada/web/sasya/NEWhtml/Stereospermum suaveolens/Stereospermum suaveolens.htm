Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A deciduous tree occurs throughout the moist parts of India.


Availability :

West Bengal and Assam supply fairly large quantities. Moderate supplies from west coast forests of Mysore and Kerala. Also from Andamans.


Conservation/ Threat Status :Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A deciduous tree, more or less pubescent. Young parts viscous hairy. Leaves simply pinnate. Leaflets 3-4 pairs, broadly elliptic, usually acuminate, often serrulate, rough above, pubescent beneath, base usually rounded and unequal sided. Main nerves 6-8 pairs with reticulate venation between.

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers sweetly fragrant, in large lax trichotomous viscidly hairy panicles. Calyx companulate, viscidly hairy; lobes 3-5, short, broad. Corolla infundibuliform, dull purple, pubescent outside, bearded inside on the lower, glabrous on the upper side; limb oblique, 2-lipped; lobes rounded, crisped-crenate, the lower 3 lobes longer than the upper 2. Filaments without a tuft of hairs at the base. Capsules straight, cylindric, slightly ribbed, somewhat rough with elevated whitish specks; valves thick, hard. Seeds with a long membranous wing at each end, the nucleus furrowed across the middle, sunk into the notches of the cylindric brown spongy dissepiment.

Floral and seed biology:

Seedling Germination: Natural regeneration is abundant from seeds and suckers. It starts early in the rainy season. Seeds fall for the most part after the fire season and seedlings are hardy.

Artificial reproduction can be carried out by direct line sowing and by transplanitng the nursery raised seedlings. For nursery purpose, fresh seeds should be sown during April-May. Transplanting the seedlings during the second rainy season, after pruning the stem down 2.5 cm. from the ground level and trimming the roots down to length of 22 cm has been found to give the best results.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Wood excellent firewood. It also makes a good quality charcoal. Calorific value : 4,724 cal., 8,504 Btu.

Wood: Wood employed for constructional work, doors and windows, planks and beams, carts, carriages and wagons, cabinet work, furniture and tool handles. Suggested for turnery.

Other uses:

Root bark constituent of an ayurvedic compound, Dasmoola. Roots, bark, flowers of medicinal use. Ashes of plant employed in the preparation of alkaline water and caustic pastes.
