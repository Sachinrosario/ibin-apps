Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A much-branched, deciduous, rather short-lived (30 years), monoecious tree, usually 7-8 m in height, native to the sub-tropical region comprising southern China Viet-Nam, Laos and Burma, and introduced into India and found growing in Himachal Pradesh, Assam, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: A much-branched, deciduous, rather short-lived (30 years), monoecious tree, usually 7-8 m in height; Leaves cordate, dark green; flowers unisexual in clusters with one or more pistillate flowers surrounded by a number of staminate flowers; fruits spheroid of sometimes top-shaped, 3.75-5.0 cm long and almost equally broad, containing 3-5 one seeded segments, with a smooth outer husk, pericarp pointed at the summit.

Miscellaneous Information

Tung oil is being increasingly used in fungicides as a drying agent. The oil has an excellent drying power, whereas various fungicidal compounds that might be used are essentially non-drying. The two characteristics are combined to produce a fungicide coating 

The oil produces irritant effects in the gastro-intestinal tract causing acute diarrhoea, loss of appetite, atony of rumen, general weakness, emaciation and death.
