HABIT & HABITAT
Perennial herbs, ca 30 cm high; root-fibrous cylindric with ellipsoid tubers hanging from them and commonly occurs in Deccan Peninsula and Central India. Common from the N. Circars and the Concon, Travancore. MYANMAR.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herbs, ca 30 cm high; root-fibrous cylindric with ellipsoid tubers hanging from them. Leaves radical, crowded at base, membranous, usually ensiform, falcately recurved, 13.5-58.5 x 1.5-4.0 cm, wavy or crisped on margins, sessile.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers in simple or shortly branched racemes; scapes 15-90 cm long with a small spathes; pedicels jointed at or below the middle. Perianth white; segments elliptic, 1.2-1.5 cm long. Capsules obovoid, ca 1.0 x 0.5 cm, shining, transversely veined, 2-lobed at apex; cells 4-6 seeded. Seeds irregularly orbicular, ca 0.2-0.3 cm in diam., black. It flowers and fruits between May- September.
CIMAP:
Inflorescence: Branched raceme ; Flower: White, bracts linear, purpulish, papery, pedicellate ; Perianth: White, segments 6, elliptic, 3-5 nerved ;Androecium: Stamens 6 adnate to the base, anthers twice as long as filaments ; Gynoecium:  Ovary 3-locular, ovules 4, style filiform, stigma minute; Fruit: Capsule white, broad 2-lobed at the tip and base, greenish-yellow, trigonous ; Seed: Flat and black. Flowering & Fruiting : July-August. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by  birds or animals.
