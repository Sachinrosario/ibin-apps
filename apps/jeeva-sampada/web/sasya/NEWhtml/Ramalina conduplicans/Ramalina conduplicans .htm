Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A fruticose lichen common on trees in open or shady places in the higher elevation in tropical regions and temperate areas.

Availability :

The species is common in Bhutan, China, Japan and Taiwan. In India it is distributed in Assam, Himachal Pradesh and Uttaranchal areas.
Conservation/ Threat Status : 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: Thallus suberect and firmly attached to the substratum by a narrow basal disc, greenish grey to stramineous, 3-8cm long, 175-425µm thick, lower surface paler than the upper surface, medulla loose, not fistulose and 40-175µm thick, apothecia subterminal or lateral and constricted at the base, disc grayish to brownish, flat or convex, asci cylindrical –clavate, spores straight or slightly curved (Awasthi 2000).

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
As spices: The tribals of Madhya Pradesh used this species as flavoring agents to increase the taste and fragrance of meat, pulses and other important vegetables (Lal et al. 1985).
