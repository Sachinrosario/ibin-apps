Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Bulbous perennial herb growing on hill slopes and also in forests in Maharashtra.

Availability :

Maharashtra, Sahyadri range, Mahabaleshwar.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Rare, endemic. 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A bulbous perennial herb, bulb ovoid 10-15 cm in height, 25-30 cm in diam., without distinct neck, roots fleshy. Leaves developed only after flowering, lorate, brightly green with entire margin, 45-60 x 7-10 cm. Scape solitary, 25-40.5 cm in height, subterete. Inflorescence umbel, 15-20 flowered, bracts 2, lanceolate 3-5 cm long, greenish-white. Flowers fragrant, perianth tube not much curved, greenish, 3-5 cm long; perianth lobes 2-5 x 1.5 x 1.9 cm, pure white, oblanceolate, obtuse, cuspidate. Stamens 6, 0.5-0.7 cm long, very short than perianth lobe; anthers 0.5 cm in length, yellow. Ovary 3-celled, style filiform, included in the perianth tube. Fruit 1-2 cm in diam., few seeded, seeds green.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:


Breeding system and varietal improvement:
It reproduces by seeds. Similarly new bulbs are formed on old bulbs.

Phenology: Species starts flowering by May and continuous upto June. Scapes appear in month of May as soon as the mist comes on the hills before rain. It produces leaves only after flowering. Vegetative growth continues upto September-October. By the end of October the aerial leaves dry.


Pollinators: Insects. Crinum species are mostly pollinated by hawkmoths.
