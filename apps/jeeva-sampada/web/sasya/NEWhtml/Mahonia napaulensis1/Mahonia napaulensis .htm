Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:  
A tall evergreen shrub found in Nepal at altitudes of 1,200-2,100 m. distributed in Asia and North and Central America. Fifteen species occur in India. which fourteen are iound in the sub-temperate Himalayas: one is confined to the hills of S. India.

Habitat:    Forests and Open areas.
Biotic Zone:Hindukush himalaya, Khasia hills and Temperate Himalayas.
Altitude:    0-8000,  
Availability : 
Misc:    Garhwal to Bhutan, Nilgiri hills and W.Nepal to Bhutan,  
Country:    India and Mergui.
Zone:East, North and North East.
State:Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya and West Bengal.
District:    Darjeeling and Kameng. Status :

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
Flowers small, yellow, in erect fascicled racemes : berries ovoid, blue-black.

Phenology : 
The plant flowers in March- May and flowering continues up to the end of the rainy season. In Darjeeling, where the plant has been reported to occur, flowering has not been observed and the growth of the plant, is stunted.
