Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Slender, erect or sparsely branched tuberous perennial herbs. Common in Ghat areas.
Availability :

Western Ghats of Peninsular India.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Common and Endemic. 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Plants slender, erect, sparsely branched above, hirsute to hispidulous, 40-120 cm tall, from a conspicuous swollen tuberous base. Leaves to triangular-ovate, 5-8 cm long, 5-6 cm broad, ternate-pinnate or bipinnate, the leaflets ovate-lanceolate to ovate, petiolate, mostly acute, the larger frequently 1-3-lobed, mucronulate-serrate, sparsely hirsute, 1-2 cm long, 0.5-1 cm broad; petiole 3-7 cm long, abruptly dilated and broadly sheathing; cauline leaves reduced upward, the uppermost often reduced to oblong-oval, bladless sheaths. Peduncles slender, 2-10 cm long, or sometimes abortive. Rays usually 4-10, slender, unequal, 1-5 cm long, hispidulous. Umbellets 6-12-flowered, about half fertile, the mature pedicels 2-5 mm long. Involucel bracteoles 3-5, oblong to ovate, venose, 3-6 mm long. Strongly radiant marginal flowers 3-8, perfect, the regular central and usually staminate flowers 3-10; petals 1-1.5 mm long, the radiant marginal petal 6-10 mm in diam.; calyx teeth small, ovate; functional style ca 1 mm long, slightly exceeding the low conical, massive stylopodium. Fruit 4.5-6 mm long, 2.5-3 mm broad, glabrous, the ribs prominent, unwinged.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence of lax terminal and lateral compound umbels, Umbellets few flowered, flowers white with coloured veins, dimorohic, the marginal radiant and perfect, the central regular and usually staminate. Fruit oval or elliptic, rounded at apex and base, strongly compressed dorsally. Pinda reproduces by sexual means.

Breeding system and varietal improvement:
It propagates through seeds

Phenology:

With start of monsoon, the plants sprout and flower during July and August. Seed shedding is observed in October-November when plant shade their aerial parts
