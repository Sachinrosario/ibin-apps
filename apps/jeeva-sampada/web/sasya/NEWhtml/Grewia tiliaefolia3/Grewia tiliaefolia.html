DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Trees, 6-15 m tall; bark peeling off. Leaves elliptic, elliptic-ovate, ovate or ovate-rotundate, 1.7-36 x 1.0-24 cm, acuminate or rounded at apex, obliquely cordate at base, serrate to crenate-serrate at margin, glabrescent above, sparsely pubescent or tomentose beneath; petioles up to 4 cm long.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Flowers 3-6, in axillary cymes; peduncles 1-2 cm long; pedicels 4-13 mm long. Sepals elliptic or lanceolate, 5-8 x 3 mm, subacute at apex, tomentose outside. Petals elliptic-oblong or spethulate, 3.0-4.5 x 1.5 mm, obtuse, notched at apex, sparsely ciliate at base, yellow. Stamens many. Drupes 2.5-5.0 x 7-10 mm, black, distinctly 2-lobed; lobes globose, sparsely hirsute.
CIMAP:
Inflorescence: Small Umbels ;Flower: buds tomentose, obovoid-oblong or subglobose, pedicels axillary, thick, bract beneath the pedicels linear-lanceolate, hermaphrodite; Calyx: Sepals pubescent outside, oblong, subacute, glabrous within; Corolla: Petals about half as long as sepals, oblong, entire, notched, glandular; Androceium: Stamens indefinite, inserted on the turbinate often glandular torus, filaments free, anther cells 2, parallel, dehiscing longitudinally;; Gynoecium: Ovary vilous, 2-celled, styles longer than the stamens, stigma somewhat irregular 5-lobed; Fruit: Drupe size of pea, black, globose; Seed: 1-2 celled; Flowering & Fruiting time: May-August.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
The roots are used in syphilis.


Chemistry:
Among the complex mixture of biologically active compounds in the bark of Grewia tiliaefolia, a plant used in folklore, lupeol, a constituent of the bark, has been used as an analytical marker indicative of the quality of the plant. A sensitive and reliable quantitative high-performance thin-layer chromatographic method has been developed for the determination of lupeol from Grewia tiliaefolia. Chloroform extracts of bark from five different sources were used for HPTLC on silica gel with benzene-ethyl acetate, 95 + 5, as mobile phase. Under these conditions the RF of lupeol was 0.40. (Badami et al, 2002)
