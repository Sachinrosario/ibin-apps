DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Tall erect shrubs, 1.2-1.8 m tall, densely wooly with silvery tomentose hairs. Stems and branches stout, bluntly quadrangular, cloathed with white soft hairs. Leaves thick, oblong-lanceolate, 6.3-10.0 x 2.0-4.5 cm, acute or acuminate at apex, acute or rounded at base, entire or crenate-serrate at margin, pale above, white below.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
   Inflorescence: Axillary whorls of dense cymes; Flower:Pale purple occasionally white;   Calyx: Ovoid, straight, 5-toothed ; Corolla: Corolla tube short, annulatewithin, upper lip erect  ;Androecium: Stamens exserted, anther 2-celled, conviving; Gynoecium: Ovary superior, bilocular, style bifid;  Fruit: Ellipsoid, smooth, compressed nutlets; Seed: Ovoid, glabrous . Flowering & Fruiting : Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

Floral and seed biology:
Flowers in verticillaster, purple, 4 cm in diam., densely wooly; bracts filiform, 1.0-1.5 cm long. Calyx 10 mm long, densely scarious outside; lobes 5, equal, lanceolate, 1 cm long. Corolla pale purple, 2-lipped, 15-30 mm long, hairy outside; upper lip 5 mm, outside villous; lower lip 1.2 cm long, papilose. Stamens 4. Nutlets ellipsoid, 3-4 mm long, compressed, inner face slightly angular, dorsal face rounded, smooth, polished brown.October-November.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
An infussion of aromatic bitter leaves is used in affection of stomach, bowels and intermittent fever. It is given to children in  colic, dyspepsia and fever arising  from teething. Decoction of plant or essential oil distilled from the leaves is used externally in rheumatism. The root, stem and leaves are used in the antidotal treatment of snake bite.

Pharmacology:
The ethanolic extract of the aerial parts of Anisomeles malabarica exhibited significant hypocholesterolemic activity in rabbits fed high cholesterol diet. (Kalhoro et al, 1997). MAPA 1998, 9802-0825.
