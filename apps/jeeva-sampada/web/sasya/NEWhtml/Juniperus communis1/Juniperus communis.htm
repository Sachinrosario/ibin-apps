The antibacterial activity of essential oil and various metabolites from Juniperus communis was studied. (Comic et al, 1997). C.A. 1999, 131, 211490w
Samples of Juniperus communis fruits were collected from different sites in Yugoslavia and the content of essential oil was determined. Mature 3-8 mm fruits were analyzed, with the content of unripe fruits £ 5%. The content of essential oil ranged between 2.30 and 2.66%. (Matove and Lavadinovic, 1999). C.A. 2001, 134, 371565q
Isocupressic acid was determined in 15 species and significant concentrations (> 0.5% dry wt.) was found also in Janipurus communis including many other plants. (Gardner and Jamer, 1999). C.A. 1999, 131, 112454f.
The essential oil was isolated by hydrodistillation and glycosides were extracted with Ethyl acetate from needles of common juniper. The obtained essential oil was fractionated on microcolumn with solvents of different polarity. All fractions were analyzed by gas chromatography - mass spectrophotmetery (GC-MS) on two columns with different polarity of the stationary phases. Sixty-three compounds were identified. The main components of juniper needle oil were: a-pinene (16.9%), sbinene (12.1%), terpinene-4-ol (7.7%), b-phellandrene (7.3%), widdrene (6.4%), g-terpinene (5.9%), b-terpinene (4.3%), a-terpinene (5.8%) and other compound sin smaller quantities. After isolation, final purification of the glycosides and enzymic hydrolysis, the liberated aglycons were analyzed in the ame way as fractions of the essential oil. Twenty-two aglycons were identified. The main aglycons were: 3-phenyl-2-propen-1-ol, (32.8%), 2-phenyl-propanol (6.0%), thymoquinone (4.6%), 3,4,5-trimethoxybenzaldehyde (4.3%), 3-methyl-2-buten-1-ol (2.8%), 2-phenylethanol (2.8%), 3-phenyl-2-propenal (2.7%), methyl-3-hydroxybenzoate (2.6%) and p-cymen-8-ol, O-methoxybenzyl alcohol, Methyl salycilate, a-methyl-benzyl-alcohol, 1-octen-3-ol and other compounds. (Mastelic et al, 2000). C.A. 2000, 133, 293520j.
