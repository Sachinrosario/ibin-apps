DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
      Inflorescence: Axillary, small cymes ; Calyx:  Lobed, funnel shaped, often deeply truncate in the female often longer than male; Corolla:  Tubular, salver shaped, shortly lobed ; Male Flower: Stamens 4-64, anthers linear, rarely short, ovary rudimentary ; Female Flower: Staminodes 0-16, ovary 4-5 celled, ovules many, style 1-4; Fruit: Globose, ellipsoid woody; Seed: Oblong and compressed. 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : March-July. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant requires well drained loamy soil.

Nutrient requirements: Plant responds well to application of manure and fertilizers.

Water management:    Plant requires irrigation during dry season.                                   

Harvesting period / Method:  Plant are harvested when plant is 3-4 month old.

Uses:
Chemistry:  

b-sitosterol, lupeol, betulin, oleanolic acid and myricyl alcohol from leaves; saponin from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.
Betulinic acid from bark, betulin from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.
b-sitosterol and its glucoside, betulin and a new monohydroxy triterpene ketone, mp. 196°C from fruit pulp. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
Betulinic acid from seeds and fruit pulp; gallic acid from fruit pulp; hexacosane, hexacosanol, gallic acid and a triterpene ketone also isolated from fruit pulp. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
A new leucoanthocyanin - leucopelargonidin-3-O-a-L-rhamnoyranoside (fig. 1) - from stem. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.
A new dihydroflavanol glycoside (II, fig. 2) isolated from roots and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.
A new ketol - nonadecan-7-ol-2-one - isolated and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
Marsformosanone (fig. 4) and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
A new glycoside - 5,7,3',4'-tetrahydroxyflavanone-3-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl(1®4)-a-L-rhamnopyranosised (fig. 3) - isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
A new lupane-type triterpene -pergrinol (fig. 5) - isolated from dried leaves and fruits and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany: Medicine for abortifacient (Fruit + Stem bark, Tarafder, 1983); Blisters in mouth (Fruit, Jain, 1965); Cholera (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Diarrhoea (Fruit, Jain, 1965); Dysentery (Fruit, Roy and Chaturvedi, 1986; Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Menorrhagia (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Tonic (Fruit, Roy and Chaturvedi, 1986; Safui and Bhattacharya, 1984); Edible (Fruit, Maji and Sikdar, 1982; Goel et al, 1984; 3; Dam and Hajra, 1981; Seed, Jain, 1964)
