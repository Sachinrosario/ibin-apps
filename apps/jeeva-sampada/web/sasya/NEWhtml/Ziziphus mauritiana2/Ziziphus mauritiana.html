DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Large shrubs or trees, 1-15 m tall; bark dark grey or nearly black, with deep vertical cracks, reddish and fibrous inside; spines solitary or in pairs, straight or one of them recurved. Leaves broadly elliptic or ovate, 2-9.8 x 1-4.3 cm, apex mucronate, margins serrulate or entire, hairy or glabrous above, brown or white silky tomentose beneath, basally 3-nerved.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers 4-6 mm across, green-white in 10-13 flowered axillary cymes or fascicles. Calyx lobes glabrous within, tomentose without. Petals spathulate. Stamens equal to petals. Drupes ca 1cm diam. Seeds 1 or 2, compressed.
