Taxonomic Details
Correct Name : Remusatia vivipara (Roxb.) Schott.
Family :  Araceae
Synonyms : Arum vivipare; Arum viviparum; Caladium viviparum; Rhus hookeri;
Common Names :
Tamil: Kalshembu

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 Tuberous aroid from which arises leaves and simple upright stems bearing scale- leaves in the axils of which are bulbils covered with hooked cataphylls, which serve to disperse them when mature.

Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: 
Tubers depressed-globuse,3-7 cm ,pinkish outside,pale-pink inside;bulbiforous shoots 15- 30 x 0.5 - 0.7 cm; leaves broad,ovate,cordate,acuminate 12-42x9-30 cm; pestioles 20-50 cm; penducules 6- 10 cm, sheathed at base; tube of spathe oviod ,2.5-4.5 cm, green outisde,pale inside; limp trapezoid, 8-12x 4-5 cm,pale green,yellow inside;spathes slightly exceeding the tube.
