REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Yellow purple racemes ; Flower: Bracteate, pedicellate, regular; Calyx: Sepals erect, lateral, saccate at the base; Corolla: petals clawed at the base of the blade ;Androecium: Stamens 6, two outer opposite, lateral ; Gynoecium: Ovary 2-celled, style short, ovule 2, stigma simple; Fruit: Pods recurved and undulate; Seed: 1, seriate, flattened. Flowering & Fruiting : Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by animals.

Pharmacology:
Cheirotoxin, alliside, erysimoside glucoerysimoside and glucobipindogulomethyloside isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Isorhamnetin-3-arabino-7-rhamnoside, isorhamnetin-3-gluco-7-rhamnoside, quercetin-3-gluco-7-rhamnoside and quercetin-7-rhamnoside isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Emmenagogic, diuretic and leaf shows antioxidant activity. (Mantle et al, 2000). C.A. 2001, 134, 188145p.

Cardio-active glucosides are present in Cheiranthus cheiri. (Hahn and Hahn, 2001). C.A. 2001, 135, 262073c.
