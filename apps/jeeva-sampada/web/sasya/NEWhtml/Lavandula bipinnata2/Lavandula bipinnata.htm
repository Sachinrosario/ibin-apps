HABIT & HABITAT

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A slender erect herb; stems simple or branched, leafy, quadrangular, finely pubescent. Leaves sessile or nearly so, 3.8-10 cm long as broad as long, pinnatipartite or very deeply pinnatisect, the lobes linear, entire, cut or toothed, obtuse or subacute, 1.5-3 mm broad (rarely broader), glabrous or pubescent above, pale and pubescent beneath. Flowers in spike, which are simple, or with few or with many branches, sometimes umbellately arranged; bracts finely pubescent, persistent, 6-8 mm long with a short broadly ovate, strongly nerved base, produced into a long capillary awn at apex. Fruiting calyx 5 mm long, tubular, slightly curved, clothed with penicillate tips. Corolla blue or white, pubescent outside, exceeding 1.3 cm long; tube 8 mm long, the lower half very slender, the upper half slightly dilated; upper lip 3 mm long, oblong, with 2 short rounded lobes; lower lip 5 partite, the middle lobe oblong, rounded, more than twice as long as the ovate obtuse lateral lobes. Nutlets x 1.25 cm, oblong-ellipsoid, smooth, very mucilaginous when wetted, black when ripe, with a large white oblong areole on the dorsal side.
