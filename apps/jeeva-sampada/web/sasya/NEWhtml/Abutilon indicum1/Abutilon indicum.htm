HABIT & HABITAT
A hairy under shrub with golden yellow flowers, abundant throughout the hotter parts of India. 
In India it is found all along the roadsides, Coastal areas, Forest clearings, forest edges, forests margins, Gardens and Roadsides, Hill slopes, hills, Open areas, Open forest, Plains, Road sides, Secondary forests and Villages, Tropical Forests, Warmer places and waste land,
BioticZone: Konkan plain, Pantropical, SubHimalayan tracts, Subtropics, Tropics and West Coast,
Soil: It grows well in disturbed soils and gravelly soils.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Annual or perennial erect subshrublike herbs, up to 1-2.5 m, multi-branched, whole plant gray puberulous. Stipule subulate, 1-2 mm, curved outwards; petiole 2-4 cm, gray puberulous and sparsely hairy with silklike long hair of 1 mm; leaf blade ovate orbicular or nearly orbicular, 3-9 ´ 2.5-7 cm, densely gray stellate puberulous, base cordate, margin irregularly serrate, apex acute or acuminate. Flowers solitary, axillary, yellow, 2-2.5 cm in diam.; pedicels ca. 4 cm, articulate near apex, gray stellate puberulous. Calyx disk-shaped, green, 6-10 mm in diam., densely gray puberulous, lobes 5, broad ovate, apex acute. Petals 5, 7-8 mm. Staminal column stellate scabrous. Carpels 15-20, circled. Style branches 5; stigma capitate. Fruit ca. 1.5 cm in diam., black, mericarps 15-20, apex truncate, slightly awned, stellate long scabrous. Seeds reniform, sparsely stellate.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
Bark,leaf, stem, seeds and roots.

Uses:
  1. Stem contain good fibre, suitable for cordage.
  2. Leaves yield a mucilaginous extract, used as a demulcent.
  3. Infusion of roots is given in fevers as a cooling remedy; also said to be used in the treatment of leprosy.
  4. Seeds are laxative and demulcent, given in the treatment of coughs.
  5. Bark is astringent and diuretic.

Harvesting Practices


Produce and Products :
The stems on retting, yield fibers which is suitable for ropes. This is used in a domestic way but does not enter into commerce.
