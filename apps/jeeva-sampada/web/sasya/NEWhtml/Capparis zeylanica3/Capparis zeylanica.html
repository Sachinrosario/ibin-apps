HABIT & HABITAT:
Climbing, terete shrub, armed with recurved stipular thorns; branches brownish, tomentose. It is found through out India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves variable, elliptic to elliptic-oblong, 2.0-7.5 x 1.5-5.0 cm, acute or retuse at apex, narrowed at base, coriaceous, shining above, glabrous. Flowers axillary or solitary or 2-3, one above the other, 3-5 cm across, white. Sepals 4 (2+2). Petals white or purplish. Stamens 30-45. Gynophore pubescent at base. Fruits berry, ellipsoid, 2-3 cm across, reddish brown, smooth. Seeds many, reniform, about 5 mm long, embedded within the pulp.
