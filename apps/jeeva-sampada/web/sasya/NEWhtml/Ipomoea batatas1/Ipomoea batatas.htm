 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
Prostrate, annual herb, glabrous or slightly hairy. It is found in Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Assam, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Laccadives.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Prostrate, annual herb, glabrous or slightly hairy. Tubers elongated or fusiform, red, white or yellow. Leaves ovate-cordate, 2.5-8.0 x 2.0-6.0 cm, angular, 3-7 deeply palmately lobed, acute at apex, cordate or truncate at base. Flowers purple, in axillary cyme; bracts caducous. Sepals elliptic, glabrous. Corolla funnel shaped. Stamens included. Capsules ovoid. Seeds glabrous.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
The tuberous roots are sweet, refrigerant, laxative, aphrodisiac, diuretic and tonic.  They are useful in vitiated conditions of pitta, burning sensation, hyperdipsia, constipation, strangury, renal and vesical calculi, diabetes and general weakness.

Uses: 

  1. In Ayurveda system of medicine the roots are sweet and used in strangury, burning sensation, thirst and urinary discharges. In Unani system of medicine roots used to prevent diarrhoea.

Chemistry
A new furanoterpenoid - dehydroipomeamarone - isolated from roots infected with Ceratocystis fumbriata. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
N-pentacosane, n-heptacosane, n-nonacosane, b-sitosterol, palmitic acid and NaCl isolated along with three unknown compounds - A, B and C with mp. 220°C, 194°C, and 133°C respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
Lubimin (fig. 1a) and oxylubimin (fig. 1b) isolated from diseased tubers and their structures elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.
Myoporone (fig. 2a) and its two stress metabolites (I, fig. 2b and II, fig. 2c) isolated from plant treated with mercuric chloride. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
Cis-zeatin riboside isolated from tubers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
A cytokinin -9b-D- glucopyranosyl-6-(3-methyl-2-butenylamino)purine -isolated from tubers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
Pigment P2 isolated from roots and identified as cyanidin- 3-(a-D-glucopyranosyl-b-D-fructofuranoside)-5-(a-D-xyloside) acylated with caffeic and ferulic acids in sugar moiety. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.
Structure elucidation of 7-hydroxycostal (fig. 3a) and 7-hydroxycostol (fig. 7b) isolated together with three selinenes from plant infected with black rot fungus, Ceratocystis fimbriata. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.
Absolute configuration of (+)ipomeamarone (fig. 8). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.
Two major proteins - sporamins A and B - separated by elcetrophoresis on polyacrylamide from total proteins of tuberous roots and partially characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
7-hydroxycostal and 7-hydroxycostol synthesized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
Luteochromene isolated from tubers of Brazilian plant and shown to be mixture of (5R,6S,5'R,8'R) and (5R,6S,5'R,8'S)5,6,5',8'-diepoxy-5,6,5',8'-tetrahydro-b-b-carotene. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.
Isolation of 5,6:5',6'-diepoxy-5,6,5',6'-tetrahydro-b-b-carotene and determination of its absolute configuration as 5R,6S,5'R,6'S. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.
Ellagic acid and 3,5-dicaffeoylquinic acid isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
Isolation of a new triterpene -boehmeryl acetate (fig. 5) from roots and its structure determination. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
Fumaric and succinic acids identified in stem. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
3-diglucosido-5-glucosides of cyanidin, peonidin, p-hydroxybenzoylcyanidin, p-hydroxybenzoylpeonidin, caffeoyl-p-hydroxybenzoylcyanidin, caffeoyl-p-hydroxybenzoyl- peonidin and caffeoylpeonidin identified in Periderm. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
Two anthocyanin pigments isolated from roots and identified as peonidin-3-diglucosido-5-glucoside with 3 molecules of ferulic acid and 1 molecule of caffeic acid and peonidin-3-diglucosido-5-glucoside with 2 molecules of ferulic acid and 1 molecules of caffeic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.
Hexadecanoic acid (47.62%), 9,12,15-octadecatrienoic acid and 12,15-decadienoic acid isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)f.
Quercetin, its 3-g1ucoside and 4,7,3'-trimethylether, kaempferol- 7,4'-dimethylether, ombuin, b-sitosterol and sucrose isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)g.
Fifteen anthocyanin compounds were identified and measured by HPLC. The anthocyanins were acylated cyanidin and peonidin type. The result suggests that the major anthocyanin composition of sweetpotato leaves is cyanidin type. (Islam et al., 2002)
