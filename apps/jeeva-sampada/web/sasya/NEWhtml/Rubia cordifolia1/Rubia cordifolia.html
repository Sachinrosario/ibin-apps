Chemistry:

·        Alizarin and its glucoside present in plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

·        A new quinizarin -1,4-dihydroxy-6-methylanthraquinone – along with 1-hydroxy-2-methylanthraquinone, nordamnacanthal and physcion isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        Four new quinines -1-hydroxy-2-methoxyanthraquinone, 1,4-dihydroxy-2-methyl-5-methoxyanthraquinone (1,4-dihydroxy-2-methyl-8-methoxyanthraquinone), 1,3-dimethoxy-2-carboxyanthraquinone and urbiadin – isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        1,4-dihydroxy-2-methylanthraquinone and 1,5-dihydroxy-2-methylanthraquinone along with 3-prenyl-5-methoxy-1,4-napthoquinone isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        Rubiconmaric acid and rubifolic acid isolated and their structures elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        Three new anthraquinones (I,II and III) along with mollugin, 1-hydroxy-2-methyl-9,10-anthraquinone, alizarin, 1,3-dihydroxy-2-ethoxymethyl-9,10-anthraquinone, lucidin primeveroside and ruberythric acid isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        Cyclic heptapeptides –RA-III, RA-IV, RA-VII isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        Methanol and chloroform extracts exhibited significant anticancer activity against P-388 lymphocytic leukaemia. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Antitumor cyclic hexapeptides – RAV and RA-VII – isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Hexapeptides – RA-I, RA-II, RA-III and RA-IV isolated as minor constituents; structure of RA-III revised. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Isolation of 1-acetoxy-6-hydroxy-2-methylanthraquione-3-O-a-rhamnosyl (1®4)-a-glucoside from roots and its structure elucidation. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Three new anthraquinones -1,4-dihydroxy-2-methyl-6-or-7-methoxyanthraquinone-from roots; b-sitosterol, scopoletol and oleanolic acid acetate also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        More new anthraquinones -1,3-hydroxyanthraquinone, 4-hydroxy-2-carboxyanthraquinone, 1,4-dihydroxy-2-hydroxymethylanthraquinone and 1-hydroxy-2-hydroxymethylanthraquinone-isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Isolation of a new fernane derivative – rubiatriol-from roots and rhizomes and its structure elucidation; alizarin and 2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxyanthraquinone also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Isolation of 2-carbomethoxy-3-(3’-hydroxy) isopentyl-1,4-naphthohydroquinone-4-O-b-D-glucoside,2-methyl-1,36-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-b-D-glucoside,2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-a-L-(3’-O-acetyl) rhamnosyl (1®2)-b-D-glucoside, 2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-a-L-(4’,6’-O-diacetyl)- rhamnosyl (1®2)-b-D-glucoside and dihydromollugin from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        A new anthraquinone isolated from roots and characterized as 2-methyl-13,6-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-a-L-rhamnosy(1®2)-b-D-glucoside; in addition, 2-methyl-1-hydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone, 2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-a-L-(6’-O-acetyl)-rhamnosyl(1®2)-b-D-glucoside, 2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-b-D(6’-O-acetyl) glucoside, 2-carbomethyoxy-3-prenyl-1,4-naphthohydroquinone-di-b-D-glucoside, rubimallin, alizarin, daucosterol and b-sitosterol isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Isolation and structure elucidation of a new anthraquinone glycoside-2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9-10-anthraquinone-3-O-b-D-xylosyl(1®2)-b-D-(6’-O-acetyl)-glucoside-from roots; 2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-b-D-glucoside, 1,2-dihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone-2-O-b-D-xylosyl(1®6)-b-D-glucoside, 1,3-dihydroxy-2-hydroxymethyl-9,10-anthraquinone-3-O-b-D-xylosyl(1®6)-b-D-glucoside, 2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9,10-anthraquinone,1-hydroxy-9,10-anthraquinon and 1,2,4-trihydroxy-9, 10-anthraquinone also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Determination of 2-methyl-1,6-dihydroxyanthraquinine-3-O-a-L-(6’-O-acetyl) rhamnosyl (1®2)-b-D-glucoside, 2-methyl-1,6-dihydroxyanthraquinone-3-O-a-L-rhamnosyl(1®2)-b-D-glucoside and ruberythric acid by HPLC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        A new naphthoic acid ester-rubilactone – isolated from roots and characterized as 3’-carbomethoxy-4’-hydroxynaphtho [1’,2’-2,3] pyran-6-one; in addition, 3’-carbomethoxy-4/-hydroxynaphtho [1’,2’-2,3] furan, dihydromollugin and 3-carbomethoxy-2-(3’-hydroxy) isopentyl-1,4-naphtho-hydroquinone-1-O-b-D-glucoside isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Two new naphthoquinones-2-carbamoyl-3-methoxy-1,4-naphthoquinone and 2-carbamoyl-3-hydroxy-1,4-naphthoquinone – isolated along with 3-carbomethoxy-1-hydroxyanthraquinone,1,4-dihydroxy-2-ethylanthraquinone, 1-hydroxy-2-methylanthraquinone,1-hydroxy-2-hydroxymethyl anthraquinone, 1,3-dihydroxyanthraquinone and tectoquinone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Two new naphthohydroquinone dimmers (I and II) and four new naphthohydroquinones – 2’-methoxymollugin, 2’hydroxymollugin, 1’-methoxy-2’-hydroxydihydromollugin and 1’,2’dihydroxydihydromollugin – isolated from roots an their structures established; mollugin, 2-carboxymethyl-3-prenyl-2, 3-epoxy-1,4-naphthoquinone,1-hydroxy-2-hydroxymethyl-9,10-anthraquinone,2-methyl-1,3,6-trihydroxy-9, 10anthraquinone and rubinocolin B also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Isolation and structure determination of a new iridoid glycoside -6-methoxygeniposidic acid-from root. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Two macrocyclic neoplasm inhibitors –TAC-A and TPC-B-isolated and found identical to RA-V and RA-VII respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Bicyclic hexapeptides –(III), RA-VI and RA-VIII – obtained from roots and their structures established and conformation-activity relationship determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Isolation of two more cyclichexapeptides – RA-IX and RA-X-from roots and their characterization. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Isolation and structure determination of four hexapeptides RA-XI, RA-XIII and RA-XIV. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Two more antitumor bicyclic hexapeptides –RA-XV and RA-XVI – isolated and their structures established. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Isolation of mollugin, furomollugin and dehydro-alpha-lapchone from the chloroform fraction of Rubia cordifolia (Linn) roots. (Gupta et al., 1999).

·        Munjistin and purpurin were identified as the major components of   anthraquinone pigments produced by callus cultures of R. cordifolia.  Anthraquinone content in calluses was 0.62-1.22% (by dry wt.) depending on the source of explants. Selection of coloured aggregates yielded a cell line with two fold increase in anthraquinone production. (Mischenko et al., 1999).

·        Two new quinoidal dimers, a new naphthohydroquinone anhydride, a known   naphthoic acid ester derivative and two known benzoic acid derivatives were   isolated from the chloroform fraction of a chloroform-methanol (1:1)  extract of  Rubia cordifolia L. (Hassanean et al., 2000)

·        A novel antitumor bicyclic hexapeptide dimer, RA-dimer A (3), was isolated   from the roots of Rubia cordifolia L. The structure of 3 was determined by analysis of spectroscopic data and chemical correlations. Peptide 3 is the first example of the RA congeners having a dimeric structure. (Hitotsuyanagi et al., 2000).

·        A bicyclic hexapeptide, RA-VII or O-methyl deoxybouvardin, isolated from Rubia cordifolia, is known to inhibit protein biosynthesis in vitro and in vivo. RA   -VII-induced cyclin D1 decrease depends on cyclin D1 degradation via the   ubiquitin-proteasome pathway and does not require additional protein synthesis.   RA-VII might actively precede the degradation process of cyclin D1 via the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway in DLD-1 cells. (Wakita et al., 2001)

Pharmacology:

·        Hexapeptides RA-III, RA-IV, RA-V and RA-VII showed antitumor activity against leukemias and ascites tumors (P-388, L1210, B-16 melanoma) and solid tumors (Colon 38, Lewis lung carcinoma and Ehrlich carcinoma ); RA-V showed potent activity against MM2 mammary carcinoma in mice. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        TPC-A (10.0 mg/kg, i.p.) administered to tumor cell P-388 bearing mice prolonged their life span by 187.4%. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        RA-X showed strong antitumor activity whereas RA-IX was devoid of such activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Cyclic peptides RA-VI, RAVIII, RA-IX, RA-X and its methyl ester (III) showed IC50 of 0.035, 0.031, 0.092, 0.180 and 0.050 mg/ml respectively against mouse leukaemia P-388 cells whereas TPC-B showed IC50 of1.40 ng/ml. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Bicyclic hexapeptides RA-XI, RA-XII, RA-XIII and RA-XIV exhibited potent antitumor activity against P-388. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Rubimallin showed antibacterial activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

·        Isolation of mollugin, furomollugin and dehydro-alpha-lapchone from the chloroform fraction of Rubia cordifolia (Linn.) roots. Mollugin showed inhibition of passive cutaneous anaphylaxis (PCA) and protection of mast cell degranulation in rats. It also exhibited considerable activity against lymphoid leukemia (P338) in mice. (Gupta et al., 1999)

·        Effect of rubidatum (Rub) on immune function in normal mice. Serum lysozyme concentration (SLC) was measured using   micrococcus lysodiekticus as a substrate. Delayed type hypersensitivity (DTH) was determined by measuring the thickness of the right hind footpad 24 h after the injection of 1 X 108 washed sRBC (50 muL 10 % sRBC). Serum hemolysin concentration was determined by OD measuring at A540 after the serum was treated with 2-mercaptoethanol. Phagocytic function of peripheral leukocyte   (Leu) were determined by the incorporated radioactivity of (3H)TdR. The   hemolytic activity of plaque forming cell (PFC) was determined by measuring the lymphocyte-mediated haemolysis of sheep red blood cell in vitro. T- and B -lymphocyte transformation (TLT and BLT) were induced by phytohemagglutinin (PHA) and lipopolysaccharide (LPS) respectively and measured by the incorporation of (3H)TdR. Rub 125, 500, 2000 mg kg-1 d-1  po to BALB/c (or NIH) mice decreased the SLC; inhibited the phagocytosing  functions of peripheral leukocytes; diminished the hemolytic activity of PFC;   decreased the HC50; inhibited the DTH reaction; and showed inhibitory  effects   on TLT and BLT. Rub has immunosuppressive effects on immune system   in mice by affecting MPHI, T, and B lymphocyte, which suggests that Rub has   inhibitory effects on both nonspecific and specific immune function. (Yang et al., 2000)

·        Effect of a triterpene isolated from the acetone soluble part of petroleum ether extract of R. cordifolia was studied on convulsions induced by maximum electro shock (MES), electrical kindling and various chemoconvulsants in rats and mice. The triterpene was found to possess anxiogenic activity. Brain GABA and 5-HT contents were raised by the compound. (Kasture et al., 2000)

·        In vivo antioxidant activity of alcoholic extract   of the roots of Rubia cordifolia Linn. (RC) and to study its influence on   ethanol-induced impairment of immune responses. Chronic administration of ethanol decreased the humoral and cell-mediated immune response, phagocytosis, phagocytosis index, TLC, GSH, CAT and SOD activities and increased the LPO. The ethanol-induced immunosuppression is due to oxidative stress and Rubia cordifolia can prevent the same by virtue of its in vivo antioxidant property. (Joharapurkar et al., 2003)
