HABIT & HABITAT
It is herbaceous plant found in Subalpine and alpine Himalaya from Himachal Pradesh to Nepal at 3000-4200 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 A short herb 1.5-3.0 m in height.  Roots very stout; radical leaves long petioled, very large, often 60 cm in diam; orbicular or broadly ovate, cordate; petioles 30-45 cm long; flowers small, dark purple or pale red, in tall, axillary panicles.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
Chemistry: Plant contained anthraquinones (2.24%) which comprised of physcion, chrysophanol, emodin, rhein, and aloe emodin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
Quantitative evaluation of free and combined states of rhein and Chrysophanol in Indain Rhubarb by TLC-Colourimetric method. (Ahmad et al., 1998) MAPA. 1998, 9803: 1717.
Computer simulation predicting separation by RP-gradient elution was used to develop an HPLC-method for the separation and detection of hydroxyanthroquinone derivatives of Rhubarb (aloe-emodin, emodin, rhein, physicon, chrysophanol). (Liu et al, 1999). C.A. 199, 131, 342103v.
Bioassay guided chemical examination of the rhizomes of Rheum emodi resulted in the isolation of two new oxanthrone esters, revandchinone-1 (fig. 1), revandchinone-2 (fig. 2), a new anthraquinone ether revandichinone-3 (fig. 3) and a new oxanthrone ether revandichinone-4 (fig. 4). (Suresh Babu et al., 2003).
NBPGR:  The drug contains a number of anthraquinone derivatives-rhein and emodin.  The plant gave sennosides A-F also.

Pharmacology: Rhubarb solution is an acceptable agent in improving the transportation of low molecule solute added in the dialyzate. (Liu and Li, 1996). C.A. 1997, 127, 144982k.
Emodin was shown to be a potent inhibitor of superoxide radicals (IC50 = 29 mM) including chrysophanol, aloe-emodin, rhein, rhapontin and physcion. The difference in inhibiting pyrogallol autoxidation might be explained by the fact that superoxide radical scavenging activity of phenolic compounds. (Yuan and Gao, 1997). C.A. 1997, 127, 156703h.
The hypoglycemic action has been studied with the 95% ethyl alcohol extract and stilbene glycoside E (exists in a large amount), which are obtained from Rheum emodi. (Li et al., 1997).
Rhein, physcion, aloe-emodin and chrysophanol isolated from Rheum emodi rhizomes exhibited antifungal activity against Candida albicans, Cryptococcus neoformans, Trichophyton mentagrophytes and Aspergillus fumigatus (MIC 25-250 microg/ml). (Agarwal et al., 2000).
