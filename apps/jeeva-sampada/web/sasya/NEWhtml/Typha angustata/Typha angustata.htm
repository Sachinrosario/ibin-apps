HABIT & HABITAT :
Aquatic or marshy rhizomatous monoecious herb, one of the commonest weed, growing along streams, aquatic places, Dried places, Hilly terrains, Marshes,  river banks, Streams, attaining considerable height in deep waters.

BioticZone: Deccan Plateau, West Coast, Western Ghats,Altitude : 0-757m

Availability :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
  Aquatic or marshy rhizomatous monoecious herb, to 1.5 m high, Rhizome clothed with hairs. Leaves radical, sheath white,blade semi-cylindrical, 150 x 1.5 cm. Inflorescence interrupted spike,densely flowered. Flowers yellow, lower female, upper male, bracts greenish, membranous. Perianth o. Stamen 1, rarely 2, anthers basifixed. Ovary stalked, hairy,1-celled; ovule solitary; style slender; stigma filiform. fruit a drupe. 2 x = 30.
