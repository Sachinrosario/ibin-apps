HABIT & HABITAT:
Trunk solitary, straight. 10-30 m high found to occur in Andaman & Nicobar Islands. Cultivated in various parts of India. Asia, Malayan Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Trunk solitary, straight. 10-30 m high, unarmed, uniformly thick, 20-40 cm in diam. Leaves 1.2-1.8 m long; leaflets numerous, 25-60 cm long, glabrous, with one to several main nerves; upper leaflets confluent.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Spadix intrafoliar, branched; MaleFlower:  Many, minute, sepals small, petals obliquely lanceolate, Stamens 3-6, anthers basifixed; Female Flower : Much large few at the base of the branch, sepal and petal orbicular, imbricate, ovary one celled, stigma 3, sessile, ovule basal ; Fruit: ovoid, hard; Seed: With a truncate base. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Dried nuts are chewed along with betel leaf while fresh ones are intoxicating. Nut is regarded as a nervine tonic and emmenagogue and is used as an astringent lotion for the eyes.


Chemistry:
Aqueous extract preparation exhibited both direct vasoconstriction and adrenaline potentition in rat hind limb perfusion test. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Leucocyanidin (acetate,m.p. 123°C) from nuts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Aqueous extract and polyphenolic fraction of Mangalore betel nut, administered, i.p. to mice, decreased nucleic acid and protein content in almost all tissues and decreased glycogen and increased sialic acid in lung and kidney tissues. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

Two procyanidin tetramers, two trimers and a dimmer isolated together with (+)catechin, (-)epicatechin and procyanidins A-1, B-1 and B-2 from seeds; all compounds, except procyanidins B-2, have C4 to C8 or C6 linkage, (-) epicatechin [C2,C3-cis] stereochemistry in upper units and (+) catechin [C2,C3-trans] stereochemistry in lower units. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

Four bioactive polyphenolic components – NPF-861A, NPF-8611A and NPF-8611B – isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Phenolic compound for preventing dental caries and gingivitis isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Phthalic,lauric,myristic, palmitic and stearic acids and bis(2-ethylhexyl)ester isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

Saccharin was leached from betel nuts with aqueous NaOH, extracted with ether after acidifaction, and determined by TLC under UV radiation. (Xiao, 1997). C.A. 1998, 128, 357w.

High resolution GC Mass spectral analysis of volatile cases present in fruits of Areca catechu confirmed the presences of arecoline (fig. 1a) and guvacoline (fig. 1b) but also indicated the related, but hitherto undetected alkaloids. (Holdsworth et al, 1998).

Arecoline and arecaidine were determined simultaneously by capillary elctrophoresis using a back ground electrolyte containing phosphate with UV detection at 185 nm. (Yuan et al, 2000). C.A. 2000, 133, 183108y.
