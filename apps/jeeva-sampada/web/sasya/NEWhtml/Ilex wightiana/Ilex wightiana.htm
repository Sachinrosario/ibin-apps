HABIT & HABITAT
 A medium-sized tree with numerous, steeply sloping branches, found on the hills of South India, particularly on Nilgiris.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves alternate, elliptic, oblong or ovate, subacute or acuminate ; flowers inclusters, white ; drupes globose, 0.25 in. diam. The wood of 7. weighting (wt., 35-40 Ib./cu. ft.) is pale white or creamy in colour, rather soft and close grained.
