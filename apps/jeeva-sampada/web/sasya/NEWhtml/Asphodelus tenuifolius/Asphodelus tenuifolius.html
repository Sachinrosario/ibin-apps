HABIT & HABITAT
A genus of annual or perennial herbs, including about 12 species, distributed in the Mediterranean region and extending eastwards to India. A. fistulosus Linn., and A. tenuifolius Cav. are found as weeds in the Indo-Gangetic plain, from Bengal to the Punjab. The former is eaten during famines.
It is also seen in cultivated and fallow fields, and in Plains, Fallow fields, Gardens and Roadsides, moist localities, Plains, Road sides,

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
An annual stem less herb. Leaves terete; fistulous, sheathing at base. Scapes seversl, simple or branched above.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
Flowers whitish, distant, laxly racemose. pedicels joined. bracts broad ovate, scarious, with a brownish keel. Perianth lobes oblong. Stamens with slightly fusiform filaments. stigma small, minute, 3 lobed.
