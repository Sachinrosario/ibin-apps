 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
Tall shrub or tree, up to 10 m tall; branches grayish brown, smooth or verrucose, lenticellate. It is found to occur in Uttar Pradesh, Tripura, Manipur, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka and Maharashtra.


Availability :

Singh & Anand Prakash, 107754 (LWG); Tripura: Agartala, 200-267 m, 09.10.1915, P.M. Debbarman, 750 (CAL); Tamil Nadu: Athikkadavu river side, Coimbatore dist., 510 m, 07.09.1985, N.C. Rathakrishnan, 82319 (CAL); Kerala: Panamkurry, Idukki dist., 400 m, 22.02.1983, C.N. Mohanan, 77922 (CAL); Karnataka: Kempuhole, Shiradighat, Hassan dist., 30.01.1969, C.J. Saldanha, 12612 (CAL).
Conservation/ Threat Status :
Plant is wild or cultivated, often found along streams but also in dry , deep boulder formation in the sub-Himalayan tract.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves 3-foliate; leaflets 8-15 x 1.5-6.0 cm, glaucous beneath, brownish tinge on upper surface; central ones elliptic or oblanceolate; laterals rhomboidal or elliptic-oblong, acute at apex. Flowers in corymb, creamish, slightly fragrant, 3-4 cm across. Sepals petaloid. Stamens excedding 24; filaments lilac to purple. Gynophore 4-6 cm long. Fruits berries, ellipsoid, 4-6 x 3-5 cm; pericarp woody, yellowish grey. Seeds dark brown, embedded in creamy pulp, tubercled.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:  Root bark extract is used in gastric troubles.
Chemistry:
Lupeol (C30H50O, mp. 214°C) is isolated from bark of Crataeva nurvula (Chakravarti and Banerjee, 1969)

Pharmacognostic studies of Leaf. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Lauric, Stearic, Undecyclic, oleic and linolenic acids were isolated from the unsaponified part of the root bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

A new pentacyclic triterpene alcohol, lupa-21,20(29) dien-3b-ol (fig. 1) has been isolated from the root bark of Crataeva nurvula and tentative structure is given. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Cerylalcohol, friedelin, betulinic acid and diosgenin were also isolated from bark.Cetyl alcohol, ceryl  alcohol, triacontane, triacontanol, b-Sitosterol and glucocapparin were isolated from fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.

Alkaloid Cadabicine and Cadabicine diacetate isolated from ethanolic extract of stem bark by using silicagel column. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).


Pharmacology:
Effect of Cretaeva nuruvala in experimental urolithiasis has been studied in rats. The elevation of the oxalate synthesizing liver enzyme, glycolate oxydase, produced by feeding glycolic acid was remarkably reduced with deviation, showing a regulatory activity on endogenous oxalate synthesis. The increased deposition of stone forming constituents in the kidneys of calculogenic rats was lowered with decoction administration. (Varalakshmi et al, 1990).

Effect of Crataeva nurvula on the bio chemistry of the small intestinal tract of normal & stone forming rats,treatment with crataeve nurvula bark decoction.( Varalakshmi  et al,1991).

Lupeol administration reduced renal excretion of calcium oxalate with reduced renal tubular damage. (Malini et al, 1995).


Ethnobotany:

Antifertility (Bark, Tarafder, 1983); Hydrocele, sores and baldness (part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Fever (Bark, Nagendra and Abraham, 1984); Guinea worms (Bark, Mishra and Billore, 1983); Postnatal (Bark, Jain and Borthakur, 1980); Stomach diseases (Bark, Nagendra and Abraham, 1984); Tonic, urinary diseases (Bark, Jain and Borthakur, 1980); Wounds (Bark, Jain, 1965); Edible (Fruit, Saxena et al, 1981; Jain and Dam, 1979).
