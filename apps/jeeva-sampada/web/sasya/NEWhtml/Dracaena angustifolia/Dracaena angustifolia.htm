HABIT & HABITAT

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A genus of shrubs or trees with richly coloured, often variegated leaves, common in the warmer parts of the Old World. About 6 species occur wild in India and a large number of exotic species, including numerous varieties, is grown in Indian gardens for their ornamental foliage.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
Leaves and Stems.

Uses

  • The leaves of the plant are used as goat fodder.
  • The Juice of the leaves is reported to be used in Java for colouring cakes and food. It occurs in tears with a glassy fracture and differs from true dragon's blood in containing no fruit scales and in not emitting the odour of benzoic acid when heated.
