Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Leucas aspera (Family Labiateae) is locally known as Gumma Bhaji and commonly known as Dronpushpi, is a common field and wasteland weed. In Chhattisgarh, Leucas is widely used as potherb. During rainy season, you can easily see this weed in village market. 



Availability :

Largely occurs in plains rarely occurs in uplands & hilly areas.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Its new leaves and tender shoots are used as vegetable. It is general belief in Chhattisgarh, that if taken regularly this weed as potherb, develops a specific smell in humanbody. This smell repells the snakes and other venoumous animals. Villagers particularly farmers prefer this weed during rainy season, when the incidences of snake bite are higher. 

In Ayurveda, anti-venum properties of different parts of Leucas have been reported.

Healthcare Properties:
1. Asthma: Grind the leaves of Leucas aspera with rock salt into a paste. Prepare tablets with this paste. Take one tablet in each time twice daily with a spoonful ginger juice or honey. (P-13) [OR-3-1-15] 
2. Conjunctivitis: Squeeze 7no leaves of Leucas aspera and extract the juice. Put one to two drops of juice in eyes twice daily for two days only (D-4) [OR-2-2-1347] 
3. Diabetes: Grind together the flowers of a single plant of Leucas aspera and 21no cloves with a paste. Take the paste once daily till cure. (A-4) [OR-1-4-527] 
4. Fever (Malaria) : Grind together a entire plant of Leucas aspera and leaves of Nyctacthes arbor-tristis and extracat the juice. Take two teaspoonfuls in each time once daily for two days only. (S-23) [OR-3-3-241] 
5. Headache: Grind the leaves of Leucas aspera and extract the juice. Inhale the juice through nostrils to instant relief from Headache. (D-4) [OR-2-2-1347] 
6. Jaundice: Grind the leaves of Leucas aspera and extract the juice after filtration. Take one teaspoonful twice daily for one week with 1/2 teaspoon honey. (P-13) 
7. Mole or Mouse bite: Grind the plant of Leucas aspera with seeds of Piper nigrum into a paste. Mix the paste in water and prepare a drink. Take the drink thrice daily for three days. N: B: (I) Take two number of Leucas aspera and 21no Piper nigrum seeds for Adult. (Ii) Take one plant of Leucas aspera and 2no of Piper nigrum seeds. (P-2) [OR-2-1-1117] 
8. Otalgia: Squeeze the leaves of Leucas aspera and extract the juice and warm the juice gently. Put this inside the eardrop by drop as eardrop twice daily for three days. (G-7) [OR-1-1-16] 
9. Ozena: Grind the leaves of Leucas aspera and extract the juice. Put 2-3 drops of the juice inside the nostril as nasal drop twice daily for 2-3 days (B-6) [OR-4-2-228 
10. Skin diseases (Itches) : Apply the leaf juice of Leucas aspera on Itches area once daily till cure after washing it properly by warm water (K-160 [OR-1-2-168] 
11. Snakebite: Grind the leaves of Leucas aspera and extract the juice. Put 3-4 drops of the juice in each nostril once for one day only. (K-16) [OR-1-2-168] 
12. Tooth ache: Chew the leaves of Leucas aspera with common salt to instant relief from toothache (G-7) [OR-1-1-16] 
13. Wound: Grind the dried leaves of Leucas aspera into powder. Apply the powder once daily on wound for 2-3 days. (P-13) (OR) Grind together the leaves and inflorescence and the rhizome of Curcuma longa into a paste. Apply the paste locally in wounds in poultry (K-7) (B-15) [OR-1-1-121] 
Other Usage's:
Holy purpose: The Paroja groups of Malkangiri offer the flower of Leucas aspera to Lord Shiva. (G-7) [OR-1-1-16]
