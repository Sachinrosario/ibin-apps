Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 A slender, prostrate or twining herb found in Bundelkhand (U.P.), Bihar, Lakhimpur (Assam), east coast and west coast from Konkan to Kerala of India.The plant is usually found in marshy thickets, hedges, river banks, margins of tanks and backwaters.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves lanceolate, oblong, entire; flowers solitary, campanulate, white ; capsules ovoid, glabrous ; seeds ovoid, dark brown or black, glabrous, margin minute- lytrigose. The plant is usually found in marshy thickets, hedges, river banks, margins of tanks and backwaters.
