HABIT & HABITAT:
Aerva lanata Juss. is an erect or prostrate herbaceous weed, common throughout the hotter parts of India and ascends to 3,000' on the hills. It also occurs in Ceylon. It is generally found in dry disturbed grounds, Dry waste land, Fields, Hills, open waste land, Plains, Road sides, Rocky habitats, rocky slopes, sandy flats, scrub forests.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Erect or prostrate with a long tap rot, branched from near the base; may branches are striate. Leaves alternate, 2-2.5 by 1-1.6 cm on the main stem. Flowers are greenish white, very small, sessile, often bisexual, in small dense subsessile axillary heads or spikes 6-13 mm. long, often closely crowed and forming globse clusters; brancteoles 1.25 mm. long, membranous, broadly ovate, concave, apiculate. Perianth 1.5-1.25 mm long; sepals oblong, obtuse, sometimes apiculate, silky- hairy on the back. Utricle broadly ovoid, acute; stigmas 2. Seed 0.85 mm. diam., sooth and polished, black.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
Shoots.

Uses: 

  1. The shoots used as vegetable. 
  2. The plant is diuretic; used in lithiasis. 
  3. The root is demulcent, diuretic; useful in strangury. 
  4. The roots are used in the treatment of headache.
