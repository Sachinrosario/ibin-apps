DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herbs. Culms tufted up to 1.5 m tall. Leaves flat, filiform above, linear,   c 45 x 0.5 cm; sheaths often coiled, glabrous; ligules c 0.5 mm long, membranous. Panicles 15-40 cmlong, narrow.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Spathe up to 5 cm long; spatheoles lanceolate, 1.2-1.8 cm long. Racemes 1.0-1.8 cm long. Spikelets 3-4 pairs. Spikelets sessile, lanceolate, c 4.5 mm long. Lower glumes 2-keeled, flat or concave, glabrous, 2-4-nerved or absent; upper glume as long as lower, 3-nerved. Lemma lanceolate, up to 5 mm long, 2-nerved; palea absent. Pedicellate spikelets c 5 mm long. Lower glume elliptic-lanceolate, membranous, 7-9 nerved. Upper glume 3-nerved. Lower floret empty.  Fruits caryopsis, oblong.
CIMAP:
Spathate panicle erect, lower most pedicels and lower raceme base not swollen, lower glume lanceolate, sharply keeled, pedicelled spikelet with lower glume lanceolate 3-7 nerved. Fruit: Caryopsis. Seeds: one. 
Floral and seed biology:
August - November.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are highly cross pollinated .Seeds are dispersed by wind.
 
Genetic variability: The reported chromosome number - 20.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation:  Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant requires well drained sandy loam for cultivation.

Nutrient requirements: Plant responds well to the application of manure and fertilizers.

Water management: Plant responds well to approximate annual rainfall.

Harvesting period / Method: Leaves are harvested when the oil constituents are high.

Detection of a-pinene (7.3), b-pinene (24.4), myrcene (17.9), terpinene (0.3), limonene (0.9), linalool (1.0), linalyl acetate (1.2), isopulegol (17.2), pulegone (10.9), Piperitone (6.6) and piperitenone (1.9%) in essential oil by GLC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
