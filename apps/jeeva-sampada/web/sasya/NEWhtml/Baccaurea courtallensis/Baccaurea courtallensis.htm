DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES 
A middle sized tree; shoots and young petioles sparsely pubescent.  Leaves 5-7 by 2-3 inch, thin; nerves 5-7 pairs; petiole 1-1.5 inch. Male racemes in large clusters from the trunk and sub solitary on the branches. An imperfectly known plant considered by beddome as the same with B. sapida, but differing altogether in the male racemes and in the small sessile female flowers.  Flowers Glabrous, male racemes tufted strict quite simple, bracts very minute, ovate acute, flowers 1/15 inch diameter, very sparsely heavy, sepals 4, sub equal rounded, stamens 4-6, female spikes 6-8 inch, flowers 1/8 inch diameter, sessile, sepals 5 nearly glabrous, small, broadly ovate, ovary sub globose, hirsute, stigmas 3, very small and sessile.
