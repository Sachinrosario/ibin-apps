DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

--tall, evergreen with a conical crown, attaining a height upto 75m & girth up to 7m.

-- the dark green foliage frequently extends to whole length of the tree, concealing the
entire bole

-- upper branches horizontal, lower ones drooping with ends curving upwards.

-- bark smooth, brown to silvery on young trees & greyish brown & deeply fissured on
old trees;

-- leaves needle like, persistent, young ones soft, flattened up to 9cm long, spirally
arranged, declinate, pectinately arranged on banen shoots;

-- male catkins sessile, clustered in axils;

-- female cones solitary, terminal, ovoid, 10.0 - 16.0cm x 4.0 - 7.5cm, violet- purple,
changing to dark brown when mature;

-- scale fan shaped;

-- seeds brown, shining, wings longer than seeds
