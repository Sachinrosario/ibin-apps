HABIT & HABITAT
Climbing herb; stem branches, slender, furrowed, glabrous. It is found in Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Assam, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Tendrils 2-fid. Leaves reniform-suborbicular, 4-10 cm long, usually little broader than long, cordate at base, more or less deeply 5-lobed, lobes rounded or subacute, margin minutely denticulate; petioles slightly scabrid. Flowers dioecious, white. Male flowers usually in pairs, one 1-flowered, the other with a receme of 5-12 flowers at the apex; bracts near the base. Calyx hairy, lobes ovate-lanceolate, acute. Petals spreading, obovate, veined. Stamens 3. Fruits ellipsoid, not ribbed, covered with ciliate bristles; operculum conical. Seeds numerous, 4-5 x 3 mm, black, slightly verrucose.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine the plant is bitter and anthelmintic. It cures bronchitis, jaundice, fever, asthma, anaemia, piles, hiccough. Roots cure tumours, bronchitis, piles, vaginal discharges, jaundice. In Unani system roots strengthen neck muscles. Fruit cures bronchitis and lung complaints.


Chemistry: Crystal structure of echinatin (fig. 3) determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).
Isolation of datiscacin, curcurbitacin B, its 2-O-glucoside and curcurbitacin S-2-O-b-D-glucoside from fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

Pharmacology: Aqueous and alcoholic extracts of seeds possess anthelmintic activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.
Ingestion by rats afflicted with experimental jaundice (induced by 2.5 mg/kg of chlorpromazine), of aqueous extract of fruits corresponding to 25 and 50 fig of crude preparation decreased serum bilirubin in 48- 72 hrs. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.
Cucurbitacin B (fig. 1b) (3.5), mp. 176°, cucurbitacin E (0.05%), mp. 234° and a bitter saponin composed of a triterpene acid, glucose, arabinose and rhamnose, from fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.
Cucurbitacin B, P'-sitosterol, two unidentified triterpene alcohols- echinatol A, mp. 144° and echinatol B, mp.167° - from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.
Constitution of cucurbitacin B established; structures of cucurbitacins A (fig. 1a) and C (fig. 2) proposed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)e.
Alcoholic extract showed definite protection against experimentally-induced liver injury by carbon tetrachloride in rats. Ether extract was found more effective than alcoholic extract. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).


Ethnobotany:
