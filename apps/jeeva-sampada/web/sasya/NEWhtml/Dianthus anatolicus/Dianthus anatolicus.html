 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
Perennial slender, densely caespitose herb, with a woody short stock, 8-35 cm tall. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh above 2700 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves narrow, linear, acuminate at apex, margin thickened; radical leaves c 3 cm long, cauline ones c 2.4 cm long. Flowers white, solitary or sometimes 2-3 together; pedicels 5 cm long or more; bracteoles broadly caespitose, sometimes foliaceous. Calyx 8.5-11.0 mm long, contracted above, teeth lanceolate, acute or mucronate at apex, margin scarious. Petals white, oblong, spathulate, 2.5-3.5 mm long, dentate, glabrous Styles 2, filiform. Capsules cylindrical, dehiscing by 4 teeth. Seeds peltate with a hilum.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
      Inflorescence: Panicled cymes; Flower: Bracteate, pedicellate, complete; Calyx: Tubular, 5-toothed, teeth subacute; Corolla: Petals with long claw, blade entire, crenate-toothed; Androecium: Stamens 10, anthers 2-celled, opening lengthwise ; Gynoecium: Ovary 1-celled, styles 2, free; Fruit: Capsule, cylindric, 4-toothed; Seed:  Discoid on columnar placenta. 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting :July- December. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
 
Genetic variability:
