DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Bark grayish-brown, rugose, exudes yellow gum; branches drooping or horizntal, grabrous. Leaves elliptic-lanceolate, 7-15 x 2-7 cm, acute or acuminate at apex, dark green, reticulately veined. Male flowers white, 3-5 in short axillary fascicles. Sepals 4, obovate, unequal. Petals 4, obovate-oblong. Stamens 12-20, inserted on a receptacle, forming a globular head. Female flowers are larger than the male, bisexual, 1-3 in terminal and axillary fascicles. Fruits berry, c 7 cm in diam., yellow or red, globose, 6-8 grooved, 6-8 seeded. Seeds ovoid, pale brown, compressed, surrounded by white or red aril.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   Male Flower: In the axils of fallen leaves, 3-4 together, pedicels thickened; sepals thick and fleshy, with membranous margins, outer sepals shorter than inner; petals more than twice as log as sepals, obovate-oblong, concave, with narrowly membranous margins; stamens numerous on short, thick androphore, monadelphous  forming globular head; Female or Hermaphrodite Flower:  Larger than the males, in terminal and axillary fascicles of 1-3; sepals and petals as in male; staminodes 10-20, unequal, in a ring round the ovary which is free, globular grooved, style 5-12 rayed, tubercled; Fruit: Berry, yellow or reddish, more or less globular, with 7-13 deep, vertical furrows; Seed: As many as the grooves of the fruit, ovoid, compressed, surrounded by white or red aril; 
Floral and seed biology:

Chemistry:
Two isoprenylated benzophenone derivatives – camboginol (fig. 1) and cambogin – isolated from latex and structure of cambogin proposed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Structure of cambogin. An enantiomer of isoxanthochymol, confirmed by X-ray studies. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Structure of camboginol revised and confirmed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Crystals of (-)-hydroxycitric were prepared from water extracts of Garcinia cambogia by precipitation as calcium or barium salt and desalting on cation exchage resin. Water was removed by distillation with immisicible solvent followed by recrystallization of (-)-hydroxycitric acid lactone in ether. (Singh et al, 1995). C.A. 1996, 124, 97375r.

The major organic acid in Garcinia cambogia is (-)-hydroxycitric acid, presenting concentration of 16-18% using HPLC with 10 mM H2SO4 as eluent citric and malic acids are less. (Jayaprakash and Sakariah, 1998). C.A. 1998, 129, 65034r.

New xanthone, garbogiol (fig. 2) was isolated from the root, known xanthone (rheediaxanthone A) and 2 known benzophenones (garcinol and isogarcinol) were obtained from the root bark. The structures were established by spectral analysis. (Iinuma et al, 1998), C.A. 1998, 129, 38773z.

Pharmacology:

Dexamethasone (10 mg/kg body weight/day, s.c.) administered rats were treated with or without Garcinia cambogia fruit extract (1 g/kg body weight/day, orally) for 8 days. The administration of dexamethasone resulted in marked increase in the levels of triglycerides and cholesterol and free acids in both plasma and liver. ( Mahendran and Devi , 2001)

Oral pretreatment with Garcinia cambogia fruit extract (1 g/kg body wt/day) for 5, 10 or 15 days protected the gastric mucosa against the damage induced by indomethacin (20 mg/kg body wt). The volume and acidity of the gastric juice decreased in the pretreated rats. The glycoprotein levels of the gastric contents which were decreased in the untreated rats, maintained near normal levels in the pretreated rats. Protein which was elevated in the gastric juice of untreated rats, showed near normal levels in the pretreated rats. Garcinia cambogia was able to decrease the acidity and to increase the mucosal defence in the gastric areas (Mahendran et al, 2002)

Hydroxycitric acid increases the release of radiolabeled 5-HT from the isolated rat brain cortex (Ohia et al, 2001)

Flavonoids from Cocos nucifera, Myristica fragrance, Saraka asoka and Garcinia cambogia exerted hypolipidaemic activity in rats. Lipid lowering activity was maximum in rats administered flavonoids (10 mg/kg BW/day) from Garcinia cambogia. A dose response study revealed biphasic activity. Higher doses were less effective in reducing lipid levels in serum and tissues, although devoid of toxic effects. (Koshy and Vijayalakshmi, 2001).

Garcinia extract and insulin were added simultaneously, the accumulation of lipid droplets was inhibited and the peak droplet area shifted to become smaller (10-20 microm(2)). The activities of glycerophosphate dehydrogenase, a marker of adipose differentiation, were not significantly inhibited by the Garcinia extract. (Hasegawa, 2001)
