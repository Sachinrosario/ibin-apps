Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A small hispidly-villous herb inhabiting the plains of Northern and Central India from Rohilkhand to sind. Also can be seen in waste lands.
BioticZone: Peninsular India,
Soil: Gravelly soil.
Misc: Chota Nagpur, Karachi to Magar Pir and Sind.
Country: Afghanistan, Arabia, Canary islands, Egypt, India and Pakistan.
Zone: Central, North and Westzone.
State: Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab and Uttar Pradesh.
District: Agra, Bilaspur and Champaran.
Availability :  
Punjab, Rajputana Desert, Malwa, Chota Nagpur, W. Peninsula.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES 
A small palnt with a perennial root; stems many from the root, prostrate, hispid, leafy, very brittle. Leaves opposite, oriaceous; petioles short, minute stipules. Minute involucre and axillary. Capsules 1.6 mm with very short styles. Seeds quadrangular, bluntly pointed with wet testa.
