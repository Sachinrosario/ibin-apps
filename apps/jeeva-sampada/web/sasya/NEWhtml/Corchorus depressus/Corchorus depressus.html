HABIT & HABITAT:
Perennial, woody, prostrate, branched herbs found in occur in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, Gujarat and Maharashtra. W. Asia, Pakistan, Tropical Africa.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial, woody, prostrate, branched herbs, young branches sparsely hairy, older ones glabrous. Leaves narrowly to broadly elliptic, 1-4 x 0.7-1.5 cm, apex and base obtuse, margin crenate-serrate, without basal filiform processes, glabrous except sparsely hairy along nerves; petioles 1.5-2.5 cm long, minutely hairy; stipules subulate, c 3 mm long. 


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Flowers 2-4, in subsessile cymes, 6-8 mm across; pedicels c 1 mm long; bracts linear-lanceolate, c 1.5 mm long. Sepals linear-oblong, 3-4 mm long, apiculate, reddish-green. Petals ovate-spathulate, 3-4 mm long, obtuse, yellow. Stamens 8-10. Capsules cylindrical, 7-22 mm long, often curved, glabrescent, beaked, 4-loculed. Seeds roughly triangular, c 1.5 mm long, obliquely truncate, black.
CIMAP:
Inflorescence: Cymes; Flower: Pedicellate, complete, hermaphrodite, yellow; Calyx: Sepals 4-5 ; Corolla: Petals 4-5, naked at the base, valvate; Androecium: Stamens numerous, free,  inserted on very short torus, anther cells two; Gynoecium: Ovary free, 3-5 celled, ovules many in each cell, style short, the apex stigmatose, hollow crenate; Fruit: Capsule subglobose,3-valved; Seed: Numerous and smooth .

Floral and seed biology:
It flowers almost through the year.

Flowering & Fruiting : October-November. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
2a,3b,20b-Urs-12-en-23b,28-dioic acid 2,3-diaceate isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Quercetin and kaempferol isolated from leaves and flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

Isolation of three new a-amyrin derivatives – cordepressic acid (fig. 1a), cordepressenic acid (fig. 2) and cordepressin (fig. 1b)– isolated from whole plant and their structures determined; sitosterol, apigenin and luteolin also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).Two novel bidesmosidic cycloartane-type glycosides, depressosides C and D were isolated from Corchorus depressus L. Their structures were elucidated as (22R)-16beta,22-epoxy-3beta,26-dihydroxy-9,19-cyclolanost-++ +24E-ene 3, 26-di-O-beta-D-glucopyranoside and (22R,24S)-22,25-epoxy-3beta, 16beta, 24-trihydroxy-9,19-cyclolano stane 3, 24-di-O-beta-glucopyranoside, respectively on the basis of chemical evidence and detailed spectroscopic studies.( Ahmad et al,2000).                                                           


Pharmacology:
2a,3b,20b-Urs-12-en-23b,28-dioic acid 2,3-diaceate showed positive analgesic results in chemical and electrical tests and negative results in thermal and mechanical (Mouse tail clip method) tests and also showed antipyretic activity but had no effect on spontaneous motor activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
