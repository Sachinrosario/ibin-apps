HABIT & HABITAT
Perennial common on plains of North-Western India from Delhi throughout the Punjab to the Indus, and distributed westward to the Atlantic, Africa and America. It can be found in sandy saline and waste lands.

Availability :  
It is distributed in Noerh West India, Punjab, ind, Baluchistan, Kathaiwar, Western Peninsula, Africa & America.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A sub-erect or decumbent perennial, 60-120 cm. tall occurring abundantly in saline tracts and fallow lands from Punjab eastwards to Uttar Pradesh and southwards to Gujarat and Maharashtra. Stems pale or purplish tinged, usually slender; leaves sub-sessile, succulent, verv variable; flowers foetid, green, turning red at main leafy spikes; fruit a turbinate utricle, member;spongy; seeds obliquely ovoid, beaked, black with shining. The plant produces woolly excrescences on to of the branches.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Branches, leaves.

Uses

  1. In Pakistan, the plant is incinerated to make a carbonate of soda, called barilla (Sajji-matti) its ash, but the product obtained is inferior in q and less in quantity to that obtained from Halo recurvum. 
  2. The plant is eaten by camels and goats, though animals need frequent watering when on this It is reported to cause a persistent black diarrhea in sheep. Animals other than camels do not browse plant under normal conditions. 
  3. The woolly excrescenses on the tips of its branches, mixed with an empyreumatic oil, are used as an application to sores on the backs of camels.
  4. The leaves are applied as a poultice to ophthalmia and as an emetic by Sindhis.
  5. The wooly excrescences are used in an oily medium to cure sores on the backs of camels. The mashed leaves employed as a poultice for the eyes. An aqueous infusion of the leaves is used as an emetic.

Other Uses
  1. The plant has been recommended for treating water logged and saline soils. It is planted on mounds or so as to be out of stagnant water and free from excess moisture. Repeated removal of the aerial portion of plant is said to help in lessening saline concentrated the soil. The dried plants are used as fuel. 
  2. It is used in place of soap for washing clothes.
