DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Trees; leaves crowded at the top of stem, pinnate, leaflets numerous, fascicled, linear, folded, spine tipped; flowers in erect or drooping axillary spadics, male flowers white, female greenish; fruits drupes, oblong- ellipsoid; seeds long, deeply grooved, rounded at the ends.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Fruits are used in preparation of beverages (Jain 1991)
The tree is tapped for neera and toddy ( Badhe & Pandey 1990)
Toddy tapped from the apex of the trunk (Ravishankar & Henery 1992)
Chinnd a local liquor obtained from palm tree known as “ Chhind”. This liquor is taken out by making a cut in pith region at the top of palm tree, when it is 10-12 years old. The liquor is collected in a container prepared from dry shell of the fruit of Lagenaria siceraria. The container is tied with the fibres of Bauhinia vahlii. It is collected during summer season. About 5 litres of chhind is collected daily from a single tree. (Sahu 1996)
The palm are ready for tapping at the age of seven years when the stem is about 0.5 met. Height, though it is preferable to wait until the 8th or 9th year. Tapping can be done for 25- 30 or many more years. The tapping season lasts for 4-6 months commencing from Oct.-Nov., the yield of sap is maximum during cold weather and decreases with the onset of hot season. For tapping the lower leaves along one side of trunk are cut off and the bark removed to expose the inner soft layer to the extent about 0.45 cm. vertically and half of the circumferenceof the tree horizontally. After few days, the tapping is started in the evening by making a V- shaped cut on the expose surfaceand scooping out a triangular area inside the V. The sap, exuding from the scooped surface is run through a bamboo spout in to an earthen vessel, internally coated with lime, and is collected early next morning. A similar , but somewhat shallow cut is made on the second evening and the sap collected in the same way. The plant is then generally given rest for the next three days, though some amount of sap is collected during night by mearly cleaning the previous next. This tapping schedule is continued for the seasonby making fresh cuts. To ensure optimum flow of the sap, fruit bunches of usally cut off. The yield of sap per palm is reported to vary considerably from 70- 310 Kg. In a season. 
Fresh unfermented sap (nira) is a refreshing sweet drink. The sap after it has undergone spontaneous fermentation for 8-10 hours is a common beverage (toddy) in certain parts of India. (Anonymous 1969).
