HABIT & HABITAT
Erect, sparsely branched herb, 50-100 cm tall, nearly leafless above, inflorescence hirsutous, otherwise glabrous, root fusiform.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves ovate, 8-15 cm in diam., ternately or pinnately decoumpound, ultimate divisions linear, 2-7.5 x 1 mm; petioles slender, 5-15 cm long; cauline leaves stronghly reduced upward. Peduncles terminal and lateral, 4-6 cm long. Involucre and involucel of 4-6 linearly lanceolate or oblong, scarious-margined, ciliolate reflexed bracts or bracteoles, 2-8 mm long. Rays 3-7, filiform, glabrate, spreading-ascending, unequal, 1-4 cm long. Umbellets 6-15 flowered, mature pedicels slender, unequal, spreading-ascending, hirtullous 2-7 mm long. Petals oval, hirsutulous dorsally; calyx obsolete; stylopodium low conocal, style slender, recurved; ovary densely hirsutulous. Fruit ovoid, 2-3 x 2 mm, slightly narrowed toward apex, rounded at base, mericarps pentagonal-subterete, densely hirsutulous, ribs inconspicuous. Vittae solitary in intervals, 3 on commissure. Carpophpre bipartite.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
Parts used: Emetic, carminative, cardiotonic, antiasthmatic, emmenagogic, stimulant, in dyspepsia, bronchitis, hiccup, bladder troubles.


Chemistry:

 Bergapten isolated from fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
a- and b- pinenes, sabinene, terpinene, a- and b-phellandrene, linalool, a-terpineol, thymol and carvacrol, present in essential oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
Detection of a-pinene, myrcene, car-3-ene, limonene, g-terpinene, p-cymene, (-)cadiene, fenchone, thymol, linalool, piperitone, seselin, a-terpinol, bergapten and isoimpinellin by GLC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 190-1979)c.
Revised structures of b-cyclolavandulic (fig. 1) acid and seselin (fig. 2). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.
7-methoxy-6-methylcoumarin and 7-hydroxy-6-methylcoumarin isolated from seeds along with angelicin, psoralen, bergapten, b-sitosterol and umbelliferone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).
NBPGR: Major constituents of the essential oil from seed are d-limonene, α-terpinene, d-linalool, dl-terpineol and dl-piperitone.  Other constituents are thymol, thymoquinol and cuminol.  P-Isopropyll-benzoic acid present is an artifact, produced by the oxidation of corresponding aldehyde or cuminol.  Major constituents from pakistanti oil are (-) –rins-bergaptene, 7-methoxy –6-methyl coumarin and umbelliferone, β-sitosterol is also reported from seeds.
