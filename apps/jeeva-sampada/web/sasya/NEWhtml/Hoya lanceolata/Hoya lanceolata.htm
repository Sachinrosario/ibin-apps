DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Stems weak, climbing, branched; internodes 0.8-2.7 cm long and 1-3 mm in diam., pubescent. Leaves opposite, decussate, lamina 1.2-5.2 x 0.4-.15 cm, ovate-lanceolate, lanceolate or rhomboid-lanceolate, acute at apex and base, thin, recurved with few hairs along margin, denser in basal part, sparsely pubescent only on the midrib above, lateral veins invisible; petioles terete, 1-2.5 mm long and ca 0.5 mm in diam., minutely grooved on upper side, pubescent.

Flowers terminal or extra-axillary, umbellate cymes, about 7 flowers; peduncles terete, 1-2 cm long, 0.5-1 mm in diam., puberulous; bracts small, ca 1 x 0.5 mm, ovate, hairy; pedicels stout, terete, 4-7 mm long, ca 0.5 in daim., puberulous. Calyx 5-lobed, divided up to base, lobes ca 2 x 1 mm, oblong-lanceolate, apex acute, margin hairy, pubescent without. Corolla rotate, ca 1.3 cm across; lobes united up to ca 3.5 mm, five, ca 3.5 x 4.5 mm, subtriangular, apex acute, margin ciliolate, pubescent within. Corona staminal, uniseriate, five-lobed, horizontally placed, stellate, adnate to the base of staminal column, ca 2.5 x 1 mm, oblong, apex acute, with spur ca 1 mm long at the base from inner margin, articulated. Stamens 5, ca 3 mm long; pollinia five, pollen masses solitary in each anther cell, yellow, waxy, 511.2-518.4 x 216-230.4 mm attached by light-brown caudicles ca 172.8 mm long, ca 129.6 mm wide to dark-brown corpuscle ca 259.2 mm long with tail and ca 86.4 mm wide. Gynostegium ca 2 mm long. Follicles usually single, 8-15.6 x 0.4-3 cm, linear, tapering to blunt tip, glabrous; seeds many, 2.5-3 x 1-1.5 mm, oblong, base acute, hairy along the margin; coma silky-white, 3-4 cm long.
