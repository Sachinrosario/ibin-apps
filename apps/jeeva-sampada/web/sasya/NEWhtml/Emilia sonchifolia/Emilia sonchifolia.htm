HABIT & HABITAT
 A very variable annual herb, 4-10 in high, with pretty small scarlet flowers, commonly cultivated in gardens. Probably a native of China ,it is loge been naturalized in this country. Found commonly in cultivated fields waste places almost through out India as weed. In Karnataka it is found in the districts of Bangalore , Chikmagalur Chitradurge, Coorg, Hassan, Kolar, Mysore , North Kanara , Raichur, Shimoga, South Kanara .

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Annual growing to 0.6m. Herbs radical and cauline leaves; lower leaves lyrate, upper semiamplexicaul.and is frost tender.
The plant prefers light (sandy), medium (loamy) and heavy (clay) soils and requires well-drained soil. The plant prefers acid, neutral and basic (alkaline) soils. It cannot grow in the shade. It requires moist soil. Heads on long stalks, purple or orange purple, many flowered (30-50), homogamous. Florets tubular, 5-lobed: the lobes long and linear. Florets pinkish in solitary and corymbose heads. Receptacles flat, scarcely honeycombed. Involucre ovate-cylindrical, in 1 row, with a calyculus; the scales linear, reflexed after flowering. Branches of the style terminated by a short hispid cone. Corolla tubes slender, 6-8mm long, white with purple tips, lobes 5; achenes 5 ribbed, hairy along ribs, pappus many.

Phenology :
Flowering and fruiting occurs throughout the year. However there are reports of flowering only in July - November in some specific places and also during October-February in some other places.

Pollinators:

Seed Biology :

Seed Germination :
It is propagated from seeds sown in October in the plains and in March on the hills.
Seed - sow early spring in a greenhouse and only just cover the seed. When large enough to handle, prick the seedlings out into individual pots and plant them out after the last expected frosts. The seed can also be sown outdoors in situ in the middle of spring.

Cultivation:
An easily grown plant, succeeding in most well-drained soils in a sunny position. Plants flower better when growing on nutritionally poor soils, producing much lusher growth on rich soils. Plants are drought tolerant once established . Plants are not frost hardy, but they succeed outdoors in Britain as a spring-sown annual. Slugs can be a problem with this plant in a wet spring. The leaves are frequently sold in local markets in Java.

Other Uses

Medicinal Uses:

1.       A tea made from the leaves is used in the treatment of dysentery .

2.     The juice of the leaves is used in treating eye inflammations, night blindness ,sore eyes & sore ears.

3.     The plant is depurative, diuretic, febrifuge and sudorific.

4.     It is used in the treatment of infantile tympanites and bowel complaints .

5.     The root is used in the treatment of diarrhoea .

6.     The plant is sudorific. A decoction of it is used as febrifuge in infantile tympanites and in bowel complaints.

7.      The plant is used also for cuts and wounds.
