HABIT & HABITAT
Monoecious, deciduous tree, it is found throughout India, in deciduous forests and on hill slopes upto 200 m, also cultivated in plains. The species is wild or planted in Ceylon, China and Malay Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Leaves appearing with flowers, distichous, linear-oblong, obtuse and apiculate at apex, truncate at base, glabrous, subsessile; stipules triangular. Flowers in axillary dense fascicles, males above females below. Male flowers: Sepals 6, oblanceolate, obtuse; stamens 3, filaments connate, disc glands 6. Female flowers: sessile, few; sepals 6, oblanecolate; styles exerted, arms 2-fid. Drupes depressed globose, fleshy. Seeds trigonous.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Fruit is acrid, sour, bitter-sweetish, cooling, alexiteric, carminative, alterative, laxative, tonic and antipyretic. It is useful in burning sensation, vomiting, biliousness, urinary discharges, thirst, leprosy, constipation, inflammations, erysipelas, piles, anaemia, strangury. It is also used in "Kapha", thirst sweats, anuiria, poisoning, "Tridosha", ophthalmia, incipient blindness. Seed is acrid, sweet, aphrodisiac and antipyretic. Seeds are useful in biliousness, asthma, bronchitis, leucorrhoea, vomiting, "vata". Flowers are cooling, aperient, refrigerent. Fruits are useful to improve apetite, heart diseases, liver complaint, cold in nose, stops nasal haemorrhage, purifies humours of the body. Ripe fruit is an astrigent. It has anticancerous property. Root bark is an astringent. Infusion of leaves with fenugreek seeds applied in chronic dysentery, bitter tonic.
NBPGR:
The root bark is astringent, and is useful in ulcerative stomatitis and gastrohelcosis. The bark is useful in gonorrhoea, jaundice, diarrhoea and myalgia. The leaves are useful in conjunctivitis, inflammation, dyspepsia, diarrhoea and dysentery. The fruit are sour, astringent, bitter, acrid, sweet, cooling, anodyne, ophthalmic, carminative, digestive, stomachic, laxative, atterant. alexeteric, aphrodisiac, diuretic, antipyretic, tonic and trichogenous. They are useful in vitiated conditions of tridosa; diabetes, cough, asthma, bronchitis. They are also useful in leprosy, jaundice strangury, diarrhoea, dysentery, cardiac disorder, intermittent fevers and greyness of hair.


Chemistry:
NBPGR: Roots yielded ellagic acid, quercetin, b-sitosterol; plant gave tannins-glucogallin, corilagin, chebulagic acid, 3-6-digalloyl glucose , fruit, a rich natural source of vitamin C, contained cytokinin like substance identified a zeatin, zeatin riboiside and zeatin, nucleotide, suspension culture gave phyllemblin.
