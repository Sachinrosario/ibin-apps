 Taxonomic Details
Correct Name : Meliosma pinnata Roxb. 
Family : Sabiaceae
Synonyms :  Millingtonia pinnata Roxb.,; Meliosma arnottiana (Wight) Walp.,; Meliosma ferruginea Blume; Meliosma ridley King;
Common Names :
Bengali: Bativa
Assam: Henguma, Banpasola, Dermi-asing, Mamoi.
 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
Medium-sized tree, found in eastern Himalayas and Assam up to an altitude of 1,500 m.
Habitat:    Dense primary forests and Dry Hills. Biotic Zone:    Eastern ghats, Himalaya and Western Ghats.

Availability : 
Misc:    Indomalaysia  and  Madgol hills.  
Country:    China and India.
Zone:    East, North East and Southzone.
State:    Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Meghalaya, Sikkim and West Bengal.
District:    Changlang, Khasi hills,  Siang, Subansiri, Tirap and Visakhapatnam.
Altitude :    0-1600,
