DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A very variable, evergreen spiny or sometimes, unarmed tree or shrub, with drooping branches, found either in a wild or cultivated state in the drier parts of India. Bark greyish brown; leaves bipinnate with 2-4 pairs of pinnae: pinnules 10-46 pairs, 5-20 mm. Long ; flowers small, yellowish in dense spikes; pods yellow, 10-25 cm. X 8-15 mm., straight or falcate, flat or cylindrical, often with transverse depressions between the seeds; seeds 10-30 in a pod, ovoid, flattened, 7 mm. x 3 mm., hard, yellowish brown, shiny.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Leaf extract as pesticide against paddy leaf spot, sheath blight, chilly fruit rot, tomato virus, redgram, cowpea, mosaci diseases.

Parts Used: Leaves


Harvesting Practices:

 Harvest matured leaves only


Produce and Products:


Chemistry:  

Carotene content, Ether, Potassium, Phosphorus.

Processing Techniques:  

Leaves grinded and added with water. 1 kg leaves in 10 litters of water, disease, 500 l/ha, Twice at 15 days interval.

Miscellaneous Information

Aquueous and alcoholic extracts of the fresh leaves show a marked antibacterial activity aginst Micrococcus pyogenes var. Aureus and Bacillus coli. Aqueous extract of leaves in reported to have toxic effect on tomato seedlings.
