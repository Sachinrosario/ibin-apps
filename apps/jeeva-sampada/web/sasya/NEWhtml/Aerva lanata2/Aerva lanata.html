DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stem and branches terete, striate, wooly. Leaves alternate, suborbicular-obovate or ovate-elliptic, 0.5-5.0 x 0.3-2.5 cm, apiculate to acute at apex, cuneate at base, entire at margin, densely white pubescent; petiole 0.2-1.0 cm long. Flowers in solitary or axillary, clustered, white, cylindrical, 4-15 mm long sessile spikes, bisexual; bracts deltoid-ovate, 0.6-1.0 cm long, mucronate, white, hairy without, persistent; bracteoles similar or slightly smaller. Tepals or perianth segments 5, densely lanate dorsally; outer 2 hyaline, ovate-oblong, 0.6-1.2 cm long and inner 3 slightly shorter and narrower with green midrib. Stamens 5, alternating with pseudostaminodes. Utricles rotundate, 0.1 cm across, compressed. Seeds reniform, c 0.7 mm long, black, shinning.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: Decoction of plant is used in cough, sore throat and diabetes and also used as diuretic.

Chemistry:

b-sitosterol, sitosteryl palmitate and a-amyrin were isolated from the Aerva lanata. (Aiyar et al, 1972).

Four flavanoid glycosides (I, II, III and IV) (Fig.1)were isolated from A.lanata and their structures determined by chemical and spectral analysis. (Zadorozhnii, et al, 1986).

b-sitosterol, Campesterol and chrysin was detected and isolated from A.lanata. (Wassel and Ammar, 1989).

Flowering and fruiting parts of A.lanata contained hemicellulose, starch, an acid soluble polysaccharide and water soluble polysaccharides, galactose, glucose, mannose, xylose, arabinose, and rhamnose contents were determined. (Mallabaev et al, 1989) C.A. 74847P, 111, 1989.

Hentriacontane, b-sitosterol, its D-glucoside, a-amyrin and betulin were identified and percentage were calculated. (Chandra and Sastry, 1989)

b-sitosterol, daucosterol, syringic acid, Vanilic acid, feruloyltyramine and feruloylchomovanillylamine were isolated. (Zapesochnaya et al, 1991) C.A. 68394e, 115,1991.

Alkaloids, aervine (10-hydroxy canthin-6-one)(Fig.2a), methylaervine (10-methylcanthin-6-one)(Fig.2b), aervoside (canthin-6-one-10,-O-b-D-glucopyranoside) (Fig.2c) and aervolanine (6, methoxy-b-carboline-1-propionic acid) (Fig.3) were isolated and their structures were confirmed. (Zapesochnaya et al, 1991) C.A.148241p,116, 1992.

Three saponin glycosides (I,II,III) were isolated, I afforded b-sitosterol and glucose, II yielded lupeol, galactose and glucose, III afforded oleanolic , galactose and glucose, rhamnose with this lauric, myristic, myristoleic ,  palmitoleic, stearic, oleic and linoleic acids  were also  identified. (Abdelwahab et al ,1997)

Carbohydrates arabinose, rhamnose, xylose, galactose, glucose, nannose and mannitol were identified in A.lanata. (Aboutabl et al, 1999) C.A. 104799,130, 1999.


Pharmacology:

Cardiovascular effects of Aerva lanta were studied which showed more potent in producing positive inotropic effect even at dose level of 10-20 mg/ml of perfusion fluid. (Tripathi et al, 1985).

A.lanta induced diuresis, urine output observed with the fresh plant extract was elevated, flowers were found to be most effective. ( Udupihille and Jiffry, 1986).

Aerva exhibited smooth muscle relaxant effect in adose dependent manner as well as significant antispasmodic activity. (Wassel et al , 1997).

Alcoholic extract of Aerva lanta produced inhibition of carrageenan induced rat paw edema and also showed significant diuretic activity. ( Vetrichelvan et al 2000).

A.lanta was found to be reduce the increase of blood sugar in alloxan  induced diabetic rats. ( Vetrichelvan and Jegadeesan, 2002).
