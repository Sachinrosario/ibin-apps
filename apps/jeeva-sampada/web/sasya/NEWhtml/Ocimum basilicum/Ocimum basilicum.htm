DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
An erect, almost glabrous herb, 30-90 cm, high, Leaves ovate-lanceolate, acuminate, toothed or entire, glabrous on both surfaces, glandular; flowers white or pale purple, in simple or much-branches racemes, often thyroid; nutlets ellipsoid, black, pitted.

Miscellaneous Information

 Seeds possess demulcent, stimulant, diuretic, diaphoretic and cooling properties; they are given internally i cases of habitual constipation and piles.

Aqueous exracts of seeds are active against Gram-positive bacteria and mycobacteria. Alcoholic extracts inhibit, the coagulase activity of Micrococcus pygones var. Aureus.

The plants is very variable and its botanical nomenclature is complicated; several designations have often been assigned to one and the same type; polymorphism and cross-pollination under cultivation have given rise to a number or sub-species, varieties and races, differing in height, habit of growth, degree of hairiness, and colour of stems, leaves and flowers; and some types and forms have been confused with other species.
