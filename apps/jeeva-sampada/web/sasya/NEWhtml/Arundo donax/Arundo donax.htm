HABIT & HABITAT:

Native to mediterranean region, found wild and cultivated, usually near water in the lower Himalayas from Kashmir to Eastern India, upto 2,400m extending Southwards to peninsular India, cultivated for ornament in gardens.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

- A tall, robust, erect, branched, perennial cane like grass, upto 8m in height and 1-4cm in diameter.
- Rhizomes almost bulbous, creeping, thick, forming knotty masses.
- Culms hollows with walls 2-7mm thick, multi-noded, internodes varying in length from 12-30cm, outer tissue siliceous, very hard, brittle, with a smooth glossy surface that turns pale golden yellow when mature.
- Leave conspicuously 2-ranked, linear-lanceolate, with persistent leaf sheaths.
- Panicles, 30-60 cm long, erect, plume-like, silky hairy, cream coloured or brown spikelets 8-15 cm long, 2-7 flowered.
- Fruits oblong.

Produce and Products:
Stem - primitive wood wind instruments, modern clarinets, oboes, bassoons, organ - bag - pipe flutes etc. for production of writing, wrapping and printing papers, for rayon-pulp.
Young canes - lattices for drying fruits, for basket and wicker work, chair-seats, mats, fishing-rods, umbrella-handles, walking sticks, pipe-stems, fish traps.
Soaked canes are crushed and made into chicken pens and boxes.
Canes - light constructional work, building crude shelters, in reinforcing concrete, mats made out of canes are employed for anchoring wall-plaster; in scientific instruments, in explosives.
Stem fiber - similar to hemp.
Rhizome - possess sudorific, diuretic and anti-lactant properties and given as decoction or infusion, employed in dropsy, fresh and young buds of rhizomes in homeopalsy.
Ethanolic extract of rhizome shows hypotensive and anti-spasmodic effect against spasm induced by histamine, serdonins acetylcholine.
Inflorescence - dyed and used for decoration.
