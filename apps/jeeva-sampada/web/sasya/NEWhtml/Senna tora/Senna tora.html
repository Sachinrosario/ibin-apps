DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves 5-10 cm long, rachis grooved, pubescent, stipules 1.3-2 cm long, caducous; leaflets 3 pairs, obovate-oblong, membranous, oblique or rounded at base, main nerves 8-10 pairs; petioles 1.2-4.0 cm long, ribbed, grooved, pubescent; glands linear-cylindric or fusiform, 1.5-4.0 mm long, between two lowest pairs of leaflets; stipules linear, 5-15 mm long, rarely falcate, subacuminate, ciliate. Racemes short, peduncle pubescent, c 3-4 mm long, bearing a single pair of flower, axillary, usually solitary; bracts linear, 2-4 mm long, acute; pedicels 0.4-1.0 cm long, pubescent. Sepals membranous, narrowly ovate, 5-6 mm long, rounded, hairy outside. Petals unequal, narrowly ovate, 8-10 mm long, rounded. Stamens 10, upper 3 reduced to staminodes. Ovary appressed hairy, ribbed; style glabrous; stigma truncate, ciliate. Pods linear-cylindric, 10-16 x 0.5 cm, obliquely septate, subtetragonous, much curved when young, sutures very broad. Seeds 25-30, rhomboid, 5 mm across, glossy.

Uses:
 Leaves and seeds are acrid, thermogenic, laxative, depurative, antiperiodic, anthelmintic, ophthalmic, expectorant and cardiotonic and useful in ringworm, pruritus, leprosy, skin diseases, hepatopathy, helminthiasis, flatulence, colic, dyspepsia, intermittent fevers, constipation, opthalmopathy, cough, bronchitis, cardiac disorder and haemorrhoids.
