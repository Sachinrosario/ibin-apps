Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

Aquatic plant, inland water.
Availability :


Conservation/ Threat Status : Introduced into India as ornamental, now found all over, but now occur as serious weed.

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Perennial aquatic plant, floating in water or partly rooting in mud in shallow waters, with numerous long fibrous roots.

Short leafy stem and showy flowers.

Leaves in rosettes, spoon or paddle shaped with rounded apex, 2-8 in. diameter.

Petioles turbinately swollen and spongy, enabling the plant to float.

Inflorescence spicate, 6-10 in. long with numerous (3-35) flowers.

Flowers mauve or lilac coloured, funnel-shaped, 1-1.5 in long.

The peduncle of the inflorescence bends over immediately after flowering and the withering flowers are pushed under water.
Fruits a capsule with numerous seeds.

Produce and Products:
Manure; fuel; compost (water-hyacinth mixed with earth cowdung, and wood ashes in the Chinese compost fashion); fodder-pig fodder in central and South China and in Malaya.

For paper, plastic industry; boards and for paper mache or mixed with cement and whiting and moulded into tiles.

fiber - unsuitable (in Cochin-China) for spinning but may be used for wicker and basket work and as substitute for cane for chair bottoms.

As a table vegetable in Formosa.Rich in carotene, for the production of carotene concentrates.
