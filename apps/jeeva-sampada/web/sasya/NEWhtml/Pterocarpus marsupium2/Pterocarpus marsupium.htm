Chemistry:  

First naturally occurring hydrobenzoin - marsupol (fig. 3) - isolated and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
Isolation of 5,4' -dimethoxy-8-methylisoflavone- 7-O-a-L- rhamnopyranoside from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
New sesquiterpene alcohol - selin-4(15)-en-1b,11-diol (fig. 4) along with b-eudesmol, erythrodiol 3-monoacetate and pterostilbene isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
New compound - marsupsin (fig. 1) isolated and its structure elucidated as 2-benzyl-2,4',6-trihydroxy-4-methoxybenzo(B)furan-3(2H)- one. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.
New C-glucosyl-b-hydroxy dihydrochalcone - pterosupin (fig. 2) together with pseudobaptigenin, liquiritigenin, isoliquiritigenin, garbenzol, 5-deoxy- kaempferol and p-hydroxybenzaldehyde isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.
Carpusin (marsupsin) isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.
Retusin- 7-O-b-D-glucopyranoside, irisolidone- 7-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside, 5,7-dihydroxy-6- methoxy- 7-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside and 7-hydroxy-5,4'-dimethoxy-8-methylisoflavone- 7- rhamnoside isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)g.
Pterostilbene, (2S)-7- hydroxyflavanone, isoliquiritigenin, liquiritigenin, 7,4'-dihydroxyflavone, marsupsin, pterosupin, p-hydroxybenzaldehyde and (2R)-3-(p-hydroxyphenyl) lactic acid isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)h.
Structure of propterol isolated from heartwood, was established as 1,3-bis(4-hydroxyphenyl)-propan-2-ol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)i.
Propterol B (fig. 5) isolated and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
Heartwood afforded liquiritigenin, oleanolic acid, pterostilbene and 4,2',4'-trihydroxychalcone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
Isolation of (-)epicatechin from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.
Isolation of 8-C-b-D-glucopyranosyl-3,7,4'-trihydroxy- and 3,7,3',4'-tetra- hydroxyflavones and 3'-C-b-D-glucopyranosyl-a-hydroxydihydrochalcone from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.
Two new flavanone glycosides isolated from roots and characterized as 7-hydroxy-6,8-dimethylflavanone- 7-O-a-L-arabinopyranoside and 7,8,4'-tri- hydroxy- 3',5'-dimethoxyflavanone-4'-O-b-D-glucopyranoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)e.
A new flavonoid - retusin-8-O-a-L-arabinopyranoside - isolated along with lupeol and naringenin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)f.
Isolation of two new aurones from flowers and their characterization as 4,6,4'-trihydroxyaurone-6-O-rhamnopyranoside (fig. 6a) and 4,6,4'-trihydroxy-7-methylaurone-4-O-rhamnopyranoside (fig. 6b). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
Two new aurones isolated from heartwood and their structures determined as 6,4'-dihydroxy-7-methylaurone-6-O-rhamnopyranoside (fig. 7a) and 4,6,3',4'-tetrahydroxyaurone-6-O-rhamnopyranoside (fig. 7b). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
Isolation of marsupsin, pterosupin and liquiritigenin from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
A novel 6,7,3',4'-tetraoxygenated homoiso-flavonoid, characterized as 6-hydroxy-7-O-methyl-3 (3-hydroxy-4-O-Me benzyl) chroman-4-one, has been isolated from the ether soluble portion of the heartwood of Pterocarpus marsupium. (Jain et al, 1997). C.A. 1997, 126, 235940r.
A new flavanol glycoside was isolated from the roots of Pterocarpus marsupium. Its structures was determined as 6-hydroxy-3,5,7,4'-tetramethoxyflavone 6-O-rhamnopyranoside by spectral data and chemical degradation. (Yadav and Singh, 1998). MAPA 9905-3479.

Pharmacology: 

Extract of heartwood showed statistically significant hypoglycaemic action in fasting rabbits three and five hours after oral administration. No harmful effect was noticed in doses which showed hypoglycaemic action. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).
Alcoholic extract of stem significantly lowered blood sugar and improved glucose tolerance of rabbits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
(-)Epicatechin, when given before or within 24 hr after alloxan, reversed hypoglycaemia in alloxan-diabetic rats. However, when (-) epicatechin (30 mg/kg, i.p. twice daily for 3 days) was given 92 hr after alloxan, there was no significant difference in blood glucose levels between control and (-) epicatechin - treated rats. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)j.
(-)Epicatechin showed positive chronotropic and inotropic effects on isolated frog heart which were blocked by propranol. In higher dose it caused hyperglycaemic in rats and this effect was blocked by propranol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)g.
Administration of ethyl acetate extract for 14 consecutive days to rats produced significant reduction in levels of serum triglycerides, total cholesterol, and LDL- and VDL-cholesterol without any significant effect on level of HDL-cholesterol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
Liquiritigenin and pterosupin significantly lowered serum cholesterol, LDL-cholesterol and atherogenic index in rats. Pterosupin also lowered serum triglycerides. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.
Pharmacological studies of Pterocarpus marsupium has been discussed in animal models and non-insulin dependent diabetic patients. (Bhandari and Grover, 1998). MAPA 9901-0200.
Pharmacognostic studies on heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
Standardisation of powder samples of heartwood of Pterocarpus marsupium was carried out using following parameters. (1) macroscopy and microscopy, (2) physicochemical evaluation: Total ash, water-soluble, acid in-soluble ash, extractive values and analysis of ash: (3) chemical profile of the drug UV-visible spectroscopy, fluorimetris study, TLC studies and HPTLC fingerprinting. (Miranda et al, 2000-2001. MAPA 2001-04-1871.
