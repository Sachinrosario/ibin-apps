 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
An erect branching herb or undershrub, rarely tree, distributed throughout the hotter parts of India from Kumaon at altitude of 4,000 ft to Sukkim, Malacca and Ceylon. Two species occur in India, a few exotics are found as weeds. An erect, branched herb or undershrub found in waste places, road sides, bunds of rice fields and fallow lands almost throughout the hotter parts of India, and ascending to 1,200 m. in the Himalayas.
BioticZone:    Konkan, Konkan plain, Malacca peninsula,  Tropics, West Coast and Western Ghats.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Erect branching herb or under shrub ; stems and branches nearly glabrous. Leaves ovate, oblong, acute, serrate with few hairs. Flowers small, pink, subsessile, densely crowded in terminal clusters. Capsules depressed-globose; valves sparingly hairy, mottled black and grey

Chemistry
Analysis of fibre gave the following values: cellulose, 80.34; lignin, 17.87; fat & wax, 1.27; nitrogen, 0.22 ; and ash, 0.497%. The fibre is used for tying bundles of rice plants and for making fishing lines ; stems are employed for horizontal ties in hut roofs.

Other Uses :
  1. Leaves are applied as poultice for sores and swellings of the abdomen. A decoction of the leaves and roots is used in dysentery. 
  2. The stem bark yields a strong, silvery white fibre. The fibre shows: elongation at break, 6.0% and intrinsic strength.
  3. Wood and Medicinal purposes.
