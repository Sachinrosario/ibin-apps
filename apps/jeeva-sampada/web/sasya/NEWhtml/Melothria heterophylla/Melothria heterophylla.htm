HABIT & HABITAT:
A large genus of annual or perennial climbing or prostrate herbs distributed in the tropics of the world. About 12 species occur in India. A scandent herb with tuberous roots found throughout India ascending up to 2,100 mts. in the hills.
Habitat:    Gardens and Roadsides, Hedges, Hill forests, Low elevation, mixed forests, near hilly streams and   Rocky habitat.
Biotic Zone:    Eastern ghats, Konkan plain, West Coast and Western Ghats.

Availability :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves polymorphous, ovate, sub-orbicular, oblong or narrowly lanceolate, undivided or variously lobed, remotely denticulate.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
Flowers small, yellow: male flowers various, sub-umbellate, umbellate, racemes or densely or laxly fascicled, female flowers solitary: fruits oblong, brown, with many sub- spherical. smooth seeds.
