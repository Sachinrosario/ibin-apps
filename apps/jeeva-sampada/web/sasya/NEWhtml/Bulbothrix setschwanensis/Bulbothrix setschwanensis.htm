Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A foliose lichen commonly grows on trees and boulders in almost all the localities up to 2000 m altitude in open places.
Availability :

In India, it is distributed in Arunanchal Pradesh, Manipur, Uttaranchal and West Bengal and outside India, it is known from China and Nepal. Arunachal Pradesh: Changlang district, Namdapha, Barmanala; Debang valley district, Roing-Anini road, near 8th mile; G.B. Garh; Lohit district, Hayuliang; Hayugam-Barpu foot track; Lower Subansiri district, Hapoli-Sorobasti road; Zero-Tamen road; Tirap district, Khonsa; West Kameng district, Sessa; New Bomdila forest. DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Thallus mineral grey to yellowish, adnate to the substratum, forms patches; 5-10 cm diam. lobes subirregularly branched, 2-5 mm wide; margins crenate with bulbate cilia; upper surface smooth; lower surface pale brown with simple rhizinae; isidia and soredia absent. Apothecia ecoronate, 3-5 mm in diam. with oval, ellipsoid, 12-16 x 6-10 µm in sized spores (Divakar, 2002).

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
The species is rich in bioactive substances and can be used for preparation of brown dye and antibiotic activity. It also shows inhibitory activities as mentioned below
(Behra & Makhija,2001).

Tyrosinase inhibitor: The tyrosinase inhibitors have become increasingly important as cosmetic and medicinal products, primariely to control melanin pigmentation.
Tyrosinase is the key enzyme in the first stage of the melanogenesis pathway, catalyzing the conservation of L-tyrosine into L-dopaquinone. Melanin synthesis inhibitors are topically used for treating localized hyperpigmentation in humans such as lentigo, nevus,ephelis,post-inflammatory state and melanoma of pregnancy.
Tyrosinase is also one of the key enzymes responsible for controlling the insect molting process and could therefore be used as an insecticide for controlling the pests and insects. The development of superoxide scavengers or tyrosinase inhibitors will contribute to the prevention of skin browning (Behra & Makhija, 2002).
Xanthine oxidase inhibitor: The species inhibites the xanthine oxidase activity . Therefore it is used for patients who suffered from hepatitis, brain tumour, hyperuricaemia and gout disorders (Behera & Makhija, 2002).

Chemistry:
Atranorin and salazinic acid.

Processing Techniques:
Growing tissue obtained from natural thallus of B.setschwanensis was cultured and subcultured following the technique of Behra & Makhija(2002).The presence of atranorin and salazinic acids in the cultured tissue has confirmed the identity of species. For the determination of tyrosinase- inhibitory activity, inhibition of xanthine oxidase and inhibition of nitro blue tetrazolium(NBT) reduction, 1 mg dried cultured tissue was extracted by 5 ml of acetone and methanol separately and incubated overnight at room temperature. The extract was filtered by Whatman filter paper no.40 and concentrated in a vaccum desiccator for 6 hours. The tyrosinase inhibitory activity was measured and calculated as presented by Behra & Makhija(2002).The acetone extract of the natural thallus and the cultured tissue of B.setschwanensis were found to have inhibitory activity on xanthine oxidase, NBT reduction and tyrosinase. The effect of various culture conditions and the time period on the growth and the bioactivities have also been presented. (Behra & Makhija, 2001)
