DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Armed, much branched shrub or small tree. Branches jointed, 3-5 winged, wings repand, sinuate. Stipular spines 2, short, sharp, divaricate. Leaves small, fleshy, obovate-oblong or spathulate, soon deciduous. Cyathia 3-nate in short axillary pedunculate cymes. Involucres hemispherical, yellow; glands 5, large, semicircular. Male flowers in groups of 5, 8 in each; anthers dehiscing vertically. Female flowers: styles 3, 2-fid. Fruit schizocarp; cocci compressed.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Cymes from the long peducles from the crown on root stock, appearing after the leaves that have fallen, 3-many flowered; Flower: Bracts at fork ovate-lanceolate, monoecious, combined in an inflorescence of many male florets surrounding female floret ; Male Flower: a stalked stamen without floral envelop ; Female Flower: Ovary 3-celled on ultimately exerted stalk in the center of greenish involucre, ovules solitary in each cell, style 3, free; Fruit: Capsule of 3 two valved compressed cocci with styles combined in the middle ; Seed: Broadly ovoid, subacute, smooth. Flowering & Fruiting : April – May  .

Floral and seed biology:
Fl. & Fr.: April – September.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
A new diterpene lactone – caudicifolin (fig. 1) isolated from rhizomes and its structure determined by X-ray studies. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Isolation and structure determination of 3-oxoatisan-16a,17-diol (fig. 2b) from rhizomes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

A new diterpene – ent-atisan-3b,16a,17-triol (fig. 2a) isolated from rhizomes and its structure elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

A new ellagic acid glycoside – 3,3’-di-O-methylellagic acid – 4’-rutinoside – isolated along with 3,4,3’-tri-O-methylellagic acid from rhizomes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.

Isolation of a new ent-seco-atisane diterpene – ent-3,4-seco-4,16a,17-trihydroxyatisan-3-oic acid (fig. 3) from rhizomes and its crystal structure determination. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

A highly oxygenated ent-abietane diterpene isolated and characterized a (4R, 4aR)-4,4a-dihydroxy-3-hydroxymethyl-7,7,10a-trimethyl-2,4,4a,5,6,6a,7,8,9,10,10a,10b-dodecahydro-phenanthro[3,2-b]furan-2-one (fig. 4). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.


Pharmacology:
From various extracts of Euphorbia acaulis , the n-hexane fraction showed marked anti-inflammatory activity in carrageenan induced oedema in rats and mice as compared to phenylbutazone and was equipotent in adrenal- ectomised rats. In chronic models of formaldehyde and adjuvant arthritis, its anti-arthritic activity was found to be superior to that of phenylbutazone. It had a diuretic effect but did not show any analgesic or antipyretic activity. (Singh et al, 1984) .


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for fever (Root, Bedi, 1978); Gout, rheumatism (Root, Maheshwari et al, 1980; Maheshwari et al, 1981); Veterinary dysentery, fever (Root, Maheshwari, 1986).
