DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES  
A shrub or small tree. Leaves ovate, deep lobed 5-12 cm long and 3-8 cm wide, with cordate base and acuminate tips, scabrous above. Fruits dark red 1 to 1.5 cm long.

Chemistry
Leaves of Morus indica Linn were extracted with different solvents of increasing polarity - petroleum, either, benzene, chloroform and water. The extracts were tested for their components. It was found that petroleum ether contains phytosterols, benzene extract contains phytosterols and alkaloids, chloroform extract contains saponins, alkaloids and phytosterols, the alcoholic extract contains saponins and tannins. The extracts were studied for their diuretic and anti-inflammatory activities. Benzene, alcoholic and aqueous extracts showed prominent anti-inflammatory and diuretic activities
