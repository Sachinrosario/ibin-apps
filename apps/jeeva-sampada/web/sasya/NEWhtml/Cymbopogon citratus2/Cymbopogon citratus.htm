Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

This densely tufted perennial of the GRAMINEAE family Probably originated in India, it is now wide spread in the tropics and is frequently cultivated in gardens and along path sides.

Availability :

Plenty
Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild, Cultivated.

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Has leaf blades tapered to both ends up to 1 meter long and 5 to 10 mm wide. The flowering pannicles are rarely formed; inflorescence are 30 to 60 cm and nodding; the partial inflorescences are paired racemes of spikelets subtended by spathes.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Anti fungal, Anti insects.

Parts Used:  

Dried leaves, Essential oil extracted from the whole plant is used as a antifungal and anti insects.


Harvesting Practices:Matured plants are harvested.


Produce and Products:


Chemistry:  

Geraniol, Geraniol acetate, Camphor, citral, camphene, etc.

Processing Techniques:  

Whole plants are steam distilled to obtain the essential oil.
