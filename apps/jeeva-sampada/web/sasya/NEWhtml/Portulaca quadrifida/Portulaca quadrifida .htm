DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES  
This is a slender, prostate, annual, succulent herb, the stems rooting at the nodes from each of, which grows a ring of hairs. The leaves are very shortly toothed, ovate-elliptic, and about 5 millimeters long. The flowers are small, terminal and solitary and are surrounded by a 4-leaved involucre and copious white hairs. There are four yellow petals. The capsule dehisces horizontally, and contains minutely tubercled seeds.

Other Uses
  1. The plant is used in skin diseases and in diseases of the kidneys, bladder and lungs
  2. In Egypt the bruised leaves are used as an anticephalic.
  3. Plant cures low fevers, asthma, cough, urinary discharges, inflammations; good for eye diseases and ulcers.
  4. The bruised fresh leaves are externally applied in akki; erysipelas.
