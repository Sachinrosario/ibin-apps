Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Armned under shrub, leaves elliptic, ovate, often spine tipped, flowers yellow, fruit capsular, found growing throughout hotter parts of India.

Availability :

Throughout hotter parts of India.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Common 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Much branched shrubs or Undershrubs, 0.5-1.5 m tall, armed with white, sharp thorns often forming clusters; stem glabrous. Leaves elliptic-oblong, 9-15 x 4-6 cm, tapering at base, entire, acuminate and often bristle-tipped, glabrous above, pubescent beneath especially when young; petioles 2-20 mm long, with 2-4, axillary, sharp thorns. Flowers solitary, axillary or in terminal short spikes; bracts foliaceous, oblong-lanceolate, 12-15 x 4-5 mm, acute, bristle-tipped; bracteoles linear-subulate, 10-12 mm long, bristle-tipped. Outer sepals unequal, oblong-lanceolate, 8-12 mm long, mucronate, the inner ones narrower and shorter than the outer sepals, mucronate. Corolla orange-yellow; tube broad, 2-2.5 cm long; limb 2-lipped; lobes oblong-obovate, rounded at apex. Stamens 2 perfect with 2 staminodes, included or slightly exerted. Capsules ovoid, 2-2.5 cm long, beaked. Seeds 4, ovoid-orbicular, 6-8 mm across, appressed hairy. Barleria prionitis is shrubby, is armed with axillary sharp spikes and bears large orange coloured flowers.
