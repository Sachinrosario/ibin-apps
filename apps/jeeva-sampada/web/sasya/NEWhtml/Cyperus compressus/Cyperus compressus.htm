 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
In Hawai'i, "naturalized in disturbed, moist sites and coastal areas, 0-300 m" (Wagner et al., 1999).  In Fiji, "a naturalized adventive, found near sea level in standing water and damp places, along rivers, on seashores, and in coconut plantations" (Smith, 1979).  "Occasional in disturbed places" (Whistler, 1988).
Availability : 
America,Asia,Ceylon,Singapore.
India:Andhra Pradesh,Arunachal Pradesh,Assam,Diu & Daman,Goa,Gujarath,Karnataka,Kerala,Maharashtra,Meghalaya,Tamil Nadu,Rajasthan,Utar Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Annuals, cespitose, culms to 40 cm high; leaves 1.5-3 mm wide, sheaths often reddish; inflorescence of a few subsessile umbels; spikelets 1-2.5 cm long, 3-5 mm wide, 12-30-flowered; rachilla not winged; achene obovoid, trigonal, the sides slightly concave, brown to almost black."  (Stone, 1970)"Tufted annuals with fibrous roots; culms relatively stout, spreading, 8-35 cm tall, trigonous, smooth.  Leaves a few per culm, basal, blades pale green, linear, flat, shorter than the culm, 1-3 mm wide; sheaths membranous, pale brown, striate.  Inflorescences umbelliform or congested, 2-10 cm long and wide, rays, when present, 2-5, spreading, 0.89-5 cm long, slightly compressed, spikes bearing 3-10 spikelets on an abbreviated axis; involucral bracts 2-4, leaf-like, unequal in length, the longest one 2-3 times as long as the inflorescence; spikelets 15-40-flowered, green, turning pale yellowish brown at maturity, oblong-lanceolate, 10-25 mm long, 2.5-3 mm wide, compressed, rachilla not winged; glumes herbaceous or thin and coriaceous, ovate or broadly so, 3-3.5 mm long, strongly folded, the keel green, acute, 3-nerved on both sides, apex acute with a straight mucro ca. 0.8 mm long; style elongate; stigmas 3.  Achenes dark brown, shiny, broadly obovoid, trigonous, 1-1.3 mm long, minutely puncticulate.
