DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual, erect, slender, glabrous herb, 7-20 cm high. Stem many, almost filiform; branches umbellate, the nodes thichened. Leaves both radical and cauline; radical leaves sessile, spathulate to linear-spathulate, 0.5-1.4 cm long, obtuse at apex, narrowed at base; cauline leaves 2-8 in a whorl, narrowly linear, 0.5-1.8 x 0.2-0.7 cm, apiculate at apex, subsessile to very shortly petioled. Peduncles subumbellate or solitary at the tips of the branches, rather long, thin, stiff. Flowers white in terminal or axillary trichotomous umbellate cymes; pedicels erect or patent, filiform. Sepals elliptic-oblong to ovate-oblong, 1.5-2.5 mm long, obtuse, with white membranous margin. Stamens 3-5. Capsules broadly ellipsoid, equaling the sepals. Seeds reniform-suborbicular, smooth, blackish-brown, irregularly reticulately veined.
