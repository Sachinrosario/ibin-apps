HABIT & HABITAT
A perennial herb with yellow flowers, is found in Maharashtra, Bihar, Orissa and Andhra Pradesh.

Habitat: Marshy Areas.
BioticZone: Konkan plain, North West Himalayas and Subtropical himalayas.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used: 
Roots.

Uses
  1. The fruits have a powerful lemon-like odour and are used in medicine, and as a condiment. 
  2. The roots taste like carrots, and are eaten as a tonic and febrifuge.

Other Uses
  1. Fruits possess carminative, diuretic, stimulant and tonic properties and are given in the form of an infusion in gastric and intestinal troubles. 
  2. The fruits yield a small quantity (c. i .5%) of a light yellow essential oil (sp. gr.15-5', 0.900; ) having a strong odour of carrot oil.
