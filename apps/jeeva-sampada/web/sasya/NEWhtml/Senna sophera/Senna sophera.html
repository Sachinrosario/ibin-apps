HABIT & HABITAT
Annual or perennial, undershrub or shrub, 2.4-3.0 m tall, pubescent when young. It is found in throughout India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves 15-20 cm long, rachis grooved with solitary conical gland at base; leaflets 6-10 pairs, opposite, ovate-lanceolate to elliptic-lanceolate, 3.5-6.5 x 1.2 cm, acute or acuminate at apex, rounded or slightly oblique at base, membranous; petioles 4-5 cm long, grooved. Stipules ovate, caduceus. Flowers yellow in axillary, short, few flowered, corymbose raceme; peduncles 0.6-2.5 cm long; pedicels 0.6-2.0 cm long, pubescent; bracts ovate-lanceolate or lanceolate, 4-5 mm long, acute or obtuse, pubescent; bracteoles absent. Sepals oblong-suborbicular, 8-10 mm long, obtuse, pubescent outside. Petals yellow, orbicular to obovate, 1.2-1.6 cm long. Fertile stamens 7; staminodes 3. Ovary appressed pubescent; stigma punctiform, ciliate. Pods 7.0-10 x 0.2-0.5 cm, thick, slightly reduced, septate between seeds. Seeds 30-40, broadly ovoid, c 4 x 2 mm, acute, compressed, dark brown; areole oval-oblong, 1.5-2.0 x 1.0 mm.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant is used as febrifuge, purgative, diuretic and tonic. Decoction of plant is used in acute bronchitis. Root used in snake bite. Leaf used against ringworm infection. Leaf and seed is used in skin disease and antiperiodic. In Ayurveda system of medicine root is used in cough, hiccough, asthma, sore throat and biliousness. In Unani system of medicine root is used for treatment of ringworm and heal wound. Fruits are useful in scorpion sting. Seeds are used in cough.
