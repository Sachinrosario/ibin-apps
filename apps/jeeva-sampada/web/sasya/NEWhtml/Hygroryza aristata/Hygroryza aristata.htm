Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 A floating glabrous grass, with culms 1.0-1.5 ft. long and narrowly oblong grains, is found almost throughout India, forming mats on the surface of water in tanks and ponds or creeping on wet ground. It is a perennial grass common in low lying areas and is often seen covering paddy fields in Assam during the rains.

Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Whole plant, Grains.

Uses

1.The grass is said to be relished by cattle.

2.In Assam only the leaves are used as fodder.

3.The grains (BENGAL WILD RICE) are gathered and eaten by the poor. They are reported to be sweet, oleaginous, digestible and cooling and useful in biliousness.
