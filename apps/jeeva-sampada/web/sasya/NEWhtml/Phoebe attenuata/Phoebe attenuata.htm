Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Large tree in subtropical regions of East Indian hills and parts of Western Ghats. Found in the eastern Himalayas and hills of Assam, up to an altitude of 1,200 m.

Tree is shade bearer and can not stand direct exposure in young stages.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium- to large-sized tree. Bark dark grey, exfoliating in papery flakes. Leaves oblong or oblanceolate, crowded at the ends of branchlets, coriaceous.

Floral and seed biology:

Seed Germination & Propagation:

Natural regeneration by seeds takes place profusely even under low cover, provided there is no competition from weeds.

Artificial regeneration is by entire transplanting, transplanting the nursery raised seedlings. Seeds are sown, immediately after collection in May-June, in shaded beds. Transplantation is done in June during the second rainy season, when seedlings are about 20 cm high. Winter transplanting has also given good results.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used: Bark

Uses:

Wood used as fuel. It is a medium quality fuelwood. Wood yields timber. Also used for furniture, bobbins. It is prized espexcially for cabinet work. Used for house construction, planking, commercial and tea-chest plywood. It is suitable for plywood, spars, propellers and air-screws in aircraft construction.
