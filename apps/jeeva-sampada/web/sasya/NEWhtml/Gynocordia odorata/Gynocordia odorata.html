DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves alternate, oblong, ovate-oblong or obovate-oblong, glabrous, short or long acuminate or cordate acuminated at apex, cuneate or rounded at base, entire along margin, dark green above, pale colour baneath, stipules caducous. Flowers deciduous. Male Flowers: pale yellow, fragrant, solitary or few in bracteae axillary corymbs on tubercles on stemms and branches. Sepals 5, connate into a cupular calyx. Petals 5, oblong, obtuse, margin densely ciliate. Stamens numerous, filaments broad towards base, wooly; anther linear-oblong. Female flowers: few on tubercles on stem and branches, sepals and petals as in males but bigger. Staminodes 10-15, carpels 5, ovary globose, unilocular, ovules numerous, styles 5, stigma hastate. Berry globose, pericarp thick, hard. Seeds numerous, ovoid or ellipsoid, irregularly compressed.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
   Inflorescence: Axillary fascicled; Flower: Sweet scented, very large, in the axil of leaves ; Calyx: Cup shaped, 5-toothed, coriaceous ; Corolla: Petals 5, with ciliate scales ; Male Flower:  Stamens many, anthers basifixed, linear, ovary ; Female Flower: Ovary globose, 1-celled, styles 5, stigma large, ovules many on parietal placenta ; Fruit: Berry subglobose, rind thick hard and tough ; Seed: 1, obovoid, compressed .

Floral and seed biology:FL. & Fr.: March- December.

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Fruits are used as anthelmintic. It is useful in bronchitis, ulcers, skin diseases, small tumours, slight inflammatio, leprosy, diabetes, gonorrhoea, fever and  piles (Ayurveda).


Chemistry:
Gynocardin (fig. 1, 6.8%) isolated from defatted seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

Three new triterpenes- odolactone (fig. 2a), acetylodollactone (fig. 2b) and odollactone (fig. 2c) – isolated from bark and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

Three new diterpenoids lactones odolide, isoodolide and hydroxyodolide were isolated from the rots of G. odorota were characterized as ent-kaur-15(16)-en-19à20—olide, ent-kaur-16(17)en-19à20-olide and 16ahydroxy-ent-kauran-19à20-olide n the basis of spectral and chemical analysis. the higher terpenoids isolated were odolactone, trichadenic acid – A and b-sitosterol (Pradhan et al, 1995).
