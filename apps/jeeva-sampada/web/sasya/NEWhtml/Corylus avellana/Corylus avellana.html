DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Male Flower: Solitary branched, in many fid spike, bracteate, perianth 0, stamens 4-8, filaments forked separating the anther; Female Flower: In pairs, in the upper bracts spikes or head, bracteolate, cell 1 ovulate, styles linear ; Fruit: Nut large woody ; Seed: 1 . 

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : August –October. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome number 2n= 22,28

GERMPLASM CHARACTERIZATION:

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: The plant is propagated through seeds, also propagated through grafting.

Soil type: The soil should be well drained light & loamy. Seeds of rarer kind can be sown in plots or flats of sandy soil placed in a green house or frame.

Nutrient requirements: --

Water management:   The plant requires average water need, do not over water. Water regularly.                           

Harvesting period / Method: The plant is harvested by hands.
