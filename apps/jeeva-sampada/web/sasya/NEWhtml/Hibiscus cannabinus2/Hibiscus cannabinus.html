DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual or perennial herbs, 2.5-4 m high; stems prickly glabrous. Leaves unlobed, upper ones palmatilobed to parted, 3-5 or 7-lobed, lobation up to the base of lamina, lobes linear, elliptic to lanceolate, 3-8 x 0.4-2 cm, acuminate at apex, middle nerve with a nectary beneath, glabrous; petioles 3-15 cm; stipules linear to subulate 3-5 mm long.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Flowers axillary solitary or in racemes; pedicels 2.5 mm long, princkly. Epicalyx segments 7-8, spreading or reflexed, up to 1.5 cm long, shorter than calyx, connate or adnate to the base calyx, sparsely covered with upturned, stiff prickles. Calyx lobes free almost to the bases, lobes lanceolate to somewhat deltoid, distinctly 3-nerved with a large nectary on midvein outside, prickly and white archnoid tomentose, outside, and glabrous inside. Corolla yellow with a crimson centre; petals 4-6 x 1-3 cm spreading, glabrous. Staminal column 1.0-2.5, antheriferous throughout. Capsules ovoid to globose, c 2 x 1.5 cm, beaked dehiscing into 5 valves, densely hairy, outside, glossy inside. Seeds c 7 x 4 mm, reniform, dotted with minute brownish scale like tufted hairs, brownish.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Plants is used in fever. Leaves are used as purgative. Flower – antibilious; seed-aphrodisiac analgesic, in bruises; leaf purgative.


Chemistry:
Aldobiouronic acid isolated after partial acid hydrolysis of hemicellulose, identified as 2-O-(4-O-methyl-a-D-glucopyranosyluronic acid)-D-xylpyranose. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Rutin and isoquercetin from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Total ash (8.1) and fatty oil (19.0%) from undecorticated seeds; palmitic, stearic, oleic and linoleic acids and b-sitosterol detected in unsaponifiable fraction by PC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Anthocyanin -cannabinidin -isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Cannabinin (fig. 1) ( delphinidin-3- b-d-glucosido-b-d-xyloside) and myrtillin (delphinidin-3-b-d-glucoside) isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Limonene, phellandrene, a-terpenyl acetate, citral, tolualdehyde isolated from leaves, fruits and seeds of egyptian plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

Cyclohexanol, glucose, fructose, sucrose and mannose along with an unidentified polyhydroxysteroid, mp 275°c and a steroid glycoside, mp. 273°c were isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

N-triacontane, n-tetracosane, n-pentacosane, n-hentriacontane, n-dotriacontane, n- tritriacontane, b-amyrin and b-sitosterol were isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.

Myricetin-3'-a-d-glucoside isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)f.

Kaempferol-3-b-l-rhamnopyranosido-7-a-l-rhamnoside isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)g.

Proanthocyanidin isolated from roots composed of ( -)epicatechol gallate and (- )epicatechol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)h.

Protocatechuic acid, vanillic acid and kaempferol-3,7-o-a-l-dirhamnofuranoside isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)i.

 hexacosane, heptacosane, octacosane, nonacosane, triacontane, hentriacontane, dotriacontane, tetratriacontane, pentatriacontane, hexatriacontane, heptatriacontane, tetracontane, hentacontane, hexacosanol, octacosanol, triacontanol, dotriacontanol and lignoceric, cerotic, montanic and melissic acids isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)j.

(-)Edpicatachol, (-)epicatechol gallate, (+) catechol and (-) epigallocatechol gallate isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Detection of tridecanoic (10.9),palmitic (38.94), stearic (tr),oleic and linoleic (37.7) and sterculic (2.68%) acid in seed oil by GLC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Two dimeric proanthocyanidins B5 and B6 (fig. 6) isolated from root bark, tentative structures of B5 as 3,5,7-3’,4’,pentahydroxyflavan dimer proposed whereas structure of B6 has been elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

The phospholipid fraction from seeds contained phosphatidylcholine (39.2), phosphatidylinositol (30.4), phosphatidylethanolamine (16.0), N-acylphosphatidylethanolamine (9.6), lysophophatidylcholine (0.8),lysophosphatidylinositol and N-acyllycophosphatidylethanolamine. Purified pectic acid isolated from unretted mesta fibres contained backbone of (1®4) - linked galacturonic acid and residues (78.0), arabinose (3.6), rhamnose (3.0) and galactose (1.2%). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
