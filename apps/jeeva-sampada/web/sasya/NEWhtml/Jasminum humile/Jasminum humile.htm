Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT                                                                       
 An erect diffuse shrub with glabrous, angular branches, found throughout the sub-tropical Himalayas from Kashmir to Nepal up to an altitude of 9,000 ft., and in Mount Abu and the hills of S. India above 5,000 ft.

Availability : 

Status :  Both wild and cultivated.

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES                                                                
 Leaves alternate: leaflets, 2-11, very variable in size ; flowers yellow, fragrant, solitary or in short terminal compound corymbose cymes; corolla lobes 5, ovate or obtuse ; carpels black when ripe with crimson juice. Flowers 6 inch in diameter, yellow in terminal corymbos panicles, bracts linear. Pedicels 2-6 inch long. Calyx with 5 very short teeth. Corolla-tube 4-7 inch long with 5 broadly ovate lobes.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
 Breeding System : They are easily propagated by seeds or cuttings.


Phenology :
In Darjeeling , they bear flowers in profusion for a long period and set seed freely. Flowering occurs in May-June and fruiting occurs in Sept-Nov.

Pollinators:
Ants, flies and honey bees.

Other Uses

1.The root is said to be useful in ringworm.

2.The milky juice exuded from incisions in the bark is effective in destroying unhealthy lining walls of chronic sinuses and fistulas.

3.The plants are grown in gardens for their fragrant yellow flowers.

4.The flowers yield an aromatic essential oil used in perfumery.

5.A yellow dye is extracted from the roots.
