Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 A shrubby herb, 1.0-1.3 m height, introduced in to India from tropical America, now natutalized. and found wild in the hills of Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, West Benhal, Sikkim, Meghalaya, Manipur, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu up to 1650 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves 12-15 cm with glands between leaflets, leaflets 3-4 pairs, elliptic-ovate; flowers yellow, in axillary corymbs; pods subcylindric, 8 cm long.

Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
Leaves.

Uses

·        The young leaves are consumed as flavouring agent.

·        The pods are eaten as vegetable. The seeds may be used as a flavouring; they are sometimes substituted for coffee.

Chemistry
The leaves contain ombium, 3-0-(2-rhamnosylglucosyl)-ombium, N1, N8-dibenzoylspermidine, physcion, 5,7-physcionanthrone. the roots contain emodin, ombium, physcion and its 8-galactoside. The pods contain 3,5,3'-trihydroxy-7, 4-dimethoxy-flavone-3-0-beta-D-(+) galactopyranoside, 3,5-dihydroxy-7, 3',4'-trimethoxyflavone-3-0-D (+)-galactosyl-o-beta-D-(+)-galactopyranoside, 1-hydroxy-6-methoxy-3-methyl-anthraquinone-8-0-beta-D-galactosyl-(1->4)-0-beta-D galactopyranoside (C28H32O15, m p 152 decomp), quercetin and chrysophanol. The flowers contain 1->4 and 1->6 isomers of rhamnetin-3-galactosyl-galactoside. They also contain oleanolic acid and docosyl, carnaubyl, ceryl and octacocyl alcohols.

Other Uses

1.The young leaves are crushed with lemon and salt and applied to cure skin diseases. Their decoction is used as a febrifuge..In Indonesia, the boiled or roasted seeds are eaten with rice.

2.Medicinal Uses: It posesses purgative and emmenogogue properties. Seeds possesses emmengogue and febrifugal properties. The seeds were found to be toxic to white leghorn chicks. The rind, if taken, removes the bile and phlegm; its infusion is reported to stimulate the growth of hair. The bark also possesses purgative emmenagogue properties. Th aqueous infusion of bark, when drunk expels bile and phlegm and also worms, if any.

3.The plant is employed in conservation of soil and planted for shade in plantations and as hedge. Yields green manure.
