HABIT & HABITAT
Annual much branched, diffuse fleshy herbs. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Orissa, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Rajasthan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stems angular, smooth, hollow. Leaves repeatedly segmented, lobules flat, linear or linear-lanceolate. Inflorescence terminal raceme; flowers bractiate, bract lanceolate, caudate, equalling the pedicel. Sepal 2, small, ovate-lanceolate, segmented, caducous, antero-posterior. Petals 4, free, rosy, tip pinkish, outer 2 lateral, unequal, one spurred,inner two antero-posterior, equal tip cohering. Stamens 6, in two bundles; filaments filiform, connate. Ovary globose, slightly compressed, one chambered; ovule 1-2, parietal, style filiform, stigma 2-lobed. Fruits globose, rugose or verrucose when dry.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plants are bitter, slightly acrid, astringent and used as laxative, diuretic and alternative. It is useful in dyspepsia and skin affection.


Chemistry:  

19-methyoctacosan-1-ol, 3-methyloctacosan-1,3-diol, C27-29 n-alkanes, b-sitosterol ansd campesterol isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
(-)tetrahydrocoptisine and protopine isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
In addition, (+)-, (±)bicuculline and (±) tetrahydrocoptisine isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.
A phhenolic protoberberine alkaloid - dehydrocheilanthifoline (fig. 1) isolated together with coptisine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.
Structure of narceimine (fig. 2) revised. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
New alkaloid - narlumidine (fig. 3) isolated together with (+) adlumidine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
(-)8-methoxydihydrosanguinarine (fig. 4) isolated from seeds along with fumariline and oxysanguinarine and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.
protopine, tetrahydrocoptisine, b-sitosterol and octacosanol identified in roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.
Structures of narceimine and narlumidine confirmed by chemical studies; protopine nitrate, tetrahydrocoptisine, (+)adlumidine and norsanguinarine isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
Crystal structure of protopine by X-ray analysis. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
A new alkaloid - dihydrocoptisine (fig. 5) isolated from seeds and its structure elucidated; in addition, norsanguinarine, adlumidine and bicuculline also obtained. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.
Isolation of a new seco-phthalide isoquinoline alkaloid - narceimicine (fig. 6) from seeds and its characterization. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.
Isolation of a new phthalide isoquinoline alkaloid - (+)papraine (fig. 10) and its structure elucidation. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
A new benzylisoquinoline alkaloid -fumarizine (fig. 8) from Pakistani plant and its characterization. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990 -1994)b.
Isolation of new secophthalideisoquinoline alkaloid -papracine (fig. 11) and its structure determination; in addition, oxyhydrastinine, noroxyhydrastinine, fumaramine, stylopine, bisnorargemonine, b-hydrastine and fumaritine isolated; crystal structure of b-hydrastine determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990 -1994)c.
Another seco-phthalide isoquinoline alkaloid -narlumicine (fig. 9) isolated from stems along with protopine, its nitrate, tetrahydrocoptisine and narlumidine and new compound characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
Aerial parts afforded two new alkaloids -papracinine (fig. 12) and paprazine (fig. 7) whose structures established; in addition, fumaritine N-oxide, parfumine, lastourvilline, feruloyltyramine, fumariflorine and N-methylcorydaldine isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990 -1994)e.

Pharmacology: 

Fumarilline produced dose-related anticonvulsant effect against maximal electroshock-induced seizures in rats; at 10.0 to 50.0 mg/kg, i.p., it produced moderate to marked passivity and decrease in motility. It is a CNS-depressant without any significant muscle relaxation activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985 -1989)e.
Monomethyl fumarate isolated for the first time from the methanolic extract of the whole plant of Fumaria indica, was characterized and screened for its antihepatotoxic activity in albino rats. The compound showed significant (P<0.01) antihepatotoxic activity against thioacetamide in vitro, and against hepatotoxicities induced by carbon tetrachloride, paracetamol and rifampicin in vivo to an extent almost similar to that of silymarin,a known antihepatotoxic agent. (Rao and Mishra, 1998)
