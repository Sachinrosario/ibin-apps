Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A handsome tree. Tree has a fast growth rate. A good wind break. Found in humid Bengal, Assam region, subhumid or humid eastern and southeastern upland, humid to semihumid and western Ghats and Karnataka plateau.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A handsome tree with a spreading shady crown. Bark grey, smooth. Leaves bipinnate, leaflets many, small, obliquely oblong, deep green.

 

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers in large, erect, terminal panicles, yellow, fragrant. Calyx is coppery red, downy. Petals crinkled about the margin. Pods flat, winged, reddish brown. Seeds 1-5, oblong, flattened, brownish.


Breeding system and varietal improvement:

Phenology:

Flowering commences by end of February.

In December leaves start yellowing. Leaf fall commences and continues till January. New leaves appear in February. 


Pollinators:



Floral and seed biology:

Seed Germination & Propagation: It can be propagated by seeds and cuttings. Germination of seeds takes several months and may be hastened by softening them by immersion in dilute acid or in boiling water for two minutes.

Best time for planting is July.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used: Whole plant and leaves.

Uses:

Wood used as fuel. Wood used as timber. Heartwood suitable for planks, coach-building, furniture and cabinet work.



Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:

Tannins and dyes


Chemistry:

Processing Techniques:

Other uses:

Tannin - Bark contains tannin which can be used for tanning. It produces a light coloured, full and strong leather of desirable feel. Wood and leaves also contain tannin.

Bark - The bark yields a dye which colours cotton yellowish brown. It is medicinal, constituent of soga. Yellow brown dye is obtained which is used in batik.
Fodder - Leaves rich in proteins and can be used as cattle feed.
Lac - It is reported to serve as a host of the lac insect.

Miscellaneous Information

 
Tree one of the most ornamental of tropical trees suited for growing in gardens, on the roadside and for shade in coffee and tea plantations in south India.

Wood: Heartwood blackish red, hard and strong, durable when protected from weather. Sapwood white, soft and light.
