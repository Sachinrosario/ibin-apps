Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A fast-growing short-lived tree occurs almost throughout the moister parts of India.

Tree springs up fast on forest clearings, fallow lands, landslips and exposed lands.

 

Availability :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A fast-growing tree, 25-30 ft. high with straight spreading branches. Bark smooth, white or bluish green to greenish brown. Branchlets pubescent. Leaves obliquely ovate, acuminate, crenate-serrulate, charteceous, somewhat rough above, clothed beneath with soft often white pubescent, base unequal-sized, rounded or subcordate. Lateral basal nerves reaching to half length of the leaf.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers in axillary pubescent cymes longer than the petiole, the male cymes rather more compact than the female and usually on different trees.

Male flowers: Sepals elliptic-lanceolate. Stamens 5. Pistillode small.

Female flowers: Sepals elliptic, ciliolate. Ovary sessile, style arms villous, slightly incurved. Drupe black when ripe.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel:Wood used as fuel. But it is a poor fuelwood. Calorific value: 3095 cals., 5571 B.t.u.). It is employed for making charcoal for the manufacture gunpowder.

Wood: The wood is light-reddish grey, moderately hard, spongy and coarse, and light (wt. 480 kg./ cu. m.).

It is used for tea-chests, match-boxes and -splints, wooden shoes and fishing-floats. It is also used for making yokes and rafters when properly smoked and seasoned.

Pulp can be used in admixture with 25-40 per cent of a long-fibred pulp, such as bamboo pulp, for the production of writing and printing papers. Pulps suitable for wrapping papers can also be made from the wood by the sulphate or soda process. Wood is used for charcoal and fireworks.

Other uses:

Bark - The bark contains tannin (16%).

Bark - A decoction of the bark is used for toughening and dyeing fishing-lines made of other fibres.

Fibre - The plant yields a long, light-brown fibre, which strips off the stem in narrow, thin bands and is easily separated after a little drying. It is used for making ropes, twine and a kind of coarse cloth.

Leaves - The leaves, which are rough, are substituted for sandpaper for polishing wood. Leaves contain 8% dry weight tanin, used for tanning fishnets.

Root, fruits, bark are of medicinal value.
