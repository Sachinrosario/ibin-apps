HABIT & HABITAT
Stemless, stoloniferous herb; roots tufted, simple white fibre clothed with fibrillae. It is found in to occur in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves obovate-cuneate, 3-10 cm long, rounded or retuse at apex, densely or closely pubescent on both surfaces. Spathes white, obliquely campanulate, c 1.3 cm long, gibbous and closed below, contracted above the middle, dialated and nearly orbicular above. Fruits membranous, ovoid. Seeds many, oblong or ovoid.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant is bitter, pungent with a flavour. It is cooling, laxative. It is useful in fevers, diseases of blood, tuberculosis glands. In Unani the roots are bitter and diuretic. It is useful in inflammation and burns.

Chemistry: Vicenin, lucenin, cyaniding-3-glucoside and two unidentified compounds, isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).
Isolation of a new stigmastane (fig. 1) and its characterization as 11a-hydroxy-24(S)ethyl-5a-chiolest-22-ene-3,6-dione; NMR assignments of 11a-hydroxy-5a-cholestane-3,6-dione revised. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
Three new sitosterol acylglycosides isolated and their structures elucidated as sitosterol-3-O-[2',4'-O-diacetyl-6'-O-stearyl]-b-D-glucopyranoside, sitosterol-3-O-[2'-O-stearyl]-b-D-xylopyranoside and sitosterol-3-O-[4'-O-staryl]-b-D-xylopyranoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
Linoleic, g-linolenic, (12R,9Z,13E,15Z)12-hydroxy-9,13,15-octadecatrienoic (II) and (9S,10E,12Z,15Z)9-hydroxy-10,12,15-octadecatrienoic acids (III), a-asarone and 24(S)ethyl-4,22-cholestadiene-3,6-dione (IV) isolated from foliage. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
The structure (3S, 5R, 6R, 7E, 9R)-3,5,6-trihydroxy-b-ionyl-3-O-b-D-glucopyranoside has been attributed to stratioside II, a component of the methanolic extract of Pistia stratiotes on the basis of its spectroscopic features and by chemical correlation. (Della et al, 1996). C.A. 1996, 125, 163319z.
Four compounds identified by spectral analysis and chemical evidence as - stigmast-4,22-dien-3-one, stigmasterol, stigmasteryl, stearate and palmitic acid. (Ling et al, 1999). C.A. 2000, 132, 47507e.
Hepatic lipid peroxidation, Superoxide dismutase and catalase enzyme activities were measured to ascertain the safe nature of he Pistacia stratiotes extract. (Tahiliani and Kar, 2000-2001). MAPA 2001-04-1989.

Pharmacology: Linoleic acid, g-linolenic acid, a-asarone and compounds (II, III and IV) inhibited growth of some microalgae in solid medium. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
Pistia stratiotes found to decreased the serum concentration of thyroid hormone and glucose. (Tahiliani and Kar, 2000). MAPA 2001-01-0301.
