 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
It is a trees, grows upto  20 m on outer lower range of the Himalaya. Jammu to Oudh. Ascending to 2000 ft. Banda and Bihar to Travanceae. Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Arunachal Pradesh, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Maharashtra, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh. Common in deciduous forests, banks of rivers, roadsides. Sri lanka, Nepal, Myanmar, Indo-china.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Deciduous, spreading, poly gamo-dioecious trees, to 20 m tall; bark grey, fistular; young shoot tomentose. Leaves elliptic or obovate-oblong, 7-15 x4-7 cm, shortly acuminate at apex, unequally rounded or subcordate at base, margin entire or coarsely serrate, coriaceous, glabrous and pubescent beneath; stipules caducous, linear.  
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Flower 4-5 mm in diam., green, male & hermaphrodite, mixed together in same fascicle. Tepals 4, calycine, linear, 1.5-2.5 mm long, pubescent. Fruits samara, obliquely elliptic or roundish, to 2.5 cm across, notched between two stigmas, with reticulately veined membranous wings. Seeds flat, exalbuminous. It flolwers and fruits between March and July.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
 Wood is used for indoor building purpose, furniture, cabinet work, ploughs, yokes, mathematical instruments, match box and splints, etc.


Chemistry:
Hexacosanol, octacosanol, b-sitosterol and b-amyrin isolated from leaves; friedelin and friedelan-3-b-ol from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

2a,3a-dihydroxyolean-12-en-28-oic acid isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

In addition, b-siosterol and hederagenin from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

2-aminonapthaquinone (0.001), friedelin (0.002), epifriedelinol (0.005), b-sitosterol (0.004) and b-D-glucoside isolated from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

Petether extracts of the seeds of Holoptelea integrifolia gave b-amyrin, friedelin, b-sitosterol, and epi-friedelinol, friedl-1-en-3-one,lupeol, b-D-glucoside and stigmasterol. (Singh and Sharma, 1996). C.A. 1996, 124, 50615d.

From the combined petether – benzene extract of the roots of Holoptelea integrifolia rare cholesterol derivatives, 24-ethylcholest-22-en-3a-ol was isolated along with tetracosane, epi-friedelinol, b-amyrin, b-sitosterol and friedelin. (Jain et al, 1998). C.A. 1998, 123, 65540c.

Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for boils (Leaf, Maheshwari et al, 1986); Bone fracture (Stem bark, Pal and Jain, 1989); Hydrocele (Stem bark, Goel et al, 1984); Inflammation in body (Leaf, Maheshwari et al, 1986); Rheumatism (Stem bark Negi et al, 1985; Seed oil, Pal and Jain, 1989); Ringworm (Stem bark Maheshwari and Singh, 1984; Mudgal and Pal, 1980, Twig sap Pal and Jain, 1989); Scabies (Stem bark, Mudgal and Pal, 1980); Scorpion sting (Leaf, Chaudhuri et al, 1985); skin disease (Leaf, Singh and Maheshwari, 1983); Ulcers (Stem bark, Apparanantham and Chelladurai, 1986); Edible (Fruit, Singh and Singh, 1981, Seed, Vartak, 1981), Vegetable (Flower, Pal and Banerjee, 1971); Fish poison (Bark, Leaf, Joshi, 1986); Symbolically tied on arm in hydrocele (Bark, Saxena et al, 1981).
