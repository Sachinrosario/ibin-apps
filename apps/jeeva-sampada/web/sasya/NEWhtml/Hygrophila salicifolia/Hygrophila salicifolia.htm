Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 An erect or ascending herb commonly met with in moist and marshy places throughout the greater part of India. H. salicifolia ; it is found in various parts of the country from Bengal to Travancore.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Flowers pale purple, in dense axillary whorls ; capsule oblong, 0.5-0.6 in. long ; seeds many, ovoid, compressed, mucilaginous, hairy. Flowers in axillary, sessile clusters, calyx lobes linear, hispid ciliate, corolla pale purple, fertile anthers subsequel.

Chemistry
Seeds yield 25% of a fatty oil and contain traces of an unidentified alkaloid, a bitter substance, and 4% ash consisting chiefly of calcium phosphate and potassium chloride.

Other Uses

1.Leaves  contain 18% ash rich in potassium and are strongly diuretic.

2.In Malaya , the leaves are used for poulticing swellings.

3. The leaves are used in toothache and for poul- ticing wounds.

4.The seeds swell into a gelatinous shining mass with water and used, in Java, in poultices for headaches and fevers.

Miscellaneous Information

1) H. phloinoides Nees is an erect, rather stout herb with lanceolate to linear-oblong leaves and deeply bilipped purple flowers, found in the south-eastern parts of India . Its leaves are used in poultices for boils and headache (Burkill, 1, 1213).

2) H. quadrivalvis Nees is a herb.
3) H. serphyllum is a procumbent herb with ovate-lanceolate leaves and purplish blue flowers, found from Bihar and central India to western and southern India . Its fruite are edible ; the leaves are eaten in times of famine (Shivnath Rai, 33 ; Gammie, Rec. hot. Surv. India , 1902, 2, i86). Hygrophila spinosa see Asteracantha.
