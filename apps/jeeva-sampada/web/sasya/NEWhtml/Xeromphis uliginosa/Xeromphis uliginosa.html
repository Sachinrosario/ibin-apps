DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Small trees, up to 6 m high; bark reddish brown, scaly; branches thick, horizontal, younger 4-angled. Leaves crowded at the end of branchlets, obovate or obovate-oblong, 6.0-12.5 x 3-5 cm, obtuse at apex, tapering to base, glabrous above, more or less pubescent and reticulately veined; lateral nerves 6-8 pairs; petioles very short; stipules triangular.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers solitary at the end of branchlets, white, fragrant, 3-5 cm in diam.; pedicels short. Calyx 1.0-1.3 cm long, fleshy green; limbs broadly tubular with 5-7 broad rounded ciliate shallow lobes. Corolla glabrous outside; tube very short with a ring of white hairs at throat; lobes 5-7, orbicular, much overlapping. Fruits 4.5-6.0 cm long, smooth, yellowish brown, crowned with persistent calyx limb. Seeds numerous, compressed, smooth.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 The unripe fruit roasted in wood ashes is used as a remedy in diarrhoea and dysentery, the central portion consisting of the stone and seeds being rejected, it is astringent. The root boiled in ghee is sometimes given in similar cases.
