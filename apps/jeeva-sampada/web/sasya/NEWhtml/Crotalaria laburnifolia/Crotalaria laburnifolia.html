REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Terminal or lateral lax few flowered racemes ; Flower: Yellow, pedicellate, bracteate, showy, hermaphrodite, complete; Calyx: Tube short, glabrous, teeth lanceolate as long a s tube; Corolla: Much exserted, yellow, glabrous, keeled;Androecium: Stamens monadelphous, anthers dimorphic ; Gynoecium: Ovary sessile, usually many ovulate, style long abruptly incurved the base, more or less bearded upwards, stigma minute and oblique; Fruit: Pods cylindric, glabrous with very long stalk; Seed: 20-30 . 

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : September - October. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
Genetic variability:

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
An alkaloid – crotalaburnine, m.p. 185°C, a pigment – lutexin and b-sitosterol isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

A pyrrolizidine alkaloid – anacrotine, m.p. 197°C – isolated from seeds, shown to be a cyclic diester of senecic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Anacrotine isolated from seeds identified as crotalaburnine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Essential amino acids identified in plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Isolation and characterization of a new flavone glycoside (fig. 1) from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

Aromadendrin-7-O-a-L-rhamnopyranosyl(1à4)-O-b-D-galactopyranoside (0.048%) determined in seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.


Pharmacology:
Crotalaburnine showed inhibitory action on contraction of guinea pig ileum induced by acetylcholine, histamine, 5-hydroxytryptamine, nicotine and barium. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Crotalaburnine (10 mg/kg, s.c.) showed same degree of inhibition of carrageenin-induced oedema in rat as phenylbutazone (100 mg/kg, p.o.). crotalaburnine, at half the dose of hydrocortisone, produced similar inhibition against cotton-pellet granuloma in rat. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
