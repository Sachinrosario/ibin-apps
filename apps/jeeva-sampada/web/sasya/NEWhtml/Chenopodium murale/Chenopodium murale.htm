Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A glabrous, erect, ribbed somewhat foetid herb, 15-45 cm in height, commonly found as a weed from Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab and Rajasthan to West Bengal extending in to peninsular India.                                                                                        
Availability :                                                                                                  
Himachal Pradesh,Karnataka,Madhya Pradesh,Maharashtra,Rajasthan,Tamil Nadu.
Status : 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves rhomboidor ovate-deltoid, 2-10 cm x 1-6 cm; flowers clustered, in laxor in dense cymes, in axillary racemesor panicles, the terminal panicle being leafless; utricles with depressed-globose, dull black, seeds.

Chemistry
Analysis of the edible leaves of edible wild herbs gave : moisture-76.2; protein-6.3; ether extract-1.1; carbohydrates-11.2; and ash-5.2%; calcium-737.2; phosphorous-65.5; iron-4.6; vitamin C-149.6 mg; beta carotene-5800 micro gram/100 g. The leaves contain high concentration of both total and soluble oxalates.
