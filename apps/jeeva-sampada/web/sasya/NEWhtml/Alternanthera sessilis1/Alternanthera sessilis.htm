HABIT & HABITAT
 Very variable prostrate to erect herb. Leaves lanceolate, glabrous, greenish pink somewhat fleshy. Inflorescence axillary and terminal. Flowers small, white. Fruits compressed. Seeds brown orbicular testa shiny black. This herb is found as a common weed of damp places along ponds and streams. It is very common in plains in moist and wet places in tank beds, rice fields channels, often in partially submerged condition. It also occurs upto 2100 m in the hills.

Seed Germination :
It can be propagated through vegetative means.
Vegetative Propagation

a) Through cuttings : Cuttings are made from strong outdoor plants and planted during late summer

b) Through divisions : Divisions of old plants are preferred. Old plants are lifted from beds after first frost, cut back to 7 to 10 cm height and carried over winter. In early spring, plants are divided and all shoots or parts bearing good roots are potted or else planted with plenty of room.

Agroclimatic requirements:
It has a  preference for sandy or gravelly soils,  but also found thriving  in clayey loams. Severe drought or water logging does not seem to affect the plant to any great extent. It is found growing in water up to 1 m or more deep, where it is usually partly floating and partly emergent.
