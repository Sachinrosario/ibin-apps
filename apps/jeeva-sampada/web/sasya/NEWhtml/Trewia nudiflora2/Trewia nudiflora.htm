HABIT & HABITAT
A tree, c. 25 m. in height and 3 m. in girth, with a clear bole up to 20 m. under favourable conditions often much buttressed at the base, found almost through out the moist and hot parts of India from Yamuna eastwards and southwards, ascending to an altitude of 1,200 m. The trees are found chiefly in the moist forests particularly along the streams and in moist and swampy places. The male trees are handsome and female trees are with comparatively shorter stems and widespreading branches. along river banks, along slopes of ghats, along streams, Banks of streams, Bunds of Fileds, Outskirts of Wet Deciduous forests in lower ghats, cultivated, deciduous forest, Forest Margins, Hedges, hills, Hotter parts, moist deciduous forest, Moist forest, Plains, river sides, Road sides, semi evergreen forest, wet places,

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Bark grey or pale-yellowish brown, exfoliating m thin flakes; leaves broadly ovate-cordate 15-23 cm. x 11-18 cm.; flowers apetalous; male flowers yellow, 1-4, in lax, drooping racemes; female flowers green, usually solidary axillary; fruits pale green drup- aceous, obscurely quadrangular, 2.5-3.8 cm. in diam borne in large numbers; seeds black, 2-5, usually 4' surrounded by yellowish, fleshy arils. . In the western ghats of Tamil Nadu, the tree reaches fine proportions, with a large and clear bole It is a moderate light-demander and fairly frost-hardy but is a sensitive to drought. It coppices vigorously and produces root suckers from the network of superficial roots. The tree is also cultivated for ornament.
