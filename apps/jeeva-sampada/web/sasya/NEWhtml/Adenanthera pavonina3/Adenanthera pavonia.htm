Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Native of South India.

Tree often planted; rare in wild state. Wild in some parts of Gujrat and Khandesh, in gardens of Bombay. Moist forests of Konkan and North Kanara, it  is nowhere abundant.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small unarmed tree; young parts glabrous. Leaves bipinnate, 8-12 in long. Petioles 2-4 in long. Pinnae 3-6 pairs, opposite, 3-6 in long. Leaflets alternate, 4-8 pairs, papery, elliptic-oblong, obtuse, glabrous, dark green above, glaucous beneath, base shortly cuneate, unequal-sided; petiolules 1/8 in long. 

Flowers in short-peduncled racemes, axillary or panicled at the ends of the branches; pedicels long, slender. Calyx minute; lobes short, triangular. Corolla pale yellow; segments united at the base only, linear-lanceolate, acute, valvate. Stamens 10, free, hardly exerted, anthers gland-crested.

Pods flat, falcately curved, pointed, tapering to the base, the valves spirally twisted after dehiscence.

Seeds 8-15, lenticular, globose, with a blunt keel, hard, smooth, shining, usually brilliant scarlet.
