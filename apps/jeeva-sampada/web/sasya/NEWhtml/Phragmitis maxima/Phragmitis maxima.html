DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stem erect, 1.2-3.0 m high, smooth, simple or branched, covered with leaf-sheath. Leaves linear, up to 4 cm broad, acuminate at apex, contracted at base, margin smooth, coriaceous, smooth; sheaths loose, glabrous.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Panicles up to 55 cm long, oblong, erect; branches widely spreading, filiform. Spikelets c 10 mm broad across the glume; pedicels capillary, smooth. Glumes glabrous; lower involucral glume oblong-lanceolate or linear, 3-5 mm long, acute, 3-nerved; upper involucral glume oblong-lanceolate, 4-6 mm long, acute, 3-nerved; lower floral glume 4-9 mm long; upper floral glume equally long or rather longer than lower.

Uses:
 Plant is sweet, acrid. It is used in cooling aphrodisiac. It is also useful in biliousness, urinary troubles, vaginal and uterine complaints, erysipelas and heart diseases (Ayurveda) (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
