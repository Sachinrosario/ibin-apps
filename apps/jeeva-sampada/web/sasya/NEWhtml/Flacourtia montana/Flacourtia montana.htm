Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
It is a tree mainly seen in evergreen forest, forests, forests of ghats, Hill slopes, moist deciduous forest, semi evergreen forest,slopes and wet deciduous forest.
BioticZone: Konkan, Konkan plain, Peninsular India, West Coast, Western Ghats and Western subalpine Himalaya.
Misc: Ankola ghat, Banda, Butpal-Ordofond, chapheli, digwal, gavata, Heart point,Kalanaddi, Kanjirakadavu, Nilambur, Matheran, Nadquem, Nadquem-Quer Hills, Nedumkayam, Nilambur, Orghani, Punnamala, Regional Research Station and Brahmavar.
Country: India,
Zone: North West, South and Westzone.
State: Goa Daman Diu, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu.
District: Chikmaglore, Coimbatore, Dakshin Kannada, Goa, Hassan, Kodagu, Kukri, Malappuram, Palaghat, Pune, Raigad, Shimoga, Simla, Sindhudurg and Uttar Kannada.
Talluk: Kankavli, Kudal, Sawantwadi and Wadgaon.
