 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
 A bushy plant, 4-8' high, occuring throughout the Deccan peninsula, ceylon and tropical Burma. An infusion of the leaves is regarded as a stomachic and alterative. In India it is found in dry areas, dry places, Dry slopes, grass land, moist deciduous forest, open waste land, Road sides and Scrub forests.
BioticZone: Deccan and Carnatic,Tropics,W. to E. slopes of ghats and Western Ghats,

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES :
A bush of 4-8 feet high, leaves ovate-oblong, deeply serrate, acute to long acuminate, 3 or almost 5-nerved, tomentose beneath, on a long petiole. Flowers minute, green, clustered, sessile forming slender puberulous spikes, occuring singly or 2-3 above thw scars of the fallen leaves. The female flowers are at the very base of the spike or on separate small axillary spikes. Styles simple, many cleft, about 2-3 times longer than the floral bracts.
