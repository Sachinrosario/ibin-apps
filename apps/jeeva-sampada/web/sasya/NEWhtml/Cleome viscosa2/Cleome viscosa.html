DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves 3-5 foliate, petiolate; leaflets 1.0-5.5 x 0.5-0.3 cm, ovate, elliptic or oblong, acute at apex. Flowers yellow, in axillary lax raceme. Sepals oblong-lanceolate, 4.5 mm long, glandular pubescent outside. Petals oblong-obovate, c 12 mm long, veined. Stamens more than 20. Capsules 5-7 x 0.5 cm, hairy, tapering at both ends. Seeds brown-black, subglobose.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Axillary growing out into lax racemes.; Flower: Yellow, pedicels terete, hairy and slender, complete, hermaphrodite and regular ; Calyx: Sepals 4, spreading, oblong –lanceolate, glandular, pubescent; Corolla: Petals 4, subequal, oblong-ovate,veined ;Androecium: Stamens exceeding 20, inserted on torus; Gynoecium: Ovary sessile, ovules many on two parietal placentation; style short; Fruit: Capsule erect hairy, obliquely striate compressed, tapering towards the apex; Seed: Brown black when ripe finely transversly striate. Flowering & Fruiting : September –June . Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:  In Ayurveda system of medicine plant causes dryness and urethral discharges. It cures cough, tumors and inflammations. It is also used in blood diseases, uterine complaints, skin diseases, itching, ulcers, leprosy and malarial fever. In Unani system of medicine leaves favours digestion and dispel intestinal fermentation. Leaf juice cures earache. It is used in malaria, piles and lumbago. Seeds are anthelmintic.


Chemistry:
Seed oil rich in linoleic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Isolation and characterization of eriodictyol-5-rhamnoside (fig. 1) (3’,4’,7-trihydroxyflavone-5-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside) from whole plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Kaempferide-3-glucuronide obtained from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

Dihydrokaempferide-3-glucuronide and docosanoic acid isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

Cleomeolide (fig. 4) isolated from leaves and its stereostructure determined by X-ray analysis. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

A new 7-phenoxycoumarin – cleosandrin (fig. 5) was isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Cleomiscosin A (fig. 2) isolated from seeds and its structure proposed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Cleomiscosin B (fig. 3) isolated from seeds and its structure established, Cleomiscin A revised. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.

Methylglucosinolate, benzyl glucosinolate, 2-phenethyl glucosinolate from plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.

5-O-methyl-eriodictyol-7-O-b-D-rhamnopyranoside isolated from plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.

Naringenin-4’-galctoside and dihydrokaempferol-4’-xyloside isolated from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)g.

Naringenin 4’-O-b-D-xylopyranosyl-(1à4)-b-D-glucopyranoside isolated and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)h.

Isolation of 3’,4’,5-trihydroxyflavone-7-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside, b-amyrin and lupeol from roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)i.

A new saponin isolated and characterized as stigmasta-3b-O-a-L-rhamnoside (fig. 6a). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)j.

Ergot-5-ene-3-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside (fig. 6b) and 5,4’-di-O-methyleriodictyol-7-O-b-D-glucopyranoside isolated and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)k.

New coummarinolignan – cleomiscosin C (fig. 7) together with cleomiscosins A and B isolated from seeds and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Two new unsaturated cembrane acids isolated and their structures established by X-ray crystallography as (1R,3E,7Z,12R)20-hydroxycembra-3,7,15-trien-19-oic acid (fig. 9) and (3E,7Z,11Z)17,20-dihydroxy-cembra-3,7,11,15-tetraen-19-oic acid (fig. 10). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

Seeds afforded new coumarinolignan – cleomiscosin D (fig. 8) which has been proved to be regioisomer of cleomiscosin C. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

A new macrocyclic diterpene – cleomaldeic acid – isolated and characterized as (3E,7E,11E)20-oxocembra-3,7,11,15-tetraen-19-oic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).


Pharmacology:
Cleomiscosins A, B and C exhibited liver protective property. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.
