DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A large tree with branchlets finely pubescent upwards. Leaves coriaceous, very variable in shape, 2-3 in long, ovate or obovate, acute or obtuse, deltoid at the base, glabrous above when mature, thinly clothed with obscure whitish pubescense beneath; petiole very short. Panicles all axillary, erecto-patent, shorter than the leaves. Rachis white tomentose; pedicels 0. Calyx pale green, 3-5 lobed; petals 4-5 , free, oblong, twice as long as the calyx, spreading. Filaments united more than halfway up. Anthers oblong, ovary 1-celled, style short, stigma capitate, ovules 1-2, basal, erect. Drupe ellipsoid, 1.2-2 cm long, obtuse or pointed.
