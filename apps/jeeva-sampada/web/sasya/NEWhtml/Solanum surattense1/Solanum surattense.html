Chemistry:

·        Crude plant extract caused hypotension which has been attributed to release of histamine by some constituents. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960 – 1969).

·        Plant powder is anti-tussive and its beneficial effect in patients of bronchial asthma and non-specific cough has been explained as due to depletion of histamine from lung and its expectorant action as due to inorgainc nitrate content. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970 – 1979).

·        A new sterol – carpesterol – characterised as (22R)22-hydroxy-6-oxo-4a-methyl-5a-stigmast-7-en-3b-yl benzoate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970 – 1979).

·        Scopoletin, esculin and esculetin isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970 – 1979).

·        Solasodine, solasonine, solamargine and b-solamargine in fruits of Nepalese plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970 – 1979).

·        Two new sterols – norcarpesterol (22x-hydroxy-6-oxo-4a-methyl-24x-methylcholest-7-en-3b-yl benzoate) and 4a-methyl-24x-methylcholest-7-en-3b, 22x-diol (I) isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970 – 1979).

·        Dry fruits contained traces of isochlorogenic, neochlorogenic and chlorogenic acids and caffeic acid (0.035%). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970 – 1979).

·        Quercetin-3-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl-(1-4)-mannopyranoside isolated together with apigenin and sitosterol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970 – 1979).

·        Solasodine isolated from berries of both normal and albino strains. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980 – 1984).

·        Isolation of solasodine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990 – 1994).
