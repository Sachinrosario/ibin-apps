DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Branches angular. Leaves alternate, elliptic, elliptic-ovate, obovate or suborbicular, acute or obtuse at apex, cuneate at base, entire at margin, glabrous. Flowers in dense fascicles, green or greenish-white, apetalous. Male flowers: numerous in a cluster; sepals 5, sub-petaloid; stamens 5, alternating with disc glands, pistillode large, 2-3 fid. Female flowers: sepals as in male; disc annular, toothed; ovary ovoid; styles 3 connate below. Fruit globose, white.
