DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Leafy shoot up to 4 m high. Leaf lamina lanceolate, up to c 100 x 15 cm, acuminate at apex, slightly pubescent or glabrous below; ligules c 1 cm long, entire. 
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers in 30-40 cm long panicles; bracts lanceolate, 2-3 x 0.8-1 cm, acute at apex, glabrous. Calyx up to 2 cm long, 2 or obscurely 3-lobed; lobes mucronate. Corolla-tube about as long as the calyx; lobes 1-1.5 cm long, rounded at the apex, dorsal lobe widest. Labellum white, streaked violet, obovate, obscurely 3-lobed, narrowed at base. Lateral staminodes inconspicuous, subulate. Capsules subglobose or oblong, marked with many fine vertical ribs.
CIMAP:
   Inflorescence: Panicles ; Flower: Hermaphrodite regular complete; Calyx: short ; Corolla: Lip of corolla white ;Androecium: only 1 perfect rest is reduced to staminodes,, anthers linear 2-celled ; Gynoecium: Ovary 3-celled ,inferior, ovules many, style slender, stiga entire; Fruit: Capsule sub-globose, oblong, marked with fine vertical ; Seed: Blackish-brown. Flowering & Fruiting : January- March. 
Floral and seed biology:
August - April.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
 
Genetic variability:

The reported chromosome number 2n=48.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds and rhizomes.

Soil type: Plant responds well to well drained rich humus soil.

Nutrient requirements: Plant requires nitrogenous fertilizers at the time of growing season.

Water management: Plant requires good irrigation facilities.                                      

Harvesting period / Method: Harvesting usually done when the seeds are mature.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine the seeds are used in asthma, bronchitis, piles, consumption, scabies, kidney, rectum and throat diseases. In Unani system the seeds are used in heart tonic and also used in headache, earache, toothache, bad humours of liver, chest and the throat (Kirtikar & Basu,1933).
