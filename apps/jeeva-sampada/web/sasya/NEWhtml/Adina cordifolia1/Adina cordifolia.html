DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
            Large deciduous trees, stems often irregularly fluted and buttressed at base, commonly reaching 6 m girth and 33 m high; bark partly dry grey or blackish and partly pale ashy or silvery white, rather rough, exfoliating in irregular woody scales. Leaves opposite, orbicular, 10-25 cm in diam., abruptly acuminate at apex, base cordate, margin entire, glabrescent above, pubescent beneath, lateral nerves 5-8 pairs; petioles stout, 3.8-8.5 cm long, pubescent; stipules orbicular or oblong, 1.2-1.7 cm long, enclosing terminal bud at middle, caducous. Flowers yellow, in globose axillary pedunculate heads, 1.5-3.0 cm in diam.; peduncles 1-4 together, 2-7 cm long. Sepals 5, linear. Corolla downy, 3.5-5.5 mm long, pubescent. Capsules cuneate, downy, splitting into 2 dehiscent cocci. Seeds many, minute, winged.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Solitary or panicled globose peduncled heads ; Flower: Hermaphrodite, regular, small, bracteolate ; Calyx:  Calyx tube 5-angled, lobes 5; Corolla: Corolla lobes 5, valvate, pubescent; Androecium: Stamens 5, on the mouth of corolla ; Gynoecium:  Ovary 2-celled, ovules numerous, style simple, stigma clavate ; Fruit:  Cluster of capsules each spiltting into 2 dehiscent cocci; Seed: 6 in each cell, minute, winged . Flowering & Fruiting: June-May. Flowers are pollinated by wind. Seeds are dispersed by birds and animals.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Plants are used in cholera. Bark is used in antiseptic, fever, scars on skin and yellowing of the body, urine complaints and worms in sores. Stem is used in eye complaints. Leaves are also useful in cold, cough, headache and wounds.
Chemistry:
An indole alkaloid – Cardifoline (fig. 2a) – isolated as pentaacetate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Adifoline (fig. 1a), m.p. 350°C, was isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

10-deoxyadifoline (fig. 1b) and 10-deoxycardifoline (fig. 2b) were isolated from ethylacetate extract of the heartwood of A. cordifolia. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Cadambine, m.p. 220°C isolated fro bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

A new flavone from stem, was characterized as 5,7-dihydroxy-3,3’,4’,5’-tetramethoxyflavone. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

7,4’-dimethoxy-5-hydroxyflavanone along with 5,7-dimethoxy-4’-ydroxyflavanone isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Stigmasta-5,22-diene-3b-O-a-L-rhamnopyranosyl(1à4)-b-D-xylopyranoside, naringenin-7-methylether-4’-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside, a-amyrin and octacosanol isolated from stem. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.

Another flavone 3’-hydroxy-5,7,4’-tri-O-methyl-8-methyl flavone  was isolated from the heartwood of A.cordifolia. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.

Flavanoids Chrysin, gossypetin, kaempferol,7,4’-di-O- methyl-6-methyl flavanone and naringenin-5-O-neohesperidoside were isolated from the heartwood of A. cordifolia. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)f.

Coumarin glycoside adicardin (mp.138-9,C20H24O12 ) was isolated  from the ethanolic extract of A. cordifolia.( Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

C-glycosyl flavones, viz quercetin-3,4’dimethylether-8-C-rhamnoside,acacetin-5-methylether-8-C-rhamnoside and genkwanin-8-C-rhamnoside were isolated from the heart wood of A. cordifolia.( Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

Long chain, Saturated, aliphatic hydrocarbons  n-heneicosane,n-tricosane,n-pentacosane,n-heptacosane,n-nonacosane,n-hentriacontane, n-tritriacontane and n-pentatriacotane and b-sitosterol were isolated from the heartwood of A. cordifolia.( Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

Urosolic acid (mp. 291-92°) and querectin-3-O-galactoside (236-38) were isolated from the leaves of A. cordifolia. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)

Pharmacology:
Anti-fertility activity with leaf extracts of A. cordifolia. (Sabir and Razdan, 1970)
