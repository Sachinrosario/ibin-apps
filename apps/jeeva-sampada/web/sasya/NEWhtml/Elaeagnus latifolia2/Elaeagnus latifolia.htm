Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

Shrub, climber or small tree, very variable habit. The plant occurs throughout the hilly parts of India. Young shoots scurfy with rust-coloured shining scales. Stem or branches more or less spiny, bark smooth and corky excrescences. Occurs in peninsular India.

Conservation/ Threat Status : 

Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A large, much branched, usually scandent shrub, often running over high trees; sometimes trunk 4" or more in diameter. Leaves numerous, variable, thin or coriaceous, from broadly elliptic to elliptic-lanceolate, obtuse, acute or acuminate, upper surface pale green clothed with small whitish scurfy scale, the lower surface glistening silvery-white, margins more or less revolute, base acute or rounded; petioles scaly.

Flowers usually many in cluster, straw-coloured, pedicels clothed with silvery scales. Perianth covered with silvery scales. Lobes ovate, acute, spreading. Style hairy. Fruit ellipsoid, with 8 strong blunt ribs, crowned by the top of the perianth, pinkish, with small whitish scabrous spots, mesocarp succulent, edible, putamen white, clothed inside with white wool.
