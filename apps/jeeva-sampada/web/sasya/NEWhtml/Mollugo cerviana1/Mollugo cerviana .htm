HABIT & HABITAT:
A herb found from the Punjab to Ceylon in the hotter and drier parts of India. Also distributed throughout the warmer regions of the world extending into Europe and N. America. Four species occur in India and found in dry and sandy localities in upper Gangetic plain, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Deccan, Carnatic and Orissa.
Habitat: Cultivated fields, Cultivated fields and Plains, Dry areas, Dry regions, Eroded ground, Forests, Middle Ghats, Moist places, , On open gravelley and waste land.
BioticZone: Coastal, Deccan Plateau and Desert zone.
Soil: It prefers gravelly soil, Sandy loam and Sandy soil.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES 
An erect slender annual, 7.5-20.0 cm. high, Leaves in whorls of 4-8 at each node: radical leaves rosulate, spathulate, linear-spathulate or obovate, cauline leaves falsely whorled or opposite, linear-oblong or sub-spathulate. Flowers numerous, in cymes; capsules globose with many pink-chestnut or yellowish seeds; root aromatic.

Other Uses
  1. Medicinal purposes and anti-bacterial.
  2. The herb is considered stomachic, aperient and antiseptic.
  3. Flowers and tender shoots are diaphoretic and given in fevers. 
  4. An infusion of the plant is given to promote lochial discharge. 
  5. Oil in which roots are boiled is used as an application for gout and rheumatism. 
  6. An alcoholic extract of the plant shows anti-bacterial activity against Escherichia coli.
