DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Leaves alternate, orbicular, palmately lobed, lobes lanceolate, acuminate at apex, glandular on either side at base. Petioles with 2-3 glands on the adaxial side. Flowers unisexual in terminal panicles. Male flowers: calyx segments 3-5, stamens numerous in branched fasicles. Female flowers: calyx spathaceous, 5 partite, ovary globose, echinate, styles 3, 2-fid, stigma papillose. Capsules prickly, 3 lobed. Seeds oblong, testa crustaceous, with large caruncle.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Inflorescence panicle cyme, male flowers at the base and female flowers at apex; Male flower bracteate, pedicellate, actinomorphic, incomplete, unisexual, staminate, pentamerous and cyclic; Perianth tepals 5, polytepalous, valvate, slightly connate at the base; Androecium stamens 5, polyandrous, antiposed, each stamens are profusely branched with the anthers borne on ultimate branches, monothecous, basifixed, introrse; Gynoecium    absent; Gynoecium: Tricarpellary, syncarpous, ovary   superior, trilocular with one ovule in each locule, placentation    axile, style absent, stigma one bright red and rachis bifid. Ovary has appendages on its outer wall ;   Fruit  regma    spiltting into cocci, caruncle is presentation apex of the seeds . 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
 Plants are cross-pollinated and seeds are dispersed by explosive mechanism of capsules.
Genetic variability: The basic chromosome number is 10. Plant is diploid with chromosome number 2n=20. Autotetraploids have been produced by using colchine and haploids are also found but in nature mainly diploids are found.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation:   Plants are propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant grows well in loamy soil although also grown in alluvial clays and sands.

Nutrient requirements: It responds well to application of manure and fertilizers. Application of 100-pound ammonium sulphate is also beneficial.

Water management: It is a rainfed crop, one or two irrigation may be given at close to monsoon.

Harvesting period / Method: When the capsule begins to turn brown, the spikes of fruit are harvested.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
 Roots are sweetish, heating, carmintive. It is useful in inflamations, pains, ascites, fever, glands, asthma, bronchitis, leprosy, diseases of rectum and head. Root bar are purgative, alterative. It is also useful in skin diseases. Root at first is boiled in water with maize ashes and again boiled with water. The sediment collected smeared on teeth and gum to relieve toothache. Leaf is useful in "vata", "kapha", intestinal warm, strangury, night blindness, earache, increase bilousness; galactagogue, applied to relieve headache, used as poultice for boils. Fomentation made with seed oil is used to cure wounds. It is also used as ointment for sores. Leaf infusion is remedy for stomach ache (zulu). Foliage is considered emmenagogue and locally applied in rheumatism, soaked in vinegar applied to forhead in case of sunstroke. Flowers are useful in glandular tumours, anal troubles, and vaginal pain. Fruit is heating and apetiser. It is also useful in tumours, pains, "vatas", piles, diseases of liver and spleen. Seed and seed oil are cathartic, aphordisiac. Seed oil is anthelmintic, alterative, purgative, tumour curing. It is useful in heart discase, slow fever, ascites, in inflamations, typhoid, pain in back, lumbago, leprosy, elephantiasis, convulsions, in "kapha", in liver troubles, boils, piles, ringworm, paralysis, asthma, dropsy, rheumatism, amenorrhoea.
NBPGR:
The roots are sweets, acrid, astringent, thermogenic, carminative, purgative, anthelmintic, emollient, diuretic, aphrodisiac, gatactagogue, sudorific, expectorant and depurative. They are useful in gastropathy such as gulma, amadosa, etc. constipation, inflammations, fever, ascites, strangury, bronchitis, cough, leprosy, skin diseases, vitiated conditions of vata, colic, coxalgia and lumbago. The leaves are diuretic, anthelmintic and galactagogue, and are useful in burns, nyctalopia, strangury and for bathing and fermentation in vitiated conditions of vata, especially, in rheumatoid arthritis, urodynia and arthralgia. Flowers are useful in urodynia and glandular lumours. Seeds are acrid, thermogenic digestive, cathartic and aphrodisiac. They are useful in dyspepsia and for preparing a pouttice to treat arthralgia. The oil obtained from the seeds is slightly bitter, acrid, sweet, antipyretic, thermogenic and viscous. It is used as a very effective purgative for all elements caused by vata and kapha. It is also recommended for scrotocele, ascities. Intermittent fever, gulma, colonitis, lumbago, coxalgia and coxitis.      


Chemistry:
