DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
  Leaves alternate or subopposite, elliptic, 7.0-20 x 4.0-12.0 cm, acute at apex, cordate or rounded at base, glabrous; younger leaves branchlets, buds contain rust coloured, shiny hairy. Flowers in axillary or terminal panical or sometimes in spikes, pubescent, c 4 mm across, sessile, yellow or white, with offensive smell; bracts exceeding the flowers. Fruits drupe, pendulous, ellipsoid with a broad base, 2-4 cm long, glabrous, 5-ribbed, yellowish green when dry, bony, obscurely angled.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine fruits are used as tonic and stomachic. They are used in the treatment of asthma, sorethroat, vomitting, hiccough, eye diseases, urinary discharges, inflammation, tumours, bleeding piles, typhoid fever, leucoderma, itching, pain, constipation, anaemia, gout and eliphantiasis. In Unani system of medicine unripe fruits are useful in ophthalmia, spleen diseases, piles, cold in the head and in paralysis. It strengthens brain, eye and gum.
