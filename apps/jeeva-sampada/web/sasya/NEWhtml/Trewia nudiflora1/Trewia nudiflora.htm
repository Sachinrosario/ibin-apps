Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A large deciduous tree. Mostly in the moist forests, particularly along the streams and in moist and swampy places. Occurs almost throughout the moist and hot parts of India from Yamuna eastwards and southwards, ascending up to an altitude of 1,200 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A large deciduous tree. Bark smooth, grey. Young shoots and sometimes leaves beneath and inflorescence clothed with cottony wool, at other times nearly glabrous. Leaves opposite, ovate, entire, acuminate, glabrous and bright green above, base cordate, 3-5 nerved. Stipules minute, acute, soon falling.

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers dioecious. The male flowers in racemes, the female flowers on long peduncles.

Male flowers: Sepals valvate, concave. Pedicels slender, short, horizontal. Stamens numerous.

Female flowers: Solitary or 2-3 together. Sepals 3-5, broad, imbricate, caducous. Ovary woolly, styles yellow. Fruit almost woody, globose, tomentose when young. Seeds rounded on the back, slightly angular on the face, smooth and polished, dark-brown.

Floral and seed biology:

Seedling Germination: Natural regeneration is from seeds. Generally they begin to fall early in the rainy season. They rot and germinate in situ as they fall, though some of the fruits get dispersed by water. The seedlings appear in small groups of 3-4. The dry and hard ground is unfavourable for germination, but seedlings readily spring up on loose moist soil. Germination is more successful if the fruits and seeds get partially or wholly burried.

Ripe fruits are collected for artificial reproduction, they are dried for a few days and the seeds are removed. Fresh seeds give very high percentage of germination. Direct sowing was reported to be more successful than transplanting of seedlings from the nursery.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Fuel: Wood used as fuel.

Wood:Wood good for packing cases, tea-boxes and for match-industry. Also utilized for making agricultural implements, dugouts, yokes, drums, barrels, planks, slate and picture frames, carved images and toys. Also suitable for reasonably good quality pencils after waxing treatment. Also found suitable for commercial plywood. Soft timber is used for making tea chests, packing cases.
