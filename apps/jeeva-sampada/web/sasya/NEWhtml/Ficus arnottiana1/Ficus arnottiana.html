REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Flower: Minute on the inner walls of the fleshy receptacles which is in pair or clusters from tubercles, basal bracts 3, membranous ; Male flower: Few, near the mouth of receptacles, sepals 3, loose, inflated,  broadly acuminate, larger than the small sub-sessile solitary anthers ; Gall and Fertile flower: Few, near the mouth of receptacles, sepals 3, loose, inflated,  broadly acuminate , the contents of ovary sessile or pedicellate, with perianth gamophyllous, lax toothed at the apex, completely investing the ovary, style elongate, stigma flat.; Fruit: An enlarged hollow cup-shaped receptacles, the inner wall studded with achenes ; Seed: Pendulous .

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : Febuarary - April . 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
