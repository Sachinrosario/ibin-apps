Taxonomic Details
Correct Name : Orthanthera viminea .Wight
Family : Asclepiadaceae
Synonyms :  Apocynea viminea, Wall.;Leptadenia viminea, Bth.,&Hook.
Common Names :
Hindi: Mahur, Khip, Mahurghas
Misc: Panjabi: Matti, mowa, lanebar, khip, chapkia.
Sind: Kip, khip.

 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
A bushy almost leafless shrub, 1.2-2.4 m. in height with stems up to 3.8 cm. in diam., found in the Himalayan tracts of Punjab and U.P. up to altitude of 900 m., especially in river beds.
BioticZone: Himalaya.
Availability :  
Misc: Corbett National Park, from Peshawar, Langasu across Nandprayag, Punjab to Oudh.
Country: India.
Zone: Central, North, North west and West,
State: Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh,
District: Chamoli and Jodhpur. 
Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Bark greyish white, smooth ; flowers in compact with flowers dull brown ; follicles solitary, elongate ; seeds loose.
