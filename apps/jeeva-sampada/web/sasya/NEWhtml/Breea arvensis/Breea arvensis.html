HABIT & HABITAT
 Erect herb, 50-85 cm high. It is found to occur in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stem many from base, usually simple, leafy. Leaves 7-12 cm long, sinuate or pinnatifid, more or less wooly beneath. Heads solitary, fascicled or corymbose, variable; male c 1-2 cm across, globose; female much longer, campanulate. Involucral bracts glabrate or cobwebby; outermost short, ovate or triangular-ovate, ending in a short spine; inner gradually longer with undulate, often curved tips; innermost narrow, linear-lanceolate, scarious. Corolla purplish. Achenes smooth, shining. Pappus brownish white.
