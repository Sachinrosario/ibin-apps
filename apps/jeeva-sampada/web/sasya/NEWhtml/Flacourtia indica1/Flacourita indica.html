HABIT & HABITAT
A small deciduous thorny shrubs or trees, armed with simple and branched thorns. It is found throughout India, in scrub forests and rocky hills up to 900-1200 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves variable, simple, elliptic, acute or acuminate at apex, crenate or serrate at margins, glabrous or pubescenyon both surfaces. Thorn sometimes bears leaves and flowers. Racemes with few flowered, axillary or terminating on shiort branchelets. Flowers unisexual, dioecious; sepals 4-7, ovate, obtuse, glabrous outer surface and pubescent inner surfaces. Male flowers: disk slightly lobulate or crenate. Stamens many, free, filaments hairy at base. Female flowers: disk entire, ovary globose, style 5-6, stigmas, slightly 2 lobed. Drupes  globose to ellipsoid, dark purple, with juice pulp and hard endocarp. Seeds 8-16, obovoids.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
     Inflorescence: Glabrous racemes; Flower:  Small, regular; Calyx: Sepals 4-5, small, imbricate; Corolla: Petals  ;Androecium: Stamens many, anthers versatile; Gynoecium:  Ovary on glandular disk, styles 2, stigma notched, ovules in pairs on placenta; Fruit: Indehiscent endocarp, with many seeds; Seed: Obovoid.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Roots are sweet, refrigerent, depurative, alexipharmic and diuretic. It is useful in vitiated condition of “Pitta” and “Vata” aphthae, poisonous bites, skin diseases, pruritus, erysipelas, strangury, nephropathy and psychopathy. Leavse are useful in pruritus and scabes. Fruits are sweet and used as appetiser, digestive and diuretic, and are useful in strangury, jaundice, gastropathy and splenomegaly.


Chemistry:  

A new phenolic glucoside ester - flacourtin (fig. 1) isolated from bark and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).
A new butyrolactone lignan disaccharide - ramontoside - isolated from heartwood and characterized as diphyllin-4-O-[b-D-glucosyl(1à4)]-b-2,3-di-O-methyl-D-xyloside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
