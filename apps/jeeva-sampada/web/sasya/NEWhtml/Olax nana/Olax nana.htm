 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
A small under shrub 60 cm. height,found in the hot valleys of the North West Himalaya from Nepal to the Punjab, ascending to an altitude of 5,000 feet. Also found in dense tropical forests, moist places, open grasslands, rocks.  found in the Himalayas, from Punjab to Sikkim at an altitude of 1,500 m., upper Gangetic plain, Chota Nagpur, North Bengal, plains and hills of Assam, Northern Circar and Gujarat.
Biotic Zone : North Circaris and  Western Himalayas. 

Availability : 
Misc: Corbett National Park, Hollong, Karaka konda, Kathiawar, Kumaon, N. Circars, 
Country: India, Nepal. 
Zone: Central, East, North, North East, South and West.
State: Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab and West Bengal.
District:  Pargana, Balaghat, Darjeeling, Hoogli, Jalpaiguri, Kameng, Rajkot and  Visakhapatnam.
