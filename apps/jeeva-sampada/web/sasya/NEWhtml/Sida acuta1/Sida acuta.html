DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual herbs or under shrubs, 0.5-2 m high, erect or ascending; stem much branched, minutely stellate hairs at first mined with some simple hairs ultimately glabrous. Leaves oblong, lanceolate to linear lanceolate, 1-9 x 0.5-2.5 cm, papery, sub-acute at apex, rounded at base, coarsely or remotely saw toothed, sparsely stellate, becoming glabrous, basally 3-5 nerved; petioles hairy; stipules 2, different, one linear to lanceolate and larger, the other bristle-like and shorter, 6-12 x 1.0-1.5 mm.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers about 1.5 cm across, axillary, solitary or 2-8 together in a cluster of 3-12 mm long; pedicels jointed above middle accrescent to 1.5 cm, star  like hairy. Calyx 5-8 mm long, bell-shaped, divided amongst halfway down; 5-fid; tube c 4 mm long; lobes triangular, c 7 x 3 mm, acuminate, stellate and simple hairy outside, glabrous inside. Corolla light yellow, obliquely obovate, 8-10 mm across; petals as long as slightly exceeding, ciliate at base, as long as calyx. Staminal column 4 mm long with simple and glandular hairs, anther-bearing towards apex. Schizocarp glabrous, toothed on dorsal margins, c 4 mm long; awn c 1.5 mm long with a grove between, glabrous, steriate, 1-seeded. Seeds triangularly ovoid, c 2 mm long, glabrous, except for hairy hilum, dark, brown.

Uses:
 Leaves are used in abortifacient, anthelmintic, antiemetic, boils, burns, gastric disorder and vermifuge. Fruits are useful in boils and burns. Roots are also useful in cool, gonorrhoea, back pain, stomachache and snake bite.
