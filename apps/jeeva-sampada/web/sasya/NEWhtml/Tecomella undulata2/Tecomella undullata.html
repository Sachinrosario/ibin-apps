DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Branches drooping, glabrous, minutely stellately hairy when young. Leaves simple, usually sub-opposite, narrowly oblong, 5–15 x 1.5–5 cm, obtuse at apex, slightly cuneate at base, entire along margin; petioles terete, 1-2 cm long, glabrous. Flowers in short, terminal cymes; pedicels terete, 1-2 cm long, stellately hairy, pedicel bases persistent. Calyx campanulate, 8-9 mm long, green or yellow; lobes broadly ovate, obtuse, mucronate, often recurved. Corolla campanulate, 5-7 cm long, yellow or orange-yellow; lobes 5, sub-equal. Stamens exserted; anterior filaments 3.0-3.5 cm long and posterior c 2.5 cm long. Style up to 4 cm long, glabrous; stigma bi-lamellate, elliptic, 3.5 mm long. Capsule linear-oblong, 15-35 x 0.8-1.0 cm, curved, smooth. Seeds 2.0-2.5 x 0.8-1.0 cm, winged.

Another new chromone glycoside -undulatoside B (fig. 2b) isolated from bark along with b-sitosterol glucoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)h.
