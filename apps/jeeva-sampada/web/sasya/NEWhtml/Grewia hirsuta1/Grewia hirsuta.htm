 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
It is a shrub bearing edible drupes, occuring throughout the greater part of India, ascending to 4,500 ft in the Himalayas.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A shrub 30-90 cm high, slender branches. leaves 5-11, oblong narrowed gradually to the apex, serrate, the tteth blunt, small, base rounded, clothed with very small stellate hairs above, densely stellate-pubescent beneath; petiole 2.5-3.8 mm long, stout, hairy; linear subulate, hairy stipules. Flowers white turning yellow, male and bisexual, 2-4 together. Fruit 7.5-10 mm in diameter more or less 2-4 lobed, shining, brown, with scattered hairs and a ring of hairs at the top of he androgynophore. The leaves are of two kinds bitter and tasteless.

Other Uses
  1. A paste of the root in water is applied to wounds to hasten suppuration and as a dressing.
  2. The bitter leaves useful in nose and eye diseases; anthelmintic. The root of botter leaved type is astringent to the bowels; useful in cholera, hydrophobia, kindney pain, piles; anthelmintic.
  3. The leaves and fruit of second variety-with tasteless leaves are purgative, expectorant, carminative, abortifacient, emmenagogue, vulnerary, galactagogue; useful in splenic enlargement, eye troubles, piles, rheumatism and pain in the joints.
