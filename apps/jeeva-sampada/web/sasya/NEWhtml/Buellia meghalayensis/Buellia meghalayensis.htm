Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A crustaceous lichen grows on moist rocks in tropical regions of Eastern Himalaya.
Availability :

The species is known from Mawsmai, Cherrapunjii area in Meghalaya, at an altitude of 1000 m. Meghalaya : Mawsmai area, 7 km from Cherrapunji.
Conservation/ Threat Status : Rare.

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

The species forms irregularly craked areolate, crustose patches on rock surface. The apothecia are black, ± immered, and 0.4-1.2 mm in diam. with plane to slightly convex disc. The asci are 8-spored with brown transversely 2- celled, 13-20 x 5-9 µm sized ascospores.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
The species has similar chemical substances of the Buellia subsoroioids Singh & Awasthi and can be used as substitute of henna dye.

Parts Used:

Chemical acids of thalli.


Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:


Chemistry:
Atranorin, norstictic acid and baeomycesic acid, and 2 unidentified compounds between R.F. class 2 and 3 and 5 and 6 ( S.R. Singh & D.D.Awasthi,1981).

Processing Techniques:
Ammonia method may be used to extract dye and is already described under species Punctelia rudecta ( Casselman,1994).
