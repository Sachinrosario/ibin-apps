HABIT & HABITAT:
Erect, shrub or crooked trees, 5-10 m tall, it is found to occur in Andhra Pradesh, Pondicherry, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Goa, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Erect, shrub or crooked trees, 5-10 m tall with yellowish brown longitudinally fissured bark and pubescent branches. Stipular thorns straight or rarely recurved, often lacking in tender shoots. Leaves obovate, elliptic-ovate, acute, retuse or mucronate at apex, subcoriaceous, dull green above, pale or grey pubescent beneath. Flowers white or creamy, in 25-30 flowered terminal corymb, fragrant, 2.2-2.8 cm across. Outer pair of sepals boat shaped. Stamens 35-50. Gynophore 1.3-2.8 cm long. Fruits berries, 1.5-2.8 cm across; pericarp coriaceous, smooth, orange-red to purple. Seeds 2-6, embedded in pink pulp.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Terminal many flowered corymb or sometimes racemes ; Flower: Bracteate, complete, usually hermaphrodite, zygomorphic, tetramerous, hypogynous  ; Calyx: Sepals 4, free at the very base in 2 series, covered with olive coloured pubescence outside, boat shaped, valvate, the inner broadly elliptic ciliate  ; Corolla: Petals 4, imbricate, oblong-obovate, inner surface pubescent; Androecium: Stamens many, inserted at the base of a long gynophore , filaments filiform, anthers dithecous, introrse, dehiscing longitudinally; Gynoecium: Ovary ovoid, pointed, glabrous or very minutely pubescent ;Fruit:Berry sub-globose, purple, smooth ; Seed: 2-6 . Flowering & Fruiting : April-May. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by cattles.
