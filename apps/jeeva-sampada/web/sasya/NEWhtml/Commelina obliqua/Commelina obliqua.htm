DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Creeping ,ascending or subscandent herb, upto 8 m long; stems glabrous or pubescent; leaves sessilate or subsessile, oblique at base, 5-18 x 1.0-5.5 cm,glabrous or puberulous or viscid hairy ; sleaths, 1-4 cm, glabrous or pubescent, ciliate or not at mouth; spathes terminal, glabrous or dense hairy; penduncles 0-4 cm; flowers violet or blue or white; capsules 4-5 mm; seeds compressed on one side.
