Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A shrub or a small tree found in north - eastern hills. The fruits turn blackish on ripening and possess scanty edible pulp.

Availability :  

Status : 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Nearly glabrous, leaves sessile 3-6 in. from elliptic lanceolate to narrowly oblong lanceolate acuminate or caudate midrib above glabrous. Branches with whitish bark and sparsingly pubescent. Leaves very variable, smaller 2-3in. and elliptic, large 5-7in. and lanceolate or linear oblong greenish when dry, base acute, nerves arched, slender; petioles 1/8-1/6 in. stipules narrow lanceolate racemes much shorter and with shorter pedicels.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY
Male racemes short slender solitary or panicled, male flowers pediceled, calyx copular shortly 3-4 lobed, stamens 2-4 inserted on tamid glabrous entire or lo9bed disk, ovary glabrous, fruit 1/3 in. long gibbously orbicular or elliptic acute, stigmas terminal or sub terminal.
