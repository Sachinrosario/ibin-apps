HABIT & HABITAT
 Annual, prostrate herb. Stems many from slender, woody rootstock, 10-20 cm high. It is found throughout the country.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves alternate, obovate-oblong, 1.0-1.2 x 0.3 cm, distantly toothed, narrowed to base. Heads yellow, discoid, solitary, axillary, sessile or subsessile, globose, 2-4 mm in diam. Involucral bracts 2-seriate, ovate to spathulate, 2 x 1 mm. Receptacles naked. Outer florets many, female, cylindric, tapering to the base; corolla 4-lobed. Disc florets few, hermaphrodite, tubular, less than 1 mm long; limb campanulate, 4-lobed. Achenes 4-angled, obtuse; angles hairy. Pappus absent.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   

Inflorescence: Heads small, globose, solitary, axillary, sub-sessile, herterogamous, disciform, yellow; Flower:  Involucre bract small, oblong, obtuse, with membranous margins ; outer flower hermaphrodite, many seriate, fertile; inner disk flower pistillate, few fertile; Pappus 0; Corolla: All regular, tubular, tube very short;Androecium:  Anther bases obtuse, entire; Gynoecium:  Style arms of female flower short, truncate; Fruit: Achenes minute, 4-angled, bristly on the angles .

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : December-Febuary. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by  wind and birds.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 The powdered obtained from the whole plant is used as a remedy for cold and toothache. It also promotes sneezing and thus relieves nasal congestion in colds.
Chemistry:
  • Taraxasteryal palmitate, traaxasteryl acetate, traxasterol, stigmasterol and arnidiol isolated from herb. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1970-1979)a .
  • Lupeol and its acetate, hexacosanol and stigmasterol isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1970-1979)b .
  • A new diterpene acid – centipedaoic acid a new flavone (I) isolated and their structures determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1980-1984)c .
  • Isolation of lupeol acetate, b-sitosterol, taraxasterol, 10-isobutyryloxy-8, 9-epoxythymol, 9, 10-diisobutyryloxy-8-hydroxythymol, arnicolide C, revifolin, helenalin and florilenalin isobutyrate from air-dried plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1985-1989)a
  • 28-O-b-D-xylopyranosides of la, 3b, 19a, 23-tetrahydroxyurs-12-en-28-oic acid, 1b,2a,3b,19a,28-pentahydroxyurs-12-en-28-oic acid, 3a,21a,22a, 28-tetrahydroxyolean-12-ene and 3a, 16a,21a,22a,28-pentahydroxyolean-12-ene isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1985-1989)b .

  • Quercetin- 3-methyl ether, quercetin-3, 3'-dimethyl ether, apigenin, aurantiamide acetate, isobutyroylplenolin (arnicolide C) and senecioylplenolin isolated as active component; 2-heptanal, 2,4-heptadienol, isobutyric acid, benzyl alcohol, cis-chrysanthenal, cis-chrysanthenyl acetate, methyl linoleate, b-gurjunene, methyl and ethyl palmitates, 2,4-decadienol, phytol, caryophyllane-2,613-oxide and dihydroactinidiolide also detected by GC-MS. (Rastogi  and Mehrotra  1990-1994)f .
  • Whole plant contained a triterpenoid saponin (3.11), essential oil (0.11 %), coumarin and a sterol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)g
  • Three antibacterial sesquiterpene lactones, identified as 6-O-methylacrylylplenolin, 6-O-isobutyroylplenolin, and 6-O-angeloylplenolin. 6-O-Methylacrylylplenolin had not been previously isolated from C. minima. All three had activity against Bacillus subtilis and Staphylococcus aureus, with 6-O-isobutyroylplenolin being the most active. (Taylor and Towers, 1998)
  • Structures of two  new triterpenoids 1beta , 2 alpha, 3 beta, 19 alpha-tetrahydroxy-urs-12-ene-28-oata (C31H50O6;mp 150 degree C) and  1b, 2b, 3 b, 19a -tetrahydroxy-urs-12-ene-28-oate (C31H50O6;mp 154 degree C) isolated from the whole plant of Centipeda minima  were  elucidated on the basis of chemical and  spectral evidence. (Rai et al., 1999).

Pharmacology:

  • Flavonoids, sesquiterpenes and amide exhibited strong activity in PCA test and significantly inhibited histamine release from rat mast cells. Activity was in the order: apigenin > quercetin-3-methyl ether > quercetin-3, 3'-dimethyl ether > isobutyroylplenolin > senecioylplenolin> aurantiamide acetate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)a .
  • Ether, methanolic and aqueous extracts exhibited antiallergy activity in passive cutaneous anaphylaxis (PCA) test. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)b .
  • Plant extract (0.5 g/kg) exhibited antitussive and expectorant activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra 1990-1994)c .
