HABIT & HABITAT
Herbaceous plant grows upto 7 cm long found in western Ghats, Nilgiri Hills, Andaman Islands, Sikkim, Meghalaya at alt. 2000 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stem up to 7 cm long with a few sheaths below. Leaves ovate-lanceolate, up to 12 cm long, acuminate at base, margin conspicuously undulate, green often shaded purple, sessile or petioled.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers in 30 cm long racemes, about 2.5 cm long, greenish, yellowish-purplish; peduncles shorter than the racemes. Sepals oblong; dorsal sepal 1-3-nerved and lateral 3-5-nerved. Petals linear, blunt, 3-nerved; lip not constricted so as to form a distinct anterior lobe, auricle of lip very variable.
Floral and seed biology:
It flowers between July- October.
