Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Coconut water is a delicious drink. Coconut toddy is nutritious and intoxicating drink. (Manilal 1989)
Sweet toddy or coconut juice is obtained by tapping the unopened spadix. Sweet toddy is refrigerant and diuretic. (Anrorymous, 1948) 

Coconut Water:

As a beverage, the water forms a refreshing drink. It is aperiant, diuretic and destroys intestinal worms. Analysis of coconut water gave the following values; water, 95.5, protein 0.1, fat <0.1, mineral water 0.4, carbohydrates 4.0, Ca 0.02, P < 0.01%, Iron 0.5 mg/100 g. Sugars forms an important constituent of the nut water. At the stage in which it is consumed as a beverage, the concentration of sugars is at its maximum and a large nut may contain in solution over an ounce of sugars.

Sweet toddy or coconut juice is obtained by tapping the unopened spadix. For this purpose the spadix is prepared by bruising with gentle taps from a small mallet, as in the case of tapping Borassus. The methods, season and stage at which tapping is done vary considerably in different places. The maximum quantity of juice is usually obtained in the third month after the commencement of tapping. The yield of sweet toddy is variable. On an average the yield of juice per palm per day is about 3, ½ lb. Tree which yield a larger number of nuts also yield a large quantity of juice. In Sri Lanka range of 600c.c.to 3,000 c.c. per day has been recorded. It is usual to tap for 8 months in a year and to rest the palm for remaining 4 months. Dwarf palms give lower yields but they have economic advantages for tapping as they are easier to tap and the palms can be planted closely. Tapping is said to improve the yield of poor bearers.

The main constituents of the fresh juice is sucrose. The ranges of analytical values for unfermented juice are: sp. gr. 280 1.058-1.077, total solids 15.2-19.7, sucrose 12.3-17.4, ash 0.11-0.41, proteins 0.23-0.32 g/100c.c. Fresh juice contains 16-30 mg. of ascorbic acid per 100 c.c. and the concentration does not change during spontaneous fermentation for 24 hours. Sweet toddy is refrigerant and diuretic.

Fermented toddy: sweet toddy is carefully collected in sterile glass vessels remains unfermented for a considerable time. As usually collected, however, fermentation rapidly sets in. Liming of collecting vessels prevents spontaneous fermentation.

Fermented toddy is consumed as a beverage. Analysis of samples shows; sp.gr. 0.998-1.033 (av. 1.012), acidity (as acetic) 0.32-0.67% (av. 0.51%), alcohal w/w, 2.7-5.8% (av. 4.2%). The following analytical values have been reported, water 98.3, protein 0.2, ether extractives 0.1, carbohydrates 1.3, mineral matter 0.1, calcium < 0.01, phosphorus 0.01%, vitamin B1< 51 U. per 100 g. (Anonymous, 1948)
