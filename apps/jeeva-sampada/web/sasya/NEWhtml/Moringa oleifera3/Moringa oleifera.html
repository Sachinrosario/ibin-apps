Chemistry:
Aldotriouronic acid from acid hydrolysis of gum, characterized as O-(b-D-glucopyranosyluronic acid)(1®6)-b-D-galactopyrnosyl (1®6)-D-galactose.

Presence of aspartic acid, glutamic acid, glycine, threonine, alanine, valine, leucine, isoleucine, histidine, lysine, arginine, phenylalanine, tryptophan, cysteine and methionne. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

Detectionof alanine, arginine, glycine, serine, threonine, valine, glutamicand aspartic acids in flowers and fruits; lysinein flowers, sucrose and glucose infloweers and sucrose in fruits. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

4-hydroxymellein (fig. 1), vanillin, octacosanoic acid, b-sitosterol and b-sitostenone isolated from stems. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

4(a-L-Rhamnosyloxy)bcnzyl isothiocyanate from seeds; instead of pterygospermine, as previously suggested, benzyl isothiocyanate found in roots. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Four auxin-Iike factors isolated from fast-growing fruits; indole acetic acid and indole acetonitrile identified. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Gum exudate contained arabinose, galactose, glucuronic acid, rhamnose, mannose and xylose in molar ratio of 14.5:11.3:3:2:1:1. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

A new glycoside moringyne (fig. 2) isolated from seeds and its structure elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

4-(a-L-rhamnosyloxy)benzyl  isothiocyanate and 4-(a-L-rhamnosyloxy)phenyl acetonitrile isolated from raw seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Mucilage named, drumstick polysaccharide (DSP), isolated from pods consisted of galactose, dextrose, xylose and sodium,potassium, magnesium and calcium salts of glucuronic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

New hypotensive thiocarbamates, niazinin A (fig. 3a), niazinin B (fig. 3b), niazimicin (fig. 3c) and an inseparable rotameric mixture of niazimins A and B (fig. 4) isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.

The Morign oleifera kernel contains 20% oil rich in b-carotene, plant sterols and lecithin.  The oil contains no unusual fatty acids. ( Njoku, and Adikwu, 1997). C.A.1998,128:240474.

Seven and Fourteen peptides from Moringa oleifera seeds were sepd. on Sephadex G-25 from acetone and ethanol prepared. (Dahot, 1997) C.A. 1997, 127:260017

A new leucoanthocyanin, characterised as leucodelphinidin-3-O-b-D-galactopyranosyl(1®4)-O-b-D-glucopyranoside, was isolated from Moringa oleifera gum. (Khare, 1997) C.A. 1997,126:341049.

Bioassay directed fractionation of the methanolic ext. of dried pods and seeds of M. oleifera resulted in the isolation of a mixt. of thiocarbamate glycosides of niazimicine and niazimin.  In the in vivo prepn., i.v. injection of the compds. (2-16 mg/kg) caused a decrease in mean arterial blood pressure and heart rate of anesthetized rats in dose dependent manner. (Jansakul, 1997) C.A. 1998, 128:265981.

Bioassay-directed fractionation led to the isolation of thiocarbamate and isothiocyanate glycosides which were also the hypotensive principles of the pods as obsd. in case of Moringa leaves. Two new compds., O-[2'-hydroxy-3'-(2"-heptenyloxy)]-Pr undecanoate and O-ethyl-4-[(a-L-rhamnosyloxy)-benzyl] carbamate along with the known substances Me p-hydroxybenzoate and b-sitosterol have also been isolated in the present studies.  The latter two compounds and p-hydroxybenzaldehyde showed promising hypotensive activity. (Faizi, 1998) C.A. 1998,129:45165.

Isolation of bioactive compounds from the seeds of Moringa oleifera Lam. were examined and from the ethanol extract were isolated the new O-ethyl-4-(2a-L-rhamnosyloxy)benzyl carbamate together with seven known compounds, 4(a-L-rhamnosyloxy)-benzyl isothiocyanate, niazimicin, niazirin, b-sitosterol, glycerol-1-(9-octadecanoate), 3-O-(6'-O-oleoyl-beta-D-glucopyranosyl) b-sitosterol and  beta-sitosterol-3-O-beta-D-glucopyranoside ( Guevara, 1999)

The composition of acyl chains and their positions in the triacylglycerols of the oil extracted from seeds of Moringa oleifera were studied by (13)C NMR spectroscopy. The unsaturated chains of M. oleifera seed oil were found to comprise only mono-unsaturated fatty acids and, in particular, two omega-9 mono-unsaturated acids, (cis-9-octadecenoic (oleic acid) and cis-11-eicosenoic acids) and one omega-7 mono-unsaturated acid (cis-11-octadecenoic acid (vaccenic acid)). (Vlahov et al, 2002)


Pharmacology:

4(a-L-Rhamnosyloxy)benzyl isothiocyanate showed antimicrobial activity; MIC in vitro against Mycobacterium phlei (40 mM/I) and Bacillus subtilis (56 mM/I). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.

Auxin activity was low during initial and later, but high during middle stages of fruit development. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.

4-(a-L-rhamnosyloxy)benzoyl isothiocyanate exhibited antibacterial activity against Bacillus subtilis but its aqueous-methanolic solution was inactive. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.

Six new and three synthetically known glycosides have been isolated from the leaves of Moringa oleifera, employing a bioassay-directed isolation method on the ethanolic extract. Most of these compounds, bearing thiocarbamate, carbamate or nitrile groups, are fully acetylated glycosides. (Faizi, 1995)

Antibacterial actions of peptides were tested against Escherichia coli, Klebsiella aerogenes, Klebsiella pneumoniae, Staphylococcus aureus and Bacillus subtilis.  Peptide 1, 3 and 6 in acetone preparedd. sample and peptide 1, 4, 6, 7, 12, 13 and 14 in ethanol prepd. sample of Moringa oleifera seeds showed good inhibitory activity against Gram +ve and Gram -ve bacteria. (Dahot, 1997) C.A. 1997, 127:260017.

Hypotensive and negative chronotropic activity of the thiocarbamate glycosides from Moringa oleifera  are unlikely to be due to the stimulation by the compds. of the muscarinic receptors of the parasympathetic or to their action as a b-adrenergic receptor antagonist, but it may act as a Ca++-channel blocker. (Jansakul, 1997) C.A. 1998, 128:265981.

The hypotensive activity of the ethanolic and aq. exts. of Moringa oleifera whole pods and their parts, namely, coat, pulp, and seed was investigated.  The activity of the ethanolic ext. of both the pods and the seeds was equiv. at the dose of 30 mg/kg.  The Et acetate phase of the ethanolic ext. of pods was found to be the most potent fraction at the same dose.  (Faizi, 1998) C.A. 1998,129:45165.

Three known thiocarbamate (TC)- and isothiocyanate (ITC)-related compounds have been isolated from the leaves of Moringa oleifera, a traditional herb in southeast Asia, as inhibitors of tumor promoter teleocidin B-4-induced Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) activation in Raji cells.( Murakami et al, 1998)

Moderate and high dose (>46 mg/kg) and daily/therapeutic high dose (7 mg/kg) of methanolic extract of Moringa oleifera affects liver and kidney functions and hematological  parameters whereas the weekly dose (3.5 mg/kg) and low and moderate daily/therapeutic dose(3.5 and 4.6 mg/kg) did not produce adverse effects on liver and kidney functions.( Mazumder et al, 1999) C.A. 1999,131:223437.

The role of Moringa oleifera aqueous leaf extract in the regulation of thyroid hormone status, was studied in adult Swiss rats. Other than the thyroid hormone concentrations, hepatic lipid peroxidation (LPO) and the activities of antioxidant enzymes, superoxide dismutase (SOD) and catalase (CAT) were evaluated. (Tahiliani and Kar , 2000).

Crude leaf extract of Moringa oleifera along with high-fat diet decreased the high-fat diet-induced increases in serum, liver, and kidney cholesterol levels by 14.35% (115-103.2 mg/100 ml of serum), 6.40% (9.4-8.8 mg/g wet weight) and 11.09% (1.09-0.97 mg/g wet weight) respectively. The effect on the serum cholesterol was statistically significant. Moringa oleifera have definite hypocholesterolemic activity. (Ghasi, 2000)

Methanolic leaf extract of M. oleifera confers significant radiation protection to the bone marrow chromosomes in mice and this may lead to the higher 30 day survival after lethal whole body irradiation. (Rao et al , 2001)

b-carotene from drumstick leaves was effective in overcoming vitamin A deficiency although serum vitamin A levels remained somewhat lower compared to the group repleted with vitamin A acetate. In terms of growth parameters, the fresh and dehydrated drumstick leaves were better than the synthetic vitamin A. (Nambiar and Seshadri , 2001)
