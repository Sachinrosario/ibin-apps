HABIT & HABITAT

Found wild in sub-Himalayan tract, from Chenab eastwards to Sarda, cultivated all over the plains of India.
Availability : CV in India - 'Jaffna' grow in S. India.
In Tamil - Chavakaceri Murunga,Chem murunga

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small or medium sized tree, about 10 m high.
Bark thick, soft, corky, deeply fissured young parts tomentose
Leaves usually tripinnate, leaflets elliptic
Flowers white, fragrant, in large panicles
Pods pendulous, greenish, 22.5-50.5 cm or more in length, triangular, ribbed
Seeds trigonous with wings on angles.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Pods, flowers, tender leaves


Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:
As vegetables, pickles.
Eaten as pot herb.
Root used as condiment or garnish.
Medicinal treatment of ascites, rheumatism, venomous bites and as cardiac and circulatory stimulants.
Root and root bark of young tree - rubefacient and vesicant.
Leaves - rich in vit. A & C - in scurvy and catarrhal affections, also as emetic.
Flowers as tonic, diuretic, and scholagogue.
Seed oil in rheumatism and gout
(Behen oil or Ben oil) - edible purpose, illumination and in cosmetics.
Oil cake - fertilizer.
Bark - coarse fiber, for making mats and twine, yields tannin juice of leaves - antibacterial property.
Gum - used in calico printing.
Wood - soft spongy wood - cellophanes, rayon
