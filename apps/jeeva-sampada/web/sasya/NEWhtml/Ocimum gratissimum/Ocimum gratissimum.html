Tall, erect, perennial, aromatic herbs, 1.2-1.8 m high found in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Orissa, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, E. Ghats, Southern Western Ghats.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Tall, erect, perennial, aromatic herbs, 1.2-1.8 m high. Leaves petiolate, elliptic-lanceolate or obovate, 6.0-12.5 x 3.5-5.5 cm, acute or acuminate at apex, cuneate at base, sparingly serrate along margin, hairy on both surfaces; petioles 1-6 cm long. 
NBPGR: Shrubby, perennial, 1.2-1.8 m. high much branched woody below; stem and branches subquadrangular, the young ones pubescent. Leaves 6.3-12.5 by 3.8-5.7 cm, elliptic-lanceolate, acute, coarsely crenate-serrate, pubescent on both sides, gland dotted, base cuneate petioles 2.5-6.3 cm long, slender, more or less pubescent. Flowers is simple or branched rather short racemes, in tolerably close whorls, rachis quadrangular, softly pubescent, bracts sessile longer than the calyx acuminate from a broad ovate base decussate and squarrose in the young inflorescence calyx – 3 m. corolla – 4mm, long pale greenish yellow, stamen-exerted net let 1.5 mm diam. Subglobose rugose, brown.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   Flowers greenish yellow-white, in simple or branched racemes in close whorls; bracts sessile, oblanceolate, longer than the calyx; pedicels 3 mm long. Calyx c 2.5 mm long, enlarged in fruits, lobes 5; upper lip 3 mm long and lower lip 2 mm long, pubescent, recurved upwards. Corolla c 5 mm long, pubescent outside, bilabiate; upper lip 4-teethed; lower lip 2 mm long, acute, pubescent without. Stamens 4, exerted. Nutlets 4, subglobose, c 0.15 cm in diam., brown.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 The plant has a pungent taste. It is used in vomiting, skin disease, erysipelas, inflammations, strangury (Ayurveda). The plant has carminative, aphrodisiac properties and used in brain, heart, liver and spleen disease. It is also prescribed to remove foul breath; strengthen gums and good for piles (Unani) (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).
NBPGR:
Plant - antirheumatic, antiparalytic, antigonorrhoeic in aphthae of children, seminal weakness, Seed: anticephalagic, in neuralgia.


Chemistry:
Essential oil from leaves and flowers contains α and β-pinene, camphene, α-terpinene, Δ–carene, myrcene,1,8-cineole, p-cymene limonene, camphor linalool, α-terpineol, thymol, methyleugenol methyl-isoeugenol, caryophyllene, humulene. β – selinene, clovene, longifoleneand a sesquiterpene alcohol, gratissimol. A variety rich in eugenol is also known. Seed mucilage contains pentoses, hexoses, uronic acids and lipids.
