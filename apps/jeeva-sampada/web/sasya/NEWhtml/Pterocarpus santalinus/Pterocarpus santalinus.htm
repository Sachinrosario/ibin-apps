Chemistry: 

Pterocarpol from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).
Isolation and structure elucidation of santalin A (fig. 3a). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
Three new sesquiterpenes -pterocarptriol (fig. 4a), isopterocarpolone (fig. 2) and pterocarpodiolone (fig. 4b)-together with b-eudesmol, pterocarpol (fig. 1) and cryptomeridiol isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
Acetyloleanolic acid, acetyloleanolic aldehyde and erythrodiol isolated from sapwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.
Structures of santalin B (fig. 3b) isolated from wood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.
Lupeol, epilupeol, lupenone, lup-(20)29-ene, b-amyrone and sitosterol isolated from bark and leaves; b-amyrin, stigmasterol, erythrodiol and betulin also isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.
Structure and absolute configuration of pterocarpol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)f.
A newtriterpene -lupenediol (fig. 5) isolated from bark and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)g.
Eudesmol isolated from heartwood found to be mixture of a-, b- and g-isomers; absolute configuration of pterocarpol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)h.
Two new couamrin glycosides isolated and characterized as 7-hydroxy-6-methoxycoumarin-7-O-[b-D-apiofuranosyl(1®6)-b-D-glucoside] and 7-hydroxy-6-methoxycoumarin-7-O-[a-L-rhamnopyranosyl(1®3)-b-D-galactosyl(1®6)-b-D-glactoside]. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
Another coumarin isolated from heartwood and its structure determined as 5-hydroxy-7-O-(3-methylbut-2-enyl) coumarin. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
Phytochemical investigation on the constituents of heartwood of Pterocarpus santalinus resulted in the isolation of a new acylated isoflavone glucoside. The structure of the new compound was elucidated on the basis of spectral studies as 4'-5-dihydroxy-7-O-Me isoflavone 3'-O-b-D-(3''-x-cinnamoyl) glucoside. (Krishnaveni and Rao, 2000). C.A. 2000, 133, 319540t.
A new isoflavone together with liquiritigenin and isoliquiritigenin has been isolated from the heartwood of Pterocarpus santalinus. Based on spectral methods, the structure of the new compound was elucidated as 6-hydroxy,7,2',4',5'-tetramethoxyisoflavone. (Krishnaveni and Rao, 2000). MAPA 2000-04-2451.

Pharmacology: 

Alcoholic extract of plant produced depression in mice and rats at a dose of 1.0 g/kg and caused significant blockade of conditioned avoidance responses at 0.5 to 2.0 g/kg. It also showed protection against electroshock seizure (ED50, 1.0 g/kg). at 1.0 to 2.0 g/kg dose it showed appreciable protection against carrageenin-induced oedema in rats. LD50 in rats was 4.0 g/kg. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)i.
MeOH extract of wood Pterocarpus santalinus as antioxidant. (Kato, 1997). C.A. 1997, 127, 33225w.
Extracts prepared from Pterocarpus santalinus significantly inhibited Tumor Nurosis Factor - alpha production in vivo. (Cho et al., 1999). MAPA 9904-2354.
A significant improvement in IgG and IgA levels was observed in the treated group. The study shows that Pterocarpus santalinus, possesses a significant potency to modify immunoglobulins and complement levels in infants. (Amarsinghe, 1999). MAPA 9902-0882.
The effect of administration of different dose of Pterocarpus santalinus (Red sanders) bark extracts in normal and diabetic rats, on blood glucose levels was evaluated. Among the three fractions (aqueous, ethanol and hexane), ethanolic fraction at the dose of 0.25 g/kg body weight showed maximum antihyperglycaemic activity. The same dose did not cause any hypoglycaemic activity in normal rats. The results were compared with the diabetic rats treated with glibenclamide and the antihyperglycaemic activity of ethanolic extract of sanders bark at dose of 0.25 g/kg body weight was found to be more effective than that of glibenclamide. (Rao et al, 2001). MAPA 2001-05-2530.
