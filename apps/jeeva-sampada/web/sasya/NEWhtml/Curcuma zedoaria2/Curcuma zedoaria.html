DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Rhizome pale yellow-white, camphor like smell. Leaves in tufts, 5-6 together, c 1 m long; lamina oblong or narrowly oblong-lanceolate, 20-60 x 8-10, acuminate at apex, glabrous with a purple cloud down the centre of the upper surface; petiole shorter than the lamina, winged.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Flowers in 10-18 x 6-8cm spikes, yellow. Coma bracts whitish at first, soon becoming bright rose-pink or crimson. Fertile bracts ovate, up to 5 x 3.5 cm, pale green with red-tinged margin; bracts adnate to each other in the lower third. Calyx 1-1.5 cm long, whitish-green with 3 small triangular teeth. Corolla tube 2.5 –3.0 cm long, funnel shaped; dorsal lobe broadly triangular, 1.3 x 1 cm; lateral lobes narrower, obtuse. Labellum orbicular, 1.5 cm wide, pale yellow, thickened and deep yellow centrally, emerginate. Capsules ovoid, 3-gonous, thin, smooth, dehiscing irregularly. Seeds ellipsoid with a white lacerate aril.
CIMAP:
   Inflorescence: Spikes; Flower: Bracts- greenish-white, crimpson or purple, complete, hermaphrodite; Calyx: Obtusely 3-toothed ; Corolla: Twice as long as calyx, funnel shaped, lateral lobes oblong, dorsal lobe larger ; Androecium: only 1 perfect rest is reduced to staminodes,, anthers linear 2-celled ; Gynoecium: Ovary 3-celled ,inferior, ovules many, style slender, stigma entire; Fruit: Capsule ovoid, 3-gonous, thin, smooth; Seed: Ellipsoid with white laceral aril. Flowering & Fruiting : October-November. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
 It flowers between July - September.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine it used in leucoderma, piles, bronchitis, asthma, tumours, tuberculous glands of neck and also used in enlarged spleen. In Unani system of medicine the rhizomes are used in tonic of brain and heart; and also used in gripping of children, pains, toothache, inflammation. (Kritikar & Basu, 1933).
