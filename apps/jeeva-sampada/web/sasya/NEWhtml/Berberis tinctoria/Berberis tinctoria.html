REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence:compound racemes; Flower:yellow, hermaphrodite, fascicled, large,complete, regular, pedicles red; Calyx: Sepals 6, with 2-3 apressed bracts, imbricate in two series; Corolla:petals 6, usually imbricate, in 2 series, usually with 2 basal glands inside; Androecium:Stamens 6, free, anther-cells opening by recurved valves; Gynoecium: Ovary simple, ovules few, basal, style short, stigma small, globose; Fruit: Berry few seeded; Seed:with crustaceous fleshy testa.

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
