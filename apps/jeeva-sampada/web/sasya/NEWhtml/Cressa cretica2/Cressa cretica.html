 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
Erect, hairy undershrub or herb, 15-38 cm high. It is found in Uttar Pradesh, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Diu, Gujarat and Rajasthan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Stems slender, profusely branched. Leaves numerous, alternate, crowded, ovate, 3-7 x 1.5-3.5 mm, acute at apex, densely silky hairy. Flowers white or pink, in small clusters in the axils of upper leaves; bracts 2, linear. Calyx elliptic, densely silky. Corolla divided half way down; lobes oblong. Stamens 5, exserted. Capsules ovoid, 4-5 mm long, pointed. Seeds solitary, chest-nut brown, glabrous.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
   Inflorescence: In small clusters in the axil of leaves ; Flower: White or pink, pedicels short, bracts linear, hairy, complete, hermaphrodite ; Calyx: Sepals 5, subequal, small, not enlarging in fruit, thin imperfect, elliptic ; Corolla: Infundibuliform, tube short, lobes 5, oblong , inflexed, hairy on the back ;Androecium: Stamens 5, exserted, filamnets filiform, anthers oblong; Gynoecium: Ovary 2-celled, ovules 2 in each cell, style 2 distinct from the base, stigma capitate ; Fruit:  Ovoid capsule pubescent at the apex and pointed; Seed: Usually solitary .

Floral and seed biology:
November – May.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.  
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome number 2n=28.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:  In Ayurveda system of medicine the plant is bitter and pungent. It enriches blood and used in consumption, leprosy, asthma, biliousness and urinary discharges. In Unani system of medicine leaves are used as tonic and improves appetite.


Chemistry:
Chemical analysis of minerals present in aqueous extractive of shrub and analysis of ash. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

A quercetin glycoside detected. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

b-sitosterol, its glucoside, n-octacosanol, umbelliferone, scopoletin, isopimpinellin and quercetin isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

The alcohol extract of fruits of Cressica cretica Linn., has afforded a new coumaranochromone glycoside, designated as Cresoside. It has been characterized as 7,4’-dihydroxy-5-methoxy coumaranochromone-7-O-b-D-glucoside on the basis of spectral and chemical methods. (Ahmed, 1998). C.A. 2000, 132, 76020w.
