Chemistry:  

Detection of cystinem glutamic acid, proline, phenylalanine and histadine by PC in protein extract. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).
Four anthraquinones (chrysophanol, emodin, physicon, and rhein) and seven flavanoides (apigenin, isoorientin, isovitexin, lyteolin, orientin, quercetin, vitexin) were isolated from Rumex vesicarius. Sterol fraction yielded b-sitosterol, stigmasterol, campesterol and cholesterol and fatty acids were identified as hexanoic, dodecanoic, midecanoic, myristic, pentadecanoic, palmitic, palmitoleic, heptadocanic, stearic, linoleic, linolenic and arachidic acid. (Easa et al, 1995). C.A. 1996, 125, 5484v.
Chemical constituents of volatile oil of Rumex vesicarius was analyzed by GC/MS. (Alkhathalan, Al-Hazimi, 1996). C.A. 1997, 127, 2978q.
HPLC, spectrophotometric and colorimetric methods for the determination of free and combined (O-glucosides) emodin and chrysophanol in Rumex vesicarius L. grown in Kuwait. The highest content of free emodin and chrysophanol was present in the roots than in stems. (Al-Gindy et al, 1998). C.A. 1998, 129, 45381y.

Pharmacology: A macro- and microscopical study of the root, stem and leaf of Rumex vesicarius. (Bishar et al, 1998). C.A. 1998, 128, 99901m


Ethnobotany: Edible as vegetable (Leaf, Shah, 1984)
