Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
Solitary or clustered, unbranched stems; leaves large, long-stalked, arching, bi-pinnately divided into leaflets which are widest above the middle and irregularly toothed at apex; fruit a fleshy berry.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A very elegant,greenish brown palm,with bushy habit, up to 12 m in height and 80 cm in girth,new shoots arising from the axillary buds very close to the ground or slightly below it.Leaves bipinnate, 1.2-2.7 long;leaflets 10-18 cm x 5.0 cm, very obliquely cuneiform,eroe;spathe almost boat-shaped,concealing the whole peduncle,deciduous,branchs of spadiz many,c 30 cm long;male flowers numerous,flesh-coloured,with reddish points,female solitary or 2-3 together,smaller and appearing after male; drupes red, 1.2 cm long, 1-seeded.

Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Seed kernels.

Uses

1.The bud or the cabbage is bitter but can be eaten after cooking.

2.The pithy core contains starch and is eaten after roasting.

3.The Kernel is edible and is used as a masticatory with betel.

Other Uses
1.The soft fibres from the leaf-sheaths are used to cauterize wounds.

2.The scanty but strong wood is used for making spinning-wheels.

3.A fibre from the base of the leaves is employed to stugg matresses.

4.It is also used in blowpipes and  as tinder.
