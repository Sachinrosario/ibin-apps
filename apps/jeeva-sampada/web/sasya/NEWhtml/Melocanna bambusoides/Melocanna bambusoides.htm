 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
A tall bamboo with stems from 50 to 70 feet long and from 12 to 13 inches in girth, found in Eastern Bengal,Chittagong,Arracan and Tenasserim. It is the common gregarious bamboo of the Chittagong Hills.

Availability : 

Country:    India.
Zone: North East and South.
State:    Assam, Karnataka and Meghalaya.
District:    Uttar Kannada

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
The fruit is large,pear-shaped, 3 to 5 inches long; edible.Culms erect, up to 21 m. in length and 9.5 cm. in diam., arising singly from a ramifying rhizome, leaves lanceolate or oblong- lanceolate.

Back to Top

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY :
Flowers in large compound panicles of drooping branches with second clustered spikelets; fruit (caryopsis) pyriform, fleshy, 7.5-12.5 cm. long, often viviparous, edible.

Seed Germination :
Melocanna bambusoides is typically gregarious and spreads by its long vigorous rhizomes. The fruits germinate soon after falling to the ground at the commencement of the rainy season. Seedlings do not thrive under shade but spring up readily in gaps. They grow vigorously and culms attain their maximum height in 5 years. Artificial regeneration may be done by sowing fruits directly at site or by transplanting nursery-raised seedlings; propagation by offsets and rhizome sections is also easy. A spacing of 3.6 in. x 3.6 m. has been recommended.
