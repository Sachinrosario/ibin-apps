HABIT & HABITAT
Rootstock tuberous, aromatic; root fibres fleshy, cylindric, not aromatic found almost throughout India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Rootstock tuberous, aromatic; root fibres fleshy, cylindric, not aromatic. Leaves 2, spreading horizontally, lying flat on the surface of the ground, round-ovate, 6.2-12.5 x 4.5-8.7 cm, deltoid-acuminate, thin, deep green, 10-12 ribbed; petioles short, channeled.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers 6-12 from the center of the plant between the leaves, fragnant, opening successively; bracts lanceolate, green, short. Calyx as long as the outer bracts. Corolla-tube 2.5 cm long; lobes lanceolate, a little shorter than the tube, pure white. Lateral staminodes cuneate-obovate, 1-2 cm long, white.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 The tubers made into powdered and mixed with honey are given in cough and pectoral affections. The tuber boiled in oil is externally applied to stoppages of the nasal organ (Rheede).


Chemistry:  

P-methoxycinnamic acid and its methyl and ethyl esters from essential oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).
Ethyl p-methoxy-trans-cinnamate isolated form rhizomes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
Isolation of deoxypodophyllotoxin and ethyl p-methoxy-trans-cinnamate from rhizomes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
New monoterpene ketone-car-3-en-5-one isolated from rhizomes and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
Structure of kaempferia camphor, isolated from essential oil of plant, was shown to be ethyl p-methoxy-trans-cinnamate. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
The title compound Et(E)-3-(4-methoxyphenyl) propenoate, C12H14O3, was isolated from rhizome of Kaempferia galanga and its structure was confirmed by an X-ray investigation. (Luger et al, 1996). C.A. 1996, 125, 53533g.
Mass spectrometric analysis confirmed that 4-butylmenthol (fig. 1) had been isolated from the rhizome of Kaempferia galanga. (Jenie and Sudibyo, 2000). C.A. 2001, 135, 355301r.
Chromatographic - Mass spectroscopic (GC-MS) analysis was carried on volatile oil isolated from Kaempferia galanga rhizomes. It was found that eight components comprised the oil, in which five of them were main components. The mass-spectroscopic analysis resulted that three of the five main compounds were b-phellandrene, a-terpineol, cinnamate, and dihydro-b-sesquiphellandrene respectively. (Retno, 2000). C.A. 2001, 135, 369224e.

Pharmacology:  

Ethyl p-methoxy-trans-cinnamate inhibited monoamine oxidase. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.
Deoxypodophyllotoxin showed cytotoxic activity by inhibiting HeLa cells. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.
Plasters were prepared containing binders, FIR-emitting material from plant extract of Kaempferia galanga, which promoted blood circulation, inhibited inflammation and controlled pain. (Song et al, 1994). C.A. 1995, 123, 123187g.
Pharmaceutical compounds containing Kaempferia with other plants is used to prevent Helicobactor pylori-related gastritis and peptic or duodenal ulcer. (Kobashi et al, 1995). C.A. 1995, 123, 237858g.
