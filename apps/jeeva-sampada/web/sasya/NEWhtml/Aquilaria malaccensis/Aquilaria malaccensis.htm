DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

- Tall evergreen tree, 18-21m, sometimes upto 40m in height, 1.5-2.5m in girth, with a moderately straight and often fluted stem.
- Leaves 5-9 cm in, thinly coriaceous, oblong-lanceolate.
- Flowers white or green or dirty yellow in terminal, sessile or shortly peduncled, umbellate cymes.

Produce and Products:
Fungus infected trees - agarwood - incense, essential oil obtained used in high class perfumery as a fixative. Uninfected wood - making bows, walking sticks, beads, rosaries, small jewel cases, pencil making (in Malaysia), writing and printing paper.
Alcoholic extract of wood - exhibit mild cardiotonic activity. Agarwood - stimulant, antiasthamatic, carminative, tonic, aphrodisiac, astringent, used in diarrhoea, dysentery, gout, rheumatism, paralysis, as a liniment in various skin diseases, preventive against fleas and list (when dusted on clothes and skin).
Agar powder - manufacture of fumigators, pastlles, agarbathies.
Agar oil from agarwood - in perfumery, scenting tobacco, betel leaves.
Spent wood -for making agarbathies.
Bark - source of writing material (sachpat) for writing religious scriptures.
Bast fiber - ropes and cloth.
