Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A fast growing tree, native of Venezuela and Colombia. Found on semi and lava plateau and Deccan islands. Tree is cultivated in many parts of India.

Thrives best in hot moist localities. It is eminently suitable for grassy blanks in swampy areas. It ca grow on dry and barren soils also, but in such situations it tends to branch low.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A tree with round spreading crown. Tree grows up to 70 ft. high with large evergreen bipinnate leaves. Leaflets oblique, ovate-oblong or roundish, up to 1½ in. long, shining above and downy beneath, folding and drooping at night or on approach of rain. Shallow groved bark colonized by epiphytes.

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers pink coloured, solitary, in head inflorescence up to 2 inches in diam. Numerous long, pink and white stamens. Pod straight, sessile, flattened or subterete and indehiscent with a sweet pulp around the seeds. 10-12 seeds.

Other uses:

The leaves and pods of the tree are esteemed as fodder for livestock. Green and tender leaves are relished by cattle.

The pods are considered to be particularly valuable as feed for cattle and horses. They contain a sugary pulp and form a rich and satisfying feed for livestock. They are said to improve the quality of milk when fed to milch cows. The pods compare favourably in composition with good quality hay and concentrates commonly used. The seeds should be crushed and mixed with the rest of the pods before feeding.

The pulp of the ripe pods is rich in fermentable sugars and may be employed for the production of alcohol.

Gum - The tree yields a dark coloured gum of inferior quality which swells up in water into a tough cartilage-like mass. It occurs in irregular tears and vermicular pieces.

Lac - It is one of the host plants of the lac insects in south India. the lac produced is of poor quality. . it is reddish n colour and is rather brittle.

Miscellaneous Information

The plant is valued for the shady shelter it affords to delicate plants.Ornamental tree planted for avenue. Fast growing tree commonly cultivated on roadside.

Much planted street tree.
Distinctive leaf movements are seen - circadian oscillations. Light induced three massive fluxes of K+, Cl-, H+ ions through motor cells of pulvinus.
Tree is a nitrogen fixer.
