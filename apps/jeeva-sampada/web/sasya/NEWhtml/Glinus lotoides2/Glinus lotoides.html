DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual, prostrate or ascending, much branched, pubescent herbs, grayish green or pinkish with age, 10-85 cm long. Leaves opposite or several at a node, 1.0-3.5 x 0.5-2.0 cm, obtuse or apiculate at apex, cuneate at base, sometimes undulate at margin; petioles slender, 0.5-0.9 cm long, stellately hairy. Flowers pinkish white, in axillary fascicles, 1-6 together; pedicels unequal, 0.1-0.3 cm long, divaricate. Sepals elliptic-lanceolate, 0.5-0.8 cm long, acute at apex, outside with many white hairs, often densely wooly. Stamens 5 to many. Ovary ovoid, quite glabrous, 5-lobed; style absent or very short; stigma usually 5, spreading. Capsules ovoid, 5-valved, shorter than the sepals. Sedds small, tuberculate, numerous, up to 0.8 mm long, brown to black.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 The dried plant is prescribed for diarrhoea and also used as purgative. It is also useful in diseases of the abdomen.


Chemistry:  

Mollugogenol A (fig. 1), mp. 250°C, isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.
Mollugogenols A and B (fig. 2) characterized as 3b,6a,16b,22-tetrahydroxy-isohopane and 3b,6a-dihydroxyhopa-15,17(21)-diene respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.
New sapogenins - mollugogenols C (fig. 4) and E - along with mollugogenols A and B isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a. 


b- and g-sitosterol glucosides and olenolic acid isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b. 
A new triterpene glycoside - mullogocin A (fig. 3a) isolated and characterized as mollugogenols A -3[a-arabinofuranosyl(1®5)-a-L-arabinofuranosyl(1®4)-b-D-glucopuranoside]. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.
Mollugogenol F (fig. 3c)isolated and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.
Structure of mollugogenol D (fig. 5) elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.
Stereostructures of mollugogenols A and E (fig. 3b) established. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)f.
Structure of mollugogenol F determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.
A new triterpeneoid glycoside isolated from seeds and characterized as 3-O-b-D-glucopyranosyl(1®4)-a-L-arabinopyranosyl - oleanolic (28®1)-b-D-glucopyranosyl ester (fig. 6). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.


Apigenin-8C-glucoside, apigenin-7-rhamnoglucoside, pelargonidin-3-sophorosido-7-glucoside, esculin and sulfuretin isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)d.


Vicenin-2 and vitexin isolated from aerial parts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)e.
A new triterpenoid sapogenin - mollugogenol G (fig. 7) isolated and its stereostructure established. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
