Taxonomic Details

Name : Parmotrema nigherrense (Nyl.) Hale
Family - Parmeliaceae
Synonyms - Parmelia nilgherrensis Nyl.
Common name - Hindi: ‘Chharila’; Marathi: Darag Phool

Back to Top

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A foliose coriaceous lichen, forms circular patches of 7-15 cm across on bark of trees or sometimes on rocks surface in temperate and alpine regions of India. The common trees bearing the growth of Parmotrema nilgherrense on their trunk and twigs are Cedrus deodara, Cinchona, Cinnamomum, Quercus leucotricho-phora and Rhododendron species. 

Availability :

One of the common species among the Parmotremas and is widely distributed throughout Indian subcontinent.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: The species has large coriaceous thallus with strongly white maculate upper surface. The lobes are broad, rotund and ciliate. The apothecia are perforated in the centre.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Traditional use as Havan material: The species is used as one of the ingredients in Havan material by the Gaddi and other hill communities that inhabit in tribal tracts and forest areas of Kangra valley, Himachal Pradesh in Western Himalaya.
As Spices: The species is sold in the name of ”Chharila” in the market along with Parmotrema tinctorum, Rimelia reticulata and Everniastrum cirrhatum. Garam Masala, is common spice in India, where this species is one of the principal component.
