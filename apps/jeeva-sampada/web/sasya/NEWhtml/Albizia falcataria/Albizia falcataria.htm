HABIT & HABITAT

Adapted to moist tropics where rainfall is 2000-2700 mm and above. One of the fastest growing tree.

Prone to wind damage.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Wood used as fuel. But it is a poor fuel.

Wood: Wood is soft, light-coloured and light to medium in weight (sp. gr. 0.30-0.35).

It can be used to make packing cases, matches, tea chests and as a potential source for paper pulp.

Miscellaneous Information

Tree is a nitrogen fixer. Nitrogen fixing tree species are planted for soil improvement.

On good sites it can be spaced closely (1000-2000) trees/ha) allowing trunks to grow straight.
