DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual or perennial, c 60 cm high, erect or diffuse herbs or under shrubs; stems with 1-2 spiny emergence at base of petioles; stems, petioles and pedicels ash-coloured, with minute stellate hairs. Leaves ovate to oblong, rarely disc-shaped, 6-30 x 4-25 mm, acute to rounded or truncate at base and apex, saw-toothed at margin, 3-5 nerved at base, both surfaces stellate hairy, glabous above; petioles 2.0-2.5 mm long; stipules linear, 1.0-2.5 mm long, hairy.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers axillary, solitary or 2-5 in clusters; pedicels 2-8 mm long, accrescent upto 8 mm, jointed above the middle. Calyx bell-shaped, 3-5 mm across, lobes free above the middle, triangular, 1-2 x 1.5-2.0 mm, ash-coloured, minute stellate and scattered simple hairy outside, glabrous inside except for apical margins. Corolla yellow or yellowish-white. Schizocarps enclosed within calyx; mericarps 5, 2-3 mm long, trigonous with a divergent, 1.0-1.5 mm long awn, apex of mericarps and awns stellate hairy, dorsally with prominent reticulation. Seeds 1.0-1.5 mm long, slightly trigonous, glabrous, brownish black.
