HABIT & HABITAT:
 It can be seen in the damp places, evergreen forest, Forests, moist deciduous forest, semi evergreen forest, tree farm.
BioticZone: Ghat, Kanara, Khasia hills, Konkan, Konkan plain, Western Ghats, Western peninsular India,

Availability :

Zone: Central, South and West zone,
State: Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu.
