Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Rare, Rooted emergent. The species is so far known only from type locality. It grows in paddy fields along the River Tarkarli, Nerurpar-Dhamapur, about 5 km away, west to Kudal in Sindhudurg district of Maharashtra at about 15-20 m altitude. The area is situated at latitude 73 0 41' N / 370 30' E. The area receives rainfall of about 200-225 cm per annum, mainly from June to September. The species is restricted to a small area of about 5-sq.km and critically endangered.

In natural habitat, the species perpetuates the dry summer in the form of dormant tubers. With on set of the monsoon, the dormant tubers sprout out in June and start producing leaves and inflorescences from July to September. During October, when paddy fields get dried, the aerial part die and tubers enter into dormancy till the next season.


Availability : Maharashtra


Conservation/ Threat Status : Endemic, Rare

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Monoecious, tuberiferous, perennial, semiaquatic herbs, 10-30 cm in height. Tubers globose to elongate, 2-3 cm in diameter; roots slender, fibrous, from the top of the tuber forming crown over tuber. Leaves: semierect, emergent; petiole stout, brittle, 5-25 cm long, acutely triangular; lamina 2-7.5 x 1.8-3.3 cm, thick, dark green, brittle, shiny, ovate-oblong, cordate or rounded at the base, acute or rounded at the apex, entire, 7-9 nerved, amphistomatic. Spathe ca 1.5 x 0.6 cm, conical, membranous, acute, caducous. Inflorescence: peduncles 20-30 cm long, thickened towards spike, slender; spike simple, 6-7 cm long, densely flowered, dark pink coloured, flowers arranged in all directions, along maturity spike elongated and turns green. Flowers: tepals 2, dark pink, obovate, obtuse ca 1.5 x 1.5 mm in flowering, ca 2 x 2 mm in fruiting, persistent, one nerved. Stamens 6, exerted; filament 2-2.2 mm long, narrow towards apex; anthers violet, 2-celled; pollen monocolpate, elliptic, 21-27 x 15-19.5 µ with reticulate ornamentation. Carpels 3, ca 1 x 0.5 mm; stigma decurrent; ovules 2, basal. Follicles 5-7 x 2-3 mm, beaked, smooth; seeds with simple testa; embryo 5-6.5 x 1.5-1.8 mm, cylindrical, plumule basal, not lying in a groove.
