DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: corymbose peduncled or sub-sessile racemes; Flower: yellow, hermaphrodite, fascicled, complete, regular; Calyx: Sepals 6, with 2-3 apressed bracts, imbricate in two series; Corolla: petals 6, usually imbricate, in 2 series, usually with 2 basal glands inside; Androecium: Stamens 6, free, anther-cells opening by recurved valves; Gynoecium: Ovary simple, ovules few, basal, style distinct, stigma capitate; Fruit: Berry red or black, glaucous; Seed:with crustaceous fleshy testa.
