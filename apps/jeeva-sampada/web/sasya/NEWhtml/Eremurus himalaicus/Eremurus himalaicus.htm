Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 A genus of stately herbaceous perennials, often with thick rootstocks, occurring in central and western Asia , particularly in Persia and Turkistan . One species, E. himalaicus Baker (Himalayan desert candle), is recorded in India . It is found on open grassy and stony hillsides in: Kashmir , Lahul and Kanawar, at altitudes of 7,000 to 10,000 ft. It has been introduced into Europe and America and is widely cultivated in gardens.
Generally it is found in drier areas and rocky slopes,
BioticZone: Temperate Western Himalaya.
Misc: Afghanistan to Himalchal Pradesh.
