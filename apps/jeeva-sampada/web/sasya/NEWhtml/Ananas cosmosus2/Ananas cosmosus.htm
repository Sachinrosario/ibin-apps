DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

- A perennial herb, 90-100 cm in height.
- Roots of two types - one occurring around the stem in a hemispherical mass with some arising form the axis; the other consisting of slender roots which penetrate the soil upto 1.2m.;
- Stem thick, stout, hidden by leaves, 20-50 cm high, 7-11 cm thick; leaves rigid, mostly densely tufted, in a rosette at the base of the stem with short internodes, sword shaped, with spiny tip and margins, 90-150 cm x 2.5-6.5 cm.
- Inflorescence compound, generally from the centre of rosette, terminal, with C150 flowers, varying in no. under different conditions.
Flower fleshy, each with a rough bract, pale blue with a sheen of faint purple.
Sorosis formed by aggregate of fruits called eyes, cylindrical, sometimes tapering, weighing normally 1.0-2.5 kg, rarely upto 10 kg.
- Shell dark, green when unripe, dark orange-yellow when ripen with some mottling.
- Fruit sometimes seedless under cultivation; fruit at the base large and getting reduced towards apex.

MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:
Curvularia eragrostidis (P. Henn.) J.A. Meyer - leaf blight.
Wallrothiella bromeliae Rehm from Assam.
Phytophthora colocasiae Racib. emend. Thomas & T.S. Ramakr. from Assam.
Arothecium sp. from Maharashtra.
Sclerotium rolfsii sacc. attacks the roots in Meghalaya.
Ceratostomella paradoxa (de Seyn) - heart rot, basal rot, fruit rot.
Boryodiplodia ananassae (Sac.) Petch - rot in fruit, both in storage and in transit.
Viral wilt transmitted by Pseudococcus breviceps All from Karnataka.
Trichothecium roseum Link ex. Fr. B. pink rot.
Nematode - Heterodesa marioni G. (Meloidogyne sp.).

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Fruit, leaf

Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:
Leaf fiber - for making sacks, curtain-cloth, matting, carpet backing, ropes, furnishing fabrics, table mats, bags, twines, threads, cloth in fine colours and strong textures.
Pulp - Paper.
Fruit - ripe fruit eaten fresh, canned or made into preserves.
Juice - alcoholic beverage, anti-scorbutic, diuretic, and refrigerant properties.
- allays gastric irritability in fever and improves appetite.
- Juice from unripe fruit - abortifacient, anthelmintic.
Cattlefeed, young sprouts of wild pt - cooked and eaten in Guatemala fresh juice of pt - applied on skin, in leprosy and elephantiasis.
Fresh jucie and leaves - purgative, a powerful anthelmintic, blood purifier, emmenagogue, abortifacient, contain enzyme - Bromelain - used as a meat tenderizer, chill proofing reagent for beer, food additive, bating reagent of hiden in paint industry to improve the stability of protein emulsifiers used in latex paints.
Citric, malic, ascorbic, oxalic acid obtained from fruit and plant.
Wax - mature portion of stem - source of starch.
