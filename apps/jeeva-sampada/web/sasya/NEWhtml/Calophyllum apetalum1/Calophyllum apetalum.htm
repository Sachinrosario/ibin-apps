Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Moderate sized tree occurring in evergreen forests of western ghats from Mysore to Travancore up to an elevation of 1,000 ft., and on the banks of rivers and steams.


Availability :

 

Found in the Western Ghats from North Kanara to Travancore, on the banks of rivers and in the evergreen forests. Available in fair quantities from south zone and limited suppliers available from west zone.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Used as fuelwood.

Wood: Sapwood and heartwood fairly sharply demarcated in the freshly felled timber, but usually less so on aging. Sapwood pale pinkish or reddish-white. Heartwood reddish-brown with darker streaks. Wood moderately heavy, average weight 655 kg/m3 (41 lb/ft3) at 12 per cent moisture content; rather lustrous; coarse-textured and interlocked-grained.

A diffuse-porous wood. Growth rings indistinct. Pores large to moderately large, clearly visible to the eye, scanty, exclusively solitary, arranged in radially oblique groups or chains, forming a characteristic pattern, occasionally plugged with tyloses or filled with gummy deposits; vessel lines prominent on longitudinal surfaces. Soft tissues apotracheal, in narrow tangential bands often ending abruptly. Rays fine to very fine, not visible to the eye but distinct, under the lens as numerous closely-spaced lines.

Wood mostly used for constructional purposes. Also used for manufacture of furniture, particularly from figured stock, heavy packing boxes, tent poles and veneers and plywood.

Known material for boat and ship building. Logs of long lengths usually used as masts and spars.
