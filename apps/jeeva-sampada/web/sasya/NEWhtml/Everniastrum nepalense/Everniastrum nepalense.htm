Taxonomic Details

Name : Everniastrum nepalense ( Taylor ) Hale
Family - Parmeliaceae
Basionym - Parmelia nepalensis Taylor.
Synonym - Cetrariastrum nepalense (Tayl.) W. Culb. & C. Culb. 
Common name - “Charilla”.

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A linear elongated lobed foliose lichen found growing on both rocks and twigs of trees in subtropical, temperate and alpine regions. 

Availability :

The species exhibit its restricted distribution in India and Nepal. In India it is widely distributed in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Manipur, Tamil Nadu, Uttaranchal and West Bengal in temperate and higher eleavation of tropical regions (Divakar 2002). In Nepal the species is quite common in all the localities in higher tropical altitude and temperate areas.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: hallus forming patches of 5 – 15 cm across, suberect to pendulous, sometimes coriaceous, loosely attached to the substratum, grey to dark grey or reddish, cilia alon margin, simple to branched, 1 – 3 mm long, upper surface smooth and convex, lower surface black, pale brown near margins, densely rhizinate, apothecia sessile to substipitate, 2 – 10 mm in diameter, disc brown to blackish brown, asci clavate, spores oval ellipsoid (Divakar 2002).

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
As spices: - A most frequently collected species of lichens in India and Nepal for preparation of spices dyes and Resinoids.
In smoking: - This species is also usedd for smoking as tobacco (Singh 1997).
For Fragrance: As the species is available quite frequently, it is used for spiritual enlightment (Havan), puriffication of the environment and as incense material.
