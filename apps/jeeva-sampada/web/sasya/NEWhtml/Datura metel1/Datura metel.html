REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
      Inflorescence: Flat cymes; Flower: Large, bracteate, pedicellate; Calyx: Calyx tubular, herbaceous, 5-toothed ; Corolla: Tubular, funnel shaped ;Androecium: Stamens 5, anthers linear, filaments free; Gynoecium: Ovary superior, hypogynous, stigma bifid ; Fruit: Capsule 4-valved, spinous; Seed: Many, compressed .

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation:  Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant grows in wide variety of soil but it prefers rich clay-loam soil

Nutrient requirements:  Farmyard manure mixed with basal dose of fertilizers is beneficial.

Water management: Irrigation is given after every 10-15 days, if there is no harvest till the final rain.                                      

Harvesting period / Method: Plant is harvested first when it completes its vegetative growth after 4 months, two more harvest is taken after gap of two months.

Potensiometric titrn. was used for the detection of total alkaloids in the leaves of D. metal after extension of the alkaloids with 10% H2SO4. The method gave good results with less sample (~ 1.5g) than required for conventional percolation processes. The total alkaloid content reached peak levels during the hot season in Ibadan (Nov - Mar), and reached min. levels during the rainy season. Chromatog. examns. Of the extracts. Showed that hyoscine occurred in greater proportion than hyoscyamine1; Scopolamine and atropine contents in the whole plants of D. metal increased gradually with the progress of developmental growth, and were most pronounced when the plant was at the end of its reproductive stage. The highest percentage of scopolamine accumulation in the root was after 16 wk. The root was the organ, which often accumulated higher amts. of atropine.
