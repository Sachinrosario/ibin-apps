HABIT & HABITAT
 Almost ubiquitous, it is found throughout India up to an elevation of 6,000 ft. It is variable, and is often confused with C. esculentus, C. bulbosus, C. scariosus and Scirpus grossus var. kysoor C. B. Clarke. In India occuring from Kuram valley, Afghanistan, Gilhat and Kashmir to Simla, GArhwal, and the Khasia hills throughout the plains.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
  A pestiferous perennial weed with dark green glabrous culms, 0-5-2 ft. high, arising from system of underground tubers. The plant has an elaborate underground system consisting of tubers, rhizomes and roots. The tubers are white and succulent when young, an hard and black when mature. It thrives on kinds of soils under varying climatic condition once established the plant spreads rapidly.The plant has an elaborate underground system consisting of tubers, rhizomes and roots.  Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used:
Tubers, Roots.

Uses:

  1. Roots are vermifuge. In native practice, they are held in great esteem as a cure for disorders of stomach and irritation of the bowels. 
  2. The roots are scraped and pounded with green ginger and mixed with honey they are given in cases of dysentery.
  3. Fresh tubers are used as galactagogue in Concan.
  4. Used as fodder.

Chemistry
The lower boiling fraction of the oil contains mainly l-alpha-pinene with traces of cineol. Th higher boiling hydrocarbon fraction contains a mixture of tri and dicyclic sesquiterpenes, the latter predominating. Two dicyclic terpenes belonging to the eudulene group of sesquiterpenes are present. A mixture of two dicyclic alcohols forming about 10% of the oil has been isolated. A new tertiary sesquiterpene compounds has been isolated.

Other Uses

  1. The tubers of the plant have an aromatic odour. 
  2. The dry tubers are sold in bazaars and used in medicine, perfumery and for the preparation of fragrant sticks called Agarbatties. 
  3. The tubers are said to be diaphoretic and astringent, and in indigenous medicine they are given for disorders of the stomach and irritation of the bowels.
