Taxonomic Details
Correct Name :  Mangifera sylvatica Roxb.
Family : Anacardaceae
Synonyms : Mangifera indica, Wall
Common Names :
Sanskrit: Kosamra
Hindi: Kosham
Bengali: Kosham
Assam: Bun am.

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
Large evergreen tree met with in Tropical Nepal and the Sikkim Himalaya, in Sylhet and the Khasia mountains. Also found in the Andaman Islands and rarely in the tropical forests of the Martaban hills and also in Open forest.
BioticZone: Himalaya and Tropics.
Alitude Minimum: 0, 1200,
Altitude Maximum: 0, 1400,
Availability : 
Misc: Near Burma nala, Namdapha, Rima Village and Namdapha.
Country: India and Nepal.
Zone: East, Islands and North East.
State: Andaman & Nicobar, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya and Sikkim.
District: Andaman, Changlang, Kameng, Khasi hills, Nicobar, Siang and Subansiri.
Status :
