Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Naturalized throughout India up to an altitude of 1,500m.

A genus of annual, biennial or rarely perennial prickly herbs, native to America. Some species are cultivated for their showy flowers. Three species are recorded from India.



Availability :

Plenty 

Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/Cultivated

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
An erect, prickly annual herb, up to 1.2m in height,  Leaves sessile, semi-amplexicaule, sinuately pinnatifid, spiny on on margins, mid-rid and veins beneath; flowers prickly, rarely unarmed; seeds small, round, blackishbrown, deeply reticulate-scrobiculate.

Other uses:

These hormonal herbicides can be sprayed on cereals and millet crops infested with the seed, but not on broad-leaved crops. The dried and powdered weed can be applied as green manure in alkaline soils, as it has s\high content of nitrogen, calcium, phosphorus and potassium, and also for reclamation of these soils.

Miscellaneous Information

In America, the latex of the plant was used for the treatment of syphilis and skin diseases. The oil, when solvent-extracted or when heated up to 250° for 15 min, loses its toxicity, whereas the screwpressed oil is toxic. 

In small amounts, it has been used as a remedy for dysentery and other intestinal affections. It has been employed as aperient in the West Indies. It is considered a remedy for skin diseases.
