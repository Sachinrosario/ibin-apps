HABIT & HABITAT:
Perennial, glabrous, branched herbs; rootstock stout, thick, completely covered with bases of radical leaves. It is found to occur in Kashmir, N.W. Himalaya alt.1400-3300 m, Himachal Pradesh, Simla.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Branches decumbent or ascending, arising from the caudex or root stock, quadrangular, purplish, 6.8-32.5 cm long, 2-3 mm in diam. at base. Leaves: radical rosulate, narrowly oblong-lanceolate or elliptic-lanceolate, spathulate, 3.0-13.5 x 0.3-1.5 cm, subacute-acute at apex, margins crenulate; coriaceous, purplish on dorsal surface; nerves 1-3, midrib prominent on dorsal surface; cauline linear-oblong, spathulate, 1.0-3.8 x 0.2-0.3 cm, acute at apex, base connate into 5-9  mm long tubes, margins revolute, crenulate; nerves solitary. Flowers blue with white dots, terminal, solitary-subsolitary, often 3-clustered, pedicellate, 5-merous, 2.4-5.5 cm long, 1.0-3.2 cm wide at throat; pedicels 4-lineolate, 0.8-3.0 cm long. Calyx tubular, campanulate; tube 0.7-1.7 cm long; intracalycular membrane distinct; lobes linear-subulate, 0.7-2.0 x 0.2-0.3 cm, acute, margins thin, crenulate; sinuses truncate. Corolla campanulate; tube 1.6-4.7 cm long; lobes ovate, 0.6-1.3 x 0.6-1.0 cm, acute; folds triangular, acute often bifid. Stamens 5; filaments subulate, winged towards base, 1.0-1.5 cm long; anthers oblong, obtuse, yellow. Ovary fusiform, subsessile-stalked, 1.5-2.5 x 0.4-0.5 cm; style attenuate, 2-3 mm long; stigma 2-lobed, papillose. Capsule elliptic-obovoid, stalked, 1.5-3.0 x 0.5-0.7 cm. Seeds numerous, oblong, trigonous; testa close, reticulate.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Solitary; Flower: Deep blue, paler in throat, spotted with green and white ; Calyx: Tubular, 5-lobed; Corolla: Narrow funnel shaped, with ovate acute, spreading lobes and small triangular lobes ;Androecium: Stamens attached to corolla tube and equal in number to corolla lobes; Gynoecium: Ovary superior of 2 fused carpels, one chambered with many ovules, style simple and stigma 2-lobed ; Fruit: Capsule;

Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : August - October. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
In Unani system of medicine plants are used as blood tonic andemmenagogue. The rhizomes are bitter, acrid and also used as therogenic, vulnerary, digestive, carminative, sialagogue, anthelmintic, laxative, anti-inflammatory, depurative, emmenagogue, galato-purifier, diuretic, sudorific, antileprotic and febrifuge. It is also useful in vitiated conditions of “kapha” and “Pita”, wounds, ulcers, dyspepsia, flatulence, colic, anorexia, helminthiasis, constipation, inflamations, skin diseases, leucoderma, amenorrhoea, dysmenorrhoea, haemorrhoids, leprosy, cardiac debility and fever.


Chemistry:
Catalpol, its 6’-cinnamoyl, 6-O-cinnamoyl, 6-O-vanilloyl and 6-O-feruloyl derivatives and aucubin isolated from roots and rhizomes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).
