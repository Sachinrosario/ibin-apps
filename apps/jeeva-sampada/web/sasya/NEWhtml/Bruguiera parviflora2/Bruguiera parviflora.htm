 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT :
It is a small tree , attaining a height of 70' found in the tidal forests of Burma and the Andamans and also in Littoral Forests and Salt marshes.
It's biotic zone is coastal plains.
Misc: Karwar, Malacca, Sunderbunds,
Country: India, Malayasia.
Zone: East, Islands and South,
State: Andaman & Nicobar, Karnataka, West Bengal,
District: Andaman and Uttar Kannada,
