DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual herbs. Culms 30-55 cm tall, erect to geniculate. Leaves linear, tapering to a slender point, rough at margins; sheaths keeled, glabrous; ligules ciliate. Inflorescence a dense, cylindrical spike like panicle, yellow at maturity; axis densely pubescent; bristles 5-20 in a clusters, 6-9 mm long.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Spikelets broadly elliptic, c 2.5 mm long. Lower glume c 1.2 mm long, 3-nerved; upper glume c 1.5 mm long, 3-nerved. Lower floret male or barren. Upper floret cymiform. Palea granular-punctate. Caryopsis rounded, ellipsoid, c 2 mm long.
