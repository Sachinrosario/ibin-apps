DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herbs, 1-2 m tall, tufted. Culms solid, 10 mm in diam. at base, pale polished, nodes glabresent. Leaves lanceolate, acuminate, glaucous; ligules scarious, 2.0-2.5 mm long, glabrous or ciliate. Panicles pyramidal, often congested, drooping. Racemes 1.5-1.7 cm long. Sessile spikelets 4.0-4.5 mm long.

UTILIZATION:
Chemistry: Geraniol and citronellal obtained from oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).
Detection of isocarpic, isovaleric, butyric and propionic acids, d-citronellal, citral, isovaleraldehyde, pelargonaldehyde, geraniol and citronellol in essential oil by GLC. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).
Essential oil contained citronellal (32.0%), citronellol (14.4) and geraniol (20.1%). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).
