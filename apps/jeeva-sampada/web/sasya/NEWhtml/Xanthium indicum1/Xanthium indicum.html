HABIT & HABITAT
 Erect herb, coarse, 1.0-1.5 m tall. It is found to occur throughout India in plains. A common weed of waste places, agricultural fields, roadsides etc.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves alternate, broadly ovate to cordate, 2.5-11.5 x 1.5-8.5 cm, acute or acuminate at apex, rounded or cordate at base, irregularly serrate along margin, 3-5 lobed, hispid on both surfaces; petioles 2-9 cm long. Male heads globose, 5-7 mm across. Involucral bracts few, 1-2-seriate, narrow, 2-3 mm long, pubescent. Florets numerous, crowded, each floret enclosed in a translucent scale; corolla whitish-green, tubular, 5-toothed. Female heads ovoid, 10-15 mm long; outer involucral bracts few, short; inner many, narrow, united. Florets 2; corolla absent. Achenes grayish black, narrowly ovoid or elliptic, 2.0-3.5 x 1.0-1.5 mm, smooth, tapering to a fine point at apex.
