DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Evergreen, trees or shrubs up to 7.5 m tall, bark corky and lenticillate. Leaves oblong-lanceolate,10-25 x 5-10 cm, narrowed at base, lower surface greyish, tomentose.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Flowers in  8-12 umbellate heads, yellowish, 5 mm in diam., surrounded by bracts. Fruits globose, 7.5 m in diam., black.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
The root is used in biliousness, burning sensations, bronchitis, consumption, fever, and leprosy (Ayurveda). Roots are also used in inflammations, over heated brain, pains in the joints, thirst, throat troubles, spleen diseasess, paralysis etc (Unani) (Kirtikar & Basu, 1933).


Chemistry:
Absolute configuration and crystal structure of (9s)sebiferine iodomethylalte (fig. 1) by X-ray analysis. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

A water-soluble mucilage containing arabinose and xylose in molar ratio of 3:4:1.0 isolated from bark and partially characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).


Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for bruises, sprain (Bark, Gaur et al, 1980).
