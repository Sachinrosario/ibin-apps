Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

A moderate sized to large deciduous tree.

Conservation/ Threat Status : 

Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

Availability: Fair supplies from Mumbai. Exploitable trees in Panch mahal and North Thana and in West Khandesh division few scattered trees. Fair quantities in small sizes available from Uttar Pradesh.

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A much-branched, variable shrub; young parts fusco-pubescent. Leaves thinly coriaceous, oblong-elliptic or oblong-lanceolate, obtuse or acute, glabrous above, glabrous or pubescent beneath, base usually rounded, a little oblique, sometimes subcordate; petioles short, pubescent. Flowers axillary, solitary; pedicels slender, bracteate at the base. Sepals small, ovate, pubescent outside, reflexed. Petals purple; the exterior sepaloid, broadly ovate, about twice as long as the sepals, pubescent; the interior ovate, acuminate, thrice as long as the exterior petals. Stamens numerous, intermixed with stiff hairs, connective slightly produced, rounded. Ovaries densely pilose, oblong, 1-2 ovuled. Ripe carpels numerous, ovoid or obovoid, subsessile, silky-pubescent.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used: Bark, Fruits, and Flowers.

Fuel: Wood good fuelwood. 

Wood mostly used for building huts and cattle sheds. Suitable for cabinet work, carving, slack cooperage, bobbins, picker arms, shuttles and billiard cues. Can be used as substitute for imported birch and beech for other purposes also.

Produce and Products:

Karee gum.

Chemistry:

Calorific values : sapwood 5,578 cal., 10,040 Btu.

Processing Techniques:

Moderately refractory timber, liable to some damage by surface cracking, splitting and warping. Green conversion promptly after felling, and stacking the sawn material in a well-ventilated shed recommended.

Other uses:

Fruit said to be eaten in some parts of Bombay.

The leaves often lopped for fodder.

Tree yields a yellow gum, known as karee gum, used as a substitute for tragacanth.

Miscellaneous Information


 Wood: Without distinct heartwood. Yellow to olive-brown, smooth, hard, moderately heavy to heavy. Average unit weight at 12 per cent moisture content 735 kg/m3. Specific gravity 0.60 – 0.84 air dry. Straight and close grained, medium textured. Presents well-marked silver grain in radial section.
