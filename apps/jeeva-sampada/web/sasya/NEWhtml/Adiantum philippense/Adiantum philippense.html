REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Stipes tufted, wiry, spreading, dark chestnut-brown; fronds simply pinnate often elongate and rooting at the tips, pinna sub-dimitate; sori in contiguous line along the edge.
