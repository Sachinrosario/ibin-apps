Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A very variable, diffusely branched, sub-erect or prostrate under shrub or herb, up to 60 cm. high, often with a woody rootstock, distributed in the moist areas throughout the hotter parts of India, ascending up to c. 2,100 m. in the Himalayas.
This can be seen on the Bunds Of Rice fields, Cultivated fields, Cultivated fields and Plains, degraded areas, Grass hills, hills, in fields, Lower Bisle Ghat, Marshy Areas, Moist areas, Moist ground, Moist localities, moist places, Moist situations, Open areas, open grasslands, Open localities in rice fields, Roadsides & shallow ditches, Plains, Ponds, River banks, River beds, Road sides, Sandy places, Sandy river beds, waste land, Weed and Weed of dry paddy fields.
