Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

A small to moderate-sized, short-lived, ornamental tree suitable for growing around water. Found Temperate Himalayan region and subtropical regions like East Indian hills and parts of Western Ghats.


Availability :


Conservation/ Threat Status : Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

 

Back to Top

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A small to moderate-sized, short-lived, ornamental tree. Leaves linear-lanceolate.

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers appearing with the leaves, in short, terminal catkins of leafy peduncles. Capsules narrowly conic, sessile, glabrous.

Breeding system and varietal improvement:

Phenology:Flowers

Pollinators:

Floral and seed biology:

Seedling Germination: Grown by branch cuttings.


MAJOR PEST AND DISEASES:

Valsa salicina Fr. – stem canker and die-back of twigs

Melampsora salicis-albae Kleb. – leaf rust

Witches’ broom - no pathogen is associated with the disease

UTILIZATION:

Produce and Products:

Activated charcoal of good quality can be obtained by treating the sawdust from the wood with a mixture of zinc chloride and hydrochloric acid as activators. Yield of activated charcoal 4.18% on the weight of undried sawdust.
