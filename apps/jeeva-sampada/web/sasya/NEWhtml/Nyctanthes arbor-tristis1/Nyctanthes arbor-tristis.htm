Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A hardy large shrub or small tree upto 10 m in height, distributed throughout India upto 1,500 m, also cultivated in gardens.
Availability : Throughout India.


Conservation/ Threat Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small trees, 2-5 m tall; branches quadrangular, scabrid with stiff whitish hairs. Leaves opposite, ovate, 5-11 x 3-7.5 cm, rounded or cuneate at base, entire or with a few distant teeth in the lower half, acute or acuminate, rough and scabrous above with short bulbous based hairs, densely pubescent beneath with appressed hairs; petioles 5-6 mm long, hairy. Flowers fragrant at night, sessile in pedunculate, axillary and terminal paniculate cymes. Bracts broadly ovate or suborbicular, 6-10 mm long, apiculate, hairy on both surfaces. Calyx narrowly campanulate, 6-8 mm long, hairy outside, truncate or obscurely toothed; teeth ciliate. Corolla white with orange-red, 6-10 mm long tube; lobes white, unequally obcordate, cuneate, as long as the tube. Anthers 2, subsessile. Fruits obcordate or orbicular, 1.5-2 cm across, compressed, 2-locular, glabrous.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
ORNAMETAL TREE

Parts Used:

Uses : All parts of the plant are used in medicine. Orange-yellow colour is prepared from corolla tubes. Leaves are very rough and are used as sand-paper for polishing wood.
