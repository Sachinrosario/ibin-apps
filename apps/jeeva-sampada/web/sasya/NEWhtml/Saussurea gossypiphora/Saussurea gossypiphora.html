HABIT & HABITAT
Small, erect herb. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Sikkim. It grows on open places, amidst boulders at 3500-5700 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
  Stem 1-28 cm high, densely covered with long white or grey wooly hairs, simple, clavate, hollow, leafy. Leaves sessile, linear, 2-15 x 0.5-1.0 cm, remotely toothed or runcinate-pinntifid, embedded in very dense wooly hairs. Heads cylindric, few or many, 1-2 cm long, deeply embedded in wooly hairs. Involucral bracts linear-oblong, short, erect, pubescent or wooly. Receptacles pitted; bristles equaling achenes. Corolla purple, 0.7-1.2 cm long. Achenes narrowly obovoid, c 4 mm long. Pappus hairs few, many, scabrid or absent

Chemistry:
Umbelliferone, its 7-O-glucoside, scopoletin, b-sitosterol, apigenin and its 7-O-glucoside isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.
Quercetin, chrysoeriol-7-O-b-D-glucoside, apigenin, its 7-O-glucoside and 7-O-neohesperidoside isolated from aerial parts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.
Isolation and structure elucidation of ergostan-3,24-diol and 2-hydroxylappaol B from aerial parts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)c.
In addition, tritriacontane, 1-tetracontene, dotriacontanoic acid , b-sitosterol, stigmast-7-en-3-ol, arctigenin, quercetin, arctiin, chrysoeriol-7-O-b-D-glucoside, apigenin, its 7-O-b-D-glucoside and 7-O-b-D-neohesperidoside also isolated from aerial parts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)d.
Plant afforded b-sitosterol, 3-stigmasterol, stigmast-7-en-3-ol and ergostan-3,24-diol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)e.
Essential oil from whole plant contained ethyl linoleate (20.0), ethyl palmitate (11.0) and methyl 8-cyclopropyl-2-octyloctanoate (8.0%). (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)f.
Heptacosane, hentriacontane, nonacosane, a- and b-amyrins and their acetates and palmitates, lupeol, its acetate, fructose, glucose and sucrose isolated from aerial parts of plant collected from Himalayas. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)g.
