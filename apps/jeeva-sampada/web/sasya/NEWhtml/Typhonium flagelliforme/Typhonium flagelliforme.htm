DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Tuberous herb; tuber 0.9-4 x 0.5-2.0 cm, sub-globose. Leaves 3-6; 3-12.5 x 2.5-7.5 cm, sagittate; oblong lanceolate; basal lobes short. Petiole 13-40 x 0.2-0.5 cm. peduncle 3-10.5 x 0.3-0.5 cm. Spathe 7-20.5 x 1.3-2.2 cm. Spadix 7-22.5 cm long, appendage 6-16 cm long, pointed, smooth, yellowish and straight. Tube 2-3.8 x 1.2-2 cm, decurved, lanceolate, produced into a slender tail; curved downwardly towards peduncle. Male inflorescence 0.3-0.6 x 0.2-0.4 cm, cylindrical; anthers subsessile, 2-4 celled; yellow with white overtopped connective, dehisce by lateral slit. Female inflorescence shortly cylindrical 0.3-0.7 x 0.3-0.5 cm. Ovaries green, one celled with basal placentation. Style absent, stigma bluntly pointed. Neuters above the female inflorescence clavate, purple headed with bifid tip, the upper neuters below the male inflorescence are subulate pointed downwardly 0.2 cm long; white smaller than lower ones. Fruit one seeded, 0.4-0.6 x 0.25-0.35 cm. seeds globose.
