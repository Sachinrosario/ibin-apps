DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A tall tree; trunk prickly when young; branches horizontal, in whorls of 3. Leaves glabrous; petioles 10-15 cm long; leaflets 5-8, lanceolate, 5-12.5 cm long; stipules filiform, caducous. Pedicels 2.5-5 cm long, in clusters of 2-8. Calyx 1.2-2 cm long, glabrous outside, hairy at the base within, 5-lobed; lobes rounded or triangular, sometimes almost obsolete. Flowers 3.8-5 cm diam., dirty white, appearing with young leavers at the ends of the branches; petals twice the length of the calyx, woolly outside. Ovary conical, glabrous. Capsules fusiform, blunt, lined with long white silky hairs. Seeds 6 mm long or more, pyriform, glabrous, black, enveloped in silky wool.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
ORNAMETAL TREE

Parts Used:

The gum used to cure diseases of the liver, spleen, blood; removes tumours, fat, pain; alexitermic (Ayurveda).
The roots and gum are tonic and fattening; antipyritic, diuretic, aphrodisiac; useful in gonorrhoea and painful micturition; good for biliousness and blood complaints. The leaves cure boils and are useful in leprosy (Yunani).
The juice obtained from the roots is considered a most valuable cure for diabetes.
The unripe fruits are regarded as demulcent and astringent.
The root is recommended as an antidote for scorpion venom.
