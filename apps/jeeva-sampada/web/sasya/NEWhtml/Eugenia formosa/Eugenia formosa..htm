Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
Tree, met with near streams on the Eastern Himalaya and in Burma. Also occurring in eastern Himalayas and Assam states of India, yields edible fruits.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A moderate sized tree with very large leaves. There are two forms, one with white and the other with red flowers. The wood is heavy, uniformly brown, close grained, takes a fine polish.
