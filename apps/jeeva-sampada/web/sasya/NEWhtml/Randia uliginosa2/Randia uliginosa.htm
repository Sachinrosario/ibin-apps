Chemistry:  

Mannitol and leucoanthocyanidin from fruits; hydrolysis of fruit extract yielded oleanolic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).
Three saponins isolated from fruits and characterized as olen-3-O-b-D-galactopyranosyl-(3®1)-a-L-arabinopyranosyl-12-en-28-oic acid methyl ester, olean-3-O-b-D-galactopyranosyl-[(6®1)-b-D-galactopyranosyl])(3®1)-a-L-arabnopyranosyl-12-en-28-oic acid methyl ester and olean-3-O-b-D-glucuronopyranosyl[(4®1)-b-D-galactopyranosyl](3®1)-a-L-arabinopyranosyl-12-en-28-oic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).
Hymexelsin and scopoletin isolated from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Pharmacology: Hymexelsin showed insect antifeedant activity towards Spodaptera litura 4th instar. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.


Ethnobotany: Medicine for boils (Fruit, Tribedi et al, 1982); Cholera, diarrohoea (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Cough (Fruit, Srivastava and Varma, 1981); Dysentery (Fruit, Srivastava and Varma, 1981; Maheshwari et al, 1980; Maheshwari et al, 1981; Root, Maheshwari et al, 1986; Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); eye complaints (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); On headache (Root, Saxena, 1986); Pimples, sores (Part not mentioned, Jain and Tarafder, 1970); Edible (Fruit, Maheshwari et al, 1981); Vegetable (Fruit, Jain and De, 1966; Maji and Sikdar, 1982; Arora, 1981; Goel et al, 1984; Bedi, 1978; Srivastava and Varma, 1981)
