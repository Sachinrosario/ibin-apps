HABIT & HABITAT
 Twining or climbing herb with white latex, it is found in Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Twining or climbing herb with white latex; stems glabrous, irregularly warty. Leaves ovate to orbicular, 4-10 x 4-8 cm, cordate at base, acuminate at apex, entire, glabrous. Flowers axillary, solitary or 2-5 on short peduncles. Bracts caducous. Corolla lavender to purplish, infundibuliform, hairy within. Capsules ovoid, 1.5-2.0 cm, glabrous. Seeds ovoid, brown to black, glabrous.

Chemistry:  

Seeds commonly used as purgative in Pakistan, contained lysergol and chancolavine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.
A glycoside - muricatin, composed of 11-hydroxy-hexadecanoic acid and 4-O-L-rhamnopyranosyl-L-rhamnopyranose - from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.
Total alkaoids in seeds estimated (0.0192%); ergoline and clavline bases found to be present. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
New alkalois - ipomine (fig. 1) isolated from seeds and its structure proposed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
Structure of ipomine revised to 1b-ipalbidinyl-(6'-O-p-coumaroyl)-b-D-glucopyranoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.
Revised structure of muricatin (fig. 2), oleic, linoleic acids, lysine, histidine, threonine, valine, leucine and isoleucine detected by GLC in seed oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.
Synthesis of julandine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).
Isolation of five glycosides - Mb1 (fig. 4h), Mb-2, Mb-3, Mb-4 and Mb-5 - from seeds and characterization of Mb-1. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
Four new laxative glycosides (I, II, III and IV, Fig. 5a-5d) isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
Alkaline hydrolysis of glycoside fraction from seeds, containing murcatins A and B. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.
Gave muricatic acids A, B and C together with isobutryic, 2(S)methylbutryic and 3-hydroxy-2-methylbutyric acids; structure elucidation of muricatic acids A and B. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.
Muricatins I-VI (fig. 4a-4f) isolated from seeds and characterized as monomers of a jalapinolic acid tetraglycoside. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)e.
Isolation and structure elucidation of muricatin VII (fig. 4g) and muricatin VIII (fig. 6) from seeds; muricatic acid C identified as glycosidic acid of mucatin VIII. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)f.

Pharmacology:  

Muricatin is a cardiac depressant, spasmolytic to smooth musculature of gut and lowers pressure of anaesthetized dog. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.
