Chemistry:
The bark of Albizzia procera, contained b- sitosterol 136-137.5° in addition to  triterpenes. (Varshney et al., 1965). Proceric acid characterized as 3b,21 b-di hydroxy -18b-olean-12-ene 28-oic acid.( fig. 2)

The seed of Albizzia procera the barks Albizzia procera the extraction of powdered plant material in the usual manner as applied in the extraction of saponins and sapogenins, b-sitosterol, m.p. 136-37.5°, the produc, m.p. 136-37° b-sitosterol. The identity was further confirmed by infra-red spectra in some cases. The bark of A. procera show the presence pf a very small quantity of saponin but not the free genin. (Vashney et al., 1965).

Four triterpenoid sapogenins in addition to machaerinic and proceric acids. (Roy and Roy, 1967).

Degraded gum from A. procera has been found to contain D-galactose, D-mannose, D-glucuronic acid and 4-O-methyl-D-glucuronic acid. Complete methylation and subsequent hydrolysis of the product afford 2,4-di-O-methyl-D-galactose (3 moles), 3,4,6-tri-O-methyl-D-mannose (1 mole) 2,3,6-tri-O-methyl-D-galactose (2 mole) and traces of 2,3,5-tri-O-methyl-L-arabinose. On the basis of these observations a tentative structure has been suggested for the degraded gum. (Farooqi, 1970).

The seeds of Albizzia procera Benth. yield a new triterpenic saponin named as proceranin-A, m.p. 180-82°, which on acid hydrolysis gives glucose, galactose, arabinose, xylose, fucose and rhamnose in the molar ratio of 4:1:1:1:1:2 and proceric acid as the genin. On the basis methylation and acid, alkaline and enzymatic hydrolyses, a partial structure (I) has been assigned to proceranin-A (Fig. 3). (Varshney and Badhwar 1972). C.A.1972,77,45522c.,Varshney et al., 1973.

The stem bark of A. procera was extracted with methanol and the methanol extract was then successively extracted with hexane, benzene, ethyl acetate and acetone and benzene, extract had four known isoflavones: 5,7-dihydroxy-4’-methoxyisoflavone (biochanin-A); 7-hydroxy-4’ methoxyisoflavone (formononetin); 5,7-4’-trihydroxyiisoflavone (formononetin); 4,7-4’-trihydroxyisoflavone (genistein) and 7,4’- dihydroxyisoflavone (diadzein) have been isolated and characterized. The total methanol extract of the heartwood was further fractionated into benzene, chloroform, ethyl acetate and methanol solubles. The benzene extract contained mostly the four isoflavones isolated from the bark. From the chloroform and ethyl acetate extracts besides the four above isoflavones, a new compound (compound-C was isolated. Compounds-C is 3,9-dihydroxypterocarpin (Fig. 1). (Deshpande and Shastri, 1977).

a-spinasterol, oleanolic acid and an unidentified compound from roots; a-spinasterol, hentriacontane and hexacosanol from leaves.(Banerji et al., 1979).

Kernels, constituting 45.5% seeds of A. procera yielded 16.8% oil with acid value 13.66, saponification value 178.28, iodine value 102.5 and unsaponifiable matter 4.11%, detection of linoleic (44.45), palmitic (25.4). oleic (20.18), stearic (4.93), arachidic (2.8) and behenic (2.24%) acids in seed oil by GC. (Jain et al.,1989). C.A. 1989, 111, 150604z.

The enzymes of a-D-mannosidase, b-D-glucosidase, b-D-fucosidase, a-D-galactosidase, b-D-galactosidase and b-D-N-acetyl-glucosaminidase were found in significant amount (7.01mmole/min/9 seed). (Surarit et al, 1995). C.A. 1995,123, 226982s.

Reversal of hydrolytic action was tested by prolonged incubation with 50% (wt/wt) monosacharide at pH=5, at elevated temperature, significant levels of oligosaccharide synsthesis was observed with a-D-mannosidase from A. procera Benth.   (Surarit et al., 1995). C.A. 1995, 124, 224585m.

The enzymes a-D-galactosidase, b-D-glucosidase, b-D-fucosidase, a-D-galactoside, b-D-galactosidase and b-D-N-acetyl-glucosaminidase were found in significant amounts (70.0 mmole/min/ 9 seed). (Surarit et al., 1995). C.A. 1995, 124, 224585m.

Four new oleanane-type triterpene glycosides, precarosides A-D(1-4), were isolated from the seeds of Albizia procera. Their structures were established by extensive NMR experiments and chemical methods. Compounds 1-3 comprised of acetic acid as the aglycon and the monoterpenic carboxylic acid linked to C-28 in 1-3 was determined as alpha-1-arabino-furanosyl-(1à4)-[beta-D-glucopyranosyl-(1à3)]-alpha-L-rhamnopyranosyl-1-(1à2)-beta-D-glucopyranosyl ester. These compounds differed in the C-3-linked sugarunit or in the configuration of the C-6` of the inner monoterpene moiety in the C-21- linked acyl unit. Compound 4 was established as the 16-deoxy analogue of the 1 (Yoshikawa et al, 1998).

Four new oleanane-type triterpene glycosides, proceraosides A-D (1-4), were isolated from the seeds of Albizia procera. Their structures were established by extensive NMR experiments and chemical methods. Compounds 1-3 comprised acacic acid as the aglycon and a monoterpenic carboxylic acid linked to a monoterpene quinovoside as the acyl moiety at C-21. The common oligosaccharide moiety linked to C-28 in 1-3 was determined as alpha-l-arabino- furanosyl-(1à4)-[beta-D-glucopyranosyl-(1à3)]-alpha-L-rhamnopyranosy l- (1à2)-beta-D-glucopyranosyl ester. These compounds differed in the C-3-linked sugar unit or in the configuration of C-6' of the inner monoterpene moiety in the C-21-linked acyl unit. Compound 4 was established as the 16-deoxy analogue of 1. (Yoshikawa et al., 1998).

A new flavanone glycoside 5-hydroxy-7,8-dimethoxyflavanone-5-O-a-L-rhamnopyranoside was isolated from the stem of Albizzia procera Benth. Its structure was characterized by means of Chemical and spectral analysis. (Yadav and Tripathi, 1999). C.A. 2000, 133, 132466f.

A novel flavonol glycoside was isolated from the acetone soluble fraction of ethanolic extract of stem of A. procera Benth which was identified as 5, 2’, 4’-trihydroxy-3, 7, 5’-trimethoxyflavonol-2’-O-b-D-galactopyranosyl-(1®4) –O-b-D-glucopyranoside (I) by various chemical degradations and spectral analysis. (Yadav and Tripathi, 2000). C.A. 2001, 135, 58486k.


Pharmacology:

Spermicidal activity against human sperms exhibited by saponin from the alcoholic extract of roots.(Banerji et al., 1979).

The acetone soluble fractions has found to show moderate and anti-inflammatory action on allrino rats by using non-immunol. Carrageenan induced hind paw oedma method. (Yadav and Tripathi, 2000)  C.A. 2001, 135, 58486k.
