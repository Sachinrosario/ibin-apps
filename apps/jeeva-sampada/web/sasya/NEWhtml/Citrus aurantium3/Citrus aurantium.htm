DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Armed small trees or shrubs; leaves elliptic-ovate or oblong, obtuse or acute at apex, petioles broadly winged; flowers solitary or in axillary clustres, white, fragrant, petals white; fruits globose, orange, pulp sweet.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
Fruits are used in preparation of liquours and other drinks. (Ambasta, 1986)
(a) Fresh juice – served direct from the extractor.
(b) Canned juice – natural pure juice canned immediately after its extracton.
(c) Sweetened juice, squash, etc.
(d) Carbonated bottled juice.
(e) Concentrate, and
(f) Powder.

The Sathgudi orange juice on sweetening and acidification yields a fine squash.

The fruits are graded into three groups (a) fruit having 2.75 inches diameter (b) fruit having 3 inches diameter and (c) fruit having diameter intermediate between (a) and (b) Fruits of the 3 groups are used separately for juice extraction to study the effect of size on the yield of juice. Extraction of juice: The fruits are thoroughly washed in running cold water, cut into halves by means of a Halving Machine, reamed on a Rosing Machine (50-60 minutes for every 100 fruits) and the pulp reamed out screened through a motorised Pulper fitted with 2mm. mesh stainless steel sieve.
