Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

A bushy shrub on saline or water-logged soils or small tree on better soils. It is found in north India in sandy or gravelly areas and low-lying saline soils and on the banks of rivers.

Flourishes in the regions of scanty rainfall and extremes of temperature. It withstands partial submergence for some time. It is a strong light demander and does not tolerate the shade of the other species. It is hardy and withstands ordinary frost. Occurs in western and central India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

A gregarious, bushy shrub or a small tree with slender erect or pendulous branches. Bark brownish, smooth when young, rough when mature. Leaves subulate-acute from a triangular semiamplexicaul base, at first imbricate, afterwards distant.

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:


Flowers hermaphrodite, numerous, crowded, in slender lateral and terminal panicled spike-like racemes. Pedicels short. Bracts very acute, immediately beneath the flowers. Sepals 5, much shorter than the petals, triangular-ovate, margins minutely denticulate. Petals oblong, rounded and often notched at the top, pink. Disk 5-lobed; lobes entire or nearly so. Stamens 5, inserted at the margins of the middle of the lobes of the disk. Ovary bottle-shaped, trigonous, truncate at the top. Styles 3, articulated to the ovary. Capsules conical, somewhat trigonous, tapering, pale pink, glabrous. Seeds with a plume of white hairs.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Wood source of firewood.

Wood: Wood is whitish and occasionally with a red tinge, open- and coarse-grained, fairly hard and tough, but not strong. It is used for making agricultural implements, and for turnery and lacquer work.

Other uses:

Twigs - Smaller twigs employed for thatching and basket-making.

Galls - Contain tannin. Favourable for production of tannin extracts for use in chrome-tanning. The chrome-hide, tanned with the extract attains a pleasant yellow olour.
