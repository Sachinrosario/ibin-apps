Chemistry:

·        b-sitosterol, oleanolic acid, arjunolic acid (1.5%), mp. 3320 and a new triterpene acid – tomentosic acid – isolated; latter characterized as 2a, 3b, 11b, 24-tetrahydroxyolean-12-en-28-oic acid. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

·        Oleanolic, maslinic and arjunolic acids and b-sitosterol isolated from heartwood. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        Maslinic lactone nd terminolic acid isolated from heartwood and characterized as 2a, 3b-dihydroxyolean-28, 13-lactone and 2a, 3b, 6b, 23-tetrahydroxyolean -12-en-8-oic acid respectively. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

·        A new triterpene acid – 3- acetylmaslinic acid – isolted from root bark and its structure elucidated; oleanolic, arjunic and arjunolic acids and arjunetin also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

·        Isolation of arjunic, arjunolic, betulinic and ellagic acids, and b-sitosterol from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).
