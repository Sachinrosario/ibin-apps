Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 A much-branched climbing shrub. 1-8 m. height, with sharp re-curved, short, compressed spines, commonly found in the Deccan peninsula. Generally it is found in hedges, near sea coast, Open forest, Swampy areas and Waste land.

BioticZone: Carnatic, Deccan and carnatic, Deccan Plateau, North Circars, Peninsular India and Western peninsular India.

Availability : 
Misc: Desert aforestation, Circar hills, Nagavalli and Okhamandal.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves deltoid or tri- angular, irregularly lobed or sinuatc. ovate or elliptic; flowers pretty, purple-blue or violet-purple in extra- axillary racemose cymes; berries globose, red or scarlet: seeds smooth or slightly pitted.

  2. Leaves are cooked and eaten as a vegetable; moisture, 84.7%.
