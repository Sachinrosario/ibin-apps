DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Deciduous trees, 15-25 m high, with large leaf scars. Leaves equally or unequally pinnate, usually 20-40 cm long, rarely up to 90 cm or more, younger tomentose, older glabrous; leaflets 8-14 pairs, alternate or opposite, 4-16 x 3-8 cm, acute to acuminate at apex, unequal at base, coarsely or irregularly toothed; petiolules with two hairy glands near base.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers large, lax in much branched panicles, greenish-yellow; pedicels slender. Calyx lobes ovate-triangular, c 2 mm long. Petals ovate-lanceolate, 3-4 x 1-2 mm, reflexed. Samaras linear, oblong to lanceolate, 4.0-7.5 x 1.0-1.5 cm, acute at both ends, reddish-brown, strongly reticulate, once or twice twisted at base.

Inflorescence: Axillary or terminal panicles; Flower: Small, polygamous, bracteolate, regular; Calyx: Sepals 5-fid, lobes equal, imbricate; Corolla: Petals 5, valvate, ; Androecium: Stamens 10, filaments short, without scales, anthers oblong, usually introrse,  2-celled dehiscing longitudinally; Gynoecium: Ovary free, 2-5 partite, styles connate, ovules one in each cell, stigma capitate; Fruit: 1-5, 1seeded samaras; wing very large, membranous ; Seed: pendulous, spraingly albuminous . Flowering & Fruiting : January – March .Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
