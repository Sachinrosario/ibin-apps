DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Stem long, slender coming off from leaf-axils of a vertical rootstock,  often reddish, internodes long, rooting at nodes. Leaves 1-3 from each node, orbicular, reniform, 1.3-6.3 cm in diam., rather broader than long, more or less cupped, base deeply cordate, entire or shallowly crenate along margin, glabrous on both surfaces; petioles variable in length, 7.5-15 cm long or more, channelled, glabrous or nearly so; stipules short, adnate to petioles forming a sheathing base. Flowers in fascicled umbels, each umbel consisting of 3-4 pink, sessile flowers; peduncles pubescent or glabrous, short, pink; bract ovate, acute. Fruit 4 mm long, ovoid, hard, with thickened pericarp, reticulately-rugose, often crowned by persistent petals, primary and secondary ridges distinct.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
   Inflorescence: Fascicled umbels, peduncle pubscent or glabrous; Flower: Sessile Flowers(rarely pedicilled), bracteate,complete, actinomorphic, hermaphrdite, pentamerous and epigynous; Calyx:  Calyx represented by narrow circular ridge with no teeth ; Corolla: Petals minute, pink, ovate and acute;Androecium: Five distinct stamens alternating with petals, form an epigynous disc, filaments inflexed in the bud, anthers dithecous, introrse and opening lengthwise; Gynoecium: Bicarpellary, syncarpous,with an inferior bilocular ovary having solitary pendulous placentation axile and an epigynous disc is present at the top of ovary, is prolonged into two styles which are thickened at the base; Fruit: Ovoid hard with thickened pericarp, reticulate, rugose, often crowded by persistent petals; Seed: .

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Stem cuttings having rooted nodes with a few leaves and seeds are mode of propagation.

Soil type: Plant is suited to wide variety of soils.

Nutrient requirements: The well decomposed farmyard manure per hectare should be applied to the field at the time of field preparation. In order to get good herb yield nitrogen should be applied in three split doses. A basal dose of P and K should be given at the time of planting.

Water management:  Light irrigation is required immediately after planting. Light irrigation is provided every 8-10 days till the plant are firmly established

Harvesting period / Method:   The outer leaves can be harvested on fortnightly basis after 4 months  onwards from growing branches. The bulk material should be harvested before onset of monsoon.

Trade & Commerce: Retail market price: Rs. 30/-kg.

Uses:
In Ayurveda system of medicine plants are used as laxative, cooling, alexiteric, antipyretic, appetiser and tonic. It also improves memory, voice and useful in lecucoderma, urinary discharge, blood diseases, bronchitis, inflammations, asthma, small pox and fever.

In Unani system of medicine whole plant is used as soporific, sedative to the nerve, tonic, cardio tonic, stomachic, carminative and diuretic. It is also useful in hiccough, asthma, bronchitis, scalding of urine, headache and appetite.

He tested positive for Centella asiatica. (Raton et. al., 1995).
