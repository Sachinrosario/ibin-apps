DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A herb with a creeping root stock.

Inflorescence purple.

Seedling germination :

Sansevieria can be propagated by rhizomes or leaf cuttings in commercial cultivation. Propagation by division of rootstocks gives good results in Philippines while suckers are used in Mexico.

For commercial production they require a warm moist climate and a well drained soil which is preferably calcareous. The cuttings are polar and must be planted with the basal portion in the ground. The plant grow to a height of about 40-700 cm in the first year.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Leaf


Harvesting Practices:
First harvest 18-30 months after planting and subsequent harvesting at intervals of C.24 months.

Produce and Products:
) Leaf fiber - terine, rope, nets, back for mats and coarse fabrics.
2) Pulp for paper of special quality.
3) In indigenous medicine, cosmetics.

fibers are obtained from leaves which are C.60 cm long. fiber is very strong. In general the fiber strands are 45-180 cm long, with 0.04083 mm of mean reducing, creamy white, quite soft and lustrous, have good tensile strength (14.68 kg/mm2). fiber is fiber than sisal fiber. Its properties less midway between the hard fibers such as sisal and the soft fibers such as jute.
