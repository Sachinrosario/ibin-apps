HABIT & HABITAT
 Stout herb, 7-35 cm high, scaberulous. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh. It grows on open slopes, alpine meadows, amidst boulders with Gentiana sp., Primula sp. Etc. 3500-5600 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Basal leaves linear-lanceolate or narrowly elliptic, 6.5-9.5 x 1.0-2.0 cm, acute at apex, coarsely toothed, narrowed into a short petiole; upper leaves half amplexicaul, acuminate, sessile. Floral bracts purplish, cymbiform, 2-5 cm long, large, sessile, subsilky. Heads 1-2 cm across; peduncles short, stout, densely wooly. Involucral bracts blackish, lanceolate, acuminate. Corolla c 1.0 cm long. Anther tails short, wooly. Achenes glabrous. Pappus white; outer bristles, scabrid.
