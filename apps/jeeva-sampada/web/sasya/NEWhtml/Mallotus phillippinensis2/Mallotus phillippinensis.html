DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Evergreen, deciduous, much branched trees. Branchlets young leaves and inflorescence rusty pubescent. Leaves alternate, petiolate, ovate or obovate-oblong or lanceolate, acuminate at apex, rounded at base, entire or sinuate-toothed at margin, glabrous above, hoary beneath with stellate tomentum and red glands. Flowers apetalous. Male flowers clustered in terminal panicles, pale brown or yellowish; sepals 4; stamens 20-30. Female flowers: solitary in simple or panicled spikes or racemes; sepals 4; ovary tomemtose and red glandular; stigmas 3. Capsules of 3, 2-valved cocci, pubescent with red glands. Seeds smooth, black.

NBPGR: A shrub or a small, much-branched, evergreen tree with a short and often buttressed bole, bark thin, grey, somewhat rough; leaves variable, broadly ovate to ovate-oblong or ovate-lanceolate, glabrous above, pubesent with numerous red glands beneath; flowers in spikes, dioecious, small; capsules globose, 3 lobed, 3 valved, 0.75-1.25 cm. diam. Densely covered with reddish brown glandular pubescence; seeds sub globes, black, smooth 4 mm. diam.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
The red powder which covers the seeds is used in medicine and considered to be efficacious in expulsion of tape-worm. Gland and hair of the fruit-anthelmintic, cathartic, styptic, in scabies, ringworm, herpes.


Chemistry:
Known cardenolides – rottleriin,mp. 212°C, and isorottlerin mp, 182°C were isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Corotoxigenin  and its rhamnoside (fig. 3a), coroglaucigenin and its rhamnoside (fig. 3b) were isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

C-methylated cinnamoyl chromene (fig. 1) and a rearranged flavanone –chromene (fig. 2) were isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Betulin – 3-acetate, lupeol, lupeol acetate, sitosetrol and bergenin isolated from heartwood; acetylaleuritolic acid, a-amyrin, sitosterol and bergenin isolated from bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

Rottlerin, and isorottlerin were isolated from leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

Five new flavanoids Mallotus A (fig.5), Mallotus B (fig. 6), 3’-prenylrubranine (fig. 4), 2’,4’-dihydroxy-6’-methoxy-3’-prenylchalcone (fig. 7) and 5,7 –dihydroxy-6-methyl-8-prenyl flavone (fig. 8) were isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Kamaladiol-3-acetate (3b-acetoxy-22b-hydroxy olean-18-ene) along with friedeli were isolated from stem bark. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

Two new chalcone derivatives (Kamalachalcone A and B) with a unique ring system caused by dimerization between a dimethylchromene ring and a phenoxyl group were isolated. (Tanaka et al, 1998). C.A. 1998, 129, 272998y.

NBPGR:  
Heartwood gave beutin – 3 – acetate, lupeol, lupeol acetate, sitosterol and bergenin, back yielded acetyl aleuritolic acid sitosterol, its glucoside, bergenin and α-amyrin; leaves gave bergenin.  Plant also gave phloroglucinol derivatives-rottlerin and a red compound, as main constituents along with small amounts or traces of isoallo – rottlerin, the yellow compound, methylene – bis – methylphloroacetophenone and two more compounds designated as kamalins 1 and 2, other phloroglucinol compounds isolated from kamala includes methylene – bis – methylacetylphloroglucinol and rottleron (possibly an artefact), a red c-methylated cinnamoyl chromen and the other a yellow chromenoflavones.  The last two compounds obtained as minor components could be artefacts also.


Pharmacology:

Rottlerin and isorottlerin showed antifertility effect. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)d.

Aqueous extract of red resinous powder (0.03g) showed lithotropic effect in albino rats, which was comparable to that of cystone (10 mg). Stone formation was induced by insertion of zinc pellets in urinary bladder of adult rats. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

Rottlerin, a 5,7-dihydroxy-2,2-dimethyl-6-(2,4,6-trihydroxy-3-methyl-5-acetylbenzy l)-8-cinnamoyl-1,2-chromene isolated from the pericarps of Mallotus phillippinensis, has been shown to be an effective CaM kinase III inhibitor. Therefore, we evaluated the effects of rottlerin on the growth and viability of glioblastoma cell lines. Rottlerin decreased growth and induced cytotoxicity in rat (C6) and two human gliomas (T98G and U138MG) at concentrations that inhibited the activity of CaM kinase III in vitro and in vivo. (Parmer et al ,1997).
