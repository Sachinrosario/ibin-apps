HABIT & HABITAT
Plants variable in size, 12-45 cm long found to occur in temperate Himalayas from Kashmir to Sikkim at altitude upto 2600 to 3300 m.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Plants variable in size, 12-45 cm long; stem tuberous at  base, sheathed. Leaves oblong, up to 10 cm long, rounded at apex.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Flowers in 25 cm long racemes, 4 mm in diam., yellowish-green; peduncles terete. Sepals lanceolate-oblong, subequal, longer than the linear petals; lips nearly circular, with thick obscure basal lobes and a narrowly lanceolate tip. July - September
