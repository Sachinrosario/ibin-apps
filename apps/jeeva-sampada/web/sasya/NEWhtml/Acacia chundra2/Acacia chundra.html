HABIT & HABITAT

A medium-sized tree, native to peninsular India and Myanmar (Burma), distributed in Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu on dry and rocky soils.

Found in Dry Deciduous forest, Scrub, Woodlands.

Availability :  
The tree is found in India, Sri Lanka, Burma India - Peninsular India Western Ghats - Allover foothills and plains, < 1000m Districts - Nasik, Ratnagiri, Savanthwadi (Mah.); Uttarakannada, Hassan (Karn.); Palghat (Kerala); Tamil Nadu; Goa;

 

Status : Not threatened, locally frequent.

But, moderately threatened in western ghats, so the tree has now been banned from felling without permit.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES

A small tree 6.0-7.5 m in height, with smooth, glabrous branches and pale, purplish brown  branchlets; Bark dark brown, peeling off profusely; Leaves bipinnate with recurved spines; Spines 0.3 cm long on a broad base; petiole gland at the base of first pairs of leaflets; leaflets 30-40 pairs, oblong up to 4 cm long and 1 cm wide. Flowers yellowish white in axillary spikes, Corolla white, thrice as long as calyx. ; pods thin, flat and stalked, 6x1 cm, subacute, 3-4 seeded. The tree is recorded as one of the hosts of  angiospermous parasites - Dendrophthoe falcata , Loranthus spp. and  Viscum spp.

Other Uses
  1. Bark is astringent and is applied to boils and ulcers as an antiseptic. A paste prepared from the bark and leaves is used externally to relieve abscesses.
  2. Wood-beams  used for posts, and making agricultural implements.
