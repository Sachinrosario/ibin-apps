Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Shrub or small tree. Common in moist deciduous and evergreen forests of India.
Distributed in Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Afghanistan, W. Asia and Tropical Africa.

Availability : Almost throughout India.


Conservation/ Threat Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small trees or large shrubs; branches slender, dark grey, sparingly hairy. Leaves distichous, obovate-lanceolate, 3-18 x 1.2-3.5 cm, acuminate at apex, serrate-dentate, chartaceous, glabrescent, base 3-nerved, rounded or subacute, 2-5 cm long. Flowers in axillary umbellate cymes. Peduncles 1.2-2 cm long, 3-flowered. Flowers white; buds ovoid, clavate, ribbed. Sepals linear oblong, 15-18 mm long, petals 3.5 x 1.5 mm, lobed. Filaments short. Gynophore glabrous with a ring of hairs at top. Drupes 1-4-lobed, globose, black or purplish.
