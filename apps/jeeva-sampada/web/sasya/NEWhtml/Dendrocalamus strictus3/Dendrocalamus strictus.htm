Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

In deciduous forests and in dry or moderate dry regions practically all over India upto 3500 ft. occurs sometimes gregariously almost to the exclusion of the growth more usually it is found as an under storey to or a mixture with deciduous trees.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A densely tufted bamboo with strong, elastic, thick walled or solid culms varying in size according to locality.
Culms usually 20-50 ft. high x 1-3 in diameter., somewhat swollen at the nodes and bearing in open situations, leafy, often deflexed branches from the base;
Upper branches curved and droopings;
Internodes 12-18 in. long;
Culm sheaths variable;
Flowering irregular and sporadic, at times gregarious over large areas;
Seeds similar in form to, but a ht. Half the size of, unhusked wheat;
