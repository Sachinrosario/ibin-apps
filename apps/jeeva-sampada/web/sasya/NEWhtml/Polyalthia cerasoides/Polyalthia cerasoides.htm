Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:

Shrub or small tree. Distributed in Bihar and Orissa, and in Assam up to 9900 m., and from Khandesh to Travancore in the west. The plant occurs on dry hills, monsoon and deciduous forests, and sometimes in evergreen forests, but is nowhere common. Covers east Indian regions.

Conservation/ Threat Status : 

Wild/ naturalised/ cultivated

Lower risk - least concern

Availability: Limited supplies from east, west and south zones.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Shrub or small tree with light grey bark, young branches minutely rufous-tomentose, the older glabrous. Leaves membranous, oblong-lanceolate, acute or acuminate, glabrous above, pubescent (afterwards glabrous) beneath, base rounded or subacute; petioles tomentose. Flowers axillary, 1-3 together from short woody tubercles, greenish. Pedicels tomentose, becoming woody in fruit wit 1 or 2 large, ovate, pubescent bracts below the middle. Sepals ovate, acute, with appressed hairs on the outside, glabrous inside, reflexed. Petals fleshy, incurved, oblong-ovate, obtuse or subacute, puberulous. Carpels many (usually more than 20), ovoid, apiculate, stalked.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used: Bark

Uses:

Wood used as fuel. Easy to saw, planes to a fine smooth surface and works well on a high speed lathe, finishing to a bright surface.Wood used for house construction, planks, rafters, packing cases and cots and for boat building. Wood suitable for turnery, joinery work, bobbins and boot lasts. Used for manufacture of plywood, matches. Timber is used for boats and carpentry.


Harvesting Practices:

Timber fairly durable under cover.

 

Produce and Products:

Chemistry:

Calorific value of wood - 4,929 cal., 8,873 Btu.

Processing Techniques: If not seasoned carefully, liable to develop fine, long, deep cracks. Green conversion and slow seasoning, preferably water seasoning, recommended.

Miscellaneous Information


 Wood: Sapwood greyish to brownish yellow, heartwood grey black, not distinct from the sapwood, lustrous, smooth. Moderately hard; moderately heavy. Specific gravity 0.51 – 0.86 air dry. Usually straight grained, medium to fine to very fine textured. Not durable.
