DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual, erect, glabrous, dichotomously branched herb with milky latex. Leaves opposite, sessile, linear-lanceolate, mucronate at apex, entire at margin, glabrous. Involucres on the forkings, 4-lobed; glands 4-5, each with 2 horns. Male flowers: filaments hairy, anther cells globose. Female flowers: ovary glabrous; styles 2-fid. Capsules glabrous. Seeds oblong, black, rugolose, with white caruncle.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Cymes ; Flower: Bracteate, monoecious,  ; Male Flower: a stalked stamen without floral envelop, filaments pubescent ; Female Flower: Ovary 3-celled on ultimately exerted stalk in the center of broadly campanulate , subsessile, glabrous outside, pubescent inside  involucre, ovules solitary in each cell, style 3, free; Fruit: Capsule of 3 two valved  cocci with styles free to the base, shortly 2-fid at the apex ; Seed: Long, ellipsoid, rounded at the base, grooved down one side, with an arillode at the oblique depressed apex. Flowering & Fruiting : August-September  .

Floral and seed biology: Fl. & Fr.: November - March

Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.  
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome numbers - 12, 16, 18, 24, 28.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Fruit is used to remove warts.

PHARMACOGNOSY    

Pharmacognostical characters and botanical identification of Suddab (Euphorbia dracunculoides) have been described. (Afaq et al, 1997). MAPA1998, 9801-0093.


Chemistry:

Euphorbol, mp. 90°C and a flavone glycoside isolated from plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Myricyl alcohol, lupeol, oleanolic, betulinic and stearic acids, quercetin, 7,8-dihydroxy- coumarin and b-sitosterol isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Quercetin-3-O-b-Dglucopyranosyl(1à4)-O-a-L-rhamnoside isolated from leaves and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989).

Nasustin B, esculetin, (-)anabellamide, acacetin and b-sitosterol-b-D-glucoside isolated from whole plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

Isolation and structure elucidation of kaempferol-3-O-glucuronic acid methyl ester and kaempferol-3-O-b-glucosyl(1à4)-b-glucuronic acid methyl ester along with kaempferol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.


Pharmacology:

Plant extract showed two primary activities, cholinergic and direct action on different muscle preparations. Acetylcholine- like action with muscarinic as well as nicotinic activities were observed. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Seeds extract showed antimicrobial activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.


Ethnobotany:
Medicine kills lice (Leaf, Maheshwari et al, 1986); Wart (Fruit, Mishra and Sahu, 1984); Edible (Seed, Bhargava, 1959).
