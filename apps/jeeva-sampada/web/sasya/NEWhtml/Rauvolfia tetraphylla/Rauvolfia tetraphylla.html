DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Floral and seed biology:
Inflorescence Terminal and lateral, few to many flowered; peduncle slender1-4 cm long rarely2-3 branched, glabrous to puberulent, minutely bracteate and pedicles slender, glabrous to puberulent; Flower small ; Calyx deeply 5 lobed. Lobes ovate, acute about 1mm long ,glabrous to puberulent without and glabrous within ; Corolla while the tube slender 2-3 mm long glabrous to puberulent without and rather densely villous within near the throat which is slightly constricted the lobes ovate to sub rotund, rounded about 1 mm long; Androecium stamens 5,included,inserted near the throat, anther ovate, acute about 1 mm long and the filament about 0.5 mm long ; Gynoecium Bicarpellary, syncarpous, subspherical about, 1.5 mm high and 2mm in diameter,1-2 ovules in each locules, placentation axile, disc annular, style slender long, glabrous, stigma   head sub-capitate, obscurely bicapitate ; Fruit follicles spherical to sub-spherical, gradually turning from   green to red and black; Seeds two in each ovule, ovate, slightly curved, albuminous.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
 Plant is pollinated by insects and seeds are dispersed through winds.
 
Genetic variability:
The plant is hexaploid with 2n=66 chromosomes.

GERMPLASM CHARACTERIZATION:

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation : Plant is propagated by seeds or vegetatively.

Soil type: Plant grows best in sandy, sandy loam and loam soils. 

Nutrient requirements: At the time of plantation 25 kg/ha of nitrogen, 22.5 kg/ha of phosphorous and potash is applied.                                                                        

Water management: Plants are benefited by monsoon rains. Harvesting period / Method: Roots are harvested during winter months and care should be taken that root bark remains intact as it contains high alkaloid contents.
