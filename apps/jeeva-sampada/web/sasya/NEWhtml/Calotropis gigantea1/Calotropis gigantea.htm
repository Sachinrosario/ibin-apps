DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Erect shrubs or small trees, up to 5 m tall. Stems woody, nodes slightly swollen, up to 8 mm thick; internodes terete, sometimes squrish, 1.2-8 cm long and 4-10 mm in diam., fugaciously cottony pubescent, grooved in the center. Latex milky. Leaves opposite, decussate, subsessile or shortly petiolate, lamina 2.6-20 x 1.2-10.5 cm, broadly obovate or elliptic-oblong, subacute or obtuse at apex, cordate with auricled base, margin entire, fugaciously pubescent on both sides, thick, lateral nerves 4-7 pairs, petioles 3-8 mm long and 2-4 mm in diam.

Flowers in lateral or axillary umbellate or sub-corymbose cymes, many flowered; peduncles squarish, 1.5-13 cm long and 2-4 mm in diam., fleshy, cottony pubescent; bracts 5-8 x 2-3 mm, ovate or lanceolate, apex acute, cauducous, margin ciliate, pubescent without; pedicels in pairs, terete, 2.5-4 cm long and ca 2.5 mm in diam., greenish-white, fulvous pubescent. Calyx 5-lobed, divided up to base, lobes 4-5 x 2.5-3 mm, broadly ovate, saucer shaped, apex acute, margin ciliate, glandular within, cottony pubescent without, glabrous within. Corolla rotate, lobes five, united up to ca 7 mm, 1.2-2.1 x 0.6-1 cm, ovate apex acute, margin revolute, spreading, glabrous. Corona staminal, five, fleshy, completely adnate to sorter than the staminal column; lobes ca 1.3 cm long and ca 7 mm wide, laterally compressed, margin ciliate, glabrous, with a recurved spur at the base, ca 2.5 mm wide and two obtuse auricles just below the rounded apex. Pollinia five, pollen masses solitary in each anther cell, yellow waxy, 1771.2-1814.4 x 633.6 mm, oblong, attached by light-brown caudicles ca 460.8 mm long, ca 57.6 mm wide to a dark brown corpuscle ca 633.6 mm long and ca 316.8 mm wide, winged. Gynostegium 1.2-2.3 cm long. Follicles paired, horizontal, 3.8-10 x 1.8-4 cm, oblong or boat shaped, with hooked tip, fugaciously cottony pubescent; seeds many, 7-7.5 x 4 mm, oblong, brown coloured, with light-brown margin; coma dull-white, silky, 3-3.5 cm long.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:

Root bark is said to be similar to ipecac in action. The plant is purgative, anthelmintic, cures leprosy, leucoderma, ulcer, tumors, and piles, disease of the spleen, the liver and the abdomen.
