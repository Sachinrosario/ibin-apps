DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herbs, growing in large tufts; rhizome short, obliquely ascending, up to 8 cm long; roots 1.0-3.5 mm thick, dusky brown. Culms robust, 0.7-2.4 m tall, 5-12 mm thick below, sharply triquetrous, scabrous on the angles. Leaves 4-7 to a culm; blade broadly linear, 45-135 x 1.5-3.0 cm, gradually narrowed to acute apex, scabrous lower midrib and margins towards apex, coriaceous, deep green; sheaths 20-60 cm long, subcylindric, straw-coloured.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Anthela compound, 20-30 cm long; bracts 4-6, patent, broadly linear; bracteoles about 3, setaceous. Spikes linear-cylindrical, subsessile, 3-6 x 0.2-0.5 cm, yelloish-brown, rachis hidden by the densely crowded spikelets. Spikelets suberect to erect-patent, linear, 5-8 mm long, subterete, 8-14 flowered; rachilla flattened. Glumes elliptic to obovate-elliptic, 1.8-2.0 x 1.0-1.25 mm, incurved-margined, subacute to mucronate at paex, 3-5 nerved. Achenes elliptic, trigonous, 1.0-1.2 mm long, apiculate at apex, maturing yellowish-brown.
CIMAP:
Inflorescence: Spike and spikelets are borne on branched inflorescence axis, suberect, dirty straw coloured ; Flower:  bracteate, bract dry  known as glumeae distichous, sessile, incomlpete ;  Perianth:  absent ;   Androecium: Stamens 2, filaments long, anthers linear , dithecous, basifixed, introrse ; Gynoecium:   tricarpellary, syncarpous, ovary superior, unilocular, ovule one, stigma 3 and feathery ; Fruit:  Nuts triquetrous, flat, style base contiguous in nut ;
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering and Fruiting: March- June. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. insects. Seeds are  dispersed by wind.
