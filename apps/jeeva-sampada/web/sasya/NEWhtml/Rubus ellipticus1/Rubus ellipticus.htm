Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 A tall, suberect bush, met with in the temporate and subtropical Himalaya, from the Indus to Sikkim and Bhotan, at altitudes between 4,000 and 7,000 ft. It also found on the Khasia hills, Manipur and Burma, also in the Western Peninsula of India, from Kanara Southwards and in the ventral parts of Ceylon.
Habitat: Along roadsides, Along valleys, at higher elevations on hills, Cultivated, Cultivated areas, evergreen forest, forest edges, forest grounds, Forests, Gardens and Roadsides, Hill slopes, Hills, Hilly regions, Open areas, Open downs, Semi evergreen forest, Shola, Shrubberies, Slopes, Stream side and Tea gardens.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
It is a tall erect sub-erect bush with yellow fruits which has flavour of a raspberry. Wood is moderately hard, light brown with very broad medullary rays.
