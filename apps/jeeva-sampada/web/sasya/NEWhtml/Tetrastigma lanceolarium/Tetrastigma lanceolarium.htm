HABIT & HABITAT :
A large, evergreen climber, ascending to 18 m., found from Kumaun to Sikkim and North Bengal, and in eastern and the peninsular India up to an altitude of 3,000 m, evergreen forest, Forests, Hill forests, low altitudes of hills, Moist area, shola, stream, wet evergreen forests.
BioticZone: Konkan plain, Peninsular India, Western Ghats,
Country: India, Malayasia and Sri Lanka.
Zone: Central, East, North East, South and Westzone.
State: Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Goa Daman Diu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
A large, evergreen climber, ascending to 18 m. Leaves 3-5-foliolate, somewhat fleshy : leaflets lanceolate-elliptic, 7.0-19.0 cm. x 2.5-5.0 cm.; flowers yellowish or green, polygamo-dioecious, in umbels; berries 10-13 mm. in diam., globose.
