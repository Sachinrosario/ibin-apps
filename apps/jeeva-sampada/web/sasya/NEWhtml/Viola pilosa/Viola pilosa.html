HABIT & HABITAT
Prostrate to subprostrate herbs found in Moist places around forests and margins of cultivation, in grasslands and alpine meadows, 1500-3500 m. Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.Pakistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, China, Thailand, Indonesia (Sumatra & Indonesia), Malaysia.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Prostrate to subprostrate herb, stem or stolons usually long, leafy. Leaves ovate to deltoid, 1.5-8.0 x 1-6 cm, shallowly cordate at base, serrate at margin; petioles 2-10 cm long, pubescent; stipules ovate, 6-15 mm long, acuminate at apex, subentire to dentate at margin. Peduncles 3-8 cm long, pilose; bracts 2, placed above middle, lanceolate to linear-lanceolate, c 6 mm long, entire.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers white or pale violet. Sepals linear-lanceolate, 4-8 x 1-2 mm, acute, entire or denticulate. Petals obovate-oblong, 1-2 cm long; basal one obovate; lateral ones oblanceolate; spur c 5 mm long, obtusely cylindrical. Capsules ellipsoid, c 5 mm diam., glabrous.
