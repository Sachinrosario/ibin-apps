HABIT & HABITAT
 Annual or biennial, erect, profusely branched, robust herbs, often undershrubs; branches ascending, 4-angular. It is found to occur in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Simla.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Stems 30.5-130.5 cm tall, 0.7-1.2 cm in diam. at abse, subwoody. Leaves: radical often present at flowering, oblong, spathulate, 6.0-8.2 x 2.5-4.0 cm, obtuse at apex; cauline ovate-lanceolate, 3.0-13.5 x 2-5 cm, acuminate at apex, sessile, amplexicaul; nerves 3-7, prominent on both sides. Inflorescence large leafy panicles of solitary-axillary or axillary and terminalclusters of 3-5 flowers; peduncles slender, 4-angular, 0.8-4.5 cm long; bracts foliaceous, sessile, ovate-lanceolate, 1.5-3.6 x 1.0-1.5 cm, acute, 3-nerved. Flowers yellow green outside, purple inside, tetramerous, drooping or erect, 5-8 mm across; pedicels unequal, 4-lineolate, 0.5-2.3 cm long. Calyx shorter than the corolla; lobes linear-lanceolate, 3-6 x 1-2 mm, acute. Corolla lobes 4, ovate, 4-7 x 3-4 mm, acuminate, coriaceous ; pits 2, submarginal at base of each lobe. Stamens 4; filaments greenish-purple, subulate, 3-4 mm long; anthers ovate, c 1 mm long, obtuse. Ovary ovoid-elliptic, sessile, 4-5 x 2-3 mm; style slender, c 2 mm long; stigma 2-lobed, purplish, papillose. Capsulew ellipsoid, nearly equal to the calyx and corolla, sessile, 8-9 x 4-5 mm. Seeds numerous, minute, globose, brownish; teesta close, alveolate.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant is bitter, equally refrigerent and thermogenic. It is used as anti-inflammatory, antipyretic, sudorific and antiperiodic and in useful in all types of fevers, especially chronic and intermittent fevers, inflammations, dipsia, burnig sensation, cough, brinchitis, gastropathy, dispepsia, skin diseases and vitiated conditions of “Pitta” and “Kapha”.
