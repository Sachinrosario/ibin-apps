DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Fruit various in shape from shortly oval, cylindrical or elongated, up to 0.9 m long; dark green to whitish, ripe fruit chang to a bright orange. Seeds smaller than those of melon.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Male Flower: In axillary fascicles of about 2-3; calyx densely hairy, tube narrowly campanulate, teeth subulate; corolla segments hairy outside, often turbinated by hairy apiculation; Stamens 3, filaments shorts, anthers free, oblong, one celled, cells linear, the connective produced into a pappilose appendage; ovary rudimentary, glanduliform, tube constricted above the ovary; Female Flower: calyx densely hairy, tube narrowly campanulate, teeth subulate; corolla segments hairy outside, often turbinated by hairy apiculation; Ovary ellipsoid, covered long bristly hairs, ovules infinite, horizontal style short, stigma three; Fruit: Dark green-white, long, cylindrical;; Seed: Numerous, narrowly ovoid. 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : June- August.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.  
 
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome numbers=20, 22.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant requires well drained loamy soil.

Nutrient requirements: Require tonnes of farmyard manure at the time of planting and 80 kg/ha nitrogenous fertilizers and at growing period give good yield.

Water management: Plant requires good irrigation facilities.                                       

Harvesting period / Method: Fruits are harveseted when they are fully mature but green.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: In Ayurveda system of medicine unripe fruit is sweet and cooling. It cures biliousness and causes flatulence. Ripe fruit is tonic and cures thirst. It causes biliousness and derangement of the blood. Root of sweet variety is used as sedative for uterine pain in pregnancy. In Unani sytem of medicine fruit is used in fever, biliousness. Seeds are diuretic, purgative and antipyretc. It enriches blood. Seed oil is used in fever and good for brain and body.
