Taxonomic Details

Correct Name : Jasminum malabaricum Wight
Family : Oleaceae
Synonyms : Jasminum latifolinm Grah. non Roxb.
Common Names :
Kannada:  Dolle kusdi balli, tirgal.
Marati:  Kundi, kusur, kusuri
Misc: :
Bombay:Mogra, ran-mogra.

Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT
 A large climbing or scan dent shrub, commonly found in Deccan, west coast and western chats from Konkan southwards to Malabar and in Nilgirls up to 4,000 ft.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Stem up to 8 inches in diam. ; branches terete, glabrous : leaves broadly ovate, acute or acuminate at the apex, rounded or sub-cordate at the base, membranous, glabrous ; flowers borne in terminal, lax, many-flowered (often 40-50) trichoto-mous cymes, white, fragrant : calyx lobes 5-7, linear ,corolla lobes 6-10, oblong or lanceolate finely acuminate ; carpals ellipsoid.
