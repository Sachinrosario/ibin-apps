UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
Ephedra gerardiana collected in the Nepalese Himalayas were assayed for the tannin and flavanoid contents of their herbal stems. The results showed the presence of the (+) – catechin and ent-epigallocatechin-(4Aà8, 2Aà 0à7) –catechin. (Naoko et al, 2000).


Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for asthma (Leaf, Gupta et al, 1981; Rawat and Pangtey, 1987); Blood purification (Root, Gupta et al, 1981); Headache (Stem, Uniyal, 1968); Hepatic disease (Root, Gupta et al, 1981); Rheumatism (Stem + Root, Gaur, 1983; Gupta et al, 1981); Edible (Fruit, Kaul et al, 1985); Snuff (Ash of Whole plant, Koelz Walter, 1979); Tooth brush (Weed, Koelz, 1979; Gupta et al, 1981)
