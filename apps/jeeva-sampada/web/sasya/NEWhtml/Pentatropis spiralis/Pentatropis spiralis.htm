 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT
A slender climber with a thin rootstock, 30-40 cm.long, found in Punjab, Delhi, upper Gangetic plain, Rajasthan and Gujarat (Saurashtra). A small genus of twining herbs or under shrubs distributed in tropical Asia, Africa and Australia.Two species occur in India.
Habitat: Hedges.
Soil: Loamy soil,Loamy soils.

Availability : 
Misc: Achanera,Amber, Bichpuri campus, Devikot, Jamuna river, Jonpur-Mangalpur, Kolayat, Nubia, Tordi sagar and tordi sagar dam,
Country: Afghanistan, India and Pakistan.
Zone: Central, North, South and west,
State: Delhi, Gujarat, Punjab, Rajasthan , Sindh and Uttar Pradesh,
District: Agra, Bharatpur, Bikaner, Jaipur, Jaisalmer and Tonk,

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Leaves fleshy, ovate, lanceolate or elliptic oblong; flowers greenish yellow in lateral umbellate cymes ; follicles lanceolate ; seeds ovate, flattened.
