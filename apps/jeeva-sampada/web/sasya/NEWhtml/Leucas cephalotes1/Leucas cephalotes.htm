DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
An annual hairy and pubescent herb 0.6-0.9m high; stems & branches obtusely quadrangular, hairy with spreading hairs. leaves 3.8-7.5 cm by 1.3-2.5 cm , ovate or ovate-lanceolate, subacute. Flowers sessile, bracts imbricate & calyx tubular, slightly curved. Corolla 2 cm long.

Other Uses
  1. In tribal regions of India, Dron pushpi is a valuable drug for snake bite. a property reported in ancient Indian literatures, and is used both externally and internally. 
  2. In many parts of India, people plant this weed in front of their homes to repel snakes and other venomous animals. 
  3. The juice extracted from leaves is used to cure skin problems. 
  4. According to Ayurveda, the plant is mild stimulant and diaphoretic and used in fevers and coughs. 
  5. The flowers mixed in honey is used as domestic remedy for cough and colds. The seed also yields medicinal oil. Labellenic acid (Octadeca – 5, 6-dienoic acid) has been reported in seed oil. Beta sitosterol have been isolated from the plant of Leucas cephalotes. 
  6. Anti bacterial activity of Leucas aspera leaf extract against Micrococcus pyogenes and Escheria coli have also been reported. 
  7. Dronpushpi is valuable homoeopathic drug and as such is used for the treatment of chronic malaria and asthama. In many parts of India particularly in North India.
