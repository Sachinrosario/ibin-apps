HABIT & HABITAT

A medium-sized deciduous tree, found throughout the grater part of India ascending to an altitude of 1500 m in the Western Himalayas.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES

Bark dark brown , wood red with dark patches and streaks, hard , branchlets long, slender, leaves broader than long, 9-11 x 15 cm rigidly coriaceous, glabrous, sub-cordate at base, margin entire, apex deeply emarginated, 7-11 nerved. Flowers with purple colour, numerous panicles, calyx limb splitting into 2 or 3 segments, prominently nerved, minutely appressed,  pubescent, petal clawed , 6x2 mm, hairy outside, blade orbicular, pale yellow, marked with dark-purple veins, stamens 5, fertile, filament, 1.5 mm, anther 0.75 mm, oblong; ovary 2 mm, style terminal, pods 10-15 cm. long, broad, flattened, gradually widenning to an obtuse tip, fruit late dehiscing; seeds 6-8.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Bark, seeds, wood

Uses

  • Gum (semla gond) - used in sizing cloth and paper, Water-proofing terraced roof.
  • Efficient binder in the manufacture of charcoal briquette, either alone or with other binders of a starchy nature.
  • In Dehra Dun, gum is used in the preparation of sweetmeats.


Harvesting Practices


Produce and Products
Semla gond (gum), timber
