DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Large tree, twigs villous. Leaves oblong or elliptic, 18.0 x 8.0 cm long, obtuse or emarginate or slightly acute at apex, rounded or cordate at base, nerves prominent, parallel, villous beneath, large, stalked glands near the juncture of petiole and lamina. Flowers in dense spike; bracteoles linear. Calyx with yellowish brown hairs. Fruits, large, c 5-cm in diam., glabrous with 5-equal wings.
