DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Ramblers; branches quadrangular with winged angles, succulent; tendrils stout, simple. Leaves ovate to reniform, 2-6 x 2-5 cm, sometimes 3-7 lobed, acute to obtuse at apex, truncate to cordate at base, subentire to denticulate at margin, glabrous; petioles 0.5-1.0 cm long; stipules broadly ovate, c 4 x 2 mm, obtuse.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers in 3-4 cm long umbellate cymes, 2.5-3.0 mm long; peduncles c 2 cm long; pedicels 2-4 mm long, glabrous. Calyx cupular, obscurely lobed, glabrous. Petals ovate-oblong, c 2 x 1.5 mm, acute, hooded, glabrous. Berries obovoid, c 5 x 4 mm, smooth except for the longitudinally encircling ridged raphe.
CIMAP:
Inflorescence: Peduncled cymes with spreading umbellate branches ; Flower: Small, polygamous, hermaphrodite, complete; Calyx: Cup-shaped, truncate or obscurely lobed; Corolla: Petals 4, ovate-oblong, acute, hooded at the apex, disk erect, 4-lobed ; Androecium: Stamens 4-5, inserted below the margin of the disk, anthers free; Gynoecium: Ovary 2-celled, ovules two in each cell, style short, stout ; Fruit: Berry obovoid or globose, apiculate, red when ripe; Seed: One. Flowering & Fruiting : July-September. 


Floral and seed biology:
It flowers between June - January.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by birds or animals.
 
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome number 2n= 24.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 A paste made of stems is applied on forehead to get relief from headache and also given in asthma. Tender branches made into a paste and mixed with milk is a purgative. Tender stems and leaves are used in the treatment of bronchitis. Juice of the plant is said to be beneficial in scurvy, used as an alterrative, applied in cases of otorrhoea and epistaxis and mixed with breast milk is given to infants in fever. The powered root is used in the treatment of fractured bones.

Chemistry:
Two steroidal principles, mp. 249°C and 136°C isolated but not characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

Two new unsymmetric tetracyclic triterpenoids – oncer-7-en-3a,21b-diol (fig. 1a) and oncer-7-en-3b,21a-diol (fig. 1b) – along with sitosterol, d-amyrin and d-amyrone isolated and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).

A new unsymmetrical triterpenoid – 7-oxo-onocer-8-ene-3b,21a-diol (fig. 2) – isolated and its structure established. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)a.

4-hydroxy-2-methyltricos-2-en-22-oe, 9-methyloctadec-9-ene, heptadecyl octadecanoate, icosanyl icosanoate, 31-methyltritriacontan-1-ol, 7-hydroxy-20-oxodocosanylcyclohexane and 31-methyl-tritriacontanoic acid isolated and their structure elucidated; taraxeryl acetate, friedelan-3-one, taracerol, b-sitosterol and isopentacosanoic acid also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994)b.

Pharmacology:

Aqueous extract showed acetylcholine-like activity on isolated ileum of rabbit and rat, uterus of rat, dog tracheal muscle and ileum in situ of dog. The responses of dog blood pressure were analogous to muscarinic and nicotinic actions of acetylcholine. It was ineffective on frog rectus muscle. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

A glucoside from plant showed negative chronotropic effect on myocardium. It was surface-acting and its effect was surmounted with excess of calcium ion. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Plant extract injected in dogs markedly hastened rate of healing in artificially produced fracture of femur. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Four marker constituents, namely, onocer-7-ene-3 alpha, 21 beta-diol, delta-amyrin, delta-amyrone and 3,3',4,4'-tetrahydroxybiphenyl of Cissus quadrangularis Linn. are defined for standardisation purposes. 3,3',4,4'-Tetrahydroxybiphenyl has been isolated for the first time from this drug. The contents of the marker constituents were quantitatively determined by HPTLC and HPLC methods in samples collected from five different geographic zones of India. (Mehta et al., 2001).
