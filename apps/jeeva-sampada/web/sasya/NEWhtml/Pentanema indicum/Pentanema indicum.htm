Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

Erect herb grows in drier parts, up to 1200 m. It is distributed in Pakistan, Mynmar, China, Sri Lanka, Thailand and Africa
Availability :

Throughout India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Herbs, erect, viscid pubescent with a woody rootstok, simple or branched at the top, up to 75 cm high; stems terete, grooved, hispidly hairy, glabrous with age. Leaves oblong-lanceolate, with semi amplexicaul base, acuminate, entire or serrate-dentate with recurved margins, 2-10 x 0.1-1 cm, scabrid, dark green above pale beneath, sessile. Heads yellow, heterogamous, radiate, solitary, axillary or terminal 1-2 cm across, on 1-6 cm long peduncles. Receptacle convex, pitted. Involucre campanulate; involucral bracts 3 to 4-seriate, linear-lanceolate, 0.2-0.4 cm long, acute, glabrous, erect. Ray florets 1-seriate, female 0.8-1.0 cm long, shortly 3 toothed; corolla yellow. Disc florets bisexual, 0.5 cm long, 5-lobed. Achenes brown, obovate, 1 mm long, obtuse, narrowed at base, thinly hairy. Pappus white, hairs filiform, up to 0.3 cm long, few.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:
As a ornamental flowering herb

Parts Used:

Annual herb with flowers of ornamental value. The plant is a good fodder and is said to be used in medicine in Madhya Pradesh, particularly for causing irreversible sterility in women.
