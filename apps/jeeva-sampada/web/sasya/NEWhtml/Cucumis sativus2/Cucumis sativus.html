HABIT & HABITAT
Annual hispid, hairy, monoecious, climbing herb, it is found to occur in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Sikkim, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Orissa, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka and Gujarat.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Annual hispid, hairy, monoecious, climbing herb. Leaves deeply cordate, palmately 3-5 lobed, 11.5-20 cm long and broad. Flowers yellow, 1.8-2.5 cm in diam. Male flowers in clusters; calyx tube campanulate with long white hairs; sepals linear; filaments very short, cohering with connective. Female flowers solitary or fascicled, peduncle robust, 1-2 cm long, ovary fusiform, hairy. Fruits oblong, greenish yellow. Seeds oblong-elliptic, white.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Male Flower: In axillary fascicles ; calyx tbular, top shaped, lobes 5; corolla segments hairy outside; Stamens 3, filaments shorts, anthers free, oblong, 1- one celled, 2-two celled, the connective produced into appendage; ovary rudimentary, ; Female Flower: calyx tbular, top shaped, lobes 5; corolla segments hairy outside ; Ovary ovoid, style short with 3 obtuse stigma, ovules many, horizontal; Fruit: Commonly cylindrical, glabrous, tubercled; Seed: Spherical, ovoid, glabrous, hairy Flowering & Fruiting : June- August. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:

Propagation: Plant is propagated through seeds.

Soil type: Plant requires well drained loamy soil.

Nutrient requirements:  Requires tonnes of farmyard manure at the time of planting and 80 kg/ha nitrogenous fertilizers and 70 kg/ha potassium and phosphorous at growing period give good yield.

Water management: Plant requires good irrigation facilities.                                       

Harvesting period / Method: Fruits are harvested when they are fully mature but green.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 In Ayurveda system of medicine unripe fruit cures biliousness, strangury. Ripe fruits cures thirst, fatigue and it causes biliousness and derangement of the blood. Roots are used as sedative for uterine pains in pregnancy. In Unani system of medicine fruit is used in thirst, fever and biliousness. Seeds are diuretic, antipyretic and it enriches the blood. Seed oil is used in fever and good for brain and body.


Chemistry:
CIMAP: A steady decrease in ascorbic acid (As A) [50-81-7] and total AsA (As A + dehydroascorbic acid) [490-83-5] of cucumbers were obsd. when stored at  5˚, but there was no decrease at 20˚ for 15 days 1; cucumber cotyledons were more sensitive to UV-B radiation than radish. This higher sensitivity might be due to reduced levels of protective flavonoid compds. in cucumber, since flavonoid content in radish was increased to control plants, esp. in older leaves. Young cucumber cotyledons also increased their flavonoid content, but after longer irradiation the deleterious effects of UV-B overcame the biosynthesis of flavonoids. Protein content of radish cotyledons was unaffected by either treatment; However, it increased in cucumber coteledon after long exposure to enhanced UV-B & waterstress compared to well watered plants exposed to contraol levels of UV-B radiation2.
