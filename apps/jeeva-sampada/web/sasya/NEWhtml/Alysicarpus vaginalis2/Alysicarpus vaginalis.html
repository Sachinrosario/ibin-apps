HABIT & HABITAT
Annual to perennial, procumbent, robust herb, 20-90 cm long, sparsely hairy. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Goa, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves simple, oblong, 1.2-4.5 x 0.8-3.4 cm, cordate at base, finely hairy beneath. Flowers pink in 6-12 flowered racemes. Calyx equalling or little longer than first joint of pod. Pods 20-30 x 2 mm, slender, joints 4-9, reticulately veined, hairy, 1-seeded, joints about half as long as broad.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   

Inflorescence: 6-12 flowered racemes; Flower: Bracteate, pedicellate, hermaphrodite, complete; Calyx: Glabrescent, equaling or slightly longer than the first joint, teeth longer than tube, linear, very acute, striate; Corolla: Pinkish, small, included, obovate, narrowed into claw, adhering to the keel; Androecium: Stamens 10, diadelphous, anthers uniform, filaments free, ; Gynoecium: Ovary sessile, ovules numerous, style incurved, stigma capitate ; Fruit: Pods sub-terete, not or very rarely moniliform, apiculate, glabrous or nearly so, reticulately veined, joints 4-8 longer than the broader; Seed: One ; 
   
Floral and seed biology:
September- November. Flowering & Fruiting: October. 


Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.
 
Genetic variability: 

The plant has reported chromosome number 2n= 16,20.
