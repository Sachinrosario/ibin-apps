DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herbs, 10-45 cm high. Stems compressed-trigonous, base tuberous. Stolons long, slender with intermittent ellipsoid aromatic tubers. Leaves linear, 2-5 mm broad. Anthela usually simple, sometimes compound; bracts 2-4, foliaceous, upto 15 cm long. 


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Spikelets 3-10 together, linear-lanceolate, 10-30 x 1-2 mm, frequently curved; rachilla wings with hyaline or red streaks, lanceolate. Glumes tightly imbricating, reddish or deep brown, broadly ovate, 3.0-3.5 mm long, muticous or mucronulate, keeled. Nuts glossy, black, obovoid-ellipsoid, trigonous, 1.5-1.7 mm long . Its Phenology is distributed between July to April.
CIMAP:
Inflorescence: Spikelets are borne on branched inflorescence axis, subtended leafy bracts ; Flower:  bracteate, bract dry greenish brown, known as glume, sessile, incomlpete ;  Perianth:  absent ;   Androecium: Stamens 3, polyandrous,filaments long, dithecous, basifixed, introrse ; Gynoecium:   tricarpellary, syncarpous, ovary superior, unilocular, ovule one, stigma 3 and feathery ; Fruit:  Nuts obovoid-ellipsoid, trigonous, glossy black. 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering and Fruiting: May- June. 
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are  dispersed by wind.
 
Genetic variability:
The reported chromosome number 2n=108.

Pharmacology:

·        Root extract possessed tranquillising activity. It had smooth muscle relaxant activity on rabbit ileum. Extract also showed significant antipyretic and antiinflammatory activities. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

·        Daily instillation of root extra  ct into eyes of 26 conjunctivitis cases decreased redness and reduced pain and ocular discharge and most patients were cured within five days. (Rastogi and Mehrotra  1980-1984)a.

·        Volatile oil had marked central nervous system depressant effects. It potentiated hypnotic action of pentobarbital in mice and anaesthetic action of scopolamine in rabbits. It also inhibited contraction of isolated ileum of rabbits at 20.0 nl/ml (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

·        Methanolic extract of rhizomes of C. rotundus could be developed as anti-inflammatory candidate for the treatment of inflammatory diseases mediated by overproduction of NO and O2-. (Seo et al., 2001)

·        Extracts of Cyperus rotundus Linn were investigated for antibacterial activity against Staphylococcus aureus (NCIM 2079), Escherichia coli (NCIM 2065), Bacillus Subtilis (NCIM 2063), Pseudomonas aeruginosna (NCIM 2036), and Proteus vulgaris (NCIM 2027) at 50 mug/disc using disc diffusion method. The acetone and ethanol extracts showed significant broad spectrum antibacterial activity. (Puratchikody et al., 2001)
