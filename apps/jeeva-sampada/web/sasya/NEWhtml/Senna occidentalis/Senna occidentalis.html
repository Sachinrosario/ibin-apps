HABIT & HABITAT
Erect undershrub, 0.6-1.5 m tall, subglabrous, branches furrowed. It is found to occur in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Sikkim, Assam, Meghalaya, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Goa, Daman & Diu, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves 10-20 cm long, with large gland at base of petiole; leaflets 3-5 pairs, ovate or ovate-lanceolate to elliptic, 2.5-10.0 x 3.5 cm, acute or acuminate or aristate at apex, rounded at base, membranous, glaucous above, finely pubescent beneath; petioles 3-5 cm long; petiolules c 2 mm long. Stipules ovate-lanceolate, 5.0-6.5 x 1.0-1.5 mm, acute-acuminate. Racemes very short, 2-4 flowered, arranged in terminal panicles; peduncles 3-8 mm long; pedicels 0.5-1.5 cm long, pubescent; bracts linear, 1-2 cm long, acute, glabrous. Sepals membranous, ovate, 0.7-1.0 cm long, acute or rounded, glabrous. Petals subequal, obovate-obtuse. Ovary short, tomentose, slender, recurved; style c 0.5 cm long, glabrous; stigma small. Pods flattened, linear, 10.0-12.5 x 0.7 cm, recurved, compressed, transversely septate, torulose. Seeds 20-30, ovoid, 0.4 cm long, smooth, shining, olive green; areole oblong or almost circular, 2-3 x 0.7-1.5 mm.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Plant is bitter, sweet, thermogenic, purgative, expectorent and febrifuge and useful in cough, bronchitis, constipation, fever, epilepsy and convulsions. Roots are bitter, acrid, thermogenic, diuretic, anti-inflammatory, digestive, stomachic and used as tonic. It is also useful in inflammation, diabetes, strangury, elephantiasis, ringworm, colic, flatulence, dyspepsia, epilepsy, convulsion and scorpion sting. Leaves are bitter, acrid, sweet, thermogenic, depurative, vulnerary, anodyne, expectorent, alexteric and aphrodisiac and useful in leprosy, erysipelas, pruritus, wound and ulcer, cough, bronchitis, hiccough, asthma and hydrophobia. In Ayurveda system of medicine root is used in cough, hiccough, asthma, sore throat and biliousness. In Unani system of medicine root is used against snake bite, ringworm infection and in healing wounds. Fruits are used in scorpion sting. Seeds are used for the treatment of cough.
