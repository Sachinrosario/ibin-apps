Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
A rambling spinous shrub, flowering throughout the year and bearing globose 1-2 seeded, white berries, about in diameter. It is often found gregarious in the drier districts of the Deccan Peninsula, especially near the coast, and in Orissa, Sundarbans, Ceylon and Burma.

Chemistry
A flavonel bioside glycoside,isorhamneten 3-o-rutinoside(narcissin) has been isolated from the leaves.

The petroleum ether extract of the air dried seeds yields  an oil (12%).the Characteristics of the oil are as follows.

Unsapon matter 2.3%,iod val 141.0,Sap val 201.5; HBr equivalent at 55o,9.8%. The Composition of fatty acid in the oil is as follows.Lauric 3.5,hymiristic 4.2Palmatic 5.2,Stearic 1.6;Oleic 15.3,linolic 28.8;linolenic 22.0;ricinoleic 9.8;malvalic 4.0 and stereatic acid 5.6%.

Other Uses
  1. The juice of the leaves is said to relieve the cough of phthisis and asthma.
  2. The bark is reputed to be an expectorant.
  3. The leaves are used for ulcers, especially after small-pox, they are ground with turmeric and gingelly oil, then applied on the surface of the body to remove the irritation of skin.
  4. The root-bark is used in muscular rheumatism. 
  5. It is good hedge plant.
  6. The leaves mixed with stembark and made into a semi-solid liquid are given to cattle for a week in rinderpest.
