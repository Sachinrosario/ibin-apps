 Other Biological Details
HABIT & HABITAT:
 Shrub or small tree found throughout India in  Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Orissa, Deccan Peninsula, Western India. Commonly planted near near and around homes as hedges, fences.
Availability :  

Specimens examined: Orissa: Birmitrapur, Sundarhgarh dist., 28.03.1992, E. Varghese, 214350 (LWG).

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Shrub or small tree. Young branches 5-angled black stipular spines arising from thick tubercles in 5 rows, black. Leaves terminal, alternate, obovate-oblong, narrowed at base, undulate, fleshy, short petiolate, deciduous. Involucre in 3-5 flowered cymes, lateral ones of cymes with short thick pedicels, central one sessile and usually male. Lobes of involucres erect, orbicular, fimbriate with transversely oblong glands. Male flowers: anthers sagittate, apiculate. Female flowers: style arms connate above middle, stigma capitate. Capsules deeply 3-1obed; cocci compressed, glabrous.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Cymes from the short peducles, the lateral one of the cyme with short thick pedicel, the central sessile and usually male; Flower:Bracts numerous, fimbriate ; Male Flower: a stalked stamen without floral envelop ; Female Flower: Ovary 3-celled on ultimately exserted stalk in the center of yellowish, hemispheric involucre, ovules solitary in each cell, style 3, connate high up, undivided; Fruit: Capsule deeply three lobed of 3 two valved compressed and glabrous cocci  ; Seed: Broadly ovoid and flat. Flowering & Fruiting : Febuarary – March  . Flowers are pollinated by insects.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Milky juice is used as purgative and expectorant. It is also used to remove warts and cutaneous eruptions. Root mixed with pepper is regarded as an antidote for snake bite.
