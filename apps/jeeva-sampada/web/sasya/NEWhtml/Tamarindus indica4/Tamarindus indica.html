DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Erect, evergreen or deciduous tree, 20 m tall, with oval crown; bark greyish, scaly, rough and warty; branchlets slender, spreading, glabrous. Leaves peripinate, 4-12 cm long; leaflets 6-16 pairs, subsessile, oblong or ovate-oblong, 0.7-1.7 x 0.3-0.6 cm, notched or slightly mucronate at apex, rounded or oblique cuneate at base, entire along margin, glabrous on both surfaces, membranous, lateral nerves forming an intra-marginate loop, subcoriaceous. Flowers in lax, axillary 2.5-10 cm long drooping raceme, yellow or redish-tinged; bracts and bracteoles ciliate. Sepals oblong-elliptic, subequal, 0.8-1.2 cm log, yellow. Petals 3, c 1 cm long, unequal, yellow, striped with red. Pods oblong, 3-20 x 2-2.5 cm, slightly laterally compressed,  with redish-brown pulp during ripening. Seeds 1-8, dark chocolate brown, smooth, obovate, c 1 cm long, compressed.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses: In Ayurveda system of medicine fruits are refrigerent and used as digestive, carminative and laxative. It is useful in disease caused by deranged bile. Bark is uesed in paralysis, urinary diseases and gonorrhoea. Leaves are used against inflammatory swelling, tumour and ringworm and useful in blood diseases, smallpox, earch and snakebite. Flowers are used in urinary discharges, bad odour in perspiration. Unripe fruits are also used in cough and blood troubles. Seeds are used in vaginal discharges. In Unani system of medicine bark is used in ulcers. Leaves reduce inflammatory swelling. Fruits are useful in liver complaints, vomitting, thirst, scabes, sore throat and stomatitis. Pulp of the fruits are used as heart tonoc and to check vomiting. Seeds are used in vertigo and giddiness.
