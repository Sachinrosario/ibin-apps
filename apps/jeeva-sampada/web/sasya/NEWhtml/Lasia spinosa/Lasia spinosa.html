Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT: 
A stout prickly herb, found along marshy places, muddy streams and swampy ground in tropical Sikkim Himalayas, Assam , West Bengal, Bihar, Orissa and Southwards to Ceylon.
BioticZone: Subtropical Himalayas,Tropics,
Altitude Minimum: 0-500,
Availability :  
Misc: Daidaighat, Near Dawki, Near Nalas, Rampa hills, Salkumar,
Country: India,
Zone: Central,East,none,North East,South,
State: Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Karnataka, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Tripura, West Bengal,
District: Champaran, Jowai,
Status :

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES 
A stout prickly herb, 60 cm. High, with thick creeping rhizome, leaves long-petioled, 15-45 cm. Long, hastate, sagittate or pinnatifid; spadix short, cylindrical, claret-coloured, densely packed with pink flowers;

Other Uses 
  1. Rootstocks and fruits are used for affections of the throat. 
  2. The juice of the rootstock is given as a remedy for piles in Ceylon. For cattle weakness boil the tuber and give it to the cattle orally for curing all internal disease. It acts as a tonic.
    Indigestion: Grind 50 g of tuber of this to make a paste. Give the paste to the cattle at a time to free from indigestion problem only for cattle's.
  3. The plant is recommended for colic, rheumatism and intestinal diseases. Leaves are used for stomach ache and other pains.
