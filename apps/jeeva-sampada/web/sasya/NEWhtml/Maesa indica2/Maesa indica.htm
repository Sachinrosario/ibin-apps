DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A large much branched shrub; twigs slender, glabrous, usually covered with numerous small lenticels. Leaves elliptic-lanceolate or ovate-oblong, regularly or irregularly serrate-dentate. Flowers very small, faintly fragrant, numerous, in compound, panicled, usually glabrous racemes; pedicels filiform; bract 1 below the pedicel, narrowly ovate, acute, not ciliate. Calyx divided rather more than ½ way down, lobes rotund-ovate, obtuse, not or sometimes faintly ciliate, glabrous on the back, marked with dark lines, tube enlarged in fruit, enclosing the berry, rugose and obscurely ribbed outside. Corolla white, marked with colored lines, divided to the middle; lobes round-ovate, somewhat abruptly and shortly contracted at the base and with slightly crenulate margins, spreading. Berry globose, creamy-white, covered almost to the apex by persistent calyx and tipped with the short style. Seeds black.
