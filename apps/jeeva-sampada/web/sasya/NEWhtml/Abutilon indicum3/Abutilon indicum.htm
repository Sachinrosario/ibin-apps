DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Herbaceous or shrubby,softly tomentose perennial up to 3 m in ht.;stem round, often tinged with purple;leaves ovate to orbicular, cordate;flowers-yellow or orange-yellow, olidary, onjointed peduncles, often forming a panicle, like terminal inflorescence due to reduction in leaves;fruits-hisped, searcely longer than calyx, anens erect,seeds 3-5, reniform, dark brown or black, tubercled or minutely stellate hairly.

Economic Potential and Uses

UTILIZATION:

Parts Used:



Harvesting Practices:


Produce and Products:
- fiber- mixed with jute in products made of the latter.
- used as drug in Ayurvedic & Unani system
- used as a febrifuge, anthelmintic, anti-inflammatory, in urinary & uterine discharges, piles, lumbago
- juice of plant-as emollient to relieve soreness of the nates in young children
- ethanolic extract of plant- anti cancer & hypothermic activity

Leaves - eaten or bleeding piles, extract as a diuretic & demulcent & as an emollient fomentation; along with ghee for diarrhoea, decoction used as mouthwash, useful in gonorrhoea, inflammation of bladder, as a wash for wounds & ulcers, for enema & vaginal infection, decoction is given for stones in bladder & as wash in eye diseases.

Chemistry:

Processing Techniques:
Retting
fiber extracted from green plants at the time of flowering & fruiting.

Flowers- eaten raw, applied to boils & ulcers, powdered flower mixed with ghee- in blood vomiting & in cough. Alcoholic extract of flower & fr.-shows activity against Micrococcus pyogenes var. aureus & E.coli Caslett. & Chalm.

Root - nervine tonic, antipyretic, mixed with honey, chaulmoorga oil & fresh paste of sandalwood-treatment of lecuoderma. Influsion of root-in haematuria & strangury.

Seed - laxative, demulcent, in treating cough, useful in gonorrhoea, glect & chronic cystitis.
