REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Heads terminal large with wide disk ; Flower: Disk flowers bisexual, corolla tubular, contracted below, with 5 equal triangular teeth,, yellow anthers apiculate not tailed, included in the corolla, style exserted, stigmabifid ; Ray florets pistillate in single row, corolla ligulate, the limb broadly oval, trifid at the apex, white above ; Fruit: Dorsally compressed, obovoid, smooth ;

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
Amides (I fig. 1a, II fig. 1b, III fig. 1c, IV fig. 1d) obtained from roots and their structures determined.(Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-79).

Analysis for the mineral elements Na, Mg, Al, Ca, V, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Sr, Mo, Ag, Cd and Ce in Anacyclus pyrethrum was carried out using inductively coupled plasma (I.C.P.). Atomic absorption Spectrophotometry and flame photometry. Mineral elements Na, Mg, K, Ca, V, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Sr, Ag and Ce are present. (Saily et al, 1994). MAPA 1995, 9502, 1171.


Pharmacology:
Polyunsaturated alkamides isolated from Anacyclus pyrethrum (L.) Possess inhibitory activity in in vitro cyclooxygenase (Sheep semial microsomes) and 5-lipoxygenase (Porcine leukocytes) assays. Activity appeard to depend on the particular structure of alkamides. (Muller-Jakic et al, 1994).

The extract of A. pyrtherum is used in India during thr preparation of chewable tobacco, on tobacco-induced mutagenesis were evaluated using Ames salmonella/ microsome array. Eugenol significantly inhibited tobacco-induced mutagenicity at concentrations of 0.5 and 1 mg/plate. A. pyretheum extract (1mg/plate) produced 74.33% inhibition. (Sukumaran and ramadasan, 1995). C.A. 1995, 122, 308532p.

Esterogenic content in herbs Anacyclus pyrethrum being used in various galactogogue preparations 70.91ppm. (Kaur, 1998). MAPA, 1999, 9903, 1978.
