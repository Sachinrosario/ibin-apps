HABIT & HABITAT:
Erect, much branched, thorny under shrub with axillary spines. It is found in Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Delhi, Gujarat, Punjab, Rajasthan.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Leaves simple, subsessile, oblanceolate or oblong, tiny grey pubescent above, glabrous beneath. Flowers red, in raceme. Lomentum falcate or straight, moniliform. Seeds reniform.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
   Inflorescence: Axillary racemes forming panicles at the ends of branches ; Flower: Red ; Calyx: Campanulate, distint, minute ; Corolla: Exserted, standard broad, keel obtuse ;Androecium: Stamens diadelphous, anthers uniform; Gynoecium: Ovary linear, sessile, 6-8 ovulate, style filiform, stigma minute; Fruit: Pods falcate or straight; Seed: 6-8 sub-reniform. Flowering & Fruiting : Flowers are pollinated by insects.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:  Plant is laxative, diuretic and expectorent. Leaves are used medicinally in asthma and fever. Flowers are used as blood coagulent and piles treatment. Oil from the leaves is used in rheumatism.


Chemistry:
From the stem of Alhagi pseudalhagi (Bieb.) Desv. Five b-phenethylamines and one tetrahydro-isoquinoline alkaloids were isolated and characterized. On the basis of the chemical transformation and spectral evidence, the alkaloids were identified as b-phentheylamine (I), N-methyl-b-phenethylamine (II) hordenine (III), 3,4-dihydroxy-b-phenethytrimenthylammonium hydroxide (IV), N-methylmescaline (V), and salsolidine (VI) (Fig .1) The roots of this plant contain essentially the same alkaloids but in poorer yields. Preliminary pharmacological studies of the total alkaloids indicated that some curative properties of the extract are due to these entities. (Ghosal and Srivastava, 1973).

From the stems of Alhagi pseudalhagi, seven b-phenethylamine and one tetrahydro-isoquinoline were isolated. The compounds were identified by m.p. the parent compounds of and their derivatives, where possible, co-TLC with authentic markers, and spectral properties. The alkaloids are b-phenethylamine (I), N-methyl-b-phenethylamine (II), N-methyltyramine (III), hordenine (IV), 3:4-dihydroxy-b-phenethyltrimethylammonium hydroxide (V), 3-methoxy-4-hydroxy-b-phenethyltrimethylammonium hydroxide (VI), N-methylmescaline (VII) and salsolidine (VIII). (Fig. 2). (Ghosal et al., 1974).

24-methylcholest-5-en-3b-ol, 24-ethylcholest-5-en-3b-ol, 24-ethylcholesta-5, 22, - dien-3b-ol, 24-ethylcholesta-5, 24 (28)-dien-3b-ol, 24-ethylcholest-7-en-3b-ol and   avenasterol -7-ene isolated along with cholesterol (Behari and Gupta, 1981).

Isolation of (+) catechin, galacatechin, (-) epigallocatechin and leucodelphinidin from aerial parts. (Islambekov et al. 1982) C.A. 1983, 98, 50389c.

Total flavonol content in A. pseudalhagi was determined by spectrophotometry and measuring the UV absorbance at 370 nm. Quercetin used as standard. (Khaiitbaev et al, 1993). C.A. 1995, 123, 222104j.

A new flavonol glucoside was isolated from the aerial parts of Alhagi pseudalhagi and elucidated as synigetin-3-O-b-D-glucoside on the basis of chemical reaction and spectral data. 14 known compounds were isolated and identified as b-sitosterol, stigmasterol, kaempferol, rhamnetin, ombuine, isorhamnetin tamarixetin, kaempferol –3-O-b-D- (6”-O-P-coumaroyl) glucoside; isoquercitin, D-3-O-methylinositol, 1-O-b-D-methylglucoside, isoswertianolin isorhamnetin-3-O-b-D-rutinoside and tyramine. (Yang et al., 1996). C.A. 1997, 126, 183822g.

Two new flavanone glycosides, alhagitin and alhagidin, have been isolated from the whole plant of Alhagi pseudalhagi and their structure established respectively as naringenin 5-methyl ether 4’-glucoside and hesperitin 7-galactosyl (1®2) [rhamnosyl (1®6] glucoside by chemical andspectroscopic methods. (Singh et al., 1999).

The polysaccharide fraction from Alhagi maurorum Medic. have been isolated by extraction. It contains soluble and insoluble fractions and sulfur. The presence of galactose, arabinose and a small amount of uronic component was found in the soluble fraction. The acetone fraction indicated no presence of sulfur. The ratio of galactose: arabinose have been determined as 3: 2. (Goncharov et al., 2001). C.A. 2001, 135, 31267c.


Pharmacology:

Pharmacological screening of the total alkaloids from the stems were conducted which showed essentially sympathomimetic activity. These results, further seem to indicate that the therapeutic properties ascribed to the plant extracts were due to the contained alkaloids. (Ghosal et al., 1974).

Antioxidant properties of yantocine, a preparation derived from Alhagi pseudalhagi of acute toxic hepatitis induced in random bred young male albino rats (90-100 g) Yantocine was added to preparations of damages plasma memberanes (PM) in ascending doses. To two experimental models acute geliotrine hepatitis and acute CCl4 hepatitis. The results indicate that the level of malonic diadehyde in the rat liver PM depended on the drugs dose in both models of hepatitis. Linear dependence was observed for doses of 1.0-5.0 gamma. The capacity of yantocine to decrease the production of lipid peroxide was confirmed in vivo. After 3 day treatment with yantocine the level of lipid peroxides decreased by 15 and 12%, respectively, while the content of total phosphlipids increased negligibly. (Fesenko and Arkhipov, 2001).
