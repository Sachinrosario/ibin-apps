Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT:
 A quick growing shrub or small tree, 2.5-3.0 m in height, occasionally reaching 6m, native to East Africa, introduced to India for ornament, found both cultivated and wild in Tamil Nadu, also found in Dehradun and Delhi.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Leaves 15-30 cm, leaflets 4-6 pairs, oval or elliptic, flowers orange yellow, in simple erect corymbose racemes; pods flat, hairy, oblong, 10 cm x 1.8 cm.
