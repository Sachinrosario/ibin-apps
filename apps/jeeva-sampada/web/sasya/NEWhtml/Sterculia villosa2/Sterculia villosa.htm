Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

In deciduous forests throughout the greater part of India, ascending upto an alt. of 1000m and in the Andamans. The tree prefers a light, sandy soil and is a strong light-demands.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small to moderate-sized, spreading tree, upto 1.6m in girth.
Bark grey, somewhat warty, light red and fibrous onside.
Leaves crowded at the ends of branches, palmately - lobed, lobes 5-7, 30-45 cm. diam.
Flowers pale yellow in panicles.
Follicles 2-5, spreading oblong, 3.5-7.5 cm long, scarlet, coriaceous.
Seeds oblong, black, shining.

Seed germination :

The seeds germinate at the beginning of the rains, soon after falling. They are some what perishable and the rate of fertility is not high, but in suitable localities, natural regeneration is adequate.

Produce and Products:

Wood - tea boxes, light packing cases, ship-building, inferior match boxes, splints, three-ply work.
Bark - fiber - cordage, rough bags, for breast bands of elephants for dragging timber and for tying cattles. Can be used for paper manufacture.
Seeds - eaten cooked or roasted.
Wood - the wood is white when first exposed, soon turning grayish brown. It is liable to severe damage by insects and fungi during air-seasoning, but cracking and twisting are absent. Wood is easy to saw and work, but difficult to bring to a good finish.

fiber - the inner layers of the bark yield a coarse and strong fiber, which strips off in broad flakes, having a net like appearance.
