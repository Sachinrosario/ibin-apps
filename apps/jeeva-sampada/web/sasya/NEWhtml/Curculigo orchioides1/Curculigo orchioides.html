HABIT & HABITAT
Perennial herbs, 10-35 cm tall, erect found in Kashmir eastwards to Assam, ascending to 2000 m; Khasia hills, Manipur, Western Ghats from the Konkan southwards. Japan,Malaysia,Indonesia, Australia.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Perennial herbs, 10-35 cm tall, erect,; rootstocks stout, short or elongate, root-fibers copious, fleshy. Leaves sessile or petiolate, linear to linear-lanceolate, 15-45 x 1.2-2.5 cm, membranous, plicate, base sheathing, glabrous or sparsely softly hairy.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers in a small racemes, bright-yellow, upper male and lower hermaphrodite; bracts lanceolate, membranous. Perianth segments elliptic-oblong, 1.3-1.7 cm long, acute. Capsules hypogyneous, ca 1.3 cm long, 1-4 seeded with a slender beak.  Seeds oblong. It flowers between June - October.
CIMAP:
 Flower: Bright yellow, distichous, the lowest in the raceme 2-sexual, the upper male and bracteate  ; Perianth: Elliptic-oblong, hairy, acute, the stipes very slender, which alone with the perianth appears above the ground ;Androecium: Stamens small, filaments very short and anther linear; Gynoecium: Ovary lanceolate, the cells 6-8 ovulate, stigma 3-cleft ; Fruit: Capsules hypogenous, with a slender beak and septa spongy ; Seed: 1-4, oblong, black and shining. 
Floral and seed biology:
Flowering & Fruiting : June-August.
Mode of pollination and seed dispersal:
Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.
 
Genetic variability:

Uses:
 The root is bitter; aphrodisiac, appetiser; used in piles, biliousness, fatigue and blood diseases (Ayurveda). Root is antipyretic; used in bronchitis, opthalmia, indigestion, vomitting, diarrhoea, gonorrhoea, hydrophobia, pains in the joints.
