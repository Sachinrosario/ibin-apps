HABIT & HABITAT
 Is a scandent, willous shrub with imparipinnate leaves found in eastern Himalayas and Assam up to an altitude of 1,200 m. A genus of scandent shrubs distributed from North-East India to S. China and Philippines. One species occurs in India.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
Shrub with imparipinnate leaves, ovate, oblong or lanceolate leaflets, panicles of minute, white, sweets scented flowers and small, obliquely oblong, black drupes.
