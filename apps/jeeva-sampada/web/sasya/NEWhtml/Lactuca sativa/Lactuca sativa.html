HABIT & HABITAT
Annual or biennial herb with thick woody rootstock. It is found Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Assam, Manipur, Tripura, Meghalaya, Orissa, Tamil Nadu.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Stem erect, 30-80 cm high, glabrous, succulent, leafy. Leaves sessile, ovate-oblong, 2.5-14.5 x 2.5-6.5 cm, succulent, minutely spinulose-toothed, base auriculate. Heads on leafy paniculate branches, many, 0.5-1.0 x 0.2-0.4 cm, erect, glabrous, manyflowered; peduncles white, erect, slender with many small bracts. Outer involucral bracts ovate, 2.5-4.5 x 1.5-2.5 mm; inner narrowly linear, 6.5-9.5 x 1-2 mm, finely membranous. Ligules yellow. Achenes black brown, oblanceolate, 5-7 mm long including the long white beak, 6-8 ribbed, glabrous or sparsely hairy towards margin. Pappus whitish, 3-4 mm, much shorter than the achenes.

Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Leaves used as hyptonic in bronchitis, asthma, poultice for burns and painful ulcers.


Chemistry:  

Campesterol, stigmasterol, sitosterol, 5-deydro-avensterol, stigmast-7-en-3b-ol and 7-dehydro-avensterol isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.
Quercetin-3b-D-glucuronide, quercetin-3b-D-glucoside, quercetin-3-O-malonyl-b-D-glucoside and luteolin-7b-D-glucurnoide isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.
A new carotenoid - lactucaxanthin (fig. 1) isolated from lettuce and its structure determined. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).
Two new sesquiterpene lactones - 3b-hydroxy-11b,13-dihydrocanthospermolide and 3b,14-dihydroxy-11b,13-dihydrocostunolide isolated from aerial parts and characterized; hydrolactucin, lactucin and lactupicrin also isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.
An unidentified compound isolated from stem and named as DLS component. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.
Isolation of new guaiane type sesquiterpene glycoside - lactuside C - along with lactuside A and macroclinside A; structure determination of lactuside C. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

Pharmacology:  

DLS component showed depressant and sedative effects on motor activity and behaviour of toad, Bufo maninus; at high doses it caused flaccid paralysis. It depressed striated and smooth muscle contraction in nerve-muscle preparations and this effect was antagonized by adinistration of Ca2+. It also decreased heart rate and ventricular contraction in normal heart or during ventricular contraction in normal heart or during tachycardia. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.
