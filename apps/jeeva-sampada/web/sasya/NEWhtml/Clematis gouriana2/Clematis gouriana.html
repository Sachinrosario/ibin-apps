REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Inflorescence: Decompound panicles much branched, yellowish to  greenish white peducles; Flower: Small, pedicellate, hermaphrodite; Calyx: Sepals 4, obovate-oblong, puberulous on both the surfaces; Corolla: Absent ;Androecium: Stamens many, anthers usually adnate, dehiscing laterally, filaments glabrous, connective not produced; Gynoecium: Multicarpellary, ovule 1, style simple; Fruit: Achenes ovoid hairy with long feathery tail; Flowering & Fruiting : October -November. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind and birds .
