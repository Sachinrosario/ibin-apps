DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Inflorescence: Solitary axillary; Flower: Ebracteate, pedicillate, complete, actinomorphic, hermaphrodite, trmerous, hypogynous and cyclic; Calyx: Sepals 2-3, concave, nearly valvate, prickly outside, horned at the apex very caudacous; Corolla: Petals 4, large, crumpled two-seriate, twice as many as sepals,  imbricate in two series, yellow with darker parallel veins, deciduous ; Androecium: Stamens many, filaments slender, anthers erect, slits lateral;Gynoecium: Ovary one –celled, ovules many, placentas filiform, style scarecly any and stigma 4-7 lobed, covered with soft spines; Fruit: Capsules oblong or elliptic, prickly, rarely unarmed ; Seed: blackish-brown, deeply-reticulato scrobiculate. Flowering & Fruiting: Throughout the year. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
Protopine nitrate (0.03%), berberine nitrate (0.05%), ceryl alcohol, b-sitosterol, an alcohol, m.p. 73° and succinic citric, tartaric and maleic acids, glucose and fructose, isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)a.

A flavanoid – isorhamnetin-7-diglucoside (fig. 1c), mp. 208°C – from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)b.

Two flavanol glycosides – isorhamnetin-3-glucoside (fig. 1a), m.p. 135° and isorhamnetin-3,7-diglucosdie (fig. 1b) were isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969)c.

Argemone oil administered in doses of 1/1000 LD50 to rats for 3 months caused reversal of albumin and globulin ratio, significant increase in GOT value and mild anaemia associated with myocardial and hepatic necrosis. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Detection of hydroxy (1.0), epoxy (2.0%) and keto fatty acids in seed oil; myristic, palmitic, oleic and linoleic acids also found. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Out of six alkaloids isolated four identified as heletrine, sanguinarine, protopine and allocryptopine; protopine (21.0, 16.0) and allocryptocarpine; (37.0 and 36.0%) determined in leaves and roots respectively of Vietnamese plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)c.

A benzophenthridine base (fig. 2) isolated in addition to norsanguinarine, dihydrosanguinarine, chelerythrine, norchelerythrine, berberine and coptisine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)d.

Crystalline mixture of 9- and 11-oxo-octacosanoic and 11-oxotriacontanoic acids isolated from seed oil. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)e.

New phenolics argemexitin (5,7,2’,6’-terahydroxyflavone) and 5,7-dihydroxychromone-7-neohesperidoside (fig. 3) were isolated from seeds and characterized. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)a.

Presence of argemexitin in seeds not confirmed; luteolin and eriodictyol isolated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)b.

Identification of isorhamnetin-3-glucoside, b-amyrin, cysteine and phenylalanine in leaves. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Isolation of norsanguinarine, berberine, protopine and b-sitosterol from roots, 3-methoxyquercetin and vanillic acid from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

Berberine (01), chelerythrine (0.03),protopine (0.028), a-allocryptopine (0.02), sanguinarine (0.004) and coptisine (0.004%) present in Egyptian plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

Tryptophan (2801); methionine (13.2), proline (12.8), alanine (10.7), tyrosine (8.8), histidine (6.7), glycine (5.1), leucine (4.2), asparitic acid (1.3) and glutamic acid (0.8%) were identified in aerial parts. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)d.

Two new compounds – mexicanol and mexicanic acid – isolated and characterized as 6,11-triacontanediol and 10-hydroxyheptadec-cis-4-enoic acid respectively, norsanguinarine, 6-acetoxydihudrosanguinarine, 6-acetonyldihydro-chelerythrine, reticuline, thalifoline, acetonylreframidine and muramine isolated from Egyptian plant. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)e.

Synthesis of sanguinarine and chelerythrine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)f.

Flowers afforded cryptopine and b-sitosterol. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

Argemone maxicana seeds was analysed for the estimation of sugar and vitamins. 13 sugars were identified. (Saraf et al, 1995). MAPA 1996, 9603, 1721.

Effects of different extracts (Petroleum ether, CHCl3, CHCl3-MeOH (9:1), MeOH), partially purified fractions and pure compounds isolated from Argemone maxicana on the electrically induced contractions of the isolated guinea pig ileu (ECI) have been investigated. CHCl3­-MeOH and MeOH extracts , dose-dependently, reduced the contractions whereas CHCl3 and petroleum ether extracts had no effect. Partially purified fractions II-V from MeOH extract significantly reduced, in a dose-dependent manner, the electrical (ECI) contractions of the ileum. The pure compounds 1 and 2 obtained from the above fraction reduced significantly and in doe-dependent manner the ECI whereas compound 3 increased them. Since the active compounds were identified respectively as protopine, allocryptine and were identified as protpine, allocruptine and berberine by NMR, the observed effects could be related mainly to these alkaloids. (Piacente et al, 1997). MAPA 1998, 9805-2852.

Aerial parts of A. maxicana afforded a new monohydric alcohol, triacontan-11-ol in addition to a known dihydric alcohol, triacontone-6,11-diol. (Sangwan and malik, 1998). C.A. 1999, 130, 12396z.

Chromatographic resolution of the crude base fraction of the whole plant of A. maxican resulted in the isolation of three known alkaloids, (±)-cheilanthifoline, protopine and nor-sanguinarine. Structure of these compounds have been established by spectral analysis and also by direct comparison with authentic samples. (Tripathi et al, 1999). C.A. 1999, 131, 182294c.


Pharmacology:
Effect of sanguinarine, a Na+K+ - ATPase inhibitor, onrefractory period of porcine ventricle was studied. Snguinarine (4.0 mg/kg) markedly increased stimulus threshold of ventricle. Sanguinarine, thus, prolonged ventricular refractoriness and this property may be useful in treatment of ventricular arrhythmias. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984)c.

Aqueous extract of leaves exhibited anti-inflammatory activity. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)g.

A method has been described for the investigation of plant poisoning cases, which may be encountered either in animals or human beings. In this study, Argemone maxicana leaf powder suspension in water was fed to mice. In post mortem studies the half digested remanents of plant material which were collected from alimentary canal were found to be comparable to the anatomy of Argmone maxicana leaf. (Verma et al, 1992). MAPA 1995, 9503, 1610.

Isoquinoline alkaloids protopin and allocryptopine were isolated which reduce morphine withdrawal. (Capasso et al, 1997). C.A. 1997, 17, 210248d.

The effect of the MeOH extract, partially purified fraction (IV), and pure compounds from the Argemone maxicana was examined on the morphine withdrawal in guinea pig isolated ileum. The, MeOH extract partially purified fraction (IV), and pure compounds isolated from A. maxicana significantly and in concentration-dependent manner reduced the morphine withdrawal. Since the pure compounds were identified as protopine and allocryptopine, the observed effects could be related to these compounds. The results of the present study suggest that isoquinoline alkaloids may be potential agents in the treatment of drug abuse. (Capasso et al, 1997). MAPA 1997, 9706, 3459.
