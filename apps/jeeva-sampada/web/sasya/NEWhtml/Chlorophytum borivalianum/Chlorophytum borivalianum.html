DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Rhizomatous herbs; sessile root-tubers 1-9, 4.5 x 1.0 cm. Leaves radical, coriceous, spirally imbricate at base, sessile, linear, 11.5-17.0 x 0.6-1.5 cm, lorate to ensiform, apex acute, flat. Scapes 15-30 cm long.


REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
Flowers in racemes, ca 2.5 cm across, in clusters of 3, white, pedicellate, bracteate; bracts linear, papery, purplish. Capsules loculicidal, greenish-yellow, trigonous, obcordate. Seeds discoid, orbicular, black. It flowers between July-August.
    Inflorescence: Raceme ; Flower: White, bracts linear, purpulish, papery, pedicellate ;  Flower: pedicellate, bracts scarious ; Perianth: White, segments 6, free, spreading, 3-5 nerved ;Androecium: Stamens 6 adnate to the base, anthers versatile ; Gynoecium:  Ovary 3-locular, ovules 4, style filiform, stigma minute; Androecium: ; Gynoecium: ; Fruit: Capsule white, obcordate, loculicidal,  greenish-yellow, trigonous ; Seed: Flat and black. Flowering & Fruiting : July-August. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind, birds or animals.

AGRO TECHNOLOGY:
Propagation: Plant is propagated by root having a portion of semi-disc for commercial cultivation as well as by seeds.

Soil type: Plant thrives well on moderately fertile but well drained loamy or sandy loam soil of open areas with good water holding capacity.

Nutrient requirements: Plant requires large amount of nutrients in very short period. Phosphatic fertilizer as well as organic manure  @ 14-16 tons/ha should be preferred

Water management: Plant may not require any irrigation however, in the event of long period of drought due to failure of monsoon irrigation is necessary, should be given in a week.

Harvesting period / Method: Harvesting should be done after rainy season in the month of December.
