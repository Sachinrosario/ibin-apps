Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT  
A genus of prostrate or scandent herbs, distributed chiefly in Africa . One species occurs in India . A scandent, monoecious herb with perennial root, occurring in the Deccan Peninsula .

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
 Stems angled. sparsely hairy ; tendrils simple, filiform ; leaves orbicular or reniform, sometimes 5-angled, cordate, entire or distantly toothed, membranous ; male flowers pale yellow, 2-4 at the apex of peduncles, female flowers solitary, rarely aggregated ; fruit a deep red ovoid berry. long, beaked : seeds few, ovoid.
