HABIT & HABITAT
Tufted herb, slightly hairy, up to 45 cm in height; rootstock thick, horizontal, covered with fibres. It is found in Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Sikkim, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Manipur and Nagaland.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
 Radical leaves persistent, deeply cordate-ovate, 2.5-7.5 cm in diam., usually toothed or sinuate, long petioled; cauline leaves few, smaller, entire or pinnate. Flowers white, in terminal corymb. Fruits oblong, compressed, hairy or glabrous.
