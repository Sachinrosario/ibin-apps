UTILIZATION:
Chemistry:

  • Hentriacontanone-10, hentriacontane, b-sitosterol, iso-adiantone and adiantone have been identified from Adiantum caudatum by Physio-chemical methods. (Singh et al, 1975).

  • Triterpenes-ferene (m.p. 170-72°C), adiantone (210°C), isoidiantone (236-37°C) were isolated from the Adiantum Caudatum. (Khosa et al, 1978).

  • Phytochemical screening of Adiantum Caudatum have been studied from the dried plant powder which showed the presence of sugar, steroids, triterpenoids and flavanoids. (Shanker and Khare, 1986). C.A. 15119a, 107, 1987.

  • Triterpene alcohol –29-nornopan-22-ol (fig. 1) was isolated from aerial part of Adiantum caudatum and its structure was elucidated along with b-sitosterol glucoside, filic-3-ene also isolated. (Gupta et al, 1990).

  • Three triterpenoids, 8a-hydroxyferna-25,7b-olide (fig. 2), 3a-hydroxy-methoxyfilicane (fig. 3) and 19-ahydroxyferna-7,9(11)-diene (fig. 4) were isolated from the fresh fronds of Adiantum Caudatum along with 13 known triterpenoids were also identified from this fern. (Tusuzuki et al, 2001).
