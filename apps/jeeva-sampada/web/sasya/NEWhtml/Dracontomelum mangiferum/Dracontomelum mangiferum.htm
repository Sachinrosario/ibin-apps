HABIT & HABITAT
A small genus of trees, distributed from south- east Asia to the Pacific.
Generally it is found in the cultivated fields and Plains and tropical evergreen forests.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES
  It is a tall, handsome tree frequent in Andaman Islands. The fruit is  depressed-globose, 1-1.5- inch in diam., yellowish when ripe and edible.

 Economic Potential and Uses

Parts used
Fruit, Flowers and Leaves.

Uses
Ripe fruits are edible; eaten as a sour relish with fish by the Malays. The flowers and leaves are used as flavouring in Moluccas.
