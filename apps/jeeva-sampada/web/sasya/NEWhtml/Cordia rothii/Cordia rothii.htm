DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small tree with gray, furrowed bark. Leaves subopposite, oblanceolate-oblong, rounded at the apex, rough above, more or less pubescent beneath, inconspicuously feather-nerved (not 3-nerved at the base), base tapering into petiole.

Back to Top

 

REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:

Flowers polygamous, in terminal or axillary pedunculate cymes, spikes heads, small, white. Calyx tubular or companulate, smooth or ribbed, veined, abconic at the time of flowering, minutely pubescent outside and with shining silky hairs inside, teeth usually 5, short, often irregular, sometimes obscure. Corolla infundibuliform, hypocrateriform, lobes 4 to 8, imbricate, reflexed or spreading. Stamens as many as the corolla lobes, exserted or included, filaments often hairy at the base. Ovary 4-celled, ovule solitary in each cell, erect, style terminal, elongate, bipartite, stigma capitate or clavate. Fruit usually with viscid pulp, drupaceous, of 4 or fewer 1-seeded cells; putamen often bony. Seeds exalbuminous, cotyledons plicate, radicle short.
