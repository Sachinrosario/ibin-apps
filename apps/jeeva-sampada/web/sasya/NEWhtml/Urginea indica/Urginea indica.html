DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:
Herbs. Bulbs ovoid, 5.5-7.5 x 3.5-5.5 cm, thick, with a long neck. Leaves linear, lanceolate or lorate, 18.5-29.5 x 0.3-0.5 cm, ensiform, acute at apex, appear after flowering. Racemes erect. 15-29 cm long, 4-30-flowered; scapes erect, 30-40cm long, brittle.
REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:   
Flowers reddish-green or variable, 04-1.2 cm long; pedicels 3-7 cm long, spreading or decurved; bracts evanescent. Perianth greenish-white; segments linear-oblong. Capsules ellipsoid, 1-2 x 0.5-1.0 cm. Seeds 4-1 per locule, black, wing scarious. February - July.

UTILIZATION:
Uses:
 Pulp is used as cardio-tonic, stimulent  expectorent, diuretic,deobstruent and in rheumatism. In large dose they are emetic and may cause depression. The alcoholic extract of bulbs are reffered to possess some anticancerous properties. Leaves are eaten during the time of scarcity.
