REPRODUCTIVE BIOLOGY/ BREEDING BEHAVIOR:
   Inflorescence: Numerous large, showy in terminal, erect racemes; Flower: pedicellate, bracteate, showy, hermaphrodite, complete ; Calyx: Glabrous, tube campanulate, upper teeth ovate, acute, divaricate, connate near the base, the 3 lower teeth much narrower, triangular, acuminate; Corolla: Much exserted, yellow with purple tinge, winged with strong rib down the middle of base ;Androecium: Stamens monadelphous, anthers dimorphic ; Gynoecium: Ovary sessile, usually many ovulate, style long abruptly incurved the base, more or less bearded upwards, stigma minute and oblique; Fruit: Pods stalked, linear, oblong slightly broader upwards; Seed: 15-20 . Flowering & Fruiting : September - November. Flowers are pollinated by insects. Seeds are dispersed by wind.

Chemistry:
An alkaloid – crotalaburnine, m.p. 194°C – flavonoids – isovitexin, m.p. 230°C, vitexin-4’-xyloside, vitexin-O-glycoside and apigenin-O-glycoside – along with b-sitosterol from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1960-1969).

New aecopyrrolizidine alkaloids – crotaverrine (fig. 1a)and O-acetyl-crotaverrine (fig. 1b) isolated from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979).

A new flavanol glycosides – kaempferol-7,4’-di-O-L-rhamnopyranoside – isolated from flowers. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)a.

Isolation of a polysaccharide from seeds. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)b.

A new necic acid lactone – 2-methyl-3(2-oxo[5H]-5-hydroxymethyl-5-methyl-furan-3-yl)propanoic acid (fig. 2) – isolated from leaves and its structure elucidated. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1985-1989)c.

Taraxerol, b-sitosterol and linoleic,palmitic, stearic,lauric,oleic, linoleic, arachidic, myristic and ricinoleic acids isolated from fixed oil of stem. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1990-1994).

3’,4’-Di-O-methyl(quercetin-3-O-a-L-rhamnopyranosyl-(1à1)-O-b-D-glucopyranoside has been isolated from the stems of C. verrucosa. (Yadav and Matthews, 1994). C.A. 1995, 122, 101572f.

Faty ascids were isolated from Crotalaria verrucosa plant and analysed by GC. The peaks were identified by comparing the retention values of peaks with those of pure compounds and their quantitative studies. (Singh et al, 1996). C.A. 1997, 127, 119152g.

Six compounds, b-sitosterol, lupeolacetate, apigenin, luteolin-7-glucoside, kaempferol and quercetin were isolated from the leaves of Crotalaria verrucosa. (Naidu et al, 1998). C.A. 1999, 131, 85479j.
