Economic Potential and Uses
UTILIZATION:

Chemistry:
Pharmacognostic studies of rhizome and root carried out. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)a.

Structures of four flavanoids – ugonins A (fig. 1a), B (fig. 1b), C (fig. 2) and D (fig. 3) – isolated from rhizomes. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1970-1979)b.

Stigmasterol, fucosterl and dulcitol isolated from plant used as antipyretic and anti-inflammatory in Chinese medicine. (Rastogi and Mehrotra, 1980-1984).


Pharmacology:


Ethnobotany:
Medicine for blisters on tongue (Leaf, Maheshwari et al, 1980; Maheshwari et al, 1981); Impotency (Root, Maheshwari et al, 1980; Maheshwari et al, 1981); Jaundice (Root, Molla and Roy, 1985); Edible vegetable (Leaf, Islam, 1983; Dagar and Dagar, 1987; Maheshwari et al, 1981).
