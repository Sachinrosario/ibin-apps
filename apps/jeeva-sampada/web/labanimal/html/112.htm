Coat Colour :

Origin :

To Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow from Halfkine Institute, Bombay in 1980.

The inbred line was developed at Cdri during the years

1983-88 from an outbred stock of Syrian Hamster.

 

 

Background :

The Syrian hamster is unique amongst laboratory animals in

that its introduction for research is precisely known. All

laboratory populations have originated from a litter captured

by Professor I. Aharoni while on expedition in Aleppo, Syria

in 1930. Thereafter animals were distributed from this colony

to laboratories throughout the world.

 

Characteristics :
