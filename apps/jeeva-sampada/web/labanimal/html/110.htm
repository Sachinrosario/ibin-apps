Characteristics :

Medium sized, well built with hind parts more rufous. Hairs

on the crown are directed backwards. Face is bare, flesh

coloured and becomes red at estrous in some females. The

tail is long , well haired and pendulous. Body length 19 to 30

cm and adult body weight 4 to 12 kg. One young per litter.

Most commonly used non-human primates are in research and drug testing. Its importance as a general purpose primate is closely related to it long history of use and the data base that has been collected. Most suitable for research in growth, behaviour and psychological studies. Reproductive physiology, drug screening, toxicology, neurology, microbiology, malaria research, vaccine production and tissue culture are some of the other important fields in which the use of rhesus is now well recognized.
