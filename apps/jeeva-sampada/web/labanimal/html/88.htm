Background :

Dr. Margaret Lewis from Wistar  stock. To Bogden and

Characteristics :

Serves as background strain for many congenic strains.

Approximately 30% of all matings are sterile, productivity

0.46/female/week. Docile, incidnce of pancreatic carcinomas,

the majority of which are acinar cell adenocarcinomas. Kidney

fibrosarcomas. More susceptible to induction of autoimmune

disease including experimental Allergic Encephalomyclitis

(EAE). Adjuvant induced Arthritis. Animal become obese on a

high fat diet.
