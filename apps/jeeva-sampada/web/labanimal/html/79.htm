Origin :

To Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow from Central Research Institute, Kasauli in1951.

 

Background :

The first use of the species as laboratory animal was

probably at the beginning of the nineteenth century,

Domesticated in South America. It was brought to

Europe in the sixteenth century and bred as a pet. The

first use of the species as a laboratory animal cannot be

traced with certainty but it was probably at the

beginning of the century.

 

Characteristics :

The anatomy of the guinea pig ear makes it superior to

most animals for otologic studies. Widely used in

immunology, particularly in studies of delayed

hypersensitivity, immune response, Biochemistry,

Toxicology, Physiology and Pharmacology. It is an

animal model for enteric amebeoasis. Also used in

studies of anaphylactic shock, Encephalomyelitis,

tuberculosis , ascorbic acid metabolism and for

diagnosis of bacterial and viral infection.
