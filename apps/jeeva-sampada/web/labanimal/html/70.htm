Origin :

Background :

Curtis 1920 at Columbia Institute of Cancer Research to

Heston in 1949. To N in 1950 at F 51. worldwide origin of F

344 is now traced back to either Heston or NIH.

 

Characteristics :

Resistant to salt hypertension and has large pituitaries.

Susceptible to Cysticercus infection and will grow many

types of transplantable tumours. Used for long term study of

xenobiotic toxicity and is a disease model for:

Phenylketonuria, Intestinal cell tumour of testis, Urinary

bladder carcinoma, Peripheral retinal degeneration and

Esophageal carcinoma.
