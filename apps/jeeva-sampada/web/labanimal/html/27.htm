Origin :

To Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow from Indian Institute of Chemical Biology, Kolkata in 1994.

 

Background :

To McDwell in 1923  who started inbreeding from Bagg.

To Snell in 1932 at F 26. To Andervont in 1935. To NIH, USA in 1935 from Andervont at F 72.

Characteristics :

Good breeding and reproductive life span. Infection with Leishmania tropica yields a suitable model for leishmaniasis . Susceptable to toxoplamosis and Pneumocystis carinii can be introduced by administration of corticosteroids, a low protein diet and tetracycline in drinking water. Recommended host for many transplantable tumours. Can be infected with mammary tumour virus by fostering to C3H. Used for raising monoclonal antibody and hybridoma production. Also used as general purpose strain in many different disciplines.

 

Genetic Profiles :

Idh-1a, Pep-3a, Akp-1b, Car-2b, Pgm-1a, Gpi-1a, Hbbd, Es-1b, Es-2b, Es-3a, Ce-2a and Trf-1b (reported)
