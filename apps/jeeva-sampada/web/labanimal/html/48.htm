Name of Laboratory Animal :

db/db Mice (Mutant Mouse Strain) 

 

Scientific Name :

Mus musculus

 

Common Name :

Mice 

 

Nomenclature :

C57BLKS/Bom-db

Generation Number :

  F 28

Coat Colour :

Black, Obese

 

Origin :

To Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow from Taconic M&B, Denmark

 

Background :

The db mutation arose in a colony of the inbred strain C57BLKS

 (Hummel –1959)

 

 

Characteristics :

The db mutation is located to chromosome 4 and is characterized

by obesity, hyperphagia, temporary increased plasma insulin

level, dgeneration of the β cells and hyperglycemia. Lack of

functional leptin receptor. This leads to a defective leptin

signaling. NIDDM (Non insulin dependent diabeties mellitus)

develops from around week 10, and week 20-25 a severe

destruction of pancreatic β cell leads to low levels of plasma

insulin and a very severe hyperglycemia. The db/db breeding has

to be done by mating the heterozygous db/+ mice, because the

homozygous db/db mouse is infertile.

 

 

Genetic Profiles :

Black a BCD P m. The db mutation is recessive and heterozygotes

are phenotypical normal.
