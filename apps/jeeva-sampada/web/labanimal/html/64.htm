Origin :

To Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow from Hafkine Institute, Mumbai

 

Background :

Cotton rats are the most abundant rodents in the southestern

 United States. Their laboratory usage has come about only

 during the last few  decades.

 

 

Characteristics :

The molar cusps are S shaped (hence the zoological name

 Sigmodon hispidus. Cotton rats are uniformly susceptible to

 Respiratory Syncytical virus infection throughout life thus

 providing a model for long term Pulmonary studies

 previously unavailable. Susceptible to infection with

 Poliovirus type I, II and III. Experimental infections of

 cotton rats have provided disease model for study of

 Fialriasis and other parasitic diseases. Organisms which

 induce diseases in cotton rats include: Litomosoides carinii,

 Burgia malayi and brugia pahangi, Angiostrongylus

 costaricensis and echinococcus mutilocularis.
