Origin :

To Central Drug Research Institute, Lucknow from NIN in 1989

 

Background :

Wistar was the first rat strain, developed at the Wistar

Institute by Helen D King in 1909. The distribution of

breeding stock from the Wistar Institute to other research

laboratories dates back to 1911. However, inspite of this

Scientific  root numerous genetic variations exists in Wistar

stock worldwide.

 

 

Characteristics :

The animals are gentle and easily handled. Highly

susceptible to nephritis. Mammary tumour incidence is

moderate. No tumours of testis or uterus at any age.

Pitutiary tumour occurs in females but not in males below

18 months. Used for research in Pharmacology, Toxicology,

Physiology and endocrinology. Also suitable for research in

microsurgery and transplantation procedures.
