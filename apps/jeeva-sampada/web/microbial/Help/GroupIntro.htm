General Information
  • Introduction page (html page) for a Group (Algae, Bacteria, Fungi etc.) of Microorganism. It consists of:
    1. Few images that belong to that Group.
    2. Brief description of the Group.
  • To view the details of a Group:
    Click on the name of the Group(given at the left hand side of the page).
  • To view the Genus Details of a Particular Group:
    Click on the "Forward" Arrow.
  • To view the Previous Page:
    Click on the "Backward" Arrow.
