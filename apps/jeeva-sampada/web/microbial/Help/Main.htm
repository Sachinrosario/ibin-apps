• India is endowed with enormous biodiversity because of its unique location representing almost all ecological niche of the world. So far as plant and animal diversity is concerned there exist some base data although they are far from complete. Data related to microbial diversity is practically non-existent because of technical problems related to study of microbial diversity. Whatever scanty data are available are incomplete, diffused and scattered. As a result we do not have even partial knowledge regarding microbial recourses of India.
                              Microbial diversity immense importance and influences various aspects of all life form including human. Understanding interplay and the roles played by microorganisms depend on the knowledge we have about them. It is, therefore essential that an inventory of microbial resources be prepared so as to utilize them for the economic well being of the nation.
Objective:

The main objective of the project is to collect all available information on microbial resources which are scattered and unorganized. Collected data will be computerized and analyzed by experts in specific areas and finally a database on microbial resources will be created.
The inventory is based on both primary as well as secondary information collected by five regional coordinators (North, South, East, West, Central) initially comprising 21 investigators (Six Principal Investigators and Fifteen Co-investigators) later, data collection, data correction and new entries were done by 12 of the original 21 investigators.
