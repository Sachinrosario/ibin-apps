General Information
  • It provides list of Species and Strains that belong to a particular Group and Genus.
  • It displays the selected Group and Genus name.
  • If the list of Species is empty then:
    Click on the "Click Here To Get List Of Species" BUTTON to get the list of Species Under Selected Group and Genus(It will take some time to fill the list)
  • Select a Species name from the list.
    • One can select the Species name :
      1. Manually by scrolling the list for the Species name.
      2. Automatically by typing the name of the Species in the box given above the list.
  • After Selecting the Species Name:
    Click on the "Search Records" Button
    To get all the strains that belongs to the selected Group, Genus and Species.(Search will take some time).
  • To view full datails of a particular strain:
    Select the particular strain from the grid.
    • Click on the "Preview" Button
      (It will take some time to generate the preview page)
  • To view the Page that contains the Genus Information:
    Click on the "Backward" Arrow.
