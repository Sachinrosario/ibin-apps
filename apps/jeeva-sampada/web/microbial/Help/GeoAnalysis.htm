General Information
  • Geographical analysis can be done in two ways:
    1. State wise distribution.
    2. District wise distribution.
State Wise Analysis
  • For State wise geographical distribution follow the steps given as under:
    • Step1:Select the state name either from the Map of India or from the dropdown box named as state.
      After selecting a particular state's name the name of the state in the map will start blinking.
    • Step 2:Click on "GO" Button to get the Group Distribution for selected State.
    • Step 3:Select a particular Group from the grid.
    • Step 4:Click on "GO" Button to get Genus Distribution for selected State and Group.
    • Step 5:Select a particular Genus from the grid.
    • Step 6:Click on "GO" Button to get Species Distribution for selected State, Group and Genus.
    • Step 7:Select a particular Species from the grid.
    • Step 8:Click on "GO" Button to get Strains Distribution for selected State, Group, Genus and Species.
    • Step 9:By selecting a particular strain one can get by clicking on the "Preview" Button the details of that strain.
  • One can go back and forth (by clicking on "Forward and Back arrows or by clicking on the Tabs named Group/Genus/Species/Strains) for the analysis of another Genus/Species/Strains i.e.
    • Repeat Steps 3-9 for analysis of another Group.
    • Repeat Step 5-9 for analysis of another Genus.
    • Repeat Step 7-9 for analysis of another Species.
    Note: For distribution of another state repeat step 1 to 9 in series.
District Wise Analysis
  • For State and District wise geographical distribution follow the steps given as under:
    • Step1:Select the state name either from the Map of India or from the dropdown box named as state.
      After selecting a particular state's name the name of the state in the map will start blinking.
    • Step2:Select the District name from the dropdown box named as District.
    • Step3:Click on "GO" Button to get the Group Distribution for selected state and district.
    • Step4:Select a particular Group from the grid .
    • Step5:Click on "GO" Button to get Genus Distribution for selected State, District and Group.
    • Step6:Select a particular Genus from the grid .
    • Step7:Click on "GO" Button to get Species distribution for selected State, District, Group and Genus.
    • Step8:Select a particular Species from the grid.
    • Step9:Click on "GO" Button to get Strains distribution for selected State, District, Group, Genus and Species.
    • Step 10:By selecting a particular strain one can get by clicking on the "Preview" Button the details of that strain.
  • One can go back and forth (by clicking on "Forward and Back arrows or by clicking on the Tabs named Group/Genus/Species/Strains) for the analysis of another Genus/Species/Strains i.e.
    • Repeat Steps 4-10 for analysis of another Group.
    • Repeat Step 6-10 for analysis of another Genus.
    • Repeat Step 8-10 for analysis of another Species.
    Note: For distribution of another state/District repeat step 1 to 10 in series.
