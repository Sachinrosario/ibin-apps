General Information
  • Analysis can be performed:
    1. State Wise
    2. Habitat Wise
    3. Important from point of view wise.
    4. Preservation Methods wise
    5. Hazardous Information wise
    6. Zone Wise.
State wise Analysis
  • Select the option "State Wise Distribution " from "Report" Menu.
  • Click on "Build Report" Button.
  • It will give the following:
    1. Total number of strains for each Group under each State.
    2. Total number of strains Group wise
    3. Total number of strains State wise.
  • To get a detailed Report:
    1. Select a column from the grid.
    2. Click on "Show Details" Button.
  • One can take a print out of the build report by clicking on the "Print" Button .
Habitat Based Analysis
  • Select the option "Habitat" from "Report" Menu.
  • Click on "Build Report" Button.
  • It will give the following report:
    1. Total strains that have habitat in Air
    2. Total strains that have habitat in Water.
    3. Total strains that have habitat in Soil.
    4. Total strains that have habitat in Plant Parts or Animal origin.
  • To get a detailed Report:
    1. Select a column from the grid.
    2. Click on "Show Details" Button.
  • One can take a print out of the build report by clicking on the "Print" Button .
Important from Point of view Based
  • Select the option "Important Point of View" from Report Menu.
  • Click on "Build Report" Button.
  • It will give the following:
    1. Total number of strains that are important from Industrial Point of View.
    2. Total number of strains that are important from Agriculture point of view.
    3. Total number of strains that are important from Environmental point of view.
    4. Total number of strains that are important from Medical point of view.
    5. Total number of strains that are important from scientific point of view.
  • To get a detailed Report:
    1. Select a column from the grid.
    2. Click on "Show Details" Button.
  • One can take a print out of the build report by clicking on the "Print" Button .
Preservation Methods Based Analysis
  • Select the option "Preservation Methods" from "Report" Menu.
  • Click on "Build Report" Button.
  • It will give the following:
    1. Total number of strains that are preserved In Liquid Nitrogen, not preserved In Liquid Nitrogen and number of strains about whom it is not known whether they can be preserved in liquid nitrogen or not.
    2. Total number of strains that are preserved By Freeze Drying, not preserved By Freeze drying and number of strains about whom it is not known whether they can be preserved By Freeze Drying or not.
    3. Total number of strains that are preserved In Active Culture, not preserved In Active Culture and number of strains about whom it is not known whether they can be preserved In Active Culture or not.
    4. Total number of strains that are preserved In Mineral Oil, not preserved In Mineral Oil and number of strains about whom it is not known whether they can be preserved In Mineral Oil or not.
    5. Total number of strains that are preserved At 4 Degrees, not preserved At 4 Degrees and number of strains about whom it is not known whether they can be preserved At 4 Degrees or not.
    6. Total number of strains that are preserved under Any Other Methods.
  • To get a detailed Report:
    1. Select a column from the grid.
    2. Click on "Show Details" Button.
  • One can take a print out of the build report by clicking on the "Print" Button .
Hazardous Based Analysis
  • Select the option "Hazardous Information" from "Report" Menu.
  • Click on "Build Report" Button.
  • It will give the following:
    1. Total number of strains that are hazardous to Human, not hazardous to Human and number of strains whose hazardous information for human is not known yet.
    2. Total strains that are hazardous to Plants, not hazardous to Plants and number of strains whose hazardous information for Plants are not known yet.
    3. Total strains that are hazardous to Animals, not hazardous to Animals and number of strains whose hazardous information for Animals are not known yet.
    4. Total strains those are hazardous to Environment, not hazardous to Environment and number of strains whose hazardous information for Environment is not known yet.
    5. Total strains that is hazardous to Other Microbes, not hazardous to Other Microbes and number of strains whose hazardous information for Other Microbes is not known yet.
  • To get a detailed Report:
    1. Select a column from the grid.
    2. Click on "Show Details" Button.
  • One can take a print out of the build report by clicking on the "Print" Button .
Zone Based Analysis
  • Select the option "Zonal Distribution " from "Report" Menu.
  • Click on "Build Report" Button.
  • It will give the following:
    1. Total number of strains for each group under each zone.
    2. Total number of strains Group wise
    3. Total number of strains Zone wise.
  • To get a detailed Report:
    1. Select a column from the grid.
    2. Click on "Show Details" Button.
  • One can take a print out of the build report by clicking on the "Print" Button .
Filtering and Detailed Report
  • For each strain it will give its Group, Genus, Species, Family, Isolated From State,District,City and Village.
  • One Can Filter the strains.
    • Data can be filtered on the basis of different combinations of Zone/Group/Genus/Species/State.
      • Select Zone/Group//Genus/Species/State from there respective dropdown boxes.
      • If anyone of the dropdown box is left balnk then it will not be counted under filtering criteria.
      • Click on "Filter" Button to filter the data.
  • One can take a print out of the build report by clicking on the "Print" Button.
  • To view full datails of a particular strain:
    Select the particular strain from the grid.
    • Click on the "Preview" Button
      (It will take some time to generate the preview page)
