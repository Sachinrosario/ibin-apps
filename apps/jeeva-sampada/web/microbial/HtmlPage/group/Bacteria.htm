Bacteria

             Bacteria are present in all the conceivable ecological niches on the earth; from cold antartica to hot springs, very acidic to alkaline enviornments, in soil, water sediments in the air, on and in animals and plants and many of them can live without oxygen. To survive in diverse envoirnment bacteria have evolved a variety of metabolism. They perform photosynthesis (Cyanobacteria), can live on organic matters and some survive as parasites. They also display diversity of shapes and sizes.
             They play important roles in cycling of C,N,O,S,P and in combatting pollution. They produce a large number of life-saving antibiotics, industrially important enzymes and some are vital components of processed food like curd,cheese etc.Only few of them are pathogenic to human and other animals and plants.

             At present about six thousand species of bacteria are known which represent a tiny fraction of total bacterial diversity. Application of new tools and techniques is helping discovery of new bacteria.
