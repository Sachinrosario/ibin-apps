GENUS :Vibrio
The members of this genus are Gram-negative, rod shaped bacteria that are curved to form a comma shape or an incomplete spiral.They show motility by mens of a single polar flagellum.They are capable of growing in the presence as well in the absence of oxygen.The species of Vibrio are widely distributed in marine as well as in the other aquatic environment and few species infect marine animals.Some Vibrio species causes intestinal human infections such as diarrhea and cholera.Its important members are Vibrio cholrae and Vibrio aerogenes.This genus comprises of 70 species.
