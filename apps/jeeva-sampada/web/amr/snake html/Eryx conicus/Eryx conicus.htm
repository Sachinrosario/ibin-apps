Other Biological Details  

HABIT & HABITAT                       

This nocturnal sluggish snake is a strong burrower in loose sand or loose silty soil; this is a pugnacious species and bites savagely, inflicting painful bites; 

Food :  food comprises small mammals, birds, reptiles amphibious and large insects; it kills the prey by constriction.

Distribution:        Whole of India : Gujarat : Bhavnagar, Banaskantha, Vadora, Surendranagar, Dangs and Kachchh districts.

 

  Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       Fairly common

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Length : This is a robust snake, body is cylindrical which tapers abruptly. Just anterior to vent, total length not exceeding 950 mm. in females and 590 mm in males; 

Head :  Head is slightly distinct from the neck. Hemipenis is not forked, sulcus bifurcates near the tip of the organ, strongly flounced and the folds are arranged in oblique series; distally these hemipenal folds unite and form large cup-shaped structures.

Tail :  tail is very short and blunty pointed; 

Eyes :   Eyes are small but distinct, surrounded by 10-15 minute scales. 

Scales :   Scale rows at the middle of body are 40-55; ventral scales, 161-196; subcaudal scales 16-24; anal is small undivided, flanked by one or two small plates on each side;

Colouration : Dorsal colouration is yellowish, brownish or grayish mixed with a dorsal series of prominent dark-brown, black-edged spots; those spots are generally fused with each other and lateral series is irregularly scattered; most of the individuals are with a dark stripe behind the eye; ventrum is yellowish or whitish, in many individuals the outer scale-rows are with small brown spots or with scattered gray flecks. 

Breeding biology :  it is viviparous snake and produces about 6-11 young at a time, the breeding season starts in May and extends up to end of July;
