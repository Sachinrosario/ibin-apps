Taxonomic Details

Generic Details :  This genus includes slender bodied snakes in which head is quite distinct from the neck; head is flat and obtusely pointed anteriorly, nasals are divided and bear large nostrils; eyes are large, with round pupil; supralabials are 9-10, 4-6 or 5-6 or 5-7 touch the eye; body scales are smooth or feebly keeled, with epical pits, usually in 17 rows, in certain individuals 15 or 18-19 oblique rows are also present; ventral and subcaudal shields are with a suture like lateral keel and a notch on each side corresponding to the keel; the last and in rare cases the penultimate ventral shield is divided; anal and subcaudal shields are divided; posterior dorsal aspect of vertebra is provided with or devoid of hypapophysis. The genus includes 5 species, four are available in Indian region.

Other Biological Details  

HABIT & HABITAT                

It is most agile, bold, diurnal, arboreal snake with tremendous climbing power and remarkable power of flight. It is found in and around human surroundings. Its food comprises small lizards, mammals, birds, snakes and insects; it has got special liking for Geckos. The snake accomplished the flight which is fact is gliding by climbing on the tall trees. The snake straightens its body, hollows the ventral surface, moves quickly with a speed and falls at an angle to the ground. In this process it keeps the body quite rigid all the time. It springs and leaps from branch to branch in a tree. coiling itself in preparation and then suddenly straightening the whole body out as it leaps across. It can cover the distances up to 4 feet and also in an upward direction. It breeds in May or June and lays, 6-12 elongate eggs in a single clutch.      

Distribution:       Gujarat : Surat and Dangs districts, Goa, Kerala, Bihar; Uttar Pradesh; Andamans and Orissa.

Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       Vulnerable on account of habitat loss during the recent years in many parts of its range.

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

Length : The total length of golden tree snake reaches up to 1100 mm.  Hemipenis extends up to the 34th caudal plate, undivided, composed of many folds and out of one sulcus passes, the area proximal to the olique folds is with large number of spines.

Body : Body elongate; head distinct from the neck; eyes are large with round pupil; tail very long.

Scales :    Body scales are feebly keeled, outermost rows are smooth and with apical pits, in 15 : 17 : 14 rows; ventral shields are 207-230; subcaudal shields are 118-138; anal shield is divided; 

Colouration :    Dorsal colouration is most variable but generally with red or orange spots or markings and a longitudinal series of oval or flower shaped flecks, which are in some individuals are separated from one another by a pair of black cross-bars; lateral aspects are greenish, each scale is with a black marginal tinge and with a black median streak; subcaudal shields are generally with black flecks, which in some individuals unite and form two longitudinal lines. 

Breeding Biology : It breeds in May or June and lays, 6-12 elongate eggs in a single clutch.
