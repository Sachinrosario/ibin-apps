Conservation status:       A rare snake.

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Length : This small uropeltid with subacuminate snout hardly exceeds 210 mm. in total length and with a body diameter of 7 mm. 

Tail :  tail is moderately compressed, its dorsal scales are smooth or feebly bi-or tricarinate; terminal tail scute is simple, compressed and ends in to a point.

Scales :   Ventrals are 139-145, two times broader than the adjacent body scales; subcaudal scales are 7-10;

Colouration : Dorsal colour is blackish-brown; ventrals and outer row of scales are red in living snakes but fades to white in alcohol, in preserved specimens.
