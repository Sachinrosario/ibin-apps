Other Biological Details  

HABIT & HABITAT                       

These are the fast burrowing snakes, which form interconnected tunnels in the termite infested areas. 

Food :  Their main food comprises the termites and small soil arthropods and their eggs.

Distribution:         Jodhpur district, Rajasthan

 

  Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       Fairly common.

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

Length :   Reaches to a maximum length of 300 mm (Standard length 280 mm, tail 20 mm)

Body :      This Thread snake is slender with prominent, hooked snout, the lower surface of which (in front of the mouth) is concave.  Total length of the snake is between 80-110 times of the  diameter of its body. Head is two times broader than the rostral, which generally extends to the level of the eyes; all the head shields are considerably enlarged; 

Eyes :   Eyes are distinctly visible and are placed in the ocular shields; 

Scales :   Scales round the body are 14

Colouration :  Dorsal colouration is brownish with a pink tinge; ventrum is slightly paler.
