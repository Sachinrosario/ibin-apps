Taxonomic Details

pit vipers

Genus  Trimeresurus Lacepede 1804.

This genus which comprises more than 22 species is distributed in India, Vietnam, China, Japan, Malaysia, Indo-Australian archipelago as far as Timor, the Philippine Islands and Celebes. The genus includes pit vipers with head is well demarcated from the neck; nostrils are small and pierced in nasal shield, eyes are moderately large, with vertical pupil; second supralabial is united with the loreal and form the anterior wall of loreal pit; upper surface of head is covered with small scales or larger shields; 2 or 3 postoculars are present, a deep pit is present on the lateral aspect of the face, between the preoculars and the loreal; a pair of large anterior genials is available, generally posterior pair is not present; scales of the body are in 17-31 rows; ventral shields are rounded; tail is moderately large or short; subcaudal shields are paired or in certain species, in rare cases are single; anal shield is not divided.

Range : South-eastern Asia : the Indo-Australian archipelago; the Philippine Islands; Celebes; and (if Bothrops is not regarded as a separate genus) South America.

Other Biological Details  

HABIT & HABITAT               Oviparous; lays 6-18 eggs, 26-40 x 23-24 mm. in size, which are concealed in a hole or hollow in the ground or in vegetable debris. The parent guards them until the young emerge.      

Distribution:       The Eastern Himalayas, Assam.

Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       Fairly Common

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

Length : The total length in males 490 mm., tail 80 mm. and in females 1100 mm., tail 150 mm.

Body : Hemipenis extends up to the 12th caudal plate; forked at the junction of the proximal third and distal two-thirds; calyculate distally; spinose proximally, the area covered by the spines occupies about twice the area covered by the calyces.

Scales :   Head is covered with small unequal, smooth,feebly imbricate scales. Scales in 23 (rarely 25) : 23 (rarely 21 or 25) : 19 (rarely 21) rows. Ventrals 127-176. Subcaudals 21-62, paired, or some or all of them single.

Colouration :   The dorsal color is light or dark brown above with large irregular, squarish patches or spots of black upon the back, and smaller ones upon the sides. Head dark brown above, pale or yellowish on the sides, with a dark brown temporal streak. Lower parts yellowish, either uniform or mottled with brown.

Breeding Biology :      Oviparous; lays 6-18 eggs, 26-40 x 23-24 mm. in size, which are concealed in a hole or hollow in the ground or in vegetable debris. The parent guards them until the young emerge.
