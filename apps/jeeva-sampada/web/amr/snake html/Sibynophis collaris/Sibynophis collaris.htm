Taxonomic Details

Genus 30. Sibynophis Fitzinger 1843

This genus comprises the hill snakes with the following characters : Head slightly differentiated from the neck; teeth are very numerous and closely set, equal in size, bayonet shaped. Body is cylindrical with smooth scales, in 17 rows.

 

Scientific Name :  

Sibynophis collaris (Gray, 1853)


Family :

Classification:               Genus . Sibinophis Fitzinger1843


Synonyms:                           


Common Names:              Collared black headed snake

Back to Top

Other Biological Details   The main food of this snake consists of Lizards and skinks

HABIT & HABITAT        Terrestrial, rock dwelling, diurnal snake, feeds on small lizard especially on small lizards, skinks              

Distribution:     Mishimi Hills ( Assam), Simla (Himachal Pradesh)

  Altitude:  Inhabiting up to a height of 10,000 feet in Eastern Himalaya ( Assam)
                          

Conservation status:       Vulnerable on account of habitat loss

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

Length : Reaches 760 mm, tail 235 mm in females, Males are smaller in legth. 

Body :  Hemipenis extends upto 9th caudal plate. 

Scales :   Ventrals 156 - 186, Subcaudals 100 - 125, Anals-2

Colouration :   Dorsal colouraration light brown, dorso median series of dark spots, Head and nape dark brown, lips yellowish, ventrum yellowish, 

Breeding Biology : Oviparous, 2-4 eggs in each clutch
