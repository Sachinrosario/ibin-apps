Other Biological Details  

HABIT & HABITAT                      

Distribution:      Sikkim, West Bengal (Darjeeling district)

  Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       Rare snake known by five examples

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Length :  The maximum length of this snake reaches up to 600 mm., tail 225 mm., in males and 545 mm., tail 205 mm., in females. 

Body : The head is quite distinct from the neck and is considerably depressed; snout projects out and almost two times longer than the eye; 

 

Scales :   Body scales in 15 : 15 : 13 ross; ventrals are 148-154; subcaudals 116-134; anals are 2.

Colouration :  Dorsal colour is greyish; ventrum is light grey with yellowish tinge; lateral aspect of head is with a broad black stripe which extends on the anterior part of body and ultimately merges with the body colour; outer margins of the ventral shields are grey, thus forming a stripe; a less prominent and thin median stripe may be present in few individuals.
