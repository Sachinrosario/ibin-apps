Other Biological Details  

HABIT & HABITAT                       Burrowing, Insectivorous or feeding on Annelid worms with poor vision and without teeth in the lower jaw

 

Back to Top

 

Distribution:         South India (Ramnad).

 

  Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       It is a rare snake and is known by few examples. Possibly all the examples of Typhlops psammeces are the variants of Typhlops braminus and the exact status depends on the availability of more material.

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Size :  This snake is comparatively more slender than braminus reaching to a maximum length of about 145 mm. 

Colouration :  Dorsal colouration is deep brown; ventrum is lighter; tail is whitish-brown.

Other characters are similar to braminus, with which it differs in have more slenderness of body; total length of the snake is between 55-75 times of the diameter of its body; increased number of transverse rows of scales i.e. between 370-400.
