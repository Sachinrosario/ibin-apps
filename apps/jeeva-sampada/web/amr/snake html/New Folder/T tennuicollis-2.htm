Conservation status:       A rare worm-snake, known by 3 examples only.

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

The description of this worm snake is based upon a much broken up, discoloured type specimen of T. theobaldianus as such the length of snake can not be ascertained. Other characters indicate that the snout is strongly projecting and rounded; eyes are not prominent; 

Tail :  tail is short, ending in a fine point; 

Scales :  22 scales round the body. 480-520 transverse rows of scales on the whole body length

Size :  Total length of snake is between 65-70 times of the diameter of its body; 

Colouration :   Colouration can not be ascertained.
