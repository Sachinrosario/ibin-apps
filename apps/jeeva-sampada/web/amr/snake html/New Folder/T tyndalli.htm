Conservation status:       A rare snake, known from 3 type specimens only.

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Size :  This slender worm snake reaches to a maximum length of 175 mm. Total length of the snake is about 50 times of the diameter of its body.

Body :  Its snout is strongly projecting, rounded; lateral nostrils; eyes are not distinctly visible;

Colouration :  Snake is deep yellow in colour.  

Tail :  tail is small, rounded and is devoid of spine; 

Scales : scales round the body are 18;
