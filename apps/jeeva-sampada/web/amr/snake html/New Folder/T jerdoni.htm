Distribution:         

Eastern Himalayas (Sikkim, Darjeeling and Duars) North-East India (Pashighat, Abor, Khasi Hills). Burma : Lashio in Upper Burma and Pegu.

   Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       Rare.

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Size :   This worm snake reaches to a maximum length of 280 mm;

body is slender, tubular; snout rounded and strongly projecting;

Colouration :  Dorsal colouration is from dark-brown to black, ventrum is light brown; snout and anal region whitish.

Tail :  Tail is short, ending into a spicule;

Scales :  22 scales round the body; total length of the snake is between 35-45 times of the diameter of its body; 260-280 transverse rows of scales on the whole body length.
