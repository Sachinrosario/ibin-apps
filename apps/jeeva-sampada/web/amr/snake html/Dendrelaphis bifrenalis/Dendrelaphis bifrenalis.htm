HABIT & HABITAT      

Distribution:       Southern India (Trivandrum, Travancore).                

Altitude:  
                          

Conservation status:       Rare Snake

 

              

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Length : The maximum length reaches up to the 1030 mm., tail 380 mm., not recorded in males. 

Scales :    Vertebrals strongly enlarged broader than the outer row of scales at mid-body. Scales in 15:15:11 or 9 rows. Ventrals 154-176. subcaudals 144-175. Anals 2.

Colouration :   Dorsal colouration is bronze brown or gray with a greenish yellow line along the outer row of scales. Head with a black lateral stripe, passing on to the neck, where it breaks up in to oblique bars. Upper lip and chin yellowish.  Ventrals and subcaudals between the  lateral keels greenish yellow, outside the keels brownish or bluish.
