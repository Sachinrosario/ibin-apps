Other Biological Details      Threat : Suspected to be threatened on account of habitat destruction and over exploitation of adults for meat and due to international trade.

HABIT & HABITAT

                       Inhabits low rocky hill area, has a great tolerance for heat, found on open hill sides.  Herbivorous and depends on vegetables and fruits.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

Size :               Small species reaching slightly over 27 cm inlength

 Body :             Head is of moderate size, upper jaw tricuspid and horny margins of the  jaw feebly denticulated. The skin at the top of the head is with a pair of prefrontal shields and a large frontal often subdivided; rest of the head is covered with smaller shields. 

 Limbs :            Fore limbs with large, imbricate pointed scales, heels with large flat scales, a patch of enlarged flattened scales on the hinder portion of the thigh.

 Tail :               Tail longer and curved in the male than the female and  terminating in a horny tubercle. 

Carapace :   elongated, narrow, flattened on the vertebral portion of the adults.   Marginals are generally serrated and flared on the posterior aspect of the carapace.  Vertebral shields five in number, costal shields four, marginals twenty two. Plastron large, truncate anteriorly, deeply notched posteriorly.

 Colouration :  Dorsal colouration is yellow or greenish yellow with variable amounts of black markings. Large black spots are present  on marginals.  Head whitish yellow but during breeding season a reddish tinge is developed on front side of the head in males .  In juveniles carapace is pure yellow with few black spots.

 

Breeding system:

.Nesting :         Generally two t o four large eggs laid in a single clutch.
