Other Biological Details

HABIT & HABITAT

               Entirely aquatic inhabiting stagnant ponds, canals and backwaters hardly emerge for basking. Avoid flowing water. Herbivorous. Most docile.

 

Back to Top

 

Distribution:

                  Occurs in Ganga, Brahmaputra and Sind River system ranging from Sind (Pakistan) to          Sylhet ( Bangladesh).

 

Extent of Occurrence
                                     

Area of occurrence:   Assam, Bihar, Meghalaya, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal

Conservation status:                    Extremely vulnerable


DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Size  :               Exhibits sexual dimorphism, female reach 65 cm in length, while males reach a maximum of 20 cm.

 

Body  :             Head is moderately large, pointed and extremely projecting snout.  The skin of the posterior portion of the head  is divided into large number of small  scales arranged in transverse rows.  

Limbs :            Limbs are with fully and broadly webbed digits and have a narrow transeversly enlarged scales.  Fore limbs are provided with five claws. 

Tail :                Tail remains short throughout its life. 

 Carapace  : Moderately depressed, unicarinate, the posterior portion of each vertebral shield has a knob like structure.  Posterior margin is also slightly serrated,  Plastron is intimately fused with the carapace by a suture.

 Colouration :   Dorsal colouration is dark brown – black with yellow margin in juveniles and adults.  Yellow margin fades out in older adults.  Keel and the inner margin of the first three costals are  blackish to dark black. Ventrum pale yellow with two large black spots on each side of the plastral bridge.  Abdominal scutes are with large black spots. Head dark brown to black, covered with orange yellow bands on top of the snout.  Limbs are brownish, margin with yellow behind. 

Breeding system:

Nesting :         Not much is known, however eggs laid are oval and deposits in a single clutch of four to six.
