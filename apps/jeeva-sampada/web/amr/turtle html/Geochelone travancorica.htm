Other Biological Details     Threat : Suspected to be threatened by habitat loss and human consumption. Flesh is relished by the local tribal people in Kerala.  Often located in Cardamom plantations of Kerala and easily detectable by the plantation workers.  

HABIT & HABITAT

                       Prefers hilly rain forest terrain upto an altitude of 1000 m.

Distribution:         

 

Extent of Occurrence                  

Area of occurrence:          Travancore hills, Cochin forests and western slopes of Western Ghats. The southern extremity of the tortoise is hilly areas of Mercara ( Coorg ) in Karnataka             

Altitude:Prefers hilly rain forest terrain upto an altitude of 1000 m.
Population:                                     

Conservation status:             Listed in Appendix – II of the CITES             

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Size :               Moderately large terrestrial hill tortoise reaching up to 30 cm in length and weighs up to 3.75 kg. 

Body :             Head moderate size and its upper jaw is tricuspid, horny margins of the jaws feebly denticulated.  Top of the head covered with small and moderate shields.  

Limbs :            Outer side of the forelimb is provided with distinctly large, imbricate pointed scales. 

 Tail :               Tail is moderately long and terminates into a horny tubercle. It is longer and curved in male than in female.

 Carapace :  Rigid, oval,  more or less dome shaped with slightly flared  margin.  Nuchal shield absent, vertebrals five; costal four and  marginals twenty three.  Plastron large, truncate anteriorly deeply notched posteriorly. 

 Colouration :  Colouration of the shield is variable from light brown, greenish yellow or pale yellow.  Shields are generally with large dark blotch and edged with a lighter band or yellow with dark band around. In most cases plastron is having black spots on the abdominal shields. Coluration of the head is light greenish with a tinge of yellow. Limbs with numerous   irregular light black spots.

 

 

Breeding system:

.Nesting :         Breeding season extends from November up to January. Lays a single clutch of  six small or medium oval  eggs.  Herbivorous, the food consists of vegetable material and has a liking for mushrooms.
