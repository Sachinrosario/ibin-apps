DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 Size :               Small tortoise, reaching a maximum length of 17 cm. 

 Body :             Head moderately small, snout shorter than the orbit and truncate, skin of the posterior portion of the head is divided into large shields.  Skull with a bony temporal arch.  

Limbs :            Limbs  are with half webbed digits, fore arm is completely covered with large imbricate pointed, horny shields. 

 Tail :               Tail is moderately long, its base and the adjacent skin of the thighs with pointed tubercles. 

Carapace :  Strongly tricarinate, generally flattened on the vertebral region, anterior and posterior margin are serrated.  Neural plate hexagonal.  Plastron smaller than the opening of the shell, truncate anteriorly and notched posteriorly, united to the carapace by a ligamentous tissue.  

Colour :           Dorsum uniformly light brown, lateral most part of the costal shield is dark brown.  Ventrum yellowish brown, with a dark spot on each plastral shield.  Head brownish with dark vermiculations, temporal region with dark edged light spots.

 

Breeding system:

Nesting :             Lays 1 – 3 eggs in single clutch
