HABIT & HABITAT

                       The subspecies prefers to live in shallow muddy ditches, lakes and marshes.  Carnivorous turtle and feeds  mainly on frogs,fishes, shrimps and snails.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 

Size :               Small, flat turtle, whose length rarely exceeds  more than 24 cm. 

 Body :             Head is moderately large, short and broad snout, its length is less than the length of the eye opening. 

 Limbs :            Limbs are fully webbed, with three claws in each foot.

 Tail :               Tail is very short.

 Carapace :      Carapace and plastron is covered with a continous  sheet of soft skin and their callosities are finely granulated. Lateral and hinder portions of the carapace are most flexible. Plastron with soft, semicircular flaps, which accomadate the retracted hind feet to be totally concealed, front portion of the plastron is quite flexible and permits the closure of the anterior shell opening quite effectively.  

Colouration :  Olive brown above, carapace is grey green with numerous black bordered yellow spots, irregularly arranged and with a light yellow marginal rim.

 

Breeding system:

.Nesting :         Nothing is known about its breeding biology except it lays 12 eggs in a single clutch.
