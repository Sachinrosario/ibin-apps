DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

 Body :      Head is moderately small, snout pointed, shorter than the orbit and project much beyond the lower jaw.  Skin of the posterior portion of the head is divided into shields.  Upper jaw is not notched in the middle.  Limbs are with fully and broadly webbed digits and have transeversly enlarged scales. 

Limbs :     Fore limbs are provided with five claws.

 Tail :       Tail remains short throughout  life and exhibits no difference between juveniles and   adults. 

 Carapace :  Depressed, with obtuse interrupted, vertebral keel, its posterior margin may be regular                    or in certain cases feebly serrated.  Plastron is equal in its length with carapace.  Strongly                  angulated laterally in juveniles, truncate anteriorly.  Hind lobe of the plastron narrower                  than the shell opening, notched posteriorly, shorter than the width of the plastral bridge. 

 Colouration :  Dorsal colouration is pale olive, Vertebral keel is blackish, ventrum is yellow, and each                  plastral shields is provided with  dark brown tinge. In many individuals the shields of                  plastron are completely black with narrow white borders. Top of the head is black.            Prominent reddish or pink spots are present behind the eyes.  Neck provided with longitudinal stripes.

 

Breeding system:

Nesting :             Lays five to eight eggs on the sand banks of rivers in  single clutch.
