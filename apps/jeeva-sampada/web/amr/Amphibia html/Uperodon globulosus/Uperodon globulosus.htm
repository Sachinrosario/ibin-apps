DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium sized baloon shaped frog.  Stouter and more strongly globular shape and longer snout.  Skin smooth above, smooth or wrinkled below.  Anal region granular an occipital fold and an indistinct fold extending from the eye to shoulder.  Head small with rounded snout and beady eyes.  Canthus rostrallis distinct and obtuse.  Interorbital width is remarkably wide, being three times the diameter of the eye.  Nostril equidistant from the eye and the tip of the snout.  Tumpanum hidden.  Fingers free.  Hind limbs short, tibio-tarsal articulation not reaching the shoulder.  Toes with rudiments of web.  Two shovel shaped metatarsal tubercles present of which the inner one is much larger than the outer. 

Colour: Dorsal surface of the body is uniform brown in colour and ventral surface white.  During the breeding season the ventral surface becomes yellowish.
