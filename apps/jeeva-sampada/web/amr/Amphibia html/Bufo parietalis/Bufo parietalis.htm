DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Large-sized toads; cranial ridges well developed, parietal ridge prominent and obliquely directed inwards, parotoid glands large, elongated; tympanum two-thirds of eye; dorsum with irregular cornified warts; toes half-webbed.

 Colour: Dorsum of uniform brown, flanks darker; beneath marbled with brown on white.  The live colouration described by Pillai (1986) is as follows :  Females light brownish yellow on head and back; side of head and body marbled with black.  Limbs banded.  Venter marbled on throat, chest and front half of belly.  Some of the larger females, however, has a uniformly dark brown crown and dorsum.  Breeding males are almost brightly coloured, the dominant colour being yellow.  Sides marbled with light or reddish brown.  Venter not marbled.  The yellow is lacking in non-breeding males collected from under stones which are brownish above.
