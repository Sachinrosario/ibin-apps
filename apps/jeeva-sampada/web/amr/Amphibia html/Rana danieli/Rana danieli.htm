DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium sized frog.  Skin finely granulated.  A prominent glandular dorso-lateral fold extending from the posterior region of the eyes and ending near the vent.  Another glandular fold extending posteriorly from below the eyes to the shoulder which ends in a glandule.  Head long. Tympanum distinct and nearly half the diameter of the eyes.  Forelimbs fairly long.  Hind limbs long.  Tibio-tarsal articulation reaching the tip of the snout.  Toes fully webbed and the tips of the toes dilated into small discs.  Subarticular tubercles prominent.  An oval large inner and a smaller outer meta tarsal tubercle present.

 Colour: Dorsal surface light brown to dark brown.  Limbs covered with dark cross bands or black bars.  Throat and breast mottled.  Ventral surface white.
