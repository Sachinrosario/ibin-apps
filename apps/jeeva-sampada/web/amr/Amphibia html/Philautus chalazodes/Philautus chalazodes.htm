DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small frog; digits with intercalary cartilage, vomerine teeth absent; lingual papilla present; canthus rostralis indistinct; tympanum hidden; fingers free; tibio-tarsal articulation exceeding eye; toes half-webbed 

Colour: Dark brown or gray above, tubercles being white.  Ventral side is of a dull yellow.  In life the dorsal surface has a uniform greenish colour while the venter is yellowish white (Günther, 1875).  The outer and inner sides of the femur are yellow, faintly marbled with green.
