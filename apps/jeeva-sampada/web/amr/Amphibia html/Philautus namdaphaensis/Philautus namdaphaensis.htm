DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small frog.  Skin more less smooth or with minute tubercles.  Throat as well as upper surface of the limbs smooth.  A cutaneous fold extending from the posterior region of the eyes upto the shoulder.  Head slightly broader than long.  Nostrils equidistant from the eyes and tip of snout.  Inter oribital width broader than the diameter of the eyes.  Tympanum indistinct.  Forelimbs fairly stout.  Fingers moderately long free and with disinct round discs as well as with intercalary ossicle.  Hindlimbs fairly stout.  Tibiotarsal articuation reaches inbetween the eyes and nostril.  Toes half webbed with inter calary ossicle.  Subarticle tubercles indistinct.  Inner metatarsal tubercle present.  Outer metatarsal tubercle absent.

 Colour: Dorsal surface dark brown speckled with irregular brown spots.  A thin faint line running middorsally from the tip of the snout to vent.  Upper surface of the limbs dark brown with indistinct cross bars.  Another thin line originating from the vent and running upto the heel through the fumer and tibia.  A broad prominent white band present in between the eyes.  Lateral portion of the thigs tibia and the groin brownish with dark blotches at certain region.  Ventral surface dirty white.
