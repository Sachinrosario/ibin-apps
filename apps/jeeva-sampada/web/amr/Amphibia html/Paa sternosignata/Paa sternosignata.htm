DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium sized frog.  Skin smooth or with small tubercles of various sizes and may be tipped with horny spinules.  Ventral surface smooth.  Head broader than long.  Snout round slightly projecting beyond the mouth.  Tympanum indistinct and nearly one third diameter of the eye.  Fingers obtusely pointed.  Subarticular tubercles of the fingers small and moderately prominent.  Hind limbs short.  Tibiotarsal articulation reaches posterior corner of the eye.  Toes obtusely pointed and sometimes swollen at the tips.  A feebly prominent inner metatarsal tubercle present.  Outermetatarsal tubercle absent.

 Colour: Dorsal surface brown indistinctly marbled.  A dark chevron shaped band usually present on the anterior part of the back.  A number of dark, vertical bands present on the lip.  Limbs with dark cross bands.  Ventral surface almost immaculate.  Throught mottled with brown.
