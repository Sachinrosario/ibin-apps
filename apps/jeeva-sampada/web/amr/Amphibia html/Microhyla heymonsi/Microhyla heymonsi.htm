DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small frog.  Body covered with smooth skin.  A faint glandular fold extending from the posterior corner of the eyes to the fore limb.  Head moderate in size, snout pointed, tympanum hidden.  Fingers free, tips of the fingers dilated into small terminal disc.  Hind limbs moderately long, tibio-tarsal articulation reaching the eye or beyond the tip of snout.  Toes webbed at the base.  Sub-articular tubercles well developed.  Two small meta tarsal tubercles present.

 

Colour: Dorsal surface of the body pink with black spots on the back.  A black lateral band extending from the tip of snout upto the groin.  This band also runs to tbe sides of the head.  Limbs are covered with numerous narrow and oblique cross bars.  Lower surface white in colour.
