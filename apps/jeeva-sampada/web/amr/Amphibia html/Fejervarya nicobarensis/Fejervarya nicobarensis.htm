DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small frog.  Skin smooth, occassionally granulated on the dorsal suface and with or without any warts.  A moderately broad glandular dorso-lateral fold extending from above the tympanum to the hip.  Ventral surface smooth.  Head longer than broad.  Tympanum very distinct.  Forelimbs short.  Fingers long and slender with feeble dermal border.  Hind limbs rather long and slender.  Tibiotarsal articulation reaching tip of snout.  Tips of toes dilated into small discs.  The upper surface separate from the lower one by a horseshoe shaped groove.  Toes half to two thirds webbed.  Both inner and outer metatarsal tubercle present. 

Colour: Dorsum varies from greyish to reddish brown with uniform darker spots.  Sides of head dark brown to black.  A faint white streak present on the upper lip.  Limbs with dark cross bands.  Ventral surface white sometimes spotted with brown.
