DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium sized toad.Skin dorsaly rough and tuberculate.A longitudinal fold present between limbs.Head broader than long, depressed.  Snount short and conical in shape. Eyes prominent, pupil horizontal.  Tympanum hidden.  Fingers free, toes almost fully webbed.  A large proiminent inner meta tarsal tubercle present.

 

Colour: Dorsal colour uniform dark brown to black.  Ventral surface greyish with brown spots.  Tips of tubercles and warts deep brown.
