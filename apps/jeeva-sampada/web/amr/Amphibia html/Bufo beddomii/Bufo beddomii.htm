DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium-sized toad; body robust; head without cranial ridges; parotoid glands narrow and not elevated; tympanum very small; dorsum rough with closely set warts; toes half-webbed. 

Colour: :  Dorsum of uniform dark brown; upper eyelid and shoulder region sometimes of light yellow; femur and tibia blackish; fingers and toes tan; ventral surface light yellow with irregular marbling of brown. According to Inger et al. (1984) the upper surface of the feet is reddish brown in life contrasting sharply with the dorsal colour.
