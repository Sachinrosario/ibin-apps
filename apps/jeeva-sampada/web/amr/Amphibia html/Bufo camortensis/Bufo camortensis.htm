DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium sized toad.  Dorsal skin rough with wrinkled scattered and spiny tubercles.  The ventral portion of the skin is wrinkled.  Head almost smooth broader than long and with prominent bony ridges.  Nostrils are laterally placed and slighly swollen.  Interoribital width more or less broader than the diameter of eye.  Tympanum distinct and semicircular in shape.  Parotid glands well developed and slightly narrow at the posterior region.  Fingers free, tips of fingers blunt.  Hindlimbs long.  Tibiotarsal articulation reaching upto the parotid glands.  Toes half webbed.  Subarticular tubercles indistinct.  Both inner and outer metatarsal tubercles present and moderate in size.

 Colour: Dorsal surface is brown and ventral surface including the warts near the vent and behind the thighs reddish brown.  The cranial ridges, the parotid glands, the dorsal warts and tubercles, tips fingers and toes as well as the margin of the upper lip slightly darker in colour.
