DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small frog, digits with an intercalary cartilage, tympanum very indistinct; pupil horizontal; dorsolateral glandular folds absent; lingual papilla and vomerine teeth absent; throat granular; tibio-tarsal articulation reaching between eye and snout, fingers with minute rudiments of web; toes more than half-webbed; digits with discs.

 Colour: Dorsum is light tan to dark brown.  A dark interorbital band often present.  Both limbs with crossbars.  Venter generally pale with brown dots on throat and breast.  In life, the dorsum has a distinct greenish tint.  Thurston (1888) who collected this species from Nilgiris has observed that they were grass-green in life.
