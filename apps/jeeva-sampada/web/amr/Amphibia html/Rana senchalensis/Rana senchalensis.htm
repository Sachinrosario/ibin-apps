DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small frog.  Skin almost smooth.  Snout rounded and projecting beyond the mouth.  Nostrils closer to the eye than to the tip of the snout.  Tympanum small, distinct and two thirds the diamter of the eye.  Fingers free and tips of the fingers swollen into small discs.  Hind limbs long.  Tibiotafsal articulation reaching beyond the tip of snout.  Toes nearly entirely webbed and with dilated tips.  Subarticulate tubercles of the toes prominent.  A small and oval inner metatarsal tubercle present.  Outer metatarsal tubercle absent. 

Colour: Dorsal surface dark brown with number of black spots.  Limbs with irregular dark bands.  Throat and lower parts of the limbs are spotted with brown.
