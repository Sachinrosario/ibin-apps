DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium sized frog.  Number of minute warts scattered on the dorsal surface of the body including the limbs.  Chest and throat generally smooth.  A cutaneous fold extending from the posterior corner of the eyes and above the tympanum upto the shoulder.  A transverse flap of skin present above the vent and on the heel.  Head slightly broader than long.  Snout long slightly longer than the diamter of the eyes.  Nostrils equidistant from the eyes and tip of snout.  Interorbital width much broader than the maximum diameter of the eyes.  Tympanum moderately distinct and about half the diameter of the eyes.  Tongue fairly large, free, bifid  behind and without papilla.  Forelimbs moderately long.  Fingers partly webbed and with intercalary ossicles and discs.  Hind limbs slender.  Toes nearly entirely webbed.  Tibiotarsal articulation reaching eyes.  A small feebly promient inner metatarsal tubercle present.  Outer metatarsal tubercle absent.

 Colour: Dorsal surface is reddish brown, Intermixed with variable black spots.  A number of faint cross bars present on the upper surface of the limbs.  A large round spot on each side behind the arm pit.  Ventral surface brownish.
