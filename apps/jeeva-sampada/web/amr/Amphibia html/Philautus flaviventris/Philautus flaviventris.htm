DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small frog.  Dorsal surface smooth and granular on the ventral region.  A cutaneous fold extending from the eye to the shoulder.  Head moderate in size.  Snout rounded as long as the diameter of the eyes.  Canthus rostralis distinct.  Loreal region slightly concave.  Nostrils much closer to the tip of snout than to the eyes.  Inter orbital width as broad as the diameter of the eye.  Tympanum small and indistinct.  Forelimbs moderate in size.  Fingers free.  Hindlimbs moderately long.  Tibiotarsal articulation reaching the eyes.  Toes half webbed.  Tips of toes dilated into moderately prominent disc.  Subarticular tubercles present.  A small inner metatarsal tubercle present.  Outer metatarsal tubercle absent.

 Colour: Dorsal surface generally dark brown with round yellow spots.  Sometimes with dark reticulation or spots.  Posterior region of the thigs also dark brown with round yellow spots.
