DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Large species. Skin is smooth or granulate, sometimes with two to three pairs of longitudinal dermal folds.  Ventral surface smooth.  Head variable in size.  Tympanum distinct, about half the diameter of the eye.  Forelimbs moderately long.  Hind limbs moderately long.  Tibio-tarsal articulation reaching in between the eye and nostril.  Toes entirely webbed, web between the toes strongly notched, not reaching the tip of the fourth toe.  A small blunt inner meta tarsal tubercle present.  Outer meta tarsal tubercle absent.

 Colour: Upper surface yellowish green or olive with dark spots.  Ventral surface white.  A narrow or broad, yellow mid-dorsal vertebral band is present.  Which is occassionally accompanied by a light line.  Limbs with irregular cross bands.
