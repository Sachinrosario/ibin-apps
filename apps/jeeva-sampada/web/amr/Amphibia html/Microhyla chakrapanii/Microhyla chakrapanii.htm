DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Small microhylid frog.  Dorsal skin smooth.  Throat, chest and part of belly rough.  Ventral part of thighs including the area around vent granular.  Dorsal part of tibia having minute tubercles.  A faint granular fold extending from the posterior corner of the eyes to the fore limb.  Forelimbs short and thin, fingers free with slightly swollen tips.  Hind limbs fairly long, tibio-tarsal articulation just reaching the eye.  Toes webbed at the base.  A small elongated inner and a small outer metatarsal tubercle present.

 

Colour: Dorsal surface brownish grey in colour with two dark patches on either side of the mid- dorsal line at the level of the shoulder.  Another pair of broad, bracket shaped patches present behind it.  Two ill defined pre-anal bands which run into the thighs.  A lateral band on each side extending from the tip of the snout almost to the groin, its upper margin sharply defined while the lower margin fades into the colour of the belly.
