DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Medium sized frog.  Skin dorsally smooth, granulate on the belly.  Head large.  Interorbital space broader than the eyelid.  Tympanum very distinct and two thirds the diameter of the eye.  First finger much longer than second.  Hindlimbs long.  Tibiotarsal articulation reaching the anterior corner of the eye.  Toes moderately long and webbed at the base.  A shovel shaped inner metatarsal tubercle present.  Outer metatarsal tubercle absent.

 Colour: Dorsally grey indistinctly marbled with brown.  A prominent band extending posteriorly from the canthus rostralis through the eye to the shoulder, expanding to a round spot on the tympanum.  Thigs black marbled with white.
