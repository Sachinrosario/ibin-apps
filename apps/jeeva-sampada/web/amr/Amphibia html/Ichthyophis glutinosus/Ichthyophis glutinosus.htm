DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A large species reaching 400 mm with well defined lateral yellow stripes. Tentacular aperture distinctly closer to eye than to nostril.

Colour: Brown below the translucent whitish skin that had sloughed off at places on the body ventrally brown, separated from the dorsal brown by a clear latral cream stipe
