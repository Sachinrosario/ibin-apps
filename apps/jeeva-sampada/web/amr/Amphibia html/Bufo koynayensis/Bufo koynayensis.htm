DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Very small toad.  Dorsal surface of the body covered with scattered tubercles of unequal size.  Ventral surface granulate.  Head moderate in size.  Snout short and slightly rounded in shape.  Nostrils swollen and laterally placed.  Interorbital width subequal to upper eyelid.  Tympanum indistinct.  Nearly one fifth the diamter of the eye.  Parotid glands inconspicuous, subtriangular and the posterior region much narrower than the anterior region.  Fingers free and not dilated.  Hindlimbs moderate.  Tibiotarsal articulatin reaching the tympanum.  Toes with rudiment of web at the basal phalenges.  A large prominent inner and a small, feebly prominent outer metatarsal tubercle present.

 Colour: Dorsal surface of the body is dark brown with a suffusion of dull chrome-yellow.  Flanks, sides of the thigs and above the arm insertions chome-yellow patches.  Occassionally dark brown patches present on the throat and abdomen. Infra-tympanic area and around the vent sometimes white in colour.
