Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 118 mm.; tail length 85 mm.

Head: Head very small in comparison to body and tapering anteriorly; body long and slender. First supraocular slightly smaller than second; fronto parietals small (smaller than interparietal); interparietal entirely separating the parietals; 5 p

Body: With 85 dorsal scales (including nuchal scales) in longitudinal series of which 16 anterior scales in two rows; scales on nape and near base of tail not fused. Limbs moderate-sized, pentadactyle, third toe longest (and also longer than the fourth); with 3 transverse lamellae under fourth and 10 under third toe.
