Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Scincidae

Genus: Sphenomorphus

Species: indicum indicum

 

Synonyms: 1853.. Hinulia indica Gray, Ann. Mag. Nat. Hist. (2) 12 : p. 388 (type loc. Himalayas); 1887.. Lygosoma indicum, Boulenger, Cat. Liz. Brit. Mus. 3 : p. 241, pl. 16, fig. 1.; 1928.. Sphenomorphus indicus, Schmidt, Copeia : p. 80.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 90 mm., tail length 130-175 mm.

Head:

Body: The body is not much elongated; rostral convex, in contact with frontonasal; frontonasal broader than long; prefrontals never contact with each other; frontal as long as or longer than the fronto-parietals and interparietal together; nuchals are not available; supraoculars are 6 in number 4 larger ones are followed by two  smaller scales; supraciliaries 8-10; temporal scales are large, 2 in number and are superposed; ear-opening is oval, tympanum is deeply sunk; Upper labials 7, the fifth and sixth below the eye, separated from it by small scales; body scales are almost equal, smooth 30-38 round the middle of the body; a pair of enlarged preanal is always present;

Limbs: limbs are well developed and moderately long, the hind-limb reaches to the elbow, digits long and compressed, 16-22 keeled lamellae beneath the fourth toe;

Tail: Tail tapers to a point, covered with equal scales, 1 1/2 to 2 times longer than the head and body;
