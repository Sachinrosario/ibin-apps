Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Gekkonidae

Genus: Cnemaspis

Species: littoralis

 

Synonyms: 1853.. Gymnodactylus littoralis Jerdon, J. Asiat. Soc. Beng. 22 : p. 469 (type loc. sea coast of Malabar, type lost).; 1871.. Gymnodactylus planipes Beddome, Madras Month. J. Med. Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 30 mm.; tail length 35 mm.

Head: Head is covered above with small granular scales which are largest on the snout which is obtusely pointed. Upper labials 8-10 and lower labials 7-8; mental shield is large, subtriangular, truncated posteriorly and slightly broader than the rostral; postmentals are small, in 2 or 3 pairs, the first pair may be in contact or be separated by a single median scale, just behind the mental shield.

Body:  Dorsum is with small, uniform, granular scales which on the flanks are intermixed with few small somewhat conical tubercles; ventrum is with slightly larger subimbricate smooth scales.

Limbs: The hind-limb is not reaching beyond the axilla; digits moderately elongated, clawed, subdigital lamellae on the basal phalanges are very large, somewhat subquadrangular, 3-5 in number, the distal shield is the largest and projecting (the somewhat

Tail: Tail is longer than the head and body, cylindrical, covered above with minute scales, generally uniform but sometimes mixed with larger tubercles, arranged in regular series, below with a median series of transverse plates. Male with 14-18 femoral pores on each side.

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India : Nilambur, Nellakota in Nilgiri Hills. Malabar coast. Elsewhere : None so far

Habitat:

This species is insectivorous, arboreal and diurnal inhabiting dense forested rocky areas.
