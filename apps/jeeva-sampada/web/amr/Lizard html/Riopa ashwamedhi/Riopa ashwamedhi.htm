_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 20-32 mm.; tail length 32-39 mm.

Head: Head prominent; distance between axilla and groin one and a half times that between tip of snout and fore limb; snout more or less acute. Head Scales: Lower eyelids scaly, central scales of the lid much enlarged and thinned to appear as a semi-transparent disc; supranasals in contact with one another behind the rostral; frontal shorter than the combined length of frontoparietal and interparietal; a pair of nuchals present; scales bordering the outer margins of parietals equal in size; ear-opening about half as large as eye-opening, with 3 to 4 minute lobules on anterior margin; supralabials 8 in number, the 5th below the middle of eye being longer than the adjacent labials;

Body: 28-32 smooth scale rows round the body; 45-60 scales down the middle of back; marginal preanals enlarged; 13-15 lamellae under the fourth toe.

Limbs:

Tail: Tail longer than head and body together, slightly thick at base and tapering to a point at the end.
