Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Gekkonidae

Genus: Cnemaspis

Species: jerdoni

 

Synonyms: 1868.. Gymnodactylus jerdoni Theobald, Cat. Rept. Asiat. Soc. Mus. : p. 31 (type loc. not known).; 1877.. Gymnodactylus scalpensis Ferguson, Rept. Fauna Ceylon, : p. 13 (type loc. Ceylon, type lost).; 1935.. Cnemaspis jerdoni, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. : p. 74..

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 40 mm.; tail length 44 mm.

Head: Head is covered above with minute granules which are largest on the snout which is obtusely pointed. Upper labials 8-10 and lower labials 7-8; mental shield is much larger, subtriangular, truncated posteriorly and broader than the rostral; postmentals are small, in 2 or 3 pairs, the first pair may be in contact or be separated by a single median scale, just behind the mental shield. Dorsum is with small uniform scales which on the flanks are intermixed with few spinose tubercles; ventrum is with much larger subimbricate, smooth scales.

Body:

Limbs: The hind-limb generally reaches beyond the axilla, digits modereately elongated, clawed; subdigital lamellae moderately large, the shields beneath the basal phalanges are large and 3-5 in number.

Tail: Tail slightly longer than the head and body, cylindrical, covered above with small subimbricate scales and regular series of larger pointed tubercles; underside of the tail is with much larger, smooth subimbricate scales, the scales of the median series are much larger than other scales. Male with 5-15 femoral pores on each side.

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India : Nilgiri Hills; Palni Hills; Anaimalai Hills; Sivagiri Hills and Lamparis Peak

Habitat:

The species is a forested rock dwelling species recorded up to 500 metres. It is a nocturnal and insectivorous species.
