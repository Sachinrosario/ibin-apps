Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Scincidae

Genus: Mabuya

Species: dissimilis

 

Synonyms: 1857.. Euprepis dissimilis Hallowell, Trans. Amer. Phil. Soc. (2) 11 : p. 78 (type loc. Bengal).; 1885.. Euprepes (Euprepis) warthii Fischer, Jahrb. Wiss. Anst. Hamb. 2 : p. 90 (type loc. Dehra Dun, United Provinces); 1935.. Mabuya dissimilis, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. 2, p. 261.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 77-92 mm., tail length 149-178 mm.

Head:

Body: Dorsal and lateral scales are equal; dorsals are with 2-3 strong keels; ventrals are with 3 strong keels; 34-38 scales round the middle of the body.

Limbs: Digits short, fourth toe is with 12-16 smooth lamellae beneath, the legs reach to the wrist.

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India : Bengal, Bihar and Rajasthan (Ajmer), Elsewhere : Pakistan

Habitat:

Prefers dry open country with plenty of bushes, sand and rocks. Diurnal, insectivorous. It lays 6-7 eggs (10x7 mm.)
