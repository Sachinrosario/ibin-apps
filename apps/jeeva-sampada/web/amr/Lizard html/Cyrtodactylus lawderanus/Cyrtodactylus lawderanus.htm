Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Gekkonidae

Genus: Cyrtodactylus

Species: lawderanus

  Synonyms: 1871.. Gymnodactylus lawderanus Stoliczka, P. Asiat. Soc. Beng. : p. 194.; 1913.. Alsophylax himalayensis Annandale, Rec. Ind. Mus. 9 : p. 305, pl. 15 (type loc. Dharampur, Simla dist.).; 1935.Gymnodactylus lawderanus, Smith Fauna Brit. Ind. : p. 58.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Small, standard length 40- 55 mm; tail length 38 - 50 mm

Head: Moderatley large with rounded snout, depressed, covered with  smaller granules.  Eyes large with elliptical pupil; Ear opening oval, half the size of the pupil.

Body: Depressed, flattened dorsoventraly, lateral fold absent or indistinct. Body covered with small rounded  imbricate scales intermixed with rounded keeled tubercles.

Limbs: Hind limbs reaches the axilla, digits are moderately larger and slender, sub digital lamellae narrower

Tail: Shorter than head and body, unsegmented, more cylindrical, swollen at the base, not so tapering. Males with 4 - 5 preanal pores arranged in angular series
