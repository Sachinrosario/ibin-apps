Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Agamidae

Genus: Oriocalotes

Species: paulus

 

Synonyms: 1845.. Calotes minor Gray, Cat. Liz. Brit. Mus. : p. 244 (type loc. Khasi Hills).; 1864.. Oriocalotes minor, Gunther, Rept. Brit. Ind. : p. 147.; 1935.. Oriocalotes paulus Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. 2 : p. 166.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 70 mm.; tail length 130 mm.

Head: Head is large with large, unequal, strongly keeled, granulate dorsal scales; one spine is present on the postorbital region and two above the ear; tympanum is naked or may be concealed under scales; Upper labials  7-9 and as many lower labials; dorsal body scales large, strongly keeled, unequal, largest scales on the lateral aspects

Body: This moderately large agamid is with a feebly compressed body. the dorsal scales in upper few rows pointing upwards and backwards, the lower ones pointing backwards only; ventral scales of the body smaller than the dorsals, strongly keeled and mucronate.

Limbs: Limbs moderately large; fourth finger is longer than third; fourth toe is longer than third; the hind-limb reaches to the ear.

Tail: Tail much longer than the head and body, round in transverse section, not swollen at the base, covered above with almost equal keeled scales, the scales on the under side of tail are squarish.
