Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Small rock gecko, measuring 42 - 50 mm, tail length 65 mm.

Head: Moderately large, covered above with slightly smaller, irregular rounded scales which are intermixed in snout region with larger rounded scales. Upper labials 10-13 and lower labials 9-12.

Body: Depressed, flattened dorsoventrally, lateral folds indistinct, Dorsum with 10 - 12 regular longitudinal series of large sub trihedral tubercles, these rows of tubercles are separated  from one to three small granular scales, belly with rounded sub imbricate scales, 28 - 36 across the middle of the body

Limbs: Dorsal aspect of the limbs are covered with keeled imbricate scales, Hind limbs reaches beyond the axilla, toes are elongated, sub digital lamellae well developed, those of the basal phalanges are almost as broad as the digit.

Tail: Tail longer than the head, dorsal aspect with small scales and rows of large spine like scales, which are smaller than the ventral scales

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: Ajmer districts of Rajasthan

Habitat:

Rock dwelling nocturnal species, Insectivorous feeding on grasshoppers, gryllids, hymenopterans, other insects and occasionally on fibers of wild plants.
