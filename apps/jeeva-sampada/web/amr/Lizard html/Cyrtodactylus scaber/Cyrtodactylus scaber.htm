Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Gekkonidae

Synonyms: 1874.. Gymnodactylus brevipes Blanford, Ann. Mag. Nat. Hist., 4, Vol. 13 : p. 453.; 1935.. Gymnodactylus scaber (Heyden), Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. : p. 42.; 1966.. Cyrtodactylus scaber (Heyden), Minton, Bull. Amer. Mus. Nat. Hist. 134 : p. 77.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Small nocturnal, whose standard length is 50 mm with its tail length of 67 mm

Head: Moderately large and depressed, covered above with small granular scales intermixed withlarge keeled tubercles. Its eyes are large with an vertically elliptical pupil; ear opening is like a vertical slit and smaller than pupil

Body: Depressed, flattened dorso ventrally,  with a feeble lateral fold; dorsal scales composed of 10 -12 rows of regularly arranged series of large, sub trihedral tubercles and one or two rows of small granular scales separating  them; median dorsal and lateral scales are of equal size and shape; belly is with about 20 -23 rows of large rounded imbricte scales across the did body.

Limbs: Above with keeled imbricate scales, digits long, slender, toes  elongate, sub digital lamellae well developed more than half the breadth of t he digit, hind limb reaches beyond the axillla

Tail: Slightly depressed, above with small 2 or 3 rows of small scales separating  the rows of enlarged median scales underside of the tail

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution:

Habitat:

Inhabits the arid region ( Desert ) but according to Minton ( 1966) also prefers to live in dry grassland, on rocky hill sides. In few cases reported to found hiding under stones/bricks/ houses. Insectivorous living on gryllids, scarabid beetles, flies, ants, and even scorpions.
