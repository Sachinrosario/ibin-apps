Class: Reptilia

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: small, luminous ecko, standard length 50 mm; tail length 36 mm

Head: Moderatley large, covered with minute rounded scales, snout with pentagonal or hexagonal scales, intermixed with rounded scales.Upper labials - 9; Lower labials - 10. Males with  a continuous series of 23 - 36 preano-femoral pores

Body: Depressed, flattened dorsovenrally, lateral fold feebly developed but distinct. Dorsum small granular scales intermixed with subtrihedral tubercles

Limbs: Hind limbs extend to the axilla, toes short, subdigital lamellae well developed, as broad as digit.

Tail: Shorter than the head and body, cylindrical, segmented, not swollen at the base, tapering to a point, covered with trihedral  tubercles

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India, Rajasthan ( Ajmer)

Habitat:

Lives on barren stony terrain with xerophytic vegetation. Luminous at night.
