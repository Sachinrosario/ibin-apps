Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Gekkonidae

Genus: Cyrtodactylus

Species: khasiensis

 

Synonyms: 1870.. Pentactylus khasiensis Jerdon. P. Asiat, Soc. Beng. : p. 75 (type loc. Khasi Hills, Assam).; 1926.. Gymnodactylus khasiensis, Hora, Rec. Ind. Mus. 28 : p. 189. pl. 7, figs. 4-6.; 1935.Gymnodactylus khasiensis, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. : p. 52.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Large gecko, the standard length of 85 mm, tail length of 100 mm

Head: Moderately large and depressed, eyes are prominent,Upper labials 10 12, lower labials 10 - 12.

Body: Depressed, flattened dorosventrally, lateral fold  is distinct with larger scales; dorsal scales on the body and limbs are small and granular intermixed with much larger rounded keeled tubercles,belly with 30 - 40 rows of  rounded imbricate scales across the middle of the body.

Limbs: Subdigital lamellae well developed, nearly as broad as the digit, hind limb reaches the axilla

Tail: Above with small flat scales, upper portion and the basal region of the tail is having enlarged scales.
