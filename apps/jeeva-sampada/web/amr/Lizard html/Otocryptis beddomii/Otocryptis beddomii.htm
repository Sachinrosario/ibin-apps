Synonyms: 1885.. Otocryptis beddomii Boulenger, Cat. Liz. Brit. Mus. 1 : p. 272, pl. 23, fig. 1 (type loc. Sivagiri Ghat, South India).; 1935.. Otocryptis beddomii, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. 2 : p. 147.;

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 45 mm.; tail length 80 mm.

Head: The head is with unequal strongly keeled scales on the dorsal aspect; interorbital distance is having 2-3 scales; Supralabials 9-11 and same is the number for the infralabials;

Body: This is a moderately large agamid with a compressed body. dorsal scales keeled, some of them are much larger and point backwards and upwards; scales on the lower region of flanks point backwards and downwards; ventral scales are large about as large as the enlarged dorsal scales and strongly keeled.

Limbs: Limbs long, slender with large, almost equal, keeled scales above. The hind-limb reaches beyond the snout;

Tail: Tail longer than the head and body, round in transverse section, covered above with sub-equal, keeled scales.

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India : Sivagiri Ghat, Cardamom Hills, in South India

Habitat:

Inhabit grassy areas on a hilly forested area (1500 metres altitude). Insectivorous and nocturnal species. One gravid female contained three oviducal eggs.
