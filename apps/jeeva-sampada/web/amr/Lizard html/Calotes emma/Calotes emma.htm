Taxonomic Details

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Agamidae

Genus: Calotes

Species: emma

  Synonyms: 1845.. Calotes emma Gray, Cat. Liz. Brit. Mus : p. 244 (type loc. "Afghanistan"); 1925.. Calotes alticristatus Schmidt, Amer. Mus. No. 175 : p. 2, (type loc. Yunnan-fu).; 1935.. Calotes emma, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. 2 : p. 195.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 115 mm.; tail length 290 mm.

Head: Head is moderately large; gular pouch is not available, but during the breeding season male developes some bulging; Head scales unequal, keeled or tuberculated; 9-12 upper labials and as many as lower labials

Body: The body is compressed. body scales large, keeled, pointing backwards and upwards, scales of the flanks generally pointing backwards or backwards and downwards and are larger than the ventral scales, ventral scales are strongly keeled and mucronate; scales in 49-65 rows at mid-body; nuchal and dorsal crests are present and well developed in both the sexes anteriorly.

Limbs: limbs are moderately developed, fourth toe is longer than third toe, third and fourth fingers are equal, the hind-limb reaches to the eye or not quite so far

Tail: tail slightly compressed covered with equal scales.

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India : Assam (Golpara to Garo Hills). Elsewhere : Burma, Thailand, South Vietnam, Southern China and Malaysia .

Habitat:

Strictly arboreal on mountain forests; insectivorous, diurnal, lays 4-12 oval eggs (17×11 mm) on excitement its head region become blackish.
