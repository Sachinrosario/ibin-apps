Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Gekkonidae

Genus: Cyrtodactylus

Species: rubidus

 

Synonyms: 1860.. Puellula rubida Blyth, J. Asiat. Soc. Beng. 29 : p. 109 (type loc. Andaman Is.); 1890.. Gymnodactylus rubidus, Boulenger, Fauna Brit. Ind. : p. 69.; 1935.. Gymnodactylus rubidus, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind., : p. 54..

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Moderatley large, standard length 75 mm; tail length 90 mm

Head: Moderatley large and depressed, large eyes, Upper labials 10 - 12; Lower labials 10 -12

Body: Depressed, distinct lateral fold with  prominent scales, dorsal scales on the body and limbs are small, granular intermixed with  larger rounded keeled tubercles.  Belly with 30 - 40 rows of imgbricate scales across the middle

Limbs: Subdigital lamellae well developed, almost as braod as the digit, hind limb reaches the axilla,

Tail: Above with small flat scales, Upper part of the basal portion has enlarged tubercles
