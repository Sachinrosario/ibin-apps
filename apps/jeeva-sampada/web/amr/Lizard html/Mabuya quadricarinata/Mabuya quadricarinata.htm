Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Scincidae

Genus: Mabuya

Species: quadricarinata

 

Synonyms: 1887.. Mabuya quadricarinata Boulenger, Ann. Mus. Civ. Genova, (2) 4 : p. 618 (type loc. Bhamo and hills to the east).; 1905.. Mabuya anakular Annandale, J. & P. Asiat. Soc. Beng. 1 : p. 143 (type loc. Cachar).; 1935.. Mabuya quadricarinata, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. 2 : p. 273.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 50 mm.; tail length 90 mm.

Head:

Body: dorsal and lateral scales are almost equal, strongly quadricarinate; nuchal shields are present, generally tricarinate; 26 or 28 scales round the middle of the body;

Limbs: digits moderately long, with 17-18 smooth lamellae beneath the fourth toe; the hind-limb hardly reaches to the elbow.
