Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Scincidae

Genus: Sphenomorphus

Species: maculatum

 

Synonyms: 1853.. Lissonota maculata Blyth, J. Asiat. Soc. Beng. 22 : p. 653 (type loc. Assam).; 1887.. Lygosoma maculatum, Boulenger, Cat. Liz. Brit. Mus. 3 : p. 242.; 1935.. Lygosoma maculatum, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. 2, p. 285.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Standard length 62 mm.; tail length 121 mm.

Head: Snout is short and obtusely pointed; rostral is concave, in contact with the frontonasal; prefrontals small, not touching one another; frontal is equal or longer than the combined distance of fronto-parietals and inter-parietal; supraoculars are 5, first is longest and fifth is smallest; supraciliaries 10-12; both the loreals are higher than long; 2 large supreposed temporal scales; tympanum is not deeply sunk; upper labials 7, the fifth and sixth are below the eye, separated from it by small scales; scales of the body smooth,

Body:  dorsal scales are much larger than the lateral scales; 32-42 scales round the middle of the body; a pair of large preanal scales is present;

Limbs: Limbs are moderately long, the hind-limb reaches to the elbow or axilla or even beyond, digits long and compressed, with 16-22 keeled lamellae beneath the fourth toe.

Tail: tail tapering gradually to a point, almost two times longer than the head and body, the median row of scales on the underside are transversely enlarged.

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India : Assam, Bengal (Parasnath hills, Sikkim, Darjeeling, Andamans and Nicobar Islands.

Habitat:

This is a terrestrial, low-mountain dwelling species, ascends up to 800 metres in Darjeeling. Insectivorous, diurnal and oviparous (lays 4-5 eggs in a single clutch).
