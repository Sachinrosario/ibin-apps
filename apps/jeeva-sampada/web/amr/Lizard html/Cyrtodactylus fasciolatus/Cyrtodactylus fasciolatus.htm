Taxonomic Details

_____________________________________________________________

Class: Reptilia

Order: Sauria

Family:Gekkonidae

Genus: Cyrtodactylus

Species: fasciolatus

 

Synonyms: 1860. Naultinus fasciolatus Blyth, J. Asiat. Soc. Beng. 29 : p. 114 (type loc. Subathu, Simla dist.).; 1876.. Gymnodactylus fasciolatus, Theobald, Cat. Rept. Brit. Ind. : p. 92; 1935.. Gymnodactylus fasciolatus, Smith, Fauna Brit. Ind. : p. 45.

_____________________________________________________________

Diagnostic Features

_____________________________________________________________

Size: Large nocturnal  rock lizard, standard length 82 mm, tail length 110 mm.

Head:

Body: Body depressed, flattened dorso-ventrally, with a distinct lateral fold, dorsal scales on body and limbs are like small granules intermixed with much larger subtrihedral tubercles, scales of the lateral fold are slightly enlarged, belly with 28 - 34 rows of large rounded  imbricate scales across the middle of the bodyFemales with  15 - 16 pre ano-femoral plates.

Limbs: Hind limbs hardly reaches axilla

Tail: Above with  small flat scales with a series of enlarged flat tubercles at the base.  A series of transversely enlarged flat tubercles underside of the tail.

Other Biological Details _____________________________________________________________

Distribution: India : Western Himalayas ( Almora, Simla, Kumaon )

Habitat:

Rock dwelling located up to 600 m , usually insectivorous.
