HABIT & HABITAT   

Common Dolphin is an intelligent and social animal preferring to live in group (School). School size ranges from 20- few hundred individuals, sometimes even to several thousands, jumping one after another in oceanic water. They can dive as deep as 280 m for 8 min. Dolphins are mostly pelagic and can be found in even 100 fathom deep waters. However, they feed mainly on fishes and squids. They move with great speed and can keep pace with ship traveling at 30 knots. 

Habitat Structure: Marine, coastal and offshore species. Habitat structure stable.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

Common Dolphin is a marine mammal with a sleek slender and streamlined body. Head small, snout prolonged into a narrow beak separated from head by a prominent groove. Dorsal fin well developed, triangular in shape and placed high with a backwardly directed tip. It is situated at the midpoint. Flippers with broad base and pointed tip. Teeth small, sharp and pointed. Number of teeth varies between 40-60 in each jaw. Tail fluke horizontally flattened with a deep notch in the middle. Cervical vertebrae 2-6 fused. Palate with a deep groove on each side. The V-shaped saddle on the back is one of the identifying characters in the field. Body dark grey to black above, while white below. Sides with stripes of fulvous grey or white with yellowish tinge. Males slightly larger than females. 

Measurements: (In meters): Total Length: Male : 1.7-2.6; Female: 1.5-2.3.  

Weight: (In Kg): 70-110
