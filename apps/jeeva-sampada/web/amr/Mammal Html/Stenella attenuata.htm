HABIT & HABITAT

Lives offshore and coastal, both. Feeds mainly on fishes, cephalopods and crab larvae. Lives in group size of few to several thousand individuals. 

Habitat Structure: Marine, offshore and coastal waters. Habitat Structure: Decreasing with general decline.

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A medium sized dolphin with a short and thick beak well demarcated from from forehead. Body clearly divided into dark upper and light (Whitish or grayish) lower portion. A black stripe connects gape with flipper. The whole body is covered with spots. A portion of rostrum and lower jaw white. Dorsal fin tall backwardly pointed and black. It is situated in the middle. Flippers falcate and black. Tail fluke notched in the middle and black in color. Teeth 35-48. Females tend to possess longer rostrum. 

Measurements: (In meters): Total Length: Male: 2.0-2.57m; Female: 1.87-2.40m. 

Weight: Not Known

Miscellaneous Information   

Intraspecific variations: No subspecies. However, overlapping variations are seen in body size, cranial measurements and colouration.

Sighting Records:  Type of Sighting: Direct and Indirect; Field Notes: Sighting at Sundarban reported by Agrawal and Alfred (1999). Sighting records of 13 specimens of Pantropical spotted dolphins from Indian waters during the period between 1800 to 2000 have also been reported by Kumaran (2000)and Kumaran (2002).
