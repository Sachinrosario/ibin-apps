HABIT & HABITAT

Bryde's whale is a purely marine form, preferring to live along mostly off shore and sometimes in shore, both, waters (Lal Mohan, 1999; Agrawal and Alfred, 1999). 

Habitat Structure: Habitat structure stable.

Distribution:

Bryde's Whale has a circum-equitorial distribution in tropical and warm waters. In Indian waters found along Arabean sea, Bay of Bengal shores and also in Indian ocean. There is a population in Bay of Bengal from Sri Lanka to straits of Malacca.

No.of Locations: Many; Type: Contiguous

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES:

A medium sized whale larger in size than B. acutorostrata but smaller than other Balaenoptera species. Head 'V' shaped with three prominent ridges from the blow hole to snout. Throat grooves extent up to naval region, 40-50 in numbers. Dorsal fin moderate in size situated at the posterior end. There is a single, narrow spout. Flippers oblong and black in colour. Tail fluke, horizontal and flat. Baleen plates 42 X 24 cm in size and 230 - 370 in number. Baleen fringes coarse, stiff and thick. There are fine white bristles on baleen plates. Body dark gray above, whitish below. Females are larger than males of same age group.  

Measurements: (In Meters): Total Length: Male: 12.2 - 14.3; Female: 12.5 - 15.5, Maximum: 15.5. 

Weight: (In tons) Average: 13.6.
