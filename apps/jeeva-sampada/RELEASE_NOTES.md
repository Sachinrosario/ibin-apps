## Jeeva Sampada – 1.10.0

The original Jeeva Sampada program (Digital Inventory of Biological Resources of India, NBDB, Department of Biotechnology) rebuilt **screen for screen**: every window was captured from the original programs running on Windows and redrawn from those pictures, with the original data live behind them.

- **Main screen**, exactly as the original: photo strips, title, "Conceived & Developed by NBDB, Department of Biotechnology, New Delhi", the scrolling line at the bottom, and the menu Animal Resources ▸ (8 databases) · Plant Resources · Marine Resources · Microbial Resources · Links.
- **Links**: State, District, the groups recorded there, Species Present, and Application (opens the database at that species), from the original pooled database.
- **Birds – Pakshi Sampada** (Digitized Inventory of Bird Resources of India), complete: Scientific Name and Common name search, the five Search windows (breeding, common name, conservation IW(P)A, endemic, migratory), Credits, and the eight buttons for a bird: Taxonomic Hierarchy, Common Name, Distribution (map of the states), Breeding, Conservation, Habit & Biotic Zone, Images (all 937 original pictures) and HTML (all 1,301 original species pages). 1,303 birds.
- **Domestic Animals** (Digitized Inventory of Domestic Animal Resources of India), complete: Species Details (species and breed), the eight buttons for a breed – Morphology, Distribution (map of the states), Synonym, Livestock farms (locations and contact agencies), Performance (the separate forms for buffalo/cattle/camel, goat, sheep and poultry), Remarks, Images (all 285 original photographs) – greyed exactly where the original greys them; Search by Distribution and Search Patent Details; the Reports (Value Products, Hatcheries, Market Information, Poultry Farms, Production Information, Patent List and the three Availability reports) with their Entire / search options, shown in the report viewer as printed by the original; and Documents (Data Sources, Patent Processes, Hatcheries). 131 breeds of 8 species.
- **Freshwater Fin fishes & Shell fishes** (Inventorization of Prioritized Freshwater Finfishes & Shell Fishes of India), complete – all four databases with their own menus and buttons: **Finfishes** (672 species: Scientific Name, Common Name, five searches – Habitat, Status, Synonym, Characteristics, River; Taxonomic Hierarchy, Common Names, Distribution map, Synonym data, Morphology, Reference, Images, HTML), **Crabs** (30: Taxonomic, Colouration, Distribution, Synonym, Morphology, Remarks-Reference, Images, HTML; searches by Habitat, Author, Synonym), **Mollusc** (1,075: Taxonomic, Distribution, Synonym, Morphology, Remarks-Reference, Images, HTML; searches by Author, Synonym), **Prawns** (72: Taxonomic, Common Names, Distribution, Synonym, Colour-Size, Reference, Images, HTML; Common Name; searches by Habitat, Author, Synonym). All 691 original pictures and all original species pages.
- **Amphibia, Reptilia & Mammals** (Digitized Inventory of Amphibia, Reptilia and Mammals of India), complete – its menus Amphibia · Mammals · Reptilia (Lizards, Snakes, Turtles & Tortoises, Present Status of Reptiles) · Snake Park Movie · Credits, with five databases: **Amphibia** (222 species), **Mammals** (144), **Lizards** (175), **Snakes** (203) and **Turtles & Tortoises** (39) – Scientific Name, Common Name, every search (biotic zone, common name, habit, status, threats, area of occupancy, extent of occurrence), the documents (checklist and endemic mammals, IUCN categories, lizards, snake bibliography, identification, venom, value and snake-bite first aid), and each species' windows (classification, common names, distribution map, synonyms, diagnostic characters, colour and size, habit and habitat, morphology, status and threats, breeding season, remarks and references, pictures, species pages). The Snake Park Movie (27 minutes) plays inside the app.
- **Insects** (Digitized Inventory of Predators and Parasitoids of India), complete: Predators (295 – Scientific Name, Common Name; Taxonomic Hierarchy, Common Name, Identification of larva and adult, Prey, Images, HTML, References, Remarks) and Parasitoids (231 – Scientific Name and the original species pages), with all the original pictures and pages.
- **Crop Pests, Natural Enemies & Pollinators** (Digitized Inventory of Crop Pests, Natural Enemies & Pollinators), complete, with the two copies of the original merged (all pollinator records, pictures and pages): **Pests** (4,634: taxonomy, common names, distribution map, diagnostic features, host details with level of infestation, stages attacked and natural enemies, remarks and references, pictures, information documents), **Vectors**, **Natural Enemies** (distribution and insect/pest, host plant details) and **Pollinators** (associated crops), every search, and the **Links** window (pests, vectors, natural enemies, pollinators and host crops linked to each other).
- **Laboratory Animals** (Digitized Inventory of Laboratory Animal Resources), complete: Common Names and Scientific Names (23 animals, 173 strains), the List of Strains, Uses (criteria search), Lab Source; the strain buttons Nomenclature, Origin, Genetic Profiles (with their original italics and superscripts), Colour, Uses, Laboratory (map), Images and HTML; Literature (132 books with covers, search by category), the Video Clips film (67 minutes), Nomenclature Rules, Guide Lines, Suppliers, Lab Equipments and Buyer's Guide.
- **Silk Worms** (Digitized Inventory of Silkworm Resources – Bharatiya Resham), complete: the opening picture and main screen (About us, Foreword, Prelude, Preface, Credits and the six pictures), Silkworms ▸ Mulberry (bivoltine, multivoltine), Tropical Tasar, Oak Tasar, Eri, Muga ▸ Information and Breeds / Hybrids (108 breeds, each with its Origin, Egg, Larva, Cocoon, Pupa, Moth, Reeling and Images pages), the Search Form for each silkworm, and the twelve films of the Movies menu.
- **Marine Resources** (Digitized Inventory of Marine Bio-Resources), complete: 4,350 species – Scientific Name, Common Name, Search by Habitat and by State, and the nine buttons Taxonomic Hierarchy, Synonyms & Common Names, Distribution, Salient Features, Major Publications, Images (5,700 pictures), References, List of Indian Experts and HTML (641 species pages). The notes the original opened in Notepad/WordPad open in a new window.
- **Plant Resources – Sasya Sampada** (Medicinal & Economically Important Plants), new in 1.10.0, complete: Species Details ▸ Vernacular Names/Common Names, Scientific Names, Species List By Major Category, Criteria Search through DataBase and through Webpages; and the ten buttons for a plant – Taxonomic Hierarchy, Common Names, Synonym Data (the floras that list it), Distribution Data (the thematic grid map), HTML Files (2,600 original plant pages), Images, Accession Details, Uses and Actions, Pest Links and Patents (26,900 patents). 1,956 plants.
- Microbial Resources opens in the next update (a message says so for now).
