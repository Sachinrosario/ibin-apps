## Jeeva Sampada – 1.0.0

Biodiversity databases of India under one roof: the 2012 Jeeva Sampada program (DBT assembling project) rebuilt without Windows 7-era components. Runs on Windows, Mac and Ubuntu, in a browser, and offline.

- **Species by place.** Choose a state and district: groups recorded there (plants, microbes, crop pests, vectors, silkworms, amphibians, lizards, snakes, turtles, birds, mammals, domestic animals, finfishes, crabs, prawns, molluscs) and the species list of each, with CSV download.
- **Find a species.** Search by genus or species across every group; see the states and districts where it was recorded.
- **Medicinal plants.** 1,997 plants with local and English names, synonyms and 11,306 patents (770 plants linked to patents).
- **Databases.** The page for the 12 separate databases (Sasya Sampada, Pest, Insects, Silkworm, Microbial, Birds, Amphibia-Mammals-Reptiles, Finfishes and shellfishes, Marine, Domestic animals, Laboratory animals, Crop pests); they are added in later updates.
- All groups: 21,814 species names and 225,326 place records in all. State names were made uniform; records are otherwise as in the original.
