## Andaman Mapping – Version 2 – Updated (2.0.0)

The 2015 Andaman Mapping program (Andaman Resource Assessment and Mapping, School of Ecology and Conservation, UAS Bangalore) with current plant names, a search-first design that works on phones, and GIS export. Runs on Windows, Mac and Ubuntu, in a browser, and offline.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **Current plant names.** All names were checked against the GBIF Backbone (Catalogue of Life) on 4 November 2026: 1,213 confirmed, 309 updated to the current accepted name (the 2015 name is kept in brackets beside it everywhere, and still works in search), 340 left unchanged for a taxonomist to check, and 351 genus-level entries ("… spp") not matched. See the **What's changed** page.
- **Search first.** One search box finds plants by scientific name, family, local name in any language (35,000 names), synonym (20,000) or habit.
- **Works on phones and tablets**, and offline.
- **One page per plant:** picture, names, local names by language, synonyms with the floras that use them, flier, where it grows, other species of the genus.
- **A map you can drag and pinch:** area shortcuts, species-count maps by habit, grid selection with the list of plants recorded.
- **GIS export:** the grids of one plant or of selected grids as GeoJSON, KML or CSV (with longitude and latitude) for QGIS, ArcGIS and Google Earth; a CSV list of the plants in selected grids.

### Pictures
One representative picture of each plant is shown. **For a better experience, download all the images from Google Drive:** the 19,000 original photographs and the fliers are in the IBIN data repository (link inside the app).
