## Eastern Ghats Mapping – Version 2 – Updated (2.0.0)

The 2015 Eastern Ghats Mapping program (Eastern Ghat Resource Assessment and Mapping) with current plant names, a search-first design that works on phones, and GIS export. Runs on Windows, Mac and Ubuntu, in a browser, and offline.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **Current plant names** (Catalogue of Life via GBIF, 2026): 1,956 confirmed, 1,048 updated (the 2015 name stays in brackets and still works in search), 591 left unchanged for a taxonomist to check, 73 genus-level entries not matched. See **What's changed**.
- **IUCN Red List status**, **current synonyms**, **common names** and **DNA-barcode counts** (GenBank rbcL, matK, ITS, trnH-psbA; BOLD) on each plant page.
- Search by scientific name, family, local name, synonym or habit; works on phones and offline.
- A draggable map with states, districts and taluks, species-count maps and GIS export (GeoJSON, KML, CSV).

Distribution records, pictures and fliers stay as in 2015. Version 1 (original screens) stays available.
