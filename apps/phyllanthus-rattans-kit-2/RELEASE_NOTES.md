**Version 2 – Updated (2.0.0).** The 2010 Indian Barcode of Life kit for *Phyllanthus* and rattans, updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario. Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah.

What is new compared with Version 1 – Original:
- Species names checked against the Catalogue of Life (release of 14 October 2026); current names shown first, each 2010 name kept beside it. Search finds both.
- GenBank (NCBI) barcode records for every species, by marker (rbcL, matK, ITS, psbA-trnH), searched under the 2010, Catalogue of Life and NCBI names.
- Guided identification that suggests the best next character; same identification results as 2010.
- India distribution map of all 1,117 localities, with GIS export (GeoJSON, KML, CSV).
- Side-by-side species comparison, illustrated glossary, phone layout and dark mode.

Installs beside Version 1 – Original; both can be used on the same computer.
