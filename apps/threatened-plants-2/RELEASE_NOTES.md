## Threatened Plants of Western Ghats – Version 2 – Updated (2.0.0)

An updated edition of the **RET Kit, A Field Guide to Threatened Plants of Western Ghats** (Prof. K.N. Ganeshaiah, School of Ecology and Conservation, University of Agricultural Sciences, Bangalore; ©2015–2016). It has a new design, current names and conservation status, and 77 more threatened species. Version 1 (the original 2015 screens) is still available separately.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **178 species:**
  - the 101 species of 2015;
  - 77 more that the IUCN Red List now lists as threatened (11 Critically Endangered, 34 Endangered, 32 Vulnerable). Each is native only to India or to India and Sri Lanka, and most of its records come from the Western Ghats.
- **Names checked in Catalogue of Life (October 2026):**
  - 12 species renamed; the old name is shown in brackets and still works in search;
  - 18 family names corrected or updated;
  - where Kew's POWO uses a different name, it is noted on the species page.
- **Conservation status:**
  - **IUCN:** the current category, year and link for each species; 60 categories changed or are new since 2015.
  - **FRLHT/TDU:** the CAMP category for each state (Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra), with the year of assessment.
  - **BSI:** the Red Data Book status (1987–90), labelled as such. BSI has published no newer list.
- **Species no longer threatened:** 11 species are no longer in a threatened category on any list. They stay in the key and are marked.
- **Identification:**
  - Pick characters on the tree. Values within one character combine with OR; values across characters combine with AND.
  - A species with no value recorded for a character is not ruled out by it.
  - "Close matches" shows species that differ in just one character.
  - "Most useful next" suggests the character that splits the remaining species best.
- **Species pages:**
  - the original species sheet;
  - 313 credited field photos (Wikimedia Commons and iNaturalist contributors);
  - every character;
  - status now and in 2015;
  - name sources.
- **"What's changed" page:** lists every rename, family change, status change, added species and species no longer threatened.
- **Feedback:** opens an e-mail to sachinrosario@gmail.com with your choices attached.
- **Offline use:** works offline after the first visit, and on phones.

### Please note
The characters of the 77 added species were filled from published descriptions:
- eFlora of India;
- biotik (Western Ghats trees);
- India Biodiversity Portal;
- Flowers of India;
- original papers.

Each species page names its sources and asks for verification. Characters a description does not give are shown as "not recorded". Eleven species are listed mainly for their status, because few of their characters have been published.
