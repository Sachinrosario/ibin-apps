## Pathanga Suchya – Version 4 – Updated (4.0.0)

The 2026 update of Pathanga Suchya, the guide to the butterflies of Bangalore by Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah (GKVK). It uses the same data as Version 3 (the faithful rebuild of the 2018 program), which stays available beside it.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **New design** in leaf green and marigold, using the original seasonality colours. It works on phones as well as computers. The butterfly lands on the identification screen when it opens.
- **Guided identification key**: tap a part of the butterfly to answer. Each answer shows how many butterflies would be left, and the app suggests the *best next* question. Characters every remaining butterfly shares are marked for you.
- **Catalogue of Life names** (release 2026-10-14) for all 120 butterflies. Where a name changed, the 2007 name is kept in brackets:
  - 99 names are unchanged;
  - 9 names changed genus or spelling standard;
  - 4 misspellings were corrected;
  - 3 missing names were added;
  - 5 names are not in the Catalogue of Life and were kept.
- **When to look for it**: month-by-month abundance for 98 butterflies, read from the original seasonality wheels. A **What flies when** page shows what is common in any month.
- **Butterfly pages** with all photos (credited), life cycle, host plants and *similar butterflies* to compare.
- **Search** by common name, current scientific name or 2007 name.
- **Quick find** by spots, patches and eye-spot rings, with plain-language labels.
