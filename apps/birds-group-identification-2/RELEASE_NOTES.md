## Birds Group Identification – Version 2 – Updated (2.0.0)

Version 2 turns the bird group key into a field tool. Every identification rule of the original is kept, and the following are added.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **Probabilities you can see.** Each bird group shows its chance as a percentage. The original only sorted the list.
- **Forgiving ranking.** One wrong or unsure answer lowers a group instead of removing it, and "Don't know" really skips a question.
- **Best next question.** The app points to the part of the bird whose answer would narrow the list most.
- **Why?** Each group lists which of your answers fit it and which do not.
- **Group pages** for all 222 groups, each with:
  - credited photographs from iNaturalist and Wikimedia Commons;
  - the group's species recorded in India (1,432 species across the 222 groups), with Catalogue of Life names (the older name shown in brackets where it has changed) and IUCN status;
  - records by state and by month;
  - similar groups.
- **Where and when filters.** Choose a state and a month to favour the groups recorded there.
- **Compare** two groups side by side, with the features that usually differ highlighted.
- **Search** by group, species or English name, plus **Undo** and **Start over**.
- **Works on phones**, and keeps working offline once opened.
- **Feet Color** can now be chosen; the original never showed this question.
- **New drawings:** 47 clean drawings replace the original's blank, blurred, watermarked or photo thumbnails. The asked-about part is shown in marigold, for example beak thickness, neck length, size against a sparrow, plain or patterned body and wings, and eye markings. Where a photo was replaced, it is still shown under Examples.
- **Feedback.** "Was this right? Tell us" opens an e-mail with your answers filled in.
