## Western Ghats Mapping – Version 2 – Updated (2.0.1)

2.0.1: a plant page has a new button, "Show all … original images and the complete flier", which fetches them from the public Pictures and Fliers pack on the IBIN Apps releases page (internet needed once; the computer keeps a copy for offline use). Everything else is as before.

The 2015 Western Ghats Mapping program (Western Ghats Resource Assessment and Mapping, School of Ecology and Conservation, UAS Bangalore) with current plant names, a search-first design that works on phones, and GIS export. Runs on Windows, Mac and Ubuntu, in a browser, and offline.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **Current plant names.** All names were checked against the GBIF Backbone (Catalogue of Life) on 6 November 2026: 2,777 confirmed, 1,667 updated to the current accepted name (the 2015 name is kept in brackets beside it everywhere, and still works in search), 381 left unchanged for a taxonomist to check, and 278 genus-level entries ("… sp") not matched. See the **What's changed** page.
- **Search first.** One search box finds plants by scientific name, family, local name in any language (68,895 names), synonym (49,758) or habit.
- **Works on phones and tablets**, and offline.
- **One page per plant:** picture, names, local names by language, synonyms with the floras that use them, flier, where it grows, other species of the genus.
- **A map you can drag and pinch:** area shortcuts, species-count maps by habit, grid selection with the list of plants recorded.
- **GIS export:** the grids of one plant or of selected grids as GeoJSON, KML or CSV (with longitude and latitude) for QGIS, ArcGIS and Google Earth; a CSV list of the plants in selected grids.

### Pictures
One representative picture of each plant is shown, so the app stays small. All the original images (32,012) and fliers are in the separate private **Pictures and Fliers pack** of Version 1.
