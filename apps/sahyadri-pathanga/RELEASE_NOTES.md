## Sahyadri Pathanga (Butterflies of Western Ghats) – Version 1 (1.0.0)

A faithful rebuild of **Butterflies of Western Ghats**. The screens and pictures are the original program's own, and every list, count, map and table comes from its original database.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- **Opening screen** and **Database Recovery Modules**.
- **Political boundary**: click a state name (or double-click the map for the whole Western Ghats) to list its butterfly families, genera and species (Karnataka: 5 families, 154 genera, 283 species).
- **Vegetation**: click one of the 11 habitat buttons to list its butterflies.
- **Gridwise (100 sq km)**: click a grid cell on the map, or enter a longitude and latitude, to list its butterflies and see its state, district and taluk.
- **Thematic maps**: genera, species and conservation value per grid.
- **Images**: the butterflies that have pictures.
- **Text search**: by common name, by state, district and taluk, by scientific name, and by butterfly and host plant.
- **Digitized Inventory** of any family, genus or species: Taxonomic Hierarchy, Maps, Images (butterfly and host plant), Distribution Data, Synonym Data, Common Names and Source.
