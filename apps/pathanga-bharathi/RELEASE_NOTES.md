## Pathanga Bharathi – Version 1 (1.0.0)

A faithful rebuild of **Pathanga Bharathi**, the butterflies-of-India database. The screens are the original program's own screens, and every list, count, map and table comes from its original database.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- **Opening screen** and **Data Recovery Modules**.
- **Political Boundary**: click a state on the map of India to see its butterfly families, genera and species (Entire India: 7 families, 437 genera, 2,043 species).
- **Biotic zone Search**: click one of the 20 biotic zones on the map or in the legend.
- **Textform Search**: by common name, by state and district, by scientific name, and by butterfly and host plant.
- **Digitized Inventory** of any family, genus or species: Taxonomic Hierarchy, state Maps (three colour bands by number of records, region labels, legend), Images, Distribution data (political zone or biotic and habitat zone, with the floras behind each record), Synonym data, Common Names and Flora List.
