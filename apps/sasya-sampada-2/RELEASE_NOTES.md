## Sasya Sampada – Version 2 – Updated names (2.0.0)

Version 1 with the names brought up to date, looking and working like the original. Only these are updated: scientific names (today's Catalogue of Life name in brackets), family, IUCN Red List status, synonyms, common names and plant DNA barcodes (rbcL, matK, ITS, trnH-psbA and BOLD). Distribution, accessions, uses, pests, patents, web pages and pictures stay as in 2012.
