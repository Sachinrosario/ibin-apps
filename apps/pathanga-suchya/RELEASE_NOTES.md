## Pathanga Suchya – Version 3 (3.0.0)

A faithful rebuild of **Pathanga Suchya 3.0** (2018), the butterfly identification program for the butterflies of Bangalore by Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah (GKVK). The screens, wording, pictures and matching rules are taken from the original program.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- **Identification Keys**: antenna, dorsal and ventral wing colour, size, tail, wing margin and wing pattern (eye spots, patches, bands, stripes, wavy markings). It fills in characters that only one value remains for, and has Clear and Previous Selection.
- **Quick finding with patches and spots**: dorsal and ventral, upper and lower wing, by spot colour, patch colours and eye-spot rings counted from the centre.
- **Butterfly View**: 8,582 photographs in 116 species folders. Click any photo to enlarge it.
- **Biology of Butterflies**: Images, Life Cycle (31 species), Host Plants (507 pictures) and Seasonality wheels (98 species).

### Differences from the 2018 program
- **Butterfly View**: in the 2018 package this stops with "File not found", because the database lists photos named like `Baronet-1.jpg` that are not in the folders. Version 3 shows the photos that are actually in each species folder.
- **Help**: the Help button opens the program's own guide page. The original `Patanga Suchya.doc` is not part of the package.
- **Opening screen**: carries the credit lines and the version number.
- **One butterfly name**: *Orsotriaena medus* appears as **Medus Brown**, its current common name. The 2007 database uses an old common name that is now considered offensive. Its photo folder already used the current name.
