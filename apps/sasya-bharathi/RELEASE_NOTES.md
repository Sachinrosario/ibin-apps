## Sasya Bharathi – Version 1 (1.0.0)

A faithful rebuild of **Sasya Bharathi (India)**, 2004-05: "Distribution, Taxonomy & Diversity of Plants of Entire India" (University of Agricultural Sciences, Bangalore). The screens are the original program's own screens, and every list, count, map and table comes from its original database.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- **Opening screen**, **Credits** (team) and **Data Recovery Modules**.
- **Political Boundary**: click a state on the map of India to see its families, genera, species and sub-species / varieties (Plant Richness of Entire India: 316 families, 4,246 genera, 27,058 species).
- **Biotic zone Search**: click one of the 20 biotic zones on the map or in the legend.
- **Gridwise Search**: click a 625 sq km grid cell.
- **Thematic Maps**: number of families, genera and species per taluk.
- **Images**: the 2,674 species with pictures (4,132 pictures).
- **Textform Search**: by common name (language / region), by state and district, or by scientific name.
- **Digitized Inventory** of any family, genus or species: Taxonomic Hierarchy, state Maps (with the Statewise thematic map and places), Images, Distribution data (political zone or biotic and habitat zone, with the floras behind each record), Synonym data, Common Names and Flora List.
- **To List Correct & Synonym Names**: valid, probably valid, synonym, probably synonym and undecided names.

**Small installer:** the Windows, Mac and Ubuntu programs carry the program, the lists, maps and screens. The detail records of each plant (distribution, synonyms, common names, floras; 70 MB) and the 4,132 pictures (160 MB) are fetched from the IBIN site when a plant is opened, so they need an internet connection. The offline zip carries everything.

### Differences from the 2005 program
- The original installer is damaged in two places: the pictures `Caralluma_umbellata1.jpg` (empty in the installer) and `Cyamopsis_tetragonoloba.jpg` cannot be recovered; the Images tab says so. Two more pictures listed in the database were never in the installer.
- Clicking anywhere inside a state (not only on its tree) selects the state; clicking anywhere inside a biotic zone selects the zone.
- The species maps are drawn from the program's own district boundary files instead of the MapX control; Layer Control is not available.
- The opening screen carries the credit lines and the version number.
