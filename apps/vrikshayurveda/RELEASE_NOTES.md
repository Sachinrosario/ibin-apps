## Vrikshayurveda – Version 1 – Original (1.0.0)

The 2011 Vrikshayurveda program rebuilt so that it looks and works as the original did, and now runs on Windows, Mac and Ubuntu, in a browser, and offline.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What it contains (all from the original database)
- **869 verses** of five works on plant science: Brihat Samhita (31), Krishi Parashara (243), Lokopakaram (59), Surapala Vrikshayurveda (299) and Upavanavinoda (237), each with the Sanskrit verse, English meaning and comments.
- **Verse recordings** for every verse (the original .wma files, converted to MP3).
- **Search** the way the original did: by Sanskrit plant name, genus and species, common name in a language, keyword (Action/Properties, Diseases/Disorders, Organisms, Others) or by book and verse number.
- **Species pages** with Taxonomic Hierarchy, Common Names, Distribution, Synonyms and Images (herbarium sheets, line diagrams, paintings and photographs: 3,754 pictures).
- The five source documents and the credits, opened from the first screen.
