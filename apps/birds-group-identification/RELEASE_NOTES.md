## Birds Group Identification (with Prob) – Version 1 – Original (1.0.0)

A faithful rebuild of **Birds Group Identification (with Prob)**. You describe a sitting or a flying bird, and the program ranks 222 bird groups by probability. The screens, wording, pictures and ranking rules are taken from the original program, and were checked against it running on Windows.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- **Sitting/standing or in flight**: choose on the opening window.
- **Click a part of the bird**: crown, eye, beak, neck, size, body, wings, tail, leg or feet. Then pick what you see from the thumbnails, or choose Don't Know. There are 21 characters for a sitting bird and 19 for a flying bird.
- **Groups Matched**: the matching groups, ranked by probability.
  - Several values of one character count as "either".
  - Thumbnails show only the values still possible.
- **Pictures**: the Images button shows the selected group's picture. The blue button shows example photos of a character.
- **Delete, Clear, Back and Home**: work as in the original.

### Differences from the original program
- **Pictures** open in the program's own viewer instead of the Windows photo viewer.
- **Images and Home buttons**: on a full-HD screen, the original hides the main window's Images and Home buttons under the Windows taskbar. Version 1 shows them.
- **Opening window** carries the credit lines and the version number.
