## Birds Identification – Version 1 (1.0.0)

A faithful rebuild of **Birds Identification 1.0** (2018), the species-level bird identification program of the IBIN project (University of Agricultural Sciences, Bangalore, with the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India). The screens, wording, pictures and matching rules are taken from the original program and its own source code.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- **Opening screen** with About Us, Credits, Introduction and Data Profile (the original PDF pages).
- **Sitting / standing** or **in flight / flying** bird: click a part of the bird picture (head, crown, eye, beak, throat, neck, body, wings, tail, legs, feet).
- **Characters** shown as picture thumbnails (colours, shapes, lengths, markings). Click a thumbnail to pick it, click it again to remove it, pick several values of one character, or "Don't Know". The blue button opens example photographs of that character.
- **List of Characters Picked**, **Groups Matched** and **Species Matched** (common name ; sex ; age) update after each click. **Images** shows the photographs of the chosen bird.
- The 2018 database: 420 entries (152 species with male, female and young plumages) of water and sea birds in 24 groups, 1,896 photographs.

**Small installer:** the Windows, Mac and Ubuntu programs carry the program, the database and all character pictures. The 1,896 bird photographs (110 MB) are fetched from the IBIN site the first time **Images** is used, so they need an internet connection. The offline zip carries everything.

### Differences from the 2018 program
- Photographs and the PDF pages open in the program's own viewer window instead of the Windows photo viewer and Adobe Reader. The viewer steps through all photographs of the bird, as the Windows photo viewer did for the folder.
- A few photograph folders hold PNG, GIF or WebP pictures, which the 2018 program could not open (it looked for JPG files only); they are shown.
- The opening screen carries the credit lines and the version number.
