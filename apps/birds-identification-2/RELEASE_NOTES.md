## Birds Identification – Version 2 – Updated names (2.0.0)

The same program as Version 1 – the same screens, buttons, thumbnails and matching rules – with the scientific names, threat status and DNA records brought up to date. Nothing of the 2018 content is removed: the new information is shown in brackets.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **Species Matched** shows each bird with its current scientific name in brackets, for example *Black Bittern (Botaurus flavicollis) ; Male ; Adult*.
- **Groups Matched** shows the family in brackets, for example *Ducks (Anatidae)*.
- Under the photographs (**Images**) a strip gives: the scientific name used in 2018 and the current name, family and order, the IUCN Red List status recorded in 2018 and the status now, and DNA records – the number of GenBank sequences and COI barcodes, with links to GenBank and BOLD Systems.

### How the data were updated (November 2026)
- **Names**: every one of the 151 scientific names was matched against the Catalogue of Life (ChecklistBank, latest release). 37 names have changed; the rest are still current. Four names in the 2018 database are misspelt and were matched by their correct spelling.
- **IUCN Red List status**: from the IUCN Red List as published in GBIF; two birds that GBIF files under an older name were checked by hand (both Least Concern). Now: Least Concern (LC) 118, Near Threatened (NT) 15, Vulnerable (VU) 10, Critically Endangered (CR) 5, Endangered (EN) 3.
- **DNA records**: GenBank (NCBI) counts of all sequences and of COI barcode sequences for each species (131 of 151 species have COI barcodes). BOLD Systems is linked for each species.
- The full list is in `name-changes.csv` in the source folder of this program.
