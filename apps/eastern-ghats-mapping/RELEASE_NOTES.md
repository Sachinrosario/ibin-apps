## Eastern Ghats Mapping – Version 1 – Original (1.0.0)

The 2015 program "Eastern Ghat Resource Assessment and Mapping" (Distribution, Taxonomy & Diversity of Plants of Eastern Ghats; Biodiversity Conservation Division, Sri Krishnadevaraya University, Anantapur, with partner institutions) rebuilt so that it looks and works as the original did, without MapInfo. The screens are the original program's own screens and pictures, and every list, count, map and table comes from its original database.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is inside
- 3,668 plant species in 246 families, 4,093 map grids, 64,874 common names, 11,134 synonyms, 165,829 distribution records.
- **Opening screen** and **Credits** (the Eastern Ghats team and the team photograph).
- **Gridwise Search**: click map grids (or drag a box) to list the species recorded there, by habit (trees, herbs, shrubs, epiphytes, regeneration).
- **Thematic Maps**: ten maps (Trees, Herbs, Shrubs, Epiphytes, Regeneration, Total species, Disturbance index, Developmental threat, Harvest threat index, Total threat) with the original's class limits and shades.
- **Textform Search**: by genus and species, by habit, or by common name in any of the languages recorded.
- **Digitized Inventory** of each species: Taxonomic Hierarchy, Distribution map, Images, Fliers, Distribution data, Synonym data and Common Names.
- Map layers as in the original: Grids, States, Districts, Taluks, Roads, Railroads, Places.
- Zoom in, zoom out, move, Info (grid details) and Entire layer tools.

### Pictures
- One representative picture per species (2,015 species) and the first slide of each flier (815 species) are built in.
- All 27,738 original images and the complete fliers (1,656 slides) are in the public **Pictures and Fliers pack** on the IBIN Apps releases page. The Windows, Mac and Ubuntu programs fetch each picture from it the first time it is shown (internet needed once) and keep a copy.

### Checked before release
Every screen and button was tested on a computer screen and on a phone screen: 34 checks (opening screen, credits, every search, every tab of the Digitized Inventory, grid selection, every map tool and layer, all ten thematic maps).

### Differences from the 2015 program
- Maps are drawn by the program itself from the original MapInfo layers; MapInfo is not needed. One district edge that the original stores as a line is drawn as a line.
- The flier folders of the original installer also hold fliers of Western Ghats species that are not in the Eastern Ghats database; only the 815 Eastern Ghats species' fliers are shown.
- The opening and credits screens carry the credit lines and the version number.
