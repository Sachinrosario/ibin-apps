## Vrikshayurveda – Version 2 – Updated (2.0.0)

Version 2 turns the 2011 program into a search-first reference. Every verse, plant and picture of Version 1 is kept.

Proprietary right holder: Dr. K.N. Ganeshaiah · Updated in 2026 by Dr. Sachin A. Rosario

### What is new
- **One search box** over verses, plants, Sanskrit names, subjects and keywords. Type in English letters (arishta) or Devanagari, or jump with "Surapala 12".
- **Current plant names.** All scientific names of the 2011 database were looked up in the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy (built on the Catalogue of Life). The 2011 name stays in brackets and is searchable. Names that the lookup answered doubtfully are marked "name under review" and keep the 2011 name until a taxonomist checks them (listed on the About page).
- **Verse pages** with the Sanskrit and English text, comments, subject, keywords and the plants named, with recitation (play, seek, speed, repeat), previous/next and a copyable citation.
- **Exact verse-to-plant links.** Version 1 matched verse "3" inside "13" and "23", as the 2011 program did; Version 2 matches the exact verse.
- **Plant pages** with all verses, Sanskrit names (Devanagari and English letters), common names by language, classification, a distribution map of India with the records by state, synonyms with the floras that publish them, and pictures with a full-screen viewer.
- **Browse** by book, by subject (138) and by keyword (806, grouped as organisms, actions, diseases, others), and the 428 Sanskrit plant names.
- Works on phones and tablets, in light or dark, and offline.
